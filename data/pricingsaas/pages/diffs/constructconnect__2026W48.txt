What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of ConstructConnect's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter annual billing now shown as $117/mo (12% discount on annual terms) alongside $129/mo monthly; previously 'Save 10% when billed annually'. In detail: Project Intelligence Starter now lists an annual price of $117/mo next to $129/mo billed monthly, labelled a "12% discount on annual terms" (the actual gap is ~9%); previously the card said "Save 10% when billed annually" with no annual price shown. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $129, Professional at $199 and Premium at $10,000.
It lands the same day as ConstructConnect's previous change, "ConstructConnect Starter now $129/mo, annual billing saves 12%" (Nov 22, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ConstructConnect puts $10,000/yr floor on Project Intelligence Premium" (Oct 4, 2026), "ConstructConnect publishes Project Intelligence pricing from $10,000/yr" (Oct 4, 2026) and "ConstructConnect reveals Premium price: starting at $10,000/year" (Oct 4, 2026). ConstructConnect has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
