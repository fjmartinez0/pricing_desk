What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Wodify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Per-plan prices now shown on each card (list price struck through, $100/month-off promo price shown): Essentials $199 → $99/mo, Accelerate $299 → $199/mo, Ultimate $499 → $399/mo. Previously cards only said '$100/month off for life' and the headline said 'starting at $199 $99/month'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $179.
It lands 69 days after Wodify's previous change, "Wodify: Starting price increased $79→$99/month per location" (Aug 4, 2026). Wodify has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
