What happened
Between the Nov 12, 2026 and Nov 13, 2026 captures of OpenAI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free tagline 'Intelligence for everyday tasks' -> 'Best for trying out ChatGPT'; Go 'Expanded access' -> 'Best for longer conversations'; Plus 'Do more with advanced intelligence' -> 'Best for advanced work and productivity'; Pro 'Maximize your productivity' -> 'Best for research and coding'. In detail: All 4 plan taglines reworded to audience-focused framing: Free->'trying out ChatGPT', Go->'longer conversations', Plus->'advanced work & productivity', Pro->'research and coding'. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Go at $8, Plus at $20, Business at $20 and Pro at $100.
It lands 2 days after OpenAI's previous change, "OpenAI reframes all four plan taglines with new Best for use-case pitches" (Nov 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenAI: all four ChatGPT plan taglines rewritten to 'Best for...' framing" (Nov 8, 2026), "OpenAI refreshes Free, Go, Plus and Pro taglines with benefit-led copy" (Nov 7, 2026) and "OpenAI: Pro plan adds up to $500/month in API credits" (Oct 30, 2026). OpenAI has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Anthropic, X, Metabase, Microsoft and Google also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
