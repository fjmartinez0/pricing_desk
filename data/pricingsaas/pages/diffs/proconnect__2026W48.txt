What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of ProConnect's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 10+ returns volume tier added to pay-per-return: individual $84.96 (list $99.95), business $101.96 (list $119.95); pay-as-you-go range narrowed from 1–49 to 1–9 returns. Existing 15% returns / up-to-35% bundle promo unchanged (now also promoted in a top banner). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered). The current page lists ProConnect Essentials 50 at $308.33, ProConnect Essentials 100 at $496.42, ProConnect Plus at $733.50, ProConnect Advanced at $1,066 and Pay-per-return - Individual at $109.95 per individual return.
It lands 21 days after ProConnect's previous change, "ProConnect pay-per-return 50+ list price ticks up to $84.95" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ProConnect rolls out ~15% discounts, cutting Individual to $93.46 per return" (Oct 26, 2026). ProConnect has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
In the same window Microsoft, Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
