What happened
Between the Oct 15, 2026 and Oct 16, 2026 captures of Krea's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New limited-time offer: 14 days of Unlimited Krea 3 fast generations (1K/2K) with a qualifying Pro or Max annual purchase; Pro and Max cards now show a '14 days of Unlimited K3' perk. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 23, 2026) lists Basic at $5, Pro at $21, Max at $63 and Business at $160.
It lands 21 days after Krea's previous change, "Krea adds Recraft V4.1 Flash with up to 7,714 images on Max plan" (Sep 25, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Krea slashes MiniMax H3 Max video quotas 4x across all paid tiers" (Sep 21, 2026), "Krea cuts MiniMax H3 Max and Turbo video limits 4x on Basic, Pro, Max, Business" (Sep 17, 2026) and "Krea quadruples MiniMax H3 Max video quota on paid plans, up to 864" (Sep 2, 2026).
