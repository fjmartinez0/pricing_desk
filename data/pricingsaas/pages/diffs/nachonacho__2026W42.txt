What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of NachoNacho's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Free NachoPay add-on introduced on Basic and Basic+: after a 5-minute KYC, up to 5 virtual cards, subscription/transaction insights and Shaman AI recommendations (previously Premium-only features); Basic Plus plan renamed Basic+ (same $199/year price); Basic plan no longer lists 'Access to Services Marketplace' or 'Write reviews for SaaS products and professional service providers'; Basic+ no l. In detail: Basic+ no longer includes 'Unlimited team members'; multi-user management now listed only on Premium ('Manage multiple users and companies'). Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, plan renamed, feature removed) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Basic Plus at $199 and Premium at $199.
It lands 101 days after NachoNacho's previous change, "NachoNacho: Basic Plus and Premium annual fee increased $129 -> $199 (+54%)" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "NachoNacho raises annual plan prices 54% to $199/year" (Jun 23, 2026). NachoNacho has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Holded, Threecolts, Shippo, Tatsoft and Smokeball also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
