What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Airwallex's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Yield returns on USD balances increased across all plans: Explore 3.45%→3.48%, Grow 3.60%→3.63%, Accelerate 3.72%→3.75%. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Grow at $12.
It lands 14 days after Airwallex's previous change, "Airwallex launches corporate card credit line (now on free Explore), SSO GA, adds 0.4% automatic tax; Multi-entity billing moves to Accelerate" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Airwallex: Yield returns increased across all plan tiers (3.30→3.42% + more)" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Airwallex: Yield returns increased across all plan tiers (3.21→3.30% + more)" (Sep 21, 2026). Airwallex has been tracked since 2023-12-01.
