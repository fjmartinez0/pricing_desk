Syncfusion bundles all 7 UI SDKs into $1,199 suite, adds Business Support
Verified Sep 14, 2026·Software Development & Testing·Direction: up
The changeUI Component Suite feature — feature added.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 7, 2026 → Sep 14, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Syncfusion's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: UI Component Suite now bundles all 7 Standalone UI SDKs (previously only Grid, Chart, and File Manager) at the same $1,199/developer/year price; every price caption now also states it includes Business Support. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists PDF Viewer SDK at $49.92, Grid SDK at $79.92, Chart SDK at $79.92, Scheduler SDK at $79.92 and Gantt SDK at $79.92.
It lands 63 days after Syncfusion's previous change, "Syncfusion splits UI Suite into 7 standalone SDKs — Grid, Chart, Diagram now " (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Syncfusion Sales splits pricing, adds 7 standalone contact-sales UI SDKs" (Jul 13, 2026). Syncfusion has been tracked since 2026-05-06.
In the same window GitHub, Unity, Stedi and Spline also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
