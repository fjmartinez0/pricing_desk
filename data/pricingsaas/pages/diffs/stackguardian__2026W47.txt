What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of StackGuardian's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pricing page rebuilt: adds Resilience policy pack and cross-region recovery (early access), migration credits for Pro/Enterprise, Founding 50 early-access program, marketplace billing, free Standard plan for startups/OSS/non-profits; Enterprise limits (5,000 SGCredits, 100 cloud accounts, 75 active workflows) now shown as Custom; pay-as-you-grow $0.20/credit replaced by $200 per 1,000 AI credits a. In detail: Pricing page rebuilt: adds Resilience policy pack (Standard+) and recovery/rollback/cross-region recovery (Pro+, early access), migration credits for Pro/Enterprise, Founding 50 early-access program, free Standard plan for startups/OSS/non-profits; Enterprise included limits (5,000 SGCredits, 100 cloud accounts, 75 active workflows) replaced by Custom, sized per organization. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, limit lowered). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 18, 2026) lists Standard at $359, Pro at $1,459 and Enterprise at $0.20 SGCredit + $1.12 cloud account per day.
