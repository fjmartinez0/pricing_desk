What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Roboflow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Roboflow Agent' capability added to the all-plans feature list: goes from idea to a deployed vision solution automatically. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Core at $39, Additional Prepaid Credits at $4 per prepaid credit, Additional On-Demand Credits at $6 per on-demand credit and Data Labeling Services at $0.05 per classification/keypoint annotation + $0.2 per polygon annotation.
It lands 21 days after Roboflow's previous change, "Roboflow drops Community Support/Forum from Public, Core plans" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Roboflow: Core plan credit allocation doubled (180→360/yr) + Free tier credits redenominated" (Jul 27, 2026). Roboflow has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
