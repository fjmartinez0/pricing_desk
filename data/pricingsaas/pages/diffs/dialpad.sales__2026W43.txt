What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Dialpad's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Sell Advanced $95→$59/user/month (-38%) and Premium $150→$79/user/month (-47%); Essentials unchanged at $39. Sell benefit bullets rewritten for all 3 tiers — Advanced/Premium now bundle Reach Booster, Live Coach, Quality Management as included; Essentials lists unlimited domestic minutes, AI transcripts/recaps/PII redaction, Coaching Teams, 1-yr retrieval, Meetings Essential. In detail: Dialpad Sell benefit bullets rewritten for all 3 tiers: Advanced/Premium now explicitly bundle previously-separate capabilities as "included" (Reach Booster, Live Coach, Quality Management incl. AI CSAT/Scorecards/Playbooks/Coaching Hub); Essentials bullets replaced with unlimited domestic minutes, AI transcripts/recaps/PII redaction, Coaching Teams, 1-yr retrieval, Meetings Essential; Dialpad Sell: Advanced plan cut from $95 to $59/user/month (-38%) and Premium cut from $150 to $79/user/month (-47%). Essentials unchanged at $39/user/month. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature change, price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $39, Advanced at $59 and Premium at $79.
Dialpad's previous recorded change was "Dialpad Sales Enhances Advanced Plan with Voicemail Drop Feature for Increased Efficiency" (Q2 2025). Dialpad has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
