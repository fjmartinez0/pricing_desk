What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of LeadStal's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan's "Unlimited Sending Credits" now carries a disclosed fair-use cap of 2,000,000 sending credits per billing period. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered). The current page lists Pro at $8, Advanced at $28, Enterprise at $79.17, Elite Credits at $90 per use and Starter Credits at $5 per use.
It lands 119 days after LeadStal's previous change, "LeadStal: Unlimited Warmup removed from all paid plans" (Jul 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LeadStal swaps credit-scraping tiers for AI outreach subscriptions" (Jun 16, 2026). LeadStal has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Microsoft, ActiveCampaign, Zoho, HubSpot and Salesforce also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
