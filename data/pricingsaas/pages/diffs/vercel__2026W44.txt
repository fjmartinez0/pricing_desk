What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Vercel's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Vercel Drives usage section (Drive Storage, Reads, Writes) and a Deployment Storage row added to the usage pricing tables; Vercel Firewall limits cut: Enterprise Custom Firewall Rules 1,000→250; IP Blocking Hobby 3→10, Pro 100→40, Enterprise 1,000→250; System Bypass Rules Pro 25→10, Enterprise 100→75. Before that Pulse recorded "Vercel slashes Firewall limits, adds metered Deployment Storage at $0.10/GB" (Oct 26, 2026), "Vercel adds Daemons with usage-based Active CPU and Memory pricing" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Vercel launches usage-based Daemons billing at $0.128/hr Active CPU" (Oct 16, 2026).
