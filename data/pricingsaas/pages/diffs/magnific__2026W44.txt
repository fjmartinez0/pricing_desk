What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Magnific's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Premium annual 50% off promo (ends Oct 26): $14.50 to $7.25/mo billed annually, list $20; annual toggle savings badge 25% to up to 63%; Kling 4.0 1080p (840 credits/3s) and Kling 4.0 1080p with video input (990 credits/3s) added to AI video model list. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Premium at $14.5, Premium+ at $33.75, Business at $55, Pro Starter at $82.5 and Pro at $210.
It lands 15 days after Magnific's previous change, "Magnific launches Magnific One image model, unlimited on paid plans to Oct 15" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Magnific Premium annual price doubles to $14.50/mo as 50% promo ends" (Oct 5, 2026), "Magnific slashes Premium annual price 50% to $7.25/mo before Sept 28 deadline" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Magnific swaps MiniMax H3 2K for Max Turbo 768p, renames Pro to Pro Starter" (Sep 14, 2026). Magnific has been tracked since 2026-06-04.
