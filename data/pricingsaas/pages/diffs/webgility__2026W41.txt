What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Webgility's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Backend order overage in plan comparison table changed from $50/mo per 100 extra orders to $15/mo per 500 orders, a much lower per-order rate. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $69, Advanced at $129, Complete at $299, Complete Enterprise at $599 and Books Done Add-on at $350.
It lands 42 days after Webgility's previous change, "Webgility adds $69 Pro plan, cuts Advanced overage fees, drops 3 add-ons" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Webgility: Books Done renamed; new $150/mo Quarterly Commerce Review add-on" (Jul 27, 2026). Webgility has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
In the same window Cin7, Shopify, Microsoft and Trace also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
