Sendcloud: pre-Oct-2025 grandfathering disclaimer removed from pricing page
Verified Sep 21, 2026·Business Operations·Direction: neutral
The changePositioning — positioning.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 14, 2026 → Sep 21, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Sendcloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: the note telling pre-Oct-1-2025 subscribers they keep their prior feature set unless they switch to the updated, differently-priced version was removed from the pricing page. In detail: Removed the grandfathering disclaimer stating that subscribers who signed up before Oct 1, 2025 keep their prior feature set unless they switch to the updated (differently priced) version; this legacy-plan carve-out is no longer disclosed on the pricing page. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Lite at EUR 28, Growth at EUR 87, Premium at EUR 175 and Pro at EUR 639.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Sendcloud. Sendcloud has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window EasyPost and Shippo also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
