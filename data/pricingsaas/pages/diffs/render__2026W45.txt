What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Render's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Render launched Sandboxes (Beta): isolated microVMs for agents, apps, and untrusted code, billed at $0.12/active CPU hour plus memory fees across 1-8 CPU tiers, with per-plan concurrency and session-length limits. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $25, Scale at $499, Postgres - 64c-256g at $3,000, Postgres - 64c-512g at $6,000 and Postgres - 128c-1024g at $11,000.
It lands 7 days after Render's previous change, "Render: Workflows comparison rows changed from concurrent task runs (20/50/100, then $10/mo per 50) to parallel task…" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Render Key Value cuts compute prices up to 45% and adds 50 GB tier; Workflows max compute halved" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Render: Workflows compute doubles to 8 CPU / 32 GB RAM" (Sep 28, 2026). Render has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Digitalocean, Railway and Google Cloud also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
