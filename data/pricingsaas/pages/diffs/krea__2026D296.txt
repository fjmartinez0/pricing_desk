What happened
Between the Oct 22, 2026 and Oct 23, 2026 captures of Krea's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed limited-time promo: Unlimited Krea 3 Fast generations for 14 days with a qualifying Pro or Max annual purchase, incl. promo banner countdown and per-plan 14-Day Unlimited Krea 3 callouts. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $5, Pro at $21, Max at $63 and Business at $160.

Before that Pulse recorded "Krea rewrites plan allowance examples: more Nano Banana 2 generations, Seedance 2.5 videos" (Oct 19, 2026), "Krea offers 14 days of unlimited Krea 3 with Pro or Max annual plans" (Oct 16, 2026) and "Krea adds Recraft V4.1 Flash with up to 7,714 images on Max plan" (Sep 25, 2026).
