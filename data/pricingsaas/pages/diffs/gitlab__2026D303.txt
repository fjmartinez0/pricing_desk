What happened
Between the Oct 29, 2026 and Oct 30, 2026 captures of GitLab's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New GitLab Orbit add-on (context graph for the software lifecycle) sold as shared-pool query packs, available for Premium and Ultimate on GitLab.com; Self-Managed/Dedicated early Nov 2026. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Premium at $29, Enterprise Agile Planning at $15, Compute Minutes at $10 per 1,000 minutes, Storage at $5 and GitLab Duo Agent Platform at $1 per GitLab Credit.
It lands 14 days after GitLab's previous change, "GitLab brings Secrets Manager to Self-Managed, Dedicated still pending" (Oct 16, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "GitLab: Flex billing FAQ overhauled — new usage-cap self-service, terms narrowed" (Sep 21, 2026) and "GitLab renames Flex 'per-use' credits to 'on-demand,' drops overage discounts" (Sep 18, 2026). GitLab has been tracked since 2025-10-05.
In the same window GitHub, Google Cloud and JetBrains also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
