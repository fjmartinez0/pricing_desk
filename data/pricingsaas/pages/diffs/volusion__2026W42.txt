What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Volusion's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Business plan $299/month now carries an asterisk: price applies with annual payment; otherwise $399 monthly (previously $299/month with no billing condition).
