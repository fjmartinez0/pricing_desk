What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Picsart's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro and Ultra cards now list trending model access (Seedance 2.5 1080p 30s, Nano Banana Pro 4K, MiniMax H3 2K 15s, Kling 3.0 4K 15s) with resolution and duration caps. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pro at $10.5 and Ultra at $60.
It lands 7 days after Picsart's previous change, "Picsart switches Seedance 2.5 quota from video count to seconds" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Picsart swaps flat daily AI limits for credit caps up to 5000 images" (Sep 7, 2026), "Picsart doubles Pro credits to 500/month, halving Ultra's usage advantage" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Picsart: Pro plan credits cut 500→250/mo; Ultra credit cap raised to 10,000+" (Aug 4, 2026). Picsart has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Corel and Canva also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
