What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Unbounce's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Agency plan changed from 'Let's talk! Get in touch for custom pricing' to 'Starting at $349 USD/month, billed annually' (self-serve price shown; contact sales for custom pricing). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $22, Build at $74, Experiment at $112, Optimize at $187 and Agency at $349.
It lands 14 days after Unbounce's previous change, "Unbounce: AI credits allowances introduced on all plans: Starter 4,000, Build 30,000, Experiment 40,000, Optimize…" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Unbounce adds MCP server support to Starter plan for AI-driven page building" (Aug 17, 2026). Unbounce has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Elementor, Builder.io, Leadpages and Convertkit also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
