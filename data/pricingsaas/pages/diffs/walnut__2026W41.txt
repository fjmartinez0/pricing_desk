What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Walnut's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: BYOK (bring your own LLM key) added as a Scale plan feature and as an available add-on, letting customers run Walnut AI on their own provider account.
