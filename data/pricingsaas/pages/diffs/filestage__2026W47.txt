What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Filestage's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free and Starter cards now state 'incl. 14 days free Business trial'. The 14-day trial already existed in the FAQ; it is now surfaced on the plan cards. Prices are unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Starter at $199 and Business at $329.
It lands 21 days after Filestage's previous change, "Filestage adds reusable Review checklists with real-time item checks" (Oct 26, 2026). Filestage has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Microsoft, Box, Dropbox, Wistia and Google also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
