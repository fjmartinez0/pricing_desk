What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Baseten's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Four new Model APIs models listed with per-1M-token pricing: Beam, Muse Spark Open, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017, Nemotron 3.5 Super VL. Existing model prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 22, 2026) lists Basic at $1.32 DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 input (per 1M tokens) + $0.3 Kimi K3 cache input (per 1M tokens).
It lands one day after Baseten's previous change, "Baseten adds DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 at $0.30 input, $1.20 output per 1M tokens" (Oct 18, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Baseten adds elephant-1 and mammoth-1 models from Base Labs, pricing TBD" (Oct 22, 2026), "Baseten cuts Reflection Beam API rates up to 72%, input now $0.40 per 1M" (Oct 21, 2026) and "Baseten adds Reflection Beam and Meta Muse Spark Open to Model APIs lineup" (Oct 17, 2026). Baseten has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Wodify and Zen Planner also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
