What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Remote's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Global Payroll starting price cut from $29 to $19 per employee per month. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Payroll at $29, Contractor Management at $29, Equity at $39, Contractor Management Plus at $99 and Professional Employer Organization at $99.
It lands 14 days after Remote's previous change, "Remote ends startup promo: 15% off EOR for early-stage companies" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Remote cuts Employer of Record price to $599, launches startup discount" (Oct 19, 2026). Remote has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Slack, Microsoft, Asana, Zoom and Google also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
