What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Jumpcloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: JumpCloud PKI added as a new row in the Zero Trust section of the package feature table (a la carte pricing $3.00/$4.00 per markdown). In detail: JumpCloud PKI added as a new row in the Zero Trust section of the feature table, included in Platform Prime only (not Platform Essentials or Platform); also sold à la carte at $3.00/user/mo billed annually or $4.00 billed monthly. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Device Management at $9, SSO at $11, Device Identity Management at $13, Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) (À La Carte) at $3 and Password Management (À La Carte) at $3.
It lands 129 days after Jumpcloud's previous change, "JumpCloud: New Agentic IAM feature + Access Requests add-on ($5/mo)" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Jumpcloud rolls out Agentic IAM across all tiers, adds $5 Access Requests" (May 5, 2026), "JumpCloud: plan lineup restructured into three Platform packages" (Apr 3, 2026) and "Jumpcloud Hides Pricing for Platform and Platform Prime Plans, Renames Platform Tier to Enterprise" (Q2 2025). Jumpcloud has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Ping Identity, Duo and OneLogin also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
