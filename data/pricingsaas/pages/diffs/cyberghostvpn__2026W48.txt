What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of CyberGhost VPN's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 2 Years + 4 Months plan cut from $2.19/mo to $1.99/mo (billed $61.32 → $55.72); Early Access 83% promo replaced by Black Friday 85% off. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists CyberGhost VPN 6 Months at $6.99, CyberGhost VPN 1 Month at $12.99 and CyberGhost VPN 2 Years at $61.32.
It lands 14 days after CyberGhost VPN's previous change, "CyberGhost VPN extends multi-year plan to 2 years+4 months, bill hits $61.32" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CyberGhost VPN: 2-Year plan price increase ($1.99→$2.19/mo)" (Sep 27, 2026), "CyberGhost: 26-month plan price cut $2.19→$1.99/mo, discount to 85%" (Sep 14, 2026) and "CyberGhost VPN cuts 2-year plan to $2.19/mo, ups Campus Deal to 83% off" (Aug 31, 2026). CyberGhost VPN has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window ExpressVPN, Bitdefender, NordVPN and Surfshark also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
