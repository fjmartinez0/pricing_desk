What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of LeadMagic's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New credit top-ups priced above plan rate ($0.024/$0.020/$0.018/$0.016 per credit); extra users $20/mo and Tables editors $49.99/mo now sold, with per-plan included seats (1/3/5/10); Daily export limits doubled on every plan: Essential 10k to 20k, Growth 25k to 50k, Professional 50k to 100k, Ultimate 100k to 200k rows per day. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essential at $82.50, Growth at $207.50, Professional at $415.83, Ultimate at $707.50 and Extra Users at $20.
It lands 7 days after LeadMagic's previous change, "LeadMagic drops $49 team seat fee, adds rate limits of 300 to 1,500 RPM by plan" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LeadMagic now charges per search result, cuts shared seats to 1-3 per plan" (Oct 19, 2026) and "LeadMagic overhauls seats: Basic to 1, Essential's "20" cuts to 3" (Aug 31, 2026). LeadMagic has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Seamless.AI and Lusha also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
