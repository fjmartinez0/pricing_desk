What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Opensense's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Email signature plans drop the separate platform license: 'Plans include a $500/month minimum and a platform license' → 'Plans have a $500/month minimum'; FAQ now states there is no separate required platform license charge. Per-user prices ($1/$3/$5) and $500/mo minimum unchanged; Integrations opened up to lower plans: Signature gains HR System integrations, SIEM, Custom SSO, Marketing automation. In detail: Integrations opened up to lower plans: Signature gains HR System integrations, SIEM, Custom SSO, Marketing automation data integrations and CRM user data/activity writeback; Pipeline gains Marketing automation data integrations and CRM writeback (previously Complete-only). New 'Included in every email signature plan' section; CRM writeback also now on all sales-tracking plans (Bronze, Silver, Gold — was Gold only); Support Hotline email support changed from '4 hour response time with front of queue priority' to '24/7 support; response-time commitments follow your contract' (all email signature plans). Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, feature change, packaging change). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 18, 2026) lists Signature at $1, Pipeline at $3, Complete at $5, Bronze at $20 and Silver at $55.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Opensense. Opensense has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
