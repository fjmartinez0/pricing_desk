What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Tempo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Every team-size tier of Tempo Timesheets' per-user pricing rose ~10-15%. Default (100 users) went from $521/mo ($5.21/user) to $599/mo ($5.99/user); the smallest 1-10 user tier changed from $1/user to a new $10.00 flat fee. In detail: Tempo Timesheets raised per-user pricing across every team-size tier by roughly 10-15%. The default/average price rose from $521/mo ($5.21/user) to $599/mo ($5.99/user); the 1-10 user tier changed from $1/user to a $10.00 flat fee. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Timesheets Data Center at $3.8 and Timesheets Cloud at $4.34.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Tempo. Tempo has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
