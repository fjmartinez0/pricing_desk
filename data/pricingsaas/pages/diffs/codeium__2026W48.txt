What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Windsurf's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Teams plan no longer lists the $40/month per full dev seat on top of the $80/month team plan fee; card now shows a flat $80 per month; Access to Devin Bots added to Max and Teams plan cards and as a new Devin Bots row in the compare table. In detail: Access to Devin Bots added to the Max and Teams plan cards, a Devin Bots bullet added to the Enterprise card, and a new Devin Bots row in the compare table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change, feature added). The current page lists Pro at $20, Teams at $80 and Max at $200.
It lands 56 days after Windsurf's previous change, "Windsurf caps Teams plan at 200 users, ending unlimited seats at $40/user" (Sep 27, 2026). Windsurf has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
