What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of PostHog's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free-tier allowance product 'Data warehouse' renamed to 'Sources'; usage note now says 'synced rows for sources'. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pay-as-you-go at $0.25 per analytics event + $0 per analytics event, Scale at $750, Group Analytics Add-on at $0 per analytics event + $0 per analytics event and Boost at $250.
It lands 69 days after PostHog's previous change, "PostHog: added Replay Vision, a new usage-based product ($0.01/credit)" (Aug 11, 2026). PostHog has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Mixpanel and Amplitude also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
