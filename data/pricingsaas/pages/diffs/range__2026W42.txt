What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Range's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Cash Savings APY raised from 3.20% to 3.50% in the features list; footnote now names High Yield Cash instead of Altruist Cash. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Premium at $329.17, Platinum at $495.83 and Titanium at $1,041.67.
