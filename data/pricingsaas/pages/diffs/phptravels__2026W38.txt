What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of PHPTRAVELS's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Positioning and licensing shifted from '100% Open Source' to 'Source code included' under a commercial licence; modification rights now depend on the selected plan, not universal as before; Web and Mobile Apps option moved from Included (free) to 'from USD 3,500'; new Additional Features step adds JSON API, Live Chat, Domain & Hosting, Lifetime Updates, Multi Currency and Multi Language add-ons. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, feature change) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Startup at $2,499 subscription, Agency at $4,999 subscription and Enterprise at $9,999 subscription.
It lands 7 days after PHPTRAVELS's previous change, "PHPTRAVELS swaps opaque quote wizard for itemized cost calculator" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "PHPTRAVELS widens API integrations from 32+ suppliers to "All" access" (Jul 13, 2026) and "PHPTRAVELS: supplier API integrations doubled from 15+ to 32+" (Jul 4, 2026). PHPTRAVELS has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Holded, Threecolts, Shippo, Tatsoft and Smokeball also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
