What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Lastpass's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Premium $3.00/mo, Families $4.00/mo, Teams $4.25/user/mo, Business $7.00/user/mo, Business Max $11.00/user/mo — previously hidden (blank price field), now publicly shown, all billed annually. In detail: Per-plan pricing became publicly visible on the cards: Premium $3.00/mo, Families $4.00/mo, Teams $4.25/user/mo, Business $7.00/user/mo, Business Max $11.00/user/mo, all billed annually, previously unrendered. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Premium at GBP 2.6, Teams at GBP 3.33, Families at GBP 3.4, Business at GBP 5.5 and Business Max at GBP 7.25.
It lands 14 days after Lastpass's previous change, "LastPass: Business Max price increase ($9→$11/user/mo)" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LastPass: new 30% off promo on Premium & Families" (Aug 4, 2026). Lastpass has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Enpass, NordPass, Dashlane, 1password and Bitwarden also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
