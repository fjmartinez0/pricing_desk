What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Adapt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Self-hosted open-source company agent developer preview delayed from January 4, 2027 to January 12, 2027. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Adapt Cloud at $3.51 per 100 vCPU-hours (sandbox, running) + $1.12 per 100 GiB-hours (sandbox memory, running).
It lands 7 days after Adapt's previous change, "Adapt: Cloud plan spend limit moves from per-team to per-member monthly cap" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Adapt drops separate Harness rate card, folds Self-hosted Free into agent rates" (Nov 8, 2026), "Adapt drops $8/user fee, swaps Managed plan for usage-only Adapt Cloud" (Nov 1, 2026) and "Adapt slashes Managed plan to $8 per user, down 43% from $14" (Oct 18, 2026). Adapt has been tracked since 2026-07-14.
In the same window Microsoft, HubSpot and Salesforce also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
