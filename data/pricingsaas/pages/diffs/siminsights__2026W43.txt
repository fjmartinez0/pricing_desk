What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of SimInsights's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New referral promotion: when a referred school program buys, both programs get 3 free months of HyperSkill. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Individual at $4.17, Classroom at $11.67, Enterprise Pro at $12.5, Education Essentials at $208.33 and Workforce Board Basic at $291.67.
It lands 28 days after SimInsights's previous change, "SimInsights adds Workforce Board tab, splits Higher Ed into two segments" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SimInsights folds teacher and homeschool plans into a single 5-tier K12 ladder" (Sep 14, 2026). SimInsights has been tracked since 2026-07-16.
In the same window Alteryx, Tableau and Sisense also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
