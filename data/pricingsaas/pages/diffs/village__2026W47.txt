What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Village's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Typical credit costs revised: warm intro paths 5→2 credits, AI search ~6→2 credits, list building ~16 credits replaced by AI screen 25 records at 1 credit; email reveal renamed Find an email; Credit packs now give fewer credits for same price ($25: 1,000→700; $50: 2,200→1,500; $100: 5,000→3,300); Business monthly pooled credits 7,500→6,700. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change, limit lowered). The current page lists Core at $1, Business at $134, VIP at $2,000, Forward-deployed at $10,000 and API Platform at $10 per active user.
It lands 7 days after Village's previous change, "Village extends 60%-off VIP promo ($2,000/mo vs. $5,000) to Nov 17" (Nov 9, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Village cuts Business credits to 7,500 and trims annual discount to 10%" (Oct 19, 2026), "Village launches $1/seat Core plan, adds $10k Forward-deployed tier" (Oct 11, 2026) and "Village: Free profile enrichment capped to 3mo cache, older data costs 1 credit" (Aug 24, 2026). Village has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Reply, Regie.ai, LeadMagic, UJET and Trellus also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
