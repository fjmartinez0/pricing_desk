What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Influencity's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan lineup expanded from 2 tiers to 4: added Discover ($158/mo) and Business ($1,118/mo); Professional ($318/mo) and Enterprise (custom) unchanged. Bulk-payment 3.5% transaction-fee disclosure also dropped. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Discover at $158, Professional at $318 and Business at $1,118.
It lands 87 days after Influencity's previous change, "Influencity: Email Sequences added + Campaigns/Projects terminology restructured" (Jul 3, 2026). Influencity has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
