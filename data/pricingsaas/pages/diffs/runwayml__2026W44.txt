What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Runway's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Added 'Generate, test and publish ads with Runway Ads' feature to Standard, Pro, and Max plans Removed Max plan's limited-time 'Unlimited Wan 3.0 & Wan 3.0 Prime' unlimited-access promotion (was available in Tool Mode until October 18). In detail: Removed Max plan's limited-time 'Unlimited Wan 3.0 & Wan 3.0 Prime' unlimited-access promotion, which was offered in Tool Mode until October 18. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $12, Pro at $28 and Max at $76.
It lands 6 days after Runway's previous change, "Runway drops Max plan's expired Unlimited Wan 3.0 promo, prices hold" (Oct 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Runway: Added 'Generate, test and publish ads with Runway Ads (New)' bullet to Standard, Pro and Max plan cards" (Oct 26, 2026), "Runway runs unlimited Wan 3.0 promo on Max plan until October 18" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Runway Max plan adds Unlimited Wan 3.0 and Wan 3.0 Prime until October 18" (Oct 15, 2026). Runway has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
