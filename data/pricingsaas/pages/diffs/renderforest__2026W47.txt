What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Renderforest's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free plan card added: $0 forever, 60 AI credits plus 10 a day (balance capped at 100), 720p template videos with watermark; before had only free-trial CTAs; Lite credits increased from 9,600 per year (800/mo) to 1,000 per month (12,000/yr); Seedance model on Lite updated from 2.0 to 2.5. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised, new plan). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 18, 2026) lists Lite at $9, Pro at $19, Business at $39, Premium Video at $24 per HD1080 export and Premium Mockup at $5 per mockup.
It lands 14 days after Renderforest's previous change, "Renderforest's Halloween discount expires, prices jump back up to 51-52% off" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Renderforest hikes Business plan annual price 34%, discount shrinks" (Aug 24, 2026). Renderforest has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Canva, Wistia and Loom also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
