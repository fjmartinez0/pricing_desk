What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Optery's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Site coverage without Expanded Reach increased from 395+ to 400+ sites on the Ultimate plan. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Core at $3.25, Extended at $12.42 and Ultimate at $20.70.
It lands 7 days after Optery's previous change, "Optery lifts Core coverage to 380+ sites, prices hold steady" (Oct 26, 2026).
