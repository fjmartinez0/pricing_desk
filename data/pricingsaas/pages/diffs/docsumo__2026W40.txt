What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Docsumo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 'Master data lookup & database mapping' moved from Business to Enterprise-only; Free's 'Private Teams/Slack channel' dropped, now offered only as Enterprise 'Priority support'. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction.
