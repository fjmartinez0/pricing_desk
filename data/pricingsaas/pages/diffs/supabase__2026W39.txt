What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Supabase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: OAuth 2.1 Server added to the Auth feature list, included in Free, Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans. In detail: Added OAuth 2.1 Server support to the Auth feature list, included in all plans starting with Free. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pro at $25 and Team at $599.
It lands 21 days after Supabase's previous change, "Supabase: Enterprise plan drops BYO Cloud support" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Supabase swaps flat $39/pipeline fee for metered $0.053/pipeline/hour billing" (Jul 20, 2026), "Supabase renames ETL feature to Pipelines in pricing table" (Jun 29, 2026) and "Supabase adds ISO 27001 certification to SOC2 on Team, Enterprise" (Jul 4, 2026). Supabase has been tracked since 2026-03-15.
