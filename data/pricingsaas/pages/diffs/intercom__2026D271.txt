What happened
Between the Sep 27, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Intercom's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Essential plan seat price dropped from $29/seat/mo to $19/seat/mo (~34% cut); Advanced ($85) and Expert ($132) seat prices unchanged, ruling out a billing-cycle toggle artifact. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Essential at $19, Advanced at $85, Expert at $132, Fin AI Agent (Standalone) at $0.99 per Fin outcome and Copilot at $29.
It lands 9 days after Intercom's previous change, "Intercom rebrands Essential automation as Fin, adds Fin external actions" (Sep 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Intercom strips Fin AI from Essential, reframes standalone agent plan" (Sep 18, 2026) and "Intercom: Essential plan's 35% new-customer promo ended" (Aug 31, 2026). Intercom has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Salesforce, HubSpot, LiveChat, Drift and Zendesk also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
