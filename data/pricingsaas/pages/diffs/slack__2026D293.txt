What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 20, 2026 captures of Slack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New "Frontline teams" add-on introduced for Enterprise+ — gives field workers Slack access for task management, agentic support, and secure Salesforce access. In detail: Added a new "Frontline teams" add-on for Enterprise+, giving field workers Slack access for task management, agentic support, and secure Salesforce access. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $7.25, Business+ at $15 and Enterprise+ at $45.
It lands one day after Slack's previous change, "Slack hides Enterprise+ price, swaps $45/user for "Contact sales" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Slack: Enterprise+ now shows a public price of $45 USD per user/month (billed annually) instead of 'Contact sales…" (Oct 14, 2026), "Slack hides $45 Enterprise+ price, now says Contact sales for pricing" (Oct 13, 2026) and "Slack puts $45 price on Enterprise+ plan, dropping "Contact sales" (Oct 4, 2026). Slack has been tracked since 2023-11-30.
