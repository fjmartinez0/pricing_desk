What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Zapier's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Zapier renamed 'Zap workflows' to 'Zaps' in the Free, Professional, and Team plan cards and feature bullets; FAQ and marketing copy still say 'Zap workflows'. No pricing or functional change - all prices and limits unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Professional at $19.99, Team at $69, Agents Pro at $33.33, Chatbots Pro at $13.33 and Chatbots Advanced at $66.67.
It lands 86 days after Zapier's previous change, "Zapier unifies AI, code, and SDK usage into one shared task-based pool" (Jul 4, 2026). Zapier has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Parabol, Workato, Make, MuleSoft and Tray.ai also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
