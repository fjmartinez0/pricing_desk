What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Melio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Melio extended its free trial from 30 days to 90 days, tripling the promotional window before customers must pick a paid plan or convert to the limited free Go plan. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Boost at $8, Core at $8 and Unlimited at $64.
It lands 69 days after Melio's previous change, "Melio: Custom approval workflows now included at Core (was Boost-exclusive)" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Melio reworded AR table rows, shifting focus to branded invoices and links" (Jun 30, 2026), "Melio: Go loses QuickBooks/Xero sync; Unlimited swaps white-glove for NetSuite" (Jul 3, 2026) and "Melio rebrands payment links feature as branded invoices" (Jun 22, 2026). Melio has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Stripe and Squarespace also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
