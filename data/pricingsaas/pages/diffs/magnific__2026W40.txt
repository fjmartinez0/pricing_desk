What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Magnific's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Limited-time 'Extra 50% OFF' promo on Premium annual billing cut the effective price from $14.50/mo to $7.25/mo. Annual savings messaging rose from 'Save 25%' to 'Save up to 63%'. Offer ends Sept 28. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Premium at $14.5, Premium+ at $33.75, Business at $55, Pro Starter at $82.5 and Pro at $210.
It lands 14 days after Magnific's previous change, "Magnific swaps MiniMax H3 2K for Max Turbo 768p, renames Pro to Pro Starter" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Magnific splits Pro into credit tiers, entry price drops to $82.50/mo" (Sep 7, 2026), "Magnific adds Wan 3.0 AI video models at launch discount" (Sep 1, 2026) and "Magnific: Premium plan price increased $7.25 -> $14.50/month" (Aug 3, 2026). Magnific has been tracked since 2026-06-04.
