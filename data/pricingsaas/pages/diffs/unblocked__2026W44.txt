What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Unblocked's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: A new summary line now discloses the monthly-billed price ($35/user/month) alongside the existing $29 annual price and the 21-day free trial; this $35 figure was not shown anywhere on the page before. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Platform at $29.
It lands 42 days after Unblocked's previous change, "Unblocked kills $19 Code Review tier, entry price jumps 53% to $29" (Sep 14, 2026). Unblocked has been tracked since 2026-05-12.
