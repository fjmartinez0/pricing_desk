What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of MeetRecord's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Mid-tier plan renamed 'Flex' to 'Growth'; usage-based pricing dropped ('Pay Only for your Usage' removed), 'Unlimited Roleplays & Usage' now included, and Dedicated Account Manager bullet added; Free plan's feature table now discloses a 'Limited Roleplays up to 60 minutes total per account' cap, and Enterprise's summary line adds 'Custom workflows' alongside AI Coaching, SSO, and Integrations. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, plan renamed) with an upward direction.
It lands 77 days after MeetRecord's previous change, "MeetRecord: Free plan capped at 60 minutes of credits, but gains Flex-only AI roleplay features" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MeetRecord: Premium plan renamed Flex, moves to usage-based pricing" (Jul 3, 2026). MeetRecord has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Avoma, Fireflies.ai, Fathom and Otter also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
