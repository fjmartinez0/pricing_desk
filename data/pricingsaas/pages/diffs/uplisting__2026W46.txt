What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Uplisting's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: New Dynamic Pricing (AirDNA Adapt) add-on at £8 per listing/month on Independent Host and Operator; included on Manager; Manager plan now includes Dynamic Pricing by AirDNA Adapt; Smart Locks add-on for Independent Host repriced from £10/month flat to £4 per listing/month. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, new add-on, add-on change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Manager at $15.30, Operator at $18, Independent Host at $91, Protect - Security Deposits at $6 and Client Statements + Portal at $12.
It lands 77 days after Uplisting's previous change, "Uplisting caps Independent Host at 4 properties, adds AI Suite with 100 monthly actions" (Aug 24, 2026). Uplisting has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
