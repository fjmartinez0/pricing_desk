What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Ahrefs's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Ahrefs Connect API integration units/mo doubled: Lite 100,000 to 200,000, Standard 400,000 to 800,000, Advanced 1M to 2M; Enterprise's 2M cap replaced with 'Custom'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Starter at $29, Lite at $129, Brand Radar AI at $199, Standard at $249 and Advanced at $449.
It lands 80 days after Ahrefs's previous change, "Ahrefs: API/credit limits raised (Lite-Advanced) + Free tier renamed" (Jul 2, 2026). Ahrefs has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Moz and Semrush also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
