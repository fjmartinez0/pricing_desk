What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Docker's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Top-of-page promo banner replaced a whitepaper-download CTA with a new $250 credit offer for Cloud Sandboxes, a concrete monetary incentive tied to a Docker product purchase. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Docker Pro at $9, Docker Team at $15, Docker Business at $24, Testcontainers Cloud Additional Minutes at $3 per 100 minutes and Docker Build Cloud Minutes (500) at $25 per 500 build minutes.
It lands 178 days after Docker's previous change, "Docker Reveals Hardened Images Pricing Starting at $5K Per Repo" (Apr 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Docker Restricts Hub Organizations, Drops Debug from Personal Plan" (Week 3, 2026), "Docker Democratizes Debugging: Free Access for All Users with Personal Plan Update" (Q4 2025) and "Docker Introduces Premium Support Add-on with 24×7 Response and Priority SLAs for Business and DHI Plans" (Q3 2025). Docker has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Google Cloud and Hypervault also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
