What happened
Between the Nov 13, 2026 and Nov 14, 2026 captures of Perplexity AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Model lineup tile swapped: Gemini 3.8 Flash (Google's fast model) replaced by Gemini 4 Hydrogen, tagged New (Google's latest model).
