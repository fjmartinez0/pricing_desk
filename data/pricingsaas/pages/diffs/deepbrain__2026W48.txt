What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Deepbrain's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Languages & Accents voiceover coverage raised from 50+ to 70+ languages on paid plans in the feature comparison table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised). The current page lists Personal at $24, Team at $55 and Interactive Avatar Standard at $99.
It lands 7 days after Deepbrain's previous change, "Deepbrain swaps unlimited AI videos for credits; Free sign-up credits cut" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Deepbrain cuts voiceover languages and accents from 150+ to 50+" (Nov 1, 2026). Deepbrain has been tracked since 2023-12-07.
