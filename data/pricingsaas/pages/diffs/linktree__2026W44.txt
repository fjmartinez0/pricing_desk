What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Linktree's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter rose from $6 to $7/mo annual ($8 to $9 monthly); Pro rose from $12 to $14/mo annual ($15 to $17 monthly). Premium unchanged at $30/$35. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $7, Pro at $14 and Premium at $30.
It lands 7 days after Linktree's previous change, "Linktree Premium now promises unlimited Instagram auto-replies" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Linktree drops selling from Free plan, gives Pro 0% sell-and-earn fees" (Oct 5, 2026). Linktree has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Buffer, Leadpages and Later also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
