What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Meet Alfred's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'AI-Powered MCP Integration' link added to the global nav and footer, flagged 'Coming soon' for AI assistants to manage campaigns via natural conversation. Not tied to a specific plan yet. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists LinkedIn Only at $39, Multi-Channel at $49 and Agency at $59.
It lands 7 days after Meet Alfred's previous change, "Meet Alfred renames plans, raises Agency to $59/seat and cuts annual discounts" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Meet Alfred raises annual prices up to $59, cuts discount to 40%" (Oct 4, 2026). Meet Alfred has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Upwork, Linked Helper, Happierleads and Dock also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
