What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of ToolJet's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added 'Public apps' row to the comparison table under user management, marked included for the two top-tier plans and not included for lower plans. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $23, Pro at $79, Team at $199 and Pay-as-you-go AI Credits at $1 per 100 AI credits.
It lands 7 days after ToolJet's previous change, "ToolJet lifts Free plan AI app generation cap to unlimited pages" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ToolJet triples entry-tier app limits, adds MCP coding-agent plugin, blocks C" (Sep 28, 2026) and "ToolJet adds $23 Basic plan and credit-free Claude Code, Codex, Cursor builds" (Sep 14, 2026). ToolJet has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
