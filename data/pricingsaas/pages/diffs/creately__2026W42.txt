What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Creately's pricing page, Pulse recorded 5 changes: Plans now bundle Basic tier of new products Atlas, Process, Blueprint, Compass and Genogram on every plan; Advanced tiers granted per product on Enterprise; Starter plan ($5/mo billed annually at $60, $8/mo monthly) removed from the lineup; existing subscribers keep their current plan; Team plan ($5/user/mo billed annually) removed from the lineup; existing subscribers keep their current plan;. In detail: Free plan limit changed from 45 items per canvas to up to 40 shapes per diagram; New Pro plan at $4 per editor/month billed annually ($48 per editor), with unlimited shapes, full export and one connected data source. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed, freemium change, new plan, feature added) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $4 and Business at $89.
It lands 181 days after Creately's previous change, "Creately switches annual discount to $36/year fixed savings framing" (Apr 13, 2026). Creately has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
