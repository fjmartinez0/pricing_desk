What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of UJET's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing page rebuilt: per-user Basic/Pro/Enterprise/Digital cards and add-on price grid replaced by CX & AI packages priced per session/minute (from $0.377-$0.715/session), plus subscription and CX Platform from $65/user/mo. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change). The current page lists Basic at $65, Digital at $69, Pro at $99, Enterprise at $120 and Chat at $10.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for UJET. UJET has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
