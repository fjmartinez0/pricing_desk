What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Salesmotion's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Self-serve API-only offer (start free, 100 free credits, pay per result) removed; developer card now says API and MCP are included in Enterprise only, with an Explore the API link; API and MCP access now stated as Enterprise-only (comparison table: 'Included in the Enterprise plan', previously 'custom team & enterprise plans'; feature list tagged Enterprise). Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change, feature removed). The current page lists Individual at $85.
It lands 14 days after Salesmotion's previous change, "Salesmotion ends Q4DEAL promo, Individual plan jumps to $85/mo from $39" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Salesmotion: New Q4 promo (code Q4DEAL, limited time): Individual plan $39/mo for first 3 months instead of $85/mo, then…" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Salesmotion caps Individual contacts at 200 credits a month" (Sep 7, 2026). Salesmotion has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Microsoft, Close, HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
