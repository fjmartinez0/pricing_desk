What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of CyberGhost VPN's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 2 Years + 2 Months plan increased from $1.99/mo ($51.74 billed) to $2.19/mo ($56.94 billed); promo discount reduced from 85% to 83% off. In detail: 2 Years + 2 Months plan price increased from $1.99/mo ($51.74 billed for 2 years) to $2.19/mo ($56.94 billed); promotional discount reduced from 85% to 83% off. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists CyberGhost VPN 2 Years at $1.59, CyberGhost VPN 6 Months at $6.99 and CyberGhost VPN 1 Month at $12.99.
It lands 14 days after CyberGhost VPN's previous change, "CyberGhost: 26-month plan price cut $2.19→$1.99/mo, discount to 85%" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CyberGhost VPN cuts 2-year plan to $2.19/mo, ups Campus Deal to 83% off" (Aug 31, 2026), "CyberGhost VPN's Campus Deal drops to 81%, price rises to $2.49/mo" (Aug 24, 2026) and "CyberGhost VPN hikes 2-year VPN pricing as discount shrinks to 83%" (Jul 27, 2026).
