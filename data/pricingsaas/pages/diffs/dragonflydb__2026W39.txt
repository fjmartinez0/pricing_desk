What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of DragonflyDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise renamed to Self-Managed, shifting from Dragonfly-hosted air-gapped infra to customer-run infra with LDAP/RBAC; added new Community Edition support offering for self-hosted users. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Business at $2,000 and Flex at $8 per GB.
It lands 35 days after DragonflyDB's previous change, "DragonflyDB: SSD Data Tiering add-on added to pricing calculator ($8.00/GB/mo)" (Aug 17, 2026). DragonflyDB has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Redis and Couchbase also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
