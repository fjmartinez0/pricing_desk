What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of CodeRabbit's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: On-demand products reworked: Security Scan renamed Deep Scan and Agent renamed Engineer; the Agent card no longer shows $0.40 per agent minute pricing or the spending-cap bullets; Essentials plan now lists Slop detection and IDE reviews (agentic code reviews in PR, CLI, IDE and agent loops); absent from the prior Essentials card. In detail: All plans, Essentials included, now list Slop detection (flags low-quality AI-generated PRs) and IDE reviews (agentic code reviews in PR, CLI, IDE and agent loops); neither appeared on the prior plan cards or comparison table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, add-on change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $24, Team at $48, Advanced at $72, CodeRabbit Agent at $0.4 per agent minute and Usage-based reviews at $0.25 per reviewed file.
It lands 2 days after CodeRabbit's previous change, "CodeRabbit renames Agent to CodeRabbit Engineer, drops $0.40/min from card" (Oct 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CodeRabbit folds usage-based reviews into new On demand products section" (Sep 21, 2026), "CodeRabbit reframes Advanced around architectural insights (copy only)" (Sep 17, 2026) and "CodeRabbit merges Cloud Coding and Slack agent add-ons into one $0.40/min Agent" (Sep 14, 2026). CodeRabbit has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
In the same window Windsurf, GitLab, Replit and JetBrains also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
