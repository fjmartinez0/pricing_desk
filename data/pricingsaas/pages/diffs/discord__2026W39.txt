What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Discord's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Nitro plan's file upload limit shown in the feature comparison table increased from 500 MB to 1 GB (Nitro Basic stayed at 50 MB). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction.
It lands 49 days after Discord's previous change, "Discord: 'second month free' Nitro promo offer removed" (Aug 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Discord: extra month free promo for new Nitro subscribers" (Jul 27, 2026). Discord has been tracked since 2026-06-24.
In the same window Microsoft, Google, SignalHire and Zoom also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
