What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Vantage's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Deepgram added to the AI integrations list on the pricing page (integrations library count 40 to 41); no price or plan changes. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $30 and Business at $200.
It lands 14 days after Vantage's previous change, "Vantage adds Metronome and Ramp, expands to 40+ native integrations" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vantage adds DigitalOcean cloud integration, expanding to 36 total" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Vantage adds Fireworks AI integration and LLM Token Allocation tracking" (Aug 24, 2026). Vantage has been tracked since 2023-11-28.
