What happened
Between the Nov 12, 2026 and Nov 13, 2026 captures of Figma's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Figma added "3D transforms (Beta)" to the feature comparison table: design in 3D natively in Figma with depth, perspective, and CSS compatibility. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Organization at $5, Enterprise at $5 and Professional at $16.
It lands 15 days after Figma's previous change, "Figma opens Specs beta to all seats, no longer limited to paid seats" (Oct 29, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Figma adds Specs beta to paid seats, puts code layers in Design tiers" (Oct 18, 2026), "Figma debuts Specs in beta, bundled with Full, Dev and Collab seats" (Oct 15, 2026) and "Figma agent exits beta, ending free ride as AI credit charges now apply" (Oct 11, 2026). Figma has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Affinity, Sketch, Webflow, Framer and Penpot also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
