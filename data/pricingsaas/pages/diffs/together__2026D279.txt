What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 6, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Serverless token prices changed: Kimi K3 cut to $2.70 in / $13.50 out per 1M tokens (from $3.00 / $15.00); Qwen3.8 Flash raised to $0.15 / $0.47 (from $0.09 / $0.28). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Managed Storage at $0.16 per GiB/month, Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, Sandbox (Code Interpreter) at $0.03 per session (60 min), GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100) and GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days).
It lands one day after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI adds Tev1 4B Experimental at $0.04 input with free output" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI adds Tev1 4B Experimental at $0.04 input with free output tokens" (Oct 2, 2026), "Together AI reveals NVIDIA HGX B300 pricing at $9.99/GPU/hr, $4.99 preemptible" (Sep 25, 2026) and "Together AI cuts Qwen3.7-Max and Qwen3.8 Flash inference prices by 40%" (Sep 23, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
In the same window OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral AI and Lambda Labs also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
