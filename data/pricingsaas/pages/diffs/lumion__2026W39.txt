Lumion rebrands View as free Axogram, scraps $19/user monthly plan
Verified Sep 21, 2026·Software Development & Testing·Direction: neutral
The changeView plan — plan renamed.
3 changes·2 captures·Sep 14, 2026 → Sep 21, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Lumion's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Custom licensing plan now explicitly requires '10 users or more' to qualify, a minimum threshold that was not disclosed previously; The 'View' plugin plan was rebranded as 'Axogram'. Axogram now appears as a product tab (badged 'New free plan') instead of the priced View plan card; Axogram (the rebranded View plugin) is now offered as a free plan; the $19 named user/month View subscription, bill. In detail: Axogram (the rebranded View plugin) is now offered as a free plan; the $19 named user/month View subscription, billed annually at $229, is no longer listed on the pricing page. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed, feature change, freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Lumion Pro at $96 and Pro Floating at $125.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Lumion. Lumion has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
