What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Veed's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Veed cut paid tiers from 3 (Creator/Pro/Studio) to 2 (Creator/Pro); Studio discontinued; Creator annual price down $147→$120; Pro annual price down $265→$228; savings messaging raised 51%→60% Pro plan's AI credit allotment cut from 30,000 credits (~7,500 AI videos) to 18,000 credits (~4,500 AI videos) per month Creator loses unlimited subtitles (now 120hr/yr), gains HD 1080p exports + unlimited video length; both lose Gen-AI Studio access. Pro loses AI voice minutes, gains unlimited subtitles, 4k exports, 50GB storage. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, limit decrease, feature change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Creator at $12.25, Pro at $24 and Studio at $38.75.
It lands 98 days after Veed's previous change, "Veed adds annual billing totals to plan cards, cuts Studio translation quota " (Jul 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Veed raises Pro plan price to $24/user amid billing changes" (Jun 22, 2026), "Veed raises Pro plan price 14% to $24/month" (Jun 1, 2026) and "Veed cuts Pro plan price to $21/mo per editor in yearly billing" (May 18, 2026). Veed has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Runway, Synthesia, OpusClip and Loom also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
