What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of HubSpot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Marketing Hub Professional's highlighted feature list added two new AI agents, 'Content Agent' (BETA) and 'Nurture Agent' (BETA). The 'Agent Hub' bullet moved down to the Starter card the same week, so Professional still includes it via 'Marketing Hub Starter, plus:'; 'Omni-channel marketing automation' was dropped from the highlights while the plan tagline still advertises it. Prices, core seats. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Enterprise at $3,600.
It lands 35 days after HubSpot's previous change, "Hubspot Marketing Enterprise Marketing: seat pricing revealed ($45/$75/mo) + Pro/Ent feature swap" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hubspot Marketing Enterprise Marketing: Additional Core Seat pricing hidden (Pro/Enterprise) + more" (Aug 10, 2026). HubSpot has been tracked since 2025-01-01.
In the same window Salesforce, Microsoft, Zoho, Freshworks and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
