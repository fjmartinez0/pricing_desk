What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Messari's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro gains CSV chart downloads and Assets API endpoints (FAQ: only Assets endpoints, was no API); Free loses screener and newsletter; Pro loses screening and diligence reports; Data streaming removed. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $375.
It lands 14 days after Messari's previous change, "Messari moves to Blockworks, renames plans Free/Pro and drops included APIs" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Messari cuts Enterprise Individual plan price 10% to $4,500/year" (Sep 14, 2026). Messari has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window Prisync, Vizly, Precog, PostHog and Mixpanel also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
