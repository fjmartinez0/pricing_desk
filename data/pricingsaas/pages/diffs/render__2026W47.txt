What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Render's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Render Sandboxes (Beta) card no longer lists '40 GB of disk included per sandbox'. Card copy also trimmed ('Can run indefinitely' dropped, but the compare table still shows Maximum session length: No limit). Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed). The current page lists Pro at $25, Scale at $499, Postgres - 64c-256g at $3,000, Postgres - 64c-512g at $6,000 and Postgres - 128c-1024g at $11,000.
It lands 7 days after Render's previous change, "Render Sandboxes now run indefinitely, Postgres adds connection pooling" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Render launches Sandboxes beta: $0.12/CPU-hour microVMs for agent workloads" (Nov 1, 2026), "Render: Workflows comparison rows changed from concurrent task runs (20/50/100, then $10/mo per 50) to parallel task…" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Render Key Value cuts compute prices up to 45% and adds 50 GB tier; Workflows max compute halved" (Oct 4, 2026). Render has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Digitalocean, Google Cloud, Aws and Railway also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
