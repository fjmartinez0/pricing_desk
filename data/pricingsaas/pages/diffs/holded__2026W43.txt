What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Holded's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added API request daily limits (Basic 70, Standard 250, Advanced 1,000) to plan cards; added Custom SMTP domain row to comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Plus at EUR 7.5, Basic at EUR 14.5, Standard at EUR 29.5, Advanced at EUR 49.5 and Premium at EUR 99.5.
It lands 35 days after Holded's previous change, "Holded: swapped Payroll accounting for expense scanner + cash flow" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Holded: reconciliation rules limits added (15/50/100)" (Aug 17, 2026). Holded has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Quickbooks, Freshbooks, Xero, Zoho and Sage also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
