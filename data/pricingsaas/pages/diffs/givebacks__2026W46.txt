What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Givebacks's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Transaction fee options narrowed: organizations can no longer split fees with the donor; only absorb or pass fees to the donor remain. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $41.58 and Premium at $49.92.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Givebacks. Givebacks has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
