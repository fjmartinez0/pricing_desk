What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Magnific's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Magnific One image model launched with unlimited desktop use on Premium+/Pro/Business until Oct 15, plus new credit-priced rows; Seedream 5.0 Pro unlimited promo ended. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Premium at $14.5, Premium+ at $33.75, Business at $55, Pro Starter at $82.5 and Pro at $210.
It lands 7 days after Magnific's previous change, "Magnific Premium annual price doubles to $14.50/mo as 50% promo ends" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Magnific slashes Premium annual price 50% to $7.25/mo before Sept 28 deadline" (Sep 28, 2026), "Magnific swaps MiniMax H3 2K for Max Turbo 768p, renames Pro to Pro Starter" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Magnific splits Pro into credit tiers, entry price drops to $82.50/mo" (Sep 7, 2026). Magnific has been tracked since 2026-06-04.
In the same window Runway, Microsoft, Leonardo.ai, Huggingface and Getty Images also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
