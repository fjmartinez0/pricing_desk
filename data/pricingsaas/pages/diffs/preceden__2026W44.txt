What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Preceden's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan FAQ now states that timelines made with Timeline Maker AI keep every generated event, plus up to 5 events of your own; plan cards and prices unchanged. In detail: Free plan FAQ now states that timelines made with Timeline Maker AI keep every generated event (beyond the 10-event cap), plus up to 5 events of your own; paid-plan wording rewritten to match unchanged plan cards. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $10 and Pro at $16.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Preceden. Preceden has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
