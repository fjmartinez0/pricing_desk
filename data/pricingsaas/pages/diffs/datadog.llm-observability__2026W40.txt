What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Datadog's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Extended data retention add-ons for LLM Observability increased across all tiers: 30-day retention $1.50->$6.50, 60-day $3->$8, 90-day $4->$9 per 10K LLM spans/month; Experiment result retention now extends from 15 days by default to 3 months when customers have an annual or month-to-month commitment. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, add-on change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $160, 90-Day Retention Add-on at $9, 30-Day Retention Add-on at $6.5 and 60-Day Retention Add-on at $8.
It lands 87 days after Datadog's previous change, "LLM Observability renamed Agent Observability, repriced per-span (Free+Pro)" (Jul 3, 2026). Datadog has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Elastic, Grafana, Sumologic, Splunk and Dynatrace also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
