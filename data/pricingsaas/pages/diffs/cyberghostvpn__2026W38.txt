What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of CyberGhost VPN's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: CyberGhost's 26-month ('2 Years + 2 Months') VPN plan price decreased from $2.19/month to $1.99/month; discount label increased from 83% to 85% off. Seasonal promo renamed from 'Campus Deal' to 'Fall Special'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists CyberGhost VPN 2 Years at $1.59, CyberGhost VPN 6 Months at $6.99 and CyberGhost VPN 1 Month at $12.99.
It lands 13 days after CyberGhost VPN's previous change, "CyberGhost VPN cuts 2-year plan to $2.19/mo, ups Campus Deal to 83% off" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CyberGhost VPN's Campus Deal drops to 81%, price rises to $2.49/mo" (Aug 24, 2026), "CyberGhost VPN hikes 2-year VPN pricing as discount shrinks to 83%" (Jul 27, 2026) and "CyberGhost VPN bundles Windows antivirus suite for $4.50/mo" (Jun 29, 2026). CyberGhost VPN has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window ExpressVPN, Surfshark and NordVPN also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
“CyberGhost” is the name CyberGhost VPN’s pricing page uses; Pulse files the change under CyberGhost VPN, the company it tracks.
