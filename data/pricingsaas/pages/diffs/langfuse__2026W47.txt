What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Langfuse's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Hackathons discount program: USD 100 in credits per month for 3 months, added to the startup/EDU/OSS discounts section; New AI Gateway row added to the plan comparison table, plus proxy logging row now mentions Langfuse AI Gateway. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $29, Pro at $199, Enterprise at $2,499 and Teams at $300.
It lands 7 days after Langfuse's previous change, "Langfuse adds AWS Marketplace yearly billing and 1h Enterprise SLO" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Langfuse adds MCP and CLI rows to AI plan lineup beside Halo agent" (Oct 26, 2026), "Langfuse renames in-app agent from Assistant to Halo in Cloud plans" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Langfuse: AI Assistant usage now tiered by plan" (Sep 14, 2026). Langfuse has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
