What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of NordVPN's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Basic 2-year plan Black Friday price cut from $3.49/mo to $2.99/mo (Save 69% to 74%; first-27-month total $94.23 to $80.73). Complete and Prime unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Basic at $3.49, Complete at $3.99 and Prime at $6.99.
It lands 7 days after NordVPN's previous change, "NordVPN drops "Android only" label on message protection, signaling iOS launch" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "NordVPN: Black Friday intro discount deepens to 78% off (Complete plan)" (Oct 26, 2026) and "NordVPN: 3 comparison-table features removed, Message Protection + 1 more added" (Aug 31, 2026). NordVPN has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window ExpressVPN, CyberGhost VPN, Ava and Surfshark also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
