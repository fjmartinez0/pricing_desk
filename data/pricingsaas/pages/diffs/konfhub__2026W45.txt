What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of KonfHub's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Lite plan platform fee increased from 2% to 2.5% (+18% GST) per transaction. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Lite at INR 2 per additional attendee beyond plan limit + $499 per use and Silver at INR 3.75 per additional attendee beyond plan limit + $1,499 per use.
It lands 237 days after KonfHub's previous change, "KonfHub Removes Free QuizHub 100-Participant Cap from Silver Plan" (Mar 10, 2026). KonfHub has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window OpenTable, Guesty, WellnessLiving, Treatwell and TouchBistro also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
