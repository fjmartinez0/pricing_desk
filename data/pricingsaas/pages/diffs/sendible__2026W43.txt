What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Sendible's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Dedicated account manager moved from Enterprise-only to Premium ($199/mo) plan card; no longer listed as an Enterprise feature. In detail: Dedicated account manager added to the Premium ($199/mo) plan card (and so inherited by Elite via "Everything in Premium"); previously listed only on Enterprise. Enterprise still includes it (tagline + "Access to all features") — its redundant card bullet was dropped. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Core at $30, Plus at $84, Premium at $169, Elite at $254 and Enterprise at $638.
It lands 28 days after Sendible's previous change, "Sendible shifts posting limits from per-user to per-workspace, sidestepping u" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Sendible switches report template limits from per-user to per-workspace" (Aug 24, 2026). Sendible has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Icon, Sproutsocial and Buffer also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
