Verified Sep 28, 2026·Collaboration & Productivity·Direction: neutral

What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Kipwise's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Kipwise Agent add-on repriced from $1,000 one-time setup (incl. $500 LLM credits) to $10/user/month or $108/user/year (min 3 users), with per-user AI credits and a new install trial offer. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Premium at $6.3, Branded at $12.6, AI Knowledge Suggestions at $7 and Kipwise Agent at $9.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Kipwise. Kipwise has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
