What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Workable's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Workable Agent Chat AI credit billing changed from 10 credits each time a candidate interacts with the chat to 10 credits per session when a candidate provides at least one answer. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $299, Premier at $599, Enterprise at $719, Texting at $89 and Performance & Engagement at $39.
It lands 14 days after Workable's previous change, "Workable trial now offers full Enterprise features, drops 15-day limit" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Workable: native language options increased 5→9 (Standard/Premier/Enterprise)" (Aug 24, 2026). Workable has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window LeveragePoint, Greenhouse, Bullhorn and BambooHR also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
