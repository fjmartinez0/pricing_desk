What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Yodeck's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Premium plan integrations line changed from 'Microsoft 365 & Google Apps' to '147+ apps (incl. Microsoft 365 & Google)', now quantifying app count. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at EUR 8, Premium at EUR 11, Enterprise at AUD 25, Embeddable Feeds at CAD 9 and Yodeck Assurance at GBP 0.8.
It lands 28 days after Yodeck's previous change, "Yodeck Enterprise adds Content Flows to fill airtime around locked content" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Yodeck swaps Webhooks for AI Agent in Premium API bundle" (Sep 14, 2026). Yodeck has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Trustpilot, FMG Suite, Pictory, Prismic and Peec AI also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
