What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Homebase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Payroll add-on promo: small teams (5 or fewer) completing setup by 12/15/2026 pay a $29/month base fee + $6 per employee paid after the 6 free months, instead of the standard $49/month + $6 (terms-gated offer). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essentials at $24, Plus at $56, All-in-One at $96, Background Checks at $30 subscription and Tip Manager at $25.
It lands 28 days after Homebase's previous change, "Homebase launches 6-months-free Payroll & Plus promo, raises Payroll base fee to $49" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Homebase: Job post boosts add-on price cut $79→$50" (Sep 14, 2026), "Homebase Hiring Assistant exits Beta, ditches per-post fee for $99/mo flat rate" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Homebase: Payroll add-on fee decreased ($49→$39/mo)" (Aug 31, 2026). Homebase has been tracked since 2026-05-06.
In the same window Deputy and 7shifts also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
