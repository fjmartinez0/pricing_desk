What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of WizCommerce's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing rebuilt around a new credit system: new Basic plan ($49/mo, $45 annual), plan credits rescaled (Starter 25/mo to 2,500/mo), free trial card removed; Starter/Core list prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $45, Starter at $225, Core at $450, Growth at $625 and Scale at $958.
It lands 91 days after WizCommerce's previous change, "WizCommerce adds UGC, Ad video features across all plans, publishes credit costs" (Jul 6, 2026). WizCommerce has been tracked since 2026-06-23.
