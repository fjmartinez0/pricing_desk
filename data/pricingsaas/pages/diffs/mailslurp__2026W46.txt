What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of MailSlurp's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Growth plan monthly price raised from $129.99 to $199.00 per month; users included (2) unchanged; Starter plan now offers a 7-day free trial; CTA changed from "Subscribe" (checkout with trial skipped) to "Start 7-day trial". Price unchanged at $19.99/month. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, trial change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $19.17, Pro at $45.83, Growth at $116.67, Inbox placement at $1 per test and Device rendering at $0.50 per render.
It lands 7 days after MailSlurp's previous change, "MailSlurp hikes Pro plan to $69, halves Free email allowance to 250" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MailSlurp adds inbox placement testing to all plans, gates warmup to top tiers" (Oct 12, 2026), "MailSlurp adds $0.01/credit email warmup usage rate + 1 more change" (Oct 5, 2026) and "MailSlurp adds AI token disclosures and email warm-up as usage caps quietly…" (Sep 21, 2026). MailSlurp has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
