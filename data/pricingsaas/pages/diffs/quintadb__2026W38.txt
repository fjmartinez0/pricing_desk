QuintaDB adds 14-day, 50,000-unit trial before Free tier kicks in
Verified Sep 14, 2026·Software Development & Testing·Direction: neutral
The changeTrial — trial change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 7, 2026 → Sep 14, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of QuintaDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New accounts now get a 14-day trial with 50,000 compute units before falling back to the permanent Free tier (10,000 units/month); previously accounts started directly on Free with no trial period. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Launch at EUR 15, Growth at EUR 25, Scale at EUR 45, Team at EUR 75 and Department at EUR 150.
It lands 7 days after QuintaDB's previous change, "QuintaDB: Enterprise plan adds guided build/implementation service" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "QuintaDB reveals Enterprise price at €500/mo, cuts 8 tiers down to 4" (Jul 6, 2026) and "QuintaDB renames tiers to Launch/Growth/Scale, swaps usage limits for Compute" (Jul 4, 2026). QuintaDB has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Notion, Smartsheet, ClickUp, Asana and monday.com also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
