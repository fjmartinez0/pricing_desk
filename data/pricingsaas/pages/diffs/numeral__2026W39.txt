What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Numeral's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Numeral expanded global reach: Enterprise plan tax coverage grew from 70+ to 90+ countries (Enterprise bullet, International section, coverage banner), and billing/ERP integrations grew from 30+ to 40+.
