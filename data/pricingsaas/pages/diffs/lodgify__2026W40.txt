What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Lodgify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: September Flash Promo launched: yearly-plan discount raised from 20% to 25% (Starter) and to 50% (Professional, Ultimate); Bi-Yearly discount also raised to 50%. New site-wide countdown banner and headline announce 'Get 50% off'; FAQ entries added about the promo. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Basic at $14, Starter at $26, Professional at $42 and Ultimate at $62.
It lands 21 days after Lodgify's previous change, "Lodgify's August Flash Promo expires, hiking monthly prices up to $62 on Ulti" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Lodgify: August Flash Promo raises annual discount to 25-50%" (Aug 31, 2026), "Lodgify: Peak Season promo raises annual discount to 50%" (Jul 13, 2026) and "Lodgify: Peak-season promo expired, discount reverted to 20%" (Jul 6, 2026). Lodgify has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Hospitable and Guesty also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
