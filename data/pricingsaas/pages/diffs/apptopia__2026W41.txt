What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Apptopia's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New 'Packages by Sector' offering added: per-sector or full-theme (Internet 6, Consumer 5, Fintech 4, Travel 3 sectors) packages bundling Base and Advanced data, MCP server and dashboard; no prices shown; Apptopia Base historical data depth increased from 8 years (updated daily; 24-hour lag) to 10 years (intraday or 24-hour lag). Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan, limit increase) with an upward direction.
