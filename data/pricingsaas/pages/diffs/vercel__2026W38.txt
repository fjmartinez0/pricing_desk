What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Vercel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro plan bundled Edge Requests, Fast Data Transfer, Blob Data Transfer, and Sandbox Data Transfer overage pricing into a new flat-rate CDN plan; Pro card bullet swapped 'Faster builds' for 'Flat Rate CDN'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 20, 2026) lists Pro at $20.
It lands 5 days after Vercel's previous change, "Vercel replaces Pro CDN overages with flat-rate included data transfer" (Sep 9, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vercel adds tax disclaimer: prices in USD, exclude VAT and GST" (Sep 1, 2026), "Vercel: Connect->Token Requests price hike + new Triggers metric" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Vercel supercharges sandboxes 5x, splits AI pricing into token and trigger fees" (Aug 27, 2026). Vercel has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Netlify, Render, Railway and Heroku also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
