What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Benchmarkemail's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: AI Assistant credit allowance increased: Free plan 150→500 credits/month, Pro plan 1,000→1,500 credits/month; Pro plan gained three new capabilities: Attach Files (shareable file links), Automated Responses (auto-email on form submission), and a Claude Connector integration for managing contacts/emails from Claude. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, limit raised). The current page lists Pro Plan at $10.
It lands 14 days after Benchmarkemail's previous change, "Benchmarkemail adds Brand Kit to form builder for logo, color and font reuse" (Nov 9, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Benchmarkemail slashes Free plan send cap to 1,000, adds $12 Pro entry tier" (Nov 1, 2026) and "Benchmarkemail adds Brand Kit to every plan, keeping logos, colors and…" (Oct 12, 2026). Benchmarkemail has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Getresponse, Convertkit, ActiveCampaign, Klaviyo and Mailchimp also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
