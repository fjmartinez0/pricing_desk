What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Fresha's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Card terminal pricing restructured: Independent changed from 'From $139 per device' to 'From $8.95 per month or $139 upfront' (new monthly option), and Team lost its free terminal ('Free, subject to processing volumes') — both plans now share the paid 'From $8.95 per month or $139 upfront' price. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change). The current page lists Team at $14.95, Independent at $19.95, AI Concierge - Messaging Only at $29.95, Smart Website at $15.95 and Data Connector at $295.
It lands 28 days after Fresha's previous change, "Fresha adds $29.95 messaging-only AI Concierge tier, unlimited messages" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fresha adds instant phone and 2-minute chat support, slows email to 2 days" (Oct 11, 2026) and "Fresha launches Smart website add-on at $15.95/month for all plans" (Sep 6, 2026). Fresha has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Acuity Scheduling also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
