What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of TurboTenant's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro card bullets changed: 'Free ACH payments' replaced by 'One-click tax-ready reports'; 'Built-in accounting tools' renamed 'Built-in financial tools'. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essentials at $12.42 and Pro at $16.58.
It lands 15 days after TurboTenant's previous change, "TurboTenant hides Autopilot $99 starting price, moves it to a call-to-discuss plan card" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "TurboTenant makes lower screening fees Pro-only, dropping Essentials perk" (Sep 28, 2026) and "TurboTenant launches Autopilot full-service plan, trims Pro to $16.48/mo" (Sep 7, 2026). TurboTenant has been tracked since 2026-08-18.
In the same window Hemlane, AppFolio and Buildium also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
