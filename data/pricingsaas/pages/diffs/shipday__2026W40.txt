What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Shipday's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan starting price increased from $2,499/month to $3,499/month; all other tiers unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Branded Elite at $99, Business AI Lite at $199, Business AI at $399 and Enterprise at $3,499.
It lands 14 days after Shipday's previous change, "Shipday quietly drops Enterprise's annual commitment and order-cap terms" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shipday reveals Enterprise pricing: $2,499/month for 10K orders, AI Agents, SLAs" (Sep 7, 2026). Shipday has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Postscript, Klaviyo, Gorgias and Twilio also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
