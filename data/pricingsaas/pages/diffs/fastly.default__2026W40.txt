What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Fastly's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Fastly added a new 'AI Platform' product category with two products: AI Runtime Control, billed usage-based per 10,000 requests ($2.50 down to $1.75 as volume grows, 100K free), and AI Firewall, priced via contact sales. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Compute Starter at $500, Network Services Basic at $1,500, Observability Starter at $1,500 and Network Services Starter at $6,000.
It lands 63 days after Fastly's previous change, "Fastly Default: Mutual TLS limit relabeled from 'mTLS' to 'domains' (quantities unchanged)" (Jul 27, 2026). Fastly has been tracked since 2023-12-09.
In the same window Google Cloud, Cloudflare, Limelight Software and Akamai Technologies also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
