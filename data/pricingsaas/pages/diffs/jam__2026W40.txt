What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Jam's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Jam launched a native Mac desktop app (camera bubble capture, screen/window/area recording, up to 4K, 10min Free / 30min paid limit) plus Auto-chapters and a browser-extension row in the comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Team at $14.
It lands 7 days after Jam's previous change, "Jam adds Transcription & captions feature, merges Fin rows into one integration" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Jam: Team plan limits increased (Recording Links 50→150/mo, Creator seats 15→50)" (Jul 3, 2026). Jam has been tracked since 2025-10-05.
In the same window Notion, Miro, Figma, Lucidchart and Whimsical also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
