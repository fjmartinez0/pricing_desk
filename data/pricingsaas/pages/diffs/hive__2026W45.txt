What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Hive's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Finance add-on now listed at +$12 (outside the +$5 default, now 'unless shown'); new Hive CRM add-on at $29 per seat/month in the add-on calculator. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $5, Teams at $12, Buzz AI at $12, Proofing & Approvals at $5 and Timesheets at $5.
It lands 98 days after Hive's previous change, "Hive adds AI credit allowances across all tiers plus new CRM, Finance add-ons" (Jul 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hive renames the Custom Dashboards add-on back to Advanced Dashboards" (Week 25, 2026), "Hive renames the Advanced Dashboards add-on to Custom Dashboards and lists AI Assistant on the Starter plan again" (Apr 13, 2026) and "Hive Introduces Buzz AI Add-On at $8/User/Month Amid Removal of AI Features from Starter and Free Plans" (Jan 11, 2026). Hive has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Notion, ClickUp, Asana and Trello also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
