Discourse sharpens tier clarity: custom migration and 50+ plugins for Enterprise
Verified Sep 21, 2026·Collaboration & Productivity·Direction: neutral
The changeBusiness, Enterprise positioning — positioning.

What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Discourse's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Migration services and plugin-count labels were clarified to distinguish tiers: Business now shows 'Standard migration services' and Enterprise shows 'Custom migration services' and '50+ additional plugins' instead of the previously identical generic wording. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $100 and Business at $500.
It lands 77 days after Discourse's previous change, "Discourse boosts Enterprise AI credit allowance to 5M daily, up from 3M" (Jul 6, 2026). Discourse has been tracked since 2026-05-04.
In the same window Mighty Networks, Microsoft and Mattermost also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
