What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Renderforest's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Black Friday sale added: Lite 20% and Pro/Business 30% off first year (Lite $7.20, Pro $13.30, Business $27.30/mo), ends in ~6 days; list prices unchanged ($9/$19/$39 annual); Free plan AI credits halved: 60 → 30 AI credits (+10 a day, still capped at 100); example allowance drops from 12 to 6 Renderforest Fast clips and from 4 to 2 min AI voice; Nano Banana Pro image credit cost cut from 40 to. In detail: Nano Banana Pro image credit cost cut from 40 to 25 credits (Renderforest 2 image 7 → 5), so included images rise: Lite 25 → 40 and Pro 60 → 96 Nano Banana Pro images per month; credit allotments and prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (freemium change, limit raised, discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Lite at $9, Pro at $19, Business at $39, Premium Mockup at $5 extra AI credits (per 1,000) and Premium Logo at $29 extra AI credits (per 1,000).
It lands 7 days after Renderforest's previous change, "Renderforest adds $0 Free plan with 60 AI credits, lifts Lite to 1,000 a month" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Renderforest's Halloween discount expires, prices jump back up to 51-52% off" (Nov 1, 2026). Renderforest has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Canva, Wistia and Loom also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
