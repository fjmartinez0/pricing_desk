What happened
Between the Nov 17, 2026 and Nov 18, 2026 captures of Pika's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added a limited-time promo: Fancy plan gets unlimited Wan 3.0 generations this week (normally metered credits), up to 10s at 720p, one at a time, ending Nov 23. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $8, Creator at $28, Fancy at $76 and Credit Pack at $1 per 60 credits.
It lands one day after Pika's previous change, "Pika reprices GPT Image 2.6 and Nano Banana 2.1, top tiers up to 33%" (Nov 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pika ends free daily H3 Flex generations for Creator and Fancy subscribers" (Nov 16, 2026), "Pika launches Team plans: Creator $35/seat, Pro $95/seat with shared credits" (Nov 15, 2026) and "Pika's GPT Image 2.6 doubles to 4 credits, halving monthly image limits" (Nov 13, 2026).
