What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Astronomer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added a new 'Otto pricing' section introducing an Otto Intelligence Fee starting at $1.25/M tokens for Astronomer's Otto data-engineering agent, plus pass-through costs from public model providers. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on). The current page lists Developer at $0.35 per use and Team at $0.42 per use.
It lands 42 days after Astronomer's previous change, "Astronomer launches Astro Observe, billed as a % of deployment compute costs" (Oct 4, 2026). Astronomer has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Datadog and Prefect also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
