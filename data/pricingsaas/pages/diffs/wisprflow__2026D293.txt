What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 20, 2026 captures of Wispr Flow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan's mobile dictation word limit changed from a disclosed numeric cap (1,000/week) to a vague 'Limited weekly use' phrase, removing the specific usage figure.
