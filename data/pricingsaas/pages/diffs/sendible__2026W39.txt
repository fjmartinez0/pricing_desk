What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Sendible's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Daily posting limit basis changed from 'per user' to 'per workspace' across all plans in the feature comparison table. Since plans include unlimited users, the limit now applies per workspace regardless of team size. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Core at $30, Plus at $84, Premium at $169, Elite at $254 and Enterprise at $638.
It lands 28 days after Sendible's previous change, "Sendible switches report template limits from per-user to per-workspace" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Sendible cuts social profile limits up to 25% in plan refresh" (Jun 29, 2026) and "Sendible drops user caps for unlimited, rebrands 4 of 5 plans" (Jul 4, 2026). Sendible has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Icon, Sproutsocial, Buffer, Hootsuite and Later also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
