What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Awesomic's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Graphic Pack $1,490→$1,799/mo and All-in-One $2,995→$3,219/mo; new per-person Agentic AI plans (Light $19, Basic $99, Pro $199, Studio $499); AI Designer $200 removed; quarterly discount 20% → up to 19%. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists AI Designer at $200, Graphic Pack at $1,190 and All-in-one at $2,396.
Awesomic's previous recorded change was "Awesomic: Plans restructured — Super discontinued, AI Designer added ($200/mo)" (Jul 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Awesomic shifts spotlight to quarterly billing with 20% savings" (Jun 15, 2026), "Awesomic adds Graphic Pack plan and swaps quarterly billing for annual commitment" (Jun 1, 2026) and "Match time for talent changed from 'Instant start with AI' to 'Up to 24 hours' for All-in-one and" (May 25, 2026).
