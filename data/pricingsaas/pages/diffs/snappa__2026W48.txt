Snappa switches to credits: Pro drops to $10/mo, Team plan axed
Detected Nov 22, 2026, 10:16 PM ETMarketing & Content
The changePro limit lowered
3 changes2 capturesNov 16 → Nov 23, 2026 UTC
What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Snappa's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Snappa moved to credit-based pricing: Team plan ($20/mo yearly, 5 users) removed, Starter renamed Free, Pro now $10/mo ($120 billed yearly) with credits; credit packs $10/$25/$75 added; Pro's unlimited downloads replaced by 10 credits/month (3,000/year annual); Free's 3 downloads per month replaced by 10 credits per month; Pro/Team feature list (Buffer/Social media integrations, custom font uplo. In detail: Pro unlimited downloads per month replaced by a credit allowance (3,000 credits a year up front on annual; edits and resizes also use credits); Free 3 downloads per month replaced by 10 credits every month; Free plan now requires a credit card at signup (was "no credit card required"; "Card required. Never charged.") while gaining "Every feature included" — previously Buffer/social integrations, custom font uploads and background removal were Pro/Team-only; Snappa moved to AI credit-based pricing (1 credit = 1 image): Team plan ($30/mo, $20/mo billed yearly, 5 users) removed, Starter renamed Free, Pro unchanged at $10/mo billed yearly ($120/yr; Save $60 implies $15 monthly) now with credits; Pro-only credit packs added (125/$10, 350/$25, 1,250/$75). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered, freemium change, packaging change). The current page lists Pro at $10.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Snappa. Snappa has been tracked since 2026-03-19.
