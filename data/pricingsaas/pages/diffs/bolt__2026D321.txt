What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 17, 2026 captures of Bolt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Collaborator limits now listed: Free up to 3 collaborators; Pro and Teams unlimited collaborators. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Pro at $18 and Teams at $27.
It lands 8 days after Bolt's previous change, "Bolt: Pro adds AI image editing, drops newest-models + MCP access" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Bolt Pro adds newest AI models and MCPs, drops AI image editing" (Oct 20, 2026), "Bolt ends Pro plan's free Bolt Forge promo as Oct 18 deadline passes" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Bolt extends free Forge promo on Pro through Oct 18" (Oct 16, 2026). Bolt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Limelight Software, Viam, Bird and GiveMe5.ai also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
