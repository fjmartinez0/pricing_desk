What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of RankUp's pricing page, Pulse recorded 5 changes: Lite plan ($20/mo; ChatGPT only, 1 project, 20 prompts) removed; lineup is now Starter, Pro, Growth; Starter monthly price cut $99/mo → $79/mo; Pro monthly price cut $549/mo → $219/mo; Pro moved to the middle card (still Most Popular); Growth monthly price raised $399/mo → $449/mo; Growth is now the top (rightmost) tier; Plan quotas rewritten: Starter prompts 100→50, content generations/optimi. In detail: Plan quotas rewritten: Starter prompts 100→50, content generations/optimizations 30→10; Pro prompts 500→150, generations Unlimited→20, optimizations 200→20; Growth prompts 300→350, generations Unlimited→30, optimizations 60→30. Projects 2/3/5 now render as '-1 project' on every plan (likely an unlimited sentinel shown raw). Both captures on Monthly toggle. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit lowered, price decrease, plan removed, price increase) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $63, Pro at $175 and Growth at $359.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for RankUp. RankUp has been tracked since 2026-09-10.
