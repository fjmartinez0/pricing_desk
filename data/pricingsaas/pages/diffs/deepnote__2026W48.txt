What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Deepnote's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added 'Public apps' to the comparison table (Team and Enterprise; not Free); private docker images registry changed from GCR to GAR. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Team at $39.
It lands 91 days after Deepnote's previous change, "Deepnote upgrades Team plan AI model to GPT-5.6 Sol from GPT-5.5" (Aug 23, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Deepnote upgrades Team plan AI access from GPT-5 to GPT-5.5" (Jul 20, 2026) and "Deepnote upgrades AI to Sonnet 4.6, adds $39/month credits to Team plan" (May 18, 2026). Deepnote has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
