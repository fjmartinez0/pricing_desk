What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Apploye's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: The existing 40%/30% intro promo (first 3 months, till 19 Nov) is now applied on the price cards: Power $8→$4.80/user/mo annual ($10.50→$6.30 quarterly, $12→$7.20 monthly), then regular price; Activity Insights add-on $3→$2.10/user/mo for 3 months, then $3; Extended Data Retention add-on now has a public price of $2/user/mo (7 years of timesheet, activity and payroll data; screenshots kept 12 mon. In detail: Extended Data Retention add-on now has a public price of $2/user/mo (7 years of timesheet, activity and payroll data; screenshots kept 12 months). Before, the comparison table only said "Available as add-on" with no price. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change, price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Elite at $4.50, Power at $8, More Screenshots at $2, Video Recording at $4 and Dedicated Onboarding at $150 per use.
It lands 6 days after Apploye's previous change, "Apploye launches 40% off Power promo as free Starter plan loses app-tracking, leave-document and expense allowances" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Apploye adds $3 Activity Insights add-on, folds Stealth Mode into Power" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Apploye adds BAA to Enterprise plan, boosting compliance coverage" (Aug 24, 2026). Apploye has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
