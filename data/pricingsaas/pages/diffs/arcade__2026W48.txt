What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Arcade's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Fixed per-video credit tables and cost calculator replaced by 'typical' credits per request. On High: 30s video 100→~90, 1-min ~150 (same), AI edit 60→~20 (within 5 min) / ~50; planning steps now use credits (<10–25). New FAQs: AI-generated clips billed per second (~1 Standard / ~3 Premium, Pro+Enterprise), failed requests free, plans before Nov 18 keep old credit prices until Dec 16; Pro plan ca. In detail: Pro plan card now lists 'All Apps' (connect apps to make videos from team tools); Apps nav item added. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, packaging change). The current page lists Pro at $49.
It lands 27 days after Arcade's previous change, "Arcade: Growth renamed Pro, per-seat pricing swapped for credit tiers" (Oct 26, 2026). Arcade has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
