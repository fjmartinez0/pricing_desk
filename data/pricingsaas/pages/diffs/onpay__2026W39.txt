OnPay swaps flat bundles for modular add-ons: Time Tracking, HR, Benefits picks
Verified Sep 27, 2026·Workforce Management·Direction: neutral
The changeTime Tracking, HR, Benefits packaging — package change.

What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of OnPay's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: OnPay's partner calculator moved from a flat all-inclusive bundle (starting at $49/mo+$6/worker, 'Advanced' badge, fixed perks checklist) to a modular base plan plus selectable Time Tracking, HR, and Benefits add-ons, with a new itemized breakdown toggle. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 27, 2026) lists Payroll Essentials at $6, Hourly Employees Bundle at $10, Growing Businesses Bundle at $10, Salaried Employees Bundle at $120 and Time Tracking at $15.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for OnPay. OnPay has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
In the same window Corel, Checkr and LexisNexis also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
