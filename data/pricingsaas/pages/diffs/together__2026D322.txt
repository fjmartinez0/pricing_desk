What happened
Between the Nov 17, 2026 and Nov 18, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Serverless Inference chat-model catalog updated: added Elorian Glimpse 1.0 ($0.60/1M input, $3.00/1M output, $0.06/1M cached); removed Rnj-1 Instruct ($0.15/$0.15) and Qwen2.5 7B Instruct Turbo ($0.30/$0.30) from the chat pricing table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100), GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days), Sandbox (Code Sandbox) at $0.04 per vCPU/hour + $0.01 per GiB RAM/hour and Serverless Inference at $0.15 per 1M input tokens.
It lands 5 days after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI: HGX B200 GPU price hikes to $9.29/hr + 3 more rates shown" (Nov 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI adds US geo-pinned models, Kimi K3 US at $3.75/$18.75, live GPU status" (Nov 11, 2026), "Together AI raises Qwen3.7-Max inference prices 67%, drops four serverless models" (Nov 7, 2026) and "Together AI adds Kimi K3.1 Pro at $2/$10 per 1M tokens, plus Ripple-65B Preview" (Nov 5, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
In the same window Huggingface, Anthropic, OpenAI, Lambda Labs and Google also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
