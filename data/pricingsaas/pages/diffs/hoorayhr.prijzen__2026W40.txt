What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of HoorayHR's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: MCP integration status changed from 'Binnenkort' (Coming soon) to 'Nieuw' (New) in the Plus plan's AI-features section, indicating the feature has now launched. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at EUR 7.5, Plus at EUR 9.25, Declaraties & reiskosten at EUR 0.5, Urenregistratie at EUR 1.5 and Multi-entiteiten at EUR 1.
It lands 14 days after HoorayHR's previous change, "HoorayHR Prijzen: 1-on-1 conversation logging removed from Core plan" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HoorayHR Prijzen teases AI Chat and MCP tools coming to Core and Plus plans" (Jul 13, 2026). HoorayHR has been tracked since 2026-06-03.
