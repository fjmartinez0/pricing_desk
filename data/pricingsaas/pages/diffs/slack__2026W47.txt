What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Slack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Slackbot's usage limits are now explicitly disclosed in the comparison table: Free/Pro show 'Consumption based', Business+ shows 'Usage limits' (contact sales to add more), Enterprise+ is 'Unlimited'; previously the row was blank/undisclosed on every plan. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change). The current page lists Pro at $7.25, Business+ at $15 and Enterprise+ at $45.
This weekly roll-up includes 2 changes Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Slack reveals $45 Enterprise+ pricing, dropping contact-sales listing" (Day 318, 2026, saved Nov 14, 2026) and "Slack shifts Slackbot to usage limits, noting 15 messages per user weekly" (Day 315, 2026, saved Nov 11, 2026). They are the same changes, not new ones. Before that, Slack's previous recorded change was "Slack hides Enterprise+ pricing, drops $45/user price for contact sales" (Nov 8, 2026). Slack has been tracked since 2023-11-30.
In the same window Microsoft, Zoom, Google, Rocket.Chat and Mattermost also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
