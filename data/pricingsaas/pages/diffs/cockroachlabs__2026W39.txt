What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Cockroach Labs's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: CockroachDB replaced Basic/Standard/Advanced with a 2-edition 'Continuum' model (Standard, Mission Critical) billed per vCPU-hour. Free Basic removed; Advanced renamed Mission Critical, repriced; Compute/storage moved from fixed per-plan tier caps (up to 200 vCPUs, up to 3-10 TiB) to usage-based metering, and new explicit per-vCPU-hour pricing appeared for CDC, PCR, and Cluster Virtualization add. In detail: Compute/storage moved from fixed per-plan tier caps (up to 200 vCPUs, up to 3-10 TiB) to usage-based metering, and new explicit per-vCPU-hour pricing appeared for CDC, PCR, and Cluster Virtualization add-ons. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $0.09 per vCPU/hour, Mission Critical at $0.16 per vCPU/hour, Data Replication (PCR) at $0.02 per vCPU/hour, Data Synchronization (CDC) - Standard at $0.01 per vCPU/hour and Cluster Virtualization at $0.02 per vCPU/hour.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Cockroach Labs. Cockroach Labs has been tracked since 2026-05-21.
