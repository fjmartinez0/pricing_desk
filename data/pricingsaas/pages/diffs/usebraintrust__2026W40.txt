Braintrust rebuilds pricing page to spotlight AIR volume model
Verified Sep 28, 2026·Workforce Management·Direction: neutral
The changePositioning — positioning.
2 changes·2 captures·Sep 21, 2026 → Sep 28, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Braintrust's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pricing page rebuilt to lead with AIR: hero, footer CTA and AIR benefits now explicitly disclose no public per-interview sticker price for AIR's volume-based model; AIR section moved to top of page; AIR's who-is-for and benefits copy rewritten (pilot/evaluation buyers vs. candidate screening; role-specific scoring; voice not one-way video); zero-EEOC bias-audit claim demoted to a compliance link. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Braintrust. Braintrust has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
