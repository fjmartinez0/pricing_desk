What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of ClickHouse's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan cards no longer show starting prices (Basic From $53, Scale From $437, Enterprise From $571 per month) or the Most popular badge; only per-unit storage/compute rates remain. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Basic at $35.33 per TB + $0.22 per TB and Scale at $0.69 per TB + $47.1 per TB.
It lands 7 days after ClickHouse's previous change, "ClickHouse adds headline plan prices from $53 to $571 per month" (Oct 5, 2026). ClickHouse has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window Datadog, Elastic, Quest Software, Grafana and Splunk also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
