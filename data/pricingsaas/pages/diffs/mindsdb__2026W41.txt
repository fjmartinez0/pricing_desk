What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash inference rates raised ($0.22/$0.66 to $0.30/$1.20 per 1M in/out); MindsHub Foundry 100% credit match promo (up to $5,000) removed. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pay as you go at $0.1 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.5 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands 2 days after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB ends 100% credit match promo, up to $5,000 for new teams" (Oct 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB hikes DeepSeek V4.1 Flash output 82% to $1.20, adds Claude Sonnet 5.5" (Oct 2, 2026), "MindsDB adds Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 models, cuts both DeepSeek V4 tiers" (Sep 28, 2026) and "MindsDB adds Jev decision model to free tier alongside MindsHub Air" (Sep 24, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Alteryx and Huggingface also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
