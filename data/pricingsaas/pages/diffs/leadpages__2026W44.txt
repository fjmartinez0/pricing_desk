Verified Oct 26, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: down

1 change·2 captures·Oct 19, 2026 → Oct 26, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Leadpages's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro ($29/mo, 25 pages, 1 domain, 12K AI credits) removed from plan cards and comparison table; entry price now Business at $49/mo ('Plans starting at $29/mo' became $49/mo). Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists HTML Pub Pro at $23, Scale at $29, HTML Pub Starter at $29, HTML Pub Business at $39 and Grow at $79.
Leadpages's previous recorded change was "Leadpages: Page & domain limits cut across Pro/Business/Grow/Starter" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Leadpages slashes custom domains while boosting storage up to 15x" (Apr 27, 2026), "Leadpages launches AI landing page builder from any brand URL" (Mar 17, 2026) and "Leadpages Raises Per-User Add-On Price 33% to $20/Month" (Mar 10, 2026). Leadpages has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
