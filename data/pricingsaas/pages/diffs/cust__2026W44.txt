What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Cust's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Cust launched 'Teammates': an AI teammate for customers on iMessage/WhatsApp, available from the Growth plan. Each conversation uses 5 credits from the plan's shared allowance; Credit costs per AI action moved from ranges to fixed amounts: interaction 0.02-0.03->0.03, health 0.16-0.23->0.2, Q&A 5-10->8, prep 8-12->10, routing 1-3->2, email 2-5->4, reply 3-5->4, run 16-24->20. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Foundation at $400, Growth at $1,200 and Enterprise at $6,000.
Cust's previous recorded change was "Cust reveals $7,500/mo Enterprise price and adds $3,600 Scale tier" (Sep 7, 2026). Cust has been tracked since 2026-04-22.
