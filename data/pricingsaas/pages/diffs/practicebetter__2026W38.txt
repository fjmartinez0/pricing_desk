What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Practice Better's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Charting Assistant add-on moved from 600 free minutes/month + $0.60/hr overage to three flat monthly tiers: $10/20hrs, $20/60hrs, $40/120hrs per practitioner; overage now 2c/min. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Starter at $25, Professional at $62, Plus at $89, Team at $155 and AI Charting Assistant - 20 Hours at $10.
It lands 35 days after Practice Better's previous change, "Practice Better: Summer sale discount expired (Pro/Plus/Team)" (Aug 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Practice Better: Summer sale — up to 35% off Professional/Plus/Team" (Aug 4, 2026). Practice Better has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
In the same window Kajabi, Acuity Scheduling, Thinkific, Mindbody and Vagaro also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
