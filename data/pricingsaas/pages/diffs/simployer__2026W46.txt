What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Simployer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Essential package added alongside the existing Complete package (Core HR, Sia AI employee, Automation Studio, Expert knowledge bank; Recruitment as an add-on). Both priced per employee via a 30-minute call, no public prices. Surveys, Equal Pay, Expert and Recruitment also sold standalone; Learn sold separately. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Simployer. Simployer has been tracked since 2026-10-15.
