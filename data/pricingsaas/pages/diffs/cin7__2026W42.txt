What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Cin7's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added disclaimer 'Pricing shown is for new customers' to the Standard ($349/mo), Pro ($599/mo), and Advanced ($1,199/mo) plan price cards, signaling that existing/renewing customers may not retain the listed rate. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Cin7 Core Standard at $349, Cin7 Core Pro at $599 and Cin7 Core Advanced at $1,199.
It lands 7 days after Cin7's previous change, "Cin7 launches Omni enterprise plan with 8 users and 5 integrations" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cin7: Advanced plan price increase ($999→$1,199)" (Aug 5, 2026). Cin7 has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Shopify, Zoho, Squarespace and Trace also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
