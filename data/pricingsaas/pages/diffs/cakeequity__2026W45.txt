What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Cake Equity's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pro plan no longer lists '100 Stakeholders included' in its feature list; Pro plan ASC 718 stock-based compensation expensing now qualified as '(Annual)'. It lands 14 days after Cake Equity's previous change, "Cake Equity adds QSBS, SPV and stock comp add-ons at $500/yr each" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cake Equity's Pro plan quietly caps stakeholders at 100, then $60 each extra" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Cake Equity: Custom plan adds Annual/Quarterly billing option" (Aug 17, 2026).
