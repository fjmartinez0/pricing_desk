What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of DrChrono's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Integrated Payments moved from a paid 'Add On' on all three tiers to included (checkmark) on Foundation, Growth and Premium, with a new footnote 4 disclosing a $5 program fee plus 3.25%+$0.30 card processing fee; Patient Statements changed from 'Per Statement' billing on all three tiers to included (checkmark) on Foundation, Growth and Premium, and now carries footnote 3 covering paper/mailed sta. In detail: Claims submission to iHCFA for Workers Comp & Auto Claims moved from included on Growth and Premium to 'Per Claim' billing, so all three tiers are now charged per claim; Patient Statements changed from 'Per Statement' billing on all three tiers to included (checkmark) on Foundation, Growth and Premium, and now carries footnote 3 covering paper/mailed statement charges. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease, add-on change, limit increase) with a downward direction.
It lands 70 days after DrChrono's previous change, "DrChrono shifts Medical Billing add-ons to per-claim usage pricing" (Jul 13, 2026). DrChrono has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
