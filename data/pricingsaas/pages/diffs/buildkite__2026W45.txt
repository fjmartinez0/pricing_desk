What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Buildkite's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Windows hosted agents added at $0.008 per vCPU minute (Small/Medium/Large); Linux ARM64 now available on every shape at the same rate as AMD64 instead of on request for Enterprise; Pro plan now includes Windows and Linux ARM64 agents (4,000 Linux AMD64 vCPU minutes); Enterprise hosted agents bullet drops private container registry and Linux ARM64 mention. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro Plan at $30.
It lands 14 days after Buildkite's previous change, "Buildkite Pro now lists overage rates for storage and downloads" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Buildkite: Personal→Free rename, Free/Pro plans gain explicit usage caps" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Buildkite: Model Providers LLM proxy pricing removed" (Aug 10, 2026). Buildkite has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window GitHub, GitLab, CircleCI and Google Cloud also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
