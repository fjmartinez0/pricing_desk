What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of BuzzyBooth's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: U.S. shipping fee of $150 per booth removed from both Monthly and Annual booth plan breakdowns; hardware, software, and commitment lines unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Annual at $199 and Flexible at $250.
It lands 7 days after BuzzyBooth's previous change, "BuzzyBooth scraps four tiers for Flexible and Annual plans from $199/mo" (Oct 12, 2026). BuzzyBooth has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Wistia, Visme, Veel, Uscreen and Profound also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
