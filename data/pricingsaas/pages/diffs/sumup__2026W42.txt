What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of SumUp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Payments Plus no longer advertises a 7-day free trial; the card now only says to cut fees if you take £3,000 or more a month. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Business Account Plus at GBP 15, POS Plus at GBP 29 and Payments Plus at GBP 189.
It lands 35 days after SumUp's previous change, "SumUp switches to yearly-first pricing: £189/year, save 17%" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SumUp hikes POS Plus add-on price from £29 to £39 per month" (Aug 17, 2026). SumUp has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Stripe and Squarespace also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
