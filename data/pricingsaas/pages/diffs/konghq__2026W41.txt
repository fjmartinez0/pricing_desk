What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Konghq's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plus card now headlines 'From $25/month plus usage' (was 'Charged per Gateway per month'); standalone $0 30-day Free trial card replaced by a 30-Day Free Trial link under AI Management tab. In detail: Konnect pricing restructured into three separately purchasable solutions (AI Management, API Management, Event Management), each with Plus and Enterprise. Plus now headlines 'From $25/month plus usage' (was 'Charged per Gateway per month'; $25/month control-plane cost already in the comparison table). The standalone '$0 for 30 days' Free trial plan card was removed and folded into a '30-Day Free Trial | no credit card required' note under Plus. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Plus at $500.
It lands 95 days after Konghq's previous change, "Konghq Insomnia bundles AI Gateway add-ons into Enterprise plan at no extra cost" (Jul 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kong: AI Gateway A2A traffic added to Plus; Paid Plugins/Manager now included in Enterprise" (Jul 3, 2026), "Konghq launches Kong Identity with $30 per million token pricing" (Mar 17, 2026) and "Konghq Restructures AI Gateway Pricing: Introduces Free and Paid Plugins, Expands AWS Marketplace Options" (Q3 2025). Konghq has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
