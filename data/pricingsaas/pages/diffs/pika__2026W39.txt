What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Pika's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Commercial license removed from Free/Starter (previously included in all plans); Priority queue and Early access to new models introduced as Fancy-exclusive perks (early access from $440/mo). In detail: New tiered feature gating: Commercial license dropped from Free/Starter (was included in all plans); Priority queue & Early access to new models introduced as Fancy-exclusive perks (from $440/mo). Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Starter at $8, Creator at $28, Fancy at $76 and Credit Pack at $1 per 60 credits.
It lands 96 days after Pika's previous change, "Pika adds Weekly billing option to subscription toggle" (Jun 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pika limits top-up credits to Create on pika.art, blocks API, MCP, apps" (Sep 23, 2026). Pika has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
