What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Oneflow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Review and AI Insights moved from free Enterprise features to a paid 'AI Review & Insights' add-on for Business and Enterprise; a new 'AI Insights Premium' add-on was also introduced for both plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction.
It lands 7 days after Oneflow's previous change, "Oneflow drops multiple workspaces from Business plan features" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Oneflow: AI Summary feature graduates from Beta to general availability" (Jul 4, 2026). Oneflow has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
