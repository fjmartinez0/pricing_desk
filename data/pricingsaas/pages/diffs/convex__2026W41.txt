What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Convex's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing note added clarifying that included resource amounts are shared across regions. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Professional at $25, Business & Enterprise at $2,500 and Free & Starter at $0.22 Database I/O (per additional GB) + $0.55 Search storage (per additional GB).
It lands 7 days after Convex's previous change, "Convex adds AI Gateway pricing, matching OpenRouter rates" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Convex swaps Enterprise-only permissions for RBAC in every plan" (Sep 21, 2026). Convex has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window Vultr, Vercel, Sumologic, SuperOps and Supabase also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
