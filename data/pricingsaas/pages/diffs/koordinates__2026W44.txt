What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Koordinates's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Australia Data Index' (Labs) card added to Data Packs, an index of public Australian Esri REST services, included with AI Pro User during the Labs phase.
