What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Paays's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Paays collapsed Basic/Essential/Enterprise into IDV Plus (single store, $249/mo) and IDV Plus for groups; Income Verifier and AML Check moved from bundled/free plan features to paid per-use add-ons; Paays added 'Paays Insights', a new fraud-analysis layer shown on every result with Watchlist, biometric consistency, application activity, and device-location signals confirmed by an analyst. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, package change) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists IDV Plus at $249.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Paays. Paays has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
