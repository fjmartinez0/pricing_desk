What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Hive's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New LLM model DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash added to the Large Language Models lineup at $0.30/$0.15 per 1M input tokens, $1.20/$0.60 per 1M output tokens, and $0.006/$0.003 per 1M cache read tokens. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction.
It lands 7 days after Hive's previous change, "Hive launches Z.AI GLM-5.3-Flash with ~66% off list intro pricing" (Sep 7, 2026). Hive has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
In the same window Microsoft, Trello, Asana and monday.com also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
