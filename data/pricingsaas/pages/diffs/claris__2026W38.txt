What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Claris's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New site-wide banner offers 15% off Remote Backup or Standby Server individually, or 25% off when purchasing both, ending September 26, 2026. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Starter (Server) at $17.5, Max (Server) at $22, Starter (Cloud) at $22, Max (Cloud) at $45 and Standby Server at $41.67.
It lands 13 days after Claris's previous change, "Claris: Starter and Max plan prices hidden ($22, $45/mo)" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Claris: Starter & Max plan pricing revealed (US$22 / US$45 per user/month)" (Aug 10, 2026) and "Claris: FileMaker Cloud hides Starter ($22) and Max ($45) plan prices" (Aug 3, 2026). Claris has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
