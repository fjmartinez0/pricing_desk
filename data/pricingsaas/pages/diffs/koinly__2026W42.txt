What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Koinly's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Added 7-day full refund guarantee (if no tax report generated), shown in a new 'Try everything before you pay' block and a new refund FAQ; no price changes; Transaction cap removed: 100,000-transaction limit on regular accounts replaced by top-ups of 1,000 transactions with no upper limit. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Newbie at $49, Hodler at $99 and Trader at $199.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Koinly. Koinly has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Quickbooks, Gate, Orb, Settle and Vendr also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
