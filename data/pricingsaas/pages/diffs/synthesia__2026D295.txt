What happened
Between the Oct 21, 2026 and Oct 22, 2026 captures of Synthesia's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Syren promo credits (limited time, valid 1 month) cut from 2,000 to 1,000 on Basic and to 1,500 on Starter and Pro; Enterprise unchanged at 2,000 per user. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $14 and Pro at $59.
It lands 12 days after Synthesia's previous change, "Synthesia launches Syren AI video agent with 2,000 free credits for a month" (Oct 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Synthesia moves to credits, cuts free plan allowance by 58% to 500 a month" (Oct 2, 2026), "Synthesia slashes Basic plan's AI video assets from 25/mo to just 3" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Synthesia decouples AI Dubbing, boosts Starter/Creator allowances up to 5x" (Sep 8, 2026). Synthesia has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
