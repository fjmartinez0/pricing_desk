What happened
Between the Aug 31, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of MotherDuck's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Purchasing Options row added to the plan comparison table — Enterprise plan customers can now pay directly or through an AWS Marketplace private offer on their AWS invoice. In detail: Added new 'Purchasing Options' row to the plan comparison table: Enterprise customers can now pay directly or through an AWS Marketplace private offer on their AWS invoice. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Business at $250 and Lite at $0.15 per F4 compute hour + $1 per AI unit.
