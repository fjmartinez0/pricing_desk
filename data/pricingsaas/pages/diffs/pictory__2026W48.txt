What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Pictory's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Black Friday annual promo deepened to 50% off all plans: Starter $25 to $12.50, Professional $29.50 to $17.50, Teams $119 to $59.50 per month; list prices $29/$59/$199 unchanged; Pictory MCP server (make videos from an AI assistant) announced and included on all plans. In detail: Black Friday promo expanded from Professional annual only to 50% off ALL plans, monthly and annual (code BLACKFRIYAYY50). Annual: Starter $25 to $12.50, Professional $29.50 to $17.50 (already on BF offer), Teams $119 to $59.50 per month. Monthly: Starter $29 to $14.50, Professional $59 to $29.50, Teams $199 to $99.50. List prices $29/$59/$199 unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change, feature added) with a neutral direction.
