What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Ignition's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Site-wide banner promo removed: 25% off for 6 months (tax season offer, ended Oct 31). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Solo at $39, Core at $99, Pro at $229, Pro+ at $399 and Deals Pipeline at $49.
It lands 21 days after Ignition's previous change, "Ignition launches 25% off for 6 months promo, ends Oct 31" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Ignition: New Enterprise plan tier added + Pro+ user limit cut to 20" (Sep 14, 2026), "Ignition drops 25% signup discount as August 31 deadline expires" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Ignition: 25%-off signup promo banner removed" (Aug 17, 2026). Ignition has been tracked since 2026-06-19.
