What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Clueso's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan's AI credit allowance changed from a recurring monthly grant (1,000 credits/mo) to a one-time, non-renewing grant (1,000 credits, one time). It lands 35 days after Clueso's previous change, "Clueso slashes Solo and Business plans 50% to mark $10M Series A" (Oct 18, 2026). Clueso has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
