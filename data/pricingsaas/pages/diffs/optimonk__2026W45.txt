OptiMonk replaces live chat with AI-agent-first instant support
Verified Nov 2, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral
The changeFeature — feature change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 26, 2026 → Nov 2, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of OptiMonk's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Live chat support (real people, ~3-minute replies) replaced by Instant support — AI agent answers immediately; a person is now reached via email or a scheduled 20-minute call. In detail: Support changed from live chat (real people, usually answering within three minutes) to AI-agent-first instant support; a person is now reached by email or a scheduled 20-minute call. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essential at $19, Growth at $69 and Premium at $179.
It lands 28 days after OptiMonk's previous change, "OptiMonk adds MCP server, monthly AI credits and unbranded Free plan" (Oct 5, 2026). OptiMonk has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Unbounce, Leadpages, Getsitecontrol, Sumologic and Convertkit also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
