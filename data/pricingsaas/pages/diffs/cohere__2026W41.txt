What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Cohere's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Model Vault Embed 4 Small replaced by Embed 5 Fast Small; hourly rate $4.00 to $3.00 and monthly $2,500 to $2,000 per instance; Embed 5 Fast Medium unchanged at $5.00/$3,250; New Embed 5 Pro model tier added to Model Vault: Small $3.00/hr ($2,000/mo) and Medium $5.00/hr ($3,250/mo). Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Embed 5 Fast at $0.08 Input tokens, Embed 5 Pro at $0.12 Input tokens, Parse 5 at $1.5 Pages, Rerank 4 Fast at $2 Searches and Rerank 4 Pro at $2.5 Searches.
It lands 4 days after Cohere's previous change, "Cohere cuts Model Vault Small to $3/hr as Embed 5 replaces Embed 4" (Oct 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cohere adds Parse 5 doc-parsing model, Medium $4/hr and XL $7/hr tiers" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Cohere adds Parse 5 to Model Vault with Medium and XL tiered pricing" (Aug 28, 2026). Cohere has been tracked since 2024-12-01.
