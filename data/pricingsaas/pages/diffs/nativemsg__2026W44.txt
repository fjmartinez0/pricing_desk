What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of nativeMsg's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Subscriber-tier plans (Starter $199, Growth $799, Pro $2,999) replaced by Build free, Launch $349/mo, Scale $1,500 commit, Enterprise; usage billed per send/session; extra agent $50 to $99/mo; Subscriber, message and conversation caps per plan replaced by 'No subscriber caps'; usage billed per send and session instead; MCP server (build with any AI tool) listed as included feature on all plans, al. In detail: MCP server (build with any AI tool) listed as included feature on all plans, alongside Full API and ExperienceAI campaign builder; Annual billing option (2 months free) removed; Monthly/Annual toggle gone, FAQ now says no blanket annual discount — Scale commits take 10-20% off usage instead. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, discount removed, package change, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Launch at $349, Scale at $1,500 and Additional Branded Agent at $99.
It lands 28 days after nativeMsg's previous change, "nativeMsg launches free RCS plan: unlimited test messages, no card needed" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "nativeMsg: nativeMsg overhauled its pricing model from message-volume tiers to subscriber-count tiers" (Aug 31, 2026). nativeMsg has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Bird, Telnyx, Google Cloud, Twilio and Plivo also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
