What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Airbyte's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Airbyte added a new 'Plus' plan ($189/mo, 40 credits) as the POPULAR tier, raised Standard's price from $10 to $20/month with credit overage, and now advertises a 30-day free trial with 400 included credits; Airbyte added explicit support SLAs: Standard now shows Portal support (no SLA, 7am-7pm EST); Pro and Enterprise Flex show Premium support (12hr critical/24hr general) plus a new Priority sup. In detail: Airbyte added explicit support SLAs: Standard now shows Portal support (no SLA, 7am-7pm EST); Pro and Enterprise Flex show Premium support (12hr critical/24hr general) plus a new Priority support add-on; Pro plan's maximum sync frequency improved from 15-minute syncs to up to 5-minute sync frequency, per its plan card copy. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, package change, limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Standard at $20 and Plus at $189.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Airbyte. Airbyte has been tracked since 2026-07-14.
