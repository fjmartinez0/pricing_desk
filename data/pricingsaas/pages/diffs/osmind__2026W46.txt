Osmind adds usage-based Osmind Intelligence AI with no monthly minimums
Verified Nov 9, 2026·Business Operations·Direction: up
The changeOsmind EHR add-on — new add-on.
1 change·2 captures·Nov 2, 2026 → Nov 9, 2026
What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Osmind's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Osmind Intelligence (AI)' row added to the plan comparison, usage-based on top of the Osmind EHR subscription, pay only for what you use, no monthly minimums. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Osmind One at $249, Lab Ordering and Results Integration at $15, Prior Authorization Management at $0.50, e-Prescribing of Controlled Substances at $49 and Credentialing at $200 per payer application + $100 per update.
It lands 7 days after Osmind's previous change, "Osmind One drops $4 SPRAVATO REMS fee, now included free" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Osmind: White-glove implementation dropped from Care Network plan" (Sep 7, 2026). Osmind has been tracked since 2026-07-14.
In the same window WP Umbrella, Uplisting, Tatsoft, SupportYourApp and Ornikar also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
