What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Sketchup's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Cyber Week promo: 25% off select annual plans with code CYBERSK26 (Nov 23-Dec 4). Pro $399 to $299.25/yr, Studio $859 to $644.25/yr; Pro Scan and Pro Civil Contractor $499 to $374.25/yr, Pro Advanced Workflows $599 to $449.25/yr. Go ($129) not discounted. List prices unchanged; Studio now lists 'Import SketchUp files into Revit' alongside the existing Revit-to-SketchUp import. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists For Universities at $4.17, For Educators at $4.58, For Students at $4.58, Go at $10.75 and Pro at $33.25.
It lands 27 days after Sketchup's previous change, "Sketchup caps 3D Warehouse downloads, ending unlimited access on all plans" (Oct 26, 2026). Sketchup has been tracked since 2023-11-23.
In the same window Spline, SimScale, Sanity, Sauce Labs and Play Digital Signage also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
