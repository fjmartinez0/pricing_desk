What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Shiprocket's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed the top-of-page promotional banner offering ₹500 recharge for ₹1000 wallet credit (code FLAT500, limited period, first recharge only) that previously appeared above the site navigation on the pricing page. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Business at INR 199, Advanced at INR 499 and Pro at INR 799.
Shiprocket's previous recorded change was "Shiprocket Expands Courier Network by 47%, Boosting Delivery Options with 25+ Partners" (Q1 2024). Shiprocket has been tracked since 2024-04-01.
