What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Stripe's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Treasury card rewritten: adds card cashback, free Stripe-to-Stripe payments and no minimums/monthly fees; drops multicurrency/stablecoin FX and fund-insurance bullets; New Terminal hardware option: Stripe Reader T600 at $329.00 listed alongside S710 and S700. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Standard at 1.5% surcharge for international cards + $0.3 per successful transaction.
It lands 4 days after Stripe's previous change, "Stripe adds $329 Reader T600 to Terminal lineup, revamps Treasury perks" (Oct 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Stripe drops T600 reader, trims Terminal lineup to M2, S710, S700" (Sep 28, 2026), "Stripe drops T600 reader, trims lineup to M2, S710, and S700" (Sep 24, 2026) and "Stripe: Metronome pricing restructured + new T600 reader ($329)" (Aug 31, 2026). Stripe has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
