What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Liner's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro/Max now list 350+ AI models (300+ LLMs, 50+ image models) instead of major models (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude); per-request credit deduction guide removed. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $14.99, Team at $26.99 and Max at $29.99.
It lands 28 days after Liner's previous change, "Liner launches "$15 free" sign-up promo with header banner and sidebar " (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Liner: Free plan Agent credits cut from 100 to 0/month" (Jul 27, 2026). Liner has been tracked since 2026-07-16.
In the same window Salesforce, Intercom, Slack and HubSpot also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
