What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Trendsi's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Vibe Selling row added to the plan comparison table: AI shopping assistant for product picks, one-click and bulk listing, AI images, SEO coach, and order and refund lookups. Marked unavailable (✗) on Free and Pro; Enterprise plan (Custom pricing, Contact Sales) removed from both the plan cards and the comparison table. It covered 3PL fulfillment, additional sales channels, additional product disc. In detail: New Max plan added to the comparison table header with a self-serve Choose Plan button. Its price renders as a blank "$/month" and its feature column is empty, so the price is not yet shown; Enterprise plan (Custom pricing, Contact Sales) removed from both the plan cards and the comparison table. It covered 3PL fulfillment, additional sales channels, additional product discounts and a dedicated account manager. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, new plan, plan removed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $29.9.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Trendsi. Trendsi has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
