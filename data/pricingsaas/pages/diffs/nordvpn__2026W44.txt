What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of NordVPN's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Complete 2-yr: $4.49→$3.99/mo (75%→78% off, $121.23→$107.73/27mo). Prime 2-yr: $7.49→$6.99/mo (69%→71% off, $202.23→$188.73/27mo). Basic 2-yr unchanged at $3.49/mo. In detail: Black Friday intro discount deepened on 2-yr plans: Complete $4.49→$3.99/mo (75%→78% off, $121.23→$107.73/27mo); Prime $7.49→$6.99/mo (69%→71% off, $202.23→$188.73/27mo). Basic 2-yr unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $3.09, Complete at $3.99 and Prime at $6.99.
It lands 56 days after NordVPN's previous change, "NordVPN: 3 comparison-table features removed, Message Protection + 1 more added" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "NordVPN drops $1M/$100K coverage amounts from Prime card Coveron bullet" (Aug 17, 2026) and "NordVPN: Coveron insurance coverage caps disclosed ($1M / $100k)" (Aug 5, 2026). NordVPN has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
