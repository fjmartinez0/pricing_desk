What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of TurboTenant's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Essentials plan no longer includes Lower Screening Fees: 'Lower screening costs' card bullet replaced by 'Priority listing review', and the Essentials checkmark was removed from the 'Lower screening fees' comparison row (now Pro-only); Renter Pricing $45 screening fee label changed from 'For Upgraded Landlords' to 'For Pro Landlords', limiting the lower renter screening fee to Pro-plan landlords (. In detail: Renter Pricing $45 screening fee label changed from 'For Upgraded Landlords' to 'For Pro Landlords', limiting the lower renter screening fee to Pro-plan landlords (Essentials no longer qualifies). Pulse files it as a product change (positioning, feature removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Essentials at $12.42 and Pro at $16.58.
It lands 21 days after TurboTenant's previous change, "TurboTenant launches Autopilot full-service plan, trims Pro to $16.48/mo" (Sep 7, 2026). TurboTenant has been tracked since 2026-08-18.
