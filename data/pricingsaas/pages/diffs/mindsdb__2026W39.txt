What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free tier restructured from a metered 5,000,000 token/month allowance on MindsHub Air to an unmetered 'free within fair use' tier with daily rate limits and a fair-use policy; MindsHub Blaze (Cerebras) rates increased: input $0.35 to $0.99, output $0.75 to $1.49, cached input $0.35 to $0.99 per 1M tokens. Pulse files it as a pricing change (freemium change, price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pay as you go at $0.1 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.5 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands 6 days after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB hikes Blaze rates up to 183%, loosens Air free-tier cap" (Sep 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB adds Jev decision model to free tier alongside MindsHub Air" (Sep 24, 2026), "MindsDB adds MindsHub Blaze and GPT-OSS 120B to rate card, from $0.15 per 1M" (Sep 14, 2026) and "MindsHub: 4 new AI models added to rate card (GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1)" (Sep 14, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Huggingface and Alteryx also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
