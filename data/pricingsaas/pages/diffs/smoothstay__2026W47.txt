What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of SmoothStay's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New done-for-you setup service: for 5+ properties SmoothStay builds guidebooks during the 14-day free trial, ready in 3 business days, limited to 10 setups a month; 60-day refund applies to every plan. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Host Plan at $5.90.
