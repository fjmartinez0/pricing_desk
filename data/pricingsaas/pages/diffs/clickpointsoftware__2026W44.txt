What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of ClickPoint Software's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: SalesExec single $72/active user plan (Starting at $450/mo for 5 users) replaced by Standard $55 and Advanced $72 per user/month, no minimum users; onboarding fee table ($775-$6,200) removed; Data Migration (import of existing CRM data during onboarding) no longer listed as included service. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists SalesExec at $72, Unlimited Dialing Plan at $155, Warm Transfer at $0.01 per minute, Call Recording at $0 per minute and Caller ID Name Lookup at $0.01 per lead.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for ClickPoint Software. ClickPoint Software has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
