What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Snaptrude's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Organization plan renamed Max; price unchanged at $100/seat/month billed yearly; Credit allowances cut: Free 75/week to 200 one-time; Pro 500/week to 400/month; Max (ex-Organization) 2,500 to 1,000 per seat/month; Max plan adds 30% more credits on top-ups. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease, feature added, plan renamed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $50 and Max at $100.
It lands 21 days after Snaptrude's previous change, "Snaptrude cuts free-plan projects from 3 to just 1" (Oct 19, 2026). Snaptrude has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
