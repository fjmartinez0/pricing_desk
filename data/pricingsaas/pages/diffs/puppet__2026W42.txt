Verified Oct 12, 2026·Cloud & DevOps·Direction: up

What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Puppet's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan cards now state standard support is included with 24x7 premium support available; comparison table support row changed from Portal (Defect Only) to Standard or Premium. In detail: Puppet Core (Commercial) technical support upgraded from Portal (Defect Only) to Standard or Premium in the comparison table; all three plan cards now state "Standard support included with 24x7 premium support available" (Enterprise and Advanced already listed Standard or Premium support). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Puppet. Puppet has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
