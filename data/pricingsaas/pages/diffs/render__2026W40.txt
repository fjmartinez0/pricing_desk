What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Render's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Render Workflows' maximum scale doubled from 4 CPU/16GB RAM to 8 CPU/32GB RAM, with a new $2.50/hour (8 CPU, 32GB RAM) tier added to the Workflows pricing table. In detail: Render Workflows' maximum scale doubled from 4 CPU / 16 GB RAM to 8 CPU / 32 GB RAM, with a new $2.50/hour (8 CPU, 32 GB RAM) compute tier added to the Workflows pricing table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Pro at $25, Scale at $499, Postgres - Pro 4GB at $55, Postgres - Pro 16GB at $200 and Postgres - Pro 64GB at $800.
It lands 86 days after Render's previous change, "Render ditches per-user pricing for flat fee, adds $100K startup credits" (Jul 4, 2026). Render has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Digitalocean, Railway, Google Cloud and Heroku also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
