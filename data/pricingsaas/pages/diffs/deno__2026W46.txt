What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Deno's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Deno Sandboxes rows removed from the pricing comparison (availability, concurrent sandboxes 3/20/Custom, volume storage 5/50 GiB) along with the Sandboxes pricing footnote; prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $20 and Builder at $200.
It lands 14 days after Deno's previous change, "Deno drops SOC2 Type 1 and DPA from Enterprise compliance table" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Deno slashes egress rates but tightens app limits and team allowances" (Sep 3, 2026). Deno has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Pulumi, Snowflake, PagerTree, ServiceNow and RunPod also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
