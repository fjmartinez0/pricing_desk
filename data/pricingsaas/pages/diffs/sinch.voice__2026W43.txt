What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Sinch's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Voice features added in Public Preview: Voice Relay (AI agent calls, $0.00/min), Streams (websocket calls, $0.00/min) and AI text to speech ($0.00 per 100 chars; standard voices free). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Phone Numbers at $0.8, In-app Calls at $3 per 1,000 minutes (make in-app calls) + $3 per 1,000 minutes (receive in-app calls), SIP Interface at $2.5 per 1,000 minutes (make calls via SIP interface) + $2.5 per 1,000 minutes (receive calls via SIP interface), International Calls at $10 per 1,000 minutes (make international calls, from US) and Domestic Calling at $8 per 1,000 minutes (receive calls, domestic PSTN, US) + $10 per 1,000 minutes (make calls, domestic PSTN, US, from).
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Sinch. Sinch has been tracked since 2026-09-09.
