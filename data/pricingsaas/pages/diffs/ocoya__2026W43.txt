What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Ocoya's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: 7-day free trial now explicitly requires a payment card (not charged during trial); trial offered on any paid plan and auto-renews monthly until cancelled; Airtable and Notion integrations added to the integrations list. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $24.17, Team at $65.83 and Agency at $165.83.
It lands 35 days after Ocoya's previous change, "Ocoya scraps unlimited Diamond tier, hikes entry price 93% in switch to Users" (Sep 14, 2026). Ocoya has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window MeetEdgar, Sproutsocial and Buffer also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
