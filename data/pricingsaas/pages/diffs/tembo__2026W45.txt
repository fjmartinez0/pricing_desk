What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Tembo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Included monthly usage allowance raised: Pro $60 to $90, Max $200 to $400; plan prices unchanged at $60 and $200/month. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $60 and Max at $200.
It lands 21 days after Tembo's previous change, "Tembo expands free trial to 10 users with unlimited repos and priority support" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Tembo lets Pro and Max users bring Claude or SuperGrok subscriptions" (Oct 5, 2026), "Tembo adds unlimited Projects to Pro/Max plans" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Tembo slashes Free plan seats from 3 users to just 1" (Sep 14, 2026). Tembo has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
