What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Octane AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise custom-solution perk changed from 'Unlimited Credits / No usage limits' to 'Unlimited Quiz Takes / no quiz-take limits or overages - AI credits still apply'. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Octane AI Basic at $43, Octane AI Plus at $170 and Octane AI Enterprise & Unlimited at $425.
It lands 6 days after Octane AI's previous change, "Octane AI: Plan comparison table rebuilt: adds AI assistant tiers (Fast/Standard/Advanced), Octane API, MCP, Shopify…" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Octane AI drops Pop-ups & opt-ins from Basic plan, cuts widgets row" (Oct 12, 2026) and "Octane AI: Annual billing option added, 15% savings" (Aug 17, 2026). Octane AI has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
