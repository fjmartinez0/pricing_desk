What happened
Between the Nov 24, 2026 and Nov 25, 2026 captures of Framer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Automations added as a plan limit: 1 on Free, 3 on Basic, 10 on Pro, Custom on Enterprise, plus a new Automations row in the comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Basic at $10 and Pro at $30.
It lands one day after Framer's previous change, "Framer quintuples free plan bandwidth, raising monthly limit from 1 GB to 5 GB" (Nov 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Framer makes free plan's 500 AI credits monthly, but FAQ still calls it a trial" (Nov 15, 2026), "Framer renames External Agents to MCP Server, free in preview" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Framer adds 6 Firewall rules to Advanced Hosting add-on alongside 6 rewrites" (Oct 2, 2026). Framer has been tracked since 2026-03-19.
