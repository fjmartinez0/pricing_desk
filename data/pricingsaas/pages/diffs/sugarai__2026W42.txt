What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of SugarAI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Standard+ $59->$68/mo, Advanced+ $85->$98/mo, Premier+ $135->$155/mo (plan renamed, 15-user minimum dropped); new Sugar Connect+ add-on at $20/user/month. In detail: Sugar Sell plans renamed, prices up: Standard+ $59->$68/mo, Advanced+ $85->$98/mo, Premier+ $135->$155/mo (15-user minimum dropped); new Sugar Connect+ add-on at $20/user/month. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $68, Serve at $80, sales-i at $85, Enterprise at $85 and Advanced at $98.
It lands 49 days after SugarAI's previous change, "SugarAI fixes garbled "sales-i" label, reveals Sugar Predict add-on" (Aug 24, 2026). SugarAI has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
