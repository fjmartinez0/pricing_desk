What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Flockler's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Premium feed allowance changed from 'Unlimited feeds (instead of 30)' to 'Unlimited feeds, priced by volume'; new FAQ says Premium includes 60 feeds with volume-based pricing for more. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Basic at $110, Business at $195 and Pro at EUR 294.
It lands 28 days after Flockler's previous change, "Flockler renames custom forms, caps AI moderation posts, scopes SSO" (Sep 28, 2026). Flockler has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Trustpilot, Hotjar, Sproutsocial and Buffer also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
