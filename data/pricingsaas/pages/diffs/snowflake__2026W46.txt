What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Snowflake's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Standard, Enterprise, and Business Critical 'Includes' feature bullets were rewritten: AI agents (CoWork, CoCo), AI search, sensitive-data detection, and HIPAA/PCI DSS/FedRAMP support added; Snowpark and Tri-Secret Secure dropped from the highlighted list. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Business Critical at $4 per credit, Enterprise at $3 per credit, Standard at $2 per credit and On-Demand Storage at $23 per TB / per month.
It lands 4 days after Snowflake's previous change, "Snowflake swaps Snowpark plan callouts for AI agents, AI search and FedRAMP" (Nov 5, 2026). Snowflake has been tracked since 2024-10-03.
In the same window Google and IBM also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
