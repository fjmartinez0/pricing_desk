What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Arcade's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Growth renamed to Pro; per-seat $50/mo (was $42.50/mo/seat annual) replaced with credit-tier billing at $59→$49/mo billed annually for 1,000 credits, scaling to $469/mo for 10,000 credits; annual discount raised 15%→20%; Free plan credits changed from 200/month recurring to 250 one-time. In detail: Mid-tier plan renamed Growth→Pro; billing switched from per-seat ($50→$42.50/mo/seat, -15% annual) to credit-tier pricing ($59→$49/mo billed annually for 1,000 credits, selectable up to $469/mo for 10,000 credits, save up to 20%); Free plan credits changed from 200/month recurring to 250 one-time. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $49.
It lands 140 days after Arcade's previous change, "Arcade upgrades free plan with unlimited AI demos and views" (Jun 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Arcade cuts free plan published demos from 3 to 1" (May 19, 2026). Arcade has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
