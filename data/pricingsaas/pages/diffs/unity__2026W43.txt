What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Unity's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Unity Asset Manager Storage overage now priced at $0.75/GB on all four plans (Personal, Pro, Enterprise, Industry); included allotment row relabeled 'Included Unity Asset Manager Storage' (10 GB/org, 50 GB/seat, 120 GB/seat). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Unity Pro at $192.5.
It lands 7 days after Unity's previous change, "Unity Enterprise now includes licensing support for offline machines" (Oct 12, 2026). Unity has been tracked since 2026-05-13.
In the same window GitHub, Unity, Stedi, Spline and Sonar also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
