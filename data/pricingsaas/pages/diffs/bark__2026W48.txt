What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Bark's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: JOY30 $30-off promo replaced by BIGDEAL code: $100 off Bark Phone ($260), $150 off Phone Pro ($450), $50 off Watch ($238); list prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Bark Home at $6, Bark App (Android) at $8.25, Bark App (iOS) at $8.25, Bark Watch at $15 and Bark Phone Pro (Starter) at $29.
It lands 14 days after Bark's previous change, "Bark drops 24-month device installments for $600/$360/$288 upfront prices" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Bark offers $30 off its Phone or Watch with limited-time code JOY30" (Nov 1, 2026) and "Bark Phone installment jumps 50% to $15/mo as Watch rises to $12/mo" (Oct 4, 2026). Bark has been tracked since 2026-05-02.
