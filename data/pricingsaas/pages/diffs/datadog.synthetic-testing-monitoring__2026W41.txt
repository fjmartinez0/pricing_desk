What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Datadog's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: DevSecOps Workflows allotment removed - Pro's 5 and Enterprise's 20 allotted workflow executions per host per month are now free when triggered from security findings and signals. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Network Path Testing at $5, API Testing at $5, Browser Testing at $12, Mobile App Testing at $50 and Continuous Testing at $79.
It lands 108 days after Datadog's previous change, "Datadog Synthetic Testing Monitoring adds Disaster Recovery with custom pricing" (Jun 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Datadog Synthetic Testing Monitoring drops AWS Fargate from DevSecOps protection" (May 4, 2026), "Datadog Synthetic Testing Monitoring: Governance Console added to Synthetic Testing Enterprise" (Apr 3, 2026) and "Datadog Synthetic Testing Monitoring launches Data Observability at $16/table/mo" (Week 3, 2026). Datadog has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Elastic, Grafana, Sumologic and Splunk also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
