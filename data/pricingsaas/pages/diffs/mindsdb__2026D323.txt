What happened
Between the Nov 18, 2026 and Nov 19, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: feature_added (models): Added GPT-6.2 Astra ($8.00/$40.00 per 1M) and GPT-6.2 Sol ($2.00/$10.00 per 1M) to the LLM Gateway model catalog feature_added (image models): Introduced a new Image models section with GPT Image 2.6 Flare and Sunburst (OpenAI) at $5 text input / $8 image input / $30 image output per 1M tokens. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 22, 2026) lists Pay as you go at $0.10 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.50 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands 8 days after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB lists Muse Ember 1.0 at $0.10 input / $0.50 output per 1M tokens" (Nov 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB adds Claude Haiku 5.5 at $0.10 input, $0.50 output per 1M tokens" (Nov 7, 2026) and "MindsDB adds GPT-6.1 Terra pricing at $1 input, $5 output per 1M tokens" (Oct 29, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Huggingface and Alteryx also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
