What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 13, 2026 captures of xAI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Plan lineup rebuilt: SuperGrok/Lite/Plus/Heavy/Business replaced by Free, Plus $8, Premium $30, Super $100, Ultra $200, Teams $30+/user; Enterprise now Custom; Cursor integration (agent, cloud agents, code hosting via Origin, Bugbot reviews) and expanded X features added to plan comparison; Plans now differentiated by usage multiples relative to Plus (4x, 15x, 50x, pooled) instead of generic hig. In detail: Plans now differentiated by usage multiples relative to Plus (4x, 15x, 50x, pooled) instead of generic higher rate limits. Pulse files it as a packaging change (positioning, feature added, package change) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Plus at $8, Premium at $30, Teams at $30, Super at $100 and Ultra at $200.
It lands 63 days after xAI's previous change, "xAI upgrades SuperGrok plan to Grok 4.6, boosts included model tier" (Aug 17, 2026). xAI has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
