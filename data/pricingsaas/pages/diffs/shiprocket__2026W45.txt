What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Shiprocket's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Top-strip first-recharge promo removed: recharge Rs 500 and get Rs 1000 in wallet with code FLAT500 (limited period offer). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Business at INR 199, Advanced at INR 499 and Pro at INR 799.
It lands 28 days after Shiprocket's previous change, "Shiprocket doubles first wallet recharge: pay ₹500, get ₹1000 with FLAT500" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shiprocket pulls ₹500-off FLAT500 wallet recharge banner from pricing page" (Sep 28, 2026). Shiprocket has been tracked since 2024-04-01.
In the same window Shopify and Squarespace also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
