What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Katteb's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Goals plan ($99/mo) renamed Business; price and features unchanged; Free account removed: the free AI assistant account note, 'Start for free' CTAs and 'Start with a free account' section are gone; FAQ no longer mentions a free assistant without a plan. Page now says 'Pick a plan' / 'Get started'; Lifetime deal added: three one-time-payment lifetime plans, limited to 50 accounts, linked from the. In detail: Lifetime deal added: three one-time-payment lifetime plans, limited to 50 accounts, linked from the pricing page (prices on /lifetime, not shown here); Slack removed from the integrations list of the $99 Goals/Business plan; now lists Telegram and work tools only. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed, new plan, feature removed, freemium change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Lite at $15.83, Business at $82.5 and Enterprise at $1,000.
It lands 7 days after Katteb's previous change, "Katteb Enterprise now starts at $1,000/mo as free tier becomes web-search AI" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Katteb overhauls Lite/Goals: goal-scheduling replaces model credits, agent li" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Katteb: full pricing re-platform to AI chat/agents model" (Sep 14, 2026). Katteb has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
