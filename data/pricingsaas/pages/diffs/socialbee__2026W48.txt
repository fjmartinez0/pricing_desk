What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of SocialBee's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday promo (code SOCIALBEEBF26, ends Dec 1): 70% off monthly plans for 3 months ($8.70/$14.70/$29.70 vs $29/$49/$99) and 50% off yearly ($174/$294/$594). The promo anchors yearly at monthly x12 ($348/$588/$1188; the table showed $290/$490/$990 before), so the effective cut off the regular annual price is ~40%. The prior birthday-sale copy (SBDAY2026) had expired June 10 and was not on the cards. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Bootstrap at $24.20, Accelerate at $40.80, Pro at $82.50, Pro50 at $149.20 and Pro100 at $274.20.
It lands 157 days after SocialBee's previous change, "SocialBee cuts yearly plan prices up to 17% across all tiers" (Jun 18, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SocialBee hikes annual plan prices 20% after removing discount" (Jun 9, 2026), "SocialBee restricts key tools to higher tiers, rebrands AI Copilot" (Apr 9, 2026) and "SocialBee Adds Content Curation and Unlimited AI to Lower-Tier Plans" (Apr 1, 2026). SocialBee has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
