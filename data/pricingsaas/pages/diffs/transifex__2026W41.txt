Transifex scraps self-serve tiers for one sales-only, AI volume-priced plan
Verified Oct 5, 2026·Software Development & Testing·Direction: up
The changeEnterprise+ packaging — package change.
2 changes·2 captures·Sep 28, 2026 → Oct 5, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Transifex's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Starter, Growth, and Enterprise+ tiers (Starter/Growth self-serve "Try free") replaced by a single plan priced on AI translation volume, available only via Contact Sales; Collaborator limits (10/30/100 by tier) replaced by unlimited collaborators in the single plan. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, limit increase) with an upward direction.
It lands 7 days after Transifex's previous change, "Transifex opens Starter and Growth to self-serve free trials" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Transifex: Personalized Onboarding moves from paid add-on to included feature" (Aug 31, 2026), "Transifex: Starter & Growth plan prices hidden (was $135/$200 mo)" (Aug 11, 2026) and "Transifex unbundles Personalized Onboarding to free, kills Training Sessions" (Jul 13, 2026). Transifex has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Lokalise and Phrase also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
