What happened
Between the Nov 10, 2026 and Nov 11, 2026 captures of OpenArt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Promo banner extra up to 50% off now also covers Seedream 5.5, alongside GPT Image 2.6, Kling 4.0 and Recraft V4.1 on Pro and Wonder plans. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $13, Plus at $27, Pro at $44, Wonder at $175 and Business at $496.
It lands one day after OpenArt's previous change, "OpenArt widens promo to 50% off Kling 4.0, GPT Image 2.6 and Recraft V4.1" (Nov 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenArt adds 30% off GPT Image 2.6 promo for Wonder and Pro until Nov 17" (Nov 5, 2026), "OpenArt launches 50% off Kling 4.0 promo, drops Stories and models bullets" (Oct 26, 2026) and "OpenArt ends 50% off Seedance and unlimited Flux 3 Image promos on Wonder" (Oct 16, 2026). OpenArt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Runway, Huggingface, Microsoft and Leonardo.ai also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
