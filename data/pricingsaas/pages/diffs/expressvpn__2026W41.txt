What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of ExpressVPN's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: ExpressAI accessible models on Express Pro comparison table reduced from 7 models to 6 models. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Basic at $3.99, Advanced at $4.49 and Express Pro at $7.49.
It lands 7 days after ExpressVPN's previous change, "ExpressVPN: Advanced $3.99->$4.49/mo, Express Pro $5.99->$7.49/mo" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ExpressVPN: ExpressAI model access expanded (6→7) + 2 more feature additions" (Sep 21, 2026), "ExpressVPN cuts Pro to $5.99/mo and Advanced to $3.99/mo on 2-year deals" (Sep 14, 2026) and "ExpressVPN swaps app-icon row for 2Yr/1Yr/1Mo billing tabs, 80% off" (Aug 31, 2026). ExpressVPN has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
