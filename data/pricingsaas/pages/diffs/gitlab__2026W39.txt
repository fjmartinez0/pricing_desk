What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of GitLab's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 'Do volume discounts apply to overage?' replaced by a 'reserved vs on-demand' usage Q&A; new self-service FAQs added for viewing Flex balance/reservations and setting on-demand usage caps; commitment allocation narrowed from 'organizations or instances' to 'Self-Managed instances' only. In detail: GitLab Flex commitment FAQ restructured: the "Do volume discounts apply to overage?" question was replaced by a new "reserved vs on-demand" usage question; two new self-service questions were added (viewing Flex balance/reservations, setting on-demand usage caps); and commitment allocation was narrowed from "across multiple organizations or instances" to "across multiple Self-Managed instances" only. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 16, 2026) lists Premium at $29, Enterprise Agile Planning at $15, Compute Minutes at $10 per 1,000 minutes, Storage at $5 and GitLab Duo Agent Platform at $1 per GitLab Credit.
It lands 3 days after GitLab's previous change, "GitLab renames Flex 'per-use' credits to 'on-demand,' drops overage discounts" (Sep 18, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "GitLab: New Flex Commitment annual billing option introduced" (Jul 3, 2026). GitLab has been tracked since 2025-10-05.
