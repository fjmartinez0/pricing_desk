Verified Nov 9, 2026·Cloud & DevOps·Direction: neutral

What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Cloudflare's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New note: AI Gateway credits can only be purchased by credit card; other payment methods such as PayPal are not supported. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists AI Gateway at $0.05 per $1 of AI inference credit purchased via Unified Billing (5% fee).
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Cloudflare. Cloudflare has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Aws, Zscaler, Fastly and Akamai Technologies also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
