Fireworks adds SOC 2, ISO 27001, 27701 and 42001 badges to pricing page
Verified Oct 26, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: up
The changeFeature — feature added.

What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Fireworks's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing page now displays SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001, ISO 27701, and ISO 42001 compliance certification badges, which were absent from the prior version. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists On-Demand at $13 per GPU hour (B200 180GB) + $15 per GPU hour (B300 288GB), Serverless at $0.08 per 10M input tokens (embeddings, up to 150M params) + $0.16 per 10M input tokens (embeddings, 150M-350M params) and Fine Tuning at $0.5 per 1M training tokens, LoRA SFT (models up to 16B params).
It lands 7 days after Fireworks's previous change, "Fireworks adds Muse Spark Open and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash to Serverless Training" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fireworks adds Muse Spark Open, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash to Serverless Training" (Oct 17, 2026), "Fireworks adds GLM 5.3 Flash with 200K context, per-token training pricing" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Fireworks adds GLM 5.3 to Serverless Training at $14.58/1M train tokens" (Sep 16, 2026). Fireworks has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Figma and Corel also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
