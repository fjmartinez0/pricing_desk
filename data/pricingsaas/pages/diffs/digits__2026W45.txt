What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Digits's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Multi-Entity row added to the plan comparison: multiple entities with one login and separate books, Unlimited on all plans; extra entities free up to 250 transactions/year, then from $35/mo. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $65, Core at $100 and Pro at $250.
It lands 42 days after Digits's previous change, "Digits: Quality Checks added; Custom Reports/Dashboards gated to Firm Partners" (Sep 21, 2026). Digits has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
In the same window OpenPhone, Bird, Telnyx, Twilio and Plivo also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
