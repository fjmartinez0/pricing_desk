What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of LearnDash's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: LearnDash replaced standalone Pro ($399/yr) and Elite ($599/yr) license tiers with site-count bundle pricing (1-100 sites, $350-$999) bundling LearnDash with Give, The Events Calendar, and Kadence. In detail: LearnDash dropped its standalone Pro ($399/yr) and Elite ($599/yr) license tiers, replacing them with site-count bundle pricing (1-100 sites, $350-$999) that bundles LearnDash with Give, The Events Calendar, and Kadence. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $33.25 and Elite at $49.92.
It lands 7 days after LearnDash's previous change, "LearnDash drops $259 Essentials plan, leaving Pro $399 and Elite $599" (Oct 19, 2026). LearnDash has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
