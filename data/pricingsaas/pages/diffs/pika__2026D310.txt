What happened
Between the Nov 5, 2026 and Nov 6, 2026 captures of Pika's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter's monthly credit allotment dropped from 900 to 600 and Creator's dropped from 3,150 to 2,600, with list price unchanged ($10/mo and $35/mo). Per-model output tables and the recommended-plan calculator scaled down to match. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $8, Creator at $28, Fancy at $76 and Credit Pack at $1 per 60 credits.
It lands 2 days after Pika's previous change, "Pika adds daily free H3 Flex generations plus 14-model credits table" (Nov 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pika adds commercial license to $10 Starter plan, launches plan recommender" (Oct 26, 2026), "Pika top-up credits now work with MCP, ending a key limitation" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Pika opens MCP to credit pack top-ups, dropping its exclusion" (Oct 19, 2026). Pika has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window Synthesia, OpenAI and Runway also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
