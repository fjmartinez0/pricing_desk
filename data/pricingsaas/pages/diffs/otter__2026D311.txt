What happened
Between the Nov 6, 2026 and Nov 7, 2026 captures of Otter's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Custom AI Meeting Templates usage-limit row removed from the plan comparison table (was Basic 5, Pro 15, Business 30 meetings/user, Enterprise Unlimited) — quota no longer disclosed per tier. In detail: Custom AI Meeting Templates per-tier usage-limit row (Basic 5, Pro 15, Business 30 meetings/user, Enterprise Unlimited) removed from the plan comparison table; quota is no longer disclosed per tier. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $8.33 and Business at $19.99.
It lands 117 days after Otter's previous change, "Otter narrows Business promo to first-time subscribers only" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Otter: AI transcription now supports 6 languages (up from 3)" (Jul 4, 2026), "Otter Adds User Limits to Salesforce and HubSpot Integrations" (Apr 6, 2026) and "Otter expands Pro integrations to Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zapier" (Apr 2, 2026). Otter has been tracked since 2023-12-07.
