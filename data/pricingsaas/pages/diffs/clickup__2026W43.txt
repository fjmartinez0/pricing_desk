What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of ClickUp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: ClickUp is testing a new pricing layout for part of its visitors. In the test layout, the Core plan rose from $8 to $9/user/month billed yearly (regular price $12 unchanged). The standard plans are unchanged (Unlimited $7, Business $12). In detail: ClickUp is testing a new pricing layout for part of its visitors. In the test layout, the Core plan is $9/user/month billed yearly (it was $8 in the previous capture; regular price $12 unchanged). The standard plans are unchanged (Unlimited $7, Business $12). The current page lists Unlimited at $7, Business at $12, Brain AI at $9, Everything AI at $28 and AI Super Credits at $10 per 10,000 credits.
