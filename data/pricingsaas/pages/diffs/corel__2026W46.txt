What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Corel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday promo (save up to 60%) replaces standard offers; 30% off first year on subscriptions; 8 more products listed (e.g. CorelDRAW Standard, AfterShot Pro, VectorPass).
