What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Educative's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Standard increased from $12 to $13/month and Premium from $16 to $17/month; annual and two-year billed totals rose ~$10/$20 across all three plans, including Premium Plus whose $21/month headline stayed flat; Course library count increased from 2100+ to 2200+ Courses for all plans (listed in Standard's 'Plan includes' and inherited by Premium/Premium Plus), shown consistently in the plan cards and the plan comparison table. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit increase, price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Standard at $12, Premium at $16 and Premium Plus at $21.
It lands 56 days after Educative's previous change, "Educative: "Fenzo" AI tutorial feature renamed to "AI Tutor"" (Jul 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Educative raises Premium prices as anniversary sale nears deadline" (Jun 30, 2026). Educative has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Coursera and Codecademy also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
