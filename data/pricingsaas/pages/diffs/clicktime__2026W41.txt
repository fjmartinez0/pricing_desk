What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of ClickTime's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed the Pay Monthly/Pay Yearly billing toggle and per-plan annual discount badges (20% Off Starter, 21% Off Team, 13% Off Premier); hero now reads 'Starter plans from $12/user/month'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Starter at $12 and Expense Tracking at $4.
It lands 76 days after ClickTime's previous change, "ClickTime reshuffles Team/Premier plan messaging, no pricing impact" (Jul 20, 2026). ClickTime has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
