What happened
Between the Nov 26, 2026 and Nov 27, 2026 captures of Synthesia's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro plan's Syren avatar video allowance increased from ~84 to ~96 videos per year (~7 to ~8 videos per month). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised). The current page lists Starter at $14, Pro at $59 and Business at $79.
It lands 3 days after Synthesia's previous change, "Synthesia cuts Pro plan to $44/month as Black Friday discount deepens to 51%" (Nov 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Synthesia splits pricing into individual/business tabs, hides Enterprise…" (Nov 18, 2026), "Synthesia swaps Syren AI time cap for tiered video quotas, cuts Basic assets" (Nov 8, 2026) and "Synthesia swaps Syren 3-minute video cap for per-plan monthly video quotas" (Nov 7, 2026). Synthesia has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
