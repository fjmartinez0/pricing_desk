What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of SignWell's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: API free documents/mo now 25 on every plan (Free 3→25, Light 15→25); Enterprise Custom → 25 free then pay per doc; separate pay-as-you-go API pricing row removed.
