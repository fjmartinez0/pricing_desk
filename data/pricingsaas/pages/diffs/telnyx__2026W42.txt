What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Telnyx's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Committed tier (from $500/mo) replaced by Growth at $2,000/mo with monthly minimum spend and a 15% discount on Voice, Messaging and Numbers; Enterprise now $5,000/mo minimum spend; Growth card drops named account manager/priority support/higher limits copy, adds automatic volume discounts and 24/7 chat or call support. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature change, price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pay As You Go at $1.
It lands the same day as Telnyx's previous change, "Telnyx Conversationalai quadruples Growth plan minimum to $2,000/month" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Telnyx: Deepgram & AssemblyAI STT rates cut to $0.005/min" (Sep 21, 2026), "Telnyx Conversationalai voice AI estimate rises to $0.06/min as Kimi GPU LLM rate climbs 50%" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Telnyx Conversationalai: Call recording, phone number & SMS rates increased" (Aug 11, 2026). Telnyx has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Google Cloud, Twilio and Plivo also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
