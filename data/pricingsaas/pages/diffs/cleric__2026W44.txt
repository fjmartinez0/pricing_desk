What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Cleric's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pro plan 'Priority support' downgraded to 'Email support' (support row now Email/Email/Shared Slack/CSM); Enterprise now annual billing only with Order Form, MSA, invoice billing and negotiated DPA; Annual Team, Pro and Scale plans now purchasable through AWS Marketplace. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added, feature removed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Team at $560, Pro at $1,200 and Scale at $2,400.
Cleric's previous recorded change was "Cleric doubles free trial credits to 1,000 and adds Microsoft Teams" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cleric halves credit price to $0.50, adds $3,000 Scale plan, drops Starter" (Oct 12, 2026), "Cleric swaps 14-day trial cap for 500-credit no-limit evaluation, renames metric" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Cleric: pricing restructured to 4 tiers, credit price cut $2→$1" (Aug 10, 2026). Cleric has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Salesforce, Pipedrive, Microsoft and HubSpot also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
