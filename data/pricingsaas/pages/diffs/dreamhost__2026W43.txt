What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Dreamhost's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Liftoff AI Website Builder added to the Web Hosting Launch, Growth and Scale plan feature lists (25-, 50- and 100-website tiers). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Web Hosting Launch at $10.99, VPS Stack 4 at $11.99, Web Hosting Growth at $12.99, VPS Stack 8 at $16.99 and DreamPress 1 at $19.99.
It lands 28 days after Dreamhost's previous change, "Dreamhost quietly drops promised free starter website perk, FAQ lags" (Sep 21, 2026). Dreamhost has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Kinsta, Hostgator and Siteground also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
