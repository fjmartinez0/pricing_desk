What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of CoderPad's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Team plan's included custom questions allowance cut from 100 to 20, shown on both the plan card and the plan comparison table; Enterprise plan's 'volume-based · no credits' billing descriptor was removed from next to the Talk to Sales CTA, with no replacement text shown in its place. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease, positioning) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Starter at $80, Team at $400 and Frontier Lite at $1,000.
It lands 7 days after CoderPad's previous change, "CoderPad revamps tiers: Custom becomes Enterprise, Team gets 5x question boost" (Sep 14, 2026). CoderPad has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
In the same window Replit and HackerRank also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
