What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 3, 2026 captures of Synthesia's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed the interactive credit calculator / plan-recommendation widget ('See what plan best fits your needs' with Video minutes, Dubbing minutes, and Survey responses sliders) that sat above the comparison table in both the monthly and annual toggle states. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Nov 4, 2026) lists Starter at $14 and Pro at $59.
It lands one day after Synthesia's previous change, "Synthesia drops $25 Roleplay learner-seat add-on, adds free trial to paid plans" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Synthesia drops Roleplay add-on for new 'Syren' video allowance tiers" (Oct 29, 2026), "Synthesia adds up to 4 months free on yearly plans, Pro yearly at $59/mo" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Synthesia cuts Syren promo credits to 1,000 on Basic and 1,500 on Starter and Pro" (Oct 22, 2026). Synthesia has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window Runway, OpusClip and Loom also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
