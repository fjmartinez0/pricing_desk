Google Workspace swaps Gems for AI skills, rewords Gemini on Starter plan
Verified Oct 12, 2026·Cloud & DevOps·Direction: neutral
The changeStarter positioning — positioning.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 5, 2026 → Oct 12, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Google's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Gemini AI wording changed: Starter bullet now 'Chat with Gemini AI'; comparison-table row 'Build custom AI experts with Gems' replaced by 'Customize AI with skills'. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $7, Standard at $14 and Plus at $22.
It lands 35 days after Google's previous change, "Google Workspace: promo discount cut from 50% to 30%/20% off" (Sep 7, 2026). Google has been tracked since 2025-12-20.
In the same window Microsoft, Anthropic, OpenAI and Metabase also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
