What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Chilipiper's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Routing & Scheduling now includes 'Concierge Live and Handoff Live' calling and 'Live Chat with a team member,' both previously Experiences-exclusive; removed from Experiences' list and upgrade pitch. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Experiences at $50, Routing & Scheduling at $1,250 and ChiliCal Standalone at $12.
It lands 87 days after Chilipiper's previous change, "Chili Piper: full pricing model overhaul to bundled annual tiers" (Jul 2, 2026). Chilipiper has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Salesforce, HubSpot, Acuity Scheduling, Calendly and Outreach also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
