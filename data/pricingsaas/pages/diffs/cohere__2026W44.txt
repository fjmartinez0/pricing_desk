What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Cohere's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Model Vault Rerank 4 Fast/Pro Medium renamed Rerank 5 and cut from $5.00/hr ($3,250/mo) to $4.00/hr ($2,500/mo); Rerank 4 Pro Large ($10.00) and Rerank 3.5 rows removed. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Embed 5 Fast at $0.08 Input tokens, Embed 5 Pro at $0.12 Input tokens, Parse 5 at $1.5 Pages, Rerank 4 Fast at $2 Searches and Rerank 4 Pro at $2.5 Searches.
It lands 21 days after Cohere's previous change, "Cohere cuts Model Vault Embed pricing 25% with new Embed 5 Fast and Pro tiers" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cohere cuts Model Vault Small to $3/hr as Embed 5 replaces Embed 4" (Oct 1, 2026), "Cohere adds Parse 5 doc-parsing model, Medium $4/hr and XL $7/hr tiers" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Cohere adds Parse 5 to Model Vault with Medium and XL tiered pricing" (Aug 28, 2026). Cohere has been tracked since 2024-12-01.
