What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Callmatic's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Starter rose from ₹2,099 to ₹4,499/month + GST (114% increase), alongside a doubled allowance; New Enterprise plan added (Contact us pricing, unlimited minutes, dedicated concurrency); Starter included allowance doubled from 700 mins/7,000 calls to 1500 mins/15,000 calls. Pulse files it as a pricing change (new plan, price increase, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at INR 4,499, Growth at INR 9,999, Business at INR 29,999 and Standard Indian Number at INR 249.
Callmatic's previous recorded change was "Callmatic ditches hidden Lite/Premium tiers for public ₹2,099–₹29,999 plans, " (Jul 1, 2026). Callmatic has been tracked since 2026-04-04.
