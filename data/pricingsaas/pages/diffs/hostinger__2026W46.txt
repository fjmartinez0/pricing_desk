What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Hostinger's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Black Friday promo deepened on 48-month plans: Premium $2.49 to $1.99/mo (80% to 85% off; strikethrough $12.19 to $12.99), Unlimited $3.49 to $2.79/mo (82% to 85% off). Upfront totals $119.52 to $95.52 and $167.52 to $133.92; renewal rates unchanged ($10.99/$16.99). Cloud Startup unchanged at $7.99; Build-with bullets 'AI assistant for your online store' and 'Node.js' replaced by 'AI coding tools. In detail: Build-with bullets 'AI assistant for your online store' and 'Node.js' replaced by 'AI coding tools' and 'Custom code' on all plans; on Premium the online-store AI assistant was included, while AI coding tools and Custom code are shown as excluded (dashed). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change, feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Premium at $1.99, Unlimited at $2.79, Cloud Startup at $9.99, Agency Startup at $29 and Agency Professional at $44.
It lands 13 days after Hostinger's previous change, "Hostinger Black Friday cuts Premium to $2.49/mo, doubles AI credits" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hostinger boosts one-time AI credits on hosting plans, up to 1,000" (Oct 19, 2026), "Hostinger clarifies AI Builder credits are a one-time gift, not recurring" (Oct 12, 2026) and "Hostinger opens Ecommerce to Premium plan, drops row from comparison table" (Sep 14, 2026). Hostinger has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Siteground, Dreamhost and Name.com also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
