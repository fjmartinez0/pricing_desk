What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of TaxSlayer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Classic price cut from $44.99 to $19.99 and Premium from $64.99 to $39.99 (plus state add-on). In detail: Classic price cut from $44.99 to $19.99 and Premium from $64.99 to $39.99; each additional state return cut from $47.99 to $39.99. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Classic at $19.99 per use, Premium at $39.99 per use, Self-Employed Guidance at $9.99 per use and State Return at $47.99 per use.
It lands 7 days after TaxSlayer's previous change, "TaxSlayer drops $74.99 Self-Employed plan, leaving just three tiers" (Nov 2, 2026). TaxSlayer has been tracked since 2026-06-24.
In the same window TurboTax and Wolters Kluwer also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
