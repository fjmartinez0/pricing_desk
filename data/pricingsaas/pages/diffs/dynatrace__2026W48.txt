What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Dynatrace's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Container Application Monitoring offering added for Kubernetes/serverless at $5/mo per container ($0.007/hr), with 16 GiB trace volume and 900K custom metric data points included; Foundation lost log correlation in context; Full-Stack dropped 10-day trace retention; hosts tiers now list included custom metric data points (4.4M, 21M) and trace volume (67 GiB). Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan, feature change). The current page lists Code Monitoring at $3.60 per container/month, Log Analytics - Pay-per-Query at $3.50 per 1,000 GiB scanned + $0.70 per 1,000 GiB-day retained, Metrics at $0.70 per 1,000 GiB-day retained + $0.15 per 100k datapoints ingested, Traces at $3.50 per 1,000 GiB scanned + $0.20 per GiB ingested and Log Analytics - Bundled Queries at $0.20 per GiB ingested + $0.02 per GiB-day retained (with included queries).
It lands 42 days after Dynatrace's previous change, "Dynatrace launches Agentic Operations at $0.10 per AI unit, AI Exploration free" (Oct 11, 2026). Dynatrace has been tracked since 2023-12-08.
In the same window Splunk, AppDynamics, Datadog and Elastic also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
