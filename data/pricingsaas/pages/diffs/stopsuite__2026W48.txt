What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of StopSuite's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Two location-based tiers ($150/mo and $250/mo, both +$0.09/stop) merged into one $250/mo + $0.06/stop plan; setup fee 'starting at $500' became a scoped one-time migration/training fee; Added Contracts & e-signatures, route balancing & capacity routing, business phone system, email marketing (1,000/mo free) and built-in AI assistants to the included feature list. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, packaging change). The current page lists stopSuite Platform at $250, Website at $25, Email Marketing - Growth at $9.75, Email Marketing - Pro at $33.75 and Email Marketing - Scale at $82.50.
