What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Quickbooks's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Workforce Payroll + Essentials list price increased from $125 to $135/mo + $7/employee (promo 50%-off price $62.50 to $67.50/mo); Workforce Premium + Plus list price increased from $203 to $228/mo + $13/employee (promo price $101.50 to $114/mo). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Workforce Payroll + Simple Start at $88, Workforce Payroll + Essentials at $135 and Workforce Premium + Plus at $228.
It lands 34 days after Quickbooks's previous change, "Quickbooks Payroll: Summer promo jumps to 90% off (was 50%)" (Aug 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Quickbooks Payroll: per-employee fee cut on all 3 plans" (Jul 27, 2026) and "Quickbooks Payroll hikes per-employee fees up to 30% across all plans" (Jul 6, 2026). Quickbooks has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Zoho, Xero, Sage, Microsoft and Freshbooks also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
