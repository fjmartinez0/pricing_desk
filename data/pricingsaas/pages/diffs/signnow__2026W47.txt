What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of signNow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Business Premium annual-billed price rose from $15 to $20 per month per user (list $30); annual savings callout fell from $180 to $120. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $8, Professional at $15, Business at $30 and Enterprise at $1.50 per signature invite.
It lands 14 days after signNow's previous change, "signNow: All four tiers renamed in place (same card positions, same prices): Starter->Business ($8/mo),…" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "signNow adds 30-day money-back guarantee badge to pricing page" (Sep 14, 2026) and "signNow spells out invite caps on plan cards: 5 a month on Business" (Aug 24, 2026). signNow has been tracked since 2026-03-18.
In the same window Pandadoc and OneSpan also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
