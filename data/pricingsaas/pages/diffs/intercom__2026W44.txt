What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Intercom's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Essential plan list price increased from $19 to $29/seat/mo; new customers get a 35% promotional discount back to $19/seat/mo, down from an unconditional $19 price. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essential at $29, Advanced at $85, Expert at $132, Fin AI Agent (Standalone) at $0.99 per Fin outcome and Copilot at $29.
It lands 5 days after Intercom's previous change, "Intercom offers new customers 35% off Essential, cutting seats to $19" (Oct 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Intercom drops Fin Million Dollar Guarantee and 90-day money-back pledge" (Oct 19, 2026), "Intercom Pro add-on starting price ticks up $1 to $100 per workspace" (Oct 12, 2026) and "Intercom launches Fin Voice AI at $1.99 per outcome, adds 90-day guarantee" (Oct 11, 2026). Intercom has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window HubSpot, Salesforce and LiveChat also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
