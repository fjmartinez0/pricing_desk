What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Kilo Code's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed the time-limited competitive-switch promo banner offering free Kilo Enterprise access (GPT and 500+ models) through a customer's existing Cursor contract, tied to Cursor's November 12 OpenAI model removal. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Teams at $15, Kilo Pass Starter at $19, Kilo Pass Pro at $49, Kilo Pass Expert at $199 and Cloud Compute at $1.20 per hour — Cloud Agent (Standard) + $0.60 per hour — Cloud Agent (Docker).
It lands 28 days after Kilo Code's previous change, "Kilo Code: Commercial use of 40K+ Anaconda packages added to Free plan" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kilo Code rebrands Teams Shared agent modes as Shared custom agents" (Oct 11, 2026) and "Kilo Code: Teams trial redefined as Enterprise trial" (Aug 30, 2026). Kilo Code has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Replit, GitHub, Cursor, Windsurf and JetBrains also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
