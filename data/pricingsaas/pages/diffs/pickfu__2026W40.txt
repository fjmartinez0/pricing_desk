What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of PickFu's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pay as you go plan's audience-targeting capacity increased: traits available went from 90+ to 100+, and supported countries for global feedback rose from 14 to 15; Pay as you go plan's survey-type bullet was rewritten from a specific '10 survey types covering every use case' to the broader claim 'All survey features and options'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists PickFu+ $999 at $83.25, PickFu+ $99/mo at $99, PickFu+ $2999 at $249.92, PickFu+ $4999 at $416.58 and PickFu+ $9999 at $833.25.
It lands 103 days after PickFu's previous change, "PickFu opens free AI analysis to all plans, drops $29 add-on" (Jun 17, 2026). PickFu has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window SurveyMonkey, Respondent, Google, Userlytics and Typeform also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
