What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Stream's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Chat Enterprise column no longer lists stated minimums: MAU '1,000,000+/mo' and concurrent '750,000+' now show 'Custom'.
