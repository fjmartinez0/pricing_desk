What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of CodeRabbit's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Add-ons section renamed to 'On demand products'; standalone Usage-based reviews card removed, replaced by table row 'Reviews beyond included limits' (pay-as-you-go self-serve, Contact sales for Enterprise). Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Essentials at $24, Team at $48, Advanced at $72, CodeRabbit Agent at $0.4 per agent minute and Usage-based reviews at $0.25 per reviewed file.
It lands 4 days after CodeRabbit's previous change, "CodeRabbit reframes Advanced around architectural insights (copy only)" (Sep 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CodeRabbit merges Cloud Coding and Slack agent add-ons into one $0.40/min Agent" (Sep 14, 2026), "CodeRabbit merges Slack and Cloud Coding agents into one add-on" (Sep 9, 2026) and "CodeRabbit drops Issue Planner from Team, Advanced and Enterprise plans" (Sep 4, 2026). CodeRabbit has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
In the same window GitLab, Replit, JetBrains and Windsurf also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
