What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Stream's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Chat usage overage rates changed on Start/Elevate plans: Channel Overages effective rate doubled (now $100.00 per 1M channels, up from $0.05 per 1K channels); Stored Message Overages reduced from $6.00 to $5.00 per 1M messages. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Start at $399 and Elevate at $599.
It lands 119 days after Stream's previous change, "Stream boosts API bandwidth 50x while raising overage rates" (May 25, 2026). Stream has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
In the same window You.com, Kickbox, GitHub and Unity also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
