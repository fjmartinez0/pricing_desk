What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Datadog's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Cloud SIEM now states workflows triggered from security findings and signals are free (other triggers billed separately); previously 'Workflows are billed separately'. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Cloud SIEM at $5 and Cloud SIEM On-Demand at $7.5.
It lands 70 days after Datadog's previous change, "Datadog Cloud Siem: free investigation allotment metric changed to per-TB" (Jul 27, 2026). Datadog has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Elastic, Grafana, Sumologic and Splunk also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
