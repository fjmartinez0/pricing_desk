What happened
Between the Oct 7, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Moodle's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: MoodleCloud Starter (50 users, 1GB) annual price raised from $160 USD to $170 USD; other USD tiers unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Starter at $14.17, Mini at $22.5, Small at $40.83, Medium at $99.17 and Standard at $175.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Moodle. Moodle has been tracked since 2026-10-07.
