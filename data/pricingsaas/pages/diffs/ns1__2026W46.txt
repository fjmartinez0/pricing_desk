What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of NS1's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Essentials increased from $99.00 to $108.00 USD/mo and Standard from $349.00 to $380.00 USD/mo; new 'Save 60%' holiday promo banner (ends Dec 14, 2026) replaced the expired 40%-off banner. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at CAD 142 and Standard at CAD 500.
It lands 7 days after NS1's previous change, "NS1 swaps Oct promo for Holiday deal, Essentials drops to $39.60/mo" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "NS1 launches up to 40% off Connect plans through Oct 31" (Oct 5, 2026). NS1 has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Google Cloud, Cloudflare and Akamai Technologies also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
