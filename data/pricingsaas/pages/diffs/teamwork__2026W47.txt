What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Teamwork's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI credits now live: monthly allowances of 2,500 (Accelerate), 7,500 (Optimize) and custom (Enterprise), replacing the 'AI included for introductory period' note. Prices unchanged. In detail: AI credits now live: monthly allowances of 1,000 (Basics), 2,500 (Accelerate), 7,500 (Optimize) and custom (Enterprise), replacing the 'AI included for introductory period' note. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Basics at $9.99 and Accelerate at $24.99.
It lands 49 days after Teamwork's previous change, "Teamwork delays usage-based AI credit billing to November 2026" (Sep 28, 2026). Teamwork has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Asana, Notion and ClickUp also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
