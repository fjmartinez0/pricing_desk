What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Chargeflow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Prevent per-scanned-transaction price cut from $0.20 (shown against a $0.40 list price) to $0.10; the struck-through $0.40 anchor is removed. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Prevent at $0.2 per scanned transaction, Prevent - Accelerator Program at $0.1 per scanned order (Year 1) + $0.15 per scanned order (Year 2), Automation at 25% per recovered chargeback, Alerts at $29 per deflected chargeback and Automation - Accelerator Program at 7.5% per recovered chargeback (Year 1) + 15% per recovered chargeback (Year 2).
It lands 117 days after Chargeflow's previous change, "Chargeflow's fraud prevention network grows to 20,000+ merchants" (Jun 23, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Chargeflow Cuts Alert Pricing to $29, Launches Startup Accelerator" (Apr 2, 2026), "Chargeflow Cuts Alerts Deflection Price 26% to $29 Per Chargeback" (Week 3, 2026) and "Chargeflow Launches Prevent Plan: First 1,000 Transactions Free, $0.2 Per Scanned Transaction After" (Q3 2025). Chargeflow has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Quickbooks, Gate, Orb, Settle and Vendr also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
