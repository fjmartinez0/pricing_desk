What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Nextiva's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Plans renamed NEXT Core/Engage/Scale; XBert AI now priced at $1.50 per conversation on each plan. Seat prices unchanged ($15/$25/$75); Plan feature lists rewritten: AI Transcription/Summarization/Answer Assist quotas (50/200/unlimited), SMS caps 100/500/unlimited; Advanced Reporting and Screen Sharing no longer listed. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, feature change) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 27, 2026) lists Core at $15, Engage at $25, Scale at $75 and XBert AI Employee at $99.
It lands 115 days after Nextiva's previous change, "Nextiva: Power Suite CX renamed to Scale + new 12-months-free switch offer" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Nextiva Halves Shared Inbox Limits on Engage and Scale Plans" (Apr 13, 2026) and "Nextiva Repositions Plan Tiers From Team Size to Customer Value" (Mar 17, 2026). Nextiva has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
