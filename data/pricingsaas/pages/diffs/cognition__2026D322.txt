What happened
Between the Nov 17, 2026 and Nov 18, 2026 captures of Devin's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Teams plan simplified from $80/month + $40/mo per full dev seat to a flat $80/month; per-seat fee and the 'per full user' comparison-table footnote were removed; Added 'Devin Bots' as a new capability across Max, Teams, and Enterprise plans, listed on the plan cards and as a new row in the plan comparison table under Devin Cloud. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change, feature added). The current page lists Pro at $20, Teams at $80 and Max at $200.
It lands 7 days after Devin's previous change, "Devin halves Team plan user cap to 100, adds ChatGPT plan support" (Nov 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Devin caps Teams plan at 200 members, ending unlimited seats" (Sep 24, 2026) and "Devin adds SpaceXAI to Devin Pro frontier model lineup" (Sep 17, 2026). Devin has been tracked since 2026-06-26.
