What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Fill's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Fill per-user prices cut ~20%: Basic $10.41 to $8.33, Plus $20.83 to $16.67, Pro $36.45 to $29.17 per month billed annually; monthly billing also lower ($12.49 to $9.99, etc.). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Basic at $8.33, Plus at $16.67 and Pro at $29.17.
It lands 56 days after Fill's previous change, "Fill reveals 7-day free trial length with new start-trial CTA guidance" (Sep 14, 2026). Fill has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
