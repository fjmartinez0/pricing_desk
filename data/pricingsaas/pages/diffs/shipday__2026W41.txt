What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Shipday's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Business AI Lite rose $159 to $199/mo and Business AI $349 to $399/mo; Business AI now lists unlimited orders per location instead of $0.25 per extra order. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Branded Elite at $99, Business AI Lite at $199, Business AI at $399 and Enterprise at $3,499.
It lands 7 days after Shipday's previous change, "Shipday hikes Enterprise plan 40% to $3,499/month, other tiers untouched" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shipday quietly drops Enterprise's annual commitment and order-cap terms" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Shipday reveals Enterprise pricing: $2,499/month for 10K orders, AI Agents, SLAs" (Sep 7, 2026). Shipday has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Postscript, Klaviyo, Gorgias and Twilio also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
