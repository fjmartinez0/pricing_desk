What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Gravitee's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pricing moved from flat unlimited-API tiers to Planet/Galaxy/Universe with modular add-ons (API, Event, Identity & Authorization, Agent Management); Planet adds a non-prod gateway and 1 module; Universe includes 2 modules. New Consumption-based tab caps requests at 50M/100M/200M+ per month. $2,500 unchanged; Marketing copy changed from 'Flat Pricing' to 'Predictable Pricing' and 'pay for gateways. In detail: Marketing copy changed from 'Flat Pricing' to 'Predictable Pricing' and 'pay for gateways & features' to 'modules & capacity, self-hosted, hybrid, or full SaaS'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (positioning, package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Planet at $2,500.
It lands 56 days after Gravitee's previous change, "Gravitee: Agent Catalog added to Planet + new feature comparison table" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Gravitee: Detailed feature comparison table removed from pricing page" (Aug 10, 2026). Gravitee has been tracked since 2025-12-10.
In the same window Konghq, Postman and MuleSoft also changed their pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
