What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Kahoot!'s pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro Max's intro discount was removed, reverting price from $69/mo ($828/yr) to $79/mo ($948/yr). Pro Plus gained a new intro discount, dropping from $49/mo to $39/mo ($588 to $468/yr). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Kahoot! 360 Pro Start at $19, Kahoot! 360 Spirit at $20, Kahoot! 360 Pro Standard at $25, Kahoot! 360 Spirit Premium at $25 and Kahoot! 360 Pro Plus at $39.
It lands 14 days after Kahoot!'s previous change, "Kahoot! flattens Pro Ultra participant cap to 5,000, drops onboarding gate" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kahoot! Pro Plus loses intro discount, price jumps to flat $49/mo" (Aug 24, 2026), "Kahoot!: Pro Plus plan price cut to $39/mo (from $49/mo)" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Kahoot!: Pro Ultra 5000-participant cap now requires onboarding" (Jul 27, 2026). Kahoot! has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Mentimeter, Typeform, Nearpod and Duo also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
