What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Verbit's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Legal tab Standard plan renamed 'Legal Transcription'; $24/month price and self-serve CTA removed in favor of 'Book a demo'; feature list cut, now led by certified transcripts; new Scopist add-on added; Legal Visor tagline changed from case-management framing to real-time deposition intelligence; 'Support for digital evidence' feature moved from the entry plan to the Legal Capture plan; Legal Tr. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden, limit increase, positioning) with a downward direction.
