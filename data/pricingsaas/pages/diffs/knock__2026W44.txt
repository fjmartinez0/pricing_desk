What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Knock's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Feature added: Starter plan gained Priority email support, a new benefit previously undifferentiated within Enterprise-only support. Plan renamed: Developer plan renamed to Free (price unchanged at $0/month, same included limits). In detail: Entry-level plan renamed from 'Developer' to 'Free'; price unchanged at $0/month and included limits (10K messages, 500 guide users, 500 AI agent credits) stayed the same; Starter plan gained 'Priority email support' as a new benefit (previously only an undifferentiated Enterprise-only benefit); Enterprise's priority support now explicitly covers Slack, email, and call. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $250 and Free at $0.01 per AI agent credit.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Knock. Knock has been tracked since 2026-09-08.
