What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Railway's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Railway added a new 'Sandboxes & VMs' product: usage-billed Linux VMs for agents/builds (~$50/GB-month memory, ~$50/vCPU-month CPU, $0.05/GB egress), with tiered sandbox capacity limits across all plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Hobby at $5, Pro at $20, Volumes (Usage) at $0.16 per GB-month, Egress (Usage) at $0.05 per GB for services and Object Storage (Usage) at $0.02 per GB-month.
It lands 79 days after Railway's previous change, "Railway: Availability Target SLAs added + Pro volume limit doubled + new Railway Agent addon" (Jul 4, 2026). Railway has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Google Cloud, Vercel, Render and Heroku also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
