What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of FarmBackup's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Base fees fell: Payroll $30 to $29, Farming $55 to $50, Contracting $75 to $72; Farming/Contracting per-user $30 to $26; Payroll adds separate $25 admin user fee; Farming plan client limit raised from up to 20 clients to up to 30 clients. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit increase, price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Payroll at $25, Farming at $50 and Contracting at $72.
It lands 84 days after FarmBackup's previous change, "FarmBackup: EUR→USD switch + Farming/Contracting price hikes" (Aug 10, 2026). FarmBackup has been tracked since 2026-05-06.
In the same window Acronis and Wasabi also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
