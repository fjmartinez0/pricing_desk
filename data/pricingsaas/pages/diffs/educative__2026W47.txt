What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Educative's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Annual prices cut $10 on every plan: Standard $149→$139/yr ($13→$12/mo), Premium $199→$189/yr ($17→$16/mo), Premium Plus $249→$239/yr ($21/mo shown in both). Struck-through list prices also dropped ($298→$278, $398→$378, $498→$478); 50% off unchanged. Early Bird badge now reads 'Up to 55% off' (was 50%). Matches known price experiment c38eeb3cbc404087; Standard adds 3,000+ Coding Interview Proble. In detail: Standard adds 3,000+ Coding Interview Problems and Resume Analyzer; Premium adds Bug Bash Round and Code Review Round interview-prep features. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease, feature added) with a downward direction. The current page lists Standard at $13, Premium at $17 and Premium Plus at $21.
It lands 49 days after Educative's previous change, "Educative cuts annual prices across all plans: Standard now $12/mo, Premium…" (Sep 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Educative hikes Standard, Premium prices; course library grows to 2200+" (Sep 20, 2026). Educative has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
