Kahoot! flattens Pro Ultra participant cap to 5,000, drops onboarding gate
Verified Sep 14, 2026·Collaboration & Productivity·Direction: neutral
The changeFeature — feature change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 7, 2026 → Sep 14, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Kahoot!'s pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro Ultra's participant limit is now shown as a flat 5,000, removing the 'onboarding required' condition previously attached to going above the 2,000-participant base. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Kahoot! 360 Pro Start at $19, Kahoot! 360 Spirit at $20, Kahoot! 360 Pro Standard at $25, Kahoot! 360 Spirit Premium at $25 and Kahoot! 360 Pro Plus at $39.
It lands 21 days after Kahoot!'s previous change, "Kahoot! Pro Plus loses intro discount, price jumps to flat $49/mo" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kahoot!: Pro Plus plan price cut to $39/mo (from $49/mo)" (Aug 17, 2026), "Kahoot!: Pro Ultra 5000-participant cap now requires onboarding" (Jul 27, 2026) and "Kahoot! Pro Max annual price jumps to $828 as discount ends" (Jun 29, 2026). Kahoot! has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Mentimeter, Typeform, Nearpod and Duo also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
