Koala: AI MCP integration exits beta, now included with every paid plan
Verified Sep 28, 2026·Collaboration & Productivity·Direction: neutral
The changeProfessional feature — feature change.

What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Koala's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Koala AI MCP (connect an AI assistant to 62 SEO tools) exited beta and is now included with every paid plan, previously limited to Professional plans and above with beta opt-in. In detail: Koala AI MCP integration exited beta and is now included with every paid plan; previously it required beta opt-in and was limited to Professional plans and above. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Starter at $20, Professional at $39, Boost at $79, Growth at $143 and Elite at $280.
It lands 7 days after Koala's previous change, "Koala hikes entry plan to $25/mo, adds Brand DNA & agent chat" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Koala adds $9 Essentials tier, drops Google Sheets integration" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Koala AI: Essentials tier cut, Boost repackaged with Brand DNA agent + credits" (Aug 31, 2026). Koala has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Notion, Evernote and Bearly AI also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
