What happened
Between the Nov 6, 2026 and Nov 7, 2026 captures of Luma AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Video-to-Video rates now billed per second of generated + reference video, with displayed rates halved (Seedance 2.0 720p ~64 → 32, Kling 4.0 Omni ~98 → 47, MiniMax H3 768p 56 → 28 credits/sec); Kling 4.0 Flash (T2V/I2V 720p 32 credits/sec) and Kling 4.0 Flash Omni (T2V/I2V 32, V2V 21 credits/sec of generated + reference video) added; Flux 3 video rates cut: Text/Image-to-Video HD 60 → 50 and FHD. In detail: MiniMax H3 Max Video-to-Video rates raised: 768p ~73 → ~82 and 1080p ~101 → ~110 credits/sec (480p ~35 unchanged); Flux 3 video rates cut: Text/Image-to-Video HD 60 → 50 and FHD 102 → 80 credits/sec; Video-to-Video HD 144 → 70 and FHD 186 → 90 credits/sec. Pulse files it as a pricing change (package change, price increase, feature added, price decrease) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Plus at $25, Pro at $75 and Ultra at $250.
It lands 12 days after Luma AI's previous change, "Luma AI adds Kling 4.0 and 4.0 Omni, drops V2V 4K and audio rows" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Luma AI adds Kling 4.0 API pricing, from 53 credits per second at 720p" (Oct 23, 2026), "Luma AI doubles Seedance video-to-video credit costs, adds Minimax H3 Max" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Luma AI: Seedance 2.0 video generation costs cut ~50%" (Sep 21, 2026). Luma AI has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Synthesia, OpenAI, Runway and HeyGen also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
