What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Neo4j's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AuraDB Business Critical's maximum memory on GCP increased from 1944GB to 2TB per instance (AWS & Azure max unchanged at 512GB). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists AuraDS Professional at $65, AuraDB Professional at $65, AuraDB Business Critical at $146 and Aura Graph Analytics at $0.4 per GB/hour.
It lands 98 days after Neo4j's previous change, "Neo4j renames Bloom to Enterprise Studio across product line" (Jun 29, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Neo4j expands GCP max memory to 1944GB for enterprise cloud" (May 19, 2026). Neo4j has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Google Cloud and DataStax also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
