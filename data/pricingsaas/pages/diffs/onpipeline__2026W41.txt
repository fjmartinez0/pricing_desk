The changePipeline positioning — positioning.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 28, 2026 → Oct 5, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Onpipeline's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pipeline plan tagline rewritten from 'Build a clear, repeatable sales process...with confidence' to 'Run your sales process with everything you need... without basic-plan limits'. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pipeline at $20, Standard at $20 and Advanced at $20.
It lands 7 days after Onpipeline's previous change, "Onpipeline cuts free trial from 30 to 14 days, adds AI request quotas" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Onpipeline adds Customer Portal and Priority Support rows to Advanced" (Aug 17, 2026). Onpipeline has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Salesforce, Zoho, HubSpot and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
