Allocations rewords SPV and Fund asset-type lines
Verified Oct 12, 2026·Finance & Accounting·Direction: neutral
The changeFund positioning — positioning.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 5, 2026 → Oct 12, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Allocations's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: SPV card asset-type rows reworded with no change to scope: Standard 'VC asset types' (table: 'VC only') now reads 'US startup investment with a single close' (card already said US startup, 1 closing event); Premium 'Any asset type' and Fund 'Any asset types' now read 'Custom assets with multiple closes', and Premium's separate 'Multiple closes supported' row was folded into that line. Fee schedule unchanged (1 close included, extra closings $2,000 each); prices unchanged ($9,950 / $19,500 / $19,500). Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Fund at $1,625, Standard SPV at $9,950 subscription, Premium SPV at $19,500 subscription, Migration - Standard SPV at $162.5 and Migration - Premium SPV at $412.5.
It lands 7 days after Allocations's previous change, "Allocations adds collapsible Offshore fee row across SPV and Fund pricing tiers" (Oct 5, 2026). Allocations has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Mosaic and SAP SE also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
