What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 13, 2026 captures of Mintlify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Hero copy changed from a first month of free credits to a 14-day Pro trial with 5,000 credits; Starter stays free. FAQ now details the 14-day no-card Pro trial. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pro at $450.
It lands 10 days after Mintlify's previous change, "Mintlify ends free 6-month Pro offer for early-stage startups" (Oct 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mintlify offers startups 6 months of Pro free, no card needed" (Oct 1, 2026), "Mintlify adds WebMCP support and Narration, both locked out of Starter" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Mintlify adds Narration row to Publishing table for Pro, Enterprise plans" (Sep 26, 2026). Mintlify has been tracked since 2024-10-21.
In the same window GitBook, Postman, Notion and Slite also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
