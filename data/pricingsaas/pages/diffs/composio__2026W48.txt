What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Composio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Standard plan ($18/seat/month) is no longer purchasable: 'Popular' badge replaced by 'Coming soon' and 'Get started' CTA replaced by 'Coming soon'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed). The current page lists Pro at $29.
It lands 14 days after Composio's previous change, "Composio adds credits-based billing, audit logs, and limits Hobby's MCP access" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Composio adds usage-priced Firewall add-on at $0.50 per 1M tokens on Pro" (Oct 26, 2026), "Composio renames Premium tools to Instant Tools, updates billing FAQ" (Oct 11, 2026) and "Composio bumps toolkit count to 1500+ across Free, Pro and Enterprise" (Sep 6, 2026). Composio has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window UiPath, n8n, Zapier and Workato also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
