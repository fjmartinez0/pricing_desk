What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of MindManager's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Professional plan's 28% annual discount was removed; the discounted $129.00/year price and 'Save 28%' badge disappeared, leaving the plan at the full $179.00/year in both the pricing card and comparison table. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Professional at $179 and Professional (One-time purchase) at $369.
It lands 115 days after MindManager's previous change, "MindManager: Essentials, Professional & one-time purchase prices hidden ($99/$179/$369)" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindManager Cuts Professional Plan 28% to $129/Year" (Apr 13, 2026). MindManager has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
