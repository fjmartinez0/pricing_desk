What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Less Accounting's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Business Pro plan starting price increased from $599/mo to $699/mo; Simple ($299/mo) and Growth ($449/mo) plans unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Simple at $299, Growth at $449 and Business Pro at $699.
It lands 70 days after Less Accounting's previous change, "Less Accounting: Growth $399→$449, Business Pro $499→$599/mo; QBO now free" (Jul 13, 2026). Less Accounting has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
