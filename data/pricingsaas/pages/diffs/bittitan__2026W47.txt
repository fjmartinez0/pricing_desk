What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of BitTitan's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Tenant Migration Bundle price increased from $57 to $60 per user; New Flex Collaboration license added at $50 per Site, Team, or Shared Drive, per 100 GB, covering SharePoint Sites, Teams, Microsoft 365 Group sites, and Google Shared Drives; Teams Collaboration ($48) and Shared Documents ($25/$48) moved to a "Licenses Being Replaced by Flex Collaboration" section, available to purchase until Dec. In detail: Teams Collaboration ($48) and Shared Documents ($25/$48) moved to a "Licenses Being Replaced by Flex Collaboration" section, available to purchase until December 31, 2026. Prices unchanged; the Teams card unit now reads "per 10GB of data" instead of "per team". Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, positioning, new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Mailbox at $14 per user, Public Folders at $114 per 10GB of data, PowerSyncPro Bundle at $20 per user/device, User Migration Bundle at $17.50 per user and Active Directory / Entra ID (SMB Edition) at $6.25 per user.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for BitTitan. BitTitan has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
