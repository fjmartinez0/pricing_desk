Attio ditches per-seat pricing for workspace plans, adds $1,000 Ultra tier
Detected Nov 15, 2026, 7:34 PM ETSales & Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

2 changes2 capturesNov 9 → Nov 16, 2026 UTC
What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Attio's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Per-seat pricing replaced by per-workspace base prices (Plus $40, Pro $100 incl. 1 user, add users extra); new Ultra plan $1,000/mo incl. 5 users; paid add-ons for extra records and objects; New plan features: SQL queries via API/MCP, data connector, data rules, audit logs, governance add-on and forward-deployed onboarding. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change, feature added). The current page lists Plus at $40, Ultra at $189, Pro at $240 and Governance Add-on at $30.
This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Attio shifts to per-workspace pricing, adds $1,000/mo Ultra plan" (Day 315, 2026, saved Nov 11, 2026). It also records changes none of that period did (feature added), so Pulse counts it as a change of its own. Before that, Attio's previous recorded change was "Attio: Formula attributes feature added to Pro and Enterprise plans" (Aug 2, 2026). Attio has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
In the same window Close, HubSpot, Notion, Salesforce and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
