What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Veed's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Creator rose from $120/yr to $12/mo billed yearly ($144/yr); Pro from $228/yr to $22/mo ($264/yr); new Studio plan at $39/mo billed yearly; max yearly saving 60% to 51%; Pro AI credits increased from 18,000 (4,500 AI videos) to 30,000 (7,500 AI videos); new Studio plan includes 180,000 credits; Pro voice allowance up to 144 hr/yr; Creator gains full audio and video catalog and Gen-AI Studio, dro. In detail: Creator drops Full HD 1080p exports and unlimited video length, upgrades Subtitles 120 hr/yr to Unlimited Auto Subtitles, and adds full audio & video catalog and Gen-AI Studio; Pro drops 4K export and 50GB storage, adds up to 144 hr/yr of AI voice and Gen-AI Studio. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit increase, price increase, feature change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Creator at $12.25, Pro at $24 and Studio at $38.75.
It lands 7 days after Veed's previous change, "Veed kills Studio plan, slashes Pro AI credits 40% as annual prices drop" (Oct 26, 2026). Veed has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Synthesia, Runway, OpusClip and Loom also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
