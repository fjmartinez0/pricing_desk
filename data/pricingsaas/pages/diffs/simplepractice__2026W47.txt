What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Simplepractice's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free credentialing offer moved from the top promo banner to a 'Free credentialing support' bullet on the Plus card, with new terms (2 payers, new customers only, via Medallion). In detail: Free credentialing offer moved from the top promo banner to a new 'Free credentialing support' bullet on the Starter card (inherited by Essential and Plus), with new terms: two payers, new customers only, via Medallion. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Starter at $49, Essential at $79, Plus at $99, AI-powered Note Taker at $35 and Insurance Navigator at $1.79 per claim filed.
It lands 7 days after Simplepractice's previous change, "Simplepractice offers free credentialing to new sign-ups in fresh promo push" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Simplepractice drops Beta label from Treatment Planner in Care Aide" (Nov 1, 2026) and "Simplepractice: Plus plan highlight 'Appointment request widget' replaced by 'Advanced analytics capabilities'; prices…" (Oct 26, 2026). Simplepractice has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
