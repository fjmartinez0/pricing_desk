What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of MYOB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New notice: MYOB Business Payroll Only and per-user Payroll premium feature prices will change from 1 November 2026; no amounts published yet. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Solo at AUD 8.25, Payroll Only at AUD 15, Lite at AUD 26.25, Pro at AUD 52.5 and AccountRight Plus at AUD 123.75.
It lands 93 days after MYOB's previous change, "MYOB: refreshed intro offers (Solo $24→$12) + AI BAS pulled from Solo" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MYOB delays AI BAS and unlimited quotes for Solo plan" (Jun 8, 2026), "MYOB boosts AccountRight discount to 60% off for new subscribers" (Jun 2, 2026) and "MYOB slashes Solo plan to $12/year and extends promos into 2027" (May 4, 2026).
