What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Hashicorp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added Agentic access management (beta) and MCP server (beta) to HCP Essentials, HCP Standard and Enterprise feature lists.
