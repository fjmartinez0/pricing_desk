What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Local Logic's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: The 'Location Reports' plan card on the Teams & Brokerages tab was renamed to 'Neighborhood Reports'; pricing (Talk to Sales) and structure unchanged; On the renamed Neighborhood Reports plan, the plain 'SSO' bullet was expanded to 'Team branding and managed access, with optional SSO', adding managed access and reframing SSO as optional. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, plan renamed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Agents & Realtors at $20, Local SDKs at $250, Neighborhood SDKs at $500 and APIs at $1,500.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Local Logic. Local Logic has been tracked since 2026-07-14.
