Clevertap adds Decisioning Agents add-on to Ultimate, replacing CleverAI Tokens
Verified Oct 12, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: up
The changeUltimate add-on — new add-on.
2 changes·2 captures·Oct 5, 2026 → Oct 12, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Clevertap's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Decisioning Agents add-on (Tesseract Decisioning Engine, 1:1 decisioning per campaign/variant/lifecycle) available on Ultimate; replaces the CleverAI Tokens and Blocks add-on card; Ultimate feature list reworked: journey automation, predictive segments and real-time recommendations replaced by 1:1 personalization, agentic orchestration and intent prediction. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, positioning) with an upward direction. The current page lists Core at $85.
It lands 49 days after Clevertap's previous change, "Clevertap renames tiers, hikes entry price to $85, adds CleverAI tokens" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CleverTap: Essentials trial disclaimer swapped for MAU-restricted 30-day free trial" (Aug 10, 2026). Clevertap has been tracked since 2023-12-05.
In the same window Customer.io, Amplitude and Mixpanel also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
“CleverAI” is the name Clevertap’s pricing page uses; Pulse files the change under Clevertap, the company it tracks.
