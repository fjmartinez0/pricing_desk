What happened
Between the Oct 1, 2026 and Oct 2, 2026 captures of Synthesia's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pricing moved to a credit-based model: Creator renamed Pro with selectable credit-volume tiers, per-minute credit rates shown, and a new per-learner Roleplay seat add-on for any plan; Basic free plan monthly credits cut from 1,200 to 500; Pro yearly credit allotment rose from 44,000 to 72,000 credits/year. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, limit decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 22, 2026) lists Starter at $14 and Pro at $59.
It lands 4 days after Synthesia's previous change, "Synthesia slashes Basic plan's AI video assets from 25/mo to just 3" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Synthesia decouples AI Dubbing, boosts Starter/Creator allowances up to 5x" (Sep 8, 2026), "Synthesia quadruples AI Dubbing minutes on Starter and Creator plans" (Sep 4, 2026) and "Synthesia: New 'Roleplay Sessions' AI coaching feature launched" (Jul 27, 2026). Synthesia has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window Runway, OpusClip and Descript also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
