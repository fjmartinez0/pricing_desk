What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of CloudTalk's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: CloudTalk MCP integration added to Essential and Expert plans (natural-language call search, transcripts, AI Voice Agent management via Claude Code/Cursor/MCP clients). In detail: CloudTalk MCP added as a new integration on Essential and Expert plans, enabling natural-language call search, transcripts, and AI Voice Agent management via Claude Code, Cursor, and MCP clients. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Starter at $25, Essential at $29, Expert at $49, AI Conversation Intelligence at $9 and Power Dialer at $15.
It lands 7 days after CloudTalk's previous change, "CloudTalk expands AI voice agents to 70+ languages, up from 60+" (Sep 7, 2026). CloudTalk has been tracked since 2026-06-18.
In the same window Twilio, Aircall, Ringcentral, Genesys and Zendesk also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
