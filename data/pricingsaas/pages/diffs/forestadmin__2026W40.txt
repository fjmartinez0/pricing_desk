What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Forest Admin's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Billing metric renamed from "audit events" to "write events" across all plan cards, the comparison-table usage-cap row, and the FAQ question. Numeric caps and overage rates ($0.10/$0.05 per event) are unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Scale at $48.
It lands 28 days after Forest Admin's previous change, "Forest (ex-ForestAdmin) launches public 4-tier pricing: $499→$2,199→$5,999/mo" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Forest Admin: product name updated to Forest.app in page copy" (Jul 3, 2026). Forest Admin has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
