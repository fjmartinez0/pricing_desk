What happened
Between the Oct 14, 2026 and Oct 15, 2026 captures of Runway's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Max plan adds Unlimited Wan 3.0 & Wan 3.0 Prime (Unlimited in Tool Mode until October 18), also listed in Max model list. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Standard at $12, Pro at $28 and Max at $76.
It lands 3 days after Runway's previous change, "Runway offers 2 free months for new Enterprise sign-ups before Oct 31" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Runway offers enterprises 2 months free usage for deals signed by 10/31" (Oct 8, 2026), "Runway slashes Pro storage to 100GB, adds Agentic collaborator and Brand Kits" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Runway slashes Pro storage from 500GB to 100GB despite new Brand Kits, MCP" (Sep 5, 2026). Runway has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Synthesia and Loom also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
