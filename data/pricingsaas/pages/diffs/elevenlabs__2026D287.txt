What happened
Between the Oct 13, 2026 and Oct 14, 2026 captures of ElevenLabs's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Eleven v4 launch promo banner ('3x credits included on Creator+ until October 12') removed after its end date; the v4 2x-credits promo section was also dropped from page markdown. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $6, Creator at $22, Pro at $99, Scale at $299 and Business at $990.
It lands 9 days after ElevenLabs's previous change, "ElevenLabs offers 3x credits with v4 launch and 83% off Starter's first month" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ElevenLabs triples credits on Creator+ plans for Eleven v4 launch until Oct 12" (Sep 30, 2026) and "ElevenLabs reveals hidden voice, format, and concurrency limits in plan tables" (Aug 27, 2026). ElevenLabs has been tracked since 2025-12-29.
