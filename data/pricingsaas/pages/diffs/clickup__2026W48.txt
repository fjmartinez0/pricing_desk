What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of ClickUp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free Forever no longer includes ClickUp Brain2 AI; Brain2 Trial with 500 credits/workspace removed. AI and credit allowance now described as paid-plans-only. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change). The current page lists Core at $9, Business at $17, Max at $100 and AI Super Credits at $10 per 10,000 credits.
It lands 21 days after ClickUp's previous change, "ClickUp folds AI add-ons into every plan, debuts $100/mo MAX tier" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ClickUp A/B test: Core plan $8 to $9/user/month (billed yearly) in test layout" (Oct 18, 2026). ClickUp has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Trello, Microsoft, Asana and Notion also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
