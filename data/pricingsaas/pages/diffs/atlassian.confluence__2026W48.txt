What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Atlassian's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New banner announces usage-based billing starts December 3, 2026; customers should review included allowance, usage and forecast in Atlassian Administration; Image watermarks feature added to the comparison table, applying customizable watermarks to images across a site or spaces. In detail: Image watermarks added to the Enterprise plan (new Enterprise card bullet and compare-table row, ticked for Enterprise only), applying customizable watermarks to images across a Confluence site or individual spaces. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, metric change). The current page lists Standard at $4.86 and Premium at $9.31.
It lands 35 days after Atlassian's previous change, "Atlassian Confluence raises Standard to $5.78 and Premium to $11.16 per user" (Oct 18, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Atlassian Confluence caps Enterprise automation, lifts Free limit 5x" (Sep 6, 2026). Atlassian has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Slack, Microsoft, GitLab, Asana and GitHub also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
