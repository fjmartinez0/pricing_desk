What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Explorium's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Explorium removed its sitewide 'AgentSource 2.0' launch promotion (countdown timer + '20% off all packages for a limited time', code AS2020) that ran across every credit package tier. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $149.99 subscription, Growth at $749.99 subscription and Scale at $7,500 subscription.
It lands 7 days after Explorium's previous change, "Explorium adds $29.99 Lite tier as Starter jumps 50% to $149.99" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Explorium: Starter/Growth prices drop to $99.99/$749.99, per-credit rates shown" (Aug 3, 2026), "Explorium: 4th of July sale discount (up to 25% off packages)" (Jul 6, 2026) and "Explorium nudges Growth plan to $749.99 per 25K-credit package" (Jun 29, 2026). Explorium has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window DAT Freight & Analytics and Cognism also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
