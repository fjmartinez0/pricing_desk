What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Heepsy's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Starter projects 5 to Unlimited; Plus creators tracked 50 to 100, projects 15 to Unlimited, outreach emails replaced by 500 sequences; Advanced results 50K to 100K, profiles 1K to 2K, creators 100 to 400; listings dropped; Audience authenticity filter and audience quality score filter added to Plus and Advanced plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $69, Plus at $149 and Advanced at $229.
