What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of OpenArt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro/Wonder promo banner rewritten: '30% off GPT Image 2.6 until Nov 17' replaced by 'extra up to 50% off GPT Image 2.6, Seedream 5.5, Kling 4.0 and Recraft V4.1'. Card badges: GPT Image 2.6 30% and Kling 4.0 50% unchanged; new Seedream 5.5 50%, Recraft V4.1 30% and Recraft V4.1 Flash 30% badges; list prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Nov 18, 2026) lists Starter at $13, Plus at $27, Pro at $44, Wonder at $175 and Business at $496.
This weekly roll-up includes 2 changes Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "OpenArt extends up to 50% off promo to Seedream 5.5 on Pro, Wonder" (Day 315, 2026, saved Nov 11, 2026) and "OpenArt widens promo to 50% off Kling 4.0, GPT Image 2.6 and Recraft V4.1" (Day 314, 2026, saved Nov 10, 2026). They are the same changes, not new ones. Before that, OpenArt's previous recorded change was "OpenArt adds 30% off GPT Image 2.6 promo for Wonder and Pro until Nov 17" (Nov 5, 2026). OpenArt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Microsoft, Runway, Huggingface and Leonardo.ai also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
