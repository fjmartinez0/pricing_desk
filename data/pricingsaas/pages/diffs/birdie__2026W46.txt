Verified Nov 9, 2026·Workforce Management·Direction: up

What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Birdie's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: SmartPlans AI section expanded into SmartTools, adding SmartMeetings AI meeting notes for supervisions and team meetings alongside SmartPlans; pay-per-use rates unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists SmartPlans at GBP 2.75.
It lands 147 days after Birdie's previous change, "Birdie raises SmartPlans rate 37.5% to £2.75 per hour" (Jun 15, 2026). Birdie has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
In the same window Workstream, Workable, Tracker, Thatch and Simployer also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
