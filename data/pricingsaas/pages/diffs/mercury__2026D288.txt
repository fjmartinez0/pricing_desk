What happened
Between the Oct 14, 2026 and Oct 15, 2026 captures of Mercury's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Priority Support added as an included feature on the Mercury Plus and Pro plan cards and the comparison table row. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Mercury Plus at $29.9, Mercury Pro at $299 and Mercury Personal at $20.
It lands 10 days after Mercury's previous change, "Mercury drops $100 Amazon gift card from Plus and Pro Xero offer" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mercury drops $100 Amazon gift card and free Xero perk from Plus and Pro" (Oct 2, 2026), "Mercury sweetens Xero bonus with $100 gift card, drops Invoicing API from paid plans" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Mercury: expense-reimbursement allowance removed from Plus & Pro" (Jul 25, 2026). Mercury has been tracked since 2026-05-12.
