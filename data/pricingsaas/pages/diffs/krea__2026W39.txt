What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Krea's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: MiniMax H3 Max and MiniMax H3 Max Turbo monthly video generation quotas cut 4x across Basic/Pro/Max/Business tiers (e.g. Max tier: 864 to 216 videos; Turbo: 1,728 to 432 videos); Business plan collaboration terminology renamed from 'team' to 'workspace' throughout feature copy and the comparison-table section header ('Teams & Admin' to 'Workspace & Admin'); no capability change. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease, positioning) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 23, 2026) lists Basic at $5, Pro at $21, Max at $63 and Business at $160.
It lands 4 days after Krea's previous change, "Krea cuts MiniMax H3 Max and Turbo video limits 4x on Basic, Pro, Max, Business" (Sep 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Krea adds Recraft V4.1 Flash with up to 7,714 images on Max plan" (Sep 25, 2026), "Krea quadruples MiniMax H3 Max video quota on paid plans, up to 864" (Sep 2, 2026) and "Krea halves MiniMax H3 Max video quotas and drops Reve 2.1 from plans" (Sep 1, 2026). Krea has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
