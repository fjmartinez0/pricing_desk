What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Streamyard's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New paid Essential plan added at $18.99/mo monthly ($14.99/mo yearly) with unlimited streaming, 2-destination multistream and 1080p; Core now builds on Essential; Free plan no longer lists 'Limited storage' and 'Dark mode' bullets. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan, freemium change) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 27, 2026) lists Essential at $14.99, Core at $35.99, Advanced at $68.99 and Business at $249.
It lands 91 days after Streamyard's previous change, "StreamYard: On-Air webinars capped at 100 viewers (Advanced)" (Jul 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Streamyard Adds Chat Overlay and Clean Thumbnails to Core Plan" (Apr 4, 2026), "Streamyard Upgrades Core Plan With Chat Overlay and Clean Thumbnails" (Week 3, 2026) and "Streamyard Introduces AI Backgrounds to Core Plan, Enhancing User Experience" (Jan 12, 2026). Streamyard has been tracked since 2025-10-05.
In the same window You.com, X and Panopto also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
