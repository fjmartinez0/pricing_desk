What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Aikido Security's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Basic plan now includes 25 AI credits/month (for AI Pentesting, Deep PR Reviews, AI Code Analysis) - previously only Pro (100/mo) and Advanced (200/mo) had any AI credit allocation. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $315, Pro at $630, Advanced at $945 and Typical Pentest at $4,000 assessments.
It lands 21 days after Aikido Security's previous change, "Aikido Security adds SIEM Integration to its Advanced plan feature list" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Aikido Security launches Max add-on with unlimited AI credits, FedRAMP now Enterprise" (Oct 12, 2026), "Aikido Security adds Code Coverage to paid plan, above PR Code Quality Checks" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Aikido Security: Container image scan limit doubled across paid tiers" (Aug 17, 2026). Aikido Security has been tracked since 2026-06-26.
In the same window Prowler, PreVeil, NordVPN, Jumpcloud and ExpressVPN also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
