Allstacks drops "limited time" free label on Product Studio Starter
Verified Oct 19, 2026·Software Development & Testing·Direction: neutral
The changeStarter free tier — freemium change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 12, 2026 → Oct 19, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Allstacks's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Product Studio Starter (Free) no longer says free 'for a limited time' with usage-based pricing starting soon; now 'Start using it today.' Free-to-start framing, no price shown. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standalone at $16.67, Growth at $33.33 and Enterprise at $50.
It lands 77 days after Allstacks's previous change, "Allstacks: new free Product Studio Starter plan launched" (Aug 3, 2026). Allstacks has been tracked since 2026-02-25.
In the same window GitHub, Atlassian, GitLab, Hashicorp and JetBrains also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
