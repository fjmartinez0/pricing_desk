What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Roofr's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Change Orders (Production Management) no longer marked 'Coming soon'; the feature is now presented as available. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $209, Scale at $299, SMS at $49, Roofr Sites at $85 and Instant Estimator at $125.
It lands 104 days after Roofr's previous change, "Roofr removes seat caps, offers unlimited users on every base plan" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Roofr launches Measure+ as $109/mo standalone plan" (Jun 10, 2026) and "Roofr upgrades Scale plan to unlimited seats and automations" (May 26, 2026). Roofr has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
