What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Xentral's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI agents & Co-pilot added to Launch and Starter (and comparison table); MCP server added to Business; header now says AI is included in every plan.
