What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of PreVeil's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Gov Community storage changed from 10 TB to 10 TB+ (table: Custom to 10TB+); Business card now also lists 10 TB+ storage; FIPS 140-3 validated encryption now included in Business (moved from the Gov Community card to the Business card; table FIPS 140-3 row now checked for Business). Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Individual at $25, Business at $30 and PreVeil Pass at $450.
PreVeil's previous recorded change was "PreVeil: Compliance claims softened: Business tagline 'meet' became 'support' compliance requirements;…" (Oct 19, 2026).
