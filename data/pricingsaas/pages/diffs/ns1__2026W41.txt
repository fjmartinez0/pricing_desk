What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of NS1's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New promo: save up to 40% on NS1 Connect plans (30% off monthly, 40% off annual) until Oct 31; Essentials discounted to $69.30/mo and Standard to $244.30/mo.
