What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Evoto's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 10% OFF promo added to Starter and Basic annual plans: Starter US$89 to US$80/yr, Basic US$149 to US$134/yr (list prices unchanged); per-credit rates drop to US$0.10 and US$0.08. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter Plan at $7.42, Basic Plan at $12.42, Basic Plus Plan at $20.17, Standard Plan at $43.42 and 200 Credits Pack at $49 subscription.
It lands 14 days after Evoto's previous change, "Evoto: Asset Hub Membership added to all annual plans" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Evoto: new Enterprise contact-sales tier added" (Aug 31, 2026), "Evoto: 7-Day Free Trial added for Starter/Basic/Basic Plus plans" (Aug 3, 2026) and "Evoto: Peak Season discount cut from up to 25% to flat 10%, promo bundle removed" (Jul 27, 2026). Evoto has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Figma, Fotor and Canva also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
