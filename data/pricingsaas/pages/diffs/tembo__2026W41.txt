What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Tembo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro and Max now support Claude, Codex and SuperGrok subscriptions (previously ChatGPT only); prices unchanged. In detail: Bring-your-own subscription line changed from 'Use your ChatGPT subscription' to 'Use your Claude, Codex, or SuperGrok subscription' on Pro and Max (cards and compare table); FAQ confirms ChatGPT still supported, so Claude and SuperGrok subscriptions are newly supported. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Pro at $60 and Max at $200.
It lands 14 days after Tembo's previous change, "Tembo adds unlimited Projects to Pro/Max plans" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Tembo slashes Free plan seats from 3 users to just 1" (Sep 14, 2026), "Tembo: Credits billing replaced with dollar usage allowance" (Aug 5, 2026) and "Tembo: Pro/Max credits cut 40-50%; new sandbox size + RAM specs disclosed" (Jul 28, 2026). Tembo has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Render, Neon, Supabase, Railway and PlanetScale also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
