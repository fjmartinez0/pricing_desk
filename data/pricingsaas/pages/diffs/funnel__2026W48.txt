What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Funnel's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Data Hub capacity raised: Business 46->47 destinations and 579->600+ connectors; Enterprise 47->48 destinations and 600+->650+ connectors; Multi-touch attribution (MTA) no longer listed in Funnel Measure Digital Measurement tier; card now lists only always-on MMM; Funnel Measure Digital Measurement now includes Media planner and budget optimization and Incrementality testing, previously listed o. In detail: Funnel Measure Digital Measurement now includes Media planner and budget optimization and Incrementality testing, previously listed only under Advanced Measurement. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised, feature removed, feature added). The current page lists Starter at $300, Business at $600 and Digital Measurement at $1,500.
It lands 35 days after Funnel's previous change, "Funnel drops Starter user cap, now offers unlimited seats and AI usage" (Oct 18, 2026). Funnel has been tracked since 2026-09-25.
