What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of AnyDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Comparison table: AnyDB Browser Extension replaced by Desktop Sync & Edit App; AnyDB Shopify Sync added on all plans; Business custom domain changed from yourcompany.anydb.com to portal.yourcompany.com. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Business at $10.
It lands 7 days after AnyDB's previous change, "AnyDB launches email conversation sync, with auto sync on Business plans" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "AnyDB adds Entra ID and Okta SSO for Enterprise, plus custom branding tiers" (Oct 5, 2026), "AnyDB: Free plan AI credits boosted to 1,000/mo + new MCP server access" (Aug 31, 2026) and "AnyDB: Business/Enterprise now capped at 50 workflows" (Aug 17, 2026). AnyDB has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window MongoDB, Elastic, Redis, MariaDB and Couchbase also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
