What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Metabase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter plan now lists Guest embeds (with "Powered by Metabase" badge) as an included feature. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Starter at $90, Pro at $517.50 and Enterprise at $1,666.67.
It lands 7 days after Metabase's previous change, "Metabase adds Data apps beta to Pro, brings 2FA to Starter plan" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Metabase adds MCP server and preferred-model AI, plus required 2FA on Pro" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Metabase quietly reveals Enterprise-only paid air-gapped deployment option" (Aug 24, 2026). Metabase has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Domo, Grafana, Tableau and Qlik also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
