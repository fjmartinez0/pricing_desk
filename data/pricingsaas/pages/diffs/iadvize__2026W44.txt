What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of iAdvize's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Search (search powered by the AI Shopping Assistant) added to key features; Engagement Widgets row replaced by narrower Conversation Starters row (Smart Banners/other widgets no longer listed). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $290, Starter at $520, Growth at $1,330 and Support Desk at EUR 80.
iAdvize's previous recorded change was "iAdvize swaps Shopper Insights reports for AI Trends, ROAI, AI Analysis" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "iAdvize drops 30-day free trial from navigation and pricing FAQ" (Sep 21, 2026). iAdvize has been tracked since 2026-06-19.
In the same window Intercom and LiveChat also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
