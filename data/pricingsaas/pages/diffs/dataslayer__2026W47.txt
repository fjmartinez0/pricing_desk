What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Dataslayer's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Starter (1 user) and Advanced (10 users) now offer Unlimited Users; Advanced plan card adds Native Claude & ChatGPT integrations, built-in AI Agent (chat, dashboards, alerts) and Atlas BI with 1 month of free trial access; Account limits are now scoped per query instead of per connector (e.g. Starter 3 accounts, Advanced 50, Pro 100). In detail: All paid plan cards (Starter, Advanced, Pro, Business) now list Native Claude & ChatGPT integrations, the built-in AI Agent (chat, dashboards, alerts) and Atlas BI: 1-month free trial on Starter/Advanced, ongoing basic Atlas capacity on Pro, custom on Business. Starter previously had no AI Insights; Advanced+ previously listed AI Insights (MCP, GPT, AI Alerts). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised, metric change, feature added). The current page lists Starter at $35, Advanced at $115, Pro at $345, Extra Connectors at $29 and Extra Accounts at $13.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Dataslayer. Dataslayer has been tracked since 2026-06-19.
