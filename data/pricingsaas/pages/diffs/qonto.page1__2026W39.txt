What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Qonto's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Account remuneration (interest) rate paid after the 2-month 5% intro boost increased on all three business plans: Essential 1.25%->1.5% AER, Business 1.75%->2% AER, Enterprise 2.25%->2.5% AER. Balance cap unchanged at €200,000. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essential at EUR 49, Business at EUR 109, Enterprise at EUR 199, Invoice Automation at EUR 12 and Project Tracking at EUR 20.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Qonto. Qonto has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
