What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Digitalocean's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI/ML section gains two new priced products: DigitalOcean Harness Runtime ($0.0535/hr, AI coding agent sandboxes) and Action Gateway ($0.10/1,000 calls, tool/MCP access gateway). Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Droplets at $4, Spaces Object Storage at $5, Volumes Block Storage at $10, Cloudways Managed Hosting at $11 and Kubernetes at $12.
It lands 62 days after Digitalocean's previous change, "DigitalOcean: GPU Droplets starting price up ($1.88→$1.91/GPU/hour)" (Jul 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "DigitalOcean launches Evaluations: LLM-as-a-Judge pricing, pay per token used" (Jul 6, 2026) and "DigitalOcean: AI Inference price cut 67% ($0.15->$0.05/M) + new Security (CSPM)" (Jul 2, 2026). Digitalocean has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Vultr, Google Cloud and Heroku also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
