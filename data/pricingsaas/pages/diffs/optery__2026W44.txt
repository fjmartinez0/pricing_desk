What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Optery's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Core automated-removal coverage rose from 375+ to 380+ sites. Counts without Expanded Reach shifted: Extended 325+ to 330+, Ultimate 400+ to 395+ (headline Extended 555+ / Ultimate 625+ unchanged). Prices unchanged ($3.99/$14.99/$24.99 monthly; $39/$149/$249 yearly). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Core at $3.25, Extended at $12.42 and Ultimate at $20.7.
It lands 7 days after Optery's previous change, "Optery trims data broker coverage by 5 sites across Core and Ultimate" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Optery trims automated removal coverage to 555+ sites on Extended plan" (Oct 12, 2026) and "Optery: Extended plan site coverage increased (555+ → 560+)" (Aug 31, 2026). Optery has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
