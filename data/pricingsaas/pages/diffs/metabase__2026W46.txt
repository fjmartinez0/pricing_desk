What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Metabase's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pro plan gained a new "Data apps (beta)" feature. Starter plan now explicitly lists Two-factor authentication (2FA) as an included feature (previously only called out under Pro). In detail: Pro plan gained a new "Data apps (beta)" feature, added to the end of its feature list; Starter plan now explicitly lists Two-factor authentication (2FA) as an included feature; Pro's SSO line dropped the "required 2FA" callout accordingly since it's now inherited from Starter. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $90, Pro at $517.50 and Enterprise at $1,666.67.
It lands 14 days after Metabase's previous change, "Metabase adds MCP server and preferred-model AI, plus required 2FA on Pro" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Metabase quietly reveals Enterprise-only paid air-gapped deployment option" (Aug 24, 2026). Metabase has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Domo, Grafana, Tableau and Qlik also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
