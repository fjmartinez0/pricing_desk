What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Serpmaster's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Hero subtitle replaced with a full bandwidth-pricing ladder, newly disclosing Enterprise volume tiers that appear nowhere in the plan cards: $2.50/GB at 250GB and $2/GB at 1TB. Visible plan-card prices (3GB $3.75 through 100GB $2.75, PAYG $4.0) are unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists 3 GB at $11.25, 10 GB at $35, 25 GB at $81.25, 50 GB at $150 and 100 GB at $275.
It lands 56 days after Serpmaster's previous change, "Serpmaster: Added a new 100GB residential proxy tier ($2.75/GB, $275/mo) and revealed a priced Pay As You Go card…" (Jul 20, 2026). Serpmaster has been tracked since 2023-11-22.
In the same window Moz, Semrush and Ahrefs also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
