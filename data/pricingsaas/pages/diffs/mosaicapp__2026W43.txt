What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Mosaic's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Money-back guarantee reworded: no longer 'every subscription backed by 5x guarantee in implementation year'; now a 5x ROI guarantee 'available', details via Contact sales. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Chello Pro at $50.
It lands 7 days after Mosaic's previous change, "Mosaic consolidates to 2-tier pricing, adds $50 Chello Pro AI add-on" (Oct 12, 2026). Mosaic has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Workleap, Wagepoint, Tracker, Time Doctor and SimInsights also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
“Mosaicapp” is the name Mosaic’s pricing page uses; Pulse files the change under Mosaic, the company it tracks.
