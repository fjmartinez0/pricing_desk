What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of ScrapingBee's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing subheadline now explicitly discloses 'Pay only for successful requests' alongside the existing cancel-anytime messaging, clarifying that failed requests are not billed against API credits. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Hobby at $19, Freelance at $49, Startup at $99, Business at $249 and Business + at $599.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for ScrapingBee. ScrapingBee has been tracked since 2026-09-09.
