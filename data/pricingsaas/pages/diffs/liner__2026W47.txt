What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Liner's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro and Max plan cards now advertise 60+ image generation models (up from 50+); the aggregate model-catalog comparison section also rose from 306 to 308 LLM models and 56 to 60 image models. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised). The current page lists Pro at $14.99, Team at $26.99 and Max at $29.99.
It lands 7 days after Liner's previous change, "Liner swaps GPT-6.1 Terra for Gemini 4 Argon in latest LLM lineup" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Liner Model API renamed Router API" (Oct 26, 2026), "Liner Pro and Max now offer 350+ AI models, up from major few (74 chars)" (Oct 18, 2026) and "Liner launches "$15 free" sign-up promo with header banner and…" (Sep 21, 2026). Liner has been tracked since 2026-07-16.
