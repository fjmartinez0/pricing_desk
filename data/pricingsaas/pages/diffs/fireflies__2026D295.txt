What happened
Between the Oct 21, 2026 and Oct 22, 2026 captures of Fireflies.ai's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added ISO 42001 AI governance certification (replacing the GDPR badge slot); HIPAA compliance expanded to include FERPA (BAA/DSA), now scoped to Enterprise plan with Private Storage. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $10, Business at $19 and Enterprise at $39.
It lands 10 days after Fireflies.ai's previous change, "Fireflies.ai publishes per-plan API rate limits, from 30/min on Free to pooled per-seat limits" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fireflies.ai publishes API rate limits: 30/min up to pooled 2,400/min" (Oct 9, 2026) and "Fireflies.ai: Added Email Assistant AI feature to Pro plan" (Aug 17, 2026). Fireflies.ai has been tracked since 2026-05-19.
