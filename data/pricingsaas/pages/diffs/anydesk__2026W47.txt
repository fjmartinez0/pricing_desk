What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of AnyDesk's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Promo changed from 20% off any license (first year only) to up to 30% off multi-year plans: 1 year 20%, 2 years 30%, 3 years 30%. Plan card prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Solo at $28.90, Standard at $49.90, Advanced at $111.90 and Connection Add-on at $44.90.
It lands 44 days after AnyDesk's previous change, "AnyDesk hikes add-on connection price 50% to $44.90 per month" (Oct 3, 2026). AnyDesk has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Microsoft, Zoho, Remote, TeamViewer and GoTo also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
