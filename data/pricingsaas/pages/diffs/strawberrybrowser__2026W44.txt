What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Strawberry's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Paid plans gain priority access at peak hours and ChatGPT-plan credit savings; new 'Included in every plan' list added; Free loses Email support and Companions/skills/apps bullets; Free plan no longer mentions a daily usage limit on its monthly credit allowance (also removed from FAQ). Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, freemium change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Intern at $17, Starter at $35, Part-time at $71, Standard at $150 and Full-time at $208.
It lands 7 days after Strawberry's previous change, "Strawberry splits pricing into tabs, adds pooled-credit Enterprise plan" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Strawberry cuts Part-time plan to $85, adds per-seat Team tiers" (Oct 12, 2026) and "Strawberry slashes free plan credits 75%, from 2,000 to 500 a month" (Oct 5, 2026). Strawberry has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Raspberry AI, Tripo, SambaNova, Wispr Flow and Hive also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
