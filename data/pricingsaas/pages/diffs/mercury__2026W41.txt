What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Mercury's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The $100 Amazon gift card perk bundled with the 6 months free Xero offer was removed from the Plus and Pro plans. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 15, 2026) lists Mercury Plus at $29.9, Mercury Pro at $299 and Mercury Personal at $20.
It lands 3 days after Mercury's previous change, "Mercury drops $100 Amazon gift card and free Xero perk from Plus and Pro" (Oct 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mercury sweetens Xero bonus with $100 gift card, drops Invoicing API from paid plans" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Mercury: expense-reimbursement allowance removed from Plus & Pro" (Jul 25, 2026). Mercury has been tracked since 2026-05-12.
