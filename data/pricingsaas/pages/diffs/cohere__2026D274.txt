What happened
Between the Sep 30, 2026 and Oct 1, 2026 captures of Cohere's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Model Vault Embed 4 replaced by Embed 5 Fast/Pro; Small tier drops $4.00 to $3.00/hr ($2,500 to $2,000/mo); Medium unchanged at $5.00/$3,250; Embed 5 Pro Small/Medium added. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Embed 5 Fast at $0.08 Input tokens, Embed 5 Pro at $0.12 Input tokens, Parse 5 at $1.5 Pages, Rerank 4 Fast at $2 Searches and Rerank 4 Pro at $2.5 Searches.
It lands 31 days after Cohere's previous change, "Cohere adds Parse 5 doc-parsing model, Medium $4/hr and XL $7/hr tiers" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cohere adds Parse 5 to Model Vault with Medium and XL tiered pricing" (Aug 28, 2026). Cohere has been tracked since 2024-12-01.
In the same window OpenAI, Anthropic, Together AI, Google and Metabase also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
