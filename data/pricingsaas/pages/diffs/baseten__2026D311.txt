What happened
Between the Nov 6, 2026 and Nov 7, 2026 captures of Baseten's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Serverless Loops training product launched with per-token pricing for GLM-5.3, GLM-5.3-Flash, and Qwen3.6 35B-A3B (prefill/cached/sample/train rates), each with a 20% limited-time intro discount. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $1, Enterprise at $1 and Basic at $1.32 DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 input (per 1M tokens) + $0.30 Kimi K3 cache input (per 1M tokens).
It lands one day after Baseten's previous change, "Baseten adds $0.16/GB/month Volumes storage to Sandboxes pricing" (Nov 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Baseten adds Kimi K3.1 Pro, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and joey-1 at $0.01/1M tokens" (Nov 5, 2026), "Baseten scraps Basic/Pro/Enterprise tiers, adds Sandboxes usage pricing" (Nov 3, 2026) and "Baseten adds Base Labs joey-1 model to Model API rate table at $0.01 input" (Oct 30, 2026). Baseten has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Wodify, Zen Planner, Pike13 and Mindbody also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
