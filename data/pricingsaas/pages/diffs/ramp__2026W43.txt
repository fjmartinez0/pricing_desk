What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Ramp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Treasury feature section renamed Banking; FDIC-insured 'business account' now called 'checking account' in plan cards and comparison table; rates (2%) unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Plus at CAD 15.
It lands 49 days after Ramp's previous change, "Ramp lifts Investment Account yield to 4.36% from 4.34% on Free plan" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Ramp: Investment Account APY increased 4.25% to 4.28%" (Jul 24, 2026) and "Ramp: Investment Account yield decreased (4.36% → 4.25%)" (Jul 23, 2026). Ramp has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
