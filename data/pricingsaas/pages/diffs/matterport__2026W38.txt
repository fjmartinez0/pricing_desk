What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Matterport's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Business plan's Autodesk integration swapped: 'Integration with Autodesk Construction Cloud' replaced by 'Integration with Autodesk Forma'. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Professional at $90.
It lands 14 days after Matterport's previous change, "Matterport rebrands Enterprise support as dedicated account manager" (Aug 31, 2026). Matterport has been tracked since 2026-04-26.
In the same window Hover, DroneDeploy and Google Cloud also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
