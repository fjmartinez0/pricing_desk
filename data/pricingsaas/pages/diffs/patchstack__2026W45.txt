What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Patchstack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan renamed to "Enterprise / Agency" and retargeted from MSPs to large agencies; still custom billing. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Developer at $69, Additional Team Seats at $24 and Additional Website Licenses at $12.50.
It lands 192 days after Patchstack's previous change, "Patchstack Expands RapidMitigate Library Past 15,000 Unique Rules" (Apr 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Patchstack Grows RapidMitigate Rules Library to 14,874, Leading the Market" (Apr 13, 2026). Patchstack has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Kinsta, Acquia and Cloudflare also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
