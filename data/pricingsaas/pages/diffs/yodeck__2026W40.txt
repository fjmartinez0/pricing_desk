What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Yodeck's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan gained a new "Content Flows" feature, letting local teams fill remaining screen airtime around a locked universal content share. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at EUR 8, Premium at EUR 11, Enterprise at AUD 25, Embeddable Feeds at CAD 9 and Yodeck Assurance at GBP 0.8.
It lands 14 days after Yodeck's previous change, "Yodeck swaps Webhooks for AI Agent in Premium API bundle" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Yodeck: Premium plan tag-based playlists feature expanded" (Jul 27, 2026). Yodeck has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
