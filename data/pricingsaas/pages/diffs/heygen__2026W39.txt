What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of HeyGen's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Voice Cloning changed from Unlimited to Expanded usage on Creator plan and Maximum Usage on Pro plan. In detail: Voice Cloning entitlement softened from 'Unlimited' to 'Expanded usage' on the Creator plan and 'Maximum Usage' on the Pro plan in the feature comparison table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 23, 2026) lists Creator at $24, Pro at $41 and Business at $119.
It lands 2 days after HeyGen's previous change, "HeyGen caps Voice Cloning usage on Creator, Pro, and Business plans" (Sep 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HeyGen rebrands Digital Twin as Video Avatar across all plans" (Sep 7, 2026), "HeyGen renames Digital Twin avatar to Custom Video Avatar, limits unchanged" (Sep 1, 2026) and "HeyGen swaps itemized credit-cost FAQ table for vague usage-based pricing" (Aug 27, 2026). HeyGen has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
