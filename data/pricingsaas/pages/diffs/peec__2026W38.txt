What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Peec AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise plan's tracked LLM model count rose from 'Up to 12' to 'Up to 13' models, driven by the new Meta Spark model being added to the full model list; 'SOC 2' line item removed from the 'Platform, team & support' comparison table, where it was previously listed as included on all four plans (Starter, Pro, Advanced, Enterprise). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, feature removed) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Starter at $80, Pro at $205, Advanced at $420, Additional Model (Starter) at $30 and Additional Model (Pro) at $70.
It lands 7 days after Peec AI's previous change, "Peec AI expands Enterprise LLM tracking to 12 models with Grok API addition" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Peec AI: Starter/Pro/Advanced plan prices revealed ($80-$420/mo)" (Aug 3, 2026) and "Peec AI adds AI Shopping model category across all plans" (Jun 22, 2026). Peec AI has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Anthropic and Huggingface also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
