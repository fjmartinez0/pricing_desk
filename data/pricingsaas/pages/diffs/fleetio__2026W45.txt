What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Fleetio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New "MCP Server" integration added under Integrations & API Access, available on Professional and Premium plans (not on Essential). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essential at $4, Professional at $7, Premium at $10 and Tools at $0.50.
It lands 6 days after Fleetio's previous change, "Fleetio raises automation limits and adds Custom Records to plans" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fleetio adds AI Data Analyst, drops Tailored Dashboards from Advanced Analytics" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Fleetio spotlights new AI Service Advisor on pricing page; prices unchanged" (Sep 14, 2026). Fleetio has been tracked since 2023-12-01.
In the same window OpenTable, Guesty, WellnessLiving, Treatwell and TouchBistro also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
