What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Trustpilot's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Enterprise plan's TrustBox widgets to showcase TrustScore & testimonials increased from 22 to 23. QR Code Review Collector added (1/5/50 active codes for Plus/Premium/Enterprise). Multi-domain management (Plus/Premium/Enterprise) and Multi-domain analytics (Enterprise) added to the comparison table. In detail: Added QR Code Review Collector to the comparison table, offering 1/5/50 active codes for Plus/Premium/Enterprise plans; Added Multi-domain management (Plus/Premium/Enterprise) and Multi-domain analytics (Enterprise) to the plan comparison table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $99, Plus at $319 and Premium at $799.
It lands the same day as Trustpilot's previous change, "Trustpilot Business adds QR Code Review Collector and multi-domain tools" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Trustpilot tightens Starter plan eligibility to new small businesses only" (Aug 17, 2026), "Trustpilot Business tightens Starter plan eligibility to new small businesses" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Trustpilot: integrations claims simplified, CSM added to top cards" (Aug 11, 2026). Trustpilot has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
