What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Lightfield's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan feature list changed: RBAC now 'Permissions by object and record', SSO adds SCIM and enforced MFA, and Sandbox environments replaced by Custom page layouts. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Starter at $89, Pro at $999, Growth at $2,999 and Additional Capture Seat at $200.
It lands 70 days after Lightfield's previous change, "Lightfield ditches usage pricing for seat plans from $89/mo, caps seats" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Lightfield: Pro and Growth plan price increases" (Aug 31, 2026). Lightfield has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
