What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Precog's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Precog replaced its opaque credits-based pricing with four public flat-annual tiers by source type: SAP $22,000 / Enterprise $27,000 / Business $13,500 / Essentials $3,950 per source-instance per year. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essentials at $329.17, Business at $1,125, SAP at $1,833.33 and Enterprise at $2,250.
It lands 56 days after Precog's previous change, "Precog scraps bundle pricing for credits-based model, drops unlimited rows" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Precog renames AI Assistants tier to AI Agents, plans unchanged" (Aug 17, 2026). Precog has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Alteryx, Tableau, Qlik and IBM also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
