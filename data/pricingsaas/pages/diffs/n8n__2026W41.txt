What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of n8n's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: AI credits renamed 'Assistant credits' with lower monthly allowances (Starter 2,300→1,600, Pro up to 13,700→9,600); Pro concurrent executions raised 20→up to 50. Starter plan gained Agents, MCP and AI nodes support and AI models without API keys; Pro gained Metric-based evaluations. In detail: AI credits renamed 'Assistant credits' with lower monthly allowances: Starter cut from 2,300 to 1,600 and Pro from up to 13,700 to up to 9,600; Pro's concurrent execution cap raised from 20 to up to 50; Starter plan now explicitly includes Agents, MCP and AI nodes support and the ability to use AI models without an API key; Pro gained Metric-based evaluations. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $20, Pro at $50 and Business at $800.
It lands 5 days after n8n's previous change, "n8n slashes Assistant credits — Starter down 30%, Pro's top tier cut 30%" (Sep 30, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "n8n rolls out AI Assistant preview for Business/self-hosted, cuts Enterprise " (Aug 27, 2026), "n8n: AI credits increased for Starter/Pro, removed from Enterprise" (Jul 18, 2026) and "n8n: AI Assistant renamed to AI Workflow Builder, credit allowances cut" (Jul 16, 2026). n8n has been tracked since 2026-05-07.
In the same window UiPath, Zapier, Parabol, Workato and Make also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
