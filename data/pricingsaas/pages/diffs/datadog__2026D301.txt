What happened
Between the Oct 27, 2026 and Oct 28, 2026 captures of Datadog's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Infrastructure Free plan (up to 5 hosts, $0) replaced by a Basic tier priced via Contact sales, for noncritical workloads; Basic hosts can mix with Pro/Enterprise; Infrastructure Pro card now lists container monitoring and OpenTelemetry support. In detail: Infrastructure: new Basic host tier (Contact sales for pricing; core system metrics, dashboards, 15-month retention, for noncritical workloads, mixable with Pro/Enterprise) replaces the $0 Free card. Free tier remains per new FAQ as up to 5 Basic hosts with 1-day retention; Infrastructure Pro card bullets now highlight Container monitoring and OpenTelemetry support (dashboards/15-month retention bullets moved to the new Basic card); container monitoring is not available for Basic hosts. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan, positioning) with an upward direction. The current page lists Infrastructure Pro at $15, Infrastructure DevSecOps Pro at $22, Infrastructure Enterprise at $23, Infrastructure DevSecOps Enterprise at $34 and Observability Pipelines at $0.1.
It lands 16 days after Datadog's previous change, "Datadog Cloud SIEM extends security signal retention to 15 months at no cost" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Datadog bundles on-prem Sensitive Data Scanner at no e" (Oct 12, 2026), "Datadog Cloud Security Management: makes Workflow Automation free and unlimited" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Datadog Cloud SIEM makes workflows free when triggered by security findings" (Oct 5, 2026). Datadog has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
