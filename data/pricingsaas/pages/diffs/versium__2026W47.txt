What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Versium's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Subscription installment payments on plans of $10,000+ changed from monthly to quarterly (feature row and pricing note). Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Credit Packages at $250 per use + $0.05 per match credit and Annual Subscriptions at $0.02 per match credit + $5,000 per use.
It lands 7 days after Versium's previous change, "Versium drops $125-minimum Pay-As-You-Go tier, adds reseller pricing" (Nov 8, 2026). Versium has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Salesforce and Treasure Data also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
