What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of fal's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Flux 3 Image (blackforestlabs/flux-3/edit-image) added to Model API pricing table at $0.024 per megapixel (41.67 megapixels per $1). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. It lands 3 days after fal's previous change, "fal adds Flux 3 image editing to rate card at $0.024 per megapixel" (Oct 9, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "fal doubles Flux 3 Image edit price to $0.048 per megapixel" (Oct 16, 2026), "fal hikes GPU rates up to 33%, adds GB200 tier and Nano Banana 2/Pro models" (Oct 5, 2026) and "fal raises H100 GPU rate to $2.49/hr, adds GB200, doubles MiniMax video price" (Oct 2, 2026). fal has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
