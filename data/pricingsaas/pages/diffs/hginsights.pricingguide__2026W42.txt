HG Insights Pricingguide rebrands RGI Platform as HG Copilots, prices unchanged
Verified Oct 12, 2026·Data & Analytics·Direction: neutral
The changeGrowth positioning — positioning.

What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of HG Insights's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Priced product rebranded from 'RGI Platform' (Revenue Growth Intelligence) to 'HG Copilots': pricing guide title, intro ('One Platform. Three modules.' → 'HG Copilots brings three modules together, powered by intelligence credits') and FAQ renamed. Plans, prices ($22K/$45K), credits, seats and 2-year-term terms unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $1,833.33 and Growth at $3,750.
It lands 56 days after HG Insights's previous change, "HG Insights Pricingguide unveils public 3-tier pricing, Starter now $22K/yr" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HG Insights Pricingguide: pricing tier table hidden, now request-a-demo only" (Aug 11, 2026). HG Insights has been tracked since 2026-05-21.
In the same window DAT Freight & Analytics, Seamless.AI and Lusha also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
