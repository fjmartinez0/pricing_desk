What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Zo Computer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: GPT Image 2 retired from the Media models catalog and replaced by two new variants, GPT Image 2.5 Flare and GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst, each priced at $0.035-$1.12 per request (down from $0.225-$1.12 for GPT Image 2).
