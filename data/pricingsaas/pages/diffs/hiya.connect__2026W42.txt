What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Hiya's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Eight-tier branded-call grid ($29 to $3,175/mo, 250-50,000+ calls) and volume slider replaced by four call-volume tabs: $99/750 calls (was 1,000), $299/2,500, $550/5,000 (was $449), $835/10,000 (was $799); new pay-as-you-go option from $0.14/call (was $0.13 overage rate). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists 750 Branded Calls at $99, 2,500 Branded Calls at $299, 5,000 Branded Calls at $550, 10,000 Branded Calls at $835 and Pay As You Go at $0.14 per branded call.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Hiya. Hiya has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
