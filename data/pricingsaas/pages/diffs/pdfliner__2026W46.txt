PDFLiner caps annual-billing 1099 e-file and TIN match allowances
Verified Nov 9, 2026·Business Operations·Direction: down

What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of PDFLiner's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 1099 e-file and TIN match allowances now capped on annual billing below 12x the monthly quota: Self-Employed 5 forms/20 TIN matches a month but only 40/100 a year on annual billing; Company growth 20/100 a month but only 80/400 a year. E-filing also now limited to Forms 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC (was generic 1099 form credits). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Basic at $2.50, Pro at $4.75, API Base 1000 at $31.50, API Base 2500 at $76.50 and API Base 5000 at $144.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for PDFLiner. PDFLiner has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window FormAssembly, Foxit and Wondershare also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
