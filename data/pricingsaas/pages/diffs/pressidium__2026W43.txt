What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Pressidium's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan cards now show monthly price ranges (Entry $25-$99, Agency $150-$542, Premium $183-$550, Enterprise from $750/mo); card CTAs changed from 'Start for $1' to 'See Plans' (the $1 first-month offer is still advertised in the site nav). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $21, Pro at $42, Growth at $67, Advanced at $92 and Freelancer at $125.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Pressidium. Pressidium has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
