What happened
Between the Nov 6, 2026 and Nov 7, 2026 captures of Krea's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Nano Banana 2.1 (1K) now costs ~25 units/image vs ~22, cutting per-plan estimates ~11%: Basic ~225→~200, Pro ~900→~803, Max ~1,800→~1,607, Business ~900→~803 (comparison table). Plan cards switched from quoting Nano Banana 2 to Nano Banana 2.1 (Basic ~112→~200, Pro 450→~803, Max 1,350→~2,410, Business 1,800→~3,214) — a model swap, not added capacity; unit allotments, prices and Nano Banana 2 estimates unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Basic at $5, Pro at $21, Max at $63 and Business at $160.
It lands 5 days after Krea's previous change, "Krea cuts LoRA training from its Free plan as paid prices hold steady" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Krea ends 14-day unlimited Krea 3 Fast promo for annual Pro and Max buyers" (Oct 23, 2026), "Krea: Nodes Agent workflow feature removed from Pro/Max plans" (Oct 21, 2026) and "Krea rewrites plan allowance examples: more Nano Banana 2 generations, Seedance 2.5 videos" (Oct 19, 2026). Krea has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Runway, Microsoft and Leonardo.ai also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
