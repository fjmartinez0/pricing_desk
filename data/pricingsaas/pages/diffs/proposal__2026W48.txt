What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Proposal.biz's pricing page, Pulse recorded 5 changes: Black Friday sale: 50% off every plan, 21-27 November (e.g. Professional $50 to $25/mo billed yearly); ends 27 Nov 11:59 pm UTC; Professional plan additional-seat add-on price cut from $12 to $10 per seat/month (now matches Business); Professional plan export now includes MS Word in addition to PDF and PPT; Free Forever plan pages per document raised from 10 to 20; Free Forever plan now includ. In detail: Free Forever plan now includes Export to PDF (previously only Send & track opens; PDF export started at Starter). It lands 14 days after Proposal.biz's previous change, "Proposal.biz ends founding member promo: Starter/Professional/Business revert to $29/$59/$149 list prices" (Nov 8, 2026).
