What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Domo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Custom Pricing no longer lists 'Custom add-ons available'; other plan-card bullets were only reworded (e.g. 'Support packages' → 'Flexible support packages', 'Unlimited users' → 'No per-seat fees'). Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction.
It lands 173 days after Domo's previous change, "Domo shifts to credit-based pricing for storage, workflows, and ML" (May 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Domo renames Free to 30-day Trial, Paid to Custom Pricing" (Apr 22, 2026) and "Domo shifts to credit-based pricing with unlimited users and no hidden fees" (Apr 13, 2026). Domo has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Alteryx, Tableau, Qlik and Sisense also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
