What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Upguard's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: UpGuard removed 'Vendor Snapshots' from Vendor Risk plans — dropped from Professional (10) and Enterprise+ (Unlimited) cards and deleted from the comparison table (was Corporate=25, Enterprise=Unlimited). Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Vendor Risk Standard at $1,750.
It lands 126 days after Upguard's previous change, "Upguard rebrands Enterprise tier to Enterprise+ with snapshot limits" (May 18, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Upguard removes public pricing for Breach Risk and Trust Exchange" (Week 10, 2026). Upguard has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Recorded Future, Tenable, Crowdstrike and SecurityScorecard also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
