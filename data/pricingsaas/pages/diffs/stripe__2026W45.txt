What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Stripe's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Global Payouts repriced: flat $1.50 local bank/debit and international payouts replaced by 50 free monthly domestic payouts, then per-rail fees (ACH $0.50/$1.00, RTP $2.50, Fedwire $25), cross-border from £0.50, debit card 0.75% + $1.50, stablecoin $0.50 + 0.5%, plus a 0.05% platform fee above $1M monthly volume; Disputes: new $15.00 dispute appealed fee added for each dispute you appeal (non-refu. In detail: Treasury: $2.00 per-wire funding option removed from Receive funds; only free ACH funding is now listed; Disputes: new $15.00 dispute appealed fee added for each dispute you appeal (non-refundable even if won; network fees extra). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Standard at 1.5% surcharge for international cards + $0.30 per successful transaction.
It lands 14 days after Stripe's previous change, "Stripe Data Pipeline adds Postgres database sync tier" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Stripe Data Pipeline adds real-time Postgres sync at $0.0025 per row" (Oct 14, 2026), "Stripe adds card cashback to Treasury, launches $329 Reader T600" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Stripe adds $329 Reader T600 to Terminal lineup, revamps Treasury perks" (Oct 1, 2026). Stripe has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
