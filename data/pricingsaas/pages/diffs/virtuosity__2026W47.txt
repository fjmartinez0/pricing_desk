What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Virtuosity's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added new 'Geotechnical Analysis 2D Advanced WorkSuite' bundle ($7,250/yr, PLAXIS 2D Advanced + GeoStudio 2D Advanced); existing 'Geotechnical Analysis 2D WorkSuite' ($10,030/yr) was renamed to 'Ultimate WorkSuite' with unchanged price and bundle contents.
