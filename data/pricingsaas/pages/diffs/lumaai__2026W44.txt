Luma AI adds Kling 4.0 and 4.0 Omni, drops V2V 4K and audio rows
Verified Oct 26, 2026·AI & Machine Learning·Direction: up
The changeFeature — feature added.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 19, 2026 → Oct 26, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Luma AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Kling 4.0 and Kling 4.0 Omni (incl. video-to-video) models added to the generation cost table; Kling Omni renamed 3.0 Omni, with V2V 4K row and V2V audio removed. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Plus at $25, Pro at $75 and Ultra at $250.
It lands 3 days after Luma AI's previous change, "Luma AI adds Kling 4.0 API pricing, from 53 credits per second at 720p" (Oct 23, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Luma AI doubles Seedance video-to-video credit costs, adds Minimax H3 Max" (Oct 5, 2026), "Luma AI: Seedance 2.0 video generation costs cut ~50%" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Luma AI slashes Seedance video model pricing up to 73% across all tiers" (Sep 19, 2026). Luma AI has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Runway, Synthesia, HeyGen and OpenAI also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
