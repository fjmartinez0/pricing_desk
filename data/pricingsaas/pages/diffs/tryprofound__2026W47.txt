What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Profound's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise Answer Engines tracked raised from up to 9 to up to 10; Qwen added to the supported Answer Engines list. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised).
It lands 7 days after Profound's previous change, "Profound adds Muse Spark to Answer Engine Insights, drops Microsoft Copilot" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Profound adds Projects to free Trial plan, renames Opportunities tool" (Oct 19, 2026), "Profound: Trial plan expanded to 50 prompts, adds Gemini + Google AI Overviews" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Profound kills $99 and $399 paid tiers for a single free trial plan" (Sep 21, 2026). Profound has been tracked since 2026-07-22.
