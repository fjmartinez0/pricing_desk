Microsoft Teams Business Teams flags usage-based AI charges beyond license fees
Verified Oct 19, 2026·Business Operations·Direction: neutral
The changePackaging — package change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 12, 2026 → Oct 19, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Microsoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Footnote 1 now discloses that some AI experiences require usage-based billing, with charges beyond license fees. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Microsoft Teams Essentials at $4, Microsoft 365 Business Basic (no Teams) at $5.4, Microsoft 365 Business Basic at $7, Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot (no Teams) at $20.3 and Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot at $23.5.
It lands 28 days after Microsoft's previous change, "Teams Premium now needs a Teams Enterprise license; Buy now removed" (Sep 21, 2026). Microsoft has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window Salesforce and Google also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
