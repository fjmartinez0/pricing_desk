What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Freshbooks's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Comparison table adds Bill Pay (Billing Solutions) and Buy Now, Pay Later with Klarna (Client Payment Options), included on all plans. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Lite at $23, Plus at $43, Premium at $70, Advanced Payments at $20 and FreshBooks Payroll at $40.
It lands 7 days after Freshbooks's previous change, "Freshbooks swaps $1/mo Lite deal for 80% off 4 months, extends Plus promo" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Freshbooks drops Lite to $1/mo for a year, trims Plus and Premium promos" (Sep 21, 2026) and "FreshBooks: 90% off promo shortened from 6 to 3 months" (Aug 3, 2026). Freshbooks has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Xero, Zoho, Quickbooks and Sage also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
