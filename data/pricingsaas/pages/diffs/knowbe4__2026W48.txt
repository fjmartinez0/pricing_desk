What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of KnowBe4's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: PhishER Plus add-on/stand-alone product renamed to Remediation & Response; prices unchanged ($1.20/$1.10 etc.). Inbound Email Security also renamed Inbound Threat Detection & Response in nav. In detail: PhishER Plus (stand-alone product or optional add-on) renamed to Remediation & Response across all currency tables; prices unchanged (USD $1.50 for 101-500 seats, $1.15 for 501-1000, quote for 1001+). Inbound Email Security also renamed Inbound Threat Detection & Response in nav. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed). The current page lists SAT Foundation at $2.40, SAT Advanced at $3.75, Compliance Plus at $0.93, KnowBe4 Student Edition at $0.17 and Remediation & Response at $1.50.
It lands the same day as KnowBe4's previous change, "KnowBe4 renames Defend email security to Inbound Threat Detection" (Nov 22, 2026). KnowBe4 has been tracked since 2026-02-25.
