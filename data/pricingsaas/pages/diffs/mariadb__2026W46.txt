What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of MariaDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Geo-distributed deployment and real-time HTAP now listed on base Platform; Conflict detection and resolution added to Ultimate; AI RAG moved from Ultimate down to Platform Plus. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction.
It lands 77 days after MariaDB's previous change, "MariaDB drops self-serve Community Server support, now Enterprise-only" (Aug 24, 2026). MariaDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Google Cloud, Microsoft and MongoDB also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
