What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of signNow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: All four tiers renamed in place (same card positions, same prices): Starter->Business ($8/mo), Professional->Business Premium ($15/mo), Business->Enterprise ($30/mo), Enterprise->Site License ($1.50/invite). Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $8, Professional at $15, Business at $30 and Enterprise at $1.50 per signature invite.
It lands 49 days after signNow's previous change, "signNow adds 30-day money-back guarantee badge to pricing page" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "signNow spells out invite caps on plan cards: 5 a month on Business" (Aug 24, 2026), "signNow strips per-plan invite allowances from its pricing cards" (Aug 17, 2026) and "signNow: invite quotas now shown on plan cards" (Aug 11, 2026). signNow has been tracked since 2026-03-18.
