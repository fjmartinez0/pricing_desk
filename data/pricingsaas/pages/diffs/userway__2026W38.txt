What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of UserWay's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Advanced Widget Plans tier expanded from a single "Talk to us" CTA into two new self-serve priced plans: Pro Plus ($1,190/year) and Ultimate ($2,490/year). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Small at $40.83, Pro Plus at $99.17, Medium at $124.17, Ultimate at $207.5 and Monitor 100 Pages at $82.5.
It lands 34 days after UserWay's previous change, "UserWay: annual discount increased 16% → 20%" (Aug 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "UserWay: plan taglines & audience messaging updated (prices unchanged)" (Jul 27, 2026), "UserWay swaps 'compliance' for 'support' language across all paid plans" (Jul 6, 2026) and "UserWay swaps Website tiers for Pro/Pro Plus/Ultimate, cuts mid-tier" (Jun 30, 2026).
