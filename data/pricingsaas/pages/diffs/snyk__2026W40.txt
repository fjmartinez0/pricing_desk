What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Snyk's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Ignite ($1,260/yr) plan removed; flat "Contact Sales" Enterprise pricing replaced with a credit-based Platform Subscription (1 credit=$1, per-capability rates 0.33-4,000 credits). In detail: Snyk eliminated the Ignite tier ($1,260/yr) and replaced flat 'Contact Sales' Enterprise pricing with a credit-based model (1 credit=$1; 0.33-4,000 credits/capability). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Team at $25.
It lands 6 days after Snyk's previous change, "Snyk drops Ignite plan, moves Enterprise to credit-based platform pricing" (Sep 22, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Snyk: platform capacity metric switched from developers to Projects" (Jul 4, 2026). Snyk has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Gitguardian, Sonar and Tenable also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
