What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Couchbase's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Capella AI Data Plane Enterprise rate rose from $0.86/hr per node to $5.36/hr per node; AI Data Plane Enterprise now includes NVIDIA's enterprise-grade support. In detail: AI Data Plane Enterprise support now includes NVIDIA's enterprise-grade support, replacing the 99.99% uptime SLA bullet. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Capella Developer Pro at $0.35 per node per hour, Couchbase Enterprise at $0.66 per node per hour, Operational Insights Enterprise at $0.67 per node per hour, Capella Basic at $0.15 per node per hour and Capella Enterprise at $0.49 per node per hour.
It lands 6 days after Couchbase's previous change, "Couchbase renames Capella Analytics to Operational Insights, pricing unchanged" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Couchbase: Capella AI Data Plane Enterprise price cut ($5.36→$0.86/hr per node)" (Aug 11, 2026). Couchbase has been tracked since 2023-12-06.
In the same window MongoDB, Elastic, Redis and DataStax also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
