What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of TermScout's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New add-ons: Additional Improvement Scorecard $6,000 each and Additional Contract Re-analysis $500/review; Certify now states $27,000 standard annual minimum (5 users x $450 x 12). Plan prices unchanged; TrustMark plans reframed around an eligibility assessment: certification/badge only if eligible, Scorecard and re-analysis on Basic now paid add-ons; AI add-on renamed AI Supplement. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed, feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Nov 17, 2026) lists Certify® Platform at $450, Basic at $900, Pro at $1,300, Premium at $1,700 and Custom FAQs at $250.
It lands 56 days after TermScout's previous change, "TermScout shifts Certify to monthly billing, add-ons hit $3K/year" (Sep 21, 2026). TermScout has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
