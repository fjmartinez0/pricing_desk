What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of UKKO.fi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added new 'UKKO Ulkomaankauppa' (foreign trade invoicing) add-on at €10/mo for billing international customers, and removed the note restricting the add-on bundle to customers without a Y-tunnus (business ID). Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists UKKO Kevytyrittäjä at 5% of invoicing + 3% of invoicing, Y-tunnus (Business ID Add-on) at EUR 4.9, UKKO Plus at EUR 12.9, UKKO Salamapalkka at EUR 15 subscription and UKKO Rahoitus at EUR 10 subscription + 5% of invoicing.
It lands 27 days after UKKO.fi's previous change, "UKKO.fi discloses 25.5% VAT added to Plus/Salamapalkka/Rahoitus pricing" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "UKKO.fi yanks 20% Yrittäjä discount banner, swaps in photo exhibition ad" (Aug 24, 2026). UKKO.fi has been tracked since 2026-03-05.
In the same window Google, You.com, CM.com and Vimeo also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
