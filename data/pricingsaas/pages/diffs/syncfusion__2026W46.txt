What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Syncfusion's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Calculator no longer mentions Document Solutions annual document / end-user volume selection; licenses now priced per developer and team size only. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists PDF Viewer SDK at $49.92, Grid SDK at $79.92, Chart SDK at $79.92, Scheduler SDK at $79.92 and Gantt SDK at $79.92.
It lands 35 days after Syncfusion's previous change, "Syncfusion drops 5-developer minimum license, keeps per-seat prices" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Syncfusion Sales ditches 5-dev minimum, shows flat per-developer pricing" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Syncfusion bundles all 7 UI SDKs into $1,199 suite, adds Business Support" (Sep 14, 2026). Syncfusion has been tracked since 2026-05-06.
