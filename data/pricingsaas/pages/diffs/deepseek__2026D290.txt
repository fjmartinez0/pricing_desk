What happened
Between the Oct 16, 2026 and Oct 17, 2026 captures of DeepSeek's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Search and Fetch APIs priced at $0.75 per 1,000 successful requests each; server-side tool calls free until per-request billing starts Nov 1, 2026. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists DeepSeek Flash at $0.15 per 1M input tokens (cache miss) + $0.3 per 100M input tokens (cache hit) and DeepSeek V4 Pro at $0.66 per 1M input tokens (cache miss) + $2.2 per 100M input tokens (cache hit).
It lands 26 days after DeepSeek's previous change, "DeepSeek: peak-hour billing now excludes Chinese public holidays" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "DeepSeek extends 50% off-peak API discount to Chinese public holidays all day" (Sep 20, 2026), "DeepSeek merges v4-flash and vision-exp into cheaper Flash V4.1" (Sep 14, 2026) and "DeepSeek reverses V4 Pro retirement, keeps API billing unchanged past Sept 14" (Sep 12, 2026). DeepSeek has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
