What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Courier's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: New add-on: "Cross-device email testing" removed from Business & Enterprise plans; replaced by "Device Preview" add-on priced from $20/month. Compliance upgrade: Enterprise row "HIPAA certification" is now "HIPAA program with BAA" (signed Business Associate Agreement now offered). Capacity increase: Developer plan log retention increased from 7 days to 30 days. In detail: "Cross-device email testing" removed from Business & Enterprise feature lists, replaced by a new "Device Preview" add-on priced from $20/month; Enterprise compliance row changed from "HIPAA certification" to "HIPAA program with BAA" — now explicitly offers a signed Business Associate Agreement. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, limit raised, feature change). The current page lists Business at $5 1,000 sends.
It lands 70 days after Courier's previous change, "Courier: Cross-device email testing added to Business plan" (Sep 6, 2026). Courier has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
