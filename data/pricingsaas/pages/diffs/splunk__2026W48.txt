Splunk scraps Observability tiers for $95 Essentials, $150 Premier editions
Detected Nov 22, 2026, 10:21 PM ETData & Analytics
The changeFeature added
2 changes2 capturesNov 16 → Nov 23, 2026 UTC
What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Splunk's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Observability Cloud tiers (Infrastructure $15, App & Infra $60, End-to-End $75) replaced by a la carte product prices plus Free, Essentials $95 and Premier $150 per host editions; bundle savings up to 31%; New Observability Cloud edition feature comparison: AI SRE included in all editions, Observability Logs, Agent Observability and AI Agent Evaluation per edition; Luna tokens renamed Eval-G1. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, packaging change). The current page lists Splunk On-Call at $5, AppDynamics Infrastructure Edition at $6, AppDynamics Premium Edition at $33, AppDynamics Enterprise Edition at $50 and Observability Cloud Essentials Edition at $95.
It lands 126 days after Splunk's previous change, "Splunk: removed "Activity-based pricing" model" (Jul 20, 2026). Splunk has been tracked since 2026-03-19.
In the same window Dynatrace, Datadog, Sumologic and Elastic also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
