What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Dovetail's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free and Enterprise plans replaced by Dovetail One Bronze ($4,800/yr + credits at 15c), Silver (25+ users) and Gold (100+ users, 30-day trial); credit-based usage pricing introduced. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Silver at $0.15 credit, Gold at $0.15 credit and Bronze at $4,800.
It lands 105 days after Dovetail's previous change, "Dovetail: AI Dashboards capped at 1 for Free + HubSpot CRM integration launches" (Jul 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Dovetail offers 60-day unlimited access promo across site" (May 18, 2026), "Dovetail Drops Mid-Tier Plan, Shifts Paid Users to Enterprise" (Apr 2, 2026) and "Dovetail adds Unlimited Agents, Docs; removes custom AI vocabulary" (Feb 23, 2026). Dovetail has been tracked since 2023-12-07.
In the same window Slack, Intercom, Mixpanel and Amplitude also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
