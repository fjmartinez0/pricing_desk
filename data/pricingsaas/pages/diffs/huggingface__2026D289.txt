What happened
Between the Oct 15, 2026 and Oct 16, 2026 captures of Huggingface's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added Nvidia RTX PRO 6000 GPU hardware options (1x/2x/4x/8x) to Spaces hardware pricing at $2.75, $5.50, $11.00 and $22.00 per hour. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists PRO at $9, Team at $20, Enterprise at $50, Inference Endpoints - NVIDIA T4 AWS (4x) at $3 per hour and Inference Endpoints - NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 AWS (1x) at $2.75 per hour.
It lands 8 days after Huggingface's previous change, "Huggingface swaps PRO's 20x inference multiplier for a flat $2 monthly credit" (Oct 8, 2026). Huggingface has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
