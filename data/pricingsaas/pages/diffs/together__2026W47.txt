What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: HGX B200 GPU rate $8.99→$9.29/hr; H200 ($5.99), B300 ($11.49) and GB300 NVL72 ($15.79) prices now publicly shown (previously Contact us); GPU Clusters reserved tiers also rose. In detail: GPU hardware rates rose across Dedicated Inference & GPU Clusters (HGX B200 $8.99→$9.29/hr); previously-hidden H200, B300 and GB300 NVL72 prices are now publicly shown. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100), GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days), Sandbox (Code Sandbox) at $0.04 per vCPU/hour + $0.01 per GiB RAM/hour and Serverless Inference at $0.15 per 1M input tokens.
This weekly roll-up includes 2 changes Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Together AI: HGX B200 GPU price hikes to $9.29/hr + 3 more rates shown" (Day 317, 2026, saved Nov 13, 2026) and "Together AI adds US geo-pinned models, Kimi K3 US at $3.75/$18.75, live GPU status" (Day 315, 2026, saved Nov 11, 2026). They are the same changes, not new ones. Before that, Together AI's previous recorded change was "Together AI raises Qwen3.7-Max inference prices 67%, drops four serverless models" (Nov 7, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
In the same window Anthropic, OpenAI, Lambda Labs, Huggingface and Google also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
