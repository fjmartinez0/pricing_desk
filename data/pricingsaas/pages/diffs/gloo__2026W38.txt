What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Gloo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Data Engine, previously bundled as 'Limited' access in the $25/mo Pro plan, is now a standalone product marked 'currently at capacity' with a request-access waitlist and no listed price. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Basic at $20, Pro at $100 and Ultra at $200.
It lands 30 days after Gloo's previous change, "Gloo: Pro Data Engine access downgraded to Limited + 1 more change" (Aug 15, 2026). Gloo has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Workato, Make, MuleSoft, Talend and Zapier also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
