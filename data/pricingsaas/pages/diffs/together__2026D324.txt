What happened
Between the Nov 19, 2026 and Nov 20, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Qwen4 Max list price increased (input $1.43→$1.50, cached $0.0713→$0.075, output $4.28→$4.50 per 1M tokens); new limited-time promo shows discounted $1.05/$0.0525/$3.15, valid until 11/23/2026. New model Tencent Hy4 added to the serverless inference catalog at $0.83/$0.042 (cached) input and $2.50 output per 1M tokens. In detail: Qwen4 Max list price increased from $1.43/$0.0713 (cached) input and $4.28 output to $1.50/$0.075 (cached) input and $4.50 output, per 1M tokens. A limited-time promo now also shows a discounted $1.05/$0.0525 (cached) input and $3.15 output, valid until 11/23/2026. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Nov 22, 2026) lists Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $4 per hour (H100), GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days), Sandbox (Code Sandbox) at $0.04 per vCPU/hour + $0.01 per GiB RAM/hour and Serverless Inference at $0.15 per 1M input tokens.
It lands one day after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI adds Qwen4 Max model at $1.43/$4.28 per 1M tokens" (Nov 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI adds Elorian Glimpse 1.0 ($0.60/$3.00), drops Qwen2.5 7B Turbo" (Nov 18, 2026), "Together AI: HGX B200 GPU price hikes to $9.29/hr + 3 more rates shown" (Nov 13, 2026) and "Together AI adds US geo-pinned models, Kimi K3 US at $3.75/$18.75, live GPU status" (Nov 11, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
