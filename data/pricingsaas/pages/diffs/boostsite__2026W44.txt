What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Boostsite's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Three plans (Free Trial, Standard $47, SEO Pro $147) replaced by one Boostsite Premium plan at $24/mo ($20/mo annual, 15% off); trial 8->10 days; Article ($2+) and Image ($10+) PowerPack add-ons added; Plan limits switched from domains/subpages caps (1,000-100,000 subpages) to 35 articles per week on Premium. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $40 and SEO Pro at $125.
Boostsite's previous recorded change was "Boostsite triples pricing tiers, hiking top plan to $147/month" (May 25, 2026). Boostsite has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
