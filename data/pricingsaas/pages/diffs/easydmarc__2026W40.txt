What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of EasyDMARC's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Added new modules: DNS Manager (uptime/DNS monitoring, backup, insights, cert monitoring), Sender Insights (investigation, verification, inbox placement, delivery analytics), Threat Intelligence, and AI Agents; Plus plan's invited-user cap changed from 1 to Unlimited in the comparison table, and email quotas now display as annual figures (100,000/mo to 1,200,000/yr) instead of monthly counts.
