What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of HubSpot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter gained Agent Hub, Agent Builder, and Data Agent (Messaging Insights BETA removed); Professional gained Content Agent and Nurture Agent, lost Omni-channel automation, and Agent Hub (moved to Starter). Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $7, Professional at $800 and Enterprise at $3,600.
It lands 35 days after HubSpot's previous change, "Hubspot Marketing Starter Marketing Hub: seat pricing revealed, features rebalanced across tiers" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hubspot Marketing Starter Marketing: Additional Core Seat pricing hidden (Pro/Enterprise) + more" (Aug 10, 2026), "Hubspot Marketing Starter drops $9/1,000 credit rate, goes vague on pricing" (Jul 27, 2026) and "Hubspot Marketing Starter rebrands Commerce Hub to Revenue Hub on pricing page" (Jun 24, 2026). HubSpot has been tracked since 2025-01-01.
In the same window Salesforce, Microsoft, Zoho, Freshworks and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
