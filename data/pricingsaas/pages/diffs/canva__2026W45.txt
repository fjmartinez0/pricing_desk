What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Canva's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Canva Free AI usage changed from 'Up to 20 Standard or Premium AI uses' to 'Limited access to Standard and Premium AI'; AI allowance footnote drops usage estimates. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $12 and Business at $20.83.
It lands 3 days after Canva's previous change, "Canva hides free plan's AI quota, swapping 20 uses for vague limited access" (Oct 30, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Canva cuts Pro to $144/year, slashes free AI uses from 200 to 20" (Sep 14, 2026), "Canva Pro drops 20% to $144/year, savings jump past 16%" (Sep 11, 2026) and "Canva reshuffles AI usage limits, rebrands allowance as usage" (Sep 9, 2026). Canva has been tracked since 2023-11-17.
In the same window Figma and Visme also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
