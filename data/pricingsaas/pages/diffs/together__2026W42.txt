What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Qwen3.8 Flash serverless price up from $0.09 input/$0.28 output to $0.15/$0.47 per 1M tokens; Kimi K3 PROMO pricing added: $2.70 input/$13.50 output (from $3.00/$15.00) per 1M tokens, valid until 10/11/2026; GPU Clusters Reserved shortest commitment term changed from 7-30 days to 3-30 days; prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature change, price increase, discount added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Managed Storage at $0.16 per GiB/month, Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, Sandbox (Code Interpreter) at $0.03 per session (60 min), GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100) and GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days).
It lands 4 days after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI cuts GPU Cluster minimum reservation from 7 days to 3, prices hold" (Oct 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI adds Reflection Beam to serverless at $0.40 per 1M input tokens" (Oct 17, 2026), "Together AI makes Muse Spark Open free until Oct 18, adds Nemotron 3.5 Super VL" (Oct 16, 2026) and "Together AI ends Kimi K3 cached-input promo; price returns to $0.30/1M" (Oct 14, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
In the same window Google, Mistral AI, Anthropic, OpenAI and Huggingface also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
