What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Ganttify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 'Publish Gantt charts' reworded to 'Share read-only charts with a link' on all plans; 'Share charts with your team' added to Small Team and Large Team Plus features. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Personal at $4.92, Small Team at $24.17 and Large Team at $40.83.
It lands 101 days after Ganttify's previous change, "Ganttify: Student discount reduced from 35% to 30%" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Ganttify cuts student discount ceiling from 35% to 30%" (Apr 13, 2026). Ganttify has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Microsoft and Asana also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
