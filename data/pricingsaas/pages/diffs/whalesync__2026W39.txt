Whalesync: free trial now needs no credit card
Verified Sep 21, 2026·Data & Analytics·Direction: neutral
The changeStarter, Plus, Pro trial — trial change.

What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Whalesync's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: All three plans (Starter, Plus, Pro) add "No credit card required" to the 7-day free trial badge; new FAQ entry answers this. In detail: All three plans (Starter, Plus, Pro) now display 'No credit card required' next to the 7-day free trial badge, and a new FAQ entry answers 'Do I need a credit card to start?'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Starter at $40, Plus at $80 and Pro at $200.
It lands 28 days after Whalesync's previous change, "Whalesync: free trial cut from 2 weeks to 7 days on all plans" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Whalesync: Personal plan dropped, Starter $20→$40 & Plus $40→$80 (+1 more)" (Aug 10, 2026), "Whalesync: Plus & Pro record sync limit increased 50,000 → 1,000,000" (Jul 27, 2026) and "Whalesync adds HubSpot connector, joining Attio and Pipedrive on Plus plan" (Jul 13, 2026). Whalesync has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
