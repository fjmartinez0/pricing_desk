What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of KodeKloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Annual billing savings headline dropped from 'up to 50%' to 'up to 21%' as the October Skillfest 'Claim Up to 50% Off' sale banner disappeared from the page. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $15, Pro at $21 and AI at $29.
It lands 14 days after KodeKloud's previous change, "KodeKloud annual savings shrink to 21% as Autumn sale ends" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "KodeKloud doubles annual discount to 50% as Autumn of Learning sale launches" (Sep 28, 2026), "KodeKloud: annual discount reduced from 50% to 21%" (Sep 21, 2026) and "KodeKloud raises annual discount from 21% to 50% in Skill-tember flash sale" (Sep 14, 2026). KodeKloud has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
