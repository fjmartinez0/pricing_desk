What happened
Between the Nov 6, 2026 and Nov 7, 2026 captures of Synthesia's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Synthesia's Syren AI video agent quota changed from a flat per-generation duration cap ('Up to 3 minutes' for every plan) to per-plan video-count quotas (1 video Basic, ~2/mo Starter, ~7/mo Pro, Custom Enterprise); the label also changed from 'Video length' to 'Video generation'. Separately, the AI Video Assets feature's explicit '3/month' numeric cap on the Basic plan was removed and its descript. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $14 and Pro at $59.
It lands 4 days after Synthesia's previous change, "Synthesia drops its interactive credit calculator from the pricing page" (Nov 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Synthesia drops Roleplay add-on for new 'Syren' video allowance tiers" (Oct 29, 2026), "Synthesia adds up to 4 months free on yearly plans, Pro yearly at $59/mo" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Synthesia cuts Syren promo credits to 1,000 on Basic and 1,500 on Starter and Pro" (Oct 22, 2026). Synthesia has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window Runway, OpusClip and Loom also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
