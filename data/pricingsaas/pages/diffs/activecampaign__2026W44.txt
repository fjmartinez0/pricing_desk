What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of ActiveCampaign's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Email/WhatsApp/Both channel selector removed from main pricing page; WhatsApp plans moved to a separate 'View WhatsApp plans' page. Email plan prices unchanged ($15/$49/$79/$145); Every plan now states it includes AI credits (500 on each plan), with more purchasable at checkout or in account. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, package change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter (Email) at $15, Plus (Email) at $49, Pro (Email) at $79 and Enterprise (Email) at $145.
It lands 175 days after ActiveCampaign's previous change, "ActiveCampaign removes 25% first-year discount and promo banner" (May 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "25% off first year limited time promotion added: Plus plan price dropped from $49/mo to $37/mo wi" (Apr 7, 2026). ActiveCampaign has been tracked since 2026-02-25.
In the same window HubSpot, Salesforce, Mailchimp, Convertkit and Klaviyo also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
