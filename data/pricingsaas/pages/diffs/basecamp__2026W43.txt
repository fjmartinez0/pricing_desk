What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Basecamp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added 'Connect your AI agents' integration (Claude, ChatGPT, Muse, Grok Bot, and others) to the core feature set included with every plan, from Free through Unlimited. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Freelancer at $25, Studio at $59, Pro at $99, Unlimited at $299 and Additional Storage at $100.
It lands 56 days after Basecamp's previous change, "Basecamp axes per-user fees for fixed pricing, cuts top-tier storage from 5TB" (Aug 24, 2026). Basecamp has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window ClickUp, Asana, Notion and Trello also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
