What happened
Between the Oct 30, 2026 and Oct 31, 2026 captures of GitLab's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New "Advanced Capacity" add-on (coming soon) for Premium/Ultimate at $12,000/top-level group/year - 50,000 req/min & 500,000 req/hour, plus daily overage from $100/day. In detail: Added 'Advanced Capacity' add-on (coming soon) at $12,000 per top-level group per year - flat annual price for higher GitLab.com API rate limits (50,000 req/min, 500,000 req/hour) plus daily overage from $100/day, for Premium and Ultimate. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Premium at $29, Enterprise Agile Planning at $15, Compute Minutes at $10 per 1,000 minutes, Storage at $5 and GitLab Duo Agent Platform at $1 per GitLab Credit.
It lands one day after GitLab's previous change, "GitLab launches Orbit add-on, a context graph sold as query packs" (Oct 30, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "GitLab brings Secrets Manager to Self-Managed, Dedicated still pending" (Oct 16, 2026), "GitLab: Flex billing FAQ overhauled — new usage-cap self-service, terms narrowed" (Sep 21, 2026) and "GitLab renames Flex 'per-use' credits to 'on-demand,' drops overage discounts" (Sep 18, 2026). GitLab has been tracked since 2025-10-05.
