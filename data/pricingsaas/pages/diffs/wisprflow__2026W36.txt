What happened
Between the Aug 24, 2026 and Aug 31, 2026 captures of Wispr Flow's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise plan renamed to 'Growth/Enterprise' and its hidden 'Contact us' price replaced with a visible starting price of from $18/user/mo (annual), shown on both the pricing cards and comparison table; Free gained SOC 2/ISO 27001 and data-deletion disclosures; Pro gained team usage analytics (was Enterprise-only); Growth/Enterprise's list rewritten, Notetaker moved from 'Coming soon' to enabled. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature change, price revealed) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 20, 2026) lists Pro at $12 and Growth at $18.
It lands 2 days after Wispr Flow's previous change, "Wispr Flow: Enterprise tier renamed to 'Growth/Enterprise'; its previously hidden 'Contact us' pricing is now published…" (Aug 29, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Wispr Flow renames Privacy Mode to broader data-sharing and retention controls" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Wispr Flow: launched Notetaker AI meeting notes + dropped 14-day Pro trial" (Aug 10, 2026). Wispr Flow has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
