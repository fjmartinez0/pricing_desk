What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Photoroom's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Plan taglines rewritten: Pro 'Sell more with professional visuals', Max 'Win customers with video ads and high-res visuals', Ultra 'Scale every sales channel with automated 4K visuals'. Prices and credits unchanged; Enterprise moved off the main plan grid into its own Enterprise tab and switched to usage-based pricing: one committed annual balance shared across Photoroom apps and API, charged per. In detail: Enterprise moved off the main plan grid into its own Enterprise tab and switched to usage-based pricing: one committed annual balance shared across Photoroom apps and API, charged per export plus an AI operation fee for generative/premium models, with negotiated rates for Visual Agents. Workflows keep running past the balance. New outcome-based Enterprise Guarantee add-on (fidelity checks with reworks, Food and Fashion annual contracts only). Enterprise column dropped from the comparison table. Still quote-only. Pulse files it as a packaging change (positioning, package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $7.5, Max at $20.99 and Ultra at $82.5.
It lands 63 days after Photoroom's previous change, "Photoroom triples AI credit pool across Pro, Max, Ultra plans—no price hike" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Photoroom: AI credits cut up to 73% + AI Backgrounds split into 4 tiers" (Aug 11, 2026) and "Photoroom: AI Fashion Models 2K generation limit removed from Pro" (Aug 5, 2026). Photoroom has been tracked since 2026-06-26.
