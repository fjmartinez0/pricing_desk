What happened
Between the Oct 22, 2026 and Oct 23, 2026 captures of Shopify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: In-person sales row reworded to 'Accept payments with a phone or card reader'; two new comparison rows added for all plans - Customer profiles (Simple) and Returns (At purchase location). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $29, Grow at $79, Advanced at $299, Plus at $2,300 and POS Pro at $89.
It lands 7 days after Shopify's previous change, "Shopify cuts Grow to $79 and Advanced to $299, reversing D288 hike" (Oct 16, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shopify raises annual Grow to $89/mo and Advanced to $359/mo" (Oct 15, 2026), "Shopify Plus drops 1% sales cashback, removing the $15K cap from its table" (Oct 2, 2026) and "Shopify: Plus payment processing rates now publicly disclosed" (Sep 21, 2026). Shopify has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Salesforce and Squarespace also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
