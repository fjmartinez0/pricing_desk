What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Funnelcast's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Core price rose from $1,250 to $1,500/month billed annually (annual savings now 25%, was 17%); monthly-billed price rose from $1,500 to $2,000/month; Core saved reports reduced from six to three; Enterprise report coverage no longer includes the current year Funnelcast report. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit decrease, price increase, feature removed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Core at $1,500.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Funnelcast. Funnelcast has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
