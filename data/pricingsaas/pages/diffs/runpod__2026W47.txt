What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of RunPod's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pod rates up (RTX Pro 6000 $2.49 to $2.69, H100 SXM $3.99 to $4.39, A100 SXM $1.79 to $2.19) and Serverless H100 $5.39 to $5.89; Clusters B200 $9.59, H100 $4.79, B300 $10.79 now priced. In detail: Secure Cloud Pod rates up on 9 of 21 GPUs (RTX Pro 6000 $2.49→$2.69, H100 SXM $3.99→$4.39, A100 PCIe $1.79→$2.09, A100 SXM $1.79→$2.19, Pro 6000 MIG 48GB $1.29→$1.39, RTX 6000 Ada $0.99→$1.09, RTX A6000 $0.59→$0.69, RTX 5090 $1.19→$1.39, L4 $0.59→$0.69); Serverless up on 5 of 13 (RTX 6000 Pro $3.49→$3.69, H100 $5.39→$5.89, A100 $2.72→$2.99, L40/L40S/6000 Ada $1.75→$1.89, 5090 $1.79→$1.89); on-demand Clusters B200 $9.59 and H100 SXM $4.79 /GPU/hr now priced (were Contact sales), B300 $10.79 added, L40S dropped. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Storage at $0.05 per GB/month, Pods - Community Cloud at $0.16 per GPU-hour, Pods - Secure Cloud at $0.27 per GPU-hour, Serverless at $0.58 per GPU-hour and Clusters at $1.79 per GPU-hour.
It lands 7 days after RunPod's previous change, "RunPod exposes Community Cloud GPU rates: H100 from $1.99/hr, A100 from $1.19/hr" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "RunPod raises Serverless GPU prices up to 25%, drops savings claim" (Nov 1, 2026), "RunPod raises GPU rates: H200 cluster jumps 47% to $6.35/hr" (Oct 19, 2026) and "RunPod raises GPU cloud rates, B200 jumps to $7.99/hr and H100 to $3.99/hr" (Oct 11, 2026). RunPod has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
