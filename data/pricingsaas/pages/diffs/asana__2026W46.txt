What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Asana's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Meetings (action items, owners, summaries) added to Asana AI included list on Advanced/Enterprise; Tables (lookup fields) added to Advanced and Enterprise feature lists. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $10.99, Advanced at $24.99 and AI Teammates at $0.60 per AI request (pay as you go) + $0.50 per AI request (prepaid usage pack).
It lands 28 days after Asana's previous change, "Asana opens Timesheets and Budgets add-on to Starter and Advanced plans" (Oct 12, 2026). Asana has been tracked since 2023-11-17.
In the same window Notion, ClickUp and Microsoft also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
