What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Zoho's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise Edition gained a new 'Support for guided selling' capability, added between the usage-based billing controls and revenue recognition bullets; Added a new promotional banner offering 1% cashback in Zoho Credits for every card transaction processed via Zoho Payments; offer valid for new users only. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $25, Premium at $59, Users Add-on at $7.5, Timesheet User Add-on at $2.5 and Document Autoscans Add-on at $8.
It lands 257 days after Zoho's previous change, "Zoho Subscriptions Rebrands Custom Plan as 'Enterprise Edition' with Enhanced Messaging and Positioning" (Jan 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Zoho Subscriptions Introduces In-App Purchase Management to Premium Plan, Enhancing User Experience and Value" (Q3 2025) and "Zoho Subscriptions Introduces Progress Invoicing Feature to Standard Plan, Enhancing Billing Capabilities" (Q1 2025). Zoho has been tracked since 2023-11-30.
In the same window Microsoft, Salesforce, HubSpot and Pipedrive also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
