What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Happierleads' pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 10-visitor refund guarantee now applies to the first 30 days on a paid plan, starting at first payment rather than trial signup. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists White Label at $0.10 leads, Starter at $1.17, Growth at $183, Pro at $356 and Business at $493.
It lands 14 days after Happierleads' previous change, "Happierleads drops 175M contact database from annual billing pitch" (Nov 1, 2026). Happierleads has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive and Leadfeeder also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
