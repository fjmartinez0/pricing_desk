What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Plecto's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Dynamic Dashboards no longer listed among Large plan features (previously 'Everything in Medium, plus' list). Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Medium at $24 and Large at $37.2.
It lands 21 days after Plecto's previous change, "Plecto adds 20% annual discount, cuts per-license prices; kills add-on bundle" (Sep 21, 2026). Plecto has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Supermetrics and Tableau also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
