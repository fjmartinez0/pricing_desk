What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of impact.com's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan price and partner payout allowance were replaced with 'Custom' pricing; previously displayed $10,000/mo with up to $82,500/mo in partner payouts included. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $12, Pro at $12 and Starter at $30.
It lands the same day as impact.com's previous change, "impact.com hides Enterprise price, drops $10,000/mo tier" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "impact.com: Enterprise price revealed ($10,000/mo), tiers restructured" (Oct 19, 2026). impact.com has been tracked since 2026-05-13.
