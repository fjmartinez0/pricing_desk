What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Ashby's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Interviewer paid add-on added — Paid Add-On across Foundations, Plus, and Enterprise plans; conducts structured, job-specific interviews with transcript-based evaluations. In detail: New 'AI Interviewer' paid add-on introduced (Paid Add-On across Foundations, Plus, and Enterprise) — conducts structured, job-specific interviews and provides transcript-based evaluations for the team to review directly in Ashby. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on). The current page lists Foundations Plan at $360.
It lands 27 days after Ashby's previous change, "Ashby adds searchable audit logs for admin and recruiting activity" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Ashby adds recruiting blocks to Advanced Scheduling add-on" (Oct 4, 2026). Ashby has been tracked since 2026-05-12.
In the same window Workable, SmartRecruiters and LeveragePoint also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
