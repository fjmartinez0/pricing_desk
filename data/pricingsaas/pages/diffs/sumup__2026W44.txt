What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of SumUp's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Hardware cashback offer (up to £100 back on selected hardware) banner removed from top of pricing page; POS Plus (£39/mo) tagline expanded from hospitality and retail functionality to include bookings functionality. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, discount removed) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 28, 2026) lists Payments Plus at GBP 15.75, POS Plus at GBP 39, Business Account Plus at GBP 15 and Invoices Plus at GBP 8.
It lands 15 days after SumUp's previous change, "SumUp drops Payments Plus 7-day free trial, pushes £3,000+ fee cuts" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SumUp switches to yearly-first pricing: £189/year, save 17%" (Sep 7, 2026) and "SumUp hikes POS Plus add-on price from £29 to £39 per month" (Aug 17, 2026). SumUp has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Stripe and Squarespace also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
