What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of RunPod's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Serverless per-hour GPU prices rose: B300 $10.65 to $11.99, B200 $8.64 to $10.79, H200 $5.93 to $6.99, H100 $4.79 to $5.39, 5090 $1.58 to $1.79, 4090 $1.10 to $1.34, L4/A5000/3090 $0.69 to $0.75; Removed 'Save 25% over other Serverless cloud providers on flex workers' claim and the 'save more with long-term commitments / discounted reserved workers' copy; now points to contract pricing. Pulse files it as a pricing change (positioning, price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Storage at $0.05 per GB/month, Pods - Community Cloud at $0.16 per GPU-hour, Pods - Secure Cloud at $0.27 per GPU-hour, Serverless at $0.58 per GPU-hour and Clusters at $1.79 per GPU-hour.
It lands 14 days after RunPod's previous change, "RunPod raises GPU rates: H200 cluster jumps 47% to $6.35/hr" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "RunPod raises GPU cloud rates, B200 jumps to $7.99/hr and H100 to $3.99/hr" (Oct 12, 2026), "RunPod hikes GPU rates on Pods: H100 up to $3.49/hr, A100 to $1.59/hr" (Sep 7, 2026) and "RunPod: GPU hourly rates increased across 9 tiers (B300, B200, H200 + 6 more)" (Aug 17, 2026). RunPod has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Modal and Together AI also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
