What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Upstage's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Solar Pro 4 launch promotion stepped down from 90% off to 70% off per its pre-announced schedule, raising the effective price from $0.03 to $0.09 per 1M input tokens ($0.006 to $0.018 cached); Removed the interactive 'Estimate your costs' calculator (Generative Intelligence and Document Intelligence cost estimator widgets with 'Add to Estimate' buttons) from the pricing page. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature removed, discount change) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Explore at $100, Build at $500, Scale at $5,000, Solar Pro 4 at $0.06 per 1M cached input tokens + $0.3 per 1M input tokens and Solar Pro 3 at $0.15 per 1M input tokens + $0.6 per 1M output tokens.
It lands 7 days after Upstage's previous change, "Upstage API: Solar Pro 4 discount extended at 70% off (was reverting to standard price)" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Upstage API ends Solar Pro 4 free period — now 90% off until Sep 10, full rates from Sep 11" (Aug 17, 2026), "Upstage API: File Search pricing dropped to Free + Embed trial/EOL extended" (Jul 28, 2026) and "Upstage API: Embed 2 post-promo rate cut ($0.10→$0.02/1M tokens)" (Jul 13, 2026). Upstage has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Together AI, OpenAI, Google, Mistral AI and Anthropic also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
