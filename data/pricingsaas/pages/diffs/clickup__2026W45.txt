What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of ClickUp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: ClickUp launched a new top-tier MAX plan ($100/mo) and folded its standalone AI Pricing page (Brain AI $9/mo, Everything AI $28/mo) into pooled AI credit allowances built into every core plan card. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $9, Business at $17, Max at $100 and AI Super Credits at $10 per 10,000 credits.
It lands 14 days after ClickUp's previous change, "ClickUp A/B test: Core plan $8 to $9/user/month (billed yearly) in test layout" (Oct 19, 2026). ClickUp has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Notion, Microsoft, Asana and Trello also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
