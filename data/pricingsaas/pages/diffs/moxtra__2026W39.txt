What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Moxo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Moxo removed the Monthly/Yearly billing toggle entirely. The page no longer offers monthly billing; Team now shows only the annual price ($5,000/yr), which is unchanged from the previously hidden Yearly pane. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Team at $416.67.
It lands 7 days after Moxo's previous change, "Moxo: Team/Scale plan usage caps cut, AI allowance switched to dollar-based" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Moxo pulls AI agent workforce and automation from $500 Team plan" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Moxo locks Pro plan into annual billing, 10x'ing flow and AI credit caps" (Jul 13, 2026). Moxo has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
In the same window Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Copper and monday.com also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
