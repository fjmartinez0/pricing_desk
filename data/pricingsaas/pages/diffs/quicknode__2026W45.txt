What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of QuickNode's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Platform API (formerly Admin API) now lists rate limits per plan: 20, 40, 60, 80 and 250 RPS; Solana gRPC Concurrent streams and Accounts per stream no longer show 'Paid add-on available' on lower plans; now '-'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, add-on removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Build at $34, Accelerate at $212, Scale at $424 and Business at $849.
It lands 7 days after QuickNode's previous change, "QuickNode: Activity Logs and Metrics API added to Enterprise plan" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "QuickNode adds security controls, 90-day retention and SQL Explorer" (Oct 19, 2026), "QuickNode: trace_transaction API credit rate up 20x (2 to 40/request)" (Aug 31, 2026) and "QuickNode cuts Build plan to $34/mo via 30% promo, saving $176/yr" (Aug 17, 2026). QuickNode has been tracked since 2026-05-04.
In the same window Alchemy also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
