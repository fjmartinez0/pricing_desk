What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Baremetrics's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Growth price rose $69/mo to $189/mo and Scale $409/mo to $749/mo; the ARR slider was replaced with fixed 'Starts At' tiers, and Payment Recovery's 10%-of-recovered option was dropped for a flat $129/mo fee; Pricing cards now disclose per-tier integration limits: Launch gets a Single Integration, Growth gets Two Integrations, and Scale offers Unlimited Integrations - absent from the prior pricing. In detail: Pricing cards now disclose per-tier integration limits: Launch gets a Single Integration, Growth gets Two Integrations, and Scale offers Unlimited Integrations - absent from the prior pricing page. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Launch at $49, Growth at $189, Scale at $749, Payment Recovery at $129 and Cancellation Insights at $129.
It lands 7 days after Baremetrics's previous change, "Baremetrics swaps tiers for ARR slider, adds free tier, slashes prices" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Baremetrics: month-to-month pricing removed from FAQ" (Aug 10, 2026). Baremetrics has been tracked since 2023-12-03.
