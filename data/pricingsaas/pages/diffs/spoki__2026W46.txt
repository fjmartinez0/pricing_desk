What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Spoki's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday promo: 20% off the quarterly Sales Plan for a full year with code BLACKFRIDAY26, valid until 30 November; replaces the generic 'Black Friday is calling' hero. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Service at EUR 39, Marketing at EUR 79, Sales at EUR 119, Voice at EUR 19.99 and SMS Two-way at EUR 19.
It lands 35 days after Spoki's previous change, "Spoki caps free messaging, replacing unlimited with 1,000 service messages" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Spoki adds AI consulting to Standard, Advanced & Premium tiers" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Spoki hikes WhatsApp Marketing credit rate 15%, real spend jumps to €308.92/mo" (Sep 14, 2026). Spoki has been tracked since 2026-08-27.
