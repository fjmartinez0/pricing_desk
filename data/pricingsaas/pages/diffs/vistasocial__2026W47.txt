What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Vista Social's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New AI credit bundles add-on ($10/mo per 1,000 credits); X (Twitter) add-on price now publicly shown ($29/mo per X profile), previously unpriced. Advanced plan gains new "Ads management" feature; Enterprise add-ons unbundled into SSO, new Audit log, and Data migration items. In detail: Added a new 'AI credit bundles' add-on ($10/mo per 1,000 extra AI credits), and now publicly discloses pricing for the existing X (Twitter) add-on ($29/mo per X profile) which previously had no price shown anywhere on the page; Advanced plan gained a new 'Ads management' feature (connect ad accounts, create and edit campaigns via Ask Vista), marked 'New'. Separately, a new 'Audit log' item was introduced under the newly unbundled 'Enterprise add-ons' section (alongside SSO and data migration, which were previously included directly in the Enterprise plan). Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, feature added). The current page lists Professional at $80, Advanced at $160, Scale at $360, Employee Advocacy Add-on at $199 and X (Twitter) Add-on at $29.
It lands 14 days after Vista Social's previous change, "Vista Social adds custom dashboards to reporting on four plans" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vista Social positions Enterprise for teams of 8+ users" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Vista Social raises prices up to 34% and halves Scale plan user seats" (Sep 28, 2026). Vista Social has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Hootsuite, Buffer, Sproutsocial and Sendible also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
