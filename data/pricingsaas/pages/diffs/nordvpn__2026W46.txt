What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of NordVPN's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Message protection no longer labelled 'Android only, iOS coming soon' on the plan comparison; iOS availability caveat removed, implying iOS support is now live. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $3.49, Complete at $3.99 and Prime at $6.99.
It lands 14 days after NordVPN's previous change, "NordVPN: Black Friday intro discount deepens to 78% off (Complete plan)" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "NordVPN: 3 comparison-table features removed, Message Protection + 1 more added" (Aug 31, 2026) and "NordVPN drops $1M/$100K coverage amounts from Prime card Coveron bullet" (Aug 17, 2026). NordVPN has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window ExpressVPN, CyberGhost VPN, Ava and Surfshark also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
