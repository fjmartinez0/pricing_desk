What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Veel's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pricing restructured: Starter became free (was $150/mo), new Launchpad tier launched at $499/mo, Pro rose $500 to $999/mo, extra-seat overage fell $100 to $25/mo, and a usage-credit meter was added; All plan cards now show a '5 integrations' badge (Shopify, Shopline, WooCommerce, Walmart, eBay); before, only one 'Shopify integration' bullet existed, buried in the comparison table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Launchpad at $399.92, Pro at $799.92 and Elite at $1,999.92.
It lands 13 days after Veel's previous change, "Veel: Content Licensing capability added across all plans" (Aug 31, 2026). Veel has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
In the same window Google, Microsoft, Ringcentral and Zoom also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
