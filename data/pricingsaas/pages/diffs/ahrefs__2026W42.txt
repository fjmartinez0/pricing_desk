What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Ahrefs's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan feature list adds 'No training on your data' (data-privacy guarantee) to the Brand Radar-style plan comparison. In detail: Enterprise plan's 'All Advanced features, plus:' list adds 'No training on your data' (data-privacy guarantee) below Forecasting & trends. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $29, Lite at $129, Brand Radar AI at $199, Standard at $249 and Advanced at $449.
It lands 14 days after Ahrefs's previous change, "Ahrefs discloses 4M Connect API units/mo for Enterprise, previously Custom" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Ahrefs doubles Connect API unit limits, drops Enterprise cap for Custom" (Sep 21, 2026). Ahrefs has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Moz, Similarweb and Semrush also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
