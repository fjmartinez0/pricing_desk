What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Salesforge's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday/Cyber Monday code BFCM26 adds 27% off on top of the 2-months-free annual discount (39% vs monthly list): annual Pro $40 to $29.20/mo, Growth $80 to $58.40/mo. Monthly list prices unchanged ($48/$96). Expect removal after promo. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro Plan at $40, Growth Plan at $80, Agent Frank at $499 and Forge Expert Double Session at $500 per use.
It lands 63 days after Salesforge's previous change, "Salesforge: new VIP treatment perk tier for $500+/mo spend" (Sep 21, 2026). Salesforge has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
