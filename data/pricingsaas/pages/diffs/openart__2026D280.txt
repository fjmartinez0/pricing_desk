What happened
Between the Oct 6, 2026 and Oct 7, 2026 captures of OpenArt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New promo banner: 30% off all Nano Banana 2.1 generations; upgrade by Oct 14 for 7 days on Pro or 14 days on Wonder. Seedance/Flux promo banner unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Essential at $7, Advanced at $14.5, Team Plan at $17.5, Infinite at $28 and Wonder at $120.
It lands one day after OpenArt's previous change, "OpenArt offers unlimited Flux 3 720p videos on Wonder plan until Oct 31" (Oct 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenArt offers up to 50% off Seedance models plus unlimited Flux 3 Image" (Oct 2, 2026), "OpenArt offers 30% off MiniMax H3 upgrades, replacing Wonder video promo" (Sep 28, 2026) and "OpenArt swaps Flux 3 promo for 30% off MiniMax H3, any plan" (Sep 22, 2026). OpenArt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Runway and Microsoft also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
