What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Backlog's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Backlog added a 'Cross-project Gantt chart' capability to the Premium plan (inherited by Enterprise), letting users view issues from multiple projects in one Gantt chart; new row added to the comparison table.
