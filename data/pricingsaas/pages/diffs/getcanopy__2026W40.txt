What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Canopy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New "API" row added to the Platform features section (Plus/Premium plans), letting firms sync data and build custom integrations. In detail: New "API" capability added to the Platform features section, letting firms sync data and build custom integrations with Canopy. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $74, Plus at $109, Premium at $149, Knowledge-Based Authentication (KBA) at $1.25 per KBA credit and Smart Intake at $11 per client credit.
It lands 49 days after Canopy's previous change, "Canopy: Tax Resolution auto-fill now covers state + federal forms" (Aug 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Canopy: Automated workflows feature moved from Plus-only to Standard plan" (Aug 3, 2026), "Canopy: Tax Workflow Automation add-on price increased from $18 to $34/credit; Close Automation exited beta and is…" (Jul 20, 2026) and "Canopy: à la carte modules replaced by tiered plans ($74-$149/user/mo)" (Jul 3, 2026). Canopy has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Zoho, Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive and Copper also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
