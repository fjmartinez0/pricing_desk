What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Jimdo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Complete plan at $29/mo added between Grow ($17) and Unlimited ($49.50); existing Start/Grow/Unlimited prices unchanged; Companion AI now included in every plan including Free, capped at 25 messages per day, with no credits or tokens to buy. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Start at $11, Grow at $17, Complete at $29, Unlimited at $49.50 and Listings Management at $5.
It lands 129 days after Jimdo's previous change, "Jimdo: New Unlimited plan launched at $49.50/mo + Brand Monitoring add-on" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Jimdo drops Premium label from customer support tier name" (Apr 27, 2026), "Jimdo Slows Grow Plan Support Response Time from 4 to 24 Hours" (Mar 31, 2026) and "Jimdo Slashes Grow Plan Support SLA, Response Time Jumps to 24 Hours" (Apr 3, 2026). Jimdo has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
