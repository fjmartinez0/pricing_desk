What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of FaceUp's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: FaceUp dropped its 3-tier plan structure (Starter/Professional/Enterprise, each quote-based) for a modular model: a baseline 'Included for everyone' platform plus optional capabilities (Hotlines, Investigations, More members, etc.) added a la carte, priced by headcount; Added two new platform capabilities not present on the prior tiered page: 'Disclosures' (New) for collecting/reviewing employee. In detail: Added two new platform capabilities not present on the prior tiered page: 'Disclosures' (New) for collecting/reviewing employee disclosures, and 'Policy management' (Coming soon) for publishing policies and tracking acknowledgements.
