What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of IONOS' pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: VPS XXL+ plan removed (was $68/mo list, $20/mo for 3 months with 1-year term; 12 vCores, 24 GB RAM, 720 GB NVMe). Lineup now VPS S+ to XL+; 3-month intro promo ended: VPS S+/M+/L+/XL+ were $2/$5/$8/$14 per month for 3 months with a 1-year term (Save $12/$27/$51/$99); now list $6/$14/$25/$47 per month, cancel anytime. List prices unchanged; hero 'Starting at' $2 to $6, 'Save up to $144' badge gone. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed, plan removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists VPS S+ at $6, VPS M+ at $14, VPS L+ at $25, VPS XL+ at $47 and Additional IPv4 Address at $5.
It lands 63 days after IONOS' previous change, "IONOS Page1 launches $2/mo VPS S+ tier as other plans jump up to 27%" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "IONOS Page1 adds 1-click app installs to every VPS+ plan, cuts intro section" (Sep 7, 2026). IONOS has been tracked since 2026-06-24.
In the same window Siteground, Dreamhost and Hostinger also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
