What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Livekit's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise/top-tier LiveKit Inference concurrency now 'Up to 600' (starts at 50, request more via dashboard) versus 50 with request-more before; Enterprise 'Volume pricing, including inference' reworded to 'Volume pricing', dropping the inference mention. In detail: Scale plan LiveKit Inference concurrency raised from 50 to up to 600 concurrent sessions (starts at 50, request more via dashboard). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, feature change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Ship at $50 and Scale at $500.
It lands 14 days after Livekit's previous change, "Livekit: Conversational intelligence split into Audio turn detection (EOT) at $0.50 per 1,000 requests and Adaptive…" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Livekit adds Twilio and WhatsApp connectors, tiered $0.004/min pricing" (Sep 28, 2026). Livekit has been tracked since 2025-01-03.
In the same window Ringcentral, Zoom, Pexip and Twilio also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
