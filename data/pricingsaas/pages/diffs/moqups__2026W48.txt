What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Moqups' pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: MCP calls per seat/month raised on all paid tiers: 500 to 4,000, 2,000 to 10,000, and 10,000 to 40,000; comparison table updated to match. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised). The current page lists Business at $6, Starter at $7 and Unlimited at $103.
It lands 142 days after Moqups' previous change, "Moqups: Annual plan reference prices lowered, discount % shrinks" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Moqups slashes Business plan 49% and launches Fresh Season sale" (Mar 16, 2026) and "Moqups Removes Free Plan Export Capability for PNG and PDF" (Mar 10, 2026). Moqups has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
