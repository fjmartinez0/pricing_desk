What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Expo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Included build concurrencies raised: Free/Starter 1 to 5, Production 2 to 15, Enterprise 5 to 20, now with macOS sub-caps (1/2/10/15); Enterprise card replaces 'Send updates to 1M+ MAUs' with 'Volume pricing for EAS Update'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $19 and Production at $199.
It lands 21 days after Expo's previous change, "Expo raises Hosting subrequest limit 10x to 10,000 on all paid plans" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Expo: Hosting Aliases limit cut to 200 (was Unlimited)" (Aug 10, 2026) and "Expo: Observe moves from free beta to paid usage-based pricing (Aug 20, 2026)" (Jul 27, 2026). Expo has been tracked since 2026-06-19.
In the same window GitHub, Unity, Stedi and Spline also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
