What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Synthesia's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Roleplay Training learner-seat add-on ($25/learner/month yearly, $29 monthly) and the per-plan 'Add learner seat' lines removed from pricing page; Starter and Pro cards now show Syren video estimates (~24 and ~84 videos/yr); Basic free allowance adds '1 Syren video - limited promo'; Starter and Pro yearly cards add a 'Try for FREE' free-trial CTA (with 'or Purchase now'), replacing 'Get started'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change, add-on removed, feature added) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $14 and Pro at $59.
It lands 4 days after Synthesia's previous change, "Synthesia drops Roleplay add-on for new 'Syren' video allowance tiers" (Oct 29, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Synthesia adds up to 4 months free on yearly plans, Pro yearly at $59/mo" (Oct 26, 2026), "Synthesia cuts Syren promo credits to 1,000 on Basic and 1,500 on Starter and Pro" (Oct 22, 2026) and "Synthesia launches Syren AI video agent with 2,000 free credits for a month" (Oct 10, 2026). Synthesia has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window Runway, OpusClip and Loom also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
