What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Koyfin's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Advisor discount for growing advisory firms (<$100M AUM) now specified as 50% off Advisor Pro for the first year, replacing vague 'major discount'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Plus at $39, Premium at $79, Advisor Core at $209 and Advisor Pro at $299.
It lands 14 days after Koyfin's previous change, "Koyfin adds Attribution to Advisor Pro plan alongside model portfolios" (Oct 26, 2026). Koyfin has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
