What happened
Between the Aug 31, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Maropost's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Commerce Cloud replaced its single 'Starting at $199/month' teaser price with a 4-tier GMV-based structure: Build $29/mo ($50K GMV), Essentials $160/mo ($500K GMV), Professional $632/mo ($2M GMV), Enterprise $1,600/mo ($10M GMV), each with declining overage rates and a new Monthly/Annually toggle (20% annual savings). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 17, 2026) lists Commerce Cloud Build at $29, Commerce Cloud Essentials at $160, Commerce Cloud Professional at $632 and Commerce Cloud Enterprise at $1,600.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Maropost. Maropost has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
