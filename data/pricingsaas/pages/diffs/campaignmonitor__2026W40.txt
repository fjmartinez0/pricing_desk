What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Campaign Monitor's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New AI Support Agent for instant 24/7 troubleshooting help added under Help & support, across Lite, Essentials, Premier, and Enterprise plans. In detail: Added an AI Support Agent under Help & support, giving instant 24/7 answers to troubleshooting questions with zero wait times, across Lite, Essentials, Premier, and Enterprise plans. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Lite at $13, Essentials at $31, Premier at $171 and Website Builder at $10.
It lands 56 days after Campaign Monitor's previous change, "Campaign Monitor: adds Drip Campaigns feature" (Aug 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Campaign Monitor splits Client dashboard into four dedicated agency views" (Jul 20, 2026) and "Campaign Monitor: Essentials & Premier prices up 20%, GROWWITHCM25 promo expired" (Jul 3, 2026).
