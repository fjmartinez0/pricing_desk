What happened
Between the Nov 3, 2026 and Nov 4, 2026 captures of Pika's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Creator and Fancy plans now include a limited-time perk of free Pika H3 Flex generations every day, called out on both plan cards and in a summary note below the pricing grid; Credits table expanded from 4 to 14 AI models (added Pika H3 Flex, Pika 2.5, more Seedance/Kling variants, Seedream 5 Lite, Ideogram 4.5, ElevenLabs v4, MiniMax Music 3.0); Free plan card tagline switched from a credit coun. In detail: Credits table expanded from 4 to 14 AI models (added Pika H3 Flex, Pika 2.5, more Seedance/Kling variants, Seedream 5 Lite, Ideogram 4.5, ElevenLabs v4, MiniMax Music 3.0); Free plan card tagline switched from a credit count to marketing copy. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $8, Creator at $28, Fancy at $76 and Credit Pack at $1 per 60 credits.
It lands 9 days after Pika's previous change, "Pika adds commercial license to $10 Starter plan, launches plan recommender" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pika top-up credits now work with MCP, ending a key limitation" (Oct 19, 2026), "Pika opens MCP to credit pack top-ups, dropping its exclusion" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Pika limits top-up credits to Pika Create; $25 starter pack briefly pulled (Sep 23–28, restored Sep 29)" (Sep 28, 2026).
