What happened
Between the Nov 19, 2026 and Nov 20, 2026 captures of GitLab's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Promotional included GitLab Credits cut: Premium $12 to $8 and Ultimate $24 to $16 per user/month; existing subscribers keep old amounts through Dec 31, 2026. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 23, 2026) lists Premium at $29, Enterprise Agile Planning at $15, Compute Minutes at $10 per 1,000 minutes, Storage at $5 and GitLab Duo Agent Platform at $1 per GitLab Credit.
It lands 20 days after GitLab's previous change, "GitLab: Advanced Capacity add-on priced at $12,000/year for higher API limits" (Oct 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "GitLab launches Orbit add-on, a context graph sold as query packs" (Oct 30, 2026), "GitLab brings Secrets Manager to Self-Managed, Dedicated still pending" (Oct 16, 2026) and "GitLab renames Flex 'per-use' credits to 'on-demand,' drops overage discounts" (Sep 18, 2026). GitLab has been tracked since 2025-10-05.
In the same window Google Cloud, Aws, GitHub and JetBrains also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
