What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Preppr's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Enterprise plan now shows a starting price of $20K/yr (billed annually), replacing 'Contact Us for Custom Pricing'; Professional plan no longer shows the additional-user price ('for 1 user; $3,000/ea add'l' removed from the card); Professional plan now lists Preppr Knowledge as an included feature; New note that Preppr is available to public agencies through Carahsoft. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price hidden, price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $250, Professional at $833 and Enterprise at $1,666.67.
