What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Ringcentral's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Maximum annual-payment savings on the Canadian RingEX page reduced from up to 33% to up to 25%. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $30, Advanced at $35, Ultra at $45, RingCentral Rooms at $49 and AI Receptionist at $55.
It lands 118 days after Ringcentral's previous change, "RingCentral pulls 12-month-free promo for switchers under 400 employees" (Jul 6, 2026).
