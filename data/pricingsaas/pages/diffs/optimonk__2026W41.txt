What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of OptiMonk's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Plans now define monthly AI credits: Free 1,000, Essential 3,000, Growth 10,000, Premium 20,000, Master custom; prices and pageviews unchanged; MCP server added on every plan to build, edit and publish popups from Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor, alongside the AI agent; Free plan is now unbranded: before the Free card showed Unbranded as not included; after, popups carry no OptiMonk branding on any p. In detail: Free plan is now unbranded: before the Free card showed Unbranded as not included; after, popups carry no OptiMonk branding on any plan, including Free. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, freemium change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essential at $19, Growth at $69 and Premium at $179.
It lands 93 days after OptiMonk's previous change, "OptiMonk: Smart Product Pages add-on removed" (Jul 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OptiMonk pulls Smart Product Pages add-on from pricing lineup" (Jun 22, 2026) and "OptiMonk rebrands AI-powered CRO tool as Smart Product Pages" (Mar 17, 2026). OptiMonk has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
