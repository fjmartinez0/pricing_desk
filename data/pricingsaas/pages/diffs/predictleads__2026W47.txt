What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of PredictLeads' pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pay-as-you-go tiers with $40 monthly minimum and 100 free credits/month replaced by prepaid credit packs ($0.04 down to $0.0025/credit), no minimum, plus one-time $20 free credits. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 18, 2026) lists Pay As You Go Credits at $4 per 100 API credits (entry pack rate) + $0.25 per 100 API credits (largest pack rate).
It lands 217 days after PredictLeads' previous change, "PredictLeads launches interactive pricing calculator and FAQ section" (Apr 13, 2026). PredictLeads has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
