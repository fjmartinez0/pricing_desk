What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Qualio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Quality event templates limit on Growth plan raised from up to 10 to Unlimited (Foundation 5 and Scale Unlimited unchanged). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised).
It lands 181 days after Qualio's previous change, "Qualio expands dev integrations with TestRail, GitLab, and Asana" (May 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Qualio overhauls pricing page, adds unlimited users and Integration Hub" (May 12, 2026). Qualio has been tracked since 2026-03-19.
In the same window Dassault Systèmes, Ava and SAP SE also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
