What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Medrecords AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Team plan added between Self-Service and Enterprise On-Prem: 10c per deduplicated page (8c at 100,000 credits, 5c at 500,000), a login per person with roles, 1 shared credit balance, $0 setup fee and no per-seat fees; 2 plans become 3; Free trial drops its 7-day time limit: was a 7-day trial on 1 case (up to 1,000 pages), now first case free, up to 1,000 pages, on any plan including Team. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan, trial change). The current page lists Self-Service at $0.10 per deduplicated page (under 100,000 credits) + $0.08 per deduplicated page (100,000+ credits) and Team at $0.10 per deduplicated page (under 100,000 credits) + $0.08 per deduplicated page (100,000+ credits).
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Medrecords AI. Medrecords AI has been tracked since 2026-11-16.
