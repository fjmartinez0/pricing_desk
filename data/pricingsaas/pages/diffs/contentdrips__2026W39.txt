What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of ContentDrips's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New API-access messaging added to pricing cards: Starter shows 'No API access', Pro shows 'Full API and MCP access, 6,000 calls/mo', Ultimate shows 'Highest API limits, 25,000 calls/mo'. Call limits unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Starter at $15, Pro at $29 and Ultimate at $52.
It lands 7 days after ContentDrips's previous change, "ContentDrips drops Free plan, shifts to $15 entry tier + new $52 Ultimate" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ContentDrips: Starter swaps Custom Ideas Generator for GIF/MP4 export" (Jul 20, 2026). ContentDrips has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window HubSpot, Mailchimp and Convertkit also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
