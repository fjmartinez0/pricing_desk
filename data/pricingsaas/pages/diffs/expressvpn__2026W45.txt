What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of ExpressVPN's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: ExpressAI quotas now listed for Basic (50 credits/day) and Advanced (100/day) alongside Pro; Basic block-list renamed Advanced Protection; Teams banner rewritten (50 licenses, up to 50% savings). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $2.99, Advanced at $4.49 and Express Pro at $7.49.
It lands 28 days after ExpressVPN's previous change, "ExpressVPN trims ExpressAI models on Express Pro from 7 to 6" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ExpressVPN: Advanced $3.99->$4.49/mo, Express Pro $5.99->$7.49/mo" (Sep 28, 2026), "ExpressVPN: ExpressAI model access expanded (6→7) + 2 more feature additions" (Sep 21, 2026) and "ExpressVPN cuts Pro to $5.99/mo and Advanced to $3.99/mo on 2-year deals" (Sep 14, 2026). ExpressVPN has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window NordVPN, Ava, Surfshark and CyberGhost VPN also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
“ExpressAI” is the name ExpressVPN’s pricing page uses; Pulse files the change under ExpressVPN, the company it tracks.
