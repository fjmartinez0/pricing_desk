What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Corel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Discount rotation across products: Painter gained a new $180 discount ($429 to $249) while PaintShop Pro Ultimate and VideoStudio Ultimate lost their 30% discounts, reverting to $99.99 from $69.99. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction.
