HubSpot Sales Starter Sales Hub: Agent Hub democratized to Starter tier + feature repackaging
Verified Sep 21, 2026·Sales & Customer Relationship Management (CRM)·Direction: neutral
The changeStarter, Enterprise feature — feature change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 14, 2026 → Sep 21, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of HubSpot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Agent Hub moved from Professional to Starter (now included at lower tier); Starter gained Agent Builder and Notetaker; Professional gained Deal Plans (BETA) and Sequences but lost Sales workspace; Enterprise lost AI transcript enrichment (BETA) but gained a Standard sandbox account. In detail: Sales Hub repackaged: Agent Hub moved Pro to Starter; Starter +Agent Builder/Notetaker; Pro +Deal Plans(BETA)/Sequences, -Sales workspace; Enterprise -AI transcript enrichment, +sandbox account. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $7, Professional at $90 and Enterprise at $150.
It lands 70 days after HubSpot's previous change, "Hubspot Sales Starter quietly drops Custom Channels API from Sales Hub Enterp" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hubspot Sales Starter: price cut $9→$7/mo/seat + AI agent reshuffle" (Jul 3, 2026). HubSpot has been tracked since 2025-01-01.
In the same window Salesforce, Microsoft, Zoho, Freshworks and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
