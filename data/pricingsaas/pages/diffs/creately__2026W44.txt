What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Creately's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Pro annual price rose from $4 to $5 per editor/month ($48 → $60/yr), the annual toggle badge changed from Save 20% to Save 37%, and the Free plan shapes-per-diagram limit increased from 40 to 45. In detail: Annual billing toggle discount badge changed from 'Save 20%' to 'Save 37%'; Pro plan annual price increased from $4 to $5 per editor/month (billed annually $48 → $60 per editor). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change, limit increase, price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $5 and Business at $89.
It lands 15 days after Creately's previous change, "Creately drops Starter and Team plans, launches $4 Pro tier with new products" (Oct 12, 2026). Creately has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
