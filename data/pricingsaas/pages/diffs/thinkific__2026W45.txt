What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Thinkific's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Exams downgraded to Plus-only and Question Banks removed: the comparison table's Exams row went from Grow/Expand/Plus to Plus only (now 'attempt limits and passing scores', no Brillium), the Question Banks row was dropped, and Grow's 'Exams and question banks' highlight was replaced by 'Bulk enrollment and imports' (CSV upload). Plus highlights also reworked (SCORM support, Learning paths, Exams a. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Start at $82, Grow at $164, Expand at $374 and Branded Mobile App at $199.
It lands 7 days after Thinkific's previous change, "Thinkific kills $54 Basic plan, doubles entry price to $109/mo" (Oct 26, 2026). Thinkific has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window LearnDash, Podia, Udemy, Mighty Networks and Kajabi also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
