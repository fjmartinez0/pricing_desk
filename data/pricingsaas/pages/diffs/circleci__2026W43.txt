What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of CircleCI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added Smarter Testing (intelligent test selection, dynamic test splitting, auto-rerun failed tests, test analytics) to all plans; included free monthly test-atom quotas of 1,000/10,000/100,000 for Free/Performance/Scale, overage billed at 3,000 credits per 100,000 test atoms. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Performance at $15.
It lands 7 days after CircleCI's previous change, "CircleCI adds macOS M4 Pro to Free plan, plus new Arm Docker betas" (Oct 12, 2026). CircleCI has been tracked since 2026-05-06.
In the same window GitHub, Atlassian, GitLab and JFrog also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
