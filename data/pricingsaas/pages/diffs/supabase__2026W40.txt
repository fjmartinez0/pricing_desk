What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Supabase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Two new metered logging rows added: 'Log Ingestion' (Free: 1 GB included; Pro/Team: 20 GB included, then $0.50/GB) and 'Log Query' (100GB included, scales as ingestion usage x100), alongside existing Log Drain addon. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pro at $25 and Team at $599.
It lands 7 days after Supabase's previous change, "Supabase: Added OAuth 2.1 Server support across all plans" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Supabase: Enterprise plan drops BYO Cloud support" (Aug 31, 2026), "Supabase swaps flat $39/pipeline fee for metered $0.053/pipeline/hour billing" (Jul 20, 2026) and "Supabase adds ISO 27001 certification to SOC2 on Team, Enterprise" (Jul 4, 2026). Supabase has been tracked since 2026-03-15.
In the same window Vercel, Railway, Neon and PlanetScale also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
