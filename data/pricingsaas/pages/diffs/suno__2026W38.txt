What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Suno's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Suno upgraded its available models across all tiers: Free moves from v4.5-all to v6-mini, and Pro/Premier move from the v4, v4.5, v4.5+, v5, v5.5 lineup to v6 and v6-wild. Plan card copy was also condensed page-wide and the 'Tune custom versions of v5.5' bullet reworded to 'Create custom models'. Pro's 30-min upload and add-vocals entitlements are unchanged - the comparison table still shows 'Up t. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro Plan at $8 and Premier Plan at $24.
It lands 4 days after Suno's previous change, "Suno quietly retires model version numbers, unifies v4-v5.5 into v6" (Sep 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Suno's download caps go live: Free 0, Pro 20, Premier 60 tracks/month" (Sep 4, 2026), "Suno Studio's Premier tier unlocks MIDI, effects, automation, and plugin design" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Suno adds download caps, credit-to-song equivalents starting 9/3/26" (Aug 17, 2026). Suno has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window OpenAI and Google also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
