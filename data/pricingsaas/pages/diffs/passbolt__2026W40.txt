What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Passbolt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Offline Mode (BETA)' row added to the Community/Pro/Enterprise feature comparison table, listed under Community features alongside Health Check and Secret history.
