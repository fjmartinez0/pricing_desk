What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Supermetrics' pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Annual discount messaging changed from flat 'Save 20%' to tiered: Starter now shows 'Save 25%', Growth shows 'Save 20%' (replacing 'Recommended'), and the banner reads 'Save up to 25% with yearly plan'; Starter's base allotment shrank (data sources 3->2, accounts/source 3->2) at the same $44/mo price. Growth gained instead: base data sources 6->7 and higher extras caps (destinations, data sources. In detail: Starter's base allotment shrank (data sources 3->2, accounts/source 3->2) at the same $44/mo price. Growth gained instead: base data sources 6->7 and higher extras caps (destinations, data sources, accounts); Added 'AI Credits' as an explicit metered allowance in the plan comparison table: Starter 1,000/mo, Growth 4,000/mo, Enterprise Custom. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit lowered, feature added, discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $44 and Growth at $177.
It lands 7 days after Supermetrics' previous change, "Supermetrics adds AI campaign management, stops listing Studio AI credit allowances" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Supermetrics renames Management API access to Platform API access" (Oct 11, 2026), "Supermetrics drops Data API & MCP access from Starter and Growth" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Supermetrics strips automated refresh, dashboards from Starter, Growth…" (Sep 14, 2026). Supermetrics has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Mixpanel, Amplitude, Google and DAT Freight & Analytics also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
