What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Sameday's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Pricing page intro dropped the 'zero surprise fees' claim and now explicitly discloses that overage beyond included minutes is billed at a per-minute or per-message rate; Scale plan's value-proposition tagline changed from touting '100% of phone calls answered' to emphasizing 24/7 coverage, including after-hours and call spikes; Scale plan's Voice cloning feature now carries a legal disclaimer r. In detail: Scale plan's Voice cloning feature now carries a legal disclaimer requiring written consent from the voice owner, not previously stated. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning, feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Launch at $449 and Scale at $789.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Sameday. Sameday has been tracked since 2026-10-06.
