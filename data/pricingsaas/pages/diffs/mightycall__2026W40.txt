Verified Sep 28, 2026·Collaboration & Productivity·Direction: neutral
The changeAutomatic Flagged Number Replacement add-on — add-on change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 21, 2026 → Sep 28, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of MightyCall's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The 'Number autorotation' add-on was relabeled to 'Automatic Flagged Number Replacement' across all plans; usage-based fee and description are unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $20, Pro at $38, Power at $54, Enterprise at $65 and Extra Phone Number at $5.
It lands 21 days after MightyCall's previous change, "MightyCall adds $15/mo number reputation and $0.65 autorotation fees" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MightyCall: Power and Enterprise pricing revealed ($54 / $65 per mo)" (Jul 3, 2026). MightyCall has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
