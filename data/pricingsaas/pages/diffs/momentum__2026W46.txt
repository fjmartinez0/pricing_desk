What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Momentum's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Core no longer lists Momentum alongside Premier Success; Max's 'full suite of AI' bullet is now 'Advanced Momentum'. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Business at $69, Transformation at $99 and Conversation Intelligence at $30.
It lands 220 days after Momentum's previous change, "Momentum Adds Minimum Pricing Thresholds Based on Implementation Scope" (Apr 3, 2026). Momentum has been tracked since 2025-11-05.
In the same window Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
