What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of MailSlurp's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Email warmup usage metered rate added to Usage rates (Pro & Growth) at $0.01/credit; controlled sends use 2 credits/recipient, provider checkpoint sends use 10.
