What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of HelloNote's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Monthly promo banner changed from September 5% off every month for new subscribers to October 10% off for new PT subscribers, user fees only. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Plus at $25 and Lite at $49.99.
