Synthesia swaps Syren AI time cap for tiered video quotas, cuts Basic assets
Verified Nov 9, 2026·AI & Machine Learning·Direction: down
The changeBasic limit — limit decrease.
2 changes·2 captures·Nov 2, 2026 → Nov 9, 2026
What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Synthesia's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Syren AI video agent usage limit changed from a flat time cap (3 minutes per video, all plans) to a plan-tiered video-count quota: Basic 1 video, Starter ~24/year, Pro ~84/year, Enterprise Custom; AI Video Assets access on the Basic plan changed from a capped allowance (3/month) to fully unavailable (✗) in the feature comparison grid. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease, metric change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $14 and Pro at $59.
It lands 2 days after Synthesia's previous change, "Synthesia swaps Syren 3-minute video cap for per-plan monthly video quotas" (Nov 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Synthesia drops its interactive credit calculator from the pricing page" (Nov 3, 2026), "Synthesia drops $25 Roleplay learner-seat add-on, adds free trial to paid plans" (Nov 2, 2026) and "Synthesia drops Roleplay add-on for new 'Syren' video allowance tiers" (Oct 29, 2026). Synthesia has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window OpusClip, Runway and Loom also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
