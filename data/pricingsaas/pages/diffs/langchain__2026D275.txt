What happened
Between the Oct 1, 2026 and Oct 2, 2026 captures of LangChain's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: LangSmith consolidated LCU ($1.50) and LSU ($1.00) into one LangChain Standard Unit at $1.00; usage rates rescaled (e.g. 0.045 LCU to 0.0675 LSU per vCPU-hr) and included units restated in LSU. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Plus at $39.
It lands 39 days after LangChain's previous change, "LangChain: new Tuned Evaluators usage pricing (0.01 LCU/eval run)" (Aug 24, 2026).
