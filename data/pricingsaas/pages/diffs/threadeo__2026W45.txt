What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Threadeo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Threadeo Sync now publishes explicit pricing: $15 per job plus $0.10 per media minute (example jobs $21-$57), with optional LEF/PTZ load files add-on at $13.50 per job. Previously only 'pay per job'; Threadeo Sync now advertises free exhibit summaries at no extra cost with every job. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Threadeo Sync at $15 per job + $0.10 per video or audio minute and LEF / PTZ Load Files at $13.50 per job.
It lands 56 days after Threadeo's previous change, "Threadeo scraps all subscriptions, wipes every price from pricing page" (Sep 7, 2026). Threadeo has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
