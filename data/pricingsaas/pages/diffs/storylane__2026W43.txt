What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Storylane's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Suite tiers (video avatar minutes: 20/125/250/unlimited) replaced by monthly AI credits: Free 200, Starter 1,000, Growth 10,000, Premium 20,000, Enterprise unlimited. Plan prices unchanged. Credit-usage tooltips also drop AI translations from Starter and Growth (now Premium+ only). Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $40, Growth at $100 and Premium at $1,200.
It lands 21 days after Storylane's previous change, "Storylane adds Deal Intelligence to Growth, opens up in-app Comments to Starter" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Storylane: Free/Starter plans de-featured, Growth gains Team Collaboration" (Aug 31, 2026). Storylane has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
In the same window Consensus, Outreach and Salesloft also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
