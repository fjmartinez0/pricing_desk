What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Intercom's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro add-on starting price increased from $99 to $100 per workspace/month. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Essential at $19, Advanced at $85, Expert at $132, Fin AI Agent (Standalone) at $0.99 per Fin outcome and Copilot at $29.
It lands one day after Intercom's previous change, "Intercom launches Fin Voice AI at $1.99 per outcome, adds 90-day guarantee" (Oct 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Intercom ends $19 Essential promo, seat price jumps to $29/month" (Oct 3, 2026), "Intercom slashes Essential plan seat price 34%, from $29 to $19" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Intercom rebrands Essential automation as Fin, adds Fin external actions" (Sep 19, 2026). Intercom has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Salesforce, HubSpot and LiveChat also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
