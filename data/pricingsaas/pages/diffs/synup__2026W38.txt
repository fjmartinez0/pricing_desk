What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Synup's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The capacity metric that differentiates tiers was renamed from 'Parallel agent tasks' to 'Parallel Agent chats' on every plan card, the Enterprise row, and the comparison table; caps (1/3/5/10) unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Startup at $79, Agency at $199, Scale at $799, Social Pro at $6 and Listings Pro at $35.
It lands 21 days after Synup's previous change, "Synup slashes top-tier location cap from 500 to 50, gates rest behind Enterprise" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Synup: API access enhanced with MCP support" (Jul 4, 2026). Synup has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Moz and Semrush also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
