What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of MailSlurp's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: 'Compare plans' feature-comparison grid removed, replaced by per-plan 'At a glance' panels; phone pricing table went from headers-only to fully populated per-country SMS/MMS/rental rates; Growth device renders rose 10→200/month, placement tests 10→100/month; Free's renders/tests fell 3→1 each; AI token allowances now disclosed per plan (Free: 5,000/month); 'Agent' connection limits (1/1/3/10 per. In detail: 'Agent' connection limits (1/1/3/10 per plan) removed entirely from cards, comparison table, and FAQ; new 'Email warm-up' feature added, included for Pro (1 sender) and Growth (3 senders). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, feature removed, package change) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Starter at $19.17, Pro at $45.83, Growth at $116.67, Inbox placement at $1 per test and Device rendering at $0.5 per render.
It lands 14 days after MailSlurp's previous change, "MailSlurp kills paid trials for Pro and Team, points users to Free plan" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MailSlurp: Free & Phone number capacity increased" (Aug 31, 2026). MailSlurp has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window HubSpot, Klaviyo and Mailchimp also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
