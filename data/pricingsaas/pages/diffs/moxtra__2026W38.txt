What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Moxo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Team plan included flows cut from 250/yr to 100/yr and Scale plan from 1,000/yr to 500/yr; AI allowance metric changed from credit-count (25,000/100,000 AI credits/yr) to dollar-value ($100/$500 of AI/yr) on both plans. In detail: Team plan included flows cut from 250/yr to 100/yr and Scale plan from 1,000/yr to 500/yr; AI allowance metric also changed from credit-count (25,000/100,000 AI credits/yr) to dollar-value ($100/$500 of AI/yr) on both plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Team at $416.67.
It lands 7 days after Moxo's previous change, "Moxo pulls AI agent workforce and automation from $500 Team plan" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Moxo locks Pro plan into annual billing, 10x'ing flow and AI credit caps" (Jul 13, 2026). Moxo has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
