What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Vantage's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Expanded native integrations from 30+ to 40+ supported providers, adding Metronome and Ramp to the SaaS integrations list. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $30 and Business at $200.
Vantage's previous recorded change was "Vantage adds DigitalOcean cloud integration, expanding to 36 total" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vantage adds Fireworks AI integration and LLM Token Allocation tracking" (Aug 24, 2026). Vantage has been tracked since 2023-11-28.
In the same window Alteryx, MuleSoft, Talend and Databricks also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
