What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Storylane's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Growth gained Offline Demos and Deal Intelligence from Premium; Starter gained In-app Comments and Alerts; Media to Demo moved from Starter to Free; added a full plan comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Starter at $40, Growth at $100 and Premium at $1,200.
It lands 27 days after Storylane's previous change, "Storylane: Free/Starter plans de-featured, Growth gains Team Collaboration" (Aug 31, 2026). Storylane has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
In the same window Consensus, Outreach and Salesloft also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
