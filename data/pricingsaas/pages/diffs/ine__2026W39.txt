Verified Sep 21, 2026·Cybersecurity & Identity Management·Direction: neutral

What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of INE's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Individual certification exam vouchers (eIAMA, eAIS, eSOC, eMAPT, and others) now display a 'Subscription required' notice; no such requirement was shown on these cards before. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Fundamentals at $29.08, Premium at $66.58, Professional at $799 and Skill Dive at $299.
It lands 7 days after INE's previous change, "INE strips "Subscription required" gating from all 12 exam cards" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "INE: eIAMA bundle price jumps $399→$599 as presale ends + new $450 cert" (Aug 31, 2026), "INE: New eIAMA + 3 months of Premium bundle launched ($399 presale)" (Aug 5, 2026) and "INE adds standalone eAIS AI Security cert for $450, or bundled at $599" (Jul 13, 2026). INE has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Microsoft and Coursera also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
