What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of OneUp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan starting price doubled from $1,000/month to $2,000/month. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $15, Intermediate at $48, Growth at $84 and Business at $240.
It lands 119 days after OneUp's previous change, "OneUp rolls out tiered social listening refresh speeds" (Jun 29, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OneUp: Starter renamed to Basic ($18→$25) + Cross-posting/MCP added + Business account cap" (Jul 4, 2026), "OneUp: Basic (formerly Starter) plan social accounts reduced from 10 to 5; scheduled posts increased from 150 to 300" (Jun 9, 2026) and "OneUp caps Business plan social accounts at 80, adds $1/mo overage" (Jun 2, 2026). OneUp has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
