What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Artlist's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Image Editor and 3D Model Creation added to the feature lists of AI Starter, AI Core and AI Creator plans. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists AI Starter at $11.99, AI Core at $23.99 and AI Creator at $41.67.
It lands 7 days after Artlist's previous change, "Artlist halves AI video caps and drops Flows and MCP from plans" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Artlist adds node-based AI Flows builder, trims model coverage on Creator/Pro" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Artlist: new Artlist MCP feature + Unlimited models expanded to 18 (Pro)" (Jul 28, 2026). Artlist has been tracked since 2026-06-04.
In the same window Shutterstock and Getty Images also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
