What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Appfollow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: In the full feature comparison table, the Team plan's API credits allowance dropped from 40,000 to 1,000 per month. Free, ASO, Essential, and Enterprise values in the same row are unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists ASO at $19, Essential at $129 and Team at $425.
It lands 7 days after Appfollow's previous change, "AppFollow: Team plan app limit cut from 15 to 10" (Sep 14, 2026). Appfollow has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Clevertap, Appsflyer and DAT Freight & Analytics also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
