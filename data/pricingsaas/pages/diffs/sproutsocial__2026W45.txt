What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Sproutsocial's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Trellis Plus add-on: 1,000 AI credits per seat/month at $28/seat/month ($35 billed monthly), available on all plans; base Trellis keeps 100 free credits; NewsWhip added as a new standalone platform alongside Influencer Marketing, with predictive alerts and velocity scoring; no Sprout plan required, demo-only. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $79, Standard at $199, Professional at $299, Advanced at $399 and Trellis Plus at $28.
It lands 70 days after Sproutsocial's previous change, "Sprout Social launches Trellis AI agent across all plans" (Aug 24, 2026). Sproutsocial has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Buffer, Sendible and MeetEdgar also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
