What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Zenzap's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI agent features (personal AI agent, bring-your-own agents, unlimited internal AI workers) once exclusive to the $190/mo Business+ tier now ship starting at the $50/mo Individual tier. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Individual at $50, Lite at $51 and Team+ at $179.
It lands 48 days after Zenzap's previous change, "Zenzap: Nonprofit discount messaging changed from 50% off to unspecified" (Aug 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Zenzap: Business+ storage cut Unlimited to 10TB + AI/feature changes" (Jul 27, 2026). Zenzap has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Stripe also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
