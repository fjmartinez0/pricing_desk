What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Cleric's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free trial evaluation credits doubled from 500 to 1,000, now granted after approval; still no time limit; Microsoft Teams support added alongside Slack and web app on all plans (Team card and comparison table). Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Team at $560, Pro at $1,200 and Scale at $2,400.
It lands 7 days after Cleric's previous change, "Cleric halves credit price to $0.50, adds $3,000 Scale plan, drops Starter" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cleric swaps 14-day trial cap for 500-credit no-limit evaluation, renames metric" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Cleric: pricing restructured to 4 tiers, credit price cut $2→$1" (Aug 10, 2026). Cleric has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
