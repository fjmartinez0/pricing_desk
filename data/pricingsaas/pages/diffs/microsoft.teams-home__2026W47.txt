What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Microsoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 30% first-year/first-month promo added for Microsoft 365 Personal ($99.99->$69.99/yr, $9.99->$6.99/mo) and Premium ($199.99->$139.99/yr, $19.99->$13.99/mo); Family plan unaffected. In detail: 30% first-year/first-month promotional discount added for Microsoft 365 Personal ($99.99->$69.99/yr, $9.99->$6.99/mo) and Premium ($199.99->$139.99/yr, $19.99->$13.99/mo); Family plan unaffected. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Microsoft 365 Personal at $8.33, Microsoft 365 Family at $10.83 and Microsoft 365 Premium at $16.67.
It lands 35 days after Microsoft's previous change, "Microsoft Teams Home cuts Family and Premium storage from 6 TB to 2 TB shared" (Oct 11, 2026). Microsoft has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window Salesforce and Google also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
