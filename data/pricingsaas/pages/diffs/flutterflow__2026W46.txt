FlutterFlow renames Growth and Business plans Pro and Max, revamps AI limits
Verified Nov 9, 2026·Software Development & Testing·Direction: neutral
The changePro, Max plan — plan renamed.
2 changes·2 captures·Nov 2, 2026 → Nov 9, 2026
What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of FlutterFlow's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Growth plan renamed Pro and Business plan renamed Max; prices unchanged ($80 and $150 first seat, $55 and $85 additional seats). Page rebranded around FlutterFlow Studio; Per-plan AI Generation request quotas (5 lifetime, 50, 200, 500 per month) replaced by a shared AI usage allowance expressed as multiples of Basic (2.5x Pro, 5x Max) with refills. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed, metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $29.25, Pro at $60 and Max at $63.75.
It lands 7 days after FlutterFlow's previous change, "FlutterFlow swaps Lite credits for Native AI Agent usage tiers" (Nov 2, 2026). FlutterFlow has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Figma, Webflow and Retool also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
