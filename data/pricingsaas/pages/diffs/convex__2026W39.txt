Convex swaps Enterprise-only permissions for RBAC in every plan
Verified Sep 21, 2026·Cloud & DevOps·Direction: up
The changeAll plans feature — feature added.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 14, 2026 → Sep 21, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Convex's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Feature Comparison table's 'Fine-grained permissions' row (Enterprise-only) was replaced by 'Role-based access control (RBAC)', now included in all plans, plus a separate 'Custom roles' row still exclusive to Business & Enterprise. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Professional at $25, Business & Enterprise at $2,500 and Free & Starter at $0.22 Database I/O (per additional GB) + $0.55 Search storage (per additional GB).
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Convex. Convex has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window Vultr, Vercel, Sumologic, SuperOps and Supabase also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
