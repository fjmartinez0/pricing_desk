What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Kurrent's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Team plan's seat cap doubled from up to 50 seats to up to 100 seats, at the same $15/user/month ($18 monthly) price. Free (5 seats) and Enterprise (unlimited) are unchanged; Team's 'Kurrent AI included' perk was replaced by a Work Items/Insights agent/Projects bundle; Free tier now gains Kurrent AI evals/summaries/titles/embeddings, previously Team/Enterprise-only. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Team at $15.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Kurrent. Kurrent has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
