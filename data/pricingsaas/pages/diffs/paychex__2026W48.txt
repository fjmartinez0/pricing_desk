What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Paychex's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Hero entry-price line 'Starting at $65 + $8 per Employee' removed from the Paychex compare-packages page; no public prices remain, all plans are quote-based. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a neutral direction.
It lands 7 days after Paychex's previous change, "Paychex publishes payroll pricing: $65/month plus $8 per employee" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Paychex ends 5-months-free promo banner, Pay Nothing Until April 2027" (Nov 8, 2026), "Paychex extends Pay Nothing Until 2027 deal but cuts free months to five" (Nov 1, 2026) and "Paychex offers 6 months free, no payments until April 2027, through Oct. 31" (Oct 19, 2026). Paychex has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
