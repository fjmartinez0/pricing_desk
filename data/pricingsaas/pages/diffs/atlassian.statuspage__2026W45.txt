What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Atlassian's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Statuspage public-page Business plan price rose from $300/month to $399/month. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Hobby at $29, Starter at $79, Startup at $99, Growth at $249 and Audience-Specific at $300.
It lands 6 days after Atlassian's previous change, "Atlassian Statuspage cuts Business plan price 25% to $300/month" (Oct 26, 2026). Atlassian has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Slack, GitHub, GitLab, Microsoft and Asana also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
