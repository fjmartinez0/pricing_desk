GitBook renames Premium to Essential, bundles AI credits into paid plans
Verified Oct 12, 2026·Software Development & Testing·Direction: neutral
The changeEssential, Ultimate packaging — package change.
2 changes·2 captures·Oct 5, 2026 → Oct 12, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of GitBook's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Premium plan renamed Essential; price unchanged at $65/site/month; AI credits now included in paid plans ($20 Essential, $100 Ultimate); credit bundles purchasable in calculator. In detail: AI usage moved to credit metering: Essential includes $20 (2,000 credits)/mo, Ultimate $100 (10,000 credits)/mo; AI Assistant 20 credits/question, Essential translations now 30 credits/1k words (was $25/50k words); extra credit bundles $47.50–$300/mo. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, plan renamed) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Essential at $12 and Ultimate at $12.
It lands 146 days after GitBook's previous change, "GitBook triples AI answer quota and adds Agent to Ultimate plan" (May 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "GitBook fixes annual pricing display, adds enterprise customer logos" (May 21, 2026), "GitBook rebrands Premium insights features as analytics across pricing page" (Apr 22, 2026) and "GitBook caps AI answers at 200, overhauls plans with audience-based labels" (Apr 6, 2026). GitBook has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
