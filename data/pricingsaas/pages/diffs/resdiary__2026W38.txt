What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of ResDiary's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Payment processing surcharge applied to Pre-orders (Order & Pay) and the Order & Pay + Events Manager Bundle add-ons increased from 1% to 1.2% on Deposits and Payments; Gift Vouchers surcharge unchanged at 3%. In detail: Payment processing surcharge on Deposits and Payments increased from 1% to 1.2% in the add-ons footnote, which covers Pre-orders, Gift Vouchers, and the Pre-orders + Events Manager Bundle; the Gift Vouchers surcharge is unchanged at 3%. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Order & Pay + Events Manager Bundle at $65, ResPhone at $50, Event Manager at $45, Gift Vouchers at $45 and PMS Integration at $15.
It lands 63 days after ResDiary's previous change, "ResDiary: Payment processing surcharge cut from 1.2% to 1%" (Jul 13, 2026). ResDiary has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window TouchBistro, Lightspeed and Toast also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
