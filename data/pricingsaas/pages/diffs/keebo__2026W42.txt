What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Keebo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pay-As-You-Go savings-share line now specifies the percent of savings generated by Keebo Warehouse Optimization, rather than Keebo generally. Prices unchanged ($0/month start). Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction.
It lands 56 days after Keebo's previous change, "Keebo: removed savings self-verification SQL script offer" (Aug 17, 2026). Keebo has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
