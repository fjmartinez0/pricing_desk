What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Remote's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Employer of Record price decreased from $699 to $599 per employee/month; Equity plan (From $39/month) removed from pricing page; Social Purpose 15% discount program for social-purpose organizations removed; Remote for Startups 15% discount added: 15% off Employer of Record and Contractor Management for first 12 months (pre-seed/seed/Series A). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added, price decrease, discount removed, plan removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Payroll at $29, Contractor Management at $29, Equity at $39, Contractor Management Plus at $99 and Professional Employer Organization at $99.
It lands 280 days after Remote's previous change, "Remote Increases Employer of Record Monthly Price by 4.5% to $699/Employee" (Jan 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Remote Introduces Contractor of Record Service at $325/month, Enhancing Direct Engagement and IP Transfer Options" (Q2 2025), "Remote Payroll Pricing Decreases by 42% to $29/Employee/Month" (Q4 2024) and "Remote Expands Contractor Management Coverage to Include 200+ Countries and Territories" (Q3 2024). Remote has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Slack, Microsoft, Google, Asana and Zoom also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
