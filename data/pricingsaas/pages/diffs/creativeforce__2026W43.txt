What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Creative Force's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Core plan (from $3,583/month platform fee + asset volume, billed annually) removed; page now shows only Enterprise with quote-based, volume-tailored pricing; Enterprise 'Unlimited Workspaces' changed to 'Multiple Workspaces'; new 'Premium onboarding & support' section with studio operations experts guiding onboarding replaces 'Premium support'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature change, price hidden) with a downward direction.
It lands 14 days after Creative Force's previous change, "Creative Force pulls Dreem AI credits add-on, moves Dreem to closed beta" (Oct 5, 2026). Creative Force has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
In the same window Wondershare, Descript and Adobe also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
