What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Abacus.AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Basic plan's top-LLM roster updated: GPT 6 Astra / GPT 6.1 Sol / Gemini 4 Carbon became GPT 6.2 Astra / GPT 6.2 Sol / Gemini 4 Neon. Prices and credits unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Basic at $10 and Pro at $20.
It lands 35 days after Abacus.AI's previous change, "Abacus.AI swaps Gemini 3.1 Pro for Gemini 4 Argon on Basic plan" (Oct 18, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Abacus.AI: GPT 6 Astra added to Basic plan's LLM lineup" (Sep 6, 2026). Abacus.AI has been tracked since 2026-05-19.
