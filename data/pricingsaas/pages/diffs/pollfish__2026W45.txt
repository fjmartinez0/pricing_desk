What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Pollfish's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: DIY Platform panel reach claim cut from 250M+ to 120M+ Global Consumers. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists DIY Platform at $0.95 per response.
It lands 121 days after Pollfish's previous change, "Pollfish: Full Service/Enterprise pricing reframed as per-market minimums" (Jul 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pollfish drops B2B floor, unifies Full Service at $5k per market" (Jun 17, 2026) and "Pollfish reveals Full Service pricing: B2C from $5k, B2B from $10k" (May 5, 2026). Pollfish has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window SurveyMonkey and Respondent also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
