What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Linktree's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Agency or Enterprise card renamed Teams and Enterprise; now shows team plans starting from $49/mo and a View team plans CTA (Growth/Scale/Enterprise team tiers) instead of Get in touch. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $7, Pro at $14 and Premium at $30.
It lands 21 days after Linktree's previous change, "Linktree raises Starter to $7 and Pro to $14 a month on annual plans" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Linktree Premium now promises unlimited Instagram auto-replies" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Linktree drops selling from Free plan, gives Pro 0% sell-and-earn fees" (Oct 4, 2026). Linktree has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Leadpages and Buffer also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
