What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Improvado's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Growth plan starting price decreased from $50,000/yr to $41,700/yr. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists MCP Only at $100, Starter at $1,666.67, Growth at $3,475, Advanced Analytics bundles at $1,666.67 and Creative Analytics at $333.33.
It lands 7 days after Improvado's previous change, "Improvado drops free tier, adds $20K Starter and $50K Growth plans" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Improvado renames Customization credits to Professional Services credits" (Oct 12, 2026) and "Improvado: Advanced Intelligence features unbundled into paid add-ons" (Aug 24, 2026). Improvado has been tracked since 2026-04-26.
In the same window Mixpanel and Supermetrics also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
