What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of LoadFocus's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Essential plan Parallel Tests raised 1 to 4; Pro plan Parallel Tests raised 3 to 10; Pro plan AI Analyses quota raised 30/month to 50/month; Added new FAQ disclosing 7-day free trial limits for Basic+ plans: 50 virtual users, 5-minute tests, 5 test runs per day (Starter trial runs at Starter's own limits). Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists API Monitoring Starter at $15.8, Page Speed Monitoring Starter at $29, API Monitoring Professional at $33.3, Load Testing Basic at $79 and Page Speed Monitoring Basic at $89.
It lands 84 days after LoadFocus's previous change, "LoadFocus slashes free plan's annual test allowance from 120 to just 10 tests" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LoadFocus: New AI Analyses quota added + trial replaces instant subscribe" (Jul 3, 2026). LoadFocus has been tracked since 2025-12-21.
