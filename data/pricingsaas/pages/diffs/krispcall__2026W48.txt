What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of KrispCall's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Top banner changed from 'Switch to KrispCall and get up to 2 months free' to a Black Friday Deals countdown (ends in 10 days) with Claim Deal Now CTA; plan prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $12, Advance at $32 and Max at $48.
It lands 21 days after KrispCall's previous change, "KrispCall swaps Halloween 15% off for up to 2 months free for switchers" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "KrispCall swaps flat $1 credit for quantified usage and tiered integrations" (Sep 28, 2026) and "KrispCall launches Max tier at $48/mo, renames plans, expands Startup program" (Sep 7, 2026). KrispCall has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
