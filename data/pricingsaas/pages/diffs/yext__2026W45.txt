What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Yext's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Yext rebranded PowerListings to 'Corvo': the paid $199-$999/yr package tiers (Emerging/Essential/Complete/Premium) were replaced by a free 30-site listing scan plus a $40/month 'Corvo Plus' add-on. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Corvo Plus at $40.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Yext. Yext has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
