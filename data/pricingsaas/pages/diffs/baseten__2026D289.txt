What happened
Between the Oct 15, 2026 and Oct 16, 2026 captures of Baseten's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL to Model APIs pricing list: $0.20 input, $0.04 cached input, $0.50 output per 1M tokens. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 22, 2026) lists Basic at $1.32 DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 input (per 1M tokens) + $0.3 Kimi K3 cache input (per 1M tokens).
It lands 18 days after Baseten's previous change, "Baseten adds GLM-5.3 Fast, retires 6 models, cuts DeepSeek V4.1 Flash cache price 77%" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Baseten slashes DeepSeek V4.1 Flash cached input 77% to $0.007 per 1M tokens" (Sep 22, 2026), "Baseten: added Kimi K3, Inkling-Small & DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 model APIs" (Aug 5, 2026) and "Baseten: Kimi K3 model added to Model APIs ($3.00/$0.30/$15.00 per 1M)" (Jul 28, 2026). Baseten has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
