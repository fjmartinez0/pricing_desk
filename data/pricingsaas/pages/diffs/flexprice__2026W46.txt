What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Flexprice's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pay as you go renamed Growth, $199/mo + 0.5% to $500/mo + 0.40%; new Launch plan $99/mo; Enterprise renamed Scale, $3,000/mo start hidden (Contact us). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Launch at $99 and Growth at $500.
It lands 28 days after Flexprice's previous change, "Flexprice swaps event tiers for $199/mo plus 0.5% revenue share" (Oct 12, 2026). Flexprice has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window Unbounce, Leadpages and Convertkit also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
