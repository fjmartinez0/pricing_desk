What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Anedot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Donor Covers Fees text changed: no longer states the donor pays the processing percentage and the organization pays only $0.30; now says donors can choose to cover processing fees. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Anedot. Anedot has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
