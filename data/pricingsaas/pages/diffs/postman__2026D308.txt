What happened
Between the Nov 3, 2026 and Nov 4, 2026 captures of Postman's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise plan dropped the 'SDK auto-distribution' capability from its feature list; no longer mentioned anywhere on the pricing page; Added a new 'Static IP egress for cloud performance testing' row to the feature comparison table, available on Solo, Team, and Enterprise but not Free. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed, feature added) with a downward direction. The current page lists Solo at $9, Team at $19, Enterprise at $0.04 per AI credit + $0.04 per VuH (cloud performance testing) and Simple Security at $6.
It lands 2 days after Postman's previous change, "Postman cuts SDK generation from Team plan, drops it from comparison" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Postman cuts SDK generation from Team plan, leaving it to Enterprise" (Oct 29, 2026), "Postman: Context Graph usage add-on introduced" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Postman adds free Context Graph tracking, metered API query overages" (Sep 23, 2026).
