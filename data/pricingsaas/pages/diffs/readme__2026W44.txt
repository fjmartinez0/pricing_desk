What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Readme's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Slack AI Writer added as a new (NEW-badged) AI feature, in Beta on two higher plans, alongside GitHub AI Writer. In detail: Slack AI Writer added as a new (NEW-badged) AI feature on the Pro card, listed in Beta for Pro and Enterprise in the comparison table, alongside GitHub AI Writer.
