What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Boomi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Support offerings renamed: Professional 'Premier Support' now 'Enhanced Support'; Enterprise 'Premier Plus support' now 'Boomi Customer Care - Expert Bundle'. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Pay-as-you-go at $99.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Boomi. Boomi has been tracked since 2026-06-27.
