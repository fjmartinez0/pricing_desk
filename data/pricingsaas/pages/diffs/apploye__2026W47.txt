What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Apploye's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Adoption Tracking (usage monitoring for ChatGPT, Claude & other AI tools) is now live in the Power plan, replacing the earlier 'AI Reporting & Insights (Soon)' placeholder; also included in Enterprise and available as a paid add-on for Elite. In detail: AI Adoption Tracking (usage monitoring for ChatGPT, Claude & other AI tools) went live, replacing the 'AI Reporting & Insights (Soon)' placeholder in Power; now included in Power/Enterprise, add-on for Elite. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Elite at $4.50, Power at $8, Activity Insights at $3, More Screenshots at $2 and Video Recording at $4.
It lands 7 days after Apploye's previous change, "Apploye adds auto-discard idle time to Elite, Power, Enterprise plans" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Apploye cuts Power plan to $4.80/user/mo with 40% intro promo through Nov 19" (Nov 1, 2026), "Apploye launches 40% off Power promo as free Starter plan loses app-tracking, leave-document and expense allowances" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Apploye adds $3 Activity Insights add-on, folds Stealth Mode into Power" (Oct 18, 2026). Apploye has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
