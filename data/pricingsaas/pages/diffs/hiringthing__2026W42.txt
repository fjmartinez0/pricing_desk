What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of HiringThing's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing repackaged as a single ATS offer with Essential and Pro tiers, starting at $80/mo (was 'Starting at $130/mo, billed annually'; ATS alone was from $76/mo). Separately sold Employee Onboarding ($100/mo start) and its 30-day trial dropped; onboarding features now listed under ATS Features. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Applicant Tracking at $80.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for HiringThing. HiringThing has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
