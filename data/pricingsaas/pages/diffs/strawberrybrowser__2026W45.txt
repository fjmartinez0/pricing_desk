Strawberry opens ChatGPT connection to every plan, no longer Pro-only
Verified Nov 2, 2026·AI & Machine Learning·Direction: neutral
The changeAll plans free tier — freemium change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 26, 2026 → Nov 2, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Strawberry's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: ChatGPT connection moved from a Pro-only bullet (use ChatGPT plan to save credits) to Included in every plan as 'Connect ChatGPT for more usage'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Intern at $17, Starter at $35, Part-time at $71, Standard at $150 and Full-time at $208.
It lands 7 days after Strawberry's previous change, "Strawberry: Free plan no longer mentions a daily usage limit on its monthly credit allowance (also removed from FAQ)" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Strawberry splits pricing into tabs, adds pooled-credit Enterprise plan" (Oct 19, 2026), "Strawberry cuts Part-time plan to $85, adds per-seat Team tiers" (Oct 12, 2026) and "Strawberry slashes free plan credits 75%, from 2,000 to 500 a month" (Oct 5, 2026). Strawberry has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Raspberry AI, Gemelo, Writer, Voxel51 and Wispr Flow also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
“Strawberrybrowser” is the name Strawberry’s pricing page uses; Pulse files the change under Strawberry, the company it tracks.
