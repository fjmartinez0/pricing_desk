What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Aiven's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing page product-tab selector expanded to include two new services, DataHub and Aiven Runtime, alongside existing PostgreSQL, MySQL, Kafka, ClickHouse, etc. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Developer at $5, Hobbyist at $12, Startup at $75, Business at $180 and Premium at $270.
It lands 80 days after Aiven's previous change, "Aiven: Tiered Storage add-on pricing revealed ($0.00012/GB/hour)" (Jul 2, 2026). Aiven has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
