What happened
Between the Aug 24, 2026 and Aug 31, 2026 captures of Vercel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Vercel Connect renamed to Token Requests, included quota cut 5K->500 requests/month, overage rate raised ~10x ($3 per 10K requests -> $3.00 per 1,000 requests); new Triggers line item added (1,000 triggers/month included, $0.95 per 1,000 triggers). In detail: Vercel Connect renamed to Token Requests: included quota cut from 5K to 500 requests/month and overage rate raised ~10x ($3 per 10K requests -> $3.00 per 1,000 requests); new Triggers line item added (1,000/month included, $0.95 per 1,000 triggers). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 20, 2026) lists Pro at $20.
It lands 5 days after Vercel's previous change, "Vercel supercharges sandboxes 5x, splits AI pricing into token and trigger fees" (Aug 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vercel adds tax disclaimer: prices in USD, exclude VAT and GST" (Sep 1, 2026), "Vercel renames Edge Config to Global Config, quotas and pricing untouched" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Vercel cuts Turbo/Observability prices, adds metered Connect, reworks Workflo" (Jul 4, 2026). Vercel has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
