Playbook drops Business plan, folds it into custom-priced Enterprise tier
Verified Sep 21, 2026·Collaboration & Productivity·Direction: neutral
The changeEnterprise packaging — package change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 14, 2026 → Sep 21, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Playbook's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Business plan ($400/mo, unlimited members, 15TB storage) was removed. Its capacity now appears under Enterprise (still Custom-priced); Enterprise's advanced features moved to a new 'Enterprise Add-ons' block. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Pro at $10 and Team at $20.
It lands 35 days after Playbook's previous change, "Playbook Free plan gains 1K included API calls/month" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Playbook: Pro & Team API call limits cut (50K→20K, 500K→200K)" (Aug 10, 2026), "Playbook: SAML SSO moved from Business to Enterprise-only" (Aug 4, 2026) and "Playbook: Pro/Team/Business monthly prices cut 20-44%, Business storage 5TB→15TB" (Jul 13, 2026). Playbook has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Notion, Trello, Smartsheet, ClickUp and Asana also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
