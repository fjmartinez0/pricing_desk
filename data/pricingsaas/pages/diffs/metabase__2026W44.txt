What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Metabase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI bullet changed from SQL generation to preferred-model AI plus MCP server; Pro security adds required 2FA and IP allowlists alongside SSO. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 27, 2026) lists Starter at $90, Pro at $517.5 and Enterprise at $1,666.67.
It lands 63 days after Metabase's previous change, "Metabase quietly reveals Enterprise-only paid air-gapped deployment option" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Metabase: Storage add-on price cut ($40/500k rows → $2/1M rows)" (Aug 3, 2026). Metabase has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Grafana, Tableau and Qlik also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
