What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Front's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New External AI agents (connect Claude, Codex, Sierra agents), Compass (AI agent QA) and MCP server rows added to the comparison tables; Smart QA ($20/seat/mo) and Smart CSAT ($10/seat/mo) add-ons and the $25/seat/mo bundle removed for Starter and Professional; both now Enterprise-only. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, add-on removed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $25, Professional at $65, Enterprise at $105, Copilot at $20 and API Rate Limit Increase at $200.
It lands 101 days after Front's previous change, "Front: Autopilot AI add-on pricing revealed ($0.05/conversation)" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Front reveals Autopilot AI pricing at $0.05 per conversation" (Jun 8, 2026), "Front drops Copilot's free 10-reply quota, makes it pure add-on for Starter/Pro" (May 4, 2026) and "Front removes Copilot and Autopilot branding from pricing add-ons" (Mar 26, 2026). Front has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Intercom, HubSpot and Gorgias also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
