What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Justworks's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Applicant Tracking System added as a key feature of PEO Basic and PEO Plus cards; plan taglines updated to mention the ATS. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Payroll at $50, PEO Basic at $79, PEO Plus at $124, EOR at $599 and Health Insurance Reimbursements (ICHRA) at $25.
It lands 14 days after Justworks's previous change, "Justworks renames ICHRA health reimbursements to CHOICE, pricing unchanged" (Oct 12, 2026). Justworks has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Paychex and BambooHR also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
