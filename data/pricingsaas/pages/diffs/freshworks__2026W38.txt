What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Freshworks's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: MCP actions add-on introduced across Growth/Pro/Enterprise Freshdesk plans at $15 per 1,000 actions. In detail: New 'MCP actions' add-on introduced across all Freshdesk plans (Growth, Pro, Enterprise) at $15 per 1,000 actions, alongside existing Connector app tasks add-on ($80/5000 tasks). Includes 1,200/6,000/12,000 actions/year by plan tier. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Growth at $19, Pro at $55, Enterprise at $89, Connector App Tasks at $80 per 5000 tasks and Freddy AI Agent Sessions at $49 per 100 sessions.
It lands 56 days after Freshworks's previous change, "Freshworks: free starter program ($0 for 1-2 agents/6mo) removed" (Jul 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Freshworks: Freddy AI Agent gains AI Agent Studio + Improve performance" (Jul 3, 2026) and "Freshworks expands Freddy AI Agent with Studio and performance tools" (Jun 22, 2026). Freshworks has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce, Intercom and Drift also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
