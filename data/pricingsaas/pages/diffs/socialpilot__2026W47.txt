What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of SocialPilot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday offer (through Dec 18): yearly discount raised from 15% to 30% and a 20% monthly discount added; list prices unchanged at $30/$50/$100/$200 per month. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $25.50, Standard at $42.50, Premium at $85, Ultimate at $170 and Extra Accounts at $4.
It lands 119 days after SocialPilot's previous change, "SocialPilot adds USD/EUR/GBP/AUD currency selector to pricing page" (Jul 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SocialPilot adds MCP Server Integration across all four paid plans" (Apr 22, 2026). SocialPilot has been tracked since 2026-03-16.
In the same window Hootsuite, Buffer, Sproutsocial and Sendible also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
