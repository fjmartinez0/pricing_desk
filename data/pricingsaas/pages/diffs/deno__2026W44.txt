What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Deno's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Compliance section (SOC2 Type 1, DPA) removed from the plan comparison table; previously Enterprise-only (Included), not included on other plans. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $20 and Builder at $200.
It lands 53 days after Deno's previous change, "Deno slashes egress rates but tightens app limits and team allowances" (Sep 3, 2026). Deno has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
