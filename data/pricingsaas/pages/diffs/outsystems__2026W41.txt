OutSystems rebrands ODC as Agentic Systems Platform, widens hosting options
Verified Oct 5, 2026·Software Development & Testing·Direction: neutral
The changeOutSystems Agentic Systems Platform, Add-ons plan — plan renamed.
2 changes·2 captures·Sep 28, 2026 → Oct 5, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of OutSystems's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Main plan renamed from OutSystems Developer Cloud (ODC) to OutSystems Agentic Systems Platform; 'priced per application, user, add-ons' blurb dropped. No prices shown either side; Hosting row (OutSystems Cloud; self-hosted option) became Deployment: fully managed cloud, on-premises, private cloud, sovereign and regulated cloud. 'Applications' row (1 medium app start) removed. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed, feature change) with a neutral direction.
It lands 93 days after OutSystems's previous change, "OutSystems: ODC starting price hidden, now contact-sales only" (Jul 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OutSystems pulls ODC pricing, replaces cost with estimate CTA" (Jun 2, 2026). OutSystems has been tracked since 2026-02-25.
In the same window Salesforce, Zoho, Microsoft and Mendix also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
