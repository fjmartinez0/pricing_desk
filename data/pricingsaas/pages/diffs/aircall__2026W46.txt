What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Aircall's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: AI Voice Agents usage pricing retiered from 500/2,500/10,000+/50,000 min bundles to 1,000/3,500/10,000 min bundles, pay-as-you-go cut from $0.49/min to flat $0.29/min, bundles now require annual commitment. AI Voice Agents standalone minimum price decreased from $100/month to $99/month. Analytics+ add-on description updated — added custom dashboards and scheduled API exports, removed mention of per-rep insights (heat maps and unlimited history retained). In detail: AI Voice Agents usage pricing restructured: pay-as-you-go cut from $0.49/min to a flat $0.29/min; bundles changed from 500/2,500/10,000+ min to 1,000/3,500/10,000 min, now requiring an annual commitment. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease, feature change, package change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $30, Professional at $50, AI Voice Agents at $99, Aircall for Salesforce Voice at $30 and WhatsApp in Aircall at $10.
It lands 21 days after Aircall's previous change, "Aircall adds $0.03 Number Reputation Dashboard to flag spam-labeled numbers" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Aircall: Custom→Enterprise rename + AI Voice Agents plan ($100/mo min)" (Sep 28, 2026). Aircall has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Ringcentral, Genesys, Microsoft and Twilio also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
