Casemark launches pay-per-job pricing: $25 summaries, no seat fees
Detected Nov 15, 2026, 7:50 PM ETBusiness Operations
The changePay-as-you-go plan new plan
1 change2 capturesNov 9 → Nov 16, 2026 UTC
What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Casemark's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New default Pay-as-you-go pricing priced per job: legal summaries $25, medical summaries $25 + $0.01/page (capped $100), transcript outputs $5 each; no subscription or seat fee. Para seat plans moved to a separate tab. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan). The current page lists Standard at $20 and Pro at $100.
It lands 14 days after Casemark's previous change, "Casemark adds Workspace tab for law firms, pricing starts at $100/user/mo" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Casemark swaps pay-per-deliverable fees for $20 and $100 Para seat plans" (Oct 18, 2026) and "Casemark overhauls AI usage billing, reveals $200/user BAA pricing" (Sep 13, 2026). Casemark has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window Relativity, Logikcull and Everlaw also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
