What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Success.ai's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter card now lists CRM integrations (HubSpot, Slack) and enrichment credits; Enterprise card adds Dedicated Customer Success.
