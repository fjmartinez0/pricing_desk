What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of CodeRabbit's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Cloud Coding Agent and CodeRabbit Agent for Slack add-ons merged into one CodeRabbit Agent add-on (still $0.40/agent minute); CodeRabbit Security add-on renamed to CodeRabbit Security Scan. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Essentials at $24, Team at $48, Advanced at $72, CodeRabbit Agent at $0.4 per agent minute and Usage-based reviews at $0.25 per reviewed file.
It lands 5 days after CodeRabbit's previous change, "CodeRabbit merges Slack and Cloud Coding agents into one add-on" (Sep 9, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CodeRabbit reframes Advanced around architectural insights (copy only)" (Sep 17, 2026), "CodeRabbit drops Issue Planner from Team, Advanced and Enterprise plans" (Sep 4, 2026) and "CodeRabbit slashes add-on overage rate from $0.50 to $0.40 per agent minute" (Sep 3, 2026). CodeRabbit has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
In the same window Replit, JetBrains, Windsurf and GitLab also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
