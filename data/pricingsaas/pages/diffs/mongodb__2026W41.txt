What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of MongoDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Atlas Infinite deployment option (public preview) added at $0.09/hour, up to 128TB per HA shard, 1M+ IOPS burst, M10+ dedicated clusters; Atlas section renamed Atlas Core. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Flex at $0.01 per hour, Dedicated at $0.08 per hour and Atlas Infinite at $0.09 per hour.
It lands 193 days after MongoDB's previous change, "MongoDB restructures Atlas tiers with new entry-level pricing" (Mar 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MongoDB reframes plans and adds features" (Mar 17, 2026), "MongoDB launches Pricing Calculator and cost optimization tools for Atlas" (Week 10, 2026) and "MongoDB removes interactive cluster sizing tool from pricing page" (Week 7, 2026). MongoDB has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
