What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Soldo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Standard plan increased from £21/month to £27/month (+VAT) and Plus plan increased from £33/month to £39/month (+VAT). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at GBP 27 and Plus at GBP 39.
It lands 171 days after Soldo's previous change, "Soldo Drops Pricing Wizard for Transparent Per-User Card Model" (Apr 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Soldo Reduces Plus and Standard Plan Card Allocations by Up to 90%, Significantly Impacting User Access" (Q4 2025), "Soldo Introduces Paid Add-On for Additional Cards at £6/Month, Enhancing User Flexibility" (Q3 2025) and "Soldo Renames Enterprise Plan to Unlimited, Maintaining Features and Pricing for Large Organizations" (Q1 2025). Soldo has been tracked since 2023-11-24.
In the same window Mercury, Stripe and Revolut also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
