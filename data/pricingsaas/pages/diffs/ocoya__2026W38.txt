What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Ocoya's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Ocoya replaced its 4-tier Workspaces-based lineup (Bronze $15, Silver $39, Gold $79, Diamond $159/mo) with a 3-tier Users-based lineup (Starter $29, Team $79, Agency $199/mo); entry price up 93%, unlimited Diamond tier discontinued; The 'Workspaces' capacity metric was removed from every plan card; Starter/Team/Agency are now scoped only by Users, Social profiles, and Credits, dropping the per-wo. In detail: The 'Workspaces' capacity metric was removed from every plan card; Starter/Team/Agency are now scoped only by Users, Social profiles, and Credits, dropping the per-workspace dimension (and its unlimited option) entirely. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, metric change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Starter at $24.17, Team at $65.83 and Agency at $165.83.
It lands 72 days after Ocoya's previous change, "Ocoya: Automation runs limit removed from all plans" (Jul 3, 2026). Ocoya has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
