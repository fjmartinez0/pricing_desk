What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Invoice Simple's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plus and Premium monthly prices now show a ~50% promotional discount ($14.99 to $7.49/mo, $21.99 to $10.99/mo) with the original price struck through. Essentials ($6.99/mo) is unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essentials at $5.83, Plus at $12.5 and Premium at $18.33.
It lands 87 days after Invoice Simple's previous change, "Invoice Simple: Plus and Premium plan prices increased" (Jul 3, 2026). Invoice Simple has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
