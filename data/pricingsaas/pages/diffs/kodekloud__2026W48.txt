What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of KodeKloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday promotion deepened: banner and annual-billing toggle now say up to 55% off (from 50%); 'Early' dropped from the sale name. Annual sale prices cut: Pro USD 21→19/mo (USD 250→228/yr, 45%→50% off), AI USD 29→26/mo (USD 342→307/yr, 50%→55% off). Standard unchanged at USD 15/mo (21% off); list prices USD 228/456/684 unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $15, Pro at $21 and AI at $29.
It lands 7 days after KodeKloud's previous change, "KodeKloud launches Early Black Friday sale, cutting annual Pro to $21/mo and AI to $29/mo" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "KodeKloud shrinks annual-plan sale from 50% off to just 21%" (Nov 8, 2026), "KodeKloud cuts annual savings claim from 50% to 21% as Skillfest sale ends" (Oct 19, 2026) and "KodeKloud annual savings shrink to 21% as Autumn sale ends" (Oct 4, 2026). KodeKloud has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
