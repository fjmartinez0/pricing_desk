What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Pipedream's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Free plan no longer lists 100 credits, 1M AI tokens, 3 workflows or 3 accounts; now every feature in development with unlimited team members, production gated to Startup; Basic plan ($29/mo, $348 billed annually; 2,000 credits, 20M AI tokens, 10 active workflows) removed as the page drops its workflow-automation tiers; Advanced plan ($49/mo, $588 billed annually; 50M AI tokens, unlimited workflo. In detail: Connect plan renamed Startup; $99/mo billed annually, 100 external users then $2/user and 10,000 credits unchanged. A $150/mo monthly price is now shown; Advanced plan ($49/mo, $588 billed annually; 50M AI tokens, unlimited workflows, premium apps, GitHub Sync) removed as the page drops its workflow-automation tiers. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed, plan removed, freemium change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Startup at $99.
