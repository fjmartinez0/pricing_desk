What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Less Accounting's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Stated value of the bundled QBO Essentials subscription rose from $75/mo to $85/mo on the $449 and $699 bookkeeping plans; plan prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Simple at $299, Growth at $449 and Business Pro at $699.
It lands 28 days after Less Accounting's previous change, "Less Accounting hikes Business Pro to $699/mo, other tiers steady" (Sep 21, 2026). Less Accounting has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
