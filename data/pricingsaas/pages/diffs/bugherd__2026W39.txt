What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of BugHerd's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: The new Pro tier caps at 5 members, down from the 10-member Studio and 25-member Premium tiers it replaces; Premium-only perks (custom branding, Jira/Asana/ClickUp/Monday integrations, private commenting, client portal, AI auto-tagging) now ship in the cheaper Pro tier, not the discontinued $125/mo Premium plan. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease, feature change) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Standard at $42, Studio at $67, Premium at $125 and Additional Member at $6.6.
It lands 80 days after BugHerd's previous change, "BugHerd: AI features added to Standard & Premium plans" (Jul 2, 2026). BugHerd has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Linear, Trello, Front, Asana and monday.com also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
