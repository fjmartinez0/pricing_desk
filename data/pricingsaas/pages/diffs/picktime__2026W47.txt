What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Picktime's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: SSO (auto-enabled for businesses with 100+ team members) is no longer listed on the Pro plan after the pricing page redesign; Pro's user selector (default 10) now states its range as 10 to 100 users; previously no upper bound was shown, with larger teams directed to support; Class packs, Vouchers and 100+ Zapier integrations on Pro are no longer marked "*Coming soon"; the redesigned page lists t. In detail: Class packs, Vouchers and 100+ Zapier integrations on Pro are no longer marked "*Coming soon"; the redesigned page lists them as included (Pro card: "Class packs, coupons and vouchers"). Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed, feature added, positioning). The current page lists Pro at $2.25 and Starter at $3.
It lands 135 days after Picktime's previous change, "Picktime: Added Reviews feature; Pro swaps Courses for Custom Names + Classpacks" (Jul 4, 2026). Picktime has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
