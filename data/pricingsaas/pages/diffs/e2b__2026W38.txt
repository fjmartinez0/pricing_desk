What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of E2b's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New pricing estimator discloses priced concurrency add-ons: Pro+ (600 concurrent) +$500/mo and Pro++ (1,100 concurrent) +$1,000/mo, replacing the vague unpriced 'extra concurrency' bullet. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $150.
It lands 245 days after E2b's previous change, "E2b Democratizes Multiple vCPU Options for Hobby Tier, Enhancing Accessibility and Flexibility" (Jan 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "E2b Pro Plan Loses Key Features: Prioritized Features and Live Support Removed" (Q2 2025) and "E2b Ultimate Plan Renamed to Enterprise, Signifying Major Branding Shift" (Q1 2025). E2b has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Coursera, Codecademy, Udemy and Pluralsight also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
