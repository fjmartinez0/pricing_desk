What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Digitalocean's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New MicroVMs product added: starting at $0.039/hour billed per second while running; storage $0.05/GiB-month, outbound transfer $0.01/GiB; no compute charges while paused. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan). The current page lists Droplets at $4, Spaces Object Storage at $5, Volumes Block Storage at $10, Cloudways Managed Hosting at $11 and Kubernetes at $12.
It lands 7 days after Digitalocean's previous change, "DigitalOcean launches Secrets Manager add-on at $0.40/secret-month" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Digitalocean launches AI Harness Runtime and Action Gateway pricing tiers" (Sep 27, 2026). Digitalocean has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Aws, Google Cloud and Vultr also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
