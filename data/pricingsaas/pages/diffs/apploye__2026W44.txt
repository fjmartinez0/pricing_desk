What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Apploye's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: New limited-time promo banner: 40% off Power and 30% off Activity Insights for the first 3 months, available till 19 Nov; Starter plan apps & URLs tracked limit lowered from 10 to 8 per person per day (org limit stays 300/day); Leave document upload & policy removed from the free Starter plan (was Limited, now not included); still included on Elite, Power and Enterprise; Expense tracking on the. In detail: Expense tracking on the free Starter plan downgraded from fully included to Limited; paid plans unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added, limit decrease, feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Elite at $4.5, Power at $8, More Screenshots at $2, Video Recording at $4 and Dedicated Onboarding at $150 subscription.
It lands 8 days after Apploye's previous change, "Apploye adds $3 Activity Insights add-on, folds Stealth Mode into Power" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Apploye adds BAA to Enterprise plan, boosting compliance coverage" (Aug 24, 2026). Apploye has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
