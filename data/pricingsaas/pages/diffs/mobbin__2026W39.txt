What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Mobbin's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Finance+ add-on ($399/mo, billed yearly, available on Team & Enterprise plans) now grants access to over 71 apps, up from over 68 apps. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Pro at $10, Team at $16 and Finance+ at $399.
It lands 7 days after Mobbin's previous change, "Mobbin Finance+ add-on expands to 68+ apps, price holds at $399/mo" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mobbin adds Deep Search and MCP to Pro, promotes Enterprise to a full tier card" (Sep 7, 2026), "Mobbin: Finance+ add-on expands to 58 apps (from 55)" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Mobbin: Finance add-on app coverage 54→55 apps" (Jul 27, 2026). Mobbin has been tracked since 2026-02-05.
