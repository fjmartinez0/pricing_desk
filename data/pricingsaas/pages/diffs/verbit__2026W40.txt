Verbit overhauls Legal-tab plans with AI-forward features, drops Scopist add-on

What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Verbit's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: All 4 Legal-tab plan cards (Legal Transcription, Legal Capture, Legal Visor, Custom Plan) had their feature bullet lists fully rewritten, shifting from capability inventories to AI-forward capabilities (voice-ID, AI inconsistency detection, AI search) and dropping prior bullets like Gen.V insights, Mobile App access, and enterprise-security wording; The Scopist Service Add-on ('pricing available. In detail: All 4 Legal-tab plan cards (Legal Transcription, Legal Capture, Legal Visor, Custom Plan) had their feature bullet lists fully rewritten into "Best for" + AI-forward capability lists (voice signature ID, AI inconsistency detection, AI search and summaries), dropping prior bullets like Automatic Gen.V insights, Access to Verbit Mobile App, and Unlimited hours/usage; The Scopist Service Add-on ('pricing available per page') was removed from the Legal Transcription plan card; no longer listed anywhere on the page. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, add-on removed) with a downward direction.
It lands the same day as Verbit's previous change, "Verbit drops Unlimited hours from Legal Capture and Visor plans" (Sep 28, 2026).
