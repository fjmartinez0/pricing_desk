What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of nativeMsg's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: nativeMsg added a new $0 'Free forever' plan: 1 branded RCS agent in test mode, unlimited messages to 5 registered test devices, full builder/campaigns/AI/API access, no card or setup fee. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $165.83, Growth at $665.83, Pro at $2,499.17, Additional Branded Agent at $50 and Additional Seat at $25.
It lands 28 days after nativeMsg's previous change, "nativeMsg: nativeMsg overhauled its pricing model from message-volume tiers to subscriber-count tiers" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "nativeMsg: public self-serve pricing revealed ($1,500-$25K+/mo)" (Jul 13, 2026). nativeMsg has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Telnyx, Google Cloud, Twilio, Plivo and Bird also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
