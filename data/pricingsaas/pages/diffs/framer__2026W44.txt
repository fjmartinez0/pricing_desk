What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Framer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 'External agents' (preview) renamed 'MCP Server' ('Connect your AI tools with Framer'), still free during preview; MCP Server also added to the Free plan card feature list. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $10 and Pro at $30.
It lands 21 days after Framer's previous change, "Framer adds 6 firewall rules to Advanced Hosting; Enterprise gets up to 50" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Framer adds 6 Firewall rules to Advanced Hosting add-on alongside 6 rewrites" (Oct 2, 2026), "Framer discloses 50 firewall rules for Enterprise hosting plans" (Oct 1, 2026) and "Framer doubles first-month AI credits, boosts static upload limit 10x" (Sep 28, 2026). Framer has been tracked since 2026-03-19.
