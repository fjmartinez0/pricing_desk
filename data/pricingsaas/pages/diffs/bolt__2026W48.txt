What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Bolt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Collaborator limits now listed: Free capped at up to 3 collaborators; Pro and Teams list unlimited collaborators. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Pro at $18 and Teams at $27.
This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Bolt caps Free at 3 collaborators, gives Pro and Teams unlimited seats" (Day 321, 2026, saved Nov 17, 2026). It is the same change, not a new one. Before that, Bolt's previous recorded change was "Bolt: Pro adds AI image editing, drops newest-models + MCP access" (Nov 8, 2026). Bolt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
