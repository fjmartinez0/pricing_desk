What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Koala's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Entry plan renamed Essentials to Starter with price up from $9 to $25/month; word allowance rose 15,000 to 45,000/mo, credits 250 to 500/mo, and Brand DNA plus agent messaging (25 msgs/mo) were added. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Starter at $20, Professional at $39, Boost at $79, Growth at $143 and Elite at $280.
It lands 14 days after Koala's previous change, "Koala adds $9 Essentials tier, drops Google Sheets integration" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Koala AI: Essentials tier cut, Boost repackaged with Brand DNA agent + credits" (Aug 31, 2026). Koala has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Notion, Evernote and Bearly AI also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
