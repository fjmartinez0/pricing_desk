What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Financial Cents's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Two limited-time switcher promos (free month on annual, or 10% off monthly; offer ended Oct 30) removed from the pricing page.
