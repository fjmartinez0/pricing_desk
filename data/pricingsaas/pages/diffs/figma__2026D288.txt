What happened
Between the Oct 14, 2026 and Oct 15, 2026 captures of Figma's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Figma Specs (Beta) added to the product lineup, available on paid seats (Full, Dev, Collab) and listed among included products. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Organization at $5, Enterprise at $5 and Professional at $16.
It lands 3 days after Figma's previous change, "Figma agent exits beta, ending free ride as AI credit charges now apply" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Figma drops cross-team publishing from Professional plan spotlight" (Sep 14, 2026), "Figma swaps team-wide publishing for unlimited files on Pro plan" (Sep 10, 2026) and "Figma tweaks Pro/Org highlight bullets, swaps unlimited files for cross-team " (Sep 7, 2026). Figma has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Framer, Webflow and Penpot also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
