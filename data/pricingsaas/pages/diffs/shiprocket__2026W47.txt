What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Shiprocket's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New top banner promo: recharge wallet with Rs 500 and get Rs 1000 credited, code FLAT500, limited period offer on first recharge. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction.
It lands 14 days after Shiprocket's previous change, "Shiprocket ends Rs 500 first-recharge promo that gave Rs 1000 wallet credit" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shiprocket doubles first wallet recharge: pay ₹500, get ₹1000 with FLAT500" (Oct 4, 2026) and "Shiprocket pulls ₹500-off FLAT500 wallet recharge banner from pricing page" (Sep 28, 2026). Shiprocket has been tracked since 2024-04-01.
In the same window BigCommerce, Shopify and Squarespace also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
