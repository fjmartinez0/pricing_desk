What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Dinero's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Dinero drops 'Assistenten' branding from plan bullets: Pro 'Automatic suggestions from the Assistant' becomes 'Accounting tasks collected in one place' (same Assistant task-inbox function); Starter 'Limited advice' and Pro 'Unlimited advice' no longer say 'from the Assistant'. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter+ at DKK 245, Pro at DKK 345 and Total at DKK 545.
It lands 108 days after Dinero's previous change, "Dinero: new top-tier Total plan launched at 545 kr./month" (Jul 2, 2026). Dinero has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Stripe, Shopify and Squarespace also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
