What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Sauce Labs's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Concurrent sessions for parallel testing on Virtual and Real Device Cloud plans reduced from '1 to 24' to '1 to 5'; prices unchanged.
