What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Travelperk's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Default landing shifted from flat platform-fee plans (Starter $0, Premium $99/mo, Pro $299/mo) to Travel+Spend per-active-user pricing (Premium $6/mo, Pro $8/mo, both +3%/booking) with a limited-time rate-lock promo banner. Spend infrastructure changed - Perk-issued cards replaced by bring-your-own-card + direct reimbursement, ERP connector switched from Datev to QuickBooks, and cashback shifted from card-spend (0.5%/1%) to hotel-rebate (3%/4.5%). In detail: Default landing pricing shifted from Travel-only flat fee model (Starter $0, Premium $99/mo, Pro $299/mo) to Travel+Spend per-active-user pricing (Premium $6/mo, Pro $8/mo+3%) with limited-time rate-lock promo; Spend infrastructure changed: Perk-issued cards replaced by bring-your-own-card + direct reimbursement; ERP connector swapped Datev for QuickBooks; cashback shifted to hotel rebates (3%/4.5%). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Premium at $6, Pro at $8 and Starter at 5% of booking value.
It lands 48 days after Travelperk's previous change, "TravelPerk: MCP capability added to feature comparison (all plans)" (Aug 4, 2026). Travelperk has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
