What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of LeadMagic's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Unmetered Search API on Professional/Ultimate now marked 'Preview pricing' (can change or end); Ultimate 'Integration consulting & SLA' becomes 'SLA by written agreement'. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Essential at $82.50, Growth at $207.50, Professional at $415.83, Ultimate at $707.50 and Extra Users at $20.
It lands 7 days after LeadMagic's previous change, "LeadMagic doubles daily export limits and adds paid seats and top-ups" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LeadMagic drops $49 team seat fee, adds rate limits of 300 to 1,500 RPM by plan" (Nov 1, 2026), "LeadMagic now charges per search result, cuts shared seats to 1-3 per plan" (Oct 19, 2026) and "LeadMagic overhauls seats: Basic to 1, Essential's "20" cuts to 3" (Aug 30, 2026). LeadMagic has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Seamless.AI and Lusha also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
