What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Great Question's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Respondent B2C + B2B participant panel add-on added for Self-serve and Enterprise; User Interviews panel add-on now Enterprise-only (dropped from Self-serve); Blind studies renamed concealed identity studies; AI global synthesis replaced by Cross-project synthesis.
