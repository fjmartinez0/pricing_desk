What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Crystal Knows's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Personality Premium 'Hidden gems about you every day' became 'Weekly Insights: new things about you every week' (daily → weekly); in the Goals section (renamed Future), Premium dropped Weekly Hidden Gems and added 'Revise any chapter of your story'; Free adds 'Your Story, free to read'. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Crystal Premium at $40.83 and Premium for Teams at $49.
It lands 14 days after Crystal Knows's previous change, "Crystal Knows: Premium Goals rewritten to weekly-to-yearly planning, Hidden Gems, 5/10/20-Year Visions; Personality adds…" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Crystal Knows drops self-serve Predictions pricing for custom Buyer Profiles" (Oct 5, 2026), "Crystal Knows renames Sales to Predictions, adds $49/seat team plan" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Crystal Knows turns Data API into Personality API with pay-per-profile pricing" (Aug 31, 2026). Crystal Knows has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
In the same window HubSpot and Salesforce also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
