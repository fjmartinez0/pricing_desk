What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of OptinMonster's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New 'MCP Server Access to Create and Manage Campaigns with AI' feature added to the Basic plan's feature list, inherited by Plus, Pro, and Growth via their 'Everything in Basic, AND' structure; New free-trial offer added: 'Try OptinMonster for free' including all Basic features with 500 impression credits, auto-upgrading to Plus when impressions are exceeded. Not present before. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, trial change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $9, Plus at $27, Pro at $37 and Growth at $67.
It lands 28 days after OptinMonster's previous change, "OptinMonster raises promo prices up to 38% as discount shrinks to 40%" (Oct 5, 2026). OptinMonster has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Unbounce, Leadpages, ActiveCampaign and Convertkit also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
