What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Dinero's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Associations (foreninger) section adds 'Automatisk kontingentopkrævning' and 'Flerbrugeradgang' feature highlights; 'Ubegrænset omsætning' reworded to 'Ubegrænsede indtægter'; new revenue-limit explainer. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter+ at DKK 245, Pro at DKK 345 and Total at DKK 545.
It lands 7 days after Dinero's previous change, "Dinero drops Assistant branding from plan bullets, prices unchanged" (Oct 19, 2026). Dinero has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Shopify, Stripe and Squarespace also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
