What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of TradingView's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan cards no longer advertise 'Try free for 30/14 days' or the 'skip trial and pay now' link; CTAs now read 'Start now'. Compare table CTAs still say 'Try free'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essential at $12.95, Plus at $29.95, Premium at $59.95 and Ultimate at $199.95.
It lands the same day as TradingView's previous change, "TradingView drops 30-day trial messaging from plan cards for Start now" (Oct 5, 2026). TradingView has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
In the same window Robinhood, NinjaTrader and Interactive Brokers also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
