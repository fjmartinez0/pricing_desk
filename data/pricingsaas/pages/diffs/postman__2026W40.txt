What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Postman's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Context Graph rows added to comparison table — free ingestion/24hr refresh on all plans; metered API queries at 1,000/mo free on Free tier and 5,000/mo on Solo/Team/Enterprise, then $0.01/query overage on all plans. In detail: New Context Graph usage rows added to the comparison table: free ingestion/24hr refresh on all plans, plus metered API queries (1,000/mo free tier, 5,000/mo on paid tiers) with $0.01/query overage. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Solo at $9, Team at $19, Enterprise at $0.04 per AI credit + $0.04 per VuH (cloud performance testing) and Simple Security at $6.
It lands 5 days after Postman's previous change, "Postman adds free Context Graph tracking, metered API query overages" (Sep 23, 2026).
