What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of TeamViewer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free trial length extended from 14 days to 30 days across TeamViewer products (header CTA and product cards). In detail: Add-on free trial length extended from 14 days to 30 days (DEX Essentials on plan cards, plus Asset Management, Endpoint Protection, Device Monitoring, TeamViewer Assist AR Lite and Mobile Device Management add-ons). Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists TeamViewer Remote Access at $24.9, TeamViewer Business at $50.9, TeamViewer Premium at $120.9 and TeamViewer Corporate at $245.9.
It lands 28 days after TeamViewer's previous change, "TeamViewer cuts add-on free trials from 30 days to 14 across all six" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "TeamViewer slashes Premium and Corporate plans by 6.6% yearly billing" (Jul 20, 2026). TeamViewer has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
In the same window AnyDesk, Zoho, Microsoft and GoTo also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
