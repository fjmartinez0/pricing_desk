What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Channel.io's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Business and Enterprise plans now list White-glove onboarding and AI setup support; ISMS-ready badge removed from Business plan. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Startup at $200, Business at $990, Basic Seat at $3, Operator Seat at $30 and AI Seat at $10.
It lands 14 days after Channel.io's previous change, "Channel.io: New Business plan at $990/mo (contact sales, $10M+ annual revenue) with $500 monthly credits, enhanced AI…" (Oct 19, 2026). Channel.io has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Intercom and LiveChat also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
