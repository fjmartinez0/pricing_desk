What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Saturn Cloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added a new pay-per-token Serverless Endpoints pricing model for 11 named open models plus size-tiered pricing for other open models, alongside unchanged dedicated GPU-hour pricing. In detail: Added a new Serverless Endpoints pay-per-token pricing model alongside existing dedicated GPU-hour pricing: 11 named open models priced per 1M input/output tokens (from $0.0125/1M input), plus size-tiered pricing for other open models (from $0.025/1M tokens). Dedicated GPU-hour prices (L40S $1.10 to GB300 priced per deal) are unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change). The current page lists Dedicated GPU Capacity at $1.10 per NVIDIA L40S GPU-hour + $1.25 per NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 GPU-hour and Serverless Endpoints at $75 per 100M input tokens — Kimi K3 (moonshotai/Kimi-K3) + $375 per 100M output tokens — Kimi K3 (moonshotai/Kimi-K3).
It lands 7 days after Saturn Cloud's previous change, "Saturn Cloud launches dedicated NVIDIA GPUs from $1.10/hr, B300 at $6.65" (Nov 8, 2026). Saturn Cloud has been tracked since 2026-05-02.
In the same window Comet, Google and Prefect also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
