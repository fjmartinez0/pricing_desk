Rydoo swaps Smart Audit for Rydoo Intelligence AI agents on Enterprise
Verified Oct 19, 2026·Finance & Accounting·Direction: neutral
The changeEnterprise feature — feature change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 12, 2026 → Oct 19, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Rydoo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan's AI feature evolved from 'Rydoo Smart Audit' (automatic AI analysis of expenses) to 'Rydoo Intelligence' (AI agents/assistants handling expense questions before spend and audit after it). Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essentials at EUR 8 and Pro at GBP 10.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Rydoo. Rydoo has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
