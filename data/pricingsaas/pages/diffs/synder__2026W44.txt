What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Synder's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Stripe credit notes' row added to the feature comparison table (self-serve Stripe credit-note sync), checked on the higher-tier plans only.
