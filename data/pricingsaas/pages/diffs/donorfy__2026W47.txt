What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Donorfy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan card no longer lists Dedicated Relationship Manager and Priority Support bullets (gone from the page outside the separate paid support plans). Private Cloud bullet also cut from the card but still listed under Enterprise Hosting in the feature list. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed).
It lands 21 days after Donorfy's previous change, "Donorfy drops £30/mo Sandboxes add-on, reveals Standard/Premier support pricing" (Oct 26, 2026). Donorfy has been tracked since 2026-05-20.
In the same window Bloomerang, Neon, Salesforce and GiveWP also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
