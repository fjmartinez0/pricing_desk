What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Blackbox AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise volume-discount messaging removed: 'volume discounts' bullet, 'discount on every token' hero copy, 'rate improves as committed spend grows' note, and the 'Why is the open-weight discount bigger?'
