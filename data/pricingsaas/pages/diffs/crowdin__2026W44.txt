What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Crowdin's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Crowdin (self-serve Free/Pro/Team) section tagline changed from 'For individuals and teams' to 'For indie developers and crowdsourcing'; section now listed below Crowdin Enterprise; 'Calculate your price' calculator removed; it let users add managers and hosted words to Pro/Team/Team+/Business plans and see total cost. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed, positioning) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $50, Team at $150, Team+ at $450 and CDN for Translations at $3 per 1M requests + $2 per 10GB data transfer.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Crowdin. Crowdin has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window X and Lokalise also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
