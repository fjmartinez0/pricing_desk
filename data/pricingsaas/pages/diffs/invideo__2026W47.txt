What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of InVideo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Max annual price cut from $250 to $75/seat/mo (list $150 struck through, new 50% OFF badge); Save $885 vs monthly replaces 'No difference'; promo model list swaps Ideogram 4.5 for Seedream 5.5; Max monthly credits cut from 8,000 to 5,000; Nano Banana Pro generations 3,200 to 2,000; Seedance 2 videos 210 to 131. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease, limit lowered) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $10, Plus at $30, Team at $47, Max at $75 and Scale at $107.
It lands 14 days after InVideo's previous change, "InVideo: Max plan price jumps $75→$250/seat as discount ends" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "InVideo halves annual Starter to $10/seat, cuts Seedance video allowances" (Oct 11, 2026), "InVideo cuts annual Plus and Max prices up to 25% as Seedance credits jump" (Oct 4, 2026) and "InVideo: 10x storage capacity increase + free guest seats added" (Sep 28, 2026).
