What happened
Between the Nov 27, 2026 and Nov 28, 2026 captures of Attio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Ask Attio allowance changed from qualitative usage tiers (Limited, More, 3x Plus, 5x Plus) to explicit credits: 200 / 1000 / 3000 / 5000 across Free, Plus, Pro, Ultra. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change). The current page lists Plus at $40, Pro at $100, Ultra at $1,000 and Governance Add-on at $30.
It lands one day after Attio's previous change, "Attio drops fixed Ask Attio credit counts for relative usage tiers across plans" (Nov 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Attio ditches per-seat pricing for workspace plans, adds $1,000 Ultra tier" (Nov 15, 2026) and "Attio shifts to per-workspace pricing, adds $1,000/mo Ultra plan" (Nov 11, 2026). Attio has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
In the same window Close, HubSpot, Notion, Salesforce and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
