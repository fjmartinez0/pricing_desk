What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Next-Cart's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Included-in-every-plan list reworded: unlimited re-migration of recent data now capped by plan data limit; 24/7 technical support becomes one-on-one help with connections and setup. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard - Basic at $39 subscription, Custom - Basic at $39 subscription, Standard - Basic+ at $59 subscription, Custom - Basic+ at $59 subscription and Standard - Growth at $89 subscription.
It lands 63 days after Next-Cart's previous change, "Next-Cart: Full pricing model overhaul to tiered Entity Points plans" (Aug 24, 2026). Next-Cart has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
