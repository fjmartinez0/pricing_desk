What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Mixpanel's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free plan saved reports limit raised from 5 per seat to Unlimited; Free plan custom properties changed from Not available to Limited (can create but not save for reuse). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised, feature added). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 17, 2026) lists Growth at $100.
It lands 28 days after Mixpanel's previous change, "Mixpanel cuts Growth yearly price to $100/month at 18M events a year" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mixpanel touts free Experiments and Feature Flags in new banner" (Sep 7, 2026), "Experiments & Feature Flags Move from Add-on to Included Usage" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Mixpanel: Agent query caps removed + AI-Powered Data Governance added" (Aug 24, 2026). Mixpanel has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Amplitude, Hotjar and Google also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
