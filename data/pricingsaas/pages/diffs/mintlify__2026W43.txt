What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Mintlify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Trial offer changed from 'first month of credits on us' to a 14-day Pro trial with 5,000 credits, no credit card required. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $450.
It lands 6 days after Mintlify's previous change, "Mintlify swaps free credits for a 14-day Pro trial with 5,000 credits" (Oct 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mintlify ends free 6-month Pro offer for early-stage startups" (Oct 3, 2026), "Mintlify offers startups 6 months of Pro free, no card needed" (Oct 1, 2026) and "Mintlify adds WebMCP support and Narration, both locked out of Starter" (Sep 28, 2026). Mintlify has been tracked since 2024-10-21.
