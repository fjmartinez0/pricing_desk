What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Avalara's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Avalara relaunched its pricing page as "Avalara Select": the opaque contact-sales bundle table (compliance package "starts at $699", separate $119 license guidance, $403/location registration) was replaced by two self-serve tiers priced per state - Core Compliance at $79/state/month ($799/state/year) and Core Compliance + SST Services at $69/state/month ($699/state/year) - plus a Custom contact-sa. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Sales Tax Registration at $403 and Business License Guidance at $119.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Avalara. Avalara has been tracked since 2026-05-04.
