What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Seedance's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan starter credits doubled from 6 to 12 (now 3 Seedance 2 Mini 480p 5s clips instead of 1, or 12 Nano Banana 2 Lite images). Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Mini Plan at $28, Standard Plan at $56 and Plus Plan at $112.
It lands 7 days after Seedance's previous change, "Seedance: Free plan starter credits cut from 12 to 6, so free accounts get 1 Seedance 2 Mini clip (was 3) or 6 images…" (Oct 26, 2026). Seedance has been tracked since 2026-10-18.
In the same window Gemelo, Writer, Voxel51, Wispr Flow and Hive also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
