What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of vPlan's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New paid 'Training en diensten' (training & services) section added: Planning-APK assessment at €199 for 2 hours, Admin/user/key-user training starting at €540, plus custom 'Optimalisatie op maat' optimization on request.
