What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Evoto's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Asset Hub Membership included as a new benefit across Starter, Basic, Basic Plus, Standard, and Standard Plus annual plans. In detail: Added 'Asset Hub Membership' as an included benefit across all annual subscription plans (Starter, Basic, Basic Plus, Standard, Standard Plus). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Starter Plan at $7.42, Basic Plan at $12.42, Basic Plus Plan at $20.17, Standard Plan at $43.42 and 200 Credits Pack at $49 subscription.
It lands 28 days after Evoto's previous change, "Evoto: new Enterprise contact-sales tier added" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Evoto: 7-Day Free Trial added for Starter/Basic/Basic Plus plans" (Aug 3, 2026) and "Evoto: Peak Season discount cut from up to 25% to flat 10%, promo bundle removed" (Jul 27, 2026). Evoto has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
