Slack pricing FAQ
How much does Slack cost?
Slack pricing starts at $7.25/user/mo on Pro, billed annually. Slack lists 4 plans on its pricing page; the Free plan is free.
Does Slack have a free plan?
Yes. Slack's Free plan is free; paid plans start at $7.25/user/mo on Pro, billed annually.
What pricing model does Slack use?
Slack uses subscription pricing across 4 plans, with a free tier.
When did Slack last change its pricing?
Nov 8, 2026: Slack hides Enterprise+ pricing, drops $45/user price for contact sales.
How often has Slack changed its pricing?
Pulse has recorded 21 pricing changes for Slack since it started tracking on Nov 30, 2023; the pricing page was last captured on Nov 9, 2026.
