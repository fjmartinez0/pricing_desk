What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Trellus's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Power's 'Superhuman for LinkedIn' relabeled 'LinkedIn inbox for Sales Navigator'; Power + Parallel 'Multi line dialing' relabeled 'Parallel dialing: multiple outbound attempts at once'. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Power at $99.99 and Power + Parallel at $149.99.
Trellus's previous recorded change was "Trellus renames Parallel plan to Power + Parallel, price unchanged at $149.99/mo" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Trellus adds dialing limit disclosures across Power, Parallel, Business plans" (Aug 17, 2026). Trellus has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window Intercom, Salesmotion, Pipedrive, Onpipeline and MeetRecord also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
