What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of PubNub's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan now allowed for live/production apps (was development & testing only; FAQ: 'small live apps on Production keysets'); Starter copy shifts from launch to growing apps. 200 MAU limit and $98 Starter price unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change). The current page lists Starter at $98.
It lands 7 days after PubNub's previous change, "PubNub puts Serverless Functions on plans: 5 free, 30 on Starter" (Nov 9, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "PubNub drops Serverless Functions from Free and Starter plan cards" (Nov 1, 2026), "PubNub adds Serverless Functions to plans: 30 on Starter, 5 test functions free" (Oct 11, 2026) and "PubNub axes Serverless Functions from Free and Starter plan features" (Sep 28, 2026). PubNub has been tracked since 2026-05-13.
In the same window Aws, Supabase and Twilio also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
