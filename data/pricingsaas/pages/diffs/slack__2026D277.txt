What happened
Between the Oct 3, 2026 and Oct 4, 2026 captures of Slack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise+ now shows a public price of $45 USD per user/month (billed annually) instead of 'Contact sales for pricing'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 20, 2026) lists Pro at $7.25, Business+ at $15 and Enterprise+ at $45.
It lands 180 days after Slack's previous change, "Slack adds CRM features with Salesforce lead and deal tracking" (Apr 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Slack Launches Slackbot AI Agent Across All Plans Including Free" (Week 3, 2026), "Slack Introduces AI Writing Assistance and Conditional Branching for Business+ and Enterprise+ Plans" (Q3 2025) and "Slack Introduces Advanced AI Tier in Business+ Plan, Enhancing AI Capabilities Across All Pricing Plans" (Q2 2025). Slack has been tracked since 2023-11-30.
In the same window Microsoft, Mattermost, Zoom and Google also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
