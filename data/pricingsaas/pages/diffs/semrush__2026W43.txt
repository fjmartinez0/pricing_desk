What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Semrush's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: SEO + AI Search Advanced plan price ($455.67/mo billed annually, $549 monthly) replaced with Custom pricing and a Request demo CTA; Advanced AI Visibility prompts to track daily 200 to Unlimited; websites 40 to 40+ and keywords 5,000 to 5,000+. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden, limit increase) with a downward direction. The current page lists SEO at $117.33, Starter at $165.17, Pro+ at $248.17, Advanced at $549 and AI Visibility Base at $99.
It lands 21 days after Semrush's previous change, "Semrush launches $0 Free plan with 10 reports a day, no credit card needed" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Semrush: Enterprise plan pitch overhauled with 11 new capabilities" (Aug 11, 2026). Semrush has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Google, Ahrefs, Moz and Similarweb also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
