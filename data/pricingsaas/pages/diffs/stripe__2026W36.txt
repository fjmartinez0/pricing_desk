What happened
Between the Aug 24, 2026 and Aug 31, 2026 captures of Stripe's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Metronome Startup plan moved from $100k included billing allotment (10M events) to $0.04/1K ingest events + 0.8% of billing volume; Custom plan now discloses data export pricing ($0.03/$0.05 per 1K). New Stripe Reader T600 hardware added to Terminal card readers at $329.00. In detail: Metronome Startup plan moved from a $100,000 included billing allotment (10M usage events) to metered pricing: $0.04 per 1K ingest events + 0.8% of billing volume. Custom plan now discloses data export pricing ($0.03 per 1K standard, $0.05 per 1K premium); New Stripe Reader T600 hardware device added to the Terminal card reader lineup at $329.00. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Standard at 1.5% surcharge for international cards + $0.3 per successful transaction.
It lands 4 days after Stripe's previous change, "Stripe launches T600 hardware reader at $329, joining M2, S710, S700 lineup" (Aug 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Stripe kills Metronome Startup's included allotment, adds Data export pricing" (Aug 27, 2026), "Stripe: Radar restructured to Pay Monthly/Pay As You Go plans + Radar Lite" (Aug 4, 2026) and "Stripe: Meters API footnote now discloses 100M events/month quota" (Jul 20, 2026). Stripe has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
