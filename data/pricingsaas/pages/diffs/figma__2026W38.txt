What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Figma's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Professional plan's highlighted Key Features swapped 'Publish resources across teams' for 'Unlimited files and projects', dropping cross-team publishing as a called-out Professional benefit. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Organization at $5, Enterprise at $5 and Professional at $16.
It lands 4 days after Figma's previous change, "Figma swaps team-wide publishing for unlimited files on Pro plan" (Sep 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Figma tweaks Pro/Org highlight bullets, swaps unlimited files for cross-team " (Sep 7, 2026), "Figma echoes Professional plan, drops "Unlimited teams" for org-wide publishing" (Sep 4, 2026) and "Figma refreshes Professional plan card copy" (Sep 3, 2026). Figma has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Framer, Penpot, Webflow, Sketch and Adobe also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
