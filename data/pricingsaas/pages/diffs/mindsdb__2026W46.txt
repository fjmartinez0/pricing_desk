What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New model Claude Haiku 5.5 (Anthropic) added to the price table at $0.10 input / $0.50 output / $0.01 cached / $0.125 cache write per 1M tokens, alongside Haiku 4.5 ($1.00/$5.00). Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pay as you go at $0.10 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.50 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands 2 days after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB adds Claude Haiku 5.5 at $0.10 input, $0.50 output per 1M tokens" (Nov 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB adds Kimi K3.1 Pro, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, GPT-6.1 Terra pricing" (Nov 2, 2026), "MindsDB adds GPT-6.1 Terra pricing at $1 input, $5 output per 1M tokens" (Oct 29, 2026) and "MindsDB adds Kimi K3.1 Pro and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model pricing" (Oct 27, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
