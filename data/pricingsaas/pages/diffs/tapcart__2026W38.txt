What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Tapcart's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: GROWTH plan's AI-powered App Studio feature was relabeled from 'AI Agent App Studio' to 'App studio AI editing', shifting emphasis from an autonomous agent to an AI editing capability; All three plans (GROWTH, SCALE, ENTERPRISE +) changed their card CTA button from 'Get Started' to 'Talk to Sales', shifting the signup motion from self-serve to sales-led. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $200, Ultimate at $400 and AI at $250.
It lands 63 days after Tapcart's previous change, "Tapcart reframes Growth, Scale, Enterprise+ taglines around benefits, not aud" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Tapcart: full tier overhaul — CORE→Growth $250→$500, ULTIMATE→Scale $500→$1,250" (Jul 6, 2026). Tapcart has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Shopify and Squarespace also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
