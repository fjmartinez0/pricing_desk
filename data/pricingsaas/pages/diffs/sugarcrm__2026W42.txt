What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of SugarCRM's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Plans renamed to Standard+/Advanced+/Premier+ with price hikes ($59->$68, $85->$98, $135->$155); 15-user minimum removed; new Precision+ (Sugar Predict) tier & $20/mo Sugar Connect+ add-on added; Legacy features (Lead/Quote/Activity mgmt, Sugar Intelligence) condensed; new AI features (AI Summaries, Sugar Cube, Smart Prompts, Forecasting) added to Advanced+; Predict unbundled into Precision+ tier. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard+ at $68, Enterprise at $85, Advanced+ at $98, Premier+ at $155 and Sugar Connect+ at $20.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for SugarCRM. SugarCRM has been tracked since 2026-05-26.
