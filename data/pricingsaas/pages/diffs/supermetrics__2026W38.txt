What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Supermetrics's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Automated data refresh (Weekly/Daily/On-demand frequency by plan) and Dashboards removed as listed capabilities from the Starter, Growth, and Enterprise plan cards and from the full feature comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Starter at $44 and Growth at $177.
It lands 76 days after Supermetrics's previous change, "Supermetrics cuts Growth plan add-on price to $149/year" (Jun 30, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Supermetrics: Starter price hike $37->$44/mo + new per-unit Extras upsells" (Jul 4, 2026). Supermetrics has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Amplitude, Mixpanel, Google, DAT Freight & Analytics and Tableau also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
