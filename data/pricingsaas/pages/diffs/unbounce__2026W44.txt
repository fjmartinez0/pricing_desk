What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Unbounce's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: AI credits allowances introduced on all plans: Starter 4,000, Build 30,000, Experiment 40,000, Optimize 80,000, Concierge from 200,000, Agency from 100,000. Prices unchanged; Build plan no longer lists popups/sticky bars and multi-step forms; scheduling now pages only; Drag-and-drop builder became Hybrid builder (NEW); AI copywriting listed under Starter. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed, feature added) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 28, 2026) lists Starter at $22, Build at $74, Experiment at $112 and Optimize at $187.
It lands 70 days after Unbounce's previous change, "Unbounce adds MCP server support to Starter plan for AI-driven page building" (Aug 17, 2026). Unbounce has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Leadpages, Convertkit and Builder.io also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
