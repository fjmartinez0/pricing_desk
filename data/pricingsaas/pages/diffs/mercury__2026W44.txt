What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Mercury's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added Mercury Payroll add-on at $100/mo plus $50 per person paid per month, waived until Dec 31, 2026, alongside the existing Mercury Books add-on. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Mercury Plus at $29.9, Mercury Pro at $299 and Mercury Personal at $20.
It lands 11 days after Mercury's previous change, "Mercury adds Priority Support to Plus and Pro plans at no extra cost" (Oct 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mercury drops $100 Amazon gift card from Plus and Pro Xero offer" (Oct 5, 2026), "Mercury drops $100 Amazon gift card and free Xero perk from Plus and Pro" (Oct 2, 2026) and "Mercury sweetens Xero bonus with $100 gift card, drops Invoicing API from paid plans" (Sep 7, 2026). Mercury has been tracked since 2026-05-12.
In the same window Stripe and Squarespace also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
