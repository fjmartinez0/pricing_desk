What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Linktree's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Plan feature lists reworked: Starter gains unlimited featured links; Pro gets 0% sell-and-earn fees and custom designs; Premium swaps affiliate shop/dynamic links for link rules, AI integrations, all-time analytics; Free plan no longer lists selling products, courses and brand offers; card now shows email and phone sign-ups instead. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change, feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Starter at $6, Pro at $12 and Premium at $30.
It lands 94 days after Linktree's previous change, "Linktree: removed Social Planning + Scheduling from all plans" (Jul 3, 2026). Linktree has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Buffer and Leadpages also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
