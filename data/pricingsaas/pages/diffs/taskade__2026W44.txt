What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Taskade's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Extra Credits ($5/pack; $5/9k, $20/45k, $50/125k) and Custom Domains ($5/year, from $4.99 first year) add-on cards removed; credit packs and in-app domain purchase still offered but prices no longer shown on the pricing page; Pro gains up to 10 custom domains (was Business+ only; comparison table Pro ✗ → 10); Version history row added to comparison (Free 7 days / Pro 1 year / Business+ 5 years). Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price hidden) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $10, Business at $25, Max at $100, Enterprise at $250 and Extra Credits at $5 per 9,000-credit pack (one-time) + $20 per 45,000-credit pack (one-time).
It lands 15 days after Taskade's previous change, "Taskade drops Claude below Enterprise, ends free credits, lists $250/mo tier" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Taskade adds $5 Extra Credits and Custom Domains add-ons, doubles Free seats" (Sep 14, 2026), "Taskade doubles Free plan seats to 2, swaps Gemini for DeepSeek" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Taskade clarifies free-tier limits: "Keep 3 apps," one-time 6,000 AI credits" (Aug 17, 2026). Taskade has been tracked since 2024-10-14.
In the same window Notion, ClickUp, Asana and Trello also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
