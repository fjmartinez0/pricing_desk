What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Wiz's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Wiz consolidated five separate product licenses (Wiz Cloud, Wiz Code, Wiz Defend, Wiz Sensor, Wiz Go Bundle for SMBs) into three unified offerings (Wiz One, Wiz Go, A la Carte) on the pricing-request form. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Wiz. Wiz has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
