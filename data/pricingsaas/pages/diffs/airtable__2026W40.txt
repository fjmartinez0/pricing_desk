What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Airtable's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added a new 'Automations per base' cap of 50 to the plan comparison table across Free, Team, Business, and Enterprise Scale — a per-base automation limit not previously shown, alongside the existing monthly automation run limits. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction.
