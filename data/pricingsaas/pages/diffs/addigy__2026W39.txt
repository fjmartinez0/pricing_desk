What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Addigy's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: iOS Management plan (standalone card for iPhone/iPad/Apple TV management) removed from the plan selector. New "Addigy Intelligence Suite" tier added between Apple MDM and Security Suite with AI-powered features (Script Assist, Smart Filters, MCP with 240+ tools); Security Suite redefined to include Intelligence functionality on top of MDM. In detail: Addigy removed the standalone iOS Management plan card (zero-touch enrollment, VPP/Apps & Books licensing, iOS/iPadOS update management, MDM policy enforcement, device inventory) from the plan selector; Added new AI-powered 'Addigy Intelligence Suite' tier between Apple MDM and Security Suite; Security Suite now includes Intelligence functionality (previously just Apple MDM). Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed, package change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Apple MDM at $8.25 and Security Suite at $16.
It lands 80 days after Addigy's previous change, "Addigy: hid MSP/add-on pricing + launched Addigy Boost training program" (Jul 2, 2026). Addigy has been tracked since 2026-02-25.
In the same window Microsoft and Jamf also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
