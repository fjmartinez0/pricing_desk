What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of HeyGen's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro adds Seedance 2.5 access, Cinematic Avatars, VEO 3.1/Nano Banana and 16+ hrs text-to-speech; Creator adds Avatar V (limited), Video Agent and HeyGen Video 1. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Creator at $24, Pro at $41 and Business at $119.
It lands 3 days after HeyGen's previous change, "HeyGen adds Voice TTS and Seedance 2.5 to Creator and Pro, prices flat" (Oct 23, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HeyGen: Voice Cloning entitlement softened (Creator + Pro)" (Sep 21, 2026), "HeyGen caps Voice Cloning usage on Creator, Pro, and Business plans" (Sep 19, 2026) and "HeyGen rebrands Digital Twin as Video Avatar across all plans" (Sep 7, 2026). HeyGen has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
