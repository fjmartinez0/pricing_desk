Supernova splits flat usage pricing into tiers, adds $9K Pilot Program
Verified Sep 14, 2026·Data & Analytics·Direction: neutral
The changeSelf-serve, AI Data Team, Enterprise packaging — package change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 7, 2026 → Sep 14, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Supernova's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Single flat usage-based pricing model split into a tiered structure: Self-serve (usage-based), AI Data Team ($10,000/mo incl. $2,000 usage), Enterprise (custom pricing), and a new $9,000 Pilot Program. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists AI Data Team at $10,000, Self-serve at $0.1 GB egress per month + $0.15 per GB hour and Pilot Program at $9,000 subscription.
It lands the same day as Supernova's previous change, "Supernova ditches flat metered pricing for tiered plans up to $10K/mo" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Supernova: tiered plans replaced with usage-based metering" (Aug 24, 2026). Supernova has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
