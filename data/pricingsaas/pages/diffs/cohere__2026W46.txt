Cohere moves Model Vault to per-GPU pricing, A100 from $2.25/hr promo

What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Cohere's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Cohere restructured Model Vault dedicated-hosting pricing from a per-model table (Embed 5 Fast/Pro, Rerank 5 Fast/Pro, Parse 5 — $3-7/hr or $2,000-4,300/mo per instance) to a per-GPU-type table (L4, A100, MI300X, H100, H200, B200) with on-demand hourly price per GPU plus a promotional hourly rate through March 31 2027 (e.g. A100 40GB: $3.25 on-demand, $2.25 promo). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Embed 5 Fast at $0.08 Input tokens, Embed 5 Pro at $0.12 Input tokens, Parse 5 at $1.50 Pages, Command R7B at $0.15 Output tokens + $3.75 Input tokens (per 100M tokens) and Rerank 5 Fast at $0.02 Rerank 5 tokens (per 1M tokens).
It lands 5 days after Cohere's previous change, "Cohere moves Model Vault to per-GPU pricing, $1-$12.50/hr from L4 to GB200" (Nov 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cohere: Model Vault Rerank 4 Fast/Pro Medium renamed Rerank 5 and cut from $5.00/hr ($3,250/mo) to $4.00/hr…" (Oct 26, 2026), "Cohere cuts Model Vault Embed pricing 25% with new Embed 5 Fast and Pro tiers" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Cohere cuts Model Vault Small to $3/hr as Embed 5 replaces Embed 4" (Oct 1, 2026). Cohere has been tracked since 2024-12-01.
