ContentDrips removes App-vs-API pricing clarification note from plans
Verified Sep 28, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral
The changeUltimate positioning — positioning.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 21, 2026 → Sep 28, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of ContentDrips's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed the note clarifying that the visible plan cards are Contentdrips App pricing, along with its link to separate API pricing, from below the Ultimate plan card. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Starter at $15, Pro at $29 and Ultimate at $52.
It lands 7 days after ContentDrips's previous change, "ContentDrips adds full API/MCP access to Pro, 25K calls on Ultimate" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ContentDrips drops Free plan, shifts to $15 entry tier + new $52 Ultimate" (Sep 14, 2026) and "ContentDrips: Starter swaps Custom Ideas Generator for GIF/MP4 export" (Jul 20, 2026). ContentDrips has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window HubSpot, Mailchimp and Convertkit also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
