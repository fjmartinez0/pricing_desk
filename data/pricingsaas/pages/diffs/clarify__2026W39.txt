What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Clarify's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Growth plan's record and object capacity changed from unlimited to fixed caps: 750k records and 10 objects, down from 'Unlimited records and custom objects'. SSO & SAML moved from Growth to Enterprise-only. Growth gained Warehouse access & Delegated sending; Starter gained LinkedIn integration; Free gained call recorder & notetaker. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease, feature change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $40 and Growth at $1,000.
It lands 14 days after Clarify's previous change, "Clarify rebuilds pricing: Growth revealed at $1,000/mo, comparison table gone" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Clarify adds team-size calculator unveiling $300-600/mo Starter+ tier" (Jul 27, 2026) and "Clarify adds Custom Agents to Free, Starter, and Growth plans" (Jul 2, 2026). Clarify has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
In the same window Salesforce, Microsoft, HubSpot, Zoho and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
