What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Inoreader's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Billing FAQ: refunds now cover monthly and annual purchases within 7 days (was annual only); upgrades start a full new billing period with unused value credited (was prorated price difference); invoices and EU VAT exemption only on Pro and higher, Supporter gets receipts and always pays VAT. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $9.99.
It lands 13 days after Inoreader's previous change, "Inoreader adds article edit history to track post-publication changes" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Inoreader swaps Pocket for Raindrop.io and Readwise in save-to export list" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Inoreader: adds Mentions AI feature to Pro tier" (Sep 21, 2026). Inoreader has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
