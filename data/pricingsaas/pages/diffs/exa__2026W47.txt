What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Exa's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Search product configurable latency improved from 450ms-1s to 300ms-1s. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Search at $7 per 1k requests, up to 10 results + $1 per 1k page AI summaries, Agent at $0.01 per search tool call + $0.10 per request (Medium effort), Contents at $1 per 1k pages, per content type + $1 per 1k page AI summaries, Deep Search at $12 per 1k requests, up to 10 results + $1 per 1k requests, additional result above 10 and Monitors at $1 per 1k requests, additional result above 10 + $1 per 1k page AI summaries.
It lands 7 days after Exa's previous change, "Exa slashes Monitors API price 87% to $2, adds $12K/yr Enterprise tier" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Exa drops Deep-Reasoning Search tier and renames Instant to Lite Search" (Oct 26, 2026), "Exa launches $4/1k Instant Search, cutting Search entry price from $7" (Oct 4, 2026) and "Exa: Free plan first-month credits cut from $30 to $20" (Sep 27, 2026). Exa has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window You.com and Google also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
