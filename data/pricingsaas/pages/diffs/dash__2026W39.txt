What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Dash's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Top-of-page promotional banner offering 50% off for switching to Dash between 7-14 September was removed; the four plan prices, storage, and download limits are unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Start Up at $98, Small Team at $215, Growing Brand at $350 and Established Brand at $530.
It lands 7 days after Dash's previous change, "Dash launches Black Friday-style 50% off promo banner (Sep 7–14)" (Sep 14, 2026). Dash has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
