What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Plooto's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: ACH payment fee rose from $0.50 to $1 on Grow and Pro; CRA payment fees rose from $5/$3/$3 to $7/$5/$5 on Go/Grow/Pro. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Go at CAD 9, Grow at CAD 32 and Pro at CAD 99.
It lands 114 days after Plooto's previous change, "Plooto: Pay by Card transaction limit increased $10,000 → $30,000" (Jul 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Plooto triples Pay by Card limit to $30K across all plans" (Jun 22, 2026), "Plooto Raises Credit Card Fees to 2.9-3.2%, Adds Amex Surcharge Tier" (Apr 3, 2026) and "Plooto Increases Paper Cheque Fees by Up to 200% While Adding New Features and Enhancements Across Plans" (Q1 2025). Plooto has been tracked since 2023-12-19.
In the same window Xero, Freshbooks, Zoho, Quickbooks and Stripe also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
