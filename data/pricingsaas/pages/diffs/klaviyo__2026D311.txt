What happened
Between the Nov 6, 2026 and Nov 7, 2026 captures of Klaviyo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Paid plans now shown: Email $20/mo and Marketing $25/mo with limited-time first-month $1 offer (up to 5,000 active profiles); previously only free plan and Build a plan shown. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Email at $20, Marketing at $25, Customer Hub at $4, Mobile messaging at $0.01 per SMS (US, Toll free) + $0.04 per MMS (US, Toll free) and Reviews at $25.
It lands 59 days after Klaviyo's previous change, "Klaviyo swaps free Composer credits for $5 usage cap, adds AI Helpdesk" (Sep 9, 2026). Klaviyo has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp and Convertkit also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
