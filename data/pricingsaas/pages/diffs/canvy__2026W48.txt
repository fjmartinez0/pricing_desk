What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Canvy's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: QR code limit removed: Free (10) and Plus (50) now get unlimited QR codes, matching Pro; New 'Scan stats history' row in plan comparison: 7 days on Free, 30 days on Plus, Unlimited on Pro. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised, feature added).
It lands 14 days after Canvy's previous change, "Canvy details QR code limits: Free 10, Plus 50, Pro unlimited, plus scan alerts" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Canvy halves Pro plan to $16/mo in limited-time promo, drops 24/7 support" (Nov 1, 2026). Canvy has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
