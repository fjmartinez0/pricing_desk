What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of QuickNode's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Comparison table adds security rows (Blocked IPs, Block range limits, Request filters), Observability (Dashboard, Retention 24h to 90d, CSV/JSON Export) and SQL Explorer platform feature. In detail: Comparison table adds security rows (Blocked IPs and Block range limits on all plans incl. Free trial; Request filters Enterprise-only), a new Observability section (Dashboard on all plans; Retention 24h Free trial/Build, 7d Accelerate, 30d Scale/Business, 90d Enterprise; CSV/JSON Export Enterprise-only) and an SQL Explorer platform feature row (Build and above). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Build at $34, Accelerate at $212, Scale at $424 and Business at $849.
It lands 48 days after QuickNode's previous change, "QuickNode: trace_transaction API credit rate up 20x (2 to 40/request)" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "QuickNode cuts Build plan to $34/mo via 30% promo, saving $176/yr" (Aug 17, 2026) and "QuickNode: Accelerate plan overage rate increased ($0.55→$0.56/1M)" (Aug 5, 2026). QuickNode has been tracked since 2026-05-04.
