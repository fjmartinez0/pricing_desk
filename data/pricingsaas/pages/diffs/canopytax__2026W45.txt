What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Canopy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Tax Workflow Automation power-up bullets reworded: 'AI powered intake, doc requests, questionnaires' and 'AI Tax Prep integrations' replaced by 'AI intake and task automations'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $74, Plus at $109, Premium at $149, Knowledge Based Authentication (KBA) at $1.25 per KBA credit and Tax Workflow Automation at $34 per client.
It lands 84 days after Canopy's previous change, "Canopy: Tax Resolution auto-fill adds state forms" (Aug 10, 2026). Canopy has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
