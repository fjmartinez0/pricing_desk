What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Ayrshare's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Ads (Facebook Boosted) add-on ($100/month) removed from add-ons list; Facebook, Instagram, and Google ads now included in Business (card + comparison table) and Premium; new comparison row shows Premium/Business/Enterprise Included, Launch Not offered (was a $100/mo paid add-on on Launch and Business). Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, add-on removed). The current page lists Premium at $129, Launch at $249, Business at $499 and Max Pack at $300.
It lands 14 days after Ayrshare's previous change, "Ayrshare turns Max Pack AI into a paid add-on, drops Premium" (Nov 1, 2026). Ayrshare has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Hootsuite, Coschedule, Buffer, Sproutsocial and MeetEdgar also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
