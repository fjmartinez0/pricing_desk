What happened
Between the Nov 10, 2026 and Nov 11, 2026 captures of Slack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Slackbot reframed: 'personal AI agent' dropped; comparison row now Consumption based (Free/Pro), Usage limits (Business+), Unlimited (Enterprise+); 15 messages/user/week noted. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Pro at $7.25, Business+ at $15 and Enterprise+ at $45.
It lands 3 days after Slack's previous change, "Slack hides Enterprise+ pricing, drops $45/user price for contact sales" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Slack reveals Enterprise+ pricing at $45 per user/month, billed annually" (Nov 3, 2026), "Slack swaps 24/7 support for tiered Success Plans, sold as paid add-ons" (Oct 28, 2026) and "Slack: Frontline teams add-on launched for Enterprise+ field workers" (Oct 20, 2026). Slack has been tracked since 2023-11-30.
In the same window Zoom, Microsoft, Google, Rocket.Chat and Mattermost also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
