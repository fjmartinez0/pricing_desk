What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of ConstructConnect's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Daily project update emails are now included in the Starter plan: the comparison table row (renamed from 'saved search update email' to 'daily emails with updated project info') gained a Starter checkmark where it was previously blank, and the Professional card dropped the now-redundant 'saved searches delivered daily' bullet.
