What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of SimScale's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Community (free) plan gains Limited access to Dynamic structural analysis, Compressible flow solver and Engineering AI (agentic AI), previously not included. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change).
It lands 49 days after SimScale's previous change, "SimScale cuts Community plan core hours from 3,000 to 2,000" (Oct 4, 2026). SimScale has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
In the same window Dassault Systèmes, Spline, Sketchup and Sanity also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
