What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Fotor's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added 'Message history' retention row to the plan comparison table: Basic keeps 7 days of AI Agent chat history, while Pro, Pro+, and Max now retain 1 year; Plan cards now show credit-to-output conversion examples (e.g. 100 credits/mo = 6 Nano Banana Pro images = 2 Seedance 2.0 videos) and Max gained a selectable credit tier menu (2k/5k/10k/20k/50k credits/mo) replacing the prior fixed 12,000 cre. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Fotor Pro at $3.99, Fotor Pro+ at $5.83, Fotor Max at $13.99, Credits - 1000/month at $21.66 and AI Headshot Team Package at $37.99 users.
It lands 28 days after Fotor's previous change, "Fotor: AI Agent chat allowance doubled (or more) across all plans" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fotor adds Seedream 5.0 Pro, growing AI image model lineup to 22" (Jul 13, 2026) and "Fotor: 2 new AI models added (Nano Banana 2 Lite, Gemini Omni Flash)" (Jul 6, 2026). Fotor has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
