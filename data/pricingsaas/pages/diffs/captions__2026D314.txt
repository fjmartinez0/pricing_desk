What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 10, 2026 captures of Captions's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Seat allocations now listed on plan cards: Max 2 total seats (1 admin + 1 additional); Frontier 3 total seats (1 admin + 2 additional). Prices and credits unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Max at $24.99 and Frontier at $69.99.
It lands one day after Captions's previous change, "Captions drops Enterprise plan card, routes scale buyers to Tesseract sales" (Nov 9, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Captions: Enterprise plan renamed to Tesseract for business, drops perks list" (Nov 5, 2026), "Captions: mid-tier plan renamed from Scale to Frontier" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Captions rebrands Scale plan to Frontier, pricing untouched" (Sep 22, 2026). Captions has been tracked since 2025-10-05.
In the same window Veed and Kapwing also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
