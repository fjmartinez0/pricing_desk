What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Pingboard's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Capabilities table row 'Streamlined compensation review workflow' (Enterprise only) replaced by 'Compensation Benchmarking' (Coming soon), now checked for Pro and Enterprise; Standard and Pro cards drop 'Starting at' and add a per-manager fee: Standard $4,999 USD +$32/manager/month, Pro $11,999 USD +$32/manager/month (billed annually). Compare-tiers header still says 'Starting at'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $416.58, Pro at $999.92 and Manager Agent at $32.
It lands 83 days after Pingboard's previous change, "Pingboard/Workleap: Manager Agent add-on ($99/mo) removed, folded into base plans" (Jul 28, 2026). Pingboard has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
