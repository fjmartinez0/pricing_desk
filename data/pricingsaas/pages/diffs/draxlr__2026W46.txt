What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Draxlr's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Model in comparison table expanded from GPT-5.5 to GPT-5.5 & Claude Opus 5.5 on the three plans that include AI model selection. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Lite at $16.67, Premium at $50, Power at $166.67, Enterprise at $316.67 and AI Credits at $10.
It lands 14 days after Draxlr's previous change, "Draxlr: Compare Plans table row renamed from 'Multi-Tenant' to 'Filters & Drill-downs'; FAQ confirms…" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Draxlr doubles Power plan to $250/month and narrows free trial to Premium" (Sep 28, 2026), "Draxlr rebrands Embed Dashboard as white-label, folds in Enterprise perk" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Draxlr caps free MCP access at 50 requests as paid tiers go unlimited" (Aug 24, 2026).
