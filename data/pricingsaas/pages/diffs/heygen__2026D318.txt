What happened
Between the Nov 13, 2026 and Nov 14, 2026 captures of HeyGen's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro plan now includes 1 Professional Voice Clone (highest-fidelity voice clone) at no extra charge, previously a $29/mo add-on. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Creator at $24, Pro at $41 and Business at $119.
It lands 22 days after HeyGen's previous change, "HeyGen adds Voice TTS and Seedance 2.5 to Creator and Pro, prices flat" (Oct 23, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HeyGen caps Voice Cloning usage on Creator, Pro, and Business plans" (Sep 19, 2026) and "HeyGen renames Digital Twin avatar to Custom Video Avatar, limits unchanged" (Sep 1, 2026). HeyGen has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Synthesia, Runway, InVideo and Pictory also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
