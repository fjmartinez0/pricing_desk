What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Kinsta's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan's displayed starting price rose from $417 to $500/month (annual billing); the upfront 'Pay today' annual totals ($3,400 Agency, $5,000 Enterprise) shown on both cards were also removed. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Single 20GB at $30, Single 40GB at $42, WP 2 at $59, Single 65GB at $75 and WP 5 at $96.
It lands 13 days after Kinsta's previous change, "Kinsta narrows Enterprise custom agreement terms to select plans" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kinsta adds free agency site hosting and dedicated enterprise servers" (Oct 19, 2026). Kinsta has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
In the same window Google Cloud and Siteground also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
