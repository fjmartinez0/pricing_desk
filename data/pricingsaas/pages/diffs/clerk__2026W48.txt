What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Clerk's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Linking Enterprise SSO connections to Organizations is no longer listed as a feature of the Enhanced B2B add-on ($100/mo or $85/mo annually); the SSO connections row for Pro and Business now states 'Connections can be linked to Organizations', indicating the capability no longer requires the add-on. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Pro at $20, Business at $250, Administration at $85, B2B Authentication at $85 and Billing at $0.70.
It lands 14 days after Clerk's previous change, "Clerk adds Minimum app version controls to force native app updates on Pro+" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Clerk adds Secret Key IP restrictions, exclusive to its Business plan" (Oct 26, 2026). Clerk has been tracked since 2026-06-23.
In the same window Supabase and FusionAuth also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
