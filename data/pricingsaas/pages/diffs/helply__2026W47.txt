What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Helply's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Credit-block pricing (from $49/mo for 500 credits, plus Volume/Enterprise) replaced by five tiered plans: Starter $99, Plus $199, Team $499, Pro $999, Growth $1,999 with bundled credits and agent seats. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 17, 2026) lists Growth at $49, Starter at $99, Plus at $199, Team at $499 and Pro at $999.
It lands 49 days after Helply's previous change, "Helply: replaced pay-per-outcome with 4 tiered plans + credits" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Helply swaps $1/ticket pricing for $0.50 per outcome, cuts minimum to $99/mo" (Sep 20, 2026). Helply has been tracked since 2026-09-08.
In the same window Jobber also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
