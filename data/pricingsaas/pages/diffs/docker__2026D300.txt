What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Oct 27, 2026 captures of Docker's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed top banner promo offering $250 in credits for Cloud Sandboxes (Claim credits). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Docker Pro at $9, Docker Team at $15, Docker Business at $24, Testcontainers Cloud Additional Minutes at $3 per 100 minutes and Docker Build Cloud Minutes (500) at $25 per 500 build minutes.
It lands 15 days after Docker's previous change, "Docker drops Build Cloud minutes from Pro, Team and Business plan listings" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Docker adds $250 Cloud Sandbox credit in place of whitepaper CTA" (Sep 28, 2026). Docker has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Google Cloud and Hypervault also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
