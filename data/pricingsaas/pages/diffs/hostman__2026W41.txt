What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Hostman's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Bare Metal "From" price returns to $90/month (from $100), continuing its week-to-week oscillation. In detail: Bare Metal "From" price on the pricing overview dropped from $100/month back to $90/month — the figure has oscillated $90/$100/$70 since the card launched (W34-W38). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Backend Apps at $4, Managed Databases at $4, Cloud Servers 1vCPU 2GB at $5, Backend Apps 1vCPU 2GB at $5 and Backend Apps 2vCPU 2GB at $6.
It lands 14 days after Hostman's previous change, "Hostman: Bare Metal price increase ($90→$100/mo)" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hostman: Bare Metal price cut ($100→$90/month)" (Sep 14, 2026), "Hostman: Cloud Servers price up $4→$7/mo, Bare Metal down $100→$70/mo" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Hostman hikes Bare Metal starting price 11%, from $90 to $100/month" (Aug 24, 2026). Hostman has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
