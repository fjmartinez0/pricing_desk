What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Ideogram's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: The Free plan ($0) column in the comparison table was replaced by a new Pay as you go plan at 1¢ per credit, and a new Credits tab adds a self-serve prepaid Enterprise option. Team dropped its 2-user minimum. Plus and Pro gain unlimited character consistency, and all plans list all image & video models. Subscription prices are unchanged. In detail: Team plan '*Minimum 2 users' requirement removed; card now reads '$20 per user/month, $240 billed annually'; Plus and Pro cards gain unlimited character consistency, and every plan now lists all image & video models; Team collaboration features are relabeled as early access. (Quality export on the Plus card already had a ✓ in the BEFORE comparison table.); Free plan ($0, weekly slow credits for eligible accounts) removed from the plan comparison table and replaced by Pay as you go; the FAQ still says eligible free accounts receive weekly credits; Pay as you go plan added at 1¢ per credit (credits on demand, all models, quality export, API access), replacing the Free plan column in the comparison table; a self-serve prepaid Enterprise option also appears under the new Credits tab. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, feature added, freemium change, new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Plus at $15, Team at $20, Pro at $42 and Pay as you go at $0.01 per credit.
It lands 4 days after Ideogram's previous change, "Ideogram lists all image and video models, unlimited character consistency" (Oct 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Ideogram more than doubles priority credits on paid plans, drops top-up fee" (Oct 5, 2026), "Ideogram more than doubles priority credits on Plus, Pro and Team plans" (Oct 3, 2026) and "Ideogram: Free tier credit guarantee restricted + Team plan queue upgraded" (Aug 24, 2026). Ideogram has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Runway, Microsoft, Leonardo.ai, Perplexity AI and Canva also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
