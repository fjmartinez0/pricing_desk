What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Crystal Knows's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Crystal renamed "Sales" to "Predictions" (same $59/mo), added annual billing ($490/yr) to Premium, and introduced a new $49/seat/month team plan for Premium via an admin panel; Crystal Premium now bundles two new apps - Vision (goal planning, unlimited future snapshots) and Rhythm (daily routines, weekly/quarterly reviews) - alongside Personality, same $49/mo price. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Crystal Premium at $40.83 and Premium for Teams at $49.
It lands 28 days after Crystal Knows's previous change, "Crystal Knows turns Data API into Personality API with pay-per-profile pricing" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Crystal Knows Premium switches to $49/mo monthly billing from $99/yr annual" (Jul 27, 2026). Crystal Knows has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
In the same window Salesforce and HubSpot also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
