What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Bizstim's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Individual + Website plan at $9.99/mo added between the free Individual and Small Business ($39.99) plans: 1 practitioner, Individual features plus a published website with own domain included; Own domain option added to Website Builder & Blog: included on Individual + Website and Enterprise, +$9.99/mo add-on on Small Business; previously no own-domain option. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan, new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Individual + Website at $9.99, Small Business at $39.99 and Enterprise at $79.99.
It lands 7 days after Bizstim's previous change, "Bizstim rolls out Website Builder & Blog: full publishing for Small Business," (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Bizstim adds Smart Learn row to pricing table, bridging Records and Products " (Jul 13, 2026). Bizstim has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
