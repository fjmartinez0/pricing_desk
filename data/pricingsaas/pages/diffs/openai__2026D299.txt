What happened
Between the Oct 23, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of OpenAI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan taglines rewritten to 'Best for...' format for Free, Go, Plus and Pro; no price or feature changes. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 30, 2026) lists Go at $8, Plus at $20, Business at $20 and Pro at $100.
It lands 8 days after OpenAI's previous change, "OpenAI: Plan taglines reworded to 'Best for...' format: Free (trying out ChatGPT), Go (longer conversations), Plus…" (Oct 18, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenAI rewrites Free, Go, Plus and Pro taglines in "Best for" style" (Oct 11, 2026), "OpenAI rewrites plan taglines across Free, Go, Plus and Pro tiers" (Oct 9, 2026) and "OpenAI moves free and Go chats to GPT-6, rewrites plan taglines" (Oct 8, 2026). OpenAI has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
