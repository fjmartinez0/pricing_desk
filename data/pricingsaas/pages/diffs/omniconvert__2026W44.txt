What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Omniconvert's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Nexus by Omniconvert plan renamed to Omniconvert Commander (formerly Nexus); tab, page heading and comparison column label updated. Prices and features unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pulse Expert at $24.17, Creator at $49, Growth at $199, Explore at $245 and Scale at $499.
It lands 21 days after Omniconvert's previous change, "Omniconvert: AI A/B Testing flipped (code→prompt to prompt→code), Free plan" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Omniconvert adds AI A/B testing—turn prompts into tests, no code needed" (Sep 28, 2026). Omniconvert has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Leadpages, Google, Convertkit and Unbounce also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
