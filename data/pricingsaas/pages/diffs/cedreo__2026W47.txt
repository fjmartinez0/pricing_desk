What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Cedreo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Personal plan increased from $69/project to $139/project; Pro plan annual rate increased from $49/month (list $69) to $79/month (list $129); Enterprise plan annual rate increased from $69/user/month (list $79) to $119/user/month (list $159). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $79, Enterprise at $119 and Personal at $139 per project.
It lands 7 days after Cedreo's previous change, "Cedreo slashes prices across all plans, Personal now $69 per project" (Nov 8, 2026). Cedreo has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Sketchup also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
