What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Chameleon's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Compass and Prism AI agents in the comparison table lost their '(beta)' label, now shown as general product names. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Startup at $279 and Growth at $1,250.
It lands 98 days after Chameleon's previous change, "Chameleon revamps pricing tiers, adds Compass and Prism AI agents in beta" (Jul 6, 2026). Chameleon has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
