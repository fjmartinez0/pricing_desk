What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of fal's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: GPU/Compute hourly pricing increased across all tiers (H100 $1.89->$2.49/h, B300 $4.49->$5.99/h, B200 $3.49->$5.49/h, H200 $2.10->$2.99/h, RTX 6000 $1.10->$1.99/h); new GB200 tier added at $5.89/h; Model APIs overhauled: added MiniMax H3 Max, Kling v3, Seedance 2, Nano Banana 2/Pro, GPT Image 2, plus new Audio Models & 3D Models sections; dropped Wan 2.5, Veo 3, Ovi, Seedream V4, Flux Kontext Pro. In detail: Model APIs overhauled: added MiniMax H3 Max, Kling v3, Seedance 2, Nano Banana 2/Pro, GPT Image 2, plus new Audio Models & 3D Models sections; dropped Wan 2.5, Veo 3, Ovi, Seedream V4, Flux Kontext Pro, Qwen. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 20, 2026) lists Audio Model APIs at $0.1 per 1,000 characters (MiniMax Speech 2.8 HD) + $0.2 per 100 seconds (ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2), 3D Model APIs at $0.08 per generation (Hunyuan Motion 1B) + $0.01 per credit (Tripo H3.1), Serverless Compute at $6 per H200 GPU hour + $4 per RTX PRO 6000 GPU hour, Video Model APIs at $0.05 per second of video output (MiniMax H3 Max) + $0.07 per second of video output (Kling 2.5 Turbo Pro) and Image Model APIs at $1 per unit (GPT Image 2) + $0.5 per 100 megapixels (Z Image Turbo).
It lands 3 days after fal's previous change, "fal raises H100 GPU rate to $2.49/hr, adds GB200, doubles MiniMax video price" (Oct 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "fal adds Flux 3 image editing to rate card at $0.024 per megapixel" (Oct 9, 2026), "fal publishes Audio and 3D model API pricing, expands video/image tables to 10 endpoints" (Oct 1, 2026) and "fal: H100 GPU list price increase ($3.99→$4.50/h)" (Aug 5, 2026). fal has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
