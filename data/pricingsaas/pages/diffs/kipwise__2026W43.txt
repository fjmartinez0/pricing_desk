What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Kipwise's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Kipwise Ticketing (chat widget, inbox, tickets) listed in early access at no extra price, as a new bullet in the AI credits add-on section. In detail: Kipwise Ticketing (chat widget, inbox, tickets) added in early access at no extra price, as a new Included bullet on the Kipwise Agent add-on card ($10/user/month). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Premium at $6.3, Branded at $12.6, AI Knowledge Suggestions at $7 and Kipwise Agent at $9.
It lands 21 days after Kipwise's previous change, "Kipwise Agent add-on ditches $1K setup fee for $10/user/month pricing" (Sep 28, 2026). Kipwise has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
