What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Kapwing's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Cloud storage limits raised across plans: Free plan now discloses 5 GB (previously not shown), Pro increased from 100 GB to 200 GB, and Business increased from 500 GB to 800 GB. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $16 and Business at $50.
It lands 14 days after Kapwing's previous change, "Kapwing slashes free-plan credit minutes: subtitling 50→10, TTS 3→1" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kapwing: Free plan export length increased (1 min → 4 min)" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Kapwing: Cloud storage now capped (was unlimited)" (Jul 3, 2026). Kapwing has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Canva, Piktochart and Adobe also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
