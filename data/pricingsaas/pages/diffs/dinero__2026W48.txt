What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Dinero's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Dinero Starter (free) now includes access to MCP (AI assistant access to the accounting); MCP previously described as a Pro/Total-only feature. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added).
It lands 28 days after Dinero's previous change, "Dinero adds auto dues collection and multi-user access for associations" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Dinero drops Assistant branding from plan bullets, prices unchanged" (Oct 18, 2026). Dinero has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Shopify, PayPal, Stripe and Squarespace also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
