What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Letta's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pro ($20/mo) moves from Letta Auto weekly+monthly quota with pay-as-you-go overage to $20/month in credits for any model; Developer Plans (usage-based API plan, $20/mo) section and tab removed; Free plan: 'Limited agents' now 'Up to 3 stateful agents'; limited Letta Auto usage removed; option to purchase credits for Letta-hosted models added. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change, package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $20 and Teams Pro at $20.
It lands 14 days after Letta's previous change, "Letta caps memory repo storage at 5 MiB on Free, 50 MiB on Pro plans" (Oct 26, 2026). Letta has been tracked since 2026-06-21.
