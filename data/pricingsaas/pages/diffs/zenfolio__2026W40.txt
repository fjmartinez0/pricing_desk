What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Zenfolio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New FAQ section discloses previously unstated selling fees: a 7% commerce fee per order, a $0.09/$0.99 digital download fee, and Stripe (2.9%+$0.30) / PayPal (3.49%+$0.49) payment processing fees. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $7, Professional at $11.5 and Advanced at $20.
It lands 28 days after Zenfolio's previous change, "Zenfolio slashes Professional team seats to 2, extends sale to Aug 31" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Zenfolio: Added "Manage Team Members" feature (5/15 seats) + 1 more change" (Jul 20, 2026). Zenfolio has been tracked since 2026-05-12.
In the same window Shopify, Format, Squarespace and Adobe also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
