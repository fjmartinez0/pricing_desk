What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Slack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Frontline teams' add-on introduced for Enterprise+, giving field workers Slack for task management, agentic support and secure Salesforce access. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $7.25, Business+ at $15 and Enterprise+ at $45.
It lands 6 days after Slack's previous change, "Slack: Frontline teams add-on launched for Enterprise+ field workers" (Oct 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Slack hides Enterprise+ price, swaps $45/user for "Contact sales" (Oct 19, 2026), "Slack: Enterprise+ now shows a public price of $45 USD per user/month (billed annually) instead of 'Contact sales…" (Oct 14, 2026) and "Slack hides $45 Enterprise+ price, now says Contact sales for pricing" (Oct 13, 2026). Slack has been tracked since 2023-11-30.
In the same window Microsoft, Google, Rocket.Chat, Mattermost and Zoom also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
