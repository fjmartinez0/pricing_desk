What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of TinyMCE's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: TinyMCE AI credit consumption tables expanded with many new LLMs: Claude Opus/Fable 5.x, Gemini 3.1 Pro and 3.5-3.8 Flash, GPT-6 and GPT-5.6 families, GPT-5.5/5.4/5.4 Mini. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for TinyMCE. TinyMCE has been tracked since 2026-07-16.
