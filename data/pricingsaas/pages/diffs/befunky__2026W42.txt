What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of BeFunky's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free plan now includes generating up to 20 AI images with a free account; Plus adds 1,000 monthly AI credits for the AI Image Generator plus top-up credits; Pro's 5,000 monthly credits now cover the AI Image Generator as well as Batch AI tools (previously Batch AI credits only). In detail: AI Image Generator launched: Free plan now includes generating up to 20 AI images with a free account; Plus adds 1,000 monthly AI credits for the AI Image Generator plus top-up credits. Compare table adds "Create Images from Text" (Free Limited, Plus/Pro included) and "Edit Images with a Text Prompt" (Plus/Pro only). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists BeFunky Plus at $6.99 and BeFunky Pro at $12.49.
It lands 145 days after BeFunky's previous change, "BeFunky launches Pro tier at $12.49/mo in new three-tier overhaul" (May 19, 2026). BeFunky has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
