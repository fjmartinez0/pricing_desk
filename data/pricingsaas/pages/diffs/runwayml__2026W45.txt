What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Runway's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Kling 4.0 launched with 25% off through Nov 30; Enterprise free-usage offer extended to up to 2 months free, sign by 12/31; App Studio (build creative apps) added to Standard, Pro and Max; Seedance 2.0 Fast removed from the model table. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, discount added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $12, Pro at $28 and Max at $76.
It lands 2 days after Runway's previous change, "Runway extends Enterprise promo to 12/31, but caps free usage at 2 months" (Oct 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Runway: Added 'Generate, test and publish ads with Runway Ads (New)' bullet to Standard, Pro and Max plan cards" (Oct 26, 2026), "Runway adds Runway Ads to paid plans, ends Max's unlimited Wan 3.0 promo" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Runway drops Max plan's expired Unlimited Wan 3.0 promo, prices hold" (Oct 20, 2026). Runway has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Synthesia, OpusClip and Loom also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
