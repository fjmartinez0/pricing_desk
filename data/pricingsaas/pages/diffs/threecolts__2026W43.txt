What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Threecolts's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Operator agentic assistant now included with every plan, with option to expand usage; shown on plan cards and in new section. In detail: AI Operator agentic assistant now included with every plan (Reseller Standard/Teams/Pro and Brands 1K/5K/10K), with a monthly usage allowance and option to expand usage; shown on plan cards and in new section. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $65, Teams at $119, Brands 1K at $149, Pro at $183 and Brands 5K at $499.
It lands 76 days after Threecolts's previous change, "Threecolts: new usage-limit table for Tactical Arbitrage add-on" (Aug 4, 2026). Threecolts has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
