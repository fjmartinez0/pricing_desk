What happened
Between the Oct 23, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Modal's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Notebooks dropped from the 'Modal Sandbox + Notebooks pricing' section heading and the footer product list; included-usage FAQ now covers only Functions and Sandboxes. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Team at $250, Compute resources at $1.1 NVIDIA A10G instance hour + $1.95 L40S GPU hour and Sandbox + Notebooks compute at $2.4 per 100 GiB-Hour + $14.19 per 100 core-hours.
It lands 21 days after Modal's previous change, "Modal adds $0.04/GiB network egress fee, with up to 100 TiB free per plan" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Modal renames Enterprise SSO from Okta to standards-based SAML" (Sep 14, 2026), "Modal adds included token usage row, renames free credits to free compute" (Sep 12, 2026) and "Modal swaps Okta SSO for SAML SSO in Enterprise audit logs bullet" (Sep 11, 2026). Modal has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Together AI and Huggingface also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
