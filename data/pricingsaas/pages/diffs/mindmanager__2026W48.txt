What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of MindManager's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday banner upgraded from a plain 'Save 10%' headline to a richer promo: 'BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW' with free Grammarly Pro (select products) plus up to 2 bonus items worth up to $298, on top of the existing 10% discount. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Professional at $179 and Professional (One-time purchase) at $369.
It lands 14 days after MindManager's previous change, "MindManager launches early Black Friday sale with 10% off Essentials, Professional and one-time purchase" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindManager quietly ends 28% annual discount, Professional jumps to $179/year" (Oct 26, 2026). MindManager has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Microsoft, X, Notion, Creately and Miro also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
