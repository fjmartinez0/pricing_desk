What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Salesmotion's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Q4DEAL promo ended: Individual plan no longer $39/mo for first 3 months; banner and FAQ removed, now $85/mo billed monthly. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Individual at $85.
It lands 14 days after Salesmotion's previous change, "Salesmotion: New Q4 promo (code Q4DEAL, limited time): Individual plan $39/mo for first 3 months instead of $85/mo, then…" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Salesmotion caps Individual contacts at 200 credits a month" (Sep 7, 2026). Salesmotion has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
