What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of PDF4me's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Prepaid API packs now explicitly need no active subscription and can be auto-recharged; previously described as stacking on top of a monthly subscription. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $2.5, Pro at $4.7, Developer / Automation at $76.5, Day Pass at $1.8 subscription and Team User Add-on at $4.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for PDF4me. PDF4me has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
