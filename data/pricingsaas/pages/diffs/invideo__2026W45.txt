What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of InVideo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Max plan's 50% annual discount was removed and billed-annually price rose from $75 to $250/seat/mo (included credits also rose from 5,000 to 8,000/mo; annual billing no longer saves vs. monthly). Export resolution cap removed for 'Your own footage' (available from Starter plan up) — changed from '4K export' to 'unlimited export'. In detail: Max plan's 50% annual discount was removed and the billed-annually price rose from $75 to $250/seat/mo; included credits rose from 5,000 to 8,000/mo and annual billing no longer saves vs. monthly; Export resolution cap removed for the 'Your own footage' capability (available from the Starter plan up) — changed from '4K export' to 'unlimited export'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $10, Plus at $30, Team at $47, Max at $75 and Scale at $107.
It lands 21 days after InVideo's previous change, "InVideo halves annual Starter to $10/seat, cuts Seedance video allowances" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "InVideo cuts annual Plus and Max prices up to 25% as Seedance credits jump" (Oct 5, 2026), "InVideo: 10x storage capacity increase + free guest seats added" (Sep 28, 2026) and "InVideo redenominates credits 10x across all plans; prices and generation counts unchanged" (Sep 7, 2026). InVideo has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
In the same window Synthesia and Runway also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
