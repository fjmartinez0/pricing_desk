What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Telnyx's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Committed/Growth plan support changed: dedicated account manager and "priority support and higher rate limits" removed; 24/7 chat or call support added; Committed plan renamed to Growth (still the middle "Best value" tier between Pay as you go and Enterprise; Enterprise now says "Everything in growth plan"); Growth (formerly Committed) monthly minimum spend raised from $500 to $2,000/mo (+300%). In detail: Growth plan discount is now a stated 15% off Voice, Messaging and Numbers, replacing the unquantified "volume discounts on everything you use". The page notes voice engine and LLM rates are the same on every plan; Growth (formerly Committed) monthly minimum spend raised from $500 to $2,000/mo (+300%). The plan moved from a "Get volume rate" contact form to self-serve "Choose your plan" checkout. Pulse files it as a pricing change (plan renamed, discount change, price increase, feature change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Growth at $2,000, Enterprise at $5,000 and Pay as you go at $0.05 voice engine per minute (orchestration + hosted STT + hosted TTS bundled) + $0 Voice API platform fee per minute.
It lands 21 days after Telnyx's previous change, "Telnyx Conversationalai voice AI estimate rises to $0.06/min as Kimi GPU LLM rate climbs 50%" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Telnyx Conversationalai: Call recording, phone number & SMS rates increased" (Aug 11, 2026) and "Telnyx Conversationalai: new 3-tier plans ($500/$5K min) + SIP trunking rates revealed" (Aug 4, 2026). Telnyx has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
