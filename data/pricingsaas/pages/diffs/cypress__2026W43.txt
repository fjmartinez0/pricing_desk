What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Cypress.io's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Business plan now lists 'Re-run optimization' among features beyond Team (Spec Prioritization, Auto Cancellation, etc.); also added to comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Team at $67, Business at $267 and Enterprise at $4 per 1,000 bundled test results.
It lands 21 days after Cypress.io's previous change, "Cypress.io: Cloud CLI and Webhooks added (Starter, Team plans)" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cypress.io slashes Starter plan seat limit from 50 to 10 users" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Cypress.io: bundled test-result overage tier added ($5.28-$4.00/1000)" (Jul 27, 2026). Cypress.io has been tracked since 2026-02-20.
