What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of OpenRouter's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Advanced notifications added under Budgets & Spend Controls, available on Business and Enterprise plans only; Enterprise BYOK limit changed from $200,000/month with no fee (5% after) to Custom. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, package change) with an upward direction.
It lands 7 days after OpenRouter's previous change, "OpenRouter adds Workload Identity Federation to Business, Enterprise plans" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenRouter renames Pay-as-you-go plan to Standard, fee stays 5.5%" (Sep 21, 2026), "OpenRouter adds Business tier: 8% fee, 1,000-workspace limit, signed DPA" (Sep 14, 2026) and "OpenRouter expands catalog to 500+ models, 80+ providers across pricing tiers" (Aug 17, 2026).
