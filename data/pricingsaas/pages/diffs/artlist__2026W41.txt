What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Artlist's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Artlist Flows and Artlist MCP & Plugins removed from AI plan feature lists (Starter, Core, Creator; MCP also from Business); Included AI video caps halved: Starter 206 to 103, Core 500 to 250, Creator 1,000 to 500; credits unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease, feature removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists AI Starter at $11.99, AI Core at $23.99 and AI Creator at $41.67.
It lands 48 days after Artlist's previous change, "Artlist adds node-based AI Flows builder, trims model coverage on Creator/Pro" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Artlist: new Artlist MCP feature + Unlimited models expanded to 18 (Pro)" (Jul 28, 2026) and "Artlist rebrands Contact-Sales tier, adds multi-model AI credit perks" (Jul 13, 2026). Artlist has been tracked since 2026-06-04.
In the same window Shutterstock and Getty Images also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
