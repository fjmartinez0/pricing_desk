What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Kinde's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Scale plan SCIM directory sync: price per extra directory raised from $35 to $65 (3 directories still included); API keys: 'Validations' (1,500,000 free, $0.0001 each) replaced by 1,000 key creations + 100,000 verifications free, $0.001 per extra creation, $0.00001 per extra verification. Pulse files it as a pricing change (metric change, price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Scale at $15.10, Pro at $25, Plus at $75 and Kinde Care at $750.
It lands 7 days after Kinde's previous change, "Kinde launches SCIM directory sync on Scale with 3 directories, $35 per extra" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kinde drops 'Coming soon' on SCIM, adds Enterprise org-scoped roles" (Nov 1, 2026). Kinde has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Clerk and Supabase also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
