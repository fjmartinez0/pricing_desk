What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of CloudFix's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan's first 30 days now explicitly include the full product: line-item detail, the fixers and the right to run them (BEFORE: only 'full resource details for 30 days'). The footnote's 'initial 30 day free tier' is now 'the Free plan has no time limit'. After 30 days, line-item detail is hidden and the savings summary by category stays free. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change). The current page lists Starter at $124.17, Growth at $415.83, Scale at $1,249.17, Enterprise at $2,499.17 and RightSpend at 25% of net new savings delivered.
It lands 42 days after CloudFix's previous change, "CloudFix RightSpend drops "no savings, no charge" for $25K+ EC2 teams" (Oct 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CloudFix nearly doubles automated fixers from 30+ to 53 across all plans" (Sep 20, 2026). CloudFix has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Kubecost also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
