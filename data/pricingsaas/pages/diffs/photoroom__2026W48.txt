What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Photoroom's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan SLA/SSO bullet now specifies SSO with Google (previously just SSO). Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Pro at $7.50, Max at $20.99 and Ultra at $82.50.
It lands 14 days after Photoroom's previous change, "Photoroom drops API credits and AI Shadows from Pro, Max and Ultra plans" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Photoroom cuts Ultra video output from 4K to HD as prices stay unchanged" (Nov 1, 2026) and "Photoroom moves Enterprise to usage-based annual balance pricing and rewrites plan taglines" (Oct 19, 2026). Photoroom has been tracked since 2026-06-26.
In the same window Squark AI, Speechmatics, Spiky.AI, Slang AI and scite also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
