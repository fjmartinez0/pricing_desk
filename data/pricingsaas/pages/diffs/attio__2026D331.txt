What happened
Between the Nov 26, 2026 and Nov 27, 2026 captures of Attio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Ask Attio credits (100/500/1,000/2,500 per month) replaced with relative usage tiers (Limited usage, More usage, 3x Plus, 5x Plus) across Free/Plus/Pro/Ultra. In detail: Ask Attio credits renamed to 'Ask Attio usage'; explicit allotments (100/500/1,000/2,500) replaced with relative tiers (Limited usage, More usage, 3x Plus, 5x Plus) on Free/Plus/Pro/Ultra cards and comparison table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change). The current page lists Plus at $40, Pro at $100, Ultra at $1,000 and Governance Add-on at $30.
It lands 11 days after Attio's previous change, "Attio ditches per-seat pricing for workspace plans, adds $1,000 Ultra tier" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Attio shifts to per-workspace pricing, adds $1,000/mo Ultra plan" (Nov 11, 2026). Attio has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
In the same window Close, HubSpot, Notion, Salesforce and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
