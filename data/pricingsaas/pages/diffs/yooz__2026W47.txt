What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Yooz's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Gold Edition now shows 'Starting at $215/month' (volume-based); optional paid add-ons listed (YoozPay, Vendor Statement Reconciliation, Attachments, Budget Management); Book a Demo card removed; Gold Edition now lists included features: omnichannel capture, smart extraction, line-level PO matching, approval routing, fraud protection, AP reporting, audit trail, ERP/DMS integration, support. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed, feature added) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Nov 18, 2026) lists Gold Edition at $215.
It lands 7 days after Yooz's previous change, "Yooz drops free trial and pushes Gold Edition buyers to book a demo" (Nov 8, 2026). Yooz has been tracked since 2026-01-28.
