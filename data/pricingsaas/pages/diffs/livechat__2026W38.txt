What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of LiveChat's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: ChatBot.com add-on ($52/mo) replaced by native AI Agent (Text) add-on from $19/user/month, 14-day free trial; new Custom skills for AI Agent add-on also launched at $19/user/month. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $19, Team at $49 and Business at $79.
It lands 63 days after LiveChat's previous change, "LiveChat swaps Key Account Manager for Product Expert support tier" (Jul 13, 2026). LiveChat has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Intercom, Tidio, Olark, Drift and Zendesk also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
