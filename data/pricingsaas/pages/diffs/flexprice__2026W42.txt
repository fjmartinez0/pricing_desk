What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Flexprice's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Event-volume tiers (Free, Build $500, Scale $1000, Mission Critical custom) replaced by Pay as you go ($199/mo + 0.5% of billing) and Enterprise (from $3,000/mo, no revenue share).
