What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Cargo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan now gives 0 credits/month (was 100 credits/month, recurring); the 100 free credits now apply once, in the first month only. New custom Enterprise plan added, plus new paid hands-on-help add-ons: Advisory ($2,000/mo), Co-build ($4,000/mo), Managed (from $10,000/mo). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists 1.5K Credits at $145.17, 2.5K Credits at $220, 17K Credits at $1,047.17, 50K+ Credits at $2,640 and Advisory at $2,000.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Cargo. Cargo has been tracked since 2026-04-13.
