What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Buildium's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added 5 new Leasing table features: Custom Prospect Form (all plans), plus Prospect Pipeline, Call Hub, Prospect Voice & Chat, and Tour Scheduling, all exclusive to the Premium plan. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essential at $55.92, Growth at $173 and Premium at $361.17.
It lands 7 days after Buildium's previous change, "Buildium adds self-service pricing calculator with 10% annual discount" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Buildium: Growth plan eSignature downgraded from unlimited to paid add-on" (Jul 3, 2026). Buildium has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
In the same window AppFolio, DoorLoop and Hemlane also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
