What happened
Between the Oct 22, 2026 and Oct 23, 2026 captures of HubSpot's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free plan's Email marketing send allowance cut from 2,000 to 300 emails per calendar month in the feature comparison table; Starter plan's email-sending metric changed from a contact-tier-scaled send limit ('5x marketing contact tier email send limit/month') to consuming shared HubSpot Credits. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change, limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $7, Smart CRM Professional at $45, Smart CRM Enterprise at $75, Revenue Hub Professional at $85 and Sales Hub Professional at $90.
It lands 32 days after HubSpot's previous change, "HubSpot Marketing Enterprise adds Content and Nurture AI agents in BETA" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HubSpot Marketing Starter gains Agent Hub, Agent Builder, Data Agent as Pro d" (Sep 21, 2026), "HubSpot Sales Enterprise swaps AI transcript enrichment for standard sandbox " (Sep 21, 2026) and "HubSpot Sales Starter Sales Hub: Agent Hub democratized to Starter tier + feature repackaging" (Sep 21, 2026). HubSpot has been tracked since 2025-01-01.
In the same window Salesforce, Pipedrive, Microsoft, Zoho and Freshworks also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
