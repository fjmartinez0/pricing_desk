What happened
Between the Oct 15, 2026 and Oct 16, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added Gemini 4 Argon (Google) to the model rate card: $2.00 input, $10.00 output, $0.10 cached input, no cache-write charge. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pay as you go at $0.1 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.5 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands one day after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB adds Meta's Muse Wonder 1.0 at $4 input, $20 output per 1M tokens" (Oct 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB replaces plan-less rate card with Free and Pay-as-you-go tiers" (Oct 12, 2026), "MindsDB hikes DeepSeek V4.1 Flash rates, ends $5,000 credit match promo" (Oct 5, 2026) and "MindsDB ends 100% credit match promo, up to $5,000 for new teams" (Oct 3, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
