What happened
Between the Aug 31, 2026 and Sep 8, 2026 captures of Modal's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Region selection surcharge dropped from 1.5-1.75x to 1.15-1.75x base compute prices, lowering the minimum premium for choosing a specific region across Starter, Team, and Enterprise plans. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Team at $250, Compute resources at $1.1 NVIDIA A10G instance hour + $1.95 L40S GPU hour and Sandbox + Notebooks compute at $2.4 per 100 GiB-Hour + $14.19 per 100 core-hours.
It lands 5 days after Modal's previous change, "Modal slashes region surcharge floor from 1.5x to 1.15x across all tiers" (Sep 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Modal adds included token usage row, renames free credits to free compute" (Sep 12, 2026), "Modal swaps Okta SSO for SAML SSO in Enterprise audit logs bullet" (Sep 11, 2026) and "Modal: Added Environment-level budgets to Team and Enterprise plans" (Aug 10, 2026). Modal has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Together AI, Lambda Labs, Paper, Huggingface and Anyscale also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
