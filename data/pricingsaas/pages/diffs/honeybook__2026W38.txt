Honeybook adopts cumulative plan-tier copy on Essentials and Premium cards
Verified Sep 14, 2026·Business Operations·Direction: neutral
The changeEssentials, Starter, Premium positioning — positioning.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 7, 2026 → Sep 14, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Honeybook's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan card copy changed from generic 'What's Included:' to cumulative framing: Essentials now says 'Includes everything in Starter plan plus:' and Premium says 'Includes everything in Essential plan plus:'. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $29, Essentials at $36.75 and Premium at $81.75.
It lands 124 days after Honeybook's previous change, "Honeybook slashes card fees to 2.7% + 10¢ for all plans" (May 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Honeybook Announces Significant Price Increases Across All Plans, with Premium Plan Rising 65% to $109/Month" (Q1 2025), "Honeybook Announces Major Price Hikes Across Plans, Essentials Up 53%, Premium Up 65%, Starter Up 81%, Effective Feb 4, 2025" (Q4 2024) and "Honeybook Launches Advanced Reports in Premium Plan, Enhancing Analytics for Users" (Q3 2024). Honeybook has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Quickbooks, Zoho and Freshbooks also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
