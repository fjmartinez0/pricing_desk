What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Slang AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Premium now leads with a NEW Superhost block (Emotionally Intelligent Conversations, 97 Languages, Flexible Responses, Enterprise Quality Assurance), included in Premium rather than sold separately; Bilingual Support (previously Premium and an à-la-carte add-on) is now included in Core; Core ($399/location) and Premium ($599/location) USD starting prices are no longer displayed; all three plan ca. In detail: Core ($399/location) and Premium ($599/location) USD starting prices are no longer displayed; all three plan cards now show only a 'Get a quote' CTA (previously 'Request a Demo') and the currency switcher is gone. The prices remain in the page HTML but are hidden by CSS (display:none). Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price hidden) with an upward direction. The current page lists Core at $399, Premium at $599, Bilingual Support at $99 and Private Events at $199.
It lands 100 days after Slang AI's previous change, "Slang AI: Direct Text AI-powered texting added to Premium plan" (Jul 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Slang AI rebrands Direct SMS as Text Links and Confirmations" (Apr 23, 2026), "Slang AI Adds Direct Text Feature for Fully Automated AI Texting" (Apr 13, 2026) and "Slang AI adds Fishbowl to boost reservation platform integrations" (Mar 18, 2026). Slang AI has been tracked since 2026-03-09.
In the same window Jasper and Phrase also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
