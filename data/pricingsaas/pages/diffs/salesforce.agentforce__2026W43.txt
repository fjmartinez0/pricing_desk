What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Salesforce's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Agentforce Max Editions card renamed to Max Edition; price (from $550/user/month) and 2.75M Flex Credits unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Agentforce Max Editions at $550, Flex Credits at $500 per 100k Credits, Conversations at $2 per conversation, Agentforce add-ons at $125 and Agentforce Industries add-ons at $150.
It lands 14 days after Salesforce's previous change, "Salesforce Agentforce: Agentforce 1 Editions renamed to Max, credits to 2.75M" (Oct 5, 2026). Salesforce has been tracked since 2025-01-03.
