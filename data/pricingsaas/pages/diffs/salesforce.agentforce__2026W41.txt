What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Salesforce's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: "Agentforce 1 Editions" tier renamed to "Agentforce Max Editions"; sub-edition list updated to Sales, Service, Sales/Service, and Industries. Flex Credits allowance for this tier increased from 2.5M to 2.75M per org per year. In detail: "Agentforce 1 Editions" tier renamed to "Agentforce Max Editions"; sub-edition list updated from Sales, Service, Field Service and Operations, and Industries to Sales, Service, Sales/Service, and Industries; Agentforce Max Editions (formerly Agentforce 1 Editions) Flex Credits allowance increased from 2.5M to 2.75M per org per year. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed, limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Agentforce Max Editions at $550, Flex Credits at $500 per 100k Credits, Conversations at $2 per conversation, Agentforce add-ons at $125 and Agentforce Industries add-ons at $150.
It lands 183 days after Salesforce's previous change, "Salesforce Agentforce rebrands buying models to PayGo and PreCommit" (Apr 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Salesforce Agentforce Launches Agentforce Voice: New AI Agent Capability for Enhanced Customer Interaction in US & Canada" (Jan 12, 2026), "Salesforce Agentforce Restructures Flex Credits Pricing and Introduces New Agentforce User License at $5/User/Month" (Q3 2025) and "Salesforce Agentforce Removes Free Foundations Tier, Introduces New Agentforce Add-ons and Flex Credits Plan" (Q2 2025). Salesforce has been tracked since 2025-01-03.
