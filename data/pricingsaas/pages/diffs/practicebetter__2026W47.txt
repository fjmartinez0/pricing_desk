What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Practice Better's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Nutrition Planning add-on at $15/month per practitioner, available on all paid plans, with a 35% off promo for 2 months if added by Dec 31, 2026. Before that Pulse recorded "Practice Better launches Connector Hub add-on for $49/mo QuickBooks sync" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Practice Better swaps free AI Charting minutes for $10-$40 monthly tiers" (Sep 14, 2026). Practice Better has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
