What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of OpenRouter's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The 'Pay-as-you-go' plan was renamed to 'Standard' across the page (nav tabs, table headers, meta description, FAQ copy). Billing model and Platform Fee (5.5%) are unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed) with a neutral direction.
It lands 7 days after OpenRouter's previous change, "OpenRouter adds Business tier: 8% fee, 1,000-workspace limit, signed DPA" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenRouter expands catalog to 500+ models, 80+ providers across pricing tiers" (Aug 17, 2026) and "OpenRouter swaps free BYOK request quotas for dollar-based fee thresholds" (Jul 6, 2026). OpenRouter has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
