What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of cPanel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Premier availability expanded from Cloud/VPS only to Cloud/VPS & Metal/Dedicated. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Solo at $27.46, Admin at $32.96, Pro at $49.46, Premier at $69.99 and WP Squared at $84.99.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for cPanel. cPanel has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Vultr, Vercel, Sumologic, SuperOps and Supabase also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
