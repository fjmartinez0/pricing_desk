What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Digits's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added Quality Checks/Advanced Quality Checks and Roles & Permissions as included capabilities across Essentials, Core, and Pro; gated Custom Reports and Custom Dashboards behind a new "Firm partners" referral for Essentials/Core (Pro keeps direct access); removed the Revenue & Spend Integrations row (Stripe, Ramp, BILL connections) from the plan comparison grid. In detail: Feature grid overhaul: added Quality Checks & Roles/Permissions to all plans; gated Custom Reports/Dashboards behind 'Firm partners' for Essentials/Core; removed Revenue & Spend Integrations row. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essentials at $65, Core at $100 and Pro at $250.
It lands 105 days after Digits's previous change, "Digits renames AI Close Agents to Agentic Close in Pro plan" (Jun 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Digits reveals accounting firm pricing tiers on public page" (Jun 1, 2026) and "Digits replaces Custom tier with $250/mo Pro plan for finance teams" (May 12, 2026). Digits has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
