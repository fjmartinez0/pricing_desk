What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Helply's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: flat $0.50/outcome (all features free) replaced by Starter $49, Pro $199, Scale $449, Enterprise Custom plans, each with a credit block priced by AI task complexity (Low 1/Medium 4/High 12 credits); SSO, audit log, custom roles, and HIPAA moved from free-for-everyone to gated by plan tier. In detail: Replaced flat $0.50/outcome pricing (all free) with 4 tiered plans ($49/$199/$449/Custom) + credit blocks (Low 1/Med 4/High 12 credits); SSO, audit log, roles & HIPAA now gated by tier, not free for all. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $42, Pro at $169 and Scale at $382.
It lands 7 days after Helply's previous change, "Helply swaps $1/ticket pricing for $0.50 per outcome, cuts minimum to $99/mo" (Sep 21, 2026). Helply has been tracked since 2026-09-08.
