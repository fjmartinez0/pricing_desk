What happened
Between the Nov 14, 2026 and Nov 15, 2026 captures of HeyGen's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Avatar engine lineup refreshed: Avatar IV/V replaced by Avatar 6, Avatar 6 Turbo and Avatar Lipsync; new Pro-only Avatar 6 Pro tier added. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Creator at $24, Pro at $41 and Business at $119.
It lands one day after HeyGen's previous change, "HeyGen Pro plan now includes free Professional Voice Clone, once $29/mo" (Nov 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HeyGen adds Voice TTS and Seedance 2.5 to Creator and Pro, prices flat" (Oct 23, 2026) and "HeyGen caps Voice Cloning usage on Creator, Pro, and Business plans" (Sep 19, 2026). HeyGen has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
