What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Tray.ai's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro, Team and Enterprise price slot copy changed from 'Talk to sales' to 'Sized to your needs'; plan CTAs now read 'Get a clear quote'. No numeric prices shown either side. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction.
It lands 6 days after Tray.ai's previous change, "Tray.ai lists a $50-a-month entry price with no per-user or connection fees" (Oct 26, 2026). Tray.ai has been tracked since 2026-05-07.
In the same window UiPath, Zapier, Workato, Make and MuleSoft also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
