What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of MSG91's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Hello's BOT AI Chat rate card grew from 1 model (My-claude, $0) to 100+ LLM models (GPT-5.x, Claude Opus/Sonnet, Gemini 3.x, Grok-4, DeepSeek, Llama-4, Mistral, Qwen, Kimi), priced per 1M input/output tokens.
