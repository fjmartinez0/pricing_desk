What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of AllTrails's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plus membership's 5th feature tile was rewritten from 'Send Routes to Garmin' (upload routes to a Garmin device) to 'Connect Garmin & COROS' (sync wearable for routes, plus activity tracking); the standalone Garmin logo badge on the tile was also removed. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Plus at $2.99.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for AllTrails. AllTrails has been tracked since 2026-06-19.
