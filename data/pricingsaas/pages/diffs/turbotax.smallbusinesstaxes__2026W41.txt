What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of TurboTax's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New TurboTax Expert Assist Personal plan added at $209 (+ state additional) for personal tax filing with Schedule C side-gig income, as a lower-cost alternative to year-round business support. In detail: New cross-sell card added below the Business plans: TurboTax Expert Assist (personal, Premium tier) at $209 (+ state additional) for personal tax filing with Schedule C side-gig income, positioned as a cheaper alternative for filers who do not need year-round business support. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Expert 365 Business at $99, Expert Assist Business - Sole Proprietors at $229 subscription, Expert Full Service Business - Sole Proprietors at $249 subscription, Expert Assist Business - S-Corps at $639 subscription and Expert Assist Business - Partnerships at $639 subscription.
It lands 49 days after TurboTax's previous change, "TurboTax Smallbusinesstaxes drops 30-day free trial for Expert 365 Business" (Aug 17, 2026). TurboTax has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
