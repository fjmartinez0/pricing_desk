What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Workleap's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Standard and Pro cards drop 'Starting at' and now show a flat annual base plus a per-manager fee: Standard $4,999 USD +$32/manager/month, Pro $11,999 USD +$32/manager/month. The comparison table header still says 'Starting at'; Comparison table row 'Streamlined compensation review workflow' (Enterprise only) replaced by 'Compensation Benchmarking' (Coming soon), now included on Pro and Enterprise. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $4,999 and Pro at $11,999.
It lands 83 days after Workleap's previous change, "Workleap: 3 compensation features go live; Pay equity analytics cut" (Jul 27, 2026). Workleap has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
In the same window BambooHR, SAP SE and Lattice also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
