What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of PreVeil's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: BUSINESS plan's compliance bullet reworded from a broad claim (HIPAA, FERPA, SOC 2, GLB, FTC, IRS standards) to a narrower claim citing only HIPAA, the FTC Safeguards Rule (GLBA), and IRS Pub 4557. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Individual at $25, Business at $30 and PreVeil Pass at $450.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for PreVeil. PreVeil has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
