What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of ToolJet's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan AI app generation limit raised from up to 2 pages to unlimited pages in the comparison table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Basic at $23, Pro at $79, Team at $199 and Pay-as-you-go AI Credits at $1 per 100 AI credits.
It lands 7 days after ToolJet's previous change, "ToolJet triples entry-tier app limits, adds MCP coding-agent plugin, blocks C" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ToolJet adds $23 Basic plan and credit-free Claude Code, Codex, Cursor builds" (Sep 14, 2026). ToolJet has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
In the same window Salesforce, Retool, OutSystems, Microsoft and Mendix also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
