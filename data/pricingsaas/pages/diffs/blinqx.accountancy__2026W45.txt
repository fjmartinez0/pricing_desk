What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Blinqx's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Rapportage module Jaarrekeningen (annual accounts) split into 'without dashboard' at 1 credit and 'with dashboard' at 2 credits; previously a single item at 2 credits. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Wealth Monitor at EUR 50 per use, Lijfrenteplanner at EUR 41.50 per use, Prognose & budgetten at EUR 20 per dossier, Robotisering ODV at EUR 11.50 per dossier and Benaderde Marktwaarde (BMW) at EUR 96.25 per use.
It lands 6 days after Blinqx's previous change, "Blinqx Accountancy: Credit scale extended with tier 6 (3,500 credits, €700) and tier 7 (5,000 credits, €1.000), both 80%…" (Oct 26, 2026). Blinqx has been tracked since 2026-06-03.
