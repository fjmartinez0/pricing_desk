What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Clarify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter swaps Multiple connected accounts for Custom objects (up to 2); Multiple connected accounts moves up to Growth, which also gains Workspace agents. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $40 and Growth at $1,000.
It lands 42 days after Clarify's previous change, "Clarify caps Growth plan at 750k records, locks SSO behind Enterprise" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Clarify rebuilds pricing: Growth revealed at $1,000/mo, comparison table gone" (Sep 7, 2026). Clarify has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
In the same window Zoho, HubSpot, Microsoft, Salesforce and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
