What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Apploye's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Activity Insights add-on at $3/user/month (included in Power), covering productive/unproductive app & URL categorization and suspicious-activity/mouse-jiggler detection that Elite previously listed as included (coming soon); Stealth Mode now included in Power instead of costing $2/user/month. Plan prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Elite at $4.5, Power at $8, More Screenshots at $2, Video Recording at $4 and Dedicated Onboarding at $150 subscription.
It lands 56 days after Apploye's previous change, "Apploye adds BAA to Enterprise plan, boosting compliance coverage" (Aug 24, 2026). Apploye has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window BambooHR, 15five and Lattice also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
