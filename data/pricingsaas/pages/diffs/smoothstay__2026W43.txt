What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of SmoothStay's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Host Plan now states each extra unit costs $3.90 (billed yearly) after the first unit at $5.90; previously only the first-unit price was shown; Host Plan now advertises a 60-day money-back guarantee (Smooth Start Guarantee) in place of 'Cancel anytime'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Host Plan at $5.9.
It lands 7 days after SmoothStay's previous change, "SmoothStay adds annual billing to Host plan at $5.90/mo, drops per-unit price" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SmoothStay swaps flat $6.90 rate for tiered pricing, adds $2.90 per extra unit" (Oct 5, 2026), "SmoothStay renames volume-discount prompt to match Units metric label" (Sep 21, 2026) and "SmoothStay adds PMS integrations and webhooks to Host Plan" (Aug 17, 2026). SmoothStay has been tracked since 2026-03-03.
In the same window Guesty also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
