Canopy bundles Coworker AI into every plan, no add-on or price hike
Verified Nov 9, 2026·Finance & Accounting·Direction: up
The changeStandard, Plus, Premium feature — feature added.
1 change·2 captures·Nov 2, 2026 → Nov 9, 2026
What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Canopy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Canopy Coworker (Everywhere AI) is now listed as an included feature on Standard, Plus, and Premium plans; FAQ confirms it's included in every plan with monthly AI credits per user and no separate AI add-on needed. Plan prices unchanged ($74/$109/$149). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $74, Plus at $109, Premium at $149, Knowledge Based Authentication (KBA) at $1.25 per KBA credit and Tax Workflow Automation at $34 per client.
It lands 7 days after Canopy's previous change, "Canopy reworks Tax Workflow Automation bullets around AI intake" (Nov 2, 2026). Canopy has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Yooz, Xero, TradingView, UKKO.fi and Taxdome also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
