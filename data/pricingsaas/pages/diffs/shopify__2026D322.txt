What happened
Between the Nov 17, 2026 and Nov 18, 2026 captures of Shopify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Introduced 'Tax managed by Shopify' with a new Tax calculation fee (0.35% on Basic/Grow/Advanced, 0.25% on Plus) after an introductory discount; added a Self-managed tax mode option in the Tax feature table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change). The current page lists Basic at $29, Grow at $79, Advanced at $299, Plus at $2,300 and POS Pro at $89.
It lands 21 days after Shopify's previous change, "Shopify drops free Shopify Academy courses perk from all four pricing plans" (Oct 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shopify simplifies in-person sales messaging, adds plan comparison rows" (Oct 23, 2026), "Shopify cuts Grow to $79 and Advanced to $299, reversing D288 hike" (Oct 16, 2026) and "Shopify raises annual Grow to $89/mo and Advanced to $359/mo" (Oct 15, 2026). Shopify has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window BigCommerce, Squarespace and Salesforce also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
