What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Dash Social's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Engage ($999/mo) and Advance ($1,999/mo) prices removed from public display; both tiers now show "Get a Demo" only. In detail: Engage ($999/month) and Advance ($1,999/month) plan prices removed from the pricing page; both tiers now show only a 'Get a Demo' CTA, matching Enterprise's contact-sales pricing. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Advance at $1,999.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Dash Social. Dash Social has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
