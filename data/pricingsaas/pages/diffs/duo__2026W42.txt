What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Duo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Agentic Visibility & Analytics added to Advantage (new) and Premier; Premier's alpha 'Agentic Identity & Access Management' renamed Agentic Governance, alpha flag removed. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Duo Essentials at $3, Duo Advantage at $6 and Duo Premier at $9.
It lands the same day as Duo's previous change, "Duo Editionsandpricing adds Agentic Visibility & Analytics to Advantage and Premier" (Oct 12, 2026). Duo has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Microsoft, Slack, SignalHire and Zoom also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
