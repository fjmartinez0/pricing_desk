What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Omniconvert's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI A/B Testing capability on the Free plan reversed direction — was "code to prompt", now "prompt to code". In detail: AI A/B Testing capability on the Free plan reversed direction: previously converted code into a prompt, now converts a prompt into code. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pulse Expert at $24.17 and Explore at $245.
It lands 7 days after Omniconvert's previous change, "Omniconvert adds AI A/B testing—turn prompts into tests, no code needed" (Sep 28, 2026). Omniconvert has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
