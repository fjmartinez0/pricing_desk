What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of SendBridge's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Replaced one-time pay-as-you-go credit purchases with recurring monthly subscription tiers (Free $0, Starter $19, Growth $49, Scale $99, Business $249/mo, Custom $290/mo) plus a Monthly/Annual toggle saving 20%. Max one-time top-up pack size dropped from 10,000,000 credits ($1,199.00) to 500,000 credits ($1,199). Paid tiers now explicitly bundle team seats (2-25), priority support, longer data retention (3-24mo), and more blacklist/DMARC monitor slots. In detail: Replaced pay-as-you-go one-time credit purchases with recurring monthly subscription tiers (Free $0, Starter $19, Growth $49, Scale $99, Business $249/mo) plus a Monthly/Annual toggle saving 20%; Max one-time top-up pack size dropped from 10,000,000 credits ($1,199.00) to 500,000 credits ($1,199) — a ~20x reduction in credits per dollar for bulk one-time purchases; New paid tiers add team seats (2–25), priority support, longer data retention (3–24 months), and more blacklist/DMARC monitoring slots across Starter through Business plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change, limit lowered, feature added). The current page lists Starter at $15.17, Growth at $39.17, Scale at $79.17, Business at $199.17 and Custom at $290.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for SendBridge. SendBridge has been tracked since 2026-10-29.
