What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Koala's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI image generation model updated from GPT Image 2.5 to GPT Image 2.6 (alongside Nano Banana 2). Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $20, Professional at $39, Boost at $79, Growth at $143 and Elite at $280.
It lands 14 days after Koala's previous change, "Koala launches Free plan, adds $29 brand slots and 3-month credit rollover" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Koala updates word-count footnote to name GPT-6.1 Sol as 2x model" (Oct 19, 2026), "Koala: AI MCP integration exits beta, now included with every paid plan" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Koala hikes entry plan to $25/mo, adds Brand DNA & agent chat" (Sep 21, 2026). Koala has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Notion and Bearly AI also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
