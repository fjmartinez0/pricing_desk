SimpleroPackaging change · Metric change · Oct 26 → Nov 2, 2026
Simplero makes inactive contacts free, counting only active ones toward limits
Verified Nov 2, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral
The changePricing model — metric change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 26, 2026 → Nov 2, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Simplero's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: All three plans now count only 'active' contacts toward their limits — people with a purchase, subscription, tag, running automation, or recent site activity. Inactive contacts are free and no longer count. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $59, Scale at $149 and Skyrocket at $249.
It lands 28 days after Simplero's previous change, "Simplero adds Shopping cart to Scale plan's included features" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Simplero slashes Starter plan site allowance in half, to just 1" (Sep 14, 2026). Simplero has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Thinkific, Leadpages, Podia, Circle and Mighty Networks also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
