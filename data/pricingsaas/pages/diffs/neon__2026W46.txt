Neon opens AI Gateway to Free plan users with $5 prepaid credits
Verified Nov 9, 2026·Cloud & DevOps·Direction: up
The changeLaunch, Scale feature — feature added.
1 change·2 captures·Nov 2, 2026 → Nov 9, 2026
What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Neon's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Gateway (LLM/model API access) is now available on the Free plan, previously restricted to Launch and Scale. Free users enable it by adding a card and buying prepaid credits ($5 minimum); the account stays on the Free plan. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Launch at $0.11 per CU-hour + $0.20 per GB-month (history/instant restore storage) and Scale at $0.20 per GB-month (history/instant restore storage) + $0.35 per GB-month (database storage).
It lands 7 days after Neon's previous change, "Neon reframes its every-plan feature grid around the backend suite" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Neon adds Realtime (Beta) to Free, Launch and Scale plans, free during beta" (Oct 19, 2026), "Neon doubles Free plan storage to 1 GB per project, up from 0.5 GB" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Neon: spending limits replaced with alert-only notifications" (Aug 17, 2026). Neon has been tracked since 2026-04-26.
In the same window Google Cloud, Vercel, Aws and Digitalocean also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
