What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Devin's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Team plan seat allowance capped: 'Unlimited team members' / 'Unlimited flex seats' changed to 'Up to 200 users', shown in both the main pricing card and the Compare Plans table.
