What happened
Between the Oct 7, 2026 and Oct 8, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: GPU Clusters reserved pricing first term bracket changed from 7-30 days to 3-30 days (shorter minimum reservation); prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Managed Storage at $0.16 per GiB/month, Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, Sandbox (Code Interpreter) at $0.03 per session (60 min), GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100) and GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days).
It lands 2 days after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI cuts Kimi K3 token prices 10% while Qwen3.8 Flash rises" (Oct 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI adds Tev1 4B Experimental at $0.04 input with free output tokens" (Oct 2, 2026), "Together AI reveals NVIDIA HGX B300 pricing at $9.99/GPU/hr, $4.99 preemptible" (Sep 25, 2026) and "Together AI cuts Qwen3.7-Max and Qwen3.8 Flash inference prices by 40%" (Sep 23, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
