What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Clay's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise adds User Groups, SCIM Provisioning, IP Allowlists, Workflow and Workbook Limits and Connection Controls (card and comparison table); 'Workbook-level credit budget' broadened to Credit Budgeting, SSO relabeled SSO/SAML, and Custom CSA/DPA (already in the table) now also listed on the card. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Launch at $54 and Growth at $185.
It lands 3 days after Clay's previous change, "Clay expands Enterprise plan with SCIM, IP allowlists and user groups" (Oct 16, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Clay: email sequencer access shifts from Free to Launch/Growth tiers" (Sep 7, 2026), "Clay reshuffles email tiers: Free drops sequencer bullet, Growth adds domains" (Sep 2, 2026) and "Clay: Growth plan ad audiences now unlimited (was 1)" (Aug 10, 2026). Clay has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
