What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Mailercloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise card replaces 'Dedicated IPs' with 'Deliverability expert support'; Dedicated account manager unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed). The current page lists Premium at $7.50.
It lands 7 days after Mailercloud's previous change, "Mailercloud trims annual billing discount from 30% to 25%, renames Email API tab" (Nov 15, 2026). Mailercloud has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Getresponse, Convertkit, ActiveCampaign, HubSpot and Mailchimp also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
