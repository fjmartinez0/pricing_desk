What happened
Between the Nov 20, 2026 and Nov 21, 2026 captures of Baseten's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added two new models to Model APIs token pricing: bison-1 at $0.10/1M input tokens and groundhog-1 at $0.035/1M input tokens. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 23, 2026) lists Pro at $1 and Enterprise at $1.

Before that Pulse recorded "Baseten launches Serverless Loops training with 20% off GLM-5.3 and Qwen3.6" (Nov 7, 2026), "Baseten adds $0.16/GB/month Volumes storage to Sandboxes pricing" (Nov 6, 2026) and "Baseten adds Kimi K3.1 Pro, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and joey-1 at $0.01/1M tokens" (Nov 5, 2026).
