What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Vendr's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Advanced Procure-to-Pay Capabilities and Autonomous Negotiation Agent (ANA) no longer labeled as Add-Ons; now listed as plain features on the Intake-to-Procure and SaaS Purchasing plans. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Platform + Autonomous at $12,000, Platform at $12,000 and Platform + Advisory at $12,000.
It lands 131 days after Vendr's previous change, "Vendr rebrands under Vertice, softens $15B data claim" (Jun 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vendr expands deal database to 130K+ for enhanced supplier insights" (Mar 18, 2026), "Vendr consolidates two-tier model into unified Platform Access" (Mar 11, 2026) and "Vendr ditches employee tiers for agent-based pricing model" (Feb 23, 2026). Vendr has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
