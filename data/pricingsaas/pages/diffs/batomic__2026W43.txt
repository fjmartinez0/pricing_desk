What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of b atomic's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: On-demand remarkets ($8/$7/$6 per remarket per carrier, 2-business-day SLA) removed from all three suites; only automated renewal remarkets at $6 remain, and the cost calculator is gone. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro Suite at $0.83 per policy per month (endorsements) + $7 per policy per carrier (on-demand remarket), Starter Suite at $0.5 per policy per month (endorsements) + $8 per policy per carrier (on-demand remarket) and Enterprise Suite at $1.16 per policy per month (endorsements) + $6 per policy per carrier (on-demand remarket).
It lands 14 days after b atomic's previous change, "b atomic replaces volume pricing with 3 per-policy tiers from 50¢ to $1.16" (Oct 5, 2026). b atomic has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
