What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Casemark's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Overhauled AI usage billing into a dollar allowance with 30% overage premium and spend limits; added a $0 free trial, revealed $200/user/month BAA pricing, and launched a $20/month Document Manager seat add-on; Pro plan's monthly AI usage allowance increased from $80 in AI credits to $100 in included usage, a 25% capacity increase at the same $100/user/month price; Teams and Enterprise gained a. In detail: Teams and Enterprise gained a shared usage allocation and Enterprise gained concierge onboarding; Enterprise dropped the '10+ users' minimum in favor of custom team sizing. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, package change, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Standard at $20 and Pro at $100.
It lands 28 days after Casemark's previous change, "CaseMark: Pro plan legal agent skills library expanded (800+ → 880+)" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Casemark rebrands Court Reporting as Source, revamps Workspace Pro to $100/us" (Jul 13, 2026), "Casemark replaces team tiers with $100/mo solo Pro plan" (Jun 29, 2026) and "CaseMark: restructured from usage-based pricing to tiered Workspace plans ($100/mo Pro)" (Jul 2, 2026). Casemark has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window Everlaw, LexisNexis, Relativity and Logikcull also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
