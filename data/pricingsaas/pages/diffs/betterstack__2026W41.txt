What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Better Stack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI SRE add-on moved from a flat $5 per million tokens to consumption-based billing with per-model prices (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, GLM, Gemini, Kimi), shown in-app. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Responder at $29, Telemetry Tera at $420, Metrics at $0.5 per GB retained per month, Web Events at $0.1 per GB ingested + $0.05 per GB retained per month and Heartbeats at $17.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Better Stack. Better Stack has been tracked since 2026-09-08.
