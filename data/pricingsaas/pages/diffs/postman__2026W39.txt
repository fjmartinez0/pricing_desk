What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Postman's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan's public price of $49/user/month (billed annually) was removed and replaced with 'Contact sales for pricing'; Get Started and Buy with AWS purchase options were also removed for Enterprise. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Solo at $9, Team at $19, Enterprise at $0.04 per AI credit + $0.04 per VuH (cloud performance testing) and Simple Security at $6.
It lands 2 days after Postman's previous change, "Postman pulls $49 Enterprise pricing, moves plan behind contact sales" (Sep 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Postman adds free Context Graph tracking, metered API query overages" (Sep 23, 2026), "Postman adds Cloud performance testing ($0.04/VuH)" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Postman unbundles cloud load testing into a $0.04/VuH usage-based add-on" (Aug 29, 2026). Postman has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Konghq also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
