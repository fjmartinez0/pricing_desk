What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Envato's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Plus and Ultimate AI model lineup updated: GPT Image 2→2.5, Nano Banana 2→2.1, HappyHorse 1.1 replaced by WAN 3.0; prices unchanged; Core and Enterprise cards gain 'Choose from the latest AI models'; new FAQ says Core, Plus and Ultimate all access the same image and video model selection. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change, feature added). The current page lists Core at $16.50, Plus at $39 and Ultimate at $168.
It lands 77 days after Envato's previous change, "Envato swaps AI model lineup: GPT Image 2, Gemini Omni, Seedance 2.0 added" (Aug 30, 2026). Envato has been tracked since 2026-06-04.
In the same window Shutterstock and Getty Images also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
