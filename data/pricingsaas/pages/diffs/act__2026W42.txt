What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Act!'s pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added RingCentral integration (calling, texting, customer communications) to Professional/Ultimate plan feature list and front-office apps table; separately, 'SMS Text Messaging' was narrowed and renamed to 'SMS Appointment Reminders' (automated reminders only, no longer general promotional/order-update texting). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Act! Advantage Standard at $30, Act! Premium Desktop at $40, Act! Advantage Professional at $45, Act! Advantage Ultimate at $60 and Desktop Sync at $10.
It lands 49 days after Act!'s previous change, "Act! adds Enterprise Engagement tier for security-heavy, call-to-deploy orgs" (Aug 24, 2026). Act! has been tracked since 2026-06-24.
In the same window Salesforce, Microsoft, HubSpot and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
