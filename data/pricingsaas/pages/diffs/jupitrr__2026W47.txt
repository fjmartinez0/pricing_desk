What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Jupitrr's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Starter price cut: annual-billed $14.50/mo to $11/mo (monthly list $29 to $22); yearly total $174 to $132; Starter videos per month reduced from 10 to 5 (uploads and exports). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease, limit lowered) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $14.50, Pro at $24.50 and Team at $89.
It lands 7 days after Jupitrr's previous change, "Jupitrr raises Starter annual price 32% to $14.50/mo, adds Levio AI credits" (Nov 9, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Jupitrr cuts Starter to $15 and Pro to $33, drops $299 Video Accelerator" (Oct 11, 2026). Jupitrr has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Hootsuite, Buffer, Sproutsocial, ContentStudio and MeetEdgar also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
