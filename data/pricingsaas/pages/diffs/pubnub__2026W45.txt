What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of PubNub's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Serverless Functions no longer listed on pricing cards: Free lost '5 test Serverless Functions, 7-day runtime'; Starter lost '30 Serverless Functions'. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $98.
It lands 21 days after PubNub's previous change, "PubNub adds Serverless Functions to plans: 30 on Starter, 5 test functions free" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "PubNub axes Serverless Functions from Free and Starter plan features" (Sep 28, 2026) and "PubNub: Serverless Functions added to Free & Starter plans" (Sep 7, 2026). PubNub has been tracked since 2026-05-13.
In the same window Supabase and Twilio also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
