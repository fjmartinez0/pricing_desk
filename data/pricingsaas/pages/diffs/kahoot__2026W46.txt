What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Kahoot!'s pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro Start's struck-through intro-offer price ($19/mo, $228/yr) was removed from the pricing page; it now shows a flat $9/month ($108/year) with no discount comparison, unlike Standard/Plus/Ultra which still show theirs. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Kahoot! 360 Pro Start at $9, Kahoot! 360 Spirit at $20, Kahoot! 360 Pro Standard at $25, Kahoot! 360 Spirit Premium at $25 and Kahoot! 360 Pro Plus at $39.
It lands 7 days after Kahoot!'s previous change, "Kahoot! slashes Pro Start annual price 53% to $9/mo with new coupon" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kahoot! revives Pro Max price to $79/mo, slashes Pro Plus to $39/mo intro" (Sep 28, 2026), "Kahoot! flattens Pro Ultra participant cap to 5,000, drops onboarding gate" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Kahoot! Pro Plus loses intro discount, price jumps to flat $49/mo" (Aug 24, 2026). Kahoot! has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Duo and Mentimeter also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
