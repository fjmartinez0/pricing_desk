What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of KickoffLabs's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free plan monthly allowance cut from 100 uniques to 50 leads per month; campaign email sending now noted as off on Free; New 'Branded QR codes' row added to the comparison table: every page gets a brand-colored QR code (PNG/SVG downloads); Hobby gets the KickoffLabs beaker logo, Premium/Business/Enterprise get your logo or initials. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, freemium change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Hobby at $13, Premium at $48, Business at $99, Enterprise at $202 and SMS Contests Add-on at $50.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for KickoffLabs. KickoffLabs has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
