What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of CoffeePals's pricing page, Pulse recorded 5 changes: CoffeePals launched a new Max tier at $79/month for 1-25 active users, slotted between Pro and Enterprise and carrying the 'Most Popular' badge. Max bundles Pulse surveys, advanced matching, connection analytics, report & data exports, AI summarization and HRIS user import; Enterprise's inclusion line moved from 'Everything in Pro' to 'Everything in Max'; Pro rose from $45/month to $49/month for. In detail: Pro rose from $45/month to $49/month for 1-25 active users, an 8.9% increase; Pro's '14-day free trial' badge was removed and its CTA changed from 'Start Free Trial' to 'Get Started' - the page no longer advertises a free trial on any paid plan; The yearly billing toggle's savings badge changed from a flat 'Save 10%' to 'Save up to 20%'; Pro's 'Advanced matching rules' bullet became 'Cross-group & lottery matching', and 'Full analytics dashboard' became 'Surveys & core reporting', as those capabilities moved up to the new Max tier. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, trial change, discount change, new plan, feature change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $49 and Max at $79.
It lands 80 days after CoffeePals's previous change, "CoffeePals: Pro plan price decreased ($49→$45/month)" (Jul 2, 2026). CoffeePals has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window SmoothStay, Slack, Sinch, Moodle and Portant also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
