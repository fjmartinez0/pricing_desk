What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Pagerduty's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plus plan seats increased from 40 to 50; AI Actions quotas (5,000/50,000/100,000) removed from Starter/Essential/Ultimate plan cards and the AI Actions row was deleted from the comparison table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Professional at $21, Business at $41, Operations Cloud Essential at $1,000, Status Pages at $89 and PagerDuty Advance for Incident Management at $415.
It lands 91 days after Pagerduty's previous change, "Pagerduty rebrands AI credits as token-based "AI Actions," adds 4 named agents" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pagerduty Aiops Advance add-on debuts 4 named Agentic AI agents" (Jul 6, 2026), "Pagerduty Service renames Advance credits to 'AI Actions' across all plans" (Jul 6, 2026) and "PagerDuty: integration catalog expanded 700+ → 750+" (Jul 4, 2026). Pagerduty has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Atlassian, Opsgenie, Grafana and Splunk also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
