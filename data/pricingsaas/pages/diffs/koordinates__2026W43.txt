What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Koordinates's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: AI credit unit rebased 100x: included/per-seat AI credits for AI Pro User went from 200 to 20,000, and top-up tiers from 200/525/2,200 credits to 20,000/52,500/220,000 credits at the same $12/$29/$117 prices; US Data Index (Labs feature included with AI Pro User) layer count increased from over 950k to over 1 million Esri layers across America. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists AI Pro User at $15, Enterprise at $64, Team Cloud Plan at $217, Business Cloud Plan at $701 and Agent at $49.
It lands 108 days after Koordinates's previous change, "Koordinates: launched 'AI Pro User' plan at $15/mo for MCP-based AI/geospatial access" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Koordinates launches AI Pro plan with storage and API usage tiers" (May 26, 2026). Koordinates has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
