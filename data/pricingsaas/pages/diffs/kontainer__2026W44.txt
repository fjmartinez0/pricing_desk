What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Kontainer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Kontainer AI add-on module: +$120 (1TB) on Professional, +$230 (2TB) on Premium, +$380 (5TB) on Enterprise. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Core at $500, Professional at $695, Premium at $1,155 and Enterprise at $2,220.
