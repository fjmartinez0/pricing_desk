What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Xero's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New-customer promo reduced from 90% off for 6 months (ended Nov 2) to 80% off for 3 months; promo prices rose (Early $2.70→$5.40, Growing $5.90→$11.80, Established $9.70→$19.40/mo). List prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Early at $27, Growing at $59 and Established at $97.
It lands 14 days after Xero's previous change, "Xero doubles new-customer promo to 90% off first 6 months through Nov 2" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Xero raises plan prices up to 8% and trims new-customer promo to 80% off" (Oct 5, 2026), "Xero swaps Syft branding for own Xero Analytics on pricing page" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Xero graduates AI auto-reconcile from Beta to full release" (Aug 17, 2026). Xero has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
