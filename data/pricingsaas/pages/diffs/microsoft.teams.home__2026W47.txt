What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Microsoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added a limited-time 30% promo (site-wide banner): Microsoft 365 Personal annual price cut from $99.99 to $69.99 for the first year, and Premium from $199.99 to $139.99 for the first year. Family plan unaffected. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Microsoft 365 Personal at $8.33, Microsoft 365 Family at $10.83 and Microsoft 365 Premium at $16.67.
Microsoft's previous recorded change was "Microsoft Teams Home cuts Family and Premium storage from 6 TB to 2 TB shared" (Oct 11, 2026). Microsoft has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window Salesforce and Google also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
