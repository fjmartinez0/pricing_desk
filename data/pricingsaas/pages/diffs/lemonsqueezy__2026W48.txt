What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Lemonsqueezy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Lemon Squeezy now states it is no longer accepting new sign-ups and will shut down March 31, 2027; the 2026 Stripe Managed Payments update banner was replaced. Listed fees (5% + 50c, $0/mo) unchanged. In detail: Lemon Squeezy now states it is no longer accepting new sign-ups and will shut down March 31, 2027, replacing the 2026 Stripe Managed Payments update banner. The "Get started" CTAs now point to Stripe Managed Payments ("Start with Managed Payments"), and the "Contact us for volume-based pricing" note was dropped. Listed fees (5% + 50¢ per transaction, email marketing $0/mo) unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Email Marketing at $8.33 and Ecommerce at 5% per transaction + $0.50 per transaction (flat fee).
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Lemonsqueezy. Lemonsqueezy has been tracked since 2025-11-05.
