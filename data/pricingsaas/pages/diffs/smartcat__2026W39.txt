What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Smartcat's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Compare plans table restructured: added Compliance Coworker and Custom coworkers rows; split Automations into workflow-limit rows; renamed Governance rows (Reports & apps, Organization Admin, Data Residency); Smartwords balance sharing improved for Anticipate/Autonomous (now shared across workspaces vs workspace-bound); Automations row replaced with explicit Global (1/2/6/30) and Personal (0/1/2/3) workflow limits.
