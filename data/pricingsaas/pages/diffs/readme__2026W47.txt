What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Readme's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Translations added to the Enterprise card's AI feature list (All Pro AI features, plus: ...), flagged NEW; previously only in the comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Pro at $250, Ask AI at $150 and Extended History & Logs at $100.
It lands 14 days after Readme's previous change, "Readme caps free Starter metrics at 7 days, adds Enterprise Ask AI API" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Readme debuts Slack AI Writer in Beta, joining GitHub AI Writer" (Oct 26, 2026), "ReadMe: Rebase & Resolve Conflicts + MCP Metrics added, Themes redesign" (Sep 28, 2026) and "ReadMe: Multiplayer editing (Pro) + Aggregated doc metrics (Enterprise) added" (Sep 7, 2026). Readme has been tracked since 2024-10-07.
