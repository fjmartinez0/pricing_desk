What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Benchmarkemail's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free plan monthly email send cap cut from 2,500 (5x contacts) to 1,000 emails per month; paid plans still send up to 10x contact limit; Pro plan adds a new 500-contact entry tier at $12/mo ($10/mo billed annually); previously Pro started at 1,000 contacts for $19/mo ($16/mo annual). Pro contact range now 500 - 100,000. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change, threshold added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro Plan at $10.
It lands 21 days after Benchmarkemail's previous change, "Benchmarkemail adds Brand Kit to every plan, keeping logos, colors and…" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Benchmarkemail: API access and list verification added to plan comparison" (Aug 17, 2026). Benchmarkemail has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp, Convertkit, Klaviyo and Constant Contact also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
