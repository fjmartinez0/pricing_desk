What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Fireworks' pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Kimi K3.1 Pro added to highlighted serverless rates: $2.00 input / $0.20 cached / $10.00 output per 1M tokens; priority $3.00 / $0.30 / $15.00. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 17, 2026) lists Serverless at $0.08 per 10M input tokens (embeddings, up to 150M params) + $0.16 per 10M input tokens (embeddings, 150M-350M params), Fine Tuning at $0.50 per 1M training tokens, LoRA SFT (models up to 16B params) and On-Demand at $13 per GPU hour (B200 180GB) + $15 per GPU hour (B300 288GB).
This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Fireworks lists Kimi K3.1 Pro on serverless at $2.00 input, $10.00 output per 1M" (Day 318, 2026, saved Nov 14, 2026). It is the same change, not a new one. Before that, Fireworks' previous recorded change was "Fireworks adds SOC 2, ISO 27001, 27701 and 42001 badges to pricing page" (Oct 26, 2026). Fireworks has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Figma, Corel, Affinity and Sketch also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
