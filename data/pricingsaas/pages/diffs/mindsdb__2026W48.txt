What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New models listed: GPT-6.2 Astra ($8/$40 per 1M tokens), GPT-6.2 Sol ($2/$10), and a paid-only Image models table (GPT Image 2.6 Flare and Sunburst, $5 text in/$8 image in/$30 image out). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Pay as you go at $0.10 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.50 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "MindsDB: added GPT-6.2 Astra & Sol models + new Image models category" (Day 323, 2026, saved Nov 19, 2026). It is the same change, not a new one. Before that, MindsDB's previous recorded change was "MindsDB lists Muse Ember 1.0 at $0.10 input / $0.50 output per 1M tokens" (Nov 11, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window RapidMiner, Huggingface and Alteryx also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
