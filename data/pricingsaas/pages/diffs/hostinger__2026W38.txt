What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Hostinger's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Premium plan gained Ecommerce support (previously excluded); 'Integrated ecommerce' was renamed to 'Ecommerce available' on all plan cards, and the Ecommerce row was dropped from the comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Premium at $2.99, Unlimited at $3.99, Cloud Startup at $9.99, Agency Startup at $29 and Agency Professional at $44.
It lands 21 days after Hostinger's previous change, "Hostinger drops Summer Sale banner as Unlimited plan price ticks up to $3.99/mo" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hostinger unlocks unlimited mailboxes per site on Cloud Startup plan" (Jul 20, 2026) and "Hostinger: Business plan renamed Unlimited, price cut $3.99→$3.79/mo" (Jul 13, 2026). Hostinger has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Name.com, Hostgator and Dreamhost also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
