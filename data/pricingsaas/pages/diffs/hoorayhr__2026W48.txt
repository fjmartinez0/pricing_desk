What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of HoorayHR's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Year-end offer: free Hiring (ATS) add-on for Plus subscribers who choose before 31 Dec, available from Feb 2027; new Hiring (ATS) add-on listed at €2.00/user/month (Soon); Plus plan no longer lists 'Own branding & multi-language'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature removed, discount added) with a neutral direction.
It lands the same day as HoorayHR's previous change, "HoorayHR offers free ATS with Plus upgrades before Dec 31" (Nov 22, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HoorayHR adds AI section with MCP and Meeting Agent to plan comparison" (Oct 26, 2026), "HoorayHR launches MCP integration in Plus plan's AI features" (Sep 28, 2026) and "HoorayHR: 1-on-1 conversation logging removed from Core plan" (Sep 13, 2026).
