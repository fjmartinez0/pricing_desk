What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Oxylabs's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Mobile Proxies plans rebuilt as a single subscription with access to all products: free Welcome credits tier added ($5 toward any product); Advanced renamed Flexible ($500/mo); Corporate ($2,500 billed monthly) became Enterprise ("plans starting at $2,500/month", Get an offer). Starter $30, Basic $100 and per-GB rates ($7.5/$6.67/$5/$3.5) unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $30, Basic at $100, Flexible at $500 and Enterprise at $2,500.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Oxylabs. Oxylabs has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
