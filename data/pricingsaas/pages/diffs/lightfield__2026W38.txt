What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Lightfield's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Dropped per-credit usage pricing (pay-as-you-go, $1,000-2,000/mo) for flat seat/workspace pricing ($89-1,999/mo, billed annually); signup CTA now offers a 14-day trial; Unlimited-seats promise dropped; Starter now capped at 3 seats/workspace, Growth at 10 total capture seats. New per-plan Records caps (50,000/250,000/750,000) appear where none existed before; Starter gains meeting recording/tran. In detail: Starter gains meeting recording/transcription and custom objects/pipeline config, loses agent automations and Sequences Beta; LinkedIn sync moves to Growth-only; replaced-tools bundle drops from seven to six. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease, package change, feature change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $89, Pro at $999, Growth at $1,999 and Additional Capture Seat at $200.
It lands 13 days after Lightfield's previous change, "Lightfield: Pro and Growth plan price increases" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Lightfield ditches seat pricing for 4-tier credit plans, unlimited seats" (Aug 17, 2026), "Lightfield ditches per-user pricing: Pro jumps to flat $899/mo, Growth axed" (Jul 20, 2026) and "Lightfield: Pro records cap increased 100K to 1M; Starter now capped at 3 seats" (Jul 6, 2026). Lightfield has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
