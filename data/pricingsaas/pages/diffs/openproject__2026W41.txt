Verified Oct 5, 2026·Project Management·Direction: neutral
The changeCommunity free tier — freemium change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 28, 2026 → Oct 5, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of OpenProject's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Date alerts for upcoming and overdue tasks no longer listed under Enterprise add-ons; page markdown now places it in the Community feature set (link anchor no longer enterprise-add-on). Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $7.25, Professional at $13.5, Premium at $19.5, BIM at $1.25 and BIM Add-on at $1.25.
It lands 55 days after OpenProject's previous change, "OpenProject: adds Multiple active sprints to feature comparison" (Aug 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenProject slashes Corporate plan minimum from 1000 to 250 users" (Jul 20, 2026) and "OpenProject: XWiki integration added to Corporate plan" (Jul 13, 2026). OpenProject has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
