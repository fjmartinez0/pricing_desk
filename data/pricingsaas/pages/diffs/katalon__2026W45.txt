What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Katalon's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The limited-time 55%-off starter offer on Katalon Studio Professional ($1,000/seat/year, until October 31) ended. The shown price returns to $1,800/seat/year (about $150/seat/month), 20% off the unchanged $2,199 list price. In detail: Katalon Studio Professional limited-time 55%-off starter offer ($1,000/seat/year, ran until October 31) ended; shown price returns to $1,800/seat/year (about $150/seat/month), the standard 20% annual saving off the unchanged $2,199 list price. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Professional at $84, Katalon Runtime Engine at $145, Cross Browser at $158 and Mobile Devices at $158.
It lands 28 days after Katalon's previous change, "Katalon moves Professional to annual-only $1,000/seat, 55% off first 3 seats" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Katalon adds free tier, renames Individual to Professional, refocuses…" (Sep 21, 2026), "Katalon: plan tiers consolidated to Individual/Small teams, True Platform moved to add-on" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Katalon slashes Studio Enterprise price 44%, adds per-seat tiered pricing" (Aug 24, 2026).
