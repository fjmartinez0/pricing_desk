What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Mailmeteor's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added 'Pipelines' feature (manage replies pipeline) to the Professional plan, marked 'New' in the feature list and comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $4.49, Premium at $13.49 and Professional at $26.99.
It lands 7 days after Mailmeteor's previous change, "Mailmeteor adds SMTP/IMAP support across all four pricing plans" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mailmeteor: Premium price up $15.99→$17.99 + 2 more changes" (Aug 11, 2026). Mailmeteor has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Mailchimp, Convertkit, HubSpot and Constant Contact also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
