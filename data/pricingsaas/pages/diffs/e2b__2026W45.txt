What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of E2b's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise minimum commit shown in the cost estimator dropped from $3,000/mo to $2,000/mo, now including 500 concurrent sandboxes. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $150.
It lands 49 days after E2b's previous change, "E2b prices concurrency add-ons: +$500/mo for 600, +$1,000/mo for 1,100" (Sep 14, 2026). E2b has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
