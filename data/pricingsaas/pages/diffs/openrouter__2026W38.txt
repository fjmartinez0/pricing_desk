What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of OpenRouter's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Business' tier introduced between Pay-as-you-go and Enterprise at an 8% platform fee, raising the workspace limit to 1,000 (vs 5 on Free and Pay-as-you-go) and adding a DPA row offering a signed copy on request. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction.
It lands 28 days after OpenRouter's previous change, "OpenRouter expands catalog to 500+ models, 80+ providers across pricing tiers" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenRouter swaps free BYOK request quotas for dollar-based fee thresholds" (Jul 6, 2026), "OpenRouter expands provider network to 70+ on all paid plans" (Jun 22, 2026) and "OpenRouter rebrands enterprise discounts as fee-only savings" (Jun 17, 2026). OpenRouter has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
