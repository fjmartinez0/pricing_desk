What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Vartion's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Premium plan price increased from EUR 50/month (minimum commitment) to a flat EUR 89/month. Free gained new case quotas (10 new/10 active cases); Premium unlimited searches/integrations replaced by pay-per-use with 1,000-case and 1000+-integration caps. Free lost self-serve onboarding and AD/CRM integration; Premium lost automated monitoring and concierge onboarding but gained custom API integrations/add-ons; Enterprise onboarding/TM integration became optional modules alongside new custom-limits and contract pricing. In detail: Free gained new case quotas (10 new/10 active); Premium's unlimited searches became pay-per-use after 30 free/category, with new 1,000-case and 1000+-integration limits; Free lost self-serve onboarding & AD/CRM integration; Premium lost automated monitoring & concierge onboarding, gained API integrations/add-ons; Enterprise onboarding/TM became optional modules; Premium plan price increased from €50/month (minimum commitment) to a flat €89/month; billing model and plan-tier structure were overhauled across Free, Premium, and Enterprise. Pulse files it as a pricing change (metric change, feature change, price increase) with an upward direction.
