What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Huggingface's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise plan price rose from $50 to $500 per user per month; NVIDIA H100 GPU instance rows (1/2/4/8 GPU, $4.50-$36.00/hr) removed from Inference Endpoints GPU price table. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature removed, price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists PRO at $9, Team at $20, Enterprise at $500, Inference Endpoints - NVIDIA T4 AWS (4x) at $3 per hour and Inference Endpoints - NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 AWS (1x) at $2.75 per hour.
This weekly roll-up includes 2 changes Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Huggingface hikes Enterprise plan 10x, from $50 to $500 per user monthly" (Day 325, 2026, saved Nov 21, 2026) and "Huggingface drops NVIDIA H100 inference endpoints from pricing table" (Day 321, 2026, saved Nov 17, 2026). They are the same changes, not new ones. Before that, Huggingface's previous recorded change was "Huggingface extends hardware price table to cover Jobs, rates unchanged" (Nov 1, 2026). Huggingface has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Together AI, Modal, Anthropic, OpenAI and Cohere also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
