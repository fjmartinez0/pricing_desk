What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Viktor's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free, Team, and Enterprise plan card taglines were all replaced with the same generic line 'Perfect to get started and explore Viktor,' removing the distinct per-tier value props (credits framing, team AI employee, CISO-ready controls). Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Team at $50.
It lands 34 days after Viktor's previous change, "Viktor: Team & Enterprise gain spend-cap controls" (Aug 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Viktor expands to 3,200+ integrations, revamps credit pricing copy" (Jun 22, 2026). Viktor has been tracked since 2026-05-19.
In the same window Hex also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
