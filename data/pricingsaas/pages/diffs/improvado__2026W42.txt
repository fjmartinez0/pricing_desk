What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Improvado's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Comparison table row 'Customization credits / yr' renamed to 'Professional Services credits / yr'; values (720 Advanced, 960 Enterprise) unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction.
It lands 49 days after Improvado's previous change, "Improvado: Advanced Intelligence features unbundled into paid add-ons" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Improvado goes self-serve: adds Free & MCP Only tiers, drops Growth plan" (Jul 20, 2026). Improvado has been tracked since 2026-04-26.
In the same window Supermetrics and Mixpanel also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
