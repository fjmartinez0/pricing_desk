What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Airwallex's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Corporate card credit line launched (Coming soon removed) and extended to the free Explore plan; Single sign-on on Grow/Accelerate no longer Coming soon; Automatic tax calculation added to Billing on all plans at 0.4% fee per transaction; Purchase orders and requests moved down from Accelerate to Grow plan; Multi-entity billing removed from Explore and Grow; now Accelerate only. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, feature removed, new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Grow at $12.
It lands 28 days after Airwallex's previous change, "Airwallex: Yield returns increased across all plan tiers (3.30→3.42% + more)" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Airwallex: Yield returns increased across all plan tiers (3.21→3.30% + more)" (Sep 21, 2026). Airwallex has been tracked since 2023-12-01.
