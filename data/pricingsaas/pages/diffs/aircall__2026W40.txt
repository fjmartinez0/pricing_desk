What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Aircall's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Custom plan renamed to Enterprise (adds explicit HIPAA/GDPR/SOC2 + SCIM); new standalone AI Voice Agents plan added at $100/mo minimum; new add-ons Branded caller ID ($0.08/call) and Spam Management ($0.03/check); AI Messaging Agents pricing simplified from tiered/prepaid model to flat $0.15-$0.35/conversation. In detail: Major pricing page redesign: Custom plan renamed to Enterprise (with explicit HIPAA/GDPR/SOC2 + SCIM compliance); new standalone AI Voice Agents plan added at $100/mo minimum; two new add-ons (Branded caller ID $0.08/call, Spam Management $0.03/check); AI Messaging Agents pricing simplified from tiered/prepaid model to flat $0.15-$0.35/conversation. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Essentials at $30, Professional at $50, AI Voice Agents at $100, Aircall for Salesforce Voice at $30 and WhatsApp in Aircall at $10.
It lands 63 days after Aircall's previous change, "Aircall pulls free AIVA minutes promo tied to World Tournament campaign" (Jul 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Aircall: Analytics+ add-on feature list rewritten" (Jul 6, 2026) and "Aircall: Essentials plan integration count boosted to 250+" (Jul 2, 2026). Aircall has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
