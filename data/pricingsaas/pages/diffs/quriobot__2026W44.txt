What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Quriobot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Google Business Messages channel removed from every plan: the bullet is gone from the Free card (inherited by Business and Agency via "All from previous plan plus"), and the row, previously checked for Free, Business and Agency, is gone from the All available features comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Business 1000 at EUR 13, Business 2500 at EUR 27, Business 5000 at EUR 49, Business 10000 at EUR 85 and Business Unlimited at EUR 143.
It lands 98 days after Quriobot's previous change, "Quriobot Business tier ticks up to $164/m from $163/m" (Jul 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Quriobot raises Business and Agency plan prices across all tiers" (May 5, 2026), "Quriobot Caps Free Plan at 500 Chats per Month" (Apr 24, 2026) and "Quriobot Shifts to Usage-Based Pricing With Five Chat Tiers" (Apr 1, 2026). Quriobot has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Intercom, HubSpot and LiveChat also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
