What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Sarvam AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Monthly plan amounts rose (Pro 2,000→9,999, Growth 10,000→49,999, Business 50,000→1,99,999 INR); Bulbul V4 Flash rates roughly halved; Business V3 TTS and STT rates cut.
