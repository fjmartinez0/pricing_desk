What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Tray.ai's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Tray now publishes an entry price: 'Starts at $50 a month' replaces the 'New' badge on the Tray Helix banner, and the FAQ now says Tray starts at $50/month (previously 'contact sales for a customized quote'); adds a note of no per-user, per-connection or per-runtime fees. iPaaS Pro/Team/Enterprise cards remain Talk to sales. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction.
It lands 98 days after Tray.ai's previous change, "Tray.ai adds Guardian Log Masking to Team plan capabilities" (Jul 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Tray.ai expands AI IDE support with Claude Code and Codex plugins" (Jun 29, 2026), "Tray.ai adds Log Streaming and Data Engineering to Enterprise plan" (May 25, 2026) and "Tray.ai merges Agent Gateway and Builder into one add-on" (May 18, 2026). Tray.ai has been tracked since 2026-05-07.
In the same window UiPath, Zapier, Workato, Make and MuleSoft also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
