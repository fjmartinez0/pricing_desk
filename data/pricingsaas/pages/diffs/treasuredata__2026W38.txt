What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Treasure Data's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Renamed the 'Modular CDP' architecture approach to 'Hybrid CDP' (matching its existing Learn-more link) and added a new sentence clarifying the two deployment models: Composable CDP (own Snowflake/Databricks/BigQuery warehouse) vs Complete CDP (managed infrastructure). Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Treasure Data. Treasure Data has been tracked since 2026-05-21.
