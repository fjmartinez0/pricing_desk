What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Aikido Security's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New 'Aikido Max' add-on: unlimited monthly credits for all AI features including Deep Review, subject to fair use policy; quote-based (Get a quote); FedRAMP ATO bullet (with 'FedRAMP Moderate is available at an additional cost' tooltip) removed from Advanced plan card; Enterprise tagline now reads 'Enterprise-grade modules, tailored pricing, and FedRAMP moderate.'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Basic at $315, Pro at $630, Advanced at $945 and Typical Pentest at $4,000 assessments.
It lands 7 days after Aikido Security's previous change, "Aikido Security adds Code Coverage to paid plan, above PR Code Quality Checks" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Aikido Security: Container image scan limit doubled across paid tiers" (Aug 17, 2026), "Aikido Security: Code Quality feature scoped down to PR-level checks" (Aug 10, 2026) and "Aikido Security: Pro/Advanced add AI credits + FedRAMP ATO" (Aug 3, 2026). Aikido Security has been tracked since 2026-06-26.
In the same window Blackbird.AI, Fastly, X-Ways Software, Prowler and PreVeil also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
