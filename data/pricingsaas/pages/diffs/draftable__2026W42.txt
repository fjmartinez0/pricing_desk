What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Draftable's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Compare API plan card added (Talk to us, priced on volume and deployment); Business and Legal plans renamed Enterprise & Non-Legal and Law Firms & Legal Teams, prices unchanged at $129/$261 per user/yr; Mac support timeline pushed back from late 2026 to late 2027 for the desktop app. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan, feature change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Enterprise & Non-Legal at $10.75 and Law Firms & Legal Teams at $21.75.
It lands 98 days after Draftable's previous change, "Draftable hikes Legal plan price to $21.75/user monthly, Business holds steady" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Draftable: Mac support for Desktop app coming late 2026" (Jul 2, 2026) and "Draftable Expands Mac Support Across Business and Legal Plans" (Apr 9, 2026). Draftable has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
