What happened
Between the Nov 4, 2026 and Nov 5, 2026 captures of Captions's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise tier renamed/rebranded; 'Enterprise' and 'Custom pricing' labels removed, now directs prospects to contact sales about 'Tesseract for business'. Enterprise tier's itemized benefits list (bulk credit discounts, custom seat options, dedicated account management team, training data exclusion, customer onboarding/priority training, early access to beta features) removed from the page. In detail: Enterprise tier's itemized benefits list (bulk credit discounts, custom seats, dedicated account mgmt, training data exclusion, priority onboarding, early beta access) removed from the pricing page; Enterprise custom-pricing tier renamed/rebranded; page no longer shows 'Enterprise' or 'Custom pricing' labels, now directs enterprise prospects to contact sales about 'Tesseract for business' instead. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature removed, plan renamed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Max at $24.99 and Frontier at $69.99.
It lands 38 days after Captions's previous change, "Captions: mid-tier plan renamed from Scale to Frontier" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Captions rebrands Scale plan to Frontier, pricing untouched" (Sep 22, 2026), "Captions: Batch post-ready clips feature added to Max plan" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Captions Max plan adds one-click clip batching from long videos" (Aug 27, 2026).
