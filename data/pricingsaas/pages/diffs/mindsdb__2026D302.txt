What happened
Between the Oct 28, 2026 and Oct 29, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New model GPT-6.1 Terra (OpenAI) added to the model price table at $1.00 input, $5.00 output, $0.05 cached input, $1.25 cache write per 1M tokens. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pay as you go at $0.1 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.5 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands 2 days after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB adds Kimi K3.1 Pro and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model pricing" (Oct 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB adds Gemini 4 Argon and Meta Muse Wonder 1.0 token pricing" (Oct 19, 2026), "MindsDB adds Gemini 4 Argon pricing at $2 input, $10 output per million" (Oct 16, 2026) and "MindsDB adds Meta's Muse Wonder 1.0 at $4 input, $20 output per 1M tokens" (Oct 15, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
