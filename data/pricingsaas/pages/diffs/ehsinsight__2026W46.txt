EHS Insight renames plans Standard and Advanced Configuration, keeps $5K price
Verified Nov 9, 2026·Business Operations·Direction: neutral
The changeSMB positioning — positioning.
2 changes·2 captures·Nov 2, 2026 → Nov 9, 2026
What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of EHS Insight's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Small & Medium Business Solution renamed Standard Configuration and Enterprise Solution renamed Advanced Configuration; $5k/yr starting price unchanged ($5,000), Advanced remains custom quote; Plan taglines reframed: SMB plan 'tailored for smaller organizations' becomes Standard 'for teams of any size'; Enterprise 'larger organizations' becomes Advanced 'organizations of any size'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (positioning, plan renamed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard Configuration at $416.67.
It lands 14 days after EHS Insight's previous change, "EHS Insight drops "only in market" claim from its SMB mobile app bullet" (Oct 26, 2026). EHS Insight has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
