What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of QASource's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: cost example changed from an annual quote ($150,000-$200,000/yr for a 3-4 engineer team) to a monthly rate ($12,000-$15,000/mo for a specifically 3-person team); final bullet now states explicitly there is no annual commitment (still 45 days notice). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Custom at $12,000.
It lands 14 days after QASource's previous change, "QASource adds 45-day exit notice to monthly billing, rates unchanged" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "QASource reveals $150K-$200K/year team pricing, drops custom-quote-only model" (Oct 5, 2026). QASource has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
