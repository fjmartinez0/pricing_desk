What happened
Between the Nov 24, 2026 and Nov 25, 2026 captures of OpenAI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: ChatGPT Business Nov 30 promo expanded: 2x included usage on Standard/Premium seats only becomes 2x usage plus fast mode at no extra cost for all seats, through Dec 31; Plan taglines rewritten: Free 'Best for trying out ChatGPT', Go 'Best for longer conversations', Plus 'Best for advanced work and productivity', Pro 'Best for research and coding'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (positioning, discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Go at $8, Plus at $20, Business at $20 and Pro at $100.
It lands 12 days after OpenAI's previous change, "OpenAI repositions all 4 ChatGPT plan taglines by use case" (Nov 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenAI reframes all four plan taglines with new Best for use-case pitches" (Nov 11, 2026), "OpenAI API firms up GPT-5.6 Sol discount deadline: Nov. 30, 2026" (Nov 8, 2026) and "OpenAI: all four ChatGPT plan taglines rewritten to 'Best for...' framing" (Nov 8, 2026). OpenAI has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
