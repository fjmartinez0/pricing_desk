What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of RunPod's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pods GPU Cloud hourly rates raised across most GPUs: B300 $7.89→$8.99, H200 $4.59→$5.29, B200 $6.79→$7.99, H100 SXM $3.49→$3.99, A100 $1.59→$1.79, RTX 4090 $0.74→$0.89. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Storage at $0.05 per GB/month, Pods - Community Cloud at $0.16 per GPU-hour, Pods - Secure Cloud at $0.27 per GPU-hour, Serverless at $0.58 per GPU-hour and Clusters at $1.79 per GPU-hour.
It lands 35 days after RunPod's previous change, "RunPod hikes GPU rates on Pods: H100 up to $3.49/hr, A100 to $1.59/hr" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "RunPod: GPU hourly rates increased across 9 tiers (B300, B200, H200 + 6 more)" (Aug 17, 2026) and "RunPod: Pods GPU price increases (RTX 6000 Ada, RTX A6000, RTX 3090)" (Jul 27, 2026). RunPod has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
