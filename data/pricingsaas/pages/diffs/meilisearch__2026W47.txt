What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Meilisearch's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Cloud moved from usage-based ($30 base) or resource-based billing to fixed per-instance pricing by region (XS $14.88 US East vs $18); disk $0.165/GiB; $0.15/GB outbound bandwidth added; Cloud bullets now list XS-XL instances, full-text/vector/hybrid search, 9 regions, unlimited members; Enterprise-grade compliance bullet dropped from Enterprise. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, packaging change). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 17, 2026) lists Cloud XS at $14.88, Cloud S at $29.28, Cloud M at $58.08, Cloud L at $115.68 and Cloud XL at $259.36.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Meilisearch. Meilisearch has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
