What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Evolia's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Human Resources plan no longer lists Automatic Leave Accrual; the 'Celebration' item is relabeled 'Anniversaries'. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $3.50, Business at $4.75, Operations at $5.75 and HR at $7.
It lands 35 days after Evolia's previous change, "Evolia adds Automatic Leave Accrual to HR plan, keeps $7/employee price" (Oct 5, 2026). Evolia has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Workstream, Workable, Tracker, Thatch and Simployer also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
