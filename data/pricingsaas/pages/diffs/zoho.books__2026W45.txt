What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Zoho's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan feature 'Receipt autoscans' expanded to 'Receipt and Document Autoscans'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $15, Professional at $40, Premium at $60, Elite at $120 and Ultimate at $240.
It lands 119 days after Zoho's previous change, "Zoho Books moves Retention Payments to Free plan, now included at no cost" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Zoho Books: Free plan vendor feature relabelled 'Manage vendors'" (Apr 4, 2026) and "Zoho Books Introduces Vendor Bills and Payments Management to Free Plan, Expanding Access for All Users" (Jan 14, 2026). Zoho has been tracked since 2023-11-30.
