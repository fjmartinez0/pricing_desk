What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Devin's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Teams changed from $80/month plus $40/mo per full dev seat to a flat $80/month with shared on-demand credits for up to 100 members; per-seat fee removed; Added 'Access to Devin Bots' to Max, Teams and Enterprise plans, with a new Devin Bots row in the compare table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change, feature added). The current page lists Pro at $20, Teams at $80 and Max at $200.
This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Devin drops Teams per-seat fee to flat $80/month, adds Devin Bots" (Day 322, 2026, saved Nov 18, 2026). It is the same change, not a new one. Before that, Devin's previous recorded change was "Devin halves Team plan user cap to 100, adds ChatGPT plan support" (Nov 11, 2026). Devin has been tracked since 2026-06-26.
In the same window Squark AI, Speechmatics, Spiky.AI, Slang AI and scite also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
