What happened
Between the Oct 15, 2026 and Oct 16, 2026 captures of Replit's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise feature list reworked: adds SCIM and audit logs, centralized security management, private networking, dedicated premium support; drops custom seat limits and static outbound IPs. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Replit Core at $18 and Replit Pro at $90.
It lands 32 days after Replit's previous change, "Replit: Removed the free "Starter" plan from the pricing page; Core ($18-20/mo) is now the entry-level tier" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Replit axes free Starter plan, ties Core and Pro bullets to Free Mode" (Sep 11, 2026), "Replit drops Core's 2 parallel agents, caps free tier to one task" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Replit tightens agent limits: Core loses parallel-agent count, free tier capped at one background task" (Sep 2, 2026). Replit has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window GitHub, Google Cloud, CodeSandbox and JetBrains also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
