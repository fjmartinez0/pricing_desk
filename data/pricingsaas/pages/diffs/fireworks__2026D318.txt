What happened
Between the Nov 13, 2026 and Nov 14, 2026 captures of Fireworks' pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Kimi K3.1 Pro model added to Highlighted Serverless rates table at $2.00/1M input, $0.20/1M cached input, $10.00/1M output ($3.00/$0.30/$15.00 Priority). In detail: New model Kimi K3.1 Pro added to Highlighted Serverless rates table at $2.00/1M input, $0.20/1M cached input, $10.00/1M output ($3.00/$0.30/$15.00 Priority). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Serverless at $0.08 per 10M input tokens (embeddings, up to 150M params) + $0.16 per 10M input tokens (embeddings, 150M-350M params), Fine Tuning at $0.50 per 1M training tokens, LoRA SFT (models up to 16B params) and On-Demand at $13 per GPU hour (B200 180GB) + $15 per GPU hour (B300 288GB).
It lands 19 days after Fireworks' previous change, "Fireworks adds SOC 2, ISO 27001, 27701 and 42001 badges to pricing page" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fireworks adds Muse Spark Open, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash to Serverless Training" (Oct 17, 2026), "Fireworks adds GLM 5.3 Flash with 200K context, per-token training pricing" (Sep 27, 2026) and "Fireworks adds GLM 5.3 to Serverless Training at $14.58/1M train tokens" (Sep 16, 2026). Fireworks has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
