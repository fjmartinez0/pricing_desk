What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Homebase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Homebase added a new interactive 'See what Homebase costs' estimator widget with Locations/Team members inputs and Include Payroll / Add Tip Manager checkboxes, placed between the add-ons section and the plan comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $24, Plus at $56, All-in-One at $96, Background Checks at $30 per use and Tip Manager at $25.
It lands 14 days after Homebase's previous change, "Homebase offers $29/mo Payroll base-fee promo for small teams, down from $49" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Homebase launches 6-months-free Payroll & Plus promo, raises Payroll base fee to $49" (Sep 21, 2026), "Homebase: Job post boosts add-on price cut $79→$50" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Homebase Hiring Assistant exits Beta, ditches per-post fee for $99/mo flat rate" (Sep 7, 2026). Homebase has been tracked since 2026-05-06.
In the same window Lightspeed, Deputy and 7shifts also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
