What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of GitLab's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: GitLab Orbit launched as a new add-on for Premium and Ultimate, sold in query packs paid in separately reserved GitLab Credits; Premium add-on blurb now mentions SDLC context for agents. In detail: GitLab Orbit launched as a new add-on for Premium and Ultimate, sold in query packs paid in separately reserved GitLab Credits; Premium and Ultimate add-on blurbs now mention SDLC context for agents. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Premium at $29, Enterprise Agile Planning at $15, Compute Minutes at $10 per 1,000 minutes, Storage at $5 and GitLab Duo Agent Platform at $1 per GitLab Credit.
It lands 2 days after GitLab's previous change, "GitLab: Advanced Capacity add-on priced at $12,000/year for higher API limits" (Oct 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "GitLab launches Orbit add-on, a context graph sold as query packs" (Oct 30, 2026), "GitLab brings Secrets Manager to Self-Managed, Dedicated still pending" (Oct 16, 2026) and "GitLab: Flex billing FAQ overhauled — new usage-cap self-service, terms narrowed" (Sep 21, 2026). GitLab has been tracked since 2025-10-05.
In the same window GitHub, Google Cloud and JetBrains also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
