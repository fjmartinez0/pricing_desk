What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Transifex's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter and Growth plans switched from a sales-gated 'Contact Sales' CTA to a self-serve 'Try free' signup, on both the plan cards and the compare-table header row. Enterprise+ remains Contact Sales only. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction.
It lands 27 days after Transifex's previous change, "Transifex: Personalized Onboarding moves from paid add-on to included feature" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Transifex: Starter & Growth plan prices hidden (was $135/$200 mo)" (Aug 11, 2026) and "Transifex unbundles Personalized Onboarding to free, kills Training Sessions" (Jul 13, 2026). Transifex has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Lokalise and Phrase also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
