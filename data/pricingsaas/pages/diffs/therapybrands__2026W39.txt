What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Ensora Health's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: TheraNest teaser expanded from a single from-$29/mo figure to $29/$59/$89 per therapist/mo. Fusion teaser dropped its flat $119/mo starting price for $25/$99/mo solo and $159/$229/mo multi-clinician (first user) plus $45/$75 per extra user. In detail: Ensora's hub page replaced single 'starting at $X/mo' teasers with full tier pricing for two products: TheraNest now discloses $29/$59/$89 per therapist/mo (was just 'from $29'); Fusion now shows $25 or $99/mo solo, $159 or $229/mo first multi-clinician user, +$45/+$75 per extra user (the old flat '$119/mo' figure is gone). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Essentials at $26.58, Advanced at $54.08 and Premier at $81.58.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Ensora Health. Ensora Health has been tracked since 2026-06-24.
