What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Motion's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter plan price increased from $750/mo to $999/mo (33% increase); Pro plan's visible $1,200/mo price was removed and replaced with a 'Let's chat' contact-sales prompt. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $999 and Pro at $1,200.
It lands 14 days after Motion's previous change, "Motion renames Starter's dedicated-VM feature to Runneth AI brain" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Motion hikes Pro plan 14.3% to $1,200/mo, Starter holds steady" (Aug 24, 2026). Motion has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
In the same window ClickUp, Asana, Notion and Trello also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
“Motionapp” is the name Motion’s pricing page uses; Pulse files the change under Motion, the company it tracks.
