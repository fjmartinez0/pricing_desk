What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Heidi's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Clinician plan now offers Standard or Max seat types with a monthly Agents usage allowance (Free gets weekly trial allowance); seat types can be mixed on one invoice. $110 price unchanged; Clinician adds AI workflows: Routines for pre-charting, billing code support and admin tasks, plus Agents (Routines, Computer use, Memory, Connectors) consuming a monthly usage limit. Clinician also gains Docum. In detail: Free tier reshuffled: Evidence answers go from Unlimited to Limited usage (fair use), Patient-context-aware answers and Live evidence suggestions drop from Limited to not included; Free gains limited Dictate, Document & session sharing, Coding and a weekly Agents trial allowance; Clinician adds AI workflows: Routines for pre-charting, billing code support and admin tasks, plus Agents (Routines, Computer use, Memory, Connectors) consuming a monthly usage limit. Clinician also gains Document & session sharing, Coding and Full-text journal access (previously not included); Clinician loses Priority support and Centralized billing; both are now Teams-only (comparison table ✓ → —, Priority support dropped from the Clinician card). Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change, feature added, package change, feature removed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Clinician at $110 and Clinician Max at $299.
It lands 42 days after Heidi's previous change, "Heidi: Personalizations feature removed from plan comparison table" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Heidi locks Practice/Teams pricing behind sales, drops EHR add-on" (Aug 17, 2026). Heidi has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Microsoft also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
