What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Mailgun's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: API keys included reduced from 2 to 1 on Free and Basic plans; Basic plan adds a Customizable dashboard feature, marked New. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, limit lowered). The current page lists Basic at $15, Foundation at $35 and Scale at $90.
Mailgun's previous recorded change was "Mailgun Introduces Bounce Classification Metric and Expands API Key Limits for Free and Basic Plans" (Q1 2025). Before that Pulse recorded "Mailgun Expands Free Plan with Tracking and Analytics Features, Enhances Basic Plan with Increased Inbound Routes" (Q4 2024), "Mailgun Reduces Foundation Plan Message Retention from 3 Days to 1 Day" (Q3 2024) and "Mailgun Increases Scale Plan Overage Rate by 37.5% and Restructures Free Plan to 100 Emails/Day" (Q2 2024). Mailgun has been tracked since 2023-11-15.
In the same window HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp, Twilio and Constant Contact also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
