What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Tango's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan now lists Guide Me for workspace members; Enterprise plan now lists an MCP connector (MCP row moved from Beta to Included in comparison table).
