What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Tyk's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing page now states billing metrics: Core priced by number of APIs managed with unlimited calls; Professional priced by data planes, environments and control planes, not traffic.
