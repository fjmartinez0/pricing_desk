What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Speechmatics's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New "Linden 1" Speech-to-Text model added, launch price $0.16/hr (was $0.21/hr), Custom for Enterprise. In detail: New 'Linden 1' Speech-to-Text model added to the pricing table, launched at a discounted rate ($0.21/hr shown as previous price, $0.16/hr current price), usage billed as Custom for Enterprise. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $0.01 per 1,000 TTS characters + $0.19 per hour (Batch Melia 1 model), Translation at $0.65 per hour of audio, Summaries at $0.12 per hour of audio, Chapters at $0.4 per hour of audio and Sentiment at $0.12 per hour of audio.
