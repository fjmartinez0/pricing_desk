What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of OpenEnvoy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Essentials ERP fit no longer lists NetSuite as available now; NetSuite now marked '(Nov '26)', i.e. a future release. QuickBooks, Sage Intacct, SFTP remain. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction.
It lands 42 days after OpenEnvoy's previous change, "OpenEnvoy adds Verified Payments, payment controls, and Org SSO to plans" (Sep 14, 2026). OpenEnvoy has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
