What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Raycast's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Raycast split Pro into Pro/Plus/Max with bundled AI credits (500/3,000/7,500), adding Plus ($20/mo) and Max ($50/mo) plans plus Teams equivalents, and removed the flat $8/month Advanced AI Add-on. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $8, Teams Pro at $12, Pro+ at $16, Teams Pro+ at $20 and Pro Max at $40.
It lands 21 days after Raycast's previous change, "Raycast locks AI behind paid plans, kills 50 free messages" (Aug 24, 2026). Raycast has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window SmoothStay, Slack, Sinch, Moodle and Portant also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
