What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Enpass's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enpass launched a 'Cybersecurity Month Sale' banner advertising up to 40% off; the Premium plan's Annual billing option now applies a real 40% discount, cutting the effective price from $23.99/year ($1.99/month) to $14.39/year ($1.19/month). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists 3-Year Plan at $1.39, Premium Plan at $1.99 and Family Plan at $2.99.
It lands 28 days after Enpass's previous change, "Enpass: Free Plan added, Lifetime Plan discontinued, Individual→Premium restructure" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Enpass: Promotional discount removed, Family & 3-Year plans up ($2.99→$3.99/mo, $47.99→$49.99)" (Aug 10, 2026), "Enpass slashes prices: Individual Plan 40% off, 3-Year Plan drops to $47.99" (Jul 27, 2026) and "Enpass: One-Time Plan ($99.99 lifetime) discontinued + Independence Day sale ended" (Jul 20, 2026). Enpass has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Lastpass, NordPass and Dashlane also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
