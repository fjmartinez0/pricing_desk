What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Datadog's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Kafka Console (self-hosted, Confluent Cloud, MSK Provisioned) moved from Contact Us for Pricing to public pricing: starting at $100 per broker host/month annual, $120 on-demand. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Data Streams Monitoring - AWS Fargate at $2.9 and Data Streams Monitoring at $15.
It lands 94 days after Datadog's previous change, "Datadog Data Streams Monitoring Data Streams Monitoring: AWS Fargate serverless add-on introduced ($2.90/instance)" (Jul 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Datadog Data Streams Monitoring adds AWS Fargate Workload Protection at $2.9/in" (May 4, 2026). Datadog has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Elastic, Grafana, Sumologic and Splunk also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
