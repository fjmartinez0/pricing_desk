What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Whatagraph's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: IQ Agents dropped the Max plan early-access/cohort-waitlist qualifier and the comparison-table note that it was not part of the plans yet — now generally available. In detail: IQ Agents dropped the 'early access' qualifier and cohort-waitlist footnote on the Max plan; the comparison table also removed the note that it was 'not part of the plans yet', indicating general availability. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Max at $1,118.
It lands 100 days after Whatagraph's previous change, "Whatagraph paywalls SSO—full SAML now a paid add-on even on top Prime tier" (Jul 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Whatagraph moves BigQuery, API, and SSO behind paid add-ons" (Jun 19, 2026), "Whatagraph Cuts Boost Plan Pricing 20% While Reshuffling Features" (Mar 31, 2026) and "Whatagraph Transitions to USD Pricing: Start Plan Now $229/month and Boost Plan $579/month Annually" (Jan 14, 2026). Whatagraph has been tracked since 2023-11-29.
