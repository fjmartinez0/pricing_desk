What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Kontainer's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Added per-plan 'Why choose this package?' row with taglines (Start organizing, Work smarter with AI, Automate & connect, Built for scale) restating existing features; Video Streaming tool add-on storage cut: Premium (+$390) 8TB → 4TB, Enterprise (included) 16TB → 4TB; Professional unchanged at 4TB (+$540). Pulse files it as a packaging change (positioning, limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Core at $500, Professional at $695, Premium at $1,155 and Enterprise at $2,220.
It lands 90 days after Kontainer's previous change, "Kontainer: User limits cut across 3 plans + 2 new features" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kontainer adds MCP AI interface to integrations pricing tier" (May 31, 2026) and "Kontainer launches AI crop tool and expands compliance certifications" (Apr 20, 2026). Kontainer has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
