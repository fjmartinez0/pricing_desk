What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Peec AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise card adds SOC 2 Type II report on request; Available models list renames Meta Spark to Muse Spark. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $80, Pro at $205, Advanced at $420, Additional Model (Starter) at $30 and Additional Model (Pro) at $70.
It lands 14 days after Peec AI's previous change, "Peec AI adds agent credits row: 500 on Starter, up to 1,000 on Advanced" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Peec AI: bot-visit allowance raised on Starter/Pro/Advanced, no price change" (Oct 19, 2026), "Peec AI moves Perplexity tracking to Enterprise-only, adds Naver AI to all plans" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Peec AI adds Meta Spark to Enterprise LLM tracking, drops SOC 2 from plan table" (Sep 14, 2026). Peec AI has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window OpenAI, Huggingface, Anthropic, Microsoft and Google also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
