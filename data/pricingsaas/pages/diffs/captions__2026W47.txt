What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Captions' pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Max and Frontier plans now include multi-seat support: Max gets 2 total seats (1 admin + 1 additional), Frontier gets 3 total seats (1 admin + 2 additional). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Max at $24.99 and Frontier at $69.99.
This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Captions lists seat counts on plans: Max gets 2, Frontier gets 3" (Day 314, 2026, saved Nov 10, 2026). It is the same change, not a new one. Before that, Captions' previous recorded change was "Captions: Enterprise plan renamed to Tesseract for business, drops perks list" (Nov 5, 2026). Captions has been tracked since 2025-10-05.
In the same window Veed and Kapwing also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
