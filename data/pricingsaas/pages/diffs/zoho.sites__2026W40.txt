Zoho Sites overhauls plans: AI credits, form caps, new collections tier
Verified Sep 28, 2026·Finance & Accounting·Direction: neutral
The changeCollections, Plus, Advanced packaging — package change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 21, 2026 → Sep 28, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Zoho's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New AI-credit allowances (1000-6000/month) and form-submission caps (50-10k/month) now appear per plan under the restructured tiers; 'collections' capacity (2) is new for Plus/Advanced; none existed before. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Lite at $1, Basic at $8, Plus at $15, Advanced at $40 and Portal Members at $8.33.
It lands 86 days after Zoho's previous change, "Zoho Sites: Starter/Pro plan and all add-on prices hidden" (Jul 4, 2026). Zoho has been tracked since 2023-11-30.
In the same window Microsoft, Salesforce, HubSpot and Pipedrive also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
