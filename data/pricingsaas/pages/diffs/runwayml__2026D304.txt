What happened
Between the Oct 30, 2026 and Oct 31, 2026 captures of Runway's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise promo changed from '2 months of usage free, sign by 10/31' to 'up to 2 months of usage free, sign by 12/31' (deadline extended, benefit now capped). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $12, Pro at $28 and Max at $76.
It lands 5 days after Runway's previous change, "Runway: Added 'Generate, test and publish ads with Runway Ads (New)' bullet to Standard, Pro and Max plan cards" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Runway drops Max plan's expired Unlimited Wan 3.0 promo, prices hold" (Oct 20, 2026), "Runway runs unlimited Wan 3.0 promo on Max plan until October 18" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Runway Max plan adds Unlimited Wan 3.0 and Wan 3.0 Prime until October 18" (Oct 15, 2026). Runway has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
