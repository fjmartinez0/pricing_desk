What happened
Between the Oct 15, 2026 and Oct 16, 2026 captures of Bolt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Bolt Forge promo reinstated on Pro: included until Oct 18 (previously through Oct 14, absent from Oct 15 capture). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 20, 2026) lists Pro at $18 and Teams at $27.
It lands one day after Bolt's previous change, "Bolt: Pro plan no longer lists 'Bolt Forge included until Oct 14' promotional feature bullet" (Oct 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Bolt drops $9 Bolt Lite preview and Pro's free Forge window from pricing page" (Oct 2, 2026), "Bolt Lite debuts at $9/month as invite-only preview, Forge free on Pro" (Oct 1, 2026) and "Bolt Pro plan adds limited-time Forge freebie until Oct 14" (Sep 21, 2026). Bolt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
