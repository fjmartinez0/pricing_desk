What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Expo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Access to Simulators (Expo Simulators, limited beta) added to the feature lists of the Starter, Production and Enterprise plan cards. In detail: Access to Simulators added to the feature lists of the Starter, Production and Enterprise plan cards, as Expo Simulators leaves limited beta (waitlist removed). Simulator minutes stay usage-based on Starter/Production with 60 min/month included until 2027; Enterprise now shows Custom. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Starter at $19 and Production at $199.
It lands 21 days after Expo's previous change, "Expo adds Expo Simulators (limited beta) with 60 included min/month on paid plans" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Expo quintuples free build concurrency to 5, lifts Enterprise cap to 20" (Oct 18, 2026) and "Expo raises Hosting subrequest limit 10x to 10,000 on all paid plans" (Sep 27, 2026). Expo has been tracked since 2026-06-19.
In the same window Rustici Software, Kilo Code, GoodBarber, Framer and Flagsmith also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
