What happened
Between the Oct 23, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Runway's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added 'Generate, test and publish ads with Runway Ads (New)' bullet to Standard, Pro and Max plan cards. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $12, Pro at $28 and Max at $76.
It lands the same day as Runway's previous change, "Runway adds Runway Ads to paid plans, ends Max's unlimited Wan 3.0 promo" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Runway drops Max plan's expired Unlimited Wan 3.0 promo, prices hold" (Oct 20, 2026), "Runway runs unlimited Wan 3.0 promo on Max plan until October 18" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Runway Max plan adds Unlimited Wan 3.0 and Wan 3.0 Prime until October 18" (Oct 15, 2026).
