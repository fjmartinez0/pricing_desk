What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Heymarket's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: 'Email' channel in the credits-mapping table was relabeled 'Broadcast email'; the billing unit (per 10 emails) and credit rate (1x) are unchanged; Eight email authoring and analytics capabilities stopped being paid add-ons: 'Rich text email composer', 'Drag and drop email builder', 'Email template gallery', 'Shareable draft emails', 'Email open rate tracking', 'Email click-through rate tracking',. In detail: Eight email authoring and analytics capabilities stopped being paid add-ons: 'Rich text email composer', 'Drag and drop email builder', 'Email template gallery', 'Shareable draft emails', 'Email open rate tracking', 'Email click-through rate tracking', 'Email reply rate tracking' and 'Email bounce & unsubscribe reporting' went from 'Add on' on all four plans to included on Plus, Pro and Enterprise and unavailable on Standard. Pulse files it as a packaging change (positioning, add-on removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Standard at $20, Plus at $41, Pro at $62 and Salesforce Add-on at $7.5.
It lands 41 days after Heymarket's previous change, "Heymarket: RCS Branded Messaging added (Plus, Pro, Enterprise)" (Aug 3, 2026). Heymarket has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Twilio, Plivo, Telnyx and Bird also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
