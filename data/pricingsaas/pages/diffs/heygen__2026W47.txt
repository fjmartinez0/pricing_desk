What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of HeyGen's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Avatar engines relaunched as Avatar 6 (Turbo, Lipsync) replacing Avatar III/IV/V; Pro gains Avatar 6 Pro and a free Professional Voice Clone (was $29/mo). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 17, 2026) lists Creator at $24, Pro at $41 and Business at $119.
This weekly roll-up includes 2 changes Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "HeyGen replaces Avatar IV/V with Avatar 6 lineup, adds Pro tier" (Day 319, 2026, saved Nov 15, 2026) and "HeyGen Pro plan now includes free Professional Voice Clone, once $29/mo" (Day 318, 2026, saved Nov 14, 2026). They are the same changes, not new ones. Before that, HeyGen's previous recorded change was "HeyGen adds Voice TTS and Seedance 2.5 to Creator and Pro, prices flat" (Oct 23, 2026). HeyGen has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window InVideo, Synthesia, Runway and Pictory also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
