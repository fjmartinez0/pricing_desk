What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Upstage's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Solar Mini 4 model added ($0.10 input / $0.01 cached / $0.40 output per 1M tokens) with a 50% launch promo ($0.05/$0.005/$0.20) until 2026-10-22 UTC; standard pricing resumes 2026-10-23. Before that Pulse recorded "Upstage API: Solar Pro 4 discount extended at 70% off (was reverting to standard price)" (Sep 7, 2026), "Upstage API ends Solar Pro 4 free period — now 90% off until Sep 10, full rates from Sep 11" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Upstage API: File Search pricing dropped to Free + Embed trial/EOL extended" (Jul 28, 2026).
