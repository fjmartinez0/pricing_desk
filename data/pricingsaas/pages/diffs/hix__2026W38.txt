What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of HIX.AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: HIX AI Max and Pro plans swapped their included LLM lineup: GPT-5.6 and Claude Opus 5 were replaced with GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1; Gemini 3.8 Flash stayed the same. Pricing and credits unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists HIX AI Pro at $9.99 and HIX AI Max at $14.99.
It lands 56 days after HIX.AI's previous change, "HIX.AI swaps GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4 Pro for Gemini 3.5 Flash in Max, Pro tiers" (Jul 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HIX.AI boosts Max & Pro plans with GPT-5.6 and Claude Opus 4.8—prices unchanged" (Jul 13, 2026). HIX.AI has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window Jasper and Writer also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
