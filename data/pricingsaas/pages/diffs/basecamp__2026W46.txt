What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Basecamp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Group chat feature name changed from 'Campfires & Pings' to 'Chats & Pings' in plan feature copy; prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Freelancer at $25, Studio at $59, Pro at $99, Unlimited at $299 and Additional Storage at $100.
It lands 14 days after Basecamp's previous change, "Basecamp raises Pro to $79/mo, Unlimited to $329 in packaging overhaul" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Basecamp adds AI agent integrations — Claude, ChatGPT, Grok Bot — to all…" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Basecamp axes per-user fees for fixed pricing, cuts top-tier storage from…" (Aug 24, 2026). Basecamp has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Asana, Notion and ClickUp also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
