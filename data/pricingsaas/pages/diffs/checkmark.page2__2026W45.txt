What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of CheckMark's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Second-year renewal prices rose $10: MultiLedger Pro+ $369 to $379, Pro $299 to $309; first-year prices unchanged.
