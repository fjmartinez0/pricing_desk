What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Simployer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Recruitment moved from included in Complete to a separate add-on available on top of either Essential or Complete; no prices published. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed).
It lands 14 days after Simployer's previous change, "Simployer adds Essential package alongside Complete, priced per employee by quote" (Nov 8, 2026). Simployer has been tracked since 2026-10-15.
In the same window Paychex and Deel also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
