What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of SecurityScorecard's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: TITAN Watch Core/Premium/Elite packages replaced by TITAN AI Watch/Assess/Secure; standalone TITAN Assess and Secure add-ons folded into the packages. Assess adds questionnaire template/review agents, risk scoring, auto-remediation; Secure (now built on Watch, gains free-start CTA) adds threat-informed presets, response automation, events feed. Full AI Agents suite, unlimited APIs and MAX readiness items dropped. Still quote-only. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for SecurityScorecard. SecurityScorecard has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
