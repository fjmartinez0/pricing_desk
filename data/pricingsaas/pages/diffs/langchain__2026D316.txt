What happened
Between the Nov 11, 2026 and Nov 12, 2026 captures of LangChain's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: LangSmith 'Deployment' product renamed 'Agent Server' in Plus plan access bullet and usage-metered pricing row; 1 free Serverless (Small) instance unchanged, rates unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Plus at $39.
It lands 38 days after LangChain's previous change, "LangChain retires LCU billing, moves all usage to $1 Standard Units" (Oct 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LangChain merges LangSmith LCU and LSU into one $1.00 Standard Unit" (Oct 2, 2026) and "LangChain: new Tuned Evaluators usage pricing (0.01 LCU/eval run)" (Aug 24, 2026). LangChain has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
