What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Maropost's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added Black Friday promotional offer: 30% off first year with an annual contract, or 30% off first 3 months with monthly billing; free onboarding included.
