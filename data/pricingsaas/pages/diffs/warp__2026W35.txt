What happened
Between the Aug 17, 2026 and Aug 24, 2026 captures of Warp's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Warp added a new 'Warp Factories' pricing section with five tiers (Pay as you go, Build, Max, Business, Enterprise) and up to $10,000 in factory usage during Early Access, and removed the 'Claude Fable 5 and Opus 5 are 50% off' promo banner from the top of the pricing page. Core Warp plans (Free $0, Build $20, Max $200, Business, Enterprise) and the 10% annual discount are unchanged. In detail: Launched a new 'Warp Factories' product line on the pricing page with five tiers (Pay as you go, Build, Max, Business, Enterprise). Pay as you go bills usage at a 20% markup with no subscription; Build/Max/Business/Enterprise cover factory usage from included plan credits with additional usage at API rates. Select teams get up to $10,000 in factory usage during Early Access; Removed the 'Claude Fable 5 and Opus 5 are 50% off for a limited time' promotional banner from the top of the pricing page; the banner slot now advertises Warp Factories Early Access instead. Pulse files it as a pricing change (new plan, discount removed) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Build at $18, Business at $45 and Max at $180.
It lands 13 days after Warp's previous change, "Warp: Agent CLI access on Free + bring-your-own API keys on Business" (Aug 11, 2026). Warp has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Vercel, Netlify, Digitalocean, Google and Render also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
