What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Fortnox's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Fakturering Standard plan added at 99 kr/mån, with invoices from 4.90 kr via Fakturaservice and from 9.90 kr via self-invoicing (fakturera själv), positioned between Fakturering Enkel (no license fee, 39 kr/self-invoice) and Fakturering Plus; The flat-fee Fakturering module (149 kr/mån) was renamed Fakturering Plus at the same 149 kr/mån price, and now states invoices from 4.90 kr via Faktura. In detail: The flat-fee Fakturering module (149 kr/mån) was renamed Fakturering Plus at the same 149 kr/mån price, and now states invoices from 4.90 kr via Fakturaservice and from 0 kr via self-invoicing (fakturera själv). Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed, new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Attest & Koll at SEK 119, Löpande Mini at SEK 149, Gör det själv Mini at SEK 159, Mini at SEK 209 and Gör det själv Bas at SEK 269.
It lands 7 days after Fortnox's previous change, "Fortnox cuts entry price to 9 kr/mo for smallest Access verification tier" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fortnox switches Enkel lön usage fee to per-employee billing model" (Sep 7, 2026). Fortnox has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
