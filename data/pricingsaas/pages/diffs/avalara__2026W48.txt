What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Avalara's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Core Compliance card feature list reworded: now lists 'automated filing and remittance with no per-return fees' and the Avalara Aviator agent hub, replacing the AvaTax/Managed Returns product lines and the compliance-support and business-system-integrations bullets. Copy rewrite, not a scope change: the unchanged FAQ already said Core Compliance includes compliance support and that Managed Returns. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Core Compliance + SST Services at $58.25 and Core Compliance at $66.58.
It lands 28 days after Avalara's previous change, "Avalara launches self-serve pricing at $79 per state, ditching contact-sales" (Oct 26, 2026). Avalara has been tracked since 2026-05-04.
In the same window Shopify, Wolters Kluwer, Stripe and Anrok also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
