What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Reflex's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise bullets swapped self-serve infra items (GCP deploy, SSO/SAML, RBAC, audit logs, dedicated support) for a guided forward-deployed-team delivery process, adding a new Custom application delivery line. Its description was also recast as a forward-deployed-team pitch, and the Most popular badge moved there from Pro. In detail: Enterprise bullets swapped infra items (GCP deploy, team workspaces/RBAC, SSO/SAML, audit logs, custom limits, dedicated support) for a guided delivery process, adding a new Custom application delivery line; Enterprise's description was rewritten from 'For teams building on their own infrastructure' to a forward-deployed-team pitch, and the Most popular badge moved there from Pro. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $25.
It lands 21 days after Reflex's previous change, "Reflex swaps Pro multipliers for $25-$200 tiers, narrows Enterprise to GCP" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Reflex moves App Code Download to Free plan, reframes Enterprise pitch" (Sep 21, 2026), "Reflex guts Pro plan: flat $200/mo replaced by $25 base + usage tiers" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Reflex: Pro plan promo price ($3/mo) expired, reverted to $200/mo" (Aug 10, 2026). Reflex has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
