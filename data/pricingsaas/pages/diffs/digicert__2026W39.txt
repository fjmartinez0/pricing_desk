What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of DigiCert's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: SSL/TLS plans reworded included benefit from 'Complete lifecycle automation, including renewals' to 'Free ACME automation included' on 4 plans; Secure Site Pro-OV's benefits base corrected to Secure Site-OV. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Secure Email for Individual at $4, Secure Email for Employee at $9, Basic - OV at $26, PKIo Private Person - Authentication/Encryption - USB Token at $33 and PKIo Private Professional - Authentication/Encryption - USB Token at $33.
It lands 7 days after DigiCert's previous change, "DigiCert extends wildcard domain purchasing to Basic and Secure Site OV tiers" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "DigiCert: New X9 PKI Certificates + Trust Lifecycle Manager" (Jul 13, 2026). DigiCert has been tracked since 2026-05-04.
In the same window Google also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
