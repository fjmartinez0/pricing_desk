What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Browserbase's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Larger browser sizes priced hourly: Large $0.24/hr (Developer) and $0.20/hr (Startup); X-Large $0.48/hr (Developer) and $0.40/hr (Startup); Enterprise usage-based. Free has Regular only; Plan cards now list Browser Sizes: Regular on Free; Regular, Large, X-Large on Developer, Startup and Enterprise. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, feature added). The current page lists Developer at $20 and Startup at $99.
It lands 21 days after Browserbase's previous change, "Browserbase adds Exa Deep search at $12/1k, renames $7 tier Exa Fast" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Browserbase swaps flat $7 search rate for per-provider pricing from $1/1k" (Oct 18, 2026). Browserbase has been tracked since 2024-12-07.
In the same window Cypress.io, Puppet and Apify also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
