What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Applitools's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter plan dropped 'AI-driven functional testing' (now exclusive to Professional+) but gained a new 'Visual AI MCP server' feature; Professional's 'Embedded Interactive Browser' capability was removed entirely. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $667.
