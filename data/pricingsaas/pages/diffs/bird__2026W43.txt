What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Bird's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Bird moved billing to a shared wallet-balance: Email funds balance from $15/mo (was direct plan); Apple Messages revealed $100/mo (was custom quote); Mailbox shows Free+paid plans (was custom agreement). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Startup at $15, Growth at $250 and Scale at $1,000.
It lands 14 days after Bird's previous change, "Bird halves US local phone number monthly rental from $1.00 to $0.50" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Bird pulls Push and RCS from Pay as you go, moves them to private beta" (Sep 28, 2026). Bird has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
