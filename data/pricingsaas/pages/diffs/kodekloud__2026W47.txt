What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of KodeKloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Early Black Friday sale: annual discount headline up from 'up to 21%' to 'up to 50%'; annual Pro $30→$21/mo ($360→$250/yr) and AI $46→$29/mo ($547→$342/yr); Standard $15/mo unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $15, Pro at $21 and AI at $29.
It lands 7 days after KodeKloud's previous change, "KodeKloud shrinks annual-plan sale from 50% off to just 21%" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "KodeKloud cuts annual savings claim from 50% to 21% as Skillfest sale ends" (Oct 19, 2026), "KodeKloud annual savings shrink to 21% as Autumn sale ends" (Oct 4, 2026) and "KodeKloud doubles annual discount to 50% as Autumn of Learning sale launches" (Sep 28, 2026).
