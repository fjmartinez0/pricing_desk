What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Tailscale's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Tailscale added 'Custom tailnet subdomains' as a new Enterprise plan benefit, listed in the Enterprise highlights bullets and added as a new row in the feature comparison table. Before that Pulse recorded "Tailscale drops Premium support, launches SLA-backed Silver, Gold, Platinum t" (Oct 15, 2026), "Tailscale clarifies Personal plan: 50 tagged resources to start" (May 18, 2026) and "Tailscale Doubles Free Plan to 6 Users, Absorbs $5 Personal Plus Tier" (Apr 13, 2026).
