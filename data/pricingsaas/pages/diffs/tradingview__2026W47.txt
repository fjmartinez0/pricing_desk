What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of TradingView's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Options volume profile and Expected move added to the Technical analysis features of the Premium and Ultimate plans (shown ✗ on Essential and Plus). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Essential at $12.95, Plus at $29.95, Premium at $59.95 and Ultimate at $199.95.
It lands the same day as TradingView's previous change, "TradingView adds Options volume profile, Expected move to Premium+" (Nov 16, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "TradingView splits market-data add-on into per-plan subscription caps" (Nov 8, 2026), "TradingView drops free trial offer from plan cards, CTAs now say Start now" (Oct 5, 2026) and "TradingView drops 30-day trial messaging from plan cards for Start now" (Oct 4, 2026). TradingView has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
In the same window Robinhood, NinjaTrader and Interactive Brokers also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
