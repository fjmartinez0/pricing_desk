What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Figma's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Figma Specs (beta) added to paid seats; Full seat now lists Figma Motion and Figma Specs; Dev/Collab seats include Specs; Specs row added to feature comparison; Code layers added to the Figma Design comparison table: create or import code on the canvas and edit it as design layers or with Figma Make tools; included on Professional, Organization and Enterprise, not Starter. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Organization at $5, Enterprise at $5 and Professional at $16.
It lands 4 days after Figma's previous change, "Figma debuts Specs in beta, bundled with Full, Dev and Collab seats" (Oct 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Figma agent exits beta, ending free ride as AI credit charges now apply" (Oct 12, 2026), "Figma drops cross-team publishing from Professional plan spotlight" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Figma swaps team-wide publishing for unlimited files on Pro plan" (Sep 10, 2026). Figma has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Framer, Webflow and Penpot also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
