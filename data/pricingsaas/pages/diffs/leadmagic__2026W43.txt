What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of LeadMagic's pricing page, Pulse recorded 5 changes: Shared team seats (3/5/10/20) replaced by table seats (1/1/2/3); whole team can view tables. Team seating moved to Enterprise/team access at $49/seat; New Tables HTTP-requests allowance on all plans (100k/200k/400k/750k per month), currently 1M/mo free in preview; Enterprise plan (from $2,490/quarter: credit price lock, custom volume, 25+ RPS search, dedicated SLA) removed; replaced by by-reques. In detail: Search API on Essential and Growth now billed at 1 credit per returned result (person, company, or job) instead of 1 credit per request; a 25-result page costs 25 credits, empty results free; Enterprise plan (from $2,490/quarter: credit price lock, custom volume, 25+ RPS search, dedicated SLA) removed; replaced by by-request Enterprise/team access and a separate Enterprise Security offering; New Enterprise Security offering: annual plans only, investment starts at $10,000; quote-based proposal for agreed scope. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change, plan removed, new plan, feature added, limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Essential at $83, Growth at $208, Professional at $416, Ultimate at $708 and Enterprise Team Seat at $49.
It lands 49 days after LeadMagic's previous change, "LeadMagic overhauls seats: Basic to 1, Essential's "20" cuts to 3" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LeadMagic: Basic plan monthly price increase ($49→$49.99)" (Jul 27, 2026). LeadMagic has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Seamless.AI and Lusha also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
