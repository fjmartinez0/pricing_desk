What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Redocly's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Per-seat Pro $10 and Enterprise $24 replaced by flat Pro $249/mo, Scale $729/mo and custom Enterprise; Respect Monitoring usage tiers ($100-$1000/mo) removed; Page caps (100 Pro, 500 Enterprise) and per-seat counting replaced by unlimited pages, seats, readers and Git contributors; Pro includes 3 projects, Scale 10; New plan features: SDK generation and publishing (early access) on Scale, Redocly.
