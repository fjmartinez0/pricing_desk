What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of CommPeak's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Feature 'AI Call Analysis' replaced by 'Speech to Text' in the CommPeak Dialer and Cloud PBX feature lists. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists DID Numbers at $2, Cloud PBX Small at $75, Cloud PBX Medium at $119, Cloud PBX Large at $199 and Cloud PBX Extra-Large at $349.
It lands 14 days after CommPeak's previous change, "CommPeak adds $2/day AI voice agent tier, splits dialer pricing" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CommPeak: TextPeak feature list refreshed (API Streams removed, Webchat + Inbox Insights added)" (Aug 17, 2026). CommPeak has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
