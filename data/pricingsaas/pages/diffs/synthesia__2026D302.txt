What happened

In detail: New 'Syren' video-generation allowance rows added to plan cards, in the slot previously occupied by the Roleplay add-on - approximately 24 and 84 videos/year shown for Pro and the next tier up. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, add-on removed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $14 and Pro at $59.

Before that Pulse recorded "Synthesia cuts Syren promo credits to 1,000 on Basic and 1,500 on Starter and Pro" (Oct 22, 2026), "Synthesia launches Syren AI video agent with 2,000 free credits for a month" (Oct 10, 2026) and "Synthesia moves to credits, cuts free plan allowance by 58% to 500 a month" (Oct 2, 2026).
