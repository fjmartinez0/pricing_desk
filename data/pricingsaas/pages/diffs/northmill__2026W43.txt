What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Northmill Bank's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Extra företagskonto no longer shows a monthly-payment price (19 kr/month); only the annual-payment price of 199 kr remains. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Grundpaket at SEK 99, Betala senare at 99% of purchase amount, POS Flex at SEK 549, Windows kassasystem at SEK 1,199 and Apple kassasystem at SEK 1,199.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Northmill Bank. Northmill Bank has been tracked since 2026-03-05.
