What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of BigCommerce's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Limited-time offer: 50% off first year on Core, Growth or Scale annual plans by Dec 31 (new customers). Annual per-month prices drop Core $29→$19.50, Growth $79→$52.50, Scale $299→$199.50; list $39/$105/$399. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $29, Growth at $79, Scale at $299 and Performance at $1,499.
It lands 14 days after BigCommerce's previous change, "BigCommerce adds express routing and priority support to Growth, Scale plans" (Nov 8, 2026). BigCommerce has been tracked since 2026-02-20.
In the same window Shopify, Squarespace and Salesforce also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
