What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of ReversingLabs's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Community+ plan dropped from $500/month to $99/year (billing period also changed monthly -> annual). JFrog Artifactory integration moved from the paid Community+ plan into the free Community plan. In detail: JFrog Artifactory integration (blocks unsafe OSS package imports) moved from the paid Community+ plan down to the free Community plan; Community+ plan price dropped from $500/month to $99/year, with the billing period changed from monthly to annual. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Community+ at $8.25.
