What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Galileo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Observability Cloud moved from Infrastructure/App & Infra/End-to-End tiers ($15/$60/$75 per host) to a la carte prices plus Free ($0), Essentials ($95/host) and Premier ($150/host) editions; Agent Observability allotment renamed from Luna tokens/fine-tuning runs to Eval-G1 (500M tokens, 5 fine-tuning runs per year). Pulse files it as a packaging change (positioning, packaging change). The current page lists Pro at $100.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Galileo. Galileo has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
