What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of LivePlan's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Member Perks' section added, giving LivePlan customers access to over $10,000 in discounts on partner tools (LegalZoom, Verizon, FreshBooks, TikTok, Gusto, B12, T-Mobile). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $15, Premium at $30 and LivePlan Expert at $30.
It lands 77 days after LivePlan's previous change, "LivePlan pulls its July 4th sale, reverts to evergreen tagline" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LivePlan launches July 4th sale: up to 60% off Premium, base prices unchanged" (Jul 6, 2026) and "LivePlan: added Idea Canvas + Pitch Presentation (replaces One-page pitch builder)" (Jul 3, 2026). LivePlan has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Zoho, Microsoft, HubSpot, Google and Clay also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
