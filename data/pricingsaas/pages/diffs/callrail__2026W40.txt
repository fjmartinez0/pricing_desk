What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of CallRail's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New FAQ entry discloses previously unpublished API usage overage pricing: free up to 1,500 calls/month, $80 USD/month for 1,501-12,000 calls, $200 USD/month for 12,001-300,000 calls. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Lead Tracking at $50, Lead Tracking Complete at $95, Lead Conversion at $150, Lead Conversion Complete at $195 and Premium Intelligence at $85.
It lands 42 days after CallRail's previous change, "CallRail's $95/mo AI Voice Assist now requires a $55/mo Lead Tracking base" (Aug 17, 2026). CallRail has been tracked since 2026-05-13.
In the same window Aircall, Twilio, Ringcentral, OpenPhone and Nextiva also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
