What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Homebase's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New limited-time '6 Months Free: Payroll & Plus Plan' promo added: add Payroll and get 6 months of Payroll and the Plus plan free (Plus shown at $0/location/month, down from $70); Payroll add-on base fee increased from $39/month to $49/month (add-on card and FAQ); per-employee fee unchanged at $6/month. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added, price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Essentials at $24, Plus at $56, All-in-One at $96, Background Checks at $30 per background check and Payroll at $49.
It lands 7 days after Homebase's previous change, "Homebase cuts job post boosts 37% to $50, drops Hiring Assistant annual billing" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Homebase drops Hiring Assistant per-post pricing for $99/mo flat rate, ex" (Sep 7, 2026), "Homebase: Payroll add-on price decrease ($49→$39/mo)" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Homebase hikes payroll add-on fee to $49/month, keeps per-employee rate" (Jul 27, 2026). Homebase has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Workleap, Wagepoint, Tracker, Time Doctor and SimInsights also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
