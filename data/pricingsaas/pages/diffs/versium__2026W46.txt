What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Versium's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pay-As-You-Go ($125 min/file) tier removed; Resellers and Distributors custom-pricing tier added; seven credit package prices itemized; subscription rate floor $0.005/credit. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Credit Packages at $250 per use + $0.05 per match credit and Annual Subscriptions at $0.02 per match credit + $5,000 per use.
It lands 112 days after Versium's previous change, "Versium locks Testing Data access behind ID checks, drops Look-alike List tool" (Jul 20, 2026). Versium has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Salesforce and Treasure Data also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
