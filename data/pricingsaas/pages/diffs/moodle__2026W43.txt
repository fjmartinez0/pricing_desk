What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Moodle's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter plan annual price cut from $170 to $160 USD; Standard plan annual price cut from $2,100 to $2,090 USD (AUD list price unchanged for both). In detail: Starter plan USD price decreased from $170 to $160/year; Standard plan USD price decreased from $2,100 to $2,090/year. AUD anchor price unchanged for both. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $14.17, Mini at $22.5, Small at $40.83, Medium at $99.17 and Standard at $175.
It lands 7 days after Moodle's previous change, "Moodle raises MoodleCloud Starter annual price to $170, up from $160" (Oct 12, 2026). Moodle has been tracked since 2026-10-07.
In the same window Google and Supernova also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
