What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Similarweb's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Ads Intel Suite feature list restructured: added "AI ads", removed "Cross channel visibility", and renamed "Search ad analysis" to "Paid Search analysis" and "Display ad analysis" to "Display & Social ads". Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Competitive Intelligence at $125, Competitive Intel & SEO & AEO at $333 and Competitive Intel, SEO & AEO & Ads at $542.
It lands 118 days after Similarweb's previous change, "Similarweb rebrands pricing tiers from Packages to Plans" (Jun 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Similarweb Launches AI Studio for Natural Language Market Insights" (Mar 11, 2026), "Similarweb adds AI Studio and revamps enterprise support messaging" (Week 10, 2026) and "Similarweb rebrands Shopper Intelligence as Retail Intelligence" (Week 7, 2026).
