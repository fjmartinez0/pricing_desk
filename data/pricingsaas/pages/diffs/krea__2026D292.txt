What happened
Between the Oct 18, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Krea's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Per-plan model allowance examples rewritten with unchanged compute units (5k/20k/60k/80k): Nano Banana 2 generations up ~75% (Pro 257 to 450, Max 771 to 1,350, Business 1,028 to 1,800, Basic 64 to ~112); Seedance examples moved to Seedance 2.5 with fewer videos (Pro 83 to ~65, Max 250 to ~197, Business 333 to ~262; Basic 20 Seedance 2.0 to ~15 Seedance 2.0 Fast). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 23, 2026) lists Basic at $5, Pro at $21, Max at $63 and Business at $160.
It lands the same day as Krea's previous change, "Krea offers 14 days of unlimited Krea 3 with annual Pro or Max plans" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Krea offers 14 days of unlimited Krea 3 with Pro or Max annual plans" (Oct 16, 2026), "Krea adds Recraft V4.1 Flash with up to 7,714 images on Max plan" (Sep 25, 2026) and "Krea slashes MiniMax H3 Max video quotas 4x across all paid tiers" (Sep 21, 2026). Krea has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
