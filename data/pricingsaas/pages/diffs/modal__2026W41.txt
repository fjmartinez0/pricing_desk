What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Modal's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: New Network Egress usage charge of $0.04/GiB added to resource costs, with included monthly egress per plan; Plans now include free monthly network egress: Starter 1 TiB, Team 10 TiB, Enterprise 100 TiB; Enterprise security bullet changed from 'SAML SSO' to 'Okta SSO' alongside audit logs and HIPAA. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, feature added, feature change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Team at $250, Compute resources at $1.1 NVIDIA A10G instance hour + $1.95 L40S GPU hour and Sandbox + Notebooks compute at $2.4 per 100 GiB-Hour + $14.19 per 100 core-hours.
It lands 21 days after Modal's previous change, "Modal renames Enterprise SSO from Okta to standards-based SAML" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Modal adds included token usage row, renames free credits to free compute" (Sep 12, 2026), "Modal swaps Okta SSO for SAML SSO in Enterprise audit logs bullet" (Sep 11, 2026) and "Modal cuts region surcharge floor to 1.15x, easing cross-region compute costs" (Sep 8, 2026). Modal has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Together AI and Lambda Labs also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
