What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Basecamp's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Package ladder renamed and repriced: Studio $59 is now Pro $79/mo (8 projects); Unlimited Edition renamed Pro Unlimited, $300 to $329/mo monthly; Pro tier is now Pro Plus; Card tier now caps at 8 active projects and 10 users (was 10 projects, unlimited users); Freelancer storage 5 GB to 10 GB. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, limit decrease) with an upward direction. The current page lists Freelancer at $25, Studio at $59, Pro at $99, Unlimited at $299 and Additional Storage at $100.
It lands 7 days after Basecamp's previous change, "Basecamp adds AI agent integrations — Claude, ChatGPT, Grok Bot — to all plan" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Basecamp axes per-user fees for fixed pricing, cuts top-tier storage from 5TB" (Aug 24, 2026). Basecamp has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Notion, ClickUp, Asana and Trello also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
