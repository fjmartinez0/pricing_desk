What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Fingercheck's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Advanced I-9 & E-Verify add-on (monthly add-on fee) added to the Add-Ons section and listed on the 360 and 360 Plus cards. Plan cards now also list which existing add-ons apply: Time & Attendance shows Advanced Support; 360 grows from 4 to 9 add-ons; 360 Plus gains an add-on list (8). Wage Garnishment, HR Compliance, Background Checks and Advanced Support already existed in the Add-Ons section. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on). The current page lists Time & Attendance at $29, 360 at $79 and 360 Plus at $129.
It lands 83 days after Fingercheck's previous change, "Fingercheck hikes per-employee add-on fees up to 50% across all three plans" (Aug 31, 2026). Fingercheck has been tracked since 2023-12-09.
In the same window Paychex and Gusto also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
