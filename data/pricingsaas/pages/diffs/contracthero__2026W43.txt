What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of ContractHero's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New entry-level Start plan added at 129 EUR per month: 80 contracts, 3 users, AI analysis and summary, with a self-serve Try it now trial CTA; Essential and above unchanged in price. It lands 112 days after ContractHero's previous change, "ContractHero replaces Bulk Download with Reporting in Pro plan" (Jun 29, 2026).
