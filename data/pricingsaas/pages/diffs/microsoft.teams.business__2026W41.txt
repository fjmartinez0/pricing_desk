What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Microsoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Business Standard w/ Copilot plan highlights now include AI chat with LLM (model) choice; "Reasoning AI" renamed to "Pre-built agents" for research/data analysis. In detail: Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot: plan highlights now mention AI chat grounded in work data with large language model (LLM) choice; "Reasoning AI for research and data analysis" renamed to "Pre-built agents for research and data analysis". Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction.
