Hostinger clarifies AI Builder credits are a one-time gift, not recurring
Verified Oct 12, 2026·Cloud & DevOps·Direction: neutral
The changeFeature — feature change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 5, 2026 → Oct 12, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Hostinger's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Builder credits line reworded to '(5/15) AI credits for website and app creation (one-time gift)', clarifying credits are a one-time gift; amounts unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Premium at $2.99, Unlimited at $3.99, Cloud Startup at $9.99, Agency Startup at $29 and Agency Professional at $44.
It lands 28 days after Hostinger's previous change, "Hostinger opens Ecommerce to Premium plan, drops row from comparison table" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hostinger drops Summer Sale banner as Unlimited plan price ticks up to $3.99/mo" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Hostinger unlocks unlimited mailboxes per site on Cloud Startup plan" (Jul 20, 2026). Hostinger has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Dreamhost, Name.com and Hostgator also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
