What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Kinsta's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday promo: 50% off 3 months on monthly plans, annual 3 months off (was 2 months free); Agency from $284 to $255; ends Dec 1, 2026, new customers only. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Single 20GB at $30, Single 40GB at $42, WP 2 at $59, Single 65GB at $75 and WP 5 at $96.
It lands 7 days after Kinsta's previous change, "Kinsta Enterprise price jumps $417 to $500/mo, drops upfront annual deals" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kinsta narrows Enterprise custom agreement terms to select plans" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Kinsta adds free agency site hosting and dedicated enterprise servers" (Oct 19, 2026). Kinsta has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
