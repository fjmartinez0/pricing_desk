What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Front's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Autopilot AI agent starting price increased from $0.05 to $0.39 per conversation. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $25, Professional at $65, Enterprise at $105, API Rate Limit Increase at $200 and Autopilot at $0.39 per conversation.
It lands 28 days after Front's previous change, "Front adds external AI agents, Compass and MCP server to plans" (Oct 12, 2026). Front has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
