What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Nacelle's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Nacelle replaced its $399/mo Standard and Custom/yr Enterprise plans with a new product, Delta, billed as a $500/mo flat platform fee plus 20% of proven incremental revenue; the 21-day free trial was dropped; AI recommendation features (intent analysis, segment personas, onboarding, support) dropped; Delta instead offers ad-account reading, creative profiling, control-group revenue measurement. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Delta at $500.
