What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Quickbooks's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Essentials: $75/mo ($37.50 promo) -> $85/mo ($42.50 promo). Plus: $115/mo ($57.50 promo) -> $140/mo ($70 promo). Advanced: $275/mo ($137.50 promo) -> $340/mo ($170 promo). Free and Simple Start plans unchanged. In detail: Essentials $75->$85/mo ($37.50->$42.50 promo), Plus $115->$140/mo ($57.50->$70 promo), Advanced $275->$340/mo ($137.50->$170 promo); Free and Simple Start unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Simple Start at $38, Essentials at $85, Plus at $140 and Advanced at $340.
It lands the same day as Quickbooks's previous change, "QuickBooks Payroll: Workforce bundle prices increase ($125→$135, $203→$228)" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "QuickBooks: Free plan added + Essentials price hike ($75→$85/mo)" (Aug 10, 2026), "QuickBooks Payroll: Summer promo jumps to 90% off (was 50%)" (Aug 10, 2026) and "QuickBooks Payroll: per-employee fee cut on all 3 plans" (Jul 27, 2026). Quickbooks has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Zoho, Xero, Sage, Microsoft and Freshbooks also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
