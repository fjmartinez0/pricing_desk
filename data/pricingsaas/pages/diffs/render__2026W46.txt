What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Render's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Render Sandboxes (Beta) session limit lifted: 'Sandboxes can run up to 30 days on Pro and above' is now 'Can run indefinitely'; Hobby maximum session length 24 hours now No limit (Pro/Scale/Enterprise already No limit); Render Postgres feature list: Connection pooling added; 1 GB SSD storage included removed; Logical backup reworded to Logical exports for paid instances. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $25, Scale at $499, Postgres - 64c-256g at $3,000, Postgres - 64c-512g at $6,000 and Postgres - 128c-1024g at $11,000.
It lands 7 days after Render's previous change, "Render launches Sandboxes beta: $0.12/CPU-hour microVMs for agent workloads" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Render: Workflows comparison rows changed from concurrent task runs (20/50/100, then $10/mo per 50) to parallel task…" (Oct 26, 2026), "Render Key Value cuts compute prices up to 45% and adds 50 GB tier; Workflows max compute halved" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Render: Workflows compute doubles to 8 CPU / 32 GB RAM" (Sep 28, 2026). Render has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Google Cloud, Aws, Digitalocean and Railway also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
