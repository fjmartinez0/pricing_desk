What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Plytix's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Add-on 'Channels' renamed to 'Feed Management' (still $0/mo); description unchanged; New AI action 'Background replacement' added to AI credits table at 30 credits per use; Plan cards rewritten: taglines shortened (e.g. 'For small teams') and bullets added listing seats, storage, CDN traffic, API calls; Pro badged Most popular.
