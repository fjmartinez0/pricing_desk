What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Xero's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Analytics value-prop copy on pricing page renamed from third-party partner 'Syft' to Xero's own-branded 'Xero Analytics'; underlying 'analytics on every plan' claim is unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Early at $27, Growing at $59 and Established at $97.
It lands 28 days after Xero's previous change, "Xero graduates AI auto-reconcile from Beta to full release" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Xero: Intro promo deepened to 90% off, extended to 6 months" (Aug 11, 2026), "Xero: announces subscription price increase effective Oct 1, 2026" (Aug 5, 2026) and "Xero shrinks new-customer discount, hikes intro pricing to $5/$11/$18" (Jul 6, 2026). Xero has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Quickbooks, Zoho, Sage, Freshbooks and Kashoo also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
