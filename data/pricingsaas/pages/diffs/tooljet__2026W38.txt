What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of ToolJet's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Added a coding-agent plugin integration (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor): building through it consumes no AI credits and does not require an extra builder seat; New 'Basic' plan added between Free and Pro at $23/builder/month, positioned around AI credits (50 end users, 2 apps, 2,000 AI credits per builder, unlimited add-on AI credits). Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Basic at $23, Pro at $79, Team at $199 and Pay-as-you-go AI Credits at $1 per 100 AI credits.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for ToolJet. ToolJet has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
