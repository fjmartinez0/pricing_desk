What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Aws's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: MWAA Serverless pricing adds a new billing line: hourly Customer Managed Tasks usage, billed per second with a 1-minute minimum, alongside AWS Managed Tasks. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Large Environment at $0.99 per use, MWAA Serverless at $0.08 per use, Medium Environment at $0.74 per use, Micro Environment at $0.29 per use and XLarge Environment at $1.98 per use.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Aws. Aws has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
