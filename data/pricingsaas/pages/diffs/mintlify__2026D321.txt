What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 17, 2026 captures of Mintlify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Entry plan renamed from Starter to Free; price remains $0/mo and limits (5 editors) unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed). The current page lists Pro at $450.
It lands 12 days after Mintlify's previous change, "Mintlify renames Automations to Autopilot, adds Enterprise-only Automations" (Nov 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mintlify swaps free credits for a 14-day Pro trial with 5,000 credits" (Oct 13, 2026), "Mintlify ends free 6-month Pro offer for early-stage startups" (Oct 3, 2026) and "Mintlify offers startups 6 months of Pro free, no card needed" (Oct 1, 2026). Mintlify has been tracked since 2024-10-21.
In the same window Read AI, Slite, Postman, Notion and GitBook also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
