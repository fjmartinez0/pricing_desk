What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Perdoo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan loses AI Agent (Spark) and AI Coach (Vince): Free card drops 'AI Assistant & Coach' and comparison table now shows '—' for Free; Premium card now lists AI Agent (Spark) and AI Coach (Vince) explicitly. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Premium at $7.20, Supreme at $8.80 and View-only licenses at $1.75.
It lands 119 days after Perdoo's previous change, "Perdoo adds ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity AI integrations to Premium, Supreme" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Perdoo: AI token allowances raised, MCP server + live AI goal review added" (Jul 13, 2026), "Perdoo Opens MCP Server Access to Free Plan for AI Tool Integration" (Apr 28, 2026) and "Perdoo Adds Free AI Assistant and MCP Server for Premium Plans" (Apr 24, 2026). Perdoo has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
