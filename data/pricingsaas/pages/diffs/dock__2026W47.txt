What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Dock's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed the tiered 'AI usage' row (Limited/Standard/High/Custom per plan) from the feature comparison table. FAQ copy changed from 'Dock AI scales with every plan' to 'Dock AI is included in every plan' with no separate credits/tokens, implying usage is no longer differentiated by tier. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised). The current page lists Standard at $350, Premium at $1,000 and Additional Seat at $50.
It lands 42 days after Dock's previous change, "Dock adds Content Lifecycle feature to Enterprise plan comparison table" (Oct 4, 2026). Dock has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Pandadoc and signNow also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
