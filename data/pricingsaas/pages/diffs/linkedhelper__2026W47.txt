What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Linked Helper's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added mid-subscription upgrade flexibility: Local licenses can now upgrade to Cloud and Standard can upgrade to Pro at any time, prorated; previously unsupported until expiration/plan end.
