What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Smoobu's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday promo (code BLACKFRIDAY26): annual discounts raised from Yearly 10%/2-Year 20% to 66%/70%; on 2-Year billing Professional Prepaid EUR28 to EUR10.50/mo and Teams Pro+ EUR44 to EUR16.50/mo (page default switched from Yearly to 2-Year). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction.
It lands 126 days after Smoobu's previous change, "Smoobu Summer Deal ends, annual/2-year discounts drop back to 10%/20%" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Smoobu boosts Summer Deal: 40% off, annual discounts jump to 33%/40%" (Jul 1, 2026). Smoobu has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
In the same window Lodgify, Hospitable and Guesty also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
