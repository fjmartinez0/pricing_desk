What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Canva's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New promo: 50% off the first 3 months of Canva Pro or Business monthly plans, shown in top banner and on Pro/Business cards (US$18→$9, US$25→$12.50 monthly). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $12 and Business at $20.83.
This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Canva offers 50% off Pro and Business for first 3 months in select countries" (Day 324, 2026, saved Nov 20, 2026). It is the same change, not a new one. Before that, Canva's previous recorded change was "Canva hides free plan's AI quota, swapping 20 uses for vague limited access" (Oct 30, 2026). Canva has been tracked since 2023-11-17.
In the same window Snappa, Figma and Visme also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
