What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Puzzle's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Complete plan accuracy claim changed from '98% accuracy' to '85-90% of transactions categorized automatically' (Starter bullet 'Up to 98% auto-categorization' reworded to match). Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $24, Core at $58, Complete at $96, Scale at $288 and Expert Reviewed at $171.
It lands 35 days after Puzzle's previous change, "Puzzle: Restructured into 3 tabs + full-service accounting, promos removed" (Sep 21, 2026). Puzzle has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
In the same window TurboTax, Trust & Will, Tripletex, Metronome and Finix also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
