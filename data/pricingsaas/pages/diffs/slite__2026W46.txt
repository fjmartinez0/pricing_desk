What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Slite's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Agent credits now shown as a comparison-table row (Pro 50/seat/month, Enterprise custom); Enterprise card adds custom credit allowance bullet. Pro's 50 monthly credits per seat unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $10 and Pro at $20.
It lands 104 days after Slite's previous change, "Slite: Service accounts added to Integrations & API comparison" (Jul 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Slite: renames tiers to Basic/Pro, hikes entry price $8->$10 + 1 more change" (Jul 4, 2026) and "Slite raises entry price 25% as AI search goes tiered by plan" (Jun 18, 2026). Slite has been tracked since 2026-03-18.
