MindsHub: 4 new AI models added to rate card (GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1)

What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: MindsHub Blaze (Cerebras), Claude Fable 5.1, GPT-6 Astra, and Gemini 3.8 Flash each added as new models on the token-based rate card, each with its own per-model price. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pay as you go at $0.1 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.5 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands 6 days after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB adds DeepSeek V4 Flash, GLM 5.3 and GLM 5.3 Flash to the model rate card" (Sep 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB hikes Blaze rates up to 183%, loosens Air free-tier cap" (Sep 15, 2026), "MindsDB adds MindsHub Blaze and GPT-OSS 120B to rate card, from $0.15 per 1M" (Sep 14, 2026) and "MindsDB quietly deletes all mentions of its 5% platform fee, rates untouched" (Sep 4, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Huggingface and Alteryx also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
“MindsHub” is the name MindsDB’s pricing page uses; Pulse files the change under MindsDB, the company it tracks.
