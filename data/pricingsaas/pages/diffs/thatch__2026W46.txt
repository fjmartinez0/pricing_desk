What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Thatch's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Standard plan feature list reworked: AI-Driven insights, HIPAA docs and Compliance reporting dropped; Plan documents, ACA affordability tools and benefits answers added. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $50.
It lands 49 days after Thatch's previous change, "Thatch renames Enterprise to Custom, reveals $2,400/year minimum pricing" (Sep 21, 2026). Thatch has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Ava, Workstream, Workable, Tracker and Simployer also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
