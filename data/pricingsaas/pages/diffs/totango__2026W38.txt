What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 18, 2026 captures of Totango's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Totango rebranded to Odie, replacing its 3-product contact-sales-only lineup with 2 products showing starting prices: Customer Knowledge Engine $500/mo, Customer Operating System $1,500/mo.
