What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Vercel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added 'Backends' capability under Remote Cache in Build & Deploy, and a new 'Marketplace Integrations' row in the Flags section. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 20, 2026) lists Pro at $20.
It lands 7 days after Vercel's previous change, "Vercel bundles Edge, Fast Data, Blob & Sandbox overages into flat CDN plan" (Sep 14, 2026).
