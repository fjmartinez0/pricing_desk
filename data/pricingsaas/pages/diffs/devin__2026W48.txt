What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Devin's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Teams drops the $40/mo per full dev seat charge; plan now shows a flat $80/month (page copy: self-serve at a flat $80/month, shared on-demand credits for up to 100 members); New 'Access to Devin Bots' feature added to the Max, Teams and Enterprise plan cards, plus a new Devin Bots row in the Compare plans table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, packaging change). The current page lists Pro at $20, Teams at $80 and Max at $200.
It lands 7 days after Devin's previous change, "Devin halves Teams user cap to 100, lets Pro/Max/Teams use ChatGPT plans" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Devin: Team plan member cap reduced to 200 (was unlimited)" (Sep 27, 2026) and "Devin upgrades Pro plan's free bundled coding model to SWE-2, pricing unchanged" (Sep 14, 2026). Devin has been tracked since 2026-05-19.
In the same window Windsurf, Replit, GitHub, Cursor and Google also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
