What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Gastromatic's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed the 'Automatisierter Dienstplan' feature module (algorithm-written shift schedule, planning types, over/under-capacity view) from the page's product feature showcase. In detail: Removed the 'Automatisierter Dienstplan' add-on (algorithm-written shift schedule, planning types, over/under-capacity view) from the "Unsere Add-Ons" section; it was also dropped from the footer product navigation. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at EUR 69.5 and Professional at EUR 99.5.
It lands 56 days after Gastromatic's previous change, "Gastromatic: Added Support & payroll features to Starter/Professional" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Gastromatic: Starter switches to flat rate (69,50€) + Professional revealed" (Aug 17, 2026). Gastromatic has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
In the same window Thinkific, CodeSignal, Ashby, Prodoscore and LumiQ also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
