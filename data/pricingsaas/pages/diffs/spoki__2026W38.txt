What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Spoki's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: WhatsApp marketing conversation credit rate increased from €0.057 to €0.0658 per conversation (+15.4%), raising the Marketing profile's real-spend example from €287.80/mo to €308.92/mo. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Service at EUR 39, Marketing at EUR 79, Sales at EUR 119, Voice at EUR 19.99 and SMS Two-way at EUR 19.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Spoki. Spoki has been tracked since 2026-08-27.
