What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Teamwork's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The launch date for usage-based AI credit billing (after the current introductory free-AI-usage period on paid plans) was pushed back two months, from September 2026 to November 2026. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Basics at $9.99 and Accelerate at $24.99.
It lands 48 days after Teamwork's previous change, "Teamwork: Free trial shortened from 30 days to 14 days" (Aug 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Teamwork.com: AI moves from free to usage-based billing (Sept 2026)" (Jul 4, 2026). Teamwork has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Notion, Trello, ClickUp, Asana and monday.com also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
