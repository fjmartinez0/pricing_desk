What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Grain's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Business plan 'Free viewer seats' bullet renamed to 'Free Lite seats'; prices unchanged ($15 Pro, $29/seat Business).
