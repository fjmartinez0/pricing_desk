CloudFix RightSpend drops "no savings, no charge" for $25K+ EC2 teams
Verified Oct 5, 2026·Cloud & DevOps·Direction: neutral
The changeAdd-on — add-on change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 28, 2026 → Oct 5, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of CloudFix's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: RightSpend add-on terms clarified: 25% of net-new savings unchanged; now commitment-free EC2 discounts 20-55%, no fee on own RIs/Savings Plans, for teams with $25K+/month EC2 spend; 'no savings, no charge' dropped. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $124.17, Growth at $415.83, Scale at $1,249.17, Enterprise at $2,499.17 and RightSpend at 25% of net new savings delivered.
It lands 14 days after CloudFix's previous change, "CloudFix nearly doubles automated fixers from 30+ to 53 across all plans" (Sep 21, 2026). CloudFix has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Kubecost also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
