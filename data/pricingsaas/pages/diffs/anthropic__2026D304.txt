What happened
Between the Oct 30, 2026 and Oct 31, 2026 captures of Anthropic's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Max's tier choice (From $100, 5x or 20x Pro usage) split into two named plans: Max at $100/month (5x Pro usage every 5 hours and every week, $100/month API credits) and Ultra at $200/month, replacing Max 20x (no 5-hour limit for verified accounts, 10x Pro usage every week, $200/month API credits, the same amount Max 20x had). 'Higher output limits for all tasks' dropped from the Max card. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $17, Team (Standard seat) at $20, Enterprise (self-serve) at $20, Max at $100 and Team (Premium seat) at $100.
It lands 19 days after Anthropic's previous change, "Anthropic gives Max subscribers monthly API credits, up to $200" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Anthropic API launches Haiku 5.5 at $0.10/$0.50 per MTok, Haiku 4.5 goes Legacy" (Oct 12, 2026), "Anthropic opens Claude Design, Slides and Docs to Free plan users" (Oct 9, 2026) and "Anthropic API launches Haiku 5.5 at $0.10/$0.50, halves Sonnet 5.5 cache reads" (Oct 8, 2026). Anthropic has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window OpenAI, Microsoft, Google, X and Mistral AI also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
