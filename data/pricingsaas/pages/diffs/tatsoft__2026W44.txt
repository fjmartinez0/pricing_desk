What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Tatsoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Community Edition now free for production use within its limits (1,000 tags/100 I/O) with community support; previously required a support and maintenance agreement. Designer 'no time limit' wording dropped. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists EdgeConnect Gateway at $750, MachineHMI 150 at $900, EdgeConnect View at $1,700, MachineHMI 500 at $1,750 and MachineHMI 1500 at $3,000.
It lands 7 days after Tatsoft's previous change, "Tatsoft: Included connectors count raised from 100+ to 120+ across hero text and EdgeConnect card" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Tatsoft: Enterprise tiers gain unlimited clients" (Aug 31, 2026). Tatsoft has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
In the same window Salesforce and Microsoft also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
