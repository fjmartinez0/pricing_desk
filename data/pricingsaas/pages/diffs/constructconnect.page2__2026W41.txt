What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of ConstructConnect's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Premium shifts from custom pricing to a published 'Starting at $10,000/year'; Professional $199/mo per market now also billed per seat; Saved searches delivered daily moved from Starter's feature list to Professional's 'All Starter features, plus' list. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed, feature removed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $117, Professional at $199 and Premium at $833.33.
It lands 84 days after ConstructConnect's previous change, "ConstructConnect Page2 's Ask Docs AI search exits beta, now generally available" (Jul 13, 2026). ConstructConnect has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
