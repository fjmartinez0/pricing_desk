What happened
Between the Oct 13, 2026 and Oct 14, 2026 captures of Atlassian's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Jira Cloud Standard rose from $7.91 to $8.48 per user/month at the page's default 300-user team size, Monthly billing (~7%). Not the 1-100 user list rate; Jira Cloud Premium rose from $14.54 to $15.56 per user/month at the page's default 300-user team size, Monthly billing (~7%). Not the 1-100 user list rate. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Standard at $7.92 and Premium at $16.25.
It lands 42 days after Atlassian's previous change, "Atlassian Jira revamps automation quotas: renames rule runs, tightens caps" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Atlassian Jira overhauls automation limits, renames rule runs to steps" (Sep 2, 2026) and "Atlassian Jira adds AI hub with Agents, Rovo Studio; renames Issue to work item" (Aug 17, 2026). Atlassian has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window GitHub, Slack, Microsoft, GitLab and Asana also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
