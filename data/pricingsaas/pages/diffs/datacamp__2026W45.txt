What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of DataCamp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Special Price promo ended: Premium $14 to $28/mo and Teams $14 to $28/user/mo billed annually; yearly savings callout $255 to $90 and Special Price badges replaced. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Teams at $14 and Premium at $28.
It lands 21 days after DataCamp's previous change, "DataCamp adds 50% Teams promo ($14/user/mo, list $28), swaps Premium $1 intro for $14/mo annual deal" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "DataCamp Premium drops to $1 for first month in new promo" (Oct 5, 2026) and "DataCamp: Premium and Teams plans double from $14 to $28/month" (Aug 10, 2026). DataCamp has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
In the same window Udemy, Coursera and Codecademy also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
