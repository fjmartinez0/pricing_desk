What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Huntress's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Huntress removed its "Example pricing for 100 units" rates (EDR $7.99, ITDR $3.60, SIEM $3.50, SAT $1.75, ISPM $3.40); cards now headline the unchanged MSRPs ($8.99/$4.80/$4.00/$2.08/$4.00) with volume pricing via Get Volume Pricing. In detail: 100-unit example volume pricing removed; cards now show MSRP as headline price (MSRPs unchanged): EDR $7.99, ITDR $3.60, SIEM $3.50, SAT $1.75, ISPM $3.40 at 100 units no longer shown. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Managed SAT at $2.08, Managed SIEM at $4, Managed ISPM at $4, Managed ITDR at $4.8 and Managed EDR at $8.99.
It lands 21 days after Huntress's previous change, "Huntress shows list MSRP prices, with EDR at $8.99 per month" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Huntress swaps volume-bracket selector for a fixed 100-unit pricing example" (Sep 7, 2026). Huntress has been tracked since 2026-02-20.
In the same window Ava, Microsoft, Crowdstrike and Sentinelone also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
