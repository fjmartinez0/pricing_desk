What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of AppointMind's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Per-plan booking caps (200/600/1,000/200-per-schedule) removed from all four tiers and replaced with 'Unlimited number of appointments'; plan unit relabeled from 'schedule' to 'appointment calendar' at unchanged counts/prices. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Enterprise at EUR 10, Standard at EUR 20, Plus at EUR 40 and Premium at EUR 60.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for AppointMind. AppointMind has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
