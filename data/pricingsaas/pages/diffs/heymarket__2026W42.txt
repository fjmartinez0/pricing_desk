What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Heymarket's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: BETA label removed from AI Agents Q&A, scheduling and contact enrichment in the feature comparison table (Plus, Pro, Enterprise); availability unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $20, Plus at $41, Pro at $62 and Salesforce Add-on at $7.5.
It lands 28 days after Heymarket's previous change, "Heymarket bundles 8 email add-ons into Plus and up, drops them from Standard" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Heymarket: RCS Branded Messaging added (Plus, Pro, Enterprise)" (Aug 3, 2026). Heymarket has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
