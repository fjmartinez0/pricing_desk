What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of KickoffLabs's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Tiers renamed Hobby to Starter and Premium to Growth with higher prices: Starter $34/mo annual ($49 monthly), up from Hobby $13 ($19); Growth $69 ($99), up from Premium $48 ($69); Business rose from $99 to $125/mo annual ($145 to $179 monthly). Enterprise unchanged at $202 ($289). Existing Hobby and Premium subscribers keep their current price; Monthly lead limits doubled on entry tiers: 500 to 1. In detail: Monthly lead limits doubled on entry tiers: 500 to 1,000 (Hobby to Starter) and 2,500 to 5,000 (Premium to Growth); Growth adds Shopify purchase scoring, custom lead tags and Kiosk mode; tracking pixels and integrations move down from Premium to the entry Starter tier. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, limit increase, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $34, Growth at $69, Business at $125, Enterprise at $202 and SMS Contests Add-on at $50.
It lands 21 days after KickoffLabs's previous change, "KickoffLabs halves Free plan allowance to 50 leads, turns off Free email sending, adds Branded QR codes" (Oct 19, 2026). KickoffLabs has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Unbounce, OptinMonster and Leadpages also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
