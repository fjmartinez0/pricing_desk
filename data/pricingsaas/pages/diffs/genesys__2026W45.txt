What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Genesys's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Fair-use section heading renamed from 'AI tokens' to 'Experience Units'; body copy still refers to Genesys Cloud AI Experience tokens included in every CX package. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Genesys Cloud CX 1 at $75, Genesys Cloud CX 2 at $115, Genesys Cloud CX 3 at $155 and Genesys Cloud CX 4 at $240.
It lands 122 days after Genesys's previous change, "Genesys: BYOI add-on availability restricted to CX3 only" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Genesys Restricts BYOI Add-On to CX3 Plan, Cuts Access for Three Tiers" (Apr 13, 2026) and "Genesys bundles associate experience into all Cloud CX tiers" (Apr 9, 2026). Genesys has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Twilio also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
