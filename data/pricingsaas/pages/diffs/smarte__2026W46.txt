What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of SMARTe's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free ($0, 10 credits) and Pro ($25/mo) plans removed; page now shows one subscription with unlimited users, $15,000 and pay-per-credit pricing from $0.30/credit; Added Bring Your Own Key section: use a SMARTe API key inside partner platforms (Clay, Apollo, Zapier etc.) on the same subscription. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Subscription at $0.30 per credit.
It lands 97 days after SMARTe's previous change, "SMARTe: SSO/Usage Reports restricted to paid tiers + Enrich API added to Pro" (Aug 4, 2026). SMARTe has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Seamless.AI, Outreach, Salesloft and Apollo.io also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
