BaseNet unbundles BaseNeQXt: data access and security now paid add-ons
Verified Sep 28, 2026·Business Operations·Direction: neutral
The changeBaseNeQXt packaging — package change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 21, 2026 → Sep 28, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of BaseNet's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Hero copy now discloses BaseNeQXt pricing is modular ('you pay for what you activate') and states that data-access and communication-security modules are separate paid extensions, a packaging disclosure absent before. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essentials at EUR 69, Advanced at EUR 99, Ultimate at EUR 149, AI Credits 1,000 at EUR 45 and AI Credits 10,000 at EUR 300.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for BaseNet. BaseNet has been tracked since 2026-06-03.
