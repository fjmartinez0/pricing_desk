What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of PerfAI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Monthly credits cut: Free 500→100, Pro 1,000→350, Startup 3,000→1,500; prices unchanged ($0/$49/$299). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $49 and Growth at $299.
It lands 28 days after PerfAI's previous change, "PerfAI launches free 15-day trial for Startup plan, no card needed" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "PerfAI cuts Pro to $49 and Growth to $299, but slashes included credits up to 44%" (Aug 24, 2026). PerfAI has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window Alteryx, Cloudera and Databricks also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
