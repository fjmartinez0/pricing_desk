What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of CommPeak's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: CommPeak Dialer pricing split into Human agent ($5.50/day per active seat, was 'from $5.50') and a new AI voice agent tier at $2.00/day per active seat. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Cloud PBX Small at $75, Cloud PBX Medium at $119, Cloud PBX Large at $199, Cloud PBX Extra-Large at $349 and Cloud PBX XX-Large at $499.
It lands 63 days after CommPeak's previous change, "CommPeak: TextPeak feature list refreshed (API Streams removed, Webchat + Inbox Insights added)" (Aug 17, 2026). CommPeak has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
