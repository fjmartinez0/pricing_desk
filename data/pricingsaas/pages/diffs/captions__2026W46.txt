What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Captions's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan card (custom pricing, bulk credit discounts, custom seats, dedicated account management) removed; replaced by a 'Making videos at scale? Tesseract for business' Contact sales banner. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Max at $24.99 and Frontier at $69.99.
It lands 4 days after Captions's previous change, "Captions: Enterprise plan renamed to Tesseract for business, drops perks list" (Nov 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Captions: mid-tier plan renamed from Scale to Frontier" (Sep 28, 2026), "Captions rebrands Scale plan to Frontier, pricing untouched" (Sep 22, 2026) and "Captions: Batch post-ready clips feature added to Max plan" (Aug 31, 2026). Captions has been tracked since 2025-10-05.
