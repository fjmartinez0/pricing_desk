What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Simplepractice's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Credentialing add-on section: list $149/application, discounted to $99/application through Nov 30 for solo practices. Free credentialing offer continues (top banner restored; two free applications when converting from trial). In detail: New Credentialing add-on section (New and Improved): list $149/application, promo $99/application until Nov 30 (solo practices only); trial-to-paid signups still get two free credentialing applications. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on). The current page lists Starter at $49, Essential at $79, Plus at $99, AI-powered Note Taker at $35 and Insurance Navigator at $1.79 per claim filed.
It lands 7 days after Simplepractice's previous change, "SimplePractice adds free credentialing support to all plans, limited to two payers" (Nov 16, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Simplepractice offers free credentialing to new sign-ups in fresh promo push" (Nov 8, 2026), "Simplepractice drops Beta label from Treatment Planner in Care Aide" (Nov 1, 2026) and "Simplepractice: Plus plan highlight 'Appointment request widget' replaced by 'Advanced analytics capabilities'; prices…" (Oct 26, 2026). Simplepractice has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
