What happened
Between the Nov 23, 2026 and Nov 24, 2026 captures of Pinecone's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Limited-time promo doubles included monthly embedding tokens (e.g. llama-text-embed-v2 5M to 10M on Starter) on Starter and Builder through Dec 31, 2026; Added gemini-embedding-2, text-embedding-3-large and text-embedding-3-small embedding models; Starter excludes gemini-embedding-2. Inference renamed Integrated Embedding/Reranking; document indexes added. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added, feature added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Builder at $20, Standard at $4 Write Units (M) + $0.15 Restore from Backup (GB), Enterprise at $0.10 GB egress per month + $500 per use, HIPAA Compliance at $190 and Developer Support at $29.
It lands 52 days after Pinecone's previous change, "Pinecone drops Starter 1M token promo, gates cross-region restore to paid tiers" (Oct 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pinecone raises Enterprise namespaces per index limit tenfold to 1M" (Sep 29, 2026) and "Pinecone launches Nexus, an agent knowledge engine, via request-only trial" (Sep 21, 2026). Pinecone has been tracked since 2023-12-22.
In the same window Qdrant, Chromatic, Elastic, Zilliz and Redis also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
