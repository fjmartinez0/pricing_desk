What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Mux's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Mux Robots renamed 'AI workflows (Mux Robots)'; Find scenes ($0.0040/min+$0.0100/job) removed; Analyze structure ($0.0050/min+$0.0300/job) and Generate contextual text ($0.00010/min+$0.0050/job+$0.0025/artifact) added.
