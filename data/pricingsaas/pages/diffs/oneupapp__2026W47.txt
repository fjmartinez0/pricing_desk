What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of OneUp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Instagram/Facebook DM automation and separate auto-reply/auto-label keyword comment features merged into one 'DM and Comment automations' capability across all plans. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 18, 2026) lists Basic at $15, Intermediate at $48, Growth at $84, Business at $240 and Enterprise at $2,000.
It lands 21 days after OneUp's previous change, "OneUp doubles Enterprise plan starting price to $2,000 per month" (Oct 26, 2026). OneUp has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Hootsuite, Coschedule, Buffer and Sproutsocial also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
“Oneupapp” is the name OneUp’s pricing page uses; Pulse files the change under OneUp, the company it tracks.
