What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Ahrefs's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan's Ahrefs Connect API integration units/mo disclosed as 4M in the comparison table (previously shown only as 'Custom'); now reads '4M, Custom'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Starter at $29, Lite at $129, Brand Radar AI at $199, Standard at $249 and Advanced at $449.
It lands 7 days after Ahrefs's previous change, "Ahrefs doubles Connect API unit limits, drops Enterprise cap for Custom" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Ahrefs: API/credit limits raised (Lite-Advanced) + Free tier renamed" (Jul 2, 2026). Ahrefs has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Similarweb, Semrush and Moz also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
