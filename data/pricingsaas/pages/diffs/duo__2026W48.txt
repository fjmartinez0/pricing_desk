What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Duo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Comparison table adds Privileged Infrastructure Access (PIA) row; Premier card lists Agentic governance; agent features renamed (Agent & NHI Discovery, Agent Governance and Control). Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Duo Essentials at $3, Duo Advantage at $6 and Duo Premier at $9.
It lands the same day as Duo's previous change, "Duo Editionsandpricing adds Privileged Infrastructure Access to Premier, renames agent governance rows" (Nov 22, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Duo adds Agentic Visibility & Analytics to Advantage, renames Premier feature" (Oct 11, 2026) and "Duo Editionsandpricing adds Agentic Visibility & Analytics to Advantage and Premier" (Oct 11, 2026). Duo has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Slack, Microsoft, Zoom and SignalHire also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
