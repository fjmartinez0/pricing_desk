What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Zoom's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Zoom Rooms camera support cut from unlimited cameras to up to 4 cameras per Zoom Room; Supports unlimited digital signage bullet removed from the Zoom Rooms highlights list. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease, feature removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 28, 2026) lists Rooms at $41.58, Zoom Enhanced Media at $20 and Conference Room Connector at $41.58.
It lands 146 days after Zoom's previous change, "Zoom Rooms adds one-click wireless share and AI recording upgrades" (Jun 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Zoom Rooms launches $25 Enhanced Media add-on, boosts annual discount to 20%" (Mar 26, 2026), "Zoom Rooms Removes Cloud Recording and Transcription Feature from Meetings Section" (Q4 2025) and "Zoom Rooms Removes Key Add-ons from Pricing Page, Impacting Workspace Reservation and Visitor Management Services" (Q3 2025). Zoom has been tracked since 2023-11-15.
