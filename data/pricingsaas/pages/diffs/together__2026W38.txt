What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Qwen3.7-Max increased from $1.25/$0.13(cached) input and $3.75 output to $2.00/$0.25(cached) input and $6.00 output; new DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model added at $0.30/$0.006(cached) input and $1.20 output per 1M tokens. In detail: New model DeepSeek V4.1 Flash added to the serverless model catalog at $0.30/1M input tokens, $1.20/1M output tokens, and $0.006/1M cached tokens; Qwen3.7-Max price increased from $1.25/1M input ($0.13 cached) and $3.75/1M output to $2.00/1M input ($0.25 cached) and $6.00/1M output. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Managed Storage at $0.16 per GiB/month, Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, Sandbox (Code Interpreter) at $0.03 per session (60 min), GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100) and GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days).
It lands 2 days after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI replaces 3 fine-tuning size tiers with 29 per-model price rows" (Sep 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI hikes Qwen3.7-Max inference 25% to $2.50/$7.50 per 1M tokens" (Sep 16, 2026), "Together AI hikes Qwen3.7-Max pricing 60% and cuts LFM2.5-8B model" (Sep 11, 2026) and "Together AI adds Qwen3.8 Flash at $0.15/1M tokens, drops Gemma 3n E4B" (Sep 1, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
