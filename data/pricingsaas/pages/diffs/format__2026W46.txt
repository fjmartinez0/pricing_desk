What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Format's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Annual promo changed from 50% off (ends Oct 31, codes PROINTRO) to Cyber November 60% off first year (ends Dec 2, code CYBER2026); Pro promo $12 to $9.35/mo, Pro Plus $15 to $14.30/mo.
