What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Logicmonitor's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Essentials $16 to $19 and Advanced $27 to $37 per Hybrid Unit; Signature $53 to $50 (no longer labeled +Edwin AI); trial 15 to 14 days; Edwin AI Event Intelligence/Agents/Automation add-ons restructured into AI-credit-metered Edwin AI; LM Uptime and SaaS Monitoring add-on rows removed; Plans now include AI Credits per Hybrid Unit: 20 Essentials, 75 Advanced, 150 Signature. In detail: Edwin AI Event Intelligence/Agents/Automation add-ons consolidated into AI-credit-metered Edwin AI; Automated Diagnostics and Remediation (previously included in Advanced/Signature) and Cost Optimization now AI Credit-metered on all plans; LM Uptime and SaaS Monitoring add-on rows removed. Pulse files it as a pricing change (package change, feature added, price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $19, Advanced at $37 and Signature at $50.
It lands 126 days after Logicmonitor's previous change, "LogicMonitor: 4 new add-ons incl. Edwin AI Agents + LM Synthetics/RUM/Endpoint" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LogicMonitor: Edwin AI add-ons renamed with expanded self-healing/remediation capabilities" (Jul 3, 2026), "Logicmonitor rebrands Edwin AI add-ons with self-healing IT focus" (May 4, 2026) and "Logicmonitor replaces volume discounts with minimum quantity pricing" (Apr 3, 2026). Logicmonitor has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Datadog, Dynatrace, Elastic and Grafana also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
