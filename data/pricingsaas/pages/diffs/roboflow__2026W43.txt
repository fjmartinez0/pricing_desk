What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Roboflow's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Teams Add-On no longer offered on Core: SSO, RBAC, Review Mode, Labeling Analytics and Labeling History changed from 'Teams Add-On' to 'Not included' on Core (still included on Enterprise); Concurrent Training Jobs now Included on Core (previously required the Teams Add-On). Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, add-on removed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Core at $39, Additional Prepaid Credits at $4 per prepaid credit, Additional On-Demand Credits at $6 per on-demand credit and Data Labeling Services at $0.05 per classification/keypoint annotation + $0.2 per polygon annotation.
It lands 21 days after Roboflow's previous change, "Roboflow adds Agent: idea to deployed vision solution, automatically" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Roboflow drops Community Support/Forum from Public, Core plans" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Roboflow: Core plan credit allocation doubled (180→360/yr) + Free tier credits redenominated" (Jul 27, 2026). Roboflow has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window Microsoft, Google and Prodigy Education also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
