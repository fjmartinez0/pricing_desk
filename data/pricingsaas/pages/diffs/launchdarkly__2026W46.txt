What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of LaunchDarkly's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Release Autopilot launched: release automation (auto-release, release plans, auto-cleanup, release agent) and guarded releases with automatic rollback are now included on every plan — limited on Developer, free on Foundation until Feb 1, 2027, included on Enterprise. Enterprise card's 'Release Assistant' bullet replaced by the Autopilot release agent; Guardian paid Enterprise add-on (Release Moni. In detail: Guardian paid Enterprise add-on (Release Monitoring, Guardrail Metrics, Proactive Failure Notifications, Automatic Pause or Rollback, Advanced Observability, Exposure Insights) retired; guarded releases and rollback are folded into Release Autopilot on every plan; Enterprise config delivery latency SLA of <150ms removed from comparison table; only the <200ms SLO remains. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on removed, feature added, feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Foundation at $10.
It lands 90 days after LaunchDarkly's previous change, "LaunchDarkly turns Guardian from a plan card into a paid add-on and drops the plan selector" (Aug 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LaunchDarkly removes per-unit observability overage prices and the billing FAQ from the pricing compare table" (Jul 2, 2026), "LaunchDarkly: Pricing restructured around CodeControl/AgentControl split" (Jul 3, 2026) and "LaunchDarkly adds MCP Server support across all plans" (Jun 10, 2026). LaunchDarkly has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Amplitude and Optimizely also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
