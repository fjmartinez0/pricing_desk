What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Mobbin's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Explore Finance+ add-on now advertises access to over 68 apps across Banking, Crypto, Investment & more, up from 65; price stays $399/mo billed yearly. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Pro at $10, Team at $16 and Finance+ at $399.
It lands 7 days after Mobbin's previous change, "Mobbin adds Deep Search and MCP to Pro, promotes Enterprise to a full tier card" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mobbin: Finance+ add-on expands to 58 apps (from 55)" (Aug 24, 2026), "Mobbin: Finance add-on app coverage 54→55 apps" (Jul 27, 2026) and "Mobbin hikes Team plan price 33%, from $12 to $16 per member monthly" (Jul 6, 2026). Mobbin has been tracked since 2026-02-05.
