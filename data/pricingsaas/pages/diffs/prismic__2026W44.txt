Prismic drops free trial on Platinum plan, pushes demo requests instead
Verified Oct 26, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral
The changePlatinum trial — trial change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 19, 2026 → Oct 26, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Prismic's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Platinum plan's 'Request a 30 day free trial' option replaced by 'Get a demo'; free trial no longer offered on Platinum. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $10, Small at $25, Medium at $150 and Platinum at $675.
It lands 35 days after Prismic's previous change, "Prismic: AI landing page builder add-on discontinued" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Prismic renames Type Builder to Slice Machine, pricing unchanged" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Prismic renames Slice Machine to Type Builder in dev-tool refresh" (Sep 7, 2026). Prismic has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Webflow, Hygraph and Netlify also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
