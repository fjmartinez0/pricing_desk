What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Getzep's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise card adds bullet to deploy to Zep Cloud (with own encryption keys) or VPC; 'Memory MCP Server seats' renamed 'Context MCP Server seats' on all plans.
