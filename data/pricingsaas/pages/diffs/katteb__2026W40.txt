What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Katteb's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Plan feature bullets rewritten: Lite/Plus's model-catalogue, task-library and team-credit bullets replaced by goal-scheduling, one-press-task, and Telegram/Slack-integration bullets on Lite and Goals; Concurrent-agent-task limits (1/2/3) and per-plan credit pools (15K/150K/unlimited) replaced by goal-concurrency limits (1/10) and agent-concurrency counts (5 on Goals, unlimited team/goals on Enter. In detail: Concurrent-agent-task limits (1/2/3) and per-plan credit pools (15K/150K/unlimited) replaced by goal-concurrency limits (1/10) and agent-concurrency counts (5 on Goals, unlimited team/goals on Enterprise). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Lite at $15.83, Business at $82.5 and Enterprise at $1,000.
It lands 14 days after Katteb's previous change, "Katteb: full pricing re-platform to AI chat/agents model" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Katteb: Pro plan price cut ($29->$17/mo)" (Jul 13, 2026). Katteb has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
