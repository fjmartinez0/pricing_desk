What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Upstage's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Solar launch promo stepped down from 70% to 50% off: Solar Pro 4 now 50% off until further notice (previously set to revert to list price Oct 11), effective input $0.09→$0.15, output $0.36→$0.60 per 1M; Solar Mini 4 moved to its pre-announced 50% off, input $0.03→$0.05, output $0.12→$0.20, ending Oct 22. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Explore at $100, Build at $500, Scale at $5,000, Solar Pro 4 at $0.36 per 1M output tokens + $0.09 per 1M input tokens and Solar Pro 3 at $0.15 per 1M input tokens + $0.6 per 1M output tokens.
It lands the same day as Upstage's previous change, "Upstage API trims Solar launch promos to 50% off, raising promo rates" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Upstage boosts Solar Mini 4 discount to 70%, sunsets Solar Pro 3 and Pro 2" (Oct 5, 2026), "Upstage API deepens Solar Mini 4 promo to 70% off, sunsets Solar Pro 3" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Upstage adds Solar Mini 4 at $0.10/1M input tokens, 50% off through Oct 22" (Sep 28, 2026).
