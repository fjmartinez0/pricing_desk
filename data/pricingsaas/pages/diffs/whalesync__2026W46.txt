What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Whalesync's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New connectors: ClickUp and MySQL added to Starter two-way sync; Jira and Zendesk added to Plus connector list. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $20, Plus at $40 and Pro at $200.
It lands 28 days after Whalesync's previous change, "Whalesync halves Starter price, adds $32 Plus entry tier, caps self-serve at 20,000 records" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Whalesync adds Copper to Plus plan's two-way CRM sync connectors" (Oct 5, 2026), "Whalesync adds Close, Klaviyo, Zoho CRM to Plus; restricts Stripe to…" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Whalesync: free trial now needs no credit card" (Sep 21, 2026). Whalesync has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Zapier, Workato, Make and MuleSoft also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
