What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Fleetio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Launched 'Service Advisor', a new AI-powered decision-support feature for repairs, promoted via a new site-wide banner, a renamed 'AI Service Advisor' nav item, and a new promo block on the pricing page itself. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Essential at $4, Professional at $7, Premium at $10 and Tools at $0.5.
Fleetio's previous recorded change was "Fleetio Doubles Professional Plan Automation Capacity from 10 to 20 Active Automations" (Q3 2025). Before that Pulse recorded "Fleetio Introduces Automations Feature to Streamline Reporting & Analysis with Tiered Limits for Enhanced Efficiency" (Q2 2025), "Fleetio Enhances Premium Plan with Parts & Labor Markup Feature for Improved Cost Management" (Q1 2025) and "Fleetio Enhances Service Program Automations and Moves Tire Management Exclusively to Premium Plan" (Q4 2024). Fleetio has been tracked since 2023-12-01.
In the same window Holded, Threecolts, Shippo, Tatsoft and Smokeball also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
