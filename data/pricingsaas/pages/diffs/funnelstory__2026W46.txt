What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of FunnelStory's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise AI Agents billing changed from consumption-based pricing to outcome-based pricing; FAQ reframes platform fee ($2,399/mo, unchanged) plus outcome-driven agent charges; Starter adds Product Docs answers and Data Apps; Enterprise adds Outcome Agents, FDEs, BYO LLM, own-cloud deploy and SAML/OIDC SSO with SCIM, replacing LLM Bridge and Custom Agentic Dashboards. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, metric change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $2,399.
It lands the same day as FunnelStory's previous change, "FunnelStory shifts Enterprise AI Agents to outcome-based pricing" (Nov 9, 2026). FunnelStory has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
