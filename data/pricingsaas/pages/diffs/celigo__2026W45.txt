What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Celigo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Dropped 'Flat-rate pricing', 'No overage fees', and 'Simple metrics' (endpoints/flows) callouts and the FAQ on volume-based pricing; replaced with vaguer edition-based, sales-led pricing messaging; Celigo Ora exited beta and now appears in a new 'Included in every edition' list (file storage, SLAs, MFA/RBAC, AI, Ora); Standard Edition's 'option to add SSO' note was dropped; Enterprise Edition dr. In detail: Enterprise Edition dropped 'Automate everything with no limits.' and 'Includes unlimited endpoints', replaced with generic 'Built for enterprise-wide automation' — no unlimited-endpoints guarantee remains. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease, feature change, package change) with a downward direction.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Celigo. Celigo has been tracked since 2026-05-02.
