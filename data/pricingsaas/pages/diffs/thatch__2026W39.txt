What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Thatch's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise plan renamed to Custom, with the Standard plan's flat '$45/employee + compliance fee starting at $25/mo' pricing replaced by an itemized calculator ('$45 x 4 employees + $50 compliance fee') and a team-size selector, and the Custom plan now discloses concrete minimum pricing (10-employee minimum, $2,400/year) instead of showing only 'Custom'. Standard also gained 'Compliance reporting (ACA, PCORI, ERISA, state mandates)' and swapped its combined AI-driven enrollment support for a new '1-on-1 benefits advising & enrollment support' line; Custom added 'Robust reporting and business services' and 'Onsite enrollment support for large employers' but dropped COBRA from its ACA compliance benefit. In detail: Standard plan gained 'Compliance reporting (ACA, PCORI, ERISA, state mandates)' and replaced its combined AI-driven enrollment support with a new '1-on-1 benefits advising & enrollment support' line; Custom plan added 'Robust reporting and business services' and 'Onsite enrollment support for large employers' but dropped COBRA from its ACA compliance benefit; Enterprise plan renamed to Custom; Standard plan pricing display changed from a flat '$45/employee + compliance fee starting at $25/mo' to an itemized calculator ('$45 x 4 employees + $50 compliance fee') with a team-size selector; Custom plan now discloses concrete minimum pricing (10 employee minimum, $2,400/year) instead of showing only 'Custom'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $50.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Thatch. Thatch has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
