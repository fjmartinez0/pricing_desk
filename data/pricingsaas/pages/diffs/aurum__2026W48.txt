What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Aurum's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black November bonuses added per plan: group masterclass on Up/Smart, 1h/2h individual consulting on Company/VIP; JUX AI free-trial copy now dated (free until 15/01/2027). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added).
It lands 7 days after Aurum's previous change, "Aurum Black Friday deal shrinks: annual discount drops from 50% to 40%" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Aurum slashes annual plans 50% in Novembro Black promo through Nov 30" (Nov 8, 2026), "Aurum gives Astrea paid plans unlimited JUX search and legal drafting" (Nov 1, 2026) and "Aurum renames "Atividades predefinidas" row to "Workflows" in comparison table" (Oct 4, 2026). Aurum has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Casemark, Actionstep and CaseFleet also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
