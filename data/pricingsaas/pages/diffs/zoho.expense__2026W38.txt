What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Zoho's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free plan gained Zoho Prepaid Mastercard and Zoho Expense MCP; Standard gained an Expense Intelligence/Zia AI suite and WhatsApp/Slack integration but lost Multi-level approval and Split expenses; Premium gained travel inventory access, Guest Booking, Duty of care, Visa management automation, and Carbon emission tracking; Premium Support add-on swapped '45 days onboarding assistance' for 'Dedicat. In detail: Premium Support add-on swapped '45 days onboarding assistance' for 'Dedicated relationship manager support'; price unchanged at $490/year. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change, feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $3, Premium at $5, Self Booking - Travel at $9.99 per trip, Premium Support at $490 and Jumpstart at $500 subscription.
It lands 41 days after Zoho's previous change, "Zoho Expense: Itemized receipt OCR moves from Premium-only to Standard" (Aug 4, 2026). Zoho has been tracked since 2023-11-30.
