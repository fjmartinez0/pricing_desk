What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of CloudTalk's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Branded Caller ID add-on price rose from $0.07 to $0.10 per call. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change). The current page lists Starter at $25, Essential at $29, Expert at $49, AI Conversation Intelligence at $9 and Power Dialer at $15.
It lands 49 days after CloudTalk's previous change, "CloudTalk: Webhooks integration added (Essential, Expert)" (Sep 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CloudTalk: CloudTalk MCP integration added to Essential & Expert plans" (Sep 13, 2026) and "CloudTalk expands AI voice agents to 70+ languages, up from 60+" (Sep 6, 2026). CloudTalk has been tracked since 2026-06-18.
In the same window Ringcentral, Aircall, Genesys and Twilio also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
