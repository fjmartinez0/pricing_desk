What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Onpipeline's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pay Monthly/Pay Annually toggle added, defaulting to annual: annual-billed per-user prices shown at $20 (Pipeline), $32 (Standard), $47 (Advanced) vs prior single price $25/$39/$58 (~20% annual discount); Autumn Sale banner added: 50% off first 3 months. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pipeline at $20, Standard at $20 and Advanced at $20.
It lands 7 days after Onpipeline's previous change, "Onpipeline rewrites Pipeline plan pitch to drop basic-plan limits" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Onpipeline cuts free trial from 30 to 14 days, adds AI request quotas" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Onpipeline adds Customer Portal and Priority Support rows to Advanced" (Aug 17, 2026). Onpipeline has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Salesforce, Zoho, HubSpot and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
