What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of OpenSolr's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Medium and Large plans' support changed from 'Priority Email & Helpdesk Support' to 'Priority Email Support' — Helpdesk channel dropped. In detail: Medium and Large plans' support level changed from 'Priority Email & Helpdesk Support' to 'Priority Email Support' — the Helpdesk channel was dropped from both plans' listed support scope. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Small at EUR 17, Medium at EUR 35, Large at EUR 53, AI & Hybrid Search at EUR 5 and Additional Solr Index at EUR 10 Solr Index.
It lands 48 days after OpenSolr's previous change, "OpenSolr: new fair-use rate limit policy (150 req/min)" (Aug 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenSolr: Yearly discount cut 25%→15%, base plan prices up" (Aug 4, 2026) and "OpenSolr: yearly discount increased 5%->25%, new Drupal/WordPress partner discount" (Jul 4, 2026). OpenSolr has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
