What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Picsart's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Limited-time Ultra promo (50% off Seedance 2.5 generations for first 7 days, upgrade by Nov 2) removed from banner and Ultra plan card badge.
