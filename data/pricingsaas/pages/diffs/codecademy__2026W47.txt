What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Codecademy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Fall Flash Sale site-wide banner removed: 'Codecademy Pro 50% off with FALLFLASH' with a live countdown (02d 03h 41m at the 11/09 capture, so the sale ended ~11/11) is gone from the top of the page. Card prices are unchanged (Plus $14.99/mo annual or $29.99 monthly; Pro $19.99/mo annual or $39.99 monthly) — the promo was applied via code at checkout, not reflected in list prices. The banner is JS-. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Plus at $14.99 and Pro at $19.99.
It lands 136 days after Codecademy's previous change, "Codecademy: removed Flash Sale 50% off Pro promo" (Jul 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Codecademy Launches Flash Sale With New MARCH26FLASH Promo Code" (Mar 31, 2026), "Codecademy Drops Study Groups and Career Services from Pro Tier" (Apr 2, 2026) and "Codecademy boosts promo: 50% off annual Pro replaces 20% bootcamp deal" (Mar 16, 2026). Codecademy has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
