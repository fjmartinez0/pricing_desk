What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Trunk's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Dynamic CI (beta) added across Free/Team/Enterprise plans; Enterprise also gains bring-your-own-cloud deployment (private preview) and 24/7 support with SLAs in a dedicated Slack channel. Free plan test-span allowance changed from 5M test spans/month (flat) to 1M test spans/committer (matches paid-plan metric). In detail: Free plan's test-span allowance changed from a flat 5M test spans per month to 1M test spans per committer, matching the per-committer metric used by paid plans; Added Dynamic CI (beta) across all plans; Enterprise gained bring-your-own-cloud deployment (private preview) and 24/7 support with SLAs in a dedicated Slack channel. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Team at $18.
It lands 22 days after Trunk's previous change, "Trunk opens Merge Queue to Free plan, drops SOC 2 Type 2 from Free tier" (Oct 5, 2026). Trunk has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
