What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Spoki's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan (0€/month, official WhatsApp API, live chat, unlimited operators/automations, ticketing) removed from pricing cards and comparison table; FAQ no longer mentions permanent Free plan. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change).
It lands 14 days after Spoki's previous change, "Spoki offers 20% off Sales Plan for a year with BLACKFRIDAY26 code" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Spoki caps free messaging, replacing unlimited with 1,000 service messages" (Oct 4, 2026), "Spoki adds AI consulting to Standard, Advanced & Premium tiers" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Spoki hikes WhatsApp Marketing credit rate 15%, real spend jumps to €308.92/mo" (Sep 14, 2026). Spoki has been tracked since 2026-08-27.
In the same window Slack, Microsoft, Zoom, Google and Discord also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
