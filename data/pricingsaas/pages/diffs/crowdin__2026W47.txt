What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Crowdin's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Pro $50 to $75/mo ($600 to $900/yr); Team $150/mo plan removed; Free hosted words 60K to 40K; Enterprise Team+ renamed Team (CTA now Subscribe); Pro hosted words 60K to 80K; Pro: unlimited private projects (was up to 2); Advanced reports and Activity stream added. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit raised, price increase, plan removed, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $75, Team at $450 and CDN for Translations at $3 per 1M requests + $2 per 10GB data transfer.
It lands 21 days after Crowdin's previous change, "Crowdin drops pricing calculator, retargets self-serve plans to indie developers" (Oct 26, 2026). Crowdin has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window X and Lokalise also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
