What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Mintlify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The $0/mo Starter plan was renamed Free; Pro now reads 'Everything in Free' and the FAQ upgrade question names Free instead of Starter. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed). The current page lists Starter at $150 and Pro at $450.
This weekly roll-up includes 3 changes Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Mintlify launches $150/mo Starter plan + 1 more change" (Day 325, 2026, saved Nov 21, 2026), "Mintlify adds $150/mo Starter plan with 10 editors, Agent and Autopilot" (Day 322, 2026, saved Nov 18, 2026) and "Mintlify renames Starter plan to Free, keeps $0 price and 5 editors" (Day 321, 2026, saved Nov 17, 2026). They are the same changes, not new ones. Before that, Mintlify's previous recorded change was "Mintlify renames Automations to Autopilot, adds Enterprise-only Automations" (Nov 5, 2026). Mintlify has been tracked since 2024-10-21.
