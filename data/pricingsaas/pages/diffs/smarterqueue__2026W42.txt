What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Smarterqueue's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plans renamed and repriced: Pro ($21/mo annual) -> Solo ($24.99/mo annual); Team ($42/mo annual) -> Business ($42.99/mo annual); new Agency tier added at $84.99/mo annual. Usage metrics renamed: 'Scheduled Posts/Profile' -> 'Timeslots/Day', 'Published Posts/Profile/Month' -> 'Queued Posts'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Solo at $24.99, Business at $42.99 and Agency at $84.99.
It lands 14 days after Smarterqueue's previous change, "SmarterQueue swaps 3 tiers for 2 new plans: Pro $21, Team $42" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Smarterqueue swaps Tweets/Week for X/Twitter Credits, boosts default 5→50, ca" (Jul 20, 2026). Smarterqueue has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
