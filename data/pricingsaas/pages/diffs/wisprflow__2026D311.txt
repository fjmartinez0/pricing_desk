What happened
Between the Nov 6, 2026 and Nov 7, 2026 captures of Wispr Flow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Notetaker added to HIPAA-ready/signed-BAA compliance scope; only Scratchpad now excluded on BAA accounts (previously both Notetaker and Scratchpad were excluded). In detail: Notetaker is now covered under HIPAA-ready/signed-BAA compliance (previously excluded); only Scratchpad remains disabled on BAA accounts. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $12 and Growth at $18.
It lands 5 days after Wispr Flow's previous change, "Wispr Flow makes Pro Notetaker unlimited and trims Free plan limits" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Wispr Flow drops exact Free plan limits, makes Pro meetings unlimited" (Nov 1, 2026), "Wispr Flow blurs Free plan mobile cap, swapping 1,000 words for "limited use" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Wispr Flow hides mobile dictation cap, swaps 1,000-word free limit for vague…" (Oct 20, 2026).
