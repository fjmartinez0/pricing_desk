What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of KeyShot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: KeyShot Enterprise: 'Application management' newly included (new Enterprise-only row in the comparison table and new bullet on the Enterprise card); the card's SSO bullet now reads 'Single sign-on / MFA'. 'Network Rendering seats' and 'KeyShot Office Hours' were dropped from the card's highlights but remain included for Enterprise per the comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists KeyShot Studio for Education (EDU) at $7.92, KeyShot Professional at $108.25, KeyShot Business at $133.25, KeyShot Network Rendering at $62.42 and KeyShot Studio NX Plugin at $29.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for KeyShot. KeyShot has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
