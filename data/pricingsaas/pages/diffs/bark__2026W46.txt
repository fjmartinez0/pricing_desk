Bark drops 24-month device installments for $600/$360/$288 upfront prices

What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Bark's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Device pricing changed from 24-month installments ($25/$15/$12 per month) to upfront prices ($600 Phone Pro, $360 Phone, $288 Watch); Bark plan prices unchanged. Total device cost is the same. In detail: Device pricing changed from Bark-run 24-month installments ($25/$15/$12 per month) to upfront prices ($600 Phone Pro, $360 Phone, $288 Watch); pay-over-time now via Affirm/Klarna at checkout. Bark plan prices unchanged; total device cost is the same. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Bark Home at $6, Bark App (Android) at $8.25, Bark App (iOS) at $8.25, Bark Watch at $15 and Bark Phone Pro (Starter) at $29.
It lands 7 days after Bark's previous change, "Bark offers $30 off its Phone or Watch with limited-time code JOY30" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Bark Phone installment jumps 50% to $15/mo as Watch rises to $12/mo" (Oct 5, 2026). Bark has been tracked since 2026-05-02.
In the same window Wagepoint also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
