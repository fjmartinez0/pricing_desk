What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Luma AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Model Marketplace credit rates reworked: Video-to-Video billing now counts generated+reference seconds (rates ~halved, e.g. Seedance 2.0 ~30→15/sec); MiniMax H3 Max rose ~73→~82/sec while Flux 3 fell 144→70/sec; Two new models added to the Model Marketplace: Kling 4.0 Flash (32 credits/sec) and Kling 4.0 Flash Omni (32 credits/sec; 21 credits/sec of generated+reference video for Video-to-Video); S. In detail: Seedream 5.0 Pro Create/Modify credit cost switched from a flat rate to a variable range depending on reference image count: 1K 24→24-38 credits, 2K 48→48-62 credits. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Plus at $25, Pro at $75 and Ultra at $250.
It lands 2 days after Luma AI's previous change, "Luma AI halves Video-to-Video credit rates, adds Kling 4.0 Flash models" (Nov 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Luma AI adds Kling 4.0 and 4.0 Omni, drops V2V 4K and audio rows" (Oct 26, 2026), "Luma AI adds Kling 4.0 API pricing, from 53 credits per second at 720p" (Oct 23, 2026) and "Luma AI doubles Seedance video-to-video credit costs, adds Minimax H3 Max" (Oct 5, 2026). Luma AI has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Synthesia, OpenAI, Runway and HeyGen also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
