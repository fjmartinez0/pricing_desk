What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Linktree's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Premium card Instagram auto-replies now labelled 'Unlimited Instagram auto-replies' (previously no unlimited qualifier). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $6, Pro at $12 and Premium at $30.
It lands 14 days after Linktree's previous change, "Linktree drops selling from Free plan, gives Pro 0% sell-and-earn fees" (Oct 5, 2026). Linktree has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Buffer, Leadpages and Later also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
