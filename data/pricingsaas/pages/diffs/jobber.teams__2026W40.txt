What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Jobber's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Annual billing promo increased from up to 25% off to up to 40% off via a new 12-month intro rate on Grow ($199→$105/mo), Connect ($139→$70/mo) and Core ($49→$21/mo), reverting to the prior annual price after 12 months; offer ends Sept 30, new customers only. In detail: Annual billing promo jumped from 'up to 25%' to 'up to 40%' off via a new 12-month intro rate on Grow, Connect and Core, reverting to the prior annual price after; offer ends Sept 30, new customers only. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Core at $24, Connect at $149, Grow at $299, Plus at $529 and Marketing Suite at $79.
It lands 35 days after Jobber's previous change, "Jobber Teams: New intro-rate promo, up to 40% off (was 25%), ends Aug 31" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Jobber Teams: Temporary annual promo pricing on Grow, Connect, Core (up to 20% off)" (Aug 11, 2026), "Jobber Teams: intro annual-discount offer expired, prices revert to standard rates" (Aug 5, 2026) and "Jobber Teams swaps flat 25% off for time-limited $3,900 first-year savings" (Jul 20, 2026).
