What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of ResDiary's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Payment processing surcharge cut from 1.2% to 1% on Deposits and Payments; two add-ons renamed with pricing unchanged: 'Pre-orders' to 'Order & Pay' ($45/mo) and 'Group Central Reservations' to 'Group Bookings' ($15/mo). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Order & Pay + Events Manager Bundle at $65, ResPhone at $50, Event Manager at $45, Gift Vouchers at $45 and PMS Integration at $15.
It lands 15 days after ResDiary's previous change, "ResDiary raises payment processing fee to 1.2%, renames Order & Pay add-on" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ResDiary raises Pre-orders payment surcharge from 1% to 1.2%" (Sep 14, 2026). ResDiary has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window TouchBistro, Lightspeed and Toast also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
