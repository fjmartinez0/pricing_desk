What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Moz's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Moz Pro added a new 'AI Sentiment Analysis' row to the plan comparison table, appearing directly above the existing 'AI Citations Data' row across the Standard, Medium, and Large plan columns. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Starter at $49, Standard at $79, Medium at $143, Large at $239 and Additional Link Tracking Lists at $25.
It lands 7 days after Moz's previous change, "Moz boosts AI Models quota, adding Perplexity and Google AI Mode across plans" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Moz adds AI Citations tracking to Standard, Medium, and Large plans" (Jul 13, 2026) and "Moz: AI Visibility Dashboards & Content Briefs added (Std/Med/Large)" (Jul 3, 2026). Moz has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
