What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of LiveAgent's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise moved from $69/$85 per agent to a $3,999/mo flat fee (80 agents included, extra agents $49 each; $3,199 annual). Plans renamed Small/Medium/Large business to Starter/Team/Business; other prices unchanged; Business plan now lists MCP server access to connect external AI tools to LiveAgent. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, package change) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 27, 2026) lists Starter at $15, Team at $29, Business at $49 and Enterprise at $3,199.
It lands 115 days after LiveAgent's previous change, "LiveAgent: Spring 75% OFF promotional discount removed across all plans" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LiveAgent ends 75% OFF spring promo, restores standard monthly plan pricing" (Apr 22, 2026) and "LiveAgent ends Valentine's Day AI promo after month-long customer unlock" (Mar 17, 2026). LiveAgent has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Intercom and Help Scout also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
