What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of HiveMQ's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: HiveMQ Lab (free self-managed) limits cut from 100 connections / 100 msgs/sec to 25 connections / 25 msgs/sec; Lab now a free software license valid 30 days, renewed manually at no charge; tagline reframed to learning/evaluation on own infrastructure. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change, limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Launch at $299, Run at $499 and Starter at $0.34 hour + $0.8 million messages.
It lands 21 days after HiveMQ's previous change, "HiveMQ revamps enterprise tiers into Connect/Contextualize/Analyze/Act, launc" (Sep 14, 2026). HiveMQ has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
