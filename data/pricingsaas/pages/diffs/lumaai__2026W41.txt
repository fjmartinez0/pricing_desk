What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Luma AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Seedance video-to-video/modify credit costs per second roughly doubled (Seedance 2.0 15-157 to ~30-~314; 2.5 22-115 to 44-230; Mini 8-16 to ~16-~32); text/image-to-video unchanged; Minimax H3 Max video model added to credit rate table: 480p 18, 768p 28, 1080p 56 credits/sec; reference images first 2-4 free then ~7-13 credits each. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 23, 2026) lists Plus at $25, Pro at $75 and Ultra at $250.
It lands 14 days after Luma AI's previous change, "Luma AI: Seedance 2.0 video generation costs cut ~50%" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Luma AI slashes Seedance video model pricing up to 73% across all tiers" (Sep 19, 2026), "Luma AI unlocks 8K upscaling in SeedVR2, breaking past the old 4K cap for 25 c" (Sep 5, 2026) and "Luma AI swaps Nano Banana for Seedream 5.0 Pro, drops Kling 2.6" (Sep 4, 2026). Luma AI has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window OpenAI, Synthesia, HeyGen and Runway also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
