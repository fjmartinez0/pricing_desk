What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Breezy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free Bootstrap plan (1 active position, unlimited candidates) removed; FAQ now says Breezy has no free plan, only a 14-day trial. Paid plans still start at $157/mo. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Startup at $157, Growth at $273, Business at $439, SMS/Text Messaging at $41 and Onboard at $49.
It lands 14 days after Breezy's previous change, "Breezy adds Interview Analysis add-on to score video interviews with AI" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Breezy drops SSL enforcement from Custom Pro Plan's optional add-on list" (Sep 28, 2026). Breezy has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Paychex and LeveragePoint also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
