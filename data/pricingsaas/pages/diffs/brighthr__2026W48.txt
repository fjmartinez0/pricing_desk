What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Brighthr's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Middle and top plans renamed: Enhanced HR support to Software, 24/7 HR advice & documents; Full HR, health and safety support & EAP to Software, 24/7 advice, documents & EAP. Prices unchanged; Plan taglines reworded to emphasise 24/7 insured HR advice from qualified experts and document templates, replacing 'optional insurance' and 'Enhanced package' wording. Pulse files it as a packaging change (positioning, plan renamed).
It lands 315 days after Brighthr's previous change, "Brighthr Enhances HR Support Plan with Optional Insurance for Comprehensive 24/7 Expert Assistance" (Jan 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Brighthr Implements 49% Price Increase for Core HR Software, Now £16.67/Employee per Month" (Q3 2025), "Brighthr Announces 27% Price Increase for Enhanced HR Support Plan, Now £21.20 Per Employee Per Month" (Q2 2025) and "Brighthr Announces 6% Price Increase for Core HR Software, Raising Monthly Cost to £8.80 per Employee" (Q4 2024). Brighthr has been tracked since 2023-12-04.
In the same window Paychex and Justworks also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
