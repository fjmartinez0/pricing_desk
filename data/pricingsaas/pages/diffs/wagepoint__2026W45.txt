What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Wagepoint's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Q4 promo of 75% off the first 6 months is gone; banner now reads 'Meet Plus' instead; Next-day deposit (eligible businesses; included in Plus, add-on for Core and Lite) and Geo-fencing (Plus) added to plan features. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, discount removed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Lite at CAD 20, Core at CAD 40 and Plus at CAD 80.
It lands 14 days after Wagepoint's previous change, "Wagepoint offers 75% off first 6 months to switchers in Q4 promo" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Wagepoint: Canada Day 59% off promo ended" (Aug 31, 2026). Wagepoint has been tracked since 2023-11-28.
In the same window Paychex and Gusto also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
