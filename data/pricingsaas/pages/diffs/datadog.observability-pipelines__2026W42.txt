What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Datadog's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New FAQ states Sensitive Data Scanner on-premise integration is included in regular Observability Pipelines price with no volume limit; the SaaS Sensitive Data Scanner is billed separately. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Observability Pipelines (Per GB) at $0.12 per GB ingested (on-demand) + $0.1 per GB ingested.
It lands 101 days after Datadog's previous change, "Observability Pipelines: log archive now searchable via new Archive Search" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Datadog Observability Pipelines adds AI Credits, OTLP ingestion pricing" (Jun 8, 2026), "Datadog Observability Pipelines replaces log rehydration with Archive Search" (May 4, 2026) and "Datadog Observability Pipelines Reduces Annual Per-GB Ingestion Pricing by 5% from $0.10 to $0.095" (Q3 2025). Datadog has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Dynatrace, Elastic, Grafana, Sumologic and Splunk also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
