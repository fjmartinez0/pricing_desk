What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Diffbot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Rate-limit metric changed from requests/min|sec to concurrent threads across all plans: Free 5/min to 1 thread, Startup 5/sec to 25 threads, Plus 25/sec to 100 threads, Enterprise 25+/sec to 100+ threads. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change). The current page lists Startup at $299 and Plus at $899.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Diffbot. Diffbot has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
