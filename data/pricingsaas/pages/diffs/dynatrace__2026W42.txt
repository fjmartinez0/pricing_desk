What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Dynatrace's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New AI section: AI Exploration included at no additional charge; Agentic Operations at $0.10 per AI unit, with per-task AI unit costs and a limited-time free inclusion within Dynatrace Assist. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Infrastructure Monitoring at $29 per host/month, Full-Stack Monitoring at $58 per 8 GiB host/month, Kubernetes Platform Monitoring at $1.4 per pod/month, Code Monitoring at $3.6 per container/month and Log Analytics - Pay-per-Query at $3.5 per 1,000 GiB scanned + $0.7 per 1,000 GiB-day retained.
It lands 101 days after Dynatrace's previous change, "Dynatrace: Log correlation democratized to Foundation & Discovery tier" (Jul 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Dynatrace Launches Telemetry Pricing With Plans Starting at $0.15/100k" (Apr 1, 2026), "Dynatrace rebrands Davis AI as Dynatrace Intelligence, overhauls pricing page" (Apr 3, 2026) and "Dynatrace Introduces Monthly Pricing Structure with New Foundation & Discovery Plans at $7/Month per Host for Enhanced Infrastructure Monitoring" (Jan 11, 2026). Dynatrace has been tracked since 2023-12-08.
In the same window Datadog, Elastic, Grafana and Splunk also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
