What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of User Interviews's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Customer success support removed from Essential plan card; Enterprise customer success manager removed from Enterprise card. In detail: "Customer success support" bullet removed from the Essential card (150-session tab; the 60-session tab still lists it); "Enterprise customer success manager" bullet removed from the Custom (Enterprise) card. The comparison table still shows Customer success. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction.
