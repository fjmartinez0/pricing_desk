What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Vercel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New usage-priced items: Vercel CI (Beta, 200 CPU min/month included, then $0.0035/CPU minute) and Custom Monitors in Observability ($0.0035 per GB scanned). Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $20.
It lands 7 days after Vercel's previous change, "Vercel slashes Firewall limits, adds metered Deployment Storage at $0.10/GB" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vercel: Vercel Firewall limits cut: Enterprise Custom Firewall Rules 1,000→250; IP Blocking Hobby 3→10, Pro 100→40,…" (Oct 26, 2026), "Vercel: Vercel Drives storage launched at $0.05/GB-month, metered reads/writes" (Oct 20, 2026) and "Vercel adds Daemons with usage-based Active CPU and Memory pricing" (Oct 19, 2026). Vercel has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Render, Railway and Netlify also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
