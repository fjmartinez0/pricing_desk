What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Syncfusion's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed the minimum 5-developer license requirement across all SDKs and the UI Component Suite. Product cards and hero/step copy now show plain per-developer pricing with no stated minimum, instead of 'min. 5 devs' plus a bundled 5-developer total price. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists PDF Viewer SDK at $49.91, Rich Text Editor SDK at $79.91, Grid SDK at $79.91, Chart SDK at $79.91 and Gantt SDK at $79.91.
It lands 84 days after Syncfusion's previous change, "Syncfusion Sales splits pricing, adds 7 standalone contact-sales UI SDKs" (Jul 13, 2026). Syncfusion has been tracked since 2026-05-06.
In the same window GitHub, Unity, Stedi and Spline also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
