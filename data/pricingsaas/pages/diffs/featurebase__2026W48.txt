What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Featurebase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Professional plan messaging channels changed: Discord replaced by Telegram (Slack, WhatsApp, Telegram). Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Growth at $29, Professional at $59, Enterprise at $99, AI Copilot Unlimited at $19 and Remove Featurebase Branding at $69.
It lands 42 days after Featurebase's previous change, "Featurebase adds API docs to Help Center with live versions and playground" (Oct 11, 2026). Featurebase has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Slite, Appcues and Productboard also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
