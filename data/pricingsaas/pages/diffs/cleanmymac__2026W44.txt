What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of CleanMyMac's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New lower-cost Essential Trio tier (Cleanup, Applications, My Clutter) added at 1.66/month for 1 device; existing full toolkit now named All-in-One at 3.33/month, unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 27, 2026) lists Plan for 1 Mac at $3.33, Plan for 2 Macs at $5.33 and Plan for 5 Macs at $10.66.
It lands 70 days after CleanMyMac's previous change, "CleanMyMac axes Essential Trio, splits All-in-One into per-device plans" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CleanMyMac: Essential Trio plan price cut ~20% ($1.66→$1.33/mo)" (Aug 11, 2026) and "CleanMyMac: Essential Trio annual price increased ($1.33→$1.66/mo)" (Aug 5, 2026). CleanMyMac has been tracked since 2026-05-12.
In the same window Ava, HappyFox, Fleetio, Webflow and Tatsoft also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
