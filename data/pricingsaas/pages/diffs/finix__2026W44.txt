What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Finix's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 3D Secure authentication fee doubled from $0.05 to $0.10 per authentication; new Name Verification fee of $0.15 added to Standard pricing. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at 0.3% above interchange + $0.15 card processing per transaction (interchange plus).
It lands 289 days after Finix's previous change, "Finix Doubles Card and ACH Disputes Fee from $15 to $30, Significantly Impacting Customer Costs" (Jan 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Finix Implements 300% Increase in ACH Subscription Billing Fee Across All Pricing Tiers" (Q3 2025), "Finix Implements Significant 220% Increase in Account Updater Fee Amid Other Pricing Changes and New Features" (Q1 2025) and "Finix Reduces Flat Rate Amex Processing Fee by 90%, Now Just 0.3% + $0.30" (Q4 2024). Finix has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Stripe and Squarespace also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
