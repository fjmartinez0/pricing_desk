What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Lyzr's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Lyzr replaced pay-per-agent-run pricing ($0.08 cloud, $0.03 VPC/on-prem) with an agent-month subscription model, led by a new Opencontroller governance layer at $250K/yr unlimited (or $500K custom). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Take Agents to Production at $8,333.33.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Lyzr. Lyzr has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
