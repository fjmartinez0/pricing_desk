Vyond: Voice Cloning unbundled, now Enterprise/Agency plans only
Verified Sep 14, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral
The changeEnterprise, Agency, Starter feature — feature change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 7, 2026 → Sep 14, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Vyond's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Voice Cloning split out of 'Custom AI Avatars & Voice Cloning' into its own item, now gated to Enterprise/Agency plans only (20,000 credits/clone, max 20/month); Starter/Professional lose access. In detail: Voice Cloning unbundled from the combined 'Custom AI Avatars & Voice Cloning' feature into its own line item; now gated to Enterprise and Agency plans only (20,000 credits per clone, max 20 voices/month) and no longer included for Starter/Professional. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $58, Professional at $100, Enterprise at $137 and Agency at $167.
It lands 34 days after Vyond's previous change, "Vyond: AI credit costs raised across the board + new Text to Video Clip feature" (Aug 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vyond boosts TTS voices to 200+, expands translation to 95 languages" (Jul 6, 2026). Vyond has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Canva, Renderforest and Wistia also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
