What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Clevertap's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Core plan (up to 5,000 MAU) now shows a limited-time promo: 50% off for 6 months, 75% off for 12 months, $85/month struck through to $21.3/month, with offer countdown. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $85.
It lands 28 days after Clevertap's previous change, "Clevertap adds Decisioning Agents add-on to Ultimate, replacing CleverAI Tokens" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Clevertap renames tiers, hikes entry price to $85, adds CleverAI tokens" (Aug 24, 2026). Clevertap has been tracked since 2023-12-05.
In the same window Customer.io, Mixpanel and Amplitude also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
