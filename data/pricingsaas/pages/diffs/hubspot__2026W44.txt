HubSpot pricing FAQ
How much does HubSpot cost?
HubSpot pricing starts at $7/seat/mo on Starter, billed annually. HubSpot lists 18 plans on its pricing page; the Free plan is free.
Does HubSpot have a free plan?
Yes. HubSpot's Free plan is free; paid plans start at $7/seat/mo on Starter, billed annually.
What pricing model does HubSpot use?
HubSpot uses subscription + usage pricing across 18 plans, with a free tier.
