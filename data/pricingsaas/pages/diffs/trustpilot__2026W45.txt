What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Trustpilot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter plan (from $99/month per domain, billed annually; 100 invitations/month, 1 user) removed from plan cards and comparison table; Free, Plus, Premium, Enterprise remain. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Plus at $319 and Premium at $799.
It lands the same day as Trustpilot's previous change, "Trustpilot Business drops $99 Starter plan, leaving Free, Plus, Premium…" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Trustpilot trims marketing integrations from 22 to 19 on paid plans" (Oct 26, 2026), "Trustpilot: Enterprise widgets 22→23 + QR/multi-domain features added" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Trustpilot Business adds QR Code Review Collector and multi-domain tools" (Sep 28, 2026). Trustpilot has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
