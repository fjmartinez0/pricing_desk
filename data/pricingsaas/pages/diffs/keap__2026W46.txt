What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Keap's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Keap's single bundled platform plan starting price rose from $299/mo to $320/mo (billed monthly); the annual-billed equivalent also rose from $2,988/year to $3,195/year. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Keap Max at $249, Text Marketing Tier 2 at $24, Text Marketing Tier 3 at $39, Text Marketing Tier 4 at $79 and Text Marketing Tier 5 at $139.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Keap. Keap has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
In the same window Zoho, HubSpot, Pipedrive, ActiveCampaign and Constant Contact also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
