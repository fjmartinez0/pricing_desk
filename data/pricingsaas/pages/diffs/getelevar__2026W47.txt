What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Elevar's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed the $500 one-off Tailored Start add-on for Predicted LTV (pLTV) and Comeback score (beta) on the Advanced and Premium plans; these rows now show only the included Fast Start configuration. In detail: Removed the $500 one-off 'Tailored Start' add-on option for Predicted LTV (pLTV) and Comeback score (beta) on the Advanced and Premium plans; these features now show only the included Fast Start configuration. It lands 7 days after Elevar's previous change, "Elevar drops Elite's $3,000/mo price, now "based on order volume" (Nov 9, 2026). Elevar has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
