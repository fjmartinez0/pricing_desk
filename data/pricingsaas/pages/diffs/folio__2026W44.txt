What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Folio's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Outgoing EUR/SEK payments abroad drop from 35 kr to 5,50 kr and incoming SEPA transfers become free (was 20 kr). KID/OCR incoming payments rise from 2 kr to 2,25 kr and the audit task from 1300 kr to 1350 kr. The Advanced-only file-based payment integration now names RGB file services instead of MPS/Nets Autopay, still at 2 kr. In detail: International transfer fees cut: outgoing payments abroad in EUR or SEK dropped from 35 kr to 5,50 kr; receiving EUR/SEK transfers from EU/EEA via SEPA dropped from 20 kr to free (0); Incoming payment fee with KID/OCR invoicing rose from 2 kr to 2,25 kr; audit task (Revisjonsoppgave) rose from 1300 kr to 1350 kr; Payment integration option changed from MPS/Nets Autopay to file services (e.g. RGB); still 2 kr per payment and Advanced plan only. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease, price increase, feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Mini at NOK 89, Standard at NOK 159 and Avansert at NOK 359.
It lands 147 days after Folio's previous change, "Folio cuts Mini plan admin seats from 2 to 1" (Jun 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Folio raises savings rates to 4.25% for deposits up to 2M NOK" (May 12, 2026) and "Folio expands 0% interest label to cover new earmarking account type" (Apr 22, 2026). Folio has been tracked since 2026-03-05.
