What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of DragonflyDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Self-Managed renamed Dragonfly Enterprise; price changed from contact-us to free up to 100 vCPUs with custom pricing beyond. Business CTA now Speak to sales instead of Start A Free POC; Enterprise gains Customized functionality and Advanced data tiering; Business SSO & MFA expanded to SSO/MFA/LDAP/RBAC; Community Edition support section removed. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, package change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Business at $2,000 and Flex at $8 per GB.
It lands 49 days after DragonflyDB's previous change, "DragonflyDB splits Enterprise into self-hosted tiers, adds Community support" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "DragonflyDB: SSD Data Tiering add-on added to pricing calculator ($8.00/GB/mo)" (Aug 17, 2026). DragonflyDB has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Couchbase, MongoDB and Redis also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
