What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of INE's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New eCDFP + 3 months of Premium bundle launched at $399 presale ($599 full price, $200 off for new subscribers); eCDFP's prior standalone $450 certification listing (no bundle) was removed from the grid. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Fundamentals at $29.08, Premium at $66.58, Professional at $799 and Skill Dive at $299.
It lands 7 days after INE's previous change, "INE gates standalone certification exam vouchers behind a subscription" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "INE strips "Subscription required" gating from all 12 exam cards" (Sep 14, 2026), "INE: eIAMA bundle price jumps $399→$599 as presale ends + new $450 cert" (Aug 31, 2026) and "INE: New eIAMA + 3 months of Premium bundle launched ($399 presale)" (Aug 5, 2026).
