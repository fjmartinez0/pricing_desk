What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Notion's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Workers (Beta) add-on dropped its trial end-date notice; page no longer states it is 'Free to try now' with credit billing starting October 15. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 18, 2026) lists Plus at $10, Business at $20, Custom domains and branding at $8 and Notion Credits at $10 per 1,000 monthly Notion credits.
It lands 6 days after Notion's previous change, "Notion ends free Workers (Beta) trial, credits billed from Oct 15" (Sep 22, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Notion drops Beta tag from Meeting Notes AI transcription" (Sep 7, 2026), "Notion drops Beta tag from AI Meeting Notes, now generally available" (Sep 4, 2026) and "Notion delays Workers Beta credit billing from Aug 11 to Oct 15" (Jul 20, 2026). Notion has been tracked since 2023-11-09.
In the same window Microsoft, Superhuman Docs, Slite and Evernote also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
