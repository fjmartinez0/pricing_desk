What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of ServiceNow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: ITSM package inclusions rewritten: Now Assist renamed ServiceNow Otto and Moveworks for ITSM cut to Moveworks Core (Foundation; Otto/Moveworks lines dropped from Advanced/Prime cards); Foundation adds Predictive Intelligence and AI Control Tower, drops Service Graph Connectors; Advanced adds Digital Portfolio Management; Prime adds LEAP and AI Agent and Skill Creation, drops DEX agent and AI Agent. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction.
