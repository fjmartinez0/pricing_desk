What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Modal's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Model APIs per-token price table added: 12 open-weight models (e.g. Kimi K3.1 Pro $2.00 prompt / $0.20 cached / $10.00 completion per 1M tokens); not covered by included Starter compute. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Nov 17, 2026) lists Team at $250, Compute resources at $1.10 NVIDIA A10G instance hour + $1.95 L40S GPU hour and Sandbox + Notebooks compute at $2.40 per 100 GiB-Hour + $14.19 per 100 core-hours.
This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Modal adds Inkling 2 Small at $0.30/$1.00 per 1M tokens, $0.03 cached" (Day 317, 2026, saved Nov 13, 2026). It also records changes none of that period did (price revealed), so Pulse counts it as a change of its own. Before that, Modal's previous recorded change was "Modal drops Notebooks from its Sandbox pricing section and product footer" (Oct 26, 2026). Modal has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Together AI, Lambda Labs and Huggingface also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
