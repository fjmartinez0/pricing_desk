What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Shopmonkey's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: CRM Essentials add-on now includes a front desk assistant capability alongside reviews, campaigns, and online booking; the 'built into Shopmonkey' phrasing was dropped. Add-on price unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $215, Clever at $359, Genius at $449, CRM Essentials at $314 and Bookkeeping at $314.
It lands 91 days after Shopmonkey's previous change, "Shopmonkey: Basic/Clever/Genius price increases + 2 new add-ons" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shopmonkey upgrades support tiers, drops 24/7 emergency option" (Jun 9, 2026). Shopmonkey has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
