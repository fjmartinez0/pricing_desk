What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Gusto's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Gusto HR Partners add-on: fractional HR support for compliance, hiring and onboarding, available to eligible Plus and Premium customers; no price listed. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Simple at $6, Solo at $6, Plus at $80, Premium at $180 and Tax-Advantaged Benefits at $200 per year.
It lands 7 days after Gusto's previous change, "Gusto drops Contractor Only plan, adds $85 Business Compliance add-on" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Gusto launches limited-time free Contractor Only plan for 1099-only teams" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Gusto re-gates custom admin roles to Premium, bundles Next-Day pay into Plus and Premium" (Sep 7, 2026). Gusto has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Paychex and BambooHR also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
