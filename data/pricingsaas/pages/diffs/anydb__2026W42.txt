What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of AnyDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Email conversation sync launched in comparison table: previously 'Coming soon' on all plans, now Manual Sync on Free and Auto Sync on Business and Enterprise. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Business at $10.
It lands 7 days after AnyDB's previous change, "AnyDB adds Entra ID and Okta SSO for Enterprise, plus custom branding tiers" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "AnyDB: Free plan AI credits boosted to 1,000/mo + new MCP server access" (Aug 31, 2026), "AnyDB: Business/Enterprise now capped at 50 workflows" (Aug 17, 2026) and "AnyDB: Solution Packs price hidden ($100/mo) + workflow cap removed" (Aug 10, 2026). AnyDB has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window MongoDB, Elastic, Redis, MariaDB and Couchbase also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
