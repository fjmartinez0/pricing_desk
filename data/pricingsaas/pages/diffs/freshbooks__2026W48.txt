What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Freshbooks' pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday promo deepened from 80% to 90% off for 4 months (Lite $2.30, Plus $4.30, Premium $7/mo; list $23/$43/$70 unchanged); adds free FreshBooks Payroll for 4 months, drops free onboarding from headline. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Lite at $23, Plus at $43, Premium at $70, Advanced Payments at $20 and FreshBooks Payroll at $40.
It lands 27 days after Freshbooks' previous change, "Freshbooks adds Klarna pay-later and Bill Pay to every plan" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Freshbooks swaps $1/mo Lite deal for 80% off 4 months, extends Plus promo" (Oct 18, 2026) and "Freshbooks drops Lite to $1/mo for a year, trims Plus and Premium promos" (Sep 20, 2026). Freshbooks has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Xero, Sage, QuickBooks and Zoho also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
