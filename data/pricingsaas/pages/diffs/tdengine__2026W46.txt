What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of TDengine's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Cloud pricing table restructured: added a new 100,000-tag tier ($6,500/mo, $78,000/yr), dropped the Starter/Small/Medium/Large/XLarge package names, and added a new disclosure that HA costs an extra 50% of the cloud plan price. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Cloud 5,000 Tags at $970, Self-Hosted 7,500 Tags at $1,041.67, Self-Hosted 10,000 Tags at $1,291.67, Self-Hosted 15,000 Tags at $1,583.33 and Cloud 10,000 Tags at $1,740.
It lands 104 days after TDengine's previous change, "TDengine: new free-forever tier for up to 5,000 tags" (Jul 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "TDengine hikes cloud annual plan prices 20% across all tiers" (May 27, 2026). TDengine has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
In the same window Datadog, Elastic, Quest Software and Grafana also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
