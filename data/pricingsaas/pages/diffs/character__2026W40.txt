What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Character.AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Character.AI added a new 'Lite' tier between Free and c.ai+, giving it intermediate values for Memory, Pins, Lorebook, Feature limits, Slow mode, and limited daily access to Premium chat styles. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists c.ai+ at $7.92.
It lands 14 days after Character.AI's previous change, "Character.AI: paid tier renamed Plus → c.ai+" (Sep 14, 2026). Character.AI has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
