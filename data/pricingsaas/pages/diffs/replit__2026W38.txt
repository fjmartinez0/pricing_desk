What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Replit's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed the free "Starter" plan from the pricing page; Core ($18-20/mo) is now the entry-level tier. Core/Pro's previously-vague "free agent usage" allowance was reworded into quantified "Free Mode" hours/project caps (e.g. Core: up to 30 hours chat, 60 projects on Free Mode). Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Replit Core at $18 and Replit Pro at $90.
It lands 3 days after Replit's previous change, "Replit axes free Starter plan, ties Core and Pro bullets to Free Mode" (Sep 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Replit drops Core's 2 parallel agents, caps free tier to one task" (Sep 7, 2026), "Replit tightens agent limits: Core loses parallel-agent count, free tier capped at one background task" (Sep 2, 2026) and "Replit: Core/Pro annual discounts rebalanced + multi-agent parallelism added" (Jul 4, 2026). Replit has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window CodeSandbox, JetBrains and Gitpod also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
