What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Holvi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing page changed from Flex/Lite/Pro/Business plan cards (€0/€9/€15/€40 monthly) to segment hub cards with 'From' prices: Solopreneurs €0, SMEs €15, Bookkeeping €79, Associations €0. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Associations Pro at EUR 15, Holvi Zen at EUR 79 and Holvi Zen+ at EUR 99.
