What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Microsoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Voice Live Pro/Standard/Lite tier headers no longer list their specific backing LLM models (e.g. gpt-realtime, gpt-4o, gpt-5 for Pro); replaced with a generic note pointing to documentation for the current list of supported AI models. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Commitment Tier Standard – 2,000 hrs/mo at $480, Commitment Tier Connected Container – 2,000 hrs/mo at $912, Commitment Tier Connected Container – 10,000 hrs/mo at $6,175, Commitment Tier Disconnected Container – 50,000 hrs/mo at $7,125 and Commitment Tier Disconnected Container – 10,000 hrs/mo at $7,410.
It lands 21 days after Microsoft's previous change, "Microsoft Speech: batch enhanced-feature no-extra-charge note removed" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Microsoft Speech: Voice Live Pro Cached Input $40→$0.40; GPT Realtime 2 added" (Aug 31, 2026). Microsoft has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window Salesforce and Google also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
