What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Mentionlytics's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: AI Visibility add-on prices raised: Essential $49→$59/mo, Advanced $99→$119, Pro $169→$209, Business $379→$479, Enterprise $559→$699; per-prompt rates up ($1.98→$2.38 etc.); AI Visibility requirement changed from Essential plan or higher to any active Social Listening plan; otherwise core Mentionlytics must be added for $39/mo. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $49, Essential at $141, Advanced at $249, Pro at $416 and Business at $624.
It lands 14 days after Mentionlytics's previous change, "Mentionlytics publishes AI Visibility add-on pricing from $49/mo across 5 prompt tiers" (Oct 26, 2026). Mentionlytics has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Hootsuite, Buffer and Sproutsocial also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
