What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Vultr's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Two NVIDIA A16 GPU Cloud Compute tiers removed: 4x GPU/64GB GPU RAM/24 vCPUs ($1.884/hr) and 8x GPU/128GB GPU RAM/48 vCPUs ($3.767/hr). Lineup now jumps from 2x GPU ($0.942/hr) straight to 16x GPU ($7.534/hr). Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Load Balancers at $10, Global Load Balancers at $10, CDN at $10, Cloud Compute Regular Performance at $2.5 per month and Object Storage Archival at $6 per TB per month.
It lands 21 days after Vultr's previous change, "Vultr: NVIDIA A16 2-GPU tier RAM cut 128GB→120GB" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vultr: new Audit Logs add-on ($0.50/GB ingestion + $0.01/GB/mo retention)" (Aug 5, 2026). Vultr has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window OVHcloud, Digitalocean, Google Cloud and Scale also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
