What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Budibase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Cloud plan trial changed from self-serve 'Start 14 day free trial' to 'Request a free cloud trial' via contact form; 14-day wording dropped. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change). The current page lists Cloud at $299, AI Credit Pack at $20 per 10,000 purchased AI credits, Additional Creators Add-on at $50 and End Users Add-on at $5.
It lands 14 days after Budibase's previous change, "Budibase cuts Pro and Premium tiers, now starts paid Cloud plan at $299/mo" (Nov 8, 2026). Budibase has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
