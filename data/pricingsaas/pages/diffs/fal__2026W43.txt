What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of fal's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Flux 3 Image (blackforestlabs/flux-3/edit-image) Model API price doubled from $0.024 to $0.048 per megapixel (41.67 to 20.83 megapixels per $1). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 20, 2026) lists Audio Model APIs at $0.1 per 1,000 characters (MiniMax Speech 2.8 HD) + $0.2 per 100 seconds (ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2), 3D Model APIs at $0.08 per generation (Hunyuan Motion 1B) + $0.01 per credit (Tripo H3.1), Serverless Compute at $6 per H200 GPU hour + $4 per RTX PRO 6000 GPU hour, Video Model APIs at $0.05 per second of video output (MiniMax H3 Max) + $0.07 per second of video output (Kling 2.5 Turbo Pro) and Image Model APIs at $1 per unit (GPT Image 2) + $0.5 per 100 megapixels (Z Image Turbo).
It lands 3 days after fal's previous change, "fal doubles Flux 3 Image edit price to $0.048 per megapixel" (Oct 16, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "fal adds FLUX 3 Max video model at $0.085/sec" (Oct 20, 2026), "fal adds Flux 3 Image editing at $0.024 per megapixel to Model API" (Oct 12, 2026) and "fal adds Flux 3 image editing to rate card at $0.024 per megapixel" (Oct 9, 2026). fal has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
