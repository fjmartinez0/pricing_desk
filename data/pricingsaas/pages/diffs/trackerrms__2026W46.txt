What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Tracker's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Launch plan ($79/user/month, up to 5 recruiters) removed from the plan comparison; Core ($99/user/month) is now the entry plan and its feature list no longer builds on Launch. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Core at $99.
It lands 21 days after Tracker's previous change, "Tracker adds AI to every tier, with agents unlocking at Professional" (Oct 19, 2026). Tracker has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
In the same window Zoho, HubSpot, Asana, Salesforce and Pipedrive also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
“Trackerrms” is the name Tracker’s pricing page uses; Pulse files the change under Tracker, the company it tracks.
