What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Exa's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Instant Search endpoint at $4/1k requests added; existing Search renamed Fast / Auto Search and stays $7; Search entry price now shown as 'From $4' instead of $7. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Search at $7 per 1k requests, up to 10 results + $1 per 1k page AI summaries, Agent at $0.01 per search tool call + $0.1 per request (Medium effort), Contents at $1 per 1k pages, per content type + $1 per 1k page AI summaries, Deep Search at $12 per 1k requests, up to 10 results + $1 per 1k requests, additional result above 10 and Deep-Reasoning Search at $1 per 1k page AI summaries + $1 per 1k requests, additional result above 10.
It lands 7 days after Exa's previous change, "Exa: Free plan first-month credits cut from $30 to $20" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Exa boosts Starter agent concurrency 3→50 and doubles QPS limits" (Sep 7, 2026), "Exa: Answer endpoint overage + AI-summary fees removed" (Aug 3, 2026) and "Exa: Free tier switched to credit-based model ($20 signup + $10/mo)" (Jul 20, 2026). Exa has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Google and You.com also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
