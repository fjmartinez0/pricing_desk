What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of GitLab's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Promotional included GitLab Credits cut: Premium $12 to $8 and Ultimate $24 to $16 per user/month; existing subscribers keep old amounts through Dec 31, 2026. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered). The current page lists Premium at $29, Enterprise Agile Planning at $15, Compute Minutes at $10 per 1,000 minutes, Storage at $5 and GitLab Duo Agent Platform at $1 per GitLab Credit.
This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "GitLab cuts Premium to $8 and Ultimate to $16 per user in credits promo" (Day 324, 2026, saved Nov 20, 2026). It is the same change, not a new one. Before that, GitLab's previous recorded change was "GitLab: Advanced Capacity add-on priced at $12,000/year for higher API limits" (Oct 31, 2026). GitLab has been tracked since 2025-10-05.
In the same window Aws, Google Cloud, GitHub and JetBrains also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
