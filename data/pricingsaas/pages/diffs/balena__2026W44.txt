What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Balena's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Added 'Custom fleet roles' feature to the Prototype, Pilot and Production plans; Added a 'Custom' additional-user role (custom roles with granular permissions for fleets) at $49/user/month. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 28, 2026) lists Prototype at $143.33, Pilot at $299, Production at $1,299, Additional User: Operator at $29 and Additional User: Developer at $49.
It lands 22 days after Balena's previous change, "Balena adds $0 Free plan with 10 devices, drops Enterprise tier card" (Oct 5, 2026). Balena has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
