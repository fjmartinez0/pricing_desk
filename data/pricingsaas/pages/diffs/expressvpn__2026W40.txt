What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of ExpressVPN's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: ExpressVPN 2-year intro pricing increased: Advanced $3.99->$4.49/mo (Save 76%->73%); Express Pro $5.99->$7.49/mo (Save 73%->67%). Basic unchanged at $2.99/mo. Total savings shown dropped from $364/$476 to $350/$434. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Basic at $3.99, Advanced at $4.49 and Express Pro at $7.49.
It lands 7 days after ExpressVPN's previous change, "ExpressVPN: ExpressAI model access expanded (6→7) + 2 more feature additions" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ExpressVPN cuts Pro to $5.99/mo and Advanced to $3.99/mo on 2-year deals" (Sep 14, 2026), "ExpressVPN swaps app-icon row for 2Yr/1Yr/1Mo billing tabs, 80% off" (Aug 31, 2026) and "ExpressVPN: Basic plan price cut $3.49->$2.99/mo + 2 more changes" (Aug 3, 2026). ExpressVPN has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window CyberGhost VPN, Surfshark and NordVPN also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
