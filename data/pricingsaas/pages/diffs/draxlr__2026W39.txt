What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Draxlr's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 'Embed Dashboard' relabeled to 'Embed Dashboard (White-label)' on the Premium, Power and Enterprise plans (Lite still excludes it); the standalone 'White Label' bullet previously listed for Enterprise was folded into this label. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Lite at $16.67, Premium at $50, Power at $166.67, Enterprise at $316.67 and AI Credits at $10.
It lands 28 days after Draxlr's previous change, "Draxlr caps free MCP access at 50 requests as paid tiers go unlimited" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Draxlr: User access permissions now tiered by plan" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Enterprise tier launched at $500/mo, Self-Host plan discontinued, AI upgrades" (Jul 2, 2026). Draxlr has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Salesforce, Microsoft, HubSpot, Zoho and Pipedrive also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
