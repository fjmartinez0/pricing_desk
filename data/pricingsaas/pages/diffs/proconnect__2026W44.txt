What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of ProConnect's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Per-return prices now show struck-through list prices with ~15% discounted rates (e.g. Individual $109.95 to $93.46; Business $129.95 to $110.46; 50+ volume tiers $64.95 to $55.21). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists ProConnect Essentials 100 at $5,957, ProConnect Plus at $8,802, ProConnect Advanced at $12,792, Pay-per-return - Individual at $109.95 per individual return and Pay-per-return - Business at $129.95 per business return.
It lands 83 days after ProConnect's previous change, "ProConnect Tax: promo discount cut 30%->25%, new $210 user license fee" (Aug 4, 2026). ProConnect has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
