What happened
Between the Oct 13, 2026 and Oct 14, 2026 captures of Slack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise+ now shows a public price of $45 USD per user/month (billed annually) instead of 'Contact sales for pricing'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 20, 2026) lists Pro at $7.25, Business+ at $15 and Enterprise+ at $45.
It lands one day after Slack's previous change, "Slack hides $45 Enterprise+ price, now says Contact sales for pricing" (Oct 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Slack puts $45 price on Enterprise+ plan, dropping "Contact sales" (Oct 4, 2026). Slack has been tracked since 2023-11-30.
In the same window Google, Rocket.Chat, Microsoft, Mattermost and Zoom also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
