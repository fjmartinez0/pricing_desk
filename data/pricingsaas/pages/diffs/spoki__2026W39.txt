What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Spoki's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI consulting added to Standard, Advanced, and Premium Support tiers; Advanced gets 1 AI prompt + 1 video call, Premium gets 2 AI prompts + periodic video calls. Basic (free) tier unaffected. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Service at EUR 39, Marketing at EUR 79, Sales at EUR 119, Voice at EUR 19.99 and SMS Two-way at EUR 19.
It lands 7 days after Spoki's previous change, "Spoki hikes WhatsApp Marketing credit rate 15%, real spend jumps to €308.92/mo" (Sep 14, 2026). Spoki has been tracked since 2026-08-27.
In the same window Microsoft, Discord, Google and Zoom also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
