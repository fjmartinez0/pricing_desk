What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of CheckMark's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Both MultiLedger plans increased in price for the 2027 version: Pro+ rose from $719 to $739 (first year) and $359 to $369 (renewal); Pro rose from $649 to $669 (first year) and $289 to $299 (renewal). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists CheckMark MultiLedger Pro at $55.75, CheckMark MultiLedger Pro+ at $61.58, Additional Companies at $50 per additional company, Additional Computer Installation at $99 per computer installation and Cloud Backup at $5.75.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for CheckMark. CheckMark has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
