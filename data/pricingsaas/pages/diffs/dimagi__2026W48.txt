What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Dimagi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New AI features added to all paid plans: AI companion (30 messages/user/month on Standard and Pro, 60 on Advanced and Enterprise) and AI translations (included on Standard, Pro, Advanced, Enterprise; not on Free). The Standard card now lists AI companion & AI translations in place of Scheduled data exports, which remains included in the compare table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Standard at $100, Pro at $500, Advanced at $1,000, Enterprise at $4,000 and OData Feeds Bundle of 100 at $350.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Dimagi. Dimagi has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
