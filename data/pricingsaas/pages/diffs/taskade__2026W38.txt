What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Taskade's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Introduced two new purchasable add-ons: Extra Credits ($5/pack, stacks on plan's monthly credit allowance) and Custom Domains ($5/year, or bring your own domain free); Comparison table limits increased: Free plan team members raised from 1 to 2; Enterprise team members changed from 'Custom' to unlimited; new 'Workspaces' row added (Free=1, all paid plans unlimited). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pro at $10, Business at $25, Max at $100, Enterprise at $250 and Extra Credits at $5 per 9,000-credit pack (one-time) + $20 per 45,000-credit pack (one-time).
It lands 7 days after Taskade's previous change, "Taskade doubles Free plan seats to 2, swaps Gemini for DeepSeek" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Taskade clarifies free-tier limits: "Keep 3 apps," one-time 6,000 AI credits" (Aug 17, 2026), "Taskade axes Starter plan, boosts annual discount from 20% to 50% off" (Jul 20, 2026) and "Taskade drops "Taskade AI" branding, leads free plan with build-first pitch" (Jul 13, 2026). Taskade has been tracked since 2024-10-14.
In the same window Notion, Trello, Todoist, ClickUp and Asana also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
