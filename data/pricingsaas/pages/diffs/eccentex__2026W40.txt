What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Eccentex's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Customer Service Cloud price decreased from $224/user/month to $149/user/month, a 33% reduction. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Business Intelligence at $39, Advanced Email Automation at $49, Smart Knowledge Base at $49, Intelligent Work Automation at $99 and AI Assisted Case Management at $119.
It lands 7 days after Eccentex's previous change, "Eccentex: pricing restructured — 3 new vertical Cloud plans, core tiers repriced" (Sep 21, 2026). Eccentex has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Dassault Systèmes, Holded, Threecolts, Shippo and Tatsoft also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
