What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Observable's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Team plan's signup button changed from 'Coming soon' to 'Get started', meaning the previously teaser-only Team tier ($40/editor/mo) is now open for self-serve signup. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $22, Team at $40 and Private viewers at $10.
It lands 21 days after Observable's previous change, "Observable relaunches pricing with $40 Team tier, Pro CTA now Coming soon" (Sep 7, 2026). Observable has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Stream, JetBrains, Metabase and Tableau also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
