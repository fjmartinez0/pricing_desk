What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Own's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Premier Success Plan changed from 30% of net license fees to included with Core, Advanced, and Max licenses (no separate fee); Premier Success Plan no longer lists 'Bundled with Unlimited Edition'; Signature feature 'Customer Success Score' renamed to 'Salesforce Health Insights'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease, feature change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Mobile App Plus at $25 and Customer Identity at $25.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Own. Own has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
