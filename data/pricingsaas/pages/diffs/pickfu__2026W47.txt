What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of PickFu's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: PickFu+ ($999+/yr credit plans) replaced by Starter $200/yr, Pro $1,000/yr and Team $5,000/yr subscription tiers; Pay as you go and Enterprise remain; Credit-matching and up-to-15% survey discount replaced by monthly panel credits (30/120/600) and platform credits (1,000-150,000) per plan. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change, packaging change). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 18, 2026) lists Starter at $16.67, Pro at $83.33 and Team at $416.67.
It lands 49 days after PickFu's previous change, "PickFu expands Pay-as-you-go: traits to 100+, countries to 15, broader…" (Sep 28, 2026). PickFu has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Google, SurveyMonkey and Respondent also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
