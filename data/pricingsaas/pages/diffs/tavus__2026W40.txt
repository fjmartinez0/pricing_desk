What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Tavus's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Maximum conversation duration cap reduced: Growth and Business plans changed from unlimited ('Maximum') to a 60-minute cap; Enterprise changed from 'Maximum' to 'Custom' (contract-negotiated) in the plan comparison table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists PALs Plus at $20, PALs Max at $50, Starter at $59 and Growth at $397.
It lands 21 days after Tavus's previous change, "Tavus: turn-taking model upgraded (Sparrow-1 → Sparrow-2)" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Tavus shifts 50%-off promo from Starter to Builder, now $29/mo" (Jul 20, 2026) and "Tavus expands developer tiers from 4 to 6, adds $22 Starter plan" (Jun 30, 2026). Tavus has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
In the same window Synthesia, Runway, Descript and Loom also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
