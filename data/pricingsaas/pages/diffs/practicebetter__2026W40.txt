What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Practice Better's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Introduced new 'Connector Hub' add-on at $49/month, syncing client, booking, and billing data with QuickBooks, Xero, HubSpot, Salesforce, Mailchimp, Google/Microsoft Workspace and more. Available on Plus and Team plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Starter at $25, Professional at $62, Plus at $89, Team at $155 and AI Charting Assistant - 20 Hours at $10.
It lands 14 days after Practice Better's previous change, "Practice Better swaps free AI Charting minutes for $10-$40 monthly tiers" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Practice Better: Summer sale discount expired (Pro/Plus/Team)" (Aug 10, 2026) and "Practice Better: Summer sale — up to 35% off Professional/Plus/Team" (Aug 4, 2026). Practice Better has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
In the same window Zen Planner, Kajabi, Acuity Scheduling, Thinkific and Mindbody also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
