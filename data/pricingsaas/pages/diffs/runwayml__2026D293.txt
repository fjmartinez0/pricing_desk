What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 20, 2026 captures of Runway's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Limited-time promo 'Unlimited Wan 3.0 & Wan 3.0 Prime in Tool Mode until October 18' removed from the Max plan after expiry; plan prices unchanged.
