What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of QuickCode's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Importers & Manufacturers plan starting price increased from $2,099/month to $3,000/month (about 43% increase) for up to 25,000 monitored products; New 'Entry Audit' feature added to the Importers & Manufacturers plan, listed between 24/7 Compliance Monitoring and Compliance Dashboard. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Broker Partner Program at $749 and Importers & Manufacturers at $3,000.
It lands 84 days after QuickCode's previous change, "QuickCode expands tariff monitoring to include Section 232 metals" (Jun 29, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "QuickCode expands tariff monitoring to include Section 232 metals (Q2 2026)" (Jul 4, 2026). QuickCode has been tracked since 2026-03-15.
In the same window Replit, Cursor, JetBrains and Windsurf also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
