What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Numeric's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Essentials $30/month/user (the only publicly shown price) removed along with Growth/Enterprise tier names; page now shows ERP Replacement, Close Management, Cash Management, and Billing & Revenue modules with demo-only CTAs, no prices or plan names. In detail: Pricing model overhauled: tiered plans (Essentials $30/mo/user, Growth, Enterprise — Custom) replaced by modular products (ERP Replacement, Close Mgmt, Cash Mgmt, Billing & Revenue); all pricing removed. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction.
