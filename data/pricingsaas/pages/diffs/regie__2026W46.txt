What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Regie.ai's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Monday CRM no longer listed as a supported CRM sync (HubSpot and Salesforce remain); Pro plan's 'power dialer' renamed 'parallel dialer'. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $41 and Team at $125.
It lands 7 days after Regie.ai's previous change, "Regie.ai adds free built-in CRM to every plan, with HubSpot and Salesforce sync" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Regie.ai launches $125/$150 Team plan, expands Free to 10 seats" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Regie.ai: relaunches as RegieGO with Free/$49 Pro plans, drops $180-$499/user legacy pricing" (Aug 24, 2026). Regie.ai has been tracked since 2026-05-02.
In the same window HubSpot and Intercom also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
