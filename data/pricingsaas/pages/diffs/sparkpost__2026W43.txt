What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of SparkPost's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Apple Messages changes from Custom quote to Pay as you go plus wallet funding from $100/month; Email paid plans from $15/month reframed as monthly wallet funding to Bird balance. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Startup at $15, Growth at $80 and Dedicated IP at $24.95.
It lands 14 days after SparkPost's previous change, "SparkPost halves US local number rental to $0.50 a month" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SparkPost: Pricing page rebuilt around a Bird balance/wallet model" (Sep 28, 2026) and "SparkPost/Bird: Email plan pricing removed, replaced by usage hub" (Aug 31, 2026). SparkPost has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
In the same window Brevo, HubSpot, Klaviyo, Twilio and Constant Contact also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
