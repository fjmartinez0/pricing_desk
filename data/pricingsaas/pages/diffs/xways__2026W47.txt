What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of X-Ways Software's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: USD list prices cut ~1-2% across products: Forensics $1,560→$1,540 (non-perp) and $4,030→$3,960 (perp); WinHex, Investigator, Imager lowered; training from $695→$680. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction.
It lands 7 days after X-Ways Software's previous change, "X-Ways nudges X-Ways Forensics to $1,560, raises WinHex and Imager" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "X-Ways Software lifts perpetual X-Ways Forensics $3,995 to $4,030, Investigator to $2,070" (Nov 1, 2026), "X-Ways rolls prices: Forensics perpetual hits $4,030 from $3,990" (Nov 1, 2026) and "X-Ways Software raises volume-tier license prices $1-$5 across Forensics, WinHex" (Oct 26, 2026). X-Ways Software has been tracked since 2026-05-04.
