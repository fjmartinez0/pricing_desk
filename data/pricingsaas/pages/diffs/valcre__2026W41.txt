What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Valcre's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Valcre Assist add-on now shows per-report pricing: Standard $250 / Premium $265 single report, with 10- and 20-report bundle discounts, comps from $5 each; SSO is now an add-on on every plan and API integrations an add-on on Professional (previously listed as Enterprise features). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed, feature change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $167 and Professional at $417.
It lands 139 days after Valcre's previous change, "Valcre raises subscription prices up to 11% across all plans" (May 19, 2026). Valcre has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
