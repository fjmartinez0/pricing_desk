What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Reflex's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: "App Code Download" moved from Pro's highlighted feature list to Free's; Enterprise plan subtitle shifted from a 'dedicated infrastructure' pitch to 'shared workspaces, security, and deployment flexibility'. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pro at $25.
It lands 28 days after Reflex's previous change, "Reflex guts Pro plan: flat $200/mo replaced by $25 base + usage tiers" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Reflex: Pro plan promo price ($3/mo) expired, reverted to $200/mo" (Aug 10, 2026). Reflex has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Salesforce, Microsoft, HubSpot, Freshworks and Pipedrive also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
