What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Vincere's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Core and Pro feature lists reframed from 'Features you get' (included) to 'Features you can add on', now optional add-ons unlocked for an additional fee on top of Startup; On-site visits (2/year) removed from the Pro plan feature list. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature removed, package change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at GBP 85, Core at GBP 95 and Startup at GBP 150.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Vincere. Vincere has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
