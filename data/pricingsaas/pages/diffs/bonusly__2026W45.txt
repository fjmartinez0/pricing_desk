What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Bonusly's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free plan ($0 forever, up to 8 users, peer recognition, Slack/Teams, mobile apps) removed; pricing page now offers only Team + Bizy ($5/user/mo, 30-day trial) and Organization + Bizy (custom); Organization plan adds Nominations, Incentives and Spot Bonuses, and External Recognition Programs to its feature list. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, freemium change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Team + Bizy AI at $4.17 and Bizy AI at $2.
It lands 122 days after Bonusly's previous change, "Bonusly: Team plan price to $5/user/month as Bizy AI bundled by default" (Jul 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Bonusly raises Team plan 67% as Bizy AI bundled into all tiers" (Jun 8, 2026). Bonusly has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
In the same window BambooHR, 15five and Qualtrics also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
