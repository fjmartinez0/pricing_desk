Planable adds top banner promoting existing Social Listening add-on
Verified Sep 21, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral
The changeSocial Listening positioning — positioning.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 14, 2026 → Sep 21, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Planable's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New closeable top-of-page banner announces 'Social Listening is live', promoting the existing Social listening add-on. Add-on price ($82.5/workspace/month) and feature bullets are unchanged, so this is marketing framing, not a pricing or entitlement change. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Basic at $33, Pro at $49, Social Listening at $82.5, Analytics at $12 and Social Inbox at $7.5.
It lands 35 days after Planable's previous change, "Planable adds Competitor Analytics to feature comparison on all plans" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Planable: Free plan removed from pricing page; MCP Connector + Public API added to paid plans" (Jul 4, 2026). Planable has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window MeetEdgar, Sproutsocial, Buffer, Hootsuite and Later also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
