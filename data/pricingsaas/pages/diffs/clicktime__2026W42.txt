What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of ClickTime's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan feature lists reworked: Team gains Work Types and Approvals/cost rates (was Advanced billing rates); Premier drops Report Studio for custom reports; Enterprise adds custom onboarding with expert. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $12 and Expense Tracking at $4.
It lands 7 days after ClickTime's previous change, "ClickTime drops billing toggle, discount badges for flat $12/user starter price" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ClickTime reshuffles Team/Premier plan messaging, no pricing impact" (Jul 20, 2026). ClickTime has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Asana, Zoho and Quickbooks also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
