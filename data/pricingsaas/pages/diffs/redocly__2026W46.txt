What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Redocly's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Scale and Enterprise plans gained three new developer customization capabilities: API functions (custom API endpoints), custom MCP tools, and custom Markdoc tags. Not included on Pro. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $249, Scale at $729 and Additional Project at $32.50.
It lands 14 days after Redocly's previous change, "Redocly swaps per-seat plans for flat $249 Pro and $729 Scale tiers" (Oct 26, 2026). Redocly has been tracked since 2026-03-30.
In the same window Aws, Postman, Readme, Konghq and MuleSoft also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
