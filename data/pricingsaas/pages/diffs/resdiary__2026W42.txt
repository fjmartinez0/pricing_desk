What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of ResDiary's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Payment processing surcharge on Deposits and Payments raised from 1% to 1.2%; Gift Voucher surcharge unchanged at 3%; Add-ons renamed: Order & Pay to Pre-orders (and bundle), Group Bookings to Group Central Reservations; prices unchanged ($45, $65, $15). Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change, positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Order & Pay + Events Manager Bundle at $65, ResPhone at $50, Event Manager at $45, Gift Vouchers at $45 and PMS Integration at $15.
It lands 28 days after ResDiary's previous change, "ResDiary raises Pre-orders payment surcharge from 1% to 1.2%" (Sep 14, 2026). ResDiary has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window TouchBistro, Lightspeed and Toast also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
