What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of GTmetrix's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Test Locations increased from 28 to 33 on the Advanced and Expert plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised). The current page lists Lite at $4.99, Core at $9.99, Advanced at $24.99 and Expert at $49.99.
It lands 14 days after GTmetrix's previous change, "GTmetrix cuts all plans 40% for Black Friday, with Lite at $2.99/month" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "GTmetrix bumps Test Locations cap to 28 on Advanced, Expert plans" (Sep 21, 2026) and "GTmetrix ends SUMMER2026 30% promo, list prices hold across all plans" (Sep 7, 2026). GTmetrix has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
