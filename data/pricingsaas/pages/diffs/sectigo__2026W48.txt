What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Sectigo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free automatic certificate renewals ('coming soon') added to DV, EV and OV SSL cards; banner now says every SSL cert includes free automation instead of promoting SCM Pro. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Single Domain DV at $120, Single Domain OV at $250, Multi-Domain DV at $335, Single Domain EV at $390 and Wildcard DV at $630.
