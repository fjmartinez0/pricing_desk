What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Figma's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Figma Specs beta availability broadened from paid seats only to all seats. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Organization at $5, Enterprise at $5 and Professional at $16.
It lands 4 days after Figma's previous change, "Figma opens Specs beta to all seats, no longer limited to paid seats" (Oct 29, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Figma adds Specs beta to paid seats, puts code layers in Design tiers" (Oct 19, 2026), "Figma debuts Specs in beta, bundled with Full, Dev and Collab seats" (Oct 15, 2026) and "Figma agent exits beta, ending free ride as AI credit charges now apply" (Oct 12, 2026). Figma has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Sketch, Webflow, Framer and Penpot also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
