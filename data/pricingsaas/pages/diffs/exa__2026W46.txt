What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Exa's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Monitors API price cut from $15 to $2 per 1k requests; Enterprise added a disclosed self-serve tier ($12,000/yr, Zero Data Retention, 25 Search QPS) via new 'Upgrade now' CTA, with SOC2/HIPAA, SSO, and custom QPS now gated behind a separate custom/contact-sales tier. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Search at $7 per 1k requests, up to 10 results + $1 per 1k page AI summaries, Agent at $0.01 per search tool call + $0.10 per request (Medium effort), Contents at $1 per 1k pages, per content type + $1 per 1k page AI summaries, Deep Search at $12 per 1k requests, up to 10 results + $1 per 1k requests, additional result above 10 and Monitors at $1 per 1k requests, additional result above 10 + $1 per 1k page AI summaries.
It lands 14 days after Exa's previous change, "Exa drops Deep-Reasoning Search tier and renames Instant to Lite Search" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Exa launches $4/1k Instant Search, cutting Search entry price from $7" (Oct 5, 2026), "Exa: Free plan first-month credits cut from $30 to $20" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Exa boosts Starter agent concurrency 3→50 and doubles QPS limits" (Sep 7, 2026). Exa has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window You.com and Google also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
