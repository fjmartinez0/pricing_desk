What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Overleaf's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Comparison table adds Offline editing duration row (2 min on Free, 10 min on paid plans) and Shared workspace row (Organizations only). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Student at $8.25, Standard at $16.75 and Pro at $33.25.
It lands the same day as Overleaf's previous change, "Overleaf lists offline editing: 2 minutes on Free, 10 on paid plans" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Overleaf: Back to School 20% institutional group discount" (Aug 31, 2026). Overleaf has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window Microsoft, Notion, Canva and Google also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
