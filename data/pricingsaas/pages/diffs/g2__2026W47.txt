What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of G2's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Starter plan eligibility restricted: now 'available to net new vendors only' (no existing paid G2 subscription); the 'small businesses, 1-100 employees' definition is dropped. Price unchanged at $299/mo or $2,999/yr; New add-ons listed: Verified Leads (pay-per-lead, BANT-qualified leads; needs a Professional+ profile) and AI Custom Research (AICR) as an add-on on all plans. No prices published. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, packaging change). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 17, 2026) lists Starter at $249.92.
It lands 42 days after G2's previous change, "G2 removes seat caps—Starter and Pro plans now offer Unlimited Users" (Oct 5, 2026). G2 has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
