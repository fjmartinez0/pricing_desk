What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Composio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Firewall add-on: not on Hobby, usage-priced add-on on Pro ($0.50 per 1M tokens evaluated), included on Enterprise; new FAQ explains Firewall billing vs Sandbox LLM tokens. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $29.
It lands 14 days after Composio's previous change, "Composio renames Premium tools to Instant Tools, updates billing FAQ" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Composio bumps toolkit count to 1500+ across Free, Pro and Enterprise" (Sep 7, 2026). Composio has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window UiPath, n8n, Zapier, Workato and Make also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
