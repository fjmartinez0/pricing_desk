What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Sanity's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Scheduled Functions limits cut: Free 5→2, Growth 10→5; Enterprise changed from Custom to a fixed 20 Functions. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Growth at $15, Extra Datasets at $999, Increased Quota at $299 and Dedicated Support at $799.
It lands 121 days after Sanity's previous change, "Sanity: SAML SSO add-on discontinued for Growth plan" (Jul 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Sanity boosts AI Credits 10x across all plans" (May 12, 2026), "Sanity Caps Free Embeddings at 500/mo, Adds Cross-Dataset References" (Apr 24, 2026) and "Sanity introduces 'Content Agent Slack, API' feature for all plans" (Mar 17, 2026). Sanity has been tracked since 2025-12-29.
In the same window Craft, Webflow, Hygraph and Netlify also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
