What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Zenfolio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Cyber November sale: 60% off first year of Professional/Advanced annual plans, ends 11/25. Card prices show promo rates (Professional $11.50 to $9.20/mo, Advanced $20 to $16/mo); FAQ list prices unchanged. In detail: Cyber November sale deepens the standing first-year annual promo from 50% to 60% off on Professional/Advanced, ending 11/25 (was Oct 31). Card prices: Professional $11.50 to $9.20/mo, Advanced $20 to $16/mo; Basic $7 and monthly list prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $7, Professional at $11.50 and Advanced at $20.
