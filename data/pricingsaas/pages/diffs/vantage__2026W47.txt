What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Vantage's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: CoreWeave added as a new supported cloud provider integration in the Integrations carousel; total integrations library count grew from 41 to 43. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Pro at $30 and Business at $200.
