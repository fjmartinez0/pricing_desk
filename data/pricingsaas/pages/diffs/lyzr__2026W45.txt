What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Lyzr's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Lyzr renamed 'Take agents to production' ($100K/yr) to 'Govern every agent', and old 'Govern every agent' (Custom) to 'Automate workflows', as billing shifted off usage-based agent-months to flat annual plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Take Agents to Production at $8,333.33.
It lands 21 days after Lyzr's previous change, "Lyzr replaces four $100K–$250K modules with free Build tier, $100K Production plan and custom Govern" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Lyzr launches free-forever Agent Studio, rebuilds pricing into buyable modules" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Lyzr swaps per-run fees for agent-month plans, adds $250K Opencontroller tier" (Sep 28, 2026). Lyzr has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Huggingface, Anthropic, OpenAI, Cohere and Google also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
