What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Deepbrain's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Personal and Team move from unlimited AI videos to a credit-based model: Personal 700 credits/month (was 720 generative credits/year), Team 1,000 shared credits/seat/month (was 1,800/year). Prices unchanged; Free plan one-time sign-up credits reduced from 16 to 12; AI Dubbing allowances changed: Free 3 dubbing uses now audio-only dubbing; Personal 120 min/month and Team 240 min/month dubbing all. In detail: Personal and Team move from unlimited AI videos to a single credit balance covering video export (9 credits/min), dubbing (3/min) and generation: Personal 700 credits/month (was 60/month, 720/year, generative only), Team 1,000 shared credits/seat/month (was 150/seat/month, 1,800/year). Team now starts at 2 seats (was 1 seat+). Prices unchanged ($24/$55 billed yearly); existing paid subscribers keep their current terms; AI Dubbing allowances changed: Free 3 dubbing uses now audio-only dubbing; Personal 120 min/month and Team 240 min/month dubbing allowances removed in favor of credits (3 credits/min). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered, packaging change, feature change). The current page lists Personal at $24, Team at $55 and Interactive Avatar Standard at $99.
It lands 14 days after Deepbrain's previous change, "Deepbrain cuts voiceover languages and accents from 150+ to 50+" (Nov 1, 2026). Deepbrain has been tracked since 2023-12-07.
In the same window Captions, Synthesia, Runway and Loom also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
