What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of SignWell's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan: custom pricing no longer scoped to teams of 50+, now large organizations with high e-signature/API volume; Dedicated Support now includes onboarding and implementation. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Light at $10 and Business at $30.
It lands 21 days after SignWell's previous change, "SignWell lifts API free tier to 25 documents a month on every plan" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SignWell adds 21 CFR Part 11 controls row to plan comparison table" (Oct 5, 2026) and "SignWell adds SMS Delivery tracking, capped at 50 credits/mo on Business" (Aug 24, 2026). SignWell has been tracked since 2026-03-18.
In the same window Pandadoc and Zoho also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
