What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of 7shifts's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Essentials plan removed (4→3 tiers); Pro plan increased $79.99→$89.99/mo; Premium plan increased $134.99→$179.99/mo; new Team Size (31+ employees) pricing selector added. Pro plan now includes "Live support"; Premium plan bundles previously-standalone add-ons Operations Overview ($6.99) and Employee Onboarding ($2.99); Premium drops "Advanced hourly labor forecast"/"Advanced labor recommendations" for "Advanced development tools and integrations". In detail: Essentials plan removed (4→3 tiers); Pro increased $79.99→$89.99/mo; Premium increased $134.99→$179.99/mo; new Team Size (31+ employees) pricing selector added; Pro adds 'Live support'. Premium bundles new add-ons Operations Overview ($6.99) & Employee Onboarding ($2.99); drops labor forecast/recommendations for 'Advanced development tools and integrations'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Essentials at $39.99, Pro at $79.99, Premium at $134.99, Employee Onboarding at $2.99 per onboarding package sent and Task Management at $12.99.
It lands 62 days after 7shifts's previous change, "7shifts: Employee Training feature added to Premium plan" (Jul 28, 2026). 7shifts has been tracked since 2023-12-01.
