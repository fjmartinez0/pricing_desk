What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Buildkite's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro plan now states overage rates: $0.05/GB-month beyond 250GB artifact storage and $0.10/GB beyond 2.5TB artifact downloads. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Enterprise at $3.5, Pro at $30, Mac M4 Medium Hosted Agent at $0.18 per minute, Linux Small Hosted Agent at $0.01 per minute and Mac M4 Large Hosted Agent at $0.36 per minute.
It lands 56 days after Buildkite's previous change, "Buildkite: Personal→Free rename, Free/Pro plans gain explicit usage caps" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Buildkite: Model Providers LLM proxy pricing removed" (Aug 10, 2026). Buildkite has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window GitHub, CircleCI, GitLab and Google Cloud also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
