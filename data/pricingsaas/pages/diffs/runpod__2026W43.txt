What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of RunPod's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: GPU hourly rates rose: B300 $9.98 to $10.65; cluster H200 SXM $4.31 to $6.35; cluster A100 SXM $1.79 to $2.15. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Storage at $0.05 per GB/month, Pods - Community Cloud at $0.16 per GPU-hour, Pods - Secure Cloud at $0.27 per GPU-hour, Serverless at $0.58 per GPU-hour and Clusters at $1.79 per GPU-hour.
It lands 7 days after RunPod's previous change, "RunPod raises GPU cloud rates, B200 jumps to $7.99/hr and H100 to $3.99/hr" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "RunPod hikes GPU rates on Pods: H100 up to $3.49/hr, A100 to $1.59/hr" (Sep 7, 2026), "RunPod: GPU hourly rates increased across 9 tiers (B300, B200, H200 + 6 more)" (Aug 17, 2026) and "RunPod: Pods GPU price increases (RTX 6000 Ada, RTX A6000, RTX 3090)" (Jul 27, 2026). RunPod has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
