What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Loop Subscriptions' pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Transaction fee restated: Starter 1.0% + 0¢ per transaction becomes 1% + 15¢ per loyalty order; Pro 0.75% + 0¢ becomes 0.75% + 10¢ per loyalty order. Plan prices $99/$399 unchanged; Feature lists reworked. Starter adds native loyalty/referral, analytics and reports, advanced payment recovery; drops 15-retry dunning and auto price updates. Pro adds cart offers, A/B testing, Shopify Markets, MCP. In detail: New per-loyalty-order fee added to the transaction fee: Starter 1.0% + 0¢ per transaction → 1% + 15¢ per loyalty order; Pro 0.75% + 0¢ → 0.75% + 10¢ per loyalty order. Percentage fees and plan prices $99/$399 unchanged; Card feature lists reworked. Starter adds native loyalty/referral programs, analytics and custom reports, advanced payment recovery campaigns; no longer lists 15-retry dunning or automatic price/shipping updates. Pro adds native cart & post-purchase offers, A/B testing, Shopify Markets, Loop MCP; no longer lists prepaid/gift subscriptions, user permissions/account logs, or 24x7 Slack support. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change, feature change). The current page lists Starter at $99 and Pro at $399.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Loop Subscriptions. Loop Subscriptions has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
