What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of LiveAgent's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Offer-price lock extended to existing customers: those who change plan by Nov 22, 2026 now get the offer price on the new plan (kept while they keep it); previously only new trials started by Nov 22 qualified; Enterprise tagline/FAQ changed from 'no limits' to 'the highest limits'; comparison-table Enterprise limits still show Unlimited, so wording only; Typical AI chat cost at the customer's pr. In detail: Typical AI chat cost at the customer's provider restated from 2-4 cents to $0.11 per chat; still no LiveAgent AI fee. Pulse files it as a pricing change (positioning, discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $15, Team at $29, Business at $49 and Enterprise at $3,199.
It lands 14 days after LiveAgent's previous change, "LiveAgent cuts prices up to 26% through Nov 22 and adds account managers" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LiveAgent replaces per-agent Enterprise pricing with $3,999/mo flat fee" (Oct 26, 2026). LiveAgent has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Intercom also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
