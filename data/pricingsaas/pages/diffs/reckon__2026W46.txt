What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Reckon's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Monthly list prices rose: Accounting Plus $24 to $27, Premium $46 to $50, Ultimate $85 to $92; 6-month promo rates rose 10% ($2.40/$4.60/$8.50 to $2.70/$5/$9.20). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Payroll Essentials at AUD 176.64, Accounting Plus at AUD 264.96, Payroll Plus at AUD 353.28, Accounting Premium at AUD 507.84 and Payroll Premium at AUD 662.40.
It lands 7 days after Reckon's previous change, "Reckon ends flat $1 promo, splits into tiered $2.40-$8.50 plans" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Reckon extends One $1-for-6-months Spring Savings promo to October 31" (Oct 5, 2026). Reckon has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Xero, Freshbooks, QuickBooks, MYOB and Sage also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
