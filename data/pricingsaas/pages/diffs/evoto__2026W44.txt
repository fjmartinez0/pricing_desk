What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Evoto's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free Halloween Preset Pack and Halloween Asset Hub access (AI scenes and backgrounds) included with every plan purchase; Asset Hub added to nav. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter Plan at $7.42, Basic Plan at $12.42, Basic Plus Plan at $20.17, Standard Plan at $43.42 and 200 Credits Pack at $49 subscription.
Evoto's previous recorded change was "Evoto adds 10% annual promo, cutting Starter to $80 and Basic to $134" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Evoto: Asset Hub Membership added to all annual plans" (Sep 28, 2026), "Evoto: new Enterprise contact-sales tier added" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Evoto: 7-Day Free Trial added for Starter/Basic/Basic Plus plans" (Aug 3, 2026). Evoto has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
