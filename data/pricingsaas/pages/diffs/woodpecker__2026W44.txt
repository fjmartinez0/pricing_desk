What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Woodpecker's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing moved from a contacted-prospects slider (from $35/mo total) to fixed tiers: Email $39/mo, Multichannel $68/mo, LinkedIn $29/mo per account, and a custom-priced Scale plan. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 27, 2026) lists Email at $26, LinkedIn at $29, Multichannel at $55, Additional Lead Finder credits at $10 and Agency Panel at $27.
It lands 28 days after Woodpecker's previous change, "Woodpecker: free email verification broadened from catch-all-only to unlimited" (Sep 28, 2026). Woodpecker has been tracked since 2026-09-09.
In the same window Seamless.AI, lemlist, Apollo.io, RocketReach and Outreach also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
