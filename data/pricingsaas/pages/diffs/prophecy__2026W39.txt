What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Prophecy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise Express plan lost the '5,000 credits/year' allocation, and its Alteryx/Informatica workflow import allowance dropped from Unlimited to a 100 import limit (also reflected in the comparison table). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Professional at $150 and Enterprise Express at $4,000.
It lands 7 days after Prophecy's previous change, "Prophecy opens Alteryx workflow import to Professional, capped at 5/month" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Prophecy: Resource-credit allocations moved to plan cards, units changed" (Aug 24, 2026). Prophecy has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window MuleSoft, Dagster Labs and Talend also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
