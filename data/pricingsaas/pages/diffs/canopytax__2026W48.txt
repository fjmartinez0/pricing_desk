What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Canopy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Time & Billing product tab no longer carries a 'COMING SOON' badge, indicating the module is now available. In detail: Premium plan ($149/user/month billed annually) launched: the COMING SOON badge above the Premium card was removed, so the tier is now available alongside Standard and Plus. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan). The current page lists Standard at $74, Plus at $109, Premium at $149, Knowledge Based Authentication (KBA) at $1.25 per KBA credit and Tax Workflow Automation at $34 per client.
It lands 14 days after Canopy's previous change, "Canopy bundles Coworker AI into every plan, no add-on or price hike" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Canopy reworks Tax Workflow Automation bullets around AI intake" (Nov 1, 2026). Canopy has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Paycargo, Solvimon, Sage, QuickBooks and ProConnect also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
