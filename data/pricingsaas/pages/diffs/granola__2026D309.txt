What happened
Between the Nov 4, 2026 and Nov 5, 2026 captures of Granola AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: "MDM/Intune app wrapping (iOS)" was removed from the Trust & Control comparison table; it was previously an Enterprise-only included feature, now no longer listed at all. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Business at $14 and Enterprise at $35.
It lands 3 days after Granola AI's previous change, "Granola AI adds voice typing to every plan, including free Basic tier" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Granola AI adds voice typing to Basic, Business and Enterprise plans" (Oct 30, 2026) and "Granola AI opens Slack to all plans, reserves SSO and HIPAA for Enterprise" (Sep 28, 2026). Granola AI has been tracked since 2026-06-08.
In the same window Synthesia and Murf AI also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
