What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Akamai Technologies's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing page reorganized from region-based pricing (North America/Europe/Asia-Pacific) to product-based pricing (Compute, Storage, Databases, Networking); adds $100/instance/month Managed Service. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists NodeBalancers at $10, LKE High Availability at $60, Infrastructure Management at $100, LKE Enterprise at $300 and PostgreSQL at $16 per node/month.
It lands 97 days after Akamai Technologies's previous change, "Akamai Technologies strips all cloud pricing tables, shifts to region-specific subpages" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Akamai Technologies quadruples image storage limits to 100 accounts, 500 GB" (Jun 19, 2026) and "Akamai Technologies: Cloud Computing Calculator (self-service tool) replaced by 'Build the Right Cloud Plan for Your Needs'…" (Jun 2, 2026). Akamai Technologies has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
