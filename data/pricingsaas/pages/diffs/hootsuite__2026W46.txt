What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Hootsuite's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Fall Sale banner: 50% off the Standard plan (half price), with a Claim 50% off link; Standard list price remains $99/user/month. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $99 and Advanced at $249.
It lands 126 days after Hootsuite's previous change, "Hootsuite: Enterprise plan hides minimum seat requirement (5+ → Custom)" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hootsuite: adds a Professional tier, drops the 25% skip-trial discount" (Jun 29, 2026), "Hootsuite: New Professional plan added, trial terms overhauled" (Jul 3, 2026) and "Hootsuite reveals trial posting limits hidden behind asterisks" (Jun 1, 2026). Hootsuite has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
