What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Dnsfilter's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: DNSFilter added an MCP Connector to the Integrations section, letting an MCP-compatible AI assistant investigate DNS activity and manage the account using the customer's own DNSFilter credentials. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Core at $1 and Plus at $2.25.
It lands 35 days after Dnsfilter's previous change, "DNSFilter: Pro renamed to Plus, price hike; Enterprise price hidden" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Dnsfilter renames Basic plan to Core, raises retention across all tiers" (Jul 6, 2026). Dnsfilter has been tracked since 2023-12-07.
In the same window Cloudflare, Akamai Technologies, Zscaler and Barracuda Networks also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
