MindsDB pricing FAQ
How much does MindsDB cost?
MindsDB prices on usage: the Pay as you go plan is $0.10 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.50 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
Does MindsDB have a free plan?
No. MindsDB does not list a free tier.
What pricing model does MindsDB use?
MindsDB uses usage-based pricing across 1 plan.
When did MindsDB last change its pricing?
Nov 11, 2026: MindsDB lists Muse Ember 1.0 at $0.10 input / $0.50 output per 1M tokens.
How often has MindsDB changed its pricing?
Pulse has recorded 29 pricing changes for MindsDB since it started tracking on Mar 1, 2026; the pricing page was last checked on Nov 13, 2026.
What does “tracked since” mean?
It's the date of the first saved copy of the pricing page that Pulse checked for changes against a later copy. For a few companies we hold older copies that Pulse never checked for changes, and those don't change this date. If Pulse has never checked two copies, it's the date of the first saved copy.
