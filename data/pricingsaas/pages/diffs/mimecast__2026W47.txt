What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Mimecast's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Email security tiers renamed from Critical/Advanced/Premium to Core/Pro/Max under 'Email & Collaboration Security', shifting from one shared feature list to tier-differentiated features; a new standalone Data Governance product (Core/Pro/Max) was added to the plans page; Added a new 'AI Security' product card (Agent Risk Center) to the plans page: a single point to discover, monitor, and govern t. In detail: Introduced a new 'AI credits per month' usage allowance on the Data Protection product's Investigation Agent, scaling from 50 credits on Core to 100 on Pro to 150 on Max - a metric that did not exist on the previous plans page; Added a new 'AI Security' product card (Agent Risk Center) to the plans page: a single point to discover, monitor, and govern the agentic enterprise, deploying fast with no policies to configure or proxies to deploy. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, packaging change).
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Mimecast. Mimecast has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
