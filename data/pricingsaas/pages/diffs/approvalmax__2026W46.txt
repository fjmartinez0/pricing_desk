What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of ApprovalMax's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Xero pricing page moves from an organisation-count estimator to Small/Medium/Large document-volume sizes (100/300/500 docs/month) with overage rates, volume discount and 14-day free trial. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists ApprovalMax Capture at $13.20, ApprovalMax Pay at GBP 24.20 and Training Package at $2,100 per use.
It lands the same day as ApprovalMax's previous change, "ApprovalMax bundles Capture free, moves to document-volume tiers" (Nov 9, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ApprovalMax adds U.S. payments: pay bills in 35 currencies via ACH" (Nov 2, 2026), "ApprovalMax hides plan prices, adds Approval Intelligence AI agents" (Oct 19, 2026) and "ApprovalMax adds AI-powered Approval Agent and Search Agent to plans" (Oct 19, 2026).
