What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of QuickBooks' pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday sale raises 3-month intro discount from 50% to 70% off (ends 11/29); intro prices Simple Start $19→$11.40, Essentials $42.50→$25.50, Plus $70→$42, Advanced $170→$102. List prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Simple Start at $38, Essentials at $85, Plus at $140 and Advanced at $340.
It lands the same day as QuickBooks' previous change, "QuickBooks Payroll deepens Black Friday promo to 70% off, Core now $15/mo" (Nov 22, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "QuickBooks: new $0/mo Free plan promo added during plan selection" (Oct 19, 2026), "QuickBooks: Essentials, Plus & Advanced plan prices increased" (Sep 14, 2026) and "QuickBooks Payroll: Workforce bundle prices increase ($125→$135, $203→$228)" (Sep 14, 2026). QuickBooks has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Xero, Sage, Microsoft, Freshbooks and Zoho also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
