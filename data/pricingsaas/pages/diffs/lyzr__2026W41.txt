What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Lyzr's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Lyzr reframed its entire pricing page from a 'Three layers. One registry.' model (Govern / Build-and-orchestrate / Train) to a 'Modules and prices' model where customers 'buy one module or combine several'; agent draw-rate classes also moved from permission-based Class A/B/C to role-based Tier 1/2/3; Lyzr introduced a named free module, 'Build, test & experiment' (Agent Studio), explicitly brande. In detail: Lyzr introduced a named free module, 'Build, test & experiment' (Agent Studio), explicitly branded 'Free forever on the Community plan' — agents in development never draw agent-months. No equivalent named free module/plan existed before this week. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, freemium change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Take Agents to Production at $8,333.33.
It lands 7 days after Lyzr's previous change, "Lyzr swaps per-run fees for agent-month plans, adds $250K Opencontroller tier" (Sep 28, 2026). Lyzr has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window OpenAI, Cohere, Anthropic, Google and Mistral AI also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
