What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of ActiveCampaign's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Active Intelligence Credits cut in comparison table: Plus 500 to 50, Starter 500 to 10; Pro and Enterprise stay at 500. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered). The current page lists Starter (Email) at $15, Plus (Email) at $49, Pro (Email) at $79 and Enterprise (Email) at $145.
It lands 28 days after ActiveCampaign's previous change, "ActiveCampaign: Email/WhatsApp/Both channel selector removed from main pricing page; WhatsApp plans moved to a separate 'View…" (Oct 26, 2026). ActiveCampaign has been tracked since 2026-02-25.
In the same window Convertkit, HubSpot, Klaviyo, Salesforce and Mailchimp also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
