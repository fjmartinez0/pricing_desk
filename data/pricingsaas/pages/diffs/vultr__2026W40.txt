What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Vultr's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: NVIDIA A16 Cloud GPU 2-GPU tier included RAM reduced from 128GB to 120GB; storage, bandwidth, and price ($0.942/hr) unchanged. In detail: NVIDIA A16 Cloud GPU (2 GPU / 32GB GPU RAM / 12 vCPUs tier) system RAM allocation reduced from 128GB to 120GB; storage, bandwidth, and price ($0.942/hr) unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Load Balancers at $10, Global Load Balancers at $10, CDN at $10, Cloud Compute Regular Performance at $2.5 per month and Object Storage Archival at $6 per TB per month.
It lands 54 days after Vultr's previous change, "Vultr: new Audit Logs add-on ($0.50/GB ingestion + $0.01/GB/mo retention)" (Aug 5, 2026). Vultr has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Digitalocean, Google Cloud, OVHcloud and Scale also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
