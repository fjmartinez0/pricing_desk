What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Factory's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Introduced a Free tier ($0/mo per individual) for bring-your-own-inference use (sign in with ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, or OpenRouter, or connect a custom API key/endpoint). Pro dropped Cloud & local background agents, usage-stat tracking, and the readiness dashboard (gained Full model availability); Plus lost its rate-limit and Droid Computers bullets; Max lost its rate-limit and early-access bullets. In detail: Pro dropped 'Cloud & local background agents', usage-stat tracking, and the readiness dashboard bullets (gained 'Full model availability'); Plus lost its rate-limit and Droid Computers bullets; Max lost its rate-limit and early-access bullets; Factory introduced a Free tier ($0/mo per individual) for bring-your-own-inference use: sign in with ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, or OpenRouter, or connect a custom API key/endpoint. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, new plan). The current page lists Pro at $20, Teams at $60, Plus at $100 and Max at $200.
It lands 126 days after Factory's previous change, "Factory: Teams plan renamed to Business + admin feature tier changes" (Jul 13, 2026). Factory has been tracked since 2026-05-19.
