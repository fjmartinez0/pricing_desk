What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Hudu's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Hudu Radar add-on restructured from a flat $9/company/mo team-size calculator to tiered per-collector plans (Starter/Growth/Scale/Enterprise) starting at $44/mo, billed annually; Hudu expanded included features at $27/user/mo: SSO, MFA/Duo, audit logs, encryption, Webhooks, IP-restricted API keys, Knowledge base, Hudu Bridge, data export, mobile app, unlimited portal users. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Hudu at $27, Hudu Radar Starter at $44, Hudu Radar Growth at $89, Hudu Radar Scale at $269 and Hudu Radar Enterprise at $449.
It lands 77 days after Hudu's previous change, "Hudu rewrites plan feature list, adds SOC 2 Type II & ISO 27001 callout" (Jul 13, 2026). Hudu has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Logicmonitor and Zendesk also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
