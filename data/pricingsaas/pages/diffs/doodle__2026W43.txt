What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Doodle's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan Sign-up Sheets bullet changed from unspecified 'Basic Sign-up Sheets' to an explicit limit of '3 basic Sign-up Sheets'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $11 and Team at $16.
It lands 14 days after Doodle's previous change, "Doodle offers 15% off Pro yearly plans with code FALL15 until Oct 31" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Doodle: Enterprise plan adds PO and invoice billing" (Sep 28, 2026), "Doodle: Team plan billing switches from flat to per-seat" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Doodle: Team plan price increase (USD 9→16/seat)" (Aug 17, 2026).
