What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Axiom's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pre-purchase compute credit discount increased from up to 30% off (never expire) to up to 40% off; the plan comparison table dropped the 'never expire' guarantee on the credits. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Axiom Cloud at $25, Directory Sync at $100, Single Sign-On (SAML SSO) at $100, Role-Based Access Control at $50 and Audit Log at $50.
It lands 28 days after Axiom's previous change, "Axiom Cloud quietly drops BYOB S3 support, pulls it from comparisons and FAQ" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Axiom: ISO 27001 certification added" (Aug 3, 2026). Axiom has been tracked since 2026-04-24.
In the same window Elastic, Datadog, Splunk and New Relic also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
