What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of SumUp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday sale banner added: shop up to 45% off SumUp hardware catalogue. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Payments Plus at GBP 15.75, POS Plus at GBP 39, Business Account Plus at GBP 15 and Invoices Plus at GBP 8.
It lands 14 days after SumUp's previous change, "SumUp drops £100 hardware cashback banner, adds bookings to POS Plus" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SumUp drops Payments Plus 7-day free trial, pushes £3,000+ fee cuts" (Oct 12, 2026), "SumUp switches to yearly-first pricing: £189/year, save 17%" (Sep 7, 2026) and "SumUp hikes POS Plus add-on price from £29 to £39 per month" (Aug 17, 2026). SumUp has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
