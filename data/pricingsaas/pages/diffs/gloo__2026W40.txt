What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Gloo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Gloo Code Basic/Pro/Ultra plans all now list 'Every agent and model', replacing tier-gated model access wording; model catalog also grew from 142 to 160 models with Fugu Ultra v2 replacing GPT-5.5 in the showcase. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $20, Pro at $100 and Ultra at $200.
It lands 14 days after Gloo's previous change, "Gloo pulls Data Engine from Pro, waitlists it at capacity" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Gloo: Pro Data Engine access downgraded to Limited + 1 more change" (Aug 15, 2026). Gloo has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Zapier, Workato, Make, MuleSoft and Talend also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
