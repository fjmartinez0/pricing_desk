What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Nifty's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Guests & clients limit introduced: Pro Unlimited to 10, Business Unlimited to 30, Free capped at 1; Unlimited plan stays unlimited. Plan prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered). The current page lists Pro at $79, Business at $124 and Unlimited at $399.
It lands 63 days after Nifty's previous change, "Nifty adds flat team-size pricing, default view no longer per-member" (Sep 21, 2026). Nifty has been tracked since 2026-09-08.
