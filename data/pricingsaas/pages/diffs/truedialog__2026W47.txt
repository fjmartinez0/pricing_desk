What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of TrueDialog's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: RCS Agents no longer priced at $9.99/mo alongside Long Codes; line now reads "Long Codes $9.99/mo." and RCS pricing is by request; New TrueDialog MCP integration (send texts from ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude and other AI assistants) added to All Plans Include and the integrations grid. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden, feature added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Small Business at $119, Business Pro at $425, Enterprise at $900, Complete Plans at $1,000 and Vanity Short Codes at $1,499.
It lands 84 days after TrueDialog's previous change, "TrueDialog: WhatsApp channel dropped from all plans" (Aug 24, 2026). TrueDialog has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
In the same window Vonage, Bird, Google, Telnyx and Twilio also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
