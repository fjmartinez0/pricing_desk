What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Fastly's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Secondary DNS product section added to pricing page: managed authoritative secondary DNS, with contact-sales pricing (no public price). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $1,500, Starter at $6,000 and Usage-Based at $0.12 per GB bandwidth + $500 per mutual TLS domain.
It lands 7 days after Fastly's previous change, "Fastly launches Network Protect, always-on defense with no scrubbing centers" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fastly adds Network Protect: always-on defense, no scrubbing centers" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Fastly launches AI Platform: usage-based Runtime Control from $1.75" (Sep 28, 2026). Fastly has been tracked since 2023-12-09.
In the same window Akamai Technologies, Limelight Software, Google Cloud and Cloudflare also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
