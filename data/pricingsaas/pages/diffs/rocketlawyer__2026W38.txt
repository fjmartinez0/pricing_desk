What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Rocket Lawyer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The Trademark services add-on (Premium Legal Services table) increased from $350 and up to $400 and up for both Plus and Pro plan members. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Standard at $12.41, Plus at $20.75 and Pro at $29.08.

Before that Pulse recorded "Rocket Lawyer drops timed guarantees from Attorney consult and discount perks" (Jul 20, 2026). Rocket Lawyer has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
