What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Digitalocean's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Secrets Manager introduced at $0.40/secret-month + $0.05/10K retrieval requests, with 25 secret-months and 100K requests included free per month. In detail: Added Secrets Manager: new product for managing application secrets, priced at $0.40/secret-month and $0.05/10K retrieval requests, with 25 secret-months and 100K requests included free per month. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on). The current page lists Droplets at $4, Spaces Object Storage at $5, Volumes Block Storage at $10, Cloudways Managed Hosting at $11 and Kubernetes at $12.
It lands 49 days after Digitalocean's previous change, "Digitalocean launches AI Harness Runtime and Action Gateway pricing tiers" (Sep 27, 2026). Digitalocean has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
