What happened
Between the Nov 13, 2026 and Nov 14, 2026 captures of Retool's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI credits are no longer capped with no purchase option; paid plans can now buy additional AI credits as purchasable credit packs. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on). The current page lists Business at $6, Team at $10 and Additional Workflow Runs at $75 workflow runs.
It lands 43 days after Retool's previous change, "Retool ends $10k Enterprise AI credit offer, pools 10,000 credits monthly" (Oct 2, 2026). Retool has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window FlutterFlow, Microsoft, OutSystems, Airtable and Mendix also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
