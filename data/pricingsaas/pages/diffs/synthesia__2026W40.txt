What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Synthesia's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Basic plan's AI-generated video asset allowance cut from 25/month (a credit-redemption option) to an explicit 3/month cap, now shown as its own 'AI Video Assets' row in the feature comparison table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 22, 2026) lists Starter at $14 and Pro at $59.
It lands 20 days after Synthesia's previous change, "Synthesia decouples AI Dubbing, boosts Starter/Creator allowances up to 5x" (Sep 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Synthesia moves to credits, cuts free plan allowance by 58% to 500 a month" (Oct 2, 2026), "Synthesia quadruples AI Dubbing minutes on Starter and Creator plans" (Sep 4, 2026) and "Synthesia: New 'Roleplay Sessions' AI coaching feature launched" (Jul 27, 2026). Synthesia has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
