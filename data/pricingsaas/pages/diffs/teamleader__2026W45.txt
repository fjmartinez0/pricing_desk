What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Teamleader's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Focus AI Pro add-on: 2,000/6,000/15,000/30,000 Pro AI credits per month at EUR 20/35/75/150 (discounted from EUR 25/45/95/180), shared per account. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists SMART at EUR 25, GROW at EUR 33, FLOW at EUR 45, Project management Booster at EUR 35 and Planning Booster at EUR 35.
It lands 21 days after Teamleader's previous change, "Teamleader debuts Focus AI with 100-300 monthly credits per account" (Oct 12, 2026). Teamleader has been tracked since 2026-06-03.
