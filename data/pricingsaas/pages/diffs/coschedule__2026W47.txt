What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Coschedule's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: X/Twitter profiles no longer billed as a separate $16/profile/month add-on; now included in the base social profile allowance on Starter, Professional, and Suite plans. In detail: X/Twitter profiles are no longer billed as a separate $16/profile/month add-on; they are now included in the base social profile allowance on Starter, Professional, and Suite plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on removed). The current page lists Starter at $29 and Professional at $49.
It lands 21 days after Coschedule's previous change, "Coschedule drops unlimited seats and profiles from Suite plan, now custom" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Coschedule caps Professional Inbox at 9 networks, drops add-ons from table" (Oct 11, 2026) and "Coschedule ditches per-seat tiers for flat $29-$69 plans" (Sep 6, 2026). Coschedule has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
