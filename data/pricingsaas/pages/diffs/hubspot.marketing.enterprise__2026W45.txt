What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of HubSpot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Social media row condensed: Enterprise no longer lists the 3 competitor streams limit; both tiers drop the 3-year post scheduling horizon. 10K posts/mo and account counts unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Enterprise at $3,600.
It lands 42 days after HubSpot's previous change, "Hubspot Marketing Enterprise adds Content and Nurture AI agents in BETA" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hubspot Marketing Enterprise Marketing: seat pricing revealed ($45/$75/mo) + Pro/Ent feature swap" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Hubspot Marketing Enterprise Marketing: Additional Core Seat pricing hidden (Pro/Enterprise) + more" (Aug 10, 2026). HubSpot has been tracked since 2025-01-01.
In the same window Zoho, Microsoft, Salesforce, Pipedrive and ActiveCampaign also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
