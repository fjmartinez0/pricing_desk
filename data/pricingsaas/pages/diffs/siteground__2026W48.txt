What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Siteground's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added a bonus to the Black Friday hosting deal: 3 months free on site extras, on top of the existing up-to-88% off hosting discount.
