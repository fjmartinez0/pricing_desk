What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Vista Social's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Reporting group gains a Custom dashboards feature (10 to 11 features), available on Professional, Advanced, Scale and Enterprise. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Professional at $80, Advanced at $160, Scale at $360, Employee Advocacy Add-on at $199 and X (Twitter) Add-on at $29.
It lands 28 days after Vista Social's previous change, "Vista Social positions Enterprise for teams of 8+ users" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vista Social raises prices up to 34% and halves Scale plan user seats" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Vista Social: included users reduced across Professional/Advanced/Scale plans" (Aug 4, 2026). Vista Social has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Sproutsocial, Buffer and Sendible also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
