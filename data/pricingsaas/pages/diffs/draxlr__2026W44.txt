What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Draxlr's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Compare Plans table row renamed from 'Multi-Tenant' to 'Filters & Drill-downs'; FAQ confirms multi-tenant/row-level isolation for embedded customers now starts at Power, no longer claimed for Premium.
