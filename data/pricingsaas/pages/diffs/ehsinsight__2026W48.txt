What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of EHS Insight's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Standard Configuration's public '$5,000 per year to start' price replaced with 'Custom quote'; FAQ no longer states the $5,000/year starting price. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard Configuration at $416.67.
It lands 14 days after EHS Insight's previous change, "EHS Insight renames plans Standard and Advanced Configuration, keeps $5K price" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "EHS Insight drops "only in market" claim from its SMB mobile app bullet" (Oct 26, 2026). EHS Insight has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
