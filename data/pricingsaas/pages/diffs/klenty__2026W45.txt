What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Klenty's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Dynamic Presence add-on (NEW) at $50 per seat/month billed annually: 350+ local numbers per seat, auto-detection and matching of prospect area code, auto-rotation, 335 US area codes.
