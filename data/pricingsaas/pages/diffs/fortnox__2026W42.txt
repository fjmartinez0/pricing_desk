What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Fortnox's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Fortnox Access verification tiers reworked: 1-99 st (29 kr/mo) split into 0-39 st at 9 kr/mo and 40-99 st at 29 kr/mo; 100-999 (99 kr) and 1000+ (199 kr) unchanged. In detail: Fortnox Access verification tiers rebanded: 9 kr/mo tier widened from 0 st to 0–39 st, and 29 kr/mo tier narrowed from 1–99 st to 40–99 st (customers with 1–39 verifications drop from 29 to 9 kr/mo); 100–999 (99 kr) and 1000+ (199 kr) unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction.
