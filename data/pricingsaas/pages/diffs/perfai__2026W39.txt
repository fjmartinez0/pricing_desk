What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of PerfAI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Startup plan ($299/mo) now offers a 15-day free trial with no credit card required; the Startup CTA button text changed from 'Start Now' to 'Start Free Trial' to match. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pro at $49 and Growth at $299.
It lands 28 days after PerfAI's previous change, "PerfAI cuts Pro to $49 and Growth to $299, but slashes included credits up to 44%" (Aug 24, 2026). PerfAI has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window Cloudera, Alteryx and Databricks also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
