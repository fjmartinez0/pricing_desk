What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Appian's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Appian platform comparison table adds Smart Search to Advanced/Premium Data Fabric and a new Cloud Secure Link row for Advanced/Premium (up to 500 connections per customer); AI Copilot for Developers (Standard/Advanced/Premium) and AI Copilot for Business Users (Advanced/Premium) rows removed from the comparison table and replaced by a single AI-Powered User Experience row for Advanced/Premium onl. In detail: AI Copilot for Developers (Standard/Advanced/Premium) and AI Copilot for Business Users (Advanced/Premium) rows removed from the comparison table and replaced by a single AI-Powered User Experience row for Advanced/Premium only; Standard loses its AI Copilot ✔, partly offset by new footnote 10 (Composer sample data, test case generation and record-type summaries available on Standard). Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed, feature added) with a downward direction.
It lands 105 days after Appian's previous change, "Appian names Snowflake a premium-only integration in pricing update" (Jun 29, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Appian: Snowflake/Apache Kafka integrations gated to Advanced+Premium tiers" (Jul 2, 2026), "Appian quietly drops AI entitlement limits across 7 features" (May 18, 2026) and "Appian rebrands Agent Studio to AI Agents in feature comparison grid" (Apr 20, 2026). Appian has been tracked since 2023-12-02.
In the same window Salesforce, Microsoft, UiPath and Workato also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
