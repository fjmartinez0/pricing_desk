What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Infragistics's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Priority Support add-on (+$300/yr) no longer advertises phone support; now promises faster access to Infragistics engineers and in-depth troubleshooting. Price unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Slingshot at $12, Slingshot Enterprise at $20, Ignite UI at $99.08, Ultimate UI for Windows Forms at $108.25 and Ultimate UI for WPF at $108.25.
It lands 14 days after Infragistics's previous change, "Infragistics adds Theming MCP server to Ignite UI AI coding toolkit" (Oct 12, 2026). Infragistics has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Syncfusion also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
