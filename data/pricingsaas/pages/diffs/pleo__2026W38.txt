What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Pleo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Vendor card allowances shrank on the two upgraded mid/top tiers (25->15, 100->50) and the top tier's credit limit was cut from £250,000 to £100,000. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Start at GBP 8, Build at GBP 12, Optimise at GBP 16, Scale at GBP 249 and Lead at GBP 499.
It lands 56 days after Pleo's previous change, "Pleo: Beyond renamed Business (bespoke pricing) + Starter credit limit" (Jul 20, 2026). Pleo has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Ramp, Expensify and Coupa also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
