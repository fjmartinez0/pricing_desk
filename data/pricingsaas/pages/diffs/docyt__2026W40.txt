What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Docyt's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Added a detailed suite-by-suite capability comparison table (Accounting, Payments, Business Intelligence Suites), replacing the prior generic 'AI Agent' feature bullet lists with itemized per-suite capabilities; Every suite now explicitly guarantees 'unlimited users' at no extra cost, a limit disclosure that did not appear anywhere on the prior pricing page. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Accounting Suite at $299, Daily Flash (Standalone) at $100 and Catch-Up Accounting at $200 per month caught up.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Docyt. Docyt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
