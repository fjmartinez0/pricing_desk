What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Streak's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro, Pro+, and Enterprise plan taglines were rewritten with more specific capability language, and new copy was added clarifying billing is per-user (unlimited contacts/pipelines/records), not per-contact. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $49, Pro+ at $69, Enterprise at $129, AI Credits 25000 at $1,000 and AI Credits 1000 at $100.
It lands 21 days after Streak's previous change, "Streak: Advanced implementation add-on now requires annual plan or 3+ team" (Aug 31, 2026). Streak has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
In the same window Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive and Copper also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
