What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 13, 2026 captures of Cursor's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Plan lineup restructured: Hobby/Individual($20)/Teams($40) replaced by Free, Plus $8, Premium $30, Super $100, Ultra $200, Teams $30+/user, Enterprise custom; New X/Grok-oriented features listed: Post/reply/follow, Premium checkmark, fewer timeline ads, creator tools, Advanced Analytics, latest Grok models. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, package change) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Plus at $8, Premium at $30, Teams at $30, Super at $100 and Ultra at $200.
It lands 41 days after Cursor's previous change, "Cursor adds contextual taglines to plan cards, from tinkerers to enterprises" (Sep 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cursor: Composer dropped from 'Generous limits' benefit (Pro/Pro+/Ultra)" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Cursor adds plan taglines, swaps Hobby's third perk to Composer access" (Jul 27, 2026). Cursor has been tracked since 2026-05-19.
