What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Bettermode's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Starter plan price cut ~63%: from $399/month to $149/month billed yearly ($4,788/year → $1,788/year). Growth and Premium prices stay the same. Annual savings callout rose from 'Save up to 16%' to 'Save up to 25%'; Starter plan now includes 'Remove Powered by Bettermode' branding removal and 'API access (limited)', both previously exclusive to the Growth tier. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease, feature added) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Starter at $149 and Growth at $1,500.
It lands 21 days after Bettermode's previous change, "Bettermode: SSO feature expanded to add JWT support (Growth plan)" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Bettermode gates AI connector to 10 requests/day on Starter, unlimited on Growth" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Bettermode drops overage-fee caveat, adds no-CC and flexible billing perks" (Jul 27, 2026). Bettermode has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window Circle, Mighty Networks, Discourse and Discord also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
