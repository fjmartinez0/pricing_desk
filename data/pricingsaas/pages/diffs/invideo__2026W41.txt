What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of InVideo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Annual-billed Plus fell $50 to $36/seat/mo (17% to 40% off) and Max $100 to $75 (33% to 50% off); monthly list prices $60/$150 unchanged; Seedance 1080p credit cost rose (2.0: 38 to 72, 2.5: 50 to 72 credits/5s), cutting Plus/Max video counts (52 to 27 and 131 to 69; 40 to 27 and 100 to 69). Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit decrease, discount change) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Starter at $10, Plus at $30, Team at $47, Max at $75 and Scale at $107.
It lands 7 days after InVideo's previous change, "InVideo: 10x storage capacity increase + free guest seats added" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "InVideo redenominates credits 10x across all plans; prices and generation counts unchanged" (Sep 7, 2026). InVideo has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
