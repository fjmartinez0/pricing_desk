What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Pulley's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pulley added a site-wide notice that it is shutting down on 12/8/26, directing customers to an FAQ and support@pulley.com; no pricing or plan changes accompany the notice. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Startup at $100, Growth at $291.67, Distributions at $375, Token Cap Table at $375 and Valuations at $10,000 subscription.
It lands 252 days after Pulley's previous change, "Pulley Removes SSO Feature from Enterprise Plan, Impacting User Access and Security Options" (Jan 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pulley Updates Growth Plan: Corrects IRS Form Number from 3912 to 3921 for Incentive Stock Option Exercises" (Q3 2025), "Pulley Introduces New Liquidity Solutions and Custom Reporting Features in Scale Plan Amid Major Plan Renaming" (Q2 2025) and "Pulley Removes Phone Support from Custom Plan, Ending Key Feature for Users" (Q3 2024). Pulley has been tracked since 2023-11-20.
