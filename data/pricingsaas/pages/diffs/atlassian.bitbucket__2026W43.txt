What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Atlassian's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Bitbucket Premium price increased from $7.25 to $8 per user per month. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $3.65 and Premium at $8.
It lands 152 days after Atlassian's previous change, "Atlassian Bitbucket drops Dynamic CI/CD pipelines from Premium plan" (May 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Atlassian Bitbucket Introduces Unlimited Workspace Storage for Standard and Premium Plans, Enhancing Flexibility for Users" (Jan 11, 2026), "Atlassian Bitbucket Standard Plan Price Increased by 10.6% Amid Feature Democratization of AI Tools for All Tiers" (Q3 2025) and "Atlassian Bitbucket Reduces Premium Plan Uptime SLA from 99.99% to 99.9%" (Q2 2025). Atlassian has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window GitHub, Slack, Microsoft, GitLab and Asana also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
