What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 17, 2026 captures of OpenArt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Promotional discount raised on all 4 plans under a new Black Friday campaign — Starter 10%→25% ($13→$11/mo), Plus 20%→30% ($27→$24/mo), Pro 22%→35% ($44→$36/mo), Wonder 27%→40% ($175→$144/mo); list prices unchanged. In detail: Black Friday sale replaced the standard promo: discount rose from 10/20/22/27% to 25/30/35/40% on Starter/Plus/Pro/Wonder ($13→$11, $27→$24, $44→$36, $175→$144/mo); list prices unchanged, added 'Save $X on your first year' framing. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $13, Plus at $27, Pro at $44, Wonder at $175 and Business at $496.
It lands 6 days after OpenArt's previous change, "OpenArt extends up to 50% off promo to Seedream 5.5 on Pro, Wonder" (Nov 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenArt widens promo to 50% off Kling 4.0, GPT Image 2.6 and Recraft V4.1" (Nov 10, 2026), "OpenArt adds 30% off GPT Image 2.6 promo for Wonder and Pro until Nov 17" (Nov 5, 2026) and "OpenArt launches 50% off Kling 4.0 promo, drops Stories and models bullets" (Oct 26, 2026). OpenArt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Huggingface, Microsoft, Runway and Leonardo.ai also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
