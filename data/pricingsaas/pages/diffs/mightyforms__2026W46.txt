What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of MightyForms's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: FormBuilder (formerly MightyForms) cut the Free plan's monthly response allowance in half, from 100 responses/month to 50 responses/month. Pro (10,000) and Business (50,000) limits are unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $32.50 and HIPAA at $99.
It lands 63 days after MightyForms's previous change, "MightyForms rebrands to FormBuilder, scraps $29-$499 tiers for free plan" (Sep 7, 2026). MightyForms has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window HubSpot, Google and Formstack also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
