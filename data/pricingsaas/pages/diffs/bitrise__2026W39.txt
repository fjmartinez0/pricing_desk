What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Bitrise's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter plan capacity raised: concurrent builds 3→4, team members up to 10→Unlimited (role-based access), private apps 2→3, and Starter now gets up to 40 free AI credits (previously none). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $89, Pro at $200, AI Credits at $50, Bitrise Insights at $12.5 and Bitrise Build Cache at $30.
It lands 7 days after Bitrise's previous change, "Bitrise touts $30/mo Build Cache entry, adds free trials on Starter and Pro" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Bitrise: Linux 2S compute moved from Starter to Pro tier" (Aug 3, 2026) and "Bitrise: Pro plan zero-margin infra minutes increased to 3,000-24,000/mo" (Jul 2, 2026). Bitrise has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window GitLab also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
