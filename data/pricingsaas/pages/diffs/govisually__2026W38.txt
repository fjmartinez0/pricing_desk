What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of GoVisually's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: CPG Compliance AI Suite plan's visible starting price of $499 per month was removed and replaced with 'Tailored pricing / Built around your workflow', pushing prospects to book a demo instead of a published rate. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Lite at $16 and Pro at $33.
It lands 77 days after GoVisually's previous change, "GoVisually slashes CPG Compliance AI pricing 67% to $499/mo" (Jun 29, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "GoVisually: Compliance AI Suite price cut ($1,500→$499/mo)" (Jul 3, 2026). GoVisually has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Runway, Wistia, Descript, Cloudinary and Vimeo also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
