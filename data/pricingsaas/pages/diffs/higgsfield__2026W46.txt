What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Higgsfield AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Added new 'Higgsfield Mangaka' AI tool (free-gen allowance on Starter/Plus/Ultra) and a new 'Credit top-ups with discount' benefit across all plans. Plus plan parallel-generation cap removed — now 'Unlimited paid parallel generations' (was capped at 6 videos & 8 images, and previously excluded from the unlimited-parallel-gens perk). In detail: Plus plan's parallel-generation cap removed: now 'Unlimited paid parallel generations' (was capped at 6 videos & 8 images concurrently, and excluded from the unlimited-parallel-generations perk entirely before). Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $19, Plus at $47, Team at $65, Ultra at $99 and Scale at $150.
It lands 129 days after Higgsfield AI's previous change, "Higgsfield AI: Starter/Plus/Ultra prices raised, Supercomputer added" (Jul 3, 2026).
