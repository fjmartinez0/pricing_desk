What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of think-cell's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Plans and pricing' FAQ discloses 50-user volume prices for all four plans: AI Suite US$38.54, Suite US$20.60, AI Suite Plus US$89.60, Essentials US$6.64 per user/month, billed annually; 50+ users may get custom pricing. Card prices (1 user) unchanged at US$9.90/30.90/50.30/99.90. FAQ 1-user examples (US$8.90/28.60/46.54/97.60) sit below the card prices. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists thinkcell Essentials at $9.90, thinkcell Suite at $30.90, thinkcell AI Suite at $50.30 and thinkcell AI Suite Plus at $99.90.
It lands 42 days after think-cell's previous change, "think-cell launches AI Suite at $50.30/user, folds Assist add-on into new plans" (Oct 5, 2026). think-cell has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
