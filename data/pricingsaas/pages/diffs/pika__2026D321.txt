What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 17, 2026 captures of Pika's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Credit cost per generation changed for Nano Banana 2.1 (5->4/120->150/520->650/1,710+->2,137+) and GPT Image 2.6 (4->3/150->200/650->866/2,137+->2,850+) credits. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change). The current page lists Starter at $8, Creator at $28, Fancy at $76 and Credit Pack at $1 per 60 credits.
It lands one day after Pika's previous change, "Pika ends free daily H3 Flex generations for Creator and Fancy subscribers" (Nov 16, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pika launches Team plans: Creator $35/seat, Pro $95/seat with shared credits" (Nov 15, 2026), "Pika's GPT Image 2.6 doubles to 4 credits, halving monthly image limits" (Nov 13, 2026) and "Pika adds Nano Banana 2 at 7 credits, boosts top-up pack image counts" (Nov 7, 2026). Pika has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window OpenAI, Synthesia and Runway also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
