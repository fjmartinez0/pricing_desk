What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Leadpages' pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise plan changed from Custom pricing / Contact sales to From $399/mo with Talk to sales CTA; hero and meta now mention Enterprise from $399/mo; Enterprise plan bullets rewritten from SSO/custom contracts/dedicated support/volume publishing to full auto-optimization loop, cross-page learning, team seats + workspaces, audit logs, 25 integrations, Dedicated CSM. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature change, price revealed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $23, Business at $39, Grow at $79, Optimize at $159 and Enterprise at $319.
It lands 21 days after Leadpages' previous change, "Leadpages drops $29 Pro plan, raising entry price to $49/mo" (Oct 26, 2026). Leadpages has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Unbounce, Squarespace, Pagecloud and Convertkit also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
