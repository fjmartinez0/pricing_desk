What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Mailchimp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Premium plan comparison table gains a new 'Enterprise SSO' row marked New!, included in Premium and not in Standard, Essentials or Free. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $13, Standard at $20 and Premium at $297.5.
It lands 273 days after Mailchimp's previous change, "Mailchimp Commerce Removes Creative Assistant Feature from Websites Pricing Page" (Jan 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mailchimp Commerce Discontinues Plus Plan, Eliminating $29/month Subscription and Transaction Fees" (Dec 2023). Mailchimp has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Convertkit, HubSpot, Klaviyo and Constant Contact also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
