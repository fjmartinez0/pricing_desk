Framer makes free plan's 500 AI credits monthly, but FAQ still calls it a trial
Detected Nov 15, 2026, 8:59 PM ETSoftware Development & Testing
The changeFree tier freemium change
1 change2 capturesNov 9 → Nov 16, 2026 UTC
What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Framer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan's AI credit allowance label changed from one-time trial framing ('500 AI credits to try') to recurring monthly framing ('500 AI credits / month'), while the FAQ note still describes it as a one-time 500-credit trial. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change). The current page lists Basic at $10 and Pro at $30.
It lands 21 days after Framer's previous change, "Framer renames External Agents to MCP Server, free in preview" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Framer adds 6 Firewall rules to Advanced Hosting add-on alongside 6 rewrites" (Oct 2, 2026), "Framer discloses 50 firewall rules for Enterprise hosting plans" (Oct 1, 2026) and "Framer doubles AI credits for first month on Basic and Pro plans" (Sep 25, 2026). Framer has been tracked since 2026-03-19.
