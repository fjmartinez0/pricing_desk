What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Windsurf's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Teams plan seat cap changed from unlimited team members / unlimited flex seats to a hard cap of 'Up to 200 users', in both the plan card and the compare-plans table. Per-seat price ($40/mo per full user) unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $20, Teams at $80 and Max at $200.
It lands 77 days after Windsurf's previous change, "Windsurf upgrades Devin's bundled model to SWE 1.7 across banner and Free-tier" (Jul 13, 2026). Windsurf has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
