What happened
Between the Nov 23, 2026 and Nov 24, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Jev 1.13.0 decision model input price rose from $0 to $0.042 per 1M tokens; New models added to pricing catalog: Gemini 4 Neon ($3/$15), Kimi K3.1 Pro (MNMA) ($2/$10), Qwen3 Embedding 8B ($0.10 input). Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pay as you go at $0.10 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.50 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands 5 days after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB: added GPT-6.2 Astra & Sol models + new Image models category" (Nov 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB lists Muse Ember 1.0 at $0.10 input / $0.50 output per 1M tokens" (Nov 11, 2026) and "MindsDB adds Claude Haiku 5.5 at $0.10 input, $0.50 output per 1M tokens" (Nov 7, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window RapidMiner, Huggingface and Alteryx also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
