What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Pulumi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise usage-based unit pricing (managed resources, ESC secrets, API calls, on-demand resources, workflow minutes, Neo tokens) replaced with "Contact sales"; e.g. managed resource pricing went from "Starting at $0.00075/hour ($0.5475/month)" to a Contact Sales link. In detail: Enterprise edition's usage-based unit pricing (managed resources, ESC secrets, API calls, on-demand resources, workflow minutes, Pulumi Neo tokens) is no longer published; every per-unit rate in the comparison table now shows "Contact sales" instead of a dollar figure. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $40, Pro at $400, Standard Support at $625, Bronze Support at $1,250 and Silver Support at $2,916.67.
It lands 14 days after Pulumi's previous change, "Pulumi: Silver and Gold support plan prices hidden behind Contact sales" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pulumi: Enterprise plan pricing revealed: 'Custom / Contact sales' replaced with self-serve $2,000/month base (2,000…" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Pulumi renames all plan tiers, adds Support tiers up to $50K/yr Platinum" (Sep 21, 2026). Pulumi has been tracked since 2026-05-02.
In the same window Google Cloud, Aws, Hashicorp and Puppet also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
