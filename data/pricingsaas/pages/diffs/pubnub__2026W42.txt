What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of PubNub's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Serverless Functions added to plan lists: Free gets 5 test functions with 7-day runtime; Starter gets 30 Serverless Functions. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $98.
It lands 14 days after PubNub's previous change, "PubNub axes Serverless Functions from Free and Starter plan features" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "PubNub: Serverless Functions added to Free & Starter plans" (Sep 7, 2026) and "PubNub adds free Serverless Functions, boosts Starter to 30 functions" (Jul 20, 2026). PubNub has been tracked since 2026-05-13.
