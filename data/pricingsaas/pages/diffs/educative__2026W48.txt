What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Educative's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Annual prices up: Standard $139 to $149, Premium $189 to $199, Premium Plus $239 to $249; list prices raised and discount badge 50% to 55% off. Matches known Standard/Premium/Plus A/B experiment; AI Breakout Sessions now listed under Standard instead of Premium; AI-Assisted Coding Interviews (New) added to Premium Plus, with AI Assisted Coding link added to footer; Premium Plus AI Mock Interview. In detail: Premium Plus AI Mock Interviews per month raised 7 to 10; Cloud Labs count shown as 320+ versus 330+; Annual prices up $10 on every plan: Standard $139 to $149 ($12 to $13/mo), Premium $189 to $199 ($16 to $17/mo), Premium Plus $239 to $249. Strike-through list prices raised ($278/$378/$478 to $331/$442/$553) and badge 50% to 55% off. Reverses the W47 $10 cut; the "Up to 55% off Early Bird" banner is gone, so the early-bird pricing ended. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit raised, feature added, price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $13, Premium at $17 and Premium Plus at $21.
It lands 7 days after Educative's previous change, "Educative cuts annual prices $10 on every plan, adds new interview prep tools" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Educative cuts annual prices across all plans: Standard now $12/mo, Premium…" (Sep 27, 2026) and "Educative hikes Standard, Premium prices; course library grows to 2200+" (Sep 20, 2026). Educative has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Play Digital Signage, Codecademy and Udemy also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
