What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of ClaritySoft CRM's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Two plans (Core $29, Complete $59) became three (Core $29, Advanced $49, Max $69, annual); Complete removed. New paid add-ons: Quick Start Onboarding from $799 one-time, Clarity Engage from $125/mo. In detail: Two plans (Core $29, Complete $59 per user/month billed annually) became three (Core $29, Advanced $49, Max $69); Complete removed. Monthly billing added ($39/$65/$89). New Quick Start Onboarding add-on from $799 one-time; existing Clarity Engage email marketing now shows prices (from $125/mo, previously blank calculator). Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change). The current page lists Core at $29, Advanced at $49, Max at $69 and Clarity Engage at $125.
