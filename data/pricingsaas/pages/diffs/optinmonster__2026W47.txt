What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of OptinMonster's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday offer (up to 60% off first year) replaces 40% intro offer; promo prices Plus $27→$24, Pro $37→$29, Growth $67→$49/mo; Basic $9 and renewal prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $9, Plus at $27, Pro at $37 and Growth at $67.
It lands 14 days after OptinMonster's previous change, "OptinMonster adds AI-powered MCP campaign tool and 500-impression free trial" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OptinMonster raises promo prices up to 38% as discount shrinks to 40%" (Oct 4, 2026). OptinMonster has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Leadpages, Unbounce, ActiveCampaign and Convertkit also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
