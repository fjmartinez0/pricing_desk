What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Vizly's pricing page, Pulse recorded 7 changes: Message-based plans (Basic $19.99, Premium $29.99) replaced by a credit-based lineup: Researcher $29, Professional $79, Team $249/mo (5 seats, $39 per extra seat), with per-plan credit add-ons. Enterprise stays custom; the monthly/yearly billing toggle is gone; Basic plan ($19.99/mo, up to 200 messages per month) removed; Premium plan ($29.99/mo, unlimited messages) removed; New Researcher plan. In detail: New Researcher plan at $29/mo with 1M credits/month, 1 GB file storage and a private workspace; Yearly billing discount (-17%) and the 25% student/educator discount offer were removed; New Team plan at $249/mo including 5 seats, $39 per additional seat/month, 10M credits/month per seat, shared Spaces and permission controls; New Professional plan at $79/mo with 10M credits/month, 10 GB cloud + 1 TB connected storage and multiple Spaces. Pulse files it as a pricing change (package change, new plan, discount removed, plan removed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Researcher at $29, Professional at $79, Team at $249 and Credit Top-up at $10 subscription + $40 subscription.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Vizly. Vizly has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
