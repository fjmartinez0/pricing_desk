What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Regie.ai's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: New per-seat Team plan launched at $125/$150 per seat/mo; Pro's annual price ($41) is now shown directly next to the $49 monthly rate instead of behind a toggle; Enterprise dropped its team-workspace bullet; Free plan capacity expanded from a single user to up to 10 people sharing one credit pool; credits now go per person (never expiring) instead of per account; Pro's 60,000/yr annual allotment. In detail: Free plan capacity expanded from a single user to up to 10 people sharing one credit pool; credits now go per person (never expiring) instead of per account; Pro's 60,000/yr annual allotment now disclosed; Dialer is no longer listed among Free plan's features; Pro's bullets now promote 'the power dialer, with local numbers you pick' as a paid-tier feature, replacing a credit-refresh assurance bullet. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change, feature removed, new plan) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $41 and Team at $125.
It lands 35 days after Regie.ai's previous change, "Regie.ai: relaunches as RegieGO with Free/$49 Pro plans, drops $180-$499/user legacy pricing" (Aug 24, 2026). Regie.ai has been tracked since 2026-05-02.
In the same window Intercom, HubSpot, Outreach, Salesloft and Drift also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
