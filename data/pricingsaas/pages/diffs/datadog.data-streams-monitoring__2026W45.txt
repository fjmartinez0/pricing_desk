What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Datadog's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Kafka Console (Data Streams Monitoring) no longer lists Confluent Cloud as supported; new FAQ says Kafka Console pricing does not apply to Confluent Cloud and it is not billed. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Data Streams Monitoring - AWS Fargate at $2.90 and Data Streams Monitoring at $15.
It lands 28 days after Datadog's previous change, "Datadog Data Streams Monitoring unveils Kafka Console pricing from $100/broker host" (Oct 5, 2026). Datadog has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Sumologic, Dynatrace, Elastic and Grafana also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
