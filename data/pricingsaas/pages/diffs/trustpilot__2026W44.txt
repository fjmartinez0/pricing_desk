What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Trustpilot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Marketing integrations in the comparison table dropped from 22 to 19 on Plus, Premium and Enterprise plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $99, Plus at $319 and Premium at $799.
It lands 28 days after Trustpilot's previous change, "Trustpilot: Enterprise widgets 22→23 + QR/multi-domain features added" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Trustpilot Business adds QR Code Review Collector and multi-domain tools" (Sep 28, 2026), "Trustpilot tightens Starter plan eligibility to new small businesses only" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Trustpilot Business tightens Starter plan eligibility to new small businesses" (Aug 17, 2026). Trustpilot has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
