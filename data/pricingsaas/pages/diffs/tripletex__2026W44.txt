What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Tripletex's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan cards rewritten with new audience taglines and bullet lists (Basis now lists Assistenten and Gratis support). Prices unchanged: 199/299/479/649 kr/mnd. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basis at NOK 199, Smart at NOK 299, Pro at NOK 479, Komplett at NOK 649 and VVS at NOK 699.
It lands 15 days after Tripletex's previous change, "Tripletex renames AI-assistenten to Assistenten in package comparison" (Oct 12, 2026). Tripletex has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
