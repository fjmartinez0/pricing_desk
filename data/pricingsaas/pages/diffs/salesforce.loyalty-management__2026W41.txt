What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Salesforce's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Agentforce 1 Editions renamed Agentforce Max Editions; availability changed from Field Service and Operations to Sales/Service; from $550/user/month unchanged; Agentforce Max Editions included Flex Credits increased from 2.5M to 2.75M per org per year. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, plan renamed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $20,000, Growth at $35,000, Advanced at $45,000 and Tableau CRM for Loyalty Management at $165.
It lands 174 days after Salesforce's previous change, "Salesforce Loyalty Management rebrands AppExchange to AgentExchange" (Apr 14, 2026). Salesforce has been tracked since 2025-01-03.
