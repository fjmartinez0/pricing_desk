Tatsoft ends zero-fee edition upgrades, now requires active maintenance
Detected Nov 15, 2026, 11:22 PM ETBusiness Operations
The changePackaging change
2 changes2 capturesNov 9 → Nov 16, 2026 UTC
What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Tatsoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Electrical Package add-on (IEC 61850 and IEC 60870-5 drivers, USD 6,000 over the base license) removed from the editions pricing note; Edition upgrades now require active maintenance (was 'ZERO fees when upgrading'); Standard Support (20% annually, business hours) became Premium Support and Maintenance (20%/yr, 16%/yr for 3 years, 14%/yr for 5), now covering all version upgrades, with defect fixe. In detail: Edition upgrades now require active maintenance (was 'ZERO fees when upgrading'); Standard Support (20% annually, business hours) became Premium Support and Maintenance (20%/yr, 16%/yr for 3 years, 14%/yr for 5), now covering all version upgrades, with defect fixes and security patches free to every license holder. Hot-standby note now names Enterprise and MachineHMI. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change, add-on removed). The current page lists EdgeConnect Gateway at $20, EdgeConnect View at $20, MachineHMI 150 at $20, MachineHMI 500 at $20 and MachineHMI 1500 at $20.
It lands 7 days after Tatsoft's previous change, "Tatsoft adds $6,000 Electrical Package and unifies hot-standby redundancy" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Tatsoft makes Community Edition free for production use up to 1,000 tags" (Oct 26, 2026), "Tatsoft: Included connectors count raised from 100+ to 120+ across hero text and EdgeConnect card" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Tatsoft: Enterprise tiers gain unlimited clients" (Aug 31, 2026). Tatsoft has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
In the same window Microsoft and Salesforce also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
