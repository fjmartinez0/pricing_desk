What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of ClickTime's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan cards now show per-user prices: Starter $12/mo annual ($15 monthly, up to 25 users), Team $15 ($19 monthly, up to 200 users), Premier $27 ($31 monthly, up to 200 users), Enterprise Custom; before, cards showed only taglines. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $12 and Expense Tracking at $4.
It lands 35 days after ClickTime's previous change, "ClickTime adds Approvals to Team plan, drops Report Studio from Premier" (Oct 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ClickTime drops billing toggle, discount badges for flat $12/user starter price" (Oct 4, 2026). ClickTime has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Zoho, Asana and QuickBooks also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
