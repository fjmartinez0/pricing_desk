What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Meet Alfred's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Plans renamed: Individual → LinkedIn Only, Business → Multi-Channel, Teams → Agency; LinkedIn Only (was Individual) annual-billed price up $35 → $39/seat/mo ($425 → $468/yr); Agency (was Teams) up $47 → $59/seat/mo (3 seats $141 → $177/mo, $1,707 → $2,124/yr); list prices $59/$79 unchanged; Multi-Channel (was Business) annual-billed price down $59 → $49/seat/mo ($713 → $588/yr); strikethrough list. In detail: Annual-billing discount cut from ~40% to 25-34% vs list ($35/$59, $59/$99, $47/$79 → $39/$59, $49/$69, $59/$79); -40% Annual and -20% Quarterly toggle badges removed; "Save 40%" → "Best value"; Multi-Channel (was Business) annual-billed price down $59 → $49/seat/mo ($713 → $588/yr); strikethrough list price $99 → $69. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change, price increase, plan renamed, price decrease) with a neutral direction. The current page lists LinkedIn Only at $39, Multi-Channel at $49 and Agency at $59.
It lands 35 days after Meet Alfred's previous change, "Meet Alfred raises annual prices up to $59, cuts discount to 40%" (Oct 5, 2026). Meet Alfred has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
