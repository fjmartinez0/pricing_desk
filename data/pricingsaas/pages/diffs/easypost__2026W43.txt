What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of EasyPost's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: EasyPost GlobalShip renamed to EasyPost Pro — same scalable shipping platform offering for growing shippers, no pricing change. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists EasyPost Suite at $20, EasyPost Nexus at $20, EasyPost Tracking API at $0.01 per shipment, EasyPost Advanced Tracking at $0.03 per shipment and EasyPost Insurance and Claims API at 1% of insured shipment value.
It lands 98 days after EasyPost's previous change, "EasyPost launches Nexus: free carrier labels or BYOCA at $20/mo" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "EasyPost: insurance/tracking price hikes, Luma pricing hidden, Enterprise cut" (Jul 2, 2026). EasyPost has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Shippo also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
