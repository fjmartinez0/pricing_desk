What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Vista Social's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Professional, Advanced and Scale prices increased on both billing terms: monthly $79/$149/$349 became $99/$199/$449 (+25%/+34%/+29%), and yearly $758/$1,430/$3,638 became $950/$1,910/$4,310 (card rates $64/$120/$304 to $80/$160/$360 per month billed annually); Included user seats cut on Advanced (4 to 3 users) and Scale (8 to 4 users) plans while included social profile counts stayed the same, al. In detail: Included user seats cut on Advanced (4 to 3 users) and Scale (8 to 4 users) plans while included social profile counts stayed the same, alongside the price increase. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, limit decrease) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Professional at $99, Advanced at $160, Scale at $360, Employee Advocacy Add-on at $199 and X (Twitter) Add-on at $29.
It lands 55 days after Vista Social's previous change, "Vista Social: included users reduced across Professional/Advanced/Scale plans" (Aug 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vista Social merges AI credits, slashes Scale from Unlimited to 3,500/mo" (Jul 2, 2026) and "Vista Social: Prices removed from plan cards; AI credit quotas cut" (Jul 4, 2026). Vista Social has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
