What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of PandaSuite's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Multi plan tagline reworded from multi-device app to native mobile+desktop app or up to 5 projects in one app; publishing copy now says one plan per application, not per device. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at EUR 290, Web at EUR 790, Single at EUR 990, Multi at EUR 1,690 and Full at EUR 1,990.
It lands 98 days after PandaSuite's previous change, "PandaSuite: pricing page restructured around an Editor Plans grid" (Jul 13, 2026). PandaSuite has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
