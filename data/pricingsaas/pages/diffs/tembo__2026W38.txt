What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Tembo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan's included workspace user allowance was cut from up to 3 users to just 1 user; Pro (5), Max (10), and all other plan terms are unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Pro at $60 and Max at $200.
It lands 40 days after Tembo's previous change, "Tembo: Credits billing replaced with dollar usage allowance" (Aug 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Tembo: Pro/Max credits cut 40-50%; new sandbox size + RAM specs disclosed" (Jul 28, 2026). Tembo has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Supabase, Neon, PlanetScale, Render and Railway also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
