What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 10, 2026 captures of Fireflies.ai's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan 'HIPAA compliance' relabeled 'HIPAA-ready (Enterprise, with BAA)', narrowing the claim to Enterprise with a signed BAA; prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $10, Business at $19 and Enterprise at $39.
It lands 15 days after Fireflies.ai's previous change, "Fireflies.ai: Enterprise security badges updated: added ISO 42001 certification, expanded HIPAA Compliant into HIPAA &…" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fireflies.ai adds ISO 42001 AI certification, expands HIPAA to cover FERPA" (Oct 22, 2026), "Fireflies.ai publishes per-plan API rate limits, from 30/min on Free to pooled per-seat limits" (Oct 12, 2026) and "Fireflies.ai publishes API rate limits: 30/min up to pooled 2,400/min" (Oct 9, 2026). Fireflies.ai has been tracked since 2026-05-19.
