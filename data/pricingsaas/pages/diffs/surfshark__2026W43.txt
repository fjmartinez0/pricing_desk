What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Surfshark's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday sale live: One cut to $2.49/mo (was $2.79; 27-mo total $67.23 vs $75.33), 'Most popular' badge swapped for 'Black Friday deal'. One+ cut to $3.99/mo (was $4.49). Starter's Save% badge rose 85->87%. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Surfshark Starter at $2.49, Surfshark One at $2.49 and Surfshark One+ at $3.99.
It lands 42 days after Surfshark's previous change, "Surfshark One price rises to $2.79/mo as intro discount narrows to 85%" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Surfshark cuts One plan to $2.49/mo, swaps bundled Search for Scam protection" (Aug 24, 2026). Surfshark has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window ExpressVPN, CyberGhost VPN and NordVPN also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
