What happened
Between the Nov 6, 2026 and Nov 7, 2026 captures of OpenAI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan taglines reworded: Free, Go, Plus and Pro 'Best for...' copy replaced with new benefit-style taglines; no prices or features changed. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 11, 2026) lists Go at $8, Plus at $20, Business at $20 and Pro at $100.
It lands 8 days after OpenAI's previous change, "OpenAI: Pro plan adds up to $500/month in API credits" (Oct 30, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenAI rewrites Free, Go, Plus and Pro taglines in "Best for" format" (Oct 26, 2026), "OpenAI: Plan taglines reworded to 'Best for...' format: Free (trying out ChatGPT), Go (longer conversations), Plus…" (Oct 18, 2026) and "OpenAI rewrites Free, Go, Plus and Pro taglines in "Best for" style" (Oct 11, 2026). OpenAI has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
