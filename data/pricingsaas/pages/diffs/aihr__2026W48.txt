What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of AIHR's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AIHR launched a 25% off Black Friday promotion (expires 2026-11-28): Certificate Program drops from $1,125 to $845 and Full Academy Access annual plan drops from $1,850 to $1,387, with a new countdown banner. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Full Academy Access at $123.33, Team License at $124.58 and Certificate Program at $1,125 per use.
It lands 49 days after AIHR's previous change, "AIHR ends 20% Academy discount, annual price jumps from $1,480 to $1,850" (Oct 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "AIHR slashes Full Academy Access 20% to $1,480 in limited-time deal" (Sep 27, 2026), "AIHR ends 20% promo: Full Academy Access back to $1,480 → $1,850/year" (Sep 20, 2026) and "AIHR: Full Academy Access sale price rose from $1,387 to $1,480 per year (list price still shown crossed-out at…" (Sep 6, 2026). AIHR has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Paychex also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
