GanttPRO adds MCP server to all plans
Detected Nov 22, 2026, 8:27 PM ETProject Management
The changeCore, Advanced, Business feature added
1 change2 capturesNov 16 → Nov 23, 2026 UTC
What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of GanttPRO's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: MCP server for AI assistants (read-only access to projects, tasks and team via API key) added to all four plans (Core, Advanced, Business, Enterprise): new comparison-table row and 'New: MCP server included in all plans' banner, which replaces the AI Gantt Chart Maker banner. AI Gantt Chart Maker itself remains included in all plans. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Core at $8, Advanced at $12, Business at $19 and Enterprise at $25.
It lands 111 days after GanttPRO's previous change, "GanttPRO: Enterprise plan swaps onboarding/support for training/conversion" (Aug 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "GanttPRO Slashes Enterprise Plan 29% to $25/User/Month" (Mar 31, 2026). GanttPRO has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Microsoft, Smartsheet and Asana also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
