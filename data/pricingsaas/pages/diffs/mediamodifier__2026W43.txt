What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Mediamodifier's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free Starter plan removed; pricing page now lists only Professional and Team, and Pro no longer says "Everything in Starter"; Professional yearly price rose from $12.50 to $15.83/mo ($150 to $190/yr); FAQ monthly price changed from $25 to $19/mo. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, plan removed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Professional at $15.83, Team at $37.5 and Extra Team Member at $7.5.
It lands 91 days after Mediamodifier's previous change, "Mediamodifier: Professional plan price increase ($12.50→$15.83/mo)" (Jul 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mediamodifier: Professional plan template cap removed, now unlimited" (Jul 3, 2026) and "Mediamodifier drops 7-day access option, pushes free-to-Pro path" (May 11, 2026). Mediamodifier has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
