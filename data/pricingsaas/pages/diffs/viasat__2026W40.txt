What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Viasat's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Viasat's Contract options section now offers a 24-month minimum service term option instead of the prior 12-month minimum, alongside the existing month-to-month no-commitment option. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Essentials at $69.99, Unleashed at $99.99 and Unleashed Max at $179.99.
It lands 70 days after Viasat's previous change, "Viasat: New CyberProtect add-on + $300 Mastercard promo extended" (Jul 20, 2026). Viasat has been tracked since 2026-06-23.
