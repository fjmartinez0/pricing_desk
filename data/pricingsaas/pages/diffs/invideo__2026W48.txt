What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of InVideo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Max list price rose $150→$250/seat/mo ($75→$125 annual) with 8,000 credits; Black Friday promo up to 75% off (pay 12 months get 24) makes Max $62.50/seat/mo for 2 years; Plus/Starter 1-year terms; Max credits increased 5,000→8,000/mo (Nano Banana Pro 2,000→3,200; Seedance 2 videos 131→210); slider options 5,000/10,000/15,000 → 5,000/8,000/10,000. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit raised, price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $10, Plus at $30, Team at $47, Max at $75 and Scale at $107.
It lands 7 days after InVideo's previous change, "InVideo halves Max annual price to $75/seat but cuts credits 8,000 to 5,000" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "InVideo: Max plan price jumps $75→$250/seat as discount ends" (Nov 1, 2026), "InVideo halves annual Starter to $10/seat, cuts Seedance video allowances" (Oct 11, 2026) and "InVideo cuts annual Plus and Max prices up to 25% as Seedance credits jump" (Oct 4, 2026). InVideo has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
