What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Countingup's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Tax pot variable interest rate increased from 1.2% to 1.45% on deposits into the tax pot. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Tier 1 (£0-£750 deposits) at GBP 5, Tier 2 (£750-£7,500 deposits) at GBP 12 and Tier 3 (Unlimited deposits) at GBP 20.
It lands 42 days after Countingup's previous change, "Countingup drops Direct Debits teaser, adds accountant data sharing" (Sep 28, 2026). Countingup has been tracked since 2025-01-01.
