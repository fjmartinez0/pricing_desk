What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of TechSmith's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Camtasia plan cards relabeled to match the unchanged compare table: Starter card drops 'Camtasia Editor (watermarked)' from Products Included (table still lists watermarked Editor for Starter), Create drops a redundant Multi-language bullet (included from Essentials), Audiate tagged limited / limited plus AI voices / full by tier, and Pro's Screencast and Assets renamed Screencast Pro and Premium.
