What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Pictory's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Allowances moved from video minutes per year plus AI credits to a single AI credit pool (24k/72k/216k credits, shown with approx. mins/yr); Team plan renamed Teams; list prices unchanged; AI video agent added to all plan feature lists, with 'Agentic video workflows now in Pictory 3' banner. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, package change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $25, Professional at $35 and Teams at $119.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Pictory. Pictory has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
