What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of GetProspect's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Included phone-number and email-verification allowances plus prepaid verification packs ($29-$2,499) replaced by a cash-back wallet (Free $1, Starter $10, Growth $20) spent pay-as-you-go at $0.12/phone and $0.80/1,000 verifications, with tiered top-up bonuses (+20% to +50%). New "Rate limits for API and AI agents" section discloses per-plan Search/Enrich request limits (Free 5/20/100; Starter 10/200/1000 & 10/300/2000; Growth 20/400/4000 & 20/600/8000). In detail: Replaced included phone-number/verification allowances and prepaid verification packs with a cash-back wallet: plans credit a wallet (Free $1, Starter $10, Growth $20) spent pay-as-you-go at $0.12/phone number and $0.80/1,000 verifications; old bulk verification packs ($29-$2,499) and the free phone-credit add-on were removed, replaced by tiered top-up bonuses (+20% to +50%); Added a new 'Rate limits for API and AI agents' disclosure showing per-plan Search/Enrich request limits (Free 5/20/100; Starter 10/200/1000 search, 10/300/2000 enrich; Growth 20/400/4000 search, 20/600/8000 enrich) - no equivalent section existed before. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $34 and Growth at $69.
