What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of CyberFortress's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Entire self-serve Product Pricing Calculator (BaaS $53/TB, DRaaS $80/TB, M365 $3.50-$36/user, Endpoint $10/device) was removed; page now shows only a 'Talk to a recovery specialist' Contact Sales form. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Backup & Recovery For Microsoft 365 at $3, Endpoint Backup & Recovery at $10, Managed Cloud Backup as a Service, Powered by Veeam (BaaS Pro Plus) at $53, Managed Disaster Recovery as a Service, Powered by Veeam (DRaaS) at $80 and Email Archiving at $5.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for CyberFortress. CyberFortress has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
