What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of OtterlyAI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: ChatGPT Ads Tracking dropped from plan cards; Brand Sentiment, Citation Source Tracking, Brand Profiling, Query Fan Out, Share of Voice and Crawlability/Content audits now listed; Multi-country support (50+) replaced by Unlimited Countries & Languages; Unlimited Brands & Competitors added across Lite, Standard, Premium, Enterprise. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Lite at $25, Standard at $160, Premium at $422, Enterprise at $1,000 and Extra Search Prompts Add-on at $85.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for OtterlyAI. OtterlyAI has been tracked since 2026-09-10.
