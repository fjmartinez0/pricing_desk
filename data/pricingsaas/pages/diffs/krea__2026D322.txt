What happened
Between the Nov 17, 2026 and Nov 18, 2026 captures of Krea's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday 70% off yearly promo: Basic 40%, Pro 50%, Max 70% off; Business effective $160 to $96/mo; compute top-up packs show $4.99-$176.99 with up to 29% off. In detail: Black Friday promo with countdown: yearly discount 40%→70% off, monthly 25% off. Effective yearly prices: Max $63→$42/mo at 60K units ($81→$54 at 80K), Business $160→$96/mo; Basic $5 and Pro $21 unchanged (Pro 50% badge comes from strikethrough list raised $35→$42). Pro/Max get 7-day Unlimited Nano Banana generations. Compute top-up packs now priced $4.99–$176.99 with up to 29% off. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a downward direction. The current page lists Basic at $5, Pro at $21, Max at $63 and Business at $160.
It lands 2 days after Krea's previous change, "Krea dangles a welcome discount on first plans for new free sign-ups" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Krea cuts Nano Banana 2.1 image estimates ~11% on every plan as cards switch to quoting 2.1" (Nov 7, 2026), "Krea cuts LoRA training from its Free plan as paid prices hold steady" (Nov 1, 2026) and "Krea ends 14-day unlimited Krea 3 Fast promo for annual Pro and Max buyers" (Oct 23, 2026). Krea has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Microsoft, Runway and Leonardo.ai also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.

Questions about this change
Did Krea raise prices in November 2026?
No — prices went down, through the discount. Between Nov 17, 2026 and Nov 18, 2026 the annual discount went from 40% to 70% (+30 pts), so Krea's pricing page showed 2 lower prices: Max: $63 → $42 (−33%); Business: $160 → $96 (−40%).

Annual discount (all plans): 40% → 70% (+30 pts); Max price: $63 → $42 (−33%); Business price: $160 → $96 (−40%). Pulse compared the captures it took on Nov 17, 2026 and Nov 18, 2026; the before/after table above lists each item as written on the page.
