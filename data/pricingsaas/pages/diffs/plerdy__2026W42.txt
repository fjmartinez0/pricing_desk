What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Plerdy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Connectors (MCP) added to plans: connect Plerdy to Claude/ChatGPT with daily AI request quotas from 100/day (Startup) up to 5,000/day (Enterprise 3); read-only access. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Startup at $21, Scale at $42, Thrive at $70, Enterprise 1 at $97 and Enterprise 2 at $161.
It lands 91 days after Plerdy's previous change, "Plerdy kills AI UX Assistant, launches Error Monitoring across all plans" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Plerdy: AI UX Assistant per-page add-on pricing removed" (Jul 4, 2026). Plerdy has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
