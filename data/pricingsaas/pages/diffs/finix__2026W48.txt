What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Finix's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Per Two-Day ACH Transaction fee row added to ACH pricing: 1.20% + $0.25 on Standard; Custom via Contact us. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on). The current page lists Standard at 0.3% above interchange + $0.15 card processing per transaction (interchange plus).
It lands 27 days after Finix's previous change, "Finix doubles 3D Secure fee to $0.10, adds $0.15 Name Verification fee" (Oct 26, 2026). Finix has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
