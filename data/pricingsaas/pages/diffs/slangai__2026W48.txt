What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Slang AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pricing hero headline changed to 'Slang AI Pricing and Plans for Restaurants' and added copy: every plan priced per location, no hidden fees, cancel anytime. Pulse files it as a pricing change (positioning, discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $399, Premium at $599, Bilingual Support at $99 and Private Events at $199.
