What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Jobber's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The time-limited 'Save up to 40%, Offer ends October 30th' promo (which added a 3-month introductory discount step on top of annual billing) expired; banner now reads 'Save up to 25% with annual billing' and intro-rate step is gone from Core, Connect, and Grow pricing cards. Steady-state prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $24, Connect at $149, Grow at $299, Plus at $529 and Marketing Suite at $79.
It lands 7 days after Jobber's previous change, "Jobber Teams: New Oct promo 'Save up to 40%, offer ends October 30th' replaces standard 'Save up to 25% with annual…" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Jobber Teams ends 40% annual promo, cuts discount to 25% and drops intro rates" (Oct 5, 2026), "Jobber Teams: Annual discount jumps from 25% to 40% via new intro pricing" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Jobber Teams: New intro-rate promo, up to 40% off (was 25%), ends Aug 31" (Aug 24, 2026). Jobber has been tracked since 2026-02-06.
In the same window Skedulo, OpenTable, Guesty, WellnessLiving and Treatwell also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
