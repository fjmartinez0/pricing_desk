What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of SimInsights's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Audience segmentation tabs restructured: 'Higher Ed' split into 'Community College' and 'University'; new 'Workforce Board' tab added alongside K-12 and Enterprise, per updated FAQ copy. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Individual at $4.17, Classroom at $11.67, Enterprise Pro at $12.5, Education Essentials at $208.33 and Workforce Board Basic at $291.67.
It lands 7 days after SimInsights's previous change, "SimInsights folds teacher and homeschool plans into a single 5-tier K12 ladder" (Sep 14, 2026). SimInsights has been tracked since 2026-07-16.
In the same window Tableau, Alteryx and Sisense also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
