What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Magnific's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: "Save 40% on Credits" Seedream 5.5 promotional banner removed from the pricing page; the banner itself advertised an end date of Dec 10 but was gone by Nov 23. In detail: Removed 'Save 40% on Credits' Seedream 5.5 promotional banner (individual subscriptions only; banner stated it would run through Dec 10 but was gone by Nov 23). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Premium at $14.50, Premium+ at $33.75, Business at $55, Pro Starter at $82.50 and Pro at $210.
It lands 27 days after Magnific's previous change, "Magnific: Premium annual 50% off promo (ends Oct 26): $14.50 to $7.25/mo billed annually, list $20; annual toggle…" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Magnific launches Magnific One image model, unlimited on paid plans to Oct 15" (Oct 11, 2026), "Magnific Premium annual price doubles to $14.50/mo as 50% promo ends" (Oct 4, 2026) and "Magnific slashes Premium annual price 50% to $7.25/mo before Sept 28 deadline" (Sep 28, 2026). Magnific has been tracked since 2026-06-04.
