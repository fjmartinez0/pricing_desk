What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Plain's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: New purchasable credit top-up packs: +1,000 credits $10, +2,000 $20, +5,000 $50, +10,000 $100; Foundation credits no longer 500/month on monthly billing (now 1,000/month); Workflows limit raised from Up to 5 to Unlimited; HIPAA added to Frontier; External AI agent (bring your own) replaced by Connected Agent at 15 credits/assignment+discussion; Labels, Teams, Account owners, Slack Enterprise Grid. In detail: HIPAA added to Frontier; External AI agent (bring your own, Frontier only) replaced by Connected Agent at 15 credits/assignment+discussion on all plans; Suggested responses added to Foundation; Labels, Teams, Account owners, Slack Enterprise Grid rows and Discourse channel removed; Foundation Business hours limited to 1 schedule; Foundation credits no longer 500/month on monthly billing (now 1,000/month); Workflows limit raised from Up to 5 to Unlimited; Customer card data freshness 12h cache to 15s. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, new add-on, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Foundation at $45 and Horizon at $99.
It lands 7 days after Plain's previous change, "Plain swaps seat bundles for $45 and $99 per-user pricing, cuts AI credits" (Oct 5, 2026). Plain has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Stripe and Squarespace also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
