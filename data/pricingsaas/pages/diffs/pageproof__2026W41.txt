What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of PageProof's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added PageProof MCP integration (connect to an AI assistant) to the PageProof Intelligence feature set across Team, Team Plus, and Enterprise; also renamed the 'Folders' organizing feature to 'Folders and subfolders' to reflect new subfolder support. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Team at $249 and Team Plus at $333.25.
It lands 28 days after PageProof's previous change, "PageProof touts scheduled proofs on Team Plus, digital signing on Enterprise" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "PageProof Team Plus tier jumps 25% to $499/month, annual scales too" (Aug 17, 2026). PageProof has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Workato, Docusign and Adobe also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
