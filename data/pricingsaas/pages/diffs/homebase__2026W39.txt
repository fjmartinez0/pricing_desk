What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Homebase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New limited-time '6 Months Free: Payroll & Plus Plan' promo added (Plus shown at $0/mo, Payroll first 6 months free); Payroll add-on base fee rose from $39 to $49/month (add-on card and FAQ). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Essentials at $24, Plus at $56, All-in-One at $96, Background Checks at $30 subscription and Tip Manager at $25.
It lands 7 days after Homebase's previous change, "Homebase: Job post boosts add-on price cut $79→$50" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Homebase Hiring Assistant exits Beta, ditches per-post fee for $99/mo flat rate" (Sep 7, 2026), "Homebase: Payroll add-on fee decreased ($49→$39/mo)" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Homebase pulls "3 months free payroll" promo banner as offer expires" (Jul 20, 2026).
