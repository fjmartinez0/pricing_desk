What happened
Between the Nov 17, 2026 and Nov 18, 2026 captures of Mintlify's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Starter plan added at $150/mo billed annually ($180 monthly) with 10 editors, Agent, Autopilot, preview deployments and 5,000 credits/mo; Pro now builds on Starter; Pro card now lists Password protection and Advanced analytics to differentiate it from the new Starter tier; both were already Pro features in the comparison table (password auth, platform/agent analytics). Pulse files it as a packaging change (positioning, new plan). The current page lists Starter at $150 and Pro at $450.
It lands one day after Mintlify's previous change, "Mintlify renames Starter plan to Free, keeps $0 price and 5 editors" (Nov 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mintlify renames Automations to Autopilot, adds Enterprise-only Automations" (Nov 5, 2026), "Mintlify swaps free credits for a 14-day Pro trial with 5,000 credits" (Oct 13, 2026) and "Mintlify ends free 6-month Pro offer for early-stage startups" (Oct 3, 2026). Mintlify has been tracked since 2024-10-21.
In the same window Read AI, Slite, Postman, Notion and GitBook also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
