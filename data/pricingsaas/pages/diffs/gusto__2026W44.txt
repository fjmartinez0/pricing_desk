What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Gusto's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Health insurance broker integration reworded (add your broker for plans Gusto can't support directly); life/disability now stated at no cost to employer, employees pay premiums. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Simple at $6, Solo at $6, Plus at $80, Premium at $180 and Tax-Advantaged Benefits at $200 per year.
It lands 14 days after Gusto's previous change, "Gusto launches HR Partners add-on with fractional compliance support" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Gusto drops Contractor Only plan, adds $85 Business Compliance add-on" (Oct 5, 2026), "Gusto launches limited-time free Contractor Only plan for 1099-only teams" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Gusto re-gates custom admin roles to Premium, bundles Next-Day pay into Plus and Premium" (Sep 7, 2026). Gusto has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Paychex and BambooHR also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
