What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Foxpass's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: BYOD certificate installer removed from Foxpass Cloud PKI (Standalone): dropped from its enrollment methods (SCEP, CSR upload, manual creation remain) and its comparison-table dot cleared. Advanced license still includes it. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Foxpass Cloud PKI (Standalone) at $0.50.
It lands 230 days after Foxpass's previous change, "Foxpass Adds Device Posture-Based Access Control to Advanced Plan" (Mar 17, 2026). Foxpass has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
