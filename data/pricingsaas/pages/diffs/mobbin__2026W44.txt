What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Mobbin's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Explore Finance+ add-on now covers over 80 apps across Banking, Crypto, and Investment, up from over 78 apps; price unchanged at $399/mo billed yearly. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $10, Team at $16 and Finance+ at $399.
It lands 15 days after Mobbin's previous change, "Mobbin adds AI credits to Pro, doubles them for Team at 600" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mobbin expands Finance add-on to 73+ apps, keeps $399/mo price" (Sep 28, 2026), "Mobbin Finance+ add-on now unlocks 71+ apps, up from 68+" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Mobbin Finance+ add-on expands to 68+ apps, price holds at $399/mo" (Sep 14, 2026). Mobbin has been tracked since 2026-02-05.
