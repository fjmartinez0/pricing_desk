What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Dreamhost's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The 'Free Handcrafted Starter Website' perk was dropped from the Launch, Growth, and Scale plan feature lists, though the FAQ still describes it as an included benefit. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Web Hosting Launch at $10.99, VPS Stack 4 at $11.99, Web Hosting Growth at $12.99, VPS Stack 8 at $16.99 and DreamPress 1 at $19.99.
It lands 80 days after Dreamhost's previous change, "Remixer AI Website Builder now free (was $1.99/mo)" (Jul 2, 2026). Dreamhost has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Hostgator, Kinsta and Siteground also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
