What happened
Between the Nov 10, 2026 and Nov 11, 2026 captures of Devin's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Team plan user cap reduced from up to 200 users to up to 100 users. Pro, Max and Team gained "Use your ChatGPT plan"; Team added multiplayer sessions, SAML/OIDC SSO, 3-tier cost controls and Slack channel support (dropped admin dashboard analytics and priority support); Enterprise swapped SAML/OIDC SSO for SCIM and role-based access control. In detail: Pro/Max/Team gained use-your-ChatGPT-plan. Team added multiplayer sessions, SSO, cost-tier controls, Slack support; dropped admin dashboard & priority support. Enterprise swapped SSO for SCIM role-based access; Team plan user cap reduced from up to 200 users to up to 100 users (plan card and comparison table). Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, limit lowered). The current page lists Pro at $20, Teams at $40 and Max at $200.
It lands 48 days after Devin's previous change, "Devin caps Teams plan at 200 members, ending unlimited seats" (Sep 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Devin adds SpaceXAI to Devin Pro frontier model lineup" (Sep 17, 2026) and "Devin swaps default model to SWE-2, ditching SWE 1.7 platform-wide" (Sep 14, 2026). Devin has been tracked since 2026-06-26.
