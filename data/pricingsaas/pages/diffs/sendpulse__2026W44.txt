What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of SendPulse's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan chat support now limited to 30 days from registration; paid plans list chat support with no time limits; Dedicated support bullet removed from a plan card. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $9.6, Pro at $11.52, Standard - Pay As You Go at $48 subscription, Pro - Pay As You Go at $57.6 subscription and Enterprise - Pay As You Go at $80.64 subscription.
It lands 125 days after SendPulse's previous change, "SendPulse cuts new-user discount to first month only" (Jun 23, 2026). SendPulse has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
