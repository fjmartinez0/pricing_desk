What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Zoom's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Conference Room Connector' add-on introduced for Workplace plans, enabling standards-based SIP/H.323 conferencing endpoints for hybrid events, priced at $49/month billed monthly. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Webinars at $83.33, Webinars Plus at $290.83, Events at $415.83, Additional Hub Host at $54.17 and Additional Co-Editor at $54.17.
It lands 168 days after Zoom's previous change, "Zoom Events adds $49/month Conference Room Connector for hybrid work" (Apr 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Zoom Events Introduces $65/month Add-ons for Events and Webinars Plus Customers" (Q3 2025), "Zoom Events Unveils Annual Subscription Pricing for Events at $1,490/User, Marking Significant Increase in Webinar and Session Costs" (Q2 2025) and "Zoom Events Webinars Plan Sees Significant 17% Price Drop to $79/month" (Q1 2025). Zoom has been tracked since 2023-11-15.
In the same window Microsoft, Google, GoTo and Ringcentral also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
