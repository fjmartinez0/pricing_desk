What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Trellus' pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Vague Standard/High dialing limits replaced by explicit calling allowances: about 1,300 US minutes/seat/week on Power and 2,000 on Power + Parallel (2,000/3,000 credits; shared, reset Mondays). Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Power at $99.99 and Power + Parallel at $149.99.
It lands 21 days after Trellus' previous change, "Trellus relabels LinkedIn inbox and parallel dialing features; prices hold" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Trellus renames Parallel plan to Power + Parallel, price unchanged at $149.99/mo" (Sep 21, 2026). Trellus has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window Reply, Regie.ai, LeadMagic, Village and UJET also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
