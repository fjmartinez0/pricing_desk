What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of RudderStack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Lookout product added with its own plans priced on activated records: Free $0 (3 activations/yr, 5,000 records/sync), Growth from $1,000/mo billed annually ($12,000/yr, 40K activated records/yr), Enterprise Custom. RudderStack Core plans (Free $0, Growth $265/mo, Enterprise) unchanged, now behind a product selector. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan). The current page lists Growth at $225.
It lands 42 days after RudderStack's previous change, "RudderStack adds Event Blocking and Bot Management to pricing tiers" (Oct 4, 2026). RudderStack has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
In the same window Mixpanel and Amplitude also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
