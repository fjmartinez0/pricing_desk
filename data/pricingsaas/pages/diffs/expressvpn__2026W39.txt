What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of ExpressVPN's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: ExpressAI accessible models increased from 6 to 7 on Express Pro. ExpressKeys 'Attach files to vault items' added (5GB Advanced, 10GB Express Pro). Identity Defender 'Caller Identification' row added (Express Pro). In detail: Added new 'Caller Identification' row to the Identity Defender feature comparison table; Added ExpressKeys capability 'Attach files to vault items' (5GB Advanced, 10GB Express Pro); ExpressAI add-on: number of accessible AI models increased from 6 to 7 on the Express Pro plan. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Basic at $3.99, Advanced at $4.49 and Express Pro at $7.49.
It lands 7 days after ExpressVPN's previous change, "ExpressVPN cuts Pro to $5.99/mo and Advanced to $3.99/mo on 2-year deals" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ExpressVPN swaps app-icon row for 2Yr/1Yr/1Mo billing tabs, 80% off" (Aug 31, 2026), "ExpressVPN: Basic plan price cut $3.49->$2.99/mo + 2 more changes" (Aug 3, 2026) and "ExpressVPN hikes 2-year promo pricing up to $2/mo, trims discounts to 67-76%" (Jul 27, 2026). ExpressVPN has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
