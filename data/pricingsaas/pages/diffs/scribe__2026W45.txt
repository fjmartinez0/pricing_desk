What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Scribe's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro Team per-seat price (Starts at 5 seats) dropped from $23 to $13 per seat per month. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro Team at $13 and Pro Personal at $25.
It lands 7 days after Scribe's previous change, "Scribe adds import for existing process docs on Pro and Enterprise plans" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Scribe raises Pro Team price 77% to $23 per seat, 5-seat minimum" (Oct 12, 2026) and "Scribe adds Autocapture AI to Pro Team, auto-documenting work without recording" (Oct 5, 2026). Scribe has been tracked since 2026-03-18.
