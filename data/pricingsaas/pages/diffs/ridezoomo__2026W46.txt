What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Zoomo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added a Black Friday promotional ticker banner offering €500 off Cargo and up to €1,000 off MATE e-bike purchases, with a countdown timer and a 'Voir l'offre' link to a dedicated deal page. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Lite at EUR 70, Pro at EUR 150, Pro Plus at EUR 160, Damage Protection at EUR 20 and Extra Battery at EUR 40.
It lands the same day as Zoomo's previous change, "Zoomo launches Black Friday sale: up to €1,000 off MATE bikes" (Nov 9, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Zoomo extends add-on battery to 12h a day, trims included range to 6h" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Zoomo extends battery add-on to 12 hours a day, cuts base to 6" (Oct 19, 2026). Zoomo has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Bolt, Hive and Bird also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
