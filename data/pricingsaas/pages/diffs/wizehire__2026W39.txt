What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of WizeHire's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Wizehire Coaches renamed to Wizehire Coaching Support; Quickstart (1 strategy session) and Growth (2 strategy sessions/month) lost their explicit session counts, now shown as a plain checkmark - only Concierge keeps a distinct Custom designation. In detail: Wizehire Coaches renamed to Wizehire Coaching Support; explicit per-tier session quantities (Quickstart: 1 strategy session, Growth: 2 strategy sessions/month) replaced with a generic checkmark - only Concierge still shows a distinct "Custom" designation. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Quickstart at $209, Growth at $449, Additional jobs at $99, Additional locations at $349 and Reactivations at $249.
It lands 35 days after WizeHire's previous change, "WizeHire drops Essentials tier as pricing shakeup hikes add-ons, Growth cost" (Aug 17, 2026). WizeHire has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
