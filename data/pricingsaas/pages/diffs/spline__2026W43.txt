What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Spline's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Explicit daily MCP tool call limits now listed per plan: Free 20, Hobby 100, Pro 200, Max 400 (previously Max only 'Higher MCP tool call limits'). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Hobby at $12, Pro at $25 and Max at $60.
It lands 42 days after Spline's previous change, "Spline splits AI tools: 'AI Agent' added to 3D + 2D editors, Remove/Upscale Image new" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Spline hikes Pro to $25 but slashes Max to $60/seat monthly" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Spline adds Max tier, renames Starter/Pro, bundles AI credits into plans" (Aug 17, 2026). Spline has been tracked since 2026-03-19.
In the same window Figma, Framer and Webflow also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
