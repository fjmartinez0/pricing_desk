What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of ChartMogul's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing restructured: new Free (up to $10K MRR) and Starter ($57/mo annual) plans added; Pro now shown as $99/mo billed annually (was $1,188/yr); monthly/annual toggle with 'save 17%' added. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $57, Pro at $99, Enterprise at $1,658.33 and CRM Pro Seat at $39.
It lands 94 days after ChartMogul's previous change, "ChartMogul: New "Starter" plan launched at $59/mo" (Jul 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ChartMogul adds Starter tier bridging Free and Pro plans" (Apr 27, 2026), "ChartMogul rebrands Saved Dashboards as Team Dashboards across all plans" (Apr 13, 2026) and "ChartMogul cuts free plan billing sources from three to one" (Apr 11, 2026).
