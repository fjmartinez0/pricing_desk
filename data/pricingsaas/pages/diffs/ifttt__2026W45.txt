What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Ifttt's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Moved to credit-based tiers: Starter ($2.09/mo) added; Pro $2.99 to $5.49/mo; Pro+ $8.99 to $13.99/mo; Free now 1,000 credits/mo; annual discount 40% to 30%; Free plan now includes all features unlocked, all services and integrations; Pro+ adds multiple accounts per service and dedicated support; compare-all-features table added; Billing metric shifted to monthly credits (Free 1,000; Starter 5,0. In detail: Billing metric shifted to monthly credits (Free 1,000; Starter 5,000; Pro 20,000; Pro+ 100,000); Applet caps (2 Free, 20 Pro) replaced by unlimited Applets; Free plan now includes all features unlocked, unlimited Applets, and all services & integrations (was 2 Applets, standard speeds, simple no-code integrations); Pro+ support upgraded from prioritized to dedicated; compare-all-features table added. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change, freemium change, package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $2.09, Pro at $5.49 and Pro+ at $13.99.
It lands 212 days after Ifttt's previous change, "Ifttt boosts annual savings discount from 25% to 40% on all paid plans" (Apr 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Ifttt Boosts Annual Billing Discount to 40%, Up From 25%" (Week 4, 2026), "Ifttt Pro Plan Monthly Price Increased by 20% to $2.99, Annual Price Up 20% to $35.88" (Q2 2025) and "Ifttt Pro+ Plan Annual Price Cut by 32% to $101.88, Major Savings for Users!" (Q1 2025). Ifttt has been tracked since 2024-11-01.
