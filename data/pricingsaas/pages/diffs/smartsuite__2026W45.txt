SmartSuite relabels Enterprise EU residency row as Support-run region move
Verified Nov 2, 2026·Project Management·Direction: neutral
The changeEnterprise positioning — positioning.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 26, 2026 → Nov 2, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of SmartSuite's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise card row 'European Data Residency' relabeled 'Region move by Support'. Entitlement unchanged: EU/US data-center choice at sign-up was already on every plan (BEFORE FAQ), and moving an existing workspace US→EU stays Enterprise/Signature-only; the move is now described as performed by Support on request instead of 'Contact sales to arrange a move'. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Team at $15, Professional at $32 and Additional storage at $10 per additional 100GB storage block.
It lands 21 days after SmartSuite's previous change, "SmartSuite removes four permission and view features from the Team plan and adds a 16th language" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SmartSuite launches Hosted MCP Server, now generally available for AI clients" (Sep 28, 2026) and "SmartSuite adds Folder / View, Table and Field Permissions to Team and drops its nonprofit coupon" (Aug 31, 2026). SmartSuite has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Notion, ClickUp, Microsoft, Asana and Airtable also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
