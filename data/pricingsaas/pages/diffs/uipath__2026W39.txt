What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of UiPath's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Basic plan tagline changed from targeting individuals/small teams to promoting essential team automations with business-grade support and reliability. Basic is evolving notice added and self-serve Try It Now trial button removed from Basic plan, leaving only direct Buy Now purchase. In detail: UiPath changed the Basic plan's tagline from targeting individuals/small teams to promoting essential team automations with business-grade support and reliability; UiPath added a 'Basic is evolving — an updated plan offering is coming soon' notice to the Basic card and removed its self-serve free-trial button, leaving only direct Buy Now purchase. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning, feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Basic at $25.
UiPath's previous recorded change was "UiPath Restructures Pricing Tiers, Removes Free and Pro Plans, Introduces Basic Plan at $25/Month" (Q2 2025). Before that Pulse recorded "UiPath Renames Automation Express to Autopilot Express, Marking a Significant Shift in Product Branding" (Q1 2025) and "UiPath Transforms Pro Plan: All Key Features Now Paid Add-Ons, Including Attended and Unattended Robots" (Q4 2024). UiPath has been tracked since 2025-01-01.
