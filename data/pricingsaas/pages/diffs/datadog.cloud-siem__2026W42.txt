What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Datadog's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Cloud SIEM security signal retention extended from 12 to 15 months at no additional cost; security logs remain indexed for 12 months. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Cloud SIEM at $5 and Cloud SIEM On-Demand at $7.5.
It lands 7 days after Datadog's previous change, "Datadog Cloud Siem makes workflows free when triggered by security findings" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Datadog Cloud Siem: free investigation allotment metric changed to per-TB" (Jul 27, 2026). Datadog has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Dynatrace, Elastic, Grafana, Sumologic and Splunk also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
