What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Handsontable's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: HyperFormula no longer offered free under open-source GPLv3; now commercial-only, with evaluation via trial license on request from Sales instead of open-source start. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change).
It lands 14 days after Handsontable's previous change, "Handsontable switches DataGrid licensing from per-developer to end-user pricing" (Nov 1, 2026). Handsontable has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Syncfusion and DAT Freight & Analytics also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
