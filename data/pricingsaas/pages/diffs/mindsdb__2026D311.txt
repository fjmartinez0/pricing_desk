What happened
Between the Nov 6, 2026 and Nov 7, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Claude Haiku 5.5 model added to rate card at $0.10 input / $0.50 output / $0.01 cached / $0.125 cache write per 1M tokens; Haiku 4.5 ($1.00/$5.00) kept. In detail: New Claude Haiku 5.5 model added to rate card at $0.10 input / $0.50 output / $0.01 cached / $0.125 cache write per 1M tokens (100K+ token prompts: $0.50/$2.50/$0.05/$0.625); an unversioned "Claude Haiku" alias row at the same rates (no long-prompt surcharge) also added. Haiku 4.5 ($1.00/$5.00) kept unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pay as you go at $0.10 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.50 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands 5 days after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB adds Kimi K3.1 Pro, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, GPT-6.1 Terra pricing" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB adds GPT-6.1 Terra pricing at $1 input, $5 output per 1M tokens" (Oct 29, 2026), "MindsDB adds Kimi K3.1 Pro and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model pricing" (Oct 27, 2026) and "MindsDB adds Gemini 4 Argon and Meta Muse Wonder 1.0 token pricing" (Oct 19, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Huggingface and Alteryx also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
