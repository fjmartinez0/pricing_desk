What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of ApprovalMax's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Standard ($49.50/mo), Advanced ($79.20/mo) and Premium ($111.00/mo) monthly prices removed from the pricing cards, replaced by 'Start a free trial' CTAs only. New 'Approval Intelligence' category added with Approval Agent (flags supplier-history discrepancies before approval, Advanced/Premium) and Search Agent (natural language search, all plans). In detail: New 'Approval Intelligence' category added to the feature comparison table, introducing 'Approval Agent' (checks bills against supplier history and flags discrepancies before approval, Advanced/Premium) and 'Search Agent' (natural language search, all plans); Standard ($49.50/mo), Advanced ($79.20/mo) and Premium ($111.00/mo) per-organisation monthly prices removed from the pricing cards; only plan names and 'Start a free trial' CTAs remain visible. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price hidden) with an upward direction. The current page lists ApprovalMax Capture at $13.2, ApprovalMax Pay at GBP 24.2 and Training Package at $2,100 subscription.
It lands the same day as ApprovalMax's previous change, "ApprovalMax adds AI-powered Approval Agent and Search Agent to plans" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ApprovalMax: Advanced $79.17→$79.20 and Premium $110.92→$111.00 per month" (Oct 12, 2026). ApprovalMax has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
In the same window Ramp, Paychex, Coupa and Expensify also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
