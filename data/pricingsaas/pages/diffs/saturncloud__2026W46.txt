What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Saturn Cloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New dedicated NVIDIA GPU capacity offering with public reference prices per GPU-hour: L40S from $1.10, RTX PRO 6000 $1.25, H100 $3.15, H200 $3.80, B200 $5.95, B300 $6.65; GB300 NVL72 priced per deal.
