Mixmax swaps tiered plans for one all-features plan priced by recipients
Verified Oct 19, 2026·Sales & Customer Relationship Management (CRM)·Direction: neutral
The changePackaging — package change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 12, 2026 → Oct 19, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Mixmax's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Copilot-based lineup (Free/Inbox $29/Engagement $49/Suite $89) replaced by one all-features plan priced by monthly recipients (Free 50, $29/100, $89/1,500, $129/3,000); add-ons now $9/member/mo. In detail: Copilot lineup (Free/Inbox $29/Engagement $49/Suite $89, Teams custom) replaced by one all-features plan priced by monthly recipients (Free 50, $29/100, $89/1,500, $129/3,000); +1,500/+5,000/+15,000 recipient packs dropped; Dialer, Advanced rules and Salesforce Insights (were Teams-only) now $9/member/mo add-ons alongside Custom Branding. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Mixmax Plan at $29, Custom Branding Add-On at $9, Advanced Workspace Rules Add-On at $9, Salesforce Insights Add-On at $9 and Dialer Add-On at $9.
It lands 98 days after Mixmax's previous change, "Mixmax: Enterprise sequence limit capped at 3,000/month (was unlimited)" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mixmax Revamps Copilot Messaging to Highlight Specific Feature Benefits" (Apr 1, 2026), "Mixmax rebrands Bundle as Suite, renames Teams offering" (Apr 3, 2026) and "Mixmax Drops Monthly Billing Option, Pushes Annual-Only Pricing" (Mar 17, 2026). Mixmax has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Salesforce, Pipedrive, HubSpot and Outreach also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
