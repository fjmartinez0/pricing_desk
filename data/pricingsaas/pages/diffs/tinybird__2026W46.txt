What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Tinybird's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: SaaS plan included storage reduced from 500 GB to 250 GB (card and comparison table 'Starts at' value). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Developer at $25, Enterprise at $58 per TB of storage and SaaS at $58 per TB of storage + $0.20 per 1,000 seconds of vCPU overage.
It lands 63 days after Tinybird's previous change, "Tinybird aligns support tiers: 'Enterprise support' becomes 'Standard or Premier" (Sep 7, 2026). Tinybird has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Datadog, ClickHouse and Elastic also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
