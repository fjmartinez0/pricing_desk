What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Mosaic's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pricing restructured: consolidated from 3 tiers (Starter, Professional, Enterprise) to 2 tiers (Standard, Enterprise); every plan now includes every feature, tiers differ only by onboarding/support level. Add-on added: optional Chello Pro AI add-on introduced at $50/user/month, billed month-to-month. In detail: Mosaic consolidated from 3 tiers (Starter, Professional, Enterprise) to 2 tiers (Standard, Enterprise); every plan now includes every feature, with the tiers differing only by level of onboarding and support rather than feature access; Introduced a new optional 'Chello Pro' AI add-on at $50/user/month, billed month-to-month with no annual commitment. Chello Base (the base AI planning assistant) remains included free in every subscription. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Chello Pro at $50.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Mosaic. Mosaic has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
