What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Lyzr's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Four-module price list ($250K/yr, $250K per 150 agent-months, $100K/yr, 2.5T-token tuning) replaced by Build $0, Production from $100K/yr, and custom-priced Govern tier; agent-month pricing dropped. In detail: Four-module price list (Govern $250K/yr, Production $250K per 150 agent-months, AI for every employee $100K/yr, fine-tuning $250K per 2.5T tokens) replaced by Build $0, Production from $100K/yr, and custom-priced Govern tier; agent-months remain the unit but published per-agent-month rates ($1,667/$2,000) dropped. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Take Agents to Production at $8,333.33.
It lands 7 days after Lyzr's previous change, "Lyzr launches free-forever Agent Studio, rebuilds pricing into buyable modules" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Lyzr swaps per-run fees for agent-month plans, adds $250K Opencontroller tier" (Sep 28, 2026). Lyzr has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
