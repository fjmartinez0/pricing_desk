What happened
Between the Nov 23, 2026 and Nov 24, 2026 captures of Framer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan monthly bandwidth limit increased from 1 GB to 5 GB. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised). The current page lists Basic at $10 and Pro at $30.
It lands 8 days after Framer's previous change, "Framer makes free plan's 500 AI credits monthly, but FAQ still calls it a trial" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Framer renames External Agents to MCP Server, free in preview" (Oct 26, 2026), "Framer adds 6 Firewall rules to Advanced Hosting add-on alongside 6 rewrites" (Oct 2, 2026) and "Framer discloses 50 firewall rules for Enterprise hosting plans" (Oct 1, 2026). Framer has been tracked since 2026-03-19.
In the same window Figma, Sketch, Squarespace and Webflow also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
