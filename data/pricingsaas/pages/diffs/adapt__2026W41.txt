What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Adapt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Open source plan renamed Developer ($0); combined Business & Enterprise section split into separate cards; On-prem tier folded into Enterprise features list.
