What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Nightwatch's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Enterprise AI prompts, AI answers, keyword research and SEO Agent credits changed from unlimited to Custom (negotiated) limits; Enterprise plan now lists Volume discounts as an included feature; Claude removed from the AI platforms tracked on Starter, Professional and Agency plans; Claude tracking is now Enterprise-only. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, feature removed, limit decrease) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Nov 3, 2026) lists Starter at EUR 79, Professional at EUR 159 and Agency at EUR 399.
It lands 153 days after Nightwatch's previous change, "Nightwatch ditches per-seat fees, takes aim at Semrush and Ahrefs" (Jun 2, 2026). Nightwatch has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Datadog, Sumologic, Dynatrace and Elastic also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
