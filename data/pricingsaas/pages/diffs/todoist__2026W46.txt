What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Todoist's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Table layout added to Pro and Business plans (Business: for team projects) and to the Task views comparison table; Beginner not included. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $5 and Business at $8.
It lands 128 days after Todoist's previous change, "Todoist quietly drops AI Agents waitlist, caps reporting history at "Full" (Jul 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Todoist drops marketing fluff, renames pricing page to Plans" (Jun 22, 2026) and "Todoist rebrands Pro activity history as Full reporting history" (Apr 13, 2026). Todoist has been tracked since 2026-03-18.
In the same window Asana, Notion, ClickUp and Microsoft also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
