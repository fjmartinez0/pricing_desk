What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Kwanzoo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing intro no longer offers a free trial; now says to start on a monthly plan to test, or book a demo for custom pricing. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Agentic Growth at $1,500, Agentic Scale-up at $2,500, Agentic Enterprise at $3,500, GTM Services Tier 2 at $5,500 and GTM Services Tier 1 at $3,500.
It lands 7 days after Kwanzoo's previous change, "Kwanzoo moves to $1,500-$3,500 Agentic plans, dropping pay-per-signal tiers" (Oct 5, 2026). Kwanzoo has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Wistia, Visme, Veel, Uscreen and Profound also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
