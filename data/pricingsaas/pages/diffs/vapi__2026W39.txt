What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Vapi's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Build/Scale two-tier pricing replaced by a four-tier Success Package model: No Success Package ($0), Core ($29/mo), Pro (10% of hosting fee, $999/mo min), Premier (custom); new $20/org/month add-on added; Zero Data Retention (ZDR), previously a $1,000/month add-on on both Build and Scale, is now included free with the Core ($29/mo) and Pro plans under the new Success Package tiers; Entry-level c. In detail: Entry-level concurrent call limit cut from 10 (Build, included) to 4 (No Success Package); regaining 10 concurrent calls now requires the paid Core plan at $29/month. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, limit decrease, add-on change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Core at $29, Pro at $999, No Success Package at $0.05 per call minute (Vapi hosting fee), HIPAA Compliance at $2,000 and Additional Concurrency at $10.
It lands 6 days after Vapi's previous change, "Vapi overhauls pricing with 4-tier Success Package model, cuts free calls" (Sep 15, 2026). Vapi has been tracked since 2026-06-26.
In the same window Microsoft, Twilio, Google, Aircall and Ringcentral also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
