What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Beebole's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Essential plan can now add on Advanced-tier features (cost management, expense tracking, budget monitoring, advanced tags, custom roles, custom fields); prices unchanged; New Beebole AI section (suggested time entries, AI assistant connection, plain-language reports, AI on Beebole servers); also desktop app/browser extension, lock dates, calendar integration rows. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essential at $9 and Advanced at $18.
