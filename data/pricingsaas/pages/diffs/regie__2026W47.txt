What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Regie.ai's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan one-time starting credits per person increased from 250 to 1,000; Pro ($41) and Team ($125/seat) prices and monthly credits unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change). The current page lists Pro at $41 and Team at $125.
It lands 7 days after Regie.ai's previous change, "Regie.ai drops Monday CRM sync, renames Pro power dialer to parallel dialer" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Regie.ai adds free built-in CRM to every plan, with HubSpot and Salesforce sync" (Nov 1, 2026), "Regie.ai launches $125/$150 Team plan, expands Free to 10 seats" (Sep 27, 2026) and "Regie.ai: relaunches as RegieGO with Free/$49 Pro plans, drops $180-$499/user legacy pricing" (Aug 24, 2026). Regie.ai has been tracked since 2026-05-02.
In the same window HubSpot and Intercom also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
