What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Metricool's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Flows add-on moved from 'No extra cost for a limited time' to $12/€10 per 2,500 monthly executions, with extra executions at $0.01/€0.01 each.
