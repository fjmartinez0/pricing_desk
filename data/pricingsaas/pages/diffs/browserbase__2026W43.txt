What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Browserbase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Search API flat $7/1k requests replaced by per-provider rates: $1/1k Parallel Fast, $5/1k Parallel Advanced, $7/1k Exa on Developer and Startup plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Developer at $20 and Startup at $99.
It lands 108 days after Browserbase's previous change, "Browserbase: new Model Gateway added across all plans" (Jul 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Browserbase Adds Free AI Model Access With $5 Token Credit" (Apr 13, 2026), "Browserbase Drops GB-Second Usage Billing From Developer and Startup Plans" (Apr 1, 2026) and "Browserbase adds free Search, Fetch, and Functions APIs across all plans" (Apr 11, 2026). Browserbase has been tracked since 2024-12-07.
