What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of LendingWise's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Enterprise plan with custom pricing (Book a Call) added above Elite: custom users, permissions, portals, forms, modules, integrations and data migration.
