What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of HubSpot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan dropped 'AI transcript enrichment (BETA)' and gained 'Capacity Limits' and 'Standard sandbox account'; Professional plan's feature list dropped the 'Agent Hub' entry. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $7, Professional at $90, Enterprise at $150 and HubSpot Credits at $9 per 1,000 credits.
It lands 90 days after HubSpot's previous change, "Hubspot Service Enterprise cuts Customer Agent trial from 28 to 14 days" (Jun 22, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hubspot Service Enterprise reveals $9/1K credits, adds Macros to Scale" (May 13, 2026), "Hubspot Service Enterprise splits into 4 tiers with $9 Starter and free trial" (Q1 2026) and "Hubspot Service Enterprise swaps Conversational bots for Intelligence tab" (Week 3, 2026). HubSpot has been tracked since 2025-01-01.
In the same window Salesforce, Microsoft, Zoho, Freshworks and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
