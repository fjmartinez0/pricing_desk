What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Grain's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Starter plan renamed Pro at the same $15/$19 price, now priced per month instead of per seat; Business now requires a 2 seat minimum; Paid meeting length capped: Starter's unlimited meeting duration becomes Pro up to 4 hours each; Business capped at 8 hours each; Enterprise adds SCIM, 1080p recording, bring-your-own LLM (Azure, Bedrock, Vertex), custom invoicing and recording/sharing rules. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, plan renamed, limit decrease) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $15 and Business at $29.
Grain's previous recorded change was "Grain: Starter team limit removed, Business "Unlimited teams" perk dropped" (Aug 31, 2026). Grain has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Intercom, Salesforce, HubSpot and Gorgias also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
