What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Figma's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Figma agent loses its Beta label (now generally available); the FAQ saying the agent is free and consumes no AI credits during beta was removed, so standard AI credit usage applies. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Organization at $5, Enterprise at $5 and Professional at $16.
It lands 28 days after Figma's previous change, "Figma drops cross-team publishing from Professional plan spotlight" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Figma debuts Specs in beta, bundled with Full, Dev and Collab seats" (Oct 15, 2026), "Figma swaps team-wide publishing for unlimited files on Pro plan" (Sep 10, 2026) and "Figma tweaks Pro/Org highlight bullets, swaps unlimited files for cross-team " (Sep 7, 2026). Figma has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Framer, Webflow and Penpot also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
