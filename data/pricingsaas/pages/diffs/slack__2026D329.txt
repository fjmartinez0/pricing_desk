What happened
Between the Nov 24, 2026 and Nov 25, 2026 captures of Slack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise+ price revealed: previously hidden behind 'Contact sales for pricing', now publicly shows $45 USD per user/month when paying annually (Contact Sales CTA retained). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Nov 28, 2026) lists Pro at $7.25, Business+ at $15 and Enterprise+ at $45.
It lands 6 days after Slack's previous change, "Slack drops Slackbot from Free and Pro, swaps Enterprise+ unlimited claim for usage guidelines" (Nov 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Slack reveals $45 Enterprise+ pricing, dropping contact-sales listing" (Nov 14, 2026), "Slack shifts Slackbot to usage limits, noting 15 messages per user weekly" (Nov 11, 2026) and "Slack hides Enterprise+ pricing, drops $45/user price for contact sales" (Nov 8, 2026). Slack has been tracked since 2023-06-01.
In the same window Microsoft, Zoom, Google, Rocket.Chat and Mattermost also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
