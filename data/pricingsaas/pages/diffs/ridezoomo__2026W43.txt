What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Zoomo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Extra battery add-on (40€/month) now up to 12h/day, up from 8h; included 17Ah battery range cut from up to 8h to up to 6h. Page locale also flipped German to Spanish. In detail: Optional extra battery add-on (40€/month) now covers up to 12 hours of use per day, up from 8. The included 17Ah battery card now says up to 6 hours (was 8), matching the existing 17Ah spec section. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Lite at EUR 70, Pro at EUR 150, Pro Plus at EUR 160, Damage Protection at EUR 20 and Extra Battery at EUR 40.
It lands the same day as Zoomo's previous change, "Zoomo extends battery add-on to 12 hours a day, cuts base to 6" (Oct 19, 2026). Zoomo has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
