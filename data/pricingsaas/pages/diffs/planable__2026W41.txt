What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Planable's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan now includes the Analytics and Social inbox add-ons instead of listing them as available paid add-ons; Social listening remains a separate $82.5/workspace/month add-on. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $33, Pro at $49, Social Listening at $82.5, Analytics at $12 and Social Inbox at $7.5.
It lands 14 days after Planable's previous change, "Planable adds top banner promoting existing Social Listening add-on" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Planable adds Competitor Analytics to feature comparison on all plans" (Aug 17, 2026). Planable has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window MeetEdgar, Sproutsocial and Buffer also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
