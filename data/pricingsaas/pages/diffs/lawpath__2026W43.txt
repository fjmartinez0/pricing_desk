What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Lawpath's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Essentials bullets updated: unlimited Atlas credits for Chat, Editor and Review added; real-time team collaboration and Suggestions/Insights wording replaced with Atlas Editor and Atlas drafting. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Business Compliance at AUD 20, Virtual Office at AUD 50, ABN Registration at AUD 55 pay as you go, Essentials at AUD 67 and ASIC Compliance at AUD 105.
It lands 105 days after Lawpath's previous change, "Lawpath: EOFY promo ends, Essentials/Legal Advice/Bundle prices rise 33-40%" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Lawpath: EOFY promotional pricing across Essentials, Legal Advice, Bundle" (Jul 13, 2026) and "Lawpath slashes prices up to 40% off in EOFY bundle deal" (Jun 1, 2026). Lawpath has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
