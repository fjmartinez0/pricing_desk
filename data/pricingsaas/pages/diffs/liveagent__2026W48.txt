What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of LiveAgent's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Per-agent seat caps raised: Starter up to 5 to up to 15 agents; Team up to 15 to up to 30 agents; Business unchanged. Seat prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised). The current page lists Starter at $15, Team at $29, Business at $49 and Enterprise at $3,199.
It lands 7 days after LiveAgent's previous change, "LiveAgent extends offer-price lock to existing customers switching plans" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LiveAgent cuts prices up to 26% through Nov 22 and adds account managers" (Nov 1, 2026) and "LiveAgent replaces per-agent Enterprise pricing with $3,999/mo flat fee" (Oct 26, 2026). LiveAgent has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Help Scout and Intercom also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
