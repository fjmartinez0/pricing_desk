What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of DataCamp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Teams now shows a "Special Price" promo of $14 per user/month billed annually, discounted from the $28 list price (50% off; BEFORE showed $28 with no promo). Premium promo also swapped: BEFORE showed the monthly view with a $1 first-month offer (list $35); AFTER shows the yearly view with a "Special Price" of $14/month billed annually (list $28, "Save $255 with Yearly"). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Teams at $14 and Premium at $14.
It lands 7 days after DataCamp's previous change, "DataCamp Premium drops to $1 for first month in new promo" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "DataCamp: Premium and Teams plans double from $14 to $28/month" (Aug 10, 2026) and "DataCamp: course catalog expanded to 790+ courses" (Jul 27, 2026). DataCamp has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
In the same window Udemy, Coursera and Codecademy also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
