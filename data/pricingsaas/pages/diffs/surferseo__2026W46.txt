What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Surfer SEO's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Plan taglines rewritten from classic SEO to AI search visibility framing (Pro, Peace of Mind, Discovery, Enterprise); prices and plan features unchanged; Activity Log retention increased in the comparison table: Discovery gains a 1 Week Activity Log (was not included), Standard 1 Week → 1 Month, Pro 1 Month → 1 Year, Peace of Mind 12 Months → 16 Months; prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, positioning) with an upward direction. The current page lists Discovery at $49, AI Search Analytics at $82, Standard at $99, Pro at $182 and Peace of Mind at $299.
It lands 42 days after Surfer SEO's previous change, "Surfer SEO hides Enterprise's $999/month, shifts tier to custom pricing" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Surfer SEO: AI Search Analytics price cut $158→$82/mo" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Surfer SEO lets AI Search Analytics stand alone, rebrands 'Compare Plans' as…" (Aug 17, 2026). Surfer SEO has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
