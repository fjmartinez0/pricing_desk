What happened
Between the Nov 5, 2026 and Nov 6, 2026 captures of Vercel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Observability trace retention raised: Hobby 1 hour to 3 days, Pro 1 day to 14 days, Enterprise 3 to 14 days; Session Tracing row renamed Trace Retention. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $20.
It lands 4 days after Vercel's previous change, "Vercel adds usage pricing for Beta CI and Observability monitors" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vercel slashes Firewall limits, adds metered Deployment Storage at $0.10/GB" (Oct 26, 2026), "Vercel: Vercel Firewall limits cut: Enterprise Custom Firewall Rules 1,000→250; IP Blocking Hobby 3→10, Pro 100→40,…" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Vercel: Vercel Drives storage launched at $0.05/GB-month, metered reads/writes" (Oct 20, 2026). Vercel has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
