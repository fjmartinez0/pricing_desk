What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of ProtoPie's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan now lists MFA and SCIM provisioning alongside SSO in the plan card and in the Security comparison rows (Basic and Pro show none). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $25, Pro at $47 and Connect Core at $20.
ProtoPie's previous recorded change was "ProtoPie splits MCP into Core and Enterprise tiers, drops Beta label" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ProtoPie: Free plan scene limit increased (2→8 per prototype)" (Aug 17, 2026). ProtoPie has been tracked since 2026-03-19.
