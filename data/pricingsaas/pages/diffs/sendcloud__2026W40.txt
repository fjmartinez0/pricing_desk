Sendcloud hides free-plan overage fee, drops €0.50/label rate from pricing table
Verified Sep 28, 2026·Business Operations·Direction: neutral
The changeFree tier — freemium change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 21, 2026 → Sep 28, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Sendcloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan's per-label overage price for exceeding the monthly parcel limit (previously €0.50/label) is no longer shown, replaced with an unavailable indicator in the comparison table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Lite at EUR 28, Growth at EUR 87, Premium at EUR 175 and Pro at EUR 639.
It lands 7 days after Sendcloud's previous change, "Sendcloud: pre-Oct-2025 grandfathering disclaimer removed from pricing page" (Sep 21, 2026). Sendcloud has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window EasyPost and Shippo also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
