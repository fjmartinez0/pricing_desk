What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Airbyte's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Per-credit rates no longer shown on plan cards: Standard 'Extra credits $5 each' and Plus 'Overage: $5.00/credit' removed; trial copy restated from '400 credits ($2,000 of usage)' to '400 credits, about 100 GB of syncs. No credit card.'; Dedicated Solutions Architect added to Pro and Enterprise Flex plan cards (not previously listed anywhere on the page); Plan taglines rewritten to 'Best for...'. In detail: Plan taglines rewritten to 'Best for...' copy; CTAs changed to Start 14-day trial / Get a capacity quote; prices unchanged ($20, $189). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden, positioning, feature added) with a downward direction. The current page lists Standard at $20 and Plus at $189.
It lands 14 days after Airbyte's previous change, "Airbyte cuts free trial from 30 to 14 days, keeps 400 credits" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Airbyte adds Audit Logging to Data Governance comparison table" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Airbyte launches $189/mo Plus plan, doubles Standard to $20/month" (Sep 21, 2026). Airbyte has been tracked since 2026-07-14.
In the same window Google and Talend also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
