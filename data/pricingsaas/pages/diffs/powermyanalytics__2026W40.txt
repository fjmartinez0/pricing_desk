What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Power My Analytics's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: A new 'Data Destinations' capability (Data Studio, Claude, ChatGPT, Power BI, Tableau) was added as an included line item to every plan, including the free Sample Data tier ('All destinations'); Custom's version also lists BigQuery, SQL, FTP & More. Custom's 'Sovereign data warehouse' feature was also relabeled 'Dedicated data warehouse'. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Business at $41.63, Grow at $83.29, Pro at $166.63, Additional Accounts (Pro) at $8.29 and Additional Account per Source (Grow) at $10.79.
It lands 77 days after Power My Analytics's previous change, "Power My Analytics adds Auth Links to Pro plan for secure client account access" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Power My Analytics doubles Pro plan accounts-per-source limit to 20, same price" (Jul 6, 2026). Power My Analytics has been tracked since 2026-06-19.
In the same window Google and Tableau also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
