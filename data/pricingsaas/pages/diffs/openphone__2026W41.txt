What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of OpenPhone's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Webhooks integration no longer labeled Beta in the feature table (graduated from beta). Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Starter at $15, Business at $23, Scale at $35, Sona Credits - Tier 3 at $49 and Sona Credits - Tier 2 at $25.
It lands 93 days after OpenPhone's previous change, "Quo: Added ChatGPT/Claude MCP, Tasks feature, and Clio CRM integration" (Jul 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenPhone adds ChatGPT and Claude MCP integration to all plans" (Jun 22, 2026) and "OpenPhone adds Tasks to all plans, unlimited for Business and Scale" (Jun 17, 2026). OpenPhone has been tracked since 2026-03-27.
