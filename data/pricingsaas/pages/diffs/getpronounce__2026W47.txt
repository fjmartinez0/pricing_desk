What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Pronounce's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: A Free plan ($0, 10 free activities, limited AI chat) is now shown as a tier alongside Pro (from $12/mo) and Unlimited (from $19/mo); paid prices unchanged; Unlimited now includes 5 hours of tutoring a month on chosen subjects plus daily calls with Florence, an AI tutor; Pro and Unlimited get 15,000 words/day AI chat.
