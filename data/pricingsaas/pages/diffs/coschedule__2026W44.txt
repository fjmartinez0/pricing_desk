What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Coschedule's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Suite plan's 'Unlimited User Seats' and 'Unlimited Social Profiles' are now 'Custom User Seats' and 'Custom Social Profiles' — unlimited no longer guaranteed, now negotiated/custom per account. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $29 and Professional at $49.
It lands 15 days after Coschedule's previous change, "Coschedule caps Professional Inbox at 9 networks, drops add-ons from table" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Coschedule ditches per-seat tiers for flat $29-$69 plans" (Sep 7, 2026) and "CoSchedule: Twitter/X add-on price doubled to $16/profile/month" (Aug 10, 2026). Coschedule has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Sendible, Sproutsocial and Buffer also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
