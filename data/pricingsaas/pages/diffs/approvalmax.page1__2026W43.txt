What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of ApprovalMax's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added a new 'Approval Intelligence' section to the Compare Features table: Approval Agent (flags supplier-history discrepancies, Advanced & Premium only) and Search Agent (natural language search, all three plans). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $49.5, Advanced at $79.2, Premium at $111, Training package at $2,100 subscription and ApprovalMax Capture at $13.2.
It lands 7 days after ApprovalMax's previous change, "ApprovalMax Page1: Advanced $79.17→$79.20 and Premium $110.92→$111.00 per month" (Oct 12, 2026). ApprovalMax has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
In the same window Ramp, Paychex, Coupa and Expensify also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
