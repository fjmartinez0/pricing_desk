What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Whereby's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Explore (free) plan now carries a 30 minute time limit per meeting, shown in the plan card and a new 'Time limit on meetings' comparison row.
