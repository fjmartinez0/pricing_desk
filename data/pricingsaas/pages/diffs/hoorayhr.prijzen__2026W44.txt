What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of HoorayHR's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New AI section added to plan comparison table: MCP (unlimited fair use on Plus) and Meeting Agent (3 meetings on Core, unlimited fair use on Plus). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Core at EUR 7.5, Plus at EUR 9.25, Declaraties & reiskosten at EUR 0.5, Urenregistratie at EUR 1.5 and Multi-entiteiten at EUR 1.
HoorayHR's previous recorded change was "HoorayHR Prijzen launches MCP integration in Plus plan's AI features" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HoorayHR Prijzen: 1-on-1 conversation logging removed from Core plan" (Sep 14, 2026). HoorayHR has been tracked since 2026-06-03.
In the same window Paychex and BambooHR also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
