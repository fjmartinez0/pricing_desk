What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of VOC AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: API Basic plan price rose from $29/month to $38/month; Basic and Pro now disclose exact credit quotas (35,000 and 105,000 credits/month) replacing vague allowance wording, plus updated taglines. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $38, Personal at $83.25, Pro at $99, Team Lite at $99.83 and Team Growth at $199.83.
It lands 7 days after VOC AI's previous change, "VOC AI splits credit pricing into SaaS and API tiers, cuts free credits 2,000 to 500" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "VOC AI: Pro plan price increased ($69→$99/mo)" (Aug 4, 2026) and "VOC AI moves Most Popular badge to Pro, upgrades Enterprise support" (Jun 23, 2026). VOC AI has been tracked since 2026-04-26.
In the same window Deepgram, Fireworks and Assemblyai also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
