What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of GiveWP's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Essentials plan ($199/year, for nonprofits starting out) removed; Give now offers only Pro ($399/year) and Elite ($599/year). Pro prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $33.25 and Elite at $49.92.
It lands 108 days after GiveWP's previous change, "GiveWP: pricing fully restructured, free plan removed (Essentials/Pro/Elite $199/$399/$599)" (Jul 3, 2026). GiveWP has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Stripe, Neon and Bloomerang also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
