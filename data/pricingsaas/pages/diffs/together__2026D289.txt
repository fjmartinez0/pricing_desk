What happened
Between the Oct 15, 2026 and Oct 16, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Muse Spark Open promo row added: input $1.00 and output $5.00 become $0.00 until 10/18/2026; site banner offers free trial through 10/18 12 p.m. PT; Serverless model rate card adds Muse Spark Open ($1.25 in / $4.25 out, $0.15 cached) and NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL ($0.15 in / $0.50 out, $0.02 cached). Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, discount added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Managed Storage at $0.16 per GiB/month, Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, Sandbox (Code Interpreter) at $0.03 per session (60 min), GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100) and GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days).
It lands 2 days after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI ends Kimi K3 cached-input promo; price returns to $0.30/1M" (Oct 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI cuts GPU Cluster minimum reservation from 7 days to 3, prices hold" (Oct 8, 2026), "Together AI cuts Kimi K3 token prices 10% while Qwen3.8 Flash rises" (Oct 6, 2026) and "Together AI adds Tev1 4B Experimental at $0.04 input with free output" (Oct 5, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
