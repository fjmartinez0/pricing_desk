What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Hive's pricing page, Pulse recorded 5 changes: Developers plan no longer lists $50+ free credits after adding a payment method; Hive Vision Language Model renamed DeployVLM (same $0.50/$2.50 per 1M tokens) amid Hive to Deploy rebrand; Developers plan drops Selected Models, 10+ Hive Models, default rate limits and AutoML training bullets for open source model library access; new CSE Text Classifier (contact sales) listed; Hive→Deploy rebrand hi. In detail: Hive→Deploy rebrand hides public per-unit rates for ~15 models, now Contact Sales: Audio Moderation $0.03/min, Speech to Text $0.02/min, Text Translation $10/M chars, AI Image + Deepfake / AI Video Detection $6 per 1,000, AI Audio Classifier $10/hr, OCR, Logo Recognition, Object Detection, Celebrity Recognition, etc. Visual/Text Moderation, LLM and AutoML prices still shown; Generate category removed: Image Generation SDXL ($3/1,000 images), SDXL Enhanced ($4), Flux Schnell ($3), Flux Schnell Enhanced/Emoji ($4), Image to Image, Text to Speech and Video Generation no longer offered; Enterprise card drops Access to all Hive Models + Moderation Dashboard, Premium Support and Multi-region support bullets; only Custom Pricing and Highest Rate Limits remain. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden, feature change, plan removed, discount removed, feature removed) with a downward direction.
It lands 7 days after Hive's previous change, "Hive cuts DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash input to $0.10 and output to $0.35 per 1M" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hive: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash token pricing cut 20%" (Sep 28, 2026), "Hive: new DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash LLM model added" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Hive launches Z.AI GLM-5.3-Flash with ~66% off list intro pricing" (Sep 7, 2026). Hive has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
In the same window Slack, Microsoft, Asana and Trello also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
