What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Gusto's pricing page, Pulse recorded 5 changes: Plan taglines rewritten; Plus adds contractor invoice management; Premium bullets replaced (Dedicated Service Advisor etc. to Premium Account Support, GBC, project costing, 25 invoice payments); Contractor Only plan ($35/mo + $6/mo per person, with a limited-time $0/mo base for the first 6 months) removed from the Pay My Team tab; FAQ mention of the plan also dropped; New Gusto Business Complian. In detail: New Gusto Business Compliance add-on: $85/mo per state on an annual contract, included in Premium, available on Simple and Plus; Performance add-on ($3/mo per person: reviews, real-time feedback, goals, surveys; Simple plan) removed from the add-ons section; New Remote I-9 ($20/submission) and E-Verify ($5/submission, on top of Remote I-9) add-ons, included on Premium, available on Simple and Plus. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, new add-on, add-on removed, plan removed) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Simple at $6, Solo at $6, Plus at $80, Premium at $180 and Tax-Advantaged Benefits at $200 per year.
It lands 7 days after Gusto's previous change, "Gusto launches limited-time free Contractor Only plan for 1099-only teams" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Gusto re-gates custom admin roles to Premium, bundles Next-Day pay into Plus and Premium" (Sep 7, 2026). Gusto has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Paychex and BambooHR also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
