What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Jobber's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Oct promo 'Save up to 40%, offer ends October 30th' replaces standard 'Save up to 25% with annual billing'; Just-me annual prices Grow $105, Connect $70, Core $21/mo for 12 months, then $149/$99/$29 (Plus/team sizes ~30% off too; monthly no-commitment and 1-year plans get 3-month promo prices); new customers only. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $24, Connect at $149, Grow at $299, Plus at $529 and Marketing Suite at $79.
Jobber's previous recorded change was "Jobber Teams ends 40% annual promo, cuts discount to 25% and drops intro rates" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Jobber Teams: Annual discount jumps from 25% to 40% via new intro pricing" (Sep 28, 2026), "Jobber Teams: New intro-rate promo, up to 40% off (was 25%), ends Aug 31" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Jobber Teams: Temporary annual promo pricing on Grow, Connect, Core (up to 20% off)" (Aug 11, 2026). Jobber has been tracked since 2026-02-06.
In the same window Skedulo, HappyFox, Fleetio, Webflow and Tatsoft also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
