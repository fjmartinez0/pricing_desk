What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Stedi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI features section rebranded as Stedi Agent; 'Stedi Agent -' product prefixes dropped and Member ID lookup and Payer finder descriptions reworded. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pay as you go at $0.3 per eligibility check + $0.3 per claim submission.
It lands 42 days after Stedi's previous change, "Stedi splits paper claims into separate CMS-1500 and UB-04 line items" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Stedi adds signed BAA to pay-as-you-go and launches CMS-1500 claim validations at $0.05" (Aug 24, 2026). Stedi has been tracked since 2026-05-13.
In the same window Stripe and Squarespace also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
