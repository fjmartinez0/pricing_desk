What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Intercom's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Fin Million Dollar Guarantee badge removed from the pricing hero, along with the 90-day money-back guarantee copy for Fin. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Essential at $19, Advanced at $85, Expert at $132, Fin AI Agent (Standalone) at $0.99 per Fin outcome and Copilot at $29.
It lands 7 days after Intercom's previous change, "Intercom Pro add-on starting price ticks up $1 to $100 per workspace" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Intercom offers new customers 35% off Essential, cutting seats to $19" (Oct 21, 2026), "Intercom launches Fin Voice AI at $1.99 per outcome, adds 90-day guarantee" (Oct 11, 2026) and "Intercom ends $19 Essential promo, seat price jumps to $29/month" (Oct 3, 2026). Intercom has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Salesforce, HubSpot and LiveChat also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
