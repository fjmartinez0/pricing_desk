What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of JFrog's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Ultimate Security no longer lists AI catalog (now only full App Risk Governance); Unified Security copy reframed as agentic SSC with automated remediation and AI asset security coverage. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $50, Enterprise X at $950, Pro X (Self-Managed) at $2,250 and Enterprise X (Self-Managed) at $4,250.
It lands 63 days after JFrog's previous change, "JFrog: 22 optional Enterprise X/+ add-ons now included by default" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "JFrog: Ultimate Security Bundle dev capacity cut 500→200" (Aug 17, 2026). JFrog has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window GitHub, GitLab, Docker and Hashicorp also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
