What happened
Between the Oct 28, 2026 and Oct 29, 2026 captures of Figma's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Figma Specs beta availability widened from paid seats to all seats. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Organization at $5, Enterprise at $5 and Professional at $16.
It lands 10 days after Figma's previous change, "Figma adds Specs beta to paid seats, puts code layers in Design tiers" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Figma debuts Specs in beta, bundled with Full, Dev and Collab seats" (Oct 15, 2026), "Figma agent exits beta, ending free ride as AI credit charges now apply" (Oct 12, 2026) and "Figma drops cross-team publishing from Professional plan spotlight" (Sep 14, 2026). Figma has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Webflow, Framer and Penpot also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
