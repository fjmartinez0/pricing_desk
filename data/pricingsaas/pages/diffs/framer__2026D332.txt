What happened
Between the Nov 27, 2026 and Nov 28, 2026 captures of Framer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Content editors seat tier ($10/editor for Basic and Pro, Custom for Enterprise) removed; only the $20/editor Additional editors tier remains. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed). The current page lists Basic at $10 and Pro at $30.
It lands 3 days after Framer's previous change, "Framer adds Automations limits: 1 on Free, up to 10 on Pro" (Nov 25, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Framer quintuples free plan bandwidth, raising monthly limit from 1 GB to 5 GB" (Nov 24, 2026), "Framer makes free plan's 500 AI credits monthly, but FAQ still calls it a trial" (Nov 15, 2026) and "Framer renames External Agents to MCP Server, free in preview" (Oct 26, 2026). Framer has been tracked since 2026-03-19.
