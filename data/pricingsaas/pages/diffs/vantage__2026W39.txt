What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Vantage's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: DigitalOcean added as a new supported Cloud integration in the Feature Comparison table's Integrations list (integration count increased from 35 to 36). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $30 and Business at $200.
It lands 28 days after Vantage's previous change, "Vantage adds Fireworks AI integration and LLM Token Allocation tracking" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vantage boosts cloud integrations to 30+, adds ElevenLabs support" (Jul 13, 2026) and "Vantage: Free trials added for paid plans + Redis, CircleCI integrations" (Jul 4, 2026). Vantage has been tracked since 2023-11-28.
