What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Peec AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Top-tier LLM models tracked limit raised from 13 to 26; Available models row revised: Perplexity added to all plans, Microsoft Copilot replaced by Bing AI Mode and ERNIE (Baidu); top plan API model list expanded (Claude, Mistral, OpenAI, Exa, etc.); Additional Models add-on list: Microsoft Copilot replaced by Bing AI Mode; prices unchanged ($30/$70). In detail: Additional Models add-on list: Microsoft Copilot replaced by Bing AI Mode; add-on prices unchanged ($30/$70/$140 per month for Starter/Pro/Advanced). Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, limit raised, add-on change). The current page lists Starter at $80, Pro at $205, Advanced at $420, Additional Model (Starter) at $30 and Additional Model (Pro) at $70.
It lands 7 days after Peec AI's previous change, "Peec AI adds SOC 2 Type II report access and renames Meta Spark to Muse Spark" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Peec AI adds agent credits row: 500 on Starter, up to 1,000 on Advanced" (Oct 26, 2026), "Peec AI: bot-visit allowance raised on Starter/Pro/Advanced, no price change" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Peec AI moves Perplexity tracking to Enterprise-only, adds Naver AI to all plans" (Sep 21, 2026). Peec AI has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
