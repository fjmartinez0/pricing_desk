What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of CLAILA's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pro monthly price raised from $9.90 to $19; the 'Free 7-day trial, cancel anytime' line under Pro was removed; Pro monthly credits increased from 600 to 1,500.
