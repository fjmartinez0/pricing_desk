What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Articulate's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Commenting in the Compare plans table moved from Coming soon to Included for Frontline and Hybrid, with new copy: "Get real-time feedback from other Articulate users" (was "Generate a preview link for any seat holder on the subscription").
