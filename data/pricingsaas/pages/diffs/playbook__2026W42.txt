What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Playbook's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan member limit cut from 3 to 1; Pro and Team per-member storage add-ons are now capped at 10TB total, where previously no cap was stated. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $10 and Team at $20.
It lands 21 days after Playbook's previous change, "Playbook drops Business plan, folds it into custom-priced Enterprise tier" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Playbook Free plan gains 1K included API calls/month" (Aug 17, 2026), "Playbook: Pro & Team API call limits cut (50K→20K, 500K→200K)" (Aug 10, 2026) and "Playbook: SAML SSO moved from Business to Enterprise-only" (Aug 4, 2026). Playbook has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Asana, Notion and Trello also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
