What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Nextiva's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Nextiva renamed and re-priced its 3 plans: NEXT Core ($15/mo)→Go ($10/mo), NEXT Engage ($25/mo)→Business ($25/mo), NEXT Scale ($75/mo)→CX ($75/mo); removed the $1.50/conversation XBert AI line shown on each plan card. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change). The current page lists Go at $10, Business at $25, CX at $75, Premium at $100 and Pay as you go at $1.50 conversation.
It lands 7 days after Nextiva's previous change, "Nextiva drops Dedicated Onboarding Team from NEXT Scale plan and support table" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Nextiva rebrands plans NEXT Core/Engage/Scale, adds $1.50 XBert AI fee" (Oct 26, 2026). Nextiva has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Microsoft, Ringcentral, Zoom and Google also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
