What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Village's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Core plan at $1/seat/month with 100 pooled credits per seat and all premium features; new Forward-deployed tier with $10k minimum, white-glove onboarding and SOC2 report; Business monthly pooled credits raised from 7,500 to 9,000 at unchanged $149/mo. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, new plan) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Core at $1, Business at $134, VIP at $2,000, Forward-deployed at $10,000 and API Platform at $10 per active user.
It lands 49 days after Village's previous change, "Village: Free profile enrichment capped to 3mo cache, older data costs 1 credit" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Village.ai: Credit-based usage system launched across all plans" (Aug 17, 2026). Village has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Storylane, SalesRabbit, Salesforce, Praiz and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
