What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Lodgify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: September Flash Promo ended: yearly discount on Professional and Ultimate cut from 50% to 20%, and the Save 50% promo banner removed. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $14, Starter at $26, Professional at $42 and Ultimate at $62.
It lands 7 days after Lodgify's previous change, "Lodgify launches Flash Promo: up to 50% off yearly and bi-yearly plans" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Lodgify's August Flash Promo expires, hiking monthly prices up to $62 on Ulti" (Sep 7, 2026), "Lodgify: August Flash Promo raises annual discount to 25-50%" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Lodgify: Peak Season promo raises annual discount to 50%" (Jul 13, 2026). Lodgify has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
