What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of ReferralHero's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Overage pricing now shown on cards: $1.50 per 1,000 emails over 25,000/mo, pay-as-you-go SMS, 2.5%+$0.50 payout fees, $0.005 per Pro subscriber over 10,000 mobile; AI Insights listed as an included feature on the Basic plan card. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $249 and Pro at $399.
It lands 70 days after ReferralHero's previous change, "ReferralHero cuts email automation cap 75%, raises SMS rates 10-30%" (Aug 17, 2026). ReferralHero has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
