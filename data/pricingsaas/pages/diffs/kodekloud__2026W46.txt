What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of KodeKloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Annual-plan 'Scary Good Skills' sale ended: toggle 'Save up to 50%' now 'Save up to 21%'; Pro 45% to 21% off (USD 21/mo, $250/yr to USD 30/mo, $360/yr), AI 50% to 20% off (USD 29/mo, $342/yr to USD 46/mo, $547/yr). List prices unchanged ($456, $684); Standard stays 21% off at $180/yr. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $15, Pro at $21 and AI at $29.
It lands 21 days after KodeKloud's previous change, "KodeKloud cuts annual savings claim from 50% to 21% as Skillfest sale ends" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "KodeKloud annual savings shrink to 21% as Autumn sale ends" (Oct 5, 2026), "KodeKloud doubles annual discount to 50% as Autumn of Learning sale launches" (Sep 28, 2026) and "KodeKloud: annual discount reduced from 50% to 21%" (Sep 21, 2026). KodeKloud has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
