What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Jimdo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Companion AI daily message limit doubled from 25 to 50 messages per day across all plans, Free included. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised). The current page lists Start at $11, Grow at $17, Complete at $29, Unlimited at $49.50 and Listings Management at $5.
It lands 7 days after Jimdo's previous change, "Jimdo adds $29 Complete plan, puts Companion AI in every tier including Free" (Nov 8, 2026). Jimdo has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Shopify, Squarespace and Webflow also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
