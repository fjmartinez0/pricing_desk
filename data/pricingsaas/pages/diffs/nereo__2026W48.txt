What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Nereo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Black Friday sale: up to 50% off annual plans; Plus yearly drops from $112 to $80/mo ($960 billed yearly, renews at $1,344). Mini and Standard stay 30% off; Nano Banana Pro 1K generation now costs 5 credits instead of 3, cutting images per plan from 133/533/1,600 to 80/320/960 on Mini/Standard/Plus. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, discount added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Mini at $28, Standard at $56 and Plus at $112.
It lands 14 days after Nereo's previous change, "Nereo triples free credits to 12, enough for 3 Seedance clips" (Nov 8, 2026). Nereo has been tracked since 2026-10-07.
In the same window Squark AI, Speechmatics, Spiky.AI, Slang AI and scite also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
