What happened
Between the Nov 17, 2026 and Nov 18, 2026 captures of CodeRabbit's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: API and MCP server access now listed as included on every plan; Enterprise 'API access' relabeled 'Admin APIs'; new FAQ explains plan coverage. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Essentials at $24, Team at $48, Advanced at $72, Usage-based reviews at $0.25 per reviewed file and CodeRabbit Agent at $0.40 per agent minute.
It lands 39 days after CodeRabbit's previous change, "CodeRabbit renames Agent to CodeRabbit Engineer, drops $0.40/min from card" (Oct 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CodeRabbit reframes Advanced around architectural insights (copy only)" (Sep 17, 2026) and "CodeRabbit merges Slack and Cloud Coding agents into one add-on" (Sep 9, 2026). CodeRabbit has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
In the same window Replit, GitHub, GitLab, Windsurf and JetBrains also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
