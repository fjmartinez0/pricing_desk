What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Onpipeline's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Autumn Sale banner (50% off first 3 months) removed from the pricing page header; list prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pipeline at $20, Standard at $20 and Advanced at $20.
It lands 7 days after Onpipeline's previous change, "Onpipeline adds ~20% annual billing discount and 50% off Autumn Sale" (Oct 12, 2026).
