What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Framer's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Basic and Pro plans now include a '2x credits first month' bonus, doubling the AI credit allotment for a new subscriber's first billing month; Advanced Hosting add-on's static file upload limit increased 10x, from 50 static files to 500 static files. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added, limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Basic at $10 and Pro at $30.
It lands 3 days after Framer's previous change, "Framer doubles AI credits for first month on Basic and Pro plans" (Sep 25, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Framer adds 6 Firewall rules to Advanced Hosting add-on alongside 6 rewrites" (Oct 2, 2026), "Framer discloses 50 firewall rules for Enterprise hosting plans" (Oct 1, 2026) and "Framer raises Pro plan static file limit tenfold from 5 to 50 files" (Sep 24, 2026).
