Shopify rewrites POS comparison, adds customer profiles and returns rows
Verified Oct 26, 2026·Business Operations·Direction: up
The changeAll plans feature — feature added.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 19, 2026 → Oct 26, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Shopify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: POS comparison section rewritten: new Customer profiles (Simple) and Returns (at purchase location) rows on all plans; row copy renamed; POS Pro $89/mo per location unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $29, Grow at $79, Advanced at $299, Plus at $2,300 and POS Pro at $89.
Shopify's previous recorded change was "Shopify simplifies in-person sales messaging, adds plan comparison rows" (Oct 23, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shopify cuts Grow to $79 and Advanced to $299, reversing D288 hike" (Oct 16, 2026), "Shopify raises annual Grow to $89/mo and Advanced to $359/mo" (Oct 15, 2026) and "Shopify Plus drops 1% sales cashback, removing the $15K cap from its table" (Oct 2, 2026). Shopify has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Salesforce and Squarespace also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
