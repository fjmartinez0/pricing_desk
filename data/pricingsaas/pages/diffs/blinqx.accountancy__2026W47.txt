What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Blinqx's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Rapportage modules consolidated: Prognose & budgetten (€20 per entity) and the separate Jaarrekeningen zonder dashboard (1 credit) and met dashboard (2 credits) rows removed; the single Jaarrekeningen module drops from 2 to 1 credit per entity. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change).
It lands 14 days after Blinqx's previous change, "Blinqx Accountancy cuts annual accounts report to 1 credit without dashboard" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Blinqx Accountancy: Credit scale extended with tier 6 (3,500 credits, €700) and tier 7 (5,000 credits, €1.000), both 80%…" (Oct 26, 2026).
