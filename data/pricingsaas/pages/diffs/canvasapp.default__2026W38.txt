What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Supernova's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Supernova moved from flat usage-metered pricing to a tiered model: new Self-serve, AI Data Team ($10,000/mo), Enterprise (custom), and a $9,000 Pilot Program; usage rates unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pilot Program at $9,000 subscription, AI Data Team at $10,000 and Self-serve at $0.15 per GB hour + $0.05 per GB-month storage.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Supernova. Supernova has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
