What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of MRPeasy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Unlimited plan API line changed from generic 'API, webhooks (for paid accounts)' to 'API Standard, webhooks' plus new 'API Pro (minimum 10 paid users)' tier. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Starter at $44.92, Professional at $63.25, Enterprise at $90.75 and Unlimited at $136.58.
It lands 146 days after MRPeasy's previous change, "MRPeasy rolls out tiered support options on pricing page" (Jun 22, 2026). MRPeasy has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
