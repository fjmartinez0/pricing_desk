What happened
Between the Nov 23, 2026 and Nov 24, 2026 captures of Suno's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday Sale added: extra 30% off annual plans, with first-year promo prices on Pro, Premier and Platinum (e.g. Pro $5.60/mo, Premier $16.80/mo, Platinum $70/mo); sale countdown shown.
