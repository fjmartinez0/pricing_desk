What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Salesmsg's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Basic and Pro merged into a single Pro plan with one credit-tier dropdown (6,000–600,000 credits/yr); Basic ($21/mo billed yearly) removed. Prices rose at every tier: 6,000 credits $21→$28/mo (+33%), 120,000 credits $277→$375/mo (+35%). Credit-tier prices now shown as total $/mo instead of +$/yr add-ons. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change). The current page lists Pro at $28, Additional Seat at $10 and Additional Number at $5.
It lands 104 days after Salesmsg's previous change, "Salesmsg: Volume-tier pricing restructured into Basic/Pro/Enterprise plans" (Aug 11, 2026). Salesmsg has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Shopify, Vonage, Klaviyo, Bird and Gorgias also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
