What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Gallabox's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise tagline now targets organisations with 50+ users (was large teams) and no longer mentions priority support. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Basic at $112, Essential at $248, Advanced at $474, Shopify Integration at $5 and Channel Add-on at $20.
It lands 104 days after Gallabox's previous change, "Gallabox: Basic $89→$112, Essential $197→$248, Advanced $377→$474 + 1 more" (Aug 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Gallabox: restructured plans, cut entry-tier users to 3, added AI credits + 1 more change" (Jul 3, 2026), "Gallabox shifts to flexible multi-year enterprise contracts" (Jun 1, 2026) and "Gallabox cuts annual discount in half, adds AI engineer upsell" (May 26, 2026). Gallabox has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Intercom, Bird, Gupshup and Twilio also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
