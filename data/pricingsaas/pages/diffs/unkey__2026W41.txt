What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Unkey's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter plan max compute per instance raised from up to 1 vCPU / 2 GB to up to 2 vCPU / 2 GB. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter (Deploy) at $5, Pro (Deploy) at $25, Pro at $25 and Business (Deploy) at $50.
It lands 55 days after Unkey's previous change, "Unkey: Starter/Pro vCPU limits cut, custom domains now unlimited" (Aug 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Unkey: Business plan vCPU cut 32→16 + new Active Keys billing dimension" (Aug 4, 2026) and "Unkey adds metered ephemeral disk pricing and SOC 2 Type II certification" (Jul 20, 2026). Unkey has been tracked since 2025-11-08.
In the same window Supabase and FusionAuth also changed their pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
