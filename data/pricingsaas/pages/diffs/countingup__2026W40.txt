What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Countingup's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed 'Direct Debits: Coming soon' from the included feature list and replaced it with 'Share data with your accountant' (secure access via Accountant Hub). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Tier 1 (£0-£750 deposits) at GBP 5, Tier 2 (£750-£7,500 deposits) at GBP 12 and Tier 3 (Unlimited deposits) at GBP 20.
It lands 49 days after Countingup's previous change, "Countingup: business loan financing limit raised to £1M via iwoca" (Aug 10, 2026). Countingup has been tracked since 2025-01-01.
In the same window Zoho, Xero and Revolut also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
