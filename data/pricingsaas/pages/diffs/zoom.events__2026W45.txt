What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Zoom's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Zoom added a new 'Conference Room Connector' Workplace add-on priced at $49/month, enabling standards-based SIP/H.323 conferencing endpoint support for hybrid meetings, alongside the existing Large Meeting, Cloud Storage, and Live Translation add-ons. In detail: Zoom added a new Workplace add-on priced at $49/month, enabling standards-based SIP/H.323 conferencing endpoint support for hybrid meetings, alongside the existing Large Meeting, Cloud Storage, and Live Translation add-ons. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Webinars at $83.33, Webinars Plus at $290.83, Events at $415.83, Additional Hub Host at $54.17 and Additional Co-Editor at $54.17.
It lands 35 days after Zoom's previous change, "Zoom Events adds $49/month SIP/H.323 room connector for hybrid events" (Sep 28, 2026). Zoom has been tracked since 2023-11-15.
