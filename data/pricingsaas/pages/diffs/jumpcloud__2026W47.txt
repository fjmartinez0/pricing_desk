What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Jumpcloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Workflows and Access Certification rows added to the Identity & Access Management comparison table. Workflows is included in all three packages (Device Identity Management, Platform Essentials, Platform); Access Certification is included in Platform only and sold à la carte at $5.00/user/month. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Device Management at $9, SSO at $11, Device Identity Management at $13, Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) (À La Carte) at $3 and Password Management (À La Carte) at $3.
It lands 7 days after Jumpcloud's previous change, "Jumpcloud adds PKI to Zero Trust lineup at $3 to $4 a la carte" (Nov 8, 2026). Jumpcloud has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Ping Identity, Duo and OneLogin also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
