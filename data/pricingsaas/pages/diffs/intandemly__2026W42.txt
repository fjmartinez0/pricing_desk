Intandemly renames tiers to Full ABX, drops account volume caps
Verified Oct 12, 2026·Sales & Customer Relationship Management (CRM)·Direction: neutral
The changePackaging — package change.
2 changes·2 captures·Oct 5, 2026 → Oct 12, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Intandemly's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Engagement tiers renamed: Full Revenue Engine is now Full ABX; Enterprise at Scale is now Enterprise. Pricing still shared on a call; Per-month account volumes (500 / 1,000 / 2,000 / 5,000+ accounts per month) dropped from all engagement tiers; now scoped by accounts the client needs opened. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, plan renamed) with a neutral direction.
Intandemly's previous recorded change was "Intandemly Transitions to Custom Pricing Model, Discontinuing All Standard Plans and Removing Free Tier for New Account-Based Sales Services" (Q3 2025). Before that Pulse recorded "Intandemly Eliminates Spin-to-Win Discounts and Free Trials, Streamlining Pricing Structure" (Mar 2024). Intandemly has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Asana, Notion and Trello also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
