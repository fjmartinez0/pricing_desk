What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of WizeHire's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Concierge plan (custom pricing) renamed Scale; price still custom, features unchanged; Top-tier tagline changed from complex hiring and compliance needs to expanding into new locations and hiring at scale. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed, positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Quickstart at $209, Growth at $449, Additional jobs at $99, Additional locations at $349 and Reactivations at $249.
WizeHire's previous recorded change was "WizeHire: Coaching benefit renamed, per-tier session counts removed" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "WizeHire drops Essentials tier as pricing shakeup hikes add-ons, Growth cost" (Aug 17, 2026). WizeHire has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
