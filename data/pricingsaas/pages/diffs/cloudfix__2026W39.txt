What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of CloudFix's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Automated fixer count in the Starter plan (inherited by Growth, Scale, and Enterprise) increased from 30+ to 53, also updated in the page's meta description. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for CloudFix. CloudFix has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
