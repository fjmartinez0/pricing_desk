What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Thinkific's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Discontinued the $54/mo Basic entry plan. Start ($109/mo) and Grow ($219/mo) shifted left one slot, keeping their prices; a new Expand tier was added at $499/mo between Grow and Enterprise Plus. Effective entry price rose from $54/mo to $109/mo. In detail: Discontinued the $54/mo 'Basic' entry plan; every tier shifted up a slot (old Start features now the $109/mo floor) and a new $499/mo 'Expand' tier was inserted between Grow and Enterprise Plus. Effective entry price rose from $54/mo to $109/mo. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Start at $82, Grow at $164, Expand at $374 and Branded Mobile App at $199.
