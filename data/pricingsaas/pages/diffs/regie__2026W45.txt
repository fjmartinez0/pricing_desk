What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Regie.ai's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free built-in CRM now advertised on every plan (Free included), with option to connect HubSpot, Salesforce or Monday CRM; new CRM section and Free-plan bullet added. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $41 and Team at $125.
It lands 35 days after Regie.ai's previous change, "Regie.ai launches $125/$150 Team plan, expands Free to 10 seats" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Regie.ai: relaunches as RegieGO with Free/$49 Pro plans, drops $180-$499/user legacy pricing" (Aug 24, 2026). Regie.ai has been tracked since 2026-05-02.
In the same window HubSpot and Intercom also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
