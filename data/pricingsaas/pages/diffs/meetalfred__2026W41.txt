What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Meet Alfred's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Annual per-user prices rose (Individual $29→$35, Business $49→$59, Teams $39→$47); annual discount 50%→40%; plans renamed from Basic/Pro/Team; new Unlimited plan with custom pricing. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $29, Team at $39 and Pro at $49.
It lands 55 days after Meet Alfred's previous change, "Meet Alfred: Pro plan price increased $25 to $49/user/month (annual)" (Aug 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Meet Alfred: Pro annual price increase ($25→$49) + summer promo ended" (Jul 27, 2026). Meet Alfred has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
