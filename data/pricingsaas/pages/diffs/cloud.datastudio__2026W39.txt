What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Google Cloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: the fixed Sept 30, 2026 end date (enforcement starting Oct 1, 2026) for free Conversational Analytics usage was removed, replaced with an open-ended promotional period backed only by an at-least-90-days-notice commitment. Token quotas and overage rates ($3.00/1M input, $20.00/1M output) unchanged. In detail: Looker's Conversational Analytics free-usage promotional period, previously fixed to end September 30, 2026 (enforcement starting October 1, 2026), is now open-ended with only an 'at least 90 days advance notice' commitment before billing/quota enforcement begins. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Conversational Analytics Overage at $3 per 1M input data tokens + $20 per 1M output data tokens.
It lands 98 days after Google Cloud's previous change, "Google Cloud Datastudio pools AI tokens per platform, adds Thinking mode" (Jun 15, 2026). Google Cloud has been tracked since 2026-04-24.
In the same window Vultr, Digitalocean, IBM and Heroku also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
