What happened
Between the Oct 10, 2026 and Oct 11, 2026 captures of Intercom's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Fin Voice AI voice support offered via sales at $1.99 per voice outcome; Pro add-on now priced 'From $100 per workspace/mo' (was '$100/mo'); 90-day money-back guarantee for Fin and spending limits/alerts added to pricing page. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Essential at $19, Advanced at $85, Expert at $132, Fin AI Agent (Standalone) at $0.99 per Fin outcome and Copilot at $29.
It lands 8 days after Intercom's previous change, "Intercom ends $19 Essential promo, seat price jumps to $29/month" (Oct 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Intercom slashes Essential plan seat price 34%, from $29 to $19" (Sep 28, 2026), "Intercom rebrands Essential automation as Fin, adds Fin external actions" (Sep 19, 2026) and "Intercom strips Fin AI from Essential, reframes standalone agent plan" (Sep 18, 2026). Intercom has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Salesforce, HubSpot and LiveChat also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
