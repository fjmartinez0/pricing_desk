What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Tyms's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: New Done for you plan added (in-house experts run books and file taxes): $83.33 per 500 entries/month billed $1,000 annually, priced at 5x the Plus seat price on the same billing schedule; Add-ons section removed: Catch-up accounting ($150 per backlog year, unlocks pre-2026 uploads + 1,000 transactions) and Credit (from $0.40/credit = 10 transactions, valid 12 months) no longer offered; Plan car. In detail: Plan cards replaced by comparison table adding bank rules, email forwarding, payroll, digital assets, multi-entity, dashboards, forecasting, approval workflows, Claude/ChatGPT access, API, Stripe/Shopify/Amazon, tax filing. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, add-on removed, new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Plus at $16.67 and Done for you at $83.33.
It lands 104 days after Tyms's previous change, "Tyms drops demo-gated pricing for self-serve tiers, free plan, AI add-ons" (Jul 20, 2026). Tyms has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
