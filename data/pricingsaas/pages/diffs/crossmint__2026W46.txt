What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Crossmint's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Embedded Wallets per-wallet rate cut: overage $0.025/MAU becomes $0.01 per Monthly Active Wallet; Custom plan 'Starts at' rate $0.02 to $0.01 per MAW; Free Monthly Active Wallets increased from 1,000 to 5,000 on Embedded Wallets and the Developer plan; Enterprise Support now lists Dedicated Forward Deployment Engineers. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price decrease, limit increase) with a downward direction. The current page lists Wallets - Custom at $0.01 per Monthly Active Wallet, Agent Wallets - Custom at $0.01 per Monthly Active Wallet and Agent Checkout at $0.05 per checkout.
It lands 42 days after Crossmint's previous change, "Crossmint slashes wallet overage fees, cutting per-MAW pricing to just $0.025" (Sep 28, 2026). Crossmint has been tracked since 2026-04-24.
In the same window Alchemy and PhantomBuster also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
