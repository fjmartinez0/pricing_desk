What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Sarvam AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pricing moved from Pay-as-you-go plus Enterprise to paid monthly tiers: Pro ₹2,000, Growth ₹10,000, Business ₹50,000 per month, with tiered per-unit rates. Pay as you go now ₹0/month; New Bulbul V4 Flash text-to-speech model (real-time, streaming, WebSocket) listed at ₹2.00 per 1,000 characters on Pay as you go, lower on higher tiers. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pay as you go at INR 2 Bulbul V4 Flash, real-time — per 1,000 characters + INR 2 Bulbul V4 Flash, streaming — per 1,000 characters, Pro at INR 2.85 Bulbul V3, streaming — per 1,000 characters + INR 2.85 Bulbul V3, WebSocket — per 1,000 characters, Growth at INR 1.7 Bulbul V4 Flash, WebSocket — per 1,000 characters + INR 0.41 Real-time — per minute, Business at INR 1.7 Bulbul V4 Flash, streaming — per 1,000 characters + INR 2.7 Bulbul V3, streaming — per 1,000 characters and Gemma 4 at INR 35 per 1M input tokens + INR 90 per 1M output tokens.
It lands 91 days after Sarvam AI's previous change, "Sarvam AI boosts bonus credits up to 66% across all plans, Business support tier" (Jul 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Sarvam AI slashes Vision API price 67% to ₹0.50 per page" (Jun 2, 2026). Sarvam AI has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
