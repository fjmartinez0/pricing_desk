What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Devin's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Teams plan user cap reduced from up to 200 users to up to 100 users in both plan card and comparison table; Added 'Use your ChatGPT plan' on Pro/Max/Team, 2 concurrent Free-mode sessions per user, 3-tier cost controls, SCIM/RBAC; Admin dashboard with analytics bullet removed.
