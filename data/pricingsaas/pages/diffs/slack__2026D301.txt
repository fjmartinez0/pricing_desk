Slack swaps 24/7 support for tiered Success Plans, sold as paid add-ons
Verified Oct 28, 2026·Collaboration & Productivity·Direction: neutral
Covered on Pulse:Slack replaces its 24/7 support row with Success Plans, making Premier a paid add-on on Pro
The changePro, Business+, Enterprise+ feature — feature change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 27, 2026 → Oct 28, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 27, 2026 and Oct 28, 2026 captures of Slack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Comparison-table Support row (24/7 support) replaced by Success Plans: Standard, Premier and Signature tiers, with Premier and Signature offered as add-ons via sales rep. In detail: Comparison-table Support row (Pro 24/7; Business+ 24/7 with 4-hr first response; Enterprise+ 24/7 priority) replaced by Success Plans: Standard included on Pro, Business+ and Enterprise+; Premier included on Business+ and Enterprise+, add-on for Pro; Signature an add-on on all paid plans (Premier/Signature purchasable via sales rep only). Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $7.25, Business+ at $15 and Enterprise+ at $45.
It lands 2 days after Slack's previous change, "Slack launches Frontline add-on for Enterprise+ field workers and Salesforce" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Slack: Frontline teams add-on launched for Enterprise+ field workers" (Oct 20, 2026), "Slack hides Enterprise+ price, swaps $45/user for "Contact sales" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Slack: Enterprise+ now shows a public price of $45 USD per user/month (billed annually) instead of 'Contact sales…" (Oct 14, 2026). Slack has been tracked since 2023-11-30.
In the same window Zoom, Microsoft, Google, Rocket.Chat and Mattermost also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
