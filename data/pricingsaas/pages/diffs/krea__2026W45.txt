Krea cuts LoRA training from its Free plan as paid prices hold steady
Verified Nov 2, 2026·AI & Machine Learning·Direction: neutral
The changeFree tier — freemium change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 26, 2026 → Nov 2, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Krea's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan loses LoRA training: the Free card's '✓ Limited LoRA training (50 images)' row is now a greyed-out '× LoRA training' (not included). Paid plans and all prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $5, Pro at $21, Max at $63 and Business at $160.
It lands 10 days after Krea's previous change, "Krea ends 14-day unlimited Krea 3 Fast promo for annual Pro and Max buyers" (Oct 23, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Krea: Nodes Agent workflow feature removed from Pro/Max plans" (Oct 21, 2026), "Krea rewrites plan allowance examples: more Nano Banana 2 generations, Seedance 2.5 videos" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Krea offers 14 days of unlimited Krea 3 with annual Pro or Max plans" (Oct 19, 2026). Krea has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Runway, Microsoft and Leonardo.ai also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
