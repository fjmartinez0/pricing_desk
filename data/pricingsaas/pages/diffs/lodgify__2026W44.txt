What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Lodgify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Dynamic Pricing add-on now offered as a 3-month free trial on Basic and Starter plans (previously unavailable on those tiers); Professional and Ultimate continue to get it included at no cost. The comparison table adds a dedicated Dynamic Pricing section with 'Price breakdown' marked NEW!. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $14, Starter at $26, Professional at $47 and Ultimate at $68.
It lands 22 days after Lodgify's previous change, "Lodgify ends September Flash Promo, cutting yearly discount from 50% to 20%" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Lodgify launches Flash Promo: up to 50% off yearly and bi-yearly plans" (Sep 28, 2026), "Lodgify's August Flash Promo expires, hiking monthly prices up to $62 on Ulti" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Lodgify: August Flash Promo raises annual discount to 25-50%" (Aug 31, 2026). Lodgify has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Ava, Hospitable and Guesty also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
