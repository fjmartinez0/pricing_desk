What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Crusoe's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: DeepSeek V4 Pro removed from Managed Inference per-token pricing table (was $1.74 input / $3.48 output / $0.15 cached per 1M tokens). In detail: DeepSeek V4 Pro removed from the Serverless Inference per-token pricing table (was $1.74 input / $3.48 output / $0.15 cached per 1M tokens). Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed). The current page lists NVIDIA H100 (80GB) On-Demand at $3.90 per GPU-hr, Serverless Fine-Tuning - Qwen3.6 35B A3B FFT at $1.20 per 1M tokens, Serverless Fine-Tuning - Llama 3.3 70B Instruct at $6 per 1M tokens, Serverless Fine-Tuning - Qwen3 8B at $0.40 per 1M tokens and Managed Inference - Yutori n2 at $4 per 1M output tokens + $0.05 per 1M cached tokens.
It lands 7 days after Crusoe's previous change, "Crusoe adds DeepSeek V4.1 Flash fine-tuning at $5.00 per 1M tokens" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Crusoe raises H200 on-demand to $5.40/GPU-hr in Dec 1 GPU price hikes" (Nov 8, 2026), "Crusoe adds DeepSeek V4.1 Flash to Serverless Inference at $0.29/1M input" (Oct 11, 2026) and "Crusoe drops Kimi K2.6 ($0.70/$3.50 per 1M) from Managed Inference lineup" (Oct 4, 2026). Crusoe has been tracked since 2026-06-18.
