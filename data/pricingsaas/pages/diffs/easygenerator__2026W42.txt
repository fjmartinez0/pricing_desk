What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Easygenerator's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro rose from $116/mo ($1,399/yr) to $125/mo ($1,499/yr); Team rose from $582/mo ($6,995/yr) to $625/mo ($7,495/yr), billed annually. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $125 and Team at EUR 541.
It lands 101 days after Easygenerator's previous change, "Easygenerator: new EasyCoach add-on, EasyAI repositioned as free-in-all-plans" (Jul 2, 2026).
