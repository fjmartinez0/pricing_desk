What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Upstage's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Solar Pro 4 promo changed from 70% off until 2026-10-19 to 50% off until further notice; Solar Mini 4 50% launch promo (ended 10-22, standard pricing from 10-23) removed. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Explore at $100, Build at $500, Scale at $5,000, Solar Pro 4 at $0.06 per 1M cached input tokens + $0.3 per 1M input tokens and Solar Pro 3 at $0.15 per 1M input tokens + $0.6 per 1M output tokens.
It lands 7 days after Upstage's previous change, "Upstage API deepens Solar Pro 4 promo to 70% off through Oct 19" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Upstage API trims Solar launch promos to 50% off, raising promo rates" (Oct 12, 2026), "Upstage API deepens Solar Mini 4 promo to 70% off, sunsets Solar Pro 3" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Upstage API launches Solar Mini 4 at 50% off: $0.05/1M input through Oct 22" (Sep 28, 2026). Upstage has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
