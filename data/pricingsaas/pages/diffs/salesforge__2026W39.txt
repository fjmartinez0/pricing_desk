What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Salesforge's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: VIP treatment tier added — spend $500+/month across Forge products to unlock a dedicated account manager, white-glove support, a shared Slack channel, and expert scaling assistance.
