Josys drops $100/mo Discovery plan for identity-based custom pricing
Verified Oct 5, 2026·Cybersecurity & Identity Management·Direction: neutral
The changeJosys Discovery' packaging — package change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 28, 2026 → Oct 5, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Josys's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Fixed $100/month Josys Discovery plan (up to 300 IDs) and custom-quote Automated Identity Governance plan replaced by a single Custom plan priced by number of identities governed. In detail: Collapsed two-tier pricing (fixed $100/month 'Josys Discovery' plan up to 300 IDs, plus custom-quote 'Automated Identity Governance' plan) into a single 'Custom' plan priced entirely by number of identities governed; the $100/month fixed tier no longer exists. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Josys. Josys has been tracked since 2026-05-04.
