What happened
Between the Nov 19, 2026 and Nov 20, 2026 captures of Docker's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pricing page rebuilt around product lines. New Cloud Sandboxes pay-as-you-go pricing published ($0.07/hr Micro up to $1.12/hr XL). Docker Desktop cards cut from Personal/Pro/Team/Business to Free/Teams/Custom: Pro is now listed only in the comparison table, and the Business card became Custom/Contact sales. Subscription prices unchanged (Pro $9, Team $15, Business $24 annual); New Sandboxes (free. In detail: New Sandboxes (free local isolated environments for coding agents) and AI Governance (custom-priced; centrally managed network, filesystem and MCP policies) added to the pricing page. The Free and Custom cards now list access to Sandboxes. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change, feature added). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 23, 2026) lists Docker Pro at $9, Docker Team at $15, Docker Business at $24, Testcontainers Cloud Additional Minutes at $3 per 100 minutes and Docker Build Cloud Minutes (500) at $25 per 500 build minutes.
It lands 23 days after Docker's previous change, "Docker: new $250 Cloud Sandboxes credit promo banner" (Oct 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Docker drops $250 Cloud Sandboxes credits promo from top banner" (Oct 27, 2026), "Docker drops Build Cloud minutes from Pro, Team and Business plan listings" (Oct 11, 2026) and "Docker adds $250 Cloud Sandbox credit in place of whitepaper CTA" (Sep 27, 2026). Docker has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Google Cloud, Aws and Hypervault also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
