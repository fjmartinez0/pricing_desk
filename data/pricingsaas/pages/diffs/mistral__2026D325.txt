What happened
Between the Nov 20, 2026 and Nov 21, 2026 captures of Mistral AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pro plan's $25.5/mo included API credits benefit removed from its feature list. Model training opt-out (previously Enterprise-only) extended to Free, Pro, and Team plans. In detail: Model training opt-out, previously Enterprise-only, was extended to the Free, Pro, and Team plans in the comparison table; Pro plan's feature list no longer includes the $25.5/mo in API credits benefit that used to be bundled with the subscription. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added, feature removed). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 22, 2026) lists Pro at $14.99 and Team at $24.99.
It lands 40 days after Mistral AI's previous change, "Mistral AI cuts Free plan API credits, gives free users Vibe CLI and IDE tools" (Oct 11, 2026). Mistral AI has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
