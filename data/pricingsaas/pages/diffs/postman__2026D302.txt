What happened
Between the Oct 28, 2026 and Oct 29, 2026 captures of Postman's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: SDK generation & distribution removed from the Team plan card and from the comparison table (previously Team included; Enterprise listed it with Auto PR feature). Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Solo at $9, Team at $19, Enterprise at $0.04 per AI credit + $0.04 per VuH (cloud performance testing) and Simple Security at $6.
It lands 31 days after Postman's previous change, "Postman: Context Graph usage add-on introduced" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Postman adds free Context Graph tracking, metered API query overages" (Sep 23, 2026), "Postman hides $49/user Enterprise price behind Contact Sales, drops AWS buy" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Postman pulls $49 Enterprise pricing, moves plan behind contact sales" (Sep 19, 2026). Postman has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Konghq also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
