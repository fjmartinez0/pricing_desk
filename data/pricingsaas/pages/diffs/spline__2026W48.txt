What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Spline's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New subscriber offer: 50% off first 3 months on monthly plans or first year on yearly. Hobby $6, Pro $12.50, Max $30/seat/mo (was $12/$25/$60); replaces 'Up to 20% off' and 2-months-free note. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Hobby at $12, Pro at $25 and Max at $60.
It lands 21 days after Spline's previous change, "Spline adds granular MCP call limits: 20/100/200/400 by tier" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Spline sets daily MCP call caps: Free 20, Hobby 100, Pro 200, Max 400" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Spline splits AI tools: 'AI Agent' added to 3D + 2D editors, Remove/Upscale Image new" (Sep 7, 2026). Spline has been tracked since 2026-03-19.
In the same window Framer, Figma, Sketch and Webflow also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
