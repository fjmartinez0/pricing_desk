What happened
Between the Oct 31, 2026 and Nov 1, 2026 captures of Wispr Flow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan word and meeting limits no longer quantified (was 2,000 words/week on desktop); Pro Meetings upgraded from 'Higher limits' to 'Unlimited', with unlimited tiers subject to abuse limits. In detail: Free plan no longer quantifies its limits: word limit '2,000/week on desktop; limited weekly use on mobile' becomes 'Limited words per week', and Meetings 'Weekly limit' becomes 'Limited Notetaker'. Pro Meetings upgraded from 'Higher limits' to 'Unlimited*'; Pro/Growth/Enterprise unlimited meetings now carry a '* Subject to abuse limits' footnote. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $12 and Growth at $18.
It lands 6 days after Wispr Flow's previous change, "Wispr Flow blurs Free plan mobile cap, swapping 1,000 words for "limited use" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Wispr Flow hides mobile dictation cap, swaps 1,000-word free limit for vague…" (Oct 20, 2026), "Wispr Flow: Free plan Android word limit reduced to 1,000/week" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Wispr Flow ends Enterprise's hidden pricing, unveils $18/user/mo Growth tier" (Aug 31, 2026). Wispr Flow has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
