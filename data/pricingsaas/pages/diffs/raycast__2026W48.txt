What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Raycast's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Included monthly AI credits on the paid card rose from 500 to 3,000 (Pro/Teams card); Raycast MCP added: lets AI apps like Claude Code and ChatGPT control Raycast; new row in comparison table (Pro/Plus/Max). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Pro at $8, Teams Pro at $12, Pro+ at $16, Teams Pro+ at $20 and Pro Max at $40.
This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Raycast launches Raycast MCP, letting Claude Code and ChatGPT control the app" (Day 324, 2026, saved Nov 20, 2026). It is the same change, not a new one. Before that, Raycast's previous recorded change was "Raycast adds $50 Max plan, splits Pro into 3 AI-credit tiers, drops $8 add-on" (Sep 14, 2026). Raycast has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
