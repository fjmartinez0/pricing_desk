What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Mobbin's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI credits added: Pro includes AI credits, Team gets 2x AI credits; comparison table shows Free not included, Pro 300, Team 600, Enterprise custom.
