What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Salesforce's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Premier Success Plan changed from 30% of net license fees to included with Core, Advanced, and Max licenses; Premier Success Plan no longer 'Bundled with Unlimited Edition'; Signature Success Plan 'Customer Success Score' replaced by 'Salesforce Health Insights'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature change, price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Growth+ at $1,250, Advanced+ at GBP 3,520, Premium+ at EUR 15,000 and Plus+ at SEK 27,500.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Salesforce. Salesforce has been tracked since 2025-01-03.
