What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Notion's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Notion removed "Unlimited" wording from its plan comparison table: paid-plan File uploads now show "Up to 5 GB" (the ~5 GB per-file cap was already disclosed in the BEFORE tooltip, so no new limit), Enterprise Page history changed from Unlimited to Custom, Free Pages & blocks dropped "Unlimited for individuals", Unlimited cells for paid Pages & blocks and External guests became checkmarks, and "Unlimited published pages" became "Published pages". Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Plus at $10, Business at $20, Custom domains and branding at $8 and Notion Credits at $10 per 1,000 monthly Notion credits.
It lands 7 days after Notion's previous change, "Notion drops "Unlimited" label from Plus plan features; limits unchanged" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Notion silently drops Workers trial notice ahead of Oct 15 billing" (Sep 28, 2026), "Notion ends free Workers (Beta) trial, credits billed from Oct 15" (Sep 22, 2026) and "Notion drops Beta tag from Meeting Notes AI transcription" (Sep 7, 2026). Notion has been tracked since 2023-11-09.
In the same window Microsoft and Superhuman Docs also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
