What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of CoinTracker's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Ultra plan price cut from $599/year to $499/year (up to 10,000 transactions); comparison table Price per year row also $599 → $499. Ultra+ adds a $999/year option for up to 25,000 transactions; the $1,999/year option for up to 50,000 transactions remains for higher volume. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease, package change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Base at $4.92, Base+ at $8.25, Prime at $16.58, Prime+ at $24.92 and Ultra at $41.58.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for CoinTracker. CoinTracker has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
