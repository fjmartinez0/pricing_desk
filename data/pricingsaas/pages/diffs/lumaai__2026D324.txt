What happened
Between the Nov 19, 2026 and Nov 20, 2026 captures of Luma AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Cost Per Video Generation table adds a new Seedance 2.5 Flash model (Text/Image-to-Video 480p 24, 720p 51 credits/sec; Video-to-Video 15/32 credits/sec).
