What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Orb's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Orb replaced its opaque 3-tier Custom-pricing/Contact-Sales model (Core, Advanced, Enterprise) with a 2-tier credit-based model: Business at $6.00/credit (less with volume discounts, self-serve signup) and Enterprise (custom, Contact Sales). Added an Enterprise Support Bundle add-on; Expanded the plan comparison table from ~16 to ~36 rows: added S3/GCS ingestion, Pricebook, credits & allocations,. In detail: Expanded the plan comparison table from ~16 to ~36 rows: added S3/GCS ingestion, Pricebook, credits & allocations, trials/coupons, simulations, commitments, multi-currency invoicing, A/R aging, collections automation, Adyen/QuickBooks integrations, revenue reports, SSO, custom roles, multiple billing entities, audit logs, and tiered 99.9%/99.99% uptime SLAs. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Business at $6 credit.
It lands 42 days after Orb's previous change, "Orb now part of Adyen, new banner touts payments tie-up" (Sep 7, 2026). Orb has been tracked since 2026-05-04.
In the same window Sumologic, Dynatrace, Datadog and Elastic also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
