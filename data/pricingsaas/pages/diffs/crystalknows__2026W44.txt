What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Crystal Knows's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Assessments plan (from $500/yr) renamed Team Assessments in the plan selector and FAQ; price unchanged; Premium Goals rewritten to weekly-to-yearly planning, Hidden Gems, 5/10/20-Year Visions; Personality adds daily hidden gems; Free Goals now Today's Plan, reflections, 1-Year Vision.
