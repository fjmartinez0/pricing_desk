What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Artlist's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan feature bullet '70+ AI Apps' renamed to '70+ AI Presets' on AI Starter, Core and Creator cards. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists AI Starter at $11.99, AI Core at $23.99 and AI Creator at $41.67.
It lands 15 days after Artlist's previous change, "Artlist adds Image Editor and 3D Model Creation to AI plans" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Artlist halves AI video caps and drops Flows and MCP from plans" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Artlist adds node-based AI Flows builder, trims model coverage on Creator/Pro" (Aug 17, 2026). Artlist has been tracked since 2026-06-04.
In the same window Shutterstock and Getty Images also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
