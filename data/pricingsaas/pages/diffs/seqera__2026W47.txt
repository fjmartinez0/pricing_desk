What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Seqera's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Cloud Basic Optimized Filesystem zero-cost Spot resiliency allowance increased from 100 GB per month to 100 TB per month. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised). The current page lists Seqera Compute at $2.50 per 100 GiB-Hour + $0.10 per CPU Hour.
It lands 104 days after Seqera's previous change, "Seqera: New Academic Program (free access) + Validated tier compliance update" (Aug 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Seqera: New "Validated" plan added for GxP/clinical use cases" (Jul 27, 2026). Seqera has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window Google and Prefect also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
