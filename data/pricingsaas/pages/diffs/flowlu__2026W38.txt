What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Flowlu's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Annual discount cut from 50% to 25%: Essential $6->$9, Advanced $11->$17, Ultimate $16->$24/user/month (list prices $12/$22/$32 unchanged). 'Back to Business' 50%-off banner removed. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Essential at $9, Advanced at $17 and Ultimate at $24.
It lands 21 days after Flowlu's previous change, "Flowlu cuts Ultimate plan to $16/user, list price also drops to $32" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Flowlu: Annual discount jumps 25%→50%, Ultimate reveals $17 price; 20% monthly banner ends" (Aug 17, 2026), "Flowlu: 'Save 20% on Flowlu monthly, forever' banner replaces Marketplace banner" (Jun 30, 2026) and "Flowlu: New "Save 20% monthly, forever" promotional banner" (Jul 3, 2026). Flowlu has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Trello, ClickUp, Asana and monday.com also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
