What happened
Between the Oct 8, 2026 and Oct 9, 2026 captures of fal's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Flux 3 Image (blackforestlabs/flux-3/edit-image) added to Image models rate card at $0.024 per megapixel (41.67 megapixels per $1). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 20, 2026) lists Audio Model APIs at $0.1 per 1,000 characters (MiniMax Speech 2.8 HD) + $0.2 per 100 seconds (ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2), 3D Model APIs at $0.08 per generation (Hunyuan Motion 1B) + $0.01 per credit (Tripo H3.1), Serverless Compute at $6 per H200 GPU hour + $4 per RTX PRO 6000 GPU hour, Video Model APIs at $0.05 per second of video output (MiniMax H3 Max) + $0.07 per second of video output (Kling 2.5 Turbo Pro) and Image Model APIs at $1 per unit (GPT Image 2) + $0.5 per 100 megapixels (Z Image Turbo).
It lands 4 days after fal's previous change, "fal hikes GPU rates up to 33%, adds GB200 tier and Nano Banana 2/Pro models" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "fal raises H100 GPU rate to $2.49/hr, adds GB200, doubles MiniMax video price" (Oct 2, 2026), "fal publishes Audio and 3D model API pricing, expands video/image tables to 10 endpoints" (Oct 1, 2026) and "fal: H100 GPU list price increase ($3.99→$4.50/h)" (Aug 5, 2026). fal has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
