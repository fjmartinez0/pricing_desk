What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of GitBook's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Advanced customization now included in Free plan (was Essential+); Free card reframes MCP server as AI ready documentation (MCP, WebMCP); Essential card lists Reusable content instead of Advanced branding. In detail: Free plan gains Advanced customization (logo, font, footer, header colors; was Essential+) and Custom fonts (was Ultimate+, now all plans incl. Essential) per comparison table; Free card reframes MCP server as AI ready documentation (MCP, WebMCP); Essential card lists Reusable content instead of Advanced branding. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essential at $12 and Ultimate at $12.
It lands 7 days after GitBook's previous change, "GitBook renames Premium to Essential, bundles AI credits into paid plans" (Oct 12, 2026). GitBook has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
