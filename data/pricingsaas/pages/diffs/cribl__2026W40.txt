What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Cribl's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: FAQ now discloses per-product billing metrics: Stream/Edge priced on GB of data ingested, Search offers 3 options (usage-based, flat rate, or Lakehouse tiered by GB/day), and Lake is priced on data volume stored. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Standard at $0.27 per GB ingested (Stream cloud worker) + $0.21 per GB ingested (Edge node), Enterprise at $0.21 per GB ingested (Edge node) + $0.32 per GB ingested (Stream cloud worker), Cribl Insights (7-day) at $450, Cribl Insights (1-month) at $900 and Cribl Insights (3-month) at $1,980.
Cribl's previous recorded change was "Cribl Unveils FinOps Center for Enhanced Data Usage Tracking and Cost Forecasting" (Q3 2025). Before that Pulse recorded "Cribl Increases Standard Plan Edge Node Limits to Unlimited, Enhancing Capacity for All Users" (Q2 2025), "Cribl Introduces Pricing Guide and Clarifies Data Volume Limits for Standard and Enterprise Tiers" (Q1 2025) and "Cribl Introduces Comprehensive FAQ Section to Clarify Pricing Structure and Regional Differences" (Q3 2024). Cribl has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
