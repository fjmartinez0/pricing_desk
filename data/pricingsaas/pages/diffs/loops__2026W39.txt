Loops overhauls Free plan: unlimited contacts, emails capped to 1,000 newest
Verified Sep 21, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral
The changeAll plans free tier — freemium change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 14, 2026 → Sep 21, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Loops's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan redefined: contacts are now unlimited (upload as many as you like on every plan) but the 4,000 monthly emails only go to your 1,000 newest contacts, replacing the prior hard cap of 1,000 subscribed contacts on Free. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Growth at $49.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Loops. Loops has been tracked since 2026-09-08.
