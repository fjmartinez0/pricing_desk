What happened
Between the Nov 17, 2026 and Nov 18, 2026 captures of Synthesia's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Pricing page split into 'For individuals' / 'For business' tabs; default view now shows only Basic, Starter, Pro - Enterprise no longer appears in the main pricing row or comparison table; Survey response allowances increased significantly: Starter from 42 to 150 responses/year, Pro from 205 to 720 responses/year, as part of the broader credits-based usage overhaul; Basic gets a new 3-minute Syr. In detail: Basic gets a new 3-minute Syren cap; Starter unlocks uncapped Syren + premium video models; comparison table gains 'Shared Credit Pool'/'Admins' rows; Pro's 'AI Video Assistant' highlight was dropped. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised, feature added, packaging change). The current page lists Starter at $14, Pro at $59 and Business at $79.
It lands 9 days after Synthesia's previous change, "Synthesia swaps Syren AI time cap for tiered video quotas, cuts Basic assets" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Synthesia swaps Syren 3-minute video cap for per-plan monthly video quotas" (Nov 7, 2026), "Synthesia drops its interactive credit calculator from the pricing page" (Nov 3, 2026) and "Synthesia drops $25 Roleplay learner-seat add-on, adds free trial to paid plans" (Nov 1, 2026). Synthesia has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window OpusClip, Runway and Loom also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
