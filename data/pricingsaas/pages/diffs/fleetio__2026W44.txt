What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Fleetio's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Active automation limits raised: Professional from 5 to 15, Premium from 40 to 75; New Custom Records feature row added to comparison table: up to 5 on Professional, up to 25 on Premium, not on Essential. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essential at $4, Professional at $7, Premium at $10 and Tools at $0.5.
Fleetio's previous recorded change was "Fleetio adds AI Data Analyst, drops Tailored Dashboards from Advanced Analytics" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fleetio spotlights new AI Service Advisor on pricing page; prices unchanged" (Sep 14, 2026). Fleetio has been tracked since 2023-12-01.
In the same window HappyFox, Webflow, Tatsoft, SpotDraft and Shopify also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
