Workstars: moved from 3-tier plans to a single $3/employee flat rate
Verified Sep 28, 2026·Workforce Management·Direction: neutral
The changePackaging — package change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 21, 2026 → Sep 28, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Workstars's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Entry ($2.00), Pro ($4.00), and Global ($5.50) per-user/month tiers replaced by one flat plan at $3/employee/month with all features included and volume discounts for 1,000+ employees. In detail: Workstars replaced its 3-tier volume-based pricing (Entry $2.00, Pro $4.00, Global $5.50 per user/month) with a single flat rate of $3/employee/month, all features included, no tiers or feature-gating. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $3.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Workstars. Workstars has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
