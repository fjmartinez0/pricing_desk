What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Katalon's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Professional now shown as $1,000/seat/year (list $2,199, 55% off first 3 seats until Oct 31) vs $84/seat/month; Monthly/Annual toggle and Runtime Engine/cloud add-ons section removed; Enterprise card now lists role-based access control and audit logs, private cloud/on-prem deployment, centralized license management, security review and onboarding services. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Professional at $84, Katalon Runtime Engine at $145, Cross Browser at $158 and Mobile Devices at $158.
It lands 14 days after Katalon's previous change, "Katalon adds free tier, renames Individual to Professional, refocuses Enterpr" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Katalon: plan tiers consolidated to Individual/Small teams, True Platform moved to add-on" (Aug 31, 2026), "Katalon slashes Studio Enterprise price 44%, adds per-seat tiered pricing" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Katalon splits Studio Enterprise into standalone $229/seat plan" (Jul 20, 2026). Katalon has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
In the same window Lambdatest, BrowserStack and Cypress.io also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
