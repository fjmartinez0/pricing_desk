What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of JOIN's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Standard plan's AI job description generator was rebranded as 'Job Studio (AI job ads)'; the linked product page and comparison-table row were renamed to match (job-description-generator to job-studio). Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $17 and Advanced at $34.
It lands 21 days after JOIN's previous change, "JOIN renames top-tier plan from Custom to Enterprise, pricing unchanged" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "JOIN: Enterprise plan renamed to Custom" (Aug 17, 2026) and "JOIN hikes Advanced plan to €34/job, drops bring-your-own contracts perk" (Jul 13, 2026). JOIN has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
In the same window SignalHire, Microsoft, Zoom, Discord and Google also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
