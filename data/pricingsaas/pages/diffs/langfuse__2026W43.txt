What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Langfuse's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: In-app agent renamed from Langfuse Assistant to Halo in the Cloud plan comparison table; usage limits row renamed too (Low/Medium/High unchanged). Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $29, Pro at $199, Enterprise at $2,499 and Teams at $300.
It lands 35 days after Langfuse's previous change, "Langfuse: AI Assistant usage now tiered by plan" (Sep 14, 2026). Langfuse has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
