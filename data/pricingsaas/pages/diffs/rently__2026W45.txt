What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Rently's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Premium adds SMS, email and voice AI plus web AI chatbot and custom options; ILS direct scheduling moves into Standard. Prices unchanged ($28/$37 per credit). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic Leasing at $28 and AI-Powered Leasing at $37.
It lands 70 days after Rently's previous change, "Rently adds visible annual credit totals to Standard, Premium plans" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Rently: Core/Plus renamed to Standard/Premium, per-listing to per-credit pricing" (Aug 4, 2026). Rently has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window OpenTable, Guesty, WellnessLiving, Treatwell and TouchBistro also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
