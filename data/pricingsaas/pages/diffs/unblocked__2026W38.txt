What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Unblocked's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: The entry-level Code Review plan ($19 USD per user/month, billed annually) was discontinued. Its pricing card and comparison-table column were both removed, leaving Platform ($29/user/month) as the cheapest paid tier — a 53% increase in the entry price point; A new 'Credits per license' row was added to the comparison table, introducing usage-based credit metering: Platform includes 300 credits/l. In detail: A new 'Credits per license' row was added to the comparison table, introducing usage-based credit metering: Platform includes 300 credits/license, Enterprise gets a custom allocation. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed, metric change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Platform at $29.
It lands 63 days after Unblocked's previous change, "Unblocked adds Microsoft SharePoint as a Knowledge Management System data source" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Unblocked shifts AI dev platform pitch to broader software focus" (Jun 19, 2026). Unblocked has been tracked since 2026-05-12.
In the same window CyberGhost VPN, ExpressVPN, Surfshark and NordVPN also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
