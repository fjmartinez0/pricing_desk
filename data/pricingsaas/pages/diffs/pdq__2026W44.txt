What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of PDQ's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan cards renamed by dropping the Connect prefix: Connect Basic/Plus/Premium are now Basic/Plus/Premium. Prices ($12/$18/$28 per device/year) unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed) with a neutral direction. It lands 70 days after PDQ's previous change, "PDQ: Connect plan tiers renamed with a 'Connect' prefix" (Aug 17, 2026). PDQ has been tracked since 2026-05-04.
