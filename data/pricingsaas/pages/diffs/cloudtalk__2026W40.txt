What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of CloudTalk's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Webhooks integration added — pushes CloudTalk events (calls, contacts) to external systems in real time. Included on Essential and Expert plans; not available on Starter. In detail: Added Webhooks integration: CloudTalk events (calls, contacts) can be pushed to your own systems in real time. Included on Essential and Expert plans; not available on Starter. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $25, Essential at $29, Expert at $49, AI Conversation Intelligence at $9 and Power Dialer at $15.
It lands 14 days after CloudTalk's previous change, "CloudTalk: CloudTalk MCP integration added to Essential & Expert plans" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CloudTalk expands AI voice agents to 70+ languages, up from 60+" (Sep 7, 2026). CloudTalk has been tracked since 2026-06-18.
