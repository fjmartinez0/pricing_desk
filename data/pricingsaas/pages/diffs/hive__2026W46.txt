Hive adds optional AI credits step to Teams plan builder, 5,000 included

What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Hive's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New optional 'Add AI credits' step in the Teams plan builder: Teams includes 5,000 AI credits/month; extra packs are one-time purchases and unused credits roll over. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $5, Teams at $12, Buzz AI at $12, Proofing & Approvals at $5 and Timesheets at $5.
It lands 7 days after Hive's previous change, "Hive launches $29/seat CRM add-on, adds Finance add-on at $12 to calculator" (Nov 2, 2026). Hive has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Asana, Notion and ClickUp also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
