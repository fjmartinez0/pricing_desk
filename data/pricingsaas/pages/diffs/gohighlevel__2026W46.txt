HighLevel rewrites plan cards: Founder gets SaaS mode, prices hold
Verified Nov 9, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral
The changeFounder, Starter, Unlimited feature — feature change.
1 change·2 captures·Nov 2, 2026 → Nov 9, 2026
What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of HighLevel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan card features rewritten: Starter drops 3 sub-accounts/24-7 support bullets for SMS, email, workflow; Founder gains SaaS mode; Unlimited gets white-label app, pass-through billing. Prices unchanged. In detail: Plan cards rebuilt and Founder moved to the Most Popular slot: Starter bullets (3 sub-accounts, unlimited contacts/users, 24/7 support) replaced by chat & phone support, Workflow/Campaign Builder, 2-way SMS/email, App Marketplace; Founder perks (Founders Club, Most Free AI) replaced by SaaS mode, Partner Network (coming soon), industry solutions, project management, meeting rooms, events; Unlimited rebilling (no markup) replaced by white-label desktop app, pass-through billing. Prices unchanged; FAQ still lists 3 Starter sub-accounts. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $970, Founder at $2,970, Unlimited at $4,970, HIPAA Compliance at $297 and AI Employee Growth Plan at $50.
It lands 7 days after HighLevel's previous change, "HighLevel renames $497/mo Agency Pro to Founder, strips SaaS Mode and API" (Nov 2, 2026). HighLevel has been tracked since 2026-06-27.
In the same window Versium, User Interviews, Unbounce, Profound and Surfer SEO also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
