What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Corel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Seasonal sale refresh: WordPerfect, PaintShop Pro Ultimate, and VideoStudio Ultimate each gained new ~30% discounts ($75/$30 off), while Painter's $180 discount was removed, reverting to full $429.00. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists PaintShop Pro Ultimate at $59.99 subscription, VideoStudio Ultimate at $64.99 subscription, CorelDRAW Go at $79.99, Photo Video Bundle Ultimate at $139.99 subscription and CorelDRAW Graphics Suite 365-day Subscription at $239.
It lands 7 days after Corel's previous change, "Corel shuffles discounts: Painter gets $180 off, PaintShop/VideoStudio lose 30%" (Sep 14, 2026). Corel has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Figma, Canva, Sketch and Adobe also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
