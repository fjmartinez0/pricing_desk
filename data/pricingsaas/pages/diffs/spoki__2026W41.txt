Spoki caps free messaging, replacing unlimited with 1,000 service messages

What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Spoki's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Comparison table row 'Free messages: unlimited' replaced by 'Service messages: 1,000 per number/month', capping previously unlimited free messaging. In detail: Free plan service messages capped: comparison table row "Free messages: unlimited" (all plans) replaced by "Service messages" — Free now limited to 1,000 per number/month, paid plans still included (✓). Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Service at EUR 39, Marketing at EUR 79, Sales at EUR 119, Voice at EUR 19.99 and SMS Two-way at EUR 19.
It lands 14 days after Spoki's previous change, "Spoki adds AI consulting to Standard, Advanced & Premium tiers" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Spoki hikes WhatsApp Marketing credit rate 15%, real spend jumps to €308.92/mo" (Sep 14, 2026). Spoki has been tracked since 2026-08-27.
In the same window Slack, Microsoft, Zoom, Discord and Google also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
