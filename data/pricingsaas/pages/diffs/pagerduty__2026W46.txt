What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Pagerduty's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: PD Reliability Platform included Starter seats cut 10 to 5 (+5 bonus), Essential events 40,000 to 25,000, Ultimate events 2,000,000 to 1,500,000; Essential seats rise 20 to 25 and AI actions 4,000 to 5,000; Ultimate AI actions rise 100,000 to 160,000; Limited-time offer: additional AI Actions included at no credits for first year on Essential, Plus and Ultimate when signing by Jan 15, 2027. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit decrease, discount added, limit increase) with a downward direction. The current page lists Professional at $21, Business at $41, Operations Cloud Essential at $1,000, Status Pages at $89 and PagerDuty Advance for Incident Management at $415.
It lands the same day as Pagerduty's previous change, "PagerDuty Incident: Essential/Ultimate event quotas cut, seats + AI actions up + 1 more" (Nov 9, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "PagerDuty Incident: AI Actions quota revealed, 1,000-100,000/year by tier" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Pagerduty boosts Plus plan to 50 seats, drops AI Actions quotas" (Oct 5, 2026). Pagerduty has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Atlassian, Opsgenie and Grafana also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
