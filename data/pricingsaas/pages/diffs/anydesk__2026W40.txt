What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Oct 3, 2026 captures of AnyDesk's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Add-on connection price rose from $29.90 to $44.90 per month (billed annually) on the Standard and Advanced plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Solo at $28.9, Standard at $49.9, Advanced at $111.9 and Connection Add-on at $44.9.
It lands 92 days after AnyDesk's previous change, "AnyDesk: 20% launch discount removed + Start Business Trial added" (Jul 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "AnyDesk ends promos, Solo and Standard plans jump up to 20%" (May 18, 2026), "AnyDesk: Enterprise tab added to the pricing page" (Apr 2, 2026) and "AnyDesk Doubles Annual Billing Discount to 20% Across All Plans" (Week 1, 2026). AnyDesk has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Zoho, Microsoft, TeamViewer and GoTo also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
