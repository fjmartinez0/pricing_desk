What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Crystal Knows's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Crystal Predictions (from $59/mo, Premium $59 and Teams) replaced by Buyer Profiles with Custom pricing and no self-serve checkout; Assessments $99/mo unchanged; Premium/Free app features rewritten: Vision becomes Relationships (LinkedIn reads, contact briefs, up to 10 free contacts); Rhythm becomes Routine with 6 free days of coaching. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature change, price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Crystal Premium at $40.83 and Premium for Teams at $49.
It lands 7 days after Crystal Knows's previous change, "Crystal Knows renames Sales to Predictions, adds $49/seat team plan" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Crystal Knows turns Data API into Personality API with pay-per-profile pricing" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Crystal Knows Premium switches to $49/mo monthly billing from $99/yr annual" (Jul 27, 2026). Crystal Knows has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
