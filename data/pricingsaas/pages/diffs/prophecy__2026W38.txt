What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Prophecy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Professional plan's comparison table row now shows Alteryx workflow import capability capped at 5 imports/month; previously this cell was blank (import capability was Enterprise Express+ only, listed there as unlimited). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 21, 2026) lists Professional at $150 and Enterprise Express at $4,000.
It lands 21 days after Prophecy's previous change, "Prophecy: Resource-credit allocations moved to plan cards, units changed" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Prophecy launches Enterprise plan to scale data teams beyond 20 users" (Jun 17, 2026). Prophecy has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window MuleSoft, Dagster Labs and Talend also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
