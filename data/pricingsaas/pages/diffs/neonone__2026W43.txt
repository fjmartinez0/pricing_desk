What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Neon One's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Neon Launch product ($49/month + transaction fees) removed from the pricing page; its product tab and card are gone. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Neon Websites (DIY) at $69, Neon Websites (Standard Build) at $69, Neon Websites (Premium Build) at $69, Neon CRM at $99 and Neon Fundraise at $158.
It lands 104 days after Neon One's previous change, "Neon One lets June 15% off Neon CRM promo expire, pulls banner and callout" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Neon One launches June Special: 15% off Neon CRM ends June 30" (Jul 1, 2026). Neon One has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window Salesforce, Microsoft, GiveWP, Bloomerang and Constant Contact also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
