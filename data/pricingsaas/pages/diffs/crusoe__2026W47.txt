What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Crusoe's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Serverless Fine-Tuning pricing adds three models: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 ($5.00/1M tokens), Qwen3.6 35B A3B FFT ($1.20) and Qwen3.8 27B FFT ($2.50). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists NVIDIA H200 (141GB) On-Demand at $4.29 per GPU-hr, Serverless Fine-Tuning - Qwen3 8B at $0.40 per 1M tokens, Serverless Fine-Tuning - DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731 at $5 per 1M tokens, Shared Disks at $0.07 per GiB/month and Serverless Fine-Tuning - Llama 3.1 8B Instruct at $0.40 per 1M tokens.
It lands 7 days after Crusoe's previous change, "Crusoe raises H200 on-demand to $5.40/GPU-hr in Dec 1 GPU price hikes" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Crusoe adds DeepSeek V4.1 Flash to Serverless Inference at $0.29/1M input" (Oct 11, 2026), "Crusoe drops Kimi K2.6 ($0.70/$3.50 per 1M) from Managed Inference lineup" (Oct 4, 2026) and "Crusoe: GPT-OSS 20B fine-tuning price $0.40->$2.50 + 2 more changes" (Sep 28, 2026). Crusoe has been tracked since 2026-06-18.
In the same window Mux, Astronomer, CloudFix, DigitalOcean and Cloudchipr also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
