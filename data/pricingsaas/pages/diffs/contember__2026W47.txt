What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Contember's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Monthly plan pricing driver reworded: no longer 'production apps and level of support' but set individually, mainly by apps in production. Support level dropped as stated factor. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning).
It lands 21 days after Contember's previous change, "Contember's Fabrika platform goes open source, Zerops named sole host" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Contember hides Foundation and Growth prices, swaps Kickstart for Fabrika" (Oct 4, 2026). Contember has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Sanity, Builder.io, Webflow, DAT Freight & Analytics and Hygraph also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
