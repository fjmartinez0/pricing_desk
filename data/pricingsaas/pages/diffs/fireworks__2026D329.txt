What happened
Between the Nov 24, 2026 and Nov 25, 2026 captures of Fireworks' pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Spot/best-effort capacity discount increased from 30% to 50% off Standard pricing; now limited to GLM-5.3 (more models to follow) — Kimi K3 and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash lost Spot-tier eligibility. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change). The current page lists Fine Tuning at $0.50 per 1M training tokens, LoRA SFT (models up to 16B params), On-Demand at $13 per GPU hour (B200 180GB) + $15 per GPU hour (B300 288GB) and Serverless at $0.08 per 10M input tokens (embeddings, up to 150M params) + $0.16 per 10M input tokens (embeddings, 150M-350M params).
It lands 11 days after Fireworks' previous change, "Fireworks lists Kimi K3.1 Pro on serverless at $2.00 input, $10.00 output per 1M" (Nov 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fireworks adds SOC 2, ISO 27001, 27701 and 42001 badges to pricing page" (Oct 26, 2026), "Fireworks adds Muse Spark Open, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash to Serverless Training" (Oct 17, 2026) and "Fireworks adds GLM 5.3 Flash with 200K context, per-token training pricing" (Sep 27, 2026). Fireworks has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Corel, Figma, Affinity and Sketch also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
