What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Nightfall AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Premier Edition card: policy line now 'Intelligent, contextual policy and no-policy experience' (was 'One policy'); inclusion line renamed 'All Complete features' to 'All Foundation features'. In detail: Premier Edition card: policy line now pitches an "Intelligent, contextual policy and no-policy experience" (was "One policy across endpoint, SaaS, and AI agents"). Inclusion line "All Complete features" corrected to "All Foundation features", matching the Foundation Edition card name already in use; FAQ package names updated to match. Prices still hidden. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction.
It lands 7 days after Nightfall AI's previous change, "Nightfall AI: plans renamed to Foundation Edition / Premier Edition" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Nightfall AI: Complete + AI Agent Security storage cut 500GB → 150GB" (Aug 11, 2026) and "Nightfall AI: discontinued DDR/DEX standalone plans + Complete storage cut 500GB→150GB" (Aug 5, 2026). Nightfall AI has been tracked since 2026-04-26.
In the same window Datadog, Microsoft, Cloudflare and Splunk also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
