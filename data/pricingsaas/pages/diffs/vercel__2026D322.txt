What happened
Between the Nov 17, 2026 and Nov 18, 2026 captures of Vercel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro plan Blob overage rates decreased: Simple Operations from $0.40 to $0.35 per 1M, and Advanced Operations from $5 to $4.50 per 1M (now labeled 'Starting at', suggesting volume tiering). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $20.
It lands 12 days after Vercel's previous change, "Vercel boosts Pro trace retention 14x to 14 days, Hobby to 3 days" (Nov 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vercel adds usage pricing for Beta CI and Observability monitors" (Nov 1, 2026), "Vercel slashes Firewall limits, adds metered Deployment Storage at $0.10/GB" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Vercel: Vercel Drives storage launched at $0.05/GB-month, metered reads/writes" (Oct 20, 2026). Vercel has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Render, Aws and Railway also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
