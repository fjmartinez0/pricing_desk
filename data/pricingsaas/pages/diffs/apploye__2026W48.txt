What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Apploye's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Promo banner (40% off Power, 30% off Activity Insights for first 3 months) deadline extended from 19 Nov to 23 Nov; Break management rows added to comparison table for Elite, Power, Enterprise: break time (paid & unpaid), one-click breaks, break policies, breaks report; Starter not included. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change, feature added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Elite at $4.50, Power at $8, Activity Insights at $3, More Screenshots at $2 and Video Recording at $4.
It lands 7 days after Apploye's previous change, "Apploye: AI Adoption Tracking replaces 'AI Reporting & Insights (Soon)' in Power" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Apploye adds auto-discard idle time to Elite, Power, Enterprise plans" (Nov 8, 2026), "Apploye cuts Power plan to $4.80/user/mo with 40% intro promo through Nov 19" (Nov 1, 2026) and "Apploye launches 40% off Power promo as free Starter plan loses app-tracking, leave-document and expense allowances" (Oct 26, 2026). Apploye has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Culture Amp, BambooHR and 15five also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
