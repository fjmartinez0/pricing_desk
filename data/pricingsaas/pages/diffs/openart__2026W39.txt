What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of OpenArt's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Access to OpenArt MCP now includes a 10% discount on Plus, Pro, and Wonder plans. Limited-time promo banner offers unlimited Flux 3 720p video generation on the Wonder plan through October 31. In detail: Limited-time promo: unlimited Flux 3 720p video generation on the Wonder plan through October 31, announced via a new banner above the plan cards; OpenArt MCP access now includes a 10% discount on Plus, Pro, and Wonder plans (Starter unaffected); new FAQ entry explains the discount. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit increase, discount added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Essential at $7, Advanced at $14.5, Team Plan at $17.5, Infinite at $28 and Wonder at $120.
It lands 6 days after OpenArt's previous change, "OpenArt adds unlimited Flux 3 720p video to Wonder plan through Oct 31" (Sep 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenArt swaps Flux 3 promo for 30% off MiniMax H3, any plan" (Sep 22, 2026), "OpenArt pulls MiniMax H3 Max unlimited video promo pushing Wonder upgrades" (Sep 11, 2026) and "OpenArt narrows Unlimited Generations perk to Wonder, swaps in MiniMax H3 Max" (Sep 7, 2026). OpenArt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
