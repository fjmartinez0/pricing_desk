What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of DeepSeek's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: DeepSeek merged v4-flash and vision-exp into one deepseek-flash (V4.1) model at lower cache-hit/miss and output prices; new footnote confirms V4 Pro pricing stays unchanged past Sept 14, 2026. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists DeepSeek Flash at $0.15 per 1M input tokens (cache miss) + $0.3 per 100M input tokens (cache hit) and DeepSeek V4 Pro at $0.66 per 1M input tokens (cache miss) + $2.2 per 100M input tokens (cache hit).
It lands 2 days after DeepSeek's previous change, "DeepSeek reverses V4 Pro retirement, keeps API billing unchanged past Sept 14" (Sep 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "DeepSeek slashes Flash pricing up to 57%, retires V4 Pro Sept 14" (Sep 11, 2026), "DeepSeek adds vision model, exempts weekends from peak pricing" (Aug 24, 2026) and "DeepSeek: API pricing restructured to peak/off-peak billing, prices roughly double" (Aug 17, 2026). DeepSeek has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
