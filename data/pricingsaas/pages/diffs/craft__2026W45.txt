Craft shifts Team plan to $5 per member, adds free student Plus
Verified Nov 2, 2026·Collaboration & Productivity·Direction: neutral
The changeTeam, Enterprise, Student packaging — package change.
2 changes·2 captures·Oct 26, 2026 → Nov 2, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Craft's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Team moved from $50/mo fixed bundle (up to 10 accounts) to $5/member/mo (min 3 members); Enterprise (contact us), free student Plus and up to 50% nonprofit/education discount added; AI allowance metric changed from monthly assistant credits (15 free, 50 Plus) to AI sessions (~20 lifetime free, ~250-500/yr Plus, higher on Family/Team). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Team at $5, Plus at $6.40 and Family at $12.
It lands 112 days after Craft's previous change, "Craft's Summer Sale offers 40% off Plus and Family — but list prices stay put" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Craft quietly drops promo code offer from pricing page" (Jun 29, 2026), "Craft: Plus and Family plans cut 40% ($8→$4.8, $15→$9/month)" (Jul 2, 2026) and "Craft adds promo code offer across all pricing plans" (Jun 8, 2026). Craft has been tracked since 2026-03-18.
In the same window Sketch, Notion, Figma, Webflow and Framer also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
