What happened
Between the Aug 17, 2026 and Aug 24, 2026 captures of Twilio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Twilio Video pricing model overhauled: dropped the 5,000 free hours plus $1/active-user-hour or $150/named-user-per-month structure in favor of pure pay-as-you-go billing at $0.004 per participant per minute. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 7, 2026) lists Conversation Intelligence at $0.01, Enterprise Knowledge at $0.02, Twilio SendGrid Marketing Campaigns at $15, Twilio for Salesforce at $15 and Twilio SendGrid Email API at $19.95.
It lands 51 days after Twilio's previous change, "Twilio: Video/Conversation Intelligence pricing overhaul + 4 new products" (Jul 4, 2026). Twilio has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Plivo, Telnyx, Bird and Google Cloud also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
