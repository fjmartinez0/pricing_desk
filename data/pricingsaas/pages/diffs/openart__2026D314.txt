What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 10, 2026 captures of OpenArt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Promo on premium model usage widened — was 30% off GPT Image 2.6 generations (Pro/Wonder, until Nov 17); now up to 50% off Kling 4.0, GPT Image 2.6, and Recraft V4.1 on Pro and Wonder plans. In detail: Promo discount on premium model usage widened: was 30% off GPT Image 2.6 generations (Pro/Wonder, until Nov 17); now up to 50% off Kling 4.0, GPT Image 2.6, and Recraft V4.1 on Pro and Wonder plans.
