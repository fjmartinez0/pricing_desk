What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of SiteGPT's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: FAQ file upload size limit raised from 10 MB to 25 MB per file; oversized files are now split into parts rather than sent to support. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $39, Growth at $79, Scale at $259, WhatsApp (Addon) at $39 and Remove SiteGPT Branding at $39.
It lands 35 days after SiteGPT's previous change, "SiteGPT adds WhatsApp add-on at $39/mo, or $468 billed yearly" (Oct 5, 2026). SiteGPT has been tracked since 2026-08-28.
