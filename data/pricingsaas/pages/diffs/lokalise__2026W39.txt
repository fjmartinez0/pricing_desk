What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Lokalise's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Explorer, Growth, and Advanced plan prices increased: Explorer $144→$149/mo, Growth $375→$379/mo, Advanced $999→$1049/mo.
