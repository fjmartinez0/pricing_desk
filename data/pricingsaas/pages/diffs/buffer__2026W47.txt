What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Buffer's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Start Page (link-in-bio landing page) removed from the plan features and comparison tables; Insights analytics history for Essentials and Team changed from Unlimited to 2-year history; Free stays at 30-day. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered, feature removed). The current page lists Essentials at $5 and Team at $10.
It lands 7 days after Buffer's previous change, "Buffer Free plan now gets 250 tags and Notes for team collaboration" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Buffer boosts API rate limits up to 3x and makes 30-day caps per channel" (Nov 1, 2026). Buffer has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Hootsuite, Coschedule, Sproutsocial and Sendible also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
