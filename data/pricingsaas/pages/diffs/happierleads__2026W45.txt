What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Happierleads's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Annual billing no longer advertises inclusion of the 175M contact database (removed from plan summary and annual-billing note); 30% annual discount unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists White Label at $0.10 leads, Starter at $1.17, Growth at $183, Pro at $356 and Business at $493.
It lands 119 days after Happierleads's previous change, "Happierleads cuts free trial to 7 days, adds AI MCP Server integration" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Happierleads: Pricing overhauled to usage-based pay-per-lead tiers" (Jul 3, 2026), "Happierleads launches 6-tier pricing, cuts entry price to $99/mo" (May 26, 2026) and "Happierleads debuts Master Inbox as key edge over rivals in new comparison" (May 5, 2026).
