What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Three models added to the pay-as-you-go rate table: GPT-6.1 Terra ($1/$5), Kimi K3.1 Pro ($2/$10) and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 ($0.30/$1.20) per 1M tokens. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pay as you go at $0.10 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.50 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands 4 days after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB adds GPT-6.1 Terra pricing at $1 input, $5 output per 1M tokens" (Oct 29, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB adds Kimi K3.1 Pro and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model pricing" (Oct 27, 2026), "MindsDB adds Gemini 4 Argon and Meta Muse Wonder 1.0 token pricing" (Oct 19, 2026) and "MindsDB adds Gemini 4 Argon pricing at $2 input, $10 output per million" (Oct 16, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Huggingface and Alteryx also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
