What happened
Between the Nov 22, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Krea's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Basic plan's monthly Seedance video allowance dropped from ~31 Seedance 2.0 Fast videos to 16 Seedance 2.5 videos (model upgraded from 2.0 Fast to 2.5, but quota nearly halved). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered). The current page lists Basic at $5, Pro at $21, Max at $63 and Business at $160.
It lands 4 days after Krea's previous change, "Krea slashes model prices up to 75% in Black Friday discount blitz" (Nov 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Krea launches Black Friday sale with up to 70% off yearly plans" (Nov 18, 2026), "Krea dangles a welcome discount on first plans for new free sign-ups" (Nov 15, 2026) and "Krea cuts Nano Banana 2.1 image estimates ~11% on every plan as cards switch to quoting 2.1" (Nov 7, 2026). Krea has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
