Dropbox launches AI in Dropbox on Standard and Advanced with 250-750 credits
Verified Sep 28, 2026·Business Operations·Direction: up
The changeStandard, Advanced feature — feature added.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 21, 2026 → Sep 28, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Dropbox's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Standard and Advanced replaced the 'Coming soon: Dash preview' AI teaser with a live 'AI in Dropbox' feature (image search, chat Q&A, insights) gated by AI credits: 250/mo Standard, 750/mo Advanced. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Plus at $9.99, Standard at $15 and Advanced at $24.
It lands 56 days after Dropbox's previous change, "Dropbox: Add-ons pricing section removed (Replay, Sign, DocSend, Reclaim.ai + more)" (Aug 3, 2026). Dropbox has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Microsoft, Google and Nextcloud also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
