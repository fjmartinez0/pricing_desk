What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Phrase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Platform name in pricing intro changed from Phrase Language Intelligence Platform to Phrase Localization Platform; prices and plans unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Freelancer at $27, Software UI/UX at $525, Professional at $525 and Team at $1,245.
It lands 152 days after Phrase's previous change, "Phrase rebrands platform to Language Intelligence" (Jun 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Phrase hikes Team plan price 19% to $1,245 per month" (Apr 6, 2026). Phrase has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Crowdin, X, Lokalise and Contentstack also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
