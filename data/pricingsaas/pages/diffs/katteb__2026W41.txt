What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Katteb's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Enterprise now shows a starting price of $1,000 a month; free account changes from 300 free chat credits to a free AI assistant that searches the web; Goals plan adds landing pages with lead forms and Google Meet meetings booked into Google Calendar; Enterprise adds white-label dashboard on own domain, extended run time and 10 team members; Agent time now quantified: Lite 30 hours/week, Goals 12. In detail: Goals plan adds landing pages with lead forms and Google Meet meetings booked into Google Calendar; Enterprise adds a white-label dashboard on own domain and extended run time, but team members are now capped at 10 (was unlimited) and "Agents on your own servers" is no longer listed; Agent time now quantified: Lite 30 hours/week, Goals 120 hours/week; unlimited chat, images and video added; Lite/Goals goal counts reworded to agents per goal. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed, feature added, limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Lite at $15.83, Business at $82.5 and Enterprise at $1,000.
It lands 7 days after Katteb's previous change, "Katteb overhauls Lite/Goals: goal-scheduling replaces model credits, agent li" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Katteb: full pricing re-platform to AI chat/agents model" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Katteb: Pro plan price cut ($29->$17/mo)" (Jul 13, 2026).
