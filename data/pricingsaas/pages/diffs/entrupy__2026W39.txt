What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Entrupy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Jewelry added as a new supported authentication category — a 4th tab (alongside Bags & Leather Goods, Footwear, and Apparel).
