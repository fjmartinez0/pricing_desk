What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of RunPod's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New FAQ section 'GPU prices per hour on Runpod' discloses Community Cloud hourly rates for H100 PCIe ($1.99), H100 SXM ($2.69), A100 PCIe ($1.19), A100 SXM ($1.39), and H200 ($3.59) alongside existing Secure Cloud rates. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Storage at $0.05 per GB/month, Pods - Community Cloud at $0.16 per GPU-hour, Pods - Secure Cloud at $0.27 per GPU-hour, Serverless at $0.58 per GPU-hour and Clusters at $1.79 per GPU-hour.
It lands 7 days after RunPod's previous change, "RunPod raises Serverless GPU prices up to 25%, drops savings claim" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "RunPod raises GPU rates: H200 cluster jumps 47% to $6.35/hr" (Oct 19, 2026), "RunPod raises GPU cloud rates, B200 jumps to $7.99/hr and H100 to $3.99/hr" (Oct 12, 2026) and "RunPod hikes GPU rates on Pods: H100 up to $3.49/hr, A100 to $1.59/hr" (Sep 7, 2026). RunPod has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Together AI, Lambda Labs and Modal also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
