What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Fresh2Wow's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Agency split into selectable sizes (8 / 15 / 25 brands, +25 custom): the single $499/mo ($4,990/yr) 15-brand plan is replaced by an 8-brand entry size 'from $249/mo' ($2,490/yr); 15/25-brand sizes are demo/contact. Agency now also gets the 7-day free trial (8-brand size), and all trials now require a card upfront with no charge until day 8 (100 credits and the +7-day no-publish extension remain); Creator connected accounts raised from 10 to 25 (Starter also 3 → 8); Agency card now lists its 8-brand entry size (5 team members, 8 brands, 40 accounts, 10,000 credits/mo) instead of the old 10 seats / 15 brands / 20,000 credits — a size option, not a cut. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised, packaging change). The current page lists Lite at $13.33, Starter at $24.17, Creator at $80.83, Agency at $207.50 and Credit Pack 500 at $19 per use.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Fresh2Wow. Fresh2Wow has been tracked since 2026-11-06.
