What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of TradingView's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan-card CTAs on Essential, Plus, Premium and Ultimate changed from 'Try free for 30 days' (14 days on Ultimate) / 'or skip trial and pay now' to 'Start now'; trial length no longer stated on cards (comparison table still shows 'Try free' buttons). Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essential at $12.95, Plus at $29.95, Premium at $59.95 and Ultimate at $199.95.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for TradingView. TradingView has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
In the same window Robinhood, NinjaTrader and Interactive Brokers also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
