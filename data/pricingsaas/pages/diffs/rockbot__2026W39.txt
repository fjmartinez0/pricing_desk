What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Rockbot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan card taglines removed and feature bullets rewritten across Music, Digital Signage, Business TV, and Music Videos cards; positioning changed but plans and prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists TV at $25, Digital Signage at $25, Music at $25, Rockbot Request at $25 and Audio Messaging at $10.
It lands 41 days after Rockbot's previous change, "Rockbot: TV curated channels increased 50+ → 65+" (Aug 11, 2026). Rockbot has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
