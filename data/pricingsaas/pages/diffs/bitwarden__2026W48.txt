What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Bitwarden's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Bitwarden Send now supports vault items in addition to text and files for Premium and Families plans. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Premium at $1.65, Families at $3.99, Teams at $4 and Enterprise at $6.
It lands 143 days after Bitwarden's previous change, "Bitwarden: Free plan demoted from pricing comparison" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Bitwarden Deprioritizes Free Tier, Pushes Premium as Default" (Apr 13, 2026), "Bitwarden Nearly Doubles Premium Price to $19.80/Year, Boosts Storage to 5 GB" (Apr 4, 2026) and "Bitwarden adds phishing blocker to Premium plan" (Feb 22, 2026). Bitwarden has been tracked since 2023-12-03.
In the same window Enpass and Lastpass also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
