What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of RevenueHero's pricing page, Pulse recorded 6 changes: Enterprise renamed Advanced with platform fee $79 to $159/mo; Outbound and Lite plans removed; SDR booking moves to a $20/mo Handoff seat add-on; Native enrichment now $79/mo for 500 credits. In detail: Advanced (formerly Enterprise) platform fee doubled from $79 to $159/mo billed yearly ($99 to $199 monthly); seat price unchanged at $35/$45; Native enrichment add-on changed from $39/mo (0-200 credit tier, yearly) with 200-500 and 500+ credit tiers to a single $79/mo yearly ($99 monthly) package of 500 credits/mo; Lite plan ($15/user/mo) removed; Inbound Enterprise plan renamed Advanced; Standalone Outbound Essentials plan ($20/user/mo yearly, $25 monthly, no platform fee) removed; SDR booking now sold only as a Handoff seat add-on on Advanced; New Handoff seat add-on on Advanced: $20 per SDR/mo billed yearly ($240/yr), $25 monthly. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, add-on change, plan removed, plan renamed, new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $79, Advanced at $159, Handoff Seat at $20, Spam Prevention at $30 and Native Enrichment at $79.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for RevenueHero. RevenueHero has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Dialpad and Outreach also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
