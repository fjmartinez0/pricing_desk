What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Tella's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Included monthly AI credits raised 50%: Pro 3,000 to 4,500 per user, Premium 6,000 to 9,000 per user; Added Auto Capture (beta): an AI agent records a web page for you with an animated cursor; runs on credits and needs a paid plan.
