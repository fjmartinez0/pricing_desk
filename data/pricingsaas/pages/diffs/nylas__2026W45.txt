What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Nylas's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Accelerator Package now a priced optional add-on at $2,400/year for Pro Annual (previously bundled with Annual); includes Shared Google OAuth App, Hos Also adds Professional Services (from $2,000/session). In detail: Add-on prices now published in a new Add-ons section: Accelerator Package (Shared Google OAuth App, Custom Auth Domain, Hosted Auth Branding, Custom Tracking Domain) $2,400/year, optional for Pro Annual only and included in Enterprise contracts (previously listed as an unpriced paid add-on); Professional Services from $2,000/session. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $15 and Pro at $43.
It lands 35 days after Nylas's previous change, "Nylas Notetaker adds 5 capabilities: dedup, pause, retention, avatars" (Sep 28, 2026). Nylas has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
