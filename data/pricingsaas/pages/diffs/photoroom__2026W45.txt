What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Photoroom's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Ultra video output cut from 4K to HD: '4k video/image resolution' row became '4K images + HD video' (4K now applies to images only; same locked row on Pro/Max). Video Generator row renamed 'Video for ads and product pages' (Max included, Pro locked; Ultra 'More video ads for heavy use'); Ultra tagline reworded. Prices and credits unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $7.50, Max at $20.99 and Ultra at $82.50.
It lands 14 days after Photoroom's previous change, "Photoroom moves Enterprise to usage-based annual balance pricing and rewrites plan taglines" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Photoroom triples AI credit pool across Pro, Max, Ultra plans—no price hike" (Aug 17, 2026), "Photoroom: AI credits cut up to 73% + AI Backgrounds split into 4 tiers" (Aug 11, 2026) and "Photoroom: AI Fashion Models 2K generation limit removed from Pro" (Aug 5, 2026). Photoroom has been tracked since 2026-06-26.
In the same window Gemelo, Writer, Voxel51, Wispr Flow and Hive also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
