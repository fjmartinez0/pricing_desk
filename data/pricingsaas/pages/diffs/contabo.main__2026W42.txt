What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Contabo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Cloud VPS 6, 8, 12, 16 and 18 now advertise 'Now with NVMe' with extra NVMe storage alongside SSD (e.g. 200GB SSD / 100GB NVMe); prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Cloud VPS 4 at $5.28, Cloud VPS 6 at $7.2, Cloud VPS 8 at $13.44, Cloud VPS 12 at $24 and Cloud VPS 16 at $35.6.
It lands 28 days after Contabo's previous change, "Contabo Main: Cloud VPS 18 & Performance VPS tier prices cut ~17% + 24-month term" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Contabo Main slashes dedicated server price 18% in flash promo" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Contabo Main: VPS/VDS/Storage VPS prices cut ~5-11% across the board" (Jul 27, 2026). Contabo has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window OVHcloud, Vultr, Digitalocean and Google Cloud also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
