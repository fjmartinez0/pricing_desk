What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Unblocked's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Platform features list adds Unblocked Code and replaces the Unblocked Chat row with Developer Q&A. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Platform at $29.
