What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Unleashed Software's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Standard support (£59/mo) added Annual Account Review and Customer Success Team access; Premier support (£159/mo) added 8 hours of Consultancy time; both dropped the 2 Hour Response SLA.
