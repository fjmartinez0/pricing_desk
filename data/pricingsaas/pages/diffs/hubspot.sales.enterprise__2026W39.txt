What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of HubSpot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Sales Hub Professional's highlighted card features swapped Agent Hub and Sales workspace for Deal Plans (Beta) and Sequences; Enterprise swapped AI transcript enrichment (Beta) for Standard sandbox account. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $7, Professional at $90, Enterprise at $150 and HubSpot Credits at $9 per 1,000 credits.
It lands 80 days after HubSpot's previous change, "Hubspot Sales Enterprise Sales: Breeze Agent credits cut 100->50/convo + $9/1K credits shown" (Jul 3, 2026). HubSpot has been tracked since 2025-01-01.
In the same window Salesforce, Microsoft, Zoho, Freshworks and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
