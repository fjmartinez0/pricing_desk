What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Guusto's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Lite plan ($125/mo, 'Crawl' tier) lost its self-serve 'Start 30 Day Trial' signup; the CTA now reads 'Talk to Sales' and links to a demo request instead of instant trial signup. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Premium at $1, Essential at $4 and Lite at $125.
It lands 70 days after Guusto's previous change, "Guusto cuts Lite plan to $125/mo, gates Essential trial behind sales call" (Jul 20, 2026). Guusto has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Discourse, Workato, Bonusly and 15five also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
