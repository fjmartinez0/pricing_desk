What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Cribl's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Cribl Detect added as a new product ("A new way to SIEM") with its own Detect Pricing link alongside Stream, Edge, Search and Lake. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $0.27 per GB ingested (Stream cloud worker) + $0.21 per GB ingested (Edge node), Enterprise at $0.21 per GB ingested (Edge node) + $0.32 per GB ingested (Stream cloud worker), Cribl Insights (7-day) at $450, Cribl Insights (1-month) at $900 and Cribl Insights (3-month) at $1,980.
It lands 7 days after Cribl's previous change, "Cribl reveals per-product pricing: usage-based fees for Stream, Edge, Search," (Sep 28, 2026). Cribl has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Datadog, Elastic, Sumologic and Splunk also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
