What happened
Between the Oct 16, 2026 and Oct 17, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New model Reflection Beam added to serverless inference pricing at $0.40 input ($0.04 cached) and $1.20 output per 1M tokens. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Managed Storage at $0.16 per GiB/month, Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, Sandbox (Code Interpreter) at $0.03 per session (60 min), GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100) and GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days).
It lands one day after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI makes Muse Spark Open free until Oct 18, adds Nemotron 3.5 Super VL" (Oct 16, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI ends Kimi K3 cached-input promo; price returns to $0.30/1M" (Oct 14, 2026), "Together AI cuts GPU Cluster minimum reservation from 7 days to 3, prices hold" (Oct 8, 2026) and "Together AI cuts Kimi K3 token prices 10% while Qwen3.8 Flash rises" (Oct 6, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
In the same window Huggingface, Google, Mistral AI, Anthropic and OpenAI also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
