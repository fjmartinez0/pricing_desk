What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Draftable's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Basic Compare card audience label changed from 'For Non-Legal Enterprise' to 'For Non-Legal Teams'; Legal card now says 'Everything in Basic PLUS'. Plan name and $129/user/yr unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Enterprise & Non-Legal at $10.75 and Law Firms & Legal Teams at $21.75.
It lands 42 days after Draftable's previous change, "Draftable adds Compare API plan, renames tiers, delays Mac app to 2027" (Oct 11, 2026). Draftable has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
