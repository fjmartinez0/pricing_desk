What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Melio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added discounted foreign-currency global card payment fees by transfer size: 2.70% from $1, 2.40% from $50,000, 1.90% from $100,000, down from the standard 2.90%. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Boost at $8, Core at $8 and Unlimited at $64.
It lands 35 days after Melio's previous change, "Melio triples free trial from 30 to 90 days before paid conversion" (Sep 14, 2026). Melio has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Stripe and Squarespace also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
