What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Hevo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free plan removed; page split into usage-based (Starter/Professional) and fixed pricing; Business Critical renamed Enterprise with fixed price for unlimited data volume per pipeline; Enterprise plan now lists unlimited data volume per pipeline as a feature, with a new comparison table row. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter - 5M Events at $265, Starter - 20M Events at $440, Professional - 20M Events at $750, Starter - 50M Events at $835 and Professional - 50M Events at $1,320.
It lands 111 days after Hevo's previous change, "Hevo trims annual discount to 12%, pushing Starter and Pro prices up" (Jul 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hevo: cuts Starter and Professional pricing by ~40%" (Apr 3, 2026), "Hevo Announces 25% Price Increase for Starter and Professional Plans, Effective Immediately" (Jan 11, 2026) and "Hevo Introduces Contract Buyout Program for ETL Customers" (Q3 2025). Hevo has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Matillion, Zapier and Workato also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
