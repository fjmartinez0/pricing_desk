What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Leapsome's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Contact-sales pricing replaced by public per-employee/month prices: HRIS Foundation €7 (free with any Talent Suite module until Dec 31), Pro €10, modules €3-7, add-ons from €1; Accelerator program waived until Dec 31. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists HRIS at $7.
It lands 14 days after Leapsome's previous change, "Leapsome adds SOC 2 Type II and EU AI Act ready badges to its trust list" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Leapsome: New 'Recruiting' module added to the Leapsome platform (Early Access): candidate pipeline management,…" (Jul 20, 2026). Leapsome has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window SurveyMonkey, BambooHR and 15five also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
