What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of scite's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Team blurb reworded: 'Add seats' became 'Put your whole team on Pro'; 'Start with a small team, Pro at $40 per seat now, Enterprise later' line removed.
