What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Village's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Business subscription: included credits 9,000 to 7,500/mo; extra member seats $19 to $1/mo; annual discount Save 20% to Save 10%; VIP white-glove 60% off promo ($2,000/mo from $5,000) deadline extended from Oct 17 to Nov 2; Core plan gains Prioritized network access and Unlimited autopilot (10,000/autopilot); contacts/calendar sync bullet reworded to Advanced relationships incl. Funding. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price decrease, discount change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Core at $1, Business at $134, VIP at $2,000, Forward-deployed at $10,000 and API Platform at $10 per active user.
It lands 7 days after Village's previous change, "Village launches $1/seat Core plan, adds $10k Forward-deployed tier" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Village: Free profile enrichment capped to 3mo cache, older data costs 1 credit" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Village.ai: Credit-based usage system launched across all plans" (Aug 17, 2026). Village has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
