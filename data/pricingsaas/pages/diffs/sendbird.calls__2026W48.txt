What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Sendbird's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New free trial offer for Sendbird Calls: up to 20,000 participant-minutes free (based on peer-to-peer voice), with Start a free trial CTA. In detail: New Free trial card on the Calls pricing page: up to 20,000 participant-minutes free (based on peer-to-peer voice). Free-trial CTAs existed before, but the trial allowance was not published; Voice/Video per-minute rates unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change). The current page lists Voice Calls at $0 per minute per user (peer-to-peer) + $0 per minute per user (server-relayed) and Video Calls at $0 per minute per user (peer-to-peer) + $0 per minute per user (server-relayed).
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Sendbird. Sendbird has been tracked since 2025-10-04.
