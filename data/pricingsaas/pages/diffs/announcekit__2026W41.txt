What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of AnnounceKit's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: MAU definition no longer states that traffic on the public changelog page never counts toward monthly active users. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Feature Requests & Roadmap at $39, Changelog at $49, NPS at $49, Knowledge Base at $55 and Product Tours at $55.
