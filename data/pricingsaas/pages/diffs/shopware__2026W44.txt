What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Shopware's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Top banner promo 'Shopware Payments ramp-up pricing for 12 months. Limited time only.' removed; replaced by Gartner Magic Quadrant banner. Plan prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. Before that Pulse recorded "Shopware drops image editor from Intelligence+ AI add-on across all editions" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Shopware extends free Intelligence+/Nexus request threshold trial to October 2026" (Aug 17, 2026).
