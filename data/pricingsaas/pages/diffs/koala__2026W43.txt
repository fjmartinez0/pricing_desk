Koala updates word-count footnote to name GPT-6.1 Sol as 2x model
Verified Oct 19, 2026·Collaboration & Productivity·Direction: neutral
The changeFeature — feature change.

What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Koala's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Word-count footnote now names GPT-6.1 Sol (previously GPT-6 Sol) as a 2x word-count model; GPT-6 Luna base and other multipliers unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $20, Professional at $39, Boost at $79, Growth at $143 and Elite at $280.
It lands 21 days after Koala's previous change, "Koala: AI MCP integration exits beta, now included with every paid plan" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Koala hikes entry plan to $25/mo, adds Brand DNA & agent chat" (Sep 21, 2026), "Koala adds $9 Essentials tier, drops Google Sheets integration" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Koala AI: Essentials tier cut, Boost repackaged with Brand DNA agent + credits" (Aug 31, 2026). Koala has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Bearly AI and Notion also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
