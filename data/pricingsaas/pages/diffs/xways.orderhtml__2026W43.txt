What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of X-Ways Software's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Small list price adjustments: Forensics non-perpetual up $5, perpetual down $5; WinHex up $1 across editions; Investigator 5+ down $5; Imager 5+ up $1; training from $1,445 to $1,449.
