What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Mosaic's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Chello Max AI add-on ($200/user/month, unlimited AI briefs, highest credit allotment) removed from the Chello AI features section; only Base (included) and Pro ($50) remain; Chello Base AI tier expanded from 'all Admins today, all roles coming Nov 1, 2026' to 'for all users'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, add-on removed). The current page lists Chello Pro at $50.
It lands 27 days after Mosaic's previous change, "Mosaic launches $200/user Chello Max AI add-on with unlimited briefs" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mosaic softens 5x ROI guarantee, moves details behind Contact sales" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Mosaic consolidates to 2-tier pricing, adds $50 Chello Pro AI add-on" (Oct 11, 2026). Mosaic has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window SmartHR, Simployer, SeekOut, Praxedo and Paychex also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
“Mosaicapp” is the name Mosaic’s pricing page uses; Pulse files the change under Mosaic, the company it tracks.
