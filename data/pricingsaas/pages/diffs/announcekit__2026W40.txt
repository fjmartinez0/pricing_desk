What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of AnnounceKit's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Tiered per-project plans (Essentials/Growth/Scale/Enterprise) replaced with fully modular pricing: 6 standalone per-product tiles plus a new 10-item add-on catalog; annual discount changed from 15% to ~20%; MAU allowance introduced: workspace now capped at 10,000 MAU included then $25 per 5,000 overage, replacing prior "No MAU-based costs / No MAU-visitor limits, unlimited visitors" messaging on. In detail: MAU allowance introduced: workspace now capped at 10,000 MAU included then $25 per 5,000 overage, replacing prior "No MAU-based costs / No MAU-visitor limits, unlimited visitors" messaging on the old tiered plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, limit decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Feature Requests & Roadmap at $39, Changelog at $49, NPS at $49, Knowledge Base at $55 and Product Tours at $55.
It lands 196 days after AnnounceKit's previous change, "AnnounceKit updates plan positioning across all subscription tiers" (Mar 16, 2026). AnnounceKit has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
