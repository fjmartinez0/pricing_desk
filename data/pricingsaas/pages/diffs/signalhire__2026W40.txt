What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of SignalHire's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Database access claim increased from 850M+ to 900M+ profiles, shown in hero copy and on Emails, Phones, Emails & Phones, and Enterprise plan feature lists; prices unchanged.
