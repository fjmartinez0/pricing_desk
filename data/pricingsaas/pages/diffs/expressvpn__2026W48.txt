What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of ExpressVPN's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday sale lowers 2-year promo rates: Basic $2.99 to $2.79/mo, Advanced to $3.99/mo, Express Pro to $6.99/mo; savings badges up to 81%. In detail: Black Friday sale ("Save up to 81%" banner) cuts 2-year promo rates: Basic $2.99 to $2.79/mo, Advanced $4.49 to $3.99/mo, Express Pro $7.49 to $6.99/mo (save badges 81%/76%/69%); list totals $419.72/$475.72/$643.72 and renewal prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $2.99, Advanced at $4.49 and Express Pro at $7.49.
It lands 14 days after ExpressVPN's previous change, "ExpressVPN launches Black Friday sale with up to 80% off 2-year plans" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ExpressVPN adds ExpressAI quotas to Basic and Advanced plans" (Nov 1, 2026), "ExpressVPN trims ExpressAI models on Express Pro from 7 to 6" (Oct 4, 2026) and "ExpressVPN: Advanced $3.99->$4.49/mo, Express Pro $5.99->$7.49/mo" (Sep 28, 2026).
