What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of TradingView's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing cards reorganized into grouped sections; the generic 'buy professional market data' add-on icon now shows a specific capacity per plan: Essential 2, Plus 4, Premium 6, Ultimate unlimited subscriptions. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essential at $12.95, Plus at $29.95, Premium at $59.95 and Ultimate at $199.95.
It lands 35 days after TradingView's previous change, "TradingView Page2 drops free trial option, swaps in "Start now" buttons" (Oct 5, 2026). TradingView has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
In the same window Robinhood, NinjaTrader and Interactive Brokers also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
