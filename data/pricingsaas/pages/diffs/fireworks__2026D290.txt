What happened
Between the Oct 16, 2026 and Oct 17, 2026 captures of Fireworks's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Serverless Training API table adds Muse Spark Open (256K; $6.19 prefill, $15.48 sample, $18.58 train) and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (256K; $0.60 prefill, $3.60 sample, $4.98 train) per 1M tokens. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists On-Demand at $13 per GPU hour (B200 180GB) + $15 per GPU hour (B300 288GB), Serverless at $0.08 per 10M input tokens (embeddings, up to 150M params) + $0.16 per 10M input tokens (embeddings, 150M-350M params) and Fine Tuning at $0.5 per 1M training tokens, LoRA SFT (models up to 16B params).
It lands 19 days after Fireworks's previous change, "Fireworks adds GLM 5.3 Flash with 200K context, per-token training pricing" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fireworks adds GLM 5.3 to Serverless Training at $14.58/1M train tokens" (Sep 16, 2026), "Fireworks rebuilds Serverless Training API catalog: drops Qwen 3.5 9B, adds DeepSeek V4 Flash and Muse Glimmer 30B" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Fireworks adds DeepSeek V4 Flash, Muse Glimmer 30B to training API" (Sep 1, 2026). Fireworks has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Figma and Corel also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
