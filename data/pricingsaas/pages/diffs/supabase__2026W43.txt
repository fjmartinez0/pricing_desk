What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Supabase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan bullet changed from 'Designated Support manager' to 'Technical Account Management'. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $25 and Team at $599.
It lands 21 days after Supabase's previous change, "Supabase adds metered Log Ingestion at $0.50/GB after 20 GB on Pro and Team" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Supabase: Added OAuth 2.1 Server support across all plans" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Supabase: Enterprise plan drops BYO Cloud support" (Aug 31, 2026). Supabase has been tracked since 2026-03-15.
In the same window Vercel, Neon, MongoDB and Railway also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
