What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Microsoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Copilot plan highlights: 'Access to Cowork, Autopilot, and advanced AI experiences' now reads 'Cowork, Code, and advanced AI experiences' (Business Standard with Copilot, With Teams view). Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Microsoft Teams Essentials at $4, Microsoft 365 Business Basic (no Teams) at $5.40, Microsoft 365 Business Basic at $7, Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot (no Teams) at $20.30 and Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot at $23.50.
It lands 27 days after Microsoft's previous change, "Microsoft Teams Business adds Cowork and Autopilot AI, with usage-based billing" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Microsoft Teams Business Teams flags usage-based AI charges beyond license fees" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Teams Premium now needs a Teams Enterprise license; Buy now removed" (Sep 21, 2026). Microsoft has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window IBM, Salesforce and Google also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
