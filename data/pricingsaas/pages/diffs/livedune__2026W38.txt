What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of LiveDune's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: LiveDune's Agency plan (for SMM agencies and brands, 100 accounts) dropped from $275/month to $225/month, an 18% cut. Blogger and Corporate plan prices are unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 21, 2026) lists Blogger at $26.1, Corporate at $45 and Agency at $180.
It lands 34 days after LiveDune's previous change, "LiveDune: Blogger/Corporate/Agency price increase ($29→$33, $50→$60, $200→$275)" (Aug 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LiveDune: Blogger checker fake-like monitoring limit increased (50 → 100)" (Jul 3, 2026). LiveDune has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Sproutsocial, Sendible, Buffer, Hootsuite and Later also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
