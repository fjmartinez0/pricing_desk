What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of OVHcloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: GPU resource unit quota per notebook/training task/node doubled from 4 to 8 across AI Notebooks GPU, AI Training Jobs GPU, and AI Deploy GPU offerings. In detail: GPU resource unit quota doubled from 4 to 8 per notebook/training task/node across AI Notebooks GPU, AI Training Jobs GPU, and AI Deploy GPU offerings. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction.
