What happened
Between the Nov 25, 2026 and Nov 26, 2026 captures of Modal's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Container concurrency limits raised: Starter 100 to 250 containers; Team 5000 to 10,000 containers (GPU concurrency unchanged at 10 and 50); New Model APIs entry: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 at $0.375 prompt, $0.0075 cached, $1.50 completion per 1M tokens (existing $0.25 variant remains). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised, feature added). The current page lists Team at $250, Compute resources at $1.10 NVIDIA A10G instance hour + $1.95 L40S GPU hour and Sandbox + Notebooks compute at $2.40 per 100 GiB-Hour + $14.19 per 100 core-hours.
It lands 10 days after Modal's previous change, "Modal adds per-token pricing for 12 open-weight models like Kimi K3.1 Pro" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Modal adds Inkling 2 Small at $0.30/$1.00 per 1M tokens, $0.03 cached" (Nov 13, 2026), "Modal drops Notebooks from its Sandbox pricing section and product footer" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Modal drops Notebooks from Sandbox pricing, bills at 3x non-preemptible rates" (Oct 26, 2026). Modal has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Together AI, Huggingface and Lambda Labs also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
