What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Luma AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Seedance 2.0 credit costs roughly halved across all resolutions - Text/Image-to-Video 480p $47->24, 720p 107->51, 1080p 240->126, 4K 959->261 credits/sec; Video-to-Video 480p 28->15, 720p 64->32, 1080p 144->77, 4K 576->157 credits/sec. In detail: Seedance 2.0 video generation credit costs cut ~50%: T2V/I2V 480p 47->24, 720p 107->51, 1080p 240->126, 4K 959->261 credits/sec; V2V 480p 28->15, 720p 64->32, 1080p 144->77, 4K 576->157 credits/sec. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 23, 2026) lists Plus at $25, Pro at $75 and Ultra at $250.
It lands 2 days after Luma AI's previous change, "Luma AI slashes Seedance video model pricing up to 73% across all tiers" (Sep 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Luma AI unlocks 8K upscaling in SeedVR2, breaking past the old 4K cap for 25 c" (Sep 5, 2026), "Luma AI swaps Nano Banana for Seedream 5.0 Pro, drops Kling 2.6" (Sep 4, 2026) and "Luma AI slashes 2K Layers add-on to 105 credits, launches 4K tier at 175" (Sep 2, 2026). Luma AI has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
