Tatsoft adds $6,000 Electrical Package and unifies hot-standby redundancy
Verified Nov 9, 2026·Business Operations·Direction: up
The changeEdgeConnect positioning — positioning.
2 changes·2 captures·Nov 2, 2026 → Nov 9, 2026
What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Tatsoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Redundancy bullets on EdgeConnect, MachineHMI and Enterprise cards unified to 'Hot-standby redundancy' (previously Device Redundancy / Redundancy Available / Enterprise Redundancy); New Electrical Package add-on (IEC 61850 and IEC 60870-5 drivers) at USD 6,000 over base license; second server priced at 50% of primary. In detail: New Electrical Package add-on (IEC 61850 and IEC 60870-5 drivers) at USD 6,000 over the base license, now listed under the edition cards. The second-server-at-50% note restates the existing Redundancy/High Availability (+50%) pricing. Pulse files it as a packaging change (positioning, new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists EdgeConnect Gateway at $750, MachineHMI 150 at $900, EdgeConnect View at $1,700, MachineHMI 500 at $1,750 and MachineHMI 1500 at $3,000.
It lands 14 days after Tatsoft's previous change, "Tatsoft makes Community Edition free for production use up to 1,000 tags" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Tatsoft: Included connectors count raised from 100+ to 120+ across hero text and EdgeConnect card" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Tatsoft: Enterprise tiers gain unlimited clients" (Aug 31, 2026). Tatsoft has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
In the same window Microsoft and Salesforce also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
