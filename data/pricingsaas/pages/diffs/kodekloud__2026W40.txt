What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of KodeKloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Annual billing discount badge on the pricing toggle increased from 'Save up to 21%*' to 'Save up to 50%*', coinciding with the 'Autumn of Learning' promotional sale banner going live. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Standard at $15, Pro at $21 and AI at $29.
It lands 7 days after KodeKloud's previous change, "KodeKloud: annual discount reduced from 50% to 21%" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "KodeKloud raises annual discount from 21% to 50% in Skill-tember flash sale" (Sep 14, 2026), "KodeKloud doubles down: annual discount jumps to 50% off with new Summer Sale" (Aug 31, 2026) and "KodeKloud ends August Saving Sprint, annual savings drop from 50% to 21%" (Aug 24, 2026). KodeKloud has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
