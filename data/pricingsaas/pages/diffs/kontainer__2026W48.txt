What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Kontainer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Compliance row changed from 'ISAE3402, ISO27001 certificate' to 'ISAE3402 based on ISO27001 controls', dropping the ISO27001 certificate claim. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Core at $500, Professional at $695, Premium at $1,155 and Enterprise at $2,220.
It lands 7 days after Kontainer's previous change, "Kontainer adds LLM Brand Guide Module as $70 add-on across three plans" (Nov 16, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kontainer launches AI add-on, from $120 for 1TB up to $380 for 5TB" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Kontainer cuts Enterprise video streaming storage from 16TB to 4TB" (Oct 11, 2026). Kontainer has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Helium 10, Jungle Scout and Databox also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
