What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Rootly's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Essentials/Enterprise tiers replaced by per-product pricing: Incident Response $20/user/mo, On-Call $20/user/mo (additive), AI SRE 'Let's talk' (requires Incident Response), Enterprise custom volume pricing. In detail: Per-product Essentials/Enterprise tabs replaced by flat per-product cards plus a single cross-product Enterprise tier (Custom volume pricing). Prices unchanged: Incident Response $20/user/mo, On-Call $20/user/mo (now explicitly additive, $40 for both). AI SRE ('Let's talk') now requires Incident Response, previously usable standalone; Essentials tier name dropped. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Incident Response at $20 and On-Call at $20.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Rootly. Rootly has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
