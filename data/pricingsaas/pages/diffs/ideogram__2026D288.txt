What happened
Between the Oct 14, 2026 and Oct 15, 2026 captures of Ideogram's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Plans now list all image and video models, unlimited character consistency, best cost per credit (Pro), early access to collaboration (Team), and an Apps & API table section; Plus generation queue upgraded from Large to Larger; Team 'Minimum 2 users' note removed. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added, feature change) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Plus at $15, Team at $20, Pro at $42 and Pay as you go at $0.01 per credit.
It lands 10 days after Ideogram's previous change, "Ideogram more than doubles priority credits on paid plans, drops top-up fee" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Ideogram more than doubles priority credits on Plus, Pro and Team plans" (Oct 3, 2026), "Ideogram: Free tier credit guarantee restricted + Team plan queue upgraded" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Ideogram: adds Canvas workspace + Extend editing feature" (Aug 5, 2026). Ideogram has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Runway, Microsoft, Leonardo.ai, Perplexity AI and Canva also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
