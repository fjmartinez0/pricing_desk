What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Klenty's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter plan now lists unlimited users/mailboxes; Growth plan added a 2,000 Phone+Email data credit allotment and Bidirectional Field Mapping for CRM sync (inherited by Plus via 'Everything in Starter & Growth'). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Dialer Basic at $40, Starter at $50, Growth at $70, Dialer Pro at $80 and Plus at $99.
It lands 21 days after Klenty's previous change, "Klenty drops Number Reputation Management from Growth Cold Calling suite" (Sep 28, 2026). Klenty has been tracked since 2026-04-22.
In the same window Pipedrive, HubSpot and Outreach also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
