What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Sanity's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Sanity Context Knowledge Bases' add-on introduced: free tier includes 1 KB/10k source chunks on all plans, self-serve upgrade to 5 KB/50k chunks for $299/month, Enterprise 5+ KB/100k chunks via Contact Sales. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on). The current page lists Growth at $15, Extra Datasets at $999, Increased Quota at $299, Dedicated Support at $799 and Sanity Context Knowledge Bases at $299.
It lands 14 days after Sanity's previous change, "Sanity: "Agent Context" renamed to "Sanity Context"" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Sanity slashes Scheduled Functions limits: Free 5→2, Growth 10→5" (Nov 1, 2026). Sanity has been tracked since 2025-12-29.
In the same window Craft, Webflow and Hygraph also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
