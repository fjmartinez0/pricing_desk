What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Smartlead's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Your Email Network (Private OAuth)' row added to the comparison table: included on Unlimited Smart (previously only the $39 Private Infrastructure add-on) and Unlimited Prime; not on Base/Pro. Prime's Private Infrastructure cell reworded '3 SmartServers + OAuth' → '3 SmartServers included'. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Base at $32, Pro at $78, Unlimited Smart at $144, Unlimited Prime at $315 and SmartServers Pro at $39 per server / month.
It lands 7 days after Smartlead's previous change, "Smartlead: New 50% off first-month promo launched" (Sep 28, 2026). Smartlead has been tracked since 2026-04-22.
In the same window Seamless.AI and Lusha also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
