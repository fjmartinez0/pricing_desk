What happened
Between the Nov 27, 2026 and Nov 28, 2026 captures of Runway's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Extra 30% off first year on yearly plans: Standard $12 to $8.40, Pro $28 to $19.60, Max $76 to $53.20/mo; yearly savings figures rose accordingly. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $12, Pro at $28 and Max at $76.
It lands 26 days after Runway's previous change, "Runway launches Kling 4.0 at 25% off and adds App Studio to paid plans" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Runway extends Enterprise promo to 12/31, but caps free usage at 2 months" (Oct 31, 2026), "Runway: Added 'Generate, test and publish ads with Runway Ads (New)' bullet to Standard, Pro and Max plan cards" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Runway drops Max plan's expired Unlimited Wan 3.0 promo, prices hold" (Oct 20, 2026). Runway has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Synthesia, OpusClip and Loom also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
