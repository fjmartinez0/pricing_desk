What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Stellafai's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Explore plan no longer lists 'lightweight outcome reporting' and 'onboarding guidance' bullets; remaining Explore bullets reworded; Hero and plan copy rewritten from 'Start small.
