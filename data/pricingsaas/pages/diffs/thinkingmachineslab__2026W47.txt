What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Thinking Machines Lab's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Checkpoint storage rate ($0.10 per GB per month) removed from the Models & Pricing page; replaced with a link to a separate checkpoint storage pricing page. Token prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Inkling 2 Small (64K) at $0.30 per million prefill tokens + $0.03 per million cached prefill tokens, GLM-5.3-Flash (256K) at $0.17 per million cached prefill tokens + $2.11 per million sample tokens, Nemotron-3.5-Super-VL-120B-A12B-BF16 (256K) at $0.11 per million cached prefill tokens + $0.57 per million prefill tokens, GLM-5.3 (256K) at $3.78 per million prefill tokens + $0.76 per million cached prefill tokens and Qwen3.5-397B-A17B (64K) at $0.33 per million cached prefill tokens + $7.50 per million sample tokens.
