What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of CyberGhost VPN's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Multi-year plan extended from 2 years+2 months to 2 years+4 months; upfront bill rises $56.94 to $61.32 at same $2.19/mo; Halloween deal relabeled Early Access (save up to 83%).
