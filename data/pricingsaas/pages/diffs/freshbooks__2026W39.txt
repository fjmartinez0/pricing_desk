What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Freshbooks's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Intro promo restructured: Lite now $1/mo for a full first year (was 90% off for 3mo at $2.30/mo); Plus and Premium discount cut from 90% to 80% off 3 months, raising promo prices to $8.60/mo and $14.00/mo. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Lite at $23, Plus at $43, Premium at $70, Advanced Payments at $20 and FreshBooks Payroll at $40.
It lands 49 days after Freshbooks's previous change, "FreshBooks: 90% off promo shortened from 6 to 3 months" (Aug 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Freshbooks doubles free trial to 6 months, launches $40 Payroll" (Jun 29, 2026). Freshbooks has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Quickbooks, Zoho, Xero and Sage also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
