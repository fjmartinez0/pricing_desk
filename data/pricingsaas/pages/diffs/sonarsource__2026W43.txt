What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Sonar's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Developer Edition language and framework support increased from 37 to 40 (adds PostgreSQL, Azure Pipelines, Jupyter Notebook). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction.
