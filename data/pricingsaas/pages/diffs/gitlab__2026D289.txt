What happened
Between the Oct 15, 2026 and Oct 16, 2026 captures of GitLab's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Secrets Manager availability expanded from GitLab.com only to GitLab.com and Self-Managed; Dedicated still coming soon. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Premium at $29, Enterprise Agile Planning at $15, Compute Minutes at $10 per 1,000 minutes, Storage at $5 and GitLab Duo Agent Platform at $1 per GitLab Credit.
It lands 25 days after GitLab's previous change, "GitLab: Flex billing FAQ overhauled — new usage-cap self-service, terms narrowed" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "GitLab renames Flex 'per-use' credits to 'on-demand,' drops overage discounts" (Sep 18, 2026). GitLab has been tracked since 2025-10-05.
In the same window GitHub, Google Cloud and JetBrains also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
