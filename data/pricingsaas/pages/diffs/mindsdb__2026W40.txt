What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added free model Jev (classification/routing) and paid models Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6 Luna, Grok 4.7 to the rate card; removed DeepSeek V4 Pro and DeepSeek V4 Flash. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pay as you go at $0.1 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.5 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands 4 days after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB adds Jev decision model to free tier alongside MindsHub Air" (Sep 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB ends 100% credit match promo, up to $5,000 for new teams" (Oct 3, 2026), "MindsDB hikes DeepSeek V4.1 Flash output 82% to $1.20, adds Claude Sonnet 5.5" (Oct 2, 2026) and "MindsDB ditches metered tokens for unmetered free tier; Blaze rates jump up t" (Sep 21, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Huggingface and Alteryx also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
