What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Kajabi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added Black Friday promotional banner offering 30% off for 6 months storewide, replacing the prior Virtual Summit event banner; redeemable via banner link, advertised as ending in ~8 days. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $143, Growth at $199 and Pro at $399.
It lands 14 days after Kajabi's previous change, "Kajabi adds 14-day free trial and 30-day guarantee to plan cards" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kajabi kills $1,678 launch offer as Sept 15 countdown expires" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Kajabi dangles $1,678 in free launch support before Sept 15 deadline" (Sep 14, 2026). Kajabi has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Getresponse, Convertkit, ActiveCampaign, Leadpages and Thinkific also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
