What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Duda's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: AI Visibility (AEO) add-on gains a new Enterprise Lite tier at $300/mo with 50 projects, 1,250 prompts, 10,000 monthly AI responses and 250 prospect-domain reports; AEO table adds a Monthly reports for prospect domains row (none on Starter, 5/30/100/250 on higher tiers). Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $19, Team at $29, Agency at $52, White Label at $149 and AI Visibility Starter at $19.
It lands 7 days after Duda's previous change, "Duda moves add-on & eCommerce pricing to new Business Plans page" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Duda splits AI pricing: Vibe overage rate half the price of Website Builder" (Sep 7, 2026). Duda has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
In the same window Jimdo, Shopify, Squarespace and Webflow also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
