What happened
Between the Nov 6, 2026 and Nov 7, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Qwen3.7-Max serverless inference pricing increased from $1.50/$0.30 (cached)/$4.50 to $2.50/$0.50 (cached)/$7.50 per 1M input/cached/output tokens (67% increase); Inkling, Cogito v2.1 671B, Llama 3 8B Instruct Lite, and Qwen3 235B A22B Instruct 2507 FP8 Throughput were removed from the Serverless Inference model lineup. In detail: Qwen3.7-Max serverless inference pricing increased from $1.50/$0.30 (cached)/$4.50 to $2.50/$0.50 (cached)/$7.50 per 1M input/cached/output tokens (67% across the board); Inkling, Cogito v2.1 671B, Llama 3 8B Instruct Lite, and Qwen3 235B A22B Instruct 2507 FP8 Throughput were removed from the Serverless Inference model lineup. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Post-Training at $9 per GPU-hour (B200, promo), Serverless Inference at $0.14 per 1M input tokens, Dedicated Inference at $5.49 per GPU-hour (H100, on-demand) + $8.99 per GPU-hour (B200, on-demand), GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100) and Fine-Tuning at $0.34 per 1M tokens (LoRA SFT, cheapest models) + $0.38 per 1M tokens (Full SFT, cheapest models).
It lands 2 days after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI adds Kimi K3.1 Pro at $2/$10 per 1M tokens, plus Ripple-65B Preview" (Nov 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI: Four serverless inference models dropped from the pricing list: Gemma 4 31B, DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731, Llama…" (Oct 26, 2026), "Together AI adds NVIDIA B200 post-training at $11/GPU/hr, $9 launch promo" (Oct 20, 2026) and "Together AI ends free Muse Spark Open promo; $1.25/$4.25 pricing now applies" (Oct 19, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
In the same window OpenAI, Huggingface, Anthropic, Google and Mistral AI also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
