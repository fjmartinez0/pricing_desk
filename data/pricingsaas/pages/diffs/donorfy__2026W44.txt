What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Donorfy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Sandboxes add-on (£30/mo extra) removed from Starter, Professional, and Enterprise plans; Standard and Premier support tiers now disclose pricing (15% of subscription; 'tailored' for Premier). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at GBP 40, Professional at GBP 71, Enterprise at GBP 308, Custom Dashboards Add-on at GBP 19 and Sandboxes Add-on at GBP 30.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Donorfy. Donorfy has been tracked since 2026-05-20.
