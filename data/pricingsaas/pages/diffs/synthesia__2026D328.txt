What happened
Between the Nov 23, 2026 and Nov 24, 2026 captures of Synthesia's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro plan's live Black Friday/Cyber Week discount deepened: effective price dropped from $59 to $44/month (34% to 51% off $89 list, $708 to $528 billed yearly); sitewide countdown banner and 'new subscribers only' terms now shown. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $14, Pro at $59 and Business at $79.
It lands 6 days after Synthesia's previous change, "Synthesia splits pricing into individual/business tabs, hides Enterprise…" (Nov 18, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Synthesia swaps Syren AI time cap for tiered video quotas, cuts Basic assets" (Nov 8, 2026), "Synthesia swaps Syren 3-minute video cap for per-plan monthly video quotas" (Nov 7, 2026) and "Synthesia drops its interactive credit calculator from the pricing page" (Nov 3, 2026). Synthesia has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
