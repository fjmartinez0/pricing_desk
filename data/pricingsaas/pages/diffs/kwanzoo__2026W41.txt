Kwanzoo moves to $1,500-$3,500 Agentic plans, dropping pay-per-signal tiers
Verified Oct 5, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral
The changeMedia Services packaging — package change.

What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Kwanzoo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Kwanzoo replaced 5-tier pay-per-signal pricing ($0-$1,500/mo) with a 3-tier Agentic subscription model ($1,500-$3,500/mo: Growth, Scale-up, Enterprise), adding GTM Services and Media Services add-ons. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Agentic Growth at $1,500, Agentic Scale-up at $2,500, Agentic Enterprise at $3,500, GTM Services Tier 2 at $5,500 and GTM Services Tier 1 at $3,500.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Kwanzoo. Kwanzoo has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Wistia, Visme, Veel, Uscreen and Profound also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
