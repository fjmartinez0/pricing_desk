What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Crelate's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Comparison table limits changed: client portal limits 5/20/20 to 10/100/100 users/portal (Business/Business Plus/Enterprise) and Business Plus API calls 60 to 120 RPM, while Premium resume parsing (500 to 350) and contact record status limits (20 to 15) were cut on Business Plus and Enterprise. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $85 and Business at $119.
It lands 105 days after Crelate's previous change, "Crelate launches $85/user Essentials plan with ATS and AI tools" (Jun 29, 2026). Crelate has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
