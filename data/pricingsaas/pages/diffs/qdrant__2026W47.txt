What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Qdrant's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Feature comparison table: the Premium tier's 'Disk Encryption with Custom Key' row dropped its '(AWS and GCP only)' cloud-provider restriction and now shows an unconditional checkmark for custom-key disk encryption. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change).
It lands 139 days after Qdrant's previous change, "Qdrant rolls out 4-tier Enterprise Support with uptime SLAs" (Jun 29, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Qdrant Splits Premium SLA into Single and Multi-AZ Uptime Tiers" (Apr 24, 2026), "Qdrant Adds GPU Indexing and CLI Automation Across All Tiers" (Apr 13, 2026) and "Qdrant launches three-tier cloud pricing with free inference models" (Apr 3, 2026). Qdrant has been tracked since 2023-12-22.
