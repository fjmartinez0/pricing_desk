Vista Social positions Enterprise for teams of 8+ users
Verified Oct 5, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral
The changeEnterprise positioning — positioning.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 28, 2026 → Oct 5, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Vista Social's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise card user line changed from 'Unlimited users' to '8+ users' (a minimum team size, not a cap); comparison table unchanged at Customizable. In detail: Enterprise plan card now targets teams of 8+ users ("Invite 8 or more users") instead of advertising "Unlimited users"; comparison table still lists Enterprise included users as Customizable. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Professional at $99, Advanced at $160, Scale at $360, Employee Advocacy Add-on at $199 and X (Twitter) Add-on at $29.
It lands 7 days after Vista Social's previous change, "Vista Social raises prices up to 34% and halves Scale plan user seats" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vista Social: included users reduced across Professional/Advanced/Scale plans" (Aug 4, 2026). Vista Social has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Sendible, Sproutsocial and Buffer also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
