What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of IP Fabric's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: licensing basis expanded from devices-only to devices + cloud objects, and support unbundled from a flat included benefit into standard/advanced/premium tiers with different SLAs. In detail: Licensing basis expanded to include cloud objects (not just devices), and 'support included' became tiered standard/advanced/premium support packages with different SLAs. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for IP Fabric. IP Fabric has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
