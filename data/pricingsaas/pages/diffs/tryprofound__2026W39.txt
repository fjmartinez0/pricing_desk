What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Profound's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Profound dropped paid self-serve tiers (Starter $99/mo, Growth $399/mo), replacing them with a single free Trial plan; Enterprise remains the only custom-priced tier; Enterprise plan's supported AI-platform coverage list swapped Grok for Exa Search; ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode/Gemini/Overviews, Microsoft Copilot, DeepSeek, and Anthropic Claude coverage unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden, feature change) with a downward direction.
