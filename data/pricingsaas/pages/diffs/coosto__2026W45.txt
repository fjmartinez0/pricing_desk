What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Coosto's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Sales Signals product removed from the pricing page in the site redesign: its tab and all three tiers are gone — Focus (from €490/mo, up to 100 key accounts), Team (from €790/mo, 250) and Strategic (from €1.190/mo, 500). Only the Coosto and Inform tabs remain. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Essential at EUR 249, Growth at EUR 299 and Pro at EUR 349.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Coosto. Coosto has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
