What happened
Between the Oct 15, 2026 and Oct 16, 2026 captures of Vercel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Vercel Daemons usage-based item: Active CPU $0.128/hr (5 hrs/mo included) and Provisioned Memory $0.0212/GB-hr (420 GB-hrs/mo included); Enterprise Custom. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 20, 2026) lists Pro at $20.
It lands 11 days after Vercel's previous change, "Vercel adds OWASP Core Ruleset firewall to Pro Plus priority projects" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vercel splits Speed Insights pricing, adds Tracing Spans beta observability" (Sep 28, 2026), "Vercel adds Tracing Spans metric, charges $0.50 per 1M span units on Pro" (Sep 25, 2026) and "Vercel splits Speed Insights: free 10K-event tier, paid Plus Events add-on" (Sep 24, 2026).
