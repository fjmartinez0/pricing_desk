What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Forest Admin's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Uptime language softened to '99.9% target' for Starter/Professional (was '99.9%'); Enterprise uptime now '99.9% SLA' (was Custom). Response-time SLA (P1 4h/P2 next day) moved from Professional to Enterprise. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $499, Professional at $2,199 and Enterprise at $5,999.
It lands 35 days after Forest Admin's previous change, "Forest Admin renames audit events to write events; caps, overage unchanged" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Forest (ex-ForestAdmin) launches public 4-tier pricing: $499→$2,199→$5,999/mo" (Aug 31, 2026). Forest Admin has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
