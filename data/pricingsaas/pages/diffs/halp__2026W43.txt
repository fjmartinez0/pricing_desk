What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Halp's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Standard plan rose from $20 to $21.50 per agent/month (+7.5%) on the Monthly billing view; Premium plan rose from $51.42 to $55.28 per agent/month (+7.5%) on the Monthly billing view. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $1,641.67 and Premium at $4,250.
It lands 7 days after Halp's previous change, "Halp adds AI reporting and Enterprise data governance to Jira comparison" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Halp caps Enterprise automations at 9,500/user/month, adds tiered limits + Ro" (Sep 7, 2026). Halp has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Slack, Microsoft, Intercom and HubSpot also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
