What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Strivacity's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Strivacity now publishes its MAU price scale: the single 'starts at $17,000/year' figure was replaced by an MAU selector (10K, 25K, 100K, 300K, 1M; 2M+ contact sales) showing a computed price, $1,417/mo billed annually ($17,000/yr) at the 10K default. Entry price unchanged; New AI Agents (agentic commerce) add-on, priced per MAU, appears in the redesigned capability table, which now compares Stri. In detail: New AI Agents (agentic commerce) add-on, priced per MAU, appears in the redesigned capability table, which now compares Strivacity against Auth0, Ping Identity and Microsoft Entra External ID. The MAU definition now counts AI agents. No add-on price disclosed. Pulse files it as a pricing change (new add-on, price revealed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists All Inclusive CIAM at $1,416.67.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Strivacity. Strivacity has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
