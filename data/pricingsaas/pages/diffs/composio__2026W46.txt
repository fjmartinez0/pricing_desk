What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Composio's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Composio shifted usage-based billing toward a 'credits' currency: Hobby now includes $2 in credits, Pro's $29 allowance is relabeled from 'usage credit' to 'credits', a new per-app credit pricing note for Composio-managed apps starts Nov 3 2026, and trigger billing now distinguishes polling vs realtime events; Feature comparison table gains a new 'Audit logs' row (Hobby limited to last 30 days, P. In detail: Feature comparison table gains a new 'Audit logs' row (Hobby limited to last 30 days, Pro/Enterprise full history); Hobby's 'Custom tools & MCP' entitlement downgraded from 'Included' to 'Shared only' (one shared toolkit per project). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $29.
It lands 14 days after Composio's previous change, "Composio adds usage-priced Firewall add-on at $0.50 per 1M tokens on Pro" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Composio renames Premium tools to Instant Tools, updates billing FAQ" (Oct 12, 2026) and "Composio bumps toolkit count to 1500+ across Free, Pro and Enterprise" (Sep 7, 2026). Composio has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window UiPath, n8n, Zapier, Workato and Make also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
