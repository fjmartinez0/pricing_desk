What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Checkr's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Rechecks' item added to the Verifications list: scheduled rechecks priced at the package price plus passthrough fees. In detail: New Rechecks add-on category added to the All add-ons list: scheduled rechecks for any package on a set cadence, priced at the package price plus any passthrough fees.
