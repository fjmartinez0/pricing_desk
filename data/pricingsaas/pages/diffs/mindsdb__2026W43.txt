What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Two models added to the token price table: Gemini 4 Argon (Google, $2.00 in/$10.00 out per 1M) and Muse Wonder 1.0 (Meta, $4.00 in/$20.00 out per 1M). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pay as you go at $0.1 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.5 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands 3 days after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB adds Gemini 4 Argon pricing at $2 input, $10 output per million" (Oct 16, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB adds Meta's Muse Wonder 1.0 at $4 input, $20 output per 1M tokens" (Oct 15, 2026), "MindsDB replaces plan-less rate card with Free and Pay-as-you-go tiers" (Oct 12, 2026) and "MindsDB hikes DeepSeek V4.1 Flash rates, ends $5,000 credit match promo" (Oct 5, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
