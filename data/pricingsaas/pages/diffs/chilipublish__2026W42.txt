What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of CHILI publish's pricing page, Pulse recorded 5 changes: Plan lineup renamed: LAUNCH, TRANSFORM, SCALE and UNLIMITED became Essential, Advanced, Premium and Custom; Essential (formerly LAUNCH) price increased from $875 to $1,050 per month, billed annually (+20%); Advanced (formerly TRANSFORM) price increased from $2,150 to $2,700 per month, billed annually (+25.6%); Premium (formerly SCALE) price increased from $6,550 to $7,300 per month, billed annu. In detail: Template designer seats and included limits reduced per tier: designers 3/5/10 to 1/3/8; smart layouts 25,000/100,000 to 18,750/50,000; design systems 100/400 to 75/200; top paid tier monthly renders 5,000 to 4,000; Premium (formerly SCALE) price increased from $6,550 to $7,300 per month, billed annually (+11.5%). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, limit decrease, plan renamed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essential at $1,050, Advanced at $2,700, Premium at $7,300 and Additional Renders at EUR 0.1 per render.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for CHILI publish. CHILI publish has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
