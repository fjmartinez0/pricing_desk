What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Help Scout's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Envoy card benefits rewritten (No dead ends/Easy setup/3-month free trial replaced by resolve more, choose how AI helps, improve); 3-month trial bullet dropped from card but still in FAQ; Free plan promoted from the 'Just need the basics?' banner (5 users, 1 Inbox, 1 Docs site) to a full pricing card at $0 next to struck-through 'Basic $19'; card now lists 1 shared email Inbox, 1 public knowledge. In detail: Free plan promoted from the 'Just need the basics?' banner (5 users, 1 Inbox, 1 Docs site) to a full pricing card at $0 next to struck-through 'Basic $19'; card now lists 1 shared email Inbox, 1 public knowledge base, limited saved replies, 25 contacts/month and no AI tools (comparison table still shows 100 contacts/mo). Pulse files it as a packaging change (positioning, freemium change). The current page lists Standard at $25, Plus at $45, Pro at $75, Additional Docs Site at $20 and Additional Inbox at $10.
It lands 105 days after Help Scout's previous change, "Help Scout: Secure customer portal added to Plus plan" (Aug 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Help Scout: SLA policies added to all paid plans; WhatsApp moved to Standard" (Jul 2, 2026), "Help Scout splits workflows into basic and advanced tiers by plan" (Jun 1, 2026) and "Help Scout adds light user tiers to Team plans, up to 50 seats" (May 26, 2026). Help Scout has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Intercom also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
