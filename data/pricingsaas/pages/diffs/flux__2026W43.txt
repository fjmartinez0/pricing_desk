What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Flux's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Build plan AI usage allowance increased from 120 to 200 ACUs/month (3x to 5x vs Explore); Pro plan increased from 400 to 800 ACUs/month (10x to 20x vs Explore).
