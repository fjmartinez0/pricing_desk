What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 17, 2026 captures of Huggingface's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: NVIDIA H100 (aws) 1/2/4/8 GPU inference endpoint instances ($4.50-$36.00/hr) removed from the Inference Endpoints hardware pricing table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed). The current page lists PRO at $9, Team at $20, Enterprise at $50, Inference Endpoints - NVIDIA T4 AWS (4x) at $3 per hour and Inference Endpoints - NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 AWS (1x) at $2.75 per hour.
It lands 15 days after Huggingface's previous change, "Huggingface extends hardware price table to cover Jobs, rates unchanged" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Huggingface adds Nvidia RTX PRO 6000 GPUs to Spaces from $2.75/hr" (Oct 16, 2026) and "Huggingface swaps PRO's 20x inference multiplier for a flat $2 monthly credit" (Oct 8, 2026). Huggingface has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Together AI, Anthropic, OpenAI, Modal and Cohere also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
