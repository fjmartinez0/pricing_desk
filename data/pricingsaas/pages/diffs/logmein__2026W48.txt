What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of GoTo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: CX Complete: AI Receptionist now answers across calls, WhatsApp, SMS, Webchat, social; WhatsApp/SMS campaigns/surveys bullet and AI call summaries replaced by AI Observability, interaction summaries, live dashboards. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change).
It lands 83 days after GoTo's previous change, "GoTo Central starting price ($80/mo) removed from Base plan" (Aug 31, 2026). GoTo has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
