What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of ToolJet's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: App limits raised across the entry-level Cloud plans: Free and Basic went from 2 apps to 10 apps, and Pro went from up to 5 apps to up to 10 apps; Added a new comparison-table row disclosing a 'Coding-agent plugin (ToolJet MCP)' that lets builders use Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor with their own coding-agent subscription, without consuming ToolJet AI credits; shown as included across all Cloud pl. In detail: Added a new comparison-table row disclosing a 'Coding-agent plugin (ToolJet MCP)' that lets builders use Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor with their own coding-agent subscription, without consuming ToolJet AI credits; shown as included across all Cloud plans; Added a new 'Bring your own LLM keys (BYOK)' comparison row showing BYOK unavailable on every Cloud plan, and updated the FAQ to state BYOK is only available as a Self-hosted Enterprise add-on and explicitly not available on ToolJet Cloud. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, feature added, feature change) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Basic at $23, Pro at $79, Team at $199 and Pay-as-you-go AI Credits at $1 per 100 AI credits.
It lands 14 days after ToolJet's previous change, "ToolJet adds $23 Basic plan and credit-free Claude Code, Codex, Cursor builds" (Sep 14, 2026). ToolJet has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
