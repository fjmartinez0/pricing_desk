What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Hospitable's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Mogul plan owner-relations bullet changed from 'Owner Statements, Invoices & Payouts' to 'Owner Portal, Statements, Payouts, and Trust Accounting (pilot)'; Invoices dropped, Owner Portal and Trust Accounting pilot added. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Host at $25.52, Professional at $51.92 and Mogul at $87.12.
It lands 14 days after Hospitable's previous change, "Hospitable rewrites plan card highlights; prices and features unchanged" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hospitable adds Inventory Management to its plans" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Hospitable: Guest Vetting, extra-device & extra-property fees increased" (Sep 7, 2026). Hospitable has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
