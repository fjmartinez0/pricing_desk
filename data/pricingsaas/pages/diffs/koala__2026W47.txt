What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Koala's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Black Friday sale added: 40% off annual plans through Dec 2 (up from the standard 20% annual discount). Annual rates now Starter $15/mo ($180/yr), Professional $29/mo ($348/yr), Boost $59/mo ($708/yr), down from $20/$39/$79; Supported model list updated from Claude Sonnet 5 to Claude Sonnet 5.5 on paid plans, including the word-count footnote. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added, feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $20, Professional at $39, Boost at $79, Growth at $143 and Elite at $280.
It lands 7 days after Koala's previous change, "Koala upgrades AI image generation to GPT Image 2.6 alongside Nano Banana 2" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Koala launches Free plan, adds $29 brand slots and 3-month credit rollover" (Oct 26, 2026), "Koala updates word-count footnote to name GPT-6.1 Sol as 2x model" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Koala: AI MCP integration exits beta, now included with every paid plan" (Sep 28, 2026). Koala has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Notion and Bearly AI also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
