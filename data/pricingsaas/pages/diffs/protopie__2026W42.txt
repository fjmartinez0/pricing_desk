What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of ProtoPie's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: ProtoPie MCP (Beta) split into tiered MCP: MCP Core on Pro, MCP Enterprise on Enterprise; comparison table now lists Studio MCP, Code MCP and Dev View; Beta label dropped.
