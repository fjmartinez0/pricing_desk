What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Stripe's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Data Pipeline adds Postgres database sync: pay-as-you-go $0.0025 per row synced (5,000 rows free/month) or $100/month subscription including 50,000 rows. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at 1.5% surcharge for international cards + $0.3 per successful transaction.
It lands 5 days after Stripe's previous change, "Stripe Data Pipeline adds real-time Postgres sync at $0.0025 per row" (Oct 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Stripe adds card cashback to Treasury, launches $329 Reader T600" (Oct 5, 2026), "Stripe adds $329 Reader T600 to Terminal lineup, revamps Treasury perks" (Oct 1, 2026) and "Stripe drops T600 reader, trims Terminal lineup to M2, S710, S700" (Sep 28, 2026). Stripe has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Squarespace also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
