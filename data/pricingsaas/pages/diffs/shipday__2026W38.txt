What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Shipday's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan card dropped its 'Annual agreement · 10,000 orders/month included' disclosure. The commitment requirement and order-cap figure no longer appear; the $2,499/month starting price itself is unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Branded Elite at $99, Business AI Lite at $199, Business AI at $399 and Enterprise at $3,499.
It lands 7 days after Shipday's previous change, "Shipday reveals Enterprise pricing: $2,499/month for 10K orders, AI Agents, SLAs" (Sep 7, 2026). Shipday has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
