What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Xentral's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Business plan tagline changed from 'For mid-sized companies that want to automate processes' to 'For companies that want to automate processes'; prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Launch at EUR 99, Starter at EUR 349, Business at EUR 649 and Pro at EUR 849.
It lands 21 days after Xentral's previous change, "Xentral puts AI in every plan, adding agents, Co-pilot and MCP server" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Xentral launches sub-€500k Launch plan, drops Reportings from Starter tier" (Aug 24, 2026). Xentral has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
In the same window Shopify, Salesforce and Microsoft also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
