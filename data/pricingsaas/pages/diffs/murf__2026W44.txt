What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Murf AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Speech to Text (Beta) API endpoint listed at $0.24/hour, with a Speech-to-Text usage meter (hours) added to the calculator; Pay as you go concurrency now split by service: up to 5 for TTS and up to 150 for STT. Free plan: 5 concurrent requests each for TTS and STT. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Creator at $19, Business at $66, Dub Pay as you go at $0.25 per credit and API Pay as you go at $0.01 per 1000 characters (TTS Falcon) + $0.1 per minute (Voice Changer).
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Murf AI. Murf AI has been tracked since 2026-05-11.
