What happened
Between the Oct 29, 2026 and Oct 30, 2026 captures of OpenAI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro plan now includes monthly API credits ($100 with Pro 100, $200 with Pro 200, or $500 with Pro 500/month) to build apps and agents. In detail: Pro plan now includes monthly API credits to build apps and agents: $100 with Pro 100, $200 with Pro 200, or $500 with Pro 500 tier. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Go at $8, Plus at $20, Business at $20 and Pro at $100.
It lands 4 days after OpenAI's previous change, "OpenAI rewrites Free, Go, Plus and Pro taglines in "Best for" format" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenAI: Plan taglines reworded to 'Best for...' format: Free (trying out ChatGPT), Go (longer conversations), Plus…" (Oct 18, 2026), "OpenAI rewrites Free, Go, Plus and Pro taglines in "Best for" style" (Oct 11, 2026) and "OpenAI rewrites plan taglines across Free, Go, Plus and Pro tiers" (Oct 9, 2026). OpenAI has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
