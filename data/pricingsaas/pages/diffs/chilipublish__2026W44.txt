CHILI publish surfaces Design Agent AI features on every pricing tier
Verified Oct 26, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral

What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of CHILI publish's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Design Agent (conversational AI assistant) and AI-driven work are now listed as included features directly on the Essential, Advanced, and Premium pricing cards, previously only visible in the detailed comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essential at $1,050, Advanced at $2,700, Premium at $7,300 and Additional Renders at EUR 0.1 per render.
CHILI publish's previous recorded change was "CHILI publish renames plans, hikes prices up to 26% and cuts designer seats" (Oct 12, 2026). CHILI publish has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Microsoft, Figma, Asana and Canva also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
