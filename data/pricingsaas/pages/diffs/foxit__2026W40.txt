What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Foxit's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Foxit eSign lowered the minimum license count required to qualify for Volume Licensing from 30 to 16 licenses, making the custom-priced tier accessible to smaller teams. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists eSign Essentials at $10 and eSign Business at $25.
It lands 21 days after Foxit's previous change, "Foxit eSign: new Volume Licensing plan added" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Foxit Pdfeditor boosts free AI credits 15x, from 20 to 300 per month" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Foxit Pdfeditor: billing toggle relabeled to 'Annual plan (paid in full)' / 'Annual plan (billed monthly)'" (Jul 6, 2026). Foxit has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
