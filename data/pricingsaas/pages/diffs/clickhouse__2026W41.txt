What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of ClickHouse's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan cards now show headline entry prices: Basic from $53/month, Scale from $437/month, Enterprise from $571/month; previously only per-unit compute/storage rates were shown. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Basic at $35.33 per TB + $0.22 per TB and Scale at $0.69 per TB + $47.1 per TB.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for ClickHouse. ClickHouse has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
