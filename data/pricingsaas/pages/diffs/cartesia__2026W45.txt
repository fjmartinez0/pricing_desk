What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Cartesia's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Voice agent per-minute rate cut from $0.06 to $0.03 on Free, Pro, Startup, Scale; LLM usage during calls changes from free for UI-created agents (limited time) to billed separately. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $5, Startup at $49 and Scale at $299.
It lands 21 days after Cartesia's previous change, "Cartesia drops unlimited seat promise from every plan on pricing page" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cartesia: Professional voice cloning capped (unlimited → 2/4/Custom)" (Aug 17, 2026). Cartesia has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window OpenAI, Huggingface, Anthropic, Cohere and Google also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
