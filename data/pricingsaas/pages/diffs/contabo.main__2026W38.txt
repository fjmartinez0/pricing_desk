What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Contabo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Cloud VPS 18 $47.04->$39.20/mo; Cloud VPS Plus 4/6/8/12/16 all down ~17% ($13.00->$10.80, $18.40->$15.20, $33.60->$28.00, $56.80->$47.20, $76.00->$63.20/mo). term note changed from "first 12 months" promo framing to a 24-month contract commitment across Core, Performance, Max Performance, Storage VPS and Dedicated Servers. In detail: VPS/Dedicated Server pricing changed from a 'first 12 months' promo framing to a 24-month contract term across Core, Performance, Max Performance, Storage VPS and Dedicated Servers; listed prices unchanged; Cloud VPS 18 dropped $47.04->$39.20/mo; Cloud VPS Plus 4/6/8/12/16 dropped ~17% ($13.00->$10.80, $18.40->$15.20, $33.60->$28.00, $56.80->$47.20, $76.00->$63.20/mo). Other tiers unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (metric change, price decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Cloud VPS 4 at $5.28, Cloud VPS 6 at $7.2, Cloud VPS 8 at $13.44, Cloud VPS 12 at $24 and Cloud VPS 16 at $35.6.
