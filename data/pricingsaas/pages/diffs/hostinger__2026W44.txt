What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Hostinger's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Black Friday promo: Premium 48-mo price $2.99 to $2.49/mo (75% to 80% off), Business $3.99 to $3.49/mo (79% to 82% off); AI credits doubled on Black Friday. Premium list price $11.99 to $12.19; Free AI email marketing for 1 year now capped at up to 100 subscribers on Business and Cloud Startup plans.
