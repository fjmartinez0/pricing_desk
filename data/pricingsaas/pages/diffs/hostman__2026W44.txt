What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Hostman's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Agents entry rate increased from $0.42 to $0.48 per 1M tokens. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Backend Apps at $4, Managed Databases at $4, Cloud Servers 1vCPU 2GB at $5, Backend Apps 1vCPU 2GB at $5 and Backend Apps 2vCPU 2GB at $6.
It lands 7 days after Hostman's previous change, "Hostman raises Bare Metal entry price from $90 to $100 a month" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hostman Bare Metal starting price flips back to $90/month from $100" (Oct 5, 2026), "Hostman: Bare Metal price increase ($90→$100/mo)" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Hostman: Bare Metal price cut ($100→$90/month)" (Sep 14, 2026). Hostman has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
