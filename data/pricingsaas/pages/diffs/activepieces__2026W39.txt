What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Activepieces's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: 'Recommended' badge removed from the Plus plan card; a new 'Enterprise control' band was added spotlighting existing Ultimate self-hosted/air-gapped governance features (SSO, SCIM, RBAC, audit logs, secret managers); The Embed offering now displays a public entry price of 'From $36k a year'; previously the Embed band carried no price at all and routed to an explainer page. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed, positioning) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Plus at $16 and Team at $166.
