What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of GoTab's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Added indefinite cost-plus processing rate lock offer for eligible operators under card processing, with 'Talk to Sales About Your Rate' CTA; Card processing footnote no longer states specific surcharges (+0.85% Amex/JCB/Corp, +1.5% international); now says additional rates may apply for other card types. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price hidden) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $15, Pro at $99 and Sync at $229.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for GoTab. GoTab has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
