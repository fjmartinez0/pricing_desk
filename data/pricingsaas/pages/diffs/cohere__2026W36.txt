What happened
Between the Aug 24, 2026 and Aug 31, 2026 captures of Cohere's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Parse 5 document-parsing model added to Model Vault, with Medium ($4.00/hr, $2,500/mo) and XL ($7.00/hr, $4,300/mo) performance tiers alongside existing Embed and Rerank models. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Embed 5 Fast at $0.08 Input tokens, Embed 5 Pro at $0.12 Input tokens, Parse 5 at $1.5 Pages, Rerank 4 Fast at $2 Searches and Rerank 4 Pro at $2.5 Searches.
It lands 3 days after Cohere's previous change, "Cohere adds Parse 5 to Model Vault with Medium and XL tiered pricing" (Aug 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cohere: New Rerank 4 Pro Large tier in Model Vault ($10/hr, $6,500/mo)" (Jul 2, 2026) and "Cohere adds Coding Models tier, expanding to 4 product categories" (Jun 15, 2026). Cohere has been tracked since 2024-12-01.
