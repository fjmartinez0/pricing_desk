What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Smarterqueue's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: SmarterQueue replaced its 3-tier lineup (Solo $24.99, Business $42.99, Agency $84.99/mo billed annually) with 2 new plans: Pro $21/mo (1 workspace, 1 user, 5 profiles) and Team $42/mo (unlimited workspaces, 3 users, 5 profiles). Usage limits moved from Posts/Profile/Day + Queued Posts to Scheduled Posts/Profile + Published Posts/Profile/Month. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Solo at $24.99, Business at $42.99 and Agency at $84.99.
It lands 70 days after Smarterqueue's previous change, "Smarterqueue swaps Tweets/Week for X/Twitter Credits, boosts default 5→50, ca" (Jul 20, 2026). Smarterqueue has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Coschedule, Sproutsocial, Buffer, Hootsuite and Later also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
