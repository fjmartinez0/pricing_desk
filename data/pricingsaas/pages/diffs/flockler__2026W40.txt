What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Flockler's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Collect & Display custom forms renamed to Submission Forms; Premium adds image-upload limits and extra field types; new 'Accessible QR codes' row added; Agency plan SSO moved from blanket-included to 'scoped per agreement'; AI moderation (Garde AI) now shows explicit post caps: Business 1,000 posts, Pro 5,000 posts, Premium unlimited — previously an uncapped checkmark feature with no stated limit. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Basic at $110, Business at $195 and Pro at EUR 294.
It lands 87 days after Flockler's previous change, "Flockler: Premium gains account manager & security reviews; Pro loses CSM support" (Jul 3, 2026). Flockler has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Trustpilot, Sproutsocial, Buffer, Hootsuite and Hotjar also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
