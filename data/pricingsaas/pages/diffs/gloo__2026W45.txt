What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Gloo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Inference product line renamed "Inference & APIs" across the pricing page; Guarded 'platform fee' reworded to 'platform price'. No amounts changed. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $20, Pro at $100 and Ultra at $200.
It lands 35 days after Gloo's previous change, "Gloo opens every agent and model to all Gloo Code plans, catalog hits 160" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Gloo pulls Data Engine from Pro, waitlists it at capacity" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Gloo: Pro Data Engine access downgraded to Limited + 1 more change" (Aug 15, 2026). Gloo has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Zapier, Workato, Make and MuleSoft also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
