What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of ExpressVPN's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Halloween promo (12-month default, save up to 66%) replaced by Black Friday Early Access Sale (2-year default, up to 80% off); on 2-year terms Advanced $4.49->$4.19/mo and Express Pro $7.49->$7.19/mo vs late October, Basic $2.99 unchanged. Renewal prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $2.99, Advanced at $4.49 and Express Pro at $7.49.
It lands 7 days after ExpressVPN's previous change, "ExpressVPN adds ExpressAI quotas to Basic and Advanced plans" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ExpressVPN trims ExpressAI models on Express Pro from 7 to 6" (Oct 5, 2026), "ExpressVPN: Advanced $3.99->$4.49/mo, Express Pro $5.99->$7.49/mo" (Sep 28, 2026) and "ExpressVPN: ExpressAI model access expanded (6→7) + 2 more feature additions" (Sep 21, 2026). ExpressVPN has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window NordVPN, CyberGhost VPN, Ava and Surfshark also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
