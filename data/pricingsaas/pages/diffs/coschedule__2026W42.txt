What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Coschedule's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Professional Advanced Inbox coverage changed from All Networks to 9 Networks; Add-Ons section removed from the comparison table: Actionable Marketing Institute ($50/month Starter & Professional, $100/month Suite), Headline Studio Pro, Hire Mia Integration, Brand Profiles and Dedicated Account Manager (CSM) add-ons no longer listed. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, add-on removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $29 and Professional at $49.
It lands 35 days after Coschedule's previous change, "Coschedule ditches per-seat tiers for flat $29-$69 plans" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CoSchedule: Twitter/X add-on price doubled to $16/profile/month" (Aug 10, 2026). Coschedule has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Sendible, Sproutsocial and Buffer also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
