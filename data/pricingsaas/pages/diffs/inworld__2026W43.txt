What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Inworld AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Professional voice clones now listed per plan: On-Demand 2, Creator 4, Builder 10, Developer 25 (was paid add-on), Growth 40 (was 1), Enterprise 100+; LLM pricing changed from 'At cost' to 'From $0.07/1M tokens' (LLM Inference) on all plan cards, plus a new hero line for LLM Inference from $0.07/1M tokens; On-Demand: 'Realtime API access' replaced by 'Speech-to-Speech access'; 'LLM models via Ro. In detail: On-Demand: 'Realtime API access' replaced by 'Speech-to-Speech access'; 'LLM models via Router' renamed 'via Inworld Inference'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (positioning, limit increase, price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Creator at $20.83, Builder at $83.33, Developer at $250 and Growth at $1,250.
It lands 7 days after Inworld AI's previous change, "Inworld AI halves Router model access on On-Demand plan from 220+ to 100+ LLMs" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Inworld AI: multilingual TTS language support expanded to 200+" (Aug 17, 2026). Inworld AI has been tracked since 2026-04-26.
In the same window Microsoft, OpenAI, Google, Anthropic and Character.AI also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
