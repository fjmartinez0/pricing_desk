Canva hides free plan's AI quota, swapping 20 uses for vague limited access
Verified Oct 30, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral

The changeFree tier — freemium change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 29, 2026 → Oct 30, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 29, 2026 and Oct 30, 2026 captures of Canva's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan's AI usage allowance disclosure changed from a quantified "Up to 20 Standard or Premium AI uses" to the vague "Limited access to Standard and Premium AI", removing visibility into the exact free AI quota. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $12 and Business at $20.83.
It lands 46 days after Canva's previous change, "Canva cuts Pro to $144/year, slashes free AI uses from 200 to 20" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Canva Pro drops 20% to $144/year, savings jump past 16%" (Sep 11, 2026), "Canva reshuffles AI usage limits, rebrands allowance as usage" (Sep 9, 2026) and "Canva: Pro plan price increased $144→$180/year" (Aug 4, 2026). Canva has been tracked since 2023-11-17.
In the same window Figma and Visme also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
