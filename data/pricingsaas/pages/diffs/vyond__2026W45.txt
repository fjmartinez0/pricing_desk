Vyond launches Turbo video tool, but pricing is sales-only
Verified Nov 2, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: up
The changePlan — new plan.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 26, 2026 → Nov 2, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Vyond's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Vyond Turbo product (command-driven video creation) added to pricing page above the plan tabs, sales-gated via Talk to Sales with no public price. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $58, Professional at $100, Enterprise at $137 and Agency at $167.
It lands 49 days after Vyond's previous change, "Vyond: Voice Cloning unbundled, now Enterprise/Agency plans only" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vyond: AI credit costs raised across the board + new Text to Video Clip feature" (Aug 11, 2026). Vyond has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Renderforest, Canva and Wistia also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
