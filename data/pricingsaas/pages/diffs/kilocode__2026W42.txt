What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Kilo Code's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Teams feature label renamed from 'Shared agent modes' to 'Shared custom agents'; prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Teams at $15, Kilo Pass Starter at $19, Kilo Pass Pro at $49, Kilo Pass Expert at $199 and Cloud Compute at $1.2 per hour — Cloud Agent (Standard) + $0.6 per hour — Cloud Agent (Docker).
It lands 42 days after Kilo Code's previous change, "Kilo Code: Teams trial redefined as Enterprise trial" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kilo Code: New Cloud Compute usage pricing + 5% credit purchase fee" (Aug 4, 2026). Kilo Code has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Windsurf, Replit, Cursor and JetBrains also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
