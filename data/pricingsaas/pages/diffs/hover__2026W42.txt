What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Hover's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: .news domain price increased from $41.99 to $46.99 per year for registration, renewal and transfer (0-9 domains tier). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists .archi at $132.99 per domain per year, .ceo at $164.99 per domain per year, .dad at $17.99 per domain per year, .adult at $149.99 per domain per year and .com.au at $19.99 per domain per year.
It lands 7 days after Hover's previous change, "Hover cuts .co domains 32% to $25.99 for registration, renewal and transfer" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hover: .xyz domain price increase ($19.99→$22.99)" (Aug 31, 2026). Hover has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Matterport and Google Cloud also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
