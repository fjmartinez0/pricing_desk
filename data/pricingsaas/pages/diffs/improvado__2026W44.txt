What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Improvado's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Free Limited tier removed; Starter added from $20,000/yr; Advanced (Custom) replaced by Growth from $50,000/yr; Advanced Intelligence ($20k/yr) and Creative Analytics ($4k/yr) add-ons priced; Mid-tier plan (Advanced to Growth) limits cut: rows 600M to 60M-200M, actions/day 200 to 40-90, MCP actions/wk 4,000 to 800-1,800, workspaces 50 to 10; Enterprise Professional Services changed from White-glove to Hands-on. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease, positioning, package change) with a downward direction.
Improvado's previous recorded change was "Improvado renames Customization credits to Professional Services credits" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Improvado: Advanced Intelligence features unbundled into paid add-ons" (Aug 24, 2026). Improvado has been tracked since 2026-04-26.
In the same window Mixpanel and Supermetrics also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
