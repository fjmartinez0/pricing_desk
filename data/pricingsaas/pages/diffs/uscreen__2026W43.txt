What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Uscreen's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Per-plan limits rows removed from plan cards: one-time sales fee (10%/5%), video storage hours, live streaming hours, admin seats; contacts/products/support moved into generic prose. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $49, Growth at $149 and App Essentials at $449.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Uscreen. Uscreen has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
