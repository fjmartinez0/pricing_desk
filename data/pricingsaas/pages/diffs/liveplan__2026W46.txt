What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of LivePlan's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Live onboarding session removed from the Standard and Premium plan feature lists (cards rewritten; Premium now lists 'All Standard Features, Plus'). Support line now reads 'Human support… Optional 1-on-1 video coaching available'. Prices unchanged ($20/$15 Standard, $40/$30 Premium). Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Standard at $15, Premium at $30 and LivePlan Expert at $30.
It lands 42 days after LivePlan's previous change, "LivePlan launches Member Perks, unlocking $10K+ in partner discounts" (Sep 28, 2026). LivePlan has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Zoho, HubSpot, Microsoft, Clay and Google also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
