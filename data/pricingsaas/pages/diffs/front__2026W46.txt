What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Front's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Front AI Autopilot add-on per-conversation starting price rose from $0.05 to $0.39; Enterprise plan 'Smart rules' feature renamed to 'AI-powered routing and tagging', also added to the comparison table. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, feature change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $25, Professional at $65, Enterprise at $105, Copilot at $20 and API Rate Limit Increase at $200.
It lands 28 days after Front's previous change, "Front makes Smart QA and Smart CSAT Enterprise-only, adds External AI agents" (Oct 12, 2026). Front has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window HubSpot, Intercom and Gorgias also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
