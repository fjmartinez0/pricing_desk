What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of SoSafe's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: SoSafe removed its disclosed starting price ('Plans start from €625/month, billed annually, includes 50 seats') from the pricing page. All four tiers now show only 'Request pricing' with no numeric price shown anywhere on the page. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Core at EUR 625.
It lands 77 days after SoSafe's previous change, "SoSafe expands Professional plan lessons library from 35+ to 40+ courses" (Jul 6, 2026). SoSafe has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Gremlin and KnowBe4 also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
