What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Langfuse's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Langfuse Assistant (in-app AI agent) usage limits added — Low on Hobby, Medium on Core, High on Pro, High on Enterprise (previously no stated cap). In detail: Added tiered usage limits to the Langfuse Assistant (in-app AI agent), previously offered without a stated cap: Low on Hobby, Medium on Core, High on Pro, High on Enterprise. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Core at $29, Pro at $199, Enterprise at $2,499 and Teams at $300.
It lands 63 days after Langfuse's previous change, "Langfuse softens HIPAA claim, splits Metrics API v2, raises Core rate limits 10x" (Jul 13, 2026). Langfuse has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
