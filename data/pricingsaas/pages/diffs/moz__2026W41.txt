Moz triples AI Visibility quotas on all Pro plans, prices unchanged
Verified Oct 5, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: up
The changeMoz Pro limit — limit increase.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 28, 2026 → Oct 5, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Moz's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Visibility quotas tripled on all Moz Pro plans: dashboards 1/2/4 to 3/6/12 and tracked prompts 50/100/200 to 150/300/600; prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $49, Standard at $79, Medium at $143, Large at $239 and Additional Link Tracking Lists at $25.
It lands 21 days after Moz's previous change, "Moz Pro adds AI Sentiment Analysis to comparison table" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Moz boosts AI Models quota, adding Perplexity and Google AI Mode across plans" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Moz adds AI Citations tracking to Standard, Medium, and Large plans" (Jul 13, 2026). Moz has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Semrush and Ahrefs also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
