What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Shopware's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Shopware Nexus add-on moves from a free introductory phase to included monthly executions (100/1,000/2,000/5,000 by plan) with €10 per extra 1,000 executions. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Rise at EUR 600, Evolve at EUR 2,400, Shopware Intelligence+ (Community Edition) at EUR 29, Shopware Intelligence+ (Rise, Evolve, Beyond) at EUR 19 and Shopware Nexus at EUR 0.01 Nexus executions.
It lands 14 days after Shopware's previous change, "Shopware drops image editor from Intelligence+ AI add-on across all editions" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shopware extends free Intelligence+/Nexus request threshold trial to October 2026" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Shopware Nexus free tier caps free API usage at 10K reqs/customer/month" (Jul 20, 2026). Shopware has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
In the same window Salesforce also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
