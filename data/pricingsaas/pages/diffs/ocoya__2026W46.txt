What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Ocoya's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Black Friday 30% off banner with countdown; monthly prices shown struck from $41/$113/$284 to $29/$79/$199 on Starter/Team/Agency. List prices unchanged; Users row (1/5/20) removed from plan cards; Social profiles (5/20/100) renamed Channels with same limits; 'Scale profiles and team members' bullet became 'Add channels as you grow'; Footer tagline changed from 'Your social media copilot' to 'Yo. In detail: Footer tagline changed from 'Your social media copilot' to 'Your marketing copilot'; nav now organized around social, SEO and soon email. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added, metric change, positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $24.17, Team at $65.83 and Agency at $165.83.
It lands 14 days after Ocoya's previous change, "Ocoya adds 28-platform Integrations row to Starter, Team, Agency plans" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Ocoya's 7-day free trial now requires a card, adds Airtable and Notion" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Ocoya scraps unlimited Diamond tier, hikes entry price 93% in switch to…" (Sep 14, 2026).
