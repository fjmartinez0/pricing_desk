DeepSeek: peak-hour billing now excludes Chinese public holidays
Verified Sep 21, 2026·AI & Machine Learning·Direction: neutral
The changePricing model — metric change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 14, 2026 → Sep 21, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of DeepSeek's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: peak/off-peak billing window redefined so Chinese public holidays (and weekends) are always billed at off-peak (50% discount) rates, previously holidays fell under normal Mon-Fri peak pricing. In detail: Peak/off-peak billing window redefined: Chinese public holidays are now explicitly off-peak (50% discount), previously they fell under normal Mon-Fri peak-hour pricing. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists DeepSeek Flash at $0.15 per 1M input tokens (cache miss) + $0.3 per 100M input tokens (cache hit) and DeepSeek V4 Pro at $0.66 per 1M input tokens (cache miss) + $2.2 per 100M input tokens (cache hit).
It lands one day after DeepSeek's previous change, "DeepSeek extends 50% off-peak API discount to Chinese public holidays all day" (Sep 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "DeepSeek merges v4-flash and vision-exp into cheaper Flash V4.1" (Sep 14, 2026), "DeepSeek reverses V4 Pro retirement, keeps API billing unchanged past Sept 14" (Sep 12, 2026) and "DeepSeek slashes Flash pricing up to 57%, retires V4 Pro Sept 14" (Sep 11, 2026). DeepSeek has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
