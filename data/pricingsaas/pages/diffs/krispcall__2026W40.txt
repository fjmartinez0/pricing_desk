What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of KrispCall's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Starter, Advance, and Max plans now show quantified included usage (call minutes, SMS segments) with a 'Worth $X' value framing, replacing the vague flat 'Free $1 Call/SMS credit' line while base prices stayed the same; Integration access is now explicitly tiered by plan: Starter's '100+ integrations' is labeled 'Basic Only' and Max's is labeled 'Advanced', whereas before both plans referenced th. In detail: Integration access is now explicitly tiered by plan: Starter's '100+ integrations' is labeled 'Basic Only' and Max's is labeled 'Advanced', whereas before both plans referenced the same generic, unqualified '100+ integration platforms' benefit. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $12, Advance at $32 and Max at $48.
It lands 21 days after KrispCall's previous change, "KrispCall launches Max tier at $48/mo, renames plans, expands Startup program" (Sep 7, 2026). KrispCall has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
