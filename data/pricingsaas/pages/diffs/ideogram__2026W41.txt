What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Ideogram's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Priority credits increased on Plus (1,000→2,400/mo), Pro (3,500→7,500/mo), and Team (1,500→3,600/user/mo) at unchanged prices. Removed the $4 per-event fee to top up priority credits on Plus, Pro, and Team — now ‘Top up credits at any time’. In detail: Priority credit allowances increased across all paid plans at unchanged prices: Plus 1,000→2,400 credits/mo, Pro 3,500→7,500 credits/mo, Team 1,500→3,600 credits/user/mo; Removed the $4 per-event fee for topping up priority credits across Plus, Pro, and Team plans; feature copy now reads 'Top up credits at any time' with no stated charge. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit increase, price decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Plus at $15, Team at $20, Pro at $42 and Pay as you go at $0.01 per credit.
It lands 2 days after Ideogram's previous change, "Ideogram more than doubles priority credits on Plus, Pro and Team plans" (Oct 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Ideogram: Free tier credit guarantee restricted + Team plan queue upgraded" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Ideogram: adds Canvas workspace + Extend editing feature" (Aug 5, 2026). Ideogram has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Perplexity AI, Microsoft, Canva and Runway also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
