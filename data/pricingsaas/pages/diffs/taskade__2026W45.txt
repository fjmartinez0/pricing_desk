What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Taskade's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Starter plan added at $6/mo ($72 billed yearly) for 3 users, between Free and Pro, with 350 automation runs, 3 agents and 5 GB storage. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $6, Pro at $10, Business at $25, Max at $100 and Extra Credits at $10 per 20,000-credit pack (one-time) + $100 per 275,000-credit pack (one-time).
It lands 7 days after Taskade's previous change, "Taskade: Extra Credits ($5/pack; $5/9k, $20/45k, $50/125k) and Custom Domains ($5/year, from $4.99 first year) add-on…" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Taskade drops Claude below Enterprise, ends free credits, lists $250/mo tier" (Oct 12, 2026), "Taskade adds $5 Extra Credits and Custom Domains add-ons, doubles Free seats" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Taskade doubles Free plan seats to 2, swaps Gemini for DeepSeek" (Sep 7, 2026). Taskade has been tracked since 2024-10-14.
In the same window Notion, ClickUp, Asana and Trello also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
