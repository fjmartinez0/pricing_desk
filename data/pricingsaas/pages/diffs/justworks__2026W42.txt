What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Justworks's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Health Insurance Reimbursements feature relabeled from ICHRA to CHOICE in plan comparison; no price change. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Payroll at $50, PEO Basic at $79, PEO Plus at $124, EOR at $599 and Health Insurance Reimbursements (ICHRA) at $25.
It lands 101 days after Justworks's previous change, "Justworks: PEO Plus price increase $109→$124/mo + new ICHRA benefit" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Justworks adds ICHRA health insurance reimbursements at $25/employee" (Jun 23, 2026), "Justworks clarifies PEO Plus coverage with explicit dental, vision labels" (Jun 16, 2026) and "Justworks raises PEO Plus plan 14% to $124/month per employee" (Jun 9, 2026). Justworks has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Paychex and BambooHR also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
