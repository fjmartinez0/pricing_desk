MindsDB replaces plan-less rate card with Free and Pay-as-you-go tiers
Verified Oct 12, 2026·AI & Machine Learning·Direction: neutral

1 change·2 captures·Oct 11, 2026 → Oct 12, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 11, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing page moved from a plan-less rate card ('no subscription or plan') to explicit Free ($0/month) and Pay as you go (prepaid balance, provider rates) plan cards; model rates unchanged; Pay as you go adds auto top-up with a monthly cap, usage and spend tracking, and caching savings passed on; the 'Your keys' (own provider accounts) block is no longer shown. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pay as you go at $0.1 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.5 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands 7 days after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB hikes DeepSeek V4.1 Flash rates, ends $5,000 credit match promo" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB ends 100% credit match promo, up to $5,000 for new teams" (Oct 3, 2026), "MindsDB hikes DeepSeek V4.1 Flash output 82% to $1.20, adds Claude Sonnet 5.5" (Oct 2, 2026) and "MindsDB adds Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 models, cuts both DeepSeek V4 tiers" (Sep 28, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Huggingface and Alteryx also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
