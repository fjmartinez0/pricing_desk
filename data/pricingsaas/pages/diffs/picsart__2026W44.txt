What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Picsart's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Limited-time banner offering free unlimited WAN 3.0 generations for Pro and Ultra subscribers (ends Oct 31) was removed from the pricing page; Monthly credits cut: Pro 500 to 250, Ultra 2,500 to 1,500; credit slider tiers lowered (Ultra 1,500-10,000+ to 1,000-9,000+); Ultra now 6x Pro; prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed, limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $10.5 and Ultra at $60.
It lands 7 days after Picsart's previous change, "Picsart ends 10% off Pro/Ultra promo, launches unlimited WAN 3.0 offer" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Picsart launches limited-time 10% off Pro and Ultra, grows AI catalog to 200+ models" (Oct 12, 2026), "Picsart Pro and Ultra list Kling 3.0 4K, Seedance 2.5 access with output caps" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Picsart switches Seedance 2.5 quota from video count to seconds" (Sep 28, 2026). Picsart has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Corel and Canva also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
