What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Crystal Knows' pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Premium features repackaged by product: Personality Premium now full results/PDF/unlimited retakes; Career and Future Premium lists removed, now 'Free in full'; Story and People products added; Free tier assessments limited to one take each (was unlimited retakes), now Premium 'Unlimited retakes'; free Career limits (10 predicted assessments, 10 contacts, 1 free guide) dropped. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change, feature change). The current page lists Crystal Premium at $40.83 and Premium for Teams at $49.
It lands 14 days after Crystal Knows' previous change, "Crystal Knows cuts Premium insights from daily to weekly, opens Your Story free" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Crystal Knows: Premium Goals rewritten to weekly-to-yearly planning, Hidden Gems, 5/10/20-Year Visions; Personality adds…" (Oct 26, 2026), "Crystal Knows drops self-serve Predictions pricing for custom Buyer Profiles" (Oct 4, 2026) and "Crystal Knows renames Sales to Predictions, adds $49/seat team plan" (Sep 28, 2026). Crystal Knows has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
In the same window HubSpot and Salesforce also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
