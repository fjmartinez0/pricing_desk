What happened
Between the Oct 20, 2026 and Oct 21, 2026 captures of Intercom's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Essential plan now shows $29 struck through to $19 per seat/mo with a 'New customers save 35%' promo for new customers. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essential at $19, Advanced at $85, Expert at $132, Fin AI Agent (Standalone) at $0.99 per Fin outcome and Copilot at $29.
It lands 2 days after Intercom's previous change, "Intercom drops Fin Million Dollar Guarantee and 90-day money-back pledge" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Intercom Pro add-on starting price ticks up $1 to $100 per workspace" (Oct 12, 2026), "Intercom launches Fin Voice AI at $1.99 per outcome, adds 90-day guarantee" (Oct 11, 2026) and "Intercom ends $19 Essential promo, seat price jumps to $29/month" (Oct 3, 2026). Intercom has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
