What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Expend's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Elevate plan now includes automatic invoice/receipt-to-expense transaction matching; the comparison-table row (previously "AI receipt-to-transaction matching", Enhanced only) now marks it Included for Elevate and Enhanced. Enhanced card still lists AI receipt-to-transaction matching. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added).
It lands 96 days after Expend's previous change, "Expend: new Inbound SWIFT top-up fee (£7)" (Aug 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Expend adds Elevate & Enhanced tiers, slashes ATM fees to zero on top plan" (Mar 8, 2026). Expend has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
