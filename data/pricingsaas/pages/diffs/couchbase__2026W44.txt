What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Couchbase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Capella Analytics renamed to Operational Insights Capella; page states pricing is unchanged (from $0.61/hr per node). Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Capella Enterprise at $0.49 per node per hour, Capella Developer Pro at $0.35 per node per hour, Capella Basic at $0.15 per node per hour, Couchbase Enterprise Server at $0.66 per node per hour + $0.66 per node per hour and Enterprise Analytics at $0.67 per node per hour + $0.67 per node per hour.
It lands 76 days after Couchbase's previous change, "Couchbase: Capella AI Data Plane Enterprise price cut ($5.36→$0.86/hr per node)" (Aug 11, 2026). Couchbase has been tracked since 2023-12-06.
In the same window MongoDB, Elastic, Redis and DataStax also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
