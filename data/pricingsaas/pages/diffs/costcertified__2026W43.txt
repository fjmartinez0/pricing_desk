What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Bolster's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Entry plan Essentials ($199/mo, billed annually at $2,388) renamed Core and cut to $155/mo ($1,860/yr); kickoff + helpdesk support no longer included (moved to paid Coaching add-on). Additional users $20 → $16/mo annual; New Enterprise tier added (Custom pricing, billed annually, 6+ users) with franchise tools and API access; Talk to sales CTA; New Coaching add-on for Core: +$44/mo billed annual. In detail: Pro now includes job scheduling and time tracking; Premium adds job scheduling, time tracking, RFQ management and PO management (Premium now 5 users); New Coaching add-on for Core: +$44/mo billed annually (+$110/mo monthly) with 1x 1:1 kickoff session and ongoing helpdesk support. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease, new plan, feature added, new add-on) with a downward direction. The current page lists Core at $155, Pro at $399, Premium at $583.25 and Additional Users at $16.
It lands 63 days after Bolster's previous change, "Bolster (CostCertified): $1,999/yr coaching add-on now bundled into Pro/Premium" (Aug 17, 2026). Bolster has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Carta also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
