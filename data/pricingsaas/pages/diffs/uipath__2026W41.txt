What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of UiPath's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Maestro bullet in the platform list split into two: Maestro Automate (automate tasks) and Maestro Orchestrate (orchestrate business processes). Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $25.
It lands 14 days after UiPath's previous change, "UiPath: Basic plan drops self-serve trial, repositions to team automation" (Sep 21, 2026). UiPath has been tracked since 2025-01-01.
