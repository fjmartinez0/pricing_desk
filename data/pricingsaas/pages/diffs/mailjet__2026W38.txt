What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Mailjet's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Essential plan rose from $17/month to $19/month and Premium plan rose from $27/month to $29/month; Free and Starter prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $8.1, Essential at $15.3 and Premium at $24.3.
It lands 41 days after Mailjet's previous change, "Mailjet: Anonymous Tracking added to Starter plan" (Aug 3, 2026). Mailjet has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
