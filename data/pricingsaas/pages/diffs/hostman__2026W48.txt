What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Hostman's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Bare Metal entry-level 'From' price decreased from $120/month to $110/month. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Backend Apps at $4, Managed Databases at $4, Cloud Servers 1vCPU 2GB at $5, Backend Apps 1vCPU 2GB at $5 and Backend Apps 2vCPU 2GB at $6.
