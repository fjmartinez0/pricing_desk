What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Warp's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Max plan included monthly agent usage now $200 (10x Build), down from 18,000 credits (12x Build), worth $240 at 1,500 credits = $20; Credit-based wording replaced: 'Reload credits' becomes 'Additional agent usage' (Free) / 'Add-on agent usage' (Build, Max); included usage restated in dollars instead of credits; Volume-based discounts on usage reloads dropped: Build and Max cards change 'Reload c. In detail: Volume-based discounts on usage reloads dropped: Build and Max cards change 'Reload credits with volume-based discounts, auto-reload, and team-wide spend cap' to 'Add-on agent usage, auto-reload, and team-wide spend cap'; no volume discount mentioned anywhere on the page. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit decrease, discount removed, positioning) with a downward direction. The current page lists Build at $18, Business at $45 and Max at $180.
It lands 49 days after Warp's previous change, "Warp launches Warp Factories with 5 tiers and $10K Early Access credit, drops Claude 50% off promo" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Warp: Agent CLI access on Free + bring-your-own API keys on Business" (Aug 11, 2026). Warp has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Google, Vercel, Render, Digitalocean and Railway also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
