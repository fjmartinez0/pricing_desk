What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Aftership's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Tracking pricing page rebuilt: annual-shipment-count slider replaced by a monthly-volume selector (default now 1,000 shipments/month) with a full per-tier price table added; headline prices now show Essentials $49/month and Premium $99/month at the new 1,000/month default vs $29/$59 shown at the old 500/month-equivalent default — per-tier prices at matching volume are unchanged (500/mo tier still. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change). The current page lists Essentials at $49 and Premium at $99.
It lands the same day as Aftership's previous change, "Aftership Feed launches Affiliates at $40/mo, drops $999 Advanced Support add-on" (Nov 22, 2026). Aftership has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Shippo and EasyPost also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
