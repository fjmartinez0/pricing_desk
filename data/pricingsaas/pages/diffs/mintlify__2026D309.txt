What happened
Between the Nov 4, 2026 and Nov 5, 2026 captures of Mintlify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: "Automations" (250 credits/update, available on Pro+Enterprise) renamed to "Autopilot". Enterprise also gains a new, Enterprise-exclusive "Automations" feature at 250 credits/update, not available on Pro. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $450.
It lands 17 days after Mintlify's previous change, "Mintlify swaps free credits for 14-day Pro trial, no card needed" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mintlify swaps free credits for a 14-day Pro trial with 5,000 credits" (Oct 13, 2026), "Mintlify ends free 6-month Pro offer for early-stage startups" (Oct 3, 2026) and "Mintlify offers startups 6 months of Pro free, no card needed" (Oct 1, 2026). Mintlify has been tracked since 2024-10-21.
In the same window Postman, Notion and GitBook also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
