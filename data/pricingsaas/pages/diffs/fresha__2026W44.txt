Fresha adds $29.95 messaging-only AI Concierge tier, unlimited messages
Verified Oct 26, 2026·Business Operations·Direction: neutral
The changeAdd-on — add-on change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 19, 2026 → Oct 26, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Fresha's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Concierge add-on split into two tiers: new 'Messaging only' tier at $29.95/location/month with unlimited messaging, and 'Calls and messaging' tier at $99.95/location/month (same price as before) now with unlimited messaging instead of 500 free messages + $0.04/extra msg; call minutes allowance (200 included, then $0.60/min) unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Team at $14.95, Independent at $19.95, AI Concierge - Messaging Only at $29.95, Smart Website at $15.95 and Data Connector at $295.
It lands 14 days after Fresha's previous change, "Fresha adds instant phone and 2-minute chat support, slows email to 2 days" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fresha launches Smart website add-on at $15.95/month for all plans" (Sep 7, 2026). Fresha has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Acuity Scheduling and Calendly also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
