What happened
Between the Oct 14, 2026 and Oct 15, 2026 captures of Tailscale's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Priority support (Premium) and dedicated professional services (Enterprise) dropped; Enterprise 'Custom MSA & SLAs' now 'Custom MSA'; table row now 'Access to Support SLAs'; New Support packages (Silver, Gold, Platinum SLA-backed) and separately scoped Support Add-ons replace Premium support section; Silver self-serve. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change, feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Standard at $8, Premium at $18, Mullvad at $5 and Add more tagged resources at $1.
It lands 3 days after Tailscale's previous change, "Tailscale adds Custom tailnet subdomains to Enterprise plan" (Oct 12, 2026). Tailscale has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Google Cloud also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
