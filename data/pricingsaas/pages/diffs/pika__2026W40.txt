What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Pika's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Top-up credits are now restricted to Pika Create on pika.art and can no longer be used with the API, MCP, iOS app, or old.pika.art. The $25 starter pack (1,500 credits) was briefly pulled from the credit-pack lineup Sep 23–28 and a 66,000-credit pack at $1,000 appeared alongside the 3,000–30,000 packs; both changes reversed Sep 29, when the $25 pack returned and the 66,000 pack was removed. In detail: Added new usage restriction for top-up credits: they are now stated to be usable only within Pika Create on pika.art, and explicitly cannot be used with the API, MCP, iOS app, or old.pika.art; The entry-level 1,500-credit top-up pack ($25) was removed; the smallest credit pack is now 3,000 credits for $50 (temporary: restored in the Sep 29, 2026 capture). A 66,000-credit pack at $1,000 appeared alongside the 3,000–30,000 packs. Both changes reversed Sep 29, when the 1,500-credit $25 pack returned and the 66,000 pack was removed. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change, add-on removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Starter at $8, Creator at $28, Fancy at $76 and Credit Pack at $1 per 60 credits.
It lands 5 days after Pika's previous change, "Pika limits top-up credits to Create on pika.art, blocks API, MCP, apps" (Sep 23, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pika: Commercial license restricted to Creator+, Priority queue/early access added" (Sep 21, 2026). Pika has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window Synthesia, OpenAI, Runway, Descript and Adobe also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
