What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of GTmetrix's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Test Locations limit increased from 25 to 28 for both the Advanced ($24.99/mo) and Expert ($49.99/mo) plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Lite at $4.99, Core at $9.99, Advanced at $24.99 and Expert at $49.99.
It lands 14 days after GTmetrix's previous change, "GTmetrix ends SUMMER2026 30% promo, list prices hold across all plans" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "GTmetrix: 30% off all PRO plans (Summer 2026 promo)" (Aug 11, 2026) and "GTmetrix: Daily API Credits increased for Lite and Core plans" (Jul 3, 2026). GTmetrix has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
