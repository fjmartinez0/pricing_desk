What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Crowdstrike's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Falcon Complete Next-Gen MDR plan renamed to Falcon Complete; bundle features and Contact sales pricing unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Falcon Go at $5, Falcon Pro at $8.33 and Falcon Enterprise at $15.42.
It lands 192 days after Crowdstrike's previous change, "Crowdstrike Adds Monthly Billing Option Across All Falcon Plans" (Apr 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Crowdstrike removes bundle comparison table and add-on module listings" (Week 7, 2026), "Crowdstrike Introduces 15-Day Free Trial for Falcon, Enhancing Access to Next-Gen Antivirus and Device Protection" (Q3 2025) and "Crowdstrike Removes Falcon Elite Plan from Pricing Page, Enhancing Falcon Go and Pro Plans with New Features" (Dec 2024). Crowdstrike has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Microsoft and Sentinelone also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
