What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of FlutterFlow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro plan's Studio label renamed to Growth and Max renamed to Business; prices unchanged ($80 and $150 monthly, annual $60/$113). In detail: Studio-side tier labels renamed: Pro in Studio → Growth in Studio, Max in Studio → Business in Studio; FlutterFlow plan names and prices unchanged ($80/$150 monthly, $60/$113 annual). Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Basic at $29.25, Pro at $60 and Max at $63.75.
It lands 7 days after FlutterFlow's previous change, "FlutterFlow renames Growth and Business plans Pro and Max, revamps AI limits" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "FlutterFlow swaps Lite credits for Native AI Agent usage tiers" (Nov 1, 2026). FlutterFlow has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
