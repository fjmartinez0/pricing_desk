Employment Hero swaps candidate sourcing for 2.3M+ talent pool access
Verified Oct 12, 2026·Workforce Management·Direction: neutral
The changeRecruitment feature — feature change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 5, 2026 → Oct 12, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Employment Hero's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Recruitment plans: 'Candidate sourcing' row replaced by 'Access to 2.3 million+ talent pool'; new footnote says matching is free but a one-off placement fee applies on hires from proactively matched candidates. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists HR Essentials at $10, Payroll at $10, HR Engage at $14, Recruitment Pro 50 at $199 and Recruitment Pro 100 at $359.
It lands 35 days after Employment Hero's previous change, "Employment Hero reveals pricing for 7 hidden add-ons in new card grid" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Employment Hero unbundles AI recruitment: now an add-on in every tier" (Aug 17, 2026). Employment Hero has been tracked since 2025-12-22.
In the same window Paychex and BambooHR also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
