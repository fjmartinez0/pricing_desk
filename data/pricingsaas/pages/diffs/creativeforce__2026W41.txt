What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Creative Force's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Dreem AI credits add-on withdrawn: 'AI Content Generation by Dreem included / Credits as add-on' line and Dreem Pro/Enterprise Integration (New) rows removed from Core and Enterprise cards, and the credit price list ($0.16/credit entry volume, $0.06/credit enterprise volume, per-asset cost table) dropped; Dreem now listed as a closed beta with an application form; Enterprise 'Multiple Workspaces'. In detail: Enterprise 'Multiple Workspaces' became 'Unlimited Workspaces'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, add-on removed) with an upward direction.
It lands 91 days after Creative Force's previous change, "Creative Force quietly drops "Unlimited Workspaces" claim on Enterprise plan" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Creative Force raises Core plan 13% to $3,583/mo" (May 12, 2026). Creative Force has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
