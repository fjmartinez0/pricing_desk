What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Browse AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Annual-only credit top-ups (1,000 credit minimum) and Setup and onboarding services from $399 for Professional/Premium listed as paid add-ons; plan prices unchanged; Premium adds 98% data-accuracy SLA; monitoring frequency now Every minute on all plans (was Hourly/5 minutes); support tiers renamed Self/Standard/Priority/Dedicated Account Manager; Personal credits 12,000/yr become 12K to 24K/yr a. In detail: New paid Setup and onboarding service from $399 (annual plans, Professional and Premium only), listed on the Professional card and in the new Add-ons card; credit top-ups in the same card were already offered at unchanged rates ($0.024 Personal, $0.017-$0.013 Professional per credit); plan prices unchanged; Premium adds 98% data-accuracy SLA; Free monitoring frequency improved from Hourly (Personal/Professional were 5 minutes) - comparison table now says Every minute on all plans, though the hero strip still says every 5 minutes; support tiers renamed Self/Standard/Priority/Dedicated Account Manager. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Personal at $38, Professional at $69 and Premium at $500.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Browse AI. Browse AI has been tracked since 2026-09-09.
