What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of E2b's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Hobby (free) plan concurrent sandbox limit cut from 20 to 10; Pro and above unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered). The current page lists Pro at $150.
It lands 14 days after E2b's previous change, "E2b cuts Enterprise minimum commit to $2,000/mo, adds 500 sandboxes" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "E2b prices concurrency add-ons: +$500/mo for 600, +$1,000/mo for 1,100" (Sep 13, 2026). E2b has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Codecademy, Udemy and Coursera also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
