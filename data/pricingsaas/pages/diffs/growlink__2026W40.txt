What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Growlink's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter plan's 14-day free trial offer was removed; billing line now reads only 'Billed monthly' and CTA changed from 'Start Free Trial' to 'Get Started'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Sprout at $25, Bloom at $250 and Harvest at $1,000.
It lands 28 days after Growlink's previous change, "Growlink: new $50 Essential tier added, capacity boosts (Professional users 3→5)" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Growlink: Root free plan discontinued" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Growlink: Bloom plan included user seats reduced (5→3)" (Aug 10, 2026). Growlink has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Holded, Threecolts, Shippo, Tatsoft and Smokeball also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
