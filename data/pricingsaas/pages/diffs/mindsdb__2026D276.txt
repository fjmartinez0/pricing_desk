What happened
Between the Oct 2, 2026 and Oct 3, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: MindsHub Foundry promo removed: 100% match on inference-credit purchases for first three months, up to $5,000, for selected teams. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pay as you go at $0.1 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.5 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands one day after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB hikes DeepSeek V4.1 Flash output 82% to $1.20, adds Claude Sonnet 5.5" (Oct 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB adds Jev decision model to free tier alongside MindsHub Air" (Sep 24, 2026), "MindsDB ditches metered tokens for unmetered free tier; Blaze rates jump up t" (Sep 21, 2026) and "MindsDB hikes Blaze rates up to 183%, loosens Air free-tier cap" (Sep 15, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Huggingface and Alteryx also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
“MindsHub” is the name MindsDB’s pricing page uses; Pulse files the change under MindsDB, the company it tracks.
