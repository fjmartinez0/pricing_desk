What happened
Between the Nov 20, 2026 and Nov 21, 2026 captures of Mintlify's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New "Starter" plan added at $150/month billed annually ($180/month billed monthly), between Free and Pro — includes Free's features plus 10 editor seats, Autopilot, Agent, and Preview deployments. Pro plan now explicitly includes "Password protection" and "Advanced analytics", not previously listed. In detail: Pro plan's included-features summary now explicitly calls out "Password protection" and "Advanced analytics" as new Pro-exclusive additions, not previously listed; New "Starter" plan added at $150/mo billed annually ($180/mo billed monthly), positioned between Free and Pro for small teams shipping fast. Includes everything in Free plus 10 editor seats, Autopilot, Agent, and Preview deployments. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, new plan). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 22, 2026) lists Pro at $450.
It lands 3 days after Mintlify's previous change, "Mintlify adds $150/mo Starter plan with 10 editors, Agent and Autopilot" (Nov 18, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mintlify renames Starter plan to Free, keeps $0 price and 5 editors" (Nov 17, 2026), "Mintlify renames Automations to Autopilot, adds Enterprise-only Automations" (Nov 5, 2026) and "Mintlify swaps free credits for a 14-day Pro trial with 5,000 credits" (Oct 13, 2026).
