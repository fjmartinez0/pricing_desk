What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of GiveWP's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pro ($399/yr) and Elite ($599/yr) tiers replaced by per-site-count plans: 1 site $350, 10 sites $599, 25 $799, 50 $899, 100 $999, all features included; Every plan now includes the full package: Give, The Events Calendar, LearnDash, and Kadence. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 28, 2026) lists 1 Site at $29.17, 10 Sites at $49.92, 25 Sites at $66.58, 50 Sites at $74.92 and 100 Sites at $83.25.
It lands 7 days after GiveWP's previous change, "GiveWP drops $199 Essentials plan, leaving Pro and Elite tiers only" (Oct 19, 2026). GiveWP has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
