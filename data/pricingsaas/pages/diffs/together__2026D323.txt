What happened
Between the Nov 18, 2026 and Nov 19, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Qwen4 Max model added to Serverless Inference (Chat) pricing at $1.43/1M input tokens, $4.28/1M output tokens, $0.0713/1M cached input tokens. In detail: Added Qwen4 Max model to Serverless Inference (Chat) pricing at $1.43/1M input tokens, $4.28/1M output tokens, $0.0713/1M cached input tokens. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 22, 2026) lists Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $4 per hour (H100), GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days), Sandbox (Code Sandbox) at $0.04 per vCPU/hour + $0.01 per GiB RAM/hour and Serverless Inference at $0.15 per 1M input tokens.
It lands one day after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI adds Elorian Glimpse 1.0 ($0.60/$3.00), drops Qwen2.5 7B Turbo" (Nov 18, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI: HGX B200 GPU price hikes to $9.29/hr + 3 more rates shown" (Nov 13, 2026), "Together AI adds US geo-pinned models, Kimi K3 US at $3.75/$18.75, live GPU status" (Nov 11, 2026) and "Together AI raises Qwen3.7-Max inference prices 67%, drops four serverless models" (Nov 7, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
