What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Qwen3.7-Max serverless inference — input $2.00→$2.50/1M tokens, output $6.00→$7.50/1M tokens (cached $0.25 unchanged). In detail: Qwen3.7-Max serverless inference pricing increased 25% — input from $2.00 to $2.50 per 1M tokens, output from $6.00 to $7.50 per 1M tokens (cached input unchanged at $0.25/1M tokens). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Managed Storage at $0.16 per GiB/month, Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, Sandbox (Code Interpreter) at $0.03 per session (60 min), GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100) and GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days).
It lands 5 days after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI hikes Qwen3.7-Max inference 25% to $2.50/$7.50 per 1M tokens" (Sep 16, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI reveals NVIDIA HGX B300 pricing at $9.99/GPU/hr, $4.99 preemptible" (Sep 25, 2026), "Together AI cuts Qwen3.7-Max and Qwen3.8 Flash inference prices by 40%" (Sep 23, 2026) and "Together AI: Qwen3.7-Max price hike + new DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model" (Sep 14, 2026).
