What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of MailSlurp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New provider/device coverage section: inbox placement on all plans; Google Workspace/M365 placement Pro+; warmup checkpoints Growth/Enterprise only; premium render targets may cost more. In detail: New Provider and device coverage section: Yahoo Mail added to inbox placement on all plans (cards listed only Gmail/Outlook); Growth/Enterprise warmup checkpoints now cover Yahoo, Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 (previously Gmail and Outlook); premium render target list expanded (iOS/iPadOS Mail, Gmail iOS, Outlook mobile, Gmail.com, Outlook.com, Yahoo.com). Usage rates now note premium targets may cost more, and light/dark renders bill as separate targets. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $19.17, Pro at $45.83, Growth at $116.67, Inbox placement at $1 per test and Device rendering at $0.5 per render.
It lands 7 days after MailSlurp's previous change, "MailSlurp adds $0.01/credit email warmup usage rate + 1 more change" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MailSlurp adds AI token disclosures and email warm-up as usage caps quietly c" (Sep 21, 2026), "MailSlurp kills paid trials for Pro and Team, points users to Free plan" (Sep 7, 2026) and "MailSlurp: Free & Phone number capacity increased" (Aug 31, 2026). MailSlurp has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
