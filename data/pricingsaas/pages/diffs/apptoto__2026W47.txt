What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Apptoto's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: AI Receptionist and AI Assistant included in every plan at $0 to enable; usage-based AI credits: $100 (5,000 credits) to start, top-up packs $20-$400; calls use 2 message credits/min; soft phone credit usage; Added AI Receptionist, AI Assistant, MCP server, webhooks and soft phone to every plan; Public Developer API replaced by Agent-friendly API. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, new add-on). The current page lists Starting Plan at $10, Reputation Management at $30 and White Glove Onboarding at $499 per use.
It lands 91 days after Apptoto's previous change, "Apptoto: HIPAA plan pricing revealed ($208/mo)" (Aug 16, 2026). Apptoto has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
