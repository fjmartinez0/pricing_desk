What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Tripletex's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan cards rewritten with new taglines and feature lists; per-card 'Legg til årsoppgjør' replaced by Årsoppgjør (990 kr/år) in the all-packages list. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning).
It lands 14 days after Tripletex's previous change, "Tripletex plan cards now promote 990 kr/år Årsoppgjør add-on on all paid tiers" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Tripletex rewrites plan cards, adds Assistenten and free support to Basis" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Tripletex renames AI-assistenten to Assistenten in package comparison" (Oct 12, 2026). Tripletex has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window X, Fortnox, Dinero and Visma eAccounting also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
