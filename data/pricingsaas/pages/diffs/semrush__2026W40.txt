What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Semrush's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Semrush added a new Free plan at $0/mo (no credit card required) offering 1 demo project, 10 reports/day across all tools, and Help Center & basic onboarding; AI Visibility features excluded. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists SEO at $117.33, Starter at $165.17, Pro+ at $248.17, Advanced at $549 and AI Visibility Base at $99.
It lands 48 days after Semrush's previous change, "Semrush: Enterprise plan pitch overhauled with 11 new capabilities" (Aug 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Semrush: SEO plans restructured into 4 tiers, Guru→Pro+ price increase" (Jul 6, 2026) and "Semrush drops outreach credits from Lead Gen add-on, adds Claude & Grok to En" (Jul 4, 2026). Semrush has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Similarweb, Ahrefs, Moz and Google also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
