What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Clicksend's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: US SMS carrier fee (TFN, 10DLC and Shortcode) raised $0.0040→$0.0042 per message (+5%). Other US rates moved ~1.5% lower in step across every channel (currency-conversion recompute, not a price change). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Growth at $0.02 per SMS, Enterprise at $0.01 per SMS, Boost at $0.03 per SMS, Scale at $0.01 per SMS and Toll Free Number at $3.47.
It lands 236 days after Clicksend's previous change, "Clicksend Slashes SMS Rates Up to 2.3% and Lowers Tier Entry Barriers" (Apr 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Clicksend Raises Dedicated Number and Shortcode Fees Up to 1%" (Mar 23, 2026), "Clicksend overhauls pricing to named plans with 7-21% savings tiers" (Mar 16, 2026) and "Clicksend Lowers Toll Free Number Fee to $3.52 Per Month" (Mar 10, 2026). Clicksend has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Mailgun, Klaviyo, Bird, Telnyx and Twilio also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
