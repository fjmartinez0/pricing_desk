What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Forest Admin's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise uptime SLA in the Reliability & support table raised from 99.9% to 99.99%. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists Starter at $499, Professional at $2,199 and Enterprise at $5,999.
It lands 14 days after Forest Admin's previous change, "Forest Admin moves response-time SLA to Enterprise, softens uptime to target" (Nov 1, 2026).
