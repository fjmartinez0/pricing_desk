What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Klaviyo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Paid plan prices now shown on a redesigned tier page: Email $20/month and Marketing $25/month (500 active profiles); the previous free-plan builder page showed no paid prices; Limited-time promo: first month of an eligible Marketing plan for $1 (list $25) for up to 5,000 active profiles; Free plan now includes Social marketing (250 profiles), Customer Agent (10 resolutions/mo) and Reviews (50 or. In detail: Free plan now includes Social marketing (250 profiles), Customer Agent (10 resolutions/mo) and Reviews (50 orders/mo); Helpdesk, already listed as free, is now capped at 10 tickets/mo. Pulse files it as a pricing change (freemium change, discount added, price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Email at $20, Marketing at $25, Customer Hub at $4, Mobile messaging at $0.01 per SMS (US, Toll free) + $0.04 per MMS (US, Toll free) and Reviews at $25.
It lands 2 days after Klaviyo's previous change, "Klaviyo adds $1 first-month deal on new $20 Email and $25 Marketing plans" (Nov 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Klaviyo swaps free Composer credits for $5 usage cap, adds AI Helpdesk" (Sep 9, 2026). Klaviyo has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp and Convertkit also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
