What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Profound's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Projects (shared team spaces) now included in the free Trial plan (was No); AI Marketer 'Opportunities' feature renamed 'Suggestions'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction.
It lands 21 days after Profound's previous change, "Profound: Trial plan expanded to 50 prompts, adds Gemini + Google AI Overviews" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Profound kills $99 and $399 paid tiers for a single free trial plan" (Sep 21, 2026). Profound has been tracked since 2026-07-22.
In the same window Dialpad and Fireflies.ai also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
