What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Kajabi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan cards now state a 14-day free trial with 30-day money-back guarantee; prior cards only had Choose Plan CTAs. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $143, Growth at $199 and Pro at $399.
It lands 49 days after Kajabi's previous change, "Kajabi kills $1,678 launch offer as Sept 15 countdown expires" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kajabi dangles $1,678 in free launch support before Sept 15 deadline" (Sep 14, 2026). Kajabi has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Thinkific, Leadpages, ActiveCampaign, Podia and Convertkit also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
