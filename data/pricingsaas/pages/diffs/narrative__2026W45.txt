What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Narrative's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New banner: lock in for 12 months and get 30% off; list prices shown struck ($15/$29/$59/$79) vs unchanged $10/$20/$40/$60. Free trial copy and Start Free Trial CTAs replaced by Get plan; Headline changed from 'Plans to fit every photographer' to 'Choose the plan that fits your workflow'; plan taglines rewritten. In detail: New Black Friday banner: lock in for 12 months and get 30% off (links to /black-friday-2026: annual plans 30% off all November, e.g. Standard $20→$14/mo, Premium $40→$28/mo, Ultra $60→$42/mo). Card prices unchanged at $10/$20/$40/$60 yearly; the struck $15/$29/$59/$79 are the existing monthly-billing prices. Free-trial subhead and Start Free Trial CTAs replaced by Get plan (trial still offered per FAQ/nav). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added, positioning) with a neutral direction. It lands 121 days after Narrative's previous change, "Narrative: AI Preset count doubled (Color+B&W) + HEIC/HEIF, People Filter added" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Narrative drops 30-day limit from Ultra plan free trial" (Apr 24, 2026) and "Narrative splits AI Presets into Color and B&W with tiered access" (Apr 13, 2026).
