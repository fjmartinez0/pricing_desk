What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Magnific's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Premium annual effective price rose from $7.25 to $14.50/mo as the 50% off September promo ended; banner and Extra 50% OFF badge removed; annual savings claim fell from up to 63% to 25%. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Premium at $14.5, Premium+ at $33.75, Business at $55, Pro Starter at $82.5 and Pro at $210.
It lands 7 days after Magnific's previous change, "Magnific slashes Premium annual price 50% to $7.25/mo before Sept 28 deadline" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Magnific swaps MiniMax H3 2K for Max Turbo 768p, renames Pro to Pro Starter" (Sep 14, 2026), "Magnific splits Pro into credit tiers, entry price drops to $82.50/mo" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Magnific adds Wan 3.0 AI video models at launch discount" (Sep 1, 2026). Magnific has been tracked since 2026-06-04.
