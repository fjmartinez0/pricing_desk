What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of LOGO.com's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Business Plan yearly-billing discount removed; first-year cost billed yearly rose from $120 ($10/mo effective) to $600 ($50/mo, full monthly rate). In detail: Business Plan's annual-billing discount was removed: first-year cost when billed yearly rose from $120 ($10/mo effective) to $600 ($50/mo, the full monthly rate). Brand Plan's 80% annual discount is unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Brand Plan at CAD 3.6 and Brand Plan Plus at CAD 11.4.
It lands 35 days after LOGO.com's previous change, "LOGO.com: Brand Plan Plus renamed to Business Plan, now $50/mo (was $38) + 5 seats" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LOGO.com caps unlimited saves at 100/5/1, adds storage and AI credit limits" (Jul 13, 2026). LOGO.com has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Looka, Figma, Canva, Piktochart and Adobe also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
