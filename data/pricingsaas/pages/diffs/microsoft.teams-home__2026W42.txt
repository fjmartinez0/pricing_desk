What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Microsoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Microsoft 365 Family and Premium (up to 6 people) storage cut from up to 6 TB (1 TB per person) to 2 TB shared. Prices unchanged ($129.99 / $199.99 per year). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Microsoft 365 Personal at $8.33, Microsoft 365 Family at $10.83 and Microsoft 365 Premium at $16.67.
It lands 98 days after Microsoft's previous change, "Microsoft Teams Home bundles Copilot into Business Standard, hikes price to $2" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Microsoft Teams Home Teams/365: Copilot Business add-on promo renewed to Q3 2026" (Jul 3, 2026). Microsoft has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window Google and Salesforce also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
