What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Optery's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Data broker removal coverage trimmed: Core 380+ to 375+, Extended 555+ to 550+, Ultimate 625+ to 620+ sites (Expanded Reach 400+ to 395+, custom total 940+ to 935+). Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered). The current page lists Core at $3.25, Extended at $12.42 and Ultimate at $20.70.
It lands 14 days after Optery's previous change, "Optery expands Ultimate plan coverage to 400+ sites without Expanded Reach" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Optery lifts Core coverage to 380+ sites, prices hold steady" (Oct 26, 2026), "Optery trims data broker coverage by 5 sites across Core and Ultimate" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Optery trims automated removal coverage to 555+ sites on Extended plan" (Oct 11, 2026). Optery has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
