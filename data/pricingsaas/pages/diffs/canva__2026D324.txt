What happened
Between the Nov 19, 2026 and Nov 20, 2026 captures of Canva's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New promo: 50% off Canva Pro or Business for the first 3 months on monthly plans (select countries; not available with free trial). List prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Nov 23, 2026) lists Pro at $12 and Business at $20.83.
It lands 21 days after Canva's previous change, "Canva hides free plan's AI quota, swapping 20 uses for vague limited access" (Oct 30, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Canva Pro drops 20% to $144/year, savings jump past 16%" (Sep 11, 2026) and "Canva reshuffles AI usage limits, rebrands allowance as usage" (Sep 9, 2026). Canva has been tracked since 2023-11-17.
In the same window Figma and Visme also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
