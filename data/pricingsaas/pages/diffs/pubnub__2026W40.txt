What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of PubNub's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Serverless Functions bullets dropped from the Free Plan ('5 test Serverless Functions, 7-day runtime') and Starter Plan ('30 Serverless Functions') 'What's included' lists. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Starter at $98.
It lands 21 days after PubNub's previous change, "PubNub: Serverless Functions added to Free & Starter plans" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "PubNub adds free Serverless Functions, boosts Starter to 30 functions" (Jul 20, 2026). PubNub has been tracked since 2026-05-13.
In the same window Supabase and Twilio also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
