What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of PageProof's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: FinalFile delivery integrations now include Google Drive alongside Dropbox, OneDrive, and SharePoint. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Team at $208.25 and Team Plus at $416.58.
It lands 21 days after PageProof's previous change, "PageProof adds Dropbox to FinalFile delivery alongside OneDrive, SharePoint" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "PageProof: FinalFile delivery destination in comparison table expanded from OneDrive to OneDrive and SharePoint" (Oct 26, 2026), "PageProof hikes Team Plus price 25% to $4,999/yr, adds AI credits to all plans" (Oct 19, 2026) and "PageProof cuts Team Plus to $399/mo, drops FinalFile and AI table rows" (Oct 12, 2026). PageProof has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Workato, Docusign and Adobe also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
