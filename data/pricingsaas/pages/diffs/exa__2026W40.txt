What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Exa's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free/Starter plan sign-up credit terms changed from $20 sign-up bonus plus $10/month to $10/month plus a $10 onboarding bonus, cutting first-month total credits from $30 to $20. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Search at $7 per 1k requests, up to 10 results + $1 per 1k page AI summaries, Agent at $0.01 per search tool call + $0.1 per request (Medium effort), Contents at $1 per 1k pages, per content type + $1 per 1k page AI summaries, Deep Search at $12 per 1k requests, up to 10 results + $1 per 1k requests, additional result above 10 and Deep-Reasoning Search at $1 per 1k page AI summaries + $1 per 1k requests, additional result above 10.
It lands 21 days after Exa's previous change, "Exa boosts Starter agent concurrency 3→50 and doubles QPS limits" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Exa: Answer endpoint overage + AI-summary fees removed" (Aug 3, 2026), "Exa: Free tier switched to credit-based model ($20 signup + $10/mo)" (Jul 20, 2026) and "Exa halves Agent per-run price ceiling to $1.00, matching X-high tier" (Jul 6, 2026). Exa has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Google and You.com also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
