What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Magnific's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Third-tier plan's card heading renamed from 'Pro' to 'Pro Starter', matching the name already used in the comparison table further down the page; MiniMax H3 2K video model (550 credits/5s, 50% off promo) replaced by MiniMax H3 Max Turbo 768p at a lower 200 credits/5s rate with no promo tag, and higher video allowances for Premium and Premium+ plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, plan renamed) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Premium at $14.5, Premium+ at $33.75, Business at $55, Pro Starter at $82.5 and Pro at $210.
It lands 7 days after Magnific's previous change, "Magnific splits Pro into credit tiers, entry price drops to $82.50/mo" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Magnific adds Wan 3.0 AI video models at launch discount" (Sep 1, 2026), "Magnific: Premium plan price increased $7.25 -> $14.50/month" (Aug 3, 2026) and "Magnific: Seedream 5.0 Pro credits ↑75→100/image (1.5K→2K)" (Jul 27, 2026). Magnific has been tracked since 2026-06-04.
In the same window Runway, Microsoft, Getty Images and Huggingface also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
