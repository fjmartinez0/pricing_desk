What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Proof's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro+ ($199/mo + transaction fees) now shows a 14-day free trial badge and a 'Start your free trial' CTA, replacing the 'Most popular' badge. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change). The current page lists Pro+ at $199 and Pro at $4 per identity verification + $10 per on-demand witness.
It lands 70 days after Proof's previous change, "Proof restructures pricing: new $199 Pro+ tier, Premium+Enterprise merged" (Sep 7, 2026). Proof has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window Grain, Fireflies.ai, Fathom, Dialpad and Avoma also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
