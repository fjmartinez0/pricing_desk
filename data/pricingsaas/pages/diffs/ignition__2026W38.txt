What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Ignition's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Enterprise plan tier introduced for larger multi-office firms, CTA "Talk to sales" Pro+ plan user limit reduced from Unlimited users to 20 users. In detail: Introduced new Enterprise plan tier for larger multi-office firms, with flexible client/user packages, enterprise-grade security, dedicated CSM, and quarterly roadmap reviews. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease, new plan) with a downward direction.
