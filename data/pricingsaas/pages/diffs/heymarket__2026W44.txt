What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Heymarket's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: MCP server integration added to the comparison table; available on Plus, Pro and Enterprise, not on Standard. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $20, Plus at $41, Pro at $62 and Salesforce Add-on at $7.5.
It lands 14 days after Heymarket's previous change, "Heymarket drops BETA tag from AI Agents Q&A, scheduling, enrichment" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Heymarket bundles 8 email add-ons into Plus and up, drops them from Standard" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Heymarket: RCS Branded Messaging added (Plus, Pro, Enterprise)" (Aug 3, 2026). Heymarket has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Bird, Telnyx, Google Cloud, Twilio and Plivo also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
