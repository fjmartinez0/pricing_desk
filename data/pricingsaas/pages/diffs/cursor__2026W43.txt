What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Cursor's pricing page, Pulse recorded 7 changes: Individual ($20 Pro/Pro+/Ultra) replaced by Free/Plus $8/Premium $30/Super $100/Ultra $200 plans bundling Grok and X Premium perks with a shared usage pool; Hobby renamed Free; Teams entry price $40 → $30+/user/mo. In detail: Teams entry price cut from $40/user/mo to $30+/user/month, with seat tiers spanning 4-50x Plus usage; Plans now bundle Grok and X features (Grok Chat, Voice, Imagine, Build, API access, Premium checkmark, analytics) with shared usage pool and tiered usage multiples; New Premium plan at $30/month (Most popular): 4x Plus usage, latest Grok models, cloud agents & automations, Bugbot code reviews, third-party models at API list price; Free tier renamed from Hobby to Free; now includes limited agent and Grok Chat requests plus basic X posting; New Super plan at $100/month: 15x Plus usage and priority model access at peak times; New Plus plan at $8/month: shared Cursor + Grok usage pool, Grok Chat/Voice/Imagine/Build, Grok API access and X Premium perks (Grok models only in Cursor); Individual plan ($20/mo, with Pro/Pro+/Ultra variants) removed; replaced by standalone Plus, Premium, Super and Ultra plans. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease, feature added, new plan, plan renamed, plan removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Plus at $8, Premium at $30, Teams at $30, Super at $100 and Ultra at $200.
It lands the same day as Cursor's previous change, "Cursor overhauls plans: Free, Plus $8, Premium $30, Ultra $200 tiers" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cursor adds contextual taglines to plan cards, from tinkerers to enterprises" (Sep 8, 2026), "Cursor: Composer dropped from 'Generous limits' benefit (Pro/Pro+/Ultra)" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Cursor adds plan taglines, swaps Hobby's third perk to Composer access" (Jul 27, 2026). Cursor has been tracked since 2026-05-19.
