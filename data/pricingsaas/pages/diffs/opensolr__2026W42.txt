What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of OpenSolr's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise plan's public starting price of EUR 550/month replaced with 'Custom priced for your clusters'; AI add-on reframed: AI now included in every package with 500 AI requests/month at no extra cost; add-on renamed 'Add more AI requests' for larger allowances. Pulse files it as a pricing change (add-on change, price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Small at EUR 17, Medium at EUR 35, Large at EUR 53, AI & Hybrid Search at EUR 5 and Additional Solr Index at EUR 10 Solr Index.
It lands 14 days after OpenSolr's previous change, "OpenSolr: Medium & Large support downgraded (Helpdesk removed)" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenSolr: new fair-use rate limit policy (150 req/min)" (Aug 10, 2026) and "OpenSolr: Yearly discount cut 25%→15%, base plan prices up" (Aug 4, 2026). OpenSolr has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Pinecone, Elastic, Algolia, Weaviate and Chromatic also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
