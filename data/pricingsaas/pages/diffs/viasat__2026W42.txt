What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Viasat's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: New Unleashed Max plan added at $109.99/mo for the first 12 months, then $179.99/mo; includes $70 off for 12 months and FREE EasyCare ($9.99/mo value); Intro promo on Essentials/Unleashed extended from $30/mo off for the first 3 months to $30/mo off for the first 12 months; 'GET $90 OFF' banner replaced by 'Starting at just $39.99/month' hero. Regular prices unchanged ($69.99/$99.99); Competitor. In detail: Competitor comparison table (Viasat vs Starlink vs Hughesnet) removed and replaced by benefit-led 'Why customers get connected' copy. Pulse files it as a pricing change (new plan, positioning, discount change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $69.99, Unleashed at $99.99 and Unleashed Max at $179.99.
It lands 7 days after Viasat's previous change, "Viasat ends $300 Mastercard reward offer for Unleashed plan switchers" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Viasat doubles minimum contract term to 24 months" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Viasat: New CyberProtect add-on + $300 Mastercard promo extended" (Jul 20, 2026). Viasat has been tracked since 2026-06-23.
In the same window Holded, Threecolts, Shippo, Tatsoft and Smokeball also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
