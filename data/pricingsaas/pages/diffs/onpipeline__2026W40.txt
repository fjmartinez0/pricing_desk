What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Onpipeline's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free trial length shortened from 30 days to 14 days, reflected in the hero subhead, FAQ answer, and signup panel copy; New 'AI' capability row added to the plan comparison table with quantified free AI request allowances: 20/month (Pipeline), 45/month (Standard), 60/month (Advanced). Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pipeline at $20, Standard at $20 and Advanced at $20.
It lands 42 days after Onpipeline's previous change, "Onpipeline adds Customer Portal and Priority Support rows to Advanced" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OnPipeline: Quote Management row relabeled to include eSignature" (Jul 4, 2026). Onpipeline has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
