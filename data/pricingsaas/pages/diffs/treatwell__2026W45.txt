What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Treatwell's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Treatwell introduced monthly subscription fees for the first time: Connect Freelancer £9.99/mo, Connect Advanced £24.99/mo, and a new Expert tier at £59/mo (14-day free trial), alongside its existing 35% new-client commission. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Freelancer at GBP 9.99, Advanced at GBP 24.99 and Expert at GBP 59.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Treatwell. Treatwell has been tracked since 2026-06-24.
