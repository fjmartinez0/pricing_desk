What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Activtrak's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Work Activity Tracking headline price changed from $10 to 'Starts as low as $7' per worker/month billed annually, which requires a 3-year subscription. In detail: Work Activity Tracking card now leads with a 3-year multi-year rate: "Starts as low as $7" per worker/month billed annually with a 3-year subscription (previously the card showed $10). The standard annual list price is unchanged at $10 in the comparison table. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Work Activity Tracking at $10, Workforce Management at $15, Productivity Optimization at $17, Workflow at $21 and Data History at $1.
It lands 77 days after Activtrak's previous change, "Activtrak adds AI Insights add-on cross-sell to Workforce Management plan" (Aug 31, 2026). Activtrak has been tracked since 2023-12-01.
In the same window Time Doctor, Harvest and Everhour also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
