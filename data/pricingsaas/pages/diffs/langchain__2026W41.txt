What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of LangChain's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: LangChain Compute Units ($1.50/LCU) retired; all usage now metered in Standard Units (LSU, $1.00). Metering rates rescaled; Fleet allowances 5/25 LCU became 7/37 LSU; Sandbox 8 LSU. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Plus at $39.
It lands 3 days after LangChain's previous change, "LangChain merges LangSmith LCU and LSU into one $1.00 Standard Unit" (Oct 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LangChain: new Tuned Evaluators usage pricing (0.01 LCU/eval run)" (Aug 24, 2026), "LangChain: VC-backed startup discount now discloses $10K credit amount" (Aug 3, 2026) and "LangChain: Startup discount program now discloses $10,000 credit amount" (Jul 28, 2026). LangChain has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
