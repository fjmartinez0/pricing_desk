What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Educative's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Annual-billed prices fell across all plans: Standard $13→$12/mo ($149→$139/yr), Premium $17→$16/mo ($199→$189/yr); Premium Plus stayed $21/mo but annual charge dropped $249→$239. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Standard at $12, Premium at $16 and Premium Plus at $21.
It lands 7 days after Educative's previous change, "Educative hikes Standard, Premium prices; course library grows to 2200+" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Educative: "Fenzo" AI tutorial feature renamed to "AI Tutor"" (Jul 27, 2026). Educative has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Udemy, Coursera and Codecademy also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
