What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Slack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise+ 'Unlimited Slackbot' replaced with 'Slackbot with no weekly message limits,' now subject to usage guidelines and advanced-feature limits; comparison-table Usage limits cell changed from 'Unlimited' to blank. In detail: Enterprise+: 'Unlimited Slackbot' claim replaced with 'Slackbot with no weekly message limits,' now subject to usage guidelines and advanced-feature limits; comparison-table Usage limits cell changed from 'Unlimited' to blank. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered). The current page lists Pro at $7.25, Business+ at $15 and Enterprise+ at $45.
It lands 7 days after Slack's previous change, "Slack opens Slackbot to Free and Pro, caps Business+ at 15 messages a week" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Slack swaps 24/7 support row for tiered Salesforce Success Plans" (Nov 1, 2026), "Slack launches Enterprise+ add-on for frontline field workers" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Slack lists Enterprise+ at $45 per user/month, ending contact-sales pricing" (Oct 4, 2026). Slack has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
