What happened
Between the Sep 29, 2026 and Sep 30, 2026 captures of n8n's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: n8n renamed 'AI Assistant' to 'Assistant' and cut the monthly credit allowance: Starter dropped from 2,300 to 1,600 credits, and Pro's top tier dropped from 13,700 to 9,600 credits. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Starter at $20, Pro at $50 and Business at $800.
It lands 34 days after n8n's previous change, "n8n rolls out AI Assistant preview for Business/self-hosted, cuts Enterprise " (Aug 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "n8n: AI credits increased for Starter/Pro, removed from Enterprise" (Jul 18, 2026), "n8n: AI Assistant renamed to AI Workflow Builder, credit allowances cut" (Jul 16, 2026) and "n8n adjusted credit limits and renamed AI Workflow Builder to AI Assistant" (Jul 13, 2026). n8n has been tracked since 2026-05-07.
In the same window Zapier, Parabol, UiPath, Workato and Make also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
