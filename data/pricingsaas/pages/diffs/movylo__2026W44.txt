What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Movylo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Boost (ad campaigns run from Movylo) expanded from Meta sponsored posts only to Google Ads or Meta Ads campaigns; no Boost price shown, plan still $99/month. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 28, 2026) lists One Simple Plan at $149.
It lands 105 days after Movylo's previous change, "Movylo triples free trial from 14 to 30 days across CTAs" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Movylo: 3-tier pricing replaced with single $99/mo Alex AI plan" (Jul 6, 2026). Movylo has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Bolt and GiveMe5.ai also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
