What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Ignitionapp's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Removed sitewide promotional banner offering 25% off for 6 months (tax season promo, offer had ended Oct 31); Added 'Schedule send proposals' and 'Shared table views' (share table views with team using preferred columns/filters/sorting) to feature comparison table. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, discount removed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Solo at $39, Core at $99, Pro at $229, Pro+ at $399 and Online Forms at $49.
It lands 21 days after Ignitionapp's previous change, "Ignitionapp launches 25% off for 6 months promo through Oct 31" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Ignitionapp caps Pro+ at 20 users, promotes Enterprise and demotes Solo plan" (Sep 14, 2026). Ignitionapp has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Ifttt, Celigo, Zapier, Workato and Make also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
