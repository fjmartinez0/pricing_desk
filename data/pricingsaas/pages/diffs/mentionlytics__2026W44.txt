What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Mentionlytics's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New credits-based AI Visibility add-on with public prompt tiers: Essential 25 prompts $49/mo, Advanced 50 $99, Pro 100 $169, Business 250 $379, Enterprise 400+ $559; plan cards now show 'from $49/mo'. In detail: AI Visibility add-on (previously listed as "Add-on" with no price) now has public prompt-tier pricing: Essential 25 prompts $49/mo, Advanced 50 $99, Pro 100 $169, Business 250 $379, Enterprise 400+ $559; plan cards now show "Add-on from $49/mo". Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $49, Essential at $141, Advanced at $249, Pro at $416 and Business at $624.
