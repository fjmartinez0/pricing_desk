GPTZero reframes Team plan as card-only self-serve, sales for POs only
Verified Oct 19, 2026·AI & Machine Learning·Direction: neutral
The changeEnterprise, Professional positioning — positioning.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 12, 2026 → Oct 19, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of GPTZero's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Team plan section repositioned as self-serve: 'Enterprise and Team Plans' heading and 'Create a team / Contact Sales' buttons replaced by a 'Professional Team Plan' card stating card payment only (sales now only for purchase orders, invoicing and larger teams), min 2 seats, and 500,000 words per member per month. Seat price unchanged at $24.99/mo billed annually. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Premium at $12.99 and Professional at $24.99.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for GPTZero. GPTZero has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window OpenAI, Huggingface and Anthropic also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
