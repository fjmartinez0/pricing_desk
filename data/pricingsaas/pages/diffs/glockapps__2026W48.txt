What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of GlockApps' pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Special Offer' tab added to the pricing product switcher (Bundle, DMARC Analytics, Special Offer, Spam Test Credit Packs); offer details not visible on page. In detail: New 'Special Offer 🔥' tab added to the pricing product switcher (Bundle, DMARC Analytics, Special Offer, Spam Test Credit Packs); the linked tab is a countdown-timed promo with two annual plans: MUST-HAVE $149/year (weekly testing, 100 spam test credits) and INBOX PRO $299/year (daily testing, 250 credits). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists DMARC Analytics Essential at $55, Essential at $59, DMARC Analytics Growth at $95, Growth at $99 and Enterprise at $129.
It lands 91 days after GlockApps' previous change, "GlockApps: Sending Accounts limit removed from pricing table" (Aug 23, 2026).
