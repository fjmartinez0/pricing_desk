What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Adapt's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Spend limit on Adapt Cloud changed from per-team to a monthly per-member cap; dropped the 'Every team on a budget' example dashboard (per-team spend breakdown). In detail: Adapt Cloud plan's spend-limit control changed from a per-team cap to a per-member monthly cap: 'Spend limits per team' became 'Monthly spend limit per member'; Removed the Adapt Cloud 'Every team on a budget' spend-tracking dashboard example showing monthly per-team/agent limits, early alerts, routing savings, and a sample per-team budget breakdown (Engineering, Support, Sales, Operations, Finance). Pulse files it as a product change (feature change, feature removed). The current page lists Adapt Cloud at $3.51 per 100 vCPU-hours (sandbox, running) + $1.12 per 100 GiB-hours (sandbox memory, running).
It lands 7 days after Adapt's previous change, "Adapt drops separate Harness rate card, folds Self-hosted Free into agent rates" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Adapt drops $8/user fee, swaps Managed plan for usage-only Adapt Cloud" (Nov 1, 2026), "Adapt slashes Managed plan to $8 per user, down 43% from $14" (Oct 18, 2026) and "Adapt renames open source plan Developer, splits Business and Enterprise" (Oct 4, 2026). Adapt has been tracked since 2026-07-14.
In the same window Microsoft, HubSpot and Salesforce also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
