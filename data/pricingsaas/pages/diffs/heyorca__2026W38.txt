What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of HeyOrca's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Top-tier plan renamed from Enterprise to Agency Added 'Import past posts' (imports recently published posts from Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn Company) to Free, Basic, and Pro plans. In detail: Top-tier custom plan renamed from 'Enterprise' to 'Agency'; Added 'Import past posts' feature (imports recently published posts from Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn Company Pages) to Free, Basic, and Pro plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $50, Pro at $126, Social Inbox Add-on at $33, Social Reports Add-on at $50 and Social Listening Add-on at $33.
It lands 7 days after HeyOrca's previous change, "HeyOrca: added Post performance metrics feature (Free limited, Basic/Pro full)" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HeyOrca splits Approvals in two, restricting Custom approvals to Pro" (Sep 1, 2026), "HeyOrca drops "coming soon": Facebook & LinkedIn first comment now live" (Aug 24, 2026) and "HeyOrca adds Trending audio for IG & TikTok across all plans" (Jul 27, 2026).
