What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of AnyDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added SSO with Microsoft Entra ID and Okta (Enterprise only) and Custom Login Page/Logos/Color branding rows (Business and Enterprise). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Business at $10.
It lands 34 days after AnyDB's previous change, "AnyDB: Free plan AI credits boosted to 1,000/mo + new MCP server access" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "AnyDB: Business/Enterprise now capped at 50 workflows" (Aug 17, 2026), "AnyDB: Solution Packs price hidden ($100/mo) + workflow cap removed" (Aug 10, 2026) and "AnyDB cuts Business plan default to $10/user/mo, adds 100-user cap" (Jul 27, 2026). AnyDB has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window MongoDB, Elastic, Redis, MariaDB and Couchbase also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
