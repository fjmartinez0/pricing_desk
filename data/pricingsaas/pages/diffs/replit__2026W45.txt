What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Replit's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan mode dropped from Core; 10 parallel agents and Invite up to 50 viewers dropped from Pro. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Replit Core at $18 and Replit Pro at $90.
It lands 14 days after Replit's previous change, "Replit rewrites Enterprise card: adds SCIM, audit logs, private networking" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Replit adds SCIM, audit logs to Enterprise, drops static outbound IPs" (Oct 16, 2026), "Replit: Removed the free "Starter" plan from the pricing page; Core ($18-20/mo) is now the entry-level tier" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Replit axes free Starter plan, ties Core and Pro bullets to Free Mode" (Sep 11, 2026). Replit has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window GitHub, Google Cloud, CodeSandbox and JetBrains also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
