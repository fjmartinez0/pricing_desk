What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of CoderPad's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Top-tier plan renamed Custom to Enterprise with 'volume-based, no credits' positioning; annual billing now labeled -20%; Team's 3-months-free perk and the Most Popular badge were both dropped; Team plan's custom question allowance increased from 20 to 100 custom questions; the 1,400+ ready-to-use question library stayed the same size. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Starter at $80, Team at $400 and Frontier Lite at $1,000.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for CoderPad. CoderPad has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
In the same window Replit and HackerRank also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
