What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Enso Connect's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Quarterly billing option (10% off) removed from the billing toggle; only Monthly and Yearly (save 20%) remain. Plan prices unchanged: Core $16, Pro $24, Max $28 per unit/month billed yearly; Comparison table cuts Pro channel limits: Brands 5→2, Website Chat Widgets 5→2, Emails 5→2, Phone lines 5→2, SMS lines 5→2, and PMS connections 10→5. Core loses its Website Chat Widget (1→not included) and ph. In detail: EnsoAI Agent building workflows and answering questions are now included on Core (before, 'Runs custom AI workflows' and 'Answers questions about your data' were Pro/Max only). AI task generation, previously Max-only ('AI Generated Tasks'), is now on Pro ('Generates and completes tasks: More usage'; Pro card 'Tasks, generated by AI'). The AI section was reorganised into EnsoAI Autopilot (limited/more/5x usage) and EnsoAI Agent (every plan, no usage limit); Comparison table cuts Pro channel limits: Brands 5→2, Website Chat Widgets 5→2, Emails 5→2, Phone lines 5→2, SMS lines 5→2, and PMS connections 10→5. Core loses its Website Chat Widget (1→not included) and phone line (1→not included); Max stays Unlimited. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, discount removed, limit lowered) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $16, Pro at $24 and Max at $28.
It lands 14 days after Enso Connect's previous change, "Enso Connect ditches custom quotes, publishes per-unit pricing from $16/month" (Nov 1, 2026). Enso Connect has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
In the same window Microsoft, Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
