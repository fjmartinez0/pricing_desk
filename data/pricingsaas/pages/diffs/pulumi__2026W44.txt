What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Pulumi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Silver support plan price hidden ($35,000/year → Contact sales) and Gold support plan price hidden ($50,000/year → Contact sales) in the Compare support plans table. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $40, Pro at $400, Standard Support at $625, Bronze Support at $1,250 and Silver Support at $2,916.67.
It lands 28 days after Pulumi's previous change, "Pulumi: Enterprise plan pricing revealed: 'Custom / Contact sales' replaced with self-serve $2,000/month base (2,000…" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pulumi renames all plan tiers, adds Support tiers up to $50K/yr Platinum" (Sep 21, 2026). Pulumi has been tracked since 2026-05-02.
