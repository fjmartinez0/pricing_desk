What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Mailmeteor's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday Week 50% off all plans: Starter $5.99→$3.00, Premium $17.99→$8.99, Pro $35.99→$18.00 per user/month; list prices unchanged;
It lands 42 days after Mailmeteor's previous change, "Mailmeteor adds new Pipelines feature to Professional plan to manage replies" (Oct 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mailmeteor adds SMTP/IMAP support across all four pricing plans" (Oct 4, 2026). Mailmeteor has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Getresponse, Convertkit, ActiveCampaign, HubSpot and Mailchimp also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
