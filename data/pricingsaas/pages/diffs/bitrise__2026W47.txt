What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Bitrise's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Hobby (free) plan bullets rewritten: team of one, small-project build credits and community Slack support replaced by invite whole team, Pay-As-You-Go scaling, and full CI/CD toolkit (Pipelines, unlimited steps. In detail: Free Hobby plan reworked: team of one → invite your whole team (unlimited members), 300 credits/mo → 500 build minutes/mo, new Pay-As-You-Go overage (1.5x per-minute, $15/mo cap), Pipelines + unlimited workflow steps; credit card verification now required (was no card needed). Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change). The current page lists Starter at $89, Pro at $200, AI Credits at $50, Bitrise Insights at $12.50 and Bitrise Build Cache at $30.
It lands 56 days after Bitrise's previous change, "Bitrise supercharges Starter plan: more builds, unlimited seats, free AI credits" (Sep 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Bitrise touts $30/mo Build Cache entry, adds free trials on Starter and Pro" (Sep 13, 2026). Bitrise has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Aws, GitHub, GitLab and CircleCI also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
