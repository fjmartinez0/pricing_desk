What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Qwen4 Max added to serverless model list with promotional pricing ($1.05 in / $3.15 out, down from $1.50/$4.50) valid until 11/23/2026; Model catalog changes: Tencent Hy4 and Elorian Glimpse 1.0 added; Rnj-1 Instruct and Qwen2.5 7B Instruct Turbo removed from serverless pricing list. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100), GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days), Sandbox (Code Sandbox) at $0.04 per vCPU/hour + $0.01 per GiB RAM/hour and Serverless Inference at $0.15 per 1M input tokens.
This weekly roll-up includes 3 changes Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Together AI: Qwen4 Max price hike to $4.50 output + 30% promo, adds Tencent Hy4" (Day 324, 2026, saved Nov 20, 2026), "Together AI adds Qwen4 Max model at $1.43/$4.28 per 1M tokens" (Day 323, 2026, saved Nov 19, 2026) and "Together AI adds Elorian Glimpse 1.0 ($0.60/$3.00), drops Qwen2.5 7B Turbo" (Day 322, 2026, saved Nov 18, 2026). They are the same changes, not new ones. Before that, Together AI's previous recorded change was "Together AI: HGX B200 GPU price hikes to $9.29/hr + 3 more rates shown" (Nov 13, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
In the same window Mistral AI, Huggingface, Anthropic, OpenAI and Lambda Labs also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
