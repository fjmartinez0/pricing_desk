What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of PeopleHR's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: HR Software per-employee monthly prices raised and 'From' qualifier dropped: Starter £3→£4, Core £5.50→£6.25, Pro £8→£9.25, Elite+ £9.50→£11. HR & Payroll bundles also raised (markdown-only, not in screenshot): Core+ £7→£7.75, Pro+ £9.50→£10.75, Ultimate+ £11→£12.50; Payroll SaaS/Managed Core unchanged at From £3/£5. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Payroll SaaS Core at GBP 3, Starter at GBP 4, Payroll Managed Core at GBP 5, Core at GBP 6.25 and Core+ at GBP 7.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for PeopleHR. PeopleHR has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
