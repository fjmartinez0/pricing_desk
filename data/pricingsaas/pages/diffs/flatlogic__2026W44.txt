Flatlogic drops Stable deploys from Pro plan, bills VMs in 24-hour blocks
Verified Oct 26, 2026·Software Development & Testing·Direction: down
The changePricing model — metric change.
2 changes·2 captures·Oct 19, 2026 → Oct 26, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Flatlogic's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Manual Deploy to Stable and Sync to Stable disabled platform-wide; Pro plan's listed "Dev & Stable environments" benefit now reads "Development environment" only. Existing Stable environments remain available for now; Hosted VM billing clause clarified: VMs are now charged in indivisible 24-hour periods from start/resume, and pausing a VM does not refund the current period. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change, feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro 25 Credits at $18, Pro 60 Credits at $45 and Pro 125 Credits at $85.
It lands 55 days after Flatlogic's previous change, "Flatlogic aligns Hermes Agent, AppWizzy Codex licensing to Free/open-source t" (Sep 1, 2026). Flatlogic has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Microsoft, Retool, OutSystems and Mendix also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
