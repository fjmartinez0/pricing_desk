What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Zenegy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Zenegy Expense moved from flat DKK 100/md (10 users incl., per-document fee) to seat-tiered pricing from DKK 249/md (1-5 users) up to DKK 2,499/md (201-500 users); per-document fee now included. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Micro at DKK 49, Startup at DKK 299, Growth at DKK 499 and Established at DKK 999.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Zenegy. Zenegy has been tracked since 2026-10-15.
