What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of CommentSold's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: AI Bestie and CommentSold MCP added as plan features; Open API and Enhanced Customer Analytics added to Large Business; TikTok/Facebook/Instagram Live Selling rows merged into one 'Social Media Selling' row; New 'AI Credits' usage metric added to the plan comparison, granting 10,000/20,000/40,000 monthly AI credits to Starter with AI, Small Business, and Large Business respectively. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $149, Starter with AI at $249, Small Business at $499 and Large Business at $999.
It lands 48 days after CommentSold's previous change, "CommentSold: sales commission fee raised 1pt across all plans" (Aug 4, 2026). CommentSold has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
