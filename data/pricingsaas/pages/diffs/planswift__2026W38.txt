What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of PlanSwift's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Single $2,000 flat Professional license replaced by two-tier per-seat plans — Essential $2,000/seat ($1,500/seat for 2+) and Core $3,000/seat ($2,500/seat for 2+), which bundles the new Takeoff BOOST AI suite.
