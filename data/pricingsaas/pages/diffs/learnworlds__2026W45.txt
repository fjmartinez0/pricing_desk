What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Learnworlds's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter plan price rose from $24 to $44/month billed annually (monthly billing $29 to $49); annual discount badge now reads 'Save up to 20%'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $24, Pro Trainer at $79 and Learning Center at $249.
It lands 35 days after Learnworlds's previous change, "Learnworlds slashes prices 15% with countdown promo through Sep 30" (Sep 28, 2026). Learnworlds has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Thinkific, Podia, Udemy and Kajabi also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
