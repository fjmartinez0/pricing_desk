Neon adds Realtime (Beta) to Free, Launch and Scale plans, free during beta
Verified Oct 19, 2026·Cloud & DevOps·Direction: up
The changeLaunch, Scale feature — feature added.

What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Neon's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Realtime (Beta) added to Free, Launch and Scale plan features and the usage table; free during beta, with compute and egress billed as usual. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Launch at $0.11 per CU-hour + $0.2 per GB-month (history/instant restore storage) and Scale at $0.2 per GB-month (history/instant restore storage) + $0.35 per GB-month (database storage).
It lands 14 days after Neon's previous change, "Neon doubles Free plan storage to 1 GB per project, up from 0.5 GB" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Neon: spending limits replaced with alert-only notifications" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Neon: AI Gateway drops post-beta "no markup" pricing guarantee" (Jul 27, 2026). Neon has been tracked since 2026-04-26.
In the same window Vercel, Digitalocean, Google Cloud and Netlify also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
