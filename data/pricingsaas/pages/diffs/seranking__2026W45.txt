What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of SE Ranking's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: AI Search add-on ($71.20-$89/mo, 200+ prompts) removed; AI visibility now bundled as SE Visible (25 prompts daily in Core/Growth) and sold standalone from $71/mo; GEO limits cut: Core prompts 100 to 25 daily, Growth 250 to 25 daily; GEO research domains (5/15) dropped; now framed as SE Visible AI Visibility tracking; Core adds Local & content marketing; Growth adds Personalized onboarding. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, package change, limit decrease) with an upward direction. The current page lists Core at $103.20, Growth at $223.20, API at $45, Agency Pack at $69 and AI Visibility at $71.20.
It lands 7 days after SE Ranking's previous change, "SE Ranking drops Perplexity from AI Search add-on's tracker" (Oct 26, 2026). SE Ranking has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window Ahrefs, Moz and Semrush also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
