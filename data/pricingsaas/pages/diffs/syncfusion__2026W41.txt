What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Syncfusion's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Syncfusion removed the 5-developer minimum license requirement; per-developer prices unchanged ($959/$1,199/$599) and 5-developer totals dropped from cards. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists PDF Viewer SDK at $49.92, Grid SDK at $79.92, Chart SDK at $79.92, Scheduler SDK at $79.92 and Gantt SDK at $79.92.
It lands the same day as Syncfusion's previous change, "Syncfusion Sales ditches 5-dev minimum, shows flat per-developer pricing" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Syncfusion bundles all 7 UI SDKs into $1,199 suite, adds Business Support" (Sep 14, 2026), "Syncfusion splits UI Suite into 7 standalone SDKs — Grid, Chart, Diagram now " (Jul 13, 2026) and "Syncfusion Sales splits pricing, adds 7 standalone contact-sales UI SDKs" (Jul 13, 2026). Syncfusion has been tracked since 2026-05-06.
