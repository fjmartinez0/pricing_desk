What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Bitrise's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added 'Manage OIDC policies' row (short-lived OIDC tokens with trust policies) to the Security & Governance comparison table, included on all plans. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Starter at $89, Pro at $200, AI Credits at $50, Bitrise Insights at $12.50 and Bitrise Build Cache at $30.
It lands 7 days after Bitrise's previous change, "Bitrise free Hobby plan now invites whole teams, adds Pay-As-You-Go scaling" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Bitrise supercharges Starter plan: more builds, unlimited seats, free AI credits" (Sep 20, 2026) and "Bitrise touts $30/mo Build Cache entry, adds free trials on Starter and Pro" (Sep 13, 2026). Bitrise has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window GitLab, Aws, GitHub and CircleCI also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
