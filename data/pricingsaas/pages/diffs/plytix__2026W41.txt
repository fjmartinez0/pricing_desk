What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Plytix's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Pricing rebuilt around a 10-step SKU selector (100 to 25K, 25K+ custom): Pro now $149-$499/mo by SKU tier (was $499 flat incl. 50,000 SKUs), Enterprise $699-$1,299/mo (was Custom), add-ons tiered $0-$399/mo (Feeds/Portals were $300, Data Sheets $200, Shopify/BigCommerce free); onboarding packages removed; Standard API calls/hour cut 5,000→2,000 and Standard CDN traffic 50GB→25GB/mo; file storage n. In detail: Free Standard plan now covers only 100 SKUs (was 500 SKUs); Standard costs $49-$349/mo above 100 SKUs; Added AI Unlimited module, Wayfair connector, sandbox/staging environment and formula attributes; Standard API calls/hour cut 5,000→2,000 and Standard CDN traffic 50GB→25GB/mo; file storage now Unlimited on all plans (was 50GB/750GB/Custom); Enterprise API now 25,000/hr and CDN 2TB (was Custom). Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change, feature added, package change, limit decrease) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Pro at $149 and Enterprise at $699.
It lands 95 days after Plytix's previous change, "Plytix swaps Enterprise's Unlimited SKUs for Custom-negotiated allocation" (Jul 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Plytix: Enterprise plan SKU limit changed from Unlimited to Custom" (Jul 4, 2026), "Plytix Cuts Standard Plan SKU Limit in Half to 500" (Mar 17, 2026) and "Plytix Cuts Standard Plan SKU Limit in Half to 500" (Mar 11, 2026). Plytix has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
