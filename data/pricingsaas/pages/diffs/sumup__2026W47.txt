What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of SumUp's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Payments Plus drops the Monthly/Yearly toggle and £189/year (Save 17%) option; now £19/month with a 12-month commitment (previously no long-term commitment, cancel anytime). Fee stays 0.99%; New Payments Max plan: £49/month, 0.69% in-person transaction fee, for businesses taking £10,000+ a month; 24-month commitment, 24/7 priority support, free card reader replacements. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change, new plan).
It lands 7 days after SumUp's previous change, "SumUp cuts hardware prices up to 45% in new Black Friday sale" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SumUp drops £100 hardware cashback banner, adds bookings to POS Plus" (Oct 26, 2026), "SumUp drops Payments Plus 7-day free trial, pushes £3,000+ fee cuts" (Oct 11, 2026) and "SumUp switches to yearly-first pricing: £189/year, save 17%" (Sep 6, 2026). SumUp has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window PayPal, Stripe, Lightspeed and Squarespace also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
