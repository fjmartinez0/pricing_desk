What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of IONOS's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro plan's bundled SEO tool swapped: 'Advanced SEO with rankingCoach Standard' removed and replaced with IONOS's new 'AI Search Manager' tool.
