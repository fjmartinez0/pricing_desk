What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of SoSafe's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: SoSafe consolidated its 4-tier pricing (Essential, Professional, Premium, Ultimate) into 2 packages (Core, Advanced), reorganizing all feature comparison rows around the new structure. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at EUR 625.
It lands 14 days after SoSafe's previous change, "SoSafe: pricing starting price disclosed (€625/mo, 50 seats)" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SoSafe pulls pricing off site, all tiers now say "Request pricing" (Sep 21, 2026). SoSafe has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
