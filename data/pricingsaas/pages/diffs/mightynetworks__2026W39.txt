What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Mighty Networks's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Migration Services moved out of the Scale plan's feature list and into Growth; Multi-Feature Spaces dropped from the Scale plan entirely; hands-on migration help threshold raised from Scale Plan to Growth Plan. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Explore at $29.17, Launch at $79, Scale at $179 and Growth at $354.
It lands 14 days after Mighty Networks's previous change, "Mighty Networks adds $354/mo Growth plan, bundles add-ons into Mighty Pro" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mighty Networks adds MCP support to Scale plan, drops Mighty Pro Slack live-ch" (Jul 20, 2026). Mighty Networks has been tracked since 2026-05-04.
In the same window Kajabi, Podia and Thinkific also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
