What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Tembo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free trial allowances expanded: 1 user, 1 repo, 1 project, open-source models only became usage allowance, up to 10 users, priority support, unlimited repos and projects; Enterprise plan now lists 'Custom seat count (unlimited)'. In detail: Free plan renamed Free trial and expanded: the 14-day duration was dropped from the card and hero, and the 1 user / 1 repository / 1 project / open-source-models-only limits became up to 10 users, unlimited repositories and projects, priority support, and use of your Claude, Codex, or SuperGrok subscription. The compare table now shows Max-level limits (Micro→XL sandbox, Basic→Advanced audit logs). Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, trial change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $60 and Max at $200.
It lands 7 days after Tembo's previous change, "Tembo lets Pro and Max users bring Claude or SuperGrok subscriptions" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Tembo adds unlimited Projects to Pro/Max plans" (Sep 21, 2026), "Tembo slashes Free plan seats from 3 users to just 1" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Tembo: Credits billing replaced with dollar usage allowance" (Aug 5, 2026). Tembo has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Render, Neon, Supabase, Railway and PlanetScale also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
