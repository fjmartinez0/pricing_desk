What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Vercel's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Speed Insights split into a free base tier (10K events/team/rolling 30 days, all plans) plus a labeled 'Speed Insights Plus Events Add-on' keeping the prior $10/project/month + $0.65/10K events pricing; Added a new 'Tracing Spans (Beta)' observability capability with 1M span units/month included on Hobby, $0.50 per 1M span units on Pro, and Custom pricing on Enterprise. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 20, 2026) lists Pro at $20.
It lands 3 days after Vercel's previous change, "Vercel adds Tracing Spans metric, charges $0.50 per 1M span units on Pro" (Sep 25, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vercel splits Speed Insights: free 10K-event tier, paid Plus Events add-on" (Sep 24, 2026), "Vercel adds Backends under Remote Cache, new Marketplace Integrations row in " (Sep 21, 2026) and "Vercel bundles Edge, Fast Data, Blob & Sandbox overages into flat CDN plan" (Sep 14, 2026).
