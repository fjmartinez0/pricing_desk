What happened
Between the Oct 14, 2026 and Oct 15, 2026 captures of Shopify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Yearly-billed prices rose: Grow $79 to $89/mo and Advanced $299 to $359/mo; monthly-billed prices and Basic unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 23, 2026) lists Basic at $29, Grow at $79, Advanced at $299, Plus at $2,300 and POS Pro at $89.
It lands 13 days after Shopify's previous change, "Shopify Plus drops 1% sales cashback, removing the $15K cap from its table" (Oct 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shopify: Plus payment processing rates now publicly disclosed" (Sep 21, 2026), "Shopify Plus unmasks exact payment-processing fees across 9 rate rows" (Sep 15, 2026) and "Shopify: MB WAY and Bizum payment methods added" (Aug 11, 2026). Shopify has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Salesforce and Squarespace also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
