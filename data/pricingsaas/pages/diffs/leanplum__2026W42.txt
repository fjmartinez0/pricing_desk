Leanplum swaps CleverAI Tokens add-on for Decisioning Agents on Ultimate
Verified Oct 12, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: up
The changeUltimate add-on — new add-on.
2 changes·2 captures·Oct 5, 2026 → Oct 12, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Leanplum's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: CleverAI Tokens and Blocks add-on card replaced by Decisioning Agents add-on (1:1 decisioning via Tesseract engine), available on Ultimate only; no price shown; Ultimate CleverAI feature bullets reworded: journey automation, predictive segments, real-time recommendations replaced by 1:1 personalization, agentic orchestration, intent prediction. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, positioning) with an upward direction. The current page lists Core at $85.
It lands 49 days after Leanplum's previous change, "Leanplum renames plans to Core/Pro/Ultimate and hikes entry price to $85" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Leanplum slashes Essentials 90% to $7.50 in Mid Year Sale, 17-day clock ticks" (Jul 20, 2026). Leanplum has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
In the same window Salesforce, HubSpot and Klaviyo also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
