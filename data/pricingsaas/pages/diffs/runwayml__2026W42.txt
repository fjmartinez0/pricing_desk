What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Runway's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Runway added a limited-time Enterprise promotion: new customers get 2 months of usage free if they sign by 10/31, with linked terms and a Contact Sales CTA. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Standard at $12, Pro at $28 and Max at $76.
It lands 4 days after Runway's previous change, "Runway offers enterprises 2 months free usage for deals signed by 10/31" (Oct 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Runway Max plan adds Unlimited Wan 3.0 and Wan 3.0 Prime until October 18" (Oct 15, 2026), "Runway slashes Pro storage to 100GB, adds Agentic collaborator and Brand Kits" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Runway slashes Pro storage from 500GB to 100GB despite new Brand Kits, MCP" (Sep 5, 2026). Runway has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Synthesia and Loom also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
