What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Claris's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Promotional banner offering up to 25% off FileMaker Server Remote Backup and Standby Server removed after the offer's Sept 26, 2026 end date.
