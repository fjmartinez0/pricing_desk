What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Spacelift's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Lineup rebuilt: quote-only Starter + (unlimited users, 1 private worker) replaced by Starter at $399/mo (10 users, 2 public workers; drift detection moved up to Business); Free promoted from an 'Always free' badge to a full $0 plan card; Enterprise + removed, with self-hosted/FedRAMP moved to Enterprise 'Available upon request' (on-prem/airgapped and executive escalation dropped); compare-plans ta. In detail: 'Day 2 automation' (Spacelift Flows) added to every plan (Free, Starter, Business, Enterprise); Free card now lists credential-less cloud integration and OIDC SSO; Business 'Dedicated Customer Success Manager' narrowed to 'Named CSM from 3 workers'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, package change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $399.
It lands 7 days after Spacelift's previous change, "Spacelift hides $20,000 Starter+ price, now asks buyers to request a quote" (Oct 26, 2026). Spacelift has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
In the same window Hashicorp, Pulumi, Puppet and Google Cloud also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
