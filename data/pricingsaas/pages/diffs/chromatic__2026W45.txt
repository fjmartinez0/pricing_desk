What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Chromatic's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan snapshots and turbosnaps changed from Unlimited to Customizable. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $179 and Pro at $399.
It lands 105 days after Chromatic's previous change, "Chromatic relabels snapshots as "billed," trims Pro's overage pricing bullet" (Jul 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Chromatic swaps vague usage caps for unlimited snapshot & TurboSnap clarity" (Jul 13, 2026). Chromatic has been tracked since 2026-05-06.
