What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Holded's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday/Cyber Monday promo raises intro discount from 50% to 70% off the first 3 months; Basic shows 10,20 €, Standard 20,70 €, Advanced 34,50 €, Premium 68,70 € (list prices unchanged). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Plus at EUR 7.50, Basic at EUR 14.50, Standard at EUR 29.50, Advanced at EUR 49.50 and Premium at EUR 99.50.
It lands 21 days after Holded's previous change, "Holded adds daily API request limits to plans and custom SMTP domains" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Holded: swapped Payroll accounting for expense scanner + cash flow" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Holded: reconciliation rules limits added (15/50/100)" (Aug 17, 2026). Holded has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
