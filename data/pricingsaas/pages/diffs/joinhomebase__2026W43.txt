What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Homebase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Payroll add-on now offers a flat $29/month rate for teams of up to 5 employees, as an alternative to the standard $49/month base fee + $6/month per employee paid. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essentials at $24, Plus at $56, All-in-One at $96, Background Checks at $30 per background check and Payroll at $49.
It lands 28 days after Homebase's previous change, "Homebase offers 6 months free Payroll and Plus, raises Payroll fee to $49" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Homebase cuts job post boosts 37% to $50, drops Hiring Assistant annual billing" (Sep 14, 2026), "Homebase drops Hiring Assistant per-post pricing for $99/mo flat rate, ex" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Homebase: Payroll add-on price decrease ($49→$39/mo)" (Aug 17, 2026). Homebase has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Workleap, Wagepoint, Tracker, Time Doctor and SimInsights also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
