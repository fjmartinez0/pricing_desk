What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Added Claude Fable 5.2 ($8/$40 per 1M tokens), Muse Ember 1.0, and a new Decision/embedding models section (Text Embedding 3 small, Jev 1.13.0). Base Claude Haiku (unversioned) model dropped from catalog; Haiku 4.5/5.5 remain. In detail: Three new models added to the MindsHub inference catalog: Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic, $8/$40/$0.20/$10 per 1M input/output/cached-input/cache-write), Muse Ember 1.0 (Meta, $0.10/$0.50/$0.004/$0.10), and a new Decision and embedding models section (Text Embedding 3 small - OpenAI, $0.02 input; Jev 1.13.0 decision model - free); Base 'Claude Haiku' (unversioned) model removed from the MindsHub inference catalog ($0.10/$0.50/$0.01/$0.125 per 1M tokens). The Haiku 4.5 and Haiku 5.5 variants remain in the catalog unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added, feature removed). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 17, 2026) lists Pay as you go at $0.10 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.50 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "MindsDB lists Muse Ember 1.0 at $0.10 input / $0.50 output per 1M tokens" (Day 315, 2026, saved Nov 11, 2026). It is the same change, not a new one. Before that, MindsDB's previous recorded change was "MindsDB adds Claude Haiku 5.5 at $0.10 input, $0.50 output per 1M tokens" (Nov 7, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Huggingface and Alteryx also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
