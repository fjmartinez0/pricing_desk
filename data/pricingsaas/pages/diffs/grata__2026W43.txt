What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Grata's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Tier capability lists expanded: Growth adds basic CRM integration/sync and O365 email/calendar connectors; Scale adds Private Market Index, advanced CRM sync, custom connectors, ingestion (setup fee + credits); Alpha adds file-drive sync, Datasite VDR sync, Datasite MCP, ingestion setup included. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction.
It lands 63 days after Grata's previous change, "Grata renames Live Deal network to Active Mandates" (Aug 17, 2026). Grata has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho and HubSpot also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
