What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of TheRundown's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Monthly data point quotas increased 5x with no price change: Starter 5M to 25M, Pro 25M to 125M, Ultra 100M to 500M (overage rates unchanged at $12.25/$7.45/$4.99 per 1M). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $39, Pro at $119, Ultra at $319, Super at $519 and Mega at $799.
It lands 14 days after TheRundown's previous change, "TheRundown softens Starter data-source and market coverage promises" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "TheRundown caps free plan at 200K/mo, expands sub-second odds to all books" (Aug 31, 2026), "TheRundown cuts data-point overage rates ~99% and narrows sub-second odds to sharp books" (Aug 24, 2026) and "TheRundown: Data source coverage expanded 18 to 20+ (added Novig, ProphetX)" (Aug 17, 2026). TheRundown has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Prisync, Vizly, Precog, PostHog and Mixpanel also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
