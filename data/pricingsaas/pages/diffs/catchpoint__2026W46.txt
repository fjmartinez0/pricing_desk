What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Catchpoint's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Essentials $16→$19 and Advanced $27→$37 per Hybrid Unit; Signature $53→$50 and renamed from 'Signature + Edwin AI'; free trial shortened 15→14 days; Edwin AI now bundled with AI Credits per Hybrid Unit (20/75/150) on Essentials/Advanced/Signature; separate Edwin AI add-ons, LM Uptime and SaaS Monitoring add-ons dropped from table; Synthetics and Internet Sonar now include 100/250/500 points per Hy. In detail: Synthetics and Internet Sonar now include 100/250/500 points per Hybrid Unit by plan, replacing add-on-only availability. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists WebPageTest Pro at $15.
