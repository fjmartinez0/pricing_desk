What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of PageProof's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Team Plus price cut from $499/month to $399/month ($4,999/year to $3,999/year). Team annual also cut from $2,999/year to $2,499/year; Team monthly unchanged at $249; FinalFile® comparison-table section removed: FinalFile delivery to work/project management tools, OneDrive, Canto and Bynder rows dropped. Only 'FinalFile delivery to MediaValet' remains (moved under Features, Enterprise-only). Card. In detail: Two comparison-table features moved from included to extra-cost ($ icon): Advanced co-branding on Team Plus (Enterprise still included) and Software Development Kit (SDK) on Enterprise (Team Plus was already extra-cost); FinalFile® comparison-table section removed: FinalFile delivery to work/project management tools, OneDrive, Canto and Bynder rows dropped. Only 'FinalFile delivery to MediaValet' remains (moved under Features, Enterprise-only). Card bullets 'FinalFile® delivery to storage' (Team Plus) and 'to DAM' (Enterprise) also removed; PageProof Intelligence® comparison-table rows removed: PI Insights: Dashboard & Reporting, PI Insights: Proofs & Comments (Team Plus+), PreProof™ (Team Plus+), PI Agents (Enterprise, extra cost) and PageProof MCP. Only the 'Easy as PI' section row (ticked on all plans) remains. Per-plan PI credit allowances (250 / 500 / 1,000) dropped from the plan cards. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease, feature removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Team at $249 and Team Plus at $333.25.
It lands 7 days after PageProof's previous change, "PageProof adds MCP integration to Intelligence suite, renames Folders to Fold" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "PageProof touts scheduled proofs on Team Plus, digital signing on Enterprise" (Sep 7, 2026) and "PageProof Team Plus tier jumps 25% to $499/month, annual scales too" (Aug 17, 2026). PageProof has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Workato, Docusign and Adobe also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
