What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of SparkPost's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pricing page rebuilt around a Bird balance/wallet model. Real per-unit rates now shown for Email ($15/mo), SMS ($0.0035/segment) and phone numbers ($1/mo); new Dedicated IPs add-on launched at $24.95/IP/month; Push and RCS, previously listed as standard pay-as-you-go channels, are now labeled Private beta with limited access, requiring a custom quote instead of self-serve usage pricing. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Startup at $15, Growth at $80 and Dedicated IP at $24.95.
It lands 27 days after SparkPost's previous change, "SparkPost/Bird: Email plan pricing removed, replaced by usage hub" (Aug 31, 2026). SparkPost has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
In the same window HubSpot, Klaviyo, Twilio, Constant Contact and Brevo also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
