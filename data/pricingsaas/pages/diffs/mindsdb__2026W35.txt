What happened
Between the Aug 17, 2026 and Aug 24, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: MindsHub split Free/Pro into Cowork plus a new Unified Inference API product; both add a 5% platform fee on rate-card prices. Gemini 3.6 Flash rates were halved; Kimi K3 gained a cache-write fee. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pay as you go at $0.1 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.5 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands 7 days after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB: Pro plan billing moved from spend-controls to prepaid balance" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsHub: Air & Luna rate cut ~80%, web app dropped, support added" (Aug 10, 2026), "MindsDB: Pro tier drops $9.95 flat fee for pay-as-you-go pricing" (Aug 4, 2026) and "MindsDB slashes per-token prices ~30% across 17 models on MindsHub" (Jul 20, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Huggingface also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
“MindsHub” is the name MindsDB’s pricing page uses; Pulse files the change under MindsDB, the company it tracks.
