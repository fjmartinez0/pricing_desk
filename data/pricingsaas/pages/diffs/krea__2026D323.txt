What happened
Between the Nov 18, 2026 and Nov 19, 2026 captures of Krea's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Black Friday model-price discounts on Pro and Max cards: Seedance 2.5 -60%/-75%, Kling 4.0 -20%/-50%, Krea 3 -25%/-50%, GPT Image 2.6 -20%/-45%, Nano Banana Pro -45%/-65%. Before that Pulse recorded "Krea cuts Nano Banana 2.1 image estimates ~11% on every plan as cards switch to quoting 2.1" (Nov 7, 2026), "Krea cuts LoRA training from its Free plan as paid prices hold steady" (Nov 1, 2026) and "Krea ends 14-day unlimited Krea 3 Fast promo for annual Pro and Max buyers" (Oct 23, 2026).
