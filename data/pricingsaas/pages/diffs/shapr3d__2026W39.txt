What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Shapr3D's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The "Compare Subscriptions" detailed feature table was removed from the pricing page. It was the only place showing Enterprise-only callouts for single sign-on (SSO), consolidated billing, customized training, and dedicated account manager — none of these are shown anywhere else on the page. In detail: Detailed 'Compare Subscriptions' feature-comparison table removed from pricing page. It was the only place SSO/single sign-on, consolidated billing, customized training, and dedicated account manager callouts were shown; none of these appear elsewhere on the page after removal. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Solo at $20.83 and Studio at $37.5.
It lands 21 days after Shapr3D's previous change, "Shapr3D: Free/Solo plans gain explicit project caps (2 / 100)" (Aug 31, 2026). Shapr3D has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
