What happened
Between the Nov 23, 2026 and Nov 24, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added a new 'Fast' serving tier for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 to Serverless Inference, priced at $0.60/1M input, $2.40/1M output, $0.012/1M cached tokens. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100), GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days), Sandbox (Code Sandbox) at $0.04 per vCPU/hour + $0.01 per GiB RAM/hour and Serverless Inference at $0.15 per 1M input tokens.
It lands 4 days after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI: Qwen4 Max price hike to $4.50 output + 30% promo, adds Tencent Hy4" (Nov 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI adds Qwen4 Max model at $1.43/$4.28 per 1M tokens" (Nov 19, 2026), "Together AI adds Elorian Glimpse 1.0 ($0.60/$3.00), drops Qwen2.5 7B Turbo" (Nov 18, 2026) and "Together AI: HGX B200 GPU price hikes to $9.29/hr + 3 more rates shown" (Nov 13, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
In the same window Mistral AI, Huggingface, Anthropic, OpenAI and Lambda Labs also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
