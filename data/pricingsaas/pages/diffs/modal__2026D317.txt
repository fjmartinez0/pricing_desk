What happened
Between the Nov 12, 2026 and Nov 13, 2026 captures of Modal's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New model Inkling 2 Small added to shared endpoints pricing at $0.30 prompt, $0.03 cached, $1.00 completion per 1M tokens. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Team at $250, Compute resources at $1.10 NVIDIA A10G instance hour + $1.95 L40S GPU hour and Sandbox + Notebooks compute at $2.40 per 100 GiB-Hour + $14.19 per 100 core-hours.
It lands 18 days after Modal's previous change, "Modal drops Notebooks from its Sandbox pricing section and product footer" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Modal drops Notebooks from Sandbox pricing, bills at 3x non-preemptible rates" (Oct 26, 2026), "Modal adds $0.04/GiB network egress fee, with up to 100 TiB free per plan" (Oct 4, 2026) and "Modal adds included token usage row, renames free credits to free compute" (Sep 12, 2026). Modal has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
