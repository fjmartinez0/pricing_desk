What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Patriot Software's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Sitewide banner with 'Offer ends 8/31' urgency messaging and its own Sign Up CTA was removed; the 50% off promo duration was also shortened from 6 months to 3 months across the accounting and payroll sections. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Basic Payroll at $17, Accounting Basic at $20, Accounting Premium at $30, Full Service Payroll at $37 and HR Software at $6.
It lands 104 days after Patriot Software's previous change, "Patriot Software doubles free trial offer for America's 250th birthday" (Jun 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Patriot Software adds subaccounts benefit to Accounting Premium plan" (May 19, 2026). Patriot Software has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
