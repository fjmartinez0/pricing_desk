What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Kahoot!'s pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pro Start annual-billed price cut from $19/mo ($228/yr) to $9/mo ($108/yr) via new 53% coupon; $19 now shown struck through; Language learning added to the Training, courses and certificates bullet on a Kahoot! 360 plan card; also a new Language Learning comparison row. In detail: Kahoot! Language Learning added: Pro Plus card bullet now reads "Training, course, certificates, incl. language learning", and a new "New! Language learning" comparison row shows Limited (1 language, 1 custom topic) on Pro Start/Standard and Unlimited on Pro Plus/Max/Ultra. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Kahoot! 360 Pro Start at $9, Kahoot! 360 Spirit at $20, Kahoot! 360 Pro Standard at $25, Kahoot! 360 Spirit Premium at $25 and Kahoot! 360 Pro Plus at $39.
It lands 35 days after Kahoot!'s previous change, "Kahoot! revives Pro Max price to $79/mo, slashes Pro Plus to $39/mo intro" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kahoot! flattens Pro Ultra participant cap to 5,000, drops onboarding gate" (Sep 14, 2026), "Kahoot! Pro Plus loses intro discount, price jumps to flat $49/mo" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Kahoot!: Pro Plus plan price cut to $39/mo (from $49/mo)" (Aug 17, 2026). Kahoot! has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Duo and Mentimeter also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
