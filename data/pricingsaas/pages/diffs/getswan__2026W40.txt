What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Swan AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed the 'Additional credits' card (Top-Ups at 150% of plan rate) and the 'Credit rollover' explainer; top-up pricing now appears only in the FAQ as fixed per-credit rates of $0.37 (Solo), $0.30 (Starter) and $0.24 (Growth), versus 150% of the $0.16 plan rate ($0.24) before. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Solo at $80, Starter at $160 and Growth at $335.
It lands 49 days after Swan AI's previous change, "Swan: Solo & Starter plan credits increased (200→625, 1k→1,250)" (Aug 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Swan AI slashes monthly credits up to 69% while prices hold steady" (Jul 13, 2026), "Swan AI hikes Solo price to $100/mo but triples its monthly credits to 650" (Jul 2, 2026) and "Swan replaces credit-tier pricing with flat fees; Growth jumps $99->$419/mo" (Jul 3, 2026). Swan AI has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Microsoft, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Mistral AI also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
