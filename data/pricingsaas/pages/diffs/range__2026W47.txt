What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Range's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Personal tax filing (1 federal, up to 2 state returns) and up to 5 received K-1s now included in Platinum; Titanium narrowed to 3+ state filing, up to 10 K-1s and prior-year returns; FBAR/FATCA and real estate rows dropped; Per-unit overage fees removed from tax filing table: $100 per additional K-1 and $500 per additional state; replaced by 'custom quote available' for prior-year returns. In detail: Platinum now includes personal tax filing (1 federal + up to 2 state returns), FBAR/FATCA, and up to 5 received K-1s, previously Titanium-only; Titanium expands from up to 3 to 3+ state returns, keeps up to 10 K-1s, and drops the Real Estate Investment Properties (up to 9) filing row; Titanium per-unit tax filing fees removed from comparison table: $100 per additional K-1 (beyond 10) and $500 per additional state; 3+ states now included, further returns via Contact Range / custom quote. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, packaging change). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 18, 2026) lists Premium at $329.17, Platinum at $579.17 and Titanium at $1,041.67.
It lands 14 days after Range's previous change, "Range raises Platinum plan annual price by $1,000 to $6,950" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Range raises Cash Savings APY to 3.50% from 3.20%, renames footnote" (Oct 11, 2026), "Range swaps Titanium card's highlighted perk to FBAR + FATCA Filing" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Range rewrites Titanium plan highlights, but entitlements stay the same" (Sep 7, 2026). Range has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Compa, Tripletex, Yooz, TradingView and Taxdome also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
