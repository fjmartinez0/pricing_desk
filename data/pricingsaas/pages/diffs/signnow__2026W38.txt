What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of signNow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added a prominent 30-day money-back guarantee badge below the pricing cards; previously only a generic 30-day refund-request policy existed buried in the FAQ. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Business at $9, Business Premium at $15, Enterprise at $30 and Site License at $1.5 per signature invite.
It lands 21 days after signNow's previous change, "signNow spells out invite caps on plan cards: 5 a month on Business" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "signNow strips per-plan invite allowances from its pricing cards" (Aug 17, 2026), "signNow: invite quotas now shown on plan cards" (Aug 11, 2026) and "signNow: Business plan loses Approval workflows, SMS sending, notarization + 2 more feature restrictions" (Jul 13, 2026). signNow has been tracked since 2026-03-18.
In the same window Nitro, OneSpan, Docusign and Adobe also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
