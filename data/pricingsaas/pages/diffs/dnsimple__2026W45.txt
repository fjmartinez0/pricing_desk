DNSimple lifts hard DNS record caps, adds $1/month overage blocks
Verified Nov 2, 2026·Cloud & DevOps·Direction: neutral
The changeSolo Pro, Teams packaging — package change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 26, 2026 → Nov 2, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of DNSimple's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Solo Pro (500) and Teams (1,000) DNS records per zone limits now allow overage at $1.00/month per extra block of 500 records, instead of a hard cap. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Solo Pro at $19, Teams at $29 and Solo at $0.50 per hosted zone per month + $0.80 per million DNS queries over included (Solo).
It lands 56 days after DNSimple's previous change, "DNSimple: DNS query overage pricing restructured (Solo $0.10→$0.80/M, Teams $0.10→$0.60/M)" (Sep 7, 2026). DNSimple has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Hover, Google, Name.com and Cloudflare also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
