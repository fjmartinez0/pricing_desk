Taxdome scraps seat plans for $0.83/client pricing, adds $200/mo AI add-on

What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Taxdome's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: TaxDome replaced 3 seat-based plans (Essentials/Pro/Business, $700-$1100/seat/yr) with one per-client plan ($0.83/client/mo, $1,500/yr min, 20 seats), a Custom tier, and new paid add-ons (AI $200/mo). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $125, AI add-on at $200, Managed Client Care add-on at $100 and Premium support add-on at $249.
It lands 299 days after Taxdome's previous change, "Taxdome Introduces Seasonal Seats for Business Plan, Enhancing Flexibility with $500 Add-On for Temporary Staff" (Jan 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Taxdome Introduces White-Labeled Mobile App for Business Plan with 25+ Seats, Enhancing Value for Firms" (Q3 2025), "Taxdome Transforms Pricing Structure with New 3-Tier Model, Introducing Essentials, Pro, and Business Plans for Tailored Client Solutions" (Q2 2025) and "Taxdome Removes 1-Hour Screen Sharing Sessions from Medium and Big Firms Support Offerings" (Q4 2024). Taxdome has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window TurboTax and ProConnect also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
