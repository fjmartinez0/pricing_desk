JourneyTrack splits Agentic AI bullet into Journi and Scout Agents
Verified Sep 14, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral
The changeDecision Intelligence feature — feature change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 7, 2026 → Sep 14, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of JourneyTrack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Decision Intelligence plan's 'Agentic AI' bullet was replaced with two distinct named agent products, 'Journi Agents' and 'Scout Agents', shown on both the plan card and the AI & Intelligence comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Journey Mapping at $3,000 and Journey Management at $5,000.
It lands 7 days after JourneyTrack's previous change, "JourneyTrack: pricing restructured, plan capacity cut + 3 integrations added" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "JourneyTrack: Multi-factor authentication (MFA) added across all plans" (Jul 13, 2026), "JourneyTrack adds Notion, Planview AgilePlace to project management integrations" (Jul 6, 2026) and "JourneyTrack adds Import AI feature to all plans including Free Basic" (Jun 22, 2026). JourneyTrack has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Google also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
