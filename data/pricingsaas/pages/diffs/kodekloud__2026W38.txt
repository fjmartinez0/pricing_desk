What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of KodeKloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Annual billing discount increased from 'Save up to 21%' to 'Save up to 50%' on the Pay Annually toggle across all plans, part of a limited-time promotional sale. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Standard at $15, Pro at $21 and AI at $29.
It lands 14 days after KodeKloud's previous change, "KodeKloud doubles down: annual discount jumps to 50% off with new Summer Sale" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "KodeKloud ends August Saving Sprint, annual savings drop from 50% to 21%" (Aug 24, 2026), "KodeKloud raises annual billing discount from 21% to 50% in August sale" (Aug 17, 2026) and "KodeKloud doubles annual discount to 50% off in Half Year, Half Price sale" (Jul 20, 2026). KodeKloud has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
