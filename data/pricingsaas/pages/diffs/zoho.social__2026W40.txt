What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Zoho's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Channels included per Brand increased: Standard and Professional went from 12 to 14 channels, Premium from 14 to 16 channels, adding Snapchat and Arattai as newly supported platforms; 'Bulk Messaging' added to the Premium plan's feature list, appearing after 'Lead generation' and before the Add-ons section. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $10, Professional at $30, Premium at $40, Agency at $230 and Agency Plus at $330.
It lands 180 days after Zoho's previous change, "Zoho Social Expands Standard Plan to 12 Channels With YouTube Support" (Apr 1, 2026). Zoho has been tracked since 2023-11-30.
In the same window Microsoft, Salesforce, HubSpot and Pipedrive also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
