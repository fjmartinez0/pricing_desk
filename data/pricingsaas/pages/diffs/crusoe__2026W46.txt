What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Crusoe's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: GPU on-demand hikes effective Dec 1, 2026: B300 $8.96 to $9.90, H200 $4.29 to $5.40, H100 $3.90 to $4.50 per GPU-hr; B200 now lists $8.50 instead of Contact sales; Self-Serve Deployments hourly rates rise effective Dec 1, 2026: B200 $9.65 to $10.20, H200 $6.00 to $7.50, H100 $5.50 to $6.30, AMD MI355X $7.70 to $8.20 per GPU-hr; B300 now lists $13.80 instead of Contact sales. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Managed Inference - DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at $0.01 per 1M cached tokens + $0.20 per 1M output tokens, NVIDIA H200 (141GB) On-Demand at $4.29 per GPU-hr, NVIDIA L40S (48GB) On-Demand at $1.50 per GPU-hr, NVIDIA H100 (80GB) On-Demand at $3.90 per GPU-hr and NVIDIA A100 PCIe (80GB) On-Demand at $2 per GPU-hr.
It lands 28 days after Crusoe's previous change, "Crusoe adds DeepSeek V4.1 Flash to Serverless Inference at $0.29/1M input" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Crusoe drops Kimi K2.6 ($0.70/$3.50 per 1M) from Managed Inference lineup" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Crusoe: GPT-OSS 20B fine-tuning price $0.40->$2.50 + 2 more changes" (Sep 28, 2026). Crusoe has been tracked since 2026-06-18.
