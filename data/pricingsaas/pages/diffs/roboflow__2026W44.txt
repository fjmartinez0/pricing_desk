Roboflow renames Serverless Inference and adds per-hour video stream rates
Verified Oct 26, 2026·AI & Machine Learning·Direction: up
The changeCore, Enterprise feature — feature added.
2 changes·2 captures·Oct 19, 2026 → Oct 26, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Roboflow's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Serverless Model Inference renamed Roboflow Cloud Inference; per-image rates kept and new per-hour Stream credit rates added (e.g. RF-DETR 0.100 credits/hour, SAM 3 0.400); Cloud Streaming (run workflows on live video streams on Roboflow GPUs, billed per second) added to comparison table, included in Core and Enterprise. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, metric change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Core at $39, Additional Prepaid Credits at $4 per prepaid credit, Additional On-Demand Credits at $6 per on-demand credit and Data Labeling Services at $0.05 per classification/keypoint annotation + $0.2 per polygon annotation.
It lands 7 days after Roboflow's previous change, "Roboflow pulls SSO, RBAC and Review Mode from Core, adds concurrent training" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Roboflow adds Agent: idea to deployed vision solution, automatically" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Roboflow drops Community Support/Forum from Public, Core plans" (Sep 7, 2026). Roboflow has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window Microsoft, Google and Prodigy Education also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
