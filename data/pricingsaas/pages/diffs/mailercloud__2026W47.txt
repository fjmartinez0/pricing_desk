What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Mailercloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Annual billing discount badge reduced from Save 30% to Save 25% on the Marketing Platform pricing toggle; FAQ text updated to match; Product tab renamed from Email API to API Platform. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change, positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Hobby at $6.30, Starter at $31.50 and Professional at $99.
It lands 125 days after Mailercloud's previous change, "Mailercloud lifts Enterprise Advanced Automations from 25 to unlimited" (Jul 13, 2026). Mailercloud has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp, Convertkit and Constant Contact also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
