What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of OpenArt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Oct 14 promos (Seedance up to 50% off + unlimited Flux 3 Image, Nano Banana 2.1 30% off, new-user 25% off) removed; Flux 3 720p unlimited-video promo continues to Oct 31. In detail: Expired Oct 14 promos removed: up to 50% off Seedance models + unlimited Flux 3 Image on Wonder, 30% off Nano Banana 2.1 generations, and the new-user offer of up to 25% off the first month on monthly plans. The unlimited Flux 3 720p videos promo on Wonder (until Oct 31) was already running and remains. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essential at $7, Advanced at $14.5, Team Plan at $17.5, Infinite at $28 and Wonder at $120.
It lands 3 days after OpenArt's previous change, "OpenArt ends 50% off Seedance and unlimited Flux 3 Image promos on Wonder" (Oct 16, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenArt Pro dropped from Seedance/Flux 3 promo; Wonder-only deal ends Oct 14" (Oct 9, 2026), "OpenArt cuts Nano Banana 2.1 generations 30% for upgraders through Oct 14" (Oct 7, 2026) and "OpenArt offers unlimited Flux 3 720p videos on Wonder plan until Oct 31" (Oct 6, 2026). OpenArt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Runway, Microsoft, Huggingface and Leonardo.ai also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
