What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Draxlr's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Power plan price doubled from $125/month to $250/month; the free trial was also narrowed from full Power-plan access to Premium-plan access only; Added Full App Embedding to hero summary, Row-level Security for Embeds, AI Dashboard Generation (replacing removed max-context-length limit), and Query Builder Custom Joins/SQL Query Variables. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Lite at $16.67, Premium at $50, Power at $166.67, Enterprise at $316.67 and AI Credits at $10.
It lands 7 days after Draxlr's previous change, "Draxlr rebrands Embed Dashboard as white-label, folds in Enterprise perk" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Draxlr caps free MCP access at 50 requests as paid tiers go unlimited" (Aug 24, 2026), "Draxlr: User access permissions now tiered by plan" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Enterprise tier launched at $500/mo, Self-Host plan discontinued, AI upgrades" (Jul 2, 2026). Draxlr has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
