What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of FinDock's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'E-mandates' add-on launched at $75/month (billed annually), giving customers a digital way to complete e-mandates connected to Salesforce; initially available in Netherlands and Sweden.
