What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Jam's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Features table gained a new 'Transcription & captions' row under Core features (same gating as Video blurring), and the separate 'Fin' and 'Fin AI Agent' rows under Jam for Customer Support were consolidated into a single 'Fin integration' row.
