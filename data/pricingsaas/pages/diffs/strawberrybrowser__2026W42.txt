What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Strawberry's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Part-time plan price cut from $100 to $85 per month; Team plan now lists per-seat tiers: Team Starter $35/seat (10,000 credits), Team Standard $150/seat (40,000), Team Ultra $350/seat (100,000), replacing "mix Free, Intern, Part-time, and Full-time seats"; Monthly credits reduced: Intern 8,000 to 6,000; Part-time 30,000 to 25,000; prices for Intern unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (package change, limit decrease, price decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Intern at $17, Starter at $35, Part-time at $71, Standard at $150 and Full-time at $208.
It lands 7 days after Strawberry's previous change, "Strawberry slashes free plan credits 75%, from 2,000 to 500 a month" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Strawberry: Team plan swaps overage note for shared credit pools" (Jul 27, 2026). Strawberry has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
