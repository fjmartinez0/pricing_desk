What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Microsoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Microsoft 365 E7 option added to Entra purchase options, bundling Entra products to secure employees and AI agents; no price visible on the rendered page. In detail: New Microsoft 365 E7 plan added to Entra purchase options, bundling the Entra Suite and Agent 365 to secure employees and AI agents: $99.00/user/month (No Teams $90.45), paid yearly. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Microsoft Entra ID P1 at $7, Microsoft Entra ID P2 at $10, Microsoft Entra Suite at $12, Microsoft Entra Workload ID at $3 and Microsoft Entra ID Governance at $7.
It lands 112 days after Microsoft's previous change, "Microsoft Security hikes Entra ID P1 to $7, P2 to $10 per user/month" (Jul 6, 2026). Microsoft has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
