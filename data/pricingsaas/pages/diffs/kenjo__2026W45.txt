What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Kenjo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New 'DATEV integration implementation' add-on added to the price calculator at €600 one-time cost, available starting from the Growth plan, to transmit payroll data between Kenjo and DATEV; Connect plan card now explicitly shows a 'PRICE ON REQUEST' label next to its Book a Demo CTA, clarifying that pricing is custom/quote-based rather than leaving it unlabeled. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, positioning) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at EUR 5.40, Growth at EUR 9, Recruitment Add-on at EUR 249 and Recruitment PLUS Add-on at EUR 399.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Kenjo. Kenjo has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
In the same window Paychex and Personio also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
