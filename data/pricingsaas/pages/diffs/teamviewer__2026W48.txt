What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of TeamViewer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday promo: save 30% on new Business, Premium and Corporate licenses until Dec 4; Premium $120.90 to $84.63/mo and Corporate $245.90 to $172.13/mo billed yearly (first year only). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists TeamViewer Remote Access at $24.90, TeamViewer Business at $50.90, TeamViewer Premium at $120.90 and TeamViewer Corporate at $245.90.
It lands 49 days after TeamViewer's previous change, "TeamViewer doubles free trial to 30 days across products" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "TeamViewer cuts add-on free trials from 30 days to 14 across all six" (Sep 7, 2026). TeamViewer has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
In the same window Microsoft, GoTo, AnyDesk and Zoho also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
