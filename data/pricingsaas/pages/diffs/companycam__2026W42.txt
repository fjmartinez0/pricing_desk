What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of CompanyCam's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Core plan now states a 2-user limit ("Includes 1 User (2 User Limit)"); previously "1 User Included" with additional users purchasable at $29 each, implying no cap. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Crew at $29, Core at $29, Scale at $29 and Marketing Suite at $79.
It lands 21 days after CompanyCam's previous change, "CompanyCam hikes Scale plan 25% to $249 while cutting Crew to $119" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CompanyCam: Tap to Pay/on-site payments now US-only; GBP toggle added" (Aug 17, 2026). CompanyCam has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
In the same window Fieldwire, PlanGrid, Atlassian, ClickUp and Basecamp also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
