What happened
Between the Oct 23, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Vercel's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Vercel Firewall limits changed: Custom Firewall Rules (Enterprise 1,000->250), IP Blocking (Hobby 3->10, Pro 100->40, Enterprise 1,000->250), System Bypass Rules (Pro 25->10, Enterprise 100->75); New 'Deployment Storage' metered item added (10 GB/month included, $0.10 per GB-month overage, Custom for Enterprise); 'Vercel Daemons' now labeled 'Beta' (status change on existing compute feature). Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, feature change, limit decrease) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $20.
It lands 6 days after Vercel's previous change, "Vercel: Vercel Drives storage launched at $0.05/GB-month, metered reads/writes" (Oct 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vercel adds Daemons with usage-based Active CPU and Memory pricing" (Oct 19, 2026), "Vercel launches usage-based Daemons billing at $0.128/hr Active CPU" (Oct 16, 2026) and "Vercel adds OWASP Core Ruleset firewall to Pro Plus priority projects" (Oct 5, 2026). Vercel has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Render, Railway and Netlify also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
