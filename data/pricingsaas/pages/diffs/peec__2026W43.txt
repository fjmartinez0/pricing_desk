What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Peec AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Monthly bot-visit allowance increased on all paid plans at unchanged prices — Starter 4M→4.5M, Pro 10M→13.5M, Advanced 25M→31.5M bot visits ($95/$245/$495/mo unchanged). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $80, Pro at $205, Advanced at $420, Additional Model (Starter) at $30 and Additional Model (Pro) at $70.
It lands 28 days after Peec AI's previous change, "Peec AI moves Perplexity tracking to Enterprise-only, adds Naver AI to all plans" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Peec AI adds Meta Spark to Enterprise LLM tracking, drops SOC 2 from plan table" (Sep 14, 2026), "Peec AI expands Enterprise LLM tracking to 12 models with Grok API addition" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Peec AI: Starter/Pro/Advanced plan prices revealed ($80-$420/mo)" (Aug 3, 2026). Peec AI has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Microsoft, OpenAI, Huggingface, Google and Anthropic also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
