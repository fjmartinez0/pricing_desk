What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of CompanyCam's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Crew plan price decreased from $129/month to $119/month (annual billing, USD); Scale plan price increased from $199/month to $249/month (annual billing, USD); Core plan gained 'Personalized price book & invoicing'; Crew plan gained 'Create proposals using voice notes and photos' as new included capabilities; Scale plan lost 'Send review requests after jobs' as an included feature; it is now only l. In detail: Scale plan lost 'Send review requests after jobs' as an included feature; it is now only listed under the Marketing Suite add-on. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price decrease, price increase, feature removed) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Crew at $29, Core at $29, Scale at $29 and Marketing Suite at $79.
It lands 35 days after CompanyCam's previous change, "CompanyCam: Tap to Pay/on-site payments now US-only; GBP toggle added" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CompanyCam renames all tiers, drops entry price to $63 but cuts to 1 user" (Jul 13, 2026). CompanyCam has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
In the same window Fieldwire, PlanGrid, Atlassian, ClickUp and Basecamp also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
