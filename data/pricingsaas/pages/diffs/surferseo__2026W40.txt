What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Surfer SEO's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan price was displayed as $999/month; it is now hidden and replaced with 'Custom' (tailored packages), moving Enterprise to contact-sales pricing. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Discovery at $49, AI Search Analytics at $82, Standard at $99, Pro at $182 and Peace of Mind at $299.
It lands 14 days after Surfer SEO's previous change, "Surfer SEO: AI Search Analytics price cut $158→$82/mo" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Surfer SEO lets AI Search Analytics stand alone, rebrands 'Compare Plans' as f" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Surfer SEO: New AI Tracker standalone plan added ($158/mo)" (Jul 27, 2026). Surfer SEO has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
