What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of OpenArt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Top pricing-page promo banner rotated from a Wonder-plan unlimited-video offer (Flux 3 720p, ends Oct 31) to a new 30% off all MiniMax H3 models offer for upgrades, ending Oct 4 11:59 PM PT. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Essential at $7, Advanced at $14.5, Team Plan at $17.5, Infinite at $28 and Wonder at $120.
It lands 6 days after OpenArt's previous change, "OpenArt swaps Flux 3 promo for 30% off MiniMax H3, any plan" (Sep 22, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenArt offers up to 50% off Seedance models plus unlimited Flux 3 Image" (Oct 2, 2026), "OpenArt: MCP access gets 10% off + unlimited Flux 3 video promo on Wonder" (Sep 21, 2026) and "OpenArt adds unlimited Flux 3 720p video to Wonder plan through Oct 31" (Sep 15, 2026). OpenArt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
