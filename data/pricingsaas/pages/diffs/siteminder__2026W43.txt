What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of SiteMinder's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: 14-day free trial messaging and 'Free Trial' CTAs removed from SiteMinder and SiteMinder Plus cards; replaced by 'Talk to us' CTAs. Prices unchanged at $135 and $179 per month; Add-ons section expanded into individual capabilities: GDS connectivity, Dynamic Revenue Plus modules (demand forecasting, dynamic pricing), Guest Engagement modules, Group booking engine, Metasearch manager. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change, positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists SiteMinder at $135 and SiteMinder Plus at $179.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for SiteMinder. SiteMinder has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window Lodgify, Guesty and iGMS also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
