What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Corel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday banner moved from 'Starts Early' to 'Preview', adding a free Grammarly Pro offer and bonus items (up to $354) on most products; list and sale prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists CorelDRAW Go at $79.99, CorelDRAW Graphics Suite 365-day Subscription at $239, Photo Video Bundle Ultimate at $139.99 per use, WordPerfect Office Standard at $249.99 per use and CorelDRAW Graphics Suite One-Time Purchase at $494 per use.
It lands 14 days after Corel's previous change, "Corel launches Black Friday sale up to 60% off, plus 30% off first-year plans" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Corel: New ~30% discounts added; Painter's $180 discount removed ($429)" (Sep 20, 2026) and "Corel shuffles discounts: Painter gets $180 off, PaintShop/VideoStudio lose 30%" (Sep 13, 2026). Corel has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Canva, Figma, Affinity and Sketch also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
