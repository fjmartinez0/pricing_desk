What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Kashoo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Autopilot cross-sell blurb reworded from 'all for the exact same price' to 'all at an affordable price'; no change to the $30/Mo plan price or its feature list. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Kashoo at $27.
It lands 80 days after Kashoo's previous change, "Kashoo: Two paid products ($20/$30 mo) merged into one free-trial plan" (Jul 3, 2026). Kashoo has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
