INE strips "Subscription required" gating from all 12 exam cards
Verified Sep 14, 2026·Cybersecurity & Identity Management·Direction: neutral
The changePositioning — positioning.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 7, 2026 → Sep 14, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of INE's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: "Subscription required" gating copy and "Already subscribed? Sign in to your account" prompt were removed from all 12 standalone certification cards (eIAMA, eAIS, eSOC, eMAPT, eCIR, eJDS, eCTHP, eWPTX, eEDA, eJPT, eWPT, eCPPT); listed exam prices are unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Fundamentals at $29.08, Premium at $66.58, Professional at $799 and Skill Dive at $299.
It lands 14 days after INE's previous change, "INE: eIAMA bundle price jumps $399→$599 as presale ends + new $450 cert" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "INE: New eIAMA + 3 months of Premium bundle launched ($399 presale)" (Aug 5, 2026) and "INE adds standalone eAIS AI Security cert for $450, or bundled at $599" (Jul 13, 2026). INE has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Microsoft, Coursera, Udemy and Pluralsight also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
