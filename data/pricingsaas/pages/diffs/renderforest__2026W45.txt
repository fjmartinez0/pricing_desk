What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Renderforest's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Halloween Sale (30% off Pro & Business) expired. Pro's annual price reverted $13.3->$19/mo and Business $27.3->$39/mo; savings badge dropped from 'SAVE UP TO 66%' to 'UP TO 51-52% OFF'; Renderforest Fast export cap halved: Lite 720p 4800->2400 videos, Lite 1080p 2400->1200, Pro 1080p 7200->3600. Annual credit totals (9600/28800/72000 credits/year) unchanged; New 'Choose your Renderforest plan' w. In detail: New 'Choose your Renderforest plan' widget added with per-plan credit equivalency tables showing conversions to Seedance 2.0, Kling 3.0 Omni, Grok Imagine, GPT Image 2, and Nano Banana 2/Pro outputs. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit decrease, discount removed, feature added) with a downward direction. The current page lists Lite at $9, Pro at $19, Business at $39, Premium Video at $24 per HD1080 export and Premium Logo at $29 per logo (vector SVG).
It lands 70 days after Renderforest's previous change, "Renderforest hikes Business plan annual price 34%, discount shrinks" (Aug 24, 2026). Renderforest has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Canva, Wistia and Loom also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
