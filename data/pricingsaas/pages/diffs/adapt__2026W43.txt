What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Adapt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Managed plan per-user price cut from $14 to $8 per user per month plus usage (about 43% decrease). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Business at $14 and Open Source at $1.75 per 100 GiB-hours (sandbox memory, running) + $0.09 per GB-month storage, after 50 GB.
It lands 14 days after Adapt's previous change, "Adapt renames open source plan Developer, splits Business and Enterprise" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Adapt: new Enterprise SSO/SCIM/audit-log add-ons + On-prem tier" (Sep 28, 2026). Adapt has been tracked since 2026-07-14.
In the same window Salesforce, Microsoft and HubSpot also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
