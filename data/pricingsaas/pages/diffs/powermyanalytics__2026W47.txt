What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Power My Analytics' pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pro plan's per-account overage pricing ($9.95 down to $4.95/mo for 1 to 61+ accounts beyond the included 20) moved behind a collapsed 'See volume rates' toggle, no longer shown by default. Advertised connector count increased from 80+ to 85+, now shown across all plans. In detail: Pro plan's per-account overage pricing table (prior $4.95-$9.95/mo tiers for accounts beyond 20) is no longer shown by default; replaced with a collapsed 'See volume rates' toggle that hides the exact rates; Advertised connector/integration count increased from 80+ to 85+, repeated in both the page intro copy and the plan-comparison tagline, applying to all plans. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden, limit raised) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Business at $41.63, Grow at $83.29, Pro at $166.63, Additional Accounts (Pro) at $8.29 and Additional Account per Source (Grow) at $10.79.
It lands 49 days after Power My Analytics' previous change, "Power My Analytics adds free Data Destinations to all plans, renames Custom's…" (Sep 28, 2026). Power My Analytics has been tracked since 2026-06-19.
In the same window Domo, Google and Alteryx also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
