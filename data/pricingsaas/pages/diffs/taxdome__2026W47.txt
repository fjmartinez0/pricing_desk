Taxdome stops counting logins as activity for active client billing

What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Taxdome's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Active client billing definition changed: any activity in past 12 months (incl. logins) now qualifying activity in plan year (uploads, signed letters, paid invoices); logins no longer count. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change). The current page lists Standard at $125, AI add-on at $200, Managed Client Care add-on at $100 and Premium support add-on at $249.
It lands 7 days after Taxdome's previous change, "Taxdome scraps seat plans for $0.83/client pricing, adds $200/mo AI add-on" (Nov 8, 2026). Taxdome has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window TurboTax and ProConnect also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
