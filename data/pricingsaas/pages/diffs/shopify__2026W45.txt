What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Shopify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free Shopify Academy courses removed from the plan feature list and comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Basic at $29, Grow at $79, Advanced at $299, Plus at $2,300 and POS Pro at $89.
It lands 5 days after Shopify's previous change, "Shopify drops free Shopify Academy courses perk from all four pricing plans" (Oct 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shopify rewrites POS comparison, adds customer profiles and returns rows" (Oct 26, 2026), "Shopify simplifies in-person sales messaging, adds plan comparison rows" (Oct 23, 2026) and "Shopify cuts Grow to $79 and Advanced to $299, reversing D288 hike" (Oct 16, 2026). Shopify has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Squarespace and Salesforce also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
