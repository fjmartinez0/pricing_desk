What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Regpack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Mid-Market/Plus swapped features: removed training sessions, consultant caps, blog access, financial reports, migration assistance, email quotas; added AI reporting, program insights, attendance tracking. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction.
