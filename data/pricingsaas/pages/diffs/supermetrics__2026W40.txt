What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Supermetrics's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The 'Data API & MCP access' row in the feature comparison table dropped its 'Limited access' badge and per-plan row caps (Starter: up to 50,000 rows/month; Growth: up to 250,000 rows/month); the row now shows no limit value. In detail: Data API & MCP access was removed from the Starter and Growth plans. Their comparison-table cells previously read "Limited access" (up to 50,000 and 250,000 rows/month) and are now blank. Enterprise still includes it. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Starter at $44 and Growth at $177.
It lands 14 days after Supermetrics's previous change, "Supermetrics strips automated refresh, dashboards from Starter, Growth, Enter" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Supermetrics: Starter price hike $37->$44/mo + new per-unit Extras upsells" (Jul 4, 2026). Supermetrics has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Amplitude, Mixpanel, Google, DAT Freight & Analytics and Tableau also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
