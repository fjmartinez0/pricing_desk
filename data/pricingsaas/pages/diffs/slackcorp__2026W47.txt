What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Slack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Slackbot opened to Free and Pro on a consumption basis (previously not included on either), Business+ capped at 15 messages per user each week, and Enterprise+ now gets Unlimited Slackbot (previously 'plan limits apply'). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Pro at $7.25, Business+ at $15 and Enterprise+ at $45.
It lands 14 days after Slack's previous change, "Slack swaps 24/7 support row for tiered Salesforce Success Plans" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Slack launches Enterprise+ add-on for frontline field workers" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Slack lists Enterprise+ at $45 per user/month, ending contact-sales pricing" (Oct 4, 2026). Slack has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Microsoft, Zoom, Google, Mattermost and Discord also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
“Slackcorp” is the name Slack’s pricing page uses; Pulse files the change under Slack, the company it tracks.
