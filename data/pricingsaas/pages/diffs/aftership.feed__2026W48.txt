What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Aftership's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Affiliates product pricing added (Core $40/month intro, $24 each additional TikTok Shop store; Enterprise custom); Advanced Support add-on ($999/month) and the Support and guidance tier section removed. In detail: Advanced Support add-on ($999/month) and the entire 'Support and guidance' tier section (Basic/Advanced/Enterprise Support) removed from the pricing page; New Affiliates product pricing added: Core plan at $40/month (intro pricing) plus $24 per additional TikTok Shop store, 90 days free trial; Enterprise tier at custom pricing for 4+ stores. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on removed, new add-on). The current page lists Basic at $8 and Scale at $49.
It lands 223 days after Aftership's previous change, "Aftership Feed swaps $1 trial banner for free returns promo" (Apr 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Aftership Feed adds per-order pricing across Basic and Scale plans" (Apr 1, 2026), "Aftership Feed Drops Per-Order Pricing and Product Listing Details" (Mar 26, 2026) and "Aftership Feed replaces free month trial with $1 first month offer" (Mar 2, 2026). Aftership has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Shippo and EasyPost also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
