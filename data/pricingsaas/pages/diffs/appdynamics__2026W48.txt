What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of AppDynamics' pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Observability Cloud's Infrastructure/App & Infra/End-to-End tiers ($15/$60/$75 per host) replaced by a la carte products (APM $40, Logs $0.60/GB) and Free/Essentials $95/Premier $150 editions; AI SRE added to Observability Cloud, included in Free (up to 15 hosts), Essentials and Premier editions. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, packaging change). The current page lists Infrastructure Edition at $6, Premium Edition at $33, Enterprise Edition at $50, AppDynamics RUM at $0.06 and Enterprise Edition for SAP Solutions at $95.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for AppDynamics. AppDynamics has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
