What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Onpipeline's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: WhatsApp integration added to the Communication & Customer Engagement comparison table; plan-fit blurb now highlights WhatsApp, email and SMS communication. In detail: WhatsApp integration added to the Communication & Customer Engagement comparison table for Standard ($39) and Advanced ($58), not Starter; plan-finder results for Standard, Advanced and Enterprise now list WhatsApp integration. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 18, 2026) lists Pipeline at $20, Standard at $32 and Advanced at $47.
It lands 21 days after Onpipeline's previous change, "Onpipeline launches Autumn Sale with 50% off first 3 months on all plans" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Onpipeline ends 50% Autumn Sale banner; list prices hold steady" (Oct 19, 2026), "Onpipeline adds ~20% annual billing discount and 50% off Autumn Sale" (Oct 11, 2026) and "Onpipeline rewrites Pipeline plan pitch to drop basic-plan limits" (Oct 4, 2026). Onpipeline has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
