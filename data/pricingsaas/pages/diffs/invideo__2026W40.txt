What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of InVideo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Storage limits increased 10x across Plus/Max/Enterprise tiers (20GB->200GB, 60GB->600GB, 200GB->2,000GB); Free guest seats added on paid tiers (Max +20, Plus +10, 190 credits each FREE). In detail: Storage limits increased 10x across three plan tiers: 20GB -> 200GB, 60GB -> 600GB, and 200GB -> 2,000GB; Free guest seats added on paid tiers: Max plan now includes +20 guest seats at 190 credits each, FREE (Plus plan gets +10 under the same terms); Starter plan explicitly shows no guest seats. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Starter at $10, Plus at $30, Team at $47, Max at $75 and Scale at $107.
It lands 21 days after InVideo's previous change, "InVideo redenominates credits 10x across all plans; prices and generation counts unchanged" (Sep 7, 2026). InVideo has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
