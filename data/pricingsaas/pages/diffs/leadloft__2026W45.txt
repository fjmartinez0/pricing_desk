What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of LeadLoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Unlimited plan renamed Starter and Scale AI renamed Growth; prices unchanged ($99 and $299/mo/user, $400 struck through); Growth drops AI SDRs, AI Writer, Playbook Builder, Deal Router, Website Integration and Success Sessions; adds 3 Managed Senders, AI CRM Agent, AI Outreach, A/B Testing, Notetaker. Starter adds 0 Managed Senders and AI features. In detail: Plan cards reworked: Growth now leads with 3 Managed Senders (Starter shows 0) and both cards list AI CRM Agent (renamed from AI SDRs), AI Prospecting, AI Outreach, AI A/B Testing, AI Notetaker and Scheduling Links. Growth card no longer lists AI Writer, Playbook Builder, Deal Router, Website Integration or Success Sessions, though the compare table still does; compare table adds AI Notetaker and AI Scheduler rows. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed, feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $99 and Growth at $299.
It lands 14 days after LeadLoft's previous change, "LeadLoft swaps Unlimited prospecting for 300 credits, adds AI Sales Agent" (Oct 19, 2026). LeadLoft has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
