What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Anthropic's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Max plans gain monthly API credits ($100 on Max 5x, $200 on Max 20x); Claude Dashboards and Motion (beta) added to plan feature lists. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $17, Enterprise (self-serve) at $20, Team (Standard seat) at $20, Max 5x at $100 and Team (Premium seat) at $100.
It lands the same day as Anthropic's previous change, "Anthropic API launches Haiku 5.5 at $0.10/$0.50 per MTok, Haiku 4.5 goes Legacy" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Anthropic opens Claude Design, Slides and Docs to Free plan users" (Oct 9, 2026), "Anthropic API launches Haiku 5.5 at $0.10/$0.50, halves Sonnet 5.5 cache reads" (Oct 8, 2026) and "Anthropic API: Opus 5.5 launches, replaces Opus 5 as flagship" (Sep 28, 2026). Anthropic has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Google, X, Mistral AI, Microsoft and OpenAI also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
