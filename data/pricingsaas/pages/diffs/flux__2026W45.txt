What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Flux's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Explore individual plan ($50/mo, 40 ACUs/month) removed; Build ($100/mo) is now the entry paid individual plan. Pro comparison now references Build; Enterprise tier drops advanced export formats (JEP30), SOC2 and security audits; adds guided onboarding and toolchain integration and a named account manager. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, plan removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Build at $80, Pro at $200, Teams at $408.33, Additional AI Usage at $2 per additional ACU and Additional Teams Seat at $81.67.
It lands 7 days after Flux's previous change, "Flux Teams jumps to flat $500/mo with 5 seats, drops trial" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Flux doubles Pro AI allowance to 800 ACUs, boosts Build to 200 at same price" (Oct 19, 2026). Flux has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window OpenAI, Microsoft, Leonardo.ai and Perplexity AI also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
