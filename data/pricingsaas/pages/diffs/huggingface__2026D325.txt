What happened
Between the Nov 20, 2026 and Nov 21, 2026 captures of Huggingface's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan price increased from $50 to $500 per user per month. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Nov 22, 2026) lists PRO at $9, Team at $20, Enterprise at $50, Inference Endpoints - NVIDIA T4 AWS (4x) at $3 per hour and Inference Endpoints - NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 AWS (1x) at $2.75 per hour.
It lands 4 days after Huggingface's previous change, "Huggingface drops NVIDIA H100 inference endpoints from pricing table" (Nov 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Huggingface extends hardware price table to cover Jobs, rates unchanged" (Nov 1, 2026), "Huggingface adds Nvidia RTX PRO 6000 GPUs to Spaces from $2.75/hr" (Oct 16, 2026) and "Huggingface swaps PRO's 20x inference multiplier for a flat $2 monthly credit" (Oct 8, 2026). Huggingface has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Together AI, Modal, Anthropic, OpenAI and Cohere also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
