What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Moz's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Tracked AI models cut from 4 to 3 on Standard, Medium and Large; Perplexity removed (now GPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $49, Standard at $79, Medium at $143, Large at $239 and Additional Link Tracking Lists at $25.
It lands 21 days after Moz's previous change, "Moz triples AI Visibility quotas on all Pro plans, prices unchanged" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Moz Pro adds AI Sentiment Analysis to comparison table" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Moz boosts AI Models quota, adding Perplexity and Google AI Mode across plans" (Sep 7, 2026). Moz has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Semrush and Ahrefs also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
