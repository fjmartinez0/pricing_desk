What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Coschedule's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter plan adds Social Media MCP (Claude, ChatGPT & More), letting users create, schedule and edit posts for all 11 networks from AI assistants. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Starter at $29 and Professional at $49.
It lands 7 days after Coschedule's previous change, "Coschedule drops $16/mo X/Twitter add-on, now included free in all plans" (Nov 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Coschedule drops unlimited seats and profiles from Suite plan, now custom" (Oct 26, 2026), "Coschedule caps Professional Inbox at 9 networks, drops add-ons from table" (Oct 11, 2026) and "Coschedule ditches per-seat tiers for flat $29-$69 plans" (Sep 6, 2026). Coschedule has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Hootsuite, Buffer, Sproutsocial and Sendible also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
