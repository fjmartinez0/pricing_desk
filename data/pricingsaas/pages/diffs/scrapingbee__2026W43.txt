What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of ScrapingBee's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New entry-level Hobby plan added at $19/mo with 75,000 API credits and 25 concurrent requests, below Freelance $49/mo. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Hobby at $19, Freelance at $49, Startup at $99, Business at $249 and Business + at $599.
It lands 28 days after ScrapingBee's previous change, "ScrapingBee clarifies: pay only for successful requests, failed calls stay free" (Sep 21, 2026). ScrapingBee has been tracked since 2026-09-09.
In the same window Puppet, Oxylabs, Beautiful.ai and Apify also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
