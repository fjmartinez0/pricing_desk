What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of UKKO.fi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Top promo banner swapped: 300 € incorporation benefit replaced by -20% extra discount on service fees for 12 months for switching accounting to UKKO. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists UKKO Kevytyrittäjä at 5% + 3% of invoicing, Y-tunnus (Business ID Add-on) at EUR 4.90, UKKO Plus at EUR 12.90, UKKO Salamapalkka at EUR 15 per use and UKKO Rahoitus at EUR 10 per use + 5% of invoicing.
It lands 42 days after UKKO.fi's previous change, "UKKO.fi launches Ulkomaankauppa add-on at €10/mo, opens it to sole traders" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "UKKO.fi discloses 25.5% VAT added to Plus/Salamapalkka/Rahoitus pricing" (Aug 31, 2026) and "UKKO.fi yanks 20% Yrittäjä discount banner, swaps in photo exhibition ad" (Aug 24, 2026). UKKO.fi has been tracked since 2026-03-05.
