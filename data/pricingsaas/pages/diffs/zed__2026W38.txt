What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Zed's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro free trial cut from a two-week trial with $20 of token credits to a 14-day trial with only $5 of usage, and trial model access narrowed to GPT-5.6 Luna only (no credit card now required). In detail: Pro free trial credits cut from $20 of token credits to $5 of usage, and trial model access narrowed to GPT-5.6 Luna only (previously all models except Claude Fable, Claude Opus, and GPT Pro). Trial length is unchanged at 14 days, and no credit card is now required. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $10 and Business at $30.
It lands 28 days after Zed's previous change, "Zed's Pro trial now blocks Claude Fable, Opus, and GPT Pro access" (Aug 17, 2026). Zed has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Cursor, Windsurf, JetBrains and Nova also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
