What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of FilterPixel's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Black Friday banner (50% off annual plans, early access to Galleries, +10GB storage) removed; list prices unchanged ($7.50/$17.50/$32.50 per month annually); Galleries, Face Search and Jersey Number Search no longer 'coming soon' and now live on all plans; Expert Profiles (1/All 9/All 9) and Style DNA profiles replace custom trainable AI profiles. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed, feature added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $7.50, Pro at $17.50, Studio at $32.50 and Extra DeepCull Shoot at $7.99 DeepCull shoots.
It lands 215 days after FilterPixel's previous change, "FilterPixel hikes all plans, Pro jumps $16.59→$34.99/mo, caps photos at 5,500" (Apr 22, 2026). FilterPixel has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
