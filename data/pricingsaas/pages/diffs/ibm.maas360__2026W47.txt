What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of IBM's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Mobile Security Suite plan added, starting at $6.00 USD, combining MDM with integrated mobile threat defense, phishing and malware protection. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan). The current page lists Essentials at $4.24, Mobile Security Suite at $6, Premier at $6.63 and Enterprise at $9.54.
