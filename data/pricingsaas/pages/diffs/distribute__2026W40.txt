What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Distribute's pricing page, Pulse recorded 5 changes: New plan-wide features bundled in: AI meeting notes, 30+ content blocks, e-signatures, stakeholder tabs, video narration, content library, Brand Kit, viewer analytics, no-buyer-login, Salesforce/HubSpot sync, Gong/Fathom imports, and Claude/ChatGPT via MCP; New Team plan at $799/month for up to 10 sellers: everything in Pro plus shared templates, a cross-seller deal view, and alerts when deals go. In detail: New Team plan at $799/month for up to 10 sellers: everything in Pro plus shared templates, a cross-seller deal view, and alerts when deals go quiet; Dante plan ($99/mo, an AI sales assistant on iMessage) renamed to Pro at the same $99/month, now sold as the full Distribute platform for one seller; 14-day free trial of Pro introduced as the page headline ('Try Distribute free for 14 days'); card required at signup, charged only after the trial; DistributeOS (deployed sales system with a dedicated strategist, sold via strategy session) removed as a plan; only a footnote now offers a call for 10+ sellers or done-for-you setup. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan, plan renamed, trial change, feature added, plan removed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $99 and Team at $799.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Distribute. Distribute has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
