What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of HubSpot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added "Handoff Summaries (Beta)" to the Professional/Enterprise feature comparison table, alongside Quality assurance score (Beta) and Customer experience score (Beta). In detail: Added "Handoff Summaries (Beta)" to the detailed feature comparison table, alongside other AI-assist items (Quality assurance score, Customer experience score). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $7, Professional at $90, Enterprise at $150 and HubSpot Credits at $9 per 1,000 credits.
It lands 49 days after HubSpot's previous change, "Hubspot Service Enterprise drops AI transcript enrichment from Enterprise plan" (Sep 21, 2026). HubSpot has been tracked since 2025-01-01.
In the same window Zoho, Microsoft, Salesforce, Pipedrive and ActiveCampaign also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
