What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Expo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Hosting 'Subrequests' limit increased from 1,000 to 10,000 per request for Starter, Production, and Enterprise plans; Free plan's limit of 10 is unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Starter at $19 and Production at $199.
It lands 49 days after Expo's previous change, "Expo: Hosting Aliases limit cut to 200 (was Unlimited)" (Aug 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Expo: Observe moves from free beta to paid usage-based pricing (Aug 20, 2026)" (Jul 27, 2026). Expo has been tracked since 2026-06-19.
In the same window GitHub, Unity, Stedi and Spline also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
