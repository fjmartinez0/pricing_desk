What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Verbit's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Legal tab's 'Standard' plan renamed 'Legal Transcription'; $24/mo self-serve price and 100hrs/mo allotment removed, CTA changed 'Get started'->'Book a demo', new Scopist Service add-on (priced per page) added; Legal Transcription features overhauled: 'Not court admissible' became 'Certified final transcripts'; Gen.V insights, export, 1-user cap, human-review upgrade dropped; digital-evidence move. In detail: Legal Transcription tier's translation language coverage increased from 28+ to 30+ languages; Legal Transcription features overhauled: 'Not court admissible' became 'Certified final transcripts'; Gen.V insights, export, 1-user cap, human-review upgrade dropped; digital-evidence moved to Legal Capture. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden, limit increase, feature change) with a downward direction.
It lands the same day as Verbit's previous change, "Verbit drops $24 Legal plan price for Book a demo, adds Scopist" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Verbit simplifies plan names: "Basic Transcription" becomes "Standard," legal" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Verbit: Legal plans renamed + Scopist add-on introduced" (Jul 27, 2026). Verbit has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
