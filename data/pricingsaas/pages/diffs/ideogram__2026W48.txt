What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Ideogram's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Ideogram 4.6 added to the credit usage table with 2K (0.8-22 credits/image) and 4K (6-60 credits/image) rendering rates; 4.5 and older rows unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Plus at $15, Team at $20, Pro at $42 and Pay as you go at $0.01 per credit.
This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Ideogram: Added a new Ideogram 4.6 image model to the credit-cost table with separate 2K and 4K rendering tiers, each…" (Day 324, 2026, saved Nov 20, 2026). It is the same change, not a new one. Before that, Ideogram's previous recorded change was "Ideogram swaps Free plan for 1¢-per-credit Pay as you go, drops Team minimum" (Oct 19, 2026). Ideogram has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Microsoft, Canva, Perplexity AI, Runway and Leonardo.ai also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
