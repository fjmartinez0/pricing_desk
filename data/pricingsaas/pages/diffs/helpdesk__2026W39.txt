What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of HelpDesk's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI resolutions top-up addon reworded from an automatic '50 AI resolutions package (auto-refill)' billed at next renewal to a manual 'Extra AI resolutions package (50)' added from billing settings; price ($49.50) and pack size (50) unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essential at $19, Growth at $79, AI Resolutions Pack (50) at $49.5 subscription and API Calls Pack (100k) at $29.5 subscription.
It lands 56 days after HelpDesk's previous change, "HelpDesk rebrands from HelpDesk by Text to HelpDesk.com, plans unchanged" (Jul 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HelpDesk overhauls plans: new Essential & Growth tiers meter AI" (Jun 30, 2026) and "HelpDesk: Relaunched as AI-agent platform, Team/Business renamed + repriced" (Jul 3, 2026). HelpDesk has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Salesforce, HubSpot, Intercom, Zoho and Zendesk also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
