What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Unity's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added Unity Asset Manager Storage row priced $0.75/GB on all four plan columns; the included-storage row is now labeled 'Included Unity Asset Manager Storage'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Unity Pro at $192.5 and Unity AI at $10.
It lands 7 days after Unity's previous change, "Unity adds offline machine licensing support to Enterprise plan" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Unity: Personal plan hides $10/mo AI subscription price after trial" (Jul 27, 2026). Unity has been tracked since 2026-06-26.
