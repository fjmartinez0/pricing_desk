What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Convertkit's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Limited-time Pro offer: extra 25% off first year; Pro yearly shows $50/mo ($593 billed first year) vs $66/mo ($790); renews at standard rate after year one; Growth Concierge team added to Pro plan feature list and comparison table. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, discount added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Creator at $33 and Pro at $66.
It lands 101 days after Convertkit's previous change, "ConvertKit (Kit): Creator + Pro plans gain new features (MCP, SMS, Signals, analytics)" (Jul 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Convertkit shifts Pro messaging to revenue analytics, reshuffles Creator tools" (Apr 3, 2026), "Convertkit Renames Creator Pro Plan to Pro, Streamlining Offerings for Users" (Jan 11, 2026) and "Convertkit Raises Creator Pro Annual Plan Price by 33.9% to $790, Marking Significant Pricing Changes" (Q3 2025).
