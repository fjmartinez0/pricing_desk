What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Jobber's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Annual billing discount deepened from 'up to 25%' to 'up to 40%', time-limited (ends Sept 30, terms apply); Core/Connect/Grow annual price drops to $21/$70/$105 for 12 months before reverting to $29/$99/$149. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Core at $29, Connect Individual at $99, Connect at $149, Grow Individual at $149 and Grow at $299.
It lands the same day as Jobber's previous change, "Jobber Teams: Annual discount jumps from 25% to 40% via new intro pricing" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Jobber's 40%-off promo expires, annual savings drop to 25%" (Sep 7, 2026), "Jobber swaps 25% annual discount for limited-time 40% off through Aug 31" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Jobber Teams: New intro-rate promo, up to 40% off (was 25%), ends Aug 31" (Aug 24, 2026). Jobber has been tracked since 2026-02-06.
