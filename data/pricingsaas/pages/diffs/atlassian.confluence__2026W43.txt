What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Atlassian's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Standard per-user monthly price rose $5.42 to $5.78; Premium rose $10.44 to $11.16. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $5.67 and Premium at $10.83.
It lands 42 days after Atlassian's previous change, "Atlassian Confluence caps Enterprise automation, lifts Free limit 5x" (Sep 7, 2026). Atlassian has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window GitHub, Slack, Microsoft, GitLab and Asana also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
