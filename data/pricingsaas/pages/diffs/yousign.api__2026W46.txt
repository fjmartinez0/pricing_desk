What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Yousign's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Youtrust Verify add-on expanded from identity/document/company to include bank account verification and fraud detection; Verify solutions card row added. In detail: Youtrust Verify add-on renamed Verify and expanded from identity/document/company verification to add Bank Account Verification, Watchlist Screening and Document fraud detection (Document Trust); now offered as a paid add-on from the API Plus plan (previously Pro card only). Signature & Seal verification now included at no cost. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists API Plus at EUR 104 and API Pro at EUR 129.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Yousign. Yousign has been tracked since 2026-06-18.
