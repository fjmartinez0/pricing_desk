What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of IONOS's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: IONOS VPS lineup restructured: new entry-level VPS S+ tier launched at $2/mo (was not offered before), while existing M+, L+, XL+, and XXL+ promotional monthly rates rose ($4->$5, $6->$8, $11->$14, $18->$20). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists VPS S+ at $6, VPS M+ at $14, VPS L+ at $25, VPS XL+ at $47 and VPS XXL+ at $68.
It lands 14 days after IONOS's previous change, "IONOS Page1 adds 1-click app installs to every VPS+ plan, cuts intro section" (Sep 7, 2026). IONOS has been tracked since 2026-06-24.
