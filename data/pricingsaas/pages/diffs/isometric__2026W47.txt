What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Isometric's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added a flat fee charged to suppliers on issuances below 1,000 certificates (amount not disclosed), a new supplier-side charge alongside the buyer-paid per-tonne fee. Pulse files it as a packaging change (packaging change). The current page lists Superpollutants at $3 per tonne, Biomass Carbon Removal and Storage at $10 per tonne, Direct Air Capture at $10 per tonne, Biosphere at $5 per tonne and Marine at $30 per tonne.
It lands 14 days after Isometric's previous change, "Isometric swaps fee tiers for a pathway calculator, adds CPI-linked fees" (Nov 1, 2026). Isometric has been tracked since 2026-06-19.
In the same window Figma, Affinity, Sketch and Canva also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
