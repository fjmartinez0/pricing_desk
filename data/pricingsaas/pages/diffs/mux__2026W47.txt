What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Mux's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Underlying video player technology referenced in the free feature comparison list changed from proprietary 'Mux Player' to open-source 'Video.js'; the feature itself remains free and included on all plans. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Pre-pay Launch at $20, Pre-pay Scale at $500, Pay as you go at $2.40 per 1,000 minutes (storage) + $0.80 per 1,000 minutes (delivery), Media-grade DRM at $100 and Custom Domains at $100.
It lands 14 days after Mux's previous change, "Mux swaps Find scenes for Analyze structure, renames Robots AI workflows" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Mux hikes live captions overage 10x to $0.024/min past 6k free minutes" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Mux adds explicit 100K free Robots AI unit quota to Free & Pay-as-you-go plans" (Sep 7, 2026). Mux has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window Google Cloud, Vimeo and Wistia also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
