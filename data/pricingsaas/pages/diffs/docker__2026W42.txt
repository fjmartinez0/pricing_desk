What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Docker's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Included Docker Build Cloud build minutes (200 Pro, 500 Team, 1,500 Business) no longer listed; Testcontainers minutes unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Docker Pro at $9, Docker Team at $15, Docker Business at $24, Testcontainers Cloud Additional Minutes at $3 per 100 minutes and Docker Build Cloud Minutes (500) at $25 per 500 build minutes.
It lands 14 days after Docker's previous change, "Docker adds $250 Cloud Sandbox credit in place of whitepaper CTA" (Sep 28, 2026). Docker has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Google Cloud and Hypervault also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
