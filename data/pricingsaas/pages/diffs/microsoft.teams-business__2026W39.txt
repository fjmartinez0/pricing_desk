What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Microsoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Teams Premium add-on eligibility narrowed to require a Microsoft Teams Enterprise license (previously any Teams license); the direct Buy now purchase option was also removed, leaving only a free trial. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Microsoft Teams Essentials at $4, Microsoft 365 Business Basic (no Teams) at $5.4, Microsoft 365 Business Basic at $7, Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot (no Teams) at $20.3 and Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot at $23.5.
It lands 77 days after Microsoft's previous change, "Microsoft Teams Business bundles Copilot into Standard, price jumps to $23.50/mo" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Microsoft Teams Business: Copilot discount promo renewed for Jul-Sep 2026" (Jul 3, 2026). Microsoft has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window Salesforce, Google, IBM and Adobe also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
