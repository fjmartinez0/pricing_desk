What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Envoy's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Critical tier (custom pricing) added to Envoy Visitors above Enterprise: approval workflows, denied party screening, visitor training/inductions, granular governance, audit-ready history; Enterprise ID Scanning (500 scans/yr, add-ons) replaced by Identity verification (document scanning, name and face verification); Enterprise gains custom admin roles, field-level retention; Premium gains dat. In detail: Enterprise ID Scanning (500 scans/yr, add-ons) replaced by Identity verification (document scanning, name and face verification); Enterprise gains custom admin roles, field-level retention; Premium gains data retention policy. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, new plan). The current page lists Premium at $362, Response at $2, Reservations at $5, Screens at $12 and Deliveries at $250.
It lands 143 days after Envoy's previous change, "Envoy: Premium price display changed to monthly, reporting feature moved to Premium" (Jul 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Envoy quietly drops Veriff branding from ID Scanning feature" (May 25, 2026). Envoy has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
