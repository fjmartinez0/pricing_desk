What happened
Between the Oct 22, 2026 and Oct 23, 2026 captures of Luma AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added Kling 4.0 (720p 53, 1080p 79 credits/sec) and Kling 4.0 Omni (text/image 53/79; video-to-video ~66/~98 credits/sec) to the API video model pricing table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Plus at $25, Pro at $75 and Ultra at $250.
It lands 18 days after Luma AI's previous change, "Luma AI doubles Seedance video-to-video credit costs, adds Minimax H3 Max" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Luma AI: Seedance 2.0 video generation costs cut ~50%" (Sep 21, 2026), "Luma AI slashes Seedance video model pricing up to 73% across all tiers" (Sep 19, 2026) and "Luma AI unlocks 8K upscaling in SeedVR2, breaking past the old 4K cap for 25 c" (Sep 5, 2026). Luma AI has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window HeyGen, Synthesia, Runway and OpenAI also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
