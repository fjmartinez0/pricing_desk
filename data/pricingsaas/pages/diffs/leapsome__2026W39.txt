What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Leapsome's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Leapsome added 'SOC 2 Type II certified' and 'EU AI Act ready' badges to the 'Always included with Leapsome' platform trust list, alongside existing ISO 27001 and GDPR compliance badges. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists HRIS at $7.
