What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Mindee's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Starter and Pro credit-volume selectors now show an itemized 'Platform fee' line with monthly dollar amounts alongside the per-credit rate; previously only a flat $/credit rate was displayed; The 'Prices are for reference only and may vary depending on user configuration' disclaimer was replaced with a credit-definition note: '1 page processed = 1 credit. More details here.'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (positioning, package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $53 and Pro at $134.
It lands 70 days after Mindee's previous change, "Mindee unlocks Confidence Scores and Polygons for Starter tier" (Jul 6, 2026). Mindee has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
