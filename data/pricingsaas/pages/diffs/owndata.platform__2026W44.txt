What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Owndata's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Premier Success Plan changed from 30% of net license fees to included with Core, Advanced, and Max licenses; 'Bundled with Unlimited Edition' note removed; Signature Success Plan: Customer Success Score renamed to Salesforce Health Insights.
