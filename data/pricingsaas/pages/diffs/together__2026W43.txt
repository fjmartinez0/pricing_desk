What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New serverless models listed: Muse Spark Open ($1.25 in/$4.25 out), Reflection Beam ($0.40/$1.20) and NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL ($0.15/$0.50) per 1M tokens. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Managed Storage at $0.16 per GiB/month, Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, Sandbox (Code Interpreter) at $0.03 per session (60 min), GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100) and GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days).
It lands 2 days after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI adds Reflection Beam to serverless at $0.40 per 1M input tokens" (Oct 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI adds NVIDIA B200 post-training at $11/GPU/hr, $9 launch promo" (Oct 20, 2026), "Together AI ends free Muse Spark Open promo; $1.25/$4.25 pricing now applies" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Together AI makes Muse Spark Open free until Oct 18, adds Nemotron 3.5 Super VL" (Oct 16, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
In the same window OpenAI, Huggingface, Google, Mistral AI and Anthropic also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
