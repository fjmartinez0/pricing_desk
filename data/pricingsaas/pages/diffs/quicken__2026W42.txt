What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Quicken's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: First-year promo offer deadline in footnote extended from October 28, 2026 to November 6, 2026. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Quicken Simplifi at $2.99, Classic Deluxe at $2.99, Quicken Business & Personal at $3.99, Classic Premier at $4.19 and Classic Business & Personal at $5.99.
It lands 7 days after Quicken's previous change, "Quicken extends first-year discount deadline four days to October 28" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Quicken extends first-year promo deadline again, now through October 24" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Quicken: annual-billing promo deadline extended (Aug 15 -> Aug 21)" (Jul 27, 2026). Quicken has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
