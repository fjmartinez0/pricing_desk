What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Personio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Core Enterprise availability SLA now states a 99.9% uptime figure (was unspecified) in both the plan card and the compare table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change).
It lands 7 days after Personio's previous change, "Personio hides €7.60 Core price, adds demo-only Core Enterprise plan" (Nov 9, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Personio raises CorePro work schedule cap 5x to 3,000, drops 10% annual discount" (Oct 19, 2026). Personio has been tracked since 2024-04-01.
In the same window Paychex also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
