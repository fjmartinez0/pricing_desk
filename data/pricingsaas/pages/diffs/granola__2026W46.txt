What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Granola AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan no longer lists MDM/Intune app wrapping (iOS) in the comparison table's Trust & Control section. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Business at $14 and Enterprise at $35.
It lands 4 days after Granola AI's previous change, "Granola AI drops MDM/Intune iOS app wrapping from Enterprise plan listing" (Nov 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Granola AI adds voice typing to every plan, including free Basic tier" (Nov 2, 2026), "Granola AI adds voice typing to Basic, Business and Enterprise plans" (Oct 30, 2026) and "Granola AI opens Slack to all plans, reserves SSO and HIPAA for Enterprise" (Sep 28, 2026).
