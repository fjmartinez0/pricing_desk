Cloudflare pricing FAQ
How much does Cloudflare cost?
Cloudflare pricing starts at $7/user/mo on SASE Pay-as-you-go. Cloudflare lists 7 plans on its pricing page; the SASE Free plan is free.
Does Cloudflare have a free plan?
Yes. Cloudflare's SASE Free plan is free; paid plans start at $7/user/mo on SASE Pay-as-you-go.
What pricing model does Cloudflare use?
Cloudflare uses subscription + usage pricing across 7 plans, with a free tier.
When did Cloudflare last change its pricing?
Nov 9, 2026: Cloudflare Aigateway credits now require credit card payment only.
How often has Cloudflare changed its pricing?
Pulse has recorded 22 pricing changes for Cloudflare since it started tracking on Nov 14, 2023; the pricing page was last checked on Nov 9, 2026.
What does “tracked since” mean?
It's the date of the first saved copy of the pricing page that Pulse checked for changes against a later copy. For a few companies we hold older copies that Pulse never checked for changes, and those don't change this date. If Pulse has never checked two copies, it's the date of the first saved copy.
Who are Cloudflare's pricing peers?
Pulse compares Cloudflare with Akamai Technologies, Aws, Fastly, Imperva, Zscaler and Microsoft Azure.
