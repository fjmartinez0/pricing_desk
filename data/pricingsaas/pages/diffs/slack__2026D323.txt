What happened
Between the Nov 18, 2026 and Nov 19, 2026 captures of Slack's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise+ card Slackbot line changed from 'Unlimited Slackbot' to 'Slackbot with no weekly message limits'; tooltip now adds 'subject to our usage guidelines' and 'advanced feature limits' caveats (was 'It's unlimited for your whole team'); Comparison table Slackbot row: Free and Pro changed from 'Consumption based' to '—' (not included); Business+ 'Usage limits' and Enterprise+ 'Unlimited' rep. In detail: Comparison table Slackbot row: Free and Pro changed from 'Consumption based' to '—' (not included); Business+ 'Usage limits' and Enterprise+ 'Unlimited' replaced by plain checkmarks. Reverses the W47 table change that opened Slackbot to Free/Pro. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed, positioning). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 22, 2026) lists Pro at $7.25, Business+ at $15 and Enterprise+ at $45.
It lands 5 days after Slack's previous change, "Slack reveals $45 Enterprise+ pricing, dropping contact-sales listing" (Nov 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Slack shifts Slackbot to usage limits, noting 15 messages per user weekly" (Nov 11, 2026), "Slack hides Enterprise+ pricing, drops $45/user price for contact sales" (Nov 8, 2026) and "Slack reveals Enterprise+ pricing at $45 per user/month, billed annually" (Nov 3, 2026). Slack has been tracked since 2023-11-30.
In the same window Microsoft, Zoom, Google, Rocket.Chat and Mattermost also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
