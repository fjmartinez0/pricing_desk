What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Aws' pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: AWS GovCloud (US) now offers Generative voices at $36.00 per 1M characters (speech requests), alongside the existing Standard $4.80 and Neural $19.20 GovCloud rates; New pricing note: creating a cloned voice is free; synthesis with cloned voices is billed per character at the Generative voice rate, pay-as-you-go with no upfront commitment. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Standard Voices at $4 per 1 million characters, Neural Voices at $16 per 1 million characters, Generative Voices (GovCloud) at $36 per 1 million characters, Long-Form Voices at $100 per 1 million characters and Generative Voices at $30 per 1 million characters.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Aws. Aws has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Digitalocean, IBM, Google Cloud and Vultr also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
