What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of LegalZoom's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: User seat allowances now listed: 1 seat (up to 2) on Personal, 3 seats (up to 10) on Family and Family & Business Mailbox plans; Family & Business Mailbox recipients with mail access reduced from 20 to 15. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, limit decrease) with an upward direction. The current page lists Personal Mailbox at $39, Family Mailbox at $59 and Family & Business Mailbox at $119.
It lands 49 days after LegalZoom's previous change, "LegalZoom hikes Virtual Mail prices up to 51% across all plans" (Sep 14, 2026). LegalZoom has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Stripe also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
