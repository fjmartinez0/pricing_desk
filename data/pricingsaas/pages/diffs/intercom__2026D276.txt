What happened
Between the Oct 2, 2026 and Oct 3, 2026 captures of Intercom's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Essential plan seat price increased from $19 to $29 per seat/month (billed annually), ending the promotional $19 rate. Advanced $85, Expert $132 and Fin $0.99/outcome unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Essential at $19, Advanced at $85, Expert at $132, Fin AI Agent (Standalone) at $0.99 per Fin outcome and Copilot at $29.
It lands 5 days after Intercom's previous change, "Intercom slashes Essential plan seat price 34%, from $29 to $19" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Intercom rebrands Essential automation as Fin, adds Fin external actions" (Sep 19, 2026), "Intercom strips Fin AI from Essential, reframes standalone agent plan" (Sep 18, 2026) and "Intercom: Essential plan's 35% new-customer promo ended" (Aug 31, 2026). Intercom has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
