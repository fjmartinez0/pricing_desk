SmartSuite launches Hosted MCP Server, now generally available for AI clients

What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of SmartSuite's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Hosted MCP Server feature moved out of 'coming soon' status and is now generally available, letting AI clients access SmartSuite data/actions through a managed, centrally governed MCP layer. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Team at $15, Professional at $32 and Additional storage at $10 per additional 100GB storage block.
It lands 28 days after SmartSuite's previous change, "SmartSuite adds Folder / View, Table and Field Permissions to Team and drops its nonprofit coupon" (Aug 31, 2026). SmartSuite has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Notion, Microsoft, Airtable, ClickUp and Asana also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
