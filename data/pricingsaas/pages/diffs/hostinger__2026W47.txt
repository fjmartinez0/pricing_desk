What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Hostinger's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Premium loses Ecommerce: its 'Ecommerce available' bullet is now a greyed-out dash row and the new comparison-table Ecommerce section marks Premium 'Not available'. On Unlimited and Cloud Startup the bullet becomes 'Ecommerce - free for 1 year' (NEW badge). Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed). The current page lists Premium at $1.99, Unlimited at $2.79, Cloud Startup at $7.99, Agency Startup at $29 and Agency Professional at $44.
It lands 7 days after Hostinger's previous change, "Hostinger deepens Black Friday deal, Premium drops to $1.99/mo at 85% off" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hostinger Black Friday cuts Premium to $2.49/mo, doubles AI credits" (Oct 26, 2026), "Hostinger boosts one-time AI credits on hosting plans, up to 1,000" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Hostinger clarifies AI Builder credits are a one-time gift, not recurring" (Oct 11, 2026). Hostinger has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Siteground, Dreamhost and Name.com also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
