What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Picsart's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Seedance 2.5 video quota switched from a video-count metric to a seconds-of-video metric: Pro ~83 videos -> ~250 seconds, Ultra ~416 videos -> ~1,250 seconds, within the same monthly credit allowance. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pro at $10.5 and Ultra at $60.
It lands 21 days after Picsart's previous change, "Picsart swaps flat daily AI limits for credit caps up to 5000 images" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Picsart doubles Pro credits to 500/month, halving Ultra's usage advantage" (Aug 24, 2026), "Picsart: Pro plan credits cut 500→250/mo; Ultra credit cap raised to 10,000+" (Aug 4, 2026) and "Picsart's Ultra boosts monthly credits to 2,500 but slashes annual discount t" (Jul 20, 2026). Picsart has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Corel, Canva and Adobe also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
