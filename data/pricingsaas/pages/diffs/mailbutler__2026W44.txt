What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Mailbutler's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Starter card no longer shows the free-forever $0* option next to the paid price; Starter now displays only $4 per user per month (yearly, $5 monthly). Free-forever watermark tooltip removed from the card; Feature group 'Inbox Collaboration' renamed to 'Inbox Organization & Collaboration' in the plan comparison; prices and plans unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change, positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Professional at $7, Smart at $11 and Business at $24.
Mailbutler's previous recorded change was "Mailbutler adds AI Smart Replies and launches Smart Automations" (Oct 19, 2026). Mailbutler has been tracked since 2026-09-09.
In the same window Salesforce, Pipedrive, HubSpot, Streak and Superhuman also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
