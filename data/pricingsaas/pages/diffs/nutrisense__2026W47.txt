What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Nutrisense's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: CGM Program prices cut: 6-month $152→$109/mo, 3-month $178→$119/mo, 1-month $212→$149/mo; 15% off promo banner replaced by 'New Lower Pricing' banner.
