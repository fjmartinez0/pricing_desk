What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Hedra's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Basic $15→$20/mo, Creator renamed to Pro $30→$50/mo, Professional renamed to Ultra $75→$100/mo. credits/month raised to match (1,500→2,000; 5,400→7,200; 14,400−18,000) plus new Ultra allowance of up to 57,600 credits/month. Teams/Enterprise unchanged. In detail: Basic $15→$20/mo; Creator renamed to Pro, $30→$50/mo; Professional renamed to Ultra, $75→$100/mo. All three individual-tier plans repriced upward this week; Monthly credit allowances raised across all individual plans: Basic 1,500→2,000; Creator/Pro 5,400→7,200; Professional/Ultra 14,400→18,000, plus new 57,600-credit allowance tier on Ultra. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $12, Creator at $24, Professional at $60 and Teams at $60.
It lands 42 days after Hedra's previous change, "Hedra: removes per-model credit-usage table + plan feature bullets" (Aug 17, 2026). Hedra has been tracked since 2026-01-28.
