Slack swaps 24/7 support row for tiered Success Plans, 15-min CSM add-on

What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Slack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Support row replaced by a Success Plans section. Before: Pro 24/7 support; Business+ 24/7 support with 4-hr first response; Enterprise+ 24/7 priority with 4-hr first response. After: Standard Success Plan (2-business-day case response) included on Pro, Business+, Enterprise+; Premier Success Plan (24/7/365, 1-hr target for business-stopping issues) included on Business+ and Enterprise+, add-on for. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $7.25, Business+ at $15 and Enterprise+ at $45.
It lands 5 days after Slack's previous change, "Slack swaps 24/7 support for tiered Success Plans, sold as paid add-ons" (Oct 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Slack launches Frontline add-on for Enterprise+ field workers and Salesforce" (Oct 26, 2026), "Slack: Frontline teams add-on launched for Enterprise+ field workers" (Oct 20, 2026) and "Slack hides Enterprise+ price, swaps $45/user for "Contact sales" (Oct 19, 2026). Slack has been tracked since 2023-11-30.
In the same window Zoom, Microsoft, Google, Rocket.Chat and Mattermost also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
