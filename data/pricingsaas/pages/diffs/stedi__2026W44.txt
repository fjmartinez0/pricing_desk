What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Stedi's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Stedi Lockbox moved from private preview/contact-us to public pricing ($49/lockbox/month, $2/mail item, $1/check deposit); new $0.30 workers' comp, auto and liability claim submission rate. In detail: Stedi Lockbox moved from private preview/contact-us to public pricing: $49/lockbox per month, $2/mail item scanned, $1/paper check deposited (requires Stedi Treasury); New Workers' compensation, auto, and liability claim submission line item added under Claims processing at $0.30/claim submission. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pay as you go at $49.
