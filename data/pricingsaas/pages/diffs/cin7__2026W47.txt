What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Cin7's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing page hero now shows an explicit 'FREE 14 DAY TRIAL' promo banner with a 'Talk to our team' CTA, replacing the prior generic 'Get a Demo' badge + 'Start a free trial' link. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change). The current page lists Cin7 Core Standard at $349, Cin7 Core Pro at $599 and Cin7 Core Advanced at $1,199.
It lands 35 days after Cin7's previous change, "Cin7 flags list prices as new-customer only on Standard, Pro, Advanced plans" (Oct 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cin7 launches Omni enterprise plan with 8 users and 5 integrations" (Oct 4, 2026). Cin7 has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Zoho, Shopify and Squarespace also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
