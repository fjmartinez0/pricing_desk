What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of PreVeil's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Compliance claims softened: Business tagline 'meet' became 'support' compliance requirements; HIPAA/FERPA/SOC2/GLB row now 'Supports requirements under HIPAA, FTC Safeguards Rule, IRS 4557, FERPA' (SOC2 dropped). Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Individual at $25, Business at $30 and PreVeil Pass at $450.
It lands 28 days after PreVeil's previous change, "PreVeil narrows Business plan compliance claim to HIPAA, FTC Safeguards, IRS " (Sep 21, 2026). PreVeil has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
