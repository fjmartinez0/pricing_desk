What happened
Between the Nov 24, 2026 and Nov 25, 2026 captures of Zapier's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New limited-time promo banner: Zapier MCP tool calls are free through Jan. 5, 2027 (previously each MCP call used two tasks from plan quota). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Professional at $19.99, Team at $69, Agents Pro at $33.33, Chatbots Pro at $13.33 and Chatbots Advanced at $66.67.
It lands 58 days after Zapier's previous change, "Zapier retires "Zap workflows" name, rebrands to "Zaps" in app" (Sep 28, 2026). Zapier has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Tray.ai, Ifttt, Parabol and Workato also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
