What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Resemble's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New product 'Resemble Agent Detection' added to the comparison table and product lineup, priced at $1 per 1,000 sessions across Flex, Team, and Business plans, with volume pricing for Enterprise. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Team at $280, Business at $800 and Flex at $1 per 1,000 sessions + $0.2 per 1,000 API calls.
It lands 63 days after Resemble's previous change, "Resemble: New Team ($350/mo) & Business ($1,000/mo) tiers + 1 more change" (Jul 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Resemble AI: Watermarking expanded from audio-only to image, video & text" (Jul 4, 2026). Resemble has been tracked since 2023-11-21.
