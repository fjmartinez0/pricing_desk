What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Invoice Simple's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 'Request client reviews' row removed from plan comparison table; the 'Automate review requests on invoices' bullet was also dropped from the free trial feature list. In detail: Invoice Simple removed its 'Request client reviews' row from the 'See Why Invoice Simple is Rated #1' competitor comparison table (vs Jobber, Invoice2Go, Bookipi, Invoicera), and dropped the 'Automate review requests on invoices' bullet from the 14-day Premium free trial feature list. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $5.83, Plus at $12.50 and Premium at $18.33.
It lands 42 days after Invoice Simple's previous change, "Invoice Simple slashes Plus, Premium prices nearly 50% off monthly" (Sep 28, 2026). Invoice Simple has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
