What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of BigCommerce's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Growth, Scale and Performance support bullets now name express routing / priority support (Growth: express routing available; Scale: express routing or priority available; Performance: priority included); Core phone-support add-on 'coming soon' footnote removed; Scale and Performance taglines rewritten: from $1M-sales revenue thresholds to multi-channel scaling and enterprise integrations/SLAs. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change, positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $29, Growth at $79, Scale at $299 and Performance at $1,499.
It lands 152 days after BigCommerce's previous change, "BigCommerce rebrands plans, adds GMV overages and drops phone support" (Jun 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "BigCommerce Restructures Payment Rates and Switches to Monthly Pricing Display" (Apr 20, 2026) and "BigCommerce cuts payment processing fee from 2.89% to 2.59%" (Mar 16, 2026). BigCommerce has been tracked since 2026-02-20.
In the same window Shopify, Squarespace and Salesforce also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
