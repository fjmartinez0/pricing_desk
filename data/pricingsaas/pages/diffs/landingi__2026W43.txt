What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Landingi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Orbit MCP Server moved down from the Scale plan to the entry-level Build plan; Brand Kit added to Optimize; Lunar Skill.md fine-tuning feature removed from Enterprise plan's included-feature list. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Build at $24, Optimize at $119, Scale at $229, Enterprise at $1,199 and Extra domain at $5.
It lands 108 days after Landingi's previous change, "Landingi: Build tier visit limit cut 5,000→2,000, AI credits added" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Landingi's Scale plan gets a visits selector, 100,000 to 500,000 visits per month" (May 30, 2026), "Landingi drops legacy add-ons, slashes monthly visit limits up to 60%" (Apr 20, 2026) and "Landingi Caps Enterprise Visits at 1M+, Ending Unlimited Access" (Mar 24, 2026). Landingi has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Convertkit, Unbounce, Leadpages and Optimizely also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
