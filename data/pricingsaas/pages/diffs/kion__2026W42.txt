Kion makes Lux AI agent a paid add-on, ending free inclusion with base license
Verified Oct 12, 2026·Cloud & DevOps·Direction: up
The changeLux (In-App AI Agent), Beyond Cloud add-on — new add-on.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 5, 2026 → Oct 12, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Kion's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Add-Ons section lists Lux (in-app AI agent) and Beyond Cloud; FAQ now says both are an upcharge to the base license, whereas Lux was previously included at no cost. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Kion Platform at $3,333.33.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Kion. Kion has been tracked since 2026-05-02.
