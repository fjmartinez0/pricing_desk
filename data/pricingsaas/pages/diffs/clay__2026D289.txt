What happened
Between the Oct 15, 2026 and Oct 16, 2026 captures of Clay's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan feature list expanded: added User Groups, SCIM Provisioning, IP Allowlists, Credit Budgeting, Workflow and Workbook Limits, Connection Controls, Custom CSA and DPA; SSO now SSO/SAML. In detail: Enterprise plan adds new governance and security features: User Groups, SCIM Provisioning, IP Allowlists, Connection Controls, and Workflow and Workbook Limits (all new rows in the comparison table). SSO relabeled SSO/SAML; existing Workbook-level credit budget renamed Credit Budgeting and Custom CSA and DPA (already in the table) now also listed on the card. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Launch at $54 and Growth at $185.
It lands 39 days after Clay's previous change, "Clay: email sequencer access shifts from Free to Launch/Growth tiers" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Clay reshuffles email tiers: Free drops sequencer bullet, Growth adds domains" (Sep 2, 2026) and "Clay: Growth plan ad audiences now unlimited (was 1)" (Aug 10, 2026). Clay has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
