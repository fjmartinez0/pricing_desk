What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Accent's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Video meetings and conferencing (up to 30 participants) removed from all plans: Team bullet, plan taglines, intro copy, and the comparison table rows and section. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Team at $6.99, Pro at $14.99, Pro+ at $19.99, Premium at $24.99 and Cloud E-Fax Service at $34.95.
