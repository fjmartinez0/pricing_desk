What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Moxo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Moxo's Scale plan no longer discloses its flow/AI-credit allowance (500 flows/yr, $500 of AI/yr); the capacity line now shows only 'Unlimited seats' alongside the unchanged Custom quote CTA. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Team at $416.67.
It lands 7 days after Moxo's previous change, "Moxo drops monthly billing, Team plan now annual-only at $5,000/yr" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Moxo: Team/Scale plan usage caps cut, AI allowance switched to dollar-based" (Sep 14, 2026), "Moxo pulls AI agent workforce and automation from $500 Team plan" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Moxo locks Pro plan into annual billing, 10x'ing flow and AI credit caps" (Jul 13, 2026). Moxo has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
In the same window Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Copper and monday.com also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
