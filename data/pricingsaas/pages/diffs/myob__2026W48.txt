What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of MYOB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Scan and store receipts no longer restricted to the MYOB Assist App on Lite, Pro and Business plans; the comparison table cells no longer carry the 'Via MYOB Assist App' qualifier. In detail: Comparison table now shows Scan and store receipts as a plain checkmark on Lite, Pro and Business instead of the "Via MYOB Assist App" qualifier. Capability unchanged: the feature modal already offered Assist App snapping plus bill upload/email in both versions. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning).
It lands 35 days after MYOB's previous change, "MYOB adds Ask Iris conversational AI chat to plan comparison table" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MYOB flags Payroll price change from Nov 1, amounts not yet published" (Oct 4, 2026). MYOB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Xero, Sage, QuickBooks, Freshbooks and Zoho also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
