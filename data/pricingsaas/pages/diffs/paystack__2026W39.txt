What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Paystack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Google Pay added to the payment methods list, alongside Apple Pay, Visa QR, and Mobile Money. In detail: Google Pay added as a supported payment method, alongside Apple Pay, Visa QR, and Mobile Money. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at 1.5% per local card transaction (+ NGN 100, capped at NGN 2,000) + 3.9% per international card transaction (+ NGN 100) and Physical Terminal (SmartPeak P1000) at NGN 100,000 subscription + NGN 85,000 subscription.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Paystack. Paystack has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
