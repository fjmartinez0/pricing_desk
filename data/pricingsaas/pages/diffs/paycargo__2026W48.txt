What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Paycargo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Quick Pay per-transaction fee increased from $18.00 to $19.95; Account per-transaction fee increased from $14.50 to $15.95; Account annual fee per user decreased from $350.00 to $295.00; Quick Pay plan transaction limit increased from USD 2,500.00 to USD 5,000.00; Added 'Quick Approval for up to $5 Million through PayCargo Finance' to the Account and Enterprise plan feature lists. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit raised, feature added, price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists PayCargo Account at $295 and PayCargo Quick Pay at $19.95 per transaction + $9.95 per use.
It lands 110 days after Paycargo's previous change, "PayCargo: Account overnight limit can now be increased on request" (Aug 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "PayCargo: PayCargo Finance quick-approval financing added to Account, Enterprise" (Jul 4, 2026), "Paycargo Adds Hidden Fee Disclosure to Quick Pay Plan" (Apr 27, 2026) and "Paycargo cuts annual account fees by nearly 16% to $295 per user" (week of Feb 23, 2026). Paycargo has been tracked since 2023-11-18.
In the same window Shippo and X also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
