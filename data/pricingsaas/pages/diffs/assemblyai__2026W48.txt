What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Assemblyai's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pre-recorded flagship model Universal-3.5 Pro renamed AssemblyAI-STT-3.7; price unchanged at $0.21/hr; Flagship pre-recorded model now supports 32 languages (was 18) with automatic language detection and a new short-clip speaker diarization model. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, plan renamed). The current page lists Universal-2 at $0.15 per hour of audio, AssemblyAI-STT-3.7 at $0.21 per hour of audio, AssemblyAI-STT-3.7-Realtime at $0.30 per hour of audio, AssemblyAI-STT-3.7-Realtime-Agent at $0.45 per hour of audio and Sync API at $0.30 per hour of audio.
It lands 91 days after Assemblyai's previous change, "Assemblyai adds FAQ clarifying Voice Agent API's flat $4.50/hr rate" (Aug 23, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "AssemblyAI renames Universal-3.5 Pro, adds 18-language diarization support" (Jul 5, 2026), "Assemblyai upgrades realtime STT to Universal-3.5 with 18-language support" (Jun 29, 2026) and "Assemblyai unifies audio limits with expanded 333h free allowance" (Apr 9, 2026). Assemblyai has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
