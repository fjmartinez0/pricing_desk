What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Ably's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pricing moved from per-million usage rates (messages, channels, connections) to MAU-based tiers; Standard $29+usage became Pro $29 billed annually; Pro $399+usage became Business $349 billed annually; Business plan (successor to Pro) now includes SOC 2 report, HIPAA BAA, branded Ably subdomain and regional routing constraints (all previously Enterprise-only), plus guided onboarding; Enterprise now also lists Dedicated CSM and burst insurance. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $349 and Business at $349.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Ably. Ably has been tracked since 2026-09-09.
