What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Kajabi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The limited-time launch offer banner ($1,678 worth of launch support for $0 if you start today, with a live countdown) is gone after its stated Sept 15 expiration; the page now opens straight into the H1 headline with no promo banner. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $143, Growth at $199 and Pro at $399.
It lands 7 days after Kajabi's previous change, "Kajabi dangles $1,678 in free launch support before Sept 15 deadline" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kajabi: New AI Tools section added (Backstage, Expert Agents, AI Credits)" (Jul 3, 2026). Kajabi has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Podia, Thinkific, Leadpages and Convertkit also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
