What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Katteb's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free $0/Test Pro $29/Agency $99 (AI Credits) tiers replaced by Lite $19/Plus $99/Pro $199 (metered model-usage credits) AI chat/agents tiers. In detail: Katteb re-platformed from an AI content-writing suite (Free $0, Test Pro $29, Agency $99/mo, AI Credits) to an AI chat/agents platform (Lite $19, Plus $99, Pro $199/mo, model-usage credits). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Lite at $15.83, Business at $82.5 and Enterprise at $1,000.
It lands 132 days after Katteb's previous change, "Katteb slashes Pro Plan 41% to $17/month from $29" (May 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Katteb: Pro plan price cut ($29->$17/mo)" (Jul 13, 2026). Katteb has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
