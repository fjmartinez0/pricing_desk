What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Bird's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Email/SMS/WhatsApp/Voice/Realtime/Mailbox moved to a shared Bird balance wallet model; Apple Messages revealed $100/mo pricing and Mailbox gained a free tier (both were custom-quote only). In detail: Bird moved Email/SMS/WhatsApp/Voice/Realtime/Mailbox to a 'Bird balance' wallet model; Apple Messages revealed $100/mo pricing, Mailbox gained a free tier (both were custom-quote only). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Startup at $15, Growth at $250 and Scale at $1,000.
It lands 14 days after Bird's previous change, "Bird halves US local number rental to $0.50 a month" (Oct 5, 2026). Bird has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
