What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Blues' pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Billing clarification: only charged when consumption exceeds included amount and a billable Event; 'Backend Events' heading renamed 'Platform Events' with Event footnotes. No price change. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Essentials at $0 ingressed events and Enterprise at $1,000.
