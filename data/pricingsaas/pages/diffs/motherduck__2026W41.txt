What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of MotherDuck's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Flights pricing changed from a flat $0.60/hr rate to size-based tiers: F4 $0.15/hr, F16 $0.60/hr (Lite needs credit card), F32 $1.20/hr; Lite gets 30 min/day free on F4; Enterprise plan now lists Bring Your Own Compute (BYOC), available on request. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, add-on change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Business at $250 and Lite at $0.15 per F4 compute hour + $1 per AI unit.
It lands 18 days after MotherDuck's previous change, "MotherDuck: AWS Marketplace purchasing option added for Enterprise" (Sep 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MotherDuck adds cloud/support pricing toggle, Business RBAC custom roles, tab" (Aug 24, 2026), "MotherDuck adds IP allowlisting to Enterprise plan for network security" (Aug 17, 2026) and "MotherDuck: Giga instance price cut $36/hr → $24/hr" (Aug 11, 2026). MotherDuck has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window ClickHouse, Snowflake and Firebolt also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
