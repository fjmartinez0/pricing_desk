What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of DoorDash's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Premier plan no longer includes 35% off Commerce Platform Pro Package perk; Premier 'Order guarantee' renamed 'Commission Rebate Guarantee' (same 20-order commission refund); 'early' dropped from complimentary Sponsored Listing visibility perk. Pulse files it as a pricing change (positioning, discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Premier at 6% pickup commission + 30% delivery commission, Basic at 6% pickup commission + 15% delivery commission and Plus at 6% pickup commission + 25% delivery commission.
It lands 173 days after DoorDash's previous change, "DoorDash rolls out 0% intro commission for new restaurant partners" (May 12, 2026). DoorDash has been tracked since 2026-05-01.
In the same window Squarespace and Seamless.AI also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
