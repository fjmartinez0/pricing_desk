What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Cake Equity's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Add-ons section now lists QSBS ($500/yr), SPV ($500/yr) and Stock-based compensation ($500/yr), each included in Pro; 409A add-on stays $1,500/yr. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Build at $83.33, Team at $229.17, 409A Valuation Add-on at $125, QSBS Add-on at $41.67 and Special purpose vehicle (SPV) Add-on at $41.67.
It lands 56 days after Cake Equity's previous change, "Cake Equity's Pro plan quietly caps stakeholders at 100, then $60 each extra" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cake Equity: Custom plan adds Annual/Quarterly billing option" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Cake Equity: Plan taglines re-targeted to funding stages (Pre Seed, Series B+)" (Aug 3, 2026). Cake Equity has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
