What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Firefly's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Scale plan added at $5,499/mo billed annually (up to 25K assets); Enterprise now defined as 25K+ assets; free 14-day trial card replaced by a trial banner below plans; Essential plan asset limit cut from up to 20K assets to up to 10K assets at the same $2,499/mo price. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease, new plan) with a downward direction. The current page lists Essential at $2,499 and Scale at $5,499.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Firefly. Firefly has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
