SmoothStay adds annual billing to Host plan at $5.90/mo, drops per-unit price
Verified Oct 12, 2026·Collaboration & Productivity·Direction: neutral
The changeHost Plan, Free Starter Plan packaging — package change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 5, 2026 → Oct 12, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of SmoothStay's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Host plan rebuilt with an Annual/Monthly billing toggle defaulting to Annual: was $6.90/mo (monthly only, first unit) + $2.90/mo per extra unit; now $5.90/mo billed annually ($70.80/yr for 1 unit, '4 months free', saves $36/yr vs monthly, implying ~$8.90/mo monthly). Per-extra-unit price no longer shown; Free Starter Plan (already in the comparison table) now gets its own card. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Host Plan at $5.9.
It lands 7 days after SmoothStay's previous change, "SmoothStay swaps flat $6.90 rate for tiered pricing, adds $2.90 per extra unit" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SmoothStay renames volume-discount prompt to match Units metric label" (Sep 21, 2026) and "SmoothStay adds PMS integrations and webhooks to Host Plan" (Aug 17, 2026). SmoothStay has been tracked since 2026-03-03.
In the same window Guesty also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
