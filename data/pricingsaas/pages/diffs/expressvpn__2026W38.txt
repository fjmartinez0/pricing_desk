What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of ExpressVPN's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Advanced plan discount deepened from 73% to 76% ($4.49/mo to $3.99/mo) and Express Pro deepened from 67% to 73% ($7.49/mo to $5.99/mo) on the 2yr+4mo intro term; annual renewal rates unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Basic at $3.99, Advanced at $4.49 and Express Pro at $7.49.
It lands 13 days after ExpressVPN's previous change, "ExpressVPN swaps app-icon row for 2Yr/1Yr/1Mo billing tabs, 80% off" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ExpressVPN: Basic plan price cut $3.49->$2.99/mo + 2 more changes" (Aug 3, 2026), "ExpressVPN hikes 2-year promo pricing up to $2/mo, trims discounts to 67-76%" (Jul 27, 2026) and "ExpressVPN: New ExpressAI product launched + World Cup promo pricing + eSIM cap" (Jul 3, 2026). ExpressVPN has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window CyberGhost VPN, Surfshark and NordVPN also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
