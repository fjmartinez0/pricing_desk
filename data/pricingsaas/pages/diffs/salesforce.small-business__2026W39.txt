What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Salesforce's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: The 'CRM suites or products by role — you decide' tab selector was removed, eliminating the entire 'Products by role' path: Sales Cloud Enterprise, Service Cloud Enterprise, Marketing Cloud Next Growth Edition, Commerce Cloud B2B Growth Edition, and Slack Business+ are gone from the page; Starter Suite and Pro Suite card taglines were rewritten (copy-only; prices unchanged at $25/user/mo and $100. In detail: Starter Suite and Pro Suite card taglines were rewritten (copy-only; prices unchanged at $25/user/mo and $100/user/mo respectively). Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed, positioning) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter Suite at $25 and Pro Suite at $100.
It lands 14 days after Salesforce's previous change, "Salesforce Small Business axes Free Suite plan, leaves only Starter and Pro" (Sep 7, 2026). Salesforce has been tracked since 2025-01-03.
In the same window Microsoft, HubSpot, Zoho and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
