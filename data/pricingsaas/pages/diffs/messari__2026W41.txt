What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Messari's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Messari moved to Blockworks: Basic/Enterprise renamed Free/Pro ($4,500/yr now shown as $375/mo billed annually, Annual/Quarterly toggle); new Blockworks Data and Monitoring contact-sales plans; Pro adds Analyst Hours (monthly market analysis calls); Included APIs removed from paid plan and FAQ now says API access is a custom package. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pro at $375.
It lands 21 days after Messari's previous change, "Messari cuts Enterprise Individual plan price 10% to $4,500/year" (Sep 14, 2026). Messari has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window Prisync, Vizly, Precog, PostHog and Mixpanel also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
