What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Skyvern's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan (5,000 credits, $0/mo) removed; Hobby and Pro now start with a 14-day trial of 30,000 credits, replacing free tier.
