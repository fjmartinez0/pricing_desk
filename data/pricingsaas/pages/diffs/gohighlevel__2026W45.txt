HighLevel renames $497/mo Agency Pro to Founder, strips SaaS Mode and API
Verified Nov 2, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral
The changeFounder plan — plan renamed.
2 changes·2 captures·Oct 26, 2026 → Nov 2, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of HighLevel's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Agency Pro plan ($497/mo, $4970/yr) renamed to Founder at the same price, with a new tagline aimed at founders, agencies and consultants; Founder (formerly Agency Pro) feature list changed: SaaS Mode, automated sub-account creation, user/agent reporting and advanced API removed; Founders Club, HL Partner Network and Most Free AI added. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed, feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $970, Unlimited at $2,970, Founder at $4,970, HIPAA Compliance at $297 and AI Employee Growth Plan at $50.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for HighLevel. HighLevel has been tracked since 2026-06-27.
In the same window Benchmarkemail, OptiMonk, Yext, Whatagraph and Vyond also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
