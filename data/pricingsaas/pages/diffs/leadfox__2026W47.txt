What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of LeadFox's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 'Support in less than 30 seconds' removed from the Help and support list of features included in all Leadfox plans. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 17, 2026) lists Fennec at $59.96, Arctic at $119.96 and Red at $252.96.
