What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Kontentino's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Client/external approvals moved from Standard into Starter: Starter's 'Internal approval workflow' became 'Internal and external/client approvals'; the Standard-only bullet was removed. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at EUR 49, Standard at EUR 109, Pro at EUR 199, Global Content Manager Add-on at EUR 100 and Analytics Add-on at EUR 54.
It lands 115 days after Kontentino's previous change, "Kontentino: replaced Starter+ tier with new Pro tier for agencies" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kontentino overhauls pricing, adds Pro plan at €199/mo" (May 13, 2026) and "Kontentino replaces pricing calculator with AI chatbot referrals" (Apr 6, 2026). Kontentino has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
