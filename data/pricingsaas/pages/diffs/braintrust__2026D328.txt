What happened
Between the Nov 23, 2026 and Nov 24, 2026 captures of Braintrust's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Renamed billing-credit unit from "Model credits" to "Braintrust credits" across plans, feature table, and docs; added 3 new FAQ entries about credits. Added new "Patterns" row to the feature comparison table (AI pattern detection across traces), billed via Braintrust credits; Loop agent's description updated to note it also uses Braintrust credits. In detail: Billing unit renamed 'Model credits' to 'Braintrust credits' across plan cards and feature table; added FAQs on what credits are, running out of credits, and Loop/Patterns usage with your own key; New 'Patterns' feature added to the comparison table (uses Braintrust credits to surface recurring trace issues); Loop agent's description now also notes it uses Braintrust credits. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change, feature added). The current page lists Pro at $249.
It lands 113 days after Braintrust's previous change, "Braintrust: token overage rates hidden across all plans" (Aug 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Braintrust discloses Enterprise Topics token overage rates" (Jul 26, 2026), "Braintrust ends flat 30-day data cap, adds paid retention extension at…" (Jul 20, 2026) and "Braintrust offers startups 6-12 months free on Pro plan" (Jul 5, 2026). Braintrust has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window Kontentino and Upwork also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
