What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Apptile's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Features exclusive to the discontinued Pro plan (300+ Design Tiles, Multi Language & Currency, Picture-in-Picture, Unlimited Standard integrations) are now included in the renamed base Plus plan; New 'Move to Apptile' migration add-ons introduced: Data Migration $2,999, Data+Media Migration $4,999, User Migration $4,999, plus Custom Development at $50/hr ($500 minimum package). Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, feature change) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Plus at $539 and Enterprise at $1,079.
It lands 34 days after Apptile's previous change, "Apptile: New Monthly Active Users usage metric introduced" (Aug 31, 2026). Apptile has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Zapier, Workato, Make and MuleSoft also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
