What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Strike Graph's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Certify plan's published price ('Starting at $10,000/yr') was replaced with 'Inquire below' on the hero card, and the '$10,000/year' figure was removed from every row of the plan comparison table, while Scale ($21,500) and Enterprise ($35,000) prices remain publicly shown. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Scale at $1,791.67, Enterprise at $2,916.67, Advanced Features Bundle at $500, AI Credits Pro at $833.33 and AI Credits Standard at $541.67.
It lands 35 days after Strike Graph's previous change, "Strike Graph: Plan card feature lists changed: Scale gained Atlas AI and MCP server access but lost Single Sign-On (SSO)…" (Aug 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Strike Graph: CMMC framework added, Certify plan adds self-serve trial" (Aug 11, 2026). Strike Graph has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
In the same window TradingView, Lightspeed and Interactive Brokers also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
