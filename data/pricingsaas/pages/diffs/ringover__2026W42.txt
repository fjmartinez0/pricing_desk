What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Ringover's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: TALK rose from $15/$24 to $19/$29 and BUSINESS from $47/$57 to $49/$69 per user/month; ADVANCED replaced by new UNIFIED ($69/$89) and ENTERPRISE (custom, 50+ users) plans. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists TALK at EUR 20, BUSINESS at EUR 49, Team Activity Analytics at EUR 5, Empower at EUR 39 and Omnichannel Messaging at EUR 29.
It lands 69 days after Ringover's previous change, "Ringover: TALK & BUSINESS plans add 3-user purchase minimum" (Aug 4, 2026). Ringover has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Aircall and Twilio also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
