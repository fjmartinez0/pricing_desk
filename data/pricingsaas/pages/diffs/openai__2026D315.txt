What happened
Between the Nov 10, 2026 and Nov 11, 2026 captures of OpenAI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: All four plan taglines reworded from feature-benefit phrasing to use-case framing: Free, Go, Plus, and Pro each got new 'Best for …' positioning text under their pricing cards. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Go at $8, Plus at $20, Business at $20 and Pro at $100.
It lands 2 days after OpenAI's previous change, "OpenAI API firms up GPT-5.6 Sol discount deadline: Nov. 30, 2026" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenAI: all four ChatGPT plan taglines rewritten to 'Best for...' framing" (Nov 8, 2026), "OpenAI refreshes Free, Go, Plus and Pro taglines with benefit-led copy" (Nov 7, 2026) and "OpenAI: Pro plan adds up to $500/month in API credits" (Oct 30, 2026). OpenAI has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Anthropic, X, Metabase, Microsoft and Google also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
