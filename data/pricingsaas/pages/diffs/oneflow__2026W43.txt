What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Oneflow's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Business plan no longer shows 'Starting at €250/month, billed annually'; card now only offers 'Request Demo' (previously 'Book a demo'). Enterprise 'Custom quote' and 'Contact sales' also replaced by Request Demo; Enterprise tagline changed from 'ready to run contracts as a core business system across regions, processes, and teams' to 'The highest level of contract intelligence, security, and cus. In detail: Enterprise tagline changed from 'ready to run contracts as a core business system across regions, processes, and teams' to 'The highest level of contract intelligence, security, and customization'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden, positioning) with a downward direction.
It lands 21 days after Oneflow's previous change, "Oneflow moves AI Review & Insights to paid add-on, adds AI Insights Premium" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Oneflow drops multiple workspaces from Business plan features" (Sep 21, 2026). Oneflow has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Pandadoc, signNow and You.com also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
