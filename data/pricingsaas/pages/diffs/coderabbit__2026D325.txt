What happened
Between the Nov 20, 2026 and Nov 21, 2026 captures of CodeRabbit's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added Triage Rules (Team+) and Security Merge Blocking (Advanced+); Essentials loses Change Stack Chat and Agent access; AI Engineer renamed Agent, Autofix renamed Fix Comments. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 23, 2026) lists Essentials at $24, Team at $48, Advanced at $72, Usage-based reviews at $0.25 per reviewed file and CodeRabbit Agent at $0.40 per agent minute.
It lands 3 days after CodeRabbit's previous change, "CodeRabbit includes API and MCP server access on every plan now" (Nov 18, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CodeRabbit renames Agent to CodeRabbit Engineer, drops $0.40/min from card" (Oct 10, 2026) and "CodeRabbit reframes Advanced around architectural insights (copy only)" (Sep 17, 2026). CodeRabbit has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
In the same window GitLab, Replit, GitHub, Windsurf and JetBrains also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
