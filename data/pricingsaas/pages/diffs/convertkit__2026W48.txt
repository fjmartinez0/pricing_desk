What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Convertkit's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Black Friday offer: 50% off first 3 months of monthly Creator plan ($39 to $20/mo), ends Dec 1, for accounts up to 500k subscribers. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Creator at $33 and Pro at $66.
It lands 42 days after Convertkit's previous change, "Convertkit offers extra 25% off Pro yearly and adds Growth Concierge" (Oct 11, 2026). Convertkit has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Getresponse, ActiveCampaign, Klaviyo, Mailchimp and beehiiv also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
