What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of ReviewWave's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The Aloha Voice add-on ($20/extension per month, with calling, VoIP desk phone, call routing, eFax, analytics, and HIPAA compliance features) was removed from Additional Enhancements, leaving only AI Website. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Activate at $299, Elevate at $499, Dominate at $699 and AI Website at $199.
It lands 69 days after ReviewWave's previous change, "ReviewWave: Elevate plan swaps After Hours AI Attendant for Missed Call Text" (Aug 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ReviewWave (Aloha): Annual/Monthly billing toggle removed" (Aug 4, 2026). ReviewWave has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window Google, G2 and Trustpilot also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
