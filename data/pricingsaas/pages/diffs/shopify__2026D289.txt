What happened
Between the Oct 15, 2026 and Oct 16, 2026 captures of Shopify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Yearly-billed prices fell: Grow $89 to $79/mo and Advanced $359 to $299/mo; Basic $29 unchanged. Reverses the D288 hike, consistent with an A/B experiment arm flipping back. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 23, 2026) lists Basic at $29, Grow at $79, Advanced at $299, Plus at $2,300 and POS Pro at $89.
It lands one day after Shopify's previous change, "Shopify raises annual Grow to $89/mo and Advanced to $359/mo" (Oct 15, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shopify Plus drops 1% sales cashback, removing the $15K cap from its table" (Oct 2, 2026), "Shopify: Plus payment processing rates now publicly disclosed" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Shopify Plus unmasks exact payment-processing fees across 9 rate rows" (Sep 15, 2026). Shopify has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Salesforce and Squarespace also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
