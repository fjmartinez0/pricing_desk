What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Gate's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: VIP fee table column renamed from 'Alpha Trading Fee' to 'Trenches Trading Fee'. In detail: Alpha (now Trenches) trading fee cut from 0.8% to 0.5% across all VIP levels (VIP0–VIP16); spot fee table column renamed from "Alpha Trading Fee" to "Trenches Trading Fee". Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists VIP3 at $0.1 spot maker fee, VIP5 at $0.09 spot maker fee + $0.1 spot taker fee, VIP10 at $0.06 spot GT taker fee + $0.04 spot maker fee, VIP11 at $0.8 alpha trading fee + $0.03 spot maker fee and VIP16 at $0.8 alpha trading fee + $0.02 spot GT taker fee.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Gate. Gate has been tracked since 2026-06-27.
