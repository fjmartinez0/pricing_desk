What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Personio's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Standard Work Schedules limit in the CorePro column raised from 600 to 3,000 work schedules in the plan comparison table (Core unchanged at 600); Footnote below the plan comparison table stating that annual subscriptions paid upfront get a 10% discount on software services was removed, along with the 'Looking for Non-profit Solutions?' link. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit increase, discount removed) with an upward direction.
It lands 198 days after Personio's previous change, "Personio: eSignature cap replaced by limits and Compare Plans table restructured" (Apr 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Personio Increases Annual Subscription Discount to 10% and Introduces Document Limit on Core Plan" (Q1 2025). Personio has been tracked since 2024-04-01.
In the same window Paychex, BambooHR and SAP SE also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
