What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of DataCamp's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Course catalog access increased from 790+ to 810+ courses on Premium and Teams plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Teams at $14 and Premium at $28.
It lands 7 days after DataCamp's previous change, "DataCamp ends Special Price promo, doubling Premium and Teams to $28/mo" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "DataCamp adds 50% Teams promo ($14/user/mo, list $28), swaps Premium $1 intro for $14/mo annual deal" (Oct 12, 2026) and "DataCamp Premium drops to $1 for first month in new promo" (Oct 5, 2026). DataCamp has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
In the same window Udemy, Coursera and Codecademy also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
