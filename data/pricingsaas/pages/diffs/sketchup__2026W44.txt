What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Sketchup's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 3D Warehouse downloads capped: Go/Essential 250 daily & 2,500 monthly, Pro 1,500 & 15,000, Studio unlimited daily & 25,000 monthly; previously unlimited downloads on all. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Student at $4.58, Educator at $4.58, Go at $10.75, Pro at $33.25 and Pro Scan at $41.58.
It lands 105 days after Sketchup's previous change, "SketchUp: Go Monthly ($19.99) and Pro Monthly ($99.99) plans discontinued" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SketchUp: added Pro Monthly plan ($99.99/mo) + Pro Scan/Advanced tiers gate Scan & Revit" (Jul 4, 2026), "Sketchup raises Pro and Studio annual prices 25% after promo removal" (Jun 22, 2026) and "Sketchup launches Pro Scan and Pro Advanced subscription bundles" (Apr 24, 2026). Sketchup has been tracked since 2023-11-23.
In the same window Brightspot, ON1, Mobbin, Mailtrap and LearnDash also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
