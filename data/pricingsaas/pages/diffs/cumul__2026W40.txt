What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Luzmo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The single all-in plan's USD price rose from $1,995/month to $2,495/month (billed annually, a 25% hike). The EUR price stayed at €1,995/month, so only the USD-denominated price increased. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $995 and Premium at $2,495.
It lands 14 days after Luzmo's previous change, "Luzmo unlocks Warp data acceleration and 500 monthly AI chats" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Luzmo: Starter plan AI conversations quota changed from 10k to Custom" (Aug 3, 2026) and "Luzmo: Starter price up 101% to $995/mo, Premium up 25% to $2,495/mo" (Jul 27, 2026). Luzmo has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Metabase, Grafana, Tableau and Qlik also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
