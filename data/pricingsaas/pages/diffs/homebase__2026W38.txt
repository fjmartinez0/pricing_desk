What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Homebase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Job post boosts add-on dropped from $79/post to $50/post. Hiring Assistant add-on no longer requires annual billing (was "$99/month, billed annually", now "Starting at $99/month"). In detail: Job post boosts add-on price decreased from $79 to $50 per post; Hiring Assistant add-on no longer requires annual billing (was "$99/month, billed annually", now "Starting at $99/month"). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Essentials at $24, Plus at $56, All-in-One at $96, Background Checks at $30 subscription and Tip Manager at $25.
It lands 7 days after Homebase's previous change, "Homebase Hiring Assistant exits Beta, ditches per-post fee for $99/mo flat rate" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Homebase: Payroll add-on fee decreased ($49→$39/mo)" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Homebase pulls "3 months free payroll" promo banner as offer expires" (Jul 20, 2026).
