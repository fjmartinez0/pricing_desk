What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Evoto's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New limited-time offer (11/13-11/22): $180 kits plus 3 months Asset Hub free (Christmas, Authentic, backdrops). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter Plan at $7.42, Basic Plan at $12.42, Basic Plus Plan at $20.17, Standard Plan at $43.42 and Standard Plus Plan at $100.42.
It lands 7 days after Evoto's previous change, "Evoto ends Halloween promo: free Preset Pack and Asset Hub perk removed, 10% off stays" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Evoto bundles free Halloween presets and AI Asset Hub with every plan" (Oct 26, 2026), "Evoto adds 10% annual promo, cutting Starter to $80 and Basic to $134" (Oct 11, 2026) and "Evoto: Asset Hub Membership added to all annual plans" (Sep 28, 2026). Evoto has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
