What happened
Between the Nov 3, 2026 and Nov 4, 2026 captures of Cohere's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Model Vault pricing restructured: per-instance Performance Tier plans (Embed/Rerank/Parse, $3-$7/hr) replaced with per-GPU on-demand rates (L4 to GB200, $1-$12.50/hr) plus promo pricing thru Mar 2027. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Embed 5 Fast at $0.08 Input tokens, Embed 5 Pro at $0.12 Input tokens, Parse 5 at $1.50 Pages, Command R7B at $0.15 Output tokens + $3.75 Input tokens (per 100M tokens) and Rerank 5 Fast at $0.02 Rerank 5 tokens (per 1M tokens).
It lands 9 days after Cohere's previous change, "Cohere: Model Vault Rerank 4 Fast/Pro Medium renamed Rerank 5 and cut from $5.00/hr ($3,250/mo) to $4.00/hr…" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Cohere cuts Model Vault Embed pricing 25% with new Embed 5 Fast and Pro tiers" (Oct 5, 2026), "Cohere cuts Model Vault Small to $3/hr as Embed 5 replaces Embed 4" (Oct 1, 2026) and "Cohere adds Parse 5 doc-parsing model, Medium $4/hr and XL $7/hr tiers" (Aug 31, 2026). Cohere has been tracked since 2024-12-01.
In the same window OpenAI, Anthropic, Metabase, Together AI and Google also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
