What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Pulumi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan pricing revealed: 'Custom / Contact sales' replaced with self-serve $2,000/month base (2,000 Credits, up to 4,750 managed resources) plus published on-demand rates for extra resources and secrets. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $40, Pro at $400, Standard Support at $625, Bronze Support at $1,250 and Silver Support at $2,916.67.
It lands 7 days after Pulumi's previous change, "Pulumi renames all plan tiers, adds Support tiers up to $50K/yr Platinum" (Sep 21, 2026). Pulumi has been tracked since 2026-05-02.
In the same window Google Cloud and Hashicorp also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
