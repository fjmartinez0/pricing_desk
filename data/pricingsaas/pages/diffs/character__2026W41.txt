What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Character.AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Basic chat styles updated: PipSqueak 2 and PipSqueak 2 Rawr replaced by PipSqueak 3 and ShortSqueak; prices unchanged ($119.88 vs $94.99/year).
