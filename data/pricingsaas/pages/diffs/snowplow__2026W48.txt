What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Snowplow's pricing page, Pulse recorded 5 changes: New Your cloud (Private Managed Cloud) plan: Snowplow runs the pipeline inside your own AWS, GCP or Azure account, with SLAs, optional cross-region outage protection and VPC peering. Custom quote; Snowplow Platform (Fully Managed & Scalable) renamed Snowplow-hosted, now badged Recommended. Still custom quote; Self-Hosted Pipeline card for previous open source users removed from the plan grid. Th. In detail: Self-Hosted Pipeline card for previous open source users removed from the plan grid. The SLULA 1.1 self-hosted licence is still offered, now only in the FAQ; Signals free tier now listed: runs on the hosted trial environment with no end date, up to 5 million events a month; 14-day free trial no longer full product. Event compression, Data Product Studio, managed auto-scaling, pipeline observability, SLAs, surge protection, technical services, advanced governance and CVE patches are now paid-plan only; Signals is limited on the trial. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan, plan renamed, positioning, freemium change, trial change).
It lands 153 days after Snowplow's previous change, "Snowplow drops free Community Edition for 14-day trial" (Jun 22, 2026). Snowplow has been tracked since 2026-05-22.
In the same window Mixpanel and Amplitude also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
