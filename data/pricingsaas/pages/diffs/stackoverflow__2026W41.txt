What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Stack Overflow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing page went from a 'Contact us for pricing' lead form to public Starter (Free, 1,000 credits/mo, 3 collaborators) and Organization (Contact sales) plans.
