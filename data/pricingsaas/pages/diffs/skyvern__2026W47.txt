What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Skyvern's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise concurrency limit raised from 100 concurrent runs to unlimited concurrent agents; Enterprise adds SAML SSO, role-based access control, SOC-2 Type 2, Dedicated Agent Phone and Email Inbox; Bitwarden integration dropped from the list. In detail: Enterprise card adds SAML SSO, role-based access control, SOC-2 Type 2 compliance (was SOC-2 Report), Dedicated Agent Phone and Dedicated Agent Email Inbox. Bitwarden integration is no longer listed on the Enterprise card but remains in the comparison table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, limit raised). The current page lists Hobby at $29 and Pro at $149.
It lands 14 days after Skyvern's previous change, "Skyvern scraps free plan, moving Hobby and Pro to 30,000-credit 14-day trials" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Skyvern adds $10/mo SMS number add-on to Pro, between 2FA and 1Password" (Sep 28, 2026). Skyvern has been tracked since 2026-09-09.
In the same window Cypress.io, Puppet, UiPath, Zapier and Make also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
