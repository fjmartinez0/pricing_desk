What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Picsart's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pro yearly-billed price rose $9.45 to $10.5/mo; Ultra $54 to $60/mo; Ultra 10% intro discount and 10% off Pro banner removed; Limited-time promo: free unlimited WAN 3.0 generations for Pro and Ultra subscribers until Oct 31 (excludes API, CLI, MCP). In detail: Extra 10% off promo ended: Pro yearly-billed price returned from $9.45 to $10.5/mo and Ultra from $54 to $60/mo (Ultra "Intro price: 10% off" badge and Pro 10% off countdown banner removed); strikethrough list prices $15/$75 and credits unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed, discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $10.5 and Ultra at $60.
It lands 7 days after Picsart's previous change, "Picsart launches limited-time 10% off Pro and Ultra, grows AI catalog to 200+ models" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Picsart Pro and Ultra list Kling 3.0 4K, Seedance 2.5 access with output caps" (Oct 5, 2026), "Picsart switches Seedance 2.5 quota from video count to seconds" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Picsart swaps flat daily AI limits for credit caps up to 5000 images" (Sep 7, 2026). Picsart has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
