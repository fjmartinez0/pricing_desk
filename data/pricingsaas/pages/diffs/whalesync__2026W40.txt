What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Whalesync's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Starter added Asana, Mailchimp, Sanity connectors; Plus added Close, Klaviyo, Zoho CRM; Stripe restricted to one-way sync; new 'Live Export connectors' feature category introduced. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Starter at $40, Plus at $80 and Pro at $200.
It lands 7 days after Whalesync's previous change, "Whalesync: free trial now needs no credit card" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Whalesync: free trial cut from 2 weeks to 7 days on all plans" (Aug 24, 2026), "Whalesync: Personal plan dropped, Starter $20→$40 & Plus $40→$80 (+1 more)" (Aug 10, 2026) and "Whalesync: Plus & Pro record sync limit increased 50,000 → 1,000,000" (Jul 27, 2026). Whalesync has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Zapier, Workato, Make and MuleSoft also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
