What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Peel Insights' pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Free trial extended from 7 days to 30 days on all paid plans (Core, Essentials, Accelerate); still no credit card required; New Bundle Offer: Peel is free for as long as a customer runs two or more Relay Commerce apps (SalesPop, Fomo, Relo, SmartrMail, SavedBy, Smartrr). List prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added, trial change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $179, Essentials at $449 and Accelerate at $809.
It lands 201 days after Peel Insights' previous change, "Peel Insights drops $179 Core plan, entry tier jumps to $449 Essentials" (May 5, 2026). Peel Insights has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
