What happened
Between the Sep 25, 2026 and Sep 26, 2026 captures of Airtable's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added a new 'Automations per base' limit of 50 automations per base to the plan comparison table, applied uniformly across Free, Team, Business, and Enterprise Scale plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction.
Airtable's previous recorded change was "Airtable Discontinues ProductCentral Add-on and Changes Enterprise Scale Portals Pricing to 'Contact Sales'" (Q3 2025). Before that Pulse recorded "Airtable Discontinues AI Add-on, Bundles AI Credits into All Plans, Enhancing Value for Users" (Q2 2025), "Airtable Introduces Portals Add-On for Guest User Access with New Pricing on Team, Business, and Enterprise Plans" (Q1 2025) and "Airtable Introduces HyperDB Feature for Enterprise Scale Plan, Enhancing Database Performance and Scalability" (Q3 2024). Airtable has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
