What happened
Between the Nov 12, 2026 and Nov 13, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Dedicated Inference on-demand GPU pricing — HGX B200 $8.99→$9.29/hr; HGX H200, B300, GB300 NVL72 now priced ($5.99, $11.49, $15.79/hr) vs Contact us. GPU Clusters HGX B200/B300 rates increased across Preemptible/On-Demand/Reserved tiers; B300 Reserved tiers revealed ($10.59, $9.89/hr vs Sold Out) Added new serverless inference model 'Inkling 2 Small' at $0.30/1M input ($0.03 cached) / $1.00/1M output tokens. In detail: Dedicated Inference on-demand GPU pricing: HGX B200 increased $8.99→$9.29/hr; HGX H200, B300 and GB300 NVL72 now show on-demand prices ($5.99, $11.49, $15.79/hr) vs 'Contact us' (Reserved tier stays 'Contact sales' for all). GPU Clusters HGX B200/B300 rates increased across Preemptible/On-Demand/Reserved tiers; B300 Reserved tiers revealed ($10.59, $9.89/hr vs Sold Out); Added new serverless inference model 'Inkling 2 Small' at $0.30/1M input tokens ($0.03 cached), $1.00/1M output tokens. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100), GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days), Sandbox (Code Sandbox) at $0.04 per vCPU/hour + $0.01 per GiB RAM/hour and Serverless Inference at $0.15 per 1M input tokens.
It lands 2 days after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI adds US geo-pinned models, Kimi K3 US at $3.75/$18.75, live GPU status" (Nov 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI raises Qwen3.7-Max inference prices 67%, drops four serverless models" (Nov 7, 2026), "Together AI adds Kimi K3.1 Pro at $2/$10 per 1M tokens, plus Ripple-65B Preview" (Nov 5, 2026) and "Together AI: Four serverless inference models dropped from the pricing list: Gemma 4 31B, DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731, Llama…" (Oct 26, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
