What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Supermetrics's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Campaign Management (create, launch and manage ad campaigns from AI chat) added to the plan feature lists on all three paid plan cards; Monthly AI credit allowances under Supermetrics Studio removed from plan cards (4,000 / 12,000 / custom credits no longer listed). In detail: Monthly AI credit allowances under Supermetrics Studio no longer listed on plan cards (4,000 Starter / 12,000 Growth / custom Enterprise); FAQ still says Studio plans include a set AI credit allowance, so the amounts are undisclosed rather than confirmed cut. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added, positioning) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $44 and Growth at $177.
It lands 28 days after Supermetrics's previous change, "Supermetrics renames Management API access to Platform API access" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Supermetrics drops Data API & MCP access from Starter and Growth" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Supermetrics strips automated refresh, dashboards from Starter, Growth…" (Sep 14, 2026). Supermetrics has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Mixpanel, Google, DAT Freight & Analytics and Amplitude also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
