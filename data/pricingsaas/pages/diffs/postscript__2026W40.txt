What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Postscript's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Postscript AI Plan pricing revealed ($699/mo, $0.003-$0.30/msg); new Email add-on teased via waitlist; Starter/Growth removed struck-through 'was' SMS rate comparisons; Enterprise plan swapped 'Onboarding Launch Specialist' and 'Priority live chat support' for 'A dedicated Customer Success Manager' and 'Option to add RCS'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed, feature change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Growth at $100, Professional at $500, Starter at $0.05 per MMS + $0.01 per SMS and Postscript AI Plan at $699.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Postscript. Postscript has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
