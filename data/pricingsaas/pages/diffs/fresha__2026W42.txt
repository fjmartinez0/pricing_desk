What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Fresha's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Chat support (typical response 2 minutes) and phone support (instant) now listed as standard customer support; email response changed from 24 hours to 2 days. In detail: Premium support add-on (Phone, chat, and Email support; $14.95/mo on Independent, free on Team) removed. Support table now lists Email, Chat support and Phone support rows: phone support is Team-only (Independent shows "-"), and typical email response slowed from 24 hours to 2 days. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Team at $14.95, Independent at $19.95, Smart Website at $15.95, AI Concierge at $99.95 and Client Loyalty at $59.95.
It lands 35 days after Fresha's previous change, "Fresha launches Smart website add-on at $15.95/month for all plans" (Sep 7, 2026). Fresha has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Acuity Scheduling and Calendly also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
