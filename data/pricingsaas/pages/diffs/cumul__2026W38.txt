What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Luzmo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Warp' data-acceleration capacity of 100 million rows and a quantified allowance of 500 AI conversations per month are now disclosed as part of the restructured plan; neither existed before. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Starter at $995 and Premium at $2,495.
It lands 42 days after Luzmo's previous change, "Luzmo: Starter plan AI conversations quota changed from 10k to Custom" (Aug 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Luzmo: Starter price up 101% to $995/mo, Premium up 25% to $2,495/mo" (Jul 27, 2026). Luzmo has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Metabase, Grafana, Tableau and Qlik also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
