What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of airSlate's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: altaFlow (airSlate) now discloses pricing on its plan cards: Standard shows a concrete 'Starting at $1.40 per document' rate, while Advanced and Enterprise show 'Priced to volume Custom rate' - all three previously showed only a tagline with no pricing cue. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Growth at $399 and Automate 360 at $799.
It lands 42 days after airSlate's previous change, "airSlate adds PDF generation to Standard, refines DocGen and Enterprise terms" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "airSlate shifts credit-based plans to quote-only pricing, adds HIPAA support" (Aug 24, 2026). airSlate has been tracked since 2026-02-25.
