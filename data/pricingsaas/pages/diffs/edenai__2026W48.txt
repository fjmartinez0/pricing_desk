What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Edenai's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Advanced AI Platform enterprise bullet expanded from 'Private deployments / specific compliance needs' to 'Dedicated data region, private deployments & compliance needs'. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change).
Edenai's previous recorded change was "Edenai Adds 5.5% Platform Fee to Self-Serve AI Gateway Tier" (week of Jan 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Edenai rebrands both plans with new product-focused names" (week of Jan 5, 2026), "Edenai Removes Key Features from Custom AI API Plan While Enhancing Universal AI API with New Functionalities" (Q2 2025) and "Edenai Transforms Pricing Structure: Consolidates Enterprise Solutions into Premium Tier and Introduces Formal Three-Tier Model" (Q1 2025). Edenai has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Huggingface, IBM, Anthropic, OpenAI and Google Cloud also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
