Datadog replaces free tier with contact-sales Basic plan, 15-month retention
Verified Nov 2, 2026·Cloud & DevOps·Direction: neutral
The changeFree tier — freemium change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 26, 2026 → Nov 2, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Datadog's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free tier ($0, up to 5 hosts, 1-day retention) replaced by a Basic tier with contact-sales pricing, 15-month retention, no container monitoring; Basic hosts can mix with Pro/Enterprise. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Infrastructure Pro at $15, Infrastructure DevSecOps Pro at $22, Infrastructure Enterprise at $23, Infrastructure DevSecOps Enterprise at $34 and Session Replay at $2.50.
It lands the same day as Datadog's previous change, "Datadog drops Confluent Cloud from Kafka Console support" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Datadog drops free Infrastructure plan, adds contact-sales Basic tier" (Oct 28, 2026), "Datadog Cloud SIEM extends security signal retention to 15 months at no cost" (Oct 12, 2026) and "Datadog bundles on-prem Sensitive Data Scanner at no…" (Oct 12, 2026). Datadog has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Sumologic, Dynatrace, Elastic and Grafana also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
