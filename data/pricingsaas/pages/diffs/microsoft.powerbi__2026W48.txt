What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Microsoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Power BI apps added to the comparison table: 'Publish apps to share and collaborate' (Pro), plus 'Create apps in Power BI Desktop', 'Connect app to existing Fabric lakehouse, warehouse, SQL database, and semantic models' and 'Includes an app database (PostgreSQL)' (Free and Pro); Pro card copy now reads 'Publish and share reports and apps in Power BI'. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Power BI Pro at $14 and Power BI Premium Per User at $24.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Microsoft. Microsoft has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
In the same window IBM, Salesforce and Google also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
