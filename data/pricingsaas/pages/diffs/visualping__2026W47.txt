What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Visualping's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Solutions support tier renamed from 'Dedicated premium support' to 'Advanced support'; new 'Extra email address in CC' alert feature on Personal 5K and above, Business and Solutions. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Starter at $10, Personal 5k at $25, Personal 10k at $50, Business 20k at $100 and Business 30k at $150.
It lands 21 days after Visualping's previous change, "Visualping opens bulk import and editing to all plans, not just Business" (Oct 26, 2026). Visualping has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window SimplePractice, Zingtree, Shiprocket, Printify and WebPT also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
