What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Seam's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Per-device pricing (Unit Access $5, High Traffic $50, Free Trial, Enterprise) replaced by monthly credit packages: Hobbyist $10/1K, Pro $96/10K, Scale $230/25K credits, plus a free start tier; Support Plans table (Growth $0, Premium $20,000/yr, Enterprise $50,000/yr with Slack channel and Sev SLAs) removed from the page. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on removed, package change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Hobbyist at $10, Pro at $10 and Scale at $10.
It lands 105 days after Seam's previous change, "Seam: Credit-based pricing replaced with flat Free/$5/$50 device tiers" (Jul 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Seam swaps Unlimited for metered caps, drops calculator in" (Jul 20, 2026). Seam has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Viam, Trunk, testRigor, Syncfusion and Sonar also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
