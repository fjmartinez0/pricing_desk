What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of LearnDash's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Essentials plan ($259/year, for solo instructors) removed; pricing now shows only Pro ($399) and Elite ($599), with Essentials features folded into Pro card as Core features. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $33.25 and Elite at $49.92.
It lands 108 days after LearnDash's previous change, "LearnDash: pricing overhauled to unified Essentials/Pro/Elite SaaS tiers" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LearnDash drops white-glove onboarding promise from Elite plan" (Jun 22, 2026). LearnDash has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Udemy, Kajabi and Thinkific also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
