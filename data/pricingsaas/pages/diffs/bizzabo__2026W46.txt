What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Bizzabo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Event Experience OS now publicly shows $499/user/month (billed annually, 3-user minimum) and $17,999/year starting price, previously shown as Talk to Sales only; 'Branding (Private-labeling)' added to the Event Experience OS included feature list. In detail: Added 'Branding (Private-labeling)' as an included line item in the Event Experience OS plan's feature list; Event Experience OS plan now publicly shows pricing ($499/user/month billed annually, 3 user minimum; starting at $17,999/year) above the Talk to Sales CTA, previously shown with no price at all. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Event Experience OS at $499.
It lands 209 days after Bizzabo's previous change, "Bizzabo renames its Virtual Production Suite add-on to Virtual Event Experience" (Apr 13, 2026). Bizzabo has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
