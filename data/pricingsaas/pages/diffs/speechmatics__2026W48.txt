What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Speechmatics' pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pre-recorded PII redaction add-on now shows $0.09/hr instead of 'On request', and is now available on Enhanced and Standard (previously Melia 1 and Oak 1 only); Model cards now label rates 'Self-serve' and add a 'Custom Enterprise pricing' option per model (Melia, Enhanced, Oak, Linden, Standard); Enterprise pricing is set via sales. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed, positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $0.01 per 1,000 TTS characters + $0.19 per hour (Batch Melia 1 model), Translation at $0.65 per hour of audio, Summaries at $0.12 per hour of audio, Chapters at $0.40 per hour of audio and Sentiment at $0.12 per hour of audio.
It lands 63 days after Speechmatics' previous change, "Speechmatics: new Linden 1 model launched at $0.16/hr" (Sep 21, 2026). Speechmatics has been tracked since 2024-10-02.
In the same window Assemblyai, IBM, OpenAI and Google Cloud also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
