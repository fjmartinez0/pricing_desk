What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Neo4j's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AuraDB Business Critical and Virtual Dedicated Cloud raised max AWS instance memory from 512GB to 2TB (GCP stays 2TB, Azure stays 512GB) and now list up to 5TB DB instance storage; AuraDB Professional now lists up to 2TB storage per instance alongside its 128GB memory cap. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists AuraDS Professional at $65, AuraDB Professional at $65, AuraDB Business Critical at $146 and Aura Graph Analytics at $0.40 per GB/hour.
It lands 35 days after Neo4j's previous change, "Neo4j boosts AuraDB Business Critical to 2TB memory per instance on GCP" (Oct 5, 2026). Neo4j has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Google Cloud also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
