What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Matterport's pricing page, Pulse recorded 5 changes: Pro3 Plus Package bundle added: Pro3 LiDAR camera plus Professional 50 Plan, $4,495 struck to $1,995, or $245/mo for 24 months. After markdown is hydration-blank; validated via OCR plus live matterport.com/plans HTML; Starter plan removed: $12/mo billed annually ($14 monthly), 5-20 Active Spaces, 3 users. The redesigned page lists only Professional and Enterprise; Business plan removed: $296/mo. In detail: Professional repriced as a space-tier ladder: entry moves from 20 spaces at $58/mo ($69 monthly) to 10 spaces at $90/mo ($108 monthly, $1,080/yr). 20 spaces now costs $125/mo; tiers run 30 $165, 50 $245, 75 $345, 100 $430, 150 $585, and 200+ is contact sales; Business plan removed: $296/mo billed annually ($355 monthly), 100-300 Active Spaces, 50 users. Its Autodesk Forma integration now sits under Enterprise; Free Forever plan card ($0, 1 Active Space, 2 users) removed from the plan lineup. Free access is now only a bottom-of-page link: 'Not ready to commit? Try Matterport for free with limited access.'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase, plan removed, new add-on, freemium change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Professional at $90.
It lands 28 days after Matterport's previous change, "Matterport swaps Autodesk Construction Cloud for Forma on Business plan" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Matterport rebrands Enterprise support as dedicated account manager" (Aug 31, 2026). Matterport has been tracked since 2026-04-26.
In the same window Hover, DroneDeploy and Google Cloud also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
