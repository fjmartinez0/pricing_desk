What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of BrowserStack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Real iOS & Android device cloud count increased from 30,000+ to 35,000+ devices across App Live/App Automate plan descriptions. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Freelancer at $12.5, Individual at $39, Team at $150 and Team Pro at $249.
It lands 145 days after BrowserStack's previous change, "Browserstack App renames Freelancer Plan section to Other Plans" (May 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Browserstack App rebrands Private Devices as Custom Device Lab" (Apr 11, 2026), "Browserstack App Removes Chat and Phone Support from Individual and Team Plans, Now Offering Only 24/7 Email Support" (Jan 13, 2026) and "Browserstack App Expands Device Catalog to 30,000+ Real iOS & Android Devices Across All Plans" (Q2 2025). BrowserStack has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
