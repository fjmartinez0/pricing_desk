What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Perplexity AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Computer model lineup: Gemini 3.8 Flash (Google's fast model) replaced by Gemini 4 Hydrogen, tagged New (Google's latest model). This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Perplexity AI swaps Gemini 3.8 Flash for Google's new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model" (Day 318, 2026, saved Nov 14, 2026).
