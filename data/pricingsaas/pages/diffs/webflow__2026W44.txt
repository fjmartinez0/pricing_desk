What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Webflow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Webflow Campaigns add-on for marketers launched at 'Starting at $2k/mo' (contact us), replacing the 'Preview Localize for free' card in the add-ons section. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Basic at $15, Freelancer (Workspace) at $16, Core (Workspace) at $19, Premium at $25 and Agency (Workspace) at $35.
It lands 26 days after Webflow's previous change, "Webflow surfaces Localize AI translation word allowances on add-on cards" (Sep 30, 2026). Webflow has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Figma, Framer and Squarespace also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
