What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Logz.io's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Distributed Tracing price cut from $0.92 to $0.30 per ingested GB/day (68% off), 10 days retention stated; applies to new consumption plans from Nov. 1, 2026. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Agentic Observability at $10 per 1M tokens or AI Agent workflow/invocation, Distributed Tracing Pro at $0.16 per 1 million spans per day, Log Management at $0.92 per ingested GB, per day, Infrastructure Monitoring at $0.40 per 1000 time series metrics, per day and Distributed Tracing at $0.16 per 1 million spans, per day.
It lands 28 days after Logz.io's previous change, "Logz.io halves metrics pricing to $0.20 per 1,000 time series daily" (Oct 5, 2026). Logz.io has been tracked since 2026-04-26.
In the same window Datadog, Sumologic, Dynatrace and Elastic also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
