What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of BugHerd's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Mid-tier plan lost 2-way Jira/Asana/ClickUp/Monday integrations, the full client-collaboration suite, and AI productivity features (moved to new top-tier Premium); mid-tier now advertises only 'More Members, More storage' over the entry plan; Mid-tier included-member count doubled from 5 to 10 while its annual price dropped ($83->$67); a new Premium tier fixes a 25-member cap that previously only. In detail: Mid-tier included-member count doubled from 5 to 10 while its annual price dropped ($83->$67); a new Premium tier fixes a 25-member cap that previously only existed as the Custom plan's stated minimum. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, feature change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $42, Studio at $67, Premium at $125 and Additional Member at $6.6.
It lands 7 days after BugHerd's previous change, "BugHerd kills $125/mo Premium, folds its perks into a 5-seat Pro tier" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "BugHerd: AI features added to Standard & Premium plans" (Jul 2, 2026). BugHerd has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Linear, Trello, Front, Asana and monday.com also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
