What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of vPlan's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Automation allowance relabeled from 'automatiseringsacties' to 'automatiseringsruns per maand' on Basic/Professional/Enterprise cards; limits unchanged (1,000/10,000/100,000), now explicitly monthly; Enterprise card security line changed from 'SSO, sessiebeheer en MFA+' to 'SSO en sessiebeheer' (MFA+ dropped from card). Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed, feature change).
It lands 21 days after vPlan's previous change, "vPlan launches paid training services with €199 Planning-APK assessment" (Oct 26, 2026). vPlan has been tracked since 2026-06-04.
In the same window Microsoft, Smartsheet, Asana and Notion also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
