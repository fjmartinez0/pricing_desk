What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Xero's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: List prices rose (Early $25->$27, Growing $55->$59, Established $90->$97); new-customer promo cut from 90% off 6 months to 80% off 3 months, raising intro prices; Auto-reconcile transactions now available on Early as an optional add-on for $5.00 per month (previously Growing/Established only; included on both); New 'Get one month free' option added alongside 'Buy now' (80% off 3 months) above the. In detail: New 'Get one month free' option added alongside 'Buy now' (80% off 3 months) above the plan cards. Pulse files it as a pricing change (trial change, price increase, new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Early at $27, Growing at $59 and Established at $97.
It lands 21 days after Xero's previous change, "Xero swaps Syft branding for own Xero Analytics on pricing page" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Xero graduates AI auto-reconcile from Beta to full release" (Aug 17, 2026), "Xero: Intro promo deepened to 90% off, extended to 6 months" (Aug 11, 2026) and "Xero: announces subscription price increase effective Oct 1, 2026" (Aug 5, 2026). Xero has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
