What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Claris's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: FileMaker Cloud Starter and Max plan prices are now publicly displayed (US $22/user/month and US $45/user/month, billed annually), where previously no price was shown next to the Buy now button. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Starter (Server) at $17.5, Max (Server) at $22, Starter (Cloud) at $22, Max (Cloud) at $45 and Standby Server at $41.67.
It lands 7 days after Claris's previous change, "Claris cuts up to 25% off Remote Backup and Standby Server, ends Sept 26" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Claris: Starter and Max plan prices hidden ($22, $45/mo)" (Aug 31, 2026), "Claris: Starter & Max plan pricing revealed (US$22 / US$45 per user/month)" (Aug 10, 2026) and "Claris: FileMaker Cloud hides Starter ($22) and Max ($45) plan prices" (Aug 3, 2026). Claris has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Salesforce, Microsoft, Mendix, OutSystems and Appian also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
