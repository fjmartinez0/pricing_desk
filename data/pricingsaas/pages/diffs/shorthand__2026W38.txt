What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Shorthand's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan's included dev & design services allocation changed from a fixed '20 hours' to an unspecified 'Tailored' amount, removing the explicit hour quantity from the plan benefit. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction.
It lands 243 days after Shorthand's previous change, "Shorthand Business Plan Price Increased by 119% to $1,750/month Amid Major Capacity Enhancements" (Jan 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shorthand Transitions to Story-Count-Based Pricing Model, Eliminating Public Prices for Plans" (Q4 2024). Shorthand has been tracked since 2025-01-03.
