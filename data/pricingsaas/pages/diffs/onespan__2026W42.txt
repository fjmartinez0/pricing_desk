What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of OneSpan's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Compare table adds Counter signing, Conditional logic and NIGO Document Validation rows, included in Enterprise only (Professional shows X); New paid SMS notifications add-on ($) for real-time SMS signing alerts; available on Enterprise, not Professional; Analytics & insights (admin transaction dashboards) row removed from the compare table; previously included in Enterprise. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, feature removed, new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Professional at $22.
It lands 90 days after OneSpan's previous change, "OneSpan adds pay-per-use Add-ons: SMS at $0.40, ID checks at $2.50" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OneSpan: Professional plan capped at 1,000 transactions/user/year" (Jul 4, 2026), "OneSpan clarifies Professional plan cap as 1,000 transactions per user annually" (May 5, 2026) and "OneSpan Caps Professional Plan at 1000 Transactions Per User" (Apr 27, 2026). OneSpan has been tracked since 2026-03-18.
