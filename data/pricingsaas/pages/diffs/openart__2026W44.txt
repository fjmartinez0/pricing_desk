What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of OpenArt's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New promo banner: 50% off Kling 4.0 generations with Wonder or Pro until Nov 20; Kling 4.0 50% OFF badges added to plan cards; One-Click Stories allowances and personalized models bullets removed from all four plan cards (e.g. Starter ~5 stories, ~13 models). Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature removed, discount added) with a downward direction. The current page lists Essential at $7, Advanced at $14.5, Team Plan at $17.5, Infinite at $28 and Wonder at $120.
It lands 7 days after OpenArt's previous change, "OpenArt drops expired Seedance, Nano Banana and new-user promos" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenArt ends 50% off Seedance and unlimited Flux 3 Image promos on Wonder" (Oct 16, 2026), "OpenArt Pro dropped from Seedance/Flux 3 promo; Wonder-only deal ends Oct 14" (Oct 9, 2026) and "OpenArt cuts Nano Banana 2.1 generations 30% for upgraders through Oct 14" (Oct 7, 2026). OpenArt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Runway, Microsoft, Leonardo.ai and Huggingface also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
