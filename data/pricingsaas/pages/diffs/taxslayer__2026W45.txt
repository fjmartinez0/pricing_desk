What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of TaxSlayer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Self-Employed plan ($74.99 + state additional) removed from the pricing comparison; only Simply Free, Classic and Premium remain. Self-employed-specific features went with it. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Classic at $44.99 per use, Premium at $64.99 per use, Self-Employed Guidance at $9.99 per use and State Return at $47.99 per use.
