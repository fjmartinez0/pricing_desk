What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of BetaTesting's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Team/admin seat limits introduced: Project capped at 3 admin accounts (was unlimited), Teams (formerly Subscription) capped at 10 (was unlimited), and Teams' own-user community capped at 200 members; Teams plan (renamed from Subscription) added a Research Tools set: advanced survey functionality (matrix, skip logic), language translations, and test-wide AI insights, none of which existed before. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, limit decrease) with an upward direction. The current page lists Teams at $299, Project at $1,500 subscription and Add-on credits at $24 per credit.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for BetaTesting. BetaTesting has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
