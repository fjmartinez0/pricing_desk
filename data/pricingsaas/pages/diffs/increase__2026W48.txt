What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Increase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Card enrichment' line item added under card services, priced at $0.02 per authorization. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on). The current page lists Wire Drawdown Origination at $15 per transaction, Wire Origination at $15 per transaction, ACH Unauthorized Returns at $15 per transaction, RTP Origination at $2.50 per transaction and Card Disputes at $15 per dispute.
It lands 14 days after Increase's previous change, "Increase adds three-ring binder checkbook to check services, pricing via sales" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Increase adds $40 fee for Hold Harmless Letter submissions" (Sep 28, 2026). Increase has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
