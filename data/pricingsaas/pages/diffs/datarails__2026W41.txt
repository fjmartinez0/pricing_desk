What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Datarails's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Plan cards repackaged: Professional gets Datarails AI Agents; Premium swaps AI Storyboards for Spend (Cards, Bill Pay, Contracts); Expert bundles Cash Management, Month-End Close, Receivables Desk; Expert plan now includes 50 Viewers alongside 15 users. In detail: Plan cards repackaged: Professional gets Unlimited Dashboards and Datarails AI Agents; Premium swaps AI Storyboards for Spend (Cards, Bill Pay, Contracts); Expert now bundles Cash Management, Month-End Close, Receivables and Desk (previously one additional product). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, package change) with an upward direction.
