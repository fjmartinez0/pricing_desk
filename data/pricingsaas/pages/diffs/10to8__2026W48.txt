What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Sign In App's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro plan Spaces (desks) limit in the Enhanced/Pro comparison table raised from 100 to 500. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised). The current page lists Core at $630, Enhanced at $1,260, Pro at $1,890, Central Record - Up to 100 Pupils at $450 and Central Record - Up to 250 Pupils at $550.
It lands 230 days after Sign In App's previous change, "SMS pricing changed from a visible per-unit rate (6p/SMS) to 'Contact for bundle price', hiding the price behind a sales inquiry" (Apr 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Sign In App Adds Executive Tier and Revamps Plan Descriptions Across Board" (Mar 16, 2026). Sign In App has been tracked since 2026-02-25.
In the same window Jumpcloud, Google Cloud and Ping Identity also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
