What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of BrowserStack's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Real iOS & Android device units advertised dropped from 35,000+ to 30,000+ in the hero and plan feature lists (Individual and Team access). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Freelancer at $12.50, Individual at $39, Team at $150 and Team Pro at $249.
It lands 28 days after BrowserStack's previous change, "Browserstack App expands its real iOS & Android device cloud to 35,000+ devices" (Oct 5, 2026). BrowserStack has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
