What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of iSpring Solutions's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Business fixed 100/300/500-user price cards replaced by an Active Users slider (25/100/300/1,000/10,000+, default 100); the static $4.46 (300 users) and $3.97 (500 users) per-user tiers ($16,050/$23,790 annual) are no longer shown, and the separate 'Less than 100 users? Request pricing' banner was removed now that the slider starts at 25 users. $6.91 at 100 users unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Business at $6.91.
It lands 49 days after iSpring Solutions's previous change, "iSpring Solutions adds "Book a demo" CTA for sub-100-user Business prospects" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "iSpring Solutions drops Summer Offer banner as promo expires post-August 31" (Sep 7, 2026) and "iSpring Solutions: Summer Offer 20% discount promotion added" (Aug 5, 2026). iSpring Solutions has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Docebo and Articulate also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
