What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of HeyOrca's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Home dashboard' row added to the plan comparison table (between Custom approval workflow and Canva integration), included on all plans: Free, Basic and Pro. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Basic at $50, Pro at $126, Reports & Listening Add-on at $67 and Social Inbox Add-on at $33.
It lands 14 days after HeyOrca's previous change, "HeyOrca bundles Social Reports and Listening into one $79 add-on" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HeyOrca: Enterprise plan renamed to Agency + Import past posts feature added" (Sep 13, 2026), "HeyOrca: added Post performance metrics feature (Free limited, Basic/Pro full)" (Sep 7, 2026) and "HeyOrca splits Approvals in two, restricting Custom approvals to Pro" (Sep 1, 2026). HeyOrca has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Metricool, ContentStudio, Hootsuite, Buffer and Sproutsocial also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
