What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Taskade's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Enterprise now shows a public $250/mo price (annual billing) with a self-serve Get Enterprise checkout instead of Custom/Contact sales; Free plan no longer includes the 6,000 one-time starter credits (1,000 on verify + 5,000 on first build); Free now starts at 0 credits — earn via referrals/sharing or buy a credit pack; Claude models removed from Free, Pro, Business and Max: model access changed. In detail: Claude models removed from Free, Pro, Business and Max: model access changed from 'GPT, Claude & more' (15+ models from OpenAI, Anthropic) to 'Frontier models from top AI labs'; Claude now only on Enterprise via bring-your-own API key (BYOK). Pulse files it as a pricing change (freemium change, price revealed, feature removed) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pro at $10, Business at $25, Max at $100, Enterprise at $250 and Extra Credits at $5 per 9,000-credit pack (one-time) + $20 per 45,000-credit pack (one-time).
It lands 28 days after Taskade's previous change, "Taskade adds $5 Extra Credits and Custom Domains add-ons, doubles Free seats" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Taskade doubles Free plan seats to 2, swaps Gemini for DeepSeek" (Sep 7, 2026), "Taskade clarifies free-tier limits: "Keep 3 apps," one-time 6,000 AI credits" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Taskade axes Starter plan, boosts annual discount from 20% to 50% off" (Jul 20, 2026). Taskade has been tracked since 2024-10-14.
In the same window Asana, Notion and Trello also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
