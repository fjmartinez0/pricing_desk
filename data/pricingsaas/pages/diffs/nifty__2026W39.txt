Nifty adds flat team-size pricing, default view no longer per-member
Verified Sep 21, 2026·Project Management·Direction: neutral
The changeAll plans packaging — package change.
1 change·2 captures·Sep 14, 2026 → Sep 21, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Nifty's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: For teams of 7-10, default pricing view switched from per-member ($10-16/member/mo) to flat rate via a new team-size stepper ($79 Pro/$124 Business for 10 people, plus new $399/mo Unlimited plan). Per-member pricing still available via a toggle for teams under 7. In detail: Replaced the '1-5 person team?' link with an interactive team-size stepper that auto-switches between per-member and flat pricing, adding new copy explaining tier thresholds for each plan. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $79, Business at $124 and Unlimited at $399.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Nifty. Nifty has been tracked since 2026-09-08.
In the same window Atlassian, ClickUp and Basecamp also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
