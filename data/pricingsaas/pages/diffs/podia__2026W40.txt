What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Podia's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free trial period shortened from 30 days to 14 days across hero heading, CTA button, and FAQ copy on the pricing page; no plan prices, limits, or features changed. Pulse files it as a packaging change (trial change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Mover at $42, Shaker at $84 and Earthquaker at $150.
It lands 48 days after Podia's previous change, "Podia: "Unlimited customers" added across all plans" (Aug 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Podia: Price increases + new Earthquaker tier at $150/mo" (Jul 4, 2026). Podia has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
