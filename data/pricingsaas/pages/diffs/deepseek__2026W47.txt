What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of DeepSeek's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New File Parsing & OCR API pricing: $0.225 per 1,000 pages; New Speech API pricing: TTS $0.45 per 10,000 characters, ASR $0.116 per hour of audio; launched at a limited-time 50% off ($0.225 / $0.058). Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on). The current page lists DeepSeek Flash at $0.15 per 1M input tokens (cache miss) + $0.30 per 100M input tokens (cache hit) and DeepSeek V4 Pro at $0.66 per 1M input tokens (cache miss) + $2.20 per 100M input tokens (cache hit).
This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "DeepSeek launches OCR and Speech APIs at 50% off, from $0.058 per audio hour" (Day 318, 2026, saved Nov 14, 2026). It is the same change, not a new one. Before that, DeepSeek's previous recorded change was "DeepSeek ends free Search/Fetch calls via Claude Code, now $0.75/1K requests" (Nov 5, 2026). DeepSeek has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Anthropic, OpenAI, X, Metabase and Google also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
