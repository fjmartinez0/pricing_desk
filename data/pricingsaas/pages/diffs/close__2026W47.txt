What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Close's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New plan-card bullets: MCP connection to ChatGPT/Claude/Grok (Solo, inherited by all) and unlimited daily standard API calls with no per-request fees (Essentials and up). In detail: Plan cards now promote existing integrations: Solo card adds "Connect your AI tools" (ChatGPT, Claude, Grok via MCP) and Essentials card adds "Build integrations that scale" (unlimited daily standard API calls, no per-request fees). MCP and API access were already listed as included in the comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Solo at $9, Essentials at $35, Growth at $99, Scale at $139 and Additional Organizations at $50.
It lands 161 days after Close's previous change, "Close launches paid Chloe AI tiers with 30-day money-back guarantee" (Jun 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Close Launches Chloe AI Sales Agent Free in Beta on Growth and Scale Plans" (Apr 13, 2026). Close has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window PayPal, Stripe and Squarespace also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
