What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Dropbox's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 'AI in Dropbox' tool list on Standard/Advanced swapped out 'Ask questions in chat', 'Get insights about files/team activity', 'Create new content from existing files' for 'Ask AI to organize/rename files', 'Convert/compress/combine files', 'Approve AI actions and undo changes' (AI credits per month unchanged: 250 Standard / 750 Advanced). In detail: AI in Dropbox capabilities changed for Standard & Advanced: added organize/rename, convert/compress/combine, approve/undo AI actions; removed chat Q&A, insights, and content creation items. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Plus at $9.99, Standard at $15 and Advanced at $24.
Dropbox's previous recorded change was "Dropbox launches AI in Dropbox on Standard and Advanced with 250-750 credits" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Dropbox: Add-ons pricing section removed (Replay, Sign, DocSend, Reclaim.ai + more)" (Aug 3, 2026). Dropbox has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Microsoft and Google also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
