What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Prezi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Offline access (desktop application) removed from Prezi's Plus and Premium plans — dropped from the Plus plan's description and the comparison-table row deleted entirely. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists EDU Plus at $4, Standard at $7, EDU Pro at $8, Plus at $19 and EDU Teams at $19.
It lands 117 days after Prezi's previous change, "Prezi replaces phone support with priority technical support" (May 27, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Prezi moves free Basic plan to its own dedicated tab" (Apr 6, 2026), "Prezi Democratizes Access with Watermark-Free Presentations Now in Standard Tier" (Jan 12, 2026) and "Prezi Introduces Standard Tier at $7/Month for Enhanced Privacy and Advanced Editing Features" (Q3 2025). Prezi has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Microsoft, Canva, Beautiful.ai, Google and Visme also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
