What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Nightfall AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: "Nightfall Complete" -> "Foundation Edition"; "Complete + AI Agent Security" -> "Premier Edition" (tier structure, features, per-user/year pricing model unchanged). In detail: Nightfall renamed its two plans: "Nightfall Complete" became "Foundation Edition" and "Complete + AI Agent Security" became "Premier Edition"; tier structure, feature inclusions, and per-user/year pricing model unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed) with a neutral direction.
It lands 48 days after Nightfall AI's previous change, "Nightfall AI: Complete + AI Agent Security storage cut 500GB → 150GB" (Aug 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Nightfall AI: discontinued DDR/DEX standalone plans + Complete storage cut 500GB→150GB" (Aug 5, 2026). Nightfall AI has been tracked since 2026-04-26.
In the same window Microsoft, Datadog, Cloudflare, Splunk and Zscaler also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
