What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Stigg's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: HubSpot integration removed from the Build (Free) plan card, which now lists Stripe only; Pro inherits Build's integrations, so it loses HubSpot as well; HubSpot CRM integration added to the Scale plan, which now reads 'Salesforce & HubSpot CRM, data destinations included' (was Salesforce CRM only); BYOC inherits it. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed, feature added) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $399 and BYOC at $3,333.33.
It lands 97 days after Stigg's previous change, "Stigg overhauls pricing: seats out, usage-based entities and new BYOC plan in" (Jul 6, 2026). Stigg has been tracked since 2026-03-18.
In the same window Stripe and Chargebee also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
