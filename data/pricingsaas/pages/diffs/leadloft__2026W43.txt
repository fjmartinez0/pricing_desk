What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of LeadLoft's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Unlimited plan swaps Unlimited Prospecting for 300 Data Credits; Scale AI adds $1,500 Data Credits; pay-per-use usage rows now priced as 5 and 1 Data Credits; Unlimited plan LinkedIn and Email Automation replaced by AI Sales Agent and Outreach Automation; email/LinkedIn reporting rows replaced by Social Outreach and Engagement Analytics. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Unlimited at $99 and Scale AI at $299.
It lands 105 days after LeadLoft's previous change, "LeadLoft: Scale AI plan price increase ($199→$299/mo)" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LeadLoft: Scale AI plan price cut from $400/mo to $199/mo/user" (Jul 3, 2026) and "LeadLoft slashes Scale AI plan 50% to $199/user, adds AI SDRs" (Jun 9, 2026). LeadLoft has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
