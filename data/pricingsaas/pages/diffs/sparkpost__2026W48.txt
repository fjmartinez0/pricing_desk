What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of SparkPost's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pricing page copy rewrite: hero 'Add money. Use it across Bird' became 'One balance across Bird'; product tags 'Pay as you go + plans with benefits' became 'Usage + funding plans'. All listed prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Startup at $15, Growth at $80 and Dedicated IP at $24.95.
It lands 35 days after SparkPost's previous change, "SparkPost moves Apple Messages to pay-as-you-go, wallet from $100/mo" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "SparkPost halves US local number rental to $0.50 a month" (Oct 4, 2026), "SparkPost: Pricing page rebuilt around a Bird balance/wallet model" (Sep 28, 2026) and "SparkPost/Bird: Email plan pricing removed, replaced by usage hub" (Aug 31, 2026). SparkPost has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
