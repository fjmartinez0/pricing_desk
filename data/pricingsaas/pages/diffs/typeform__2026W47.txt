What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Typeform's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New FAQ announces discounts for nonprofits, NGOs and students, linking to a discount program page. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Basic at $28, Plus at $56, Business at $91, Talent at $119 and Growth Flow at $266.
It lands 126 days after Typeform's previous change, "Typeform's Enterprise tier ditches "unlimited" for "custom" responses and seats" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Typeform swaps "Unlimited" for "Custom" limits on Enterprise plan" (Jul 13, 2026), "Typeform launches Research Flow, rebrands Growth Pro as Growth Flow with trial" (Jul 4, 2026) and "Typeform swaps Enterprise for Growth Flow in revamped plan lineup" (Jun 10, 2026).
