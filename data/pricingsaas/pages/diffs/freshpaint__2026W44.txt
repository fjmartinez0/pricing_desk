What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Freshpaint's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Scale drops priority support/dedicated CSM and expert guidance for dedicated account/implementation experts and annual Labs; Perform priority support reworded; Comply integrations reworded; Scale plan now lists greater usage and project capacity (unquantified). Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature change, limit increase) with an upward direction.
Freshpaint's previous recorded change was "Freshpaint: Surveys moved from Comply to Perform plan" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Freshpaint: Full AI access moved down from Scale to Perform tier" (Sep 7, 2026). Freshpaint has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window Salesforce, Mixpanel and Amplitude also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
