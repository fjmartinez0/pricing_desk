AnyDB makes AI Teammates free on every plan, shifts to shared task-based limits
Verified Oct 26, 2026·Business Operations·Direction: up
The changeEnterprise feature — feature change.
2 changes·2 captures·Oct 19, 2026 → Oct 26, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of AnyDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Workflows/executions reframed as a shared AI Teammates-or-workflows + tasks allowance (Free 3/50, Business 50/10,000, counts unchanged; Enterprise now custom capacity); AI Teammates included on every plan, with new External MCP connectors (Business+), A2A support and Bring your own LLM rows. Prices unchanged ($10/$12 Business). In detail: Automation allowance reframed: workflows/executions now a shared AI Teammates-or-workflows + shared tasks allowance (Free 3 / 50 tasks; Business 50 / 10,000 tasks, counts unchanged); Enterprise 50 workflows / 10,000 executions / 20,000 AI credits now custom capacity; AI Teammates now included on every plan (Free 3, Business 50, Enterprise custom AI workforce); new rows: External MCP connectors for AI Teammates (Business and Enterprise, not Free), A2A support (all plans), Bring your own LLM (Free/Business supported, Enterprise controls). Pulse files it as a product change (feature change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Business at $10.
AnyDB's previous recorded change was "AnyDB swaps browser extension for desktop app, adds Shopify sync" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "AnyDB launches email conversation sync, with auto sync on Business plans" (Oct 12, 2026), "AnyDB adds Entra ID and Okta SSO for Enterprise, plus custom branding tiers" (Oct 5, 2026) and "AnyDB: Free plan AI credits boosted to 1,000/mo + new MCP server access" (Aug 31, 2026). AnyDB has been tracked since 2026-07-06.
In the same window MongoDB, Elastic, Redis, MariaDB and Couchbase also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
