What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Prowler's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Realtime exploitable issues, toxic combinations and vulnerability detection added to Prowler Cloud only; Private Cloud explicitly excludes Realtime. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Prowler Cloud at $79.
It lands 7 days after Prowler's previous change, "Prowler adds AWS Marketplace purchase for Private Cloud enterprise tier" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Prowler: Prowler Cloud free trial shortened from 15 days to 7 days (no credit card still required); paid prices…" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Prowler ditches resource-copy for cloud-accounts calculator, adds free trial" (Sep 28, 2026). Prowler has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Proton Pass, Proof, Optery, NordVPN and Mimecast also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
