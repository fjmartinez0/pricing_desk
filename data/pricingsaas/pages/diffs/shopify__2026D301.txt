What happened
Between the Oct 27, 2026 and Oct 28, 2026 captures of Shopify's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Shopify removed the "Free Shopify Academy courses" perk from the plan comparison table; it was previously included (checkmark) on all four plans (Basic, Grow, Advanced, Plus). Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Basic at $29, Grow at $79, Advanced at $299, Plus at $2,300 and POS Pro at $89.
It lands 2 days after Shopify's previous change, "Shopify rewrites POS comparison, adds customer profiles and returns rows" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shopify simplifies in-person sales messaging, adds plan comparison rows" (Oct 23, 2026), "Shopify cuts Grow to $79 and Advanced to $299, reversing D288 hike" (Oct 16, 2026) and "Shopify raises annual Grow to $89/mo and Advanced to $359/mo" (Oct 15, 2026). Shopify has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Salesforce and Squarespace also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
