ContentDrips drops Free plan, shifts to $15 entry tier + new $52 Ultimate
Verified Sep 14, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: neutral

What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of ContentDrips's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free ($0) plan discontinued, replaced by a 7-day/50-credit trial. Ladder repriced/renamed: new Starter $15/mo entry tier; old Starter->Pro $29/mo; old Teams->Ultimate $52/mo with new API-calls metric (up to 25,000 calls/mo). In detail: Free ($0) plan discontinued, replaced by a 7-day/50-credit trial. Ladder repriced/renamed: new Starter $15/mo entry tier; old Starter->Pro $29; old Teams->Ultimate $52 w/ new API-calls metric. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Starter at $15, Pro at $29 and Ultimate at $52.
It lands 56 days after ContentDrips's previous change, "ContentDrips: Starter swaps Custom Ideas Generator for GIF/MP4 export" (Jul 20, 2026). ContentDrips has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
