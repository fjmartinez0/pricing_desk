What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Google Workspace's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Business Plus promotional discount deepened from 20% off to 50% off for the first 3 months, dropping the introductory price from $17.60 to $11/user/month (list price unchanged at $22); Business Starter promotional discount deepened from 20% off to 50% off for the first 3 months, dropping the introductory price from $5.60 to $3.50/user/month (list price unchanged at $7). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 21, 2026) lists Starter at $7, Standard at $14 and Plus at $22.
It lands 7 days after Google Workspace's previous change, "Google Workspace: Starter+Plus promo cut from 50% to 20% off" (Sep 7, 2026). Google Workspace has been tracked since 2026-05-07.
In the same window Zoho, Microsoft and Nextcloud also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
