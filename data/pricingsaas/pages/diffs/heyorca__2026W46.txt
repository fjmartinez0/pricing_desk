What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of HeyOrca's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Basic add-ons consolidated: standalone Social Reports ($59) and Social Listening ($39) add-ons removed; new Reports & Listening bundle at $79/workspace/month leads the add-on list. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Basic at $50, Pro at $126, Social Inbox Add-on at $33 and Reports & Listening Add-on at $67.
It lands 56 days after HeyOrca's previous change, "HeyOrca: Enterprise plan renamed to Agency + Import past posts feature added" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HeyOrca: added Post performance metrics feature (Free limited, Basic/Pro full)" (Sep 7, 2026), "HeyOrca splits Approvals in two, restricting Custom approvals to Pro" (Sep 1, 2026) and "HeyOrca drops "coming soon": Facebook & LinkedIn first comment now live" (Aug 24, 2026). HeyOrca has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
