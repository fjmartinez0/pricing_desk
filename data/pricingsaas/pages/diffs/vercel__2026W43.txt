What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Vercel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Vercel Daemons usage products: Active CPU (5 hrs/mo included, from $0.128/hr) and Provisioned Memory (420 GB-hrs/mo included, from $0.0212/GB-hr). Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 20, 2026) lists Pro at $20.
It lands 3 days after Vercel's previous change, "Vercel launches usage-based Daemons billing at $0.128/hr Active CPU" (Oct 16, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vercel: Vercel Drives storage launched at $0.05/GB-month, metered reads/writes" (Oct 20, 2026), "Vercel adds OWASP Core Ruleset firewall to Pro Plus priority projects" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Vercel splits Speed Insights pricing, adds Tracing Spans beta observability" (Sep 28, 2026). Vercel has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Render, Railway and Netlify also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
