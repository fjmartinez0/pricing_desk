What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Pagecloud's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Plan differentiation moved from numeric limits (visitors, pages, forms, popup impressions) to relative AI usage multipliers (Launch 1x, Grow 2x, Optimize 5x); per-site add-on pricing no longer shown; Optimize team members cap raised from 15 to 100. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, package change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Launch at $22, Grow at $36, Optimize at $69, Agency at $116 and Agency+ at $196.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Pagecloud. Pagecloud has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
In the same window Shopify, Webflow and Squarespace also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
