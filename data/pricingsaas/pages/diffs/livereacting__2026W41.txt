What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of LiveReacting's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: 24/7 streaming promo changed from 40% off first year to 27% off with code KICKSTART2026 until Oct 31, plus 45% off first 5 months monthly; yearly toggle now says save up to 20% (was up to 45%); API access and MCP server for AI agents added as listed features on paid plan cards; previously API access appeared only under Custom plan. In detail: 24/7 streaming new-customer offer reworded and extended: first-year offer price unchanged at $23.99/mo (~$287.9 first year), but badge now says 27% off vs the $33 yearly rate (was 40% off vs $39.99 monthly), code KICKSTART2026 named with an Oct 31, 2026 deadline, and a new month-to-month option added: 45% off the first 5 months ($21.99/mo). Yearly toggle badge changed from up to 45% off to save up to 20%. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Small at $16, 24/7 Streaming at $33, Medium at $33, Countdown at $33 and Large at $83.
It lands 125 days after LiveReacting's previous change, "LiveReacting hikes 24/7 streaming plan promo rate to $287.88" (Jun 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LiveReacting adds enterprise streaming features to Custom Plan" (Apr 1, 2026) and "LiveReacting Adds Interactive FPS and 4K Quality Toggles to Pricing Page" (Mar 10, 2026). LiveReacting has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
