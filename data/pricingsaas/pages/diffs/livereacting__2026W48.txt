What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of LiveReacting's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Promo code swapped from KICKSTART2026 (40% off first year, 24/7 plan only, expired Nov 30) to Black Friday BF2026 (50% off first year, now on Small/Medium/Large/24-7 plans, ends Dec 4, 2026). In detail: Promotional code swapped from KICKSTART2026 (40% off first year, 24/7 streaming plan only, expired Nov 30) to Black Friday code BF2026 (50% off first year, extended to Small, Medium, Large and 24/7 streaming plans, ends Dec 4, 2026). Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Small at $16, 24/7 Streaming at $33, Medium at $33, Countdown at $33 and Large at $83.
It lands 21 days after LiveReacting's previous change, "LiveReacting extends KICKSTART2026 promo, up to 45% off, to Nov 30" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LiveReacting adds API access and MCP server to paid plans, extends 24/7 promo to monthly" (Oct 4, 2026). LiveReacting has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
