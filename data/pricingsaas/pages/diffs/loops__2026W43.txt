What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Loops's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Subscriber slider replaced by fixed Free / Growth / Enterprise cards; Growing Audience ($49/mo, 5,000 contacts) renamed Growth and Custom Plan renamed Enterprise, prices unchanged. In detail: Subscriber slider replaced by fixed Free / Growth / Enterprise cards; slider tiers Growing Audience ($49/mo, 5,000 contacts) renamed Growth and Custom Plan renamed Enterprise (1.5M+ contacts), prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Growth at $49.
It lands 28 days after Loops's previous change, "Loops overhauls Free plan: unlimited contacts, emails capped to 1,000 newest" (Sep 21, 2026). Loops has been tracked since 2026-09-08.
