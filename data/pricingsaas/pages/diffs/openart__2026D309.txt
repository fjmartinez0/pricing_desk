What happened
Between the Nov 4, 2026 and Nov 5, 2026 captures of OpenArt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New promo banner: 30% off GPT Image 2.6 generations with Wonder or Pro until Nov 17, with GPT Image 2.6 30% OFF badges added to the Pro and Wonder cards. The Kling 4.0 50% off promo (until Nov 20) continues via its card badges; its banner was replaced. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $13, Plus at $27, Pro at $44, Wonder at $175 and Business at $496.
It lands 10 days after OpenArt's previous change, "OpenArt launches 50% off Kling 4.0 promo, drops Stories and models bullets" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenArt drops expired Seedance, Nano Banana and new-user promos" (Oct 19, 2026), "OpenArt ends 50% off Seedance and unlimited Flux 3 Image promos on Wonder" (Oct 16, 2026) and "OpenArt Pro dropped from Seedance/Flux 3 promo; Wonder-only deal ends Oct 14" (Oct 9, 2026). OpenArt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Runway, Huggingface, Microsoft and Leonardo.ai also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
