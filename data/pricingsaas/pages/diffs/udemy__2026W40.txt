What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Udemy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Team Plan's 'Back to Business' 30% first-year promo expired: price reverts from $252.00 to full $360.00/user/year, and the promo terms footnote is gone from the page. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Team Plan at $30.
It lands 98 days after Udemy's previous change, "Udemy adds Google Certificates to Enterprise with Credly badges" (Jun 22, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Udemy expands Team Plan with AI features and skill proficiency tools" (Jun 19, 2026), "Udemy Removes 25% Team Plan Discount, Raising Price to $30/User/Month" (Apr 9, 2026) and "Udemy Team Plan Shifts from USD to GBP, Ends 25% Promotional Discount" (Mar 26, 2026). Udemy has been tracked since 2026-03-19.
