What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Shopware's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Image editor removed from the Shopware Intelligence+ AI add-on feature list, for both the Community Edition (€29/month) and Rise/Evolve/Beyond (€19/month) variants. Copilot, Data Insights, 3D Preview Generator, and CAD to 3D remain. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Rise at EUR 600, Evolve at EUR 2,400, Shopware Intelligence+ (Community Edition) at EUR 29, Shopware Intelligence+ (Rise, Evolve, Beyond) at EUR 19 and Shopware Nexus at EUR 0.01 Nexus executions.
It lands 42 days after Shopware's previous change, "Shopware extends free Intelligence+/Nexus request threshold trial to October 2026" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Shopware Nexus free tier caps free API usage at 10K reqs/customer/month" (Jul 20, 2026). Shopware has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
In the same window Salesforce and Adobe also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
