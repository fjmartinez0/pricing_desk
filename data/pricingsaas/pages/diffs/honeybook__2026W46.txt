What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Honeybook's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Annual-billing discount deepened (Fall savings promo): annual-billed prices now shown as a discount off the $36/$59/$129 monthly prices. Starter $29 to $30/mo (15% off), Essentials $49 to $38/mo (35% off), Premium $109 to $83/mo (35% off); savings headline up to 18% to up to 35%. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $29, Essentials at $36.75 and Premium at $81.75.
It lands 56 days after Honeybook's previous change, "Honeybook adopts cumulative plan-tier copy on Essentials and Premium cards" (Sep 14, 2026). Honeybook has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
