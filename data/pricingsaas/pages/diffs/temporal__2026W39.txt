What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Temporal's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Essentials/Business/Enterprise plans replaced by Pay-As-You-Go ($50→$25/M actions, no base fee) + separate Support Add-Ons. Features unlocked at every tier. Signup credits $1,000→$150. In detail: Temporal moved from bundled plans to usage-based Pay-As-You-Go (no base fee, $50→$25/M actions) + Support Add-Ons (Business $500/mo). Features unlocked at every tier; signup credits cut $1,000→$150. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pay-As-You-Go at $10, Business at $500 and SCIM at $500.
It lands 63 days after Temporal's previous change, "Temporal swaps Enterprise support add-on for Dedicated Platform Architect" (Jul 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Temporal: Provisioned/TRU capacity billing exits Public Preview" (Jul 4, 2026). Temporal has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
