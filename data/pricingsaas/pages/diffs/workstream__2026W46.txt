What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Workstream's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Hero offer 'Get 65% off your first year of payroll when you switch to Workstream' removed in page redesign (hero now 'AI included'); 8-months-free top bar and LIMITED OFFER section unchanged; New Managed Services add-on (Get in touch): payroll run for you by a payroll specialist, bookkeeping handled by Workstream, HR support from experts, approve in one tap; AI now included in every package (Voi.
