What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Kurrent's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Team plan repriced from $15-18/user/month (up to 100 seats) to $30/seat/month billed monthly, sold as single seat or 5+ seats; Free tier renamed Local, seats reduced 5→1. Local (formerly Free) tier now includes Work Items, evaluations, and Insights agent via BYOK model API key — previously Team-exclusive. In detail: Local (formerly Free) tier now includes Work Items, evaluations, and Insights agent via BYOK model API key — previously exclusive to the paid Team plan; Team plan repriced from $15-18/user/month (up to 100 seats) to $30/seat/month billed monthly, now sold as a single seat or 5+ seats; Free tier renamed Local with seats reduced from 5 to 1. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, package change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Team (1 seat) at $30 and Team (5+ seats) at $100.
It lands 6 days after Kurrent's previous change, "Kurrent free plan drops 5 seats, Micro deployment; now Apple silicon only" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kurrent doubles Team plan free trial to 60 days, no card required" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Kurrent doubles Team plan seat cap to 100 at same $15/user price" (Sep 28, 2026). Kurrent has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
