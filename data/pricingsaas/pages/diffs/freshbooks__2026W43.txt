What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Freshbooks's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Promo changed from $1/mo first-year Solopreneur Lite offer to 80% off for 4 months (Lite $4.60/mo); Plus/Premium 80% off extended from 3 to 4 months. List prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Lite at $23, Plus at $43, Premium at $70, Advanced Payments at $20 and FreshBooks Payroll at $40.
It lands 28 days after Freshbooks's previous change, "Freshbooks drops Lite to $1/mo for a year, trims Plus and Premium promos" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "FreshBooks: 90% off promo shortened from 6 to 3 months" (Aug 3, 2026). Freshbooks has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Quickbooks, Xero, Zoho and Sage also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
