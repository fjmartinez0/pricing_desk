What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Acuity Scheduling's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Premium plan price increased from $49 to $55 per month (annual billing); markdown also shows monthly-billing price $61 to $69.
