What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of PowerOffice's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Årsoppgjør (annual accounts) module changed from 'Januar 2027' launch teaser to published prices: from 1095,- per year (Nano 695, Mikro 1095, Regnskap 1395). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with a neutral direction.
It lands 110 days after PowerOffice's previous change, "PowerOffice: Regnskap split into 3 tiers + 5 new modules launched" (Jul 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "PowerOffice: Regnskap plan prices increased across the board" (Jul 4, 2026). PowerOffice has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Microsoft, Exa, Xero and Visma eAccounting also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
