What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of ALLDATA's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Solution Packs product group renamed to Bundles (Tech Essentials, Business Essentials, Collision Planner Essentials); prices unchanged at $254.60/$312.30/$359.10 per month. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Repair at $209, Collision at $249, Tech Essentials Pack at $254.60, Business Essentials Pack at $312.30 and Manage Online at $329.
It lands 28 days after ALLDATA's previous change, "ALLDATA hikes Diagnostic Intelligence to $59/month, up from $49" (Oct 11, 2026). ALLDATA has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
