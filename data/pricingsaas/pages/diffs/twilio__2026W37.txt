What happened
Between the Aug 31, 2026 and Sep 7, 2026 captures of Twilio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Twilio Video pricing model changed from usage-based per-participant-per-minute billing ($0.004/participant/min) to a hours-free-then-per-active-user model (5,000 hours free, then $1/active user hour or $150/named user per month). Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Conversation Intelligence at $0.01, Enterprise Knowledge at $0.02, Twilio SendGrid Marketing Campaigns at $15, Twilio for Salesforce at $15 and Twilio SendGrid Email API at $19.95.
It lands 2 days after Twilio's previous change, "Twilio Video ditches $0.004/minute for 5,000 free hours, then $1/hour" (Sep 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Twilio ditches per-user Video pricing for pay-as-you-go at $0.004/min" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Twilio: Video/Conversation Intelligence pricing overhaul + 4 new products" (Jul 4, 2026). Twilio has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Plivo, Telnyx, Bird and Google Cloud also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
