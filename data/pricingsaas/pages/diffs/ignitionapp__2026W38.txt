What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Ignitionapp's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Enterprise moved from a generic 'Contact sales' panel into the main plan carousel as a full card (tagline, 11-item feature list built on Pro+, 'Talk to sales' CTA), while Solo was dropped from the carousel and demoted to a footnote link; Pro+ plan's user seat allowance changed from 'Unlimited users' to a capped '20 users', on both the plan card and the feature comparison table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, limit decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Solo at $39, Core at $99, Pro at $229, Pro+ at $399 and Online Forms at $49.
It lands 73 days after Ignitionapp's previous change, "Ignition: Added FYI integration (Pro) and Custom reporting via API (Pro+)" (Jul 3, 2026). Ignitionapp has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Workato, Make and Zapier also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
