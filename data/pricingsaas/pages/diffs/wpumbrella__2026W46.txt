What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of WP Umbrella's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New one-off Security audit service from 30€/site: full review of files, database, accounts and logins with a verdict and client report; cheaper in packs; Added Two-Factor Authentication for administrators and a per-site Security Score; update automation now allows scheduling plugin, theme and core updates with rollback.
