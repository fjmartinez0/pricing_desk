What happened
Between the Nov 19, 2026 and Nov 20, 2026 captures of Ideogram's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added a new Ideogram 4.6 image model to the credit-cost table with separate 2K and 4K rendering tiers, each priced in credits per image (e.g. 0.8-22 credits for 2K, 6-60 credits for 4K). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 22, 2026) lists Plus at $15, Team at $20, Pro at $42 and Pay as you go at $0.01 per credit.
It lands 32 days after Ideogram's previous change, "Ideogram swaps Free plan for 1¢-per-credit Pay as you go, drops Team minimum" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Ideogram lists all image and video models, unlimited character consistency" (Oct 15, 2026), "Ideogram more than doubles priority credits on Plus, Pro and Team plans" (Oct 3, 2026) and "Ideogram: Free tier credit guarantee restricted + Team plan queue upgraded" (Aug 24, 2026). Ideogram has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Canva, Perplexity AI, Microsoft, Runway and Leonardo.ai also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
