What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Gusto's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Workers' compensation pricing changed from "Starting at $14/mo" to "Pay only for premiums and up to $5/mo for pay-as-you-go"; card moved into a new Business insurance section; New Business insurance products added: General liability, Professional liability (Errors & Omissions), Business Owner's Policy (BOP) and Commercial property — each "Pay only for premiums" (via NEXT Insurance). Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change, new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Simple at $6, Solo at $6, Plus at $80, Premium at $180 and Tax-Advantaged Benefits at $200 per year.
It lands 7 days after Gusto's previous change, "Gusto: Health insurance broker integration reworded (add your broker for plans Gusto can't support directly);…" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Gusto launches HR Partners add-on with fractional compliance support" (Oct 12, 2026), "Gusto drops Contractor Only plan, adds $85 Business Compliance add-on" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Gusto launches limited-time free Contractor Only plan for 1099-only teams" (Sep 28, 2026). Gusto has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Paychex and BambooHR also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
