What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Omniconvert's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Omniconvert Explore added 'AI A/B Testing - code to prompt' to its A/B Testing feature list, letting users create tests with AI prompts instead of writing code. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Pulse Expert at $24.17 and Explore at $245.
It lands 88 days after Omniconvert's previous change, "Omniconvert kills Reveal, launches Nexus free during beta—no card needed" (Jul 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Omniconvert: Reveal discontinued, replaced by free-beta Nexus" (Jul 4, 2026). Omniconvert has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
