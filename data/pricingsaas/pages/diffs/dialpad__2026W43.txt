What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Dialpad's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Dialpad renamed its "AI Agent" product/pricing tab to "Resolve" (same tab position, same /ai-agent/ URL). Conversation-based pricing model is unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (plan renamed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Connect Standard at $15, Connect Pro at $25, Sell Essentials at $39, Sell Advanced at $59 and Sell Premium at $79.
It lands the same day as Dialpad's previous change, "Dialpad Sales Sell slashes Premium to $79/user (-47%), Advanced to $59 (-38%)" (Oct 19, 2026). Dialpad has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Microsoft, Google, Zoom and Twilio also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
