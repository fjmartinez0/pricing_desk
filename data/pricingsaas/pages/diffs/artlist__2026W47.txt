What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Artlist's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Top AI tier renamed from 'AI Creator' to 'AI Pro': price rose from $69.99 ($41.67/mo annual) to $99.99 ($59.99/mo annual) and included credits increased from 80,000 to 120,000; Along with the AI Creator to AI Pro upgrade: AI Images cap rose 8,000 to 12,000, AI videos 500 to 750, AI voiceover 16 to 24 hours, and AI songs 533 to 800 per month; 'Artlist Flows' and 'Artlist MCP & Plugins' dropped fr. In detail: Marketing copy simplified: 'Access to 100+ models' / 'Unlimited generations on select models' condensed to 'for all models' on AI Starter and AI Core plans; 'Artlist Flows' and 'Artlist MCP & Plugins' dropped from plan feature lists; AI Pro gained 'Shared workspace & credits' and Custom Business gained 'Access to Artlist Catalog' plus an expanded enterprise feature set. Pulse files it as a pricing change (positioning, feature change, price increase, limit raised) with an upward direction. The current page lists AI Starter at $11.99, AI Core at $23.99 and AI Pro at $41.67.
It lands 7 days after Artlist's previous change, "Artlist now guarantees customer data never trains AI models" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Artlist renames 70+ AI Apps to AI Presets on three paid plan cards" (Oct 26, 2026), "Artlist adds Image Editor and 3D Model Creation to AI plans" (Oct 11, 2026) and "Artlist halves AI video caps and drops Flows and MCP from plans" (Oct 4, 2026). Artlist has been tracked since 2026-06-04.
In the same window Shutterstock and Getty Images also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
