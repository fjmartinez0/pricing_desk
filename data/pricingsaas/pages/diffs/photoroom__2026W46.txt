What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Photoroom's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Adjustable AI Shadows removed from Pro, Max and Ultra AI-credit tools table (was up to 800/2,500/7,500 generations); now labeled Enterprise only with Not included on paid plans; API/MCP credit wallet dropped from Pro, Max and Ultra: FAQ previously said each paid plan includes API/MCP credits worth the plan price; now says app plans include no API allowance and the API has its own Basic/Plus/Enterp. In detail: API/MCP credit wallet dropped from Pro, Max and Ultra: FAQ previously said each paid plan includes API/MCP credits worth the plan price; now says app plans include no API allowance and the API has its own Basic/Plus/Enterprise plans. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. The current page lists Pro at $7.50, Max at $20.99 and Ultra at $82.50.
It lands 7 days after Photoroom's previous change, "Photoroom cuts Ultra video output from 4K to HD as prices stay unchanged" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Photoroom moves Enterprise to usage-based annual balance pricing and rewrites plan taglines" (Oct 19, 2026), "Photoroom triples AI credit pool across Pro, Max, Ultra plans—no price hike" (Aug 17, 2026) and "Photoroom: AI credits cut up to 73% + AI Backgrounds split into 4 tiers" (Aug 11, 2026). Photoroom has been tracked since 2026-06-26.
