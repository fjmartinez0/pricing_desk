What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Aptible's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: LLM Gateway base fee cut from $400/mo to $200/mo + token usage. Startup Program credit fell to $100/mo, and the token benefit changed from $50/mo recurring credits to a $100 one-time starting balance. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Production at $499 and AI Gateway at $400.
It lands 14 days after Aptible's previous change, "Aptible: Endpoint (load balancer) price increase ($0.05→$0.06/hr)" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Aptible: AI Gateway add-on launched at $400/mo" (Jul 2, 2026). Aptible has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Railway, Vercel, Netlify, Render and Heroku also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
