What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Sumologic's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Introduced 'DojoAI Credits' add-on for Essentials and Enterprise Suite - the flat 10 prompts/user/day DojoAI cap is now 'Included 10 prompts/user/day' plus purchasable pooled monthly capacity; SOC Analyst Agent now requires SIEM + DojoAI Credit (previously just SIEM + activation). Added 'Root Cause Agent' to the DojoAI comparison table for Essentials and Enterprise Suite, status shown as 'In development'. In detail: Introduced a new 'DojoAI Credits' add-on: the flat 10 prompts/user/day cap on DojoAI (Mobot) is now labeled 'Included 10 prompts/user/day' with an option to 'Add DojoAI Credits for pooled monthly capacity' on both Essentials and Enterprise Suite. The SOC Analyst Agent's unlock requirement also changed from 'SIEM and additional activation required' to 'Requires SIEM + DojoAI Credit'; Added a new 'Root Cause Agent' row to the DojoAI comparison table for Essentials and Enterprise Suite ('Agent that automatically investigates alerts, correlates logs and metrics to deliver verifiable root cause findings, and helps engineers resolve incidents faster'), status shown as 'In development' for both plans. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $3.14 per TB scanned.
It lands 69 days after Sumologic's previous change, "Sumo Logic: New MCP Server AI integration added to Dojo AI" (Aug 11, 2026). Sumologic has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Dynatrace, Datadog, Google Cloud and Elastic also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
