Whatagraph launches IQ Agents with tiered usage and sales-priced add-ons
Verified Nov 2, 2026·Marketing & Content·Direction: up
The changeMax, Prime feature — feature added.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 26, 2026 → Nov 2, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Whatagraph's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: IQ Agents launched (nav moved off waitlist) with a new 'Agent usage on Max and Prime' section: Base usage included in every plan (reporting workflows/alerts, managed MCP); paid Mid and Heavy usage tiers (price via sales, per new FAQ) add computer use & browser and AI visibility, with video/motion ads on Heavy only. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Max at $812.
It lands 21 days after Whatagraph's previous change, "Whatagraph: IQ Agents graduates from early access to generally available" (Oct 12, 2026). Whatagraph has been tracked since 2023-11-29.
In the same window Supermetrics, DAT Freight & Analytics and Dash also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
