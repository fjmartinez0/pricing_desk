What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Huggingface's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Compute hardware pricing section renamed from 'Spaces Hardware' to 'Spaces and Jobs Hardware', extending the hardware price table to cover Jobs; hourly prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists PRO at $9, Team at $20, Enterprise at $50, Inference Endpoints - NVIDIA T4 AWS (4x) at $3 per hour and Inference Endpoints - NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 AWS (1x) at $2.75 per hour.
It lands 17 days after Huggingface's previous change, "Huggingface adds Nvidia RTX PRO 6000 GPUs to Spaces from $2.75/hr" (Oct 16, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Huggingface swaps PRO's 20x inference multiplier for a flat $2 monthly credit" (Oct 8, 2026). Huggingface has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window OpenAI, Anthropic, Modal, Cohere and Together AI also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
