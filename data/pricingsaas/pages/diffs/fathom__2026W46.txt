What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Fathom's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enterprise plan now shows a public price of $35/month per user (annual only) instead of 'Talk to sales'; Enterprise plan now lists custom-built summaries designed for the organization and custom BAA eligibility. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with an upward direction. The current page lists Team at $15, Premium at $16 and Business at $25.
It lands 118 days after Fathom's previous change, "Fathom now offers up to 2 years free for startups, 10 free non-profit seats" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fathom adds HIPAA BAA and Okta SCIM in enterprise security push" (Jun 29, 2026) and "Fathom reveals per-tier lookback limits for Ask Fathom feature" (Jun 1, 2026). Fathom has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
