What happened
Between the Nov 18, 2026 and Nov 19, 2026 captures of Perplexity AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Computer model lineup: Google model renamed from 'Gemini 4 Hydrogen' to 'Gemini Neon' and now carries a 'New' badge. In detail: AI model lineup ("Multiple AI models deliver richer answers"): Google model updated from 'Gemini 4 Hydrogen' to 'Gemini Neon', now with a 'New' badge. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 22, 2026) lists Pro at $17 and Max at $167.

Before that Pulse recorded "Perplexity AI swaps Gemini 3.8 Flash for Google's new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model" (Nov 14, 2026), "Perplexity AI adds xAI's Grok 4.7 to Pro lineup, drops Anthropic's Claude Haiku 5.5" (Oct 9, 2026) and "Perplexity AI swaps Grok 4.7 for Claude Haiku 5.5 in model lineup" (Oct 8, 2026).
