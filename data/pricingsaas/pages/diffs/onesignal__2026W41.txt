What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of OneSignal's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: A/B Testing row in comparison table now quantifies limits: Free/Growth 'Standard' became up to 2 variants; Pro/Enterprise 'Multivariate' became up to 10 variants. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Growth at $19.
It lands 28 days after OneSignal's previous change, "OneSignal swaps unlimited free push for a 1,000 MAU cap" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OneSignal: New Audit Logs retention disclosure (48hrs–90 days by plan)" (Jul 13, 2026). OneSignal has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
