What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Fastly's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Network Protect security product added to the Secure pricing list: always-on network defense, no scrubbing centers or hardware; contact sales for pricing. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. The current page lists Compute Starter at $500, Network Services Basic at $1,500, Observability Starter at $1,500 and Network Services Starter at $6,000.
It lands 21 days after Fastly's previous change, "Fastly Default launches AI Platform: usage-based Runtime Control from $1.75" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fastly Default: Mutual TLS limit relabeled from 'mTLS' to 'domains' (quantities unchanged)" (Jul 27, 2026). Fastly has been tracked since 2023-12-09.
In the same window Akamai Technologies, Limelight Software, Google Cloud and Cloudflare also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
