What happened
Between the Nov 19, 2026 and Nov 20, 2026 captures of Raycast's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added Raycast MCP (Model Context Protocol server) to the Pro+ plan, letting AI apps like Claude Code and ChatGPT control Raycast. In detail: Added new "Raycast MCP" capability (Model Context Protocol server) letting AI apps like Claude Code and ChatGPT control Raycast; explicitly marked "New" on the core-features list and comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 22, 2026) lists Pro at $8, Teams Pro at $12, Pro+ at $16, Teams Pro+ at $20 and Pro Max at $40.
It lands 67 days after Raycast's previous change, "Raycast adds $50 Max plan, splits Pro into 3 AI-credit tiers, drops $8 add-on" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Raycast locks AI behind paid plans, kills 50 free messages" (Aug 24, 2026). Raycast has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
