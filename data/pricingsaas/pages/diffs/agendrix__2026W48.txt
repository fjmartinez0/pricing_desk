What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Agendrix's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Payroll product section added: $30 per pay run + $2.25 per employee paid; includes payroll processing, government remittances, T4/RL-1 slips, timesheet data, no setup fees. It lands 77 days after Agendrix's previous change, "Agendrix: ISO/IEC 27701 compliance claim dropped" (Sep 6, 2026). Agendrix has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
