What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Odoo's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Odoo introduced a new 'Light User' license at US$8.90, positioned beneath the Custom plan pricing with a 'More info' link; two new FAQ entries explain the restricted-access role; Odoo added 'Agentic AI' to the Custom plan's included capabilities, listed alongside Odoo Studio. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $31.1, Custom at $61 and Light User at $8.9.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Odoo. Odoo has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
