What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Enpass's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Enpass restructured personal plans: added a new Free Plan ($0 forever, 25 items); discontinued the Lifetime Plan ($99.99 one-time); renamed Individual Plan to Premium Plan with an Annual/3-year billing toggle; removed the End of Summer Sale banner and the Premium plan sale discount (Family Plan retains its ongoing 25% off). In detail: Pricing restructured: new Free Plan added ($0/25 items); Lifetime Plan ($99.99) discontinued; Individual Plan renamed Premium (3-yr toggle); End of Summer Sale discount removed. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists 3-Year Plan at $1.39, Premium Plan at $1.99 and Family Plan at $2.99.
It lands 35 days after Enpass's previous change, "Enpass: Promotional discount removed, Family & 3-Year plans up ($2.99→$3.99/mo, $47.99→$49.99)" (Aug 10, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Enpass slashes prices: Individual Plan 40% off, 3-Year Plan drops to $47.99" (Jul 27, 2026), "Enpass: One-Time Plan ($99.99 lifetime) discontinued + Independence Day sale ended" (Jul 20, 2026) and "Enpass launches Independence Day Sale with up to 40% off" (Jun 30, 2026). Enpass has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Lastpass, NordPass, Dashlane, 1password and Bitwarden also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
