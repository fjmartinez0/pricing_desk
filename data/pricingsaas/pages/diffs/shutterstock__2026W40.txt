What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Shutterstock's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Yearly/Monthly/No contract billing toggle removed from top of pricing page; all three subscription plans now display prices prefixed with 'From' instead of a single fixed monthly rate. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Image downloads at $25, Unlimited Images at $29, Image packs at $29 subscription and Unlimited Plus at $69.
Shutterstock's previous recorded change was "Shutterstock Enhances Enterprise Offerings with Custom Licenses, AI Features, and Increased Asset Library to 500M+" (Q2 2024). Before that Pulse recorded "Shutterstock Implements Major Pricing Overhaul: Shifts from 'Credits' to 'Downloads' and Removes Free Trial Option" (Q1 2024). Shutterstock has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Canva, Getty Images and Adobe also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
