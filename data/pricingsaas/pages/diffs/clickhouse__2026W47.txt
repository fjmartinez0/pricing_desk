What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of ClickHouse's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: AWS self-serve compute+storage starting price cut from $0.125 to $0.056/unit/hr (55% decrease); the Postgres 'BETA — 50% off during beta' promo badge was also removed from the cost-estimator service-type selector; ClickHouse Managed Postgres moved from Beta to General Availability ('Available in Beta on all three plans' → 'Available on all three plans'); description updated to 'Fully managed Post. In detail: ClickHouse Managed Postgres moved from Beta to General Availability ('Available in Beta on all three plans' → 'Available on all three plans'); description updated to 'Fully managed Postgres with NVMe-backed performance and native connectivity to ClickHouse for real-time analytics.'. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature change, price decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Nov 17, 2026) lists Basic at $25.30 Storage - per TB + $0.22 Compute - per unit/hr, Scale at $25.30 Storage - per TB + $0.30 Compute - per unit/hr and Enterprise at $25.30 Storage - per TB + $0.39 Compute - per unit/hr.
It lands 35 days after ClickHouse's previous change, "ClickHouse hides starting prices on plan cards, drops Basic's $53 entry point" (Oct 11, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ClickHouse adds headline plan prices from $53 to $571 per month" (Oct 4, 2026). ClickHouse has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window Datadog and Elastic also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
