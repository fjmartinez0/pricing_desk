What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Hightouch's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Agentic Marketing Platform product list renamed: Ad Studio -> AMP for Advertising, Lifecycle Studio -> AMP for Lifecycle; no price or inclusion changes.
