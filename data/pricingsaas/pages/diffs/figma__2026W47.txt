What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Figma's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Figma Design feature list gains '3D transforms (Beta)' (design in 3D with depth, perspective, CSS compatibility). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Organization at $5, Enterprise at $5 and Professional at $16.
This weekly roll-up includes the change Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Figma adds 3D transforms beta, bringing native depth and perspective design" (Day 317, 2026, saved Nov 13, 2026). It is the same change, not a new one. Before that, Figma's previous recorded change was "Figma opens Specs beta to all seats, no longer limited to paid seats" (Oct 29, 2026). Figma has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Framer, Affinity, Sketch, Webflow and Penpot also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
