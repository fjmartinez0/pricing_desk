What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Katalon's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pricing restructured: added a $0 Free tier, renamed Individual to Professional (price unchanged, $184->$84/seat/mo), and removed the Small Teams/True Automation plan ($209->$167/seat/mo) from the default view; Enterprise plan tagline changed from 'System of record for teams & AI agents' to 'For regulated and large-scale organizations', repositioning the target audience toward regulated/large-scal. In detail: Enterprise plan tagline changed from 'System of record for teams & AI agents' to 'For regulated and large-scale organizations', repositioning the target audience toward regulated/large-scale orgs. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change, positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Professional at $84, Katalon Runtime Engine at $145, Cross Browser at $158 and Mobile Devices at $158.
It lands 21 days after Katalon's previous change, "Katalon: plan tiers consolidated to Individual/Small teams, True Platform moved to add-on" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Katalon slashes Studio Enterprise price 44%, adds per-seat tiered pricing" (Aug 24, 2026), "Katalon splits Studio Enterprise into standalone $229/seat plan" (Jul 20, 2026) and "Katalon ditches monthly seats for annual tiers, adds AI MCP server integration" (Jul 6, 2026). Katalon has been tracked since 2026-04-15.
