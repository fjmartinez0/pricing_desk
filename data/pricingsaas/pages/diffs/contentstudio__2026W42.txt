What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of ContentStudio's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Quarterly billing option added to the billing toggle: -14% on Standard/Advanced/Agency plans and -11% on the API plan, alongside existing Monthly and Yearly options. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added) with a neutral direction. The current page lists API Plan at $15, Standard at $19, Advanced at $49 and Agency Unlimited at $99.
It lands 105 days after ContentStudio's previous change, "ContentStudio reveals monthly rates: up to 40% more than annual plans" (Jun 29, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ContentStudio: blog publishing & curated content automation removed" (Jul 2, 2026) and "ContentStudio rebrands video credits as AI video clipping credits" (Jun 1, 2026). ContentStudio has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Coschedule, Sproutsocial and Buffer also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
