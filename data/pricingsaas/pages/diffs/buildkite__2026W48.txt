What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Buildkite's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Test Engine adds OpenTelemetry traces: Free 1GB/month hard cap, Pro 10GB/month then $0.50/GB, Enterprise 600GB/year then $0.50/GB. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Pro Plan at $30 and Enterprise Plan at $0.50 OpenTelemetry traces GB per year.
It lands 21 days after Buildkite's previous change, "Buildkite adds Windows hosted agents at $0.008 per vCPU minute" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Buildkite Pro now lists overage rates for storage and downloads" (Oct 18, 2026). Buildkite has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window GitLab, Aws, Google Cloud, GitHub and CircleCI also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
