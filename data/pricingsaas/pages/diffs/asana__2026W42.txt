Asana opens Timesheets and Budgets add-on to Starter and Advanced plans
Verified Oct 12, 2026·Project Management·Direction: neutral
The changeTimesheets and Budgets, Starter, Advanced add-on — add-on change.
1 change·2 captures·Oct 5, 2026 → Oct 12, 2026
What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Asana's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Timesheets and Budgets add-on now available on Starter, Advanced or Enterprise plans, previously Enterprise only. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $10.99, Advanced at $24.99 and AI Teammates at $0.6 per AI request (pay as you go) + $0.5 per AI request (prepaid usage pack).
It lands 101 days after Asana's previous change, "Asana: Enterprise+ demoted from standalone card to inline upsell" (Jul 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Asana removes standalone Enterprise+ plan, now part of Enterprise tier" (May 4, 2026), "Asana Lists Timesheets Add-On at $5.99/User/Month, Exits Sales-Only Model" (Apr 11, 2026) and "Asana reveals monthly pricing options and new Timesheets add-on at $5.99/user" (Mar 16, 2026). Asana has been tracked since 2023-11-17.
In the same window Microsoft, Notion and Trello also changed their Project Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
