What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of PageProof's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Team Plus increased $3,999/yr ($399/mo) to $4,999/yr ($499/mo), 25% hike; Team unchanged at $2,499/yr ($249/mo). new AI 'Proofing Intelligence' (PI) credits + Insights/PreProof bundled into every plan (Team 250 credits, Team Plus +500 credits, Enterprise 1,000 credits). In detail: New AI-powered 'Proofing Intelligence' (PI) credits and Insights/PreProof features bundled into every plan: Team gets 250 PI credits + Insights Dashboard & Reporting; Team Plus gets +500 PI credits + Insights Proofs & Comments + PreProof™; Enterprise gets 1,000 PI credits + PI Agents + PreProof™ with Agents; Team Plus plan price increased from $3,999/year ($399/month) to $4,999/year ($499/month), a 25% increase. Team plan price unchanged at $2,499/year ($249/month). Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Team at $249 and Team Plus at $333.25.
It lands 7 days after PageProof's previous change, "PageProof cuts Team Plus to $399/mo, drops FinalFile and AI table rows" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "PageProof adds MCP integration to Intelligence suite, renames Folders to Fold" (Oct 5, 2026), "PageProof touts scheduled proofs on Team Plus, digital signing on Enterprise" (Sep 7, 2026) and "PageProof Team Plus tier jumps 25% to $499/month, annual scales too" (Aug 17, 2026). PageProof has been tracked since 2026-03-02.
