Livekit adds Twilio and WhatsApp connectors, tiered $0.004/min pricing
Verified Sep 28, 2026·Software Development & Testing·Direction: up
The changeAdd-on — new add-on.

What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Livekit's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Twilio connector and WhatsApp connector add-ons let customers bridge Twilio Programmable Voice and WhatsApp Business calls into LiveKit rooms, tiered by concurrent calls (5/20/100/Custom) with $0.004/min and $0.003/min overage pricing. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Ship at $50 and Scale at $500.
It lands 84 days after Livekit's previous change, "LiveKit lets Scale plan users request higher Inference concurrency limits" (Jul 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LiveKit: Zero data retention + Custom voices added to LiveKit Inference" (Jul 3, 2026). Livekit has been tracked since 2025-01-03.
In the same window Zoom, Pexip, Twilio, Ringcentral and Agora also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
