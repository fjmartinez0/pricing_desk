What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Tango's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Voice Transcription is now included in the Free plan (previously not included; available in Pro and Enterprise). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Pro (Team) at $15 and Pro (Personal) at $22.
It lands 14 days after Tango's previous change, "Tango adds Guide Me to Free and an MCP connector to Enterprise" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Tango adds MCP integration in beta to paid workspace plans, Free tier left out" (Oct 5, 2026). Tango has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Slack, Microsoft, GoTo, Zoom and Google also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
