What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of LendingWise's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Plan cards rebuilt: Starter gains Loan Inbox/SMS and required-doc hard stops; Pro gains loan servicing, fund management, draw management and AI doc review; MCP/AI agents API access added; Elite Loan Servicing, previously listed without limit, now capped at 150 active loans, with 3 funds/200 investors and 1,000 AI doc review docs/mo; Enterprise stays unlimited; Month-to-month contracts dropped: t. In detail: Month-to-month contracts dropped: the footnote 'Month-to-month, with quarterly or annual options at discounted pricing' is now 'Annual agreement: pay monthly, or save with quarterly or annual billing', and the FAQ 'We do not believe in forcing annual contracts' answer was removed; Annual/Monthly billing toggle added (Annual default, 'Save 20%'): the old $119/$600/$1,595 list prices are now annual-billed rates, while paying monthly costs $149/$749/$1,995 for Starter/Pro/Elite (+25%). Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature added, limit decrease, package change, price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $119, Pro at $600 and Elite at $1,595.
It lands 28 days after LendingWise's previous change, "LendingWise adds custom-priced Enterprise plan above Elite tier" (Oct 5, 2026). LendingWise has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Zoho, Zamp, Tyms, Stripe and ParentPay Group also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
