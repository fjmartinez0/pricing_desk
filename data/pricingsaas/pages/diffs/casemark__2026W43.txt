What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Casemark's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pay-per-deliverable pricing (summary $25, medical $25-$100, transcription $5/hr) replaced by Para seat plans: Standard $20, Pro $100 per seat/month, Enterprise contact us; seat fee credited to usage wallet. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Standard at $20 and Pro at $100.
It lands 35 days after Casemark's previous change, "Casemark overhauls AI usage billing, reveals $200/user BAA pricing" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CaseMark: Pro plan legal agent skills library expanded (800+ → 880+)" (Aug 17, 2026). Casemark has been tracked since 2024-11-16.
