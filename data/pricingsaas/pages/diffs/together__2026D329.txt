What happened
Between the Nov 24, 2026 and Nov 25, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Qwen4 Max promo (valid until 11/23/2026) expired; price reverted from $1.05/$0.0525(cached)/$3.15 to $1.50/$0.075(cached)/$4.50 per 1M tokens. New Kimi K3.1 Pro (US geo-pinned) model added at $2.50/$0.25(cached)/$12.50 per 1M tokens. In detail: Added new 'Kimi K3.1 Pro - US geo-pinned' model to the dedicated/geo-pinned pricing table at $2.50 input / $0.25 cached / $12.50 output per 1M tokens; Qwen4 Max's limited-time promo (valid until 11/23/2026) expired; pricing reverted from discounted $1.05 input / $0.0525 cached / $3.15 output back to full $1.50 input / $0.075 cached / $4.50 output per 1M tokens. Pulse files it as a pricing change (new add-on, discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100), GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days), Sandbox (Code Sandbox) at $0.04 per vCPU/hour + $0.01 per GiB RAM/hour and Serverless Inference at $0.15 per 1M input tokens.
It lands one day after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI: new Fast tier for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash ($0.60/1M input)" (Nov 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI: Qwen4 Max price hike to $4.50 output + 30% promo, adds Tencent Hy4" (Nov 20, 2026), "Together AI adds Qwen4 Max model at $1.43/$4.28 per 1M tokens" (Nov 19, 2026) and "Together AI adds Elorian Glimpse 1.0 ($0.60/$3.00), drops Qwen2.5 7B Turbo" (Nov 18, 2026).
