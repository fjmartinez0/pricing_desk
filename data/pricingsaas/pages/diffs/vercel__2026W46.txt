What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Vercel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Trace retention increased and metric renamed from Session Tracing to Trace Retention: Hobby 1 hour to 3 days, Pro 1 day to 14 days, Enterprise 3 days to 14 days; Tracing Spans Beta badge dropped. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $20.
It lands 3 days after Vercel's previous change, "Vercel boosts Pro trace retention 14x to 14 days, Hobby to 3 days" (Nov 6, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Vercel adds usage pricing for Beta CI and Observability monitors" (Nov 2, 2026), "Vercel slashes Firewall limits, adds metered Deployment Storage at $0.10/GB" (Oct 26, 2026) and "Vercel: Vercel Firewall limits cut: Enterprise Custom Firewall Rules 1,000→250; IP Blocking Hobby 3→10, Pro 100→40,…" (Oct 26, 2026). Vercel has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Render, Aws and Railway also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
