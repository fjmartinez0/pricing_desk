What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Markate's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Owner Operator annual price rose from $39.95 to $45.95/month ($479.40 to $551.40/yr); annual discount reduced from 20% to 8%. Team base price shown also rose to $45.95. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Team at $5, Owner Operator at $39.95, Automated Lead Capture Contact Form at $10, Customer Finder 360 at $1.1 per postcard and Winning Sales Proposal Templates at $10.
It lands 181 days after Markate's previous change, "Markate Corrects Annual Savings to 20%, Renames Paid Feature Labels" (Apr 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Markate Repositions as Service Operations Platform With Pay-Per-Use Model" (Apr 1, 2026) and "Markate Raises Base Plan Prices 25% to $49.95/mo Across Tiers" (Mar 10, 2026).
