What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Pulumi's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: All four Pulumi plan tiers renamed (Individual->Free, Team->Essentials, Enterprise->Pro, Business Critical->Enterprise); new paid Support plan tier added (Standard $625/mo to Platinum $50,000/yr); Free plan dropped Pulumi Neo access but gained Pulumi Deployments and basic Pulumi ESC; Essentials replaced community support with SLO-backed ticket support; Enterprise gained BYOK for Neo; Essentials. In detail: Essentials plan removed its 10-user cap (now unlimited); custom policy pack limits changed from 25 policies/1 pack to 10 packs uncapped, and policy enforcement mode downgraded to Advisory only. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase, package change, feature change) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Essentials at $40, Pro at $400, Standard Support at $625, Bronze Support at $1,250 and Silver Support at $2,916.67.
It lands 96 days after Pulumi's previous change, "Pulumi Neo adds per-token pricing at $3/M after 5M free tier" (Jun 17, 2026). Pulumi has been tracked since 2026-05-02.
In the same window Google Cloud and Hashicorp also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
