What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of DigiCert's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Basic-OV and Secure Site-OV SSL cards now show a 'Convert this to a wildcard domain' option, extending wildcard-domain purchasing (previously exclusive to Secure Site Pro-OV) to both lower OV assurance tiers, with new wildcard prices of $82/mo (Basic-OV) and $206/mo (Secure Site-OV). A 'Recommended' badge now appears above the Secure Site Pro-OV card. Standard-domain prices are unchanged at $31/$52/$129 per month. In detail: A 'Recommended' badge now appears above the Secure Site Pro - OV card, steering Organization Validation buyers to the top SSL/TLS tier. No badge appeared on any OV tier in the prior capture (the Extended Validation track already badged Secure Site Pro - EV); Basic-OV and Secure Site-OV SSL cards now show a 'Convert this to a wildcard domain' link, extending wildcard-domain purchasing (previously exclusive to Secure Site Pro-OV) to both lower OV assurance tiers. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 21, 2026) lists Secure Email for Individual at $4, Secure Email for Employee at $9, Basic - OV at $26, PKIo Private Person - Authentication/Encryption - USB Token at $33 and PKIo Private Professional - Authentication/Encryption - USB Token at $33.
It lands 63 days after DigiCert's previous change, "DigiCert: New X9 PKI Certificates + Trust Lifecycle Manager" (Jul 13, 2026). DigiCert has been tracked since 2026-05-04.
In the same window Google also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
