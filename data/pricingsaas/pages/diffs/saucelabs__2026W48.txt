What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Sauce Labs' pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Real Device Cloud ($199/mo annual, $249 month-to-month) became "Automated and Live on Virtual & Real Devices" at $209/mo annual, $259 month-to-month (+$10); Sauce Mobile App Distribution no longer lists $249/mo annual, $299 monthly; now "Available as a standalone product. Contact sales for pricing."; Sauce Visual add-on now lists a paid upgrade: 10,000 snapshots/mo for $79 (500 snapshots/mo still. In detail: Sauce Visual add-on now lists a paid upgrade: 10,000 snapshots/mo for $79 (500 snapshots/mo still included). Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden, price increase, new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Live Testing at $39, Virtual Device Cloud at $149 and Real Device Cloud at $209.
It lands 35 days after Sauce Labs' previous change, "Sauce Labs cuts parallel test sessions from 24 to 5 on device clouds" (Oct 19, 2026). Sauce Labs has been tracked since 2026-02-20.
In the same window Katalon, BrowserStack and Lambdatest also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
