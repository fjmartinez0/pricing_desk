What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of LeadMagic's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Per-plan table seat limits (1/1/2/3) replaced by Tables preview where everyone with access can edit free; the $49 per-seat requirement for team access is removed; Per-plan Finder & Validation rate limits added (300/600/1,000/1,500 RPM; previously 600/min across standard plans), plus 10M records/month cap on unmetered API browse for Pro and Ultimate. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Essential at $83, Growth at $208, Professional at $416 and Ultimate at $708.
It lands 14 days after LeadMagic's previous change, "LeadMagic now charges per search result, cuts shared seats to 1-3 per plan" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LeadMagic overhauls seats: Basic to 1, Essential's "20" cuts to 3" (Aug 31, 2026). LeadMagic has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Seamless.AI and Lusha also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
