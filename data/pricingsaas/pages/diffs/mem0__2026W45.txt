What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Mem0's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Four-tier pricing (Hobby, Starter $19, Pro $249, Enterprise) replaced by two plans: Free and Custom; self-serve Starter and Pro removed, Enterprise renamed Custom (talk to us); Free tier now gives 10,000 adds/1,000 retrievals for 30 days, then 50,000 adds/10,000 retrievals monthly with own LLM key plus usage-based billing; previously 10,000 adds/1,000 retrievals; User profiles feature added to the.
