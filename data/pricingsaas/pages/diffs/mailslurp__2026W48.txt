What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of MailSlurp's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Apple Mail & Outlook desktop previews now covered in included preview runs for Free and Starter (previously not included); Rendering allowance unit changed from 'Device previews' to 'Preview runs' on all plans (Free 1, Starter 5, Pro 10, Growth 200 /mo): each included run now covers one email across all selected clients, including premium devices; usage beyond the allowance is billed per successf. In detail: Rendering allowance unit changed from 'Device previews' to 'Preview runs' on all plans (Free 1, Starter 5, Pro 10, Growth 200 /mo): each included run now covers one email across all selected clients, including premium devices; usage beyond the allowance is billed per successful target; Starter is no longer hard-capped on rendering: 'Fixed limits. Upgrade for more.' replaced by 'Additional rendering is billed per successful target' — Starter can now continue past its 5 included preview runs at the per-target rate (from $0.50), previously only Pro and Growth could. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change, packaging change, feature added). The current page lists Starter at $19.17, Pro at $45.83, Growth at $166.58, Inbox placement at $1 per test and Device rendering at $0.50 per render.
It lands 14 days after MailSlurp's previous change, "MailSlurp hikes Growth plan 53% to $199/mo, adds 7-day trial on Starter" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MailSlurp hikes Pro plan to $69, halves Free email allowance to 250" (Nov 1, 2026), "MailSlurp adds inbox placement testing to all plans, gates warmup to top tiers" (Oct 11, 2026) and "MailSlurp adds $0.01/credit email warmup usage rate + 1 more change" (Oct 4, 2026). MailSlurp has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
