What happened
Between the Nov 25, 2026 and Nov 26, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: GPU Clusters preemptible hourly rates rose: NVIDIA HGX H100 $1.99 to $2.00 and HGX H200 $2.99 to $3.00 per GPU per hour. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100), GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days), Sandbox (Code Sandbox) at $0.04 per vCPU/hour + $0.01 per GiB RAM/hour and Serverless Inference at $0.15 per 1M input tokens.
It lands one day after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI: Qwen4 Max promo expires + 1 more change" (Nov 25, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI: new Fast tier for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash ($0.60/1M input)" (Nov 24, 2026), "Together AI: Qwen4 Max price hike to $4.50 output + 30% promo, adds Tencent Hy4" (Nov 20, 2026) and "Together AI adds Qwen4 Max model at $1.43/$4.28 per 1M tokens" (Nov 19, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
In the same window OpenAI, Mistral AI, Huggingface, Anthropic and Lambda Labs also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
