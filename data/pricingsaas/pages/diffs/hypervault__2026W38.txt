What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Hypervault's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Business and Enterprise plans add 'Device management' (view/manage devices accessing the workspace) and an 'Enable or disable private vault' admin toggle, new in both plan cards and the comparison table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Individual+ at EUR 12, Business at EUR 39, Families at EUR 49, Enterprise at EUR 59 and Storage Add-on at EUR 10.
It lands 73 days after Hypervault's previous change, "Hypervault: Families plan gains monthly billing at €5.99/mo" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hypervault adds monthly family plan at €5.99, raises support thresholds" (Jun 23, 2026). Hypervault has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Enpass, Lastpass, NordPass, Dashlane and 1password also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
