What happened
Between the Nov 4, 2026 and Nov 5, 2026 captures of DeepSeek's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Removed the note that Search/Fetch API calls made via DeepSeek Harness, Claude Code, or Codex were free of charge until Nov 1, 2026 — per-request billing ($0.75/1,000 requests) now applies to those calls as listed in the table. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists DeepSeek Flash at $0.15 per 1M input tokens (cache miss) + $0.30 per 100M input tokens (cache hit) and DeepSeek V4 Pro at $0.66 per 1M input tokens (cache miss) + $2.20 per 100M input tokens (cache hit).
It lands 17 days after DeepSeek's previous change, "DeepSeek launches Search and Fetch APIs at $0.75 per 1K requests from Nov 1" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "DeepSeek launches Search and Fetch APIs at $0.75 per 1,000 requests" (Oct 17, 2026), "DeepSeek: peak-hour billing now excludes Chinese public holidays" (Sep 21, 2026) and "DeepSeek extends 50% off-peak API discount to Chinese public holidays all day" (Sep 20, 2026).
