What happened
Between the Nov 10, 2026 and Nov 11, 2026 captures of MindsDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Muse Ember 1.0 (Meta) model listed at $0.10 input / $0.50 output per 1M tokens; new Decision and embedding models table (Text Embedding 3 small $0.02, Jev 1.13.0 $0). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Pay as you go at $0.10 input tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M) + $0.50 output tokens (MindsHub Air, per 1M).
It lands 4 days after MindsDB's previous change, "MindsDB adds Claude Haiku 5.5 at $0.10 input, $0.50 output per 1M tokens" (Nov 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "MindsDB adds GPT-6.1 Terra pricing at $1 input, $5 output per 1M tokens" (Oct 29, 2026) and "MindsDB adds Kimi K3.1 Pro and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model pricing" (Oct 27, 2026). MindsDB has been tracked since 2026-03-01.
In the same window Huggingface and Alteryx also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
