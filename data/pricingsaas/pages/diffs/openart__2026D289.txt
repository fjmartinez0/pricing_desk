What happened
Between the Oct 15, 2026 and Oct 16, 2026 captures of OpenArt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Expired Oct 14 promos removed: up to 50% off all Seedance models + Unlimited Flux 3 Image on Wonder, and 30% off Nano Banana 2.1 generations; per-model Seedance/Nano Banana OFF badges and Flux 3 Image UNLIMITED badge dropped from Pro/Wonder cards. Flux 3 720p unlimited-videos banner (Wonder, until Oct 31) remains. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Essential at $7, Advanced at $14.5, Team Plan at $17.5, Infinite at $28 and Wonder at $120.
It lands 7 days after OpenArt's previous change, "OpenArt Pro dropped from Seedance/Flux 3 promo; Wonder-only deal ends Oct 14" (Oct 9, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenArt cuts Nano Banana 2.1 generations 30% for upgraders through Oct 14" (Oct 7, 2026), "OpenArt offers unlimited Flux 3 720p videos on Wonder plan until Oct 31" (Oct 6, 2026) and "OpenArt launches up to 50% off Seedance and unlimited Flux 3 Image" (Oct 5, 2026). OpenArt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
In the same window Huggingface, Runway, Microsoft and Leonardo.ai also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
