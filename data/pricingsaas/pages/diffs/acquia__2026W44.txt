What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Acquia's pricing page, Pulse recorded 4 changes: Acquia Search quotas reshuffled (Elite 4.2M/1M docs to 250K/250K; Premium 850K to 250K queries); support contacts raised from 4/4/10 to Unlimited on all tiers; Removed Acquia Site Studio (1 Site included) row and Acquia Migrate Accelerate (AMA) feature row from the plan comparison table; Code Studio and Content Hub add-ons removed from the Cloud Platform Add Ons list; Cloud IDE and Edge remain; El. In detail: Elite plan's 99.99% Uptime SLA changed from included to Add-On (now paid on both Premium and Elite). Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on removed, feature removed, limit decrease) with a downward direction.
