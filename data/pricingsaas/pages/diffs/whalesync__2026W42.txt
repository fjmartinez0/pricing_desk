What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Whalesync's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Starter price halved from $32/mo to $16/mo at 2,000 records (billed yearly $384 to $192). Plus now starts at a new 2,000-record tier for $32/mo ($384/yr), down from a 5,000-record minimum at $64/mo ($768/yr). Pro unchanged at $160/mo; Largest self-serve record tier cut from 50,000 to 20,000 on Plus and Pro (50,000 option removed); book-a-demo threshold lowered from 50,000 to 20,000 records. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease, limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $40, Plus at $80 and Pro at $200.
It lands 7 days after Whalesync's previous change, "Whalesync adds Copper to Plus plan's two-way CRM sync connectors" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Whalesync adds Close, Klaviyo, Zoho CRM to Plus; restricts Stripe to one-way " (Sep 28, 2026), "Whalesync: free trial now needs no credit card" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Whalesync: free trial cut from 2 weeks to 7 days on all plans" (Aug 24, 2026). Whalesync has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Zapier, Workato, Make and MuleSoft also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
