Penpot drops Enterprise Add-ons section, bills Unlimited per editor
Verified Sep 21, 2026·Software Development & Testing·Direction: down
The changeEnterprise feature — feature removed.
2 changes·2 captures·Sep 14, 2026 → Sep 21, 2026
What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Penpot's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Removed the full feature-comparison grid and entire 'Enterprise Add-ons' section (Audit logs, SCIM role assignment, Guest access controls, Plugins Allowlist incl. Agentic AI, centralized admin console, org management, advanced team permissions), replaced by a condensed ~15-row table; Billing unit relabeled on both paid tiers: Unlimited now bills per editor ($7/editor/month, was $7/user/month) and. In detail: Replaced the full feature-comparison grid (Design, Design systems, Prototype, Collaboration, Devtools, Administration & Security, Support) and the Enterprise Add-ons card with a condensed 8-row table. SSO, choice of hosting region and IP whitelisting carried over; audit logs, SCIM role assignment, guest access controls, Plugins Allowlist incl. Agentic AI and the centralized admin console are no longer disclosed anywhere on the page; Billing unit relabeled on both paid tiers: Unlimited now bills per editor ($7/editor/month, was $7/user/month) and Enterprise now bills per member ($25/member/month, was $25/user/month). List prices are unchanged -- only the billed seat type changed. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature removed, metric change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Unlimited at $7, Enterprise at $25 and Private Server at $4,166.67.
It lands 70 days after Penpot's previous change, "Penpot slashes Enterprise pre-order discount from 50% to 25% off" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Penpot halves Enterprise version-history retention window to 90 days" (Jul 6, 2026) and "Penpot: Enterprise plan restructured from a flat capped fee (never pay more than $950/month regardless of team size)…" (Jul 4, 2026). Penpot has been tracked since 2026-03-19.
