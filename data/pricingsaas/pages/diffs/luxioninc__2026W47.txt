What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of KeyShot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: KeyShot Studio Business: Office Hours now labeled Americas only; Network Rendering options reworded as add-on options (not included). Pulse files it as a product change (feature change). The current page lists KeyShot Studio for Education (EDU) at $7.92, KeyShot Professional at $108.25, KeyShot Business at $133.25, KeyShot Network Rendering at $62.42 and KeyShot Studio NX Plugin at $29.
It lands 7 days after KeyShot's previous change, "KeyShot adds Application management to KeyShot Enterprise plan" (Nov 8, 2026). KeyShot has been tracked since 2026-07-04.
