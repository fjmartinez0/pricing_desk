What happened
Between the Nov 10, 2026 and Nov 11, 2026 captures of Anthropic's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Claude Dashboards (beta) rebranded as Claude Data; new Claude Sheets (beta) and Claude Whiteboards (beta) tools added for Pro, Max, and Ultra plans (not Free). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Pro at $17, Team (Standard seat) at $20, Enterprise (self-serve) at $20, Max at $100 and Team (Premium seat) at $100.
It lands 11 days after Anthropic's previous change, "Anthropic splits Max into Max ($100) and Ultra ($200), replacing Max 20x" (Oct 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Anthropic gives Max subscribers monthly API credits, up to $200" (Oct 11, 2026), "Anthropic opens Claude Design, Slides and Docs to Free plan users" (Oct 9, 2026) and "Anthropic API launches Haiku 5.5 at $0.10/$0.50, halves Sonnet 5.5 cache reads" (Oct 8, 2026). Anthropic has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window OpenAI, X, Microsoft, Google and Mistral AI also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
