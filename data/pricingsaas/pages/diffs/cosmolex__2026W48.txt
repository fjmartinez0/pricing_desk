What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of CosmoLex's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: 'More ways to grow' add-on showcase swaps the CosmoLex Pay card for a new Sign & Share card (secure file sharing, built-in eSignature, audit trail). Payments remain built into every plan and LexShare/LexSign was already listed as an add-on, so no entitlement or price change. No prices shown. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Standard at $99, Elite at $129, CosmoLex Websites at $149 and CosmoLex CRM at $147.
It lands 98 days after CosmoLex's previous change, "CosmoLex: Pricing overhauled to Core $99/Pro $129 per-user plans" (Aug 17, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "CosmoLex: Plan pricing hidden — Standard/Elite prices removed from page" (Jul 2, 2026). CosmoLex has been tracked since 2026-03-20.
In the same window Clio, Rocket Matter and MyCase also changed their Finance & Accounting pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
