What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Prowler's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Private Cloud/Enterprise tier now offers AWS Marketplace purchase and deployment in customer's AWS account; deployment copy lists AWS explicitly alongside other clouds. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Prowler Cloud at $79.
It lands 21 days after Prowler's previous change, "Prowler: Prowler Cloud free trial shortened from 15 days to 7 days (no credit card still required); paid prices…" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Prowler ditches resource-copy for cloud-accounts calculator, adds free trial" (Sep 28, 2026). Prowler has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Yousign, PreVeil, NordVPN, Jumpcloud and ExpressVPN also changed their Cybersecurity & Identity Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
