What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Skyvia's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The 'Looker Studio Connector' pricing section was relabeled 'Data Studio Connector' in the on-page section nav and footer link; the linked anchor/URL and the $35/mo Standard plan itself are unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Backup Standard at $7, Connect Basic at $15, Query Standard at $15, Looker Studio Connector Standard at $28 and Connect Standard at $39.
It lands 48 days after Skyvia's previous change, "Skyvia: Enterprise adds detailed 'Custom annual package' feature list" (Aug 11, 2026). Skyvia has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
