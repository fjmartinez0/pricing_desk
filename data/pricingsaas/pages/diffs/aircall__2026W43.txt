What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Aircall's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Number Reputation Dashboard add-on at $0.03 per reputation check, showing how carriers label your numbers (Healthy, At Risk, or Spam); Branded caller ID add-on now billed $0.08 per call/brand instead of $0.08 per call. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change, new add-on) with an upward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $30, Professional at $50, AI Voice Agents at $100, Aircall for Salesforce Voice at $30 and WhatsApp in Aircall at $10.
It lands 21 days after Aircall's previous change, "Aircall: Custom→Enterprise rename + AI Voice Agents plan ($100/mo min)" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Aircall pulls free AIVA minutes promo tied to World Tournament campaign" (Jul 27, 2026). Aircall has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Microsoft and Twilio also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
