What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Sketch's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free MCP server added to all subscriptions and the Mac-only license (Standard card, compare table, FAQ: no AI credits or add-ons); prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $12, Professional at $24, Enterprise at $44 and Mac License at $120.
It lands 121 days after Sketch's previous change, "Sketch renames Business to Professional, shifts features, lowers Enterprise…" (Jul 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Sketch lowers Enterprise entry to 15 editors, reshuffles Pro perks" (Apr 13, 2026), "Sketch hides Private Cloud pricing, broadens Enterprise targeting" (Apr 3, 2026) and "Sketch Opens Enterprise Plan to Mid-Size Organizations" (Mar 26, 2026). Sketch has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Figma, Webflow and Framer also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
