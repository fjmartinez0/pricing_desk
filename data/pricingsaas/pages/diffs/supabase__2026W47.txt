What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Supabase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Free plan Auth Audit Logs retention increased from 1 hour to 24 hours; Pro (7 days) and Team (28 days) unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit raised). The current page lists Pro at $25 and Team at $599.
It lands 28 days after Supabase's previous change, "Supabase swaps Enterprise support manager for Technical Account Management" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Supabase adds metered Log Ingestion at $0.50/GB after 20 GB on Pro and Team" (Sep 28, 2026), "Supabase: Added OAuth 2.1 Server support across all plans" (Sep 21, 2026) and "Supabase: Enterprise plan drops BYO Cloud support" (Aug 31, 2026). Supabase has been tracked since 2026-03-15.
