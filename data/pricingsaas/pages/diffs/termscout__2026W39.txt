What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of TermScout's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Certify Platform's rate card moved from an annual quote ($5,400/seat/year, $27k annual minimum) to a monthly rate with mandatory annual prepay ($450/seat/month). Two add-ons flipped from one-time fees to annual recurring charges; TrustMark AI jumped from a $250 one-time fee to $3,000/year. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Certify™ Platform at $450, Basic at $900, Pro at $1,300, Premium at $1,700 and Custom FAQs at $250.
It lands 41 days after TermScout's previous change, "TermScout: Premium plan features swapped ($1700/mo unchanged)" (Aug 11, 2026). TermScout has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Coupa, Contra and Determ also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
