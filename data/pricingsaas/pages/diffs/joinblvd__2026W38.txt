What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Boulevard's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Essentials plan price rose from $140/mo to $143/mo per location, and its promotional "Normally $176" reference price and discount asterisk were removed. Premier and Prestige keep their summer-promo pricing display unchanged; Essentials plan's bundled allowances increased: free texts 100 to 250/mo, free email blasts 500 to 1,000/mo, storage 25GB to 50GB. The headcount bullet was reworded from "Up. In detail: Essentials plan's bundled allowances increased: free texts 100 to 250/mo, free email blasts 500 to 1,000/mo, storage 25GB to 50GB. The headcount bullet was reworded from "Up to 5 professionals" to "Up to 5 providers (bookable calendars)" (count unchanged). Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit increase, price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Essentials at $143, Premier at $234 and Prestige at $328.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Boulevard. Boulevard has been tracked since 2026-06-23.
