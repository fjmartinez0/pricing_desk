What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Kontainer's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New add-on module 'LLM Brand Guide Module' added at +$70 on three plans in the Add on modules table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new add-on). The current page lists Core at $500, Professional at $695, Premium at $1,155 and Enterprise at $2,220.
It lands 21 days after Kontainer's previous change, "Kontainer launches AI add-on, from $120 for 1TB up to $380 for 5TB" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kontainer cuts Enterprise video streaming storage from 16TB to 4TB" (Oct 11, 2026). Kontainer has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Jungle Scout and Databox also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
