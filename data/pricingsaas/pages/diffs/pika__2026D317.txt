What happened
Between the Nov 12, 2026 and Nov 13, 2026 captures of Pika's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: GPT Image 2.5 became GPT Image 2.6 and its cost rose from 2 to 4 credits per image; images per month halve (Starter 300→150, Creator 1,300→650, Fancy 4,275+→2,137+). Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered). The current page lists Starter at $8, Creator at $28, Fancy at $76 and Credit Pack at $1 per 60 credits.
It lands 6 days after Pika's previous change, "Pika adds Nano Banana 2 at 7 credits, boosts top-up pack image counts" (Nov 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pika quietly slashes Starter and Creator monthly credit caps by 33–17%" (Nov 6, 2026), "Pika adds daily free H3 Flex generations plus 14-model credits table" (Nov 4, 2026) and "Pika adds commercial license to $10 Starter plan, launches plan recommender" (Oct 26, 2026). Pika has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
In the same window OpenAI, Synthesia and Runway also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
