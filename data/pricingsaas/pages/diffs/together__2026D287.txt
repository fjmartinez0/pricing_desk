What happened
Between the Oct 13, 2026 and Oct 14, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Kimi K3 cached input token price in the Serverless Inference (Chat) pricing table rose from $0.27 to $0.30 per 1M tokens; standard input $3.00 and output $15.00 unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Managed Storage at $0.16 per GiB/month, Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, Sandbox (Code Interpreter) at $0.03 per session (60 min), GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100) and GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days).
It lands 2 days after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI hikes Qwen3.8 Flash prices up to 68%, adds 10% off Kimi K3 promo" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI cuts GPU Cluster minimum reservation from 7 days to 3, prices hold" (Oct 8, 2026), "Together AI cuts Kimi K3 token prices 10% while Qwen3.8 Flash rises" (Oct 6, 2026) and "Together AI adds Tev1 4B Experimental at $0.04 input with free output" (Oct 5, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
In the same window Google, Mistral AI, Anthropic, OpenAI and Huggingface also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
