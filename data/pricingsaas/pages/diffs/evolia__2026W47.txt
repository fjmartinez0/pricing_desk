What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Evolia's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added "Automatic Leave Accrual" feature to the HR plan ($7.00/employee/month). Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Essentials at $3.50, Business at $4.75, Operations at $5.75 and HR at $7.
It lands 7 days after Evolia's previous change, "Evolia drops Automatic Leave Accrual from Human Resources plan, prices hold" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Evolia adds Automatic Leave Accrual to HR plan, keeps $7/employee price" (Oct 4, 2026). Evolia has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window ClickTime, Apploye, Workstream, Workable and Tracker also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
