What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of HubSpot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New footnote under the Service Hub Starter plan cards: discounts are available for new customers only. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $7, Professional at $90, Enterprise at $150 and HubSpot Credits at $9 per 1,000 credits.
It lands 49 days after HubSpot's previous change, "Hubspot Service Starter adds AI Agents promo carousel for Customer Agent" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hubspot Service Starter: Starter plan renamed to Starter Customer Platform" (Aug 31, 2026). HubSpot has been tracked since 2025-01-01.
In the same window Zoho, Microsoft, Salesforce, Pipedrive and ActiveCampaign also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
