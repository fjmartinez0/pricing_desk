What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of TurboTenant's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Autopilot full-service management now shown as a fourth plan card beside Free, Essentials and Pro, with a call-to-discuss CTA and no price; the Do it for me toggle is gone. In detail: Autopilot full-service management, previously behind the Do it for me toggle at "Plans starting at $99 per month", is now a fourth plan card beside Free, Essentials and Pro with no price, only "Call to discuss your rentals" and a phone number; the toggle is gone. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Essentials at $12.42 and Pro at $16.58.
It lands 14 days after TurboTenant's previous change, "TurboTenant makes lower screening fees Pro-only, dropping Essentials perk" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "TurboTenant launches Autopilot full-service plan, trims Pro to $16.48/mo" (Sep 7, 2026). TurboTenant has been tracked since 2026-08-18.
In the same window AppFolio and Buildium also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
