What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Heepsy's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: FAQ entry 'Do you offer any discounts?' (up to 25% off yearly plans, plus 20% discount for NGOs) removed in page redesign; the 20% NGO discount is no longer disclosed anywhere on the page. Annual 'Save 25%' toggle and prices ($69/$149/$229 annual; $89/$199/$299 monthly anchor) remain; Enterprise/custom-plan section now lists included features: custom database limits, dedicated account manager, cu. In detail: Enterprise/custom-plan section now lists included features: custom database limits, dedicated account manager, custom integrations & API access, SLA & priority support, and custom contract terms. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Starter at $69, Plus at $149 and Advanced at $229.
It lands 77 days after Heepsy's previous change, "Heepsy sweetens annual billing pitch: discount messaging jumps to 25% off" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Heepsy cuts Plus to $149 and Advanced to $229, slashes result limits" (Jun 30, 2026) and "Heepsy: Plus price cut $199→$149, Advanced $299→$229 + 2 more changes" (Jul 3, 2026). Heepsy has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
