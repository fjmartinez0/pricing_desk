What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Signeasy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Plan copy renamed the sendable unit from documents/contracts to envelopes (Send unlimited envelopes; 5 envelopes/month on Personal). Prices and limits unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning). The current page lists Personal at $10, Business at $20, Business Pro at $30, API Intermediate at $124.92 and API Advanced at $249.92.
It lands 227 days after Signeasy's previous change, "Signeasy Drops Teamspace Feature, Bumps Annual Savings to 33%" (Apr 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Signeasy Launches Intelligent Contract Management Pricing Tier" (Mar 18, 2026), "Signeasy adds promotional banner touting 33% yearly plan savings" (Feb 24, 2026) and "Signeasy Extends Year-End 30% Off Sale Through January 5th" (week of Dec 29, 2025). Signeasy has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
