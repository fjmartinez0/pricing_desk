What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Picsart's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Pro yearly-billed price cut from $10.5/mo to $9.45/mo (list $15) alongside a new 'Intro price: 10% off' label on the Ultra card; Ultra model catalog count raised from 140+ to 200+ leading AI video, image and audio models; Pro trending list now shows Seedance 2.0 (4K 15s) instead of Seedance 2.5 (1080p 30s). In detail: Limited-time 10% off promo (48-day countdown banner, Ultra badge Most powerful → Intro price: 10% off): Pro yearly $10.5 → $9.45/mo and Ultra yearly $60 → $54/mo per seat; list prices $15/$75 unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount added, feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Pro at $10.5 and Ultra at $60.
It lands 7 days after Picsart's previous change, "Picsart Pro and Ultra list Kling 3.0 4K, Seedance 2.5 access with output caps" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Picsart switches Seedance 2.5 quota from video count to seconds" (Sep 28, 2026), "Picsart swaps flat daily AI limits for credit caps up to 5000 images" (Sep 7, 2026) and "Picsart doubles Pro credits to 500/month, halving Ultra's usage advantage" (Aug 24, 2026). Picsart has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
In the same window Corel and Canva also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
