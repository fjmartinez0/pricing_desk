What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of TileDB's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: TileDB rebranded to Tile.ai, removing its pricing page. Per-seat ($10/mo), per-vCPU ($50/mo), and DSE ($250/hr) rates and the $31,200/yr calculator are gone, replaced by a gated early-access signup form. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price hidden) with a downward direction. The current page lists Build Your Plan at $10 and Dedicated Success Engineer (DSE) at $250 per hour.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for TileDB. TileDB has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
