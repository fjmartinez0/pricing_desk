What happened
Between the Oct 8, 2026 and Oct 9, 2026 captures of OpenArt's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Seedance/Flux 3 Image promo banner no longer lists Pro (ended Oct 7); promo now applies to Wonder only until Oct 14. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Essential at $7, Advanced at $14.5, Team Plan at $17.5, Infinite at $28 and Wonder at $120.
It lands 2 days after OpenArt's previous change, "OpenArt cuts Nano Banana 2.1 generations 30% for upgraders through Oct 14" (Oct 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "OpenArt offers unlimited Flux 3 720p videos on Wonder plan until Oct 31" (Oct 6, 2026), "OpenArt offers up to 50% off Seedance models plus unlimited Flux 3 Image" (Oct 2, 2026) and "OpenArt offers 30% off MiniMax H3 upgrades, replacing Wonder video promo" (Sep 28, 2026). OpenArt has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
