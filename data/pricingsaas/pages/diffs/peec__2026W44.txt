What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Peec AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Agent credits included' row under Peec agent: Starter 500, Pro 750, Advanced 1000, Enterprise Custom. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Starter at $80, Pro at $205, Advanced at $420, Additional Model (Starter) at $30 and Additional Model (Pro) at $70.
It lands 7 days after Peec AI's previous change, "Peec AI: bot-visit allowance raised on Starter/Pro/Advanced, no price change" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Peec AI moves Perplexity tracking to Enterprise-only, adds Naver AI to all plans" (Sep 21, 2026), "Peec AI adds Meta Spark to Enterprise LLM tracking, drops SOC 2 from plan table" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Peec AI expands Enterprise LLM tracking to 12 models with Grok API addition" (Sep 7, 2026). Peec AI has been tracked since 2026-04-29.
