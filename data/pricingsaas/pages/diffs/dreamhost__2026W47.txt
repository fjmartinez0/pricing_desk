What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Dreamhost's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Professional Email starting price dropped from $1.67/mo per mailbox (billed annually) to $1.49/mo per mailbox on a 2-year term; adds 25 GB/100 GB mailbox note. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Web Hosting Launch at $10.99, VPS Stack 4 at $11.99, Web Hosting Growth at $12.99, VPS Stack 8 at $16.99 and DreamPress 1 at $19.99.
It lands 28 days after Dreamhost's previous change, "Dreamhost adds Liftoff AI Website Builder to Launch, Growth and Scale plans" (Oct 18, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Dreamhost quietly drops promised free starter website perk, FAQ lags" (Sep 21, 2026). Dreamhost has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Kinsta and Siteground also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
