What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Apploye's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added 'Auto-discard idle time' row to comparison table: automatically removes detected idle time from tracked hours; included in Elite, Power, Enterprise, not Starter. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Elite at $4.50, Power at $8, Activity Insights at $3, More Screenshots at $2 and Video Recording at $4.
It lands 7 days after Apploye's previous change, "Apploye cuts Power plan to $4.80/user/mo with 40% intro promo through Nov 19" (Nov 2, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Apploye launches 40% off Power promo as free Starter plan loses app-tracking, leave-document and expense allowances" (Oct 26, 2026), "Apploye adds $3 Activity Insights add-on, folds Stealth Mode into Power" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Apploye adds BAA to Enterprise plan, boosting compliance coverage" (Aug 24, 2026). Apploye has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window BambooHR, 15five and Lattice also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
