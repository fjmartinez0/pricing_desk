What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Homebase's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Job post boosts add-on price cut from $79 to $50 per post; Hiring Assistant add-on dropped its 'billed annually' requirement, now listed as 'Starting at $99/month'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Essentials at $24, Plus at $56, All-in-One at $96, Background Checks at $30 per background check and Payroll at $49.
It lands 7 days after Homebase's previous change, "Homebase drops Hiring Assistant per-post pricing for $99/mo flat rate, ex" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Homebase: Payroll add-on price decrease ($49→$39/mo)" (Aug 17, 2026), "Homebase hikes payroll add-on fee to $49/month, keeps per-employee rate" (Jul 27, 2026) and "Homebase: 3-month free payroll promo ended" (Jul 20, 2026).
