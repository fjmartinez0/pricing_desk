What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Proposify's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Build rose $35 to $39/user/mo (annual $420 to $468). Grow and Scale switched from flat monthly fees to per-user pricing ($65 and $107/user/mo); underlying annual totals unchanged; Scale: Salesforce premium integrations became generic add-ons; Premium onboarding and support replaced by Dedicated Success Manager and priority support; tagline now targets large teams. Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature change, price increase) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 27, 2026) lists Build at $39, Grow at $65 and Scale at $1,066.67.
It lands 105 days after Proposify's previous change, "Proposify: Basic and Team plans get unlimited templates (caps removed)" (Jul 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Proposify makes Basic plan document sends unlimited, up from 10 a month" (Jul 2, 2026), "Proposify touts "Unlimited" Basic sends, but overage table still caps at 10" (Jul 4, 2026) and "Proposify hides Business plan pricing, moves to custom quotes" (Apr 3, 2026). Proposify has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Zoho, Quickbooks, Stripe, Freshbooks and Honeybook also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
