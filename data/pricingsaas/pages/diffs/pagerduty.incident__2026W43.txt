What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Pagerduty's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: AI Actions allotments became explicit numeric caps for PD Reliability Platform tiers (Starter 1,000, Essential 4,000, Plus 16,000, Ultimate 100,000 per year); previously shown only as an unquantified AI actions bullet with no comparison-table row. In detail: AI Actions allotments became explicit numeric caps for PD Reliability Platform tiers (Starter 1,000, Essential 4,000, Plus 16,000, Ultimate 100,000 per year); previously the plan cards listed only an unquantified 'AI actions' bullet, and no AI actions row existed in the feature comparison table. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Professional at $21, Business at $41, PagerDuty Advance for Incident Management at $415, Status Pages at $89 and Stakeholder License at $150.
It lands 189 days after Pagerduty's previous change, "Pagerduty Incident expands integrations catalog from 700 to 750 across all plans" (Apr 13, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pagerduty Incident Increases Incident Types from 2 to 3 in Professional Plan, Enhancing Incident Management Capabilities" (Q2 2025) and "Pagerduty Incident Enhances Enterprise Plan with Full Chat Experience and Post-Incident Reviews, Expanding Features for Advanced Incident Management" (Q1 2025). Pagerduty has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window Atlassian, Opsgenie and Grafana also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
