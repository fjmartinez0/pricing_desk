What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Cal.com's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Terminology rename in plan cards: 'event types' became 'links' (Free: Unlimited links & calendars; Teams: Managed & collective links). Prices and inclusions unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Teams at $12 and Organizations at $28.
It lands 28 days after Cal.com's previous change, "Cal.com limits Free plan workflows to templates, custom reserved for Teams+" (Oct 12, 2026). Cal.com has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
