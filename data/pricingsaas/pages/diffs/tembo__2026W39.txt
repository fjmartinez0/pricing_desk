What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Tembo's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Tembo added 'Projects' as a new counted resource alongside repositories: Free now includes 1 project, and Pro/Max now include unlimited projects, shown on plan cards and the compare-plans table. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Pro at $60 and Max at $200.
It lands 7 days after Tembo's previous change, "Tembo slashes Free plan seats from 3 users to just 1" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Tembo: Credits billing replaced with dollar usage allowance" (Aug 5, 2026) and "Tembo: Pro/Max credits cut 40-50%; new sandbox size + RAM specs disclosed" (Jul 28, 2026). Tembo has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Supabase, Railway, Neon, PlanetScale and Render also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
