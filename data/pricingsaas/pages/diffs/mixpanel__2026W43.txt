What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Mixpanel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Growth plan estimator at 18M events/year: yearly-billed price fell from $120/month to $100/month ($1,200 total annual cost); monthly-billed price unchanged at $140. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Growth at $0.28 per 1K events (first 1M included free).
It lands 42 days after Mixpanel's previous change, "Mixpanel touts free Experiments and Feature Flags in new banner" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Experiments & Feature Flags Move from Add-on to Included Usage" (Aug 31, 2026), "Mixpanel: Agent query caps removed + AI-Powered Data Governance added" (Aug 24, 2026) and "Mixpanel: Replaced Mixpanel Agent with Web analytics + Spark AI query builder" (Aug 10, 2026). Mixpanel has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Google and Amplitude also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
