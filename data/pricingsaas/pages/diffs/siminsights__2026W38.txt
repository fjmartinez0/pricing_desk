What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of SimInsights's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: K12 pricing consolidated: the separate 'More accessible options for everyone' section (Teacher, Individual & Homeschools, Classroom) merged into a single 5-tier K12 ladder; a $2,000/yr extra-site add-on appeared on Education Premium; 'Save 20%' annual discount badge disappeared. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Individual at $4.17, Classroom at $11.67, Enterprise Pro at $12.5, Education Essentials at $208.33 and Workforce Board Basic at $291.67.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for SimInsights. SimInsights has been tracked since 2026-07-16.
In the same window Tableau, Alteryx and Sisense also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
