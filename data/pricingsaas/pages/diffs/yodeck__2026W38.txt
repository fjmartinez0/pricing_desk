What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Yodeck's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Premium plan feature bullet 'Yodeck API & Webhooks' changed to 'Yodeck AI Agent & API' - Webhooks reference dropped, new AI Agent capability added alongside API access. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Sep 28, 2026) lists Basic at EUR 8, Premium at EUR 11, Enterprise at AUD 25, Embeddable Feeds at CAD 9 and Yodeck Assurance at GBP 0.8.
It lands 48 days after Yodeck's previous change, "Yodeck: Premium plan tag-based playlists feature expanded" (Jul 27, 2026). Yodeck has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
In the same window Wistia, Visme, Veel, Uscreen and Profound also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
