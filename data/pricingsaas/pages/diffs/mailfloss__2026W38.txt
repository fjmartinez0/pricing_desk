What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of mailfloss's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: mailfloss introduced a perpetual free tier — 500 email verifications a month, every month, at no cost — alongside its existing paid subscription plans and 7-day trial. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Lite at $24, Business at $49, Pro at $174 and Prepaid Credits at $500 subscription.
It lands 73 days after mailfloss's previous change, "mailfloss: Added annual billing (17% off); hid overage & prepaid discounts" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "mailfloss introduces annual billing with 17% savings" (Jun 22, 2026). mailfloss has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Kickbox, Wistia, Visme, Veel and Uscreen also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
