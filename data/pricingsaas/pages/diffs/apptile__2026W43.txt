What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Apptile's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Live streaming allowances now include viewer minutes: Plus 20K, Premium 50K per month, with $3.99 per additional 1K viewer minutes; live hours and $50/hour overage unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (metric change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Plus at $539 and Enterprise at $1,079.
It lands 14 days after Apptile's previous change, "Apptile launches $4,999 migration add-ons, folds Pro features into Plus plan" (Oct 5, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Apptile: New Monthly Active Users usage metric introduced" (Aug 31, 2026). Apptile has been tracked since 2026-02-26.
In the same window Zapier, Workato, Make and MuleSoft also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
