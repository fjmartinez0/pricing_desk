What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Langfuse's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Added IAM role authentication for Amazon S3 to the plan comparison; Response time SLO redefined as Severity 1 with Best effort on Hobby/Core/Pro and committed 1h 24/7 on Enterprise (was n/a, 48h, Custom); Contract duration for Core and Pro now Monthly or Yearly Commitment (was Monthly); Billing via AWS Marketplace now offered as Yearly Commitment on Core and Pro. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added, feature change) with an upward direction. The current page lists Core at $29, Pro at $199, Enterprise at $2,499 and Teams at $300.
It lands 14 days after Langfuse's previous change, "Langfuse adds MCP and CLI rows to AI plan lineup beside Halo agent" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Langfuse renames in-app agent from Assistant to Halo in Cloud plans" (Oct 19, 2026) and "Langfuse: AI Assistant usage now tiered by plan" (Sep 14, 2026). Langfuse has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
