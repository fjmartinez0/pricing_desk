What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Wherobots's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Professional plan's headline price was reframed from 'FROM $95/MO' to '$0 to Start' with on-demand pricing copy, and the trial CTA was reworded from 'Start 14 day/$95 free trial' to '14 day free trial for up to $95 in usage'. Underlying $95/month usage minimum is unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Professional at $95, Innovation at $3,000 and Premium Support at $1,000.
This is the first pricing-page change Pulse has recorded for Wherobots. Wherobots has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
