What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Jobber's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Expired annual promo ended: 'Save up to 40%, offer ends Sept 30' became 'Save up to 25%'; first-12-month intro prices ($105/$70/$21) removed, annual rates now $149/$99/$29. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount removed) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Core at $24, Connect at $149, Grow at $299, Plus at $529 and Marketing Suite at $79.
It lands 7 days after Jobber's previous change, "Jobber Teams: Annual discount jumps from 25% to 40% via new intro pricing" (Sep 28, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Jobber Teams: New intro-rate promo, up to 40% off (was 25%), ends Aug 31" (Aug 24, 2026), "Jobber Teams: Temporary annual promo pricing on Grow, Connect, Core (up to 20% off)" (Aug 11, 2026) and "Jobber Teams: intro annual-discount offer expired, prices revert to standard rates" (Aug 5, 2026). Jobber has been tracked since 2026-02-06.
