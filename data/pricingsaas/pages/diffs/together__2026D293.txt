What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 20, 2026 captures of Together AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: New Post-Training on-demand product added to pricing: NVIDIA HGX B200 at $11.00/GPU/hr list, launch promo $9.00/GPU/hr valid until 12/14/2026, billed per minute; New model DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 added to serverless inference pricing at $0.30 input, $0.006 cached, $1.20 output per 1M tokens. Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, new plan) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 21, 2026) lists Managed Storage at $0.16 per GiB/month, Provisioned Throughput at $0.05 per PTU/minute, Sandbox (Code Interpreter) at $0.03 per session (60 min), GPU Clusters (On-demand) at $3.99 per hour (H100) and GPU Clusters (Reserved) at $3.69 per hour (H100, 3-30 days).
It lands one day after Together AI's previous change, "Together AI ends free Muse Spark Open promo; $1.25/$4.25 pricing now applies" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Together AI adds Reflection Beam to serverless at $0.40 per 1M input tokens" (Oct 17, 2026), "Together AI makes Muse Spark Open free until Oct 18, adds Nemotron 3.5 Super VL" (Oct 16, 2026) and "Together AI ends Kimi K3 cached-input promo; price returns to $0.30/1M" (Oct 14, 2026). Together AI has been tracked since 2025-12-23.
