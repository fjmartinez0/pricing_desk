What happened
Between the Nov 4, 2026 and Nov 5, 2026 captures of Snowflake's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Standard, Enterprise, and Business Critical feature callouts rewritten: Snowpark, multi-cluster compute, Tri-Secret Secure replaced with AI agents (CoWork/CoCo), AI search, and HIPAA/PCI DSS/FedRAMP support. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Business Critical at $4 per credit, Enterprise at $3 per credit, Standard at $2 per credit and On-Demand Storage at $23 per TB / per month.
It lands 86 days after Snowflake's previous change, "Snowflake: default pricing hidden — now requires platform/region selection" (Aug 11, 2026). Snowflake has been tracked since 2024-10-03.
In the same window Google and IBM also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
