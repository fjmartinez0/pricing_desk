What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Salad's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Per-second GPU prices increased for High, Medium, and Low priority tiers across most RTX GPU classes effective Nov 1, 2026 (e.g. RTX 5090 High $0.500→$0.600/hr-equiv, Medium $0.417→$0.483, Low $0.333→$0.367); Lowest priority price is unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists GPU Instances at $0.18 RTX 5080 (16GB VRAM) instance-hour + $0.09 RTX 3090 (24GB VRAM) instance-hour, General Purpose Instances at $0.01 General Purpose 2GB / 1 vCPU instance-hour + $0.02 General Purpose 8GB / 4 vCPU instance-hour, CPU-Optimized Instances at $0.02 CPU-Optimized 8GB / 4 vCPU instance-hour + $0.01 CPU-Optimized 2GB / 1 vCPU instance-hour and Memory-Optimized Instances at $0.01 Memory-Optimized 8GB / 1 vCPU instance-hour + $0.05 Memory-Optimized 32GB / 4 vCPU instance-hour.
It lands 56 days after Salad's previous change, "Salad warns SaladCloud price hike lands September 12, current GPU rates hold" (Sep 7, 2026). Salad has been tracked since 2026-07-05.
