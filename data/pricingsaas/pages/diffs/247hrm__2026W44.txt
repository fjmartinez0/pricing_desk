What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of 247HRM's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New cost section publishes Core ₹5,999, Premium ₹11,999 base plus per-employee rates, annual rates, Startup Lite/Premium packs and add-on prices; before only Professional ₹8,999 showed.
