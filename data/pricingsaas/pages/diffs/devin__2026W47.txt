What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Devin's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Teams gains 2 concurrent Free-mode sessions per user, 3-tier cost controls, Slack support, multiplayer sessions; Use your ChatGPT plan on Pro/Max/Teams; Admin dashboard and priority support dropped; Teams plan user cap reduced from up to 200 users to up to 100 users. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit lowered, feature added). The current page lists Pro at $20, Teams at $80 and Max at $200.
