What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Streak's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New MCP Server integration (connect Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) added to Integrations & Automation, included in Pro, Pro+ and Enterprise; Automations description rewritten to trigger-based multi-step automations. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Pro at $49, Pro+ at $69, Enterprise at $129, AI Credits 25000 at $1,000 and AI Credits 1000 at $100.
It lands 56 days after Streak's previous change, "Streak sharpens Pro/Pro+/Enterprise messaging, confirms per-user billing model" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Streak: Advanced implementation add-on now requires annual plan or 3+ team" (Aug 31, 2026). Streak has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
In the same window Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
