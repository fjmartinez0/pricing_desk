What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Fireflies.ai's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: The 'API access' row in the plan comparison table, previously a checkmark on every plan, now lists API rate limits: Free 30/min and 500/day, Pro 60/min and 5,000/day, Business 60/min per paid seat pooled (up to 1,200/min), Enterprise 60/min per paid seat pooled (up to 2,400/min). Pulse files it as a product change (feature change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 22, 2026) lists Pro at $10, Business at $19 and Enterprise at $39.
It lands 3 days after Fireflies.ai's previous change, "Fireflies.ai publishes API rate limits: 30/min up to pooled 2,400/min" (Oct 9, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Fireflies.ai: Added Email Assistant AI feature to Pro plan" (Aug 17, 2026). Fireflies.ai has been tracked since 2026-05-19.
In the same window Google, Microsoft and Zoom also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
