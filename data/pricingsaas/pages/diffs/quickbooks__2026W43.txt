What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Quickbooks's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added promotional callout surfacing the Free plan during plan selection — "Just starting out? Get organized and get paid. $0/mo, no credit card required. See QuickBooks Free.". In detail: Added new promotional callout surfacing the Free plan during plan selection: 'Just starting out? Get organized and get paid. $0/mo, no credit card required. See QuickBooks Free.'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Simple Start at $38, Essentials at $85, Plus at $140 and Advanced at $340.
It lands 35 days after Quickbooks's previous change, "QuickBooks: Essentials, Plus & Advanced plan prices increased" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "QuickBooks Payroll: Workforce bundle prices increase ($125→$135, $203→$228)" (Sep 14, 2026), "QuickBooks: Free plan added + Essentials price hike ($75→$85/mo)" (Aug 10, 2026) and "QuickBooks Payroll: Summer promo jumps to 90% off (was 50%)" (Aug 10, 2026). Quickbooks has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
