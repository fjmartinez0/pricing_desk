What happened
Between the Oct 22, 2026 and Oct 23, 2026 captures of HeyGen's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Creator gains HeyGen Voice (up to 10h text-to-speech) and HeyGen Video 1; Pro gains HeyGen Voice (16+h TTS) and Seedance model upgraded 2.0 to 2.5. Prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Creator at $24, Pro at $41 and Business at $119.
It lands 32 days after HeyGen's previous change, "HeyGen: Voice Cloning entitlement softened (Creator + Pro)" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HeyGen caps Voice Cloning usage on Creator, Pro, and Business plans" (Sep 19, 2026), "HeyGen rebrands Digital Twin as Video Avatar across all plans" (Sep 7, 2026) and "HeyGen renames Digital Twin avatar to Custom Video Avatar, limits unchanged" (Sep 1, 2026).
