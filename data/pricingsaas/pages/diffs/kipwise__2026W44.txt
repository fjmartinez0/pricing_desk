What happened
Between the Oct 19, 2026 and Oct 26, 2026 captures of Kipwise's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Kipwise merged the Agent ($10/user/month) and standalone Knowledge base plan (from $7/mo, Premium/Branded/Enterprise tiers) into one Agent plan now including the knowledge base, support desk and Tasks. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Kipwise Agent at $9.
It lands 8 days after Kipwise's previous change, "Kipwise opens early access to Ticketing at no extra cost" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kipwise Agent add-on ditches $1K setup fee for $10/user/month pricing" (Sep 28, 2026). Kipwise has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
