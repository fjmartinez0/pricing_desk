What happened
Between the Oct 7, 2026 and Oct 8, 2026 captures of Huggingface's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: PRO plan's included inference credits benefit changed from a relative multiplier ('20x included inference credits') to an explicit absolute monthly dollar allowance ('$2 of included inference credits monthly'). Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 16, 2026) lists PRO at $9, Team at $20, Enterprise at $50, Inference Endpoints - NVIDIA T4 AWS (4x) at $3 per hour and Inference Endpoints - NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 AWS (1x) at $2.75 per hour.
It lands 97 days after Huggingface's previous change, "Enterprise SCIM provisioning & Team SSO OIDC added + 2 more changes" (Jul 3, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Huggingface reframes PRO pitch from crowd count to value promise" (Jun 8, 2026), "Huggingface upgrades free GPUs to RTX Pro 6000 with 96GB VRAM" (Jun 1, 2026) and "Huggingface adds NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 GPU to AWS Inference Endpoints" (May 25, 2026). Huggingface has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window OpenAI, Anthropic, Together AI, Modal and Cohere also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
