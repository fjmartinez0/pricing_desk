What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Baremetrics's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Static per-tier pricing replaced by an ARR slider; added free tier to $180K ARR, cut Growth's price to $69/mo (was $189) and Scale's to $409/mo (was $749); added 10%-of-recovered option for Payment Recovery. Pulse files it as a packaging change (package change) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Launch at $49, Growth at $189, Scale at $749, Payment Recovery at $129 and Cancellation Insights at $129.
It lands 49 days after Baremetrics's previous change, "Baremetrics: month-to-month pricing removed from FAQ" (Aug 10, 2026). Baremetrics has been tracked since 2023-12-03.
In the same window Stripe and Recurly also changed their Data & Analytics pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
