What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of Veel's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Comparison table: Starter storage limit doubled 25GB to 50GB; Pro/Elite Basic AI Tools monthly generation limit dropped 750 to 150 and 1,000 to 750. Main pricing cards/FAQ still show unchanged AI credit values. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Launchpad at $399.92, Pro at $799.92 and Elite at $1,999.92.
It lands 7 days after Veel's previous change, "Veel cuts Starter to free, hikes Pro to $999 in overhaul" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Veel: Content Licensing capability added across all plans" (Aug 31, 2026). Veel has been tracked since 2026-04-28.
