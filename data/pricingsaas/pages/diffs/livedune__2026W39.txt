What happened
Between the Sep 14, 2026 and Sep 21, 2026 captures of LiveDune's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Agency plan's monitored social media accounts capacity in the plan comparison table dropped from 100 to 50 accounts; base plan price ($225/mo) unchanged; Agency plan's 'Extra account cost' add-on fee (charge per additional monitored account beyond plan limit) decreased from $1.50 to $1. Pulse files it as a pricing change (limit decrease, price decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Blogger at $26.1, Corporate at $45 and Agency at $180.
It lands 7 days after LiveDune's previous change, "LiveDune cuts Agency plan price 18% to $225/month for SMM agencies and brands" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LiveDune: Blogger/Corporate/Agency price increase ($29→$33, $50→$60, $200→$275)" (Aug 10, 2026) and "LiveDune: Blogger checker fake-like monitoring limit increased (50 → 100)" (Jul 3, 2026). LiveDune has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Sendible, Sproutsocial, Buffer, Hootsuite and Later also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
