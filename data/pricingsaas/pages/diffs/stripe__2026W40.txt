What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Stripe's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Stripe Reader T600 ($329.00) removed from the Card readers lineup on the Terminal pricing page, leaving only the M2, S710, and S700 readers. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 19, 2026) lists Standard at 1.5% surcharge for international cards + $0.3 per successful transaction.
It lands 4 days after Stripe's previous change, "Stripe drops T600 reader, trims lineup to M2, S710, and S700" (Sep 24, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Stripe adds $329 Reader T600 to Terminal lineup, revamps Treasury perks" (Oct 1, 2026), "Stripe: Metronome pricing restructured + new T600 reader ($329)" (Aug 31, 2026) and "Stripe launches T600 hardware reader at $329, joining M2, S710, S700 lineup" (Aug 28, 2026).
