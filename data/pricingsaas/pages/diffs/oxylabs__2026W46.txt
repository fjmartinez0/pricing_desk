What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Oxylabs's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Starter plan monthly price cut from $30/mo to $15/mo; Starter Mobile Proxies included volume reduced from up to 4 GB to up to 2 GB. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price decrease, limit decrease) with a downward direction. The current page lists Starter at $15, Basic at $100, Flexible at $500 and Enterprise at $2,500.
It lands 28 days after Oxylabs's previous change, "Oxylabs adds free $5 Welcome credits, renames Advanced to Flexible" (Oct 12, 2026). Oxylabs has been tracked since 2026-06-25.
In the same window Pulumi, Snowflake, PagerTree, ServiceNow and RunPod also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
