What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Range's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Platinum plan annual price increased from $5,950/year to $6,950/year. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Premium at $329.17, Platinum at $495.83 and Titanium at $1,041.67.
It lands 21 days after Range's previous change, "Range raises Cash Savings APY to 3.50% from 3.20%, renames footnote" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Range swaps Titanium card's highlighted perk to FBAR + FATCA Filing" (Sep 14, 2026) and "Range rewrites Titanium plan highlights, but entitlements stay the same" (Sep 7, 2026).
