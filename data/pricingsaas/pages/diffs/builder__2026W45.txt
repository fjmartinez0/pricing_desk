What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Builder.io's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Credit terminology simplified: 'Agent Credits' renamed to 'Credits' across plan cards and comparison table; allowances (60/500/Custom) unchanged. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $24 and Team at $40.
It lands 28 days after Builder.io's previous change, "Builder.io renames Fusion and Publish to Code and Content, adds Project Hosting" (Oct 5, 2026). Builder.io has been tracked since 2026-05-14.
In the same window Figma, Squarespace, Webflow and Framer also changed their Software Development & Testing pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
