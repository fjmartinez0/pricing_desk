What happened
Between the Nov 18, 2026 and Nov 19, 2026 captures of Pika's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added GPT 2.5 Sunburst (Quality) as a new image generation model at 1 credit/generation, yielding 600/2,600/8,550+ generations per month on Starter/Creator/Fancy plans. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). Pulse's latest capture (Nov 22, 2026) lists Starter at $8, Creator at $28, Fancy at $76 and Credit Pack at $1 per 60 credits.
It lands one day after Pika's previous change, "Pika offers unlimited Wan 3.0 generations on Fancy plan through Nov 23" (Nov 18, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Pika reprices GPT Image 2.6 and Nano Banana 2.1, top tiers up to 33%" (Nov 17, 2026), "Pika ends free daily H3 Flex generations for Creator and Fancy subscribers" (Nov 16, 2026) and "Pika launches Team plans: Creator $35/seat, Pro $95/seat with shared credits" (Nov 15, 2026). Pika has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
