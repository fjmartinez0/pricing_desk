What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Awesomic's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Every expert plan (Graphic Pack, All-in-One, Dedicated expert) now includes one bonus Awesomic Light subscription at no extra cost. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Light at $19, Basic at $99, Pro at $199, Studio at $499 and Graphic Pack at $1,799.
It lands 7 days after Awesomic's previous change, "Awesomic hikes Graphic Pack to $1,799/mo, launches $19 Agentic AI plans" (Oct 26, 2026). Awesomic has been tracked since 2023-12-03.
In the same window Sketch, Figma, Webflow and Framer also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
