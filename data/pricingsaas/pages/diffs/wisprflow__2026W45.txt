What happened
Between the Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 2, 2026 captures of Wispr Flow's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro Notetaker meetings now 'Unlimited*' (was 'Higher limits'); Free meetings 'Limited Notetaker' and Free word cap restated from 2,000/week desktop to 'Limited words per week'. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Pro at $12 and Growth at $18.
It lands one day after Wispr Flow's previous change, "Wispr Flow drops exact Free plan limits, makes Pro meetings unlimited" (Nov 1, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Wispr Flow blurs Free plan mobile cap, swapping 1,000 words for "limited use" (Oct 26, 2026), "Wispr Flow hides mobile dictation cap, swaps 1,000-word free limit for vague…" (Oct 20, 2026) and "Wispr Flow: Free plan Android word limit reduced to 1,000/week" (Sep 21, 2026). Wispr Flow has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
