What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of Upstash's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Search product removed from the Upstash pricing product navigation (Redis, Vector, QStash, Workflow, Box, Blob remain); Insights added to Prod Pack features (console Insights, listed in comparison table and observability row); Free tier max data size changed from 256MB to 250MB. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change, plan removed, add-on change) with a downward direction. The current page lists Fixed 250MB at $5, Fixed 1GB at $10, Fixed 5GB at $50, Fixed 10GB at $100 and Fixed 50GB at $200.
It lands 68 days after Upstash's previous change, "Upstash: new FAQ on instant no-signup free Redis DB" (Aug 4, 2026). Upstash has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
In the same window Google, MongoDB, Supabase, Cloudflare and PlanetScale also changed their Cloud & DevOps pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
