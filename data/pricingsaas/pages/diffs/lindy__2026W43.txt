What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Lindy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Team plan entry price raised from $29.99 to $49.99 per user per month: the 3,000-credit ($29.99) and 4,000-credit ($39.99) tiers were removed, so the minimum per-user tier is now 5,000 credits. Per-tier prices from 5,000 credits up and the $10 per 1,000 top-up rate are unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists Plus at $49.99, Pro at $99.99 and Max at $199.99.
It lands 63 days after Lindy's previous change, "Lindy scraps $8K assistant tier, slashes Plus to $29.99/user with credit pools" (Aug 17, 2026). Lindy has been tracked since 2024-11-17.
In the same window UiPath, n8n, Zapier and Make also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
