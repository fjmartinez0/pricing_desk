What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Prezi's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Annual-billed monthly prices rose: Standard $7→$9/mo, Plus $19→$24/mo, Premium $29→$37/mo. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price increase) with an upward direction. The current page lists EDU Plus at $4, Standard at $7, EDU Pro at $8, Plus at $19 and EDU Teams at $19.
It lands 63 days after Prezi's previous change, "Prezi drops offline desktop access from Plus and Premium plans" (Sep 21, 2026). Prezi has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
