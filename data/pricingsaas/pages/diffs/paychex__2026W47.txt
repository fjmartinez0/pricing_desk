What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Paychex's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Paychex now publicly shows starting pricing for payroll packages: $65/month plus $8 per employee; previously all packages were request-pricing only. Pulse files it as a pricing change (price revealed) with a neutral direction.
It lands 7 days after Paychex's previous change, "Paychex ends 5-months-free promo banner, Pay Nothing Until April 2027" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Paychex extends Pay Nothing Until 2027 deal but cuts free months to five" (Nov 1, 2026) and "Paychex offers 6 months free, no payments until April 2027, through Oct. 31" (Oct 19, 2026). Paychex has been tracked since 2026-05-05.
In the same window Gusto and OnPay also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
