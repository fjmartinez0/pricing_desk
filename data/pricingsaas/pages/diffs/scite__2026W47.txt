What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of scite's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro plan now offers pay-as-you-go billing for MCP and API usage beyond included credits, charged per call on the next invoice with a settable monthly spending limit; a new FAQ entry explains the overage billing mechanics. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added). The current page lists Basic at $12 and Pro at $40.
It lands 21 days after scite's previous change, "scite Pro adds team seats at $40 each, with Enterprise as next step" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "scite drops its $250 Team plan" (Oct 19, 2026), "scite ends fall sale, Pro back to $50/mo and free Connect plan dropped" (Oct 4, 2026) and "scite slashes prices 30% for fall sale — Basic drops to $14, Pro to $35" (Sep 28, 2026). scite has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
