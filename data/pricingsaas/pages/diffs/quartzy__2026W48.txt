What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Quartzy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New 'Small Team? Quartzy is Free' banner: platform fee waived for academic labs up to 3 full-time members and companies up to 5 FTEs, plus up to $3,000 Shop credit. Replaces the limited-time subscriber banner; $50/$250 prices unchanged. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change). The current page lists Academia at $50 and Industry at $250.
It lands 56 days after Quartzy's previous change, "Quartzy drops Oct 31 deadline on $750-$3,000 Shop credit offer" (Sep 28, 2026). Quartzy has been tracked since 2026-07-07.
In the same window Mosaic also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
