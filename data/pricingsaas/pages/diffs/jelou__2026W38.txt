What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Jelou's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Memory capacity cut across all paid tiers: Free 1MB to 100KB, Builder 100MB to 1MB, Growth 1GB to 100MB. New $0.20-per-additional-MB overage fee introduced for Builder and Growth once the lower cap is hit; Enterprise stays Custom. Pulse files it as a packaging change (limit decrease) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 5, 2026) lists Builder at $25 and Growth at $299.
It lands 56 days after Jelou's previous change, "Jelou splits WhatsApp billing, renames Consumable to Usage credits, adds $0.0" (Jul 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Jelou: Merged separate 'Workflow Executions' and 'Credits for AI nodes' metrics into a single 'Consumable credits'…" (Jul 13, 2026). Jelou has been tracked since 2026-06-24.
In the same window Twilio, Google, Plivo, Telnyx and Bird also changed their AI & Machine Learning pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
