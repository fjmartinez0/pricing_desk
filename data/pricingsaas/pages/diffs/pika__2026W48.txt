What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of Pika's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Image credit costs cut in monthly-credits table: Nano Banana 2.1 5 to 4 credits/gen; GPT Image 2.6 renamed GPT 2.6 Sunburst (Quality), 4 to 3 credits/gen, so more images per plan; New image model GPT 2.5 Sunburst (Quality) added to the credits table at 1 credit per 1K image (600 / 2,600 / 8,550+ images on Starter/Creator/Fancy). Pulse files it as a packaging change (feature added, limit raised). The current page lists Starter at $8, Creator at $28, Fancy at $76 and Credit Pack at $1 per 60 credits.
This weekly roll-up includes 4 changes Pulse first recorded in a shorter period: "Pika drops Fancy plan's Unlimited Wan 3.0 promo as offer expires" (Day 327, 2026, saved Nov 23, 2026), "Pika adds GPT 2.5 Sunburst image model at 1 credit per generation" (Day 323, 2026, saved Nov 19, 2026) and "Pika offers unlimited Wan 3.0 generations on Fancy plan through Nov 23" (Day 322, 2026, saved Nov 18, 2026) and 1 more. They are the same changes, not new ones. Before that, Pika's previous recorded change was "Pika ends free daily H3 Flex generations for Creator and Fancy subscribers" (Nov 16, 2026). Pika has been tracked since 2026-05-15.
