What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of HelloNote's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Monthly promo banner rotated October to November; 10% monthly savings offer broadened from new PT subscribers (user fees only) to new PT and SLP subscribers. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Plus at $25 and Lite at $49.99.
It lands 13 days after HelloNote's previous change, "HelloNote drops 50% first-month promo, caps $30 part-time plan at 100 notes" (Oct 26, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "HelloNote swaps 5% promo for 10% off new PT subscribers in October" (Oct 5, 2026) and "HelloNote swaps 30-day free scribe trial for 5% monthly discount deal" (Sep 7, 2026). HelloNote has been tracked since 2026-02-27.
In the same window Notion, Microsoft and Google also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
