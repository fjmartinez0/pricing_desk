What happened
Between the Oct 5, 2026 and Oct 12, 2026 captures of LOGO.com's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Yearly billing promo cut from SAVE 80% to SAVE 25%: Brand Plan first-year $3/mo ($36 first year) replaced by $11.25/mo billed yearly; list prices $15/$50 unchanged, default toggle now Monthly. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Brand Plan at CAD 3.6 and Brand Plan Plus at CAD 11.4.
It lands 21 days after LOGO.com's previous change, "LOGO.com: Business Plan annual discount removed ($120→$600/yr)" (Sep 21, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "LOGO.com: Brand Plan Plus renamed to Business Plan, now $50/mo (was $38) + 5 seats" (Aug 17, 2026). LOGO.com has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
