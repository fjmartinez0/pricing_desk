What happened
Between the Oct 20, 2026 and Oct 21, 2026 captures of Krea's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: "Access to Nodes Agent" dropped from the Features & License comparison table (was included for Pro, Max, Business, Enterprise); part of an active A/B oscillation vs. "Recraft V4.1 Flash added" at this same table row (seen 3x since 2026D268). In detail: "Access to Nodes Agent" removed from the Features & License comparison table; the Node Editor workflow agent capability is no longer listed for any plan tier. Pulse files it as a product change (feature removed) with a downward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 23, 2026) lists Basic at $5, Pro at $21, Max at $63 and Business at $160.
It lands 2 days after Krea's previous change, "Krea rewrites plan allowance examples: more Nano Banana 2 generations, Seedance 2.5 videos" (Oct 19, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Krea offers 14 days of unlimited Krea 3 with Pro or Max annual plans" (Oct 16, 2026), "Krea adds Recraft V4.1 Flash with up to 7,714 images on Max plan" (Sep 25, 2026) and "Krea slashes MiniMax H3 Max video quotas 4x across all paid tiers" (Sep 21, 2026). Krea has been tracked since 2026-05-16.
