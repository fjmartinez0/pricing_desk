What happened
Between the Sep 21, 2026 and Sep 28, 2026 captures of Gusto's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New "Contractor Only" plan card now appears in the main pricing grid for businesses without W-2 employees, shown as a limited-time offer at $0/mo (regular $35/mo) plus $6/mo per person; previously only mentioned in FAQ text, not shown as a selectable plan card. Pulse files it as a packaging change (new plan) with an upward direction. Pulse's latest capture (Oct 12, 2026) lists Simple at $6, Solo at $6, Plus at $80, Premium at $180 and Tax-Advantaged Benefits at $200 per year.
It lands 21 days after Gusto's previous change, "Gusto re-gates custom admin roles to Premium, bundles Next-Day pay into Plus and Premium" (Sep 7, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Gusto: Workers' compensation add-on price now disclosed ($14/mo)" (Jul 3, 2026). Gusto has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
In the same window BambooHR and Paychex also changed their Workforce Management pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
