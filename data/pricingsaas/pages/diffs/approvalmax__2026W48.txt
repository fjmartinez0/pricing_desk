What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of ApprovalMax's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Top-tier plan support perk changed from 'Get phone support' to 'Live video help' in the 'Everything in Advanced plan, plus' list. Pulse files it as a product change (feature change).
It lands the same day as ApprovalMax's previous change, "ApprovalMax swaps phone support for live video help on Premium plan" (Nov 22, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "ApprovalMax moves Xero pricing to document-volume tiers with overage rates" (Nov 8, 2026), "ApprovalMax bundles Capture free, moves to document-volume tiers" (Nov 8, 2026) and "ApprovalMax adds U.S. payments: pay bills in 35 currencies via ACH" (Nov 1, 2026).
