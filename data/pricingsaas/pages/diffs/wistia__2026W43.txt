What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Wistia's pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Credits added to plan cards: Free 3,000+ lifetime, Business and Business + Signals 5,000+ monthly, Enterprise 12,500+ monthly; Business + Signals add-on bundle renamed from Automation Suite to Signals; prices unchanged at $329/mo. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added, positioning) with an upward direction. The current page lists Business at $79 and Signals at $250.
It lands 91 days after Wistia's previous change, "Wistia shifts Business plan storage cap from fixed 250 GB to open-ended" (Jul 20, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Wistia rebrands enterprise support role to Customer Success Manager" (May 12, 2026), "Wistia Discontinues Plus Plan, Enhances Pro Plan with Media Limit Increase to 500 and User Limit to 3+" (Jan 14, 2026) and "Wistia Announces Major Price Hike: Plus Plan Extra Media Cost Jumps 10x from $0.20 to $2.00 per File" (Q1 2025). Wistia has been tracked since 2023-11-15.
In the same window Loom, Panopto and Vimeo also changed their Marketing & Content pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
