What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of Robinhood's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New Crypto fee-schedule tab added to the pricing page alongside US Stocks, Margin Investing, and Options & Futures, disclosing $0 commissions and $0 account creation fees for crypto trading via Bitstamp UK Ltd.
