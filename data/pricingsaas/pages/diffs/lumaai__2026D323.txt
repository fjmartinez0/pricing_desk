What happened
Between the Nov 18, 2026 and Nov 19, 2026 captures of Luma AI's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: MiniMax H3 Max extra reference images (after the 2–4 free) now cost 7–18 credits each, up from ~7–13; Gemini Omni Flash (3–10 sec videos) cheaper: Text/Image-to-Video 720p moved from 'up to 370 credits' per video to 36 credits/sec (max 360 for 10 sec); Modify 720p cut from 39 to 38 credits/sec; Ray3.14 no longer offers the HDR+EXR (3×) output option; only SDR (base) and HDR (2×) remain (Ray3.2 sti. In detail: Ray3.14 no longer offers the HDR+EXR (3×) output option; only SDR (base) and HDR (2×) remain (Ray3.2 still lists HDR+EXR). Pulse files it as a pricing change (feature removed, price increase, price decrease) with a neutral direction. Pulse's latest capture (Nov 22, 2026) lists Plus at $25, Pro at $75 and Ultra at $250.
It lands 10 days after Luma AI's previous change, "Luma AI halves Video-to-Video rates, adds two Kling 4.0 Flash models" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Luma AI halves Video-to-Video credit rates, adds Kling 4.0 Flash models" (Nov 7, 2026), "Luma AI adds Kling 4.0 API pricing, from 53 credits per second at 720p" (Oct 23, 2026) and "Luma AI doubles Seedance video-to-video credit costs, adds Minimax H3 Max" (Oct 4, 2026).
