What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Aura's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: New group-purchase section: volume discounts available on orders of more than 10 memberships (per-person pricing, 1- or 2-year memberships); call sales for a quote.
