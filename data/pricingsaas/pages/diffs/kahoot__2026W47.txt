What happened
Between the Nov 9, 2026 and Nov 16, 2026 captures of Kahoot!'s pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Pro Start plan price raised from $9/month ($108 billed annually) to $15/month ($180 billed annually). In detail: Pro Start coupon discount reduced (coupon pro_start_53 → pro_start_22): effective price raised from $9/month ($108 billed annually) to $15/month ($180 billed annually); $19 list price unchanged. Pulse files it as a pricing change (discount change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Kahoot! 360 Pro Start at $15, Kahoot! 360 Spirit at $20, Kahoot! 360 Pro Standard at $25, Kahoot! 360 Spirit Premium at $25 and Kahoot! 360 Pro Plus at $39.
It lands 7 days after Kahoot!'s previous change, "Kahoot! quietly drops Pro Start's $19 slash-price, now a plain $9/mo" (Nov 8, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Kahoot! slashes Pro Start annual price 53% to $9/mo with new coupon" (Nov 1, 2026), "Kahoot! revives Pro Max price to $79/mo, slashes Pro Plus to $39/mo intro" (Sep 28, 2026) and "Kahoot! flattens Pro Ultra participant cap to 5,000, drops onboarding gate" (Sep 13, 2026). Kahoot! has been tracked since 2026-02-28.
In the same window Typeform and Duo also changed their Collaboration & Productivity pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
