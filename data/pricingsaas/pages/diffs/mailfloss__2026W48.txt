What happened
Between the Nov 16, 2026 and Nov 23, 2026 captures of mailfloss' pricing page, Pulse recorded 2 changes: Typo Fixer decoupled from plan tiers into a standalone optional paid add-on; previously bundled starting at Business ($59/mo, 25,000 credits), now not included on Lite and sold as a separate paid subscription on any plan. Attentive added as a supported integration (platform count 40→41); trial-flow copy now notes Attentive checks each new subscriber as they arrive. In detail: Typo Fixer decoupled from plan tiers into a standalone optional paid add-on; previously it was bundled in starting at the Business plan ($59/mo, 25,000 credits), now it is not included in Lite and must be purchased as a separate paid subscription on any plan; Attentive added as a supported integration; supported email platform count increased from 40 to 41, and the free-trial copy now notes Attentive checks each new subscriber as they arrive.
