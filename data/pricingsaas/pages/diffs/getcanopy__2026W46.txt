What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Canopy's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Canopy Coworker AI now listed as included in Standard, Plus and Premium plans with monthly AI credits per user, no AI add-on needed; badge Beta to Open Beta; new FAQs and comparison row. Pulse files it as a product change (feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Standard at $74, Plus at $109, Premium at $149, Knowledge-Based Authentication (KBA) at $1.25 per KBA credit and Smart Intake at $11 per client credit.
It lands 42 days after Canopy's previous change, "Canopy: Added API access to Platform feature set" (Sep 28, 2026). Canopy has been tracked since 2023-11-14.
In the same window Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive also changed their Business Operations pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
