What happened
Between the Nov 2, 2026 and Nov 9, 2026 captures of Smartsheet's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Control Center renamed Advanced Portfolios (NEW) in Enterprise-plan inclusions; copy rewritten and Business add-on eligibility dropped in comparison table. In detail: Control Center add-on rebranded Advanced Portfolios (NEW) on the Advanced Work Management card; comparison table and Premium features section now list it as an Enterprise-only add-on, dropping Business add-on eligibility. Pulse files it as a packaging change (add-on change) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Pro at $9, Business at $19, Data Shuttle at $100 and Dynamic View at $125.
It lands 128 days after Smartsheet's previous change, "Smartsheet turns free 'Viewer' into 'Contributor'; adds AI copilot to Pro" (Jul 4, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Smartsheet brings AI tools to Pro and Business plans" (Jun 18, 2026), "Smartsheet renames Viewers to Contributors with expanded permissions" (May 4, 2026) and "Smartsheet Rebrands Resource Management and Simplifies Feature Names" (Apr 3, 2026). Smartsheet has been tracked since 2024-10-01.
