What happened
Between the Sep 7, 2026 and Sep 14, 2026 captures of HubSpot's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Added a new AI Agents promo carousel below the plan comparison: 'customer agent' intro copy, an illustrative credits-usage dashboard graphic (Breeze Customer Agent, 25,400 total credits used), a credit estimator tool (still loading at capture), and resolution stats (70%+ of conversations auto-resolved, 39% faster ticket resolution). No plan, price, or entitlement changed. Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Starter at $7, Professional at $90, Enterprise at $150 and HubSpot Credits at $9 per 1,000 credits.
It lands 13 days after HubSpot's previous change, "Hubspot Service Starter: Starter plan renamed to Starter Customer Platform" (Aug 31, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Hubspot Service Starter: Email template limit doubled (5,000 → 10,000) on Starter" (Jul 20, 2026), "Hubspot Service Starter cuts free Customer Agent trial from 28 to 14 days" (Jul 1, 2026) and "Hubspot Service Starter: price cut to $7/mo/seat + Free plan gains Revenue tools" (Jul 3, 2026). HubSpot has been tracked since 2025-01-01.
In the same window Zoho, Freshworks, Salesforce, Microsoft and Pipedrive also changed their CRM pricing pages. Those moves are listed further down, and the Pulse Agent can compare them side by side.
