What happened
Between the Sep 28, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026 captures of Viktor's pricing page, Pulse recorded one change: Team and Enterprise plan taglines rewritten: Team now reads 'Put Viktor to work across your team' and Enterprise now reads 'Tailored controls, billing, and support' (both previously generic 'Perfect to get started and explore Viktor'). Pulse files it as a product change (positioning) with a neutral direction. The current page lists Team at $50.
It lands 21 days after Viktor's previous change, "Viktor flattens Free, Team, and Enterprise taglines into one generic line" (Sep 14, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Viktor: Team & Enterprise gain spend-cap controls" (Aug 11, 2026). Viktor has been tracked since 2026-05-19.
