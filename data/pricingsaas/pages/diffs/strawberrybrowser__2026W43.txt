What happened
Between the Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 19, 2026 captures of Strawberry's pricing page, Pulse recorded 3 changes: Pricing page now split into Individual/Team/Enterprise tabs; Team seat pricing (Starter $35, Standard $150, Ultra $350) no longer painted on default view; Enterprise with pooled credits added; Paid tiers now list Expert mode, AI model choice (Claude, OpenAI) and credit tracking; Priority/Dedicated support lines no longer shown on Intern, Part-time and Full-time cards; Free plan monthly allowance. In detail: Free plan monthly allowance now carries a daily usage limit alongside 500 credits per month. Pulse files it as a packaging change (freemium change, package change, feature added) with an upward direction. The current page lists Intern at $17, Starter at $35, Part-time at $71, Standard at $150 and Full-time at $208.
It lands 7 days after Strawberry's previous change, "Strawberry cuts Part-time plan to $85, adds per-seat Team tiers" (Oct 12, 2026). Before that Pulse recorded "Strawberry slashes free plan credits 75%, from 2,000 to 500 a month" (Oct 5, 2026) and "Strawberry: Team plan swaps overage note for shared credit pools" (Jul 27, 2026). Strawberry has been tracked since 2026-07-15.
