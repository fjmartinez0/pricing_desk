THEIR RATIONALE
Synthesia hasn't published a rationale for the cut, and the page still frames the allowance as 'limited time only,' so this reads as a mid-flight trim to the promo rather than a new policy. It lands amid an unusually dense run of pricing activity: six changes in the last 90 days against a typical 44-day gap between moves, including two earlier credit cuts in the past month — the free plan's monthly allowance was reduced 58% and Basic's AI video asset cap went from 25 to 3. Trimming Syren's promo credits so soon after launch likely reflects the same cost-control instinct, suggesting the free-credit introduction was more generous than Synthesia intended to sustain.

The timing stands out more than the size of the cut: Synthesia gave away 2,000 free Syren credits on Oct 10 and had cut Basic's share to 1,000 by Oct 22 — inside the same one-month promo window it advertised. That's consistent with the market's broader credits-and-usage-billing churn, which touched 243 pricing changes across 175 companies in the last 180 days (12.1% of everything tracked), led by companies like Lumaai and Ideogram tuning credit economics repeatedly.
