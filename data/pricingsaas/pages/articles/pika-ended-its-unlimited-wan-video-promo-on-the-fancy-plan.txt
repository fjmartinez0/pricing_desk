DISCOUNT ENDED
Pika ended its Unlimited Wan video promo on the Fancy plan
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 23, 2026
Pika removed the Unlimited Wan 3.0 promo from its Fancy plan.
See before and after
Enlarge
Pika’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 23, 2026
What changed
Pika pulled the "Unlimited Wan 3.0" promo from its Fancy plan: the feature-list bullet advertising unlimited Wan 3.0 generations, the pricing-page banner, an FAQ entry describing the offer, and the "Unlimited" label attached to Wan 3.0 in the credits table are all gone. Pulse detected the removal on Nov 23, 2026, the date the limited-time offer was due to expire.
Pika's own description for the update says plan prices are unchanged — this is a feature-list and banner cleanup tied to the promo's stated end date, not a repricing of the Fancy plan.
Why it matters
For a product leader watching the AI video/image category, the signal here isn't the Wan 3.0 promo itself — it's the pace. Pulse has tracked Pika's pricing page since Jun 8, 2026 and logged 15 real changes, 14 of them in the last 90 days, with the gap between changes averaging about 12 days; this one landed just 4 days after Pika added a new image model on Nov 19, 2026. Teams benchmarking against Pika should treat any current perk — a credit cap, a bundled model, a promo — as provisional rather than a durable part of the plan.
This is also Pika's second promo pullback this month: on Nov 16, 2026 it ended a free daily H3 Flex generation offer for Creator and Fancy subscribers. Running short, time-boxed perks and then retiring them looks like it's becoming a standard move for Pika rather than a one-off. If you're pricing a comparable AI tool, treat a 'free' or 'unlimited' perk on a competitor's page as a trial balloon, not a committed cost structure. Watch whether Pika turns these into a recurring tactic ahead of its next price change, rather than isolated cleanup.
In context
The Unlimited Wan 3.0 badge itself was short-lived: Pika added it as a time-boxed Fancy perk on Nov 18, 2026 and is already retiring it just days later [[pika/2026D322]], which is the sharper read here — on Pika's pricing page, these look like hooks rather than durable upgrades. The company has also repriced its top image-model tiers by as much as 33% this month — Pulse tracked the change on Nov 17, 2026 [[pika/2026D321]]. Pulling a promo once it has served its purpose isn't unique to Pika: Pulse tracked 108 discount-removal changes among 3,741 total pricing changes across all companies in the last 180 days — 2.9% of the total — with Picsart [[picsart]] and Openart [[openart]] doing it most often among Pika's closer AI-tool peers. Other AI tools pulled promos the same week: Filterpixel ended its Black Friday sale and Magnific pulled a 40%-off credits promo weeks ahead of its stated end date, both on Nov 23, 2026 [[filterpixel/2026W48]] [[magnific/2026W48]].
Common questions
Does this change what current Fancy subscribers pay?
No — Pulse's diff for this update lists only the Fancy plan's promo materials (the bullet, banner, FAQ entry and Wan 3.0 "Unlimited" label) as changed; it shows no change to any plan's price, including Fancy's own listing.
Is this a one-off, or part of a pattern for Pika?
Part of a pattern, not a one-off — Pika's pricing page has changed often enough this year that any single perk should be treated as temporary. Check the dated history on this page before quoting an old Pika promo to a prospect or in a comparison.
Should product teams expect Pika to reintroduce a similar perk?
The record doesn't show that yet — Pulse has no prior instance of Pika re-adding a lapsed promo. Given how often Pika's packaging moves, it's worth rechecking the pricing page rather than assuming today's plan list is final.
