PACKAGING CHANGE
Vercel launches metered Drives storage at $0.05/GB-month
Vercel added a new "Vercel Drives" section to its pricing page, inserted between the existing Sandbox and Daemons rows, with its own metered pricing: Drive Storage at $0.05 per GB-month, Drive Reads at $0.0015 per GB, and Drive Writes at $0.004 per GB.

WHY IT MATTERS
As more of Vercel's platform becomes its own metered line item, the sticker price on a plan tells less of the story -- a team's real bill depends on how many of these primitives it touches. Watch whether the next changes keep splitting formerly-included resources into pay-per-unit add-ons, and whether the roughly 12-day cadence between changes keeps compressing.

THEIR RATIONALE
Vercel frames this as a new platform primitive rather than a change to existing prices, consistent with its recent pattern: this is the company's seventh pricing change tagged to credits and usage billing, and it landed just 4 days after the Daemons compute billing launch on Oct 16 -- well inside its usual roughly 12-day gap between changes, with 11 changes in the last 90 days. Storage is now billed the same metered way as compute, sandboxes and CDN.

THE SIGNAL
This is Vercel's second usage-metered product in four days, following a run that includes Tracing Spans billing and the CDN overage rework in September. Each change adds its own per-unit meter rather than folding into existing plan prices, so the platform is accumulating separately billed primitives rather than consolidating them. Market-wide, 241 of the 1,995 pricing changes tracked in the last 180 days (12.1%), across 175 companies, involved credits and usage billing, so Vercel's shift fits a broader move toward consumption pricing -- though few peers are splitting a single platform into this many separately metered lines this fast.
