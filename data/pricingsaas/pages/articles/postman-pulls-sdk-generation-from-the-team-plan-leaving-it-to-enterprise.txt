Watch whether Postman pairs more Team removals with Enterprise-only positioning, and whether its historically slow ~85-day cadence between changes keeps compressing the way it has recently.

THEIR RATIONALE
Postman hasn't framed this publicly, but its own history makes the move stand out: this is the first feature removal in its tracked record — 1 of 26 events since monitoring began in April 2024. It follows two other boundary-drawing moves in recent weeks: unbundling cloud load testing into a usage-based add-on in August, and pulling Enterprise's $49 price point behind contact-sales in September. Read together, Postman looks to be narrowing what Team gets while making Enterprise harder to price-shop.
