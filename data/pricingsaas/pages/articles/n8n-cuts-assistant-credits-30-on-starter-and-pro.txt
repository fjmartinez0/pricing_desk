PACKAGING CHANGE
N8n cuts Assistant credits 30% on Starter and Pro
n8n renamed its AI-powered building assistant from "AI Assistant" to "Assistant" and cut its monthly credit allowance on two plans: Starter drops from 2,300 to 1,600 credits a month (a 30% cut), and Pro's top credit tier falls from 13,700 to 9,600 (also down 30%).

WHY IT MATTERS
For buyers evaluating n8n's Assistant, or any usage-metered AI feature elsewhere, the lesson is that a generous credit allowance at launch isn't a durable commitment — it's a number vendors are actively re-pricing as real usage costs come in. Watch whether n8n's Enterprise tier, already cut once in August, takes another hit, and whether the naming finally settles now that "Assistant" has stuck for more than a few weeks.

THEIR RATIONALE
n8n frames this as part of an ongoing tuning of the Assistant feature rather than a one-off correction — it's the company's fourth credit or naming adjustment to this exact feature since mid-July, arriving 34 days after its last change and matching a cadence of roughly one pricing move every three weeks, with five changes in the past 90 days alone. The pattern reads as a vendor still calibrating how many AI credits it can afford to give away as usage scales, not one that considers the allowance settled.

THE SIGNAL
The headline number undersells how unstable this feature's pricing has been: in eleven weeks n8n has renamed it twice (AI Assistant to AI Workflow Builder, back to AI Assistant, now just Assistant) and moved the credit allowance in both directions before landing on this cut. Credits-based billing is one of the index's most common levers right now — 134 changes across 108 companies in the last six months — but most of that activity is companies adding or raising credits to sweeten a launch, not repeatedly slashing the same allowance. n8n's whipsawing puts it in a smaller, less comfortable group: vendors whose AI feature economics didn't hold up on the first, or third, try.

IN CONTEXT
This is n8n's fourth credit tweak to its AI assistant since renaming AI Workflow Builder to AI Assistant in mid-July, followed by a swap back to AI Workflow Builder with a credit cut days later and then an increase for Starter and Pro two days after that. Five weeks after rolling out an Assistant preview for Business/self-hosted while trimming Enterprise, both surviving tiers get cut roughly 30%. Credits and usage billing is common ground across the index — 134 changes at 108 companies in six months, led by repeat movers like Luma AI and Koala — but most of that is tier launches or hikes, not a vendor cutting the same allowance four times in eleven weeks.
