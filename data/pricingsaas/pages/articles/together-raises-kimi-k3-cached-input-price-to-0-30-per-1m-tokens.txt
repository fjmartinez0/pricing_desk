Together raises Kimi K3 cached-input price to $0.30 per 1M tokens
In the serverless inference pricing table, Together raised the Kimi K3 cached input rate from $0.27 to $0.30 per 1M tokens.

WHY IT MATTERS
Teams running Kimi K3 with heavy prompt caching now pay $0.30 per 1M cached input tokens, 11% more than yesterday's listed $0.27, while standard input and output stay at $3.00 and $15.00. If $0.27 was the promo rate, Kimi K3 is now back at full list price on all three lines. Watch whether Together runs another Kimi K3 promo or moves the standard rates next.

THEIR RATIONALE
Together made no announcement; it is a single edit to the pricing table. The old $0.27 cached rate is exactly 10% below the new $0.30, and Kimi K3 carried a 10%-off promo ($2.70 input / $13.50 output, from $3.00 / $15.00, listed as valid until Oct 11) first captured on Oct 6. Standard input and output were already back at $3.00 / $15.00 in the Oct 13 capture, so this edit most likely brings the cached rate back to list after the promo ended rather than setting a new price.
