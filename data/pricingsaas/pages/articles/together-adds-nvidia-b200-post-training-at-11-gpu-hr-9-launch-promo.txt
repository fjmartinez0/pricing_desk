Together adds NVIDIA B200 post-training at $11/GPU/hr, $9 launch promo
Together added a new Post-Training product to its pricing page: on-demand training infrastructure on NVIDIA HGX B200 GPUs, billed per minute, at $11.00/GPU/hr list with a launch promo price of $9.00/GPU/hr valid until 12/14/2026.

WHY IT MATTERS
The B200 post-training launch extends Together's infrastructure stack beyond serverless inference and fine-tuning into on-demand training compute, competing more directly with dedicated GPU-cloud providers on raw hardware access rather than managed model endpoints. Watch whether the $9.00 launch promo reverts to the $11.00 list price when it expires 12/14/2026, and whether Together keeps layering new hardware SKUs onto this page at its current pace.

THEIR RATIONALE
Together doesn't publish a stated rationale, but discounting a brand-new product's list price — $9.00 against an $11.00 list, valid until 12/14/2026 — is a familiar land-grab move: get usage on the books on new infrastructure before reverting to list price. Pairing the launch with another new model (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017) on the same day fits Together's pattern of shipping GPU capacity and inference pricing together, as fast as the underlying hardware and model landscape moves.
