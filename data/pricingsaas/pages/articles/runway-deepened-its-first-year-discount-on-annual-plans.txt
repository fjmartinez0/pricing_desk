Runway deepened its first-year discount on annual plans
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 28, 2026
Runway cut its annual-plan first-year prices by an extra 30%.
See before and after
Enlarge
Runway’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 28, 2026
What changed
On Nov 28, 2026, according to the page Pulse saved that day, Runway added an extra 30% off the first year of its annual plans: Standard to $8.40 a month from $12, Pro to $19.60 from $28, and Max to $53.20 from $76 — each already a discounted annual rate.
Runway's previous recorded change was 26 days earlier, on Nov 2, 2026, according to Pulse, when the company launched Kling 4.0 at 25% off and added App Studio to paid plans.
Why it matters
Layering an extra 30% on top of an already-discounted annual price is a Black Friday-season move, not a change to Runway's list price — the $12/$28/$76 annual reference rates on the page Pulse saved on Nov 28, 2026 have not moved, only the time-limited layer on top of them has. For a product leader watching usage-based AI tools, this is a reminder that stacked, first-year-only discounts can make vendors' sticker prices look far apart when their actual multi-year cost is closer; check whether a quoted monthly rate is a first-year promo or the ongoing price before comparing vendors.
Runway has made 8 pricing changes in the last 90 days, a faster pace than its average of about one change every 28 days since Pulse started tracking it on Nov 17, 2024, which fits a company leaning on promos and model-access bundles to compete into the holiday season rather than moving its base price structure.
Watch whether the extra 30% off on the page Pulse saved on Nov 28, 2026 expires on a fixed date once the Black Friday season ends or stays on the page longer.
In context
Runway has leaned on time-limited promos before: its last tracked change, on Nov 2, paired a 25%-off Kling 4.0 launch with an extended Enterprise free-usage offer, according to Pulse, and this extra 30% keeps the same playbook running into the Black Friday season. Pulse tracked 285 pricing changes built around new discounts across the companies it tracks in the last 180 days, and Runway is one of the more frequent users of that pattern; peers layering similar seasonal discounts this week include Suno and TeamViewer. The sharper read: this is a seasonal layer on Runway's existing annual pricing, not a repricing — the underlying $12/$28/$76 annual rates on the page Pulse saved on Nov 28, 2026 are unchanged, and Runway's own Kling 4.0 launch discount is time-boxed through Nov 30, so watch for a similar end date on this one.
Common questions
Is the extra 30% off permanent or a limited-time promo?
Treat it as time-limited until Runway's page says otherwise. The company has run similar seasonal discounts before and let them expire on a fixed date; check the live pricing page before locking in a multi-year cost estimate rather than assuming today's rate holds.
What should we do before comparing Runway's price to a competitor's?
Normalize to the ongoing, non-promo rate before comparing: strip out any first-year-only discount for every vendor in the comparison, not just Runway, since quoted monthly rates increasingly reflect a time-boxed layer rather than the list price.
