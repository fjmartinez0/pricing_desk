PACKAGING CHANGE
GitLab prices a new Advanced Capacity add-on at $12,000 a year for higher API limits
GitLab added a new "Advanced Capacity" add-on, marked "coming soon," for Premium and Ultimate customers: a flat $12,000 per top-level group per year that raises GitLab.com API rate limits to 50,000 requests per minute and 500,000 requests per hour, with daily overage priced from $100 a day for groups that exceed those limits.

WHY IT MATTERS
For GitLab customers whose pipelines, bots or AI workloads hit API rate limits, the listed answer is now a $12,000-a-year add-on per top-level group rather than a plan upgrade. Watch whether Advanced Capacity ships at this price once it leaves "coming soon," and whether the Enterprise Capacity Pack the card mentions for sustained need gets a public price.

THEIR RATIONALE
GitLab's card pitches Advanced Capacity as higher GitLab.com rate limits for groups that run heavy API, automation or AI workloads, with daily overage for occasional spikes priced separately from the flat annual fee. It lands one day after GitLab's Orbit context-graph add-on, a much shorter gap than GitLab's usual change every ~44 days.

THE SIGNAL
The flat annual fee plus metered daily overage mirrors a consumption-pricing pattern spreading across the index. It's also GitLab's third move into credits-and-usage pricing since June, after Flex Commitment's unified seat-and-credit billing and the shift from discounted per-use overage to flat on-demand pricing. Read together, GitLab is moving more usage-heavy capacity out of the base Premium and Ultimate plans and into separately priced lines.

IN CONTEXT
GitLab's Advanced Capacity add-on, priced at $12,000 per top-level group per year, arrives a day after the Orbit context-graph add-on launched, its second new add-on in as many days. It follows Flex Commitment's unified seat-and-credit billing in June and GitLab's move to flat on-demand credit pricing in September. Credits and usage billing is also a market-wide pattern: the index has tracked changes of this kind at 200 companies in the last 180 days, including Docker's Cloud Sandboxes credit promo and Highlight's expanded AI credit limits. For GitLab, Advanced Capacity is its third move in this pattern since June, each one putting more of the cost of heavy use onto a separately priced line rather than the base plan.
