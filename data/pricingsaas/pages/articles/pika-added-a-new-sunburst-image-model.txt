PACKAGING CHANGE
Pika added a new Sunburst image model
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 19, 2026
Pika added a GPT 2.5 Sunburst image model priced at 1 credit per generation.
See before and after

Pika’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 19, 2026
What changed
Pika added GPT 2.5 Sunburst (Quality) as a new image generation model priced at 1 credit per generation, according to Pika's pricing page on Nov 19, 2026. That works out to roughly 600 Sunburst generations a month on the Starter plan, 2,600 on Creator and 8,550 or more on Fancy, per Pika's pricing page on Nov 19, 2026, scaling with each plan's existing monthly credit allowance.
Sunburst is new to Pika's lineup, not a reprice of an existing model — it is a fresh row in the image-models table on Pika's pricing page.
Why it matters
For a product leader watching usage-based pricing, Sunburst at 1 credit per generation sits well below the 3 and 4 credits Pika now charges for GPT Image 2.6 and Nano Banana 2.1, so customers can run far more images on an existing plan without Pika changing any headline subscription price. Credit-based pricing lets a vendor add perceived value, such as a new named model, while the dollar price on each tier stays the same.
Pika has been adjusting credit costs all month. On Nov 13, 2026 it doubled GPT Image 2.6 to 4 credits, then on Nov 17 it lowered GPT Image 2.6 from 4 to 3 credits and Nano Banana 2.1 from 5 to 4, per Pika's pricing page. Teams that model blended cost per generation, not just the plan price, will track this best. Watch whether Sunburst's 1-credit rate holds or gets repriced the way GPT Image 2.6 has been twice in a week.
In context
Pulse has tracked Pika since Jun 8, 2026, logging 14 real pricing changes across 27 comparisons, including 13 in the last 90 days alone. Sunburst arrives a day after a Wan 3.0 promo on the Fancy plan and two days after the Nano Banana 2.1 and GPT Image 2.6 credit cost cut. Across the companies Pulse tracks, credit and usage-based billing changes have been common lately: 414 pricing changes at 276 companies in the last 180 days, 12.2% of the 3,406 changes Pulse tracked in that window, touched credits or usage, with Mintlify and Lumaai among the most active besides Pika itself. Pika doubled GPT Image 2.6's credit cost to 4 and halved its monthly image limits on Nov 13, 2026, then cut it back to 3 credits on Nov 17. Per-generation cost keeps moving by model.
Common questions
How should we size this against our own usage?
Multiply your team's expected monthly image generations by Sunburst's per-generation credit cost, then check that total against your plan's included credit allowance on Pika's pricing page — anything beyond the allowance draws from Pika's top-up credit packs, which this pack does not price.
How often does Pika change its pricing?
About once every 12 days on average since Pulse began tracking Pika on Jun 8, 2026 — rapid enough that a product leader copying this credit model should expect to re-check costs monthly rather than pricing once and walking away.
Is this launch part of a broader squeeze, or just a nice freebie?
Read it alongside the rest of November: Pika also launched paid Team plans on Nov 15, 2026 at $35 and $95 a seat and slashed Starter and Creator's monthly credit caps by 33 to 17% back on Nov 6, 2026, per its pricing page — the cheap new model sits inside a month of changes that add paid tiers and tighten free usage elsewhere.
