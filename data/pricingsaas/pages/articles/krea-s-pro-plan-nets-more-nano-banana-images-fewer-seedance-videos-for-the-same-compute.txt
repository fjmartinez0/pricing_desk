PACKAGING CHANGE
Krea's Pro plan nets more Nano Banana images, fewer Seedance videos for the same compute
Krea rewrote the example model allowances shown on its pricing cards while leaving each plan's underlying compute units unchanged (5k/20k/60k/80k across Basic, Pro, Max and Business).

WHY IT MATTERS
For buyers comparing cost per output, these example numbers are the only visibility into how a compute budget translates into actual generations, and Krea has redrawn that math more than once in recent weeks. Watch whether the shift toward Nano Banana 2 and away from Seedance previews a broader repricing of video generation relative to image generation, or whether Krea swings the allowance numbers again at its next update.

Nano Banana 2 example generations rise about 75% on Pro (257 to 450) while Pro's compute units stay at 20k/month

THEIR RATIONALE
Because the compute-unit quotas themselves didn't move, this reads as a reallocation rather than a straightforward capacity boost: Krea is showing customers that the same monthly budget now stretches further on Nano Banana 2 images, at the cost of fewer Seedance videos now that the pricier Seedance 2.5 model is the example used. Leading with the image-generation number lets Krea show a win of about 75% more generations even though the Seedance side of the ledger moved the other way.

That whiplash fits Krea's overall pace: 9 of its 13 tracked pricing changes have landed in the last 90 days.
