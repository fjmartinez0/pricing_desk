Postman drops SDK auto-distribution from its Enterprise plan card
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 4, 2026
Postman removed SDK auto-distribution from its Enterprise plan.

What changed
Postman's Enterprise plan card no longer lists 'SDK auto-distribution', and the capability is not mentioned anywhere else on the pricing page. In the same update, Postman added a new 'Static IP egress for cloud performance testing' row to the feature comparison table, available on the Solo, Team and Enterprise plans but not on Free. Pulse links that new row to an active test on Postman's Team plan card that has run since Oct 29, 2026, so that part may be one arm of an experiment rather than a settled change.
Why it matters
Enterprise buyers who used SDK auto-distribution no longer see it listed on the top plan, and the page gives no replacement or explanation, so it is worth confirming with Postman before assuming it is gone. The Team plan's SDK generation line is also part of a live test, which suggests Postman is still deciding where its SDK tooling sits across plans.
In context
Pulse has tracked Postman's pricing page since Apr 1, 2024, logging 12 real changes across 50 comparisons Postman. This change lands 6 days after the Oct 29, 2026 capture that showed SDK generation removed from the Team plan [[postman/2026D302]], much sooner than Postman's average gap of about 78 days. Pulse now tags that Team plan change as one arm of an active A/B test, and the live page on Nov 4, 2026 still carries a Team card version that includes SDK generation and distribution, so the Team removal is not settled. Across the market, 128 pricing changes at 118 companies involved feature removals in the last 180 days, 4.8% of the 2,688 changes tracked.

Postman made 9 pricing changes in the last 365 days and 5 in the last 90 days, per Pulse's tracking — frequent enough that a product leader watching Postman should expect quarterly-ish moves, not annual ones.
Does the new Static IP egress row cost extra?
