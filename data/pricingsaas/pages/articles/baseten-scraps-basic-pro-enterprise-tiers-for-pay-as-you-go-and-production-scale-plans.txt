PACKAGING CHANGE
Baseten scraps Basic/Pro/Enterprise tiers for Pay as you go and Production scale plans
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 3, 2026
See before and after
Enlarge
Baseten’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 3, 2026
What changed
The Basic, Pro and Enterprise plan cards are gone, replaced by two tiers: Pay as you go, a self-serve usage-based tier covering Model APIs, Dedicated Inference, Training and Sandboxes, and Production scale, a custom-priced tier with volume discounts and reserved, priority compute. The page also adds Sandboxes usage pricing — $0.0655/hr for CPU, $0.0211/GB-hr for RAM and $0.20/GB-mo for snapshots — and removes the joey-1 model ($0.01 input) from the Model APIs token pricing table.
Why it matters
For AI infra buyers, this removes a decision point — which named tier to pick — in favor of paying for what's used, with enterprise terms reserved for teams who ask. It also signals that pricing-page complexity in this category keeps shifting from seat-like tiers toward metered line items, with the Sandboxes rate card now sitting alongside Model APIs and Dedicated Inference. Watch whether Baseten's Production scale tier starts publishing reference pricing of its own, the way Model API rates did this month, or stays quote-only.
In context
Baseten has been one of the index's most active pages since Jul 20, 2026, with 11 real changes at a roughly 10-day cadence, most recently adding the joey-1 model at $0.01 input just four days before this one, and before that cutting Reflection Beam rates by up to 72%. This change breaks that pattern: instead of another row in the Model APIs table, Baseten replaced its named Basic/Pro/Enterprise ladder with two usage tiers, Pay as you go and Production scale, and gave Sandboxes its own metered rate card. It lands inside a wider shift: usage and credit-based billing touched 323 pricing changes across 224 companies in the last 180 days, about 12% of everything the index tracked, so a model-hosting platform collapsing named tiers into pay-as-you-go pricing fits the category norm.
