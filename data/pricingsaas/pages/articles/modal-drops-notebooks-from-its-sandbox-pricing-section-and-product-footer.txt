PACKAGING CHANGE
Modal drops Notebooks from its Sandbox pricing section and product footer
Modal dropped Notebooks from its pricing page: the section heading changed from "Modal Sandbox + Notebooks pricing" to "Modal Sandbox pricing," Notebooks was removed from the footer's product list, and the included-usage FAQ now covers only Functions and Sandboxes.

WHY IT MATTERS
Removing a product from pricing-page real estate is often an early signal before a bigger consolidation, bundling, deprecation or a rename, rather than the end state itself. Watch whether Notebooks disappears further from Modal's product surface in the weeks following this pricing-page cleanup, or whether it resurfaces folded into Functions or Sandboxes pricing instead.

THEIR RATIONALE
Modal doesn't explain the change on the page itself, but pulling Notebooks out of both the section heading and the footer's product list, while narrowing the included-usage FAQ to Functions and Sandboxes, suggests a step back from marketing Notebooks as a separately billed product rather than just a wording tweak. It fits Modal's broader habit of editing page structure and terminology, it swapped Okta SSO wording for SAML SSO on Sep 11 and rebranded plan features on May 18, more often than it runs price changes.
