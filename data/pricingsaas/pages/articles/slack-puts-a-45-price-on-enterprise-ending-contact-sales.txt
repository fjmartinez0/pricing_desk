PRICE REVEALED
Slack puts a $45 price on Enterprise+, ending "Contact sales"
Slack's Enterprise+ plan page now shows a public price of $45 USD per user per month, billed annually, where it previously said "Contact sales for pricing." The plan description, feature list, and "Contact sales" CTA button are unchanged — only the price line itself went from hidden to disclosed.

WHY IT MATTERS
If $45 holds as a real anchor rather than a placeholder ceiling, it gives buyers and competitors a public number to negotiate against for the first time, and it puts pressure on other collaboration vendors that still gate top-tier pricing behind "Contact sales" to consider the same move. Watch whether Slack keeps the price listed through its next refresh, or quietly reverts to "Contact sales" if it finds the disclosure is undercutting its enterprise sales motion.

THEIR RATIONALE
Slack hasn't stated a reason for the change, but it's a deliberate break from the company's usual pattern: of 39 tracked pricing-page events since June 2023, 24 were feature additions and only this one was a price reveal. It also arrives after a 181-day quiet stretch, nearly double Slack's typical ~94-day gap between changes, following an April update that added CRM features rather than touching price. Publishing a number here, rather than continuing to gate the top tier behind a sales call, reads as a considered move toward making Enterprise+ easier to evaluate without talking to a rep first.

THE SIGNAL
The $45 figure matters less than the fact that it now exists publicly at all: enterprise software vendors typically keep their top tier behind "Contact sales" specifically so reps can work variable pricing by account size and deal size. Slack choosing transparency here lands inside an active broader trend — seat pricing moved in 66 changes across 57 companies in the last 180 days, from Intercom cutting its Essential seat price 34% to $19 to Bugherd doubling seats while cutting price to $67 — but most of those are rate changes, not disclosures. A plain list price lets prospects benchmark Enterprise+ against Microsoft Teams and Zoom without a sales conversation, narrowing how much room reps have to negotiate while shortening the evaluation cycle.

IN CONTEXT
Slack's reveal comes 181 days after its last tracked change, CRM features added in April, well past its own roughly 94-day average cadence, and it's the only price reveals event among 39 changes logged since June 2023 — the other 24 were feature additions. Elsewhere, seat pricing has been the busiest lever in the market: Intercom cut its Essential seat price 34% to $19 and Bugherd doubled seats to 10 while cutting price to $67, both within the past week. Those are rate changes; Slack's is a disclosure — moving Enterprise+ from "Contact sales" to a plain number — a rarer and arguably bigger shift in how Slack wants its top tier perceived.
