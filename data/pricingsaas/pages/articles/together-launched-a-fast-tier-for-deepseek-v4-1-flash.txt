What changed
Together added a new 'Fast' serving tier for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 to its Serverless Inference lineup, priced at $0.60 per 1M input tokens and $2.40 per 1M output tokens — exactly double the existing base tier's rate on both figures — plus $0.012 per 1M cached tokens, per the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 24, 2026. The base tier ($0.30 input, $1.20 output per 1M tokens) and the US geo-pinned variant for the same model were unchanged.
Pulse has tracked Together's pricing page since Dec 29, 2025 across 60 comparisons, logging 47 real changes — 25 of them in the last 90 days, at an average pace of one every 7 days. This is the company's second change in 4 days: on Nov 20, 2026, Pulse recorded Together raising Qwen4 Max's output price to $4.50 alongside a 30% limited-time promo and the addition of a Tencent Hy4 model.
Why it matters
For a product leader buying inference, this is Together following a pattern increasingly common in AI serving: same model, multiple tiers priced by speed rather than capability. Fast costs twice the base rate, per the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 24, 2026 — a straightforward latency premium a team can quantify against its own request volume, rather than a new model to evaluate from scratch.
It also lands four days after Together's last change, on Nov 20, 2026 per Pulse's tracking, when it raised Qwen4 Max's output price and added another new model — continuing a near-weekly cadence of catalog and pricing changes that makes Together's serverless pricing page worth checking before any usage-based budget review, not just once a quarter. Watch whether 'Fast' tiers become a standard way AI inference providers monetize latency, the way seat tiers once monetized access — the premium here is a data point for what that markup looks like elsewhere.
In context
Together's pricing page moves fast: Pulse has tracked 47 real changes since Together first appeared on Dec 29, 2025, and this Fast tier follows a Nov 20, 2026 price hike on Qwen4 Max by just four days. The pattern lens counts 10 changes in Together's own history with credits-and-usage billing, and the market around it is just as active: 479 pricing changes across 302 companies touched usage billing in the last 180 days, 12.4% of everything Pulse tracked in that window, with peers like Affinda and Arcade also restructuring usage-based rates this week. The sharper read: AI inference pricing is segmenting by speed as much as by model choice now — a 'Fast' SKU at double the base rate is Together pricing latency directly, not just tokens, and it's a preview of how usage-based products elsewhere may start charging for responsiveness rather than just volume.
Common questions
How would I think about pricing a similar 'fast' tier for my own usage-based product?
Price the premium as a multiple of your existing rate rather than a flat add-on — Together's Fast tier is exactly double its base rate, which gives customers an easy mental model: pay more, get priority.
What should I check before assuming Together's serverless pricing is stable?
Check the current pricing page directly — Together changes its page at a fast clip, and this is its second update in a matter of days, so treat any price here as a snapshot rather than a committed rate.
How does this compare with what other AI inference and usage-based products are doing right now?
Similarly active — peers like Affinda and Arcade also restructured usage-based pricing this week, suggesting speed-based tiering may become a standard lever, though the record doesn't show whether competitors to DeepSeek specifically are following Together's lead.
