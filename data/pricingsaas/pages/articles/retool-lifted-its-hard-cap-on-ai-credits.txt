PACKAGING CHANGE
Retool lifted its hard cap on AI credits
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 14, 2026
Retool let paid plans buy extra AI credit packs beyond the old cap.

What changed
Retool updated the "What are AI credits?" answer on its pricing FAQ, saved by Pulse on Nov 14, 2026. The old wording said every plan's monthly AI credit pool came with "no add-on, no separate billing," meaning a team that used up its pool had no way to get more that month. The new wording keeps the same monthly pool but adds that paid plans can now buy additional credits as purchasable credit packs, removing that hard cap.
Why it matters
For product leaders this is less about Retool specifically and more about where usage-based AI pricing is heading: letting a team buy past its pool, rather than forcing an upgrade to a bigger seat tier or adding a flat AI add-on fee, keeps the base price intact while still billing for heavy AI usage. Pulse tracked 377 pricing changes across 259 companies touching credits and usage billing in the last 180 days — 12.2% of the 3,079 changes it tracked overall — so Retool is following a well-worn pattern rather than inventing one. If you run a credit-pooled product, the open question this raises is whether a pure "buy more credits" model keeps customers happier than a tiered pool increase would, since it adds an extra purchase decision instead of simply raising the plan. Watch whether Retool starts nudging the credit-pack price itself once the purchase option has been live for a few months — that is usually the next move once a vendor validates customers will pay for overage.
In context
Pulse's credits pattern lens counts six Retool pricing changes on this pattern since Apr 2024. Pulse tracked the most recent one on Oct 2, 2026, when Retool pooled AI credits at up to 10,000 a month and ended a $10k Enterprise credit offer, 43 days before this change. Before that, Pulse tracked that Retool opened pay-as-you-go credit packs to every paid plan on Jun 22, 2026, so this FAQ edit is the first time a purchase option has applied once a plan's own pool runs dry, rather than sitting as a separate add-on. Across the companies Pulse tracks, 377 pricing changes touching credits and usage billing landed in the last 180 days, 12.2% of everything tracked, with Pika, Lumaai and Mintlify moving on it most often. The sharper read: Retool is still tuning where its AI-usage meter sits against the seat price, and dropping the line "no add-on, no separate billing" from its FAQ is consistent with letting heavy users buy past the pool instead of hitting a flat cap.
Common questions
How much would extra AI credits cost under this change?
Retool's FAQ update doesn't list a per-pack price — that's set at checkout when a team buys more, so check Retool's pricing page for the current rate before budgeting for overage.
Is this a one-off tweak or part of a bigger pattern?
There's no way to tell from this FAQ alone — Retool doesn't publish a roadmap, so the best signal is whether a credit-pack price shows up on the pricing page in the next update; today's edit only confirms the purchase option exists, not its cost.
Should we offer purchasable usage packs on top of our own pooled limits?
A purchase option for overage can avoid forcing an upsell to a bigger plan when you already pool a resource per seat — but publish the pack price clearly, since Retool's own update doesn't show one yet.
