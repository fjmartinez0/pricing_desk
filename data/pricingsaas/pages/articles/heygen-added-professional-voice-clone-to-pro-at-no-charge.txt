Heygen added Professional Voice Clone to Pro at no charge
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 14, 2026
Heygen added its Professional Voice Clone add-on to the Pro plan for free.

What changed
According to Heygen's pricing page as of Nov 14, 2026, the Pro plan's feature list now includes Professional Voice Clone — the company's highest-fidelity voice clone — at no extra charge, where it was previously priced at $29/mo. Pro's other listed features (4K video export, Access to Seedance 2.5 and Cinematic Avatars) are unchanged, and Creator and Free plan pricing on the same page is unchanged.
Why it matters
For product leaders weighing seat-based AI video tools, folding a previously separate add-on into the base Pro price raises Pro's perceived value without changing the sticker price — a move that can pull upgrades from Creator but compresses add-on revenue per seat. Per Pulse's tracked history, this follows Heygen capping Voice Cloning usage across Creator, Pro and Business plans on Sep 19, 2026, so the timing is notable: the same capability area got a usage ceiling earlier and now gets priced at zero inside Pro. Watch whether Heygen pairs this inclusion with a tighter cap on Professional Voice Clone specifically, the way a freebie sometimes arrives alongside a new limit elsewhere.
In context
Heygen has made 6 real pricing changes since Pulse began tracking it on Jul 20, 2026, averaging about one every 22 days Heygen. Its last move, on Oct 23, 2026, added Voice TTS and the Seedance 2.5 model to Creator and Pro with prices unchanged [[heygen/2026D296]] — a feature addition, the same category as today's. Feature additions are a broad industry pattern too: Pulse tracked 736 of 3,079 pricing changes (23.9%) across 549 companies adding features in the last 180 days, including Together's Nov 13, 2026 GPU rate changes [[together/2026D317]] and MindsDB's Nov 11, 2026 addition of Muse Ember 1.0 pricing [[mindsdb/2026D315]]. Set against Heygen's own Sep 19, 2026 cap on Voice Cloning usage [[heygen/2026D262]], giving away a once-paid voice clone reads as Heygen trading a usage-limit story for a bundling one, not a simple discount.
Common questions
If I'm already on Pro and paying for Professional Voice Clone, what happens to that charge?
It should disappear from your bill — it's the same feature, just folded into the base Pro price starting Nov 14, 2026, per Heygen's pricing page.
What should current Pro subscribers do about this?
Nothing — the change applies automatically on Heygen's page; just confirm your next invoice no longer carries a separate line item for it, and contact support if it still does.
Could Heygen tighten usage limits on this feature now that it's free?
That's the pattern worth watching, not a certainty — nothing on Heygen's current page sets a cap specific to this clone type, so monitor your next render for any new ceiling rather than assuming today's limits hold indefinitely.
