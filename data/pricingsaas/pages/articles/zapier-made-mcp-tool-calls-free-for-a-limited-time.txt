Zapier made MCP tool calls free for a limited time
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 25, 2026
Zapier is giving away MCP tool calls through a limited-time promo.
See before and after
Enlarge
Zapier’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 25, 2026
What changed
Zapier added a site-wide banner to its pricing page announcing that MCP tool calls are free through Jan. 5, 2027, per the banner. Before this change, each MCP call used two tasks from a plan's task quota; the banner suspends that charge for the promo period without touching any other plan pricing or quotas.
Why it matters
For a product leader, this is a usage-growth play, not a pricing change: Zapier is removing the task cost that normally gates AI-agent tool calls through Zapier MCP, making it cheaper to experiment with agentic workflows during the promo window. Temporarily zeroing out the cost of a usage-metered action is a classic way to seed adoption of a newer product surface before it has to earn its keep in the task quota.
Watch whether Zapier converts this into a permanent allowance for MCP calls once the promo ends on Jan. 5, 2027 per its own banner, or whether usage reverts to costing two tasks per call — and whether your own usage-metered AI features could use a similar free window to drive adoption without touching list price.
In context
Zapier moves slowly compared with most Pulse-tracked companies: Pulse has logged just 2 pricing changes from Zapier in the last 90 days and 6 in the last 365, averaging one every 81 days since it began tracking the company on Jan 5, 2024 — this change comes 58 days after Zapier's last move, a Sep 28, 2026 rebrand of "Zap workflows" to "Zaps" [[zapier/2026W40]]. Zapier has no earlier discount of this kind on record, so a free-for-a-limited-time banner is new territory for its pricing page rather than a repeat play. It lands in the middle of a Black Friday wave: Pulse tracked new discounts in 282 of 3,864 pricing changes across 223 companies in the last 180 days, with Openart and Krea among the most frequent, and peers like Otter and Suno running their own Black Friday cuts this same week. What the raw banner understates is the target: unlike most of those peers discounting subscription price, Zapier is discounting usage of a specific, newer product surface (MCP tool calls) rather than a plan.
Common questions
Does this change pricing for Zapier's other tools, like Zaps or Tables?
No — the waiver is scoped to tool calls made through Zapier MCP specifically; everything else on the task-based plans, including Zap runs, keeps using tasks from the same quota as before.
What happens to MCP usage after Jan. 5, 2027?
Zapier's banner only promises the free period through that date; nothing in the diff says the two-tasks-per-call cost returns automatically, so teams relying on the promo should confirm pricing before building it into a permanent workflow.
Is giving away usage of a newer feature a good way to drive adoption?
It's a low-risk lever when the feature is usage-metered rather than seat-priced: Zapier loses a small, bounded amount of task revenue during the promo while building habit around Zapier MCP before deciding how to price it long-term.
