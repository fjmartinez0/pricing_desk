OpenAI expanded its ChatGPT Business promo to all seats
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 25, 2026
OpenAI added fast mode to its ChatGPT Business signup promo for every seat.

OpenAI’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 25, 2026
What changed
Per OpenAI's pricing page, the offer still requires signing up by Nov 30 and still runs through Dec 31, but it now bundles fast mode at no extra cost alongside the existing 2x usage bonus, and eligibility was widened from Standard and Premium seats only to all seats.
Why it matters
For a product leader, this is a sign OpenAI is leaning harder on a limited-time hook rather than changing list price: adding a premium feature (fast mode) to the giveaway, and removing the seat-tier restriction, both widen who the offer reaches without touching the sticker price on Business seats. That's a cheap way to pull fence-sitters into a workspace before the Nov 30 signup deadline OpenAI's pricing page sets, while the usage window it lists through Dec 31 stays fixed.
Watch whether fast mode and the wider seat eligibility end with the promo on Dec 31, or carry over as the new baseline for ChatGPT Business seats.
In context
OpenAI last touched its pricing page on Nov 13, 2026, rewriting all four ChatGPT plan taglines by use case — 12 days before this change. Pulse has tracked 22 pricing changes from OpenAI in the last 90 days alone, well above its average pace of one every 29 days since it began following the company in Nov 2023. Seat-based pricing tweaks are a small slice of the market overall — Pulse tracked seat-pricing changes in 204 of 3,864 pricing moves across 146 companies in the last 180 days — and OpenAI's own last one was in April 2026, when it cut the Business plan from $25 to $20 per user per month, billed annually Business price cut. What the raw diff understates is the asymmetry: OpenAI is sweetening a signup promo while its underlying Business seat price hasn't moved since that April cut.
Common questions
If we already created a ChatGPT Business workspace, do we need to do anything?
No re-signup is needed to keep the coverage you already had under Standard and Premium; the action item is timing — only a workspace created by the Nov 30 cutoff on OpenAI's pricing page qualifies for the wider, fast-mode-included version of the offer.
Is this a sign OpenAI is cutting ChatGPT Business prices?
No — treat it as a giveaway of incremental usage credits and fast-mode access to win new workspaces before a deadline, not a new price point; it's not something to compare against a competitor's list price.
Should we widen our own trial or signup promos the way OpenAI did?
Expanding who qualifies, rather than deepening the discount, is a low-cost way to grow a promo's reach near its deadline — worth doing if your bottleneck is eligibility, not price sensitivity.
