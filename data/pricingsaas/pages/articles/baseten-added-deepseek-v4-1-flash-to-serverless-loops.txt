Baseten added DeepSeek V4.1 Flash to Serverless Loops
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 24, 2026
Baseten added a new AI model to its Loops training pricing.
See before and after
Enlarge
Baseten’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 24, 2026
What changed
Baseten added DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 to its Serverless Loops token pricing table, at $0.48 prefill, $0.10 cached prefill, $2.88 sample and $3.98 train per 1M tokens, 20% off for a limited time, according to Baseten's pricing page on Nov 24, 2026. The other models already on the Loops table, including GLM-5.3 and GLM-5.3-Flash, are unchanged.
Why it matters
For a product leader evaluating inference and training infra vendors, the takeaway isn't DeepSeek's specific rate — it's that Baseten is treating its Serverless Loops catalog the same way it treats its Model APIs catalog: new models arrive priced and discounted rather than requiring a separate launch cycle. Pulse has recorded 14 pricing changes on Baseten's page in the last 90 days, averaging one every 8 days, so a 20%-off intro rate on a newly added model is closer to Baseten's default motion than a one-time promotion. Watch whether that introductory discount survives past its "limited time" window the way Baseten's other Loops models have (or haven't) once they've been on the table a while.
In context
Baseten's page moves fast — averaging a pricing change every 8 days — and this one lands just 3 days after Baseten added bison-1 and groundhog-1 to its Model APIs catalog on Nov 21, 2026. Loops itself is young: Baseten launched Serverless Loops training with a 20% intro discount on GLM-5.3 and Qwen3.6 on Nov 7, 2026, per Pulse's tracked diffs, so this is the same playbook applied to a newly added model rather than a new product motion. Market-wide, Pulse tracked 479 credits-and-usage-billing changes across 302 companies in the last 180 days, 12.4% of the 3,855 changes tracked. The sharper read: Baseten is building out Loops the same way it built its Model APIs catalog — adding models continuously with an introductory discount — rather than pricing the product once and leaving it static.
Common questions
How does Baseten's 20%-off intro pricing compare with what competitors charge for similar training workloads?
The pack doesn't include other vendors' training-token rates for comparison, so that benchmark isn't available here — the safer move is comparing Baseten's post-discount rate against your own current training spend rather than against a market figure Pulse doesn't carry.
How does the intro discount get removed once it ends?
The pack doesn't show that mechanism — the badge just says "20% off for limited time" with no end date, the same wording Baseten used when Loops launched on Nov 7, 2026, so there's no way yet to tell from Pulse's record how or when these introductory rates expire.
Does Baseten's Model APIs catalog use the same pricing structure as Loops?
No — Model APIs prices a single per-1M-token input/output rate per model (for example, bison-1 and groundhog-1 from $0.035/1M tokens, added Nov 21, 2026 per Pulse's tracked diffs), while Loops breaks pricing into prefill, cached prefill, sample and train rates for training workloads — different metrics for a different workload.
