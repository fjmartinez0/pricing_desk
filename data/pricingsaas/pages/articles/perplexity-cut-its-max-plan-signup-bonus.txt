Perplexity cut its Max plan signup bonus
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 25, 2026
Perplexity lowered its Max plan's credit signup bonus.

Perplexity’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 25, 2026
What changed
Perplexity's Max plan cut its limited-time signup bonus from $450 to $350 in Computer credits, according to the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 25, 2026. The same pricing page also dropped the itemized bonus-credit line for Pro and Max — which had spelled out 4,000 and 35,000 credits — leaving the credit total off the page entirely, according to the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 25, 2026.
Why it matters
For a product leader watching usage-based pricing, this is a small lever pull rather than a core reprice: Perplexity trimmed how many credits a new Max signup gets, not the plan price itself. Pulse tracked 480 pricing changes involving credits and usage billing among 303 companies in the last 180 days — 12.4% of the 3,864 changes it tracked in that window — so trimming a bonus or free-credit allotment, rather than touching the sticker price, is a common first move when a team wants to protect margin without raising headline prices.
Perplexity itself has made 14 changes to its pricing page in the last 365 days, 11 of those in just the last 90 days, averaging roughly 10 days between changes, according to Pulse's record — a cadence built from small, frequent dials rather than big public repricings. Watch whether Perplexity follows this bonus cut with a trim to Pro's or Max's monthly credit allotments.
In context
Perplexity's previous pricing-page move, per Pulse, came six days earlier, on Nov 19, 2026, when it swapped Gemini 4 Hydrogen for Gemini Neon in its AI model lineup Nov 19 model swap — one of 11 changes in the last 90 days, nearly all model swaps rather than price or credit moves. This is the first credit change Pulse has recorded for Perplexity: earlier moves, such as an Aug 17, 2026 model-lineup swap that left plan prices, credits and features unchanged Aug 17 lineup swap, did not touch credits, so one credit cut isn't yet a pattern for Perplexity itself. It does fit a broader shift: Pulse tracked 480 credit-and-usage-billing changes across 303 companies in the last 180 days, 12.4% of everything it tracked in that window, with Pika (14 mentions) and Lumaai (9) the most frequent movers and recent examples at Braintrust and Devin this past week. For Perplexity, a smaller signup bonus paired with a dropped credit line reads less like belt-tightening on current users and more like trimming the number it uses to win new ones.
Common questions
How should we size a signup bonus for our own product?
If you offer an upfront credit grant to new signups, size it the same way: multiply your per-account bonus by expected monthly signups to see the margin trade-off a cut like this one represents at your volume.
Is this part of a broader pattern at Perplexity, or a one-off?
Not as a credit-specific move — this is the first time Pulse has seen Perplexity touch a credit figure, even though the company has reshuffled its AI model lineup repeatedly this quarter; see In context for the cadence.
Should we trim a signup bonus instead of cutting list price when margin tightens?
It's worth considering when your acquisition math leans on an upfront credit grant: trimming the welcome bonus keeps your advertised price intact while still tightening margin on new signups — though with only one example here, it's too early to call it a trend worth copying.
