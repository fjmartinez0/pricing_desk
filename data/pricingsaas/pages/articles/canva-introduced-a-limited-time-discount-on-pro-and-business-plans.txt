Canva introduced a limited-time discount on Pro and Business plans
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 20, 2026
Canva offered 50% off Pro and Business for the first three months on monthly plans.

What changed
Canva replaced the banner across the top of its pricing page: an AI Pass add-on promo gave way to a new offer of 50% off Pro or Business for the first three months, according to the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 20, 2026. The discount applies to monthly billing only, is limited to select countries, and is not available alongside Canva's free trial. List prices for every plan are unchanged — the cut only shows up during the promo window, after which billing reverts to the standard monthly rate.
Why it matters
For a product leader managing seat-based plans, a short, geo-targeted discount like this is a way to pull in new monthly subscribers without touching the list price — useful when annual commitments already carry their own built-in discount and the team doesn't want to disturb existing customers' invoices. Pairing the offer with a free-trial exclusion matters too: it pushes undecided users to convert on a paid plan rather than stack a trial on top of a discount, which keeps the effective price floor intact. Watch whether Canva lets the offer run past the first three months without announcing it, or pulls it on schedule — a promo that keeps extending is functionally a price cut, even if the list price never moves.
In context
Canva's pricing has not sat still in 2026: Pulse's record shows Pro's annual price rising from $144 to $180 in July 2026 July's increase, then dropping 20% back to $144 in September 2026 September's cut, and the free plan's AI allowance swapped for a vaguer limited access just 21 days before this discount, on Oct 30, 2026 the AI quota swap. That is four changes in the last 90 days against Canva's own roughly 48-day average gap since Nov 2023, per Pulse's record, a faster pace than usual, not a one-off. The new discount's restrictions, monthly billing only, select countries, no stacking with a free trial, echo how Canva narrowed free-trial access to its AI tools in June 2026 the trial limit: incentives keep getting fenced off from each other rather than combined. For a peer team, a short discount like this is cheap to test against a pricing page this busy; the harder question is whether users who convert during the promo stick around once the list price returns in month four.
Common questions
How do we size this for our own team?
Multiply whatever monthly rate you're quoted by your headcount, then apply the discount before it reverts to the standard rate in month four.
What happens once the three months are up?
The item doesn't spell out an expiration mechanic, but with list prices stated as unchanged, billing should return to the standard monthly rate after the discount window — check Canva's terms before you budget past month three.
Why doesn't the discount stack with a free trial?
Canva doesn't give a stated reason, but it fits a pattern: across the pricing changes Pulse tracked in the last 180 days, 164 of 3,414 (4.8%) touched free trials, and Canva itself narrowed free-trial access to its AI tools in June 2026 — keeping trials and discounts from compounding looks like policy, not a one-off restriction.
