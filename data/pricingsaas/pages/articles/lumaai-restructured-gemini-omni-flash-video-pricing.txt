PRICE DECREASE
Lumaai restructured Gemini Omni Flash video pricing
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 19, 2026
Lumaai moved Gemini Omni Flash video pricing to per-second billing.

What changed
Lumaai restructured Gemini Omni Flash video pricing (3-to-10-second clips) from a flat per-video rate to per-second billing: Text-to-Video and Image-to-Video at 720p moved from up to 370 credits per video to 36 credits per second, capping at 360 credits for a 10-second clip, and the Modify 720p rate was cut from 39 to 38 credits per second, per Lumaai's pricing page as of Nov 19, 2026.
Separately, per Lumaai's pricing page as of Nov 19, 2026, Ray3.14 lost its HDR+EXR output tier (a 3x-cost multiplier option) — only SDR (base) and HDR (2x) remain for that model, while Ray3.2 still offers HDR+EXR. MiniMax H3 Max's extra reference images, the ones beyond the first 2 to 4 free, now cost 7 to 18 credits each, up from roughly 7 to 13 credits, per Lumaai's pricing page as of Nov 19, 2026.
Why it matters
For a product leader running usage-based AI pricing, Lumaai's move from flat per-video to per-second billing on Gemini Omni Flash is a structural change, not just a price cut: per-second metering ties cost directly to clip length, rewarding short generations and penalizing long ones in a way a flat per-video rate never did — worth modeling against your own typical clip length before assuming the new rate is cheaper for you specifically.
The same diff also narrows an output tier (Ray3.14's HDR+EXR) while raising a different cost (MiniMax H3 Max reference images) — a sign Lumaai is actively tuning margin model by model rather than moving prices in one direction. Watch whether Ray3.2's HDR+EXR option survives the next update or gets trimmed too, which would signal Lumaai retiring that output format rather than just cutting one model's tier.
In context
Lumaai has reworked pricing frequently this year: per Pulse's tracked history, it cut Seedance pricing up to 73% on Sep 19, 2026 [[lumaai/2026D262]], doubled Seedance video-to-video costs on Oct 5, 2026 [[lumaai/2026W41]], then halved Video-to-Video rates again on Nov 7, 2026 [[lumaai/2026D311]] — and today's diff cuts one model's rate while raising another's, on a page it has changed every 13 days on average since tracking began Jul 20, 2026. Pulse tracked 414 credits-and-usage-billing changes across 276 companies in the last 180 days, and Pika is part of that same wave, offering unlimited Wan 3.0 generations on its Fancy plan through Nov 23 [[pika/2026D322]]. The sharper read: in AI-video pricing, cheaper and more expensive are often true of the same company in the same month, model by model, so a single headline number rarely describes the whole page.
Common questions
Is Gemini Omni Flash cheaper now or more expensive?
It depends on clip length — the per-second structure benefits short clips, so run the math against your typical generation length before assuming the new rate saves you money across the board.
What happened to Ray3.14's top output tier?
HDR+EXR is gone as an option; only SDR and HDR remain on that model, while Ray3.2 still lists HDR+EXR, so check which Ray version your workflow depends on before this spreads further.
Why did MiniMax H3 Max reference images get pricier?
Lumaai's page gives no stated reason for the increase — worth watching whether it's an isolated adjustment or the start of a broader reference-image repricing.
