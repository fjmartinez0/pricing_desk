Wisprflow made Free plan Notetaker meetings unlimited

What changed
Wisprflow's Free plan Notetaker access changed from "Limited Notetaker access (Mac, Windows, iPhone)" to "Unlimited meetings · 30-day meeting history on desktop," on the Wisprflow pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 21, 2026.
Notetaker, Wisprflow's AI meeting-notes feature, first appeared on the Free and Pro plans on Aug 10, 2026, the day Pulse first tracked it. Pulse has tracked five Free plan changes from Wisprflow since that Aug 10, 2026 launch. Pulse also tracked Wisprflow extending Notetaker to iPhone two days earlier, on Nov 19, 2026.
Why it matters
For a product leader, this says more about Wisprflow's growth funnel than about Notetaker specifically. A free tier that keeps gaining real capability — unlimited meetings and a genuine 30-day history, not just a bigger sample — lowers the switching cost of trying the product and raises the bar competitors have to clear to still look generous by comparison. It's also a one-way lever: once a capped feature goes unlimited on a free plan, pulling it back reads as a downgrade, so Wisprflow's differentiation between Free and Pro now has to come from somewhere other than this usage ceiling — seats, admin controls, or integrations.
Watch whether Wisprflow pairs this kind of free-tier loosening with new Pro-only capability, so paid differentiation doesn't erode, or whether it keeps using the free plan as a wider top-of-funnel while monetization shifts toward seats and admin features instead.
In context
Pulse has tracked five Free plan changes from Wisprflow since Notetaker launched on Aug 10, 2026 [[wisprflow/2026W33]], most recently adding Notetaker to iPhone on Nov 19, 2026 [[wisprflow/2026D323]] and swapping a disclosed 1,000-word mobile cap for a vague phrase on Oct 20, 2026 [[wisprflow/2026D293]]. Across its full tracked history, Pulse has logged 9 real pricing changes from Wisprflow since Aug 3, 2026, averaging one every 13 days — a cadence that makes this week's move look less like a one-off than a free plan still being tuned in public. The broader market backs that read: Pulse tracked 209 pricing changes across 166 companies touching free plans in the last 180 days, 6.1% of the 3,421 changes tracked in that window. What the unlimited-meetings headline understates is the direction: Wisprflow keeps removing caps and hiding the exact numbers behind them rather than adding new ones, favoring growth and simplicity on Free over precise metering — the opposite of the usage-based tightening some AI tools are applying to their own free tiers.
Common questions
Does this change reach Wisprflow's paid Pro plan too?
No — this move is scoped to the Free plan. Pulse tracked Pro's Notetaker meetings becoming unlimited on Nov 1, 2026, so the gap on this specific limit between Free and Pro has now closed.
What should we check before copying this on our own free tier?
Whether a usage cap is actually what's blocking signups, or whether people are already converting past it for other reasons — loosening a limit only grows the funnel if that cap, not something else like seats or support, was the real friction.
Is this the typical way companies are changing free plans right now?
Not quite. Pulse also saw Renderforest and Deepbrain adjust their free plans on Nov 16, 2026, and Doodle and Pubnub make free-plan changes of their own in the same stretch — removing an existing limit, as Wisprflow did here, is one of several different moves Pulse is tracking in free plans this season, alongside companies adding fresh credits or new feature callouts instead.
