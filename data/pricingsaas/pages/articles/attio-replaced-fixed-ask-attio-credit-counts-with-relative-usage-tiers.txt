Attio replaced fixed Ask Attio credit counts with relative usage tiers
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 27, 2026
Attio replaced fixed Ask Attio credit allotments with relative usage tiers.

What changed
Attio renamed Ask Attio credits to Ask Attio usage and, according to the Attio pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 27, 2026, replaced the fixed monthly allotments — 100, 500, 1,000 and 2,500 — with relative tiers labeled Limited usage, More usage, 3x Plus and 5x Plus across the Free, Plus, Pro and Ultra plans. The same four tier names now also appear in Attio's plan-comparison table in place of a specific credit count on each card.
Why it matters
For a product leader shipping metered AI features, this is the credits-to-tiers playbook: swapping a fixed monthly count for named relative tiers removes the one number a prospect can budget against, and lets Attio tune the real usage ceiling behind each tier without touching the price or publishing a new cap. It also removes an easy point of comparison for a competitor's pricing page or a customer's own usage math.
The tradeoff cuts the other way for buyers: a team evaluating Plus versus Pro can no longer tell from the page alone how many more Ask Attio queries the upgrade buys, only that it is "more." Watch whether Attio later attaches real numbers back to these tier names once usage patterns settle.
In context
This is Attio's second pricing move in 90 days and its first since shifting to per-workspace pricing and adding a $1,000/mo Ultra plan on Nov 11, 2026, just 16 days earlier — a company that has changed its pricing page every 33 days on average since Pulse began tracking it on Jun 8, 2026. For context, Pulse tracked 483 pricing changes involving credits or usage billing among 306 companies in the last 180 days, 12.5% of all 3,873 changes it tracked in that window, with Pika and Lumaai moving on it most often. The sharper read: trading a fixed credit count for named relative tiers is the same unbundling move AI-feature vendors are making elsewhere — Braintrust renamed its own credit unit on Nov 24, 2026 — swapping a visible ceiling for one a vendor can redraw without a pricing-page change. For a product leader, the pattern matters more than Attio's specific tiers: once a usage limit stops being a number, it stops being something your prospects can compare across vendors.
Common questions
What would a team moving from Plus to Pro actually gain in Ask Attio usage?
Attio doesn't say: the new tiers are named, not sized, so the only way to compare them today is to test Ask Attio on each plan directly rather than read the plan-comparison table.
Should we rename our own usage caps the way Attio just did?
Only if you're ready to give up a number prospects could check against competitors: Attio traded a concrete figure for a label only its own page defines, which works best when your usage economics, not your marketing, are the harder thing to copy.
Does this change what Ask Attio costs on any plan?
No — Pulse's diff shows only the usage-tier relabeling on Free, Plus, Pro and Ultra; it carries no price change for Attio's plans.
