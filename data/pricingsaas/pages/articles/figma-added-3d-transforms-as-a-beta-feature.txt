Figma added 3D transforms as a beta feature
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 13, 2026
Figma added 3D transforms as a new beta feature across every plan.

3D TRANSFORMS (BETA)·first seen Nov 13, 2026
What changed
Figma added a new row, 3D transforms (Beta), to the feature comparison table for Figma Design. Pulse detected the change on Nov 13, 2026, and the capability appears with a checkmark across all its plans — Starter, Professional, Organization and Enterprise — rather than being gated to a single tier.
The listing describes 3D transforms as native 3D design inside Figma, with depth, perspective and CSS compatibility, per the feature description on Figma's pricing page. This is the feature's first appearance on the comparison table, per Pulse's tracking of Figma, so there is no earlier beta-entry date to report.
Why it matters
For a growth-stage SaaS or AI company watching Figma, this is a packaging signal more than a pricing one: 3D transforms ships to every tier at once instead of being reserved for Enterprise, continuing a pattern of broad feature rollout rather than tier-gating. Pulse tracked 8 pricing-page changes for Figma in the last 90 days, with the previous one — opening Specs beta to all seats — coming just 15 days earlier, on Oct 29, 2026, per Pulse's tracking of Figma's pricing page. That cadence, real functionality added to the comparison table every few weeks, suggests Figma is still competing on capability breadth across its whole base rather than moving features up the ladder to extract more per seat. Watch whether 3D transforms stays free at every tier once it exits beta, or whether Figma repeats the Specs pattern and later restricts earlier-stage access.
In context
Figma already uses beta labeling more than most companies Pulse tracks: per Pulse's tracking, Specs debuted in beta on Oct 15, 2026 and opened to all paid seats on Oct 29, 2026 Specs debuted in beta opened to all seats. Pulse counted 65 beta-label changes across 45 companies in the last 180 days — 2.1% of the 3,077 pricing changes it tracked in that window — with HubSpot (7 changes) and Vercel (5 changes) using the label most often, and Figma itself appearing on 5 of those lines HubSpot Vercel. Other companies are moving the opposite way this month: Vercel and Tango both dropped beta tags from features they'd shipped earlier Vercel Tango. For Figma, beta has mostly been a short staging ground rather than a long caveat — Specs moved from debut to full availability inside two weeks, per Pulse's tracking — so 3D transforms landing in beta across every tier reads as an early, low-commitment preview of a capability Figma is likely to broaden or start differentiating on soon.
Common questions
Does this cost extra on any plan?
No — 3D transforms (Beta) shows a checkmark on Starter through Enterprise alike on Figma's pricing page, so teams don't need to upgrade or add anything to get it once it's live for their seat.
Will it stay free once it exits beta?
Figma's record cuts both ways, per Pulse's tracking: Specs kept full access after its beta ended on Oct 29, 2026, while Figma's AI agent began charging AI credits once its beta ended on Oct 12, 2026 — so the comparison table is worth rechecking when 3D transforms graduates rather than assuming either outcome.
