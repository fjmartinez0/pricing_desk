Lumaai added a new Seedance video model
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 20, 2026
Lumaai added a new Seedance 2.5 Flash video model to its pricing page.

What changed
Lumaai added a new model, Seedance 2.5 Flash, to its Cost Per Video Generation table: Text-to-Video and Image-to-Video run 24 credits/sec at 480p and 51 credits/sec at 720p, while Video-to-Video runs 15 credits/sec at 480p and 32 at 720p, according to the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 20, 2026. The existing Seedance 2.0 and Seedance 2.0 Mini rows are unchanged — this is a new row alongside them, not a repricing.
Why it matters
For a product leader running usage-based or credits-metered pricing, the signal here is not really the new model — it's the cadence: Lumaai shipped this addition one day after cutting a different model's rate, and credits and usage billing is the lever it pulls almost every time it touches its pricing page, whether that means a new model, a rate change, or both. If you're competing on model breadth inside a credits economy, a once-a-week catalog refresh may no longer be fast enough to keep up. Watch whether rivals start matching this release cadence, or whether Lumaai settles down once its catalog stabilizes — a company shipping pricing changes this often is still finding its model, not running a mature one.
In context
Pulse has tracked Lumaai since Jul 20, 2026 and already logged 11 real pricing changes, 10 of them in the last 90 days — this is its second change in two days, following a Nov 19, 2026 cut to Gemini Omni Flash's video rate and the removal of Ray3.14 HDR+EXR yesterday's cut. Pulse shows nearly every one of those changes touched credits or usage billing: it halved Video-to-Video rates while adding Kling 4.0 Flash models on Nov 7 the Nov 7 overhaul, and slashed Seedance pricing by up to 73% in September 2026 September's Seedance cut. At roughly a 12-day average gap between changes, per Pulse's record, Lumaai moves faster than most: across the companies Pulse tracks, 416 pricing changes touched credits or usage billing in the last 180 days, 12.2% of the 3,414 tracked. For a peer team, the model here is not set usage pricing and leave it; it is closer to a software release cycle, with rates and catalog shipped incrementally.
Common questions
Does this affect what we already pay for Seedance 2.0 or 2.0 Mini?
No — the item confirms Seedance 2.0 and 2.0 Mini rows are unchanged; Seedance 2.5 Flash is a new, separate line, not a repricing of what you're using today.
How should we handle a pricing page that moves this often?
Check Lumaai's rate card before every project estimate, not just once a quarter — recent changes have landed a day or two apart, so a rate you quoted last month may already be stale.
Should we expect similarly volatile rates from other video-model vendors?
Treat this as a category trait, not a Lumaai quirk: Pulse tracked 13 credits-related pricing changes from Pika alone in the last 180 days, so confirm current rates before you build a cost model on any single vendor's numbers.
