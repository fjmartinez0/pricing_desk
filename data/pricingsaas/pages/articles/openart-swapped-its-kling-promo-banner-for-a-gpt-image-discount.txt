Openart swapped its Kling promo banner for a GPT Image discount
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 5, 2026
Openart swapped its Kling 4.0 promo banner for a 30%-off GPT Image 2.6 discount.

What changed
Openart's pricing page lists a 30% discount on GPT Image 2.6 generations for Wonder and Pro subscribers until Nov 17. The banner it replaced had advertised 50% off Kling 4.0 generations for Wonder and Pro until Nov 20, per Openart's pricing page. Openart first launched that Kling 4.0 promo on Oct 26, 2026, according to Pulse's tracked history. Its 50%-off Kling 4.0 badges remain live on the Pro and Wonder plan cards, according to the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 5, 2026, so Wonder and Pro buyers now see both promos running at once, just with GPT Image 2.6 in the banner slot.
Why it matters
Pulse has tracked 25 real pricing changes on Openart's pricing page since it began tracking the company on May 16, 2026 — about one every 7 days — including 6 that added a new discount, so this banner swap fits a weekly rhythm, not a one-off. Running Kling 4.0 and GPT Image 2.6 discounts side by side lets Openart keep rotating the spotlight toward whichever model needs a demand boost without ever touching list price.
For a growth-stage SaaS or AI team, the lesson is about cadence more than this specific discount: a banner swap costs nothing to reverse or administer, so it's a cheap lever to test which model or feature draws upgrades. Teams selling credits or compute-metered plans should watch whether rotating, model-specific discounts like this move more users to upper tiers than a blanket percentage off everything would.
In context
Pulse's pattern tracking shows Openart has launched six new discounts since it began tracking the company on May 16, 2026. The most recent was the 50%-off Kling 4.0 promo, which Openart launched on Oct 26, 2026, per Pulse's tracked history. Market-wide, Pulse counted 127 new-discount changes across 101 companies in the last 180 days — 4.7% of the 2,691 pricing changes it tracked in that window. No other company in that window matched Openart's pace; Runway ran its own Kling 4.0 discount in the same period, per Pulse's tracked history. The sharper read: Openart isn't discounting its Starter-through-Wonder list prices at all, it's discounting specific models inside those plans, rotating which generation type gets the spotlight — the same seat-to-usage pattern where AI companies increasingly compete on per-model promos rather than seat price.
Common questions
What should we watch for next on this promo?
Whether the GPT Image 2.6 banner runs to its listed end date or gets swapped again before then — a pattern of early swaps rather than expirations would mean the "limited-time" framing is really a rotating spotlight tool, not a real deadline.
What doesn't Pulse's record show here?
Redemption or usage data — the diff tracks banner and badge text on the page, not how many Wonder or Pro customers actually used either discount, so the real business impact of swapping promos isn't something this record can confirm.
