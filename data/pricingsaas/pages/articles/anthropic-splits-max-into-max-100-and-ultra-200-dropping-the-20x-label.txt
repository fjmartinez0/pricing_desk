PACKAGING CHANGE
Anthropic splits Max into Max ($100) and Ultra ($200), dropping the 20x label
Anthropic replaced the single Max plan's two-tier usage toggle ("From $100, 5x or 20x Pro usage") with two named plans.

WHY IT MATTERS
Separating Max and Ultra lets Anthropic price, market and change its top tier without touching Max. Buyers now compare two named plans with explicit limits: Max at 5x Pro usage every 5 hours and every week, and Ultra at 10x Pro usage every week with no 5-hour limit for verified accounts. Watch whether Anthropic widens the price gap between the two now that they are separate, and whether other AI vendors with a single top-end usage toggle follow with the same split.
BY THE NUMBERS
Max 20x renamed Ultra at $200/month, keeping its $200/month API credit allotment
Max now $100/month with 5x Pro usage per 5 hours and per week, plus $100/month in API credits
Ultra: 10x Pro usage per week, no 5-hour limit for verified accounts
4 pricing changes in the last 90 days; previous change was 19 days ago
THEIR RATIONALE
Anthropic doesn't frame this as a price change: the dollar amounts and credit allotments for both tiers carry over unchanged from the old From-$100 toggle. Giving each option its own name and page row reads as a packaging clarification. Ultra becomes a distinct product rather than a modifier on Max, from a company that touched its Max plan just 19 days earlier, on Oct 12. Dropping "Higher output limits for all tasks" from the Max card narrows what the $100 tier explicitly promises.

THE SIGNAL
Anthropic has now changed its Max plan three times in about six weeks: the Sept 17 Pro/Max revamp, the Oct 12 API-credit addition, and this split. That is tighter than the company's usual 36-day gap between changes of any kind. Splitting a bundled tier into two separately priced, separately named plans is a packaging move in a busy part of the market: 284 pricing changes across 201 companies touched credits or usage billing in the last 180 days. Naming Ultra gives Anthropic a dedicated upsell target instead of a toggle inside Max.
