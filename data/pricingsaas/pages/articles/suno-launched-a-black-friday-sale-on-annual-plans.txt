Suno launched a Black Friday sale on annual plans
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 24, 2026
Suno cut its annual plan prices with a new seasonal discount.

Suno’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 24, 2026
What changed
Suno added a Black Friday Sale banner with a countdown timer, stacking an extra 30% off annual plans on top of its already-discounted annual pricing and setting first-year promo rates of $5.60 per month for Pro, $16.80 for Premier and $70 for Platinum, according to Suno's pricing page on Nov 24, 2026. The plan features, monthly pricing and page copy are otherwise unchanged.
Why it matters
For a product leader, the sequencing here is the real story: Suno added its Platinum tier just one day before layering a Black Friday discount on top of the whole plan lineup, which suggests the company is using the holiday window to push adoption of a packaging change it just made rather than running the sale in isolation. A discount stacked on top of already-discounted annual pricing means the renewal price a year from now is the real test of whether customers accepted the new packaging or just the lower introductory rate — worth tracking whether Suno's list prices move again once the promo expires. For AI-subscription pricing broadly, a first-year promo rate is a cheap way to reset the anchor price a buyer compares against, so watch whether competitors follow with their own stacked, time-boxed discounts rather than permanent cuts.
In context
Suno has made 7 real pricing changes since Pulse started tracking its page in Jun 2026, averaging one every 26 days, and this discount lands just 1 day after Suno launched its new Platinum plan. This is also the first time Pulse has recorded Suno running a site-wide discount, arriving in the same Black Friday week as Bitdefender's sale of up to 58% off on Nov 23, 2026 and Codecademy's 60% off Pro, also on Nov 23, 2026. Pulse tracked 279 new-discount changes across 220 companies in the last 180 days, so a seasonal promo stacked on existing annual pricing is now common practice rather than a standout move. The sharper read: a pricing move one day after the Platinum launch is far faster than Suno's own 26-day average gap, suggesting the Platinum launch and this discount are a single coordinated Black Friday push rather than separate decisions.
Common questions
Has Suno been tightening limits elsewhere while discounting price?
Yes — Suno added download caps and credit-to-song equivalents in Aug 2026, live by Sep 2026 per Pulse's tracked history, so this discount lands on a plan that already saw a capacity cut this year.
What's mechanically different about a stacked promo versus a straight price cut?
A stacked promo layers a temporary reduction on top of an already-discounted annual price, so reverting after the holiday just means removing a banner rather than restoring a changed number on the page — a lighter rollback than an outright price cut.
How common is this kind of stacked annual discount right now?
About 7.2% of the 3,855 pricing changes Pulse tracked market-wide in the last 180 days were new discounts like this one, so it's common enough that a seasonal promo on annual pricing shouldn't read as a standout signal about Suno specifically — the base rate is already high enough to expect it somewhere most weeks.
