WHY IT MATTERS
For IT buyers, SCIM and audit logs are what move Replit from a developer tool to something procurement can approve — most enterprise security reviews require both. Losing the explicit "static outbound IPs" and "custom seat limits" bullets is worth a question for existing Enterprise customers who rely on IP allowlisting or negotiated seat caps, even if "private networking options" covers similar ground. Watch whether Replit ever publishes Enterprise pricing, or whether it stays a Custom/Contact sales tier indefinitely.

THEIR RATIONALE
This Enterprise update follows a stretch of tightening on Replit's self-serve tiers: on Sep 11 it axed the free Starter plan and tied Core and Pro's remaining perks to Free Mode usage, days after tightening agent limits and capping the free tier at one background task on Sep 2. Rather than cut Enterprise too, Replit added the compliance checklist items (SCIM, audit logs) that large security teams require before approving a contract — while dropping two specific line items: static outbound IPs and custom seat limits.
