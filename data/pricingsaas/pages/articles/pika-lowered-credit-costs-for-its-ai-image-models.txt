Pika lowered credit costs for its AI image models
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 17, 2026
Pika cut the credit cost per image for GPT Image 2.6 and Nano Banana 2.1.
See before and after
Enlarge
Pika’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 17, 2026
What changed
Per the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 17, 2026, Pika lowered the credit cost of GPT Image 2.6 and Nano Banana 2.1: GPT Image 2.6 dropped from 4 to 3 credits per generation, and Nano Banana 2.1 dropped from 5 to 4 credits per generation. Pika's plan credit budgets did not change, so the lower per-image rate raises how many images each plan's monthly credits buy.
For GPT Image 2.6, Pika's pricing page shows the Starter plan's monthly image allotment rising from 150 to 200, Creator from 650 to 866, and Fancy from 2,137+ to 2,850+ — up to a 33% gain, as saved by Pulse on Nov 17, 2026. For Nano Banana 2.1, Pika's pricing page shows Starter rising from 120 to 150 images, Creator from 520 to 650, and Fancy from 1,710+ to 2,137+, also as of Nov 17, 2026.
Why it matters
For a product leader watching AI tools shift from flat seats to usage-based credits, this is the rarer move: Pika just made its own usage meter more generous instead of less. Cutting the per-image rate on GPT Image 2.6 and Nano Banana 2.1, without touching the Starter, Creator or Fancy plan prices, means existing customers get more output from the same spend — a cheaper way to defend against churn than a price cut on the plan itself.
It also lands right after Pika tightened the same lever: Pulse recorded GPT Image 2.6's per-generation cost doubling to 4 credits on Nov 13, 2026 (which halved the images each plan covered) and Starter and Creator's monthly credit caps being cut by 33–17% on Nov 6, 2026. Today's cut only partly unwinds that squeeze: GPT Image 2.6 settles at 3 credits, short of its rate from before the increase, so teams building on Pika's API should watch whether this is a one-time correction or the start of a steadier cadence before locking in usage assumptions.
In context
Per Pulse's tracking, Pika has repriced its usage meters more often than almost anyone else: 12 real changes since Jun 8, 2026, averaging one every 14 days, including ending free H3 Flex generations just a day before this cut and doubling GPT Image 2.6 to 4 credits on Nov 13. Today's move partly undoes that squeeze rather than reversing it outright — GPT Image 2.6 settles at 3 credits, short of its pre-increase rate — while Nano Banana 2.1 gets a smaller cut on top of credit caps Pika already trimmed on Nov 6. Pulse tracked 392 credits-and-usage pricing changes across 265 companies in the last 180 days, eight of them at Pika, so this looks like one data point in an unusually fast-moving meter, not a one-off correction.
Common questions
Does this change touch Pika's top-up credit packs too?
No — the record only covers the per-generation rate inside each plan's monthly allotment; it says nothing about top-up pack pricing, so check that page separately before assuming your overage cost shifted as well.
What should a team on Pika's Creator plan actually do about this?
Nothing needs changing today — just recompute your expected monthly image count at the new per-generation rate before setting an internal usage cap; the allotment already moved in your favor.
