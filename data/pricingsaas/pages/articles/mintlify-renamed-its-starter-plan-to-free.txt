What changed
Mintlify renamed its entry-level plan from Starter to Free on Nov 17, 2026, according to Pulse, keeping the price at $0 per month and the limit at 5 editors unchanged. No other terms on the page changed.
Why it matters
For a product leader, this is pure positioning, not a pricing move: Mintlify's lowest tier already cost nothing, so calling it Free rather than Starter removes any ambiguity for a visitor deciding whether to sign up. A rename like this often precedes or accompanies tightening limits elsewhere in the funnel, since "free" sets a clearer expectation that the paid tiers are where real capability lives. If your own entry plan has a name that undersells what it costs, this is a cheap test to try before touching price. Watch whether Mintlify adjusts the Free plan's limits in its next change, since a rename this close to other feature work on the page can be the first half of a bigger packaging move.
In context
Pulse has tracked 25 real pricing changes on Mintlify since Oct 21, 2024, but this is only its second plan rename — the first swapped Enterprise for Custom on Nov 8, 2025 Nov 2025. Its previous change came just 12 days earlier, renaming Automations to Autopilot on Nov 5, 2026 Nov 5. Plan renames are a minor pattern across the market: Pulse tracked 74 of 3,196 pricing changes in the last 180 days touching a rename, spread across 70 companies, with Captions and Salesforce the most frequent movers at three each. Most of those recent renames came bundled with a bigger packaging change — Flutterflow renamed Growth and Business to Pro and Max while revamping AI limits on Nov 9, 2026 — which makes Mintlify's clean, limits-unchanged rename the simpler version of the pattern.
Common questions
Should we rename our own $0 tier if it isn't called Free?
If "Free" more clearly states what the tier costs than your current name, this costs nothing to test — nothing about the plan's economics needs to change to run it.
Could this rename be a prelude to a Free-tier limit cut?
Not based on the record so far: Mintlify's only earlier rename wasn't followed by a reported limit change on that plan, but it's worth checking Mintlify's next move before assuming Free stays as generous as Starter was.
Does calling it Free change how the plan shows up in search or comparison tools?
Possibly — a plan literally named Free is more likely to surface in searches like "tool name plus free plan" than one named Starter, though Pulse's data doesn't measure search behavior directly.
