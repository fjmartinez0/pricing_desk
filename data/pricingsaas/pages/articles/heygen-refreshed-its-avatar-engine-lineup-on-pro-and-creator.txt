PACKAGING CHANGE
Heygen refreshed its Avatar engine lineup on Pro and Creator
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 15, 2026
Heygen added Avatar 6 Pro to its Pro plan and replaced Creator's older Avatar engines.
See before and after

Heygen’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 15, 2026
What changed
Heygen added Avatar 6 Pro, a new line in the Pro plan's "Pro only features" box, described on the page as Heygen's most lifelike avatar yet. The same update swapped what the Creator plan lists for its AI Avatar engines, replacing Avatar IV and Avatar V with Avatar 6 Turbo and Avatar Lipsync, per Heygen's own description of the change. Neither plan's monthly price changed.
Why it matters
For a product leader, this is a reminder that AI video and model vendors increasingly use engine-name refreshes — new engines and features layered onto an existing plan — as a lower-friction way to show progress than raising prices. Pulse tracked 740 feature-addition changes across 549 companies in the last 180 days, 24% of the 3,089 pricing-page changes it tracked in that window, making this one of the most common moves in SaaS and AI pricing today.
If your own roadmap ships model or engine upgrades, bundling them into an existing tier the way Heygen did here avoids resetting what customers expect to pay while still giving sales and marketing a new capability to point to. Watch whether Heygen prices Avatar 6 Pro separately later. A day earlier it went the other way on Professional Voice Clone, moving it from a $29/mo add-on to free on Pro, one of the 6 Heygen pricing changes Pulse tracked in the last 90 days.
In context
Heygen refreshes this page often: Pulse has tracked 7 real pricing changes since it began tracking the company on Jul 20, 2026, averaging about one every 19 days, and this is Heygen's second Pro plan update within a day, following a change Heygen made the day before that made Pro's Professional Voice Clone free after charging $29/mo for it. The move itself is a rename-and-refresh rather than a price change: Creator's listed engines swap from Avatar IV and Avatar V to Avatar 6 Turbo and Avatar Lipsync, while Pro picks up the new Avatar 6 Pro feature with no listed price change. Other AI vendors have leaned on the same playbook this week — Fireworks added a new model lineup and Together revised its GPU pricing — shipping new names and specs rather than new sticker prices. For a product leader, the sharper read is that engine refreshes like this have become a default way to show progress on an AI product without resetting the price a customer already budgeted for.
Common questions
What should I check before assuming this is a discount in disguise?
Line up the full feature list against a cached copy of the page — a renamed engine at the same monthly price, like this one, is not a discount; only a changed dollar figure or credit allotment would make it a real price move.
What happened to the 4K video export feature Pro used to list first?
It's still part of Pro's feature set — Avatar 6 Pro simply took its spot at the top of the Pro only features box, per Heygen's pricing page.
