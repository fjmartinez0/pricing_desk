PACKAGING CHANGE
Datadog replaces its $0 Infrastructure Free card with a Basic tier priced via Contact sales
Datadog retired the self-serve $0 Infrastructure Free card (up to 5 hosts, core system metrics, 1-day retention) and replaced it with a Basic tier priced via Contact sales, aimed at noncritical workloads and mixable with Pro or Enterprise hosts in the same account.

WHY IT MATTERS
Small teams can still run up to 5 Basic hosts free, but the $0 card is gone from the plan lineup, and pricing for Basic beyond that requires a sales conversation. At the same time, the Pro card now leads with container monitoring and OpenTelemetry support, which are not available on Basic hosts. Watch whether Datadog brings the same Contact sales gate to other product lines' free tiers.

THEIR RATIONALE
Datadog frames Basic as coverage for "noncritical workloads" rather than a replacement for Free, and lets those hosts mix with Pro or Enterprise in the same account - positioning it as a cost-control tier for secondary infrastructure rather than an onboarding path. The timing lines up with Datadog's normal rhythm: 86 days after its last change (CoScreen's removal), close to its ~79-day average gap, so this reads as routine page maintenance rather than a reaction to anything specific.

A tier anyone could see at $0 on the pricing cards now shows Contact sales, even though a free allotment of up to 5 Basic hosts survives in the FAQ.
