DeepSeek launched OCR and Speech APIs at a launch discount
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS

What changed
According to DeepSeek's pricing page as of Nov 14, 2026, the company added two priced API add-ons: a File Parsing/OCR API at a launch price of $0.225 per 1,000 pages, and Speech APIs covering Text-to-Speech at $0.45 per 10K characters and Speech-to-Text/ASR at a launch price of $0.058 per hour of audio, down from a standard $0.116 per hour. Both the OCR and Speech-to-Text launch prices are discounted 50% for a limited time, per DeepSeek's pricing page as of Nov 14, 2026.
Why it matters
For product leaders stitching together multi-modal AI pipelines, this adds two more metered line items to budget alongside DeepSeek's model-token pricing: OCR billed by the page, speech billed by the character or the hour. DeepSeek's own history is the sharper read here — its Search and Fetch APIs were listed at $0.75 per 1,000 requests, with server-side tool calls free until per-request billing started on Nov 1, 2026, according to Pulse's record [[deepseek/2026D290]] [[deepseek/2026D309]]. The 50% launch discount on OCR and Speech-to-Text, per DeepSeek's pricing page as of Nov 14, 2026, is another introductory rate, so the full rate may follow. Watch whether DeepSeek doubles the OCR and Speech-to-Text rates once the introductory window closes, the way Search/Fetch's free period gave way to metered billing.

Pulse tracked DeepSeek's previous add-on, Search/Fetch, launching on Oct 17, 2026 at $0.75 per 1,000 requests with server-side tool calls free until Nov 1, 2026; Pulse detected the end of that free period on Nov 5, 2026 [[deepseek/2026D290]] [[deepseek/2026D309]]. Set against that pattern, DeepSeek's 50% launch discount on OCR and Speech-to-Text, per its pricing page as of Nov 14, 2026, is an introductory rate on a new metered line; Pulse's record has one earlier DeepSeek add-on whose free period ended on a stated date.
Common questions
How do we estimate the monthly cost of using DeepSeek's new OCR or speech APIs?
Multiply DeepSeek's per-unit launch rate — by the page for OCR, by the character for Text-to-Speech, or by the hour for Speech-to-Text — by your expected monthly volume, and build in headroom since the launch pricing has no published end date.
Could the 50% launch discount end, and how would we know?
DeepSeek hasn't published an end date — the item calls it only a 'limited-time' launch price, per its pricing page as of Nov 14, 2026. Treat it as temporary and recheck before relying on today's per-unit rate for long-term budgeting.
Does this change DeepSeek's core per-token model pricing?
Not as reported — the diff only lists the two new OCR/Speech add-ons, with no mention of token-rate changes elsewhere. If per-token cost matters to your workload, confirm current rates directly on DeepSeek's pricing page before budgeting, since add-on launches and model-price moves are tracked separately.
