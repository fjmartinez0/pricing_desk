Krea launched a Black Friday sale
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 18, 2026
Krea cut its yearly plan prices by as much as 70%.

Krea’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 18, 2026
What changed
Krea cut its yearly plan prices for a Black Friday sale, with a countdown banner on its pricing page: the discount scales from 40% off up to 70% off on Max, while the monthly cycle gets a flat 25% off, per Krea's pricing page as Pulse saved it on Nov 18, 2026. At Krea's 60K-unit Max tier, the discounted yearly rate is $42 a month, down from $63, per Krea's pricing page as Pulse saved it on Nov 18, 2026. At the 80K-unit tier, the rate falls from $81 to $54 a month, per Krea's pricing page as Pulse saved it on Nov 18, 2026. Business drops from $160 to $96 a month on the annual plan, while Basic stays at $5 and Pro at $21 — Pro's 50% discount badge reflects a markup to a $42 strikethrough reference price rather than a cut to the $21 rate itself, according to Krea's pricing page as Pulse saved it on Nov 18, 2026.
Pro and Max subscribers also get seven days of unlimited Nano Banana generations during the promo, and Krea's compute top-up packs are discounted as much as 29%, now priced $4.99 to $176.99 per pack, per Krea's pricing page as Pulse saved it on Nov 18, 2026.
Why it matters
For a product leader watching how AI-generation vendors manage demand, Krea's move matters less for the discount itself than for how often it appears: Krea has changed its pricing roughly every 9 days since Pulse began tracking it in Jun 2026, and discounts keep recurring in its recent history — a welcome offer for new signups added Nov 16, 2026 [[krea/2026W47]], and a 14-day unlimited-generation perk added Oct 16, 2026 and pulled a week later [[krea/2026D289]] [[krea/2026D296]]. That cadence suggests Krea treats pricing as a lever it pulls constantly rather than a fixed rate card — useful context if you're building a competing offer and assuming rivals' list prices hold steady.
This specific promo is worth watching for two things: whether the discounted rates hold past the Black Friday window or snap back the way October's unlimited-generation perk did, and whether the compute top-up discounts signal Krea is trying to pull usage-based spend forward into Q4 alongside the yearly-plan discounts. A team evaluating Krea for a project with a hard deadline should lock in the yearly rate now rather than assume it is permanent — watch whether Krea's pricing page reverts to list price once the promo ends.
In context
Krea's Black Friday cut lands in a crowded week: Pulse tracked 177 new-discount changes across 144 companies in the last 180 days (5.4% of all 3,298 pricing changes it tracked), and Krea itself ranks among the more frequent discounters at 3 changes in that window Krea, behind Openart's 7 and Runway's 4. The timing lines up with peers — Pulse tracked Koala cutting annual plans 40% for Black Friday and Lodgify slashing yearly plans 60%, both on Nov 16, 2026. The sharper read: this sale fits what is already a frequent habit of discount and capacity tweaks — a 9-day average cadence since Jun 2026 — so a buyer should not assume it is a one-off.
Common questions
Should we expect this discount to disappear right after Black Friday?
Possibly — Krea's October 14-day unlimited-generation perk ended exactly on schedule a week after launch [[krea/2026D296]], so treat this as a limited-time rate rather than Krea's new list price, and check back after the countdown ends before committing a budget to it.
Does this Black Friday discount cover Krea's Enterprise plan?
Pulse's record of this change doesn't list Enterprise, so there's no evidence the cut extends there — if you're evaluating Krea at that scale, check its pricing page directly rather than assuming the Black Friday terms apply.
