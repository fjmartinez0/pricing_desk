Wispr Flow drops numeric Free plan limits, makes Pro meetings unlimited
Wispr Flow's Free plan no longer states exact limits: the word cap "2,000/week on desktop; limited weekly use on mobile" is now just "Limited words per week," and the Meetings "Weekly limit" line now reads "Limited Notetaker." On paid tiers, Pro Meetings moved from "Higher limits" to "Unlimited*," with Pro, Growth and Enterprise's unlimited meetings now carrying a new "*Subject to abuse limits" footnote.

WHY IT MATTERS
