Vercel adds usage-based Daemons billing at $0.128/hour Active CPU
Vercel added a new Vercel Daemons section to its pricing page with two usage-based line items: Active CPU billed at $0.128 per hour with 5 hours per month included, and Provisioned Memory at $0.0212 per GB-hour with 420 GB-hours per month included.

THEIR RATIONALE
Vercel ships Daemons pricing with the metering baked in from day one rather than folding it into existing plan limits — Active CPU at $0.128/hour with 5 hours included, Provisioned Memory at $0.0212/GB-hour with 420 GB-hours included, Enterprise custom. The timing is on Vercel's usual cadence: this is one of 22 real pricing changes since tracking began Dec 29, 2025, landing 11 days after it added an OWASP Core Ruleset firewall to Pro Plus priority projects, close to its roughly 12-day average gap. It's also a continuation of a pattern: counting this change, 6 of Vercel's recent moves have built on credits and usage billing, including splitting AI pricing into token and trigger fees and swapping Pro CDN overages for flat-rate included data transfer.

THE SIGNAL
The bigger picture is architectural as much as it is pricing: Vercel keeps reaching for metered billing on every new compute surface it ships — sandboxes, Connect, CDN, AI tokens, and now background-process Daemons — rather than bundling usage into existing plan tiers. With 6 of its recent changes now built on credits-and-usage billing, the model looks less like one-off pricing tweaks and more like Vercel's default answer for monetizing anything new it ships.
