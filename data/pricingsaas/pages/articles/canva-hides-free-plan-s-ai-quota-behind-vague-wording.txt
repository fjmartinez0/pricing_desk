Canva drops the "up to 20" AI uses figure from its Free plan
Canva's Free plan pricing page no longer states a number for its AI allowance: the "AI usage" line that read "Up to 20 Standard or Premium AI uses" now reads "Limited access to Standard and Premium AI." The "up to 20" figure appeared on Sep 9, 2026, when Canva cut the Free allowance from "up to 200 Standard AI uses or 20 Premium AI uses" to a combined "up to 20 Standard or Premium AI uses." Nothing else on the Free card changed: price, CTA and the rest of the feature list are untouched.
