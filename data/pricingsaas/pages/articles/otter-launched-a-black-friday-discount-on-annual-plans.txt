Otter launched a Black Friday discount on annual plans
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 24, 2026
Otter cut its annual plan prices in half for a limited-time sale.

Otter’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 24, 2026
What changed
Otter added a Black Friday banner offering 50% off the Pro and Business annual plans, each via its own promo code at checkout, according to Otter's pricing page on Nov 24, 2026. The underlying list prices and plan copy are unchanged; the discount is a code-redeemed offer layered onto the existing annual pricing, and the banner carries no countdown or end date.
Why it matters
For a product leader, this is a reminder that a seasonal discount is a different lever from a repriced plan: it moves units without touching list price or packaging, and it disappears the moment the promo window closes. Pulse tracked 279 new-discount changes across 220 companies in the last 180 days, so running a Black Friday code alongside unchanged list pricing is now a common pattern rather than a one-off move — a pricing-page comparison at this time of year should expect a banner, not a changed number. Watch whether Otter's promo turns into a durable price change or ends after Black Friday.
In context
Otter has made 14 real pricing changes since Pulse started tracking its page in Jan 2024, averaging one every 74 days, most recently dropping per-tier AI meeting template usage limits 17 days before this one. Otter has run plan-level promos before, including a limited-time Pro annual price and a Business "20% off for 3 months" offer, but this is its first Black Friday promo-code offer covering both Pro and Business annual plans in Pulse's record. The move lands in a crowded Black Friday week: on Nov 23, 2026, Acronis halved its Premium plan, Bitly cut up to 50% off annual plans, and Canva discounted its first three months of Pro and Business, per Pulse's record. Pulse has tracked 279 new-discount changes across 220 companies in the last 180 days, so a seasonal promo code is now closer to table stakes than a differentiator. The sharper read: Otter is using a redemption code rather than a visible price cut, which lets it run the promotion without changing what shows on the page once Black Friday ends.
Common questions
Does the 50% discount apply automatically, or do I need a promo code?
The banner requires entering a separate code for each plan at checkout (one for Pro annual, one for Business annual); it is not reflected in the listed price until the code is applied.
Is this part of a broader trend, or is Otter acting alone?
Pulse tracked 279 new-discount changes across 220 companies in the last 180 days, and several other companies, including Acronis, Bitdefender and Bitly, launched Black Friday sales in the same week — this is seasonal, not company-specific.
Will this discount outlast Black Friday?
The banner carries no countdown or end date, but Otter has no prior site-wide discount on record, so there's no precedent yet for how long this kind of promo typically runs on its page.
