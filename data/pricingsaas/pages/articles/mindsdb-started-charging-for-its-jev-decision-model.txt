PRICE INCREASE
MindsDB started charging for its Jev decision model
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 24, 2026
MindsDB started charging for a model that used to be free.
See before and after
Enlarge
MindsDB’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 24, 2026
What changed
MindsDB began charging $0.042 per 1M input tokens for the Jev 1.13.0 decision model (previously $0) and added Gemini 4 Neon ($3 input / $15 output), Kimi K3.1 Pro (MNMA) ($2 input / $10 output) and Qwen3 Embedding 8B ($0.10 input, embedding-only) to its rate card, according to MindsDB's pricing page on Nov 24, 2026. Jev's output price stays at $0 on MindsDB's pricing page as of Nov 24, 2026, and the rest of the catalog is unchanged.
Why it matters
For a product leader running usage-based AI infrastructure pricing, the signal isn't the size of the new rate, it's that MindsDB moved a previously free model to a paid rate without a broader relaunch: Jev input now costs $0.042 per 1M tokens, while its output stays at $0. Pulse has recorded 18 pricing changes on MindsDB's page in the last 90 days, averaging one every 8 days, so its rate card works as a live price list: new models arrive already priced, and a free model can pick up a price at any update. Watch whether other API and infrastructure vendors follow the same script, shipping a model free to build usage and pricing it later, rather than pricing on day one.
In context
MindsDB's last pricing change landed just 5 days before this one, when MindsDB added GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol plus a new Image models category on Nov 19, 2026. Price increases aren't new here either: MindsDB hiked DeepSeek V4.1 Flash output 82% on Oct 2, 2026, and raised Blaze rates up to 183% on Sep 15, 2026, per Pulse's tracked diffs. Market-wide, Pulse tracked 479 credits-and-usage-billing changes across 302 companies in the last 180 days, 12.4% of the 3,855 changes tracked, with Pika (14 changes) and Lumaai (9) the most frequent in that period. On a rate card that moves every 8 days on average, one model's price moving is one of many routine updates, not an outlier.
Common questions
Has MindsDB's overall pricing model changed recently, or just individual model rates?
Yes — on Oct 12, 2026 MindsDB replaced its old plan-less rate card with Free and Pay-as-you-go tiers; today's change is a rate update within that newer structure, not a shift in the underlying plan model.
Should we expect prices on other individual models to drift up over time too?
MindsDB's event history leans the other way: of 39 tracked events since Mar 2026, 12 are new models being added versus far fewer price changes, so an upward drift on any single model is the exception more than the rule on this page.
How does Qwen3 Embedding 8B's price compare with MindsDB's other recent model additions?
It matches MindsDB's cheaper recent additions — Claude Haiku 5.5 and Muse Ember 1.0, both listed at $0.10 input / $0.50 output on Nov 7 and Nov 11, 2026 per MindsDB's pricing page — rather than pricier models like GPT-6.1 Terra at $1 input, added Oct 29, 2026.
