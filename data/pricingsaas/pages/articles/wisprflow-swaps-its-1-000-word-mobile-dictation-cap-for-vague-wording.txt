Wisprflow swaps its 1,000-word mobile dictation cap for vague wording
Wisprflow's plan-comparison table for mobile dictation word limit on its Free plan now reads “Limited weekly use” in place of the explicit 1,000-word weekly cap it had listed since Sep 21, 2026; the desktop line still reads 2,000 words a week.
