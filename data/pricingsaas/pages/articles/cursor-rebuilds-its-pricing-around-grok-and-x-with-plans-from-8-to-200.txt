PACKAGING CHANGE
Cursor rebuilds its pricing around Grok and X, with plans from $8 to $200
Cursor retired its Hobby/Individual ($20/mo)/Teams ($40/user/mo) lineup, where the Individual card offered Pro, Pro+ and Ultra options, and replaced it with Free ($0), Plus ($8/month), Premium ($30/month, marked "Most popular"), Super ($100/month) and Ultra ($200/month) individual plans, plus a per-seat Teams plan starting at $30/user; Enterprise stays custom-priced.

WHY IT MATTERS
The cheapest paid plan drops from $20 to $8 (Plus, Grok models only), Teams now starts at $30 per user instead of $40, and the top of the ladder runs to $200. The move bets on one subscription that bundles AI coding with consumer social features. Watch whether Cursor's next change, likely within its usual ~26-day cadence, builds out the X/Grok bundle further or trims pieces that don't resonate with its core developer base.

THEIR RATIONALE
This is Cursor's first pricing restructure since tracking began on Jul 20, 2026, and its 4th change in 90 days, in line with its roughly 26-day average cadence — it landed 35 days after the last change, a round of contextual taglines added to plan cards. Before that, it dropped Composer from the "Generous limits" perk in August and added plan taglines in July. Pairing the repricing with X social features across the whole ladder suggests Cursor is optimizing for a combined AI-coding-plus-social subscription, not a simple price adjustment.

THE SIGNAL
Pricing restructures aren't rare across the index: 238 of the 1,740 changes tracked in the last 180 days (13.7%), at 204 companies. But this is the first one in Cursor's change history, and it rebuilds the whole plan ladder around a new feature category in one diff. That's a sharp break from Cursor's recent rhythm of small positioning moves: taglines, a trimmed perk, then taglines again. The same Free-to-Ultra lineup, at the same prices, now appears on xAI's pricing page too, so this reads as one shared Grok, X and Cursor subscription rather than a Cursor-only repricing.

IN CONTEXT
Cursor's pricing has moved in small steps since tracking began: contextual taglines added to plan cards in September, Composer dropped from the "Generous limits" perk in August, and a July tagline update before that. A full rebuild of the plan lineup around Grok and X features breaks that rhythm. The new Free, Plus ($8), Premium ($30), Super ($100), Ultra ($200) and Teams ($30+/user) lineup is identical to the one xAI now shows on its own pricing page, where the same plans list Cursor Agent, cloud agents, code hosting via Origin and Bugbot reviews. Restructures are common across the index (238 of 1,740 changes in the last 180 days, at 204 companies), but this is the first one Cursor has made.
