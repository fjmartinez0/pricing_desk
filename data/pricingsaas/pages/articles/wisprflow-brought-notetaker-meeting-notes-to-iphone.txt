Wisprflow brought Notetaker meeting notes to iPhone
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 19, 2026
Wisprflow extended its Notetaker app from Mac and Windows to iPhone.
See before and after

What changed
Notetaker, Wisprflow's AI meeting-notes feature, is now listed for iPhone on the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 19, 2026 — the Free plan card's access line and the "Available where you work" section both add iPhone alongside Mac and Windows, while Android stays marked as on the roadmap.
Wisprflow launched Notetaker on Aug 10, 2026, for Mac only, per Pulse's tracked history [[wisprflow/2026W33]]. This iPhone release is the first time the feature has reached a second platform since then.
Why it matters
For a product leader building a mobile-first feature, Wisprflow's staged rollout is a reminder that platform parity doesn't have to ship all at once — iPhone first, Android still pending, with the Free plan getting the same access line as paid Mac and Windows users. That signals Wisprflow is using platform coverage, not plan gating, as this cycle's growth lever: Notetaker sits on Free as well as Pro, so expanding where it runs — not what it costs — is how the company is choosing to add value right now.
Watch whether Android support lands before Wisprflow's next pricing-page change, and whether that update also turns the Free plan's blurred usage caps (see In context) into a mobile limit of its own.
In context
Pulse's tracked data show Wisprflow shipping pricing-page changes about every 14 days on average since tracking began Aug 3, 2026, and this rollout lands 12 days after it added HIPAA/BAA compliance for Notetaker [[wisprflow/2026D311]]. The company has also used the Free plan itself as a lever lately — dropping exact usage limits [[wisprflow/2026D305]] and swapping a disclosed numeric dictation cap for vaguer language [[wisprflow/2026D293]] within the past month — so extending Notetaker to iPhone fits a pattern of adjusting what Free includes rather than its price. Pulse tracked 208 free-plan changes across 166 companies in the last 180 days, and peers are moving the same way: Doodle just added an iOS/Android callout to its own free tier [[doodle/2026W47]], and Pubnub opened its free plan to live production use [[pubnub/2026W47]]. The sharper read: as AI note-taking tools compete on platform coverage rather than price, where a feature runs is becoming as important a lever as what it costs.
Common questions
Does this change what the Free plan costs?
No — this diff only touches platform availability; Wisprflow's Free plan access line gains iPhone with no new price mentioned on the page.
When will Android get this feature?
Wisprflow's pricing page still marks Android as on the roadmap, with no date given; Pulse will log it separately if the company commits to one.
Should we expect a price change to follow?
Not based on this diff — Wisprflow's recent moves have touched plan limits and compliance, not price, so this platform expansion reads as reach-building rather than monetization.
