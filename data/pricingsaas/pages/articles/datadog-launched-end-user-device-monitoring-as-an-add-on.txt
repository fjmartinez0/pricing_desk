Datadog launched End User Device Monitoring as an add-on
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 11, 2026
Datadog launched End User Device Monitoring at $5 per device, per month.

What changed
Datadog added a new End User Device Monitoring product, covering device health visibility, root-cause analysis and ticketing sync. Pulse detected the change on Nov 11, 2026, according to Datadog's pricing page.
The product is priced at $5 per device per month on annual billing, or $7.20 per device on-demand, per Datadog's pricing page as of Nov 11, 2026.
Why it matters
This is a new product line priced per device rather than per seat or host, extending Datadog's metering surface into endpoint fleets — relevant for any team pricing IT or device-management tooling. The $5-per-device-per-month annual rate against a $7.20 on-demand rate (per Datadog's pricing page, Nov 11, 2026) gives a concrete anchor for how much Datadog charges for billing flexibility, something packaging teams can benchmark against their own annual-vs-monthly spread. For a product leader, the add-on also signals Datadog is willing to ship net-new line items rather than folding new capability into existing plans, coming just 14 days after Datadog dropped its free Infrastructure plan and added a contact-sales Basic tier on Oct 28, 2026, per Pulse's tracked history. Watch whether End User Device Monitoring stays a standalone per-device charge or gets folded into a higher Datadog bundle later.
In context
Pulse has tracked 15 real pricing-page changes at Datadog since Nov 14, 2023, averaging a change every 75 days, and it has launched new paid products before, including Experiments in Apr 2026 and Data Observability: Quality Monitoring in Jan 2026, per Pulse's tracked history. It follows Datadog dropping its free Infrastructure plan and adding a contact-sales Basic tier just 14 days earlier, on Oct 28, 2026, per Pulse's tracked history [[datadog/2026D301]] — a pattern of tightening what's free while adding new paid surface area. Datadog isn't alone in adding standalone add-ons: Pulse tracked 166 new-add-on changes across 135 companies in the last 180 days, led by Baseten with 8 [[baseten]], Vercel with 5 [[vercel]] and Gusto with 4 [[gusto]]. Device monitoring is a narrower bet than most of that activity, billed flat per device instead of metered by usage. Watch whether Datadog extends per-device pricing to other parts of its platform or keeps it isolated to endpoint monitoring.
Common questions
How do we estimate our own bill before enabling it?
Count how many endpoints you'd enroll and apply whichever Datadog rate you'll use, annual or on-demand; Datadog's item description doesn't mention a minimum device count or a free trial for the add-on, so check with your Datadog rep if you want to pilot it on a handful of machines first.
Is there a cheaper way to try this before committing to an annual plan?
Datadog's pricing page doesn't mention a free trial or pilot period for End User Device Monitoring as of Nov 11, 2026 — only the monthly and on-demand rates. Ask your Datadog rep whether a short pilot is available before signing an annual commitment.
Does this change pricing for Datadog's existing Infrastructure or APM plans?
No — End User Device Monitoring is a new, separate product; Datadog's pricing page shows no change to Infrastructure, APM or other existing plan prices as of Nov 11, 2026. Check your current plan's page directly if you want confirmation nothing else moved.
