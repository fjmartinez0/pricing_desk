Together let its Qwen4 Max promo price expire
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 25, 2026
Together's Qwen4 Max promo expired, returning the model to its post-hike list price.

What changed
Together's Qwen4 Max promo, a limited-time discount introduced alongside a list-price increase on Nov 20, 2026 and valid until Nov 23, 2026, expired on schedule, according to Together's pricing page saved by Pulse on Nov 25, 2026. The model's per-1M-token price reverted from the discounted $1.05 input / $0.0525 cached / $3.15 output to its full $1.50 input / $0.075 cached / $4.50 output, the same list price set when the promo began, according to the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 25, 2026. Separately, Together added a new "Kimi K3.1 Pro — US geo-pinned" model to its dedicated pricing table at $2.50 input / $0.25 cached / $12.50 output per 1M tokens, according to the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 25, 2026.
Why it matters
For a product leader, the Qwen4 Max story is a reminder that a promo price has a shelf life by design: Together paired a list-price increase with a limited-time discount, and letting that discount lapse on schedule — not a surprise hike — is what pushed the price back to $4.50 output per 1M tokens, according to Together's pricing page saved by Pulse on Nov 25, 2026. If you run a similar intro-price-plus-expiry structure, the lesson carries over: build the expiry into your own messaging, because customers and competitors alike will otherwise read a silent reversion as a fresh price increase.
The second change is more telling: Together now prices the same Kimi K3.1 Pro model differently by region — $2 input / $10 output per 1M tokens for the earlier Kimi K3.1 Pro listing versus $2.50 input / $12.50 output for the US geo-pinned version added this week — according to the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 25, 2026. Geo-pinned and dedicated-capacity pricing is a lever worth watching if any of your own infrastructure costs vary by region.

Per Pulse's tracked history, Together moves fast: this comes a day after it added a new "Fast" tier for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash on Nov 24, 2026 Nov 24 Fast tier, part of 26 changes in the last 90 days, averaging about seven days apart. The Qwen4 Max promo was never meant to last: per Together's pricing page, the list-price increase and the limited-time discount — to $1.05 input / $0.0525 cached / $3.15 output — landed on the same day the price hike and promo, so today's reversion is the promo working as designed, not a fresh decision. Common questions
Is Together's Qwen4 Max back to its original price, or still elevated from the recent hike?
Still elevated — the promo's expiry reverts it to the raised list price, not the rate from before that increase; if you're comparing costs over time, use the current number as your new baseline.
Why would a company raise list price and discount it at the same time?
It lets the sticker price rise while new customers still see a competitive number during the transition — worth testing if you're planning your own price increase and want to soften the initial sting.
What does the new geo-pinned Kimi K3.1 Pro pricing suggest about Together's roadmap?
If any of your own infrastructure costs vary by region, check whether a flat global rate is leaving money on the table the way Together's original Kimi K3.1 Pro price did before this split.
