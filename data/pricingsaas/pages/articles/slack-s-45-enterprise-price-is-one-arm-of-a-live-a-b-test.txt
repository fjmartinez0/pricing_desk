Slack's $45 Enterprise+ price is one arm of a live A/B test
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 3, 2026
See before and after
Enlarge
Slack’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 3, 2026
CONTACT SALES FOR PRICING·first seen Oct 4, 2026
What changed
Slack's Enterprise+ pricing page shows a $45 per-user/month price, billed annually, in place of "Contact sales for pricing" in this capture. A probe of the live page found both prices appearing across repeated fresh fetches, with an A/B-testing script detected — not every visit shows the same figure. The same line has already changed repeatedly since Oct 4, 2026: revealed, then hidden on Oct 13, 2026, and revealed again on Oct 14, 2026 — this capture is the fourth change to it.
Why it matters
For buyers evaluating Enterprise+, the $45 figure isn't yet reliable: it may not show to every visitor, and a quoted sales price could land above or below whatever the test settles on. For the category, a company that has long kept its top tier behind "Contact sales" testing a public number at all suggests even high-touch enterprise pricing is being pulled toward posted prices. Watch whether $45 holds across the next several fetches without reverting to "Contact sales" — that would show the test has concluded, not just that Slack flipped again.
In context
This is the fourth time the $45 Enterprise+ figure has flipped since Slack first showed it on Oct 4, 2026, followed by hiding it again on Oct 13, 2026 and showing it once more on Oct 14, 2026. A probe of the live page found an A/B-testing script running, with different fetches returning different results — the signature of a test assigning visitors to different arms, not a settled price. Slack has kept iterating on Enterprise+ meanwhile, with a swap of 24/7 support for paid Success Plans on Oct 28, 2026 and a Frontline teams add-on on Oct 20, 2026 — active work on the tier even as its headline price stays unresolved.
