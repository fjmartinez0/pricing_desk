PACKAGING CHANGE
Together cuts GPU Cluster minimum reservation from 7 days to 3, prices hold
Together's GPU Clusters reserved-pricing table changes its shortest commitment bracket from '7-30 days' to '3-30 days.' Per-GPU hourly rates for that bracket, including $3.69/hr for NVIDIA HGX H100, are unchanged — only the minimum reservation length is cut, making the reserved-rate discount accessible on shorter commitments.

For buyers evaluating reserved GPU capacity, the real signal is cadence: Together has changed GPU or inference pricing 23 times in 90 days, making its page less a fixed price list and more a live feed. THEIR RATIONALE
Together changes its pricing page roughly every week — this is its second move in two days, following Tuesday's 10% cut to Kimi K3 token prices. At a company running GPU and inference pricing almost like a live spot market, loosening the reservation commitment rather than cutting the rate itself reads as a volume play: lower the barrier to the discount tier, not the discount, to pull more usage into reserved capacity without touching headline rates.

THE SIGNAL
This is Together's fifth pricing restructure on record, including a September rebuild of fine-tuning pricing from 3 size tiers to 29 per-model rows and a March rename of GPU Cloud to GPU Clusters with new reservation tiers. Restructures account for 207 of 1,450 index-wide changes in six months (14.3%, the second-most-common category after feature and price moves). Loosening reservation terms while leaving rates flat is a subtler lever than the price cuts and hikes Together has run on inference models almost weekly this quarter — it's capacity-utilization tuning, not price competition.
