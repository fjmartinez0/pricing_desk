PRICE INCREASE
fal raises serverless H100 rate to $2.49/hr, adds GB200 GPU, doubles MiniMax video pricing
fal's serverless GPU pricing moved across the board: H100 instances rose from $1.89 to $2.49 per hour (up 32%), B300 instances rose from $4.49 to $5.99 per hour (up 33%), and a new GB200 GPU tier was added to the lineup.

WHY IT MATTERS
For teams building on fal, this is a direct cost-of-goods increase on two of its most-used GPU tiers plus a 2x jump on one of its video models - margin that has to be absorbed or passed through to end customers. For fal, layering in GB200 support signals it's racing to keep pace with Nvidia's newest hardware generation while monetizing the older fleet harder. Watch whether this is the start of a broader repricing across fal's remaining GPU and model catalog, given it's now made three pricing moves in ten weeks, or whether this settles back into the roughly 30-day cadence its track record shows.

THEIR RATIONALE
fal hasn't framed this specific move with public rationale, but the pattern is legible from its own cadence: this is fal's third real pricing change since tracking began in late July, landing just one day after it finished publishing its Audio and 3D model API price lists on Oct 1. The sequence reads as a deliberate overhaul - first expose more of the pricing surface, then move the GPU rates on it. It's also the company's second GPU rate increase in two months, following an H100 list-price bump from $3.99 to $4.50/hr in early August, suggesting underlying compute costs (or margin targets) are the real driver rather than a one-off repricing of a single SKU.

THE SIGNAL
Price increases are common across the Pulse Index right now - 45 changes across 42 companies in the last 180 days, a bit under 4% of everything tracked in that window - but most of the examples are SaaS seat or plan hikes (Shipday's Enterprise tier up 40%, Cumul up 25%). fal's move is a reminder that the same inflationary pressure is hitting raw AI infrastructure pricing too: GPU-hour rates and per-second inference costs, the inputs other AI products build their own pricing on top of. A roughly 32-33% jump on H100 and B300 serverless rates, plus a doubled per-second rate for MiniMax video generation, will ripple into the margins of anyone pricing a product on top of fal's API.
