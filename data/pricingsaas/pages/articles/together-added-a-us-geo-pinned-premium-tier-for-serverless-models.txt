PACKAGING CHANGE
Together added a US geo-pinned premium tier for serverless models
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 11, 2026
Together added a premium, geo-pinned US tier priced about 25% higher for some serverless models.
See before and after
Enlarge
Together’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 11, 2026
What changed
Pulse detected the change on Nov 11, 2026. Together added a new ‘US geo-pinned’ premium pricing tier carrying roughly a 25% surcharge for 7 serverless models, according to Together's pricing page as of Nov 11, 2026.
The change also merged Together's GPU Cluster reserved-pricing structure: the 91-180-day and 181-day-plus tiers combined into a single live-availability 91-day-plus tier, per Together's pricing page as of Nov 11, 2026.
Separately, Together raised the Tev1 4B Experimental input price from $0.04 to $0.042 per 1M tokens, according to Together's pricing page as of Nov 11, 2026.
Why it matters
The headline move is pricing data residency directly: Together is charging roughly 25% more to pin inference to US infrastructure for 7 serverless models, according to Together's pricing page as of Nov 11, 2026, which gives compliance-focused buyers a concrete number to budget against and gives packaging teams elsewhere a benchmark for what a location guarantee is worth on usage-priced infrastructure. Consolidating the GPU Cluster reserved-pricing tiers from 91-180/181-plus days into a single 91-plus-day tier, per Together's pricing page as of Nov 11, 2026, simplifies the reserved-capacity ladder, removing a now-defunct middle bracket. For a product leader, the broader signal is cadence: Pulse has tracked 42 real pricing-page changes at Together in the last 365 days, so this bundle of unrelated moves in a single diff is typical of how the company ships pricing updates, not an isolated event. Watch whether other inference providers start charging a premium for geographic pinning the way Together just did.

Together is among Pulse's newest tracked companies, followed since Dec 29, 2025, and also among its most active: Pulse counts 42 real changes out of 53 pricing-page checks, averaging a change every 7 days, per Pulse's tracked history. Common questions
Which 7 models get the US geo-pinned option?
Check Together's live pricing page for the exact roster — the item doesn't name all 7 by model name, only that each now carries a geo-pinned badge as of Nov 11, 2026, per Together's pricing page. Confirm the badge is on the model you use before assuming the surcharge applies.
Does merging the GPU Cluster day tiers raise or lower reserved pricing for 91-180 day commitments?
Together's pricing page doesn't say whether the new 91-day-plus rate, as shown on Nov 11, 2026, is higher or lower than the old 91-180-day bracket it replaced. If you're mid-commitment in that range, ask your Together account contact directly rather than assuming the merge was neutral.
Does the geo-pinning surcharge also apply to GPU Cluster reserved pricing?
The item only describes the geo-pinned surcharge applying to 7 serverless models; it doesn't mention the surcharge extending to Together's GPU Cluster reserved pricing, which merged its day-based tiers separately in this same update as of Nov 11, 2026. Confirm with Together directly if you need geo-pinning on reserved capacity too.
