Pika added a Black Friday discount banner to its pricing page
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 26, 2026
Pika added a discount banner linking to its Creator plan checkout.

Pika’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 26, 2026
What changed
A site-wide banner on Pika's pricing page now advertises 60% off for Black Friday, with a live countdown timer and a Sign Up button routing to the Creator plan's monthly checkout, a change Pulse detected on Nov 26, 2026. The banner was absent from Pika's pricing page the day before, and Pulse has no earlier record of it in Pika's tracked changes. Nothing else on the page moved: the plan cards, credits and listed prices below the banner are unchanged.
Why it matters
For a product leader watching Black Friday moves, this is a reminder that top-of-funnel discounting often shows up as a site-wide banner rather than a change to the pricing grid itself: the Creator plan's listed price is untouched, but conversion urgency — a live countdown, a blunt discount headline — is doing the work instead. That keeps the sticker price intact for existing customers while pulling new signups through a time-boxed offer, a cheaper lever to pull than repricing a tier outright, and one that's easy to roll back once the week is over.
Watch whether Pika is starting to lean on urgency marketing as a standing acquisition channel rather than a one-off: a banner that disappears cleanly once the countdown hits zero is a seasonal push, but a company that keeps rotating through promos — as Pika has for most of November — may be treating discount banners as a recurring lever rather than a holiday exception.
In context
Pulse has tracked Pika since Jun 8, 2026 and logged 16 real changes across 28 comparisons, including 15 in the last 90 days, roughly one every 11 days. This banner fits that pattern: three days earlier Pika pulled the Fancy plan's unlimited Wan 3.0 promo as it expired [[pika/2026D327]], after a week of that same offer [[pika/2026D322]] and, before that, a daily free-credits perk on Creator and Fancy [[pika/2026D308]], so Pika leans on temporary perks and banners rather than its sticker prices. Peers are doing the same this week: Pulse logged Otter cutting Pro and Business annual plans 50% on Nov 24, 2026 [[otter/2026D328]], and Codecademy discounting Pro 60% on Nov 23, 2026 [[codecademy/2026W48]]. Pulse tracked 283 new-discount changes across 224 companies in the last 180 days, about 7.3% of its 3,872 tracked changes in that span, so this kind of banner is common, though Pika's cadence makes it an unusually active mover on promotions. What the banner understates: the Creator plan's actual price didn't move, so a signup today still pays the same rate once the countdown ends.
Common questions
How long does the 60% off deal last?
Pika's page runs a live countdown instead of naming a fixed end date; per the page Pulse saved on Nov 26, 2026, about 7 days remained on the clock at that point, so check the banner directly for the current time left rather than assuming a calendar date.
Does this change what a Creator plan seat actually costs?
No — Pulse found no change to the Creator plan's listed price on Pika's pricing page. The banner is a conversion nudge, not a repricing, so size the cost the same way you would have before: by Creator's standard monthly rate times seat count.
Should we run a similar countdown banner for our own pricing page?
Only if the move is truly temporary and reversible: Pika left its sticker price untouched and leaned on a countdown banner instead, which lets you test urgency-driven discounting without permanently repricing a tier. Make sure your own banner collapses cleanly when the clock hits zero, unlike a quiet price cut that's easy to forget to reverse.
