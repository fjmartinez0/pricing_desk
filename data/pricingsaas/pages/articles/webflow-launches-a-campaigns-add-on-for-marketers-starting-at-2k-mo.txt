The more telling move is what got replaced: a free, no-commitment Localize preview card is now a contact-sales, four-figure product pitch in the exact same slot. That's Webflow trading give-away real estate for a monetized one once it has conviction in a new product, on a page averaging a change every 38 days rather than sitting still.
