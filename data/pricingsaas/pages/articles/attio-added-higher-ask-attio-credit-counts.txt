Attio added higher Ask Attio credit counts to its pricing table
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 28, 2026
Attio brought back explicit credit counts for its Ask Attio feature.

What changed
Attio's pricing page showed explicit Ask Attio credit counts again on Nov 28, 2026, per the page Pulse saved that day: 200 credits on Free, 1,000 on Plus, 3,000 on Pro and 5,000 on Ultra. These are not the old numbers. Before Nov 27, 2026, the page listed 100, 500, 1,000 and 2,500, so every plan's listed allowance is now higher. On Nov 27, Attio had replaced those counts with relative labels (Limited usage, More usage, 3x Plus and 5x Plus), and the plan cards still use those labels; the new counts sit in the comparison table and follow the same ratios, with Pro at 3x and Ultra at 5x the Plus figure.
Pulse has tracked 3 pricing changes at Attio in the last 90 days, this one included, following a Nov 11, 2026 restructuring that moved the company to per-workspace pricing and added a $1,000-per-month Ultra plan, according to Pulse.
Why it matters
Pulse recorded two changes to Attio's Ask Attio allowance on back-to-back days, and the second was not a rollback: the counts that came back are higher than the ones removed. For a product leader shipping a credit-based AI feature, Attio's page now pairs a relative label on each plan card with a hard number in the comparison table, which keeps the upgrade story simple while still giving buyers a figure to budget against.
Attio's broader move to per-workspace pricing, which added a $1,000-per-month Ultra plan on Nov 11, 2026 per Pulse's tracking, suggests the company is still tuning its packaging, not just this one feature.
Watch whether the 200, 1,000, 3,000 and 5,000 credit counts hold on the pages Pulse saves next.
In context
Attio is young on Pulse's tracker, first logged on Jun 8, 2026 with 7 real pricing changes since, and its Ask Attio allowance is one of the most unsettled pieces of its page: Pulse recorded a swap to relative tiers on Nov 27 and explicit, higher credit counts in its comparison table the very next day, on Nov 28, 2026. That allowance sits on a pricing model that is itself new, since Attio moved to per-workspace billing and added a $1,000-per-month Ultra plan on Nov 11, per Pulse's tracking. Pulse tracked 484 pricing changes touching credit and usage-based billing across the companies it tracks in the last 180 days, including similar relabeling moves recently at ClickUp and Cargo. The sharper read: Attio has not settled on how to talk about Ask Attio's limits yet, so a number quoted off this page today is more likely to move again soon than one from a company with a longer track record.
Common questions
Should we use the $200/1,000/3,000/5,000 figures in a comparison deck right now?
Given Attio changed this same number two days in a row, check the live pricing page before you quote a specific credit count in a proposal or comparison deck — the figure here may already be out of date by the time you read this.
How do Attio's new credit counts compare with its old relative tiers?
The old labels did not map to a fixed number, so there is no direct conversion. If you were quoted a tier under the old system, ask Attio support which numeric allowance replaces it.
