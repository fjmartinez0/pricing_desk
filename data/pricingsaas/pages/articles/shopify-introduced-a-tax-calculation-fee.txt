PACKAGING CHANGE
Shopify introduced a tax calculation fee
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 18, 2026
Shopify added a percentage-based fee for tax calculated on its platform.

TAX MANAGED BY SHOPIFY·first seen Nov 18, 2026
What changed
Per Shopify's pricing page as of Nov 18, 2026, a new 'Tax managed by Shopify' row appears in the Tax comparison table alongside a Tax calculation fee of 0.35% on Basic, Grow and Advanced plans and 0.25% on Plus, charged after an introductory discount period. Shopify's pricing page as of Nov 18, 2026 also added a 'Self-managed tax mode' option to the same Tax feature table.
Why it matters
For a product leader, this is Shopify adding a fee to tax calculation, a line that carried no fee on its pricing page before: automated tax calculation now carries a 0.35% fee on Basic, Grow and Advanced (0.25% on Plus) per Shopify's pricing page as of Nov 18, 2026, charged as a percentage rather than a flat amount; the page does not say what the percentage is applied to. That's a usage-style fee added on top of plan pricing — worth watching if you're weighing the same move on a feature your own product currently bundles.
Pulse has not tracked an earlier pricing-restructure change on Shopify's page before this one, according to Pulse's tracking — a notable first for a company that otherwise changes its pricing slowly, averaging 44 days between changes per Pulse. Watch whether the 'introductory discount' mentioned on the page is time-limited: if 0.35%/0.25% are the post-discount steady-state rates, merchants may be paying less than that today and should check Shopify's page directly before budgeting for the listed rate.
In context
Shopify rarely touches pricing: Pulse tracked its previous change, dropping the free Shopify Academy courses perk, on Oct 28, 2026, 21 days before this one, and before that a same-week reversal — a Grow and Advanced price rise on Oct 15, 2026 undone the very next day by a cut back to $79 and $299. This is Shopify's first pricing-restructure-type change on Pulse's record, a company that otherwise averages 44 days between changes, per Pulse. Market-wide, Pulse tracked 380 pricing-restructure changes across 319 companies in the last 180 days — 11.5% of the 3,298 changes it tracked in that window — with companies like Vercel and Lyzr touching this pattern most often recently. The sharper read: Shopify is adding a percentage fee for tax calculation rather than changing a plan-tier price, a different lever than the Grow and Advanced price moves that defined its recent changes.
Common questions
Does this fee replace or stack on top of payment-processing fees?
Shopify's pricing page doesn't say — the new Tax calculation fee sits in the Tax section of the feature table, separate from the payment-processing rate rows Shopify revealed in full on Sep 15, 2026, so budget for it as an addition unless Shopify states otherwise.
Is this Shopify's first time changing tax-related pricing?
Essentially yes for this type of change — Pulse has not tracked an earlier pricing-restructure change on Shopify's page before this one, though Shopify revealed its exact payment-processing fees on Sep 15, 2026 and dropped a Plus cashback perk on Oct 2, 2026, both unrelated to tax.
