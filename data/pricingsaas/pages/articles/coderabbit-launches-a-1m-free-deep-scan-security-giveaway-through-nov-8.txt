WHY IT MATTERS
For a code-review tool, a free security-scan giveaway is a low-cost wedge to get more repositories running Deep Scan before enterprise renewal and upsell conversations happen. Watch whether CodeRabbit converts the giveaway into a standing Deep Scan pricing line once the promo ends Nov 8, and whether other AI dev-tool vendors follow with their own time-boxed feature giveaways the way discount and credit promos have already spread across the index.

THEIR RATIONALE
CodeRabbit frames this as a giveaway rather than a price move — $1M worth of free Deep Scan security scans, bounded by a date (Nov 8) rather than by seats or usage. It's the company's first new-discount-style promo since Pulse began tracking its page on May 18, 2026, a departure from its usual pattern of plan and add-on restructuring: 16 changes in the past year, about one every 10 days, most recently renaming Agent to CodeRabbit Engineer and dropping its $0.40/min overage rate 16 days ago on Oct 10. Giving the scan away free suggests CodeRabbit is prioritizing adoption of its Deep Scan security feature over near-term revenue from it.

THE SIGNAL
The move sits inside a broader pattern: 94 pricing changes across 75 companies in the last 180 days added new discounts or promos (4.6% of the 2,028 changes Pulse tracked in that window), with Openart (5), Runwayml (3), Bolt (3) and Krea (2) among the most frequent users of the pattern. CodeRabbit's version is unusual in what it's promoting — not a plan discount but a security feature — after a year in which the company mostly added, merged and renamed agent and add-on products rather than touching its core plans. A time-boxed, link-driven giveaway like this reads as demand generation for Deep Scan rather than a durable repricing.

IN CONTEXT
