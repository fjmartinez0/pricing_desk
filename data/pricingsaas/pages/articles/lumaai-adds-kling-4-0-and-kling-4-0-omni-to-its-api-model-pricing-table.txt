PACKAGING CHANGE
Lumaai adds Kling 4.0 and Kling 4.0 Omni to its API model-pricing table
Lumaai's API model-pricing table gains new rows for Kling 4.0 (text/image-to-video at 53 credits/sec for 720p, 79 for 1080p) and Kling 4.0 Omni, which adds the same 53/79 credits/sec for text and image-to-video plus video-to-video at ~66 credits/sec (720p) and ~98 credits/sec (1080p).

WHY IT MATTERS
Lumaai treats third-party model pricing as a living table rather than a product decision - it has made 8 real pricing changes since Jul 20, 2026, among the fastest cadences in the index. For API-resale vendors in this category, the model-pricing table (not the headline plan page) is where competitive and margin pressure shows up first. Watch whether Lumaai revises this table again soon: it already touched Seedance's own credit costs twice, on Sep 19, 2026 and again on Oct 5, 2026.

THEIR RATIONALE
By slotting Kling 4.0 straight into the existing per-model credit table alongside Seedance and Minimax, Lumaai continues to treat new third-party video models as a pricing-table update rather than a packaging change - consistent with how it added Nano Banana 2 and a Seedance 2.0 4K tier in July.
