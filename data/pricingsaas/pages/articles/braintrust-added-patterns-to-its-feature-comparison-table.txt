Braintrust added Patterns to its feature comparison table

What changed
Braintrust added a new 'Patterns' row to its plan comparison table: an AI capability that surfaces recurring issues across traces, billed through Braintrust credits like the rest of the platform's usage, and available as a checkmark feature on every plan from the free Starter tier to Enterprise. The existing Loop agent's description was also updated to note that it, too, draws on Braintrust credits. On the same day, Braintrust renamed its billing-credit unit from 'Model credits' to 'Braintrust credits' across the plan cards, the feature comparison table and its docs, and added three new FAQ entries about how credits work.
Why it matters
For a product leader, this is less about the credit rename — a packaging label, not a price change — and more about where Braintrust chose to put a new capability: inside the existing credit meter rather than behind a new paid tier or add-on SKU. Patterns joins Loop as another AI capability billed through the same currency as token usage, data processed and scores, so a team's bill moves with how much of the new feature it actually runs rather than with a flat per-seat fee.
That is the practical shape of the seat-to-usage shift: adding capability without adding a line item. Watch whether Braintrust keeps stacking new features onto its existing credit meter instead of shipping separate paid add-ons — that would mean usage-based billing has become its default distribution mechanism for new AI capability, not just a tool for overage control.
In context
Braintrust has spent this year adjusting its credits more than anything else on its pricing page: it disclosed Enterprise Topics' token overage rates on Jul 27, 2026, then hid them again on Aug 3, 2026, and added Topics to the table with its own credit pricing on Jun 8, 2026 [[braintrust/2026W31]] [[braintrust/2026W32]] [[braintrust/2026W24]]. Today's move is Braintrust's first pricing-page change since that Aug 3 hide — a 113-day gap against its roughly 69-day average, per Pulse's tracking history. Credits and usage billing is a busy pattern across SaaS pricing: Pulse tracked 479 changes across 302 companies in the last 180 days, 12.4% of the 3,854 changes it tracked, with Pika's fourteen moves and Lumaai's nine the most frequent in that window [[pika]] [[lumaai]]. Braintrust itself has made only four moves in this pattern, so today reads less like chasing a trend and more like folding another AI feature — Patterns, alongside the existing Loop agent — into a unit it already used, simply renamed for clarity.
Common questions
If we renamed a billing unit, what should we learn from how Braintrust did it?
Braintrust changed only the label — credit-to-dollar ratios, overage pricing and the FAQ structure kept their shape, just reworded under the new name. Pairing the rename with a concrete feature add (Patterns) gives customers and press something besides the name change to focus on.
How would a finance or billing-ops team notice this without reading the pricing page?
Only through invoice line items relabeling from 'Model credits' to 'Braintrust credits' — the statement total does not move, so this is a reconciliation footnote to flag internally, not a cost review trigger.
