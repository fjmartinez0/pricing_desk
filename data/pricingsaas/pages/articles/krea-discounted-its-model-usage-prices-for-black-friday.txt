Krea discounted its model usage prices for Black Friday
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 19, 2026
Krea discounted five AI models' usage prices, up to 75% off.

Krea’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 19, 2026
What changed
Krea added a 'Black Friday model prices' discount box to its Pro and Max plan cards, per the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 19, 2026: Seedance 2.5 is 60% off on Pro and 75% off on Max, Kling 4.0 is 20% off on Pro and 50% off on Max, Krea 3 is 25% off on Pro and 50% off on Max, GPT Image 2.6 is 20% off on Pro and 45% off on Max, and Nano Banana Pro is 45% off on Pro and 65% off on Max. The box is marked 'Limited time offer' with no end date shown, per that same Nov 19, 2026 page.
Why it matters
This is a usage-price discount, not a plan-price cut: it lowers what a generation costs on five specific models rather than changing what Pro or Max cost per month. For a product leader, that is an important distinction when reading any 'up to X% off' headline — the savings only materialize for teams that actually burn through credits on Seedance 2.5, Kling 4.0, Krea 3, GPT Image 2.6 or Nano Banana Pro; a light user sees no change to their bill.
It is also a cheap way to drive usage during a slow-decision holiday window without touching the subscription price list, which stays intact once the promo ends. Watch whether Krea converts any of this into a permanent repricing — similar usage-based promos have expired cleanly in Krea's own history rather than becoming the new normal.
In context
Krea leaned into Black Friday twice in two days: Pulse tracked a sitewide yearly-plan sale of up to 70% off on Nov 18, 2026, cutting Max to $42/mo at 60K units, and this new box adds per-model usage discounts the next day, both marked limited-time on Krea's pricing page. Pulse tracked 190 pricing changes adding new discounts across 153 companies in the last 180 days, 5.6% of the 3,406 changes it tracked in that window; Openart and Krea itself are the most frequent movers in that pattern. For a product leader, the sharper read is that Krea is now discounting on two axes at once — subscription price and per-generation usage cost — temporarily narrowing the gap between a team on Max's bundle and one on Pro topping up discounted models individually. Krea has ended time-limited perks quickly before, closing a 14-day Krea 3 offer on Oct 23, 2026.
Common questions
Is Max's bigger discount worth the higher monthly price?
It depends on usage: Max's discount runs deeper than Pro's on every model in the box, but Max also costs more per month, so the per-generation savings only outweigh that gap once a team's usage is high enough — Krea's page doesn't show the break-even point.
How does this compare to yesterday's Black Friday sale?
They're different levers — yesterday's sale discounted the subscription price of Krea's yearly plans, while this one discounts the per-generation cost of specific models; a team's total savings depend on whether it buys annual, burns through model credits heavily, or both.
