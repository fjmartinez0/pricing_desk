THE SIGNAL
Pulse Index's capture records one price per day, so a page running a live pricing test can produce a result that looks like a hike-then-reversal even when no deliberate repricing happened — one capture simply lands in a different test arm than the next. A genuine price correction usually comes with some signal: a promo badge, an end date, a change in copy. Here there is none of that, and an automated check for A/B-testing vendors and experiment markers on Shopify's pricing page came back without a clear signal — expected, since JavaScript-rendered pricing is invisible to that kind of check, not proof a test isn't running.
