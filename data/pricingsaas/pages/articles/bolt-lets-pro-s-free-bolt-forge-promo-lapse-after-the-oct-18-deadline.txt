Bolt lets Pro's free Bolt Forge promo lapse after the Oct 18 deadline

WHY IT MATTERS
Time-boxed inclusions like this let Bolt pull users toward Pro without touching list prices, and they are easy to reverse. Watch whether Bolt brings back a similar time-boxed Forge offer, given how often it has touched Pro's feature set recently, or whether Forge becomes a separate paid add-on.

THEIR RATIONALE
The removal lines up exactly with the deadline Bolt itself set when it offered Forge free on Pro — this was always framed as a time-boxed inclusion, not a benefit being cut outright. Bolt had been actively managing that same deadline very recently: its previous tracked change, three days earlier, extended the same Forge promo through Oct 18. Letting it lapse now reads as the trial window simply running its course rather than being pulled early.
