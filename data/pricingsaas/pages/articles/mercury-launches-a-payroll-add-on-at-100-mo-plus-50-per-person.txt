PACKAGING CHANGE
Mercury launches a Payroll add-on at $100/mo plus $50 per person
Mercury added a new Payroll add-on alongside its existing Mercury Books add-on, priced at $100/mo plus $50 per person paid per month, with the fee waived until Dec 31, 2026.

WHY IT MATTERS
Add-ons let Mercury monetize adjacent workflows, books and now payroll, without touching its core banking price, the same playbook a wide swath of the index is running to grow revenue per account without a headline price increase. Watch whether the Dec 31, 2026 waiver becomes Mercury's standard on-ramp for every new add-on, and whether Payroll starts billing before Books does once the waiver lapses.
