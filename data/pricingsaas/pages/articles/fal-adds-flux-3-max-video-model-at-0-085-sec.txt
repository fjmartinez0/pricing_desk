WHY IT MATTERS
fal is racing to keep its model catalog current across video, image and audio at once rather than one vertical at a time, which matters for buyers comparing per-second and per-output rates across providers — the new price-per-output column makes that comparison easier, but also makes pricing moves like the recent Flux 3 Image doubling more visible. Watch whether FLUX 3 Max pricing holds at $0.085/sec or follows the same quick-increase pattern as Flux 3 Image editing, and whether dropping ElevenLabs from the featured Audio list signals a shift toward newer third-party or in-house audio models.

THEIR RATIONALE
fal doesn't state a rationale, but the shape of the change reads as a catalog refresh: add the newest models (FLUX 3 Max, FLUX.1 schnell, several audio models), make the comparison easier to use with a new price-per-output column, and trim the featured list by dropping ElevenLabs' TTS and sound-effects rows specifically — not ElevenLabs as a provider, which stays listed.
