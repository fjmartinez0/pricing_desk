Jasper dropped its Copyscape plagiarism checker
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 21, 2026 · Last updated Nov 22, 2026
Jasper removed its Copyscape-powered plagiarism checker from its plan comparison table.

What changed
Jasper pulled the "Plagiarism Checker" row from its plan comparison table. The row, which covered scanning content for sources using the company's add-on for Copyscape, had been checked for both the Pro and Business plans; it no longer appears on the page at all, for either plan. Everything else around it in the comparison table — Remix Content, Marketing Editor, Rephrase & Rewrite — is unchanged.
Why it matters
A plagiarism checker is a compliance and trust feature, not a core generation capability, so pulling it from the comparison table rather than re-gating it behind a higher tier is a packaging signal: Jasper is editing down what it promises on the page, not what it charges for. For a product leader, a dropped row is worth noticing because it rarely stands alone — Pulse tracked 176 feature removals across 159 companies in the last 180 days, so trimming comparison tables is a live pattern, not a one-off.
In context
Jasper has dropped features from its pricing page before, including Instant Campaigns from Pro in Oct 2025 and Performance Analytics from Business in Apr 2025, since Pulse started tracking the page in Jul 2024. Pulse has recorded changes landing on Jasper's page roughly every 78 days on average over that span, and the previous change — a Jul 2026 expansion of Agents library access for Pro Jasper unlocks full Agents library access for Pro — came 138 days before this change, per Pulse's tracking. Jasper is far from alone in trimming comparison-table rows right now: Slack tested pulling Slackbot from Free and Pro and Huggingface dropped NVIDIA H100 endpoints from its table made similar cuts in the same week, part of a broader clean-out of legacy rows — Pulse tracked 176 feature removals across 159 companies in the last 180 days. None of these companies cut a price alongside the removal; the pattern reads as tidying rather than cost-cutting.
Common questions
Does this mean Jasper no longer checks for plagiarism at all?
The pack only shows the row removed from the comparison table for Pro and Business; it doesn't say whether the capability moved elsewhere in the product or was dropped outright, so check Jasper's own pricing page or docs for the current answer.
Is this part of a bigger cleanup, or a one-off?
Jasper has quietly dropped other features from its page before, and this lands the same week other companies trimmed their own comparison tables — worth watching whether Jasper's next changes keep clearing legacy rows rather than adding features, or whether plagiarism checking resurfaces as a paid extra.
