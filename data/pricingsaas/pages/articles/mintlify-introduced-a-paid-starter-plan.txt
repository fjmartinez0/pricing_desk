Mintlify tested a paid $150 Starter plan
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 18, 2026
Mintlify added a new $150-a-month Starter plan to its pricing page.
See before and after

What changed
Mintlify's pricing page now shows a new Starter plan priced at $150 a month on annual billing, or $180 a month billed monthly, with 10 editors, the Agent and Autopilot AI features, preview deployments and 5,000 AI credits a month, per the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 18, 2026.
The Pro plan card, which now builds on Starter, also lists password protection and advanced analytics to set it apart from the new, cheaper tier, even though Pro's comparison table already included both features.
This looks like a live A/B test rather than a settled plan change: Pulse fetched mintlify.com/pricing 12 times on Nov 18, 2026, and the paid Starter ($150 and $180) appeared on 3 of the 12 fetches, tied to a mintlify starter variant cookie set to 'treatment'. The other 9 fetches ('control') showed no $150 or $180 price.
Why it matters
For a product leader watching the seat-to-usage shift, this is Mintlify testing a fill for a gap it created the day before: it renamed its old free Starter plan to Free, then reused the Starter name for a new paid tier gated by editor seats and a monthly AI-credit cap rather than unlimited use, per the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 18, 2026. If it ships, that's a classic three-rung ladder — free, capped-paid, uncapped/custom — that lets Mintlify charge for AI usage like Agent and Autopilot without raising the price of its free tier.
A team sized for the new Starter tier should expect to hit its credit cap before it needs Pro, and to budget for Agent and Autopilot as paid features rather than assuming they come free. The fact that Pro's card now calls out password protection and advanced analytics — features Pro already had, per this same Nov 18, 2026 update — suggests Mintlify wants the gap to Pro to read clearly next to the new tier. Watch whether the test rolls out to all visitors, and whether Pro's price or feature list changes with it.
In context
Mintlify has touched its pricing roughly every 27 days since Pulse started tracking it in Oct 2024, and this is its second change in as many days: it renamed the old free Starter plan to Free [[mintlify/2026D321]], then reused the Starter name a day later for this new $150-a-month tier, per the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 18, 2026. The company has leaned on credit caps as its usage lever all year — it revealed AI credit costs in Sep 2026 [[mintlify/2026D252]], swapped free credits for a Pro trial in Oct 2026 [[mintlify/2026D286]], and renamed Automations to Autopilot while walling the Enterprise-only version off from Pro [[mintlify/2026D309]]. Credits and usage billing show up in 400 of the roughly 3,298 pricing changes Pulse tracked across 268 companies in the last 180 days, among them Pika and Invideo, so Mintlify is following a market-wide shift rather than inventing one. The sharper read: Mintlify has renamed its free tier to Free and is now testing a paid tier under the old Starter name that bundles Agent, Autopilot and 5,000 AI credits a month.
Common questions
Is Starter's 5,000-credit cap the same as Pro's?
No — Pulse's tracking shows Mintlify raised Pro's monthly AI-credit allowance from 5,000 to 10,000 in July 2026, double the new Starter plan's 5,000-credit cap, so moving up to Pro still buys more AI usage than seat count alone suggests.
What happens to teams already on the old free plan?
Nothing — their plan was simply renamed from Starter to Free, with the price and editor limit carried over unchanged. The new, paid Starter name only applies to the mid-tier plan introduced here.
