PACKAGING CHANGE
Pika launched Creator Team and Pro Team plans
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 15, 2026
Pika launched Creator Team and Pro Team, its first seat-based pricing plans.
See before and after
Enlarge
Pika’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 15, 2026
What changed
Pika added two new seat-based plans alongside its existing individual plans: Creator Team at $35 per seat per month with a 2-seat minimum and 5,200 shared credits per month, and Pro Team at $95 per seat per month with the same 2-seat minimum and 17,100 shared credits per month, per Pika's pricing page as of Nov 15, 2026. Both plans pool their credits across the team rather than allotting them per person.
Why it matters
For a product leader, Pika's move matters because it layers a seat-priced team tier on top of a credits-based individual product — a hybrid a growing number of AI tools are testing as they look for packaging that scales with both people and usage. Pulse tracked 165 seat-pricing changes across 121 companies in the last 180 days, 5.3% of the 3,089 pricing-page changes it tracked in that window, so seat-based tiers are still a minority move among AI tools generally.
If you're considering a similar shift, Pika's 2-seat minimum on both new tiers is worth copying: it sets a floor that keeps per-seat revenue predictable without locking out small teams. Watch whether Pika's individual Creator and Pro plans stay untouched now that Team exists, or whether their credit caps keep shrinking the way they did on Nov 6 and Nov 13, 2026, per Pulse's tracking.
In context
This is Pika's first move into seat pricing: Pulse's tracking shows no earlier seat-pricing change on Pika's page, even though Pika made 9 pricing changes in the last 90 days, mostly on credits and caps rather than seats. The new Creator Team and Pro Team tiers sit beside Pika's existing individual plans rather than replacing them, arriving after Pika doubled GPT Image 2.6's credit cost on Nov 13, 2026 and cut Starter and Creator's monthly credit caps by 33 to 17 percent on Nov 6, 2026, per Pulse's tracking. Elsewhere in AI tools, Captions added seat counts to its own plans on Nov 10, 2026, while usage-based credits remain Pika's main lever on the individual side. For a product leader, the read is that Pika is hedging between two packaging models at once, hoping a team tier wins larger accounts while it keeps squeezing credits on the individual side.
Common questions
What if my team only has one person who needs Pika?
A lone user is served more cheaply by Pika's existing monthly Creator or Pro plan than by either Team tier, since both new tiers require at least two paid seats.
Is there anything above Pro Team for bigger teams?
Yes — Pika's pricing page still routes larger teams to an Enterprise plan with custom credits and no listed per-seat price.
