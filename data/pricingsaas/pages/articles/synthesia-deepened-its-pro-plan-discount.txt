Synthesia deepened its Pro plan discount
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS

What changed
Synthesia deepened its live Black Friday discount on the Pro plan, cutting the effective price from $59 to $44 a month — 51% off the $89 list price, up from 34% off — and billing yearly subscribers $528 instead of $708, according to Synthesia's pricing page on Nov 24, 2026. The offer now carries new-subscribers-only terms and a sitewide countdown banner.
Why it matters
For a product leader, the headline price matters less than the terms around it: this discount is restricted to new subscribers, on a pricing page that has already been rebuilt several times in recent weeks — Synthesia split its page into individual and business views and moved to a credits-based model before layering this promo on top. That sequencing suggests Synthesia is using Black Friday to drive new sign-ups into its post-restructure pricing rather than discounting its way through the restructure itself. Watch whether the discount reverts automatically once the countdown ends, or whether "new subscribers only" becomes Synthesia's default framing for future promotions.
In context
Synthesia's page has moved fast lately: Pulse tracked 11 pricing changes in the last 90 days, and this lands just 6 days after Synthesia split its pricing into separate individual and business tabs and hid Enterprise on Nov 18, 2026. Discounting isn't new here, but the direction has flipped: on Oct 22, 2026, Synthesia cut Syren promo credits from 2,000 to 1,000 on Basic and 1,500 on Starter and Pro, per Pulse's tracked diffs, tightening a different discount, while this move deepens Pro's headline price cut instead. Market-wide, Pulse tracked 162 discount changes across 97 companies in the last 180 days, 4.2% of the 3,855 changes it tracked. The sharper read: Synthesia is making two kinds of pricing moves at once, restructuring the page and running a time-boxed discount, so this discount says more about Black Friday urgency than where pricing lands once the sale ends.
Common questions
Does the new-subscribers-only restriction on the $44 rate mean existing Pro customers keep paying $59?
The item doesn't specify renewal pricing for existing subscribers as of Nov 24, 2026 — it only says the $44 offer is for new subscribers — so current Pro customers should check their own billing page rather than assume either rate applies to them.
Is this discount connected to Synthesia's recent move to a credits-based pricing model?
Not directly — the credits restructuring happened earlier, on Oct 2, 2026, and the page-tab split happened Nov 18, 2026; this Black Friday deepening is a separate, time-boxed promotional layer on top of whichever structure is currently live.
Should we expect the $44 rate to still be live after the countdown ends?
Pulse's record doesn't show how Synthesia's past limited-time offers resolved once their countdowns ended, so there's no historical pattern here to lean on — treat the countdown banner's stated end date as the only signal available until Pulse saves what replaces it.
