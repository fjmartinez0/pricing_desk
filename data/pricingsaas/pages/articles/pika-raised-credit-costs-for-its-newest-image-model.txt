PACKAGING CHANGE
Pika raised credit costs for its newest image model
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 13, 2026
Pika doubled the per-image credit cost of its GPT Image 2.6 model.
See before and after
Enlarge
Pika’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 13, 2026
What changed
On the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 13, 2026, Pika renamed its GPT Image 2.5 line to GPT Image 2.6 and raised its cost from 2 credits to 4 credits per image. The same update cut monthly image allowances roughly in half, per that Nov 13, 2026 snapshot of Pika's pricing page: Starter falls from 300 to 150 images, Creator from 1,300 to 650, and Fancy from 4,275+ to 2,137+.
Why it matters
For a product leader shipping AI image generation, this is a reminder that your own per-unit costs can move even when you have changed nothing on your side: Pika repriced one underlying model and shrank what counts as "included" in the same stroke, raising the real cost of hitting a team's usage targets, not just the sticker price. If your own packaging passes a wrapped model's cost through 1:1, build in room for the vendor to reprice that model again rather than assuming today's credit math holds for the life of a contract.
Watch whether Pika keeps pairing a model upgrade with a credit-cost increase the next time it adds a frontier model. Pulse has also flagged this credit-cost change as possibly one arm of a test (Nano Banana 2 at 7 credits vs GPT Image 2.6 at 4), so confirm it persists before treating it as permanent.
In context
Pulse has tracked eight Pika changes to credits or usage billing in the last 180 days, part of 377 such changes across 259 companies in that window (12.3% of the 3,077 changes Pulse tracked in the last 180 days) — one of the category's most frequent movers alongside Lumaai and Mintlify. Six days earlier, per Pulse's tracked history, Pika added Nano Banana 2 at 7 credits on Nov 7, 2026, and a day before that cut Starter and Creator credit caps on Nov 6, 2026. The pattern reads less like a one-off repricing and more like Pika tightening credit economics model by model as it adds pricier image options. Elsewhere this week, Captions listed seat counts per plan and Cargo tightened its free tier, both narrowing what an entry plan includes. For a team evaluating Pika, the credit table under the headline plan price is moving almost as often as the plans themselves — worth checking before assuming last month's cost-per-image still holds.
Common questions
Does this apply to images we already generated, or only going forward?
Only going forward — per the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 13, 2026 this is a change to the live rate card, and nothing in Pulse's tracked history for Pika shows it repricing usage retroactively.
What should we do before our next renewal with Pika?
Re-run your expected monthly image volume against the new caps before renewing: Starter, Creator and Fancy allowances were all roughly halved in this update, so a workflow sized to the old allowance may need a higher tier or a top-up pack sooner than planned.
Did Pika say why it raised the cost and cut the caps?
No — Pulse's record and Pika's pricing page do not carry a stated reason for the repricing or the lower caps; the pack shows the what, not the why.
