Synthesia raised Pro plan's Syren avatar video allowance
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 27, 2026
Synthesia raised Pro's Syren avatar video allowance to about 96 a year.
See before and after
Enlarge
Synthesia’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 27, 2026
What changed
Pro's Syren avatar video allowance rose from about 84 to about 96 videos a year (roughly 7 to 8 a month), per Synthesia's pricing page as of Nov 27, 2026. The plan's other credits — Studio video minutes, Dubbing minutes and Survey responses — are unchanged, and the move does not touch Pro's monthly price or billing terms.
Why it matters
For a product leader watching usage-based SaaS pricing, this is a capacity lever: Synthesia is loosening a volume cap on Syren while leaving the published price untouched, which lets it answer "you capped my usage" complaints without reopening the price list. Pulse has tracked 12 Synthesia pricing changes in the last 90 days and 20 in the last 365 days, against a roughly 32-day average gap between changes since it started following Synthesia in Nov 2024 — well above its own normal cadence, a sign Syren's quota is still being tuned rather than settled.
If you're building usage caps into your own plans, treat a published allowance as a snapshot, not a contract: watch whether Synthesia nudges Syren's limit upward again before its next price move.
In context
Pulse's record shows Synthesia raising usage limits before: AI Dubbing minutes jumped in September 2026 [[synthesia/2026D247]] and survey-response caps rose in a credits overhaul on Nov 18, 2026 [[synthesia/2026D322]]. Pulse tracked 12 Synthesia pricing changes in the last 90 days and 20 in the last 365 days since it started following the company in Nov 2024. Pulse counted 256 changes across 207 companies touching limit increases in the last 180 days, about 6.6% of the 3,876 changes it tracked, with Peec, Mailslurp and Datadog moving most often. The sharper read: this quota change came three days after Pulse recorded Synthesia's Black Friday cut to $44 a month on Nov 24, 2026 [[synthesia/2026D328]], so both the price and the allowance behind it are moving at once. Modal made a similar move this week, raising its Starter and Team container caps.
Common questions
Is this bump big enough to change how a team budgets Syren usage?
Barely — the allowance moves from about 7 to about 8 videos a month, so a team already brushing against the old cap gets a little more room, but one that needs more should still expect to upgrade or buy extra capacity rather than wait for the next bump.
Why isn't there a stated reason for the increase?
Synthesia's pricing page and change log don't include a stated reason for the bump, and Pulse's record found no public comment — so there's no company quote to attribute a motive to.
What should we actually track if we use Synthesia's Pro plan?
Watch your monthly Syren usage against the allowance rather than the yearly figure, and recheck the pricing page before renewal — Synthesia has touched this page more often than most vendors Pulse tracks, so terms you saw at signup may not match what's live today.
