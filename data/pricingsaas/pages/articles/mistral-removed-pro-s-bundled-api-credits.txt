PACKAGING CHANGE
Mistral removed Pro's bundled API credits
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 21, 2026
Mistral removed the $25.5/mo API credit perk from its Pro plan.

What changed
Mistral's Pro plan no longer lists its $25.5/mo bundled API credits as a feature, per Mistral's pricing page as saved by Pulse on Nov 21, 2026. Pro's other listed features — faster responses, better image generation, all-day coding — are unchanged, per the same Nov 21, 2026 page.
The same page's comparison table also expands model training opt-out: previously listed only for Enterprise, it now appears for Free, Pro, and Team as well.
Why it matters
Mistral is unbundling a usage perk while leaving the rest of Pro's listed features untouched, per Mistral's pricing page as saved by Pulse on Nov 21, 2026 — a reminder that a stable plan name doesn't mean stable inclusions over time. Teams running meaningful API volume through Pro should re-check their usage against Mistral's limits now that the $25.5/mo credit cushion, per Mistral's pricing page as saved by Pulse on Nov 21, 2026, is gone, and budget for overage or a plan move if they were relying on it.
Opening model training opt-out to Free, Pro, and Team removes a data-governance gate that used to sit behind an Enterprise sales conversation — useful leverage if your own packaging still reserves opt-out for top-tier or custom deals. Watch whether Mistral pairs this with a renewed push on paid API usage now that the included credits are gone.
In context
Mistral trimmed Free plan API credits on Oct 12, 2026, just 40 days before this change, and before that added bundled API credits to entry plans when it folded Education into Pro on Aug 17, 2026 — pulling Pro's credit line now continues that pattern of tuning credit allowances rather than a one-off cut. Pulse tracked 419 credits-and-usage-billing changes across 276 companies in the last 180 days — 12.2% of the 3,421 changes it tracked in that window — with Pika (13) and Lumaai (9) touching credit terms most often, including Lumaai's Nov 20 Seedance 2.5 Flash launch priced per credit rather than a flat rate. The training opt-out expansion is the quieter half of this diff, and likely matters more to enterprise buyers comparing vendors on data-handling terms than the credit trim does to anyone tracking sticker price. Together the two moves read as Mistral hardening its usage-based economics while softening a governance objection in the same release — the kind of trade AI vendors are making as they compete past the seat metric.
Common questions
What happens to a Pro team that was relying on that credit allowance?
Nothing changes automatically — Mistral simply stopped listing the credit allowance as a Pro perk, per its pricing page saved Nov 21, 2026, so usage that used to draw on it now needs its own budget or a higher-capacity plan.
Should we expect other AI vendors to pull back included credits too?
Pulse tracked 419 credits-and-usage-billing changes across 276 companies in the last 180 days, and most of the recent ones — like Lumaai's and Pika's per-credit model launches — are about how credits are priced, not removing them outright, so Mistral's straight cut is the less common move.
How does this timing compare with Mistral's usual pace of changes?
It follows Mistral's Oct 12, 2026 cut to Free plan credits by 40 days, per Mistral's tracked pricing history — a short gap for a company whose changes average 58 days apart.
