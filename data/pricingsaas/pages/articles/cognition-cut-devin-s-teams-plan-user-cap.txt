Cognition cut Devin's Teams plan user cap
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS

Cognition halved the Devin Teams plan's user cap.

What changed
Cognition's Devin Teams plan user cap dropped from up to 200 users to up to 100 users, updated on both the plan card and the comparison table, according to Devin's pricing page as saved by Pulse on Nov 11, 2026. The same update added "Use your ChatGPT plan" login support to the Pro, Max and Teams tiers. Teams also gained multiplayer sessions, SAML/OIDC SSO, three-tier cost controls and Slack channel support, while losing its admin dashboard analytics and priority support; Enterprise swapped SAML/OIDC SSO for SCIM and role-based access control.
Why it matters
Cutting the Teams user cap in half while adding multiplayer sessions, SSO, tiered cost controls and Slack support to the same plan is a seat-cap squeeze, not a simple downgrade: Cognition is making its Teams tier more capable but less able to host a large team on its own, nudging bigger buyers toward Enterprise, where pricing is custom. For a product leader watching the seat→usage arc, a richer feature set paired with a lower seat ceiling reads as a funnel move, not a value cut. A team that outgrows the new Teams cap now has to talk to sales rather than self-serve — worth flagging before a growing team assumes it can simply add more Teams seats. Watch whether Cognition keeps shrinking the Teams ceiling, or holds here while Enterprise absorbs the growth.
In context
Cognition has made 6 real pricing changes (7 events, 20 comparisons) since Pulse began tracking Devin's page on Jul 6, 2026, averaging one every 24 days per Pulse's record. This is its second straight cut to the Teams cap: unlimited seats became capped at 200 on Sep 24, 2026 Sep 24 cap, and the ceiling now drops to 100 on Devin's pricing page, 48 days later. The update also adds "Use your ChatGPT plan" login support, part of a broad pattern — Pulse tracked 327 feature changes across 269 companies in the last 180 days (10.7% of 3,068 tracked), including Cloudflare and Livekit on Nov 9, 2026. Cognition's own cadence has been steady too, per Pulse's record: SWE-2 replaced SWE 1.7 as the default model on Sep 14, 2026 Sep 14 model swap, and SpaceXAI joined Pro on Sep 17, 2026 Sep 17 SpaceXAI add. The sharper read: Cognition is bundling real capability into Teams while tightening who can buy it without a sales call — a packaging move dressed as a feature update.
Common questions
What happens to a Devin Teams workspace that needs more than 100 seats?
Teams caps out at 100 users on Devin's pricing page as saved by Pulse on Nov 11, 2026; past that, a workspace must move to Enterprise, where the flat per-seat rate becomes a custom quote negotiated directly — worth getting before a team crosses the cap, since the pack doesn't say whether seats added under the prior cap are grandfathered.
Is the seat-cap cut part of a broader industry trend, or specific to Cognition?
Pulse's pack shows no market-wide data on seat-cap cuts — only that feature additions like the new ChatGPT plan support are common (327 of 3,068 tracked changes in the last 180 days). The cap cut itself shows up only in Cognition's own record, twice now in 48 days.
