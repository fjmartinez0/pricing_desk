Baseten launched Serverless Loops training
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS

Baseten launched per-token pricing for its new Serverless Loops training product.

What changed
Baseten launched a new Serverless Loops training product with per-token pricing for GLM-5.3, GLM-5.3-Flash and Qwen3.6 35B-A3B across prefill, cached, sample and train rates, each carrying a 20% limited-time introductory discount, per the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 7, 2026. Listed rates per 1M tokens are $3.02 prefill, $0.60 cached prefill, $9.72 sample and $11.66 train for GLM-5.3; $0.67, $0.13, $1.69 and $4.46 for GLM-5.3-Flash; and $0.36, $0.07, $1.07 and $1.71 for Qwen3.6 35B-A3B, per Baseten's pricing page on Nov 7, 2026. The product sits alongside Baseten's existing Model API and Sandboxes usage pricing as a new, separately billed line.
Why it matters
Serverless Loops puts Baseten in the business of selling model training by the token, not just inference — a new line alongside the Model API and Sandboxes usage pricing it already runs. For a product leader bundling AI capability into their own product, this is a signal that infrastructure providers are pricing fine-tuning and continued-training workloads the same way they price inference: usage-metered, per-model, with an introductory discount to pull in early volume.
Watch how long the limited-time intro discount lasts — Baseten's page gives no end date — and whether Baseten extends per-token training pricing to more of its models; that would confirm this is a platform-wide pricing primitive, not a three-model launch.
In context
This is Baseten's second pricing-page change in as many days: on Nov 6, 2026, per Pulse's tracking, it added $0.16/GB/month Volumes storage to Sandboxes pricing, and the company has now made 14 real pricing changes since Pulse began tracking it on Jul 20, 2026 — about one every 8 days. Per-token model pricing is Baseten's default move: it added Kimi K3.1 Pro, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and joey-1 to its Model APIs table on Nov 5, with joey-1 at $0.01 per 1M input tokens, and cut Reflection Beam rates up to 72% on Oct 21, per Pulse's tracking. Pulse tracked 327 of the 2,703 pricing changes recorded across 224 companies as credits-and-usage-billing moves in the last 180 days. The sharper read: Baseten is treating per-token pricing as its default packaging primitive for every new capability — training included — rather than folding new products into flat plan tiers the way Basic, Pro and Enterprise once did.
Common questions
How long does the 20% intro discount last?
Baseten's listing calls it a limited-time discount without a stated end date — worth checking the live pricing page before you budget off the discounted rate.
How does Serverless Loops training pricing compare with Baseten's existing Model API rates?
Both are billed per token, but Serverless Loops adds a training-specific rate for sample and train tokens that standard Model API inference pricing doesn't carry — the new product extends Baseten's usage-based model rather than replacing it.
Should we expect more per-token-priced products from Baseten?
Treat this launch as a template to test rather than a guarantee: weigh the per-token rate against what equivalent work would cost on Baseten's existing Sandboxes compute pricing before committing a workload to it.
