Shopify rewords its in-person sales pitch, adds two new comparison rows
The In-person sales section's lead row changes from 'Casual, in-person sales at pop-ups and events' to 'Accept payments with a phone or card reader.' Two new comparison rows are added for all plans: Customer profiles, listed as Simple, and Returns, listed as At purchase location.

WHY IT MATTERS
Pricing pages aren't just dollar figures - the words next to a checkmark decide whether a buyer even notices a capability, and Shopify has rewritten this same in-person-sales row since it first appeared on Jul 4, 2025 as 'In-person selling by phone or POS device.' Adding the two comparison rows, Customer profiles and Returns, is a small trust signal: it suggests Shopify is auditing its comparison table for gaps, not just its price column. Watch whether more packaging and copy edits follow, especially after a volatile pricing week like Oct 15-16.
