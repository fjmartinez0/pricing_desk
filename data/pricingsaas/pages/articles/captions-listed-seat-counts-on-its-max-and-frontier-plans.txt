PACKAGING CHANGE
Captions listed seat counts on its Max and Frontier plans
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 10, 2026
Captions started showing exact seat limits on its paid plans.

Captions’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 10, 2026
What changed
Captions added a new line to the “What’s included” list on its Max and Frontier plan cards, spelling out each plan’s seat allocation for the first time. Max now shows “2 total seats (1 admin + 1 additional seat)” and Frontier shows “3 total seats (1 admin + 2 additional seats),” per Captions’ pricing page on Nov 10, 2026. Prices and credit allotments on both plans are unchanged.
Why it matters
For a product leader, this is a packaging-transparency move, not a pricing one: Captions is telling buyers exactly how many seats a plan includes before they have to dig through docs or ask sales, which shortens the path from pricing page to purchase for small teams. If your own plans bundle a fixed number of seats inside a flat price, this is a cue to check whether that number is stated as plainly on your cards as the price itself — buyers increasingly expect to do that seat math without leaving the page. Watch whether Captions extends the same seat-count line to Enterprise (now Tesseract for business), which still sells on custom seat options rather than a fixed number.
In context
This is Captions’ second pricing-page update in five days: on Nov 5, 2026 it renamed its Enterprise plan to Tesseract for business and dropped its perks list Nov 5, 2026. Spelling out seat counts on the card itself, rather than leaving buyers to infer admin-plus-additional math, fits a broader push toward disclosing seat structure up front: Pulse tracked 163 seat-pricing changes across 120 companies in the last 180 days, 5.3% of the 3,065 pricing changes it recorded in that window, with Intercom the most active mover on seat pricing in that stretch. Leadmagic added paid seats and seat top-ups to its own plans on Nov 9, 2026 Nov 9, 2026, another sign that seat math is becoming something buyers can see rather than ask for. For a product leader, the sharper read is that Captions isn’t changing what a seat costs or how many seats Max or Frontier include — it’s closing the gap between what a plan includes and what the card actually shows, a disclosure move rather than a packaging one.
Common questions
Does the admin seat count toward the total, or is it extra?
It counts toward the total: Max’s “1 admin + 1 additional seat” means a 2-person team fits without upgrading, and Frontier’s “1 admin + 2 additional seats” fits a 3-person team — useful math if you’re sizing which plan a small team needs.
Is Captions likely to keep updating its plan cards like this?
Probably — Pulse has tracked six changes to Captions’ pricing in the last 365 days, averaging one every 67 days. Enterprise, rebuilt around custom seat options when it was added on Mar 30, 2026, hasn’t gotten the same seat-count treatment as Max and Frontier yet — worth watching as Captions keeps iterating on its plan cards.
