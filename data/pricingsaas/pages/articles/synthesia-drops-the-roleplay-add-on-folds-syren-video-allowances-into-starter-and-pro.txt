Synthesia drops the Roleplay add-on, folds Syren video allowances into Starter and Pro
Synthesia removed the standalone Roleplay Training add-on entirely - the full-width banner pitching 'AI roleplay practice for your team' at $25-$29/learner/month, plus the compact 'Roleplays + Add learner seat' row that had sat on the Starter and Pro cards.

WHY IT MATTERS
Synthesia has adjusted Syren-related pricing more than once in recent weeks - launching it with free credits on Oct 10, 2026, cutting those credits on Oct 22, 2026, and now folding the former Roleplay add-on into the plan's allowance - which says less about where the economics will land than about how unsettled the credit model still is. Buyers evaluating Starter or Pro should watch whether the roughly 24 and 84 video/year Syren allowances hold steady, or get trimmed the way the promo credits already were, cut from 2,000 to 1,000-1,500 within weeks of launch.

THEIR RATIONALE
Synthesia hasn't explained the swap in its page copy, but its own cadence tells the story: this change landed just 3 days after its previous one - adding up to 4 months free on yearly plans, Pro yearly at $59/mo, on Oct 26, 2026 - far faster than its typical roughly 39-day gap between changes, and one of 7 changes it has made in the last 90 days. It follows a run of Syren-specific adjustments: launching Syren with a 2,000-credit promo on Oct 10, 2026, then cutting that promo to 1,000-1,500 credits on Oct 22, 2026. Folding the Roleplay allowance into the plan's existing credit box, instead of charging for it as a separate per-learner add-on, reads as consolidation onto one credit surface rather than a growing list of add-ons.

THE SIGNAL
The specific trade - cutting a $25-$29 per-learner add-on and replacing it with a shared usage allowance baked into the plan - sits inside a broader seat-to-credit shift playing out across the index: seat pricing touched 128 changes across 98 companies in the last 180 days, about 5.5% of everything tracked. Arcade made nearly the same trade this week, swapping per-seat pricing for credit tiers, while others are pulling the opposite direction - Scite just added $40 team seats to its Pro plan. For Synthesia, which has leaned into credits since restructuring its whole plan lineup around them on Oct 2, 2026, Roleplay's seat-based pricing never really had room to settle before it was absorbed into the base allowance.

IN CONTEXT
Roleplay Training only became a paid, per-learner add-on when Synthesia restructured its plans around credits on Oct 2, 2026 - now it's gone, folded into the Syren video allowance that debuted as a 2,000-credit promo on Oct 10 and was already cut to 1,000-1,500 credits on Oct 22. This is Synthesia's fastest turnaround yet, landing just three days after it added up to 4 months free on yearly plans. Elsewhere in the index, Arcade made a similar seat-to-credit trade this week, even as Scite moved the other way by adding paid team seats - a reminder the market hasn't settled on one direction for seat-based add-ons.
