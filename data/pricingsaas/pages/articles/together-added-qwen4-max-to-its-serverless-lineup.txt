What changed
Together added Qwen4 Max to its Serverless Inference (Chat) pricing table; Pulse detected the change on Nov 19, 2026. Per Together's pricing page on Nov 19, 2026, the model is priced at $1.43 per 1M input tokens, $4.28 per 1M output tokens and $0.0713 per 1M cached input tokens, alongside the company's other serverless chat models.
Why it matters
For a team weighing inference vendors, Qwen4 Max gives Together another token-metered option to benchmark against its own stack and against other providers' chat models — but it also means one more row in a pricing table that Together revises often. A team that pins its cost model to a single Together model risks drifting out of date fast, since the company has changed pricing roughly weekly over the past year. Watch whether Qwen4 Max's rate moves again within its first few weeks the way several of Together's other new serverless models have.
In context
Together has saved 57 pricing-page comparisons since Dec 29, 2025, with 45 of them real changes — 23 in the last 90 days alone, a pace of about one every 7 days, according to Pulse's record. This addition landed one day after Together added Elorian Glimpse 1.0 at $0.60/$3.00 per 1M tokens on Nov 18, 2026, and follows Kimi K3.1 Pro at $2/$10 per 1M tokens, which Together priced on Nov 5, 2026, per Pulse's record — part of a steady run of new-model rows, with feature additions accounting for 16 of the company's 64 tracked events since Dec 29, 2025. Pulse tracked 400 pricing changes touching credits and usage billing across 268 companies in the last 180 days, so token-metered catalogs like Together's are moving faster than most. What a single new-model row understates is the upkeep: a team pinned to one Together model needs to keep re-checking the table rather than assuming a stable rate card.
Common questions
What other kinds of pricing moves has Together made, besides adding models?
Beyond new models, Together's last 64 tracked events include 13 price increases, 7 price decreases, 6 feature removals and 4 pricing restructures, per Pulse's data since Dec 29, 2025.
How should a team estimate what Qwen4 Max will cost them?
Multiply Qwen4 Max's per-token rates by expected request volume rather than assuming a flat seat price — unlike Together's seat-based products, this line item scales entirely with usage, so cost tracks consumption, not headcount.
