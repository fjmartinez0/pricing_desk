Baseten cuts Reflection Beam rates up to 72%, four days after launch
Baseten cut per-1M-token rates for its Reflection Beam Model API: input from $1.30 to $0.40, cached input from $0.13 to $0.04, and output from $4.30 to $1.20 - up to 72% off the launch rates.
