PACKAGING CHANGE
Anthropic launched Claude Sheets and Whiteboards
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 11, 2026
Anthropic added Claude Sheets and Whiteboards and renamed Dashboards to Data.
See before and after
Enlarge
Anthropic’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 11, 2026
What changed
Anthropic renamed Claude Dashboards, a beta feature, to Claude Data on its pricing page. Two new beta tools joined the feature list alongside it: Claude Sheets and Claude Whiteboards. All three are listed under the Pro, Max and Ultra plans; the Free plan does not get any of them.
Why it matters
This keeps Anthropic's packaging strategy consistent: expand what a seat includes rather than touch the price. Sheets and Whiteboards push Claude further into document- and whiteboard-style work, the kind of workflow that otherwise lives in a separate tool, while Free users stay on a narrower core product that nudges them toward Pro.
For a product leader, the lesson is about scope, not price: Anthropic is making its paid tiers worth more without moving the price tag, the same move it made when Design, Slides and Docs joined paid plans. Watch whether Sheets and Whiteboards stay bundled into the seat price once they leave beta, or whether Anthropic starts charging for them the way it has for other usage-heavy features.
In context
Pulse has tracked Anthropic's pricing page since Jan 3, 2025 and logged 18 real changes in that span, 5 of them in the last 90 days. Per Pulse's tracking, this is Anthropic's second change in the last 11 days, following Anthropic's Oct 31, 2026 split of Max into a $100 tier and a $200 Ultra tier [[anthropic/2026D304]]. Anthropic has used the beta label to ship product ahead of pricing before: Claude Design, Slides and Docs carried it until reaching Free on Oct 9, 2026 [[anthropic/2026D282]], and Claude Dashboards arrived in beta on Oct 12, 2026 [[anthropic/2026W42]]. Pulse tracked 64 of 3,068 pricing changes involving beta labels in the last 180 days, with HubSpot [[hubspot]] and Vercel [[vercel]] using the pattern most and Anthropic logging 2 of those changes. The sharper read: Sheets and Whiteboards start in beta already gated above Free, not drifting toward a paywall the way some betas do — the open question is whether they get priced on their own once they graduate, as Figma's agent did on exiting beta, when usage credits began [[figma/2026W42]].
Common questions
Will Sheets and Whiteboards reach the Free plan too?
Nothing in Anthropic's record commits to it. Other betas have graduated into Free before, so it's worth checking back periodically rather than assuming the current paid-only gate is permanent.
Does this change what a Pro, Max or Ultra seat costs?
No — this diff is a feature addition and a rename, not a price move. Check Anthropic's own pricing page for current seat rates before you budget off this change.
Should we worry about overlap with tools we already pay for?
Check whether your team's current spreadsheet or whiteboard tool already covers the same workflow. Anthropic is bundling this scope into seats you already own rather than billing for it separately, which could free up a line item.
