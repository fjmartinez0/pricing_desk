PACKAGING CHANGE
Tailscale drops Premium support for SLA-backed Silver, Gold and Platinum tiers
Tailscale removed Priority (Premium) support and Enterprise's dedicated professional services, replacing them with three new SLA-backed support packages, Silver, Gold and Platinum, plus separately scoped Support Add-ons.

WHY IT MATTERS
Making Silver self-serve lets customers buy a named SLA package without a sales conversation, while Enterprise loses its dedicated professional services and its custom terms now read simply "Custom MSA." Watch whether Gold and Platinum pricing becomes public or stays behind a sales gate, and whether other infrastructure vendors start carving out self-serve SLA tiers the same way.

THEIR RATIONALE
Tailscale frames this as a support-tier restructure rather than a price change: a single named Premium tier becomes three graduated SLA packages, with the Silver option now self-serve rather than gated behind a sales conversation. It's Tailscale's first tracked add-on-change move, and it lands just three days after a smaller Enterprise feature addition (custom tailnet subdomains on October 12), suggesting Tailscale is actively iterating on its page this month after a quieter stretch (2 changes in the last 90 days).
