PACKAGING CHANGE
Synthesia raised its survey response allowances
By Rob Litterst, Co-founder, PricingSaaS
Published Nov 18, 2026
Synthesia raised its survey response allowances as part of a credits overhaul.
See before and after
Enlarge
Synthesia’s pricing page · Change detected Nov 18, 2026
What changed
Synthesia raised the annual survey response allowance on its Starter plan from 42 to 150 and on Pro from 205 to 720, according to the pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 18, 2026, continuing the shift to credits-based usage limits it began on Oct 2, 2026. Basic also picked up a new 3-minute cap on Syren, Synthesia's AI video agent, while Starter unlocked uncapped Syren access and premium video models, according to the same pricing page Pulse saved on Nov 18, 2026.
The update also restructured the pricing page itself, splitting it into separate "For individuals" and "For business" tabs: the default view now shows only Basic, Starter and Pro, and Enterprise no longer appears in the main pricing row or the plan comparison table. The comparison table gained new "Shared Credit Pool" and "Admins" rows, while Pro's "AI Video Assistant" highlight was dropped.
Why it matters
For a product leader watching usage-based pricing, this looks as much about trust-building during a transition as about generosity. Synthesia moved its core plans to credits on Oct 2, 2026, then spent the following weeks adjusting the limits repeatedly — tightening Basic's free allowance, launching Syren credits, cutting Syren promo amounts, and now loosening the Starter and Pro survey caps. That cadence suggests Synthesia is still tuning where its credit ceilings bite, and raising responses — a metric that is cheap to serve relative to video generation — is a low-cost way to soften pushback on the broader overhaul.
Hiding Enterprise from the default pricing view is the more structural signal: it moves large-account pricing fully behind a "For business" tab and a "Book demo" CTA, keeping the self-serve page focused on individual buyers comparing Basic, Starter and Pro. Teams sizing Synthesia against a self-serve competitor should expect this segmentation to continue — Enterprise terms are increasingly a sales conversation, not a published price. Watch whether Synthesia keeps adjusting credit and response ratios on this near-weekly cadence, or whether this settles the model for a while.
In context
Synthesia has made eight changes to its credits and usage limits since introducing a unified credit system in October 2025, most recently swapping Syren's flat video cap for per-plan monthly quotas on Nov 7, 2026 and dropping its interactive credit calculator on Nov 3, 2026. Credits and usage billing is a market-wide pattern too: Pulse tracked 400 pricing changes across 268 companies touching it in the last 180 days, 12.1% of the 3,298 changes it tracked in that window. Pika made 12 of those changes and Lumaai made 7 over that same 180-day window, per Pulse's tracking — the most of any company. On Nov 16, 2026, Invideo halved its Max annual price to $75/seat on its pricing page while cutting credits from 8,000 to 5,000. The sharper read: Synthesia is still calibrating where its new credit ceilings sit after October's overhaul, and pairing that recalibration with a tab split that quietly moves Enterprise pricing out of the self-serve page.
Common questions
How should a team size the new survey allowance against its actual usage?
Multiply the plan's new cap — 150 responses a year on Starter, 720 on Pro, per Synthesia's pricing page as of Nov 18, 2026 — by how many survey-driven projects run per year; if that falls short, the real limit is the plan's overall credits pool, not a separate purchase, since Synthesia bills surveys through the same per-plan allowance it uses for video and dubbing.
If a team already has an Enterprise quote, does this page change affect it?
No — this only changes how Enterprise is discovered on the page, not its terms; existing quotes and custom agreements aren't described as affected. Teams evaluating Synthesia for the first time should now expect to request pricing through the "For business" tab rather than compare it inline with Basic, Starter and Pro.
