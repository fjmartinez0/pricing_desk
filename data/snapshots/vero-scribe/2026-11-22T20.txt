Questions about plans and billing.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every account starts with a 7-day free trial with full access, then keeps 10 free actions every month on the Free plan.
How much does Pro cost?
Pro is $89 a month billed yearly, or $99 month to month, with unlimited actions.
Are there discounts for students and residents?
Yes. Students, residents, and other trainees get a discount on Pro. Contact us for the details.
What does Enterprise add?
Everything in Pro, plus SSO and organization management, custom data retention policies, a team dashboard with analytics, centralized invoice billing, custom onboarding, and a dedicated support team.
Is patient data protected on every plan?
Yes. Every plan is HIPAA compliant and SOC 2 Type II certified. Audio is never stored, and patient data is never used to train AI models.
