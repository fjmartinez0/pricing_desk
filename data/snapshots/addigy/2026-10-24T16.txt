Frequently Asked Questions
How does Addigy’s Apple MDM pricing work?
Addigy plans are priced per device, per month — no multi-year contracts required (however, buyers can lock-in lower rates with multi-year plans if they choose). Plans start at $8.25 per Mac per month. MSP pricing is available at lower per-device rates. iOS devices (iPhones, iPads, and Apple TVs) are available as an add-on at a separate per-device rate. Schools and large organizations can contact Addigy for custom discounted pricing.
Contact us for a quote tailored to your environment.
How does Addigy’s Security Suite or Assist pricing work?
Addigy’s Security Suite includes Apple MDM + SentinelOne EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response) with 24/7 MDR (Managed Detection and Response), CVE (Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures) tracking, integration with Microsoft Conditional Access via Entra ID and Intune, and automated compliance enforcement for CIS, NIST, CMMC, and DISA STIG frameworks.
Security Suite pricing starts at $16 per Mac per month. MSP pricing is available. Contact us for a custom quote.
Does pricing include support? What kind of Apple MDM support does Addigy offer?
Yes, support is included in all Addigy plans at no additional cost. Addigy provides US-based support with expanded EU hours to all customers — MSPs and Corporate IT teams alike — covering day-to-day Apple device management and product troubleshooting.
Every customer also has 24/7 access to Addigy’s support site, which includes nearly 400 knowledge base articles, and an AI-powered support chat bot — so you can get fast, accurate answers whenever you need them, without waiting on a ticket.
For larger organizations with more complex or high-volume support needs, Addigy also offers Premium Support. Contact us to learn more.
For those looking to go deeper, Addigy Boost is a guided training and support program for customers that pairs MDM admins directly with an Apple Solutions Architect. The program helps admins master the platform, sharpen their configuration, and have expert backup as they grow.
What does onboarding, migration, and getting started with Addigy look like?
Every new Addigy customer receives a complimentary kickoff session with our team to walk through initial setup — from device enrollment and policy configuration to getting your first devices under management. For customers migrating from an existing MDM platform like Jamf, Kandji, or Mosyle, Addigy offers professional migration services scoped to your current environment.
Reach out to discuss your migration needs and get a quote.
Can I try Addigy before I purchase it?
Yes. Addigy offers a free 14-day trial so you can evaluate the platform before committing. No credit card is required to start a free trial. The trial gives access to the full Addigy platform, including the agent, Apple MDM and DDM features, the multi-tenant dashboard, and real-time device monitoring. MSPs evaluating Addigy for their client environments and Corporate IT teams evaluating it for internal use are both eligible for the free trial.
Start a Free trial here. For the best experience and a guided tour, we recommend a product demo with our expert team.
How does Addigy Intelligence pricing work?
Addigy Intelligence is a tier of the Addigy Apple device management platform priced per device and available through custom pricing alongside Apple MDM and Security Suite.
Through October 31, 2026, Addigy Intelligence is included for every Addigy customer at no additional cost, so teams can evaluate it on their own Mac fleet before deciding. After that date it becomes a paid tier — request custom pricing and we’ll size it to your device count.
Are Addigy Intelligence features included in the Security Suite?
Yes. Security Suite includes every Addigy Intelligence capability, plus advanced endpoint detection, compliance enforcement, and 24/7 threat response. The tiers are cumulative: Apple MDM is the core platform, Addigy Intelligence adds Apple expertise on demand, and Security Suite adds security and compliance on top of both. Existing Security Suite customers receive Addigy Intelligence at no additional cost.
Do I have to use AI tools to use Addigy?
No. Addigy’s Apple MDM platform works exactly as it always has, and Addigy Intelligence is a separate tier you choose. Even within Addigy Intelligence, nothing acts on a Mac without an administrator approving it first — there is no autonomous mode. Some customers adopt the tier specifically to restrict AI: the AI compliance benchmark lets you govern the AI tools you approve and block the ones you don’t.
