Three Ways to Manage Apple. One Platform.
Start with what you need and add capabilities as you grow. Every Addigy plan includes the full Apple MDM platform and support for Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV management. No per-seat or per-policy charges.
Pricing at a glance: Addigy has three plans, each priced per device. MDM starts at $8.25 per Mac per month and covers enrollment, policies, apps, OS updates, monitoring, and remote access. Intelligence Suite adds AI assistants, compliance benchmarks (CIS, NIST, DISA STIG, CMMC), and CVE patching for Prebuilt Apps. Security Suite starts at $16 per Mac per month and adds SentinelOne EDR with 24/7 MDR and Microsoft Conditional Access. iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV are billed at a lower per-device rate.

Frequently Asked Questions
How is Addigy priced?
Addigy is priced per device, based on your device count and the products you run: Apple device management, the Intelligence Suite, or the Security Suite.
MSPs: Billed monthly, so your costs scale with your client base.
Internal IT: Billed annually, sized to your fleet.
Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial of all features. For a quote sized to your device count or client base, talk to sales and get a demo.
What's the difference between MDM, Intelligence Suite, and Security Suite?
MDM is the full Apple management platform: enrollment, policies, apps, OS updates, monitoring, remote access, device security, identity, and API.
Intelligence Suite adds AI assistants, historical Report Builder, the Compliance Engine for CIS, NIST, DISA STIG, and CMMC benchmarks, Shadow AI control, and CVE patching for Prebuilt Apps.
Security Suite adds SentinelOne EDR with Vigilance 24/7 MDR, CVE tracking across all third-party apps, Microsoft Conditional Access, and audit-ready compliance evidence.
How does Addigy compare to Jamf?
Both are Apple-focused; the difference shows up in the day-to-day. Addigy keeps a live connection to every device, so drift and failed policies are caught and fixed as they happen, not at the next check-in, with end-user support in the same console.
Jamf leads with automation and lifecycle management and is broadening into identity and ZTNA; Addigy’s only mission is Apple, with day-one support for every macOS and iOS release. Any admin can run it, with no certification to earn, and remote control and multi-tenancy are built in rather than bought separately. Teams that switch most often tell us the difference in support is night and day. See the full comparison: Addigy vs. Jamf
How does Addigy compare to Iru and Mosyle?
All three started Apple-focused. Iru now covers Windows and Android; Mosyle is a single-tenant platform built around one organization’s fleet. Addigy’s only mission is Apple: a real-time connection to every device with on-demand end-user support, true multi-tenancy for MSPs, and MDM and remote management in one console. See the comparisons: Addigy vs. Iru · Addigy vs. Mosyle.
