Plans & billing
Is there a free trial?
We no longer offer a free-credits milestones program. New accounts choose a paid plan at signup, get an API key immediately, and can cancel anytime.
Can I cancel anytime?
You can cancel or downgrade a self-serve subscription through your dashboard on the billing settings page at app.leadmagic.io/settings/billing. Annual billing and custom agreements follow their stated terms; contact support if you need those terms reviewed. Once you cancel, you keep full access to your remaining credits and all platform services through the end of your current billing period. After that date paid execution stops. Existing credits keep their recorded terms and any original validity dates. If you downgrade, your retained subscription-pool limit becomes 2× the new plan's monthly allotment when the change takes effect. Excess credits remain available until the expiry dates shown in Billing and Credits history. The billing change follows its separately stated renewal date.
How can I get better per-credit pricing?
Move to a higher-tier plan or switch to annual billing. Annual billing is 10× the monthly price, two months free, and the Ultimate annual plan brings credits as low as $0.0071 each vs $0.0198 on Essential monthly.
How do I request team access?
Team access is available by request; a higher plan price does not automatically enable custom access or rates. During the Tables preview, everyone with Tables access can edit without editor selection, checkout or separate editor fees. Editor licenses are not currently sold. Request team access with your access requirements. Requests are reviewed before access and terms are agreed.
How is Enterprise Security priced?
Enterprise Security is available only on annual plans. Investment starts at $10,000. Request Enterprise Security with your security requirements for a proposal covering the agreed scope. Team access is reviewed separately; the starting investment does not establish a feature bundle or final quote.
Does API-result recovery require Enterprise/team access?
No. The Enterprise/team request does not apply to API-result recovery exports. Recovery still depends on verified account access and the availability of the original results. Contact support to investigate; recovery does not authorize new paid enrichment.
What happens if my payment is past due or fails?
If your subscription payment is past due or fails, your account will be frozen immediately. While frozen, you will lose all access to the API, LeadMagic CLI, MCP server, and the web dashboard. To unfreeze your account and restore service, simply log into your dashboard and update your payment method on our billing page at app.leadmagic.io/settings/billing to clear any outstanding invoices.
What is your refund policy?
Payments are non-refundable except where required by law or stated in a signed agreement. You can cancel anytime and keep access through the end of your paid billing period. For a duplicate charge or billing error, email billing@leadmagic.io. See the Refund Policy.
Credits
How does the credit system work?
One credit pool across all endpoints. Credits are only deducted on successful enrichment. Different services cost different amounts (e.g. email validation is 0.25 credits, email finder is 1 credit). On standard monthly plans, including Basic, your new monthly subscription-credit pool is capped at 2× your current monthly allotment. Credits above that limit remain available until the expiry dates shown in Billing and Credits history. A downgrade changes the subscription-pool allowance on its displayed effective date; it does not shorten the period already paid for. Paid top-ups are outside that cap. Existing top-ups keep their recorded terms. New top-ups purchased under explicitly accepted updated terms have 12-month validity and may expire earlier at actual subscription termination. Check Billing and Credits history for their saved amounts and expiry dates.
How is LeadMagic email finder API pricing calculated?
LeadMagic email finder API pricing is based on credits, not seats. Email finder lookups cost 1 credit when a valid result is returned, email validation costs 0.25 credits per conclusive valid or invalid result, and unknown validation results and failed or empty finder lookups do not deduct credits. The same shared credit pool also covers data enrichment API, mobile finder, company search, job data, CLI, MCP, and CSV workflows.
What are credit rollovers?
New monthly subscription credits issued under the updated policy can bank up to 2× the monthly allowance. Existing credits keep their recorded terms and stay outside the new cap, alongside paid top-ups. New top-ups bought under explicitly accepted updated terms have 12-month validity and may expire earlier when the paid subscription actually terminates. Any initial policy expiry requires at least 20 days after delivered notice, and longer saved obligations remain protected. Continuous renewals preserve access; scheduled cancellation keeps access through the paid-through date. Existing top-ups keep their original recorded validity. Annual and custom contracts follow their recorded terms.
Should I top up or upgrade?
A paid top-up covers a short-term gap at the top-up rate. If you regularly run out, compare a larger plan and annual billing for a lower unit cost and more included credits next period. An upgrade can also raise Email Finder and Validation throughput. Checkout shows the amount due, credit adjustment and effective date before you confirm. Compare plans or review Billing.
How do I avoid running out again?
Enable auto top-up in Billing if you want automatic purchases at your chosen low-balance threshold. Review the purchase amount, price and spending limit before opting in. Auto top-up buys additional credits; it does not upgrade the plan or raise its RPM allowance. If the same gap repeats, compare an upgrade for ongoing savings. Manage funding.
Can my team edit during the Tables preview?
During the Tables preview, everyone with Tables access can edit. There is no editor selection, checkout or separate editor fee. Editor licenses are not currently sold. Existing workspace permissions and session controls still apply. Enrichment credits and external provider usage keep their stated charges.
Search
How does the Search trial work?
The Search trial lasts 14 days with no automatic paid charge. Search results are retained for 14 days. Professional and Ultimate include unmetered browse at 25 rows/page within 10 million records/month and 13,333 searches/day. Enrichment, reveals and non-browse operations retain their stated credit prices. Choose a paid plan explicitly in Billing to continue after trial.
Is People, Company & Jobs Search included in my plan?
Yes, on every current plan. The in-app People, Company, and Jobs Search finders are included, and searches cost no credits. Build and filter lists at no per-search cost, with a daily export allowance that grows by tier (10,000 rows/day on Essential up to 100,000 on Ultimate). Revealed emails (1 credit) and mobile numbers (5 credits) bill at standard rates.
What does “unmetered search API” mean?
On Professional ($499/mo) and Ultimate ($849/mo), browsing through the People, Company, and Jobs Search APIs is unmetered. Browse requests spend no credits and have a sustained rate of 5 requests/second on Professional and 10 requests/second on Ultimate, measured account-wide across the search endpoints, within fair-use ceilings of 10 million records/month and 13,333 searches/day per account, at up to 25 rows/page. On Essential and Growth, the same API costs 1 credit per returned person, company, or job. A page returning 25 people costs 25 credits; an empty result costs nothing. Contact details and enrichment keep their standard credit prices. Fair use and internal-use restrictions apply: search is for your organization's own workflows, not for serving results to clients or end users, reselling data, or replicating the database.
Do searches cost credits?
In the app, searching and filtering spend no credits on Essential and higher plans. On the API, Essential and Growth pay 1 credit per returned person, company, or job, with no charge for empty results. Browsing spends no credits when your plan includes the unmetered search API (Professional and Ultimate, within 10 million records/month and 13,333 searches/day at up to 25 rows/page). Contact data always bills credits: 1 per revealed email, 5 per mobile number, and standard rates for every enrichment endpoint (Email Finder, Email Validation, and Mobile Finder).
What are the search export limits?
Each plan includes a daily search-export allowance: 10,000 rows/day on Essential, 25,000 rows/day on Growth, 50,000 rows/day on Professional, 100,000 rows/day on Ultimate. Export and non-browse operations retain their stated credit prices; a row allowance is not a zero-credit entitlement. In-app browsing also has a fair-use viewing ceiling to protect the index. Need more? An explicit custom agreement may define different search throughput or export limits; a plan price alone does not.
API & integrations
What are the API rate limits?
Email Finder and Email Validation each have a separate organization-wide rolling 60-second allowance: Basic 300 RPM, Essential 300, Growth 600, Professional 1,000 and Ultimate 1,500. All API keys and credit banks share each family allowance. Bulk and Tables lookups use that same allowance when queued work runs. Other products have no commercial RPM cap; platform, provider and search RPS safeguards still apply. A 429 rejection consumes no credits: wait for Retry-After. Published limits may change unless an explicit custom agreement fixes them.
What integrations are included?
All plans include API, LeadMagic CLI, and MCP access. LeadMagic also works with Clay, Apollo, Instantly, Smartlead, Zapier, Make, and more.
How does the personal/free email gate work at signup?
To prevent platform and API abuse, personal or free inboxes (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) must add a payment method or verify a work email before continuing onboarding. Signing up with a business email bypasses this check.
