Plans & billing
Is there a free trial?
There are no free credits. You choose a plan at signup, get an API key right away and can cancel anytime.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel or downgrade in Billing. You keep access and your credits until the end of the period you paid for, and credits keep the expiry dates shown in Billing. Annual and custom agreements follow their own terms.
How can I get better per-credit pricing?
Choose a bigger plan or pay annually. Annual billing costs 10 months for 12, and Ultimate annual brings credits down to $0.0071 each, vs $0.0198 on Essential monthly.
How do I request team access?
Team access is available on request. Tell us who needs access and we'll review it with you.
How is Enterprise Security priced?
It's available on annual plans and starts at $10,000. Send us your requirements and we'll reply with a proposal.
What happens if my payment is past due or fails?
Your account is paused right away: the API, CLI, MCP server and dashboard stop working. Update your payment method in Billing to pay the open invoice and restore access.
What is your refund policy?
Payments are non-refundable except where required by law or stated in a signed agreement. You can cancel anytime and keep access through the end of your paid billing period. For a duplicate charge or billing error, email billing@leadmagic.io. See the Refund Policy.
Credits
How does the credit system work?
You get one credit balance for every endpoint, and credits are only used when we return a result. Email Validation costs 0.25 credits and Email Finder 1 credit; the rate card lists the rest.
How is LeadMagic email finder API pricing calculated?
By credits, not seats. A found email costs 1 credit and a validated email 0.25. Failed lookups and unknown results cost nothing. The same balance covers mobiles, company data, search, the CLI, MCP and CSV uploads.
What are credit rollovers?
Unused monthly credits roll over, up to 2× your monthly allowance. Credits you already have and paid top-ups keep their own terms and don't count toward that cap. Annual and custom plans follow their contract. Full details are in the Terms.
Should I top up or upgrade?
A top-up covers a one-off gap. If you run out every month, a bigger plan or annual billing costs less per credit and raises your Email Finder and Validation speed limits. Checkout shows the price before you confirm. Compare plans.
How do I avoid running out again?
Turn on auto top-up in Billing to buy credits when your balance runs low. You choose the amount and a spending limit. If it keeps happening, upgrading is usually cheaper.
Can my team edit during the Tables preview?
Yes. During the preview, anyone with Tables access can edit, with no extra editor fee. Workspace permissions still apply, and lookups in a table use credits as usual.
Search
How does the Search trial work?
Search has a 14-day trial with no automatic charge. To keep using it afterwards, choose a paid plan in Billing.
Is People, Company & Jobs Search included in my plan?
Yes, on every plan. Searching in the app costs no credits, and you can export 10,000 to 100,000 rows a day depending on your plan. Revealing an email costs 1 credit and a mobile 5.
What does “unmetered search API” mean?
On Professional ($499/mo) and Ultimate ($849/mo), browsing results through the Search API costs no credits. You can make 5 requests/second on Professional and 10 requests/second on Ultimate across your account, within fair-use limits of 10 million records/month and 13,333 searches/day at up to 25 rows/page. On Essential and Growth, each returned result costs 1 credit and empty results are free. Contact details always use credits. You can reuse results in your own work and products, but not copy LeadMagic's database.
Do searches cost credits?
Not in the app. On the API, Professional and Ultimate browse without credits within fair-use limits, and Essential and Growth pay 1 credit per returned result. Revealed emails (1 credit) and mobiles (5 credits) always cost credits.
What are the search export limits?
Each plan has a daily export allowance: 10,000 rows on Essential, 25,000 rows on Growth, 50,000 rows on Professional, 100,000 rows on Ultimate. Contacts revealed in an export still cost credits. Need more? Ask us about a custom agreement.
API & integrations
What are the API rate limits?
Email Finder and Email Validation each allow 300 requests/minute on Essential, 600 requests/minute on Growth, 1,000 requests/minute on Professional, 1,500 requests/minute on Ultimate, shared across all your API keys. Other endpoints have no per-minute plan limit. If you hit a limit you get a 429 error, nothing is charged, and you can retry after the wait it gives you.
What integrations are included?
All plans include API, LeadMagic CLI, and MCP access. LeadMagic also works with Clay, Apollo, Instantly, Smartlead, Zapier, Make, and more.
Why do Gmail and Yahoo signups need an extra step?
To prevent abuse, signups with a personal inbox (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) add a payment method or verify a work email first. Work emails skip this step.
