Frequently Asked Questions
Can’t find the answer you are looking for? Reach out to our
support team.
Do all plans include the full platform?
Yes, Canopy is an all-in-one accounting practice management solution. Every Canopy plan includes the complete Canopy practice management software suite, including CRM, workflow, document management, billing, client portal, payments, and core AI capabilities. This ensures your firm is never locked out of essential features based on your selected plan.
What’s the difference between Canopy’s pricing tiers?
The primary difference between tiers is the depth and power of the available features. While the core tools remain consistent, higher tiers of the Canopy practice management software unlock advanced automation, deeper firm insights, and more complex coordination capabilities.
Which plan is right for my firm?
Start with where your firm is today. If you’re getting organized, Standard is a good fit. If you’re scaling operations, Plus or Premium will give you more automation and control. Most firms move up to the next tier of Canopy’s practice management system as they grow.
Can I upgrade my subscription later?
Yes, with Canopy’s practice management platform you can move between tiers as your needs evolve, without switching systems or reimplementing anything.
Can I trial the platform before committing?
Absolutely. You can explore the full Canopy practice management platform to see how it works across your firm before making a final decision. Your trial includes access to core tools such as client management, workflows, document management, billing, and the AI coworker, so you can experience how all-in-one practice management works together in one system compared to separate tools. Schedule a demo and talk with a sales representative to set up a trial.
What are add-ons and when are they needed?
Each of Canopy’s practice management pricing plans includes baseline access to capabilities like Smart Intake, workflows, and tax resolution. Add-ons are there if you need to scale usage or handle more complex workloads, not something you’re forced to buy upfront.
What are client credits?
Client credits power Tax Workflow Automation: Smart Intake and Smart Delivery. Each credit gives you unlimited use for one client for the rest of your subscription term. Every plan includes 5 credits, renewed each term, and you can add more anytime.
How are credits applied to my account and used?
A credit is applied the first time you use Tax Workflow Automation for a client: generating a questionnaire or document checklist with AI, sending a questionnaire with “Pre-Fill with AI”, or creating a tax delivery task. Questionnaires and checklists you build and send without AI never use a credit.
Why is there a limit on AI usage?
AI infrastructure requires significant resources, and usage can vary greatly between different accounting firms. Instead of increasing prices for everyone, Canopy’s practice management software includes a baseline of AI features and allows you to scale costs based on your actual automation needs.
How do I track my credit usage?
You can monitor your firm’s usage directly in the Canopy platform. Most firms start with their included credits and then expand their available balance as they see the time-saving impact of the AI tools.
Do credits cover multiple returns or deliveries?
Yes. One credit per client covers unlimited AI intake and deliveries for that client for your subscription term, including extensions and re-deliveries.
What type of support does Canopy provide?
We have won awards for our support and implementation! With Canopy’s practice management software, you’ll have access to world class support and implementation teams. You can chat in, call in to speak directly with a support team member, or send an email to our team. In addition to our support teams, you’ll also have access to self-guided resources like our knowledge base articles, in-app guides, video tutorials, and Canopy University.
