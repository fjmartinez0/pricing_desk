FAQ
What is Zoho One?
Zoho One is an unified platform designed to create more connected and agile organizations. It features 50+ business applications that enhance how different areas of a company (Sales, Marketing, Customer Service, Human Resources, Finance, etc.) work and collaborate, while also offering better user management, connecting the entire organization, providing advanced analytics, and more.
What editions are available in Zoho One?
We currently have two editions: Essentials, designed for companies starting their digitalization process, which includes all the necessary tools for a company to carry out all its sales, customer service, collaboration, marketing and finance functions smoothly.
The Standard Edition, on the other hand, includes more than 50 applications and all necessary services for a company of any size to operate efficiently and connected.
What's included in Zoho One Essentials?
Essentials includes everything a small company needs to begin their digital journey, including a CRM, Collaboration, finance management, marketing, customer service and HR tools. To see the list of apps included and what you can do with Zoho One Essentials visit this link.
What's included in Zoho One's standard plan?
Zoho One includes more than 50 enterprise-level online applications to grow sales, market your business, do your accounting, communicate with teammates and customers, and much more. This plan includes web, mobile, and installed versions of our applications, as well as browser extensions and other useful extras. For more information on what's included, visit our Applications page.
If I already have Zoho One's Standard edition, Can I downgrade to the Essentials plan?
Yes, but you'll have to downgrade all the users associated with your organization. If you want to downgrade, you can get in touch with us by sending an email to sales@zohocorp.com
Can I upgrade from Zoho One's Essentials plan to Standard edition?
Yes, but you will have to upgrade all the users associated with your organization. If you want to upgrade, you can get in touch with us by sending an email to sales@zohocorp.com
I just started my free trial for Zoho One Essentials plan. Why can I see all applications?
When you start your free trial, you will have access to Zoho One's full potential for the duration of the trial. When the trial ends, you can decide whether to stay with the Essentials Plan or the Standard Plan.
How much does Zoho One cost?
It depends on the plan and licensing model you choose:
Essentials: The Essentials plan costs per user per month with annual licensing.
Standard Plan, flexible users: The Zoho One standard plan costs per user per month with annual licensing. However, if you decide to purchase Zoho One licenses for all users in your organization, you might be eligible for the All-employees model.
Standard Plan, All-employees: If you decide to purchase Zoho One licenses for your entire team, you might be eligible for the Zoho One All-employees price, which is per user per month with annual licensing.
*Zoho One prices do not include VAT.

Team Users: A Quick Overview
Organization vs. Team levels in Zoho CRM
Data in Zoho CRM is organized into data modules, and your CRM users are granted access to the modules relevant to them. Modules and users are available at two levels — the organization (or org) level, and team level.
Org modules
contain data closely tied to your sales processes. The users who work on these modules are org users, and they have full-scale CRM access. This license is ideal for sales reps, sales managers, presales teams, and central CRM admins.
Note: Any mention of just "users" in Zoho CRM refers to this type of user.
Team modules
contain niche data particular to specific teams that support the sales process at various stages. The users who work on these modules are team users. Outside of their team modules, they only have partial access to CRM data and functionality. This license is ideal for all non-sales users, like marketing, support, legal, finance, and others.

If you are a new user, you can sign up for a free trial of Zoho CRM and add team users from the Setup page.
If you are an existing customer, or you're making your first purchase, you can buy team user licenses from the Add-ons page of the Zoho Store.
Note: Teamspaces, team modules, and team users are available only in the new CRM For Everyone UI. If you are an existing customer, please ensure you have switched to the new UI before trying team users.

You are not bound by any contracts or commitments. Zoho CRM is a pay-as-you-go service (monthly or yearly), so you can make changes to your plan whenever you need to.

Of course you can! Log in to Zoho CRM, click the Upgrade link on the Home page, and follow the steps on the Subscription page to switch to a new plan.
