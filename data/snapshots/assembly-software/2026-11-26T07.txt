Frequently Asked Questions
Can we check what the AI produces?
Yes, that's how it's built. Every fact cites back to its source, you can open the exact document behind any sentence in one click, and attorney approval is required before anything goes out. Valuation stays the attorney's call; the AI never estimates a number.
How does migration from a legacy or generalist platform work?
We have proven migration playbooks for the platforms PI firms typically switch from. Your CSM owns the migration end-to-end, including data mapping, document conversion, and team training. Most migrations land in 60 to 90 days.
How does pricing scale as we grow?
Plans are priced per user per month, billed annually: Essentials at $109 and Premium at $139. NeosAI Platinum is quoted to your firm. Demands Plus pricing scales with your caseload.
How is Demands Plus billed?
Demands Plus is billed to the firm, not to the firm's clients. The first time a firm uses Demands Plus on a case, Neos adds a charge record to that case's AI Usage tab. This record shows what Assembly charged the firm, so the firm has an audit trail. It isn't a case expense, it doesn't appear on the Value tab, and nothing is billed to the client. Whether to pass the cost on to a client is the firm's decision under its own fee agreements and ethics obligations.
Is our case data used to train AI models?
No. Prompts and responses aren’t used to train the models, aren’t shared with other customers, and aren’t shared with OpenAI or any other model provider. The information is stored in our US Azure region, encrypted, and processed within Microsoft’s global Azure network.
What is Demands Plus, and who can use it?
Demands Plus builds cited medical chronologies and demand letters inside Neos from the records already in the case. It's available to NeosAI Platinum firms. If you're on Essentials or Premium, your specialist can price the upgrade and Demands Plus together.
