Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
What is included in the subscription fee? Are there any hidden costs?
Your subscription includes full access to MRPeasy’s core functionality based on your chosen plan –
production planning, shop floor control, inventory and warehouse management, purchasing, CRM,
reporting, and built-in integrations.
There are no hidden costs. MRPeasy has no paywalls for any functionality, software updates, support,
or access to learning resources. You are not required to pay extra for mandatory implementation or
training packages to get started. Any optional onboarding services, like training hours or advanced
support, are clearly priced separately.
How does the bulk discount work as my manufacturing team grows?
MRPeasy includes a bulk discount starting from the 11th user in your subscription, to help you scale more affordably. After your team reaches 10 users, adding more users will cost a flat rate of $79/month per 10 new users. This bulk discount applies to every pricing plan.
Example: Let’s say you’re on the Enterprise plan ($99/user) and you add two new users so your team grows from 10 to 12 users. Your first 10 users still cost $99 each ($990). The 2 users you just added are included in a 10-user bundle, which costs $79 in total. This means your total subscription now comes to $1069 ($990 + $79). If you reach 20 users and need to add more, simply add an additional 10-user bundle for another $79, and so on.
Simple. Predictable. Built for growing manufacturers.
How many users do I need, and how do I change my plan later?
MRPeasy is priced per user per month. To control costs, we recommend only providing user seats to employees who actively use the system. Many of our customers start with a small number of users, such as production managers or implementation staff, and expand access as needed. After the first 10 users, each subsequent bundle of 10 users is available at a fixed rate of $79/month.
You can add or remove users and upgrade or downgrade your MRPeasy plan at any time through the
software as your business requirements change.
Is MRPeasy suitable for my business size and type?
MRPeasy is built for small and medium-sized manufacturers and distributors (up to 200 employees). It
combines ease of use with production, inventory, and warehouse management features that SMEs need
the most.
While the software has a manufacturing focus, supporting a wide range of production types such as
make-to-order, make-to-stock, and batch production, MRPeasy is also suitable as a standalone
inventory management solution for e-commerce businesses.
How can I migrate to MRPeasy from my previous ERP system?
Migrating from another ERP system to MRPeasy is straightforward for most data. Simply export your key
records, like inventory items, bills of materials, customers, and vendors, to CSV, which can then be
loaded into MRPeasy via our built-in import tools.
MRPeasy provides import templates and formatting guidelines to streamline this process. Most
customers migrating from previous systems import the bulk of their data within a few days.

How can I get training hours, and how much do they cost?
MRPeasy training sessions focus on helping you learn to use specific MRPeasy features effectively. To
request training, submit a support ticket and specify the topic you’d like to cover.
Training is billed at $150 per hour. We recommend booking 1 hour per topic to allow for a focused session. Once we receive your topic request, we’ll assign the appropriate specialist, issue an invoice, and confirm a training time after payment. Scheduling typically takes 2–3 business days.
