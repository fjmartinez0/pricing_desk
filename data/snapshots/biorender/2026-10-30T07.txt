FAQs
What forms of payment do you accept?
You can use your credit card to pay for any plan. For any multi-user annual license, we also accept purchase orders (PO) and can invoice you annually; please contact us to request a PO or invoice purchase. Monthly subscriptions must be paid via credit card.
Can I use figures that I make on the free plan for publication and/or commercial use?
No, we do not allow figures made on the free plan to be used for publications or commercial use. Please upgrade to one of our subscription plans in order to attain publication/commercial rights for your BioRender figures. For more details on what plans are required for specific use cases, see https://help.biorender.com/en/articles/5898558-where-can-i-use-my-illustrations.
Is there student pricing?
Yes, we offer all students an extra discount. Sign up with your .edu or academic email and your special student price will appear when you go to upgrade. Student pricing is only available to individual students paying out of pocket. If your subscription fees are being reimbursed by your PI or lab, you must purchase an individual or lab plan.
What if I'm an undergrad?
BioRender offers special pricing for verified undergrad students, contact us for more details. Undergrad student pricing is only available to individual students paying out of pocket. If your subscription fees are being reimbursed by your PI or lab, you must purchase an individual or lab plan.
What if I’ve paid for an individual plan, then my organization decides to purchase a team plan?
If your team or organization decides to purchase a team subscription after you’ve already paid for an individual subscription, we will happily refund you for the remainder of your annual individual subscription. Please contact us to arrange a refund.
If I add a team member, will they be able to see all my figures?
Other users cannot see figures you make in BioRender unless you explicitly share your file with them.
What if I work for a nonprofit?
We offer our academic pricing for all registered non-profits.
Should I upgrade my account?
Whether or not you should upgrade to a BioRender subscription depends on you and your team’s particular needs. BioRender Basic is good for simple presentations and for personal and educational use. If you want to publish your figures for commercial use, download your figures in high quality, and/or create more than 5 figures, you’ll need to upgrade your BioRender account.
How do we sign up as a team?
To sign up as a team, simply choose the “Industry team” plan when upgrading your account. Select the number of members you have on your team, purchase the subscription, and then invite others to join your BioRender team via email.
How long is my free trial?
You can try a BioRender paid account free for 14 days. To activate your BioRender paid account, you'll need to enter a credit card. You can cancel at any time during your trial and you won't be charged.
How does BioRender’s pricing work?
When you first sign up, you are automatically on BioRender's Basic, our free plan. You can use BioRender free of charge but there are some feature limitations and you can't use illustrations for publications or commercial purposes.
