AI Deploy
Easily deploy machine learning models and applications to production, create your API access points effortlessly, and make effective predictions.
Find out more
CPUs
AI Deploy deploys your applications or models via Docker on a cluster, which then boots computing nodes (called replicas). Each deployment is defined by a minimum and maximum number of nodes, which you choose. Each node you launch is billed per minute, depending on its lifespan and power. The cluster will meet all your specifications, whether it is fixed or adapts automatically to the workload.
To deploy a model into production, you can get a price estimate in the OVHcloud Control Panel. You have an unlimited number of deployment requests.
Get guaranteed CPU resources that are adapted to your test, pre-production and even basic machine learning phases.
Deploy up to 12 resource units per node. Object Storage is unlimited.

Pricing model
All Public Cloud services are billed based on usage: you only pay for the resources you consume, for the time you actually use them.
You can start and stop your resources at any time, according to your needs, to optimise your consumption.
Most Public Cloud services are billed per second, with a minimum billing duration of one minute per resource. Each month, a single invoice summarises all your usage over the period concerned.
For more information, please refer to the Public Cloud services Specific Terms and Conditions.
