Udemy Business Plans FAQs
What’s the difference between the Udemy Business Team Plan and Enterprise Plan?
The content collection is similar; however, Team Plan is designed for small teams and doesn’t include:
Single Sign-On (SSO) integration
Advanced administrative and user management capabilities
Enterprise system integrations (LMS, HRIS, etc.)
Access to paid add-on features (AI Connectors, AI Role Play, and premium content collections in additional languages such as Japanese or Korean)
What content is included with Udemy Business plans?
Udemy Business provides access to content created by real-world practitioners across AI, cloud computing, cybersecurity, data science, leadership and management, project and product management, software development, and programming.
What features are available for learners and admins in the Enterprise Plan?
The Udemy Business Enterprise Plan offers advanced features designed to develop organization-wide skills at scale with measurable impact. These include:
In-course AI Assistant for personalized learning support
6,000+ AI Role Plays to practice real-world scenarios with interactive, AI-powered simulations
500+ skills assessments to identify gaps and align learning with business goals
Goal-focused learning recommendations tailored to individual needs
Admin recommendations to guide learners at scale with curated content
Skills Mapping and AI-Powered Learning Paths, curated AI paths, and in-course labs for hands-on practice.
Certification prep for over 300 exams
AI-powered coding exercises and practice tests
Create custom content and learning paths
Analytics and adoption reports
Does Udemy Business include AI and generative AI training?
Yes. Udemy Business supports end-to-end AI learning, from foundational concepts to advanced, role-specific, and enterprise transformation topics.
The Team Plan and Enterprise Plan include content beyond AI:
Team Plan: Offers access to 28,000+ courses across business, technology, leadership, and more, along with curated AI and machine learning courses for introductory and intermediate skill levels.
Enterprise Plan: Provides full access to the entire Udemy Business catalog, including 30,000+ courses spanning AI, business, technology, leadership, compliance, and more. This plan also includes advanced features such as multilingual content, teach functionality for creating custom content, assessments, customizable learning paths, and advanced analytics.
These plans are ideal for organizations seeking a comprehensive learning solution that goes beyond AI to address a wide range of skill development needs.
For organizations focused solely on AI learning, Udemy Business offers two tailored base plans:
AI Readiness Plan: Designed for foundational AI literacy across broad workforces, this plan includes a curated collection of 45-50 AI courses (available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, and Japanese), AI topic assessments (new in Q2 2026!), and starter learning paths. Ideal for large groups, the plan requires a minimum of 100 seats.
AI Growth Plan: Focused on advanced AI development for targeted teams, this plan features a larger course collection, curated role-specific and technology paths, in-course AI topic assessments (new in Q2, 2026!), and hands-on practice opportunities. With multi-language support across 14 languages, it’s designed for smaller, focused cohorts with a minimum of 21 seats.
Both AI-specific plans are designed to deliver fast, focused AI learning outcomes, while the Team and Enterprise Plans provide a broader, organization-wide learning solution that includes AI and much more.
What analytics and reporting does Udemy Business provide?
Udemy Business offers analytics to track learner adoption, engagement, and skill development.
The Enterprise Plan unlocks advanced dashboards, skill insights, course performance data, and reporting for labs and assessments (with the Udemy Business Pro add-on), helping organizations measure real learning impact.
Can Udemy Business integrate with our LMS or HR systems?
Yes, the Udemy Business Enterprise Plan supports single sign-on (SSO), LMS and LXP integrations, APIs (user, course, and reporting), and workplace tools such as Slack.
We also offer AI Connectors as an add-on to Enterprise Plan customers.
As an early adopter of the Model Context Protocol (MCP), AI Connectors enable AI agents to work together, delivering personalized learning experiences in the flow of work, tailored to your specific business context and data.
These integrations allow learning to fit seamlessly into existing systems and workflows and are only available to Enterprise Plan customers.
Can employees learn in languages other than English?
Yes. Enterprise Plan customers get access to a multi-language course collection with 53% of the courses in non-English languages, including Arabic, English, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Mandarin, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Turkish, and Vietnamese.
Japanese and Korean collections are available as premium add-ons for an extra charge.
This enables global teams to learn in their local language while maintaining consistent skill standards.
What are the key add-ons available for Udemy Business?
Udemy Business Pro: Adds labs, assessments, and advanced reporting for hands-on learning.
AI Role Play Enhanced: Provides advanced, hands-on AI role-play scenarios and simulations for interactive practice and realistic skill application.
AI Connectors: Enables integrations for hands-on AI practice, linking learning labs to external tools, data, or customer systems for realistic exercises.
Premium Language Packs: Offers additional language collections, such as Japanese and Korean, as paid add-ons.
​​Add-ons are available for an additional fee and are not included in the base plan price.
Is there a free trial or demo available?
Yes. The Team plan includes a free trial so organizations can explore the platform before purchasing.
For Enterprise plans, you can request a personalized demo tailored to your organization’s learning and AI transformation goals.
Does Udemy Business offer nonprofit pricing?
Yes. Registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organizations can request special nonprofit pricing for Udemy Business plans.
Contact the Udemy Business team to learn more about eligibility and available options.
What is Premium Support, and who is it best for?
Premium Support is a paid add-on for Enterprise customers, providing 24/7 SLA-backed support with priority escalations and a dedicated team, including a Technical Account Manager (TAM) and Customer Success Manager (CSM).
It’s ideal for large or distributed enterprises, regulated industries, or organizations with complex integrations, high learner volumes, or mission-critical learning needs that require faster response times and proactive technical partnership.
