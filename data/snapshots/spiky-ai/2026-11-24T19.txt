FAQ
Questions about plans and billing.
How much does Spiky cost?
Spiky Pulse has a Free plan at $0. Paid plans are Plus at $15, Pro at $24 and Premium at $40 per user per month on annual billing, or $18, $29 and $49 billed monthly. Enterprise pricing is custom. Spiky Whisper and Spiky Signals are quoted by our sales team.
Is there a free version of Spiky?
Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and covers 3 meetings per user per month, with Spiky Scribe, Ask Scribe, transcription, meeting summarization and topic detection.
What does a trial include?
Trials are equivalent to the Premium plan, excluding CRM functionality. No credit card is required to start one.
How much do we save with annual billing?
Up to 18%. Premium, for example, is $40 per user per month billed annually against $49 billed monthly.
How much does Spiky Whisper cost?
Whisper is quoted by our sales team. Whisper and Spiky Pulse can be purchased separately, and the right setup depends on your use case, so talk to your sales representative. You use Pulse to configure Whisper.
What is the difference between real-time EQ coaching and Whisper?
Real-time EQ coaching runs in the meeting panel and is part of the Pulse plans: monologue tracking and participant insights on every plan, with call momentum, playbook tracking and battlecards on Premium. Whisper is a separate product that runs in the desktop app on macOS and Windows, with no bot in the meeting.
Can Spiky record meetings without a bot?
Yes. Bot-free recording with the Spiky desktop app is included in the Plus, Pro and Premium plans, so nothing joins the meeting. Spiky Whisper, quoted separately, adds live coaching during the call.
How is Spiky Signals priced?
Signals pricing depends on factors such as the number of users and reporting frequency, and is offered with an annual commitment, payable monthly. Your sales representative can share a tailored quote.
Does Signals replace Gong?
No. Signals is not a recorder and does not replace Gong or similar tools. It reads recordings from Gong, Chorus, Read.ai, Otter, Fireflies and Spiky Pulse, and analyzes them alongside your CRM outcomes.
What is a quota?
A quota is the allowance on what each user can do. Users on the Free plan can analyze 3 meetings per month per license, and users on the Pro plan can analyze up to 30.
What are seats?
A seat is one user account with access to Spiky. All seats in the same organization must be on the same plan; you cannot have one seat on Pro and another on Premium.
Which plan includes CRM integration?
CRM and Slack integrations start on the Pro plan. Custom-field CRM integration is on Premium.
What does Enterprise add to Premium?
A 1 working day SLA, priority support (7-day support through Slack, in a dedicated Slack channel) and API access, along with 5+ passive seats and customization options. Enterprise pricing is set with your team.
What are the payment options?
Monthly subscriptions or discounted yearly subscriptions. For help choosing, write to sales@spiky.ai.
Can we change plans later?
Yes. Talk to the team and we will move you to the plan that fits.
How does Spiky store our data?
Spiky is compliant with GDPR and KVKK. Recordings and all other data, such as transcripts, are stored in Amazon data centres and encrypted at rest and in transit.
Are on-premise deployments and role-play on this price list?
No. On-premise deployments are priced as a custom offer after a technical scoping, and role-play programs are scoped with your team. Book a demo for a quote.
