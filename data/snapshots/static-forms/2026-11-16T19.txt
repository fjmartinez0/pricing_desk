What happens when you hit your limit
Most form backends fail silently or bill you quietly. We do neither.
01
Heads-up, early
While form submission notifications and usage alerts are on, we email you at 80% and 90% of your quota on paid plans, and once at 80% on Free.
02
You always know
Past the quota, Free and Starter hold new submissions in your inbox instead of rejecting them, and nothing is billed. We never bill you by surprise to keep delivering.
03
Busy month? Opt in to overage
Pro and Agency can keep accepting submissions at $1.00 per 1,000 extra submissions, billed monthly. Off by default. Free and Starter hold them instead.

Frequently asked questions
Do I need a credit card to start the free trial?
No. Start your 7-day free trial without entering any payment information. We only ask for payment details when you decide to continue after the trial.
How does the 7-day free trial work?
The trial opens up the Pro or Agency feature set — integrations, form rules, custom sending domain, branding removal and the rest — on a 500-submission trial quota rather than the full plan quota. Agent access, autoresponder emails, AI Reply and CC recipients wait until you subscribe. No credit card required to start. At the end of your trial you can subscribe to continue, or your account reverts to the Free plan. Starter has no trial: at $6 a month it is priced to start on day one.
Is the Free plan really free forever?
Yes. The Free plan is a real plan, not a trial: 250 submissions every 30 days, with no end date and no credit card. Paid plans exist for when you need more volume or power features, not to unlock the basics.
What is the difference between Starter and Pro?
Starter ($6/month) covers everything a single site or freelance practice needs: 3,000 submissions a month, unlimited forms, file uploads, autoresponders, AI Reply, advanced CAPTCHA, custom webhooks, and Slack, Telegram and Discord alerts. Pro ($9/month) adds 25,000 submissions a month, custom email templates, the Google Sheets, Notion, Airtable and Mailchimp syncs, the native Zapier app, conditional form rules, a custom sending domain, branding removal, CC recipients, up to 3 team members, and optional overage.
Can I connect ChatGPT, Claude or another agent?
Yes, on a paid Starter, Pro or Agency workspace. Add the MCP URL from Agent access in your provider settings and authorize one workspace through OAuth; all forms you can currently access are included automatically. Compatible clients do not need manual client keys. Configuration, form management and optional submission reads start on Starter; rule changes require Pro or Agency. Supported workspace settings and Pro or Agency owner team administration are in staging preview and are not available in production yet. Agent access is unavailable on Free and trials. Your provider must support remote MCP and OAuth, and may require a particular plan or administrator permission.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan anytime?
Yes, from the Stripe billing portal in your account. Plan switches take effect immediately in both directions: an upgrade opens the new features right away, and a downgrade drops you to the cheaper plan there and then. Stripe invoices or credits the unused part of your cycle, so you only pay for what you used. Cancelling is different — you keep your plan until the end of the period you have already paid for.
What happens if I exceed my submission limit?
While form submission notifications and usage alerts are on, we email you at 80% and 90% of your quota on paid plans, and once at 80% on Free, so the limit does not arrive unannounced. On Free and Starter, submissions past the quota are held in your inbox, not rejected: the visitor still sees success, but nothing is emailed or forwarded and the contents stay hidden. Up to 100 are held per period; after that new submissions get a 429. They are released when your quota resets or you move to a plan that covers them, and you deliver them from your inbox. On Pro and Agency you can opt in to overage billing so submissions keep flowing; without it, and during a trial, new submissions are rejected with a 429. You can upgrade at any time, or wait for your quota to reset: after each 30-day period on Free, with your billing period on monthly plans, or every 30 days on annual plans.
Is there a money-back guarantee?
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied with our service within the first 30 days of your paid subscription, contact us for a full refund, no questions asked.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, cancel anytime from your account settings. Your subscription remains active until the end of your current billing period, and you will not be charged again.
