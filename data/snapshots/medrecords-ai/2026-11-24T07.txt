How Team works.
1 workspace for the whole organization, at the same page rates as Self-Service. Each person has their own login, and the owner sees every case, every credit and every action.

1 balance, with limits

FAQ
What buyers and security reviews ask.
Is there a cheaper tier, or a minimum?
Yes, in bulk, and there is no minimum on Self-Service or Team. Both are the full platform at 10¢ per deduplicated page, 8¢ at 100,000 credits and 5¢ at 500,000. 1 credit is 1 page. A report is $10 (100 credits). A 40-page file bills $4.
What counts as a page, and how do duplicates work?
You are billed per deduplicated page. Exact duplicates, which are common when the same visit arrives from 3 providers, are detected before billing and never charged.
What is the Team plan, and who is it for?
Team is 1 workspace for an organization where several people work the same files: hospitals, clinics, IME and QME practices, paralegal teams and medical-legal BPOs. Each person has their own login with 2-step sign-in, cases belong to the organization, and 1 credit balance pays for everyone.
How is Team priced? Is there a per-seat fee?
No per-seat fee and no setup fee. Team uses the same page rates as Self-Service, 10¢ per deduplicated page down to 5¢ at 500,000 credits, paid from 1 shared balance. Case Q&A on Team is 1 credit per question. Credits never expire.
Who can see a case on Team?
The owner, admins and case managers see every case. Members work only the cases they create or are added to, with edit or view-only access set per case, and the activity log shows who opened, changed and exported each one.
Can we cap what each person spends?
Yes. The owner or an admin sets a monthly credit limit per person or for the whole team, and reads usage by person or by case for each month.
Is anything held back for a higher plan?
No. Every deliverable and capability is on all 3 plans, including Steer and cross-examination. Team adds people, roles, case assignment, spending limits and the activity log. Enterprise On-Prem adds the deployment inside your network, SAML 2.0 SSO and SCIM, claims-system integration, a dedicated CSM with SLA-backed response, and a quarterly security review.
Can Medrecords run without our records leaving our network?
Yes. On Enterprise On-Prem the full stack runs inside your own infrastructure or private cloud, with an air-gap option, so records never leave your network. On Self-Service and Team, records run in our HIPAA-compliant cloud under our BAA.
Do you sign a BAA?
Yes. Self-Service is covered by our BAA at signup, and on Team the BAA is signed with the workspace owner at setup, with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit. An on-prem deployment keeps PHI inside your own network, so there is no transit for us to cover, and a BAA or DPA is available on request.
Is our data used to train your models?
Never. It is contractual, in the BAA, not a settings toggle. On-prem deployments send no telemetry and make no callbacks.
How is this different from human-review services and other AI platforms?
Human-QA vendors sample. Medrecords machine-verifies every extraction on every page, with deterministic citation verification plus an independent cross-model check, at page 1 and at page 10,000. Most AI platforms also require your records to transit their cloud; here you can run the full stack inside your own. We help your experts make the call. The last word is theirs.
How long does setup take, and does IT need to be involved?
Self-Service access opens within 1 business day of your demo, with no IT ticket. A Team workspace opens after your demo: the owner is set up, then invites everyone else by email, and we walk your admins through roles. Enterprise On-Prem takes about 4 weeks, scoped with your infra team, with SSO and API setup as part of the deployment.
Can we see output on our own file before buying?
Yes, on any plan. Book a demo, then run a free trial on 1 of your own cases, up to 1,000 pages. Pick your hairiest records. You get a cited chronology and summary back, handled under our BAA, with no card and no commitment. A follow-up meeting then goes over your results.
