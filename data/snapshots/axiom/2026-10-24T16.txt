SPEND AND OVERAGES
How does the Always Free allowance work?
Every organization gets a free allowance each month, and you only pay for usage above it.
On Personal, that's 500 GB of data loading compute, 10 GB-hours of query compute and 25 GB of storage, with no credit card needed. On Axiom Cloud, it's 1 TB of data loading compute, 100 GB-hours of query compute and 100 GB of storage, included in the $25 monthly platform fee.
What happens when usage exceeds the Always Free allowance?
On Axiom Cloud, you pay as you go for anything above the allowance. There's no separate overage rate: extra usage is billed at the normal rates, and those rates get lower as your monthly volume grows.
Can I set a spending limit?
Yes. You can set a spending limit and spend alerts in the Console. If you reach the limit, usage beyond it is paused rather than billed.
How do volume discounts work?
They apply automatically. Data loading compute and query compute are priced in tiers, and each tier is cheaper per unit than the one before, so the more you use in a month, the less each extra GB or GB-hour costs. There's nothing to negotiate or request. The rates are in the next answer.
What do data loading compute, query compute and storage cost?
Data loading compute and query compute are billed in compute credits, at $1 per compute credit if you pay as you go. Rates are graduated: each rate applies only to the usage within its tier. Each month, the first 1,000 GB of data loading compute and 100 GB-hours of query compute are free.
Storage is charged on compressed data, which moves to cheaper tiers as it ages. The first 100 GB is free.

PLAN FIT
What's the difference between Personal and Axiom Cloud?
Personal is for individual developers and small projects. It's free, needs no credit card, and keeps data for 30 days.
Axiom Cloud is for teams. It costs $25 a month plus usage, comes with a larger free allowance, lets you choose how long to keep data, and lets you turn on paid add-ons yourself in the Console. Both run on the same platform.
Can I pre-purchase compute credits, or is everything pay as you go?
Both. Pay as you go is the default. If you prefer, you can commit to a yearly volume of compute credits for 12, 24 or 36 months and pay less per compute credit. To find your rate in the table below, pick the row for the compute credits you'll use each year, then the column for how long you commit. For a 24- or 36-month commitment, you can pay the whole amount upfront or one year at a time. The rate is the same either way.

Can I self-serve add-ons without a sales call?
Yes. Once you're on Axiom Cloud, you can turn on SSO, RBAC, Directory Sync and Extended Audit Logs yourself in the Console.
CUSTOM AND PROCUREMENT
Do you support AWS Marketplace billing?
Yes. You can pre-purchase compute credits through AWS Marketplace.
Can I get invoice or PO-based billing?
Yes. When you pre-purchase compute credits on Axiom Cloud, you can pay for them by invoice or purchase order. The platform fee, storage and any add-ons are invoiced monthly.
Do you offer migration and services support?
Yes. Partners like 4Data can take on professional services engagements, including migrations. Axiom's own team also provides support directly.
SPECIFIC FEATURES
Are MCP server access and the SRE skill priced separately?
No. The MCP server, SRE skill and Metrics skill are included with Axiom Cloud at no extra cost, and agents have no separate rate limits. Queries run by agents count toward your query compute in exactly the same way as queries run by people.
What's the data-residency story?
You can keep your data in the region you choose. Axiom's edge architecture also lets you write data to several regions from a single organization.
