Questions & Answers
How much does Qonversion cost?
Pro is free while your MTR is at or under $7,000 a month — no credit card, no trial clock, every feature included. Once you pass $7K, you pay 0.8% of your tracked revenue. No seat fees, no per-app fees, no feature tiers, no hidden fees — the price is based solely on tracked revenue.
What happens when I cross $7K MTR?
Your bill becomes 0.8% of your total tracked revenue — not just the amount above $7K. At $8K MTR that's $64 a month; at $50K it's $400. We email you as you approach the threshold and show a bill forecast in the dashboard, so the first invoice is never a surprise.
What if my revenue drops back below $7K?
Your bill follows your revenue. Pricing is calculated monthly: any month you finish at or under $7K MTR is free, regardless of what previous months looked like.
What is MTR?
MTR stands for Monthly Tracked Revenue. It's the total revenue processed through Qonversion's SDK in a calendar month. This includes all subscription purchases, renewals, and one-time purchases tracked by our system.
Is everything really included?
Yes. Analytics with LTV and cohorts, A/B experiments, the No-Code Paywall Builder, Refund Keeper, every integration, raw data export, unlimited apps and seats — all included, from your first day. Enterprise adds services, not features: custom contracts, an uptime SLA, a Customer Success Manager, and a dedicated onboarding engineer.
How does Qonversion handle data privacy and security?
Qonversion is fully compliant with GDPR and CCPA. We use industry-standard encryption, secure data centers, and strict access controls. Your user data is never sold or shared with third parties.
What if I need an integration that's not available?
You can use Webhooks to connect with virtually any service, and Enterprise customers can request custom integrations. We're also constantly adding new integrations based on customer feedback.
