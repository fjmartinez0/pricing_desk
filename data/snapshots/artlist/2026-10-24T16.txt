Frequently asked questions
How do AI credits work?
Your credits renew every month and can be used across all AI tools in your plan. If you run out, you can get more at any time. On a Business plan? You can choose a custom credit amount to match your needs.
Can I try Artlist for free?
Yes! You can generate free voiceovers, images, a video, and music to get a sample of Artlist’s AI offering.
Which AI models does Artlist offer?
You’ll always get the best AI image, video, voiceover, and music generators available. When a model update is released, it will automatically appear on Artlist.
What is the difference between the AI plans?
With all AI plans, you can choose the number of monthly credits that best fits your project needs.
AI Starter: Starts at 7,500 credits per month, which only includes AI voiceover generation. From 16,500 credits per month, you get AI voiceover, video, image, music generation, and Artlist Studio access.
AI Creator, AI Professional and AI Pro Plus: Includes access to all AI Tools (video, image, voiceover, and music generation), plus Artlist Studio for advanced end-to-end production. You can choose between 80,000 to 4,000,000 credits per month. Includes unlimited generations on certain models.
Max: Includes everything Artlist has to offer — stock assets and AI tools. You can choose between 7,500 to 120,000 credits per month. Includes unlimited generations on certain models.
Max Business: Built for businesses with 50+ employees and agencies of any size that want a ready-to-use solution with access to all of the AI products, stock, and a commercial license.
Artlist Enterprise: Made for global teams that need a customized solution with AI products, stock, scalable features, a tailored enterprise license, and advanced security and control.
Talk to an Enterprise expert to get a custom plan tailored to your needs.

What are the benefits of an annual plan?
An annual plan gives you more for less. Get all the newest models automatically added to your plan. You’ll also save money and get more credits to ensure you have everything you need to create, all year long.
How does Unlimited work?
Unlimited lets you generate content on select leading models without using your credits. It’s designed to help you stay in your creative flow and iterate freely.
Generation speed: You get a set number of fast generations each month. Once those are used, you still have unlimited access — generations simply process at a standard speed.
Using credits: If you need a speed boost, you can toggle off Unlimited to use your credits for fast processing.
Parallel generations: When using Unlimited, your plan includes a limit on how many assets you can generate at the same time.
Models and settings: Unlimited is available for certain models and settings. To use others, simply switch back to using your credits.
Fair use: Unlimited is for human use only. To keep the platform fast for everyone, we don't permit automation, scraping or account sharing.
What are fast generations?
With Unlimited, you get a set number of fast, top-priority generations each month. Once these are used, you can still create with Unlimited — generations simply process at a standard speed. Your monthly amount depends on your plan (for example, 4,000 fast generations with AI Professional).
What are parallel generations?
Parallel generations are the number of assets you can create at the same time. For example, if your plan includes 10 parallel generations, you can have 10 different assets generating simultaneously (e.g. 3 videos and 7 images). To speed up your workflow and create even more assets at once, you can upgrade your plan at any time.
