Can I try Fill before committing to a paid plan?
Yes! Fill offers a free trial on all plans—Basic, Plus, and Pro—so you can explore the platform and its features at no cost. Whether you want to test eSignature requests, create reusable templates, or try custom branding, the free trial lets you experience Fill’s full capabilities. Start your free trial today!
How does Fill handle healthcare compliance?
Our Pro plan includes full HIPAA compliance with a BAA at no extra cost. Unlike competitors who charge premium rates for healthcare features, we've built compliance into our core platform specifically for regulated industries.
How does Fill protect our sensitive documents?
Every plan includes end-to-end encryption, detailed activity logs, and secure cloud storage. Pro plans add specialized compliance features like HIPAA protection. Our security infrastructure was built specifically for industries with strict regulatory requirements.
Can Fill connect with our existing systems?
Yes. Pro plans integrate seamlessly with Google Workspace, Dropbox, and thousands of apps through Zapier. You also get API access for custom connections to your specific business tools.
Does Fill work on all our devices?
Yes. Access Fill through any web browser or our dedicated mobile app to create, send, and track documents from wherever you work.
What support options are available?
All plans include self-service help resources and email support. Pro plans get priority assistance with faster response times from specialists who understand compliance requirements.
