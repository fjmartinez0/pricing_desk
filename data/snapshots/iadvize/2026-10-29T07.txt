Frequently Asked Questions
Find quick answers about plans, pricing, and what’s included.
Book a demo
What is counted as a conversation?
A conversation is counted when there’s at least one message from the shopper and one response from the AI Shopping Assistant. One conversation can span discovery, consideration, and checkout—capturing the full buyer journey in a single, value-generating interaction.
What is a SKU?
SKU stands for Stock Keeping Unit—a unique identifier for each product in your catalog. Our AI Shopping Assistant uses your SKUs to recommend relevant products and personalize responses at scale, helping shoppers find exactly what they need.
Why is pricing based on SKUs and conversations?
Because we price based on value and usage—not seats or sessions. Conversations represent the number of shopper journeys we guide from intent to action. SKUs reflect the range of products we help you sell through personalized AI answers and recommendations. This model ensures you only pay for what delivers ROI.
What happens if I exceed the conversation or SKU limits in my plan?
No worries — you’ll be notified before reaching the limit. Our team will help you explore the best upgrade path or assess your needs for a custom enterprise plan.
