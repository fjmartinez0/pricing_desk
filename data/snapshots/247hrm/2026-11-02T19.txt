Costs explained
What does HRMS software cost in India?
Prices updated 28 October 2026
The HRMS software price in India is usually quoted in two parts: a monthly base subscription for the platform, and a per-employee charge that grows with headcount. If you are comparing HR software pricing across vendors, those are the two numbers to line up. 247HRM uses the same structure. The Core plan starts at ₹5,999 per month and covers up to 50 employees, then ₹99 per employee per month above that. Professional starts at ₹8,999 per month with ₹159 per employee, and Premium at ₹11,999 per month with ₹199 per employee. Paying for a year upfront takes 15% off both the base and the per-employee rate. Teams under 30 people can start on a fixed-term launch pack instead: ₹36,000 for six months on Startup Lite, or ₹60,000 on Startup Premium. Both packs include payroll operations assistance. Every amount on this page is exclusive of GST.

How is a 247HRM quote calculated?
A quote has three parts. First, the base subscription for the plan you pick, which covers up to 50 employees. Second, a per-employee charge for every employee above 50. Third, any add-on modules you choose, such as recruitment at ₹3,500 per 50 employees, timesheets at ₹100 per employee per month, or geo-fencing at ₹50 per employee per month. Take a 120-person company on Professional. The base is ₹8,999, and the 70 employees above the first 50 are charged at ₹159 each, which is ₹11,130. That is ₹20,129 a month before GST. Paying for 12 months upfront drops the same setup to a ₹7,649 base plus ₹9,450 for the extra employees, or ₹17,099 a month. Implementation, training, support and product updates are part of the subscription, not separate line items.
What is already included in the price?
Payroll software, statutory processing, implementation and support are part of the subscription. These are the statutory rules the payroll engine is built against.
Provident fund processing follows the contribution and filing rules published by the Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation.
Employee state insurance follows the rules published by the Employees’ State Insurance Corporation.
Tax deducted at source is computed against the slabs and forms published by the Income Tax Department.
Professional tax and labour welfare fund are applied per state, and GST on your subscription is invoiced at the rate applicable under the Goods and Services Tax regime.
Implementation, configuration, training, ongoing support and product updates are included in every plan, not quoted as extras.
FAQ
HRMS Software Pricing FAQs
All costs shown upfront. or at renewal.
What does the per-employee price include?+
The per-employee charge covers the full plan you choose (Core, Professional or Premium) including ongoing platform updates, mobile ESS, statutory reports and support. Specialized modules and payroll operations assistance are priced separately so you only pay for what you use.
How is annual billing different from monthly?+
Annual billing pays for 12 months upfront and applies a 15% discount across the base subscription and per-employee fee. You can still scale headcount mid-cycle; overage is billed at the discounted annual rate.
Can we mix Startup and Regular plans?+
Startup launch packs are designed for early-stage teams up to 30 employees. At employee 31 the account transitions to a regular plan with no migration fee. Payroll operations assistance can continue as an add-on either way.
Is GST included in the listed price?+
No. All listed amounts are exclusive of GST. The applicable GST is added at invoicing based on your billing state and entity type.
Do you support multi-entity payroll?+
Yes. Unlimited group companies and locations are included on every plan. Entity-level chart of accounts, statutory IDs, pay calendars and approval flows are configured during managed implementation.
What happens to historic data when we migrate?+
Standard template-based migration is included where applicable. Complex historical migrations (multi-year payslips, ledger reconciliation, statutory continuity) are scoped separately and quoted upfront before any work begins.
How quickly can we go live?+
SME customers can go live quickly with minimal setup time. Enterprise rollouts are tailored based on your entities, locations, shifts, statutory scope and integrations.
