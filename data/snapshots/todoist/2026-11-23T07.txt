Frequently asked questions
How much does Todoist cost?
The Beginner plan is free. Pro is US $5/month billed yearly (US$60/year), or US$7 billed monthly. Business is US$8 per user/month billed yearly (US$96 per user/year), or US$10 per user billed monthly, plus local tax. View prices in your preferred currency at the top of this page.
What plan should I choose for a team of two?
Two Pro plans give you the ability to invite each other into up to 5 team projects, assign tasks, dates, and deadlines, and comment as work happens.
The Business plan gives you admin controls, central team billing, up to 500 team projects, and additional capabilities like the People tab and advanced project insights so you can quickly see where things stand across your team.
Still deciding? Try Business free for 14 days and pick the plan that fits how you work.
How does Business plan pricing work? Do I pay for everyone on the team?
You pay for each member of the team. You can invite up to 1,000 guests – people outside your organization you invite to specific projects – for free. Every paying member also gets Pro features in their personal projects at no extra cost. If you add a member mid-cycle, you only pay a prorated amount for the rest of your billing period.
What would a team of 10 pay to manage shared tasks in Todoist?
US $80/month on yearly billing (US $960 for the year), or US $100/month on monthly billing, plus local tax. Guests you invite to projects are free, and all 10 members get Pro features for their personal projects, too.
Will I get notice before my price changes?
Yes. We let you know before any changes are applied at your next renewal.
Is the Beginner plan really free?
Yes. You get 5 personal projects, task reminders, recurring tasks, 10 AI-powered task capture sessions/month, integrations including Todoist’s ChatGPT and Claude connectors, and access across all your devices.
Are reminders included on the Beginner plan?
Yes. Any task with a date and time gets an automatic reminder. Pro and Business add custom reminders, which allow you to set reminders at specific times, at a location, or on repeat.
When does it make sense to move from the Beginner to Pro plan?
Usually when you reach a limit, like five personal projects or three filter views. Or when you want to plan your time, not only your tasks, with calendar layout, task durations, deadlines, and custom reminders. Pro also includes unlimited Ramble and task capture (fair use limits apply), full reporting history, and Todoist Automations during the public beta. You can try Pro free for 7 days.
How does Todoist’s price compare with heavier project management tools?
Todoist is a shared task system that moves your team’s work forward, so you’re not paying for someone to babysit the project management system itself. Getting started is free: on the Beginner plan (free), you can create a team, invite up to 1,000 people to it, and collaborate on up to five team projects.
With heavier tools, the expensive part is the seats you keep paying for after teammates stop using it. Todoist is the tool your team will actually stick with. Teammates add and complete tasks from anywhere, routine work is put on autopilot with clear owners, and you can see where things stand without sending a single follow up.
Can I try a paid plan?
Yes. Pro comes with a 7-day free trial, and Business comes with a 14-day free trial.
You can also create a free team on the Beginner and Pro plans so you can see how Todoist works as a shared task system for your team before you upgrade to the Business plan.
Can I cancel or downgrade any time?
Yes. Cancel from your billing settings whenever you like, and you’ll keep your paid features until the end of your billing period. All your data stays – projects above your new plan’s limits become view-only until you upgrade again or archive a few. Yearly subscriptions can be refunded within 30 days of purchase or renewal.
