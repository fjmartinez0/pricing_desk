FAQs
How much does Wingspan cost?
Workforce management is $10 per Monthly Active Payee (MAP) each month for contractors and $20 per employee each month for seasonal, part-time, and temporary W-⁠2 employees. If you just need payouts, you pay per transaction. There is no fee per worker, and you pay for each payout and service you use. Add-ons work with both.
Which plan is right for me?
Workforce management is a good fit if you pay workers often or have many onboarding steps for each worker. Each worker counts one time per month, so your cost stays flat. Just need payouts is a good fit if your team already budgets per payment, like with a payment processor. We can quote both for you.
What counts as a Monthly Active Payee (MAP)?
A MAP is a worker with any activity in the month. The monthly price includes worker account setup, tax ID verification, document e-signing, payouts in every currency, the payout volume fee, 1099 filing, invoicing to your clients, the Wingspan Wallet and Standard support. Each worker counts one time per month, even with many events.
Counts as a MAP: a tax or identity check, a background check, an insurance or other document check, a completed e-signature, a 1099 filing, and a payment.
Does not add a MAP: an invite that the worker never acts on, a first payment within two months of onboarding, and a 1099 for a worker that you already paid that year.
Example: a worker completes a tax check and gets paid three times in March. That worker is one MAP for March.
We onboard many workers, but only some of them get work each month. Do you charge for everyone we onboard?
No. With workforce management, we only charge for Monthly Active Payees. A worker who gets an invite but never starts onboarding does not count. A first payment within two months of onboarding does not add another MAP. With pay per transaction, you pay for the payouts and services you use.
How often can I pay workers?
You can pay daily, weekly, or twice a month. With workforce management, more payments do not cost more, because each worker counts one time per month. With pay per transaction, you pay for each payout.
Does filing a 1099 cost extra?
Not with workforce management. If you paid a worker on Wingspan during the tax year, their 1099 filing is included in the MAP fee and does not add another MAP. With pay per transaction, a 1099 filing is $2.00.
How do you price international contractors and W-⁠2 employees?
International contractors use the same plan that you choose for US contractors. With workforce management, payouts in every currency are included. With pay per transaction, a standard payout in USD or CAD is $0.50, a standard payout in tier 1 currencies is $1.00, and a standard payout in all other supported currencies is $2.50. Seasonal, part-time, and temporary W-⁠2 employees cost $20 per active employee, per month. W-⁠2 payroll is available in the US only.
Can I keep my own background check provider?
Yes. Wingspan works with the tools you already use, including your background check provider.
Why do some lines say Contact sales for price?
Those prices depend on your volume, your workers, and your setup. Talk to us and we will quote them.
