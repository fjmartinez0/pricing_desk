FAQs
Can I buy HiveMQ online without talking to sales?
Yes. You can buy HiveMQ Launch and Run online with transparent monthly pricing and direct online checkout. For larger deployments or custom commercial terms, contact HiveMQ for an Enterprise quote.
How do Launch and Run compare with Self Managed Enterprise?
HiveMQ Launch and Run plans are fixed packages with monthly fixed pricing, designed for fast deployment and simplified purchasing, and are for a single broker deployment. HiveMQ Self Managed Enterprise plan is designed for larger or more complex deployments that need custom sizing, more flexibility, or additional support options.
How is pricing calculated for Self Managed deployments?
HiveMQ Launch and Run plans are packaged into fixed monthly tiers so you can get started without custom sizing. Enterprise pricing is based on capacity, primarily concurrent connections and message throughput.This gives smaller teams a simple self-service path, while larger deployments can be priced based on their specific requirements.
How does HiveMQ pricing compare to other MQTT brokers and industrial data platforms?
HiveMQ pricing is based on capacity, primarily connections and message throughput, rather than fixed infrastructure-only limits. This approach aligns cost with actual usage and helps teams scale deployments without redesigning their architecture as demand grows.
How do I size my deployment or choose the right plan?
Sizing depends on concurrent connections and message throughput. If you are unsure, you can start with Launch or Run or contact HiveMQ for a tailored recommendation. Launch is a good fit for earlier or lower-throughput production workloads, while Run is better suited for steadier production workloads that need higher throughput and customer support.
Can I get predictable pricing as I scale?
Yes. HiveMQ Launch and Run plans provide fixed monthly pricing. Enterprise pricing is based on capacity and can be structured with defined commercial terms as your deployment grows. This gives you a predictable starting point today, with a clear path forward if your requirements expand over time.
What pricing model fits my use case?
HiveMQ Launch and Run plans are a good fit when you want a simple and predictable path to production. Whereas Self Managed Enterprise plan is suited for larger deployments or environments that require more flexibility in scaling and support.
Can HiveMQ self-managed pricing be customized?
HiveMQ Self-Managed Enterprise pricing can be tailored based on capacity and commercial requirements. Please contact us to discuss an Enterprise option.
Is HiveMQ self-managed pricing plans a good fit for high volume MQTT workloads?
Yes. HiveMQ is designed to handle high throughput and large numbers of concurrent connections in production environments. Pricing scales based on capacity, which allows you to plan for high volume workloads without changing pricing models as you grow.
FAQs
Which HiveMQ Cloud plan should I choose?
HiveMQ Starter plan is suited for testing and small scale production workloads. HiveMQ fully-managed Enterprise plan is designed for business critical systems that require scale, customization, and defined support levels. For a free self-hosted option, explore Lab.
How does HiveMQ Cloud pricing compare to other MQTT solutions?
HiveMQ Cloud fully-managed MQTT platform offers multiple pricing models depending on your stage. The HiveMQ Cloud Starter plan scales with usage. The HiveMQ Cloud fully-managed Enterprise plan supports capacity based pricing for larger workloads.
How is pricing calculated in HiveMQ Cloud?
Pricing depends on the plan. HiveMQ Cloud Starter uses a usage based model with runtime and message volume. HiveMQ Enterprise is priced based on capacity and requires a custom quote.
How does pricing scale as my usage grows?
Scaling depends on the plan. HiveMQ Starter plan scales with runtime and message volume. HiveMQ Cloud fully-managed Enterprise plan scales based on capacity and deployment requirements.
Can I get predictable or fixed pricing in the HiveMQ Cloud?
The HiveMQ Cloud Starter uses a usage-based pricing model. The HiveMQ Cloud Enterprise uses custom pricing, which is better suited when you need more predictable commercial structure at scale.
Which HiveMQ Cloud plans include the Broker REST API?
The Broker REST API is available with Starter and Cloud Enterprise.
What support levels are available for HiveMQ fully-managed plans?
Support varies by plan. HiveMQ Cloud Starter includes Silver support with a 99.95 percent uptime SLA. HiveMQ Cloud Enterprise includes Silver, Gold, or Platinum support with higher uptime SLAs.
Can pricing be customized for HiveMQ Cloud Enterprise use?
Yes. Enterprise plans can be tailored based on capacity, scale, and commercial requirements.
Which MQTT Cloud pricing model works best for high throughput use cases?
For high throughput workloads, pricing models that scale with capacity tend to be more predictable than purely usage based models. In HiveMQ Cloud, Enterprise plans are designed for these scenarios, where consistent performance and predictable scaling are required.
