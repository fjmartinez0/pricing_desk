Frequently Asked Questions
Can I add more RAM or local storage without upgrading my CPU?
No, RAM and local disk storage are bundled into the specific VM size. You cannot purchase extra RAM or local disk space individually for an existing instance plan. If you need more of these specific resources, you must resize your instance to the next tier. However, you can add highly scalable auxiliary storage to any instance by attaching Block Storage volumes or using Object Storage.
Does pricing vary by region?
Yes, pricing for some plans is different depending on the region.
Is egress included with my CPU?
G6 and G7 plans include some network transfer (egress) in their hourly rate. This egress may be pooled and used for your VM. Newer VM families, such as the G8 dedicated compute series, no longer include bundled egress.
What forms of payment do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. You can add credit to your account by transferring funds from a PayPal account. Maintaining a valid credit card on file with your account is a requirement of our Master Services Agreement. Read this guide on how billing works.
What CPU plan options are available?
Akamai Cloud offers Dedicated CPU, Shared CPU, and High Memory plans to suit various workload requirements.
Can I prepay for my cloud computing services?
Yes, you can make payments that front-load your account with credit. When your account gets invoiced for services used, we apply the prepaid credit amount before charging the payment card on file with your account.
If my VM is powered off, will I be billed?
Yes. We maintain your saved data and reserve your ability to use other resources like RAM, transfer, etc., even when your VM is powered off. You will receive a bill for any other active service, such as Longview Pro or an extra IP. If you want to stop receiving a bill for a particular service, you need to remove it from your account entirely.
