Frequently Asked Questions
Do you have questions left unanswered?
Contact us or schedule a call.
How much does SocialBee cost?
Our SocialBee price starts at $24/month on the annual Bootstrap plan and scales up based on team size, number of social profiles, and features you need. Standard plans range from Bootstrap to Pro, while Agency plans (Pro50, Pro100, Pro150) are built for teams managing multiple clients. You can compare exact social media management pricing on our pricing page and pick the plan that fits your workflow.
Is SocialBee cheaper than hiring a social media manager?
Yes. A typical social media manager cost runs into thousands per month in salary alone, while SocialBee’s social media management pricing packages start at a fraction of that, with scheduling, AI content generation, analytics and approval workflows built in. Many teams use SocialBee to reduce hiring needs entirely or to make one team member far more productive.
Can I get free access to SocialBee University with a yearly plan?
Yes. Email us at [email protected] after purchasing a yearly plan, and we’ll send a 100% discount code for University. The course is built by ConciergeBee’s freelance social media managers and covers the real skills needed to manage social media well. It’s a great fit for small business owners, beginner managers, and anyone growing their online presence.
What happens after my 14-day free trial ends?
Posting stops automatically once your trial expires. When you upgrade to a premium plan, everything is unlocked once again. Your settings, schedule and content are all stored exactly as they were when you left them, so there is no impact on your experience throughout the changeover.
What billing options do you offer?
We offer monthly and yearly billing. When compared to monthly pricing, annual plans come with a discount of 16%. You can upgrade/downgrade or switch between monthly and yearly billing at any time, directly from your account.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept credit and debit cards, PayPal, and Amazon Payments, giving you flexibility no matter how your business handles payments.
Are there any contracts involved?
No. Signing up doesn’t require a credit card, and there are no contracts or long-term commitments. Without any questions being asked, you are free to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time.
Am I being charged VAT, GST, or other additional taxes on my purchase?
Prices shown on our site and in the app don’t include VAT, GST, or other regional taxes. Applicable taxes are calculated and added at checkout based on your location.
Do you support NGOs or educational institutions?
Yes. NGOs and educational institutions get dedicated support and options built around their needs; see how SocialBee supports nonprofits and how SocialBee supports educational institutions.
Are agency plans any different than your regular Pro plan?
Agency plans (Pro50, Pro100, Pro150) include the same features as Pro. The difference is scale, more connected profiles, more workspaces, and more invited users, built for agencies managing multiple clients and outsourcing social media management services.
Do you also offer social media marketing services?
Yes, through ConciergeBee, our in-house agency. Specialists can manage content creation, video, community management, LinkedIn lead generation, and ad campaigns on your behalf, so you get software and hands-on service in one place if you need it.
