Questions about pricing and billing
Billing, renewal and add-ons, answered.
Which subscription lengths can I choose?
You can choose a monthly, 12-month or 24-month term. Longer terms cost less per month. Add-ons follow the same term as your plan.
What happens when my first subscription period ends?
Your plan renews automatically at its regular price. First-term discounts and the free domain apply to your first term only. We remind you by email 6 weeks before your term ends.
How do I cancel, and how much notice do I need?
Cancel in your account, under Plan & Billing, at least 1 month before your term ends. Consumers in Germany and the Netherlands renew month to month after their first term and can then cancel any time with 1 month’s notice.
Can I change my plan or add-ons myself?
Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or change your term, and add, cancel or undo add-ons, all in Plan & Billing. You don’t need to contact support.
Is there a free trial or a money-back guarantee?
The Free plan is your trial: no time limit and no credit card. If you buy a paid plan as a consumer and a right to withdrawal exists in your country, you also have a 14-day right of withdrawal.
Do your prices include VAT?
Yes. Every price on this page includes VAT, so no VAT is added at checkout. Many tools show prices without VAT, so check before you compare.
How are add-ons billed?
Add-ons are billed with your plan, on the same term, and renew with it. Each add-on ends when you cancel it.
Does Jimdo take a cut of my sales or bookings?
No. Jimdo takes 0% commission on sales, bookings and payment links, on every plan. When a client pays online with JimdoPay, a payment processing fee per transaction applies, charged by Jimdo: see processing fees.
What does my domain cost after the free period?
Your first domain is free for your first term on any paid plan, then €26 a year. It stays yours: you can transfer it out any time.
How quickly does a person reply?
Companion AI and the help centre are there 24/7. You get a first reply within 2 working days on Start, 24 hours on Grow and Complete, and 12 hours on Unlimited, and a person from our team whenever you ask for one.
Do I need to buy AI credits?
Companion AI is in every plan, Free included, with 25 messages a day. There are no credits to buy, and it only makes a change after your OK.
Do I pay per person or per business?
Per business. Your plan covers your website and your business tools, not a number of seats.
I already have a plan. What changes for me?
There’s nothing you need to do. The prices shown on this page don’t automatically change the terms of your existing subscription.
