Pricing FAQs
Can I cancel at anytime?
Yes, you can cancel anytime no questions asked. Cancellations take effect at the end of the billing period. We would also highly appreciate any feedback that can improve our service.
How does per employee pricing work?
CanIPhish limit the unique email addresses that can be emailed, based on the number of employees specified in your subscription. These email addresses are refreshed monthly.
Do monthly subscriptions have any commitments?
Monthly subscriptions have no commitments. You simply pay for a subscription on a monthly basis. You can cancel at any time and it will take effect at the end of the billing month.
Can I upgrade and downgrade subscriptions?
For annual subscriptions, upgrades occur immediately with the invoice being prorated based on the old and new plans. Downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period.
What do I get with an Enterprise subscription?
The Enterprise subscription provides everything included in the Professional subscription, but also includes Voice Phishing, Single Sign-On, Platform White-Labelling, Dark Web Breach Monitoring, and Premium Customer Support. Enterprise subscriptions are billed annually.
What payment methods & currencies are accepted?
We accept payment in various currencies, including: USD, AUD, NZD, CAD, EUR, and GBP. We support various payment methods depending on location, including: credit card (Global), direct debit (Australia and Europe), or international money transfer (Global).
Can I get a quote before making a purchase?
Yes, absolutely. Quotes can be issued using our quote request form and are ideal for organizations with a more formal procurement process. Should you decide to go ahead, a purchase order can be included on the subsequent invoice.
The online pricing calculator only goes up to 1,000 employees, I have more.
We provide our platform to organizations of any size. If you have more than 1,000 employees, simply fill out our quote request form and we'll prepare a custom quote tailored to your organization.
