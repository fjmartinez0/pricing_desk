FAQs
Do TTS, STT, and Agent concurrency limits affect each other?
No. TTS, STT and voice agents each have their own concurrency pool, so you can run all three at once without one using another's capacity.
How do model credits and voice agent rates work within each plan?
Every plan includes two monthly allowances:
Credits for the speech models, spent on TTS and STT at their rates.
Prepaid dollars for voice agents, at no extra cost.
For example, Scale costs $299/month and includes 8M credits plus $299 for voice agents. Free includes $1 for voice agents. Enterprise pricing is custom.
How many voice agent minutes do I get per plan?
At the standard $0.03/minute rate, each plan's prepaid agent dollars cover about:
Free: 33 minutes
Pro: 2 hours, 47 minutes
Startup: 27 hours, 13 minutes
Scale: 166 hours, 7 minutes
These numbers do not include telephony or LLM usage.
How many credits do I need?
Sonic text-to-speech: 1 credit per character, about 750 to 800 credits per minute of audio.
Ink speech-to-text: 3 credits per second of audio, or $0.39 per hour on the Scale plan.
How many credits do I need for Pro Voice Cloning?
Training a Professional Voice Clone is free. Speech from a clone costs 1 credit per character, unless your Enterprise plan says otherwise.
How many Professional Voice Clones can I have?
Slots come with your plan and are counted per organization:
Startup: 2 slots
Scale: 4 slots
Enterprise: custom
Free and Pro: none
More answers are in the Pro Voice Cloning FAQ.
What happens to my rollover credits if I change my pricing tier?
You keep every credit you have paid for. Your rollover cap resets to 2x your new plan's monthly credits. Once your balance drops below it, credits roll over again up to the new cap.
What happens if I upgrade to a higher subscription tier?
The new tier's credits are added to your current balance right away. With rollover, you can hold up to 2x your monthly credits.
What if I cancel or downgrade my tier in the middle of a payment period?
You keep your credits until you use them. Your account stays on its current tier until the end of the billing period, then moves to the new one.
When does my subscription renew?
Every month on your purchase date. A plan bought on March 31 renews on April 30, May 31 and so on.
What happens if I use more model credits than I have in my account?
Paid plans can turn on overages and keep going past a zero balance, at a rate set by tier:
Pro: $65 per 1M credits
Startup: $45 per 1M credits
Scale: $38 per 1M credits
With overages off, new requests are blocked once your credits run out.
What happens if I use more prepaid voice agent dollars than I have in my account?
Calls keep running at your plan's voice agent rate.
How and when are overages charged?
Overage charges start once you go past what your plan includes.
How are break tags counted in billing?
Each break tag counts as 1 credit in the transcript of a TTS request.
Is Cartesia SOC 2 Type II certified?
Yes. Cartesia is SOC 2 Type II certified.
Can I deploy the models on-premise or in a virtual private cloud?
Yes. Talk to our sales team about your setup.
