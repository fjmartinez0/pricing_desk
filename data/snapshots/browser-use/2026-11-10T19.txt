FAQ
Is there a subscription?
No. Credits from $5. 1 credit = $1.
Do credits expire?
Top-ups never expire. The $1 signup credit is one-time.
How is browser time billed?
$0.02 per hour, billed by the minute, 1-minute minimum.
What does traffic cost?
$5/GB on home-IP proxies. $0.20/GB direct or your own proxy.
How do I run more browsers at once?
By total paid: $100 gets 50, $1,000 gets 250, $5,000 gets 500, $25,000 gets 1,000.
Do you offer enterprise agreements?
Yes. Custom concurrency, custom SLAs, dedicated support, annual credit pools and a custom data policy. Talk to sales.
Can we run it on-prem?
Yes. Browsers and agents deploy on-prem for enterprise customers, through sales.
Is Browser Use SOC 2 compliant?
Yes, SOC 2 Type II. Request the report in our trust center. HIPAA, zero data retention and a signed DPA are available.
