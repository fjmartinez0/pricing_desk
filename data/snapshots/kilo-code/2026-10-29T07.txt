Get Started — It's Free
Your 14-day trial is an Enterprise trial, so your team can evaluate Enterprise features before choosing a plan. When the trial ends you choose your plan: Teams for self-serve collaboration, or Enterprise for governance, security review, and SLA commitments.
Understand the three parts of pricing
Kilo separates platform access, AI inference, and cloud compute so you only pay for what you use and need.
Platform plan
Free for individuals. Teams and Enterprise add collaboration, security, governance, and support.
AI inference
Choose free models, local models, BYOK, Kilo Gateway, or Kilo Pass.
Cloud compute
Usage-based compute for cloud features, charged separately from inference.
Choose how you pay for AI inference
Use free or local models, pay as you go through Kilo Gateway, bring your own provider keys, or subscribe to Kilo Pass for bonus credits.
