Frequently Asked Questions
Message credits
What counts as a message credit?
A message credit, shown on your plan as an Automated Message Credit, is used each time Apptoto sends an automated message on your behalf. This includes reminders, confirmations, follow-ups, notifications, campaign messages, and auto-replies.
SMS (up to 300 characters): 1 message credit
Voice call (up to 2 minutes): 1 message credit
Email: 1 message credit
International messages: Messages sent outside the US and Canada count as 2 message credits, with the same limits (300 characters per SMS, 2 minutes per voice call). Text messages and voice calls are supported in some, but not all, countries. See our list of supported locations.
AI Receptionist calls and soft phone calls also use message credits. See "What does the soft phone cost?" below, and "AI Receptionist and AI credits" for the AI Receptionist.
Are manually sent messages free?
Yes, in the US and Canada. If you send a message manually through Apptoto's "Compose" feature or reply manually to a client, those messages do not count against your message credit limit.
Do incoming replies from clients count against my message credits?
No. Incoming replies to auto messages are free, including confirmations like "1" or "C." Manual conversations are free, too: if you reply to a client yourself and you two text back and forth, none of those messages count against your message credit limit.
The one exception is auto-replies. If you've set Apptoto to watch for certain responses and reply instantly (for example, sending directions when someone confirms), each auto-reply the system sends uses 1 message credit, since Apptoto is sending it automatically on your behalf.
Do compliance messages count against my message credits?
Yes. Compliance messages are sent by Apptoto on your behalf, so they count against your message credit limit like any other automated message. These messages keep your reminders compliant with carrier rules and the TCPA, protecting your business from blocked messages and fines. Opt-in confirmations are only sent once per new contact, rather than with every message; therefore, compliance messages typically add just 1-3% to monthly volume.
How many message credits will I need per month?
Use this formula: appointments per month x automated messages per appointment = base message credits. Then add about 5% for auto-replies and compliance messages.
"Messages per appointment" is the number of auto-messages in your sequence. A typical setup sends 2 to 3: a booking confirmation, a reminder the day before, and sometimes a same-day reminder or follow-up.
Solo provider: 80 appointments x 2 messages = 160 message credits, plus buffer, about 170 per month
Small business: 400 appointments x 3 messages = 1,200 message credits, plus buffer, about 1,260 per month
High-volume practice: 1,200 appointments x 3 messages = 3,600 message credits, plus buffer, about 3,800 per month
If the AI Receptionist will answer calls, add 2 message credits for each minute it spends on the phone. For example, 100 three-minute calls a month add 600 message credits.
Want an instant estimate? Try our pricing calculator. Enter your monthly appointments and message setup to find the best plan for your business.
What if I use more message credits than my plan allows?
If you have "Allow Overages" enabled, messages will continue to deliver at a rate of $0.12 per message credit. If disabled, messages will stop once you've reached your message credit limit. Overages can be waived if you upgrade to the next plan during the current billing cycle.
AI Receptionist and soft phone calls use message credits too, so they follow the same rule.
What does the soft phone cost?
On calls you make (outbound), the first 5 minutes are free. After that, the call uses 1 message credit for every 2 minutes. Calls you answer (inbound) use 1 message credit for every 2 minutes from the start.
If a call goes to the AI Receptionist, AI Receptionist rates apply to that call.
AI Receptionist and AI credits
New
What does it cost to turn on the AI Receptionist?
Nothing. The AI Receptionist and AI Assistant are included in every plan, with no setup fee and no monthly fee. You pay only for what you use, and your account comes with 5,000 AI credits (a $100 value) to start.
What is the difference between message credits and AI credits?
Message credits cover delivery: each minute of a call, each text, and each email. They come with your plan every month and are the same credits your reminders use.
AI credits cover the work the AI Receptionist does to understand a caller and respond. You buy them in packs of $20, $50, $100, $200, or $400, and only the AI Receptionist uses them.
What does an AI Receptionist call cost?
Each call uses 2 message credits per minute, plus AI credits for the work done on the call. A typical 3-minute call uses 6 message credits and about 3 AI credits.
Texts and emails the AI Receptionist sends use 1 message credit each, plus AI credits.
When does billing start on a call?
Billing starts when the AI Receptionist answers. Every answered call counts, including short calls.
If a caller goes silent during the call, the AI Receptionist checks in after 1 minute and hangs up 20 seconds later. Calls end at 30 minutes.
Is chatting with the AI Assistant free?
Yes. Chatting with the AI Assistant is free. It does not use message credits or AI credits.
How do I buy more AI credits?
AI credits come in 5 packs: $20, $50, $100, $200, or $400. You can also turn on auto top-off, which buys a pack for you when your balance runs low. To buy AI credits, go to Settings > Account & Billing and click Purchase AI Credits.
Where is the AI Receptionist available?
The AI Receptionist answers calls, texts, and emails for businesses in the US and Canada.
