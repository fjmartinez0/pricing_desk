FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Everything you need to know about our pricing
What is a monitored account?
A monitored account is a company your team actively tracks for signals, research updates, and contacts. You can swap accounts anytime as territories change. On-demand research is available for any company — monitoring is for continuous, automated tracking.
What happens when I reach my account limit?
You can remove inactive accounts to free up space, upgrade your plan for more capacity, or add additional monitored account bundles. Your team keeps full access to signals, research, and contacts across all active accounts.
How do contact credits work?
Every plan includes verified email addresses and direct-dial phone numbers. The Individual plan comes with 200 contact credits per month — one credit per email address revealed, and one per phone number. Custom team and enterprise plans are sized to your team's volume.
What CRMs does Salesmotion integrate with?
Native Salesforce and HubSpot connectors are available on every plan: included with Enterprise; on other plans we switch them on on request. API and MCP access is available on custom team & enterprise plans for custom workflows.
How long does setup take?
Most reps go from signup to first signal in under an hour. No IT involvement required. Custom team & enterprise rollouts include a founder-led onboarding session.
What if I need more than one seat?
Custom team and enterprise pricing is available. Book a 30-minute call with the founder. We'll scope a rollout that fits your account universe, seat count, and CRM.
What's the contract commitment?
Individual is month-to-month. Cancel anytime — no penalties, no lock-in. Custom team & enterprise plans support annual billing at a discount.
How is Salesmotion different from ZoomInfo or Sales Navigator?
ZoomInfo and Sales Navigator focus on contact data and prospecting lists. Salesmotion monitors your target accounts for real-time buying signals, builds AI-generated account briefs, and drafts personalized outreach tied to real company events. It turns signals into ready-to-send outreach, not just data.
Do you find new accounts, or only monitor a list I give you?
Both, on every plan. Bring the accounts you already own and Salesmotion monitors them. To find new ones, open any tracked account and click Discover: Salesmotion returns lookalike companies ranked by similarity on industry, size, technology stack and business model, typically 10 to 15 per reference account. One click adds a company to your tracked accounts, and signal monitoring, scoring and research start right away. Lookalike discovery is included in the Individual plan and in every team and enterprise plan.
Can I pay for Salesmotion myself as an individual rep?
Yes. The Individual plan is $85/mo on your own card, billed monthly with no annual contract. You can cancel any time from Settings, Billing, and access continues to the end of the period you paid for. Every billing period produces a PDF invoice you can download for an expense claim, and you can add your company's billing details in the same tab first.
