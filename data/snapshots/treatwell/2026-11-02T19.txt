Frequently asked questions
How much does Treatwell cost for salons in the UK? +
Treatwell starts from £9.99 a month for salons in the UK. Treatwell Connect is £9.99 a month on the Freelancer plan or £24.99 a month on the Advanced plan with unlimited team members. Treatwell Expert is £59 a month with unlimited team members. All prices exclude VAT.
What is the difference between Treatwell Connect and Treatwell Expert? +
Treatwell Connect helps salons win new clients through the Treatwell marketplace, while Treatwell Expert is a complete salon management toolkit for established salons. Connect puts you in front of over 280,000 UK clients booking every month, with simple tools to manage bookings. Expert offers advanced marketing, reporting and admin tools, and Expert salons can join the Treatwell marketplace at any time.
How much commission does Treatwell charge? +
Treatwell charges 35% commission only on a new marketplace booking, with no minimum fee. Repeat clients and direct bookings are 0%. A client counts as new if you haven’t successfully provided a service to them through a Treatwell booking in the last 365 days. Commission is charged plus VAT, which VAT-registered salons can reclaim.
Does Treatwell charge commission on repeat clients? +
No, Treatwell doesn’t charge commission on repeat clients. A client only counts as new again if you haven’t successfully provided a service to them through a Treatwell booking in the last 365 days.
Does Treatwell charge commission on bookings from my website or Instagram? +
No, bookings from your own website and social media are always 0% commission. That covers your booking widget, your website, and the Book Now button on your Google and social profiles.
Can I move my existing clients to Treatwell? +
Yes, you can import your existing clients or add them to your calendar when you join Treatwell.
Does Treatwell offer a free trial? +
Yes, Treatwell Expert comes with a 14-day free trial, and no credit card is needed. You can cancel any time after your trial.
How many team members can I add on Treatwell? +
You can add unlimited team members on Treatwell Expert and on Treatwell Connect’s Advanced plan. Expert is £59 a month and Connect Advanced is £24.99 a month. The Connect Freelancer plan, at £9.99 a month, is designed for people working on their own.
Can I take card payments with Treatwell? +
Yes, you can take card payments with Treatwell, both in your salon and online. Use card machines or Tap to Pay in the salon, accept online pre-payments, and get daily payouts straight to your account.
Is Treatwell good for freelancers and small salons? +
Yes, Treatwell Connect’s Freelancer plan is built for people working on their own and costs £9.99 a month. It includes your profile on the Treatwell marketplace, a digital calendar, free SMS reminders and online booking.
