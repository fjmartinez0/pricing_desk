Frequently asked questions
AIR volume pricing, plus Talent Marketplace and Nexus.
How does volume-based pricing work for AIR?+
AIR uses volume-based pricing on the number of interviews conducted. The rate per interview decreases as volume grows. Braintrust does not publish a public per-interview sticker price. See AIR, Try AIR, and AI interview software cost.
Do you offer a free trial or pilot?+
Yes — we offer scoped pilots across all three products. AIR can typically be live within days. Nexus automations often go live in under 30 days. Talent Marketplace pilots can be kicked off within 48 hours of alignment on roles. See Try AIR.
What does "performance-based pricing" mean for Nexus?+
Nexus is priced as a share of the savings it generates. We identify the cost of your manual processes, automate them, and take 25% of the measured savings. You keep the other 75% — with zero upfront development cost.
Is there a platform fee for Talent Marketplace?+
There are zero platform fees for talent. Our model is built around making talent accessible and affordable — so you're only paying for the talent you hire, at rates that beat traditional agencies by 30–70%.
Can I use multiple Braintrust products together?+
Absolutely. Many of our customers use AIR to screen candidates at scale and Talent Marketplace to fill roles — creating an end-to-end hiring workflow. Nexus can also be layered on top to automate the operational work around hiring.
What ATS and HRIS systems do you integrate with?+
AIR has native integrations with Greenhouse, Lever, and Workday, with more in the pipeline. Nexus integrates with anything that has an API — including your existing HR stack, finance tools, and sales systems.
Is Braintrust SOC 2 compliant?+
Yes. Braintrust is SOC 2 Type II certified. AIR bias-audit and hiring-AI documentation is published separately from this pricing page. See How AIR stays compliant and AIR compliance.
How quickly can we get started?+
Talent Marketplace can source matched candidates within 48 hours of posting. AIR can be configured and live within a week for most roles. Nexus automations are typically deployed in under 30 days — no developers required on your end.
Need an AIR volume plan?
AIR pricing is scoped to interview volume. The team can also walk through Talent Marketplace and Nexus.
