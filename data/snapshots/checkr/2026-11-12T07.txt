Drug tests & health assessments
Drug test and health assessment costs vary based on testing method (breath, hair, or urine), clinic or onsite test location, and substances tested.
Clinic-based drug test
Test candidates for any combination of substances, from 4-panel to 10-panel tests. Tests include breath, hair, or urine. Pricing varies by panel selection and clinic location. A $90 fee may apply for clinics with expanded hours.
Starting from $60/test
Onsite drug test
Test candidates on your premises for a preset combination of drugs, from 4-panel to 10-panel tests. Pricing varies by panel selection.
Starting from $37/test
Occupational health assessment
Evaluate a candidate’s fitness for certain jobs. Includes physical evaluations and lift tests, hearing and vision tests, TB tests and x-rays, immunizations, and respiratory and pulmonary function tests.
Starting from $23/test
Random drug and alcohol test
Test randomly selected employees for any combination of substances, from 4-panel to 10-panel tests. Tests include breath, hair, or urine. Pricing varies by panel and clinic selection, plus a $10/test fee.
Starting from $10/test
Clinics with expanded hours are available for post-hire drug testing scenarios, including random, reasonable suspicion, and post-accident. A $90 after hours fee applies when a test is administered Monday through Friday between 7pm-7am and Saturday or Sunday.
