Frequently asked questions
What are Monthly Tracked Users (MTUs)?
MTUs are the number of uniquely identified user profiles sent to Chameleon in the prior 30 days. Your pricing scales with this number so you only pay as your active user base grows.
What happens during the free trial?
You get full Enterprise-level access for 14 days. Build, test, and publish experiences with all AI agents and features unlocked. No credit card required to start.
How do AI credits work?
Some Chameleon features, including Compass (user context) and Prism (personalization) use AI credits. New accounts start with 10,000 credits to try those features. Read more about AI credits here.
What's the difference between Pro and Growth?
Pro is self-serve with monthly billing starting at $750/mo. Growth adds the Ranger agent for always-on optimization, Interactive Demos, HelpBar, dedicated CSM support, and coaching — billed annually with sales assistance.
Can I switch between plans?
Yes. You can upgrade from Pro to Growth at any time — your account transitions seamlessly. If you need to adjust, our team will work with you to find the best fit.
How does Chameleon compare to other adoption tools?
Chameleon is the only adoption platform with four purpose-built AI agents that build, optimise, understand, and personalise your in-app experiences. Most tools offer builders — we offer outcomes.
