FAQ
Does the service include an unlimited number of sales invoices at no extra cost?
Yes. You can send an unlimited number of sales invoices without any invoice-specific fees.
Can I add an unlimited number of receipts and accounting documents?
Yes. The number of receipts and accounting documents does not affect the monthly price of the service.
Will the monthly price stay the same even if my business becomes more active?
Yes. The monthly price does not depend on the number of invoices, receipts or accounting documents. This makes your accounting costs predictable as your business grows.
Are there any hourly or transaction-based fees for accounting?
There are no hourly or transaction-based fees for standard monthly accounting. Additional charges may apply, for example, to overdue accounting work or to reopening and correcting an already closed accounting period.
Are VAT returns and tax returns included in the monthly price?
Yes. We take care of the VAT returns and tax returns included in the service at no extra cost.
Does UKKO.fi handle the bookkeeping for me, or is it only software?
UKKO Entrepreneur is more than accounting software. You get an easy-to-use tool as well as access to accounting professionals who take care of your statutory filings and help you with your bookkeeping.
Can I get help from an expert without an additional fee?
Yes. Support from our accounting professionals is included in the monthly price. You can get help with using the service and with accounting-related questions. Our accounting team also processes your documents with the support of automation to keep your accounting running smoothly.
When will I start paying for the service?
You can always try the UKKO Entrepreneur service for sole traders and limited liability companies free of charge for 30 days, even if you add income during the trial period. If your business has not yet received any income after 30 days, free use continues until you add your first income, for a maximum of six months.
How much does it cost to set up a business through UKKO.fi?
We do not charge for setting up a business or for our help with the process. Setting up a sole trader business through us is free of charge. When setting up a limited liability company, you only pay the Finnish Patent and Registration Office’s official fee of 300 €.
Are there any setup or transfer fees when changing accounting firms?
No. We terminate your agreement with your current accounting firm and add the opening balances to your accounts at no extra cost. You do not have to manage the change on your own.
Does the price include an income statement for Private Traders or financial statements for Limited Companies?
Yes. As a Private Trader, you get an income statement to help you track your business income, expenses and profit. For Limited Companies, financial statements are included in the service, with no separate fee.
