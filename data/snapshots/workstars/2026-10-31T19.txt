Questions
Good to know before you get in touch
Is $3 the only fee I'll ever pay?
Your platform subscription is one flat rate. Reward spend is a separate, employee-driven budget you control directly.
What happens as our headcount changes?
Your monthly bill adjusts automatically — no renegotiating, no re-signing, no manual updates needed.
Is onboarding charged separately?
No. Full implementation and ongoing support are included in your monthly rate from day one.
Do larger teams get a better rate?
Yes, automatically. Organizations above 1,000 employees receive a discounted rate without a custom negotiation.
Do budgets get charged upfront?
No — You are only charged when an employee redeems for a reward, not when budgets are loaded.
Is support included, or a paid add-on?
Included. You get a dedicated point of contact as part of your standard subscription.
