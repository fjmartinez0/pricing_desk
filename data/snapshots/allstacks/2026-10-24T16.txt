Frequently asked questions
Is Product Studio really free to start?
Yes, Allstacks Product Studio is free to start and use all capabilities up to a credit limit, with daily limits set. You get access right away, can connect any of your tools, and use its full agentic abilities from day one.
Can we do a free trial?
We offer a couple of options for you to try our software before purchasing. Start usingAllstacks Product Studio for free with all capabilities included or setup a quick intro with our team to discuss a tailored proof of concept for Software Engineering Intelligence and Software Cost Capitalization to scale across your team.
Can I purchase Cost Capitalization on its own?
Yes. Software Cost Capitalization is available standalone, or bundled with Growth (capped at 500 contributors) or Enterprise (no contributor limit) for additional savings.
Is there a limit to the number of tools you can integrate?
No. We don't limit or charge for integrations. Connect all available integrations, such as Claude, GitHub Copilot, GitHub, Bitbucket, Jira, Azure DevOps, and more.
What's the difference between Growth and Enterprise?
Growth is built for engineering teams on shared infrastructure, with delivery tracking up to 500 contributors, and standard 24-hour support response times. Enterprise is built for org-wide delivery data tracking at scale with dedicated infrastructure, unlimited contributors and history, 8-hour support, and a dedicated Customer Success Manager.
Do you offer discounts for bundling products?
Yes. Combining Software Cost Capitalization with Growth or Enterprise, or bundling multiple Allstacks products, unlocks savings over buying separately. Talk to us about platform pricing for your mix.
What security and compliance certifications does Allstacks have?
Allstacks holds SOC 2 Type II certification for the Software Engineering Intelligence platform and SOC 1 certification for Software Cost Capitalization. Enterprise plans add dedicated data ingestion IP addresses and a site-to-site VPN option for additional network security. Learn more about our security approach.
What are the contract terms?
The Allstacks Product Studio Starter plan is free to start with a certain amount of credits. To get access to more, upgrade to the Standard or Pro plans in the product.
Allstacks Software Engineering Intelligence and Software Cost Capitalization plans are billed annually per contributor at a minimum of 1 year contract term.
