Ship faster without compounding costs
Most visual testing tools snapshot every test on every build. As your team ships faster, you run more builds against a bigger test suite, and cost compounds.
TurboSnap tracks file changes to determine which tests are affected, then captures snapshots only for those tests. Unaffected tests reuse their previous snapshots, which count less toward your usage. How it works
