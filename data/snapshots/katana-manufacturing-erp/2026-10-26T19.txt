Frequently asked questions
Can’t find what you’re looking for? Our team can walk you through how Advantage would work for your business.
Talk to our team
How much does Katana cost?
Katana is packaged as Advantage, a custom annual contract. We scope your needs, map your tech stack and current workflows, and work out what to set up around them. You get one price for everything in the agreed scope of work, with no piecemeal invoices or surprise charges. If you’d like to try Katana first, you can start on the Free plan with up to 30 SKUs at no cost.
What is Katana Advantage?
Advantage is a setup personalized around your business. Our implementation team scopes, sets up and looks after your integrations, automations and dashboards, all under one annual contract.
Who is Advantage for?
Businesses of any size that want a setup personalized around them, running their way. Usually they have a lot of integrations to connect, or workflows that rely on workarounds today.
How long does it take to get started?
Most Advantage setups are ready in weeks. Your customer success manager and implementation manager guide you from kickoff to go-live.
What support do I get?
Advantage includes an assigned customer success manager and an implementation manager, 24/7 live support, and regular inventory consultations. Free includes our AI assistant and Knowledge Base.
What’s included in the Free plan?
Free includes all Katana features, within a 30 SKU limit. You
also get unlimited users, integrations and inventory locations, plus API access. For your first 15 days you can work with unlimited SKUs. After that, you’ll need to bring your catalog down to 30 SKUs or talk to our team about upgrading. For e-commerce, only Shopify currently integrates with Free. It works well for testing Katana, trying it with a sample catalog, or running lightweight workflows.
Can I try Katana before buying?
Yes. Start on Free right away, with no credit card. You can also book a live demo with a product specialist.
I’m on Standard, Professional, Professional Plus or Core. What happens to my plan?
We’ll contact you directly about any changes to your plan. If you’d like to talk about it sooner, or about moving to Advantage, get in touch with your Customer Success Manager.
What’s included in the price? Are there add-ons or hidden fees?
Everything you need to run Katana is in the price: unlimited users, inventory, manufacturing, purchasing, traceability, warehouse management, the shop floor app, integrations, implementation, a dedicated implementation manager and a customer success manager. Your quote lists everything we’ll build for you, so nothing is added later.
Can we customize Katana to fit our own process?
Yes. On Advantage you can add for example custom fields to items are live, sales orders and quotes, use them in print templates and the API, and have our engineers build custom automations, dashboards and integrations around how your team works. Bundles and kits are handled with bills of materials, so stock updates as components are used.
Does Advantage include API access and custom integrations?
Yes. Advantage includes the Katana API and the Katana MCP server, which lets AI assistants read and update your Katana data. Our engineers also build and maintain custom integrations to the other tools you use, such as EDI, a CRM or a 3PL, as part of your plan.
Do you charge per user?
No. Every Advantage plan includes unlimited users, so you can add your warehouse, production and sales teams without paying per user.
