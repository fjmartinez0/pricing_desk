Pricing questions
Are there per-user fees?
No. We allow unlimited user accounts. There are no per-user or per-seat fees.
Does the contract auto-renew every year?
No. About a month before your subscription expires, we send an invoice to your account owner. Pay it to continue with your current plan, or let us know if you want to add or remove modules.
Is support or training extra?
No. Tech support is free, by toll-free number, email and a web-based support form. Web-based training sessions are free too.
What happens to our data if we cancel?
We'll work with you to return all uploaded documents and a Microsoft Excel copy of your data.
