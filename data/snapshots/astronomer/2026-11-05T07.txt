How Astro pricing works
Astro’s pricing model is usage-based. Deployments and Workers are billed separately on a metered basis, with additional costs for dedicated clusters, Astro Observe, and AI token usage.
Cluster pricing
Standard multi-tenant clusters are included on all plans at no cost. Dedicated single-tenant clusters are available starting at $2.40/hr for enhanced networking and security.
Deployment pricing
Each deployment includes the scheduler, webserver, and triggerer needed to run your pipelines. Deployments can be sized to fit your scheduling complexity and Dag volume. Deployments run continuously, with fixed hourly pricing based on size.
Worker pricing
Workers execute your individual Airflow tasks. Unlike other managed Airflow providers, workers on Astro automatically scale to zero when idle, so you never pay for unused capacity. Choose from 7 worker sizes to meet your task requirements (A5 to A160) starting at $0.13/hr.
*Networking costs are passed through from the cloud provider.
Observe pricing
Astro Observe provides detailed cost and performance metrics, one-click root cause analysis, and complete lineage of your Airflow pipelines. Observe pricing is set as a percentage of your deployment and worker compute costs.
