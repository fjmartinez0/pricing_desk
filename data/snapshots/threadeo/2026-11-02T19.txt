Pricing FAQ
How much does Threadeo Sync cost?
$15 per job plus $0.10 per minute of video or audio. A 2-hour deposition costs $27. LEF/PTZ load files are an optional add-on at $13.50 per job.
Why choose Threadeo over other sync tools?
Many sync tools can leave work to your team: converting MPEG-1 to MP4, uploading exhibits after the sync is done, hunting for QC points, running one job at a time and removing timestamps from transcripts. Threadeo does all of it in one upload, with 99%+ accuracy, typically 0–1 QC points per job, and unlimited jobs in parallel.
What's included in the price?
Encoding, parallel jobs, uploading transcripts, exhibits and video all up front, automatic exhibit linking, every standard load file, captions, free exhibit summaries, the productivity dashboard (Organization accounts), clip creation and export, unlimited collaborators and cloud hosting. Only LEF/PTZ load files cost extra.
Is there a subscription, monthly fee, or per-user fee?
No. There are no subscriptions, per-user fees, monthly fees, or ongoing fees. You pay only for the jobs you sync, and you can add as many collaborators as you like (Organization accounts).
What files can I upload?
Video in MP4, MOV, MPEG-1, MPEG-2 or WMV, and audio in MP3, AAC, FLAC or WAV. Our systems automatically encode video files if needed. ASCII transcripts with timestamps on the left are good to upload. Exhibits of any type can be uploaded, and exhibit summaries are generated at no cost for most PDF and image exhibits.
How is Threadeo Summaries priced?
Per transcript, with no subscription, seats or contracts. You're only billed for the transcripts you analyze.
