Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need a credit card to start the free trial?
No. You can start your 14-day trial without a credit card. Explore the full platform and connect your data before committing to a paid plan.
How is ARR calculated for pricing?
Pricing is based on the annual recurring revenue (ARR) tracked inside ChartMogul. ARR is calculated from your active subscription data, so your plan reflects the revenue you’re actually managing in the platform.
What happens if our ARR grows beyond our plan limit?
As your ARR increases, we’ll move you to the next tier.
Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time?
Yes. You can change plans as your business evolves. You can manage your plan directly in the app or contact us for help with plans or pricing.
What counts as a billing source?
A billing source is any billing system you connect through an integration built and maintained by ChartMogul, such as Stripe or Chargebee, or by our partner SaaSync, such as Paddle or Xero. Each connected billing system counts as one billing source.
Everything else counts as a custom or third-party source: data you send through the API or CSV uploads, Google Sheets, automation tools like n8n and Zapier, and integrations built by other vendors, like Subbly or WHMCS. These don't count toward your billing source limit. Your plan's custom and third-party source limit is shown in the plan comparison above.
Do you offer custom pricing or startup discounts?
If you are over $10M ARR or have a complex setup, our Enterprise plan is tailored to your needs.
If you are an early-stage startup, you may qualify for our Startup Program. It offers a $50 per month discount on any paid plan for 12 months, a total value of $600. You can review eligibility requirements here: https://chartmogul.com/startups/
Startups under $120K ARR can use the Free plan at no cost.
