Discover Features Included in Every Plan
Work with Events, not Individual Fields

All Events are automatically tagged with their physical location at no extra charge. Our geolocation capabilities include the fusion of GPS, cellular tower, and Wi-Fi triangulation.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Notecard Cell+WiFi module?
Notecard Cell+WiFi is a secure, low-power IoT system-on-module that combines LTE Cat-1 cellular and Wi‑Fi connectivity in a single device. It’s designed for fast, scalable deployment of connected products with minimal code and hardware complexity.
How do I add both cellular and Wi‑Fi to my IoT device?
With the Notecard Cell+WiFi, you can easily integrate dual connectivity by plugging the module into your host microcontroller or Notecarrier via the M.2 edge connector. It automatically manages connections and allows fallback between Wi‑Fi and cellular using the same developer-friendly JSON API.
What LTE bands and Wi‑Fi standards are supported?
The Notecard Cell+WiFi has variations that support NB-IoT, LTE Cat M, LTE Cat 1, LTE Cat 1 bis, and connects to 2.4GHz 802.11b/g/n Wi‑Fi networks. This ensures broad compatibility with carriers and routers worldwide.
Does the Notecard Cell+WiFi support GPS for tracking?
Yes. It includes a built-in GNSS module, providing GPS location tracking for asset monitoring, geofencing, and time synchronization applications.
What microcontrollers are compatible with Notecard?
Notecard is MCU-agnostic. It communicates via UART, I2C, or USB, and works with virtually any microcontroller or single-board computer, including STM32, ESP32, Raspberry Pi, Arduino, and more. Blues has also created two lightweight MCUs based on the Adafruit Feather architecture: the Cygnet and Swan.
Is the Notecard secure for off-internet communication?
Absolutely. All data is encrypted on device and sent via secure, off-internet tunnels to Notehub. The Notecard includes built-in ECC certificates and hardware-based authentication to protect your devices end-to-end.
What is the power consumption of the Notecard Cell+WiFi?
Notecards are optimized for low-power applications. In idle mode, the Notecard Cell+WiFi consumes as little as 8µA, making it ideal for battery-powered and energy-constrained deployments.
Is cellular service included with the Notecard?
Yes. Each Notecard Cell+WiFi includes 500MB of cellular data and 10 years of service with no subscription fees or contracts. You get reliable global connectivity right out of the box with flexible pricing to fit your needs.
Does the Notecard support automatic failover between Wi‑Fi and cellular?
Yes. Notecard Cell+WiFi can be configured to prioritize either cellular or Wi‑Fi and automatically fall back to the other when a stable connection is unavailable, ensuring reliable connectivity at all times.
What is an Event? Give me examples.
An Event is a record Notehub creates each time you, your devices, or your cloud apps send data or commands through your Notehub project. You pay for every Event except Platform Events, the behind-the-scenes housekeeping that keeps your devices connected and healthy.
Examples of billable Events are:
A device sends a reading to Notehub
Your app sends a command or Note to a device through the Notehub API
You change a setting (environment variable) that needs to reach a device
An example of Platform Event is:
When a device reports radio access technology (RAT) connection statistics
Is there a starter kit for the Notecard Cell+WiFi?
Yes. Blues offers a Notecard Cell+WiFi Starter Kit that includes everything you need to get started: a Global Notecard Cell+WiFi, a Notecarrier F, an antenna, a Feather MCU, and a link to our quickstart guide. It’s the fastest way to prototype and test connected applications.
