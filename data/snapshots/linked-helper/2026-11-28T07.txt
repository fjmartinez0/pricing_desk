Frequently Asked Questions
What do I get on the free trial?
Full Pro access for 14 days — every feature, no daily caps. No credit card required, and nothing renews automatically: the trial simply ends and campaigns pause.
Can my whole team try Linked Helper for free?
Yes. Every LinkedIn account you add gets its own trial, and they share one end date — add three accounts on day one and two more on day seven, and all five finish together. So a team can evaluate the product at the same time, not one person at a time.
What’s the difference between Local and Cloud Data Storage?
Local-based Storage keeps it on your own device and costs less. Cloud-based Storage keeps it on Linked Helper servers, so you can reach the same workspace from another device, share it with teammates, get automatic backups and receive leads through incoming webhooks. Everything else — the app, the automation, the feature set — is identical.
Is Linked Helper a cloud-based LinkedIn automation tool?
No. Linked Helper is desktop software: it runs on your own computer, or on a VPS you control. That is true of both versions. Cloud-based Storage version is about where your data lives, not about running your account from our servers — we never do that.
Where is my LinkedIn session stored?
On the machine you run Linked Helper on — your computer or your own VPS. It never reaches our servers, on either version.
Where is my Linked Helper data stored?
On Local-based Storage, in folders on your own device. On Cloud-based Storage, on Linked Helper servers in the United States, which is what lets you open the same workspace from a second device.
Does it run while my computer is off?
No — on either version. The automation runs from the machine you control, so that machine has to be on with the app running. Cloud-based Storage changes where the data is kept, not where the work happens.
For 24/7 operation, you can rent a VPS and run Linked Helper on it instead.
Can I switch from Local-based to Cloud-based Storage later?
Yes. You can upgrade any Local license to Cloud at any time, including licenses on a subscription. Your remaining time is converted at Cloud prices, so the term gets shorter instead of costing you extra. If the remaining time is too short to reach your next renewal, you'll see the exact amount before you confirm. If the license is in use, the LinkedIn account and its data move to the cloud automatically.
What’s the difference between Standard and Pro?
Four things. Pro lifts the 20-per-day cap on eleven advanced actions; it adds messaging-history export to CSV, to webhooks and to your CRM; it includes 3,100 Data credits a month instead of 620; and it comes with roughly double the AI credits. Everything else — every feature, including all the AI ones — is the same on both plans.
Can I upgrade from Standard to Pro mid-term?
Yes. You can upgrade a Standard license to Pro at any time, including licenses on a subscription. Your remaining time is converted at Pro prices, so the term gets shorter instead of costing you extra. If the remaining time is too short to reach your next renewal, you'll see the exact amount before you confirm.
What exactly is limited on Standard?
Several advanced actions are capped at 20 per day — Message to LinkedIn group members, Message to LinkedIn event attendees, Invite connections to a LinkedIn event or webinar, Invite connections to follow your company in LinkedIn, Invite connections to join a LinkedIn group, Like and comment posts and articles, Auto-tag prospects under posts with the “Boost post” action, Send a contact to your CRM natively, Send people profiles to 3rd parties via webhooks, Send organization profiles via webhooks.
You can compare Standard and Pro by switching the toggle to “Show only differences.”
Do Workspace members require separate licenses?
No. A license is tied to a LinkedIn account, not to a person — you buy licenses for the accounts you automate, and teammates get access through workspace roles. One caveat worth knowing up front: you can add users without a license only in your first workspace; any additional workspace needs at least one license of its own.
I run an agency. Can I keep clients separate?
Yes. Give each client their own workspace: data, accounts and permissions stay inside it, and people without access to a workspace cannot see that it exists. You can invite a client as a Guest with view-only access.
Can I pay by invoice?
Yes. Checkout goes through FastSpring or PayPro Global, and both can issue an invoice for the order. The W-9 tax form is available from the payment provider.
Can I add more licenses later?
Yes, two ways. Buy one-time licenses, which start their own term at the full price. Or extend your subscription: the top-up is prorated against the days left in the current billing cycle. Added licenses must match the existing license type and subscription duration. Because the discount is calculated on the resulting volume, adding licenses can move you into a bigger discount.
What is a license tied to?
1 license = 1 LinkedIn account. You can switch the connected account at any time, but one license cannot run two accounts at once. Licenses can also be reassigned between Workspaces whenever needed.
Do term and volume discounts stack?
Yes. For example, 76 yearly Standard licenses come out at $4,890.60 — a 64% total discount compared with renewing monthly.
I bought 10+ licenses but got no discount. Why?
The volume discount requires all licenses to be the same type, the same duration, and bought under one subscription or one order. Buying them separately does not qualify.
Do my credits expire?
Data credits never expire — neither the ones included with a license nor the ones you buy. AI credits work differently: packages you buy separately never expire, but AI credits included with a license run out when that license does.
Is each license a separate credit balance?
No. Credits from every license go into one balance for the workspace, and any connected LinkedIn account can spend them. Buying twenty licenses means twenty times the credits in a single pot, not twenty separate pots.
What AI features do I get, and on which plan?
All of them, on both plans: AI Messages, AI ICP Detection, AI Commenter, AI Reply Assistant, the AI Message Generator and the AI text editor. No AI feature is locked to Pro. What differs is the allowance: a Standard license comes with 250 to 1,500 AI credits depending on the term, a Pro one with 500 to 3,000 — roughly double. Either way you can buy more.
Do I need my own ChatGPT subscription for the AI features?
No. Linked Helper has its own built-in AI engine, so there is no separate subscription and no API key to paste in.
What does the AI personalize messages from?
From profile data — role, company, experience and the other fields you choose. It does not write from someone’s recent posts. If a required field is missing, Linked Helper visits the profile to collect it, and skips the lead if it still cannot.
What does a credit actually buy?
An email costs 1 credit, profile info 1, social and messaging links 2, company data 2, and a phone number 10.
Will I be charged automatically?
Only if you turn auto-renewal on yourself. By default a license simply runs out and you decide whether to buy another one.
