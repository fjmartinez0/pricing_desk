OpenEnvoy Pricing FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Which ERP systems are supported?
Essentials is designed for modern cloud ERPs used by small finance teams. Scale supports mid-market systems such as NetSuite, Sage, and Microsoft Dynamics 365. Enterprise supports global ERP environments including SAP, Oracle, Workday, and other complex, multi-entity architectures.
How long is implementation?
Essentials is available instantly. Scale customers typically go live in under 90 days, and Enterprise implementations follow a custom rollout plan.
What do I need to sign up?
A work email and your ERP login. Connect QuickBooks, Sage Intacct, or NetSuite and process your first invoice the same afternoon. Organization SSO is included on Scale, and Enterprise Directory Services on Enterprise.
What is Ask OE?
Ask OE is our set of agentic data tools, included in every plan. Ask about a job, a document, or your data in plain language and get an answer with its source. It supports over 200 languages. It is separate from the preconfigured analytics dashboards, which vary by plan.
What counts as a transaction?
A transaction is a successfully processed page or API call on the OpenEnvoy platform.
Do token rates change with AI costs?
No. Your token rate is locked for the duration of your contract, even if underlying AI costs change.
Can I upgrade later?
Yes. You can move from Essentials to Scale or Enterprise at any time as your volume and requirements increase.
Do tokens expire?
No. Tokens roll over from year to year until they are used.
Do you offer volume discounts?
Yes. We offer tiered discounts for prepaid bundles, including up to 20% off at higher volumes.
What is Verified Payments?
Verified Payments confirms you're ready to pay before money moves — then runs card, ACH, and wire payments with the same controls and the same audit trail. Available in the United States.
What are payment controls?
A payment control determines who can submit a payment and who approves it before release. Dual control is included in every plan, so submitting and approving are always separate actions. Growth and Enterprise enforce it more strictly, including requiring two administrators to set up payments.
