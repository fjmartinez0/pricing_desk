FAQs
Do you offer a free trial?
Yes! You get 10 days free trial. No credit card needed to start - just sign up and try everything out.
What happens after my trial ends?
Your account will pause, and you won't be charged anything. You can then choose a plan to reactivate and keep all your data.
Do you charge per employee or per organization?
We charge per employee. So if you have 15 people in your organization, you pay for 14 users. The owner account is free of charge.
Can I add or remove team members anytime?
Absolutely. You can add or remove new people whenever you want. Your billing adjusts automatically at the next cycle or when you add new members.
Is there a discount for annual billing?
Yes, you get a 30%+ discount on all the plans if you pay yearly instead of monthly. We also have quarterly pricing that gets you a good discount. Check it out on the pricing plans “quarterly” tab.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay through our secure payment processor, Paddle. They handle everything safely.
Is there a setup or cancellation fee?
Nope! No hidden fees. You can start anytime and cancel anytime without extra charges.
How secure is my employee data?
Very secure. All data is encrypted and stored safely. We only track work activity - never personal passwords or sensitive information.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes take effect immediately.
Do you offer a money-back guarantee?
Yes, Apploye offers refunds on all of its paid plans, but only if you request one within the first 30 days after your purchase.
