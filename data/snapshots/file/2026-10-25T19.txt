How it works
How outcome-based pricing works
One finished outcome is one Billable Run. Technical failures, duplicates fileAI caused, reruns fileAI triggered, and tokens and model calls are never billed.
01
You send a file, record, or case
Whatever starts your workflow: an invoice, a bank statement, a claim, or a policy change.
02
The Agent Flow does the work
It applies your logic, cross-checks evidence, resolves exceptions, and routes approvals.
03
It reaches a defined outcome
A posted entry, a resolved case, or a validated update. An exception counts too, if your contract says so.
04
That's one Billable Run
Charged at the agent's run price. Nothing is billed before this point.

FAQ
Frequently asked questions
How is this different from per-page pricing?
Per-page pricing charged for every page, whatever the result. Outcome-based pricing charges only for successful work. Extraction isn't the main deliverable anymore: Agent Flows now apply rules, reconcile documents, resolve exceptions, and post results. That finished result is what you pay for.
Is outcome-based pricing common?
Not yet. A June 2026 Bain & Company review of nearly 200 B2B SaaS companies found that about 10% of AI-native and hybrid vendors use it.
What triggers a charge?
A flow reaching a defined, successful outcome. An exception can count as complete if your contract defines it that way.
How is an outcome defined?
As the finished result the flow delivers. Each fileLedger agent has one clear outcome. fileForge and fileShield outcomes can be customised to your process.
Do I pay if a run fails?
No. Technical failures, duplicates fileAI caused, and reruns fileAI triggered are never billed.
How do volume bands work?
Choose a band by your expected annual run volume. The price per run drops as your committed volume rises.
