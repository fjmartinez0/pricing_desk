Pricing, clearly explained
Know exactly what you pay for
One Pro plan, flexible commitment options, and clear pricing before you start.
1
Does Akiflow have a free plan?
No. Akiflow has no free plan. New customers receive a 7-day full-access trial; a card is required, and the selected subscription begins after the trial unless it is cancelled.
2
How much does Akiflow Pro cost?
Akiflow Pro is $34 per month when billed monthly, or $19 per month when billed yearly ($228 once per year). Paying yearly saves 44%. The plan is called Pro, not Premium.
3
Is the AI Meeting Assistant included?
Aki, the AI executive assistant, and the Akiflow MCP Connector are included in Pro. The AI Meeting Assistant for recording, transcripts, summaries, and action items is an optional add-on.
4
What is included in Akiflow Pro?
Pro includes tasks, subtasks, calendar planning, integrations, power features, Aki, the hosted Akiflow MCP Connector, and a free 1:1 onboarding call.
5
Do prices differ by country?
Akiflow may apply local purchasing-power pricing in selected countries. The checkout price shown for your location is the price that applies.
6
Does Akiflow offer a student discount?
Yes. Eligible university students and researchers can receive 40% off Pro Yearly for life after academic-email verification. Contact support@akiflow.com to verify eligibility.
7
Is there team pricing?
Yes. Team pricing is handled directly by Akiflow rather than published as a self-serve per-seat rate. Contact the sales team to discuss the right setup for your organization.
F.A.Q.
Is Akiflow for people like me?
Akiflow is designed to simplify your work and boost productivity by merging your tools into one powerful platform and presenting your calendar and to-do list in a single unified view. Regardless of whether your goal is to achieve productivity, improve organization, or excel in time management, Akiflow is ready to be your reliable ally. Most of our users are Founders, CXOs, Freelancers and Software developers.
Does my free trial limit my access to features?
Absolutely not! Your free trial grants you complete access to all of Akiflow's extensive features. We believe in providing you with the full experience, allowing you to explore the platform's capabilities and discover how Akiflow can truly enhance your productivity.
How can I be sure that my data is secure?
At Akiflow, we prioritize data security, taking various measures to ensure your information is protected. Rest assured, your data is safe with us, and you can confidently use our platform without worry. To learn more about our security measures, please visit the provided link. Your peace of mind is important to us!
If I have a support question, how do I reach you?
If you have any questions or require assistance, please don't hesitate to contact our support team. You can reach out to us by clicking on the chat icon or by sending an email to support@akiflow.com. You can also join our community on Slack. We’re here to help you find the best support and will do our best to address any doubts that you may have.
Do you offer discounts?
Yes, we do offer discounts for students. If you belong to any of these categories, please reach out to us for a discount by sending an email to support@akiflow.com. We'll be happy to assist you with the discounted pricing.
Additionally, we offer a yearly pricing plan that provides a 40% savings compared to the total cost of monthly pricing over a year. This is another great way to take advantage of our services at a reduced rate.
How do you take payments?
We accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Maestro, Discover), Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal for monthly and yearly plans.
Can you explain your cancellation policy?
Akiflow offers a 7-day free trial, ensuring ample time for cancellation. Upon cancellation, your subscription remains active until the end of the billing cycle with no additional charges or fees.
Where can I find my invoices?
To access your invoices, simply log into your Akiflow account and go to Settings > My Account > Billing > Manage > Billing History. From there, you will be able to view and download any past invoices associated with your subscription.
