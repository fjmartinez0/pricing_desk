Pricing, clearly explained
Know exactly what you pay for
One Pro plan, flexible commitment options, and clear pricing before you start.
1
Does Akiflow have a free plan?
No. Akiflow has no free plan. New customers receive a 7-day full-access trial; a card is required, and the selected subscription begins after the trial unless it is cancelled.
2
How much does Akiflow Pro cost?
Akiflow Pro is $34 per month when billed monthly, or $19 per month when billed yearly ($228 once per year). Paying yearly saves 44%. The plan is called Pro, not Premium.
3
Is the AI Meeting Assistant included?
Aki, the AI executive assistant, and the Akiflow MCP Connector are included in Pro. The AI Meeting Assistant for recording, transcripts, summaries, and action items is an optional $19-per-month add-on.
4
What is included in Akiflow Pro?
Pro includes tasks, subtasks, calendar planning, integrations, power features, Aki, the hosted Akiflow MCP Connector, and a free 1:1 onboarding call.
5
Do prices differ by country?
Akiflow may apply local purchasing-power pricing in selected countries. The checkout price shown for your location is the price that applies.
6
Does Akiflow offer a student discount?
Yes. Eligible university students and researchers can receive 40% off Pro Yearly for life after academic-email verification.
7
Is there team pricing?
Yes. Team pricing is handled directly by Akiflow rather than published as a self-serve per-seat rate. Contact the sales team to discuss the right setup for your organization.
