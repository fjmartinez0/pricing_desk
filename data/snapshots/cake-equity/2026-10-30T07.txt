Frequently asked questions
Which Cake plan is right for me?
Your first 5 stakeholders are always free. Not sure which plan fits? Use the quote calculator above.
Build is for companies getting their cap table in order; tracking SAFEs, running fundraising scenarios, and managing early equity grants.
Team is for startups fundraising at scale, collaborating with attorneys, and issuing employee equity. 409A valuations included.
Pro is for companies that need stock-based compensation reporting, compliance monitoring, and more advanced reporting requirements.
What is a stakeholder?
Anyone who currently holds securities in your company - shareholders, option holders, note holders, RSU and RSA holders, and warrant holders.
Is 409A included?
Yes. Team and Pro include two 409A valuations a year. On Build, or if you need another one, add a 409A to any annual plan for $1,500.
Is managed migration free?
Yes, and it's accurate. Whether you're moving from Carta, Pulley or a spreadsheet, we handle the full transfer for free. From SAFEs and option grants to vesting schedules, and you check everything is right before we switch you over. You can see your cap table in Cake before you pay. Most migrations finish in 48 hours (2 to 5 business days).
How do add-ons work?
Add-ons are available on annual plans, and you can add them now or later: 409A valuation ($1,500 a year), QSBS ($500), special purpose vehicle ($500) and stock-based compensation ($500). Pro includes all of them.
Can I pay quarterly?
Yes, on Build and Pro. Team is billed annually. Paying annually for Build saves $500 a year. Add-ons are only available on annual plans.
