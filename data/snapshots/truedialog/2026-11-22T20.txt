Frequently Asked Questions
Clear, predictable pricing. Built for enterprise communications.
How is TrueDialog’s pricing structured?
Our contracts are annual contracts, and our pricing is based on a platform fee plus a contracted monthly message volume. Our pricing is transparent, with no hidden mark-ups or fees. This ensures fair, transparent and predictable costs. Customers with higher volumes receive significant volume discounts.
Are there setup fees or onboarding costs?
No. We do not charge any setup fees. Onboarding, training, account provisioning, and compliance guidance are included in your platform subscription at no extra charge.
Is Rich Business Communication (RCS) available?
Yes, TrueDialog is proud to offer a visual composer for RCS messages, one of the first in the market. Front line teams can launch RCS campaigns without the need for developers. Rich Business Messaging (RBM) is the future of text messaging.
Are there overage fees if I exceed my message limit?
Yes. Messages sent above your contracted volume are billed at a simple, transparent per-message rate. Overage rates are published and predictable, with no hidden surcharges.
What if my messaging is seasonal?
We understand many customers have seasonal spikes. Our team will help structure your contract to accommodate fluctuations, so you avoid over-contracting during slower months.
Do you offer annual billing discounts?
Yes. Customers who pay annually receive a discount.
Do you charge sales tax or carrier fees?
Applicable state/local taxes are applied where required. Carrier pass-through fees are billed directly and transparently.
How do you ensure compliance with carrier regulations?
Our platform has an automated 10DLC registration feature, and our team partners with you for all 10DLC and short code registrations. Further, we offer guidance on opt-in/opt-out handling and TCPA compliance so you stay fully protected.
What type of support is included?
All customers receive U.S.-based expert support by phone, email, and chat. Enterprise customers are assigned a dedicated Customer Success Manager. Our expert support team is available by phone and chat M-F from 7a-7p Eastern. We area also available by email, text, support ticket 24/7. We provide emergency support nights and weekends for urgent issues.
How does enterprise SMS pricing scale with volume?
Pricing drops as volume increases. TrueDialog offers automatic volume discounts and even deeper committed‑use pricing for very high send counts, giving enterprises lower per‑message costs as they scale.
What fees are typically included in high-volume SMS pricing?
High‑volume programs usually include a platform plan, per‑message rates, and optional add‑ons like short codes or branded URLs. TrueDialog keeps fees simple and transparent, no markups, no hidden charges, so you only pay for what you use.
How do carrier and compliance fees affect SMS costs?
Carriers charge small per‑message pass‑through fees based on the route and message type, and some campaigns require 10DLC registration or vetting fees. TrueDialog passes these through at cost, helping teams stay compliant without inflated expenses.
