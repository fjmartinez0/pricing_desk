Questions worth asking
Straight answers on how the one plan works.
Can I start small?+
The public plan starts at the price shown above, billed annually. There is no smaller tier, because there is no reduced version of the product. You get the complete platform from the start. What varies is the usage part, which follows how your customers adopt analytics. You can test the full product on a free trial before committing.
What happens after the free trial?+
The trial gives you the complete product, including white-label, self-service and AI. When you are ready to go live, the walkthrough sets up your plan.
How does usage change what I pay?+
Usage is measured monthly. Your licence includes 500 AI conversations and 100 million Warp rows. Beyond the Warp volume you pay €0.25 / $0.25 per extra million rows. Beyond the AI volume you pay the unit rate fixed in your contract, so the rate holds when adoption moves. Some contracts scale on monthly active users instead, agreed at signature.
What happens if adoption spikes?+
You pay the same unit rate on the extra volume. No retroactive back-billing, no re-tiering, no renegotiation before renewal.
What's a Monthly Active User?+
A Monthly Active User is an end user who actively uses your embedded analytics experience during a monthly billing period. It is not an internal user seat.
What's an AI conversation?+
An AI conversation is a governed interaction between an end user and Luzmo AI in your product. The plan includes 500 conversations per month.
What's Warp?+
Warp is data acceleration for higher-volume analytics workloads. Your licence includes 100 million synced rows every month, then €0.25 / $0.25 per extra million. It is capacity, not a locked feature or a separate product tier. The volume of data you analyse is never capped.
Is billing annual only?+
Yes. The public price is billed annually, which keeps your platform fee fixed for the full contract term.
