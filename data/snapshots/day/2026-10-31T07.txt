FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Do you charge for seats, usage, or something else?
We charge per agent. Each virtual worker (Agent) you deploy to a human colleague is a flat monthly rate. No per-seat fees, no usage-based pricing.
Do I need to pay for everyone on my team?
No. Only pay for the virtual workers (Agents) you deploy to your human colleagues to manage.
Do you offer annual billing?
Yes, we offer annual billing with a 20% discount. Contact us to set up annual billing for your account.
Can I start with a lower tier and upgrade later?
Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade your agent tier any time. Billing adjusts immediately, no waiting for the next cycle.
Can I have more than one agent?
Yes. Each agent gets its own job description, skills, and pool of automated skill slots. Two Professional agents give you 10 automated skills across two specialized roles — many teams pair a BDR agent for prospecting with an AE agent for pipeline.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No long-term contracts, no commitments. Cancel anytime from your account settings.
