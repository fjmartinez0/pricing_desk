Frequently Asked Questions
Does Breezy HR have a free plan?
No. Breezy doesn't have a free plan, but you can try every feature free for 14 days with no credit card. The trial includes 100,000 Breezy Intelligence credits, enough to have AI score about 200 applicants, and the credits never expire. Paid plans start at $157 a month when you pay annually.
How much does Breezy HR cost?
Breezy has four plans:
Startup: $157 a month billed annually, or $189 billed monthly
Growth: $273 a month billed annually, or $329 billed monthly
Business: $439 a month billed annually, or $529 billed monthly
Custom Pro: priced by quote
Every plan includes unlimited users and unlimited open positions, and the price doesn't change as your company grows. Annual billing gets you two months free. Breezy Intelligence, the AI that scores and screens applicants, can be added to any plan starting at $30 per 100,000 credits.
Which Breezy plan is right for my team?
Startup is for teams that hire regularly. It covers job posting to more than 50 job boards, a branded careers site, interview self-scheduling, video interviews, and email and calendar sync.
Growth is for teams that are scaling. It adds multiple hiring pipelines, scorecards, interview guides, employee referrals, reference checks, e-signatures and up to five locations per job.
Business is for high-volume hiring. It adds offer management, approvals, custom roles and permissions, HRIS integrations, assessment integrations and priority support.
Custom Pro includes everything in Business, plus a dedicated account manager.
Breezy Intelligence works with every plan, so you get AI applicant scoring, resume fraud checks and candidate summaries whichever plan you pick.
What AI recruiting features does Breezy have?
Breezy's AI tools are called Breezy Intelligence:
Applicant Insights scores each applicant from 0 to 10 against your job description and up to five criteria you choose, and explains each score. Personal details are removed before scoring, and your team still makes every hiring decision. Applicant Insights is independently audited by Warden AI, and the results are public.
Resume Audit flags resumes that look auto-generated, copied or stuffed with keywords.
Candidate Email Verification sends applicants a code and filters out disposable email addresses, which keeps spam applications out of your pipeline.
Activity Summary turns a candidate's emails, notes and interviews into a short recap with suggested next steps.
The Breezy MCP server connects Breezy to AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT, so you can review candidates, move stages and add notes from those tools.
Interview Analysis scores recorded interviews against your criteria and is sold as a separate subscription.
How do Breezy Intelligence credits work?
Breezy Intelligence runs on credits, starting at $30 per 100,000. Scoring one applicant uses about 500 credits, and a resume audit uses up to 600. That means 100,000 credits covers about 200 applicant scores. Credits never expire, so you aren't paying for unused capacity in slow hiring months. Every free trial starts with 100,000 credits.
How does Breezy compare to Workable?
Breezy charges a flat price per plan no matter how many employees you have. Workable raises its prices as your headcount grows. Breezy's Growth plan is $273 a month with unlimited users and positions, and you can pay month to month on any plan.
On AI, Breezy Intelligence scores every person who applies and shows its reasoning. Workable's AI agent scores only the candidates it sources. Breezy also checks resumes for fraud and verifies applicant emails. Breezy is rated 4.5 out of 5 on Capterra and 4.4 out of 5 on G2.
How do I switch to Breezy from Workable?
Breezy's implementation team moves your data from Workable for free, usually within five business days. That includes your candidates, jobs, notes, attachments and stage history. Once you're live, Breezy Intelligence starts scoring new applicants against your criteria from the first day, so your team can work through the pipeline faster after the move.
How does Breezy handle a high volume of applicants?
Breezy Intelligence scores every applicant against your criteria as they apply, flags suspicious resumes and filters out spam applications, so your team starts with the strongest candidates instead of reading every resume. Bulk actions and candidate automations move groups of applicants through your pipeline, and you can build a different pipeline for each type of role.
What does Breezy integrate with?
Every plan connects to Google Workspace and Office 365 email and calendars, Slack, background screening providers, and Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams for video interviews. The Business plan adds HRIS integrations and assessment tools like Criteria Corp and HackerRank. Breezy Intelligence also connects to AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT through the Breezy MCP server, so your recruiters can work with candidate data from the tools they already use.
Is Breezy HR a good fit for growing and midsize companies?
Yes. Breezy is built for growing and midsize companies, and more than 17,000 companies use it to hire. Every plan includes unlimited users, so hiring managers and interviewers don't cost extra seats, and your price stays the same as your headcount grows. Breezy Intelligence was designed for midsize hiring teams that get more applicants than they can review by hand. It scores each one, flags fraud and summarizes candidate history. EU and UK customers get EU data centers and GDPR tools on every plan.
