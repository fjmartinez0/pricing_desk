FAQs
How is a Bark Phone different from a phone with the Bark app?
Because Bark’s software is built directly into the phone, it automatically monitors texts, emails, social media, and apps. Bark also can't be deleted from the Bark Phone, which helps ensure the settings you put into this cell phone for kids will stay.
The Bark Phone also offers exclusive features that the app doesn’t, including:
Contact approvals
App install approvals
Remote alarms
Daily screen time limits
Completely tamper-proof settings
Does a Bark Watch have to be connected to a Bark Phone to work?
Nope! The Bark Watch is a standalone device with its own phone number, talk & text, and other features. There's no need to purchase a Bark Phone when you’re buying the Bark Watch.
How much does the Bark Watch cost?
The Bark Watch is a one-time purchase of $288, which covers the device only. A Bark service plan is required to activate your Bark Watch.

Cell service and parental controls
A Bark App subscription for the entire family, covering unlimited kids and devices, including phones and tablets you already own

How much does the Bark app cost?
Pricing depends on your child's device. A single subscription covers every child and device in your family, with no limit on accounts, devices, or kids.
iOS (iPhone/iPad) and Android: $14/month or $99/year
With either plan, you can:
Monitor and receive alerts for your child's social media, texts, email, and web activity
Manage screen time and block specific websites and apps
Track your child's location with live GPS and location alerts
For iPhone and iPad families: Apple's privacy protections mean third-party apps can't read what's on your child's device. The Bark Sync helps close that gap. It's a smart charger that scans your child's iPhone or iPad for potential issues every time it plugs in, no extra steps required. The Bark Sync is optional and available for $6/month or $49/year, and requires a Bark app subscription.
How does content monitoring work?
Add any of your child's devices or social media accounts to be monitored. There's no limit, so you can connect as many devices and accounts as you need on every plan. When you connect a device, Bark will also scan up to 60 days of its text history to catch anything you may have missed.
For some social media platforms, monitoring requires entering your child's login credentials during setup. This lets Bark access the account's activity directly. We'll let you know during setup which platforms need this.
Once connected, our AI scans text, images, video, and audio for potential dangers, including conversations in English, Spanish, and Afrikaans, and sends you alerts for anything concerning.
You can also customize how sensitive you'd like the alerts to be. Don't care about profanity but want all alerts about drugs? No problem.
Does Bark have an iOS app?
Yes! Download the Bark parent app here to review alerts, add children, connect accounts, update notification settings, and more.
You'll also need to install the Bark Kids app on your child's iPhone or iPad — it's what enables Bark to block apps and sites and track your child's location.
To monitor texts, photos, videos, and more on your child's iOS device, you'll need the Bark Sync — a smart charger that scans your child's device every time it plugs in (or the Bark Desktop App on a home computer). It's the easiest way to get the most out of Bark on iOS. Learn more about Bark for iOS here.
What can I do with Bark's parental controls for iOS?
Bark's parental controls for iOS let you manage screen time, block websites and apps, and track your child's location 24/7, all from the Bark app.
To get full content monitoring for things like texts, iMessages, photos, videos, WhatsApp messages, and Kik messages, you'll also need the Bark Sync. Apple's strict privacy restrictions prevent third-party apps from directly monitoring iOS devices, which is why we built it: a smart charger that scans your child's iPhone or iPad each time it plugs in to close that gap.
Some platforms require you to connect by entering your child's login credentials during setup. We'll let you know during setup which ones need this.
Once everything's set up, if something concerning is found, you'll get a timely alert so you can check in and make sure everything is okay. Learn more about what Bark monitors on iOS devices here.
Together, these features build a powerful digital safety net to help you protect your child online and in real life.
Does Bark have an Android app?
Yes! Download the Bark parent app here to review alerts, add children, connect accounts, update notification settings, and more.
You'll also need to install the Bark Kids app on your child's Android device — it's what enables Bark to monitor content, manage screen time, block websites, and track your child's location. Learn more about Bark for Android here.
What can Bark's parental controls for Android devices do?
Bark's parental controls for Androids give you the power to monitor your child’s online activities in a groundbreaking way.
Our content monitoring uses advanced AI to scan messages, posts, comments, replies, web searches, and so much more to find potential dangers. If something concerning is found, you’ll get a timely alert so you can check in and make sure everything is okay. Learn more about what Bark monitors on Android devices here.
In addition to monitoring, Bark also lets you manage screen time, block websites & apps, and track location 24/7.
These key features work together to build a powerful digital safety net to help you protect your child online and in real life.

Smart TVs

Tablets
Computers
Cell phones
How do the Bark Home's internet parental controls work?
The Bark Home’s internet parental controls work by allowing you to block websites and apps and set screen time schedules on your child’s home devices — things like TVs, tablets, gaming consoles, and computers. It works on phones — iOS and Android — too!
You can also help protect them from potentially inappropriate content like porn, gaming sites, and excessive violence. You can also set up schedules where your kid can only access the internet on these devices at certain times, like after dinner or only on weekends. It’s up to you!
The Bark Home is a small device that plugs into your home’s Wi-Fi router, and it works by allowing or not allowing content at different times.
Our internet parental controls can give you peace of mind that your child is not getting into anything they shouldn’t, which is the perfect way to help keep them safe online and in real life.
