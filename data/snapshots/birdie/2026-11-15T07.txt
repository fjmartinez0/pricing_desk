FAQs
With rostering, care management, finance and more in one place, Birdie connects your people, processes and data, so everyone’s on the same page.
How is pricing calculated?
No matter your business size, we’ve designed a pricing model that sets you up for success. Each of Birdie’s plans has a monthly cost based on the number of hours of care you schedule. This ensures your investment in technology ties to your revenues rather than your cost base - meaning that we're all aligned to your success, and there's no need for shared logins or the like. You'll get full transparency into how your invoice is calculated, and as your business grows, we'll work with you to ensure your investment in Birdie is driving a strong return for your business. To get a quote, please get in touch with us!
What kind of support do you offer? Do I have to pay for it?
Through years of working with care agencies, we’ve seen that online chat is the best way to get your query dealt with as quickly as possible. Birdie’s live chat means you and your team can reach out to a member of our support team and expect a response in 10 minutes or less. The best thing? It’s free. However, we know that for some people, there's something important about being able to speak to a person to solve a problem. Our Premium Support offers just that, for an additional fee. You can also access hundreds of articles through the Birdie Help Centre, talking you through the key aspects of the system.
How much time will it take me to get Birdie set up?
We’ve built our app and software to be intuitive. That means that you won’t need much training to get started, and nor will your care staff. There’s a bit of work on your side to get all your clients added to the app, but how long this takes is up to you. And of course, the Birdie team are on hand to help you every step of the way, to make sure your transition is as smooth as possible. You can book a demo any time.
What types of care businesses use Birdie?
Birdie supports care businesses of all sizes to lead the way in care and grow to new heights with the power of data. From providers getting ready to onboard their first client to those with years of operating behind them, Birdie can support you. Franchise business or independent model? No problem. Local authority, private contracts or a mix of both? We’ve got you.
How is data security and GDPR compliance handled?
Birdie takes data security and privacy very seriously. We are fully compliant with the General Data Protection Regulations and Data Protection Act 2018 which ensures that your personal information is protected, handled and stored correctly. We don’t just meet, but exceed, the NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit standards and are also certified to meet ISO27001 and Cyber Essentials Plus.
How do I measure care quality?
The Birdie Quality Score (Q-Score) is created in line with the CQC’s Key Lines of Enquiry. Our Q-Score report will help you to provide the highest quality of care and work to achieve an "Outstanding" rating. This powerful data can also help you know where you may stand for inspections at any moment. The Q-Score is based on the information you input throughout Birdie.
What if I have time left on my existing contract?
If you're using an alternative system but want to use Birdie before you get to the end of your contract then speak with us and we'll see what we can do to help!
