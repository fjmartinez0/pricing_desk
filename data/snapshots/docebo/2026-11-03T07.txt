Frequently asked questions
What counts as an active or billable user?+
Pricing is based on active learner usage, not simply the number of accounts in your platform. Choose the user model that best matches how your learners engage with training.
What happens if we exceed our usage plan?+
You can continue supporting your learners as usage grows. Additional users are handled according to your agreement, and you can upgrade your plan when a higher tier better fits your needs.
Can we choose monthly active users or annual users?+
Yes. Docebo offers Monthly Active User, Yearly Active User, and Registered Active User models, so you can choose the approach that best matches your audience and engagement patterns.
Are administrators and system owners included without consuming paid seats?+
Pricing is based on active learner usage, not simply the number of accounts. We’ll help you account for administrators, instructors, and other platform users when selecting the right model.
Can we trial or run a proof of concept before committing?+
Evaluation options depend on your use case. Your Docebo team can help define a focused proof of concept around your goals, workflows, integrations, and success criteria.
What support, onboarding, implementation, and ongoing services are included?+
Every package includes a defined onboarding and support experience. Higher-touch options and professional services are available for complex implementations, migration, custom integrations, and ongoing guidance.
How are integrations and connectors counted?+
Docebo supports 400+ integrations across HR, CRM, identity, content, analytics, collaboration, and more. Plans include a defined integration allowance. Basic SSO, webhooks, and common API use cases are supported, while embedded experiences and additional integration needs may require an add-on or professional services.
What contract terms, billing cadence, renewal, and price protection apply?+
Commercial terms are tailored to your organization and documented in your agreement. Your Docebo representative can walk you through available term lengths, billing options, renewal requirements, and any applicable price-protection terms.
Can we start with a smaller scope and add users, modules, or capabilities later?+
Yes. You can expand your Docebo solution as your learning programs grow by adding users, modules, integrations, or services. Your account team will help align changes with your goals and agreement.
How do AI credits work?+
Docebo offers an AI Credit Pool for flexible, shared usage or a Per-Seat option for predictable budgeting. Usage-based AI operations draw from credits, while core AI experiences, including the AI assistant, remain included. Admins can define access, set limits, and monitor usage in the Usage Dashboard.
