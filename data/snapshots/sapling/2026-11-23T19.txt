Frequently Asked Questions
I subscribed to a Sapling premium plan, but my premium features don’t seem to have been activated.
First, make sure you've registered an account with the same email you used to subscribe. You should receive a confirmation email with instructions within a few minutes after subscribing.
If you don’t receive premium suggestions on websites you already have open, please try refreshing the page.
Do you offer a free trial?
Yes, for individuals. Once you register and confirm your email, you’ll automatically be enrolled in a free trial. The trial is available once per account.
Should I choose Pro or the API for AI detection?
Pro is a flat monthly or annual plan for checking documents yourself on sapling.ai and in the Sapling extensions and add-ins, up to 100,000 characters per check. Results checked on sapling.ai can be shared with a link.
The API is billed per character with a one-time $5 minimum, accepts up to 200,000 characters per request, and is built for adding AI detection to your own software. API responses are returned as JSON and do not include a shareable result link. Both use the same default detector model.
I didn't receive a confirmation email.
Please remember to check your spam folder. If you still can't find the confirmation email, please contact support@sapling.ai. Your IT admin may also be filtering emails from @sapling.ai.
How does pricing work for teams?
Pricing varies depending on the number of users/seats and the duration of the contract. Please contact us for a quote.
What payment methods are supported?
Sapling uses Stripe for payments and subscriptions. You can pay with a credit card by clicking the “Subscribe” button above.
If you wish to purchase Sapling using another method, please try here or contact us.
Do you have a discounted plan for students, nonprofits, and military members?
Yes; please contact us. Nonprofits can also see our nonprofit pricing.
What security measures does Sapling have in place?
Please contact us for detailed documents on our security procedures as well as Sapling’s enterprise-grade infrastructure and testing.
What if I have more questions?
You may find the answer at our billing-specific FAQ page here. Or just reach out via email and we’ll get back to you shortly!
