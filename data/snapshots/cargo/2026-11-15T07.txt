Frequently Asked Questions
Get answers to common questions about Cargo's features, pricing, and implementation
What are credits and how are they consumed?
Every usage is paid from one pool of credits:
- Tools and actions: what the interface shows for each. An extractor that brings records into a model costs its price per record.
- AI agents: model tokens, per 1,000 tokens, at each model's rate.
- Workflow steps: 100 steps = 1 credit.
- Saved records: 1,000 inserts = 1 credit.
- Hosting: 50 credits a month per app, 15 per worker, 10 per custom domain, plus 10,000 requests or analytics events = 1 credit.
- Mailboxes: 100 to 160 credits a month each.
Can I change my plan anytime?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll get immediate access to additional credits. If you downgrade, your new plan takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
What happens if I run out of credits?
You'll receive notifications as you approach your credit limit. When you run out, you can either upgrade your plan or purchase additional credits. Your existing workflows will pause until you add more credits, no data is lost.
Do you offer refunds?
We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with Cargo within the first 14 days of a paid subscription, contact us for a full refund. No questions asked. The refund policy states how to ask and how a plan change works.
What are workflows?
Workflows are automated processes designed to manage leads or companies. They allow you for example to score leads, enrich their data, perform AI-driven research, and assign them to the appropriate sequence or team.
What are models?
Models are data tables that serve as inputs for your workflows. They can include CRM like Salesforce, third-party data from sources like Sales Navigator, or product data like Amplitude.
Is Cargo secure?
Protecting customer data is our top priority. Cargo maintains SOC 2 Type II compliance and supports SSO authentication. For more details, contact us at security@getcargo.io.
How does Cargo compare to alternatives?
Cargo combines data enrichment, AI agents, and workflow automation in one platform, replacing multiple point solutions. See how we compare to Clay, Zapier, and Workato.
