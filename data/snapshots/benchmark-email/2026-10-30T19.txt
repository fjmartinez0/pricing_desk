FAQs
What happens if I exceed the contact limit?
No panic necessary. If you go over your contact limit, we’ll notify you and give you the option to upgrade your plan so you can keep sending without interruption. We won’t auto-upgrade or surprise you. You’re in control, always.
Can I upgrade at anytime?
Absolutely. If you’re growing fast, you can upgrade your plan at any time right from your account settings. Need to do more with less? Downgrading is easy. Managing your plan and usage is totally in your hands.
Do I get all features in the free plan?
Yes. Every feature is on every plan, including Free. What changes is how much room you get. Free covers up to 500 contacts, 1,000 emails a month, one user, one domain, two API keys, and 150 AI credits a month. Paid plans raise those limits, add list verification, and make Benchmark branding removable.
Why pay for 500 contacts when the free plan covers 500?
Room to send. Free covers 500 contacts and 1,000 emails a month. Pro at 500 contacts sends up to 5,000 emails a month, adds 1,000 AI credits, includes list verification, lets you add up to 10 users, and removes Benchmark branding. It's $12 a month (or $10 a month billed annually) to get all of the Pro perks for the 500 contact plan.
What are AI credits?
AI credits power the AI Assistant in the email and form builders. Bigger requests use more credits, so writing a full paragraph from detailed instructions uses more than a quick headline. Free plans get 150 credits a month, enough to try it out. Paid plans get 1,000. Your credits refresh at the start of each billing cycle.
Can I remove the Benchmark branding?
Yes, on any paid plan. On the free plan, Benchmark branding shows on every form and email. Upgrade, and you can turn it off in Footer Settings.
How are additional users billed?
All plans include 1 user. On paid plans, expanding your team is a breeze. We bill $15 per month a la carte for each additional user, no plan upgrade required. You’ll know exactly what the cost is before anything changes, so there are no surprises.
Just a heads-up: if you're on the free plan, you’ll need to move to a paid plan before you can invite teammates.
What is the send limit?
Send limits are set to keep your sender reputation safe!
Free plans can send up to 1,000 emails a month.
Paid plans can send up to 10 times the contact limit of their plan each month, so a 5,000-contact plan can send 50,000 emails.
Can I change my billing frequency?
Absolutely. You can switch from monthly to annual billing. As your needs change, you can also upgrade or downgrade your plan to find the option that works best for your business.
What methods of payment do you accept?
We like to keep things simple—and flexible. Here’s how you can pay for your Benchmark Email plan:
Cards
We accept all of the most popular plastic: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Diners Club, JCB, and China UnionPay payments from customers worldwide.
Digital Wallets
Prefer to tap and go? We’ve got you covered with Alipay, Amazon Pay, Apple Pay, Cash App Pay, Google Pay.
Stripe Link
Already use Stripe for other purchases? Great! Just breeze through checkout using Stripe Link, no extra steps needed.
