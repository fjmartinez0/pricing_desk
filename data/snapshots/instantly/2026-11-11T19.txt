Can I really connect and warm up unlimited accounts with one subscription?
Yes! You can connect and warm as many accounts as you need from any provider.
I’ve never done email outreach before, will this work for me?
Absolutely, we’ve even prepared a full step-by-step tutorial on how we launch our campaigns.
What if I need to have more than 25,000 leads or send more than 100,000 emails?
You can upgrade your account to send as many emails as you need :).
Do you also provide leads I can send emails to?
Yes we do! You can use Instantly Lead Finder to find your ideal prospects.
Is Instantly completely automated?
After you’ve set up your campaigns - it runs completely on autopilot. All you need to do is make sure there’s enough leads in your campaigns and that’s it.
