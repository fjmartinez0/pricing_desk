FAQ
What size companies do you work with?
We focus on businesses worth $500K to $100M, and most buyer demand is for companies worth $1M to $10M. The Seller Plan requires at least $100K in annual profit (SDE or EBITDA). Smaller businesses can still get a free valuation and test buyer interest with a free anonymous listing.
What financials do you need to complete the free valuation?
We can complete a valuation with just one year of financial data. However, providing more information helps create a more accurate and comprehensive assessment. Ideally, you can share your Profit & Loss (P&L) statement for the trailing 12 months or the most recent full year. If those aren't available, we can also work with your tax returns.
How do you get to your valuation?
At Baton, we use a combination of proven methods to determine the value of your business, including cash flow multiples, discounted cash flow (DCF) analysis, and sales comparisons. We also incorporate information and insights from your answers during the sign-up process. This personalized assessment helps us highlight your business’s strengths, reflecting both its current performance and future potential for a well-rounded valuation.
What is your fee structure?
Your business’s valuation and the 1:1 meeting with our team to review results are always free, it’s part of our mission! You can also host an anonymous listing free of charge to gauge buyer interest.
On our Seller Plan there is no monthly retainer. You list with Baton exclusively for a three-month minimum, then month to month, and we take a 6% success fee only when your business sells. Our current average time to close is 6 months.
What if my business doesn't sell?
Sellers get offers in 6 weeks, and deals average 6 months from listing to close. If we don’t close within the three-month minimum, you continue month to month and can cancel any time after that. There is no monthly retainer, so if your business doesn’t sell you pay nothing. We only earn our success fee when we sell your business, which is why we are so committed to doing so!
What if I sign on with Baton and a buyer I am already speaking with becomes more serious and is eager to purchase my business?
No problem! We can put a clause in the contract that if your buyer submits an LOI within 60 days, we'll lower our commission to 3% from 6%. But we have leveraged owner-fed buyer leads against our own, increasing the final offer amount.
