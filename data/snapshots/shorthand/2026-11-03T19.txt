How do I publish my content?
We offer two main publishing methods:
Shorthand-hosted publishing: our simplest option, available on all annual plans.
We manage your content for you on Amazon S3 servers using the CloudFront content delivery network to deliver stories quickly and reliably to your audience.
You can bring your own subdomain (e.g., yoursubdomain.yourbrand.com) or choose a shorthandstories.com subdomain (e.g., yourbrand.shorthandstories.com).
Once your content is live, your audience can view directly via your chosen domain, or you can embed it in any web page — including your top-level domain or intranet site — by copying and pasting a short code snippet.
Self-hosted publishing.
Manage your content on your own hosting. We support a range of self-hosted publishing methods, including self-managed Amazon S3, WordPress and Drupal plugins, and API publishing.
Need help with selecting a publishing method?
Get in touch
and we'd be happy to advise on which one is best for you.
What is a published story?
A published story is any single page of content that has been made live on a Shorthand-hosted or self-hosted site, or exported for publishing via ZIP download or API. If you’re on a plan with capped story inclusions, such as Core or On Demand, publishing a story uses one credit.
If you unpublish a story, the credit is not returned. However, you can update the story at any time and re-publish it to the same or a different URL without using an additional credit.
Do publishing credits expire?
Yes. Unused publishing credits expire at the end of each yearly cycle and do not roll over. New credits are added on your plan anniversary. This applies whether your plan is billed monthly or yearly.
How do I cancel a subscription?
To cancel your subscription at the end of your current billing cycle, simply get in touch with our team.
After you cancel your subscription, you will still be able to log in to your workspace, but you will no longer be able to publish or export content. Content published with any Shorthand-hosted publishing method will no longer be live after the subscription ends.
What currencies do you accept?
All monthly billed plans are priced and paid in US dollars (USD). Annually billed plans can be priced and paid in US dollars, Pound Sterling (GBP), Euro (EUR), and Australian dollars (AUD). The prices we offer in GBP, EUR and AUD will generally reflect the listed USD price converted at prevailing foreign exchange rates, but may take into account other regional considerations. Please get in touch with our sales team to discuss an annual plan in one of our supported currencies.

What support do you provide?
We have flexible support and customer success offerings to suit every user of Shorthand — whether you're a member of a small team or a large enterprise.
All plans include free training webinars, access to a comprehensive knowledge base, and email support.
On the Core plan, take advantage of design support for one story per year in the form of a co-creation session. On the Unlimited plan, you’ll receive three co-creation sessions.
Enterprise plan customers receive priority support and a dedicated Success Manager — with options for custom SLAs and bundling custom design and development services into your annual plan.
Regardless of which tier you’re on, our support is something customers rave about. Don't just take our word for it — check out our reviews on G2.com.
Is Shorthand secure and suitable for regulated industries?
Yes. Shorthand is SOC 2 Type II compliant and ISO 27001 certified, and is used by banks, government agencies, and other organisations with strict security and compliance requirements. Security is built into how we run the product, infrastructure, and team.
