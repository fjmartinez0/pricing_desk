Pricing Questions?
Can I cancel anytime?
No. Subscriptions run for the full term, and mid-term cancellations aren't eligible for a refund. If you need flexibility, Project Intelligence Starter is available month-to-month. Takeoff and other Project Intelligence products are offered on annual contracts with quarterly or annual billing. We also include onboarding, training, and tools to help ensure you see value quickly.
Do I need to commit to an annual contract?
Not always. Project Intelligence Starter is month-to-month. All other products are sold on annual agreements with quarterly or annual billing options.
Do you offer a free trial or proof of value?
Yes. For Project Intelligence Professional and Takeoff products, our team offers a guided proof-of-value with training so you can evaluate the platform before committing. If you prefer a self-serve option, Project Intelligence Starter is available month-to-month.
Can I get a refund if I don't use the product?
No. We don't offer refunds for unused time, features, or one-time purchases. That said, our training and support teams are here to help you adopt the platform and get a strong return on your investment.
Can I pause my subscription instead of canceling?
No. Subscriptions can't be paused, and payments can't be suspended during the contract term.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. You can upgrade at any time. Downgrades take effect at renewal, not mid-term. During your contract, we can also tailor your setup to better fit your needs.
Do you charge overages?
No. Pricing is fixed and includes access to the full project and contact data in your selected market(s). There are no overage fees for Takeoff products.
Do you offer discounts for volume or multi-year agreements?
Yes. Discounts are often available for larger packages — such as multiple markets, products, or additional seats. Talk to Sales to build a plan that works for you.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
How does seat pricing work?
Professional is $2,200/yr for your first seat, then $1,700 for each additional seat, so you can scale the team as you grow.
What is Auto Takeoff?
Auto Takeoff, part of the Takeoff Boost™ suite and available in ConstructConnect Takeoff Professional, is the AI tool that automatically detects and measures items on your drawings for you. That means you spend less time tracing and more time on the estimate.
Can I cancel anytime?
No. Subscriptions run for the full term, and mid-term cancellations aren't eligible for a refund.
Can I pause my subscription instead of canceling?
No. Subscriptions can't be paused, and payments can't be suspended during the contract term.
Can I try ConstructConnect Takeoff before I buy it?
Absolutely. Talk to sales and we'll set you up with a trial so you can experience ConstructConnect Takeoff Professional on a real project of your own before you commit.
