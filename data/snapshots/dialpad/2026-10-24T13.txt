How does conversation-based pricing work?
Dialpad prices Agentic AI the same way customers experience it: one conversation at a time.
You purchase a pool of AI Agent Credits
Credits are used only when AI delivers value
If no work is done, you’re not charged
A conversation is billable only when the AI retrieves information or executes a real action like scheduling, routing, or order lookup.

What type of analytics do I get access to?
All plans offer real-time dashboards with metrics for all calls, individual agent metrics, as well as call history and historical data that allows you to dive into insights about your team and their calls anytime you need. Premium plans include Admin and Reporting APIs. Additional analytics are available with respective AI feature add-ons.
How does Dialpad Support help with agent coaching and onboarding?
Managers can coach and monitor agent performance with features like Listen In, Barge In, and Take Over. Real-time guidance prompts agents with steps to follow for common questions that arise in customer calls as they happen.
Do I have to purchase an annual contract?
Our Advanced accounts are available on both month-to-month as well as annual plans, while Premium accounts are available on annual plans only. Annual plans are our most popular option as they provide a discount from our regular pricing.
How does Dialpad Support reduce average handle time?
Smart call routing, real-time assistance, and automatic call summaries help agents resolve issues faster, reducing handling time.
How does Dialpad’s AI Live Coach help agents during calls?
AI Live Coach provides knowledge base suggestions and generated responses, ensuring agents have instant access to relevant information.
What are Dialpad’s AI Live Coach Cards?
AI Live Coach Cards surface relevant help articles and responses based on what the customer is saying, enabling faster resolutions.
How does Dialpad Support help to improve CSAT scores?
Dialpad enhances agent performance through training insights, sentiment analysis, and post-call analytics, leading to better customer interactions and satisfaction. Plus, AI CSAT scores 100% of calls, so support teams can easily measure CSAT and learn how to improve it.

What coaching features are available with Dialpad Sell?
With Dialpad Sell, managers can coach and monitor their reps with features like Listen In, Barge In, and Take Over.
Additionally, managers can see call quality in real-time with sentiment analysis and call transcription. Dialpad AI empowers reps with real-time guidance, such as "Talking too fast", and keeps interactions consistent with up-to-date knowledge.
What real-time reporting do you offer?
Both Advanced and Premium Dialpad Sell plans offer a real-time dashboard with metrics for inbound and outbound calls, individual rep metrics, as well as call history and historical data that allows you to dive into insights about your team and their calls anytime you need. Additionally, Premium plans include Admin and Reporting APIs.
Do I have to purchase Dialpad Sell on a contract?
Our Advanced accounts are available on both month-to-month as well as annual plans, while Premium accounts are available on annual plans only. Annual plans are our most popular option, as you lock down the same rate for the duration of your contract, along with a discount from our regular pricing.
What type of support is included with my account?
Advanced plans get access to 24/7 phone support, while Premium plans get 24/7 phone support and Enterprise SLA. We also have a wide range of help center articles available at help.dialpad.com.
How does Dialpad Sell automate sales processes?
Dialpad automates tasks like call logging, CRM updates, post-call summaries, and follow-up reminders, reducing manual work and improving efficiency.
What are Dialpad’s AI Playbooks?
AI Playbooks provide structured sales strategies with real-time prompts and guidance, helping reps navigate different sales scenarios with best practices.
Can Dialpad Sell integrate with CRM platforms?
Yes, Dialpad integrates seamlessly with CRMs like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zoho, automatically syncing call data and insights.

Can I use Dialpad internationally?
You can place your calls with Dialpad all over the world. Our international rates are some of the lowest in the industry. Additionally, we support international local numbers in over 50 countries.
What AI features are included in Dialpad Connect?
You'll get real-time call transcription, voicemail transcriptions, call summaries and automated action items with AI Recaps, and real-time analytics and reporting.
What are Dialpad’s AI Recaps?
AI Recaps provide automatic summaries of calls and meetings, highlighting key points, action items, and decisions made.
Which devices can use Dialpad Connect?
Dialpad is available on desktops, laptops, smartphones (iOS & Android), tablets, and desk phones.
How easy is it to set up Dialpad Connect?
Dialpad Connect is designed for quick deployment with a cloud-based setup that requires minimal IT involvement.
Why might the price vary?
The price you see on the website may differ from your actual price due to regional pricing and currency based on your country. Contact our Sales team to get a custom quote based on your needs, for your region.
