What the minute?
A "minute of video" is simply one minute of your content — which stays consistent regardless of input quality or complexity. We price in minutes, not file size, because it matches with what actually matters (video duration) and lets us optimize your video performance without unpredictable billing surprises.
Oh, and we don’t round up to the nearest minute.

How are we doing the math?
Estimates include applicable credits and plan costs at the listed rates. Contact sales for custom pricing.
Storage assumes 60% cold, 10% infrequent, and 30% frequent storage. Adjust this mix in the additional options for your library. Model retention, proration, and the one-month minimum for basic quality separately.
GB equivalents use 3.33 Mbps as the default. You can adjust that in additional options. Resolution alone doesn't establish the bitrate or duration of a migration library. Delivery inputs should include buffered or preloaded video, even when it isn't watched.
Delivery cost is calculated across available resolutions to simulate typical delivery patterns. For example, 1080p video is split evenly between 720p and 1080p, while 4K is split across all four resolutions.
Selected Mux Robots workflows use your uploaded minutes and estimated asset count. Estimates assume 3 questions per asset for Ask questions, 10-second sampling for Content moderation, one language for Caption translation and Audio translation, and 1 edit per asset for Edit captions.

We hear this a lot. With such affordable pricing, what's the catch? Simple answer: there isn't one. Video should be a utility of the internet, not a luxury. We've built Mux to be scalable and efficient. These smart infrastructure decisions allow us to pass substantial cost savings directly to you, regardless of your size.
You can't build scalable video cheaper yourself, and you shouldn't have to try.
Just-in-time encoding
When preparing a video for streaming, the standard practice is to first encode the whole video into a compatible format and file size, as well as smaller size versions for slower networks. Mux instead has built just-in-time encoding, meaning we only create streaming versions of your video when a viewer is going to watch them. This saves on encoding and storage costs, which we pass on to you with lower input and storage prices. This is also how we offer great features like super fast upload-to-playback, on-demand thumbnails, and instant clipping.
High scale
Mux Video powers billions of streams every month across hundreds of millions of videos, which gives us significant economies of scale just to start. Our infrastructure is then purpose-built for video, letting our experts tune every layer of the stack for cost efficiency and performance compared to generic cloud infrastructure, which has to support every kind of file and workload. We blend multiple CDNs plus our own POPs in order to achieve both lower costs and higher performance. All of these things allow us to pass more savings on to you, both generally and with deeper discounts as you scale. Learn more about our volume tiers and discounts.
Smart infrastructure
Mux is built by people who deeply understand video — not just the tech, but how content lives and breathes in the real world. We know how audience size, engagement patterns, and content life cycles evolve over time, and we bake that knowledge into automated workflows that continuously optimize for cost and efficiency. Some of this happens under the hood, invisible to you. Other times, it shows up clearly in the product — for example, all newly uploaded videos start in cold storage, with up to 60% savings, and are automatically moved back after 30 days of inactivity. You don’t have to think about it — we make smart decisions for you that keep your costs low, especially at scale.

What is video quality?
Quality refers to how faithfully the delivered video matches your source at the same resolution. Higher quality uses more bits and increases costs, but can be beneficial for high-production-value videos. Learn more.

When should I use MP4s or static renditions?
MP4s and other static renditions are often used for background videos on a website or to enable downloading by viewers. Learn more.
What is Mux Robots?
Mux Robots hosts AI workflows for your Mux assets. With simple API calls (or directly in the Mux dashboard) you can summarize a video, moderate it, translate captions, generate chapters, find key moments, and much more. If you already have a preferred AI vendor or want to build your own, use @mux/ai. Learn more.
How do I estimate my baseline video cost?
Your baseline is the video you upload and store: basic quality has free input, and storage is priced per minute stored each month. From there, add delivery for what your viewers watch and Mux Robots for any AI workflows you run. Every plan includes 100,000 free delivery minutes and 100,000 free Robots units each month. Use the pricing calculator above to estimate your monthly cost, or read our estimation guide for a deeper walkthrough.
How does pricing change as I scale?
You can see the full pay-as-you-go usage rates, including automatic volume discounts, in our pricing guide. Mux also offers custom contract pricing for larger workloads, with additional discounts available above $3,000/month. Those contract rates are negotiated separately, require a quote from Mux, and aren't published in our guides. You can estimate pay-as-you-go cost with our pricing calculator.
