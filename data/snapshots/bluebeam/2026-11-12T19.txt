Frequently asked questions
Choosing a Plan
What is Bluebeam Revu?
Bluebeam Revu is our flagship product – a powerful, versatile, and easy-to-use desktop application helping professionals communicate clearly, collaborate effectively, and optimize PDF workflows. It increases teams’ productivity with discipline-specific and customizable markup tools, precise measurements set to drawing scale, real-time collaboration on a single source of truth, and process efficiency tools that streamline your workflows. Check out our release notes to see what’s new.
Will Bluebeam work on my web browser or mobile device?
Yes! Bluebeam subscribers can extend the power of Revu to their web browser or mobile device for seamless collaboration on the go. You can also invite collaborators to mark up in real time via web and mobile – no Revu installation required.
What is Studio?
The Studio environment in Revu serves as your virtual project room for real-time collaboration. Our Core and Complete plans include full Studio capabilities. Studio Projects gives you a single centralized location to store documents—even entire building projects—in the cloud. And Studio Sessions lets project teams review, mark up and update the same files at the same time.
What is Bluebeam Max?
Bluebeam Max is a new premium subscription plan for AEC organizations and professionals who want to gear up for a faster, AI-driven world. Max supercharges Revu with AI to accelerate drawing reviews, automate workflows and bridge the gap between 2D and 3D.
How long is the Bluebeam Max introductory price valid?
Bluebeam is offering Bluebeam Max at an introductory price of $590 USD per user per year. Users who purchase at the introductory price will lock in this price through their renewal in 2027. Subsequent renewals will be at standard list price, announced at a future time.
What operating system does Bluebeam Revu run on?
Bluebeam Revu runs on Windows® and is best used on a laptop or desktop. Subscribers can also extend the power of Revu to mark up or collaborate on the go via web browser or mobile device.
My clients don’t have Bluebeam. If I send them a file, will they be able to view it?
Yes! Non-subscribers can set up a Bluebeam ID to access files. Collaborators can mark up and measure in real time via their web browser or mobile device – no installation required.
What support and training options do you offer?
We offer premium technical support by email and phone, as well as an extensive library of free how-to articles and videos. We also offer an array of training options to help individuals and teams get up to speed with Bluebeam, including self-guided Bluebeam University courses (included with subscription), instructor-led training, expert consulting and more.
What’s the difference between Basics and Core? Why choose Core instead of Basics?
Basics is built strictly for individual PDF viewing, basic length/area markups, and document management. Core is the essential standard for project teams, and includes the ability to host and manage Studio Sessions/Projects, advanced measurements (perimeters, volumes, angles, counts), and crucial CAD plug-ins (AutoCAD, Revit, etc.).
Core gives project managers and engineers full control over workflows, making it the minimum requirement for collaborative project environments. Unlike Basics, Core allows you to initiate collaboration rather than just join it, work directly with CAD files, or use multi-faceted 2D/3D measurement tools.
Should I choose Core or Complete?
Core handles professional markups and standard team collaboration, while Complete is built for power users, estimators, and teams that need to automate and accelerate workflows.
Complete bridges the gap between manual markup and automation. You should upgrade to Complete if your workflow relies on:
Dynamic Fill for fast, complex measurements and vector-based structural boundaries and user-defined manual boundaries.
Quantity Link to instantly sync measurement totals directly with Microsoft Excel.
Batch Automations (Batch Link, Batch Slip Sheet, and Batch Signatures/Seals) that save hours of repetitive document prep on large drawing sets.
What’s the difference between Complete and Max?
Complete delivers heavy-duty automation and takeoff tools, while Max is Bluebeam’s most powerful tier, powered by AI to deliver optimal efficiency and outcomes.
Max includes everything in Complete plus:
AI-powered drawing reviews and comparisons to spot changes and anomalies
AI integration via MCP and AI multi-view Stitching
Magic Markups (Duplicate as, Convert to, Offset) and Connected Studio Sessions with Revit for streamlined, advanced coordination
We recommend Max to teams looking to eliminate manual review bottlenecks using our most powerful technologies.
How do Basics, Core, Complete and Max plans compare overall?
Here’s a simple breakdown:
Basics ($260): Best for individual markup, viewing, and basic document management. (Note: Cannot host Studio Sessions or use advanced measurements)
Core ($330): Best for project teams needing CAD integrations, Studio hosting, and full measurement suites. Most popular choice for standard engineering/PM workflows.
Complete ($440): Best for estimators and teams requiring advanced takeoffs, Excel Quantity Link, and heavy batch automations.
Max ($590): Best for organizations wanting peak performance through AI-powered drawing reviews, Magic Markups, and automated stitching.
Buying and Using Bluebeam
How do I buy Bluebeam and what payment options do you accept?
You can purchase a Bluebeam plan in our webstore or through your reseller. We accept Visa, MasterCard and American Express. To place a purchase order, please email a formal PO as an attachment to [email protected]. Please allow up to three business days for us to process your order. If you’re interested in buying more than 50 plans for your team, check out our enterprise page.
I’m using an older version of Revu, and I want to upgrade to subscription. What are my options?
You can find step-by-step instructions for upgrading your licenses on the Bluebeam Revu 20 EOL page. Please note that Revu 20 reaches End of Support on July 31, 2026, and End of Life (EOL) on December 31, 2026, after which it will no longer receive technical support or updates. To avoid workflow disruptions, visit the Bluebeam Revu 20 EOL page to learn how to transition your team to our modern subscription plans (Basics, Core, Complete, or Max).
Can I buy different Bluebeam plans for different members of my team?
Yes! You can easily assign different Bluebeam plans to different individuals or groups in your organization, based on their needs and workflows. All plans are billed annually per user.
Once I’ve purchased, how do I give myself or other users access to Bluebeam software?
If you’re the purchaser, you’ll be designated as the Org Admin, with the ability to manage user activity for Bluebeam plans. To give yourself or others access to Bluebeam software, log in to the Org Admin with your BBID. Then, add yourself or others to the desired plan(s). Each new user will then receive an email with instructions on how to access both Bluebeam Revu and Bluebeam for web and mobile. For more information, check out the Org Admin Guide.
How do I add new team members to my account?
To add new team members to your account, go to the webstore, log in using your Bluebeam ID (BBID) and purchase the desired number of subscriptions. If you’re the purchaser, you’ll be designated as the Org Admin, with the ability to manage user activity for Bluebeam subscriptions. To give yourself or others access to Bluebeam software, log in to the Org Admin with your BBID. Then, add yourself or others to the desired plan(s). Each new user will then receive an email with instructions on how to access the software. For more information, check out the Org Admin Guide.
What is the Org Admin?
The Org Admin is where org admins can easily add new users plus track and manage their activity, giving them enhanced administrative visibility into plan usage. For more information, check out the Org Admin Guide.
What is your refund policy?
We’ll happily refund your purchase if a request is made within 30 days.
Contact Bluebeam Customer Service with the following information:
Your name, email and phone number
Web order number
Reason for the return
Amount to be refunded
Date of purchase
Note: Credit card purchases will be refunded to the same credit card used for the original purchase. A purchase order will be refunded or issued a credit depending on the status of payment.
