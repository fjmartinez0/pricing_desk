Frequently Asked Questions
How much does Baremetrics cost?
Baremetrics has three plans: Launch starts at $49/month for businesses up to $360K ARR, Growth starts at $189/month for $360K–$3.6M ARR, and Scale starts at $749/month for $3.6M+ ARR.
Is there a discount for annual billing?
Yes. Annual plans can come with up to a 35% discount on the listed prices.
What add-ons does Baremetrics offer?
Two add-ons are available on any plan:
Payment Recovery (+$129/month), which turns failed payments into recovered revenue with automated reminders, customizable card forms, and in-app paywalls
Cancellation Insights (+$129/month), which collects real-time cancellation feedback and automates post-cancel follow-up emails
Does Payment Recovery pay for itself?
Baremetrics guarantees ROI on Payment Recovery: if it doesn't recover more revenue than your Baremetrics subscription costs, your next month is free.
Can I build a custom plan?
Yes. You can mix and match analytics features, integrations, and support levels to build your own plan, and adjust it as your business grows.
