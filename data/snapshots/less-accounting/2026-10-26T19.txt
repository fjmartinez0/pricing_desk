Questions? Answers.
Quick answers to questions you may have. Can’t find what you’re looking for? Check out our full documentation.
How does pricing work?
All bookkeeping services are based on a monthly subscription model. That means you pay per month. Your plan is based on your monthly revenue. Our best service for small businesses is based on your needs. You can get the best online bookkeeping services for small businesses that have tight budgets. We can also create a custom plan just for you to keep your average monthly expenses low.
Which pricing is right for my business?
During the discovery call, we’ll evaluate your business’s monthly revenue to find your perfect subscription package and monthly fee. LessAccounting online bookkeeping services are customer-driven, we want to help small businesses to determine the pricing plans that best fit the accounting services they need.
How long does it take to do my bookkeeping each month?
Once our accounting team receives all of your documents for your small business, your books for the month will be ready within 15 business days. This gives us enough time to make any necessary adjustments before the end of the month.
Do you support international businesses?
Less Accounting supports any company that is a US entity, no matter where you are based in the world. We can also provide bookkeeping services to foreign subsidiaries with consolidated reporting.
What's the difference between the tiers?
Tiers are based on business complexity; revenue, transaction volume, and number of accounts. We’ll help you pick the right fit on your call.
Can I switch tiers later?
Yes. Most clients start in one tier and move up as the business grows. We’ll proactively flag when an upgrade makes sense.
Do I need to already have QuickBooks?
No. Every tier includes a QBO subscription if you don’t have one.
I have more questions?
We encourage you to schedule a discovery call first to learn more. Our team will be happy to tell you more.
