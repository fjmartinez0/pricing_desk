FAQ
Frequently asked questions
What should buyers compare when evaluating Awardco plans, reward value, and total cost?
Compare the plan and platform scope separately from the budget for employee rewards. To estimate total cost, model your employee population, countries and currencies, reward mix, and expected redemption patterns. In your proposal, confirm which fees or costs apply to reward funding, payment or transactions, shipping, taxes, fulfillment, implementation, support, integrations, and any add-ons or professional services. Some eligible reward channels may offer zero-markup fulfillment; confirm which options and locations qualify for your program.
Are reward costs, add-ons, and other Awardco products included in the plan?
Not necessarily. Your plan provides access to the Recognition Types and platform features identified in the applicable Order Form. Examples of separately priced options include:
Premium add-ons: A-Pay®, Company Store, Non-Employee Recognition, and Awardco Connect.
Separate product: Awardco Engage, including survey capabilities beyond Simple Surveys.
Professional Services add-ons: Technical Account Manager (TAM), fee-based Center of Excellence (COE) consulting engagements, and Dynamic Support.
Funding management tools are included with plans, but redemption funding and available rewards are governed by the applicable agreement and marketplace configuration. Check your Order Form for the fees and terms that apply.
Can organizations start small and expand later?
Yes. Organizations can start with a focused plan and expand as their recognition strategy grows.
Service Awards Only supports service-anniversary programs.
Automated + 2 includes automated service anniversary, onboarding, and birthday programs, plus two additional active programs within any other Recognition Type.
Select, Advanced, Premier, and Enterprise provide access to unlimited programs within two, three, four, or all Recognition Types, respectively.
Eligible premium add-ons and separately purchased products may also be added as needs change. Expansion is subject to the applicable plan, Order Form, product availability, and commercial terms, so confirm the details before planning future programs.
What is the difference between Select, Advanced, Premier, and Enterprise?
The primary difference is the number of Recognition Types included. Each plan supports unlimited programs within its included Recognition Types:
Select: Any two Recognition Types.
Advanced: Any three Recognition Types.
Premier: Any four Recognition Types.
Enterprise: All Recognition Types, plus new Recognition Types released during your Order Form term. Premium add-ons, professional services, and separately priced products are excluded from this additional access.
All four plans include Awardco Platform Features & Support and the Recognition & Reward Product Features, unless otherwise noted. Choose based on the Recognition Types and programs your organization needs now and expects to add—not just employee count or a feature checklist.

Every Recognition & Rewards plan includes Awardco Platform Features & Support. Recognition & Reward Product Features are also included unless otherwise noted. Examples include:
Budget Builder / Manager and advanced reporting.
User management, roles, permissions, and authentication, including SSO.
Configurable branding, notification controls, communication integrations such as Slack and Microsoft Teams, and REST API access.

Admin technical support, end-user support, the Awardco Admin Community, and customer-success resources. Customers without a dedicated Customer Success Manager receive adoption guidance and product updates through shared customer-success resources.
The Recognition Types and programs you can run depend on your plan. Premium add-ons, professional services, and separately purchased products such as Awardco Engage are not automatically included.
What are Awardco Recognition Types?
Recognition Types are categories of recognition use cases. A Recognition Program is a distinct configuration within a type—for example, a peer-to-peer program within Spot Recognition.
Awardco has five Recognition Types:
Moments: Celebrates professional and personal milestones. Automated Moments uses dates from employee data, such as hire dates and birthdays. Personal Moments brings people together to celebrate life events such as a new child, graduation, or retirement.
Spot Recognition: Supports peer-to-peer, manager-to-employee, monetary, non-monetary, and special-event recognition programs.
Nominations: Allows employees to be nominated for awards or achievements based on configured eligibility.
Earn: Recognizes employees for meeting established criteria, such as completing training, volunteering, or participating in wellness activities.
External Recognition: Allows people outside the organization, such as customers, vendors, or partners, to recognize employees.
A plan’s Recognition Type limit is different from its program limit. For example, Select supports unlimited programs within any two Recognition Types. Choosing Moments and Spot Recognition could support service-anniversary, birthday, peer-to-peer, and manager-to-employee programs within those two types.
When Moments is included in Select or a higher plan, both Automated Moments and Personal Moments are available. Service Awards Only and Automated + 2 include only the Automated Moments programs listed in their plan features. Program settings, eligibility, approvals, and reward options depend on the Recognition Type and plan.
Which Awardco plan is right for my organization?
Start with the Recognition Types and programs your organization needs today, then consider the use cases you expect to add:
Service Awards Only: For organizations focused exclusively on service-anniversary programs.
Automated + 2: For organizations that want automated service anniversary, onboarding, and birthday programs, plus two additional active programs within any other Recognition Type.
Select: For organizations that need unlimited programs within any two Recognition Types.
Advanced: For organizations that need unlimited programs within any three Recognition Types.
Premier: For organizations that need unlimited programs within any four Recognition Types.
Enterprise: For organizations that need unlimited programs across all Recognition Types.
Every plan includes Awardco Platform Features & Support. Premium add-ons, professional services, and separately priced products can be evaluated separately based on your needs. Confirm the selected plan, included Recognition Types, program limits, and commercial terms in your Order Form.
How does Awardco organize its plans and pricing?
Recognition & Rewards plans are organized around the Recognition Types and program allowances included in each plan. Service Awards Only and Automated + 2 support focused use cases, while Select, Advanced, Premier, and Enterprise offer unlimited programs within two, three, four, or all Recognition Types, respectively.
The five Recognition Types are:
Moments: Automated recognition for service anniversaries, onboarding, and birthdays, plus Personal Moments for professional and personal life events, subject to your plan.
Spot Recognition: Peer-to-peer, manager-to-employee, monetary, non-monetary, and special-event recognition.
Nominations: Award and achievement programs with configured eligibility and nomination options.
Earn: Programs that recognize employees for meeting established criteria, with optional approvals, points, and award options.
External Recognition: Programs that allow customers, partners, vendors, and other people outside the organization to recognize employees.
Awardco Engage is an employee listening product purchased separately from Recognition & Rewards plans. Premium add-ons and fee-based professional services are also priced separately as identified in your Order Form.
For new startups and small businesses with 100 or fewer employees, flexible packages start at $3,000. This is a starting price, not a universal rate. Final pricing depends on the selected plan and scope, including the employee population, integrations, add-ons, and services as applicable. Fees, payment, term, and other commercial terms are governed by your Order Form and agreement.
