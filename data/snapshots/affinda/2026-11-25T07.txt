Answers to common pricing questions
Short answers on usage, paying, add-ons, and how to get a price.
How is usage counted?
One credit processes one page. Reprocessing a document uses credits again. Billing on a per-document or other custom metric can be negotiated where appropriate.
How does paying monthly work?
Add a card in the app. We charge it on the 1st of each month for the pages you processed the month before, at US$0.15A$0.20 a page, for up to 10,000 pages a month.
How do prepaid credits work?
Buy credits in the app at US$0.20 each, paid up front, and use them as you process pages. Bought credits expire 12 months after purchase. Prepaid credits are charged in US dollars.
How is pricing calculated?
Pricing is based on the pages you process and how you pay. Up to 10,000 pages a month, pay monthly by card or buy prepaid credits in the app. Above that, talk to us about volume pricing, monthly or on an annual commitment. GST is added for Australian customers.
How do I get a price?
The prices are on this page. Start free, then buy credits or set up monthly billing in the app. Processing more than 10,000 pages a month? Tell us your volume and we will talk about volume pricing and terms.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. A free account includes 200 credits to use within 14 days. No card needed.
What happens if I exceed my annual commitment?
Overage is charged at your committed rate. There is no penalty or uplift for growth, so your per-page economics remain predictable.
Does unused commitment roll over?
No. Annual commitment is designed to cover the usage level you expect to process during the contracted period.
What sits outside standard usage pricing?
Platform add-ons and professional services sit outside standard usage pricing. These are scoped and contracted separately.
Can I move from monthly to annual later?
Yes. Monthly can be a flexible starting point, and annual commitment can be added once your volume becomes more predictable.
How do you handle data security, residency, and compliance?
Affinda supports secure document workflows with access controls, compliance support, and regional deployment options. For detailed security and compliance information, visit the Trust Center or talk to us about your requirements.
