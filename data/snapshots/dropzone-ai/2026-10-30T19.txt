Evidence-backed answers
Every verdict ships with the full reasoning trail. Every tool queried, every piece of evidence weighed, fully auditable months later when somebody asks what ran and why.
Coachable in natural language
Guide the agents the way you would guide a new analyst. No code and no playbooks. You give direction in natural language, and the correction persists and shapes how the next pass runs.
Unlimited programmatic actions
Automated containment runs at machine speed at no extra cost, scoped to blocking malicious IPs and disabling compromised accounts, and firing only on threats the AI agents have confirmed.
100+ integrations
Deploys in about an hour against the SIEM, EDR, cloud and identity tooling you already run. No data migration and no new agent on the endpoint.
Threat intel already in motion
GreyNoise, ReversingLabs and other premium feeds are included at no additional cost, already wired into how the agents investigate and hunt.
Access and control
Role-based access decides what your team can reach. Consent profiles decide what Dropzone AI can do in your tenant. Change either whenever, and our actions land in System Events next to yours.
