Frequently asked questions
What are compute units?
Monthly capacity is one shared pool for your project. It reflects both platform activity and the general footprint of data stored in QuintaDB. Usage resets on the first day of each calendar month.
What consumes compute units?
The same monthly pool covers active workloads and your stored project footprint—in broad terms, operations plus the data, files, and structure you keep in QuintaDB.
Are features the same across all plans?
Free is for one person. Paid plans add people in your portal, your own domain and brand, and support. Between paid plans only the monthly capacity differs.
Is there separate billing for users or data rows?
No per-user and no per-row billing on standard plans. Stored data and system activity both draw from the same monthly capacity pool.
What happens when monthly capacity is used up?
When monthly capacity is exhausted, active workloads may be paused until the next monthly reset or until you upgrade to a higher capacity plan.
How is Enterprise different?
Enterprise adds dedicated infrastructure, custom SLA, personal support, optional on-premise deployment, and bespoke security and integration options — in addition to negotiated compute capacity.
What happens after the 14-day trial?
Every new account gets 14 days of 50k compute units at no cost. If there is no payment by the end of the trial, the account switches to Free: nothing is deleted, but if your usage is above Free capacity, your portals and public forms pause for other people until you pay.
