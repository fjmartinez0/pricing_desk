Marketing Platform FAQ
Is pricing based on the number of contacts or emails I send?
Your plan price is based on the number of contacts you store, not the number of emails you send. Paid plans include unlimited email sending (under fair use), so your cost stays predictable even in high-volume months. Use the contact slider above to see your exact price at your list size.
What does "unlimited emails (fair use)" mean?
You can run as many campaigns and automations as you need without per-email charges. Fair use simply means sending should reflect normal marketing activity to a list of people who opted in — it's there to protect deliverability for everyone, not to cap genuine usage.
How much do I save by paying annually?
Annual billing saves you 25% compared to paying month to month. You're charged once for the year and locked in at the lower rate for all twelve months.
What happens to my price as my contact list grows?
Pricing moves up in tiers as you cross contact thresholds. You'll see the next tier before you reach it, and you can upgrade at any time — we never stop your sending mid-campaign when you approach a limit.
Is the Free plan really free, and do I need a credit card?
Yes. The Free plan is free forever with 12,000 emails a month to 1,000 contacts, and no credit card is required to start. You only enter payment details if and when you choose to upgrade.
Can I upgrade or downgrade later, and how is billing handled?
You can change plans anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated, so you only pay the difference for the remainder of your cycle. Downgrades apply from the start of your next billing cycle.
Which currencies can I pay in?
You can be billed in USD, GBP, or INR — switch using the currency selector at the top of the plan section. Applicable taxes (such as VAT/GST) may be added at checkout based on your billing location.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. There's no lock-in contract — you can cancel from your account at any time and keep access until the end of your current billing period.
Is the Marketing Platform price the same as the Email API price?
No — they're two separate products with their own pricing. This page covers the Marketing Platform. If you need transactional sending from your app, the Email API starts at $7/month and is billed separately. You can run both from one account.
