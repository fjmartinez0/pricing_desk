Frequently asked questions
What happens when I sign up?
You can integrate a Google, Microsoft or Apple calendar with your booking page. You don't need to add payment details: trial all features for 14-days, then upgrade to the plan of your choice, or use the free-forever version.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards, including Visa, MasterCard and American Express. We accept payment by PO and bank transfers by prior agreement only.
Are you going to charge me tax on top?
If you're based in the UK, we will add GB VAT (20%) to your subscription cost. Users from the rest of the world are not charged additional tax.
What's the deal with the free plan?
With our free-forever plan, you get a powerful booking page with all the basic features we know you need. All we ask is that your booking page and notification emails carry our 'Powered for free' button.
What happens if I need an extra calendar?
All of our paid plans include extra calendars. Add a calendar - or a few! - to make sure your availability reflects all areas of your life. They can also be used to offer different types of availability - like for different locations.
Do you charge on a monthly basis?
You can choose to pay monthly by credit card. You can also opt for annual or two-year subscriptions that come with additional discounts.
Are my payment details secure?
All payment data is processed and handled by Stripe who offer the most stringent levels of security in the payments industry. YouCanBookMe does not keep or store any credit card information.
What happens if I change my mind?
No worries! We have a 30 day money back guarantee if you change your mind after signing up for one of our paid plans.
Do you offer discounts for schools?
In certain circumstances yes, we do! Find out if you're eligible.
What kind of support do you offer?
Customers on a paid plan receive full premium support, including 1:1 support requests and scheduled callbacks. Customers on a trial plan also receive premium support for the duration of their trial.
Free plan users can access our support site, community forum, and helpful video resources. You can upgrade at any time to access 1:1 premium support.
Do you offer Enterprise pricing?
Get in touch with our Sales team if you’d like to set up an account with more than 50 team members.
