Frequently asked questions
How much does Strivacity cost?
Strivacity pricing starts at $17,000 per year for up to 10,000 monthly active users (MAUs) and scales with your MAUs, so your price grows with your business rather than jumping between feature tiers. Every subscription includes core CIAM capabilities and a dedicated single-instance. Use the pricing slider on this page to see your price, or contact sales for 2 million MAUs or more.
How is Strivacity priced?
Strivacity is priced by monthly active users (MAUs). Your price follows a published scale that rises smoothly as your MAUs grow, so there are no feature tiers and no price cliffs between plan levels. The price per MAU falls as your volume grows.
What counts as a monthly active user?
A monthly active user (MAU) is a unique person that signs in to your applications at least once in a calendar month. Customers, business partners and business users all count toward your MAUs and are priced the same way.
How does Strivacity pricing work for B2B scenarios?
Strivacity prices B2B scenarios the same way as B2C: by monthly active users (MAUs). There is no additional charge for partners or organizations, and the base license includes an unlimited number of organizations, so one instance can serve B2C, B2B, B2B2x and partner use cases. Auth0, by comparison, sells B2B on separate plans that cost more than its B2C plans at the same MAU count.
What is included in Strivacity’s price?
Every Strivacity subscription includes core CIAM capabilities and dedicated infrastructure: a dedicated single-instance with a sandbox environment, a 99.99% SLA, unlimited applications, brands, integrations and B2B organizations, adaptive MFA, passkeys and passwordless sign-in, bot detection, breached password detection, consent management, no-code orchestration, branding and analytics.
What costs extra with Strivacity?
Identity verification, identity fraud detection and phone number fraud detection are priced per transaction. Everything else in the capability comparison on this page is included.
Is Strivacity single-tenant, multi-tenant or single-instance?
Strivacity is single-instance. Each customer gets a dedicated, independently deployed instance with isolated compute, application services and data infrastructure, and no infrastructure is shared between customers, while the platform is still delivered as SaaS. Single-tenant products dedicate the application environment but may share underlying infrastructure, and multi-tenant products share both.
What uptime does Strivacity guarantee?
Strivacity’s standard service level agreement is 99.99% uptime, included with every subscription.
Is Strivacity accessible and WCAG compliant?
Strivacity’s customer sign-up and sign-in journeys are evaluated by a third-party auditor against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 at Levels A and AA. Strivacity’s Accessibility Conformance Report, based on VPAT version 2.5, is available through the Strivacity Trust Center at security.strivacity.com.
How does Strivacity pricing compare with Auth0, Ping Identity and Microsoft?
Strivacity publishes pricing from 10,000 to 1 million MAUs and includes a dedicated single-instance, adaptive MFA and bot detection as standard. Based on their published materials, Auth0 offers these as Enterprise add-ons, Ping Identity sells them as separate products or licenses, and Microsoft Entra External ID relies on third-party integrations for bot detection. See the comparison table on this page for sources.
Does Strivacity offer volume discounts?
Yes. Strivacity’s price per MAU falls as your volume grows, and the published scale already reflects it. For 2 million MAUs or more, or multi-year agreements, contact sales for custom pricing.
