Ans: Yes. CloudTest Pro supports deployment flexibility, including on-premise, private cloud, or air-gapped environments, making it suitable for organizations with strict data governance or compliance requirements.

Ans: Absolutely. All CloudTest packages support manual testing, and both Go and Pro include support for 60+ automation frameworks such as Appium, and Selenium.
