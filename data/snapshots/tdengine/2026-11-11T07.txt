Self-Hosted TDengine All-in-One Pricing Plans
TDengine All-in-One is free to use for up to 5,000 tags. For deployments above 5,000 tags, TDengine is offered as an annual license or as a perpetual license with optional annual support.
You can deploy wherever you like — at the edge, in a container or VM, on a local server, or in a public, private, or hybrid cloud.
The free tier can be used for evaluation, PoC, or small production deployments. Official TDengine support is available through optional paid annual support.

Support
Standard Support is included with all annual plans. This includes software updates, security patches, and access to the TDengine support team during business hours.
Free-tier deployments do not include Standard Support by default. Customers using TDengine up to 5,000 tags may purchase optional annual support for $3,375/year.
Premium Support (24×7 coverage for Severity 1 incidents) is available at an additional cost.
Fully Managed TDengine All-in-One Pricing Plans
TDengine Cloud delivers TDengine as a fully managed cloud service in Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud Platform.
The 5,000-tag free tier applies to self-hosted TDengine license usage. Fully managed TDengine Cloud plans include managed infrastructure, hosting, operations, and service costs, and are priced separately.

Optimized query engine

Data subscription

Read caching

Stream processing

Edge–cloud sync

Industrial data connectors
