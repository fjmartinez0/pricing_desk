Frequently asked questions
Can I try GanttPRO for free?
We offer a 14-day free fully-featured trial period. Click “Sign up free” and in a few seconds, you will be able to invite your team and create projects. All your work will be saved after purchasing a subscription.
What payment options do you accept?
GanttPRO supports several payment methods, including major credit cards and PayPal. If you prefer to issue an invoice for your company, we can also accommodate payments via Wire Transfer or Purchase Order. Please contact our sales team for more details on these options.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits, students, and educators?
GanttPRO gladly supports health-related, educational and non-profit organizations all around the globe and would be glad to offer you a 50% discount on any annual plan. To get a discount, please reach out to us at support@ganttpro.com.
Is it possible to upgrade a license by increasing the number of seats or switching to a different package plan?
Absolutely! You can upgrade your license at any time to add more seats or to transition to a higher tier of our package plans. For help with the upgrade process or to discuss various options, please reach out to our sales team.
How does GanttPRO ensure data security?
We place the utmost importance on the stability and reliability of our services. Our backup system ensures real-time database replication, along with daily backups, to safeguard your data. Additionally, our payment partner, PayPro Global, fully adheres to Level 1 Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI-DSS) requirements. This guarantees that all necessary measures are in place for the secure and private processing of your payment information.
All data is stored in secure Microsoft Azure data centers within the EU.
To learn more about all the privacy and security measures we take, please refer to our security page.
