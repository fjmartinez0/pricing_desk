Frequently Asked Questions
Credits & Pricing
How are credits counted?
Enrich: 1 credit per page of results, and a page holds up to
1,000 results.
Discover: 1 credit per returned result.
Track: 1 credit per sent company signal when
filters are enabled, 0.1 credit per sent
company signal when they are not.
Do credits expire?
Yes. Credits stay valid for 12 months after you buy the pack. Credits you
have not used by then expire.
Does pagination increase credit usage?
Yes, on Enrich each page is a separate request and costs 1 credit.
To use fewer credits, ask for up to 1,000
results per page.
Do filters or parameters affect pricing?
Not on Enrich and Discover. Parameters like limit, dates, categories and locations do not change the
price. On Track, a company signal sent with filters enabled costs
1 credit and one sent without filters costs
0.1 credit.
What happens when I run out of credits?
Further requests return a 402 error (limit exceeded).
No additional credits are used.
Buy another pack to continue. You can watch your usage on your subscription dashboard or through the API.
