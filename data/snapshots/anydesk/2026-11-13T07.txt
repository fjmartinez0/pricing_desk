All you need to know
Do you offer a monthly subscription and monthly payment?
Currently, we offer subscriptions on an annual basis and annual billings.
Do I need to renew my contract every year?
We understand that business continuity is important for you. Hence, your contract will renew automatically unless you let us know beforehand.
What payment options do you offer?
We accept payments via Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, and PayPal. Bank transfers are accepted in Germany, Austria and Switzerland.
What is a user?
A User is the initiator of a session. If you want to start Remote Access, you need a valid license assigned to your respective account, for example via your own AnyDesk address or alias.
What are registered devices?
With each paid license, devices can be registered according to the size of the license. A user can, for example, log in and start sessions on up to 3 devices in parallel.
What is a Namespace?
You can rename your AnyDesk address (e.g. 123 456 789) to an available alias in the @AD domain (example. My.Name@AD). The Namespace option allows you to specify an individual Namespace available only to you, e.g. My.Name@CompanyName.
Where can I get an overview on the features that AnyDesk provides?
Our features are explained in detail on our
feature pages and in the
Help Center
.
Does every session participant need a paid license?
No, only the initiator of a session needs a license, participants are free.
What is the difference between the free and the paid license?
AnyDesk is free for personal use and offers a limited set of features and support. Professional users need a license. Click here to find more details on the benefits of a paid license. If you are still unsure, click here to learn more about the differences between personal and professional use.
What are the differences between the paid licenses and how do I know which license fits my needs?
We offer 4 paid licenses: Solo, Standard, Advanced, and Ultimate. Each license comes with a set of features and customization options tailored to fit different use cases and business sizes. To learn more about the individual differences between our licenses, we recommend this Help Center article. Learn which licenses we recommend to single-users, small to medium-sized businesses, and large corporations.
