Questions about Landingi plans and pricing
What is Landingi pricing?
Landingi pricing is based on the plan and billing cycle you choose. The platform offers Build, Optimize, Scale, and Enterprise plans, with different limits for active landing pages, monthly visits, users, custom domains, AI credits, and advanced optimization features. Annual billing offers a lower effective monthly price than monthly billing.
Landingi pricing starts at $24 per month when billed annually. The platform offers four plans designed for different landing page volumes, traffic levels, team sizes, and optimization needs:
Build: $24/month
Optimize: $119/month
Scale: from $229/month
Enterprise: from $1199/month
Annual billing in Landingi includes two months free compared with monthly billing. Plan availability, traffic limits, and final pricing may vary based on the selected configuration.
Does Landingi offer a free trial?
Yes. Landingi offers a 14-day free trial for every plan. These trials give you full access to all the plan’s features.
Your trial will end when you decide to upgrade, hit trial traffic limits (limited to 200 visits for all plans in the trial), or reach the end of your trial period without canceling. After the trial ends, your credit card will be charged, and you’ll be subscribed to the plan of your choice.
Towards the end of the trial period, we’ll send you a reminder email that your credit card is about to be charged. You can cancel your free trial at any time during the free trial period. Log into your account, go to the Account Management tab, select Subscriptions, and click Cancel. Your credit card will not be charged.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan during the free trial?
You can upgrade or downgrade your Landingi plan at any time during your trial.
What payment methods does Landingi offer?
We accept all major credit cards and PayPal. We also support transactions in a range of currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, PLN, BRL.
What happens when my Landingi trial ends?
If you choose one of Landingi’s paid plans after 14 days, you’ll become a paid subscriber to the plan you selected when registering for your free trial. Your charges will depend on the plan level and billing cycle you choose. During checkout, you’ll see the exact amount you’ll be charged (including taxes) and the date of your first payment.
Do I need to provide my credit card information to get started with Landingi?
We sometimes request your valid credit card details to safeguard against fraudulent activity in our Platform and to ensure uninterrupted service when your free 14-day trial ends and transitions into a paid subscription. You have the option to cancel your account or update your payment method at any time during the trial period.
If you can’t provide a credit card for some reason, please contact our Support Team, and they will enable a trial for you anyway. It is crucial to confirm that you are a real Customer.
What are credits in Landingi?
Credits in Landingi are virtual tokens used to access some usage-based features, such as AI-powered page generation in Lunar, background removal, and Programmatic Landing Pages.
Each Landingi plan includes a monthly credit allowance. When you use a feature that requires credits, the appropriate number of credits is deducted from your account. You can track your credit usage in the platform and purchase more credits if needed.
Do all Landingi plans include AI landing page generation?
Yes. Lunar, Landingi’s AI landing page generator, is available on all current Landingi plans: Build, Optimize, Scale, and Enterprise. Using Lunar requires credits, and each plan includes a monthly credit allowance. If you use all your credits, you can upgrade your plan or purchase more credits.
Which Landingi plan is best for small businesses and solo marketers?
The Build plan is designed for startups, small businesses, and solo marketers who need to create and publish a few landing pages. It includes up to 10 active landing pages, 2,000 monthly visits, one custom domain, and 2,500 credits per month. If you need more domains, you can buy one more for $5.month. In Landingi’s Build plan, you can use the Lunar AI Page Generator, a classic landing page builder, forms, integrations, AI Assistants, basic analytics, and pre-designed sections.
Which Landingi plan is best for professional marketers?
The Optimize plan is built for freelancers and professional marketers who run multiple campaigns and need conversion data to improve performance. It includes up to 100 active landing pages, 30,000 monthly visits, three custom domains, 10,000 credits per month, EventTracker, Smart Sections, Multi-lang Personalization, and Solis AI Insights. You can buy an extra domain for $5/month (up to 6 extra domains).
Which Landingi plan is best for agencies and marketing teams?
The Scale plan is designed for agencies and marketing teams that manage landing pages across multiple campaigns, clients, or markets. It includes unlimited active landing pages, 10 custom domains, 20,000 credits per month, subaccounts, Orbit MCP Server, Programmatic Landing Pages, and 100,000-500,000 monthly visits. You can buy an extra domain for $5/month (up to 89 extra domains).
Which Landingi plan is best for enterprise teams?
The Enterprise plan is intended for high-scale marketing operations that need more than 1 million monthly visits, up to 100 custom domains, advanced security options, and tailored support. It also includes the highest monthly credit allowance of 60,000 credits. In Landingi’s Enterprise plan, you get domain whitelisting and a dedicated account manager.
What is Landingi used for?
Landingi is an AI landing page operation system used to create, publish, optimize, and manage landing pages for marketing campaigns.
Marketing teams use Landingi to move from campaign idea to execution faster. The platform helps them build landing pages, test variants, capture leads, connect pages with their marketing stack, and manage landing page operations at scale – without relying on developers for every update.
