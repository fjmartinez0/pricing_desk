How does the 14-day free trial work?
Once you sign up for 7shifts, you’ll immediately get access to all features of 7shifts Pro plan for 14 days. If you have any questions, our responsive support team is happy to give you a hand. There is no credit card needed to start the trial. Test out 7shifts without any strings attached.
Do I have to sign a contract?
Term agreements are available for customers looking for an Enterprise discount model. You have the option to pay monthly or annually. If you cancel, we’ll bill you for the current month, but won’t bill you again if you’re not under a contract.
What form of payment do you accept?
We take payment by credit card as well as ACH (automatic bank withdrawals), by request. You choose whether you want to pay monthly or annually. We’ll charge your credit card or bank accordingly at each period.
What happens at the end of the trial?
Once your 14-day trial ends, you can choose to continue on our free “Comp” plan, or subscribe. If you choose to subscribe, you’ll pick a plan that works for you and enter your credit card information. If you don’t choose either, your account will expire.
Do you offer discounts to restaurant franchises or groups?
Yes, we do. We are always open to talking with restaurant franchises and organizations that have multiple locations. Contact us and we can work out a plan that works best for everyone.
