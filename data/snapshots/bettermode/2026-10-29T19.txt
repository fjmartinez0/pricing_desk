Got questions?
We’ve got answers.
Find quick answers to common questions about features, plans, customization, and more.
What problems does Bettermode solve?
Bettermode helps marketing and CS teams use community to drive engagement, retention, and revenue. We put your customers, content, and conversations in one place so you can see what's working and turn users into advocates.
What security and compliance standards does Bettermode meet?
Security is built in. Bettermode is SOC 2 Type II compliant and supports GDPR and enterprise-grade data protection standards. Your data stays secure, owned by you, and handled with proven best practices.
How does migration from an existing community platform work?
We help you move your users, content, and structure from your current platform. We’ll guide the process end-to-end so you can switch over with minimal downtime.
How does Bettermode integrate with our existing stack?
Bettermode connects to Salesforce, Hubspot, Intercom, Segment, and other tools you're already using. Need custom workflows? Use our APIs and webhooks.
How does Bettermode support customers after launch?
You get ongoing support after launch. We help with strategy, share best practices, and show you how to track results.
