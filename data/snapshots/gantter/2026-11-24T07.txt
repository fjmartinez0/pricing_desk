QUESTIONS
Pricing questions, answered
Do I need a credit card to start a trial?
No. The 7-day trial includes every feature and Brena®. You only enter card details when you move to a paid subscription.
What happens if I don't subscribe after my trial?
You can still open and view your gantterAI™ files and MS Project files. To edit existing projects or create new ones, you need a paid subscription.
Will my subscription renew automatically?
Yes. Monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period.
What payment methods do you accept?
Monthly and annual plans can be paid online by credit or debit card. Annual plans for more than 20 licenses can also be paid by purchase order, invoice, wire transfer or check.
Do collaborators need a license to view my projects?
No. Anyone you share a project with can view it. Only people who create or edit gantterAI™ files need a paid license.
Do you offer discounts for schools and non-profits?
Yes: 50% off for educational institutions and 30% off for qualified non-profits. Send us a message to request your discount.
