Every list price, by monthly sessions
LogRocket prices by the sessions you capture each month. Analytics events, error events, and logs are unlimited on every plan. The Free plan includes 1,000 sessions a month, free forever. Core starts at $83 a month for 10,000 sessions. Pro includes LogRocket Galileo and starts at $292 a month for 10,000 sessions. At 100,000 sessions a month and up, Pro is the plan, from $717 a month. Enterprise, from 4,000,000+ sessions a month, is custom annual pricing with unlimited seats. Prices are monthly equivalents on an annual plan, in US dollars. Last updated November 12, 2026.
At 250,000 sessions a month and up, the calculator also estimates Conditional Recording, which records only the sessions you choose: ≈ $860 at ~40% captured at 250,000 sessions, billed annually. The estimate depends on how many sessions you capture, so the final price comes from your configuration.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does LogRocket really include unlimited analytics events and errors?
Yes. Every plan includes unlimited analytics events, error events, and logs — you only pay for the session replays you capture.
Can I capture only the sessions that matter?
Yes. With Conditional Recording you can record a targeted subset of sessions instead of everything, which keeps costs down at higher volumes.
Can I change my plan or session volume later?
On a month-to-month plan you can change plans or session volume anytime. On an annual plan you can increase your quota whenever you need to, but downgrades take effect at the end of your annual term.
Is discounted annual pricing available?
By default, LogRocket is billed as a monthly subscription. If you would like to pre-pay for an annual LogRocket subscription, you will receive a discount for annual billing. For annual billing options, contact sales.
Is there a free trial?
Yes — you can try LogRocket free for 14 days.
Do you offer a LogRocket self-hosted solution?
Yes, contact us for details on our enterprise plans.
