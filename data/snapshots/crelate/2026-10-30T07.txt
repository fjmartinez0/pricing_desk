Questions about plans and cost.
How much does Crelate cost?
Essentials starts at $85 per user per month paid annually, and Business is $119 per user per month, billed annually or monthly. Business Plus and Platform pricing are custom, and custom pricing is available for unique scenarios and use cases.
Which plan includes AI?
Crelate is AI-native on every plan, not AI as an upsell. Essentials ships with AI sourcing and AI support. The AI Assistant lands on Business, the Advanced AI Assistant on Business Plus, and the Insights and Data Quality Agents are available through Platform pricing when you want agents working your database continuously.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. You can start a free trial without talking to sales, and bring a sample of your own data to test the workflow you actually run.
What does onboarding and compliance cost?
Crelate Hire is priced per hire and adds advanced onboarding checklists, state and local tax forms, WOTC integration, per-client, per-job and per-location templates, and an employee portal.
How does Crelate connect to a back office provider?
The Crelate Data Sync Service is priced on volume and frequency, and covers back office integrations, CRM integrations including HubSpot, Monday, Salesforce and Dynamics, and providers such as People 2.0, Sense, CSV, and ServiceNow.
Do you offer a nonprofit discount?
Yes. Qualifying 501(c)(3) nonprofits receive discounted pricing on Business, Business Plus, and Enterprise subscriptions, as well as on Professional Services, implementations, and select add-ons. Third-party services such as job board postings are not eligible.
