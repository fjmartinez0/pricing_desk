How Restaurant Payment Processing Rates Are Calculated
Card mix, average ticket size, and processor pricing can all affect the cost of each transaction. USEZPAY, MenuSifu's payment services subsidiary, supports integrated payment processing that connects POS, payments, and settlement in one system.
See the math
What Affects Restaurant Payment Processing Rates?
Card Type and Card Mix
Regulated debit sits at the lower end. Premium and Amex can exceed 3%. The cards your customers use drive your blended rate — and no processor can change that.
Average Ticket Size
A $0.22 fixed fee on a $10 sale is 2.2%. On a $100 sale, it's 0.22%. Bubble tea and QSR feel this hardest.
Processor Markup
80% of processing costs go to banks and networks, with no room for negotiation. The only variable is the processor markup. Fewer intermediaries mean better rates. This is where USEZPAY changes the math.

Structure
✓ Interchange Plus — fixed markup on top of interchange. Transparent base rate and fee, separately stated.
✕ Cost Plus — the "cost" component isn't always clearly defined, making it easier for additional or less transparent fees to be bundled in.
✕ Interchange Plus (occasionally flat rate). Headline rate looks competitive, but add-on fees (chargeback, statement, gateway) bring the effective rate back in line.

费率结构
✓ Interchange Plus：在银行基础费率之上加收固定服务费。基础费率与手续费清晰展示，一目了然。
✕ Cost Plus：“成本”构成往往定义不明，容易在其中包含额外或不透明的费用。
✕ Interchange Plus：表面费率看似诱人，但拒付、账单、网关等各类附加费会拉高实际成本。
数据打通
✓ 数据闭环：POS订单与支付交易在同一系统关联。
✕ 部分集成：POS数据与支付数据由不同平台分别管理。
✕ 完全脱节：刷卡机独立运行，与POS或订单数据没有任何关联。
售后支持
✓ 一站式响应：POS和支付问题均由同一家公司内部处理。
✕ 服务分散：POS故障找软件商，支付问题找支付商，两头对接。
✕ 银行只管硬件：银行仅负责刷卡机维修；POS系统的技术支持需自行解决。
连锁 / 加盟管控
✓ 管控力强：总部可统一掌控资金流、结算及所有门店的汇总报表。
✕ 管控受限：各门店通常独立结算，难以实现总部统一管理。
✕ 管控极低：银行侧重于单店处理，而非针对多店运营场景设计。
