Frequently asked questions
Should I upgrade to Pro or Premium?
If you share lots of videos with users, customers, or followers, Premium is the better fit. Otherwise, Pro should be enough. You can change plans at any time.
How do Credits work?
Credits pay for the Tella Agent, the AI editor that edits your video from a chat in the editor. Each message uses some credits, depending on how much work the agent does and which model it uses. Auto Capture (beta), where an AI agent records a web page for you, uses credits for each take. Pro includes 4,500 credits per user each month and Premium 9,000, pooled across the workspace. Included credits reset monthly and don't roll over. Need more? Buy a one-time top-up, or add extra monthly credits for the whole workspace. Enterprise credits depend on your contract. Free trials don't include credits. How credits work.
Can I buy more credits?
Yes. Workspace owners can buy one-time top-ups from 25,000 credits for $25 up to 2,500,000 for $2,250, with up to 10% off larger amounts. Purchased credits never expire and are only used once the month's included credits run out. Extra monthly credits add the same amounts every month at the same prices.
Do you offer SSO and/or SCIM?
Yes. SSO and SCIM are available as add-ons on any paid plan and are included with Enterprise. Learn more on our Enterprise page.
Is Tella SOC 2 compliant?
Yes. Tella is SOC 2 Type II compliant on every plan. The SOC 2 Type II report is available to Enterprise customers under NDA. Learn more in the Tella Trust Center.
Do you offer any education discounts?
Yes. See our Tella for Education program.
Do you offer any nonprofit discounts?
Yes. See our Tella for Good program.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes. Your subscription stops at the end of the current billing period, and you will not be charged for the next one.
