Our Approach to Value

Everything you need, all in one place.

You have questions? We have answers.
How much does Zenfolio cost?
Zenfolio offers three plans. Basic starts at $7/mo (billed annually), Professional at $11.50/mo (billed annually), and Advanced at $20/mo (billed annually). Monthly billing is also available at $9, $23, and $40/mo respectively. Every plan includes a custom website, client galleries, proofing, and 24/7 support.
Is there a free trial? Do I need a credit card to start?
Yes. You can try Zenfolio free with no credit card required. The trial gives you access to explore the platform, build your website, and set up galleries before choosing a plan.
What’s the difference between the Basic, Professional, and Advanced plans?
All three plans include a custom website, client galleries, and proofing. The key differences:
Basic is designed for photographers who want to share galleries and showcase their work. It includes 15 GB of storage and core delivery features.
Professional adds selling tools (prints, digitals, packages), integrated lab fulfillment, Volume Wizard (up to 5 uses), and 150 GB of storage. Booking and Scheduling is available as a $10/mo add-on.
Advanced includes unlimited JPEG storage, 4K video support, unlimited Volume Wizard and QR Code Workflow uses, up to 15 team members, and Booking and Scheduling at no extra cost.
What fees apply when I sell prints or digitals through Zenfolio?
Zenfolio keeps selling fees simple and transparent. Here’s what applies to each order:
Commerce fee
A 7% commerce fee is charged per order, calculated on the order subtotal (before tax and shipping).
If a coupon is applied, the fee is calculated after the discount.
Digital download fee
When your order includes digital files, a small download fee is added on top of the commerce fee:
Single image or video: $0.09 per file
All images/videos (full gallery or bulk download): $0.99 flat
Payment processing fee
This is charged by your chosen payment processor:
Stripe: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
PayPal: 3.49% + $0.49 per transaction
That’s it, no surprises
These are the only selling-related fees. There are no additional listing fees, monthly selling fees, or hidden charges.
Can I cancel my subscription? What’s the refund policy?
You can cancel your subscription at any time by contacting our support team. If you cancel within 30 days of your initial signup or annual plan renewal, a refund is available as a courtesy. Refunds are not available for monthly plan cancellations or for subscriptions beyond the 30-day window. Your account remains active until the end of your current billing period.
How much do I save with an annual plan?
Annual plans offer significant savings compared to monthly billing. On the Professional plan, for example, you pay $11.50/mo annually instead of $23/mo monthly. That’s 50% off. Advanced drops from $40/mo to $20/mo annually. Basic goes from $9/mo to $7/mo. Annual plans are billed as a single payment for the full year.
What happens to my photos and website if I downgrade or cancel?
If you downgrade, the change takes effect at the start of your next billing period. Your website and galleries stay intact, but features not included in the new plan will become unavailable. If your storage exceeds the new plan’s limit, you won’t be able to upload new files until you’re within the limit; existing files are not deleted.
If you cancel, you can do so directly from Account Settings > Billing. Your account remains active through the end of your current billing period, giving you time to download your files. After that, your website goes offline and your content becomes inaccessible. We recommend backing up your photos before your billing period ends.
How much storage do I get? Can I store RAW files?
Basic includes 15 GB of storage (approximately 4,500 photos). Professional includes 150 GB (approximately 60,000 photos). Advanced includes unlimited JPEG storage plus 150 GB of video storage with 4K support. All plans support JPEG, PNG, GIF, and HEIC files, as well as MP4 and MOV video. RAW files are not stored on Zenfolio; the platform is designed for processed images ready for delivery and sale. If you need additional storage beyond your plan, flexible add-on storage is available up to 100 TB.
Does my plan include everything I need for volume photography (sports, schools, events)?
Professional and Advanced plans both include the Volume Wizard, QR Code Workflow, bulk folder generation, and gallery registration forms. On Professional, Volume Wizard and QR Code Workflow are available for up to 5 uses. On Advanced, both are unlimited, and integrated booking and scheduling is included at no extra cost. If you’re running a high-volume operation with multiple events per season, Advanced gives you the most room to scale.
Is Zenfolio worth it compared to SmugMug or Pixieset?
Zenfolio brings your website, client galleries, proofing, selling, booking, and marketing tools into a single platform. Many photographers find that consolidating these tools saves both money and time compared to subscribing to separate services. For a detailed side-by-side comparison, see Zenfolio vs. SmugMug or Zenfolio vs. Pixieset.
