Frequently Asked Questions
How do on-site rentals work?
We ship everything you need (check-in kiosks, badge printers, and iPads) right to your venue a few days before your event. When you're done, simply pack them up in the provided return boxes.
Are there setup or enrollment fees?
No! Standard self-serve onboarding and access to our standard email/chat support help center are included in all plans at no extra cost. There are no mandatory enrollment fees. If you require custom data migration or dedicated hands-on training, our Account Management add-on is available.
Can I upgrade my plan mid-year?
Absolutely! You can upgrade your plan or add optional modules at any time. We will prorate the cost for the remainder of your billing cycle.
Do I need to pay for a full year upfront?
We offer both monthly and annual billing options. However, annual plans come with up to an 80% discount compared to monthly billing.
Who owns the attendee data?
You do. You maintain 100% ownership of your attendee data, and you can export your lists at any time in standard CSV or Excel formats. We never sell or market to your attendees.
Does the platform integrate with my CRM?
Yes, our platform includes out-of-the-box integrations with popular CRMs like Salesforce and HubSpot, plus thousands of other tools via Zapier, keeping your marketing and sales data perfectly in sync.
Can I pay by invoice or ACH?
Yes! We support payment by invoice, ACH, and wire transfer for our Corporate and Scale plans on an annual billing cycle. For Personal, Pro, Business, and Agency plans, we accept all major credit cards.
Do you offer a non-profit discount?
Yes, we offer a 15% discount on our annual plans for verified 501(c)(3) non-profit organizations and educational institutions. Please contact us at [email protected] to apply.
