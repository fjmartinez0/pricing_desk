FAQs
When does the ElevenCreative Black Friday sale end?
The ElevenLabs Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale ends on December 1st. Until then, eligible customers can save up to 55% when they choose an annual plan. Creator annual costs $99 for the year. Pro newcomers also get the full 55% off, while people already paying monthly for Pro get 50% off if they move to annual billing.
How much does each ElevenCreative plan cost and what is included?
On ElevenCreative, Free is $0 with 10,000 credits. Starter is $6 per month with 30,000 credits, Creator $22 with 121,000, Pro $99 with 600,000, Scale $299 with 1,800,000 credits, and Business $990 with 6,000,000 credits. Enterprise is custom. Credits are shared across all products. Free to Pro include 1 Full seat, Scale 3 seats and Business 10, and every plan adds 20 Basic seats. First-month offers (Starter $1, Creator $11) can be used once.
Which ElevenCreative plan suits a small team?
Starter to Pro include 1 Full seat plus 20 Basic seats. Scale ($299 per month, 1,800,000 credits) includes 3 seats, and Business ($990, 6,000,000 credits) includes 10. Add up the credit costs of the products you use and pick the plan that covers it. Enterprise offers custom terms and volume discounts.
How many credits does each ElevenCreative product use?
Text to Speech uses 1 credit per character. Speech to Text uses 330 per minute. Eleven Music uses about 900 per minute, and the exact cost varies by plan and is shown before you generate. Sound Effects use 200 per generation or 40 per second with manual duration. Voice Changer and Voice Isolator use 1,000 per minute. Dubbing uses 2,000 to 13,500 per minute depending on the product (see the dubbing FAQ). Image & Video cost is shown before you generate.
How do text characters and credits work for Text to Speech?
On the website, every model costs 1 credit per character. Through the API, Eleven v4, v3, and Multilingual v2 also cost 1 credit, while Turbo, Flash, and v3 Conversational models cost 0.5. In the web app, a single generation can run to 10,000 characters on Eleven v4 and 5,000 on other models.
How much does AI dubbing cost?
Dubbing is billed per source minute for each target language. In the app, automatic dubbing uses 2,000 credits per minute with a watermark or 3,000 without. Dubbing in ElevenCreative uses 5,000 or 10,000, and Dubbing v2 uses 13,500. Enterprise pricing is custom.
How much do Image & Video generations cost on ElevenLabs?
Image & Video is included from Starter upward and uses shared credits. Free includes images only, up to 3 per day. Cost varies by model, resolution, and length and is shown before you generate. API use needs Pro or above.
When do my ElevenLabs credits reset, do they roll over, and can I top up?
Credits reset each billing cycle, starting the day you subscribed. On paid plans, unused credits roll over for up to two months, up to 2x your quota (balance capped at 3x). Rollover ends if you downgrade or cancel and does not apply to Free. Pay-as-you-go top-ups are separate and expire 12 months after purchase. Your balance is on the subscription page.
Do you offer annual billing and what does it cost?
ElevenLabs offers annual billing with two months free. Annual prices are $60 (Starter), $220 (Creator), $990 (Pro), $2,990 (Scale), and $9,900 (Business), which is $5, $18.33, $82.50, $249.17, and $825 per month. Until December 1, 2026, Black Friday lowers the first-year price of Creator and Pro.
Am I charged for every generation and can I regenerate for free?
Credits are charged per generation and not per download. On the website, you’re able to get 2 free regenerations within 2 hours if the text, voice, and model stay the same. This does not apply to ElevenAPI or Enterprise workspaces.
What happens if I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my subscription?
Moving to a higher plan restarts your billing cycle straight away, and any credits you haven't used carry over into the new allowance. Moving down or cancelling keeps your current plan running until the cycle ends. After that your workspace drops to Free and leftover paid credits are lost. You can cancel whenever you like.
What payment methods and currencies does ElevenLabs accept?
Pay by credit card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay. UPI is also available in India, where charges are in INR, while all other customers pay in USD. Choose monthly or yearly billing. Prepaid cards cannot be used directly. Prices exclude taxes, levies, and duties, which are added at checkout.
