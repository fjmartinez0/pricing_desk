What do you mean, no rate limits?
Most AI tools throttle you — hourly message caps, cooldown timers, degraded models at peak hours. Bearly doesn't. You have credits, you spend them. Send 50 messages in 10 minutes if that's how you work. We'll never slow you down.
How does the free plan work?
A small daily credit allowance so you can try things out. Some features are limited to paid plans. Upgrade when you're ready.
Wait, so Bearly is free?
Essentially, yes. Your subscription converts dollar-for-dollar into AI credits at our cost. If you use your credits, you're only paying for the raw AI.
What's the difference between the paid tiers?
Credit budget, plus Claude Fable from Analyst up. Otherwise every paid plan has the same features, so pick the tier that matches how much AI you use. You can switch anytime.
Is canceling easy?
One click on the billing page. No hoops.
Do you train on my data?
No. Your conversations, documents, and files are yours. We don't use them for training, we don't sell them, and we don't look at them. Every chat and all files are individually encrypted.
What happens when I run out of credits?
Your credits refresh every month, on yearly plans too. We'll never surprise you with an overage charge.
I have a feature request.
We'd genuinely love to hear it. Email support@bearly.ai or hit us up on X. We read everything and ship fast — some of our best features started as user emails.
Who do I contact for support?
support@bearly.ai — we read every email from a real person. Paid plans get priority.
The short answer
How much does Bearly cost?
Bearly is free to start with a personal email: a small daily allowance and no credit card. Pro is $20 a month, Analyst $60, and Research $100. Each paid plan gives you its price in AI credits every month, with no rate limits. Opus needs Pro, and Fable needs Analyst. Enterprise pricing is set per company and adds single sign-on, SCIM, and audit logs.
