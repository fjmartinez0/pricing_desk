What are AI credits?
AI credits are used when Chloe and other AI features take action for you, such as enriching a lead, chatting with Chloe beyond your weekly allowance, or running an AI step in a Workflow.
AI credits & shared limits
Included per user each month and pooled across your team, up to 10,000 on Essentials, 15,000 on Growth, and 20,000 on Scale. Credits refresh monthly and do not roll over. Additional credits are available.
AI Call Assistant (paid add-on)
Transcribes and summarizes phone calls. Two charges: $50/month per account, plus $0.02 per recorded minute.
Additional organizations
$50/month each. Growth and Scale include one additional organization free.

Close pricing FAQs
Still deciding? Our team can help you pick a plan, plan a migration, or set up your first workflows.

How much does Close cost?
Close costs $9/month for Solo (one user), $35 per user/month for Essentials, $99 per user/month for Growth, and $139 per user/month for Scale, billed annually. Billed monthly, the plans are $19, $49, $109, and $149. Calling and SMS are billed separately based on usage, and every plan includes monthly AI credits.
Which Close plan is right for my team?
Solo is for one seller working alone. Essentials is for small teams that need shared leads, tasks, reporting, and Local Presence. Growth is for teams that want to make more calls and automate follow-up, with Power Dialer, Workflows, and AI Playbooks. Scale is for high-volume calling teams that need Predictive Dialer, Live Call Coaching, and advanced permissions. You can start on any plan and upgrade as your team and call volume grow.
Is Close free?
Close doesn’t have a free plan, but you can try it free for 14 days with no credit card. The trial includes calling and AI credits, so you can call real leads and try Chloe and AI Agents before you pay. After the trial, plans start at $9/month for Solo, billed annually.
What happens when my free trial ends?
If you choose a plan, you keep working in Close without interruption. If you don’t, your account pauses and your data stays available for a limited period before it’s deleted.
Are there any costs beyond the plan price?
Yes. Calling and SMS usage fees are not included in plan costs; you only pay for what you use. Most outbound calls cost about $0.02 per minute, phone numbers start at about $1/month, and SMS rates vary by country. AI usage beyond your included monthly credits is billed separately. AI Call Assistant is an optional add-on at $50/month per account plus $0.02 per recorded minute.
Do I need a separate phone system or sales dialer with Close?
No. Close includes built-in calling on every plan, so you can make and receive calls without switching tools. Growth adds the Power Dialer to call through lead lists automatically, and Scale includes the Predictive Dialer for high-volume outbound calling. Calls are logged in Close automatically. Calling minutes and phone numbers are billed separately.
How do AI credits work?
AI credits pay for AI that does work for you: AI Agents, AI Enrichment, Auto Updates, AI steps in Workflows, and Chloe chats beyond your weekly allowance. Lead summaries, email thread summaries, AI Notetaker, AI Rewrite, and Suggested Actions don’t use credits. Each plan includes credits per user every month (500 on Solo; 1,000, 1,500, or 2,000 per user on Essentials, Growth, and Scale), pooled across your team up to 10,000, 15,000, or 20,000. Credits reset monthly, don’t roll over, and you can buy more anytime.
Which plans include Chloe and AI Agents?
Every Close plan, including Solo, includes Chloe and AI Agents. Chloe is your AI assistant: ask about your leads, pipeline, or last call, or have her take action in Close. AI Agents work for you on ongoing jobs like calling and qualifying new leads. Growth and Scale add AI Playbooks, Auto Updates, and AI steps in Workflows.
Can I connect Close to ChatGPT, Claude, and other AI tools?
Yes. Every Close plan includes access to our MCP server, which lets you connect compatible AI assistants and agents to your CRM. Use tools like ChatGPT and Claude to access sales information, analyze conversations, update records, and take supported actions in Close. Available features and setup requirements vary by AI tool.
Learn more about Close MCP
Does Close include API access?
Yes. Close provides a REST API for building custom integrations, connecting your applications, and automating sales processes. API requests are subject to rate limits, but Close does not have a published daily API request quota or separate per-request API fees.
Explore the Close API
Does Close have API rate limits?
Yes. Close uses endpoint-specific rate limits to maintain reliability. Many commonly used endpoints support high request volumes, allowing businesses to build integrations, AI agents, and automations without a daily API request quota. Limits vary by endpoint.
View the API documentation
Can I change or cancel my plan anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans can be upgraded, downgraded, or canceled anytime. Annual plans can be upgraded during the term, and downgrades or cancellations take effect at the end of the term. You can add users as your team grows, and new seats are prorated. Every new purchase comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee.
Can I bring my existing CRM data into Close?
Yes. Close offers free migration help for standard moves from HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, and other CRMs, and you can import data yourself with built-in importers or a spreadsheet. Complex migrations, such as multiple systems, heavy cleanup, or custom requirements, may include professional services fees.
Do I need to talk to sales?
No. You can sign up, start a trial, pick a plan, and set up your team without talking to sales. Our team is available if you want help choosing a plan, planning a migration, or setting up Workflows.
