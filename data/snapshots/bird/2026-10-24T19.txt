How billing works
Is a top-up a subscription?
A manual top-up adds money to your Bird balance. Some product plans also add a recurring deposit to that balance; others include capacity. Check each plan’s published terms. Usage draws from your balance, and unused balance carries forward.
Do I need a paid plan for each product?
No. Each product has its own plan, and a product without one uses pay-as-you-go rates and default limits. A paid Email, SMS, Voice, WhatsApp or Apple Messages plan adds its monthly payment to your shared Bird balance, which can pay for any product’s usage at its applicable rate.
What is the difference between a funding plan and a capacity plan?
A funding plan adds its monthly payment to your Bird balance, and usage is charged from that balance from the first send. A capacity plan’s fee buys included usage, such as Realtime connections and daily messages, and adds nothing to your balance. Each plan card shows which kind it is.
What does a higher plan give me?
Higher tiers raise that product’s limits: sending speed for Email, SMS, WhatsApp and Apple Messages, concurrent calls and calls per second for Voice, and connections and daily messages for Realtime. Some plans also lower usage rates. Compare the plans on each product’s pricing page; an upgrade applies once its payment succeeds.
What happens if I have custom pricing?
Your agreed rate replaces the standard rate for that product. Other products keep their own rates. Charges still draw from your shared Bird balance.
Are phone numbers and dedicated IPs one-time purchases?
No. Phone numbers and dedicated IPs have recurring rental charges. Setup and registration fees can also apply. The product pricing page separates these charges from usage.
Which currency will I pay in?
Charges use your account’s billing currency. Select a currency on the product pricing page to see published rates. Carrier fees, provider fees and taxes may be additional.
Which products can I start with for free?
Use the Free to start filter to find free tiers and included tools. A free product or tier can still have paid channel usage, capacity limits or optional add-ons; its pricing page explains the distinction.
Can I use Push and RCS immediately?
Push and RCS are in private beta. Their pricing pages remain available, but access is limited. Contact Sales to discuss your use case and commercial terms.

Questions about email pricing
How many emails does my deposit include?
A paid deposit adds the published amount to your shared Bird balance. If a plan rate is published above, the send estimate assumes you spend the full deposit on email at that rate, before tax. It is an estimate, not an included allowance. Other Bird usage reduces the balance available for email.
Can I spend my Email plan balance on other products?
Yes. Your Bird balance is shared across products. Spending it on another product leaves less balance available for email. Unused funds carry forward.
How should I compare Email plans with other providers?
Compare the deposit and per-message charge separately. A Bird deposit becomes spendable balance; a competitor subscription may include a fixed monthly sending allowance. Check the same currency, sending volume and add-ons before estimating cost.
