FAQ
Frequently asked questions
How much does 11x cost?
11x Outbound starts at $36,000 per year on the Growth plan, billed annually. Pro and Enterprise pricing is custom, based on contact volume, end users, and channels.
Does 11x publish its pricing?
Yes. Growth plan pricing is published on the pricing page, and Pro and Enterprise plans are custom-quoted based on volume and scope.
Does 11x require an annual contract?
Annual is the default, though Pro and Enterprise plans support two- and three-year commitments with discount tiers.
Are there hidden costs beyond the plan price?
No. Every plan bundles contact data, email deliverability, warmup, inbox rotation, meeting scheduling, CRM sync, and onboarding. You only need to bring your own CRM license.
Is there a setup or onboarding fee?
No. Onboarding is included in every tier with no implementation fee, configuration charge, or per-integration cost.
How does 11x compare to hiring an SDR?
A fully loaded SDR costs $8,000 to $12,000 per month in salary, benefits, tooling, and management. 11x Outbound starts at $36,000 per year, billed annually, covers your full ICP, and is live after 2 weeks of standard onboarding.
How do 11x Outbound and 11x Inbound work together?
11x Outbound handles prospecting, personalized outreach across email and Social, and meeting booking. 11x Inbound qualifies inbound leads in real time on voice and chat. Both come from one provider, under one contract.
What happens at renewal?
Price increases at renewal are capped, and multi-year commitments lock your current rate for the full term.
