Web Hosting FAQs
What is web hosting?
Web hosting provides the technologies and infrastructure needed to store, manage, and deliver your website files (like code, images, and other files) to users over the internet. Your website content is like the furniture and products in a store, your domain name is the street address, and web hosting is the building itself - without it, there's nowhere for your site to exist.
Web hosting includes:
Storage space for your website files, databases, media, and email accounts
Server resources including processing power, memory (RAM), and bandwidth for visitor traffic
Domain connectivity that links your domain name to your actual website files
Uptime reliability through redundant systems and monitoring - the difference between 99.5% and 99.9% uptime means 3.6 hours versus 43 minutes of monthly downtime
Performance optimization via caching, CDN integration, and server configurations that directly affect load times (a one-second delay reduces conversions by 7%)
With SiteGround, you also get:
Enterprise-grade security - including SSL certificates, firewalls, malware scanning, and DDoS protection
Email hosting with professional email addresses using your domain name
Backup systems that automatically create restore points (up to 30 days) to protect your data
What are the different types of web hosting?
There are four main types: shared hosting (multiple sites share one server — most affordable, best for smaller sites), VPS hosting (a partitioned slice of a server for more control), dedicated hosting (an entire server to yourself — for high-traffic or specialized needs), and cloud hosting (resources spread across multiple servers for scalability and reliability).
