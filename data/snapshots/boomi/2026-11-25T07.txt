Yes, you can upgrade your plan at any time as your needs grow.

Plans are available on an annual subscription basis. Pay-as-you-go is available on a monthly subscription basis. Contact sales for flexible options.

User limits vary by plan. Contact sales for specific details about your needs.

Yes. Base includes community support, while higher tiers offer priority and dedicated support options.
