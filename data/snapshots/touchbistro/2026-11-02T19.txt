FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What’s included in the starting price of $89 and what is considered an add-on?
TouchBistro’s Point of Sale bundle starts at $89 per month and includes our full suite of POS software. This means you have access to all our POS features, including Tableside Ordering, Floor Plan & Table Management, Menu Management, Staff Management, and Reporting & Analytics – no features are locked or off-limits.
For an additional cost, you can add on solutions such as a Kitchen Display System, Inventory Management, Online Ordering, Loyalty, Reservations, and more.
Does TouchBistro offer product bundles?
Yes, TouchBistro offers bundled pricing. Choose from our Point of Sale, Essentials, and Custom bundles to create the perfect POS software, hardware, and payments setup for your business needs.
Does TouchBistro offer hardware financing?
Yes, TouchBistro offers hardware financing for venues with two or more terminals. This allows you to minimize upfront costs by paying for your hardware in monthly installments.
Is 24/7 customer support included in the monthly subscription price?
Yes, free customer support is included with your TouchBistro subscription. Our customer success team is available via phone and email 24/7 (including all holidays) at no additional cost.
Do I have to pay extra for software updates?
No, TouchBistro does not charge for app updates. Your TouchBistro POS will be continuously updated without additional fees.
Does TouchBistro charge commission fees for TouchBistro Online Ordering?
No, TouchBistro does not charge any commission fees on online orders, unlike other third-party providers.
Will my payment processor be compatible with a TouchBistro POS?
TouchBistro is compatible with a wide range of payment processing solutions. Whether you’re looking for an integrated processor or a standalone system, we’ll walk you through our many payment options and help you choose the right solution for your business needs.
