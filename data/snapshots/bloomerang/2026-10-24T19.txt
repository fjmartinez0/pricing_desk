BLOOMERANG PRICING FAQ
Questions? We’ve got answers.
[ 1 ]
How scalable is your pricing as we grow?
[ ]
Many platforms increase costs dramatically with each tier or additional user. Bloomerang, however, offers a predictable, constituent-based pricing model—so your budget won’t spike unexpectedly as you grow your fundraising and your team.
[ 2 ]
Are there hidden fees for implementation or extra features?
[ ]
We keep pricing transparent and inclusive. Bloomerang’s proposals include core products like fundraising tools, CRM, and volunteer management paired with training and onboarding—no surprise add-ons for core features down the road.
[ 3 ]
Will we lose access to our data or get locked in?
[ ]
You own your data. We provide easy exports at any time—no proprietary tokens or migration hurdles and no risk of being stranded mid-donor campaign.
[ 4 ]
We have complex workflows and custom fields—can Bloomerang handle that?
[ ]
Absolutely! We amplify what you do best rather than asking you to change how you work. Create custom fields for moves management, wealth screening, major-donor workflows with assigned fundraisers, and journey automation—all seamlessly integrated into the main database.
[ 5 ]
How much training and support is available?
[ ]
Our extensive training resources include onboarding and data conversion, responsive support, in-app education, weekly courses, an annual customer conference, and a robust knowledge base. We act like an extension of your team. Our dedicated support staff is always available via phone, email, or live chat to help you maximize success.
[ 6 ]
How do you ensure clean, reliable data?
[ ]
Bloomerang integrates your core nonprofit tools to minimize data admin and maximize your donor understanding and data accuracy. In addition, we use nightly NCOA updates, deceased donor suppression, duplicate detection, and smart data imports—protecting you from messy data issues that cost time and money.
[ 7 ]
What ROI can we expect?
[ ]
Nonprofits using the Bloomerang Giving Platform raise 47% more within 5 years of upgrading and increase first-time donor retention rates 28.5 points higher than industry averages. Get a personalized ROI for your nonprofit here.
