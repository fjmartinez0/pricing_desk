Mechanics
Three rules that keep invoices boring
A limit is not a meter
Reaching your project limit prompts you to add a project or change plan. It never creates a surprise charge and it never takes a published site down.
You choose every line on the invoice
Your invoice has your plan and the extra projects you added. Nothing else. There is no usage line, no seat line and no overage.
Unlimited rides on rate limits
Unlimited pages, readers and AI beta usage carry per-minute and per-day rate limits that normal use never touches. Hitting one slows requests. It never creates a charge. Enterprise plans can raise them.
Already a customer
Nobody gets a surprise
If you subscribed before these plans, you are on one of our earlier plans: Redoc, Revel, Reef or Realm. They are closed to new subscriptions, and nothing changes for you unless you choose it.
Here is exactly how it works
Nothing changes on its own. Your plan, your price and your billing page stay exactly as they are. You can keep them for at least a year from this announcement.
Your billing page shows the plan that matches what you use. We map by capability, not by what your old plan was called. If you use SSO today, that is Scale. If you use RBAC or SCIM as well, you keep them as add-ons on Scale or move to Enterprise, your choice. If you use none of these, that is Pro.
Seats stop being counted the day you move. Invite everyone. Readers and git contributors were never charged and still are not.
You move when you want to. One click on your billing page, effective immediately with a prorated credit, or at your next renewal.

Frequently asked questions
Do you really not charge for seats?
Correct. Owners, members, committers, billing users and viewers are all free in unlimited numbers on every plan. The price is per plan, not per person.
Do you charge for readers or page views?
No. Public docs and private docs behind a login are both unlimited. Traffic is covered by the same rate limits that protect the service today, and hitting one slows requests rather than billing you.
What counts as a project?
A project is a published site with its own domain, repository connection and settings. Pro includes 3 and Scale includes 10. Both can add projects one at a time for $39 a month each, or $390 a year on annual billing. Enterprise projects are unlimited.
What happens when we hit the project limit?
Creating the next project prompts you to add one or change plan. Existing projects keep building and serving. Nothing is charged until you confirm.
Are pages really unlimited?
Yes. Pages are not counted or billed on any plan. Very large sites are subject to fair-use build and hosting limits, which are the same limits the product enforces today. If you expect more than ten thousand pages, talk to us so we can plan capacity with you.
Which AI features are on which plan?
There are two kinds. For your readers: the AI Assistant in your docs and the MCP server are on every plan, the embeddable widget is on Scale and up, and MCP analytics is Enterprise. For your team: Redocly Agent pull request reviews and the project initializer are on every plan, and drafted fixes are Enterprise. All of it is beta with fair-use limits.
How will AI features be priced?
AI features are included in beta with usage limits, and nothing AI-related appears on your invoice today. When they reach general availability, each plan will include an AI allowance and additional usage will be priced in dollars with a spend limit you control. We will publish the prices and the date at least 60 days ahead.
Can we pay annually and what is the discount?
Yes. Annual billing is two months free: Pro is $2,988 a year and Scale is $8,748 a year. Switch in the billing page at any time; the change applies at your next renewal.
I am on an earlier Redoc, Revel, Reef or Realm plan. What happens to me?
Nothing, unless you choose to move. Your billing page shows the plan that matches what you use and its price; moving is one click. You can stay on your current plan for at least a year. See Existing customers above.
Can we get your W-9 or tax details?
Yes. Our W-9 and tax information are available on the billing page of the website.
