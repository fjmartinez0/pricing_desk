SST EXPLAINED
What is Streamlined Sales Tax (SST)?
The Streamlined Sales Tax (SST) program is a cooperative effort between state governments and businesses to simplify sales tax compliance for companies selling into multiple states. Avalara is one of only a handful of SST Certified Service Providers recognized by the SST Governing Board. State-funded compliance in up to 25 states: Participating states cover the cost of using Avalara, including tax calculation, returns preparation and filing, remittance, and audit liaison support for qualifying volunteer sellers.

Do you qualify for SST?
Remote sellers with economic nexus in SST states may qualify for state-funded services. You may be eligible if your business:
Sells into participating states without a fixed physical presence
Has less than $100,000 in property and less than $100,000 in payroll in an SST state (starting January 1, 2027; under $50,000 prior)*
Holds inventory in marketplace fulfillment centers (e.g., Amazon FBA)—starting January 1, 2027, marketplace-held inventory no longer counts toward property limits when the marketplace collects and remits the tax
Operates in ecommerce, retail, manufacturing, marketplaces, or B2B
Is already registered in a participating state — you can easily convert to SST
Is an international business selling into the U.S. with no physical presence

SST is priced lower for eligible customers because the economics are different in participating SST states for qualifying volunteer sellers. That state funding changes the cost model, which is why the SST path is available at a lower price point for businesses that qualify.
Who qualifies for SST services?
Most remote sellers, including ecommerce businesses, marketplaces, manufacturers, and B2B companies, may qualify if they sell into participating states without a significant physical presence. To qualify as a volunteer seller, your business must meet the following criteria during the 12-month period preceding registration in each state:
No fixed place of business in the state for more than 30 days
Less than $100,000 of property in the SST state (starting January 1, 2027; under $50,000 prior). Starting January 1, 2027, property does not include inventory stored in a marketplace fulfillment center (such as Amazon FBA) if the marketplace collects and remits the tax.
Less than $100,000 of payroll in the SST state (starting January 1, 2027; under $50,000 prior). International sellers selling into the U.S. without a physical location can also qualify. Businesses already registered in participating states can convert to SST.
International companies selling into the U.S. without a U.S. physical location can also qualify. Already registered in a participating state? You can still convert to SST. Talk to an expert to confirm your eligibility.
We have economic nexus in SST states — does that disqualify us?
Not necessarily. Economic nexus by itself does not disqualify a business from SST volunteer participation. The eligibility criteria focus on physical presence, payroll, and property, not transaction volume or economic thresholds. We still need to review your state-by-state footprint across those specific factors to confirm eligibility. Talk to an expert and we’ll walk through your qualification profile.
Does choosing Core Compliance + SST Services mean all of our compliance is free?
No. State-funded services apply only to SST-eligible transactions in participating states and only for qualifying volunteer sellers. If you operate in non-SST states, or in SST states where you don’t meet the volunteer seller criteria, standard AvaTax and Managed Returns pricing applies. Our team will be clear about exactly what’s covered, and what isn’t, for your specific footprint.
How does audit support and state notice coordination work with SST Services?
As your Certified Service Provider, Avalara serves as your audit liaison with participating SST states. When a participating state initiates an audit or calculation inquiry for a qualifying volunteer seller, state authorities coordinate directly with Avalara first rather than auditing your team directly. Avalara supplies required transaction records directly to the state, significantly minimizing audit disruption. Note: Customers remain responsible for accurate product taxability mapping, destination street addresses, and valid exemption certificates.
Which states participate in SST?
State-funded sales tax compliance is available in 24 participating SST member and associate states, plus Pennsylvania, which independently operates a similar state-funded program, for a total of 25 states: Arkansas, Georgia, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Michigan, Minnesota, Nebraska, Nevada, New Jersey, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania (independent state program), Rhode Island, South Dakota, Tennessee, Utah, Vermont, Washington, West Virginia, Wisconsin, and Wyoming.
Will Avalara help me register for SST?
Yes. Avalara is happy to help businesses that qualify for SST register in all SST-participating states for free when they enroll in the program using Avalara AvaTax.
Can I still sign up for SST if I’m already registered in an SST-participating state or was previously registered?
Yes. If you’re already registered in a participating state or you were previously registered, it will not impact your SST qualifications.
Can I still use Avalara products in states that don’t participate in SST?
Yes. Even though a nonparticipating state won’t cover the cost, you can still take advantage of Avalara as your cost-effective sales tax compliance solution.
