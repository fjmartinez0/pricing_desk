Frequently Asked Questions
What's the difference between Articulate 360 AI and Frontline?
Articulate 360 AI is the complete platform for L&D experts who need full control: Deeper authoring, review, distribution, and management. Frontline is built for teams across the business to quickly turn changing knowledge into effective, trackable learning. Organizations that need both can combine them with Hybrid.
What is Nova?
Nova is Articulate’s AI agent—the intelligence behind credit-powered features like kit generation, AI avatars, and multi-format translation across both Articulate 360 AI and Frontline. Nova is built into the platform, available to all users, and fueled by credits.
How do credits work?
Credits fuel advanced AI workflows including kit generation, AI Avatars, and multi-format translation. Your organization buys a pool of credits and spends them flexibly across these workflows. Anything included in your plan never touches your credits, so everyday work doesn’t cost extra.
Admins can see detailed balance and usage at any time, and credits will be returned for a failed generation. Credits are valid through the end of your subscription term.
How many credits do I need?
It depends on how much you create. Credits are spent across kits, AI Avatars, and translation. As a rough guide, a Frontline plan with 100,000 credits covers around 1 kit with 5 artifacts translated into 1 language and 30 minutes of AI avatar video. For help understanding your specific needs, review our credits knowledge base article or reach out to your sales representative.
Do unused credits roll over?
No. Credits expire at the end of the subscription term.
Can we add more credits during the year?
Yes. You can purchase additional credits as your needs grow. Available pack sizes and pricing depend on your agreement.
Is Frontline priced per seat?
No. Frontline is priced through a subscription-level credit bundle, not per seat, so everyone on your team can create. Advanced workflows draw from your organization’s credit pool, but included capabilities don’t use credits.
Can Frontline generate individual formats?
In Frontline, Nova generates formats as part of a training kit. Within the kit, a user can add specific formats at any time. There is no standalone format generation possible outside of a kit. In Articulate 360 AI, however, there is no “kit,” and users can generate standalone individual formats.
Do I need an LMS to use Frontline?
No. Frontline lets you publish and share trackable learning directly, so an LMS isn’t required.
How can I add Reach?
Reach is the LMS included in every Articulate 360 AI Teams subscription, with room for 300 active learners per year. Simply publish your training to Reach 360 from your authoring app to get started. If you have more than 300 active learners, you can subscribe to Reach 360 Pro. It’s priced on the number of active learners you need, starting at $3,600 per year for 1,200 active learners, and it adds fuller reporting plus content and user management inside Articulate 360 AI.
Contact sales to size Reach Pro for your team, or learn more here.
What is the Hybrid plan?
Hybrid brings Articulate 360 AI and Frontline together under one agreement for organizations that need both comprehensive course authoring and fast, scalable learning creation across the business. It simplifies purchasing and includes centralized administration, credit management, and dedicated customer support.
Is my content used to train AI models?
Articulate does not use customer content to train its generative AI models. We apply enterprise-grade security, privacy, and AI-governance practices to protect customer data. Learn more in the Articulate Trust Center.
Can I try Articulate 360 before I buy?
Yes, a free trial is available for both Articulate 360 AI and Frontline. The trial is 14 days long and includes a generous credit allowance to try advanced workflows. You can start a trial now for free.
Do I have to pay in U.S. dollars?
No. Articulate is available through regional resellers that support local currencies and purchasing requirements. Available currencies and purchasing options vary by location.
