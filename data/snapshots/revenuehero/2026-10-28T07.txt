FAQ
Questions about pricing
How does RevenueHero pricing work?
Each plan is a flat monthly platform fee for your account ($79 on Essentials, $159 on Advanced, billed yearly) plus a monthly price for each seat. A seat is a rep who takes meetings. There is no seat minimum. Add-ons on Advanced are priced separately.
What does the platform fee cover?
Everything in your plan, for the whole account: unlimited routers and forms, the Routing Log and reports, CRM and workflow integrations, every admin, onboarding and support on Slack Connect. There is no separate setup fee. It stays the same whatever your team size. You pay on top only for seats and add-ons.
Who needs a seat?
Each rep who takes meetings through RevenueHero needs a seat. An SDR who books meetings for AEs needs a Handoff seat, available on Advanced. Admins who build routers and read reports do not need a seat.
Is there a seat minimum?
No. A team of three reps pays for three seats.
Is there a limit on meetings or form submissions?
No. Every plan includes unlimited meetings and form submissions, with no overage charges.
Is there a free trial?
No. RevenueHero connects deeply with your GTM stack and needs proper setup and team enablement to work well, which a trial can't give you. Start on the monthly plan instead. There's no lock-in.
How long does implementation take?
Most teams are live in under a week. Building a router for a form takes about an hour and connecting your CRM takes just a click. The real timeline depends on how quickly your team can review and sign off, on the routing rules.
How much do I save with yearly billing?
Yearly billing lowers the platform fee and the price of every seat. Here is Advanced with 10 seats and 5 Handoff seats:
Billed monthly: $199 + (10 × $45) + (5 × $25) = $774 a month
Billed yearly: $159 + (10 × $35) + (5 × $20) = $609 a month
That saves 21%, or $165 a month and $1,980 a year. To price your own team, use the price calculator.
How do the add-ons work?
Add-ons are available on Advanced. Handoff seats are $20 per SDR a month on yearly billing ($25 monthly). Spam prevention is $30 a month. Native enrichment is $79 a month on yearly billing ($99 monthly) for 500 credits a month, refreshed every month. For more credits, talk to sales.
We use Chili Piper, HubSpot Meetings or Calendly today. How does switching work?
Teams that switch to us most often come from one of those three, and 50+ have moved from Chili Piper alone, most of them at renewal. Our migration guide covers what changes, who needs to be on the calls and how long it took other teams.
Is RevenueHero secure?
RevenueHero is SOC 2 Type 2 compliant. Advanced adds Okta SSO. We share our security documentation during your evaluation.
