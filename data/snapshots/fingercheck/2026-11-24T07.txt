Pricing FAQs
What pricing plans does Fingercheck offer?
Fingercheck offers four plans: Time & Attendance, 360, 360 Plus, and 360 Pro, a custom-priced plan for businesses that need extra configuration.
Do you have a minimum monthly price?
Yes, each pricing plan includes a monthly subscription fee and a per-employee fee. The Time and Attendance, 360, and 360 Plus plans have a minimum spending requirement equivalent to 5 employees.
How is the pricing structured for Fingercheck’s plans?
Fingercheck plans are structured on a per-employee, per-month subscription cost. The monthly cost varies depending on your chosen plan and the number of employees in your organization.
Do I have to sign a long-term contract to use Fingercheck?
No, Fingercheck offers flexibility. We offer a monthly pricing structure with no long-term commitments.
Are there any additional fees or hidden costs with Fingercheck’s pricing plans?
Fingercheck is fully transparent with our pricing. When you speak with our sales team, you’ll receive a complete quote with every line item clearly laid out. We also offer optional add-ons and services that may carry additional costs, and those will be included in your quote so you know exactly what you’re paying for.
Does Fingercheck integrate with other software?
Yes! We work with accounting programs like QuickBooks, Rent Manager, Sage, and other software types to ensure Fingercheck fits perfectly into your day-to-day operations. Learn about our integrated tools and partners that improve your Fingercheck experience.
How many times can I run payroll per month?
Every Fingercheck plan with payroll comes with unlimited payroll runs. We’ll never charge you extra for things like off-cycle payrolls.
How can I upgrade or downgrade my plan if my business needs change?
You can easily upgrade or downgrade your Fingercheck plan in-app or by contacting our support or sales teams. They will guide you to ensure your plan aligns with your current needs.
I have more questions. Is there someone I can speak with?
Sure thing! Just fill out this form here, and one of our team of experts will get in touch with you to schedule a chat at your convenience.
