More about plans & pricing
What is Mediamodifier?
Mediamodifier goes far beyond being just an online graphic design tool. You're joining an evergrowing ecosystem of apps and services which enables the seamless creation of visual marketing assets across all platforms - from social media, to print templates.
Start with a design, instantly visualize your idea, share it with your team or clients or automate the process with the API. With weekly new content and constant development based on customer feedback, you're not buying a design tool, but an entire team to support you.
Built for entrepreneurs and marketers, we help you to create a better experience for your clients, so you can get your work done better and faster. In addition to our intuitive design editor, our easy-to-use templates, and our extensive mockup library, we also offer an NFT maker, a Mockup API, and a print-on-demand plugin. Additional payments may be required to access extra features.
How does Mediamodifier’s pricing work?
The Professional plan costs $19 a month or $15.83 a month when billed annually ($190/year).
The Team plan costs $45 a month or $37.50 a month when billed annually ($450/year). This plan includes up to 5 members. Additional members can be added for $9 a month or $7.50 a month when billed annually ($90/year).
Prices are in USD. VAT or local sales tax is added at checkout, depending on your country.
What forms of payment do you accept?
You can purchase Mediamodifier with any major credit or debit card, Paypal, or Apple Pay.
Will I be charged sales tax?
All prices displayed are VAT exclusive. VAT will be added to EU customers based on the Member State VAT rate where the customer is located unless a valid VAT number is provided.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You keep Pro features until the end of your current billing period, even if you cancel before renewal.
Is there a sign-up cost on top of my monthly fee?
No, there are no hidden costs to sign up.
Can I change my plan later?
Yes. You can upgrade, switch plans, or move to a team setup whenever your workflow changes.
Do you offer refunds?
Because this is a digital product, refunds are limited once an order is completed. That said, if something is not as described or you run into download issues, we will fix it or review a refund request.
Refund requests can be sent to contact@mediamodifier.com within 14 days of purchase, with a short explanation of the issue.
