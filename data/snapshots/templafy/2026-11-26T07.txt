FAQ
Which Templafy plan is right for me?
Free is ideal for individuals and small teams getting started with on-brand presentations and custom document agents. Business offers the same core features with unlimited slide creation for everyday use. Enterprise supports organization-wide document creation with advanced integrations, automation, brand control and governance.
Is the Free plan really free?
Yes. The Free plan costs nothing, with no credit card required. It includes the core product features, up to five seats and 50 slides shared across your workspace. You can work individually or invite teammates.
What’s the difference between Free and Business?
Free and Business include the same product features, including custom document agents, company templates, a shared content library and collaboration tools. The difference is creation usage: Free includes 50 slides per workspace, where Business offers unlimited slide creation, subject to fair usage.
How much does Business cost?
Business costs $40 per seat per month. You can use it individually or invite teammates, with pricing based on the number of seats in your workspace.
Can I create my own document agents and use my company branding?
Yes. Both Free and Business let you create reusable custom document agents, use company templates and branding, and provide context through uploaded files. You can also share agents and approved content with teammates in your workspace.
Can I use Templafy in PowerPoint and other AI assistant?
Yes. Both Free, Business and Enterprise work with the Templafy PowerPoint add-in and connect to Claude, ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot. You can create presentations through your preferred workflow and continue editing them in PowerPoint.
What additional capabilities does Enterprise offer?
Enterprise adds centralized brand, content and compliance controls, connections to company knowledge and business systems, and advanced document automation. It supports deploying agents and approved content across departments and regions, with capabilities configured to your organization’s needs.
How is Enterprise priced?
Enterprise pricing is tailored to your organization’s requirements, deployment and selected capabilities. Contact our team to discuss your needs and receive a custom price.
