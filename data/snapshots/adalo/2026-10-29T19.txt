What is Adalo?
Adalo is the visual AI app builder for database-driven web apps and native iOS and Android apps—one version across all three platforms. See every screen on one canvas, preview on any device, and visually direct the AI to refine your app before publishing to the Apple App Store and Google Play. Plans start free for building and testing, with app store publishing from $65/month on the Builder plan.
What is the cheapest way to get a native app on the App Store without coding?
Build and test your whole app on Adalo's Free plan, then upgrade to the Builder plan at $65/month when you're ready to publish a native iOS and Android app—new Builders get their first month for $30. Unlike platforms that produce web wrappers, Adalo generates true native builds (IPA and AAB) from a single visual canvas where you can see every screen, preview on device, and visually direct the AI—then publish directly to the Apple App Store and Google Play.
Can I build an app for free with Adalo?
Yes. Adalo's Free plan is free forever and lets you build unlimited test apps with up to 500 records per app and unlimited screens. You can design your entire app on Adalo's visual canvas—see every screen at once, preview on any device, and use AI to generate and refine your app—before deciding to publish.
What's the difference between the Builder and Business plans?
Builder ($65/month) is for personal projects and smaller apps: AI building included each month, 2 published apps, 5 app editors, App Store, Google Play and web publishing, one instant update a month, custom domains, integrations (Stripe, Maps, push notifications and your own custom actions), custom fonts, geolocation and design versions. Localization, crash monitoring and analytics are available as paid add-ons. Business ($200/month) is built for production and ready for whatever your business needs: everything in Builder plus more AI building each month, 5 published apps, 10 app editors, unlimited instant updates, every line of the code Ada writes, localization, crash monitoring, analytics, the Collections API and external databases.
How is Adalo billed?
Pay monthly, quarterly or yearly. Builder is $65/month, $175.50/quarter or $624/year, and Business is $200/month, $540/quarter or $1,920/year: quarterly billing saves 10% and yearly billing saves 20%. The Free plan is free forever and needs no credit card. New Builders get their first month for $30 on Builder with monthly billing.
How does Adalo's pricing compare to Bubble and FlutterFlow?
Adalo's Builder plan is $65/month for web and native app store publishing with a built-in relational database and no Workload Units or per-action fees. Bubble starts at $69/month plus Workload Unit overage charges for web and mobile building. FlutterFlow starts at $39/month per editor but requires external databases and services, which add to the total cost. Adalo includes a built-in database, and a single version that publishes as a Progressive Web App, a native Android app and a native iOS app with no per-action fees.
Does Adalo charge per user or per action?
No. You pay a flat monthly fee with no per-action metering and no per-seat pricing, so you won't face surprise overage fees as your app grows, unlike platforms that meter by Workload Units. The one optional extra is AI credits, if you want more AI building than your plan includes.
Are there hidden costs when building with Adalo?
No. Adalo's pricing is straightforward. Each plan has a flat monthly fee. You won't encounter Workload Unit overages, per-action metering, or mandatory third-party database fees, and extra AI credits are only charged if you choose to buy them. The main additional costs are an Apple Developer account ($99/year) and Google Play account ($25 one-time) if you're publishing to the app stores, which are required by Apple and Google regardless of which platform you build with.
How much does it cost to publish an app to the App Store with Adalo?
Adalo's Builder plan at $65/month includes publishing to both the Apple App Store and Google Play, and new Builders get their first month for $30. You'll also need an Apple Developer account ($99/year) and a Google Play Developer account ($25 one-time fee). You can build and fully test your app on the Free plan first, then upgrade to Builder when you're ready to publish—so you only pay once your app is ready to go live.
Can I build and edit with AI in Adalo?
Yes, with Ada, Adalo's AI assistant, you can use natural language to describe what you want and Ada will build it for you. You can even then tell her things you want to change, be it one thing or many things at once, and she'll change whatever you wish.
Does AI come at extra cost?
Ada is included in every Adalo plan, along with AI building each month: 5 prompts a month on Free, and more on Builder and Business, which have no monthly prompt limit. If you need more, buy extra AI credits any time; nothing is charged unless you do.
Does Adalo have an enterprise plan?
Yes. Adalo Blue (blue.adalo.com) offers dedicated infrastructure and custom monitoring for scale, can deploy on premise, and connect to any datasource, including legacy systems that lack any form of API.
