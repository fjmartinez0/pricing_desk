Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need a subscription to use BeFunky?
While many of BeFunky’s tools are free to use or preview for free, you will need a subscription to use our premium features.
Which plan is right for me?
BeFunky Plus is a great fit for everyday photo editing and design. Pro is best for more advanced workflows, including batch AI editing and background removal.
What's the difference between Plus and Pro?
Plus gives you premium photo editing and design tools, plus 1,000 AI credits each month for the AI Image Generator. Pro adds Batch AI Editing and Batch Background Remover, along with 5,000 AI credits each month for the AI Image Generator and Batch AI tools. Learn more about the differences between BeFunky Plus and Pro.
What are credits and how do they work?
Credits are used to create images with the AI Image Generator and to use Batch AI tools. Plus includes 1,000 AI credits each month for the AI Image Generator, while Pro includes 5,000 AI credits each month for the AI Image Generator and Batch AI tools. Plus and Pro users can also purchase Top-up Credits whenever they need more.
What happens if I run out of credits?
Plus and Pro users can purchase additional credits at any time to continue using AI-powered tools that use credits.
Do unused credits roll over each month?
Monthly credits included with your Plus or Pro subscription do not roll over. Any unused Top-up credits will roll over as long as your subscription remains active.
Can I use BeFunky on multiple devices?
Yes, you can access BeFunky on your smartphone, computer, or tablet. As long as you’re signed in, your subscription works across all supported devices.
Will my plan renew automatically?
Yes, your subscription will automatically renew at the end of each billing cycle (monthly or annual) unless you cancel. Here's more info on our Renewal Policy.
Can I switch my billing from Monthly to Annual?
Yes, you can switch from Monthly to Annual billing at any time from your Account page.
Can I switch between Plus and Pro?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade between Plus and Pro at any time from your Account page.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes! You can cancel your BeFunky Plus subscription at any time from your Account page. Here's more info on how to cancel your subscription.
Will I lose access to premium features if I cancel?
You’ll continue to have access to premium features until the end of your current billing cycle.
