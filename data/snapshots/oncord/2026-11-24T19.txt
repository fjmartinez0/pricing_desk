Frequently Asked Questions
Is support included?
Unlimited e-mail support is included with all Oncord business and enterprise plans. Reporting shows that our turnaround time to address
non-urgent issues is usually less than 12 hours, and enterprise plans receive priority support. Support for Oncord lite plans is provided
through our Community Forum.
What if I decide to migrate away from Oncord?
You're not locked-in with a fixed term contract with Oncord. You'll setup a new website elsewhere, and just need to let us know when it's
time to terminate your Oncord account. We'll ask you to confirm that you've downloaded a back-up of your data before we terminate your
account.
Does my website need to be hosted by Oncord, or can I host elsewhere on my own server?
The Oncord application can't be installed on another web host. Bundling our platform and hosting infrastructure allows us to manage web
hosting, security and platform updates for you. Oncord web hosting is almost always faster and more secure than shared web hosting
options.
Can I use Oncord's Marketing or Commerce features without an Oncord website?
Every Oncord account starts with a website, including secure hosting and a connected contact database. Marketing and Commerce are optional
add-ons that work alongside your website, rather than as standalone services.
What counts as a send towards my monthly Marketing limit?
Both email and SMS messages count towards your monthly Marketing send limit. Each email or SMS message sent to an individual recipient
counts as one send.
I don't need an online store, do I still require the Commerce features?
The Commerce features are required if you're processing sales via Oncord, such as, running paid events, memberships or integrating with
accounting software.
What payment options do you accept?
You can pay your monthly Oncord subscription via any of the following payment methods:
- Visa debit and credit cards.
- Mastercard debit and credit cards.
- American Express debit and credit cards.
What happens if I exceed my e-mail hosting storage?
Where you've opted to host mail with Oncord, each inbox will cost $5 per month, per 15G.
Examples:
One inbox using 5G costs $5
One inbox using 16G costs $10
Two inboxes using 5G each costs $10
Two inboxes using 16G each costs $20
