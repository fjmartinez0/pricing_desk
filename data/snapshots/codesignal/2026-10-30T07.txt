Frequently asked
questions
What is CodeSignal?
CodeSignal is an AI-native skills platform with three products: Hire (skills assessments and AI interviews for recruiting teams), Learn (practice-based upskilling for individuals and organizations), and Education (career-readiness programs for academic institutions). All three run on the same validated, objective data on what people can actually do.
Which plan is right for me?
Use Hire if you’re assessing and interviewing candidates, Learn if you’re building your own skills or upskilling a workforce, and Education if you’re an institution running career-readiness programs. Each has its own plans, and you can get started on your own or talk to our team.
What assessments are included in each plan?
To see what assessments are included in each plan, visit the Assessment Explorer.
How much does CodeSignal cost?
It depends on which suite you use. Hire starts at $79 per month billed annually, Learn is free for individuals and Cosmo+ is $24.99 per month, and Education, plus enterprise Learn and Hire plans, is custom-priced. Compare the plans for each in the tabs above.
How do CodeSignal Hire credits work?
In CodeSignal Hire, a credit is used when a candidate starts an assessment, AI interview, or live interview, not when you send one. Credits are interchangeable across all three formats, so you only spend a credit when a candidate actually begins, never for unopened invitations.
What happens if I run out of Hire credits?
You keep hiring without interruption. Once you use your included credits, CodeSignal charges on-demand credits at $20 each, billed on your next cycle, so there’s no hard stop mid-search. You can track your usage anytime on your account’s Billing page.
Is CodeSignal Learn free, and what does it cost?
CodeSignal Learn is free for individuals to start, with unlimited Cosmo+ access for $24.99 per month. Teams and Enterprise plans add team management, analytics, and integrations at custom pricing. Learn is subscription-based, so there are no per-credit charges.
How is CodeSignal priced for schools and universities?
CodeSignal Education is custom-priced by program size, from a single Department Pilot to Multi-Program and Institution-wide rollouts. Individuals can start with CodeSignal Learn for free. Contact our team for a quote tailored to your institution’s learners and programs.
Is CodeSignal secure and compliant?
Every plan across Hire, Learn, and Education includes SOC 2 Type II, GDPR and CCPA compliance, and data encryption at rest and in transit. Enterprise and institution-wide plans add custom DPA, SSO/SCIM/JIT, custom security review, and audit logs.
Can I upgrade or change my plan later?
Yes. Plans scale within Hire (Build, Grow, Pro), Learn (Individuals, Teams, Enterprise), and Education (pilot through institution-wide), and you keep the same platform and data as you grow.
