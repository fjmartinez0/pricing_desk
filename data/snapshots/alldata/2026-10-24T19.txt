Frequently Asked Questions
How do you buy ALLDATA?
Our flagship products, ALLDATA Repair® and ALLDATA Collision®, are available for an annual subscription rate with monthly payments. There are two ways to buy today: one is through a Business Solutions Manager who can show you a live demo, help you customize a bundle that works best for your business, and process the order for you. Or you can purchase most products online yourself, where you can also review product information, customer testimonials, and how-to videos. Existing subscribers can also add products through the customer portal.
Are all ALLDATA products online?
Yes, ALLDATA’s repair information and shop management systems (SMS) are exclusively online solutions. We no longer offer discs or locally installed information. If you see a disc version for sell, you’re not getting the real ALLDATA. In addition to our online offerings, there is an ALLDATA Tech-Assist® hotline service for diagnostic support and troubleshooting.
Is any of the information in ALLDATA rewritten?
No, none of the OEM information has been rewritten. ALLDATA has agreements with the majority of OEMs to publish mechanical and collision repair procedures as they are made available directly from the OEMs. In addition to OEM information, ALLDATA offers non-OE interactive diagrams, top-ranked diagnostic solutions from ALLDATA Find-A-Fix (driven by data), and probable causes/verified fixes from our ALLDATA Community forum.
How do I determine which ALLDATA products are right for my shop?
Check out our most popular bundles or contact a Business Solutions Manager to get a demo of all products and help you customize a product bundle specific to your business needs.
Who uses ALLDATA’s repair information?
ALLDATA is primarily used by auto repair professionals (general repair shops, auto body, dealerships and other specialty repair shops) to ensure vehicles are repaired properly, back to OEM specifications. Today, more than 400,000 technicians trust ALLDATA’s industry leading repair information, diagnostic solutions, and shop management tools. ALLDATA is also used by educators to teach automotive repair and by insurance companies to refer to OEM requirements.
What is the difference between ALLDATA Repair® & ALLDATA Collision®?
Both products offer:
Unedited OEM repair information for 95% of vehicles on the road today
OEM repair and maintenance procedures, diagnostic information, TSBs, DTCs, and more
300k non-OE interactive color wiring diagrams (in addition to OEM diagrams)
Probably causes & verifies/suggested repairs in ALLDATA Community
Support from the ALLDATA Library team for hard-to-find OEM data
ALLDATA Collision adds information that is primarily used by autobody shops, like OEM procedures for sectioning and structural repairs, handling of new materials, panel replacement sensors, manufacturer position statements, and more.
Can I add additional products at any time?
Yes, you can add most products through the Customer Portal or by calling in and speaking with a representative. The only productyou can’t add on your own is ALLDATA Manage Online. Reach out to your Account Executive or Business Solutions Manager if you’d like to learn more.
