FAQ
Build vs. buy a webhooks service?
We created Svix because we believe that when it comes to webhooks, buying is much preferable to building. Buying means that you don't have to spend your precious engineering time and effort onto maintaining the webhooks system. For a thorough comparison, please refer to the build vs. buy matrix.
How do I receive webhooks?
Svix supports sending, receiving, and testing webhooks. The best way to receive webhooks from external services is to use Svix Ingest. Svix Ingest lets you receive webhooks with confidence thanks to highly reliable and low latency system that can handle high throughput and traffic spikes.
Can I send, receive, and test webhooks for free?
Yes! Svix offers a generous free tier for sending, receiving and testing webhooks. No credit card required! If you're only looking to test your webhooks, you can use the Svix Play webhooks tester without even signing up!
How does Svix count webhook usage?
Svix pricing consists of a base price and usage based price depending on the amount of webhooks you send. With Svix, you only pay for what is actually sent on your behalf, meaning that only attempted messages are counted towards usage. Messages filtered by Svix (e.g. when there are no endpoints) and retries are both free.
How does the Professional free trial work?
Professional plans start with a 30-day free trial that includes every Professional feature. When the trial ends, billing starts at $490 per month plus any usage beyond the 50,000 included messages.
Can Svix handle our scale?
We process billions of messages for our customers, and we power top companies like Brex, Benchling, and Drata. We can most likely handle your scale, but please reach out if you have any specific questions or requirements.
Does Svix offer SLAs?
We offer uptime SLAs of 99.999% on Enterprise, 99.99% on Professional, and 99.9% on Basic. The Free plan does not include an SLA.
