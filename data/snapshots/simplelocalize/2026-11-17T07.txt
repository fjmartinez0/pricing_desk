Frequently asked questions
What are translation keys and how do they compare to words or strings?
A translation key is one text in your app, for example checkout.button = "Pay now". You pay for keys, not for languages or words: one key translated into 20 languages is still one key. Many tools bill per word or per string in every language instead. For example, 500 keys with ~5 words each in 10 languages would count as ~25,000 words or 5,000 strings there. Learn more about translation keys.
Who counts as a team member?
Team members are users you invite to your workspace: developers, translators, reviewers and admins. Pending invitations also count toward the limit. People who only suggest translations via public suggestions are contributors, they don't count toward the limit and are unlimited on all plans. On the Business plan, you can add 25 more team members at any time.
What happens when I exceed the translation keys limit?
Nothing is deleted. We will send you an email, and creating new keys, importing and exporting will be paused until you add more keys to your plan (+500 keys at a time), remove unused keys or switch to a bigger plan.
Is there a free trial?
Yes, every new account starts with a 14-day trial of the Team plan, no credit card required. After the trial, your account moves to the free Community plan unless you choose a paid plan.
What are auto-translation characters?
Auto-translation characters are counted for every target language. For example, translating "Hello World" (11 characters including space) into 5 languages uses 55 characters. If your texts have ~30 characters on average, 50,000 characters translate around 1,600 texts. Need more? Add 250k characters at any time as a one-time top-up.
Do auto-translation characters roll over?
Yes, unused characters roll over to the following months and stay valid while your subscription is active. You can collect up to 500k (Developer), 1M (Team) or 2M (Business) unused characters.
Can I use my own API keys for auto-translation?
Yes, you can use your own API keys for DeepL, Google Translate, OpenAI and other providers on all paid plans. Translations made with your own keys don't use auto-translation characters from your plan.
Can I change my plan or limits?
Yes, you can switch plans at any time and extend your current plan without upgrading: add translation keys, auto-translation characters or, on the Business plan, team members. The price is adjusted based on the remaining time in your billing cycle.
Do prices include VAT?
No, prices exclude VAT and other applicable taxes. If you have a VAT number, add it during checkout, and if you are eligible, VAT will be deducted from your invoice. You can also add it later on the invoice page. See how to add a VAT number.
What are per-customer translations?
Per-customer translations allow you to create custom translations for each of your customers. This is useful when you want to provide personalized content or translations for specific users. Learn more about per-customer translations.
Do you offer custom plans?
Yes, we offer custom plans for large teams and enterprises, including higher limits, a dedicated account manager and payment via purchase order. Contact us at contact@simplelocalize.io to learn more.
