Frequently asked questions
What are translation keys?
One translation key can be translated into many language translations. Such approach makes your costs more predictable. Do not confuse it with a 'translation' or 'string'. Learn more about translation keys.
How do I get a VAT refund?
Fill your VAT number during the checkout process, and if you are eligible, VAT will be deducted from your invoice. If you did not add it during the checkout, you can add it later on on the invoice page. See how to add a VAT number.
What are auto-translation characters?
Auto-translation character is a character that is sent from your source translation to external service like Google Translate to translate it to another language. For example, translating "Hello World" (11 characters including space) to 5 languages will consume 55 characters (11 characters x 5 languages).
Do auto-translate characters rollover?
Yes, unused auto-translation characters rollover to the following months and they are valid until your subscription plan is active. You can gather up to 2 million unused characters in your account.
Can I use your own API keys for auto-translation?
Yes, you can use your own API keys for auto-translation services. Using your own API key doesn't consume auto-translation characters from your SimpleLocalize plan.
Can I change plan limits?
Yes, you can switch between plans at any time. You can also change your current plan limits, such as the number of translation keys, adding more auto-translation characters, or adding more users. The price will be adjusted accordingly based on the remaining time in your billing cycle.
What are per-customer translations?
Per-customer translations allow you to create custom translations for each of your customers. This is useful when you want to provide personalized content or translations for specific users. Learn more about per-customer translations.
Do you offer custom plans?
Yes, we offer custom plans for large teams and enterprises. Contact us at contact@simplelocalize.io to learn more.
