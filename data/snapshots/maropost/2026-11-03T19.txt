Frequently Asked Questions
Plans, GMV limits, overages, and billing for Commerce Cloud.
How is Commerce Cloud priced?
+
Each plan is a monthly subscription with an included GMV (gross merchandise value) allowance. Build includes $50K GMV. Essentials, Professional, and Enterprise include $500K, $2M, and $10M. The monthly price you see is the subscription; GMV above the allowance is billed as an overage.
What happens if I exceed my included GMV?
+
You keep selling. Volume over the plan limit is billed as a percentage of GMV based on your plan. Overages are automatic — you do not need to buy a new pack mid-month.
Do you offer a free trial?
+
Yes, we offer a free trial of Marketing Cloud, Service Cloud, and Commerce Cloud.
How does annual billing work?
+
Annually is 20% off Essentials, Professional, and Enterprise. The amount shown is the monthly equivalent, billed once a year. Build stays at list price on annual and monthly — there is no Save 20% on that tier.
Can I change plans as GMV grows?
+
Yes. Moving up raises the included GMV and lowers the overage rate. Talk to us before a large jump so the cutover lands on the right tier instead of running a month of high overage.
Which currencies can I pay in?
+
USD, AUD, EUR, and CAD. Use the currency control on this page to preview list prices. Checkout currency is set when you subscribe or when sales writes the order.
Frequently Asked Questions
Contacts, email volume, SMS add-ons, and billing for Marketing Cloud.
How is Marketing Cloud priced?
+
Plans are based on included marketing contacts and email sends. Build includes 50K contacts and 300K emails. Essentials includes 250K contacts and 2M emails, Professional 1M and 10M, and Enterprise 5M and 50M. Email is in the subscription; SMS is not.
What happens if I go over my included contacts?
+
Additional contacts are billed based on your plan. Overages are applied automatically when your contact list exceeds the plan limit.
Is SMS included in the plan price?
+
No. SMS is an add-on: a one-time setup fee, a monthly prepaid tier that includes a set number of segments (including a low-volume Tier 0), then per-segment rates by send region (United States, Canada, Europe, Australia). Open Explore SMS add-ons on this page for setup fees and tiers. MMS and RCS are billed separately where they are offered.
Do you offer a free trial?
+
Yes, we offer a free trial of Marketing Cloud, Service Cloud, and Commerce Cloud.
How does annual billing work?
+
Annual billing is 20% off Essentials, Professional, and Enterprise. Prices on the cards are monthly equivalents billed once a year. Build does not get the annual discount. SMS add-on rates are separate from the annual platform discount.
Can I change plans later?
+
Yes. Move up when the contact or email allowance is no longer enough — higher tiers include more volume and a lower per-contact overage. SMS stays an add-on on every plan.
Frequently Asked Questions
Ticket allowances, per-ticket overages, and billing for Service Cloud.
How is Service Cloud priced?
+
Plans are priced by included tickets per month: 200 on Build, 2,000 on Essentials, 6,000 on Professional, and 20,000 on Enterprise. Agents, users, and customer contacts are unlimited on every plan. The subscription is the monthly (or annual) platform fee; extra tickets are overage.
What happens if I go over my included tickets?
+
Additional tickets are billed based on your plan. Overages are applied automatically when ticket volume exceeds the plan limit.
Do you offer a free trial?
+
Yes, we offer a free trial of Marketing Cloud, Service Cloud, and Commerce Cloud.
How does annual billing work?
+
Annually takes 20% off Essentials, Professional, and Enterprise. The card price is the monthly equivalent, billed once a year. Build stays at list price either way.
Are there long-term contracts?
+
Monthly plans renew month to month. Annual plans are prepaid for the year at the discounted rate. Changing tiers mid-term is possible — sales will prorate or align the next invoice to the new ticket allowance.
Can I change plans later?
+
Yes. If ticket volume is climbing, a higher tier usually costs less than staying on a lower plan and paying the higher per-ticket overage. Compare the included allowance and overage rate before you switch.
Frequently Asked Questions
Search-session allowances, overages, and billing for Merchandising Cloud.
How is Merchandising Cloud priced?
+
Plans are priced by included search sessions per month: 50K on Build, 250K on Essentials, 1M on Professional, and 5M on Enterprise. The subscription covers the session allowance; traffic above that is overage.
What happens if I go over my included sessions?
+
Additional sessions are billed based on your plan. Overages apply automatically when search traffic exceeds the plan cap.
How does annual billing work?
+
Annual billing is 20% off Essentials, Professional, and Enterprise. You still see a per-month equivalent on the cards, billed once a year. Build does not receive the annual discount.
Can I change plans later?
+
Yes. If search traffic is outgrowing the allowance, moving up increases included sessions and lowers the per-1,000 overage. That is usually cheaper than staying on a smaller plan through a peak season.
Which currencies can I pay in?
+
USD, AUD, EUR, and CAD. Switch currency on this page to preview list prices. The currency on the order is locked at checkout or when sales sets up the subscription.
