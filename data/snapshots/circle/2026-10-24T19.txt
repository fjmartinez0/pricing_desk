Questions & Answers
Can I schedule a consultation with a real person?
Absolutely. If you’re considering signing up for Circle’s Professional and above, you can always schedule a consultation with our Sales team here. If you’re already a paying customer, please reach out directly to support@circle.so.
Does Circle offer a money back guarantee? (Yes!)
We always offer a 30-day, 100% money back guarantee. If for any reason, you decide that Circle isn’t the right fit for your community, just let us know! We’ll get you a full refund, no questions asked.
I'm just getting started. Do you offer any helpful training, coaching, or extra support?
We do! When you sign up for Circle you’re not building alone – you immediately get access to our private community where you can connect with over 27K members. Inside, you’ll be able to connect with other members, access live workshops, weekly coaching calls, office hours, behind-the-scenes tours, Q&A sessions, expert interviews, and more! You’ll receive an email within minutes of signing up.
Do you offer migration services to get me started on Circle?
Yes, we offer dedicated migration services to make the switch to Circle easy!
Payment migrations:
Our free payment migration service transfers active subscriptions and payment information from your legacy platform to Circle. Request your free migration to see if you qualify.
Course migrations:
Our course migration service moves your existing course content to Circle, simplifying your tech stack and keeping your students engaged on a single platform. This service is available for free for Annual Business plans and above. Apply today to see if your courses qualify for migration.
Email Hub migrations
Our Email Hub migration service moves your existing contacts, automations, and email marketing assets into Circle, so you can manage your audience and campaigns in one place. This service helps ensure a smooth transition without losing key data or workflows. Apply today to see if your account qualifies for migration support.
