What counts as a unit?
Each home, condo, or lot in your community counts as one unit. A 48-home neighborhood is 48 units.
How much does PayHOA cost?
One plan with every feature, priced by the number of units: from $49 a month billed yearly ($54 billed monthly) for 1–25 units, up to $249 a month billed yearly ($275 billed monthly) for 401–500 units. Over 500 units, it’s $0.55 per unit a month, the same monthly or yearly. The calculator above prices your exact size.
Plus Gable AI: on by default, free for your first 3 months, then from $24.50 a month, billed with your plan. You can turn it off from your subscription page.
Who pays the online payment fees?
You decide, in your fee settings. Online payments cost $2.45 per bank transfer and 3.5% + $0.50 per card payment. Your community can absorb these fees or charge homeowners a convenience fee to cover them. You set it separately for card and bank transfer payments. Homeowners can always pay by check instead, which you record in PayHOA for free.
Where does the money go when homeowners pay?
Homeowner payments land in your community’s Treasury, a financial account powered by Stripe. From there, the board pays vendors, reimburses homeowners, and moves money to the association’s own accounts. Card numbers stay with Stripe.
What happens when the 30-day trial ends?
Nothing is charged during the trial, and you don’t need a card to start it. If you keep PayHOA, billing starts the day after your trial ends, monthly or yearly, whichever you choose. Gable AI stays free until 3 months after sign-up, then it’s billed with your plan.
Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?
Yes, anytime. Yearly billing costs about 9% less than paying month to month, for example $59 instead of $65 a month for 26–50 units. Communities over 500 units and management companies pay the same $0.55 per unit either way.
Are there setup, onboarding, or data import fees?
No. Our Onboarding Specialists import your units, owners, starting balances, and custom fields and help you set up, at no charge. Most communities are up and running in a few days.
How much does Gable AI cost?
Gable AI comes switched on with every new PayHOA account, and your first 3 months after sign-up are free. After that, it’s billed the same way as your plan: $27 a month on the $54 monthly plan, or $24.50 a month on the $49 plan billed yearly. The price grows with your community’s size, and over 500 units and for management companies, it’s $0.27 per unit a month. Don’t need it? Turn it off from your subscription page.
Already a PayHOA customer? You get 3 free months of Gable too.
Are there any limits on storage, messages, or homeowners?
No. Every plan includes unlimited storage, unlimited texts, emails, and calls, and no cap on homeowners or admins.
How does pricing work for management companies?
$0.55 per unit a month, with a $275 monthly minimum. Each community gets its own account, and you get one login for all of them. Gable AI is $0.27 per unit a month after the first 3 months. Talk to our team about moving a portfolio over, or see how PayHOA works for management companies.
Does PayHOA charge sales tax?
In certain states, yes. Because of the Wayfair decision, we’re required to collect sales tax in states where we meet specific sales thresholds. If it applies in your state, you’ll see it on your invoices automatically.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you’ve paid for, and prepaid time isn’t refunded, so monthly billing is the most flexible. We keep your data for 30 days after you cancel.
