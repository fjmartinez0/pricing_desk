Data Privacy Compliance is Complex and Overwhelming
Compliance is becoming increasingly more difficult with the amount of data privacy legislation constantly being drafted worldwide. In our interconnected world, you may need to follow laws you didn’t even know existed. Trying to adhere to all these regulations on your own can be confusing, time-consuming, and potentially risky.
Termly Makes It Easy and Fast
Termly’s compliance solutions can ease the burden and help you meet the most stringent legal requirements. We offer vetted and easy-to-use solutions for data privacy laws from countries around the world. From consent management to policy creation and automatic updates, we are your all-in-one solution to the hassle of ever-evolving data privacy requirements.

Does Termly work with lawyers and legal experts?
Yes, Termly works with a team of lawyers and legal experts to ensure our consent manager and policy generators stay compliant.
How long does it take to generate a policy?
The time it takes to generate a policy will vary based on the complexities of your business. You may be able to generate a policy in as little as ten minutes.
Which laws does Termly cover?
Termly’s products are designed to cover 28 different data privacy laws from around the globe (see comprehensive list), including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA), and 16 other state-level U.S. data privacy laws.
Are subdomains included in my plan?
Subdomain scanning is included in the Pro+ plan.
How is the free plan different from the paid plan?
The free plan is designed for businesses still in the start-up or development phase, while the paid plan is designed for businesses seeking complete, ongoing compliance.
