Frequently asked questions
How do credits work?
You consume credits whenever the AI generates a video or makes a change you ask for. Everyone in your workspace shares one credit balance, and every plan includes all three models:
High(Claude Opus 5.5): our highest-quality videos
Medium(Claude Sonnet 5.5): strong quality for fewer credits
Light(Claude Haiku 5.5): Lowest credit cost for generation and AI edits.

What is a credit?
A credit is the unit we charge for the AI work that builds your video. Generating a video costs credits, and every change you ask the AI video editor to make costs credits.
Every plan includes a set number of credits, and everyone in your workspace spends from the same balance.
What uses credits?
Before you send an instruction to the AI, the screen shows you what that turn will cost. Credit cost has multiple variables, including model, length and AI edits. Changes you make yourself cost nothing: trimming a clip, moving a caption, replacing an image, reordering scenes.

How do different AI models impact credit usage?
On each subscription plan, you can build videos with a choice of three models: Light(Claude Haiku 5.5), Medium(Claude Sonnet 5.5), and High(Claude Opus 5.5) (default). You pick the model before you generate, and the screen shows the credit cost for that model before you send anything. You can’t switch between models once you start generating a video.
High(Claude Opus 5.5): Maximum detail and polish for complex, high-stakes videos. Uses about 1.25× the credits of Medium (Sonnet 5.5) to generate, and about 2× for AI edits.
Medium(Claude Sonnet 5.5): Best balance of speed, quality, and credits. Handles detailed instructions and longer scripts.
Light(Claude Haiku 5.5): Lowest credit cost for generation and AI edits.

Do credits roll over? Do they expire?
It depends on how you pay.
Monthly. Credits do not roll over. Your balance resets to your plan's allowance on each billing date.
Annual. You receive one twelfth of your yearly allowance each month, and anything you do not spend stays in your balance for the rest of the term. Nothing is lost month to month; the balance returns to zero when your term renews.
Free. The 250 credits are one-time. They do not refresh.
What happens when I run out of credits mid-month?
Generating stops until you have credits again. You keep access to everything you have already made, and you can still edit it by hand, publish it and export it.
On a paid plan you have two options: move up to a higher level, which gives you the larger allowance straight away, or wait for your balance to refresh on your next billing date. We do not sell one-off credit packs, and you cannot buy a second allowance at the level you are already on.
On the free plan the 250 credits do not refresh, so moving to a paid plan is the only way to keep generating.
How does this work for a team?
Credits belong to the workspace, so there is one balance and everyone in the workspace spends from it.
The free plan is one person. A Pro plan holds up to 10 people, across every role. If you have more than 10 people you need Enterprise, where the number of seats needed is custom to your contract.
Does this replace my current Arcade plan? What happens to my demos?
Arcade continues to support interactive demos! You can access both video and interactive demos from your login, but interactive demos are sold separately and are priced on their own page. A demo subscription does not include AI video credits, and an AI video subscription does not include access to any paid interactive demo features.
Can I cancel or downgrade at any time? What happens to unused credits?
You can cancel or downgrade at any time. The change takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for, and you keep your plan and your credits until then.
Credits have no cash value. We do not refund them (though we can replenish credits in the event of a mistake) and we cannot pay them out, so anything left in your balance when the plan ends is gone.
What if I need more than the top plan?
Enterprise is built for teams who need to control how video gets made and who can use it.
It includes single sign-on, role-based access control so you decide who can generate and who can only watch, SOC 2 reporting, data residency and retention controls, and support for the security review your procurement team runs. It also includes unlimited brand kits, so every brand you work on has its own, and a personal customer success manager who runs your onboarding and platform success.
Credits are custom to your team’s needs and will be granted in one lump at the start of your term and reset when the contract renews. Enterprise is invoiced, and the contract terms are negotiated with you.
How long can a video be, and what can I export?
Publishing and exporting are free on every plan and do not use credits.

Can I use these videos commercially? Who owns them?
You own the videos you make. On any paid plan you can use them commercially, including in advertising, sales material and anything else you publish.
On the free plan your videos carry an Arcade watermark.
Can I use my own brand assets?
Your brand is what the video gets built from. The AI uses your fonts, your colors and your logo to build every scene, so the finished video looks like your company made it.
You keep those assets in a Brand Kit. Free and Pro plans include one. Enterprise includes as many as you need, which is what agencies and companies with several brands use.
