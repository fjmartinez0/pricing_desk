Frequently asked questions
What is an event?
Each record that is inserted, updated or deleted in the destination (data warehouse, database, etc.) is counted as one event.
Can I get add-ons on top of all packages?
You can get a variety of add-ons on professional and business critical plans. Add-ons are currently not available for free and starter plans.
Can I get support to shift to Hevo from my current ELT solution?
Yes, you can! Contact sales to find out more about our professional services add-on, through which you can receive extensive onboarding assistance.
Can I build multiple pipelines from a connector?
Yes, you can create any number of pipelines from the same connector.
How do I switch to a different plan?
You can switch to a different plan at any point by speaking to our team.
What happens if I exceed the number of events available in my plan?
Events usage above your subscription plan's quota is considered On-Demand and is charged additionally on top of your Plan.
What support options are accessible?
We provide live chat and email support with dedicated SLAs across different tiers. Please contact our team to know more about SLAs.
How many users can be added to my account?
Depending on the plan, you’ll be able to add 5, 10, or unlimited users to your account. Users are the number of email IDs added to the Hevo platform.
