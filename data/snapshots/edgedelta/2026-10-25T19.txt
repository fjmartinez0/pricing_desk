Questions
How credits work.
What do credits pay for?
+
Credits pay for the work your agents do: AI investigations, model inference, and data storage. Creating agents, connectors, and workflows does not use credits, and there is no limit on how many you set up.
How many credits come with the Pro plan?
+
The Pro plan includes $20 of credits every month with the $20 subscription. When your agents need more, you can buy additional credits from the app.
What happens when my credits run out?
+
Agents pause usage-based work when your balance reaches zero, so you are never billed for more than you bought. If you want agents to keep running, turn on auto-buy and Edge Delta tops up your credits as they are used. Auto-buy is off by default.
Can I cap how much I spend?
+
Yes. Set a monthly spending cap and Edge Delta will not bill more than that amount in a month, even with auto-buy turned on.
Is there a free trial?
+
Yes. The Hobby plan is a free 14-day trial with full access to AI teammates, connectors, and evaluations. No credit card is required.
How long is my data kept?
+
The Pro plan keeps data and agent memory for 30 days. Custom plans set retention to match your compliance needs.
