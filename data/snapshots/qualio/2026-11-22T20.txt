Frequently asked questions
How is Qualio priced?
An annual platform subscription with three plans. The fee is flat per plan and grows with your compliance surface: the domains you carry, the sites you run, the systems you connect, and the licensed users who author, review, and approve records. It is never priced per basic user, per message, or per AI action. There is no usage metering: the AI does more work for you without the bill moving.
Is the AI extra?
No. Approvals, audit trails, provenance, and validation packs are part of the platform fee and are never metered.
Which plan is right for us?
Formalizing quality for the first time, with an audit or certification coming: Foundation. Compliance as a year-round operation across multiple frameworks, with more systems to connect: Growth. A mature, complex compliance estate, whether that is multiple entities and sites, deep validation and security demands, or executive-level governance needs: Scale.
How do seats work?
Everyone in your company can read and train at no cost, in every plan. The people who author, review, and approve records are licensed users; plans include a set of them, and more are a priced add-on.
Can we add domains, sites, systems, or users mid-term?
Yes. That is the designed expansion path, and it follows the same shape as your company's growth. New market, new framework: add a compliance domain, with included coverage first (Foundation includes 3 domains, Growth includes 5), then a per-domain add-on. New site or entity: add an instance; when governance itself becomes the job, that is the Scale conversation. More connected systems: core integrations (SSO, OneDrive/SharePoint, Jira) come with every plan, and the full Integration Hub, developer API, and MCP access come with Growth and above. A bigger quality team: add licensed users, while company-wide read and train access stays unlimited and free at every plan. Adding surface never re-opens the whole contract, and you never re-buy the platform to grow.
What does implementation involve?
Guided implementation with document migration included, scoped by plan. Deployments typically go live in under 60 days; 30 Technology went from first contact to live in under two months.
Is the system validated for GxP environments?
A validation pack ships with every major release, so you can adopt new releases without a parallel validation project.
Which standards does Qualio cover?
ISO 13485, FDA QMSR, EU MDR, MDSAP, GxP, 21 CFR Part 11, ISO 42001, and more.
