FAQ
Can I use a paid plan month to month?
All paid plans require a 12-month annual contract. Choose either annual upfront payment or monthly installments.
What is the difference between annual upfront payment and monthly installments?
With annual upfront payment, you pay for the full year at once. Monthly installments split the same 12-month commitment into 12 payments. Monthly installments cost 15% more in total than annual upfront payment. Both payment methods include the same amount of credits.
What are credits?
Credits are the monthly usage allowance included with your plan. As you use features, credits are deducted at each feature's rate.
Can I combine CX, Marketing, and Team credits?
Credits are allocated separately for each service and cannot be transferred. For example, unused CX credits cannot be used for Marketing sends.
Do unused credits roll over to the next month?
CX and Team credits are issued monthly and must be used within that month. Unused credits expire and do not roll over. Marketing credits are issued for the full year at the start of the contract and can be used anytime during the contract term.
Will service stop when I use all my credits?
No. After you use all your credits, you will be billed on demand based on what you use. On-demand rates are higher than annual contract rates. To use lower rates, add more credits with an add-on.
What are add-ons?
Add-ons are annual prepaid packages for purchasing additional credits at a lower rate. They are available by CX, Marketing, or Team service, not for individual features.
I'm already on a paid Channel Talk plan. Will my plan change?
Your current paid plan will remain unchanged and will not be migrated automatically. New features such as Voice ALF, AI Marketing, and Work AI are available only with the AI Edition.
Do the displayed prices include VAT?
A 10% VAT is not included.
