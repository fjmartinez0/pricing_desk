Questions about pricing
Contact us
Is the Free plan really free?
Yes, and you don't need a credit card. Free includes up to 100 artworks, 20 room downloads a month, one collection and a portfolio website with 3 subpages.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel Plus or Pro from your account whenever you like. If you only need a break, you can pause your subscription instead.
Is there a money-back guarantee?
Yes. Contact our support team within 14 days of your purchase to ask for a refund. Images downloaded with a refunded plan lose their license.
Can I change plans later?
Yes. Switch between Plus and Pro, or between monthly and yearly billing, from your account. You see the new price before you confirm.
Which currency will I pay in?
Prices are shown in euros or US dollars, depending on where you are. VAT or sales tax may be added at checkout, depending on your country.
Is the domain included in Pro?
Yes. Your own .art, .com or .eu domain is included in the Pro price, with no extra fees.
