Frequently Asked Questions
Can I use Flux for free?
After an individual plan trial of premium features, you will continue to have access to your Flux account, which is all you need to interact with public projects and the Flux community. To maintain the ability to edit private projects, export them, use AI, and more, a paid plan is required.
How long is my individual plan trial?
New individual plan trials are 7 days. Checkout shows your trial length before you start. Existing trials keep the length they started with.
Why is there a hold on my card when I start a Pro trial?
When you start a Pro trial, Flux places an authorization hold on your card for the full subscription amount of the plan and billing period you select. The hold is not a charge or an extra fee. When the trial ends and your paid plan starts, Flux captures up to the first invoice amount from the hold and releases any unused amount.
What happens if my card verification fails?
If Flux cannot place the hold, Flux pauses AI features and asks you to update your payment method to continue your trial. Open Account menu → Settings → Payments, then select Payment Method → Change Payment Method. When you add a new card, Flux tries the card verification again.
Does Teams have a free trial?
New Teams purchases start as paid subscriptions and do not include a trial. Existing Teams subscriptions and trials keep their current terms.
What are ACUs?
Agent Compute Units (ACUs) are how we meter AI usage in Flux. Agent mode uses ACUs. Chat mode does not use ACUs. Every plan includes AI usage that replenishes each month. How far it goes depends on the complexity of your tasks. You can check your current usage and pay-as-you-go terms in your profile settings.
What are editors?
An editor is anyone who can make edits to a project based on its sharing permissions. Teams is priced per editor, so everyone you add to your organization is billed at your plan's per editor price.
Can you send me an invoice?
Invoicing is available on the Enterprise plan.
How do I cancel my plan?
You can easily cancel your plan at any time. From your Flux profile, click on the account menu in the upper right and visit Plans & Payments.
Do you charge applicable consumption taxes?
Flux is required to charge sales tax or consumption tax in certain countries and US states. Applicable sales tax, VAT, GST, or similar consumption tax will be applied at checkout after the billing information is provided and before checkout is complete.
How can I contact the sales team?
You can contact our sales team to discuss your specific needs.
