What is a Unit?
A “Unit” is a block of browser time of up to 30 seconds per browser connection. Longer-running automations use an extra Unit for every 30 seconds. Session-reconnects count as new browser connections and incur a fresh Unit charge.
6 Unitsper MB for Residential Proxy
