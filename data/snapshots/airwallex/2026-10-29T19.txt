Frequently asked questions
Does the monthly fee apply to each of my accounts?
No, Airwallex will only charge the monthly fee per each business legal entity that has signed up with Airwallex. You may have multiple accounts under one business legal entity, but you will only be charged per each business legal entity.
Are there additional fees besides the monthly plan fee?
In addition to the monthly fee, existing transaction fees for FX, Transfers, and Payment processing will still apply. Details on these fees are provided in the table above.
I'm a current Airwallex customer, will I be able to continue on my current pricing?
For current customers that signed up to Airwallex before June 1st 2025, your current transaction pricing will not change and you will be placed in the Explore tier that has no monthly fee on July 1st 2025. If you wish to try our more advanced capabilities in the Grow or Accelerate tiers you'll be able to upgrade your plan.
