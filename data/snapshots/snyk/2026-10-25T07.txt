Frequently asked questions
What should I do if I’m interested in accessing Snyk’s Evo capabilities?
Currently, Snyk Evo capabilities are not available via the Free or Team plan. Access to these capabilities, such as AI Pentesting or Coding Agent security, requires an Enterprise Platform Subscription - book a demo here to contact sales
How does Snyk count developers?
Snyk defines contributing developers as developers who have made a commit to a private repo monitored by Snyk in the last 90 days. We do not count contributions to public (open source) repos. Contributor counts are displayed on Snyk’s Usage page.
How does Snyk count tests?
Snyk keeps separate test counts for each Snyk product (Snyk Open Source, Snyk Code, Snyk Container, and Snyk IaC) and each Pricing plan. Want to see what counts as a test? Click here for more details.
What are the test limits under the ‘Free’ plan?
If you sign up with our ‘Free’ plan, the limits are: Open Source, 200 tests; Code, 100 tests; IaC, 300 tests; Container, 100 tests. Want to know more about the usage settings? Click here for more details.
Does Snyk store any credit card information?
No. All credit card activity and information is handled by our third-party provider, Stripe (see Stripe’s Terms and Services).

Frequently asked questions
What happens when my credits run out?
Exhausting your prepaid credits will not, in itself, result in you losing access. If you use up your contracted credits before your term ends:
Usage is still captured – Any consumption beyond your initial credit pool is tracked as on-demand consumption and will be invoiced in arrears.
Top up mid-contract – Just reach out to your Snyk account team and we can add credits to your existing contract.
Do my credits ever expire?
Credits must be used within the term of your order, after which any unused credits will expire and cannot be refunded or transferred.
How do I track my credit usage?
Real-time credit usage is visible in your Snyk admin dashboard, with per-capability breakdown and threshold alerts. See here for more information around our billing and usage settings.
Can I use credits across different capabilities?
Yes. Credits apply across the full Snyk platform. Admins can switch capabilities on and off yourself to shift usage toward whichever capabilities matter most - including immediate, no-friction access to new GA products when made available by Snyk.
