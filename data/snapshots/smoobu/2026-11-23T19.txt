Have questions?
Can I test Smoobu vacation rental software?
Yes. Once you've signed up, you'll be able to test Smoobu Professional thoroughly for free for 14 days: no credit card details and no obligations. You can try Smoobu with all our features included - with no restrictions. You will not be charged if you decide not to subscribe after the free trial.
Do I pay more for the different features?
At Smoobu, all core features are included. Write-access accounts: €15 per account per month on every billing cycle, no duration discount; the first 2 are free on Teams Pro+.
Dynamic Pricing is an optional add-on: €12.99 per property per month for your first three properties, €10.99 for the 4th to 8th, €8.99 for the 9th to 13th and €7.99 from the 14th. Try it free for 30 days on Professional Prepaid, or for 2 months on Teams Pro+.
Smoobu Smart Devices, powered by Seam, is another additional feature. Each connected lock costs €4 per month. This charge is automatically added to your existing Smoobu subscription after your free trial concludes. While most lock providers don't charge additional monthly fees, some, such as Nuki, require a Smart Hosting Set, which costs €69 per device per month.
Your 14-day free trial begins as soon as you connect your first smart lock. Enabling the Smart Devices Portal in your settings without connecting a device will not start the trial.
Are there any setup fees or commissions for my bookings on Smoobu?
No. With Smoobu, there are no setup or onboarding fees. Our Professional Flex plan offers all the benefits of Professional Prepaid with a 0.9% commission fee per booking. All our other plans are commission-free.
Does Smoobu offer additional discounts?
Enjoy a 10% discount when you sign up for an annual plan, or save 20% with a two-year plan. If you own or manage more than 20 properties, please get in touch with us for a personalized quote.
Do I need to download any software to use Smoobu?
No. You don't need to download any software. Smoobu is entirely cloud-based and available anytime, anywhere, and on all your internet-connected devices. You can also manage your bookings on the go using our Smoobu iOS and Android apps.
Can I connect my own domain to the website builder?
Yes, you can with all our Smoobu plans.
I currently still use a different PMS. Can I switch to Smoobu without problems?
Yes, a switch to Smoobu is effortless. Here’s everything you need to know.
Can I connect my Airbnb, Booking.com or Expedia listings if I use Smoobu?
Yes, with all our Smoobu plans, you can automatically connect and sync your various availability calendars from Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia, and over 100 booking portals.
Can I switch my Smoobu plan?
You can modify your subscription at any time, with changes taking effect at the next renewal.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
For your Smoobu plans, you can cancel your subscription with 30 days' notice. Cancellations will always take effect at the next renewal.
