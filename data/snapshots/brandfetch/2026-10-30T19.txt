How does pricing work?
Usage is metered in brand fetches, one per call. $129/month (or $1188/yearly) includes 2,500 brand fetches per month shared across Brand API, Brand Context and Transaction API. Fetches reset monthly and do not roll over. Overage is $0.10 per fetch. Logo and Brand Search APIs have separate limits. Requests for brandfetch.com are free and do not count toward your quota. See pricing.
What happens when I exceed my quota?
Brand fetches over your monthly limit are billed at $0.10 each, up to an overage cap you set. You'll get usage alerts at 80% and 100%. Logo API and Brand Search API are soft-capped: going over triggers a notification, not a hard stop.
Can I use your data commercially?
Yes, for internal use cases like displaying a logo in your app. Sublicensing the data to your customers or including it in your own API responses requires a specific agreement. Contact sales if that's your use case.
Can I try Brandfetch before I buy?
Yes. The Free plan gives you 100 brand fetches, plus the Logo API up to 1M requests/mo and the Brand Search API up to 500K requests/mo, free. No credit card required. Enough to test most integrations end-to-end. You can also preview the data on our playground.
How many brands do you cover?
Over 50 million brands and growing, continuously refreshed from the open web. Our APIs also resolve in real time: any brand with a valid domain returns data, across every industry and region.
Does Brandfetch obtain each brand's permission?
No, but brands can claim and manage their profile directly on brandfetch.com, making us the only platform with first-party brand data. Beyond that, our data is aggregated from publicly available sources on the open web.
When should I use Logo API vs Brand API?
Use Logo API when you only need a logo, for example to embed a brand mark in your UI. It's optimized for that, served via a fast CDN, and runs on its own quota separate from credits.
Use Brand API when you need the full brand record: logos, colors, fonts, firmographics, and more in a single call. Brand API responses can be cached for 30 days to reduce credit usage. Logo API images can be cached locally per our usage guidelines.
What integrations do you offer?
Brandfetch integrates with several platforms so you can use brand data without writing custom code:
MCP for AI agents and assistants
Zapier for automated workflows
n8n for self-hosted automation
Make.com for visual workflow building
How often do you update your dataset?
Brand data stays fresh through several mechanisms:
API requests trigger a background recrawl if the brand hasn't been refreshed in 3 months, so the index stays current with usage
Claimed brands are managed directly by their owners, always current
Curated brands are managed by our QA team, who also track rebrands across the web
Crowdsourced updates flow in continuously from users of our plugins and integrations
Need a brand refreshed now? Submit a brand update request.
What logo formats do you support?
Logos are delivered in SVG when available, with PNG conversions for broader compatibility. If SVG isn't available, you get the highest-quality PNG we can find, with a transparent background. See our docs for more details.
How do I request a brand update?
Submit your request at brandfetch.com/update. Our QA team will update the brand with its latest information.
Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?
Yes. Qualified startups and nonprofits get 40% off paid plans for the first 12 months. Reach out to sales to apply.
