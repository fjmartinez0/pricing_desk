Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about Bleemeo's pricing
Are there any hidden costs beyond the per-server price?
No hidden costs, but the server count is not the only line. On Professional, each server includes 1,000 metrics and 20 containers, and the account includes 5 uptime monitors (at least one per server); beyond that, metrics, containers and monitors are billed. Logs are pay-as-you-go at 0,50 €/GiB ingested, and AWS, Azure, SNMP and vSphere resources are billed per object — cloud accounts, S3 and Blob storage, Trusted Advisor and vSphere clusters are free. Users, dashboards, alerts and API access are never billed. Every rate is listed in the comparison table above.
How does Bleemeo compare to Datadog or New Relic?
Datadog and New Relic charge $18+/host/month before add-ons for logs, APM, or custom metrics. Bleemeo starts at 10,99 €/host/month and includes 1,000 metrics per server, dashboards and alerting — no add-on products to buy; logs are simply pay-as-you-go at 0,50 €/GiB. You get comparable visibility at a fraction of the cost, plus an agent that auto-discovers services without manual configuration. See our detailed Datadog alternative comparison.
Do I need a dedicated person to manage Bleemeo?
No. Bleemeo is designed to be low-maintenance. The agent installs in minutes, auto-discovers services, and applies sensible default thresholds. There is no infrastructure to run, no database to tune, and no dashboards to build from scratch. Most teams manage Bleemeo as part of their normal workflow — no dedicated hire required.
Can I migrate from Prometheus, Grafana, or Nagios?
Yes. Bleemeo's agent natively scrapes Prometheus exporters, so your existing instrumentation works without changes. Grafana users can point dashboards at Bleemeo's PromQL-compatible API. For Nagios or Zabbix users, Bleemeo's auto-discovery replaces most manual check definitions — typically resulting in faster setup and less maintenance.
Is Bleemeo reliable enough for production workloads?
Absolutely. Bleemeo has been running in production since 2015 and monitors critical infrastructure for companies across Europe and beyond. The platform is built on redundant, highly available infrastructure in the Paris region. You can check our track record on our status page.
Is there any vendor lock-in?
No. Bleemeo collects metrics through open standards — Prometheus exporters, OpenTelemetry, and standard protocols. Our agent, Glouton, is open source. Your data is always accessible via our API, and you can export it at any time. If you ever decide to leave, your monitoring configuration stays with you.
Where is my data stored? Is Bleemeo GDPR compliant?
Metrics and logs are stored in the EU, in the Paris region (France). Bleemeo is GDPR compliant. We never sell your data; the few processors outside the EU (transactional email, mobile push, federated sign-in) operate under Standard Contractual Clauses, and we provide data processing agreements (DPA) on request. See our security page for details.
What is a server?
A server is any machine running our monitoring agent — physical servers, virtual machines, or cloud instances. Each unique host counts as one server. Kubernetes nodes are also counted as servers.
How does billing work?
Billing is calculated hourly and invoiced monthly. You only pay for the servers actually monitored during each hour — if a server is removed mid-month, you stop paying for it immediately. Prices shown are per month for simplicity, but the actual charge is based on hourly usage. We accept credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), SEPA direct debit, and bank transfers. All prices are shown excluding VAT — applicable taxes are added at checkout based on your location.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), SEPA direct debit for European customers, and bank transfers. Enterprise customers can arrange invoice billing.
