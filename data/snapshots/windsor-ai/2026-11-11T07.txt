FAQs
What is a data source?
A data source is the original location (platform, website, app) where data is generated or stored. In Windsor.ai, you select a data source from which to pull data into any destination for further analysis. Windsor.ai supports 350+ data sources, including Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Facebook Ads, Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, etc.
What is an account?
An account is one account ID inside a data source, such as one Google Ads account, one GA4 property, one Facebook page or one Shopify store. Your plan’s account limit is a total across all your data sources. For example, the Basic plan’s 75 accounts could be 25 Google Ads accounts, 25 Facebook Ads accounts and 25 GA4 properties.
See how Windsor.ai counts data sources and accounts for more examples, including agencies, Google Sheets tabs and Business Profile locations.
Do Claude, ChatGPT or Data Studio count as data sources?
No. They are destinations, and every destination is included on every plan. Only the platforms you pull data from count toward your data source limit.
Are Facebook Ads and Facebook Pages one data source or two?
Two. Ads and organic data come from different connectors, so Facebook Ads, Facebook Pages and Instagram each count as one data source. Instagram ads are included in the Facebook Ads connector.
Does a Google Ads manager account (MCC) count as one account?
No. Google Ads is one data source, and each client account you select under the MCC counts as one account. Twenty client accounts under one MCC count as 20 accounts.
What data destinations are supported in Windsor.ai?
We support Power BI, Data Studio, Google Sheets, Excel, BigQuery, Snowflake, Tableau, PostgreSQL, MySQL, Python, Amazon S3, Azure Blob Storage, Azure SQL, Redshift, Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, and Windsor MCP server (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, and other AI chats).
What is a scheduled sync, and how are they counted?
A scheduled sync (destination task) allows you to sync data from Windsor.ai to your preferred database, data warehouse, file storage, or Google Sheets. We currently support destination tasks for Google Sheets, BigQuery, Snowflake, Amazon S3, Amazon Redshift, Azure SQL, Azure Blob Storage, PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Databricks.
On the Basic plan, you can set up a maximum of 5 active scheduled syncs, where each sync corresponds to one table updated once per day. All plans starting from Standard provide unlimited destination tasks and hourly/daily scheduled syncs.
Additionally, on the Basic plan and above, you can perform backfills to load historical data into your table.
Note that on the Basic plan, each backfill also counts as a scheduled sync.
What does unlimited users mean, and how to add new users to my Windsor.ai account?
You can invite unlimited team members to your account for seamless collaboration. Simply click on your User Name → Invite Team Member in the onboard app and start adding colleagues to work together efficiently.
How often is my data updated in the preview table?
Windsor.ai refreshes the cache every 6 hours. To access real-time data, you need to manually refresh it in the onboard app using the Clear Cache button.
What is the external authentication feature?
You can send an authorization link to external users (e.g., teammates or clients) so they can connect a data source under your account, without sharing credentials or registering at Windsor.

Does Windsor.ai have any limitations?
API requests are subject to rate limits to ensure fair usage. The current rate limits are:
600 requests per minute
10,000 requests per day
If you exceed these limits, you’ll receive a 429 status code. The response will include headers indicating your remaining quota and when it will reset.
Can I request or build a custom connector with Windsor.ai?
Yes, we can add this upon request. To get started, add it to our feature requests board, and we’ll plan and deploy it as soon as possible.
For immediate custom connector development without a queue, consider our Enterprise plan.
Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time, and we’ll automatically apply prorated billing so you only pay for what you actually use.
If you upgrade mid-cycle:
You’ll be charged only for the remaining time on the higher plan, minus a credit for the unused time on your current plan.
Example:
You’re on the $23/month plan.
On June 16 (midway through your billing cycle), you upgrade to the $118/month plan.
Here’s what happens:
You get a $11.50 credit for the unused half of the $23 plan.
You’re charged $59.00 for the remaining half of the $118 plan.
Total due immediately: $47.50
On your next billing date (July 1), you’ll be billed the full $118 for the new plan.
If you downgrade mid-cycle:
The downgrade takes effect immediately, and the lower plan’s limits apply from that moment. Nothing is charged when you downgrade. The unused time on your current plan, minus the cost of the new plan for the rest of the cycle, goes to your account balance as a credit. The credit pays your next invoices automatically until it is used up.
Example:
You pay $118 for a month of Standard on October 1. On October 16, halfway through the cycle, you downgrade to Basic ($23/month).
You get a $59.00 credit for the unused half of Standard.
The remaining half of Basic costs $11.50.
$47.50 goes to your account balance.
On November 1, the $23 Basic invoice is paid from that balance, and $24.50 is left for the next one.
To keep your current plan’s limits until the end of the cycle, downgrade shortly before your renewal date.
If you switch between monthly and yearly billing:
The switch also takes effect immediately, and your new billing cycle starts that day. The unused time on your current plan becomes a credit, and the credit is subtracted from the first invoice on the new plan. If the credit is larger than that invoice, which is common when you move from a yearly plan to a monthly one, the rest stays on your account balance and pays future invoices.
Are there any setup or onboarding fees?
There are no setup or hidden onboarding fees with any of our plans. Subscribe and start using Windsor.ai right away.
I stopped using Windsor.ai some time ago and just paid again — why do I still have an outstanding balance?
Windsor.ai subscriptions are billed in advance and renew automatically unless cancelled.
If you’re on a monthly plan:
You’re billed for the upcoming 30-day period. If the subscription wasn’t cancelled, invoices may have continued to generate each month, and unpaid invoices create an outstanding balance.
If you’re on an annual plan:
You’re billed for the upcoming 12-month period. If the subscription wasn’t cancelled, an annual renewal invoice may have been created, and if it wasn’t paid, it can appear as an outstanding balance.
Can I pause my subscription for some time?
At this time, we do not offer a “pause” option. You can cancel your subscription anytime and reactivate it later when needed:
– For monthly plans, your account stays active until the end of the current billing period, and billing will resume only if you reactivate.
– For annual plans, the full year is paid upfront. Cancelling stops the auto-renewal at the end of the term but doesn’t pause or refund the remaining months of the current year.
Is my data safe when I use Windsor.ai?
We take data security very seriously. Windsor.ai is GDPR compliant and SOC 2 Type II — independently audited. Our servers are hosted in a world-class data center in Germany.
For more information, check our Privacy Statement.
