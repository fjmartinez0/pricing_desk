FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Is the Hobby plan really free?+
Yes. The Hobby plan is hard-capped, needs no credit card, and never produces a surprise bill. You get 100K tool calls a month, unlimited connections, custom tools and MCP, and 3 team members. When you reach a cap, usage pauses until the next month or until you upgrade.
What is the difference between my own app and a Composio-managed app?+
With your own app you connect your own OAuth application or API keys and get the full 100K free tool calls a month. Composio-managed apps are a shared OAuth application we run so you can skip setup. They include 20K free tool calls a month, then bill at an extra $0.0002 per tool call on top of the base rate.
How does the $29 Pro plan work?+
Pro is $29 a month and includes usage credit that resets every month with no rollover. Past the included free amounts, overage bills on the rate card. You can set spend caps so you never get a surprise bill.
What happens when I hit a limit?+
On Hobby, usage pauses at the cap with no charge until the next month or until you upgrade. On Pro, you keep running and pay overage, and spend caps let you set a ceiling.
How are Instant Tools billed?+
Use Instant Tools without separate API keys or subscriptions. Provider charges come from your Composio balance with 0% markup. Regular tool-call charges apply. Rates may change. With your own provider credentials, no Instant usage charge applies. Your provider bills your account, and regular Composio tool-call charges still apply. Read the Instant Tools guide.
What are the optional add-ons?+
Add-ons can be usage-based charges or one-time charges. Some usage-based add-ons apply automatically based on how a call runs, such as direct execution outside a Session, proxy execution, or running in the sandbox. Others you enable for security or compliance, such as zero data retention and IP allowlisting. Advanced white-labeling is a one-time per-connection charge. BAA is a $0.0003-per-tool-call compliance add-on on Pro, and Enterprise includes it.
How is Firewall billed compared with Sandbox LLM tokens?+
On Pro, Firewall costs $0.50 per 1M tokens evaluated. Firewall is included on Enterprise. Sandbox LLM tokens cover model calls Composio runs for you in Sandbox.
How do add-ons add up?+
Most calls are just the base tool-call rate. Usage-based add-ons are small extras that apply only when a call actually uses that capability, so you never pay for one you are not using. BAA is a $0.0003-per-tool-call compliance add-on on Pro, and Enterprise includes it. For example, a call that runs in the sandbox adds a small sandbox amount, and a proxy-execute call is still one tool call at the base rate with a small proxy add-on on top. On Pro you can set spend caps for metered usage, so metered usage never goes past a ceiling you choose. One-time add-ons are separate.
What is proxy execute?+
Proxy execute is an escape hatch for calling an API endpoint we have not wrapped as a tool, or for advanced request options a standard tool cannot express. Composio still handles auth, so you never touch raw credentials. It is billed as one tool call at the base rate plus a small proxy add-on for the pass-through, not as two separate charges. Learn more.
Can I avoid a surprise bill?+
Yes. The Hobby plan is hard-capped, and on Pro you can set per-meter spend caps so spend never goes past a ceiling you choose.
Still have questions? Reach out to support@composio.dev.
New pricing applies to signups on or after August 15, 2026. Existing customers stay on their current plan through December 31, 2026. Instant tool calls are billed for all customers, including those who signed up before August 15, from September 10, 2026.
Signed up before August 15, 2026? Your earlier pricing is grandfathered through December 31, 2026. Email support@composio.dev for your plan's details.
