FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Which plan is right for me?+
Start on Free if you work alone or in a team of up to 3 members. Choose Standard when you have more than 3 members, want to share more than 5 connections, need more than 3 custom MCP servers, or need SSO or Firewall. Every Standard seat also comes with $18 in credits each month. Choose Enterprise when you need SCIM, SIEM streaming, deployment in your own cloud, or dedicated support. If you build agents with the Composio SDK, see Platform pricing.
How do seats work?+
Standard costs $18 per seat per month, and each member of your workspace is a seat. Every seat adds 10,000 tool calls and $18 in credits to one pool that your whole workspace shares. A member who uses more draws more from the pool. The pool resets at the start of each billing month, and nothing unused rolls over.
How do credits work?+
Credits pay for anything billed by usage, such as Instant tools and extra tool calls. Every Standard seat adds $18 in credits to your workspace each month.

What are connections and skills?+
A connection is an app account you link, such as your Gmail. Sharing a connection lets other members use it without linking their own account. A skill is a set of reusable instructions that your team writes once and shares. Free lets you share up to 5 connections and 25 skills, and Standard lets you share all of them. Every plan lets you link up to 10 accounts per app.
What is Firewall?+
Firewall controls what your AI can do in your apps. It checks each tool call against your policies and blocks or flags calls that break them. It also finds personal data and secrets in tool results and can scrub or block them. You can write policies in plain English, such as “Never share customer data outside the company,” or set policies on the apps, resources, and fields a call can touch. Only plain-English policies use tokens, which measure the text Firewall reads in each call and result. Firewall is available on Standard and Enterprise.
What does Enterprise add?+
Enterprise can run in your own cloud, and Composio engineers build connectors for your internal apps and help your team roll out. SCIM keeps members in sync with your identity provider, and SIEM streaming sends audit and tool call logs to tools such as Datadog or Splunk. IT admins get free seats, so you pay only for members who use Composio.
