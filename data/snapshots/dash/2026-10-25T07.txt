Pricing FAQs
How much does Dash cost?
Dash starts at $109 a month (£79) for the Start Up plan, with Small Team at $239/month, Growing Brand at $389/month and Established Brand at $589/month. All plans include unlimited users and every feature, including AI features. The difference between tiers is how much storage and how mnay monthly downloads you need. Paying annually saves 10%, and there's also a 10% non-profit discount available.
Check our pricing table for more information.
Are any features locked to higher plans?
All features are available on every plan, from AI-powered search and auto-tagging to branded portals, Shopify integration and bulk resizing. You pick a plan based on how much storage and how many downloads you need, not which features you want.
What counts as a download in Dash?
Every time you get an asset out of Dash, we class that as a download. That's hitting the download button straight from Dash, but also downloading images from Dash using integrations (like Canva or Hootsuite) and using embedded links. However, we don't count individual views of assets through embedded links as a download. There's more informatino about that here, and if you have any questions on this, just get in touch!
‍
Why is Dash cheaper than most DAMs?
Dash's affordable DAM pricing doesn't mean it's less powerful than other digital asset management tools.
We built Dash for growing brands, not massive corporations. DAMs for those businesses are designed around complex IT requirements, lengthy implementation and dedicated DAM administrators. Those costs get passed on to the user.
‍
Dash focuses on what marketing and ecommerce teams actually need: a high-quality, fast tool to store, find, share and deploy theirvisual content. It's so quick setting up that there's no implementation fee, simple enough that you don't need a full-time admin, and priced on storage and downloads rather than seats
What happens if I go over my Dash storage allowance?
We won't stop you from uploading content if you're about to go over the storage allowance of your plan. Your subscription will increase automatically to allow for this, instead. You can always reduce it back down by deleting content you don't need, or asking us for help!
How do I pay for Dash?
You can sign up for Dash within the app using your credit card details. We only provide the option to pay via bank transfer for our annual Established Brand plan. If you pick this, we'll provide a proposal and send an invoice annually to your billing contact.
Does Dash offer onboarding?
No matter which pricing plan you pick, you'll get access to resources to help you get started - such as our Help Center and friendly live product support team. Once you're set up on Dash digital asset management, our teams will continue to be on-hand to provide quick, speedy support.
What's more, if you're on our Small Team, Growing and Established plans you'll get tailored onboarding support including a welcome call with our specialists to help you get set up.
How easy is it to move all my visual content over to Dash?
If you're moving from Googel Drive, Dropbox or SharePoint, just use our integration to easily move your files across. You can even keep your existing file structure!
If you're coming from another DAM provider, and they're trying to charge you extra for an export, just let us know. We'll do our best to move all your files over, and we'll do it for free! Get in touch if you'd like more information.
Does Dash offer a non-profit discount?
Yes, we sure do! Charities, not-for-profits and educational institutions can benefit from an extra 10% off. Just get in touch during your trial if this is something you'd like to apply for.
Does Dash charge per user?
The good news - no! Every Dash plan includes unlimited users, so you can add your whole team, freelancers and agencies without the bill creeping up. You can add as many admins, guests, and contributor users as you like. Pricing is instead based on the storage and downloads you need.
Can I get Dash for free?
Dash offers a free 14-day trial with full access to every feature, and with no need for a credit card. We don't offer free plans, but the trial gives you enough time to upload yourassets and see how Dash can work for your team.
Does Dash offer enterprise pricing?
Yes. Dash's Established brand plan is built for brands with high creative output, starting at $589 (£449) a month with unlimited users, unlimited downloads and as much storage as you want. Once you go over 1TB, extra storage is charged at a 50% discounted rate.
Chat with our sales team if you have any questions!
How does Dash pricing compare to Bynder, Brandfolder or Canto?
Enterprise DAMs like Bynder, Brandfolder and Canto are built for organisations with big budgets. They're typically priced on custom quotes, often with implementation fees and longer contracts. Dash publishes its pricing openly, includes unlimited users and every feature, runs on a 30-day contract rather than long commitments, and most brands are set up in days rather than months.
How does Dash pricing compare to Google Drive and Dropbox?
Google Drive and Dropbox are cheaper than Dash per gigabyte. But they're built for storing files and collaborating in documents, not managing a visual library. Dash adds AI search, auto-tagging, custom fields like SKU and campaign, branded portals for retail partners, built-in cropping and resizing, and integrations with Shopify, Canva and Hootsuite. Most brands move to Dash when the hours lost hunting for the right image start costing them more in lost revenue and time.
What happens if I go over my Dash monthly download allowance?
We won't prevent you from going over your agreed monthly downloads. If this happens, we'll be in touch to see if you want to change your allowance. If your usage exceeds your allowance for another consecutive month, we'll automatically increase it. Just get in touch with us at any time to reduce it again.
Will I need training?
Dash has been designed to be intuitive, so intensive training is rarely needed. You can take a quick course in the Dash Academy to learn at your own pace, or take advantage of our free onboarding calls to get you set up in no time.
How long are Dash contracts?
We won't tie you into lengthy contracts. If you decide Dash isn't the right fit for you, then you'd only need to give us 30 days notice from your next billing date.
How secure is Dash?
Dash is ISO 27001 certified and hosts all assets on Amazon Web Services in the EU (Ireland), with every file automatically backed up to a separate EU region (Stockholm) as soon as it's uploaded. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, the platform is regularly penetration tested, and access to infrastructure is restricted to named, authorised accounts. Dash has never had a security incident affect a customer account, and commits to notifying customers within 24 hours of any breach under GDPR. You own your data at all times and can export it whenever you need.
More detail can be found in Dash's full security overview.
Does Dash offer pricing in USD, GBP, EUR, AUD and CAD?
Yes. Dash pricing is available in GBP, USD, EUR, AUD and CAD. Plans start at £79, $109, €99, $149 AUD and $139 CAD per month. Just use the toggle above the pricing table to see every plan in your currency.
