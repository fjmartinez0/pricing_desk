FAQ
01. How is Accelevents pricing structured?
Professional costs $7,500 per event and covers one event. Business starts at $13,500 per year and includes unlimited events, so it costs less than Professional once you run two or more events in a year. Enterprise and White Label are custom priced, include unlimited events, and add multi-department controls and full brand control. All plans include unlimited admin seats and 24/7 live support with an average response time of 21 seconds.
02. Which plan should I choose for one event versus many?
Pick Professional for a single event at $7,500 per event. If you'll run two or more events in a year, Business at $13,500 per year with unlimited events costs less, and adds cross-event reporting and an event listing page. Move to Enterprise for department-wide operations, or White Label when you need brand-forward experiences. Each option includes unlimited admin seats, so collaboration is not gated.
03. What makes the Business plan a good fit for growing programs?
Business is designed for organizations running multiple events. You get unlimited events, unlimited admin seats, an event listing page, and cross-event reporting for portfolio-level performance. Custom reporting tracks what matters, while integrations, flexible registration, and kiosk or staff-managed check-in support consistent execution across your calendar.
04. What does Enterprise add for larger organizations?
Enterprise layers in capabilities for complex, multi-department programs: approval workflows, call for speakers, API and webhooks, and unlimited organizer profiles, all on top of unlimited events and seats. Custom pricing aligns to your structure, governance, and data needs while keeping planning and execution in one platform.
05. What is included in the White Label option?
White Label removes Accelevents branding, supports a custom URL and email domain, and provides a branded mobile app with a store listing. You still benefit from unlimited events and admin seats, with optional Single Sign-On. It is ideal when your brand must lead every attendee touchpoint.
06. Which in-person features help with onsite success?
Onsite tools include customizable kiosk check-in, a badge designer with on-demand printing, and lead capture for exhibitors. These reduce lines, keep data accurate, and help sponsors quantify value. A mobile event app keeps attendees informed, and 24/7 support with an average response time of 21 seconds helps resolve peak-moment issues quickly.
07. How are virtual events handled in pricing?
Virtual functionality is available as a paid add-on. Use a native broadcast studio, capture recordings, offer unlimited on-demand viewing, and apply automated transcription. Sponsor placements and a Command Center support programming at scale. Add this to any plan to serve remote audiences without separate tools.
08. What analytics and reporting are available across plans?
Export all data, use UTM and tracking links, and build cross-event and custom reports to see ROI. Portfolio views surface trends, while per-event dashboards highlight registrations and engagement. This visibility helps you refine pricing tiers, marketing tactics, and content, and it simplifies stakeholder readouts.
09. Which integrations and developer options are supported?
Native integrations include HubSpot, Salesforce, Marketo, and more. Extend workflows with Zapier, API access, webhooks, and custom JavaScript, keeping registrations, attendance, and engagement signals flowing into your CRM and marketing stack without manual exports.
10. How do I get exact pricing and next steps?
Book a discussion to confirm fit and map your requirements. We will review your event portfolio, walk through features, recommend a plan, and outline next steps, including a tailored quote if needed. Enterprise and White Label options are customized and we will cover details on the call. All plans include 24/7 live support with an average response time of 21 seconds.
