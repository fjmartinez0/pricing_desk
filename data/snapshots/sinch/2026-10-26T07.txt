Voice API Calling
The pricing model is structured on a transparent, pay-as-you-go basis, with total costs calculated as the sum of all utilized call legs. A "call leg" is defined as a single connection, and a full call may be comprised of one or more legs. This structure provides the flexibility to combine various voice channels—including traditional phone lines (Public Switched Telephone Network (PSTN)), SIP interfaces, in-app calling, voice streams and voice relay. In addition to standard per-minute costs, a suite of features are available for enhancement and may be added to the Service as needed.

How does pricing work?
Here is an example demonstrating how the pricing is calculated for a specific call scenario. Please note that the per-minute rates used below are for illustrative purposes.
Example: SIP Inbound to PSTN Outbound with Call Recording
In this scenario, a call comes in via a SIP connection to the Sinch platform and is then connected to a number on the Public Switched Telephone Network (PSTN), and the entire call is recorded.
SIP Inbound (receive call): $0.0025/minute
PSTN Outbound (make call): $0.01/minute
Call Recording: $0.002/minute
Total $0.0145/minute
For a 10-minute call, the total cost would be 10 minutes * $0.0145/minute = $0.145.
What's the price for number masking?
Number masking is not a priced feature in itself, but is priced on the included call legs and used Sinch number(s). During a PSTN-to-PSTN call, both parties see a Sinch virtual number as the caller ID, effectively masking their actual phone numbers.
The example also includes the monthly cost of the phone number used for the inbound call.
Phone number: $0.80 monthly and $0.80 setup fee
PSTN Inbound (receive call): $0.008/minute
PSTN Outbound (make call): $0.01/minute
Total: $0.018/minute, $0.80 monthly and setup fee
For a 10-minute call, the usage cost would be 10 minutes * $0.018/minute = $0.18. The total monthly cost would be $0.98 + $0.80 (one-time fee for the number), assuming this is the only call made during the month.
Is SIP interface the same as Elastic SIP Trunking pricing?
No, SIP interface is part of programmable voice and is only covering one call leg that is used to connect SIP infrastructure. It does not include the PSTN charges.
