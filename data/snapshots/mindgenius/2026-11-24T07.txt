Not sure which fits?
Choose Desktop if you work on Windows and want your maps held as files on your own machines and network. They can sit on a shared network folder, so colleagues pick them up and work on them in turn, and nothing leaves your environment.
Choose Online if you need several people in the same map at the same time, or a view across every project at once, or you are on a Mac. See Online pricing.
Can a team share Desktop maps?
Yes, and many do. Maps are files, so they can live on a shared drive and be picked up by whoever needs them next. People work on them in turn rather than at the same time, and each person needs a licence.
Can I buy a perpetual licence?
Not online. Desktop is sold online as a yearly subscription, which keeps you on the latest release for as long as it is active. If you need a perpetual licence, talk to us and we will sort it directly.
Does my price change at renewal?
Renewals are charged at the price current at the time of renewal.
Can we stop the AI entirely?
Yes. MindGenius 20 has no AI in the build at all. Not disabled, not hidden, not there. Read the plain English answer on AI, data and security.
Both builds come with a free 14 day trial. It works offline and no card is needed.

Not sure which fits?
Choose Online if you want every project held together in one place with a view across all of them at once, or you work on a Mac. One person running six projects gets the same overview as a team of thirty, and when there is more than one of you, everyone works in the same project at the same time. Everything is held on Microsoft Azure, with the availability and data protection enterprise, government and education require.
Choose Desktop if you want your maps as files on your own machines, or need to work with no connection. See Desktop pricing.
Is Online only for teams?
No. Plenty of people run their own projects on Online, because it keeps every project in one place and shows what needs attention across all of them. Everything on the plan works with a single seat.
Can we turn the AI off?
Yes, for everyone. Your super administrator sets it for the organisation and individual users cannot override it. Read the plain English answer on AI, data and security.
What happens when the trial ends?
If you do not subscribe, the account becomes a free single project plan rather than switching off. You keep one project with full Mindmap, Taskboard and Timeline functionality, for as long as you want it.
Already a customer and need to update payment details?
Billing lives inside your account. Sign in, then open Account and Billing to change your card, view invoices or manage your subscription.
