Frequently asked questions
How are TOPdesk's prices calculated?
TOPdesk’s prices are based on the number of agents. The more agents you add to TOPdesk, the lower your average price. Use the filters at the top of this page to estimate your subscription costs or reach out to us for an exact quote for your business.
What is an agent?
An agent is someone who handles tickets within TOPdesk. If they can open and adjust a ticket, they are considered an agent. Users who simply view dashboards are free.
What does TOPdesk offer for this price that other vendors don't?
In addition to our software, which includes unlimited assets and end users, TOPdesk offers many unique benefits:
No additional costs
In-house, skilled support team
In-house software development
Local implementation consultants and trainers
Data hosted in your area
What does TOPdesk cost if I have 50+ agents?
You can use the filters at the top of this page to calculate your price based on the number of agents. With that many agents, your business is large. We suggest getting in touch with us to discuss your specific needs.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my subscription at any time?
You can upgrade your TOPdesk plan at any time during your contract. Downgrades are possible at the end of your current contract term. Simply get in touch with us to discuss your needs.
Are there any hidden charges in TOPdesk's plans?
We like to be transparent, so there are no additional costs other than the prices mentioned on our website. Please note that our prices do not include taxes and are subject to change. Your subscription includes software, hosting, updates and support. In the unlikely situation that you use more data than is stated in our fair use policy, you will be charged extra.
Keep in mind that additional services such as implementation of the software by one of our consultants do come at a surcharge. Please reach out to us to discuss the best solution for your situation.
Do I need to pay extra for TOPdesk updates or new features?
TOPdesk updates are free. You will receive these updates through our bi-weekly release.
