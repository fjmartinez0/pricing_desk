Q. QuestionWhat does HR software include?
A.Answer
BrightHR’s software gives you the end-to-end tools that remove HR admin burden from your people management. This includes tools like rota management, time tracking, absence management, expense tracking, document storage and payroll reporting. But with BrightHR we also include other features such as an online advertising marketplace and employee perks platform, a learning management system, e-learning and chat tools to access expert HR advice helping you stay compliant.

Q. QuestionDo I have to purchase all features?
A.Answer
With BrightHR you have a choice of software packages, HR, Health & Safety or a combined HR and health & safety software package. You also have the choice of adding HR or health & safety advice and documentation services. Within BrightHR all of the features, such as rota management, timekeeping, clocking in and out, absence management and sick leave, expense tracking and document storage are included in the package. This is offered at a cost effective fixed price based on employee numbers. The features work together with the simplest of design, giving you the necessary tools you need to manage your business.
