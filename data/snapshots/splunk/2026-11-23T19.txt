Observability Cloud Pricing FAQ
Which edition is right for me?
Both editions combine metrics, events, logs, and traces for end-to-end observability. Choose Essentials to operate and troubleshoot core digital services, including applications, cloud and Kubernetes infrastructure, and operational logs, with AI-assisted troubleshooting. Choose Premier to protect and automate complex digital services. It adds Digital Experience Monitoring, Database Monitoring, Secure Application, AI Agent Evaluation, and higher included capacity.
How is pricing calculated?
Pricing is per host, per month. Essentials is $95/host and Premier is $150/host. Editions start at 30 hosts. Premium Success Plans are available for an additional 10%.
How much do I save with an edition?
Editions cost 23% less than buying the same capabilities à la carte with Essentials, and 32% less with Premier. Need just one capability? Individual products start at $15/host, such as Infrastructure Monitoring for infrastructure-only needs.
Is AI SRE included in every edition?
Yes. AI SRE is included in every edition at no extra cost. That includes the AI Assistant / Troubleshooting Agent and the Remediation Agent built with Anthropic. It doesn't just find issues. It helps you fix them.
What's included per host?
Essentials:
Observability Logs: 0.5 GB/day, 15-day retention
Infrastructure: 40 containers or functions, 600 custom metrics
APM: 80 monitoring and 400 troubleshooting MetricSets, 20.48 MB trace/min, 10.24 MB profiling/min
Agent Observability: 5K spans/month
Premier: everything in Essentials, plus:
Observability Logs: 1 GB/day, 15 day retention
Agent Observability: 20K spans/month
AI Agent Evaluation: 8M Luna tokens/month
Digital Experience: 10K uptime, 10K API, and 1K browser tests/month; 10K RUM and 2K Session Replay sessions; 10K DXA sessions; 3 GB Business Journey events
Database Monitoring: 0.1 database host license and 20 custom metrics
Secure Application: 800 MB of security events (vulnerabilities and threats)
APM containers and profiled containers aren't metered separately. Usage is measured by trace and profiling volume.
What if I need more than what's included?
Add capacity or capabilities à la carte. For example, if 100 Essentials hosts include 50 GB/day of logs and you need 100 GB/day, you add 50 GB/day of Observability Logs capacity. You only pay for what's above your included amount.
Can I keep using my Splunk Cloud or Splunk Enterprise logs?
Yes. Observability Logs handles your operational logs inside the observability workflow. Keep Splunk Cloud for logs that also support security, ITSI, compliance, longer retention, or broader analytics.
What's included in the Free Edition?
Monitor up to 15 hosts with full platform features and no time limit. Log monitoring is coming soon.

General Pricing FAQ
How does Splunk pricing work?
Splunk products are priced to provide optimal value for the way you use them and for your deployment. We offer Activity-based, ingest, workload, and entity pricing. Choose the products and pricing models that best fit your needs. You have control over costs and usage, as well as the flexibility to change later.
See Splunk Platform pricing, Splunk Security pricing, and Splunk Observability pricing for details.
How does Splunk prevent surprise overage bills?
Unlike other vendors that penalize you for unexpected data spikes, Splunk offers predictable pricing models. We only charge for overages when you consistently exceed your purchased data ingestion or storage capacity.
This approach offers you the flexibility to extend beyond your subscription limits during critical incidents while maintaining control over your costs. You can continuously monitor usage, and if you require increased capacity, you can easily adjust your allocation via the Splunk Support Portal.
Can I shift my budget between different Splunk products?
Yes. With Cloud Flex, you can reallocate your spend across the entire Splunk portfolio based on what you are actually using, without needing to restart the procurement process. Cloud Flex covers Splunk Platform, Security, Observability, AI, Storage, and Federated Search products, giving you ultimate financial agility.
What kind of ROI can I expect from Splunk?
With numerous deployment options, AI innovation, and our commitment to support for cloud, hybrid, and fully on-premises, air-gapped environments, Splunk helps turn your data into insights and outcomes.
As a flexible platform, Splunk allows you to only purchase the solutions you need and optimize your data ingestion, which can lead to cost savings and increased value while reducing your total cost of ownership (TCO).
Are there limits on Splunk usage or the number of users?
There are no explicit limits regarding the usage or total number of users allowed to use Splunk at one organization.
Splunk usage is generally governed by licensing models that specify capacity thresholds such as data ingestion volumes or user seat counts, depending on the product and deployment type. We give you the flexibility to choose the pricing model and limits that fit your needs.
Contact us if you have questions or need help optimizing data ingestion and usage within license limits.

How can I manage consumption and control my costs in Splunk?
Splunk provides data management strategies and tools to help you balance operational visibility with cost efficiency, including:
Ingest and Edge Processors: Filter, redact, and route data to focus only on what matters, reducing unnecessary data ingestion and storage costs before data is indexed.
Federated Search: Access and analyze data where it resides (like Amazon S3), avoiding costly data movement and duplication.
Real-time Monitoring: Track usage through access tokens and alerts to detect unexpected consumption spikes and take corrective actions instantly.
Do you provide volume discounts?
Yes. Volume discounts are available for each of our different plans and solutions. Contact a Splunk pricing expert for details.
Do the Splunk pricing plans include technical support?
Yes. Splunk product purchases include standard support. Support offerings include all major and minor software updates and technical support. Premium support is also available.
