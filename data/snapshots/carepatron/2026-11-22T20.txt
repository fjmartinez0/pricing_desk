Frequently asked questions
What does “per user” mean?
Plus and Advanced are priced for each user in your practice, so a practice of three pays for three users.
What happens when my trial ends?
You move to the Free plan. Your clients, notes and settings stay where they are. Reminders and other paid features stop until you choose a plan.
Is there a cancellation term or minimum contract?
No, you can cancel your subscription at anytime, and you won't be billed again. We want you to stay with us because you love Carepatron, not because we locked you into a contract.
What fees will I be charged?
If you are on a paid subscription, you will be billed monthly or annually depending on what you choose. We accept a range of online payment methods.
Are there any upfront costs?
There are no upfront costs! You will not be asked for your payment details unless you decide to upgrade your account to a paid version.
