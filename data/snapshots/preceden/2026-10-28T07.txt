Common Questions
What does the free plan include?
You can create an unlimited number of timelines with up to 10 events on each one, customize how they look, export them to PDF, PNG, PowerPoint, CSV, and Excel, and share them with a link. Timelines made with Timeline Maker AI keep every event the generator created, and you can add up to 5 events of your own. Paid plans remove the event limit, add spreadsheet imports and hide Preceden's branding, and the higher-priced plan also adds collaborators, a custom logo and a custom domain.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You can cancel your subscription from your account page at any time, and you will not be charged again. Your subscription stays active for the rest of the period you already paid for.
Will you send an annual renewal reminder?
Yes. We email you a reminder 15 days before your annual renewal, and we email you a receipt after every payment.
Do you offer refunds?
Yes. You can email us within 15 days of any payment, and we will issue you a full refund.
