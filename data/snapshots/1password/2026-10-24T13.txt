Frequently Asked Questions
How does 1Password compare to competitors?
Our plan comparison page makes it easy to compare 1Password with other password managers, including pricing, features, and plan options.
What’s the difference between 1Password Individual and Families?
The Individual plan is designed for one person. The Families plan includes everything in Individual, plus the ability to invite up to five family members, share unlimited vaults, and manage access with simple admin controls.
How much does 1Password cost for individuals and families?
In USD, the Individual plan costs $2.99 per month with annual billing during the current promotion. The Families plan costs $4.49 per month with annual billing during the current promotion. Regular prices are $3.99 per month for Individual and $5.99 per month for Families.
Promotional prices are available to new 1Password customers for their first year with annual billing.
Does 1Password have a free trial?
Yes. You can try 1Password Individual or Families free for 14 days before choosing a plan.
Can I share 1Password with my family?
Yes. The Families plan lets you invite up to five family members, share unlimited vaults, and manage access with simple admin controls. Each family member can keep personal information private while sharing items through shared vaults.
Does 1Password work across my devices?
Yes. 1Password works across your devices and helps you securely save, manage, and autofill passwords and other information wherever you use it.
Does 1Password offer discounts for Individuals or Families?
1Password occasionally offers promotional pricing for new customers. Current promotions are limited to the first year with annual billing. Check the pricing options above for the latest available offer.
What payment methods can I use to pay for 1Password?
You can pay for a 1Password subscription by ACH direct debit for eligible US bank accounts or by credit card. You can also use a 1Password gift card if your account is activated on 1Password.com.
Is 1Password worth the money?
1Password is worth paying for if you want more than basic password storage. Paid plans combine secure password generation, autofill across your devices and browsers, secure sharing, Watchtower alerts, and encrypted storage for documents and other sensitive information. They also include additional security features such as a Secret Key and Travel Mode.
Strong security should be easy to use every day. 1Password pairs powerful protection with a clean, intuitive interface that makes it easy to find what you need, save new logins, and share items securely. Reviewers at PCMag, CNET, and Wirecutter regularly praise 1Password for its usability, approachable design, and gentle learning curve.
Whether 1Password is right for you depends on the features, level of protection, and ease of use you want compared with free tools or other paid password managers. Compare 1Password vs other password managers to decide which option best fits your needs.
