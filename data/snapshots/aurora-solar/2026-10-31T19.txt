Frequently asked questions
What plan is right for me?
Basic: Perfect for solar professionals just getting started, the Basic Plan provides all the essential tools for residential design and sales, including 3D visualizations, financial analysis, and automatic system design.
Premium: For businesses ready to take on more complex projects, the Premium Plan enhances design capabilities with features like LIDAR-assisted modeling, bankable shade reports, and advanced battery storage modeling.
Enterprise: Ideal for teams scaling up and seeking enterprise capabilities, the Enterprise Plan encompasses all Premium features and adds enterprise functionalities such as API integration, secure user permissions, and access to premium services, like plan sets and engineering stamps. Pricing is customized based on specific business needs.
Still unsure? Our sales team is happy to help you find the right plan for your business.
How do I pay for Aurora? Can I pay by credit card or can you invoice me?
• Basic and Premium plans can be paid for by credit card.
• Enterprise plans can be paid for via invoice or credit card.
Is there a minimum commitment?
• There is no minimum commitment for our monthly Basic and Premium plans.
• There is a one year commitment for our annual Basic and Premium plans.
• Contact sales to learn about the minimum commitment for Enterprise plans.
Can I cancel my purchase at any time?
Yes, you can cancel your purchase at any time.
If you are on a monthly plan, your cancellation will take effect at the end of your monthly billing period.
If you are on an annual plan, your cancellation will take effect at the end of your annual billing period.
What if I need more projects per month or want to add more users?
You can purchase additional licenses directly in the Aurora App or contact Sales to inquire about upgrading to an Enterprise plan.
