Mockoon Pro FAQ
Features
What is an "API mock"?
An API mock is a collection of endpoints created in the desktop application or Mockoon Pro embedded web application to simulate a real API. Each mock can include an unlimited number of endpoints, rules, stateful routes, and more. For more information, please refer to the documentation.
Can I self-host Mockoon Pro?
Yes. Mockoon Pro is self-hosted by default, giving you control over your infrastructure and data. Follow our installation guide to get started.
Do you offer a managed cloud service?
Yes. Managed cloud is available only as an Enterprise option, including hosting, operations, and managed API mock deployments. Contact us to discuss your requirements.
What is the difference between Team and Enterprise support?
Team plan customers benefit from next business day support. Enterprise customers receive dedicated support with a guaranteed initial response within four open business hours. For more information, please refer to the terms of service.
Do you offer an availability SLA?
The self-hosted Team plan runs on infrastructure you operate, so its availability is under your control. Enterprise managed cloud includes a contractual SLA defined in your agreement. See our terms of service and Trust Center for details.
We are using the open-source version, but we are interested in getting priority support. Can I purchase it separately?
Yes, we offer custom services that can include priority support. Do not hesitate to contact us to discuss your needs.
Billing
Do you offer a free trial?
Yes. Eligible companies can request one 14-day self-hosted Mockoon Pro trial with five license slots using a verified work email. No account or payment method is required, and the trial does not renew automatically. Start your free trial.
Can I evaluate the Enterprise plan?
The self-service trial covers the self-hosted Pro experience. Contact us to discuss an Enterprise evaluation, managed cloud, or specific deployment requirements.
How are Mockoon Pro licenses allocated?
Each license is a slot that you can assign to either one user seat or one concurrently running mock instance. For example, 10 licenses can be split between 6 users and 4 deployed mock instances, and you can adjust this allocation as your needs change.
What payment methods do you accept?
Team licenses can be purchased by credit card through our payment provider, Paddle. Enterprise customers can also pay by purchase order and invoice.
I have specific billing requirements, can you help me?
We can provide you with tailored billing solutions. Do not hesitate to contact us to discuss your needs.
Do you offer discounts for schools, bootcamps or students?
We provide educational institutions with substantial discounts. Do not hesitate to contact us to become a partner.
Do you offer discounts for open-source projects?
We provide free licenses for eligible open-source projects. Do not hesitate to contact us to discuss your needs.
How can I cancel my subscription?
For a Mockoon Pro subscription, use the management link provided with your order or contact us. Existing Mockoon Cloud customers can continue to manage their subscription from the account page.
Can I get a refund?
Fees are generally non-refundable, but exceptional requests may be considered case by case. Please review our terms of service or contact us for help.
VAT
Prices are in EUR and exclude taxes (VAT, etc.). Applicable taxes are calculated during checkout based on your location and customer type.
Security & privacy
How does Mockoon handle my data?
Mockoon is local-first, and self-hosted Mockoon Pro stores application data on infrastructure you control. Mockoon processes mock data only when you use Enterprise managed cloud, legacy Mockoon Cloud, or deliberately provide it for support. For full details, see our Trust Center and privacy policy.
Are you compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations?
Yes. We support customers operating under GDPR, CCPA, the UK Data Protection Act, and PIPEDA. You can review our compliance posture, sub-processors, and security controls in our Trust Center.
Where can I report a security vulnerability?
Please follow our responsible disclosure process described in our security policy. More information is available in our Trust Center.
