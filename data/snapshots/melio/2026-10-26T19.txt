FAQ
Is there a Melio free plan?
Yes. Melio Go has no subscription fees and includes all the main features needed to start paying and getting paid online. Learn more about Melio’s plans here.
What happens at the end of my 90-day trial?
If you haven’t subscribed to a paid plan by the end of your 90-day period, your account will be automatically converted to the limited free Go plan.
What happens to my account and data if I don’t upgrade to a paid plan?
If you choose to stick with the free Go plan at the end of your 90-day trial, you will lose access to premium features, and any data from those premium features will be deleted.
Does Melio offer Accounts Receivable (AR) capabilities?
Yes. All Melio plans include the AR tools SMBs need to get paid quickly and easily:
Create and send professional invoices
Track payments in real time, on desktop or mobile
Sync seamlessly with your accounting software
Get paid digitally by card or bank – including a shareable payment link and the ability to defer fees
What is the best plan for my business?
Melio offers five flexible plans designed to fit different business needs. The best one for you depends on your team size, workflows, and payment volume:
Go (Free plan): Best if you want simple, flexible tools to pay and get paid with no monthly subscription fees.
You’ll get 5 free ACH payments per month, credit card payments (even when vendors don’t accept cards), invoice creation, payment link generation, and up to 10 QuickBooks/Xero syncs. Perfect for keeping things light and cash-flow friendly.
Core: Best for businesses ready to scale their workflows.
Core gives you the structure and automation to keep finance operations running smoothly. With unlimited QuickBooks Online/Xero sync, approval workflows, and batch payments, it’s built for teams that need to move faster and stay compliant.
You’ll also get 20 free ACH payments each month, tools to manage W-9s and 1099s for subcontractors, branded invoice creation for getting paid, and the ability to add team members with customized roles and permissions.
Boost: Best for larger or more complex finance teams, including accounting firms.
Boost is built for businesses handling more complexity—more people, more rules, and higher payment volumes. It connects with QuickBooks Desktop, offers advanced approval routing based on vendor, amount, or user, and lets you track expenses by class or location.
With 50 free ACH payments each month and priority support (including phone callbacks), Boost keeps your team organized and fully in control as you scale.
Unlimited: Best for businesses that need maximum flexibility.
Unlimited gives you complete freedom to grow without limits. You’ll get unlimited ACH transfers, unlimited users at no extra cost, and advanced roles and permissions to manage access across your team.
It also includes premium support and dedicated onboarding to help you hit the ground running as your operations expand.
Platinum (Invite-only): Best for high-volume businesses processing over $300K/month in credit card payments in need of a tailored solution. Platinum offers custom transaction pricing, card-to-wire transfer options, API access, and enterprise-level integrations.
