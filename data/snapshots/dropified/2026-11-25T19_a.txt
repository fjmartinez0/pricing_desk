Pricing Questions? We've Got Answers.
Do you limit products?
+
No. Every plan includes unlimited product imports.
Do you limit stores?
+
Monthly includes 1 store, yearly (bought on dropified.com) includes 5. Need more? Contact support to add extra stores. If you subscribed through the Shopify App Store, your plan includes 1 store. Shopify App Store billing does not support multiple stores. To use more than one store, subscribe from this page.
Do I need to upgrade for advanced features?
+
No. Every feature is in every plan, including Captcha Solver.
What about AI usage?
+
Every plan includes AI credits. Typical use stays inside that amount.
Is the 14-day trial free?
+
It is $1 for 14 days with full access. Then $50/mo, or $500/yr (2 months free).
Is there a free plan or an Enterprise plan?
+
No. There is one plan.
Can I cancel anytime?
+
Yes. Cancel from your account Profile page, under Plan & Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current monthly or yearly billing period. You keep access until then. If you subscribed through the Shopify App Store, delete the Dropified app from your Shopify admin to end that subscription.
What happens if I switch between monthly and yearly?
+
The change takes effect immediately. If you upgrade, a prorated charge is added to your next invoice. If you downgrade, a credit for unused time is applied to your next bill. You can do this yourself in Plan & Subscriptions.
Which store platforms does Dropified support?
+
Dropified connects to Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, GrooveKart, and Wix stores.
Which suppliers can I auto-order from?
+
AliExpress, eBay, and Alibaba. You can import from other sites, including Amazon, and place those orders yourself.
Will I run out of AI credits each month?
+
Typical use stays inside your included credits. Limits apply to prevent abuse.
