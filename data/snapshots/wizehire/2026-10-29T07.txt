What is Wizehire ?
Wizehire is an award-winning, end-to-end hiring platform that marries innovative software with the hands-on expert support you need to grow your team with confidence. Over 18,000 businesses trust Wizehire to help grow their teams.
Why choose Wizehire?
Choose Wizehire to manage your hiring journey from start to finish in one easy-to-use platform. Benefit from our user-friendly interface, industry-specific Wizehire Coaches, and tools that help attract, evaluate, and onboard top talent efficiently. We simplify the hiring process, saving time and resources while improving hiring outcomes. Plus, we have 4.8 review ratings on platforms like Capterra, G2 and SoftwareAdvice.
How does Wizehire help me with hiring?
Wizehire offers over 350 industry-specific job templates, posts jobs to over 100 job boards with one click, providing customizable evaluation tools, and offering expert coaching tailored to your industry. This ensures you attract qualified candidates, screen them effectively, and make well-informed hiring decisions.
How does Wizehire compare with other hiring platforms?
Wizehire stands out with its combination of a robust technology platform and personalized coaching. Unlike many other hiring platforms, Wizehire offers industry-specific guidance, making it a comprehensive solution that addresses both the technical and strategic aspects of hiring.
Where can I find reviews about Wizehire?
You can find reviews about Wizehire on their website’s testimonials section, as well as on third-party review sites like G2, Capterra, and Trustpilot.
Does Wizehire integrate with other softwares?
Yes! We offer integrations with Checkr for backgrounds checks, with Gusto, ADP, UKG and Paylocity for payroll.
