Frequently asked questions
Anything else, just ask — our team responds in under 2 hours.
Can I pick any plan, regardless of my property count?
Yes, as long as your portfolio fits. Independent Host is capped at 4 properties; every other plan is open to you at any size. Your monthly price is whichever is higher: the plan's per-property rate, or the plan's floor. Pick a bigger plan and you pay its floor until you grow into it.
What's the difference between a flat plan and pay as you go?
Flat plans are a fixed monthly price. Pay as you go is £0/month plus a commission on each booking — 3% for 1–4 properties, 1.5% for 5–10. Pay as you go suits variable or seasonal booking volume; flat suits steady volume and predictable costs. You can switch at any time.
How do add-ons work?
Every feature is available on every plan, either bundled in or as a paid add-on. A 3-property host who wants the Plus direct booking site can add it to Independent Host rather than moving up a plan.
Is there a long-term contract?
No. Monthly plans roll month to month and you can cancel anytime. Annual billing is available on Operator and Manager only, at 10% off the monthly price.
What happens when I switch from another platform?
Every plan includes a free 1:1 onboarding session. We'll walk you through importing your listings, rates and calendars, and our support team is on hand while you get set up.
