Common questions
How much does route management software cost?
Ours is public: $250 a month plus $0.06 per stop, with every feature included. A business running 2,000 stops a month pays $250 + $120 = $370 a month. There is also a one-time migration and training fee, scoped to the size of your operation.
What counts as a "stop"?
A stop is a single visit to a location for a service or delivery. If you service the same address 4 times in a month, that's 4 stops.
Are there per-user or per-vehicle fees?
No. Users, drivers, and vehicles are unlimited. Add dispatchers, office staff, and trucks without your subscription changing.
What's included?
Everything: routing and optimization, the driver app, GPS tracking, CRM, billing, the customer portal, online signup and ecommerce, inventory, inspections, timesheets, SMS, the business phone system, contracts and e-signatures, departments, and the AI assistants (powered by Anthropic and OpenAI models). No feature is locked behind a higher plan, because there is only one plan. Optional add-ons are a website ($25/month, designed and built for free when you sign up) and email marketing above the free 1,000 marketing emails a month.
What's the migration fee for?
It covers the migration specialist who configures your account, imports your existing data, sets up your portal, and trains your team. It scales with the scope of the work: a small operation coming off spreadsheets costs less than a large one moving off another system.
Do I need separate subscriptions for SMS, GPS, or billing?
No separate subscriptions. Every feature is built into the platform. For SMS and Voice, you bring your own Twilio account and pay Twilio's wholesale rates directly. We use your API key to power those features, so there's no middleman markup. Card processing is built into stopSuite billing, with utility rates for businesses that qualify, and we quote your fees in plain numbers. The AI Assistant includes monthly credits shared by your team; heavier use is billed by usage, and you can set a monthly budget. The stopSuite Guide is free.
Why is a demo required?
stopSuite handles a lot. We want to make sure it's the right fit for your operation, and that you understand the full scope of what you're getting. The demo is a conversation, not a sales pitch.
