Frequently asked questions
Everything you need to know about the product and billing.
Do you offer a free trial?
Yes, all of our plans include a 7 day free trial. In addition, if you cancel within a week
of your free trial ending and want a refund, we will issue a 100% refund, no questions asked
🙂
Does OneUp support post approvals?
Yes, OneUp has a robust post approval feature with email notification workflows built in. Learn more about it here.
Does OneUp support direct posting to Instagram?
Yes, OneUp supports direct posting of single-image posts, multi-image posts (carousels), Reels, Trial Reels, Stories, and Collabs to both Business and Creator Instagram accounts (even works with Instagram accounts that are NOT linked to a FB Page 😊). Click here to learn more about how Instagram publishing works.
Does OneUp support replying to comments and DMs?
Yes, OneUp supports replying to comments on YouTube, TikTok, Threads, Bluesky, LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram (including comments on Meta Ads). OneUp supports reply to DMs on Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp (including sending broadcasts).
Does OneUp support personal Facebook
Profiles?
Yes, OneUp supports scheduling posts to personal Facebook Profiles,
as well as Pages and Groups. Click here to learn how personal Facebook Profile posting
works.
Does OneUp support scheduling posts to Google
Business
Profiles?
Yes. OneUp supports scheduling "What's New", "Event", and "Offer" posts on Google
Business Profiles
(with Call-To-Action buttons), scheduling images to the Photos section of Google
Business Profiles, and
replying to Google reviews (with AI-suggested replies) 🤓
Does OneUp support Social Listening?
Yes, OneUp’s Social Listening feature allows you to add the keywords you want to monitor (like your brand, your competitors, or any word), and get email notifications about mentions. You can read what our customers say about OneUp. Learn More
How long will OneUp be around, and how many
people use
OneUp?
OneUp started in 2016, and over the years, we've helped tens of thousands of users publish over 44 million posts. We are a self-funded, profitable company (not reliant on VC funding) and will be around for years to come.
You can read what our customers say about OneUp here, and learn more about the OneUp team here.
Is OneUp approved by the social networks?
Yes, OneUp had to be reviewed and approved by all the social networks and only uses the official APIs of every social network. Your accounts are 100% safe.
What counts as a social account?
Every single Facebook Profile, Facebook Page, Facebook Group, Instagram account, LinkedIn Profile, LinkedIn Page, X account, YouTube account, Threads account, Bluesky account, Snapchat profile, TikTok account, Pinterest account, Discord server, and Google Business Profile location that are connected to OneUp each count as 1 social account.
For example, if you connected 3 Facebook Pages and 2 X accounts, and a Google
Business Profile
with 5 locations, that would count as 10 total social accounts. Each Google Business
Profile location
counts as
a social account, even if all of them are connected to the same GBP account. Click here for more info on Google Business Profiles.
Does OneUp have a Zapier integration?
Yes, OneUp has a Zapier integration 🤓
Does OneUp have an API & MCP?
Yes, OneUp has an API & MCP. You can find more info on OneUp’s API & MCP here.
What currency will I be billed in?
OneUp bills in USD. For foreign transactions, the currency
conversion will happen after the charge and will be performed by
your credit card provider.
Payments are secured by Stripe.
