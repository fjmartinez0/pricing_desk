Pricing questions, answered
How does the credit system work?
Match credits are how you pay for data in Versium REACH. Each tool and data point, such as an email, phone number, or firmographic detail, uses a set number of credits from your package or subscription balance. See credit costs for each tool.
Can I try Versium REACH before I buy?
Yes. Create a free account to explore the platform. After we verify your business, you get access to testing data or API test credits to assess data quality for your use case.
Why do I need to verify my business?
Versium REACH gives access to consumer and business identity data. We verify every business before granting data access to keep that data secure, compliant, and used for legitimate purposes.
What is the difference between Credit Packages and Subscriptions?
Credit Packages are prepaid blocks of 5,000 to 500,000 match credits, starting at $250. Credits never expire, so they suit project-based or seasonal needs.
Annual Subscriptions start at $5,000 per year for 250,000+ credits. They offer the lowest cost per match, from about $0.02 to as low as $0.005, plus overage protection and phone support.
Both plans include every data tool, API access, and the MCP Server.
Can I pay monthly or quarterly?
Yes, quarterly payments are accepted on annual subscriptions of $10,000 or more, and monthly payments on $100,000 or more (and most Reseller plans). Your annual credit allowance is split into monthly or quarterly installments to match your payment schedule. Subscriptions below $10,000 and all Credit Packages are paid up front.
Do unused credits roll over?
Credit Packages: credits never expire. They stay in your account until you use them.
Subscriptions paid up front: you receive your full annual allowance at the start of your term. Your balance resets when the term ends.
Subscriptions paid monthly or quarterly: credits arrive in matching installments. Some plans roll credits between periods, but all reset at the end of the 12-month term.
Check your agreement for your specific rollover terms.
What happens if I run out of credits?
Subscriptions: your workflows and API integrations keep running. Extra usage is billed monthly as overage at your plan's fixed rate.
Credit Packages: refill manually from the Versium REACH dashboard, or set up auto-refill to top up your balance automatically.
You can also move to a higher volume subscription anytime for a lower cost per match.
Do List Insights cost credits?
No. List Insights generate automatically for each audience and show its demographics or firmographics as charts, graphs, and maps.
Do you offer reseller pricing?
Yes. We build custom pricing for resellers, distributors, and bulk file licensing. Resellers must be registered data brokers. Talk to sales to discuss your model.
Are there setup or onboarding fees?
No. Your cost is tied only to your credit package or subscription volume.
What is your cancellation policy?
Subscriptions are 12-month contracts, and the discounted rates depend on completing the full term. To stop automatic renewal or discuss your account, contact your account manager or email customer-success@versium.com.
Do data licensing rights end when my subscription ends?
Yes. Your data licensing rights end when your subscription ends. Indefinite licensing rights are available at an additional cost. We arrange these case by case, so talk to sales about your needs.
