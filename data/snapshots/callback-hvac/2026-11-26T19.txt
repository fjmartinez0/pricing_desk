Straight answers
Pricing questions HVAC owners ask
What does CallBack HVAC cost?+
Self-Serve software is $99/month per HVAC company after the 14-day no-card trial. Guided Setup adds a one-time $149 setup fee, then the same $99/month software. Your phone provider bills separately for number rental, business-texting registration and campaign fees, messages and carrier fees, and voice calls or forwarding. Provider charges can apply during the software trial. Total cost depends on your provider, registration and usage; the software price is not an all-in phone-service price.
Can I cancel during the trial?+
Yes. The software trial is 14 days and no credit card is required to start. Self-Serve is $99/month after the trial once you add payment. Guided Setup is a separate $149 one-time fee charged when you add payment; it does not replace the monthly software price.
Which phone providers can I use?+
Twilio is the standard setup using your own paid account and approved texting number. Telnyx and Plivo integrations are available for setup review; contact us to confirm availability and requirements before starting a trial with either. They are not automatically enabled for every account. Each company uses one provider; switching an existing connection requires a separate migration review.
When does the trial start, and can I use it before phone setup is ready?+
Your 14-day no-card software trial starts after you confirm your email, connect and verify an eligible business number, complete the owner-consented Press 1 test, and the provider confirms delivery of the test text. If phone setup or carrier review is incomplete, your account stays pending and the trial clock does not run. Phone-provider charges are separate and may apply during setup or the trial.
Can I keep my current business number?+
Check compatibility before changing your phone routing. CallBack needs an approved provider number and a different destination number for forwarding calls to your team. Your current setup determines whether and how your advertised number can be used. Voicemail answering can affect whether a call is treated as unanswered. Test an answered call, an unanswered call with text consent, a received text and a reply before using the flow with customers. Your team still handles callbacks and bookings.
What does AI CSR Live do?+
AI CSR Live is a separately reviewed pilot for one defined overflow or after-hours call path. Live customer routing stays off unless the pilot is approved and enabled. It is not included in the 14-day free trial.
What does the AI CSR Pro pilot add?+
The AI CSR Pro pilot adds deeper intake guidance, stronger urgency routing, more structured summaries for complex calls, and priority setup support. It also requires separate approval before customer-facing use.
What is the difference between the AI CSR Live and Pro pilots?+
Live reviews a simpler defined overflow or after-hours call path. Pro adds more guided qualification, stronger routing rules, and priority setup support. Both remain off for live callers unless separately approved and enabled.
Does AI CSR replace my staff or answering service?+
No. The first-sale product is still missed-call text-back and lead recovery. An approved AI CSR pilot can support one defined call path; your team still owns booking, dispatch decisions, customer follow-up, and quality control.
Is AI CSR included in the free trial or $99/month Self-Serve plan?+
No. The $99/month Self-Serve plan covers the core call-capture workflow, including missed-call text-back, lead records, dashboard handling, and tracking. Guided Setup is an optional $149 one-time fee. AI CSR pilots are separately reviewed paid add-ons, remain off for live callers unless approved and enabled, and are not included in the 14-day free trial.
Can I cancel after the trial?+
Yes. CallBack HVAC is month-to-month. You can cancel your subscription at any time from inside the app, and your account stays active until the end of the current billing period.
What if I have more than one HVAC business location?+
Each company account covers one connected provider number and one HVAC business. For multiple locations, contact our team through the contact page to discuss the setup before creating accounts.
What is Twilio A2P 10DLC?+
A2P 10DLC is the registration process for sending application-based business texts to US recipients from local 10-digit numbers through Twilio. It applies even at low message volumes. You register your business and messaging use case in Twilio as part of setup. Registration is required for this number type, but it does not guarantee that every text will be delivered.
