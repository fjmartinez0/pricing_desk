Frequently Asked Questions
Which subscription should I use to perform massing studies for a new urban development?
Urban Planner has 2D/3D massing and would be suitable for your needs.
Can I use TestFit to generate detailed typology solutions for mixed-use developments?
Yes, the Site Solver subscription provides detailed typology solutions suitable for mixed-use developments
I'm interested in generative design capabilities. Which subscription should I consider?
Generative design is only available with the Site Solver subscription.
Does the Site Solver subscription include a dedicated account manager?
Yes, Site Solver subscribers receive support from a dedicated account manager.
Does TestFit work for international customers?
Yes, TestFit works globally with the same core features and functionality. Some data layers (such as parcel data, flood zones, wetlands, or zoning information) may not be available in all countries. From a support and training standpoint, TestFit is currently available in English only, and our support and training teams operate during U.S. business hours (9:00 AM–5:00 PM CST).
I need to integrate with Revit and SketchUp. Which subscriptions support this?
Integration with Revit and SketchUp is supported in all plans. Check out all our integrations on the TestFit integrations page.
If I want to upgrade my plan, how do I do that?
You can email sales@testfit.io, and we’ll be happy to help!
