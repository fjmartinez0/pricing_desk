FAQ.
How do credits work?⌄
Credits are how Lindy measures work, and bigger jobs use more. Pick the monthly credits your team needs, starting at 5,000 for $49.99 a month. Every active user in your workspace is billed, from $49.99 a month for 5,000 credits each, and everyone draws from one shared pool.
Do unused credits roll over?⌄
Monthly credits refresh at the start of each billing cycle and don't roll over. Top-up credits are yours until you use them.
What happens if we run out of credits?⌄
Nothing breaks and there is no surprise bill. Lindy pauses credit-using actions until your credits reset at the start of your next cycle. Need more before then? Admins can top up at $10 per 1,000 credits, or move to more monthly credits anytime.
Who actually needs a paid seat?⌄
Anyone who uses Lindy takes a seat, whether they sign up directly, join your workspace, or @mention Lindy in Slack. New teammates who join through Slack get a 7-day free trial before the seat is billed; direct signups are billed right away.
How does billing and seat management work?⌄
Teammate is one workspace subscription: every seat is on a single bill and all credits pool together. Admins manage seats and credit allocations in one place. Remove a seat any time and it stays active through the cycle you've paid for, with no mid-cycle proration, and Lindy won't re-add anyone an admin removed.
Will Lindy do anything without my approval?⌄
No. Anything with outside impact waits for approval such as sending an email, updating a ticket, posting to another channel, publishing a doc. Read-only lookups from approved sources do not require approval.
Is our data secure?⌄
Yes. Your data is encrypted, never sold, and never used to train models. Lindy is SOC 2 and GDPR compliant, and Enterprise adds SSO, audit logs, and HIPAA with a signed BAA.
Can I try Lindy for free?⌄
Yes. Start with $50 in free credits, no credit card required. The free credits last 7 days. After that, paid usage starts at $49.99 per user per month.
What happens if we cancel?⌄
Your workspace stays active through the cycle you've paid for, with no mid-cycle proration. After that, credit-using actions stop.
Does a returning teammate get another free trial?⌄
The free week is one-time per teammate. If someone leaves and comes back, they pick up right where the team left off with immediate access, going straight onto a billed seat.
