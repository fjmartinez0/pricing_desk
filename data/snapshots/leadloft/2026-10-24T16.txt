Is there a discount for annual subscriptions?
Yep! Annual subscriptions get 2 months free - upgrade at any time or ask our team about them!
What if I need more than one workspace to manage my clients?
LeadLoft is designed to support advisors, agencies, and consultants. You can simply create a new Workspace to manage different clients or accounts.
What if we need multiple users?
Every paid LeadLoft account comes with 1 user. If you need more than 1 user, you can simply invite them via the LeadLoft dashboard. Each additional users beyond the first is an additional $99/mo under the Unlimited plan.
What if I need help getting set up?
We offer a 1:1 onboarding session for every Unlimited LeadLoft customer. This includes an online meeting where we'll help to set up your account with best practices.
Does LeadLoft support agencies, advisors, and consultants?
Yep! LeadLoft was designed with 3rd parties in mind. Owners and Admins can even send on behalf of other users so it's easy to manage email campaigns for clients.
Are there video tutorials to help us get started?
Yep! You can access LeadLoft's video tutorials from our video academy at any time.
