FAQ
Why don't you offer a tiered subscription model?
As restaurant operators and owners ourselves, we know running a restaurant is complex enough. We exist to make your back office straightforward and simple (as much as it can be).
The same initiative extends to our pricing. That’s why we’re the only company in the industry to offer flat, all-inclusive pricing for our restaurant management system.
I'm a Toast user, what's a pass-through API fee?
API is a special type of integration that enables sales data from your POS to flow more reliably into MarginEdge (meaning more efficient performance reporting for you!).
In order to help maintain this integration for MarginEdge, Toast charges an additional fee equal to 10% of your subscription cost for each location each month. This fee is separate from your monthly MarginEdge subscription, since MarginEdge passes this fee from Toast to you, but is visible in your subscription billing module.
What is the Restaurant Management Suite fee for Toast users?
To use Toast API integrations, Toast customers need to pay $50 per month per location for the Toast "Restaurant Management Suite" subscription. This subscription unlocks API integrations (the connection that allows your Toast sales to flow into MarginEdge) and provides Sales Benchmarking, Restaurant Analytics and Menu Publishing. This subscription fee remains flat whether you add one or fifty integrations. This fee is billed directly by Toast, not by MarginEdge.
You’re really not going to charge me extra for all the invoices and bills we have?
Nope! You have enough variable costs in your restaurant and we don’t want to be one of them.
Bill Pay is free and unlimited for our US clients. We currently do not offer Bill Pay for our Canadian clients but are always working on increasing our service offerings!
Are there any cancellation fees?
Our goal is to always provide unquestionable value so you never want to leave (but if you have to, we won’t charge you for it).
The one exception: Annual plans are paid upfront at a 10% discount and are non-refundable if you cancel early. For the fine print, you can find full details in our Terms & Conditions.
Note for SmartScale customers: There is a $500 fee for scales not returned.
How does onboarding work?
Every new location starts with an onboarding package, and we offer options based on your needs. Each one includes hands-on support from our Implementation Team to get you up and running.
For 1-4 restaurant locations
Choose from three onboarding packages designed to guide you through setup.
For multi-unit groups with 5+ locations
Our Multi-unit Onboarding Packages are built for long-term success. You'll work with your rep to custom build a package that fits your needs.
Some partner programs include discounted or waived onboarding.
What payment plans are available?
MarginEdge subscription
MarginEdge offers a monthly billing for $350 per location or a once-and-done annual billing option for a 10% discount (which saves you $420 a year!).
Freepour bundle
You can bundle Freepour with your MarginEdge subscription for an additional $150 per location per month. The 10% discount is also available if you choose to be billed annually, totaling a combined savings of $600 per year.
SmartPrep bundle
You can bundle SmartPrep with your MarginEdge subscription for an additional $95 per location per month. The 10% discount is also available if you choose to be billed annually, totaling a combined savings of $534 per year.
I’m an accountant firm serving restaurants, what’s pricing like for us?
We love working with accounting groups and we can offer special pricing. If you haven’t yet, check out our page for restaurant accountants to learn more about how we work with accounting groups.
Do you charge sales tax?
Certain US states and international countries have a required sales tax. If your subscription includes sales tax you will see the amount applied on the checkout page.
