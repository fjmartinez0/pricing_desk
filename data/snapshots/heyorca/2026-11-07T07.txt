Frequently Asked Questions
Which HeyOrca plan supports add-ons?
Add-ons are available for HeyOrca Basic plans. You can choose from two available add-ons: Reporting & Listening and Social Inbox.
Can I mix and match different HeyOrca plans?
Yes, you can add any combination of HeyOrca Free, Basic, and Pro workspaces to your account. This means you can use HeyOrca's paid plans to manage your most advanced, high-touch clients, and use HeyOrca Free plans to take care of your early-stage clients, personal brands, and passion projects.
Does HeyOrca support two-factor authentication (2FA) or single sign-on (SSO)?
Yes! HeyOrca offers two secure sign-in options to help protect your account: Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) and Google Single Sign-On (SSO). When you choose 2FA, you'll enter a code from your authenticator app plus your password when you log in. If you choose SSO, you'll use your Google account to log in to HeyOrca.
Does HeyOrca have a free plan?
Yes! HeyOrca has a free plan for social media managers and brands with light posting needs. After signing up for HeyOrca Free, you’ll get two weeks of free access to our Pro plan (which comes with unlimited posts, users, and more – yay!). After two weeks, your account will seamlessly transition back to the free plan's features. And yes, HeyOrca Free is *actually* free, and there’s no credit card required.
How many social media accounts are included in HeyOrca?
All workspaces come with up to 10 social media accounts. Need more? Additional accounts are just $10 each per month.
Does HeyOrca provide social media reports and analytics?
We sure do! HeyOrca Pro comes with advanced social media reports – so you can prove your impact to your team, clients, and other stakeholders.
In your reporting dashboard, you’ll see the following social media reports: Facebook, Instagram, Instagram Demographics, LinkedIn Personal, LinkedIn Company Pages, TikTok, and Custom Reports. You can use these reports to track follower growth, measure engagement, discover top posts, compare date ranges, and more. You can also automate your reports to be emailed to your key contacts on the schedule of your choice.
Not a HeyOrca Pro user? Choose Reports & Listening add-on to apply social media reports to your Basic plan.
