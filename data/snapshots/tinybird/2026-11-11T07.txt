FAQs
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan anytime?
Yes! Tinybird offers flexible plan management. On Developer plans, you can resize your workspace resources (vCPU, memory) at any time through self-service controls. This means you can scale up during peak periods and scale down when demand decreases, ensuring you only pay for what you need. SaaS plans offer vertical scaling on shared infrastructure, while Enterprise plans provide the most flexibility with dedicated infrastructure and both vertical and horizontal scaling options. Learn more
What are vCPU overages?
vCPU overages happen when your workload temporarily exceeds the vCPU allocation included in your plan. Instead of throttling your queries, Tinybird allows them to burst beyond your base vCPUs and charges $0.0002/second for the extra compute used. This applies to Developer and SaaS plans. Enterprise plans include dedicated infrastructure with custom vCPU allocations, so overages do not apply. You can monitor your vCPU usage in the Organization settings to stay on top of costs. Learn more
Can I scale horizontally with Tinybird?
All Tinybird plans include 2 replicas by default for high availability. Enterprise plans support additional horizontal scaling, allowing you to add more replicas to your dedicated cluster to increase query throughput or customize your load balancing strategy. This allows you to distribute query load across multiple nodes, ensuring consistent performance as your data and traffic grow. Developer and SaaS plans support vertical scaling, while Enterprise plans offer both vertical and horizontal scaling with dedicated infrastructure. Unlike self-hosted ClickHouse®, Tinybird handles all the complexity of cluster management, sharding, and replication for you. Learn more
Why is Tinybird so affordable compared to self-hosted ClickHouse?
Tinybird's shared infrastructure model (available on Developer and SaaS plans) allows us to offer competitive pricing while maintaining high performance. By sharing resources across multiple customers, we can optimize costs and pass those savings to you. This shared model doesn't compromise performance. You get enterprise-grade ClickHouse® performance without the overhead of managing dedicated infrastructure, making it cost-effective for teams of all sizes. Enterprise plans use dedicated infrastructure for maximum isolation and performance guarantees. Learn more
What do I get beyond just a ClickHouse cluster?
Tinybird provides a complete managed analytics platform, not just a ClickHouse® cluster. Beyond the database, you get automatic ClickHouse® version upgrades, free automated daily backups, managed ingestion through our Events API and connectors (Kafka, S3, GCS), scalable REST APIs that turn your SQL queries into production endpoints instantly, managed workers for data transformations, automatic schema migrations, built-in security with SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliance, comprehensive monitoring and observability tools, and a superior developer experience with intuitive tooling and workflows. All of this is managed for you, so you can focus on building features instead of managing infrastructure. Learn more
How does Tinybird provide fine-grained usage stats and monitoring?
Tinybird offers comprehensive observability built into the platform. You get detailed usage statistics for queries, data ingestion, API calls, and resource consumption through service data sources like tinybird.pipe_stats, tinybird.datasources_ops_log, and tinybird.jobs_log. These provide real-time and historical metrics with sub-second latency, helping you monitor performance, track costs, and optimize your data pipeline. This transparency ensures no surprises, you can see exactly how your resources are being used and make informed decisions about scaling. Learn more
What limits do I have with my plan?
Each Tinybird plan has specific limits for resources like vCPU, memory, storage, and feature usage (connectors, sinks, copy pipes). Free plans have the most restrictions, while Developer, SaaS, and Enterprise plans offer increasing flexibility. SaaS plans support up to 32 vCPUs on shared infrastructure, while Enterprise plans offer unlimited vCPUs on dedicated infrastructure with additional features like horizontal scaling and cluster management. You can view all limits in the pricing table above or check the documentation for detailed information. Learn more
What kind of support do I have with my plan?
Community Slack is available to everyone, and Developer includes email support. SaaS and Enterprise include Standard support, with priority email, live chat, and a dedicated customer Slack channel. Premier support is available for SaaS S-24+ and Enterprise. It adds a designated Customer Success engineer, prioritized SLA, on-call requests, monthly partnership meetings, and onboarding support. Your agreement defines your support plan, pricing, and terms. Learn more
What are the organization features?
Organizations provide a centralized way of managing workspaces and members in a region. From the Organization settings section you can monitor resource usage and check your current plan's usage and billing if you're on a paid plan. Learn more
