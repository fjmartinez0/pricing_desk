FAQs
How much does Clarify cost?
Unlike legacy CRMs, you don’t pay for seats; you only pay for the work Clarify’s AI does to help your team sell more, measured in credits. Each plan includes a fixed number of credits that reset monthly. You can purchase more credits as needed on paid plans.
What are credits?
Credits power the work Clarify’s AI does on behalf of your team, including meeting prep and summaries, deal creation and summaries, field updates, task creation, and workflows run. All the stuff that needs to be done, that you’re guaranteed AI will do much faster and more efficiently.
How can I predict my Clarify monthly bill?
Your cost is your plan fee plus any usage beyond your monthly credit allowance, with no per-seat charges. To estimate usage, multiply expected activity by the credit rates, for example 30 credits per meeting summary or 1 per Campaign email. Free features like enrichment and meeting recording never affect your bill.
Can I control credit consumption?
Yes! You’re in compete control. You can:
Enable or disable overages for your Clarify workspace
Toggle on/off specific features that consume credits e.g., Meeting summaries, Task creation
You'll receive email notifications at 80%, 90%, and 100% of your credit allowance.
What happens when I run out of credits?
When your workspace’s credit balance reaches zero, AI features pause until your next billing cycle. Paid plans can turn on on-demand credits in Settings > Workspace > Billing to keep features running at a per-credit rate, with an optional dollar-based spend cap. Meeting recording is never credit-gated, so the notetaker keeps joining and recording your calls regardless of your balance. Free features such as background enrichment are also unaffected.
