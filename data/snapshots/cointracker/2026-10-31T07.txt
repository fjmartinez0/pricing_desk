Frequently asked questions
How plans, limits and transaction counts work.
Crypto tax guides
Counting and limits
How do you count transactions?
We count the transactions in the tax year you're filing. Activity from earlier years doesn't count against this year's plan, so your price doesn't go up because you've been in crypto a long time. Some tools count your entire history instead.
What doesn't count toward my limit?
Spam tokens and dust never count, on any plan, and referral rebates never count on paid plans. On Prime and above, staking rewards and interest don't count either. On Base and Base+, staking rewards don't count until they add up to $25 in proceeds for the year. If most of your activity is rewards rather than trades, you'll often land on a lower plan than your raw count suggests.
Do I pay again for previous tax years?
No. One plan covers your tax forms for every year you've traded. If you're catching up on three years at once, you pay the same as someone filing only this year.
What happens if I go over my plan's limit?
You move up to the plan that covers your count and pay only the difference. What you've already paid is credited, so upgrading mid-year never costs more than buying the right plan at the start.
Can I change plans after I've paid?
You can move up at any point in the year and pay only the difference. Moving to a lower plan happens at renewal. Your forms reflect the plan you hold when you download them.
Does my plan renew automatically, and how do I cancel?
Yes. Your plan renews each year at your current tier, so your forms and features are ready for the next tax season. You can turn off auto-renewal any time in your subscription settings. If your activity changes, you can move up or down a plan when it renews.
How many transactions does the Free plan include?
You can connect as many wallets and exchanges as you like on Free. Each wallet can hold up to 50,000 transactions, and a wallet over that limit needs a paid plan.
Choosing a plan
Is CoinTracker worth it?
It depends on your year, and you can check before you pay: connect your accounts on Free and preview your tax summary. One plan covers every year you've traded, your numbers go straight into TurboTax or H&R Block, and from Prime up, staking rewards don't count toward your limit.
Why is CoinTracker more expensive than other crypto tax tools?
Often it isn't, once you see what counts. Staking rewards, interest, rebates, spam and dust don't use up your limit here, and one plan covers every past year. Some tools count your full history or charge again for each past year, so the total can end up higher even when the sticker price is lower.
The rest of the difference is what happens to your numbers. Our in-house tax team decides how each type of transaction is categorized, and that's what determines whether your return holds up. A cheaper report with the wrong cost basis costs more than it saves.
I only made a few trades. Is there a cheaper option?
Yes. Base is our entry plan and first-time filers with a simple year pay an introductory rate for their first year. You can connect everything and see your full tax summary before you decide to buy.
What's free, and what needs a paid plan?
Free covers portfolio tracking across every wallet and exchange, automatic categorization, cost basis across transfers and a tax summary preview, so you see the whole picture before paying anything. Tax forms, TurboTax and H&R Block integrations, tax loss harvesting and DeFi positions need a paid plan.
Can I get a refund?
Yes. You can request a refund within 30 days of purchase, as long as you haven't downloaded tax forms. Contact support to request one.
Which payment methods do you accept?
All major credit cards and digital wallets. You can also pay in USDC on any plan, priced in US dollars. You'll see the full list at checkout, and you can update your payment method any time.
Do you offer discounts or promo codes?
You may be eligible for a promo through one of our partners. Promos can't be combined, and if you qualify for more than one, we apply the one that saves you the most. Promos apply to your first year only.
Which countries do you support, and what currency are prices in?
CoinTracker supports filers in 40+ countries, with local currency, local cost basis rules and local integrations. You can check out in your local currency.
Is there an option for high-volume or complex years?
Yes. Full Service starts at $3,499 a year and covers up to 300,000 lifetime transactions. Bigger histories get a custom quote. A dedicated account manager works your accounts with you, including hands-on help fixing errors and reconciling. Learn about Full Service.
Can I see my gains before I buy a plan?
Yes. On Free, you can connect your wallets and exchanges, add your full history and preview your tax summary at no cost. You only need a paid plan to download tax forms.
Coverage and accuracy
Do you support DeFi, NFTs and staking?
Yes, across EVM chains and Solana. DeFi activity is categorized on every plan. From Prime up, lending, staking and liquidity positions also show in your portfolio. Staking rewards are categorized automatically.
What if my exchange already sent me a 1099 or 1099-DA?
We reconcile your records against what the exchange reported. An exchange only sees activity on its own platform, so a 1099-DA can miss the cost basis of crypto you transferred in. That gap is a common reason exchange-reported gains come out too high.
What if my numbers look wrong?
Usually a wallet is missing or a transfer wasn't matched to its other side, and we flag both. You can fix categories yourself, ask CoinTracker's AI agent, or get hands-on help from Full Service.
How long does this take?
Connecting your accounts takes a few minutes. Most of the time goes into reviewing anything we flag, so complex years with many blockchains and protocols take longer.
Can I regenerate my report after fixing my data without paying again?
Yes. Regenerate your report as many times as you need, for this year and any past year.
