Do you offer discounted pricing to Accounting and Bookkeeping Firms?
Yes!
As an accounting firm, you can enjoy discounts by linking your clients' accounts to your Plooto account.
How to Get Discounts:
To be eligible for discounts, your clients' Plooto accounts must be linked to your firm’s account, and each client must have an active Plooto subscription. Simply having user access doesn't count. To check linked clients:
•Toggle to your firm's Plooto account
•Select your company name in the top right
•Go to Client Settings
Subscription Discounts:
•Waived Firm Subscription: Have 3 or more active Grow or Pro clients linked to your firm's account and get your firm’s base subscription waived.
•3+ Pro clients = waived Pro subscription
•3+ Grow clients = waived Grow subscription
•Clients on the Go plan are not eligible.
•Tiered Client Discounts: If you're paying for 3+ client accounts (Grow/Pro plans), you can qualify for tiered discounts:
•3-10 clients: 10% discount
•11-24 clients: 15% discount
•25+ clients: 20% discount
Note: Clients paying for their own subscription do not count toward tiered discounts.

Do you offer competitive pricing for international payments?
Save on expensive wire fees with Plooto's international payments. Plooto streamlines how you pay vendors worldwide by not charging foreign exchange transfer fees*. Learn more about international payments and what countries and currencies are supported.
*Excludes fees on non foreign exchange, cheque, and same-currency transactions. Industry and discretionary rates apply, and are subject to change. A complete conversion cost estimate is viewable when payment is created or via our foreign exchange calculator.
