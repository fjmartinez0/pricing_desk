Pricing questions, answered
How much does AskCody cost?
AskCody starts at $5,149 per year for the Platform Fee, which includes every feature, 5 rooms, 10 workspaces and 1 reception. You add the rooms, workspaces and receptions you need beyond that, and every new customer pays a one-time Assisted Onboarding fee from $2,200. Use the price estimator for an indicative price, and talk to a consultant for your exact quote.
What is included in the Platform Fee?
Every feature in AskCody: room and desk booking, meeting services such as catering, visitor management, maps, meeting room displays, dashboards, reporting and insights, plus the integration with Exchange and Microsoft 365 and single sign-on with Entra ID. The Platform Fee also includes 5 rooms, 10 workspaces, 1 reception and Basic Support.
What counts as a room, a workspace and a reception?
A room is a bookable meeting or conference room. A workspace is a bookable desk or flexible workspace. A reception is a front desk or lobby where you welcome and register visitors. You scale each one on its own, so you only pay for the spaces you manage.
Do you charge per user?
No. You pay for the rooms, workspaces and receptions you manage, not for the number of employees who book them.
How does volume pricing work?
The prices on this page are our standard prices. For larger workplaces, the price per room, workspace and reception goes down with volume, and your consultant applies volume pricing in your quote. The Platform Fee is the same for everyone. Public sector and government organizations can also talk to us about public sector and framework agreement pricing.
Why is Assisted Onboarding required?
Because a good start decides how fast your teams get value. Assisted Onboarding is about six hours of guided sessions to plan, set up, configure and review your platform with our team. It is a one-time fee from $2,200, and it is part of every new subscription, also when you choose Premium Support or a Managed Service.
What is the difference between Premium Support and a Managed Service?
Premium Support gives you faster responses, priority in the support queue, extended onboarding, implementation help and training, and quarterly business reviews. With a Managed Service, our specialists run the platform with you: setup and tuning of rooms, users, rules and policies, plus monitoring and troubleshooting. You can combine the two. See the service plans page for the details.
How do billing and renewal work?
We bill annually, and your subscription renews each year. We notify you about the upcoming renewal three months before the end of your subscription period, and that is also the notice period if you want to end it: give us written notice at least three months before the period ends. The full terms are in our terms and conditions.
Can we add rooms, workspaces or receptions later?
Yes. Start with what you need today and add licenses as your workplace grows. Your consultant updates your subscription, and every new license comes with every feature.
Which currency are the prices in?
The prices on this page are in US dollars and exclude VAT and other taxes. Your consultant confirms your final price in your quote.
Is AskCody secure?
AskCody runs on Microsoft Azure, uses Entra ID for single sign-on and user management, and has an ISAE 3000 assurance report. Read more about our commitment to GDPR.
