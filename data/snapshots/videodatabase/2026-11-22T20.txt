Common questions
How does the free trial work?
Pro and Strategist both start with 7 days free. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel before then.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
How do Strategist team seats work?
Strategist includes 3 seats: you plus 2 teammates, invited by email. Each extra seat is $49/month.
