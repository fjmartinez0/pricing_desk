Pricing questions, answered
Can I buy more than one product?
Yes. Pick a plan for each product (Tracking, Returns, Shipping). Each one is priced on its own volume, so you only pay for what you need. Start with one product and add more anytime from your AfterShip admin. They all run on one platform.
What happens if I go over my quota?
Your service keeps running. Usage above your plan’s included quota is billed as overage at 1.3x your plan’s unit price. Your unit price is your plan’s list price divided by its included quota for the same billing period. Discounts and promotions aren’t included, and rates are rounded to the nearest cent. For example, a $100/month plan with 1,000 included shipments has a unit price of $0.10, so each extra shipment costs $0.13. If you often go over, moving up to a plan with a higher quota usually costs less.
This applies to Tracking and Returns. Shipping has no overage, so merchants move up a plan when they need more labels.
Can I switch plans at any time?
You can upgrade or downgrade the existing plan as your business requirements change. It can be changed from the billing page.
How does Enterprise pricing work?
Enterprise plans are custom. We price them around your volume, the number of brands and stores you run, your integrations and your support needs. Enterprise includes a dedicated CSM and onboarding, enterprise SLAs and SSO, multi-store and multi-brand management, and custom integrations and API limits. Talk to our sales team for a quote.
How do you count shipments for AfterShip Shipping?
Each outbound tracking number counts as one shipment, except when it’s part of a multi-piece shipment. A master tracking number and its linked packages count as one shipment in total. Return labels don’t count toward your Shipping quota. Voiding an outbound label doesn’t restore quota.
How is SMS pricing calculated?
SMS pricing is based on the destination. Our fair pricing ensures you will only be charged on the SMS sent. View SMS pricing.
What payment methods do you accept?
AfterShip accepts all major credit and debit cards. You can modify existing payment methods from the billing page.
What is your uptime guarantee?
AfterShip guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime. If it is not met, we'll refund 10% of whatever you pay for the downtime period.
