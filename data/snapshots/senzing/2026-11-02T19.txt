How Senzing Pricing Works
Senzing subscription pricing is annual and is based on the current count of Data Source Records (DSRs) in your Senzing database. A DSR is a single record that is mapped and loaded into Senzing. Annual pricing scales as your data grows across 51 volume tiers: 10M DSRs is $58,560 per year, and 1B DSRs is $1,497,000 per year. The published tiers stop at 1B, but Senzing doesn’t: it is proven in production at ~100B records.
Quoted prices are based on up-front payment for the full term and acceptance of the standard End User License Agreement (EULA). Custom terms and conditions trigger custom prices. Senzing support is always included in your paid subscription at no additional charge, regardless of licensed DSRs. Without a license, Senzing runs in evaluation mode limited to 500 records. To try it with more, Ask Senzing can email you a free evaluation license. To get a quote for your specific number of records, or Partner Program pricing, contact us. See Data Source Records (DSRs) Explained for how records are counted.
If you want to learn more about DSRs and pricing, see Data Source Records (DSRs) Explained.
Want a quote on your specific number of records? Contact Us for Partner Program pricing and other special programs.
