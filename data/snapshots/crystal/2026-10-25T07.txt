FAQ
What does Crystal Premium include?
The advice Crystal writes from your assessments. For your personality: insights written from the results on your profile, the full guide library, and a PDF of your profile. For your career: what your career adds up to, where it could go next, the kind of work that suits you, which skills to grow, and the people you work with: predicted assessments on LinkedIn with no limit, how to work with each person, and briefings before your meetings. For your goals: Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly and Yearly Planning, each plan mapped to your goals and updated as you reflect, the 5, 10 and 20-Year Visions, the analysis on every check-in, and comparisons with the people in your life. Every day: hidden gems about you beside your free gem, and Weekly Hidden Gems on your week. $49 a month, cancel anytime.
What can I do for free?
Take every assessment, as often as you like, and keep every result, your streak and your history. Every day you get Today’s Gem, the Morning Check-in with a plan for your day, and the Evening Reflection, and every weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly reflection is free too. You also get your profile, showing the results you choose, up to 10 contacts, and your 1-Year Vision with your goals and practices. On top of that, Crystal writes 1 free sample of each kind of advice: the analysis on each kind of check-in, a memo on an assessment like Skills or Stress, a guide, and how to work with 1 person. Crystal for Chrome predicts up to 10 people on LinkedIn before it asks you to go Premium. None of it expires.
Is there a free trial?
Crystal has no timed trial. Every assessment is free for good, and instead of a trial you get 1 free sample of each kind of advice Crystal writes, with no card and no clock. After each sample, that kind of advice is Premium.
What is the difference between the Team Assessments tiers?
Starter is the place to begin if you have a small team to assess. Basic fits regular use, and Pro is for teams that don’t want to think about limits. Pro also includes personality curriculums and resources for running your own personality workshop. Every plan is billed annually, and the limits cover the full year.
What happens if I cancel?
Everything you made stays yours, and your assessments stay free. Guides stop updating, your profile insights stop showing to anyone else, and Crystal stops writing your weekly to yearly plans and your hidden gems. Today’s Plan and every reflection stay free. Resubscribe anytime and pick up where you left off.
Can my company buy Premium for the whole team?
Yes, that is Crystal Premium Team: $49 per seat, per month, the same price as Premium. An admin buys it from billing settings in the app, seats assign as members join, and every member's full profile is shared with the team by default.
Does Premium include Buyer Profiles, Team Assessments or the Personality API?
No. Buyer Profiles, Team Assessments (for your teams or clients) and the Personality API are sold separately, and none of them requires Premium. Buyer Profiles are set up per team, Team Assessments starts at $500 a year, and the API starts with self-serve credits.

How do I get the Personality API and MCP server?
Create an API key on the developer dashboard. The API and MCP server are credit-based: you pay per profile created and per read, re-reads in the same billing cycle are free, and the prompt endpoint costs nothing. Larger volumes and enterprise terms go through sales.
