FAQ
What does Crystal Premium include?
The full version of Crystal's products. In Personality: the full results of every assessment, full PDF downloads, unlimited retakes, and your full profile published. In Story: a personalized plan every day, the patterns, tensions, and recommendations Crystal sees in your reflections, and unlimited snapshot revisions. In People: full insights for your coworkers, relationships, and groups, full PDF downloads, and custom company branding. $49 a month, cancel anytime.
What can I do for free?
In Personality, take every assessment once, get a summary of every result, and share your basic profile with the people who follow you. In Story, every reflection, every day, is free, and so are your plans, habits, and notes, and the headline of every snapshot. In People, get the basics of every comparison, your workplace and groups, and a sentence about you and each coworker. In Career, your job search, Role Preview, and your companies. In Future, every Vision and every goal. None of it expires.
Is there a free trial?
Crystal has no timed trial. Every assessment, reflection, goal, and plan is free for good, with no card and no clock. Premium adds the full version of each product.
What is the difference between the Team Assessments tiers?
Starter is the place to begin if you have a small team to assess. Basic fits regular use, and Pro is for teams that don’t want to think about limits. Pro also includes personality curriculums and resources for running your own personality workshop. Every plan is billed annually, and the limits cover the full year.
What happens if I cancel?
Everything you made stays yours, and your assessments, reflections, goals, and plans stay free. You go back to a summary of each result, the basics of each comparison, and the headline of each snapshot, and Crystal stops writing your personalized daily plan and the patterns it sees. Resubscribe anytime and pick up where you left off.
Can my company buy Premium for the whole team?
Yes, that is Crystal Premium Team: $49 per seat, per month, the same price as Premium. An admin buys it from billing settings in the app, seats assign as members join, and every member's full profile is shared with the team by default.
Does Premium include Buyer Profiles, Team Assessments, or the Personality API?
No. Buyer Profiles, Team Assessments (for your teams or clients) and the Personality API are sold separately, and none of them requires Premium. Buyer Profiles are set up per team, Team Assessments starts at $500 a year, and the API starts with self-serve credits.

How do I get the Personality API and MCP server?
Create an API key on the developer dashboard. The API and MCP server are credit-based: you pay per profile created and per read, re-reads in the same billing cycle are free, and the prompt endpoint costs nothing. Larger volumes and enterprise terms go through sales.
