Ways to purchase Huntress
Huntress is channel-first, and most teams purchase Huntress through a partner, which is recommended. Either way you get the same platform and the same 24/7 SOC wrecking hackers on your behalf — what changes is who handles the deployment, day-to-day management, and where your pricing and packaging comes from.
Through an MSP
An MSP runs Huntress for you — the rollout, the alerts, the escalations, the tuning, all of it. When purchasing through an MSP there are no Huntress-required minimum seat counts. Same SOC, plus a team that owns the day-to-day and includes Huntress as a key component of a total solution.
Through a Reseller or a Referral Partner
Rather run it in-house? Purchase Huntress where you make your other technology purchases. When purchasing through a reseller there is a Huntress-required 50 minimum seat count per product. Our SOC still hunts, investigates, and stages remediations around the clock, while you handle deployment, acting on what the SOC sends over, and integration it into your workflows.
Pricing FAQs
I still have some questions, who can I reach out to?
Fill out the form on this page to book a meeting with our team to discuss pricing.
What is your free trial process like?
Our free trials are the same fully featured version as our paid products, including 24/7 SOC support. If you decide to buy, no need to redeploy—eliminating dual efforts!
How can I estimate my costs with Huntress?
Huntress has straightforward, transparent pricing, based on the number of endpoints (EDR), identities (ITDR), data sources (SIEM), and learners (SAT). For our most accurate pricing, fill out the form to schedule a demo so we can assess how Huntress fits into your organizational goals.
What is the standard contract structure?
1. MSPs:
Term: Standard term is 12 months.
Billing: Monthly, billed in arrears based on deployed usage.
Mid-term changes: You may increase your minimum commitment mid-term to access a lower rate. Doing so restarts the 12-month term from the new signing date.
Overages: Usage above your committed minimum is not currently auto-billed and is handled manually.
2. Direct Customers:
Term: Standard term is 12 months.
Billing options:
Monthly: Billed monthly based on total deployed usage. Example: If your license minimum is 250 and 275 endpoints are deployed, you are billed for 275.
Annual: One-time annual payment for the committed minimum, with monthly billing for overages. Example: If your license minimum is 250 and 275 endpoints are deployed, you are billed monthly for the 25 overage endpoints.
Mid-term upgrades: You may increase your deployed usage during the term; overages are billed according to your selected billing option.
Overages: Overages are not currently auto-billed and are processed manually.
3. Resellers:
Term: Standard term is 12 months.
Billing options: Annual.
Overages: Not currently auto-billed and handled manually.
Where can I purchase Huntress?
Huntress is a channel-first company. We work with thousands of partners globally. We try to make it easy to purchase through your preferred partner, be it a managed service provider, technology reseller, or the Azure Marketplace. If you only source products directly, we can support that, too if necessary.
Do you offer multi‑year terms for price protection?
No. If this is a requirement, talk to your Huntress account team to see what we can do based on the business justification. Use the form at the top of the page to request a meeting if you’re unsure who your rep is.
How does Huntress pricing compare to other MDR providers?
Huntress is designed and priced for organizations of all sizes. Our simple per‑unit pricing includes 24/7 SOC monitoring and remediation—no feature gating into higher tiers and no add‑on fees to “unlock” response.
Are there any setup or onboarding fees with Huntress?
There are no separate setup or onboarding fees with Huntress — with the right global permissions, an IT admin can usually deploy the entire Huntress platform in under 1 hour.
Does Huntress have partner pricing?
Yes—Huntress offers programs for both Managed Service Providers and Value‑Added Resellers. All offerings are fully managed and backed by our 24/7 SOC with simple, predictable per‑unit pricing. To learn more or get pricing, fill out the form at the top of this page, and our team will reach out.
What does "Managed" include?
All of our managed products include our 24/7 human SOC to investigate threats and stage remediations as part of the price, not an add-on. What you own is deploying Huntress, acting on our SOC's recommendations, and integrating into your security stack.
Can I buy Huntress direct?
We recommend you buy Huntress through your MSP or your preferred technology reseller. That being said, it is possible to purchase Huntress directly. There is a Huntress-required 50 minimum seat count per product when doing so and you will be responsible for deployment, portal monitoring and management, responding to incident reports from our SOC, and tech integrations.
