FAQ
The questions that will decide your purchase
Everything people ask us before they pay — answered here, no fine print.
Talk to our team →
What's the difference between a Landbot chat, a WhatsApp conversation, and an AI chat?+
• A Landbot chat is a structured conversation between your bot and an end user, following a rule-based flow. To interact with a bot, a user starts a session through specific actions such as clicking buttons, using keywords, or interacting with triggers and decision points with predefined responses.
• A WhatsApp conversation follows Meta’s messaging rules. When an end user sends your bot a message, a 24-hour messaging window opens, during which you can exchange messages freely. After 24 hours of inactivity, only pre-approved Message Templates can be sent, which may incur additional charges.
• AI chats are handled fully or partially by AI agents. This allows your bot to understand user intent and provide personalised, real-time responses rather than relying solely on a predefined flow. AI chats are tracked and billed separately from WhatsApp and Landbot chats.
What happens when my 14-day trial ends?+
You're automatically moved to Free, the free plan. No charge — we don't ask for a card to try it. Your agents and your data stay put.
Which currency do I pay in, and can I change or cancel?+
In euros if you bill from the EU and in dollars everywhere else — we detect it automatically. And you can upgrade, downgrade or cancel in two clicks from your account, no retention calls. Annual billing comes with 20% off.
Am I locked into a 12-month contract?+
No. Monthly plans are month to month and you cancel whenever you want. The annual plan is a 12-month commitment only because it carries the 20% discount — your choice, never a requirement to use WhatsApp or any other channel.
Do you use my chats to train AI? Are you GDPR compliant?+
Your chats aren't used to train models and aren't sold to third parties. We're a European platform with GDPR, SOC 2 and a DPA available on request.
Which AI model do you use, and how do you stop it inventing answers?+
Answers are grounded in your own knowledge base, not in what the model thinks it remembers. And you can combine button-based flows for the critical paths with AI only where it helps — the hybrid that keeps you in control. All included, on every plan.
Are human handoff and integrations included?+
Handing a chat to a person with full context is included from Starter, with a shared inbox for your team (each person uses a seat). HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, Google Sheets and webhooks are in Professional and up.
I can't build it myself. Do you build it for me?+
Yes: with Agent Studio our team designs, builds and runs your agent — and then hands it over for you to maintain if you want. It's included in Business and available as a service on any plan.
