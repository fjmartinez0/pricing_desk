Common questions
Everything you need to know about credits, plans, and coverage.
How does the fourteen-day trial work?
Choose Essentials, Growth, or Scale in Billing and approve your subscription. Your trial includes 14 days of credits (half your plan’s monthly amount). Need more? Start your paid plan early from Billing. One trial per organization, for accounts that have never paid.
When will I be charged?
Nothing is charged during the trial. When it ends, your plan bills monthly, or the full year upfront on annual plans, plus taxes.
How do I cancel my trial?
Cancel from Billing before the trial ends and you won’t be charged. Your account moves to Free, and unused trial credits stay available.
Is the Free plan different from the trial?
Yes. Free needs no credit card. The trial gives you a paid plan for 14 days and needs a payment method or Shopify billing approval.
What counts as a conversation credit?
One credit is consumed when Alhena's AI resolves or assists a customer interaction. Spam, irrelevant queries, and immediate human handoffs that don't involve AI resolution cost zero credits.
What can I do with optimization credits?
Optimization credits power actions that improve your AI visibility. A PDP optimization (1 credit) analyzes a product page and generates recommendations for better AI engine placement. FAQ generation (1 credit) creates AEO-optimized FAQ content with schema markup. A blog brief (2 credits) produces a full content outline designed to earn AI engine citations. These are included with your plan each month.
What happens if I go over my plan limits?
You're never cut off. If you exceed your plan's conversation or optimization credits, additional actions are billed as overage credits at $1.20 each. Conversations cost 1 overage credit. Optimization actions cost 3-5 overage credits depending on the type. You can set alerts in your dashboard so there are no surprises.
Which AI engines do you monitor?
Paid plans monitor all major AI engines including ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini. We add new engines as they gain consumer adoption. The Free plan monitors one engine.
Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades apply at the next billing cycle. Annual plans can be upgraded mid-cycle; contact us for annual plan changes.
Do unused credits roll over?
Credits reset each billing cycle and do not roll over. If you consistently have unused credits, you may be on a larger plan than you need.
I need more than 1,200 conversations a month.
Contact us for Enterprise pricing. We'll build a custom plan with volume discounts, a dedicated CSM, daily training, custom integrations, and a fully managed AI visibility program.
