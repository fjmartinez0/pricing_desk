FAQs
What is FrontStream?
Founded in 2007, FrontStream is a fundraising and donor management platform that has helped over 31,000 nonprofits, schools, and socially-minded corporations simplify fundraising events and campaigns, charity auctions, corporate giving programs, and payments.
Who uses FrontStream?
Nonprofits, schools, charities, and corporate CSR teams use FrontStream to manage peer-to-peer fundraising, auctions, donor data, event ticketing, online giving, workplace giving, and payments.
Can my organization use FrontStream?
FrontStream works with schools, nonprofits, and corporations based in the United States and Canada. All nonprofits are verified by their U.S. EIN (Employer Identification Number) or Canadian Charity Registration Number.
Can I run peer-to-peer campaigns on FrontStream?
Yes, FrontStream has robust features for custom peer-to-peer donation pages, team fundraising, and participant tracking. Learn more about peer-to-peer fundraising >
Does FrontStream support virtual and hybrid events?
Yes, including virtual auctions and galas, live streams, and hybrid giving campaigns. Learn more about virtual and hybrid events >
Can I create custom donation forms?
Yes, users can customize and brand mobile-friendly donation and event forms. We securely process donations by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and more. And we offer “Donor Covers the Fee” functionality.
What is BiddingForGood?
BiddingForGood is FrontStream’s charity auction platform and community. Together we’ve raised $600M (and counting!) for schools and nonprofits through 45,000+ BiddingForGood charity auctions.
Can I promote my charity auction to a wider audience?
Yes, customers can open their charity auction to BiddingForGood’s 2M+ member community. Nonprofits that open charity auctions to the BiddingForGood community raise 37% more per auction on average!
Does FrontStream have donor management tools?
Yes, FrontStream offers donor CRM features like contact records, donation history, segmentation, communication logs, and reporting.
Can I track campaign performance and donor activity?
Yes, real-time analytics and dashboards are included for tracking donations, participants, emails, and more. Learn more about donor management >
Is payment processing included?
Yes, FrontStream processes $1.4B+ annually. We’re Level 1 PCI Compliant, and offer secure processing for donations, ticketing, and auction payments. We also work with over 1,700 companies to integrate payment forms, simplify multi-location payments, and pay less in processing fees.
What payment methods are accepted?
FrontStream accepts payment by credit/debit cards, ACH, Apple Pay, and Google Pay, depending on configuration.
Are there transaction fees?
Yes, we pride ourselves on fee transparency. We also offer Donor Covers the Fee, which donors will cover ~50% of the time. Contact Sales to have a candid conversation.
What is the Frontstream Global Fund?
FrontStream Global Fund is a Donor-Advised Fund (DAF) to help small nonprofits quickly process funds without underwriting or a merchant account.
Can FrontStream integrate with my other systems?
Yes, FrontStream products integrate with Constant Contact, Mailchimp, QuickBooks, Google Pay, Apple Pay, ACH, SailPoint, Gusto, Bambora, Pinch, Stripe, and Global Impact. API access may also be available. Book a tour to learn how FrontStream integrates with your existing tools.
What is ApproveForGood?
ApproveForGood is an online platform that helps businesses streamline in-kind donation requests, manage inventory, and report on impact. FrontStream acquired ApproveForGood in 2015, and last year we processed over 220,000 in-kind donation requests.
What kind of support is available?
FrontStream has a help center, email and phone support, onboarding assistance, and training resources. If you ever need an extra set of hands, our support team can also build your campaign page, or travel onsite to help run fundraising events. Contact Sales to learn more.
