Questions about pricing
What is a credit, and how many will I need?
One page is one credit. Ten rows of data is one credit. You pay whichever is more, so a list page with 50 products costs 5 credits, and a page that returns two fields still costs 1. Every run shows its cost in your dashboard before it goes.
Why do some sites cost more?
Sites like Amazon, LinkedIn and eBay have heavy anti-bot protection, so robots there run through residential proxies. Pages on those sites start at 2 to 10 credits instead of 1. You can see the full list in the credit guide.
Why did my deep scrape cost more than I expected?
Pulling a list is cheap. Opening each item is not. Taking 100 products off a listing page costs 10 credits, and opening each of those 100 product pages for detail costs 100 more. Same products, ten times the cost, depending on whether you need what is on the individual pages.
What happens if I run out of credits?
Your robots pause rather than charging you by surprise. On an annual plan you can top up from 1,000 credits at a time, or move to a higher volume whenever you need to. On a monthly plan, moving up a tier is how you add capacity.
Do unused credits roll over?
No, credits reset at the end of your billing cycle. Annual plans are the way around that. You get the full year of credits upfront, so you can spend them when a project needs them rather than pacing them month to month.
How do I stop monitoring from using up my credits?
Two settings control it. Frequency decides how often a robot checks, from every five minutes to monthly, and every check is a run. Change size decides how much a page has to change before it counts, from any change through to gigantic. A page checked daily runs about 30 credits a month. Hourly is closer to 720.
What counts as a website?
One domain, including everything under it. So yourcompany.com/pricing, help.yourcompany.com and jobs.yourcompany.com all count as one. If you delete every robot for a site, the slot frees up for another. Extra websites are $4 a month on Personal and $2.40 on Professional, billed annually.
Do I need a paid plan to use the API?
No. The REST API, webhooks and bulk runs are on every plan, including the free one. What changes between plans is how many credits you have to spend through them.
Is the free plan really free?
Yes, and it stays free. It is not a trial, so there is no credit card and no expiry date. You get 50 credits a month and the same platform everyone else runs on.
Can I change plans later?
Yes, from your billing page. Changing plans charges the new plan in full straight away and starts a fresh billing period, so mid-cycle moves are best made when you are ready to use the new volume.
What happens when I outgrow Professional?
Professional runs up to 360,000 credits a year. Above that is Premium, where the volume is set around what you actually need and the cost per credit keeps coming down. Premium also comes with our team building and maintaining the robots for you, so most people move for the service as much as the volume.
Do you offer discounts?
Annual billing saves 20%, which works out to $216 a year on Professional and $120 on Personal at the same volume. There are further discounts for non-profits, educational institutions and startups. Get in touch and we will sort it out.
What is your refund policy?
Every paid plan comes with a 60-day refund guarantee. If it is not working for you, tell us within 60 days and we will refund you.
