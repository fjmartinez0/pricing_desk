Frequently Asked Questions
Is Beacons really free?
Yes. The Free plan has no monthly cost and includes your link in bio, store, media kit, Smart Reply and 50 email sends a month. Beacons takes a 9% seller fee on Free plan sales, so we only make money when you do.
How does annual billing work?
Annual plans are billed once a year and include 2 months free: Creator is $100 a year, Creator Plus $300 and Creator Max $1,000.
Does every plan include Beam?
Yes. Beam, your AI teammate, is on every plan. Daily AI credits set how much you can ask: 30 on Free, 300 on Creator, unlimited on Creator Plus and Max.
What’s the difference between plans?
Creator adds a free custom domain, advanced SEO tools, 300 daily AI credits and 500 emails a month. Creator Plus adds 0% seller fees, Buy Now, Pay Later, tracking pixels, unlimited courses, memberships and email. Creator Max adds Google Workspace, white-glove onboarding, priority support, an NFC card and VIP access to Beacons events.
Are there seller fees?
Free and Creator have a 9% Beacons seller fee on store sales and affiliate commissions. Creator Plus and Creator Max have 0%. Payment-processing fees may still apply.
