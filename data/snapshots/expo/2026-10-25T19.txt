Do you offer annual billing and ACH payment options?
ACH is available only for annual payments from Enterprise plan customers. Contact us for more information.
What if I'm on the Free plan and need just a few more builds this month?
If you are on a Free plan and have used your monthly quota of free builds and updates, upgrade to the Starter plan.
Does Expo store my credit card information?

Do leftover monthly build credits roll over?
No, credits do not roll over or accrue. At the beginning of each billing period, the included resources that come with your plan will be reset back to their original allotments.
What are monthly active users (MAUs) and how are they calculated for updates?
A monthly active user (MAU) is someone who downloads at least one update during the billing period. Multiple downloads by the same user count as a single MAU. Users who only check for updates but do not download anything are not counted as MAUs.

Can I cancel at any time?
Yes. You can cancel at any time. Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period. Any overage incurred during that time will be billed at the end of the period. After that, you won't be charged again.
