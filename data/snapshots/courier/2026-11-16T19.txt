Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Full billing rules are in the billing docs. There’s also a plain-text version of this page.
How much does Courier cost?
Courier has three plans. Developer is free and includes 10,000 sends a month, and Business is pay as you go at $0.005 for every send. Enterprise has custom volume pricing below $0.005 per send. You pay per send, not per seat or per channel.

What counts as a "send," and do test messages count?
One send is one template sent to one recipient. A template that delivers by email, SMS, push, Slack, and in-app still counts as one send, so adding channels never raises your Courier bill. Sends from Test and custom environments count the same as Production.

What happens when I go over 10,000 sends, and how do I estimate my bill?
Add a credit card to move to Courier’s Business plan. Business charges $0.005 for every send, including the first 10,000, so 50,000 sends a month costs $250 and 200,000 costs $1,000. There are no seat or channel fees.

Does Courier offer volume or enterprise discounts?
Yes. The Enterprise plan offers custom volume pricing below $0.005 per send, plus RBAC, observability integrations, a dedicated Slack channel, and an enterprise SLA. Pricing is quoted by sales based on your volume.

Can an AI agent send notifications through Courier, and what does it cost?
Yes, at the same price as any other send. Agents use Courier’s MCP server, CLI, or SDKs, all included on the free Developer plan. On Business every send is $0.005, with no per-seat or per-agent fee.
