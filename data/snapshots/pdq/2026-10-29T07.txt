FAQs
Is there a minimum purchase for PDQ Connect?
Yes, there is a minimum purchase requirement for PDQ Connect. PDQ Connect starts at 100 devices per year. Additional devices may be added.
What’s included in the free PDQ Connect trial? Are there any limits?
Your 14-day free trial includes full access to PDQ Connect’s core device management features for up to 250 devices, no credit card required.
Some advanced automation and integration capabilities are limited during the initial trial to prevent misuse. See a current list of what is available and what is not in the trial experience.
If you need to evaluate these features, we're happy to enable full access — just reach out at sales@pdq.com.
Do you offer any discounts for PDQ Connect?
Yes, we offer these discounts:
10% off for nonprofits and schools
Current PDQ Deploy & Inventory customers
Flexible billing for MSPs
Scaled pricing for 500+ devices
If any of these discounts apply, contact us or schedule a demo to get started.
How am I charged when I add devices to PDQ Connect?
Your bill is based on your plan and the number of devices in your account. Add more devices at any time. Once a month, we'll automatically add your new devices to your plan, prorated for the remainder of your subscription period.
Check out this article for more information.
Do you have options designed for managed service providers (MSPs)?
Yes. We offer flexible pricing and billing options for managed service providers. Contact us to discuss your setup, and we’ll recommend a plan that fits.
How do subscriptions to PDQ Connect work?
Subscriptions to PDQ Connect are annual and renew automatically. You can cancel anytime by emailing connect-sales@pdq.com. Your plan will stay active until the end of your current billing cycle.
I already use PDQ Deploy & Inventory. Are there discounts available?
Yes! If you're already using PDQ Deploy & Inventory, we’ll work with you to bundle pricing and features that fit your environment. Find out more about Connect — or send us a quick email to talk to a product expert about your options.
