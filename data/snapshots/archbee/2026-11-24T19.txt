Frequently asked questions
Have a different question and can't find the answer you're looking for? Reach out to our support team by sending us an email and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
What is a team member vs a reader?
A team member is a person who contributes to your documentation, most likely somebody from your team. You are charged for extra team members. A reader is a person who reads the content - they can be an external person or your team member. You are not charged for readers.
What is a space?
A space in Archbee is the way you group documents under a single scope: a team, a project, or a topic. Spaces can be private to your team or published externally to a custom domain. Spaces are a foundational element of your product knowledge management. They act as a top-level container for documents you create in Archbee. Each space has a document tree, and each document can have 0-n children documents.
What use cases do you support?
We currently support a variety of use cases, to cover all your external and internal documentation needs: product documentation, developer documentation, user guides, onboarding & training, process documentation, design documentation, customer support documentation.
What are the included tokens for the AI add-on? How is overage charged?
The AI add-on includes different token allocations based on your plan:
•Growing - 20M tokens/month
•Scaling - 40M tokens/month
•Enterprise - 100M tokens/month
Additional tokens beyond your allocation are charged at $1.2 per 1M tokens. Tokens are used for AI-powered features like question answering and content generation (Writer Agent, AI Write or AI Translations). Setting soft limits and hard limits for token consumption is available.
Note: if overage exceeds 1.2B additional tokens in a billing cycle, we may invoice for the overage before the next regular billing cycle.
What AI model are you using?
We are using OpenAI models hosted on Microsoft Azure OpenAI service for customer data privacy reasons. Depending on the use case, the API availability and reliability, we select from the latest available models (with fallbacks) to ensure a great question-answering experience.
We do not train anything on the data, and Microsoft states they do not train anything on the data.
Read Microsoft terms and conditions here:
https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-foundry/responsible-ai/openai/data-privacy?tabs=azure-portal
And here: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-foundry/openai/overview
Can I export my data?
Absolutely — your data and your docs are yours. Easily export your docs individually or export your whole organization's content. Documents are exported in a Markdown format, or rendered PDF files.
What security practices does Archbee follow?
Archbee is SOC 2 Type 2 and GDPR certified. Check out our online security portal at https://security.archbee.com. To get the report, contact us at support@archbee.com and will need you to sign an NDA before providing it.
How secure is Archbee?
We regularly test our security with outside firms and security freelancers. We also use daily backups, encryption at rest and in transit, a PCI Service Provider Level 1 payment processor (Stripe), and secure Azure & AWS services to keep your data safe.
What type of support is offered?
We offer customer support through our live chat, or at support@archbee.com. We try to answer any questions in less than 30 minutes, but depending on your plan it might take up to 48 hours. If a ticket requires engineering time, it can take 72 hours or more.
Where's my data located?
Your data lives in AWS data centers: us-east-2 (Ohio) for our US data centers and eu-central-1 (Frankfurt) for our EU data centers. We use CDNs and caches so that you & your users get a fast user experience no matter where you are in the world.
How are you using my data?
We don't use your data to train any machine learning models. We collect usage events that help us improve our product over time. We will never share any of your data with third party companies or governments.
How does the Archbee Cloud-Prem work?
We deploy our software to an AWS subaccount that you own and pay for. On our side, there is a dedicated DevOps engineer that makes sure you are up and running with high uptime similar to our own SaaS cloud, regular updates and customizations if necessary. This is possible only on custom Enterprise plans starting at $100K per year and minimum 3-year contracts. Talk to our sales team for details at sales@archbee.com.
Who owns the documents my team/company creates?
Your company does. At any point in time, at your request, we will send you an exported file with any kind of data we hold and/or destroy it and all of its backups.
What happens when the free trial ends?
When the 14-day free trial ends, you can upgrade to a paid plan. A free offering is not available at the moment.
Can I add or remove team members?
You can add or remove team members at any time from account settings > team management section. If you add members, you are billed the prorated amount right away.
Can I change plans after I sign up?
Yes, you can switch from monthly to annual payments or the other way around.And you can also upgrade from one plan to another, or downgrade to a lower plan, should your needs change.You can make the change directly in account settings, or contact support@archbee.com
What happens if I am not satisfied with my Archbee purchase?
If you're not satisfied with Archbee - product or service, we offer a refund within your first paid month. No questions asked.
What do I need in order to use it?
Archbee works in any modern browser (or you can download your MacOS, Windows or Linux apps) - create an account to start a free trial.
Do you offer discounts?
Yes, we offer discounts for several organizations:
• Free Growing plan for open source software;
• 50% discount for non-profit organizations and for educational purposes;
• Our startup program: 50% off on any of our plans for 2 years, available for early-stage companies — apply here.
Any other questions?
Feel free to email us at support@archbee.com with any other questions. We'll be happy to answer!
