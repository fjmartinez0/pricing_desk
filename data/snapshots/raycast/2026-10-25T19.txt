FAQs
You’ve got questions. We’ve got answers
Why is Raycast free for personal use?
We think of Raycast as a productivity layer that everybody should use to get work done faster. To make it accessible, we don't charge for the individual plan. The plan covers all built-in extensions, such as Clipboard History, Calendar or Window Management and provides access to all public extensions built by our community.
Is the Raycast Free and Team plans available for organizational use?
Yes, the Raycast Free and Team plans are available for both personal and organizational use, including in corporate environments. You are not required to upgrade to a paid tier to use the free features for your organization.
Is it possible to get the Pro plan without AI?
No, we only offer Pro plans that include AI. However, you can choose to turn off AI completely in Settings, which will disable all AI features.
What is free and paid in Raycast AI?
Raycast AI is included in paid Pro plans. Eligible new accounts can start a free Pro trial with access to Pro models. Advanced AI models are included with Pro+ and Max, and are not part of the trial.
What are AI credits?
AI credits are how AI usage is measured in Raycast. Every paid plan includes a monthly amount of credits that renews each month. Unused monthly credits roll over for one extra billing cycle, so each monthly grant expires 60 days after it's granted. Top-up packages add credits that expire 12 months after purchase and require an active paid subscription to use. Free trial credits expire 7 days after they're granted, and if you upgrade during the trial, they're used before your monthly credits. Cancelling doesn't change expiry dates, but you'll need to resubscribe to use any remaining credits, as AI isn't available on the Free plan. Each model spends credits at its own rate, so cheaper models go further. Your balance is shown in Raycast Settings under Account. Learn more in the manual.
Is the free Team plan a trial?
No, the free plan for Teams doesn't expire, and isn't a trial. You can use this plan for as long as you want within your organization, however you are limited to just 30 shared Snippets, 30 shared Quicklinks, and 5 Commands across all of your Extensions.
How many seats do I get in a Team plan?
There is no minimum or maximum amount of seats that your team can have. You add users to your plan when you need to add to your team, and pay per user, per month.
Can I have personal Extensions and Team Extensions?
Yes, you can create personal Extensions that are personalized to you, and speed up your productivity, and have team Extensions that can be shared around in your organization for everyone to use.
Team Extensions will be available in the Store command, behind a filter for your Team. This is where all of your Team Extensions will live, and where you can install them.
Can I be part of multiple organizations?
Yes, there is no limit to how many organizations you can be part of. It's important to remember to avoid using multiple email addresses for different organizations, as you need to log in to the app in order to access the shared Snippets, Quicklinks and Extensions.
Does Raycast have a Student Program?
Yes, Raycast offers a Pro plan 50% discount to verified students. You can apply for it by completing this form. Please note that the student discount only applies to the Raycast Pro plan.
