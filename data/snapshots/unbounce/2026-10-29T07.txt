FAQs
How does the free 14-day trial work?
Generate more leads and sales with Unbounce—the leading landing page platform built for marketers and agencies. Your 14-day trial gives you full access to the plan of your choice, no credit card required. You won’t be charged during your trial, and you’ll only be asked for payment details if you decide to continue with a paid plan afterward.
We’ll check in with you during your trial and email you again near the end to remind you it’s wrapping up. When you’re ready, you can choose a plan by navigating to Account Management → Subscriptions.
If I decide Unbounce is right for me, what do I need to do?
It’s simple. When your 14-day trial ends, you can enter your credit card details to subscribe to the plan that best fits your needs. You can also subscribe at any time during your trial; you don’t have to wait the full 14-day trial period to sign up.
What else should I know about Unbounce’s free trial?
Your trial includes full functionality across Unbounce’s features so you can explore everything the platform offers. Certain limits apply—like a single user, no custom domains, and some traffic restrictions—but you’ll still have everything you need to build, publish, and optimize landing pages.
Can I change my plan?
You can change from a Build, Experiment, or Optimize plan by logging into your Unbounce account, going to “Account Management”, and selecting “Subscriptions”. You can upgrade to a higher-tier plan at any time, but switching to a lower-tier plan will only take effect at the end of your current billing cycle (monthly or annually).
How does hosting work? Can I connect my own domain?
Our Build, Experiment, and Optimize plans all include free hosting through Unbounce. You can also publish your landing pages directly to your own domain or WordPress website (in which case you’ll be responsible for hosting).
Does Unbounce have an MCP?
Yes, Unbounce’s MCP connects to AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT and is available on all plans, including during your free trial.
What counts as a conversion? What counts as a visitor?
We log a conversion when someone completes your assigned conversion goal on a landing page. Common conversion goals include clicking a call to action button or submitting a form.
A visitor is a person who views your landing page at least once in a given month. Using cookies, we ensure each visitor only counts once toward your limit—no matter how many times they return.
What happens if I exceed my conversion or traffic limits?
If it looks like you’re close to exceeding your plan’s conversion or traffic limits, we’ll make sure to give you a heads-up by email and in-app messages so you’re never left guessing. In the event that you go over your limit, your pages will keep running as expected, and any overage fees will be charged as a one-time amount based on your extra usage. You can also choose to upgrade to a higher plan if you need more room.
Learn more about what happens if you exceed limits on your plan.
How can I access Professional Services?
Professional Service offerings such as personalized onboarding, page migrations, landing page redesign, and conversion-focused consults are available for purchase with any plan, at any time. You can learn more about these services here.
Is Unbounce GDPR compliant?
Businesses are responsible for including GDPR (and/or CASL) compliance in their marketing strategy. The best way to ensure GDPR compliance is to consult with legal counsel.
Unbounce can help you align with GDPR best practices through functionality you can apply to your campaigns. For example, using our landing page builder, you can easily add checkbox fields to your forms that allow visitors to consent to your privacy and data storage practices.
