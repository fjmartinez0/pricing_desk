Frequently Asked Questions.
Do you offer enterprise-solutions?
Absolutely. We support large-scale operations with shared team workspaces, SSO, and volume discounts. You get the flexibility to use our DIY tool for speed, or leverage our parent company, Prodege, for complex full-service projects.
Do I need to pay a subscription?
No. You can create an account and build surveys for free. You only pay when you launch and collect responses. Start small or scale big – you’re in complete control.
What’s included with a free account?
Everything. Unlimited teammates, access to our suite of AI research tools, 24/7 live human support, cost calculators, and the ability to draft preview surveys. Only pay when you launch your project.
What if I just want to test out Pollfish?
Good news – creating and setting up surveys is completely free! You only pay at checkout, once you decide to launch. That means you can build a draft survey, explore targeting and see your exact pricing in real time… all without spending a cent.
