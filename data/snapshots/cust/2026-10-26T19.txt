Pricing questions
Which plans include Teammates?
Growth, Scale and Enterprise include a Teammate for your customers on iMessage and WhatsApp. Each customer conversation uses 5 credits from your plan’s shared monthly allowance. Meet Teammates.
What are credits?
Credits are the shared monthly allowance for AI work in Cust. Teammate conversations on Growth and above, customer questions, meeting prep, emails, workflow conditions, agents, health scores, and interaction analysis draw from the same balance.
How much work do credits cover?
Using the full allowance for one activity, Growth’s 4,500 credits cover about 900 Teammate conversations. For other activities, 1,000 credits cover about 125 customer questions, 250 personalized emails, 100 meeting preps, 50 agent runs, 5,000 health scores, or 33,000 interaction analyses. These estimates use the rounded average costs shown above.
Can I buy more credits?
One-time credit packs are available in Cust: 1,000 credits for $400, 4,500 for $1,500, 10,000 for $3,000, or 25,000 for $6,250. Top-up credits are used after your monthly allowance and do not reset each month. All prices exclude VAT and other applicable taxes.
How do seats work?
Every plan includes unlimited seats. CS, support, RevOps, and leadership can all work in Cust under the same plan price.
What integrations are included?
Foundation includes email, calendar, CRM, and call transcript connections. Growth adds support tickets, product issues, and Slack conversations as customer context. Scale adds data warehouse connections and CRM field updates. Enterprise adds custom imports. View supported integrations, or request a new data source for $500 per integration.
What add-ons are available?
Shared Inbox & Ticketing and Custom Apps each cost $500 per month. Additional integrations cost $500 per integration.
How does billing work?
Choose monthly or annual billing. Annual prices are shown as monthly equivalents, billed once per year, and save 20%. All prices exclude VAT and other applicable taxes.
