Got questions
Frequently asked questions
How do discovery and the trial work?
Signing up opens a 3-day discovery period, no card: test orders only. To take real payments, choose a plan and add a card: its 15-day trial starts. Nothing is charged before it ends, and we bill nothing on trial sales.
Which plan should I choose?
Above €8,000 excl. VAT in monthly sales, Growth costs you less than Launch. Above €30,000 excl. VAT, Scale costs less than Growth. If you’re just starting out, pick Launch: there’s no point paying for a cart or ten users before your first sale. We never switch your plan for you.
What happens when the trial ends?
The first payment is charged at the price and date shown. We email you 7 days before. Stopping before then costs nothing and pauses your account, as when discovery ends without a plan. Nothing is deleted, your pages stay online, a plan reopens everything.
Are prices shown with or without VAT?
Our prices are shown excluding VAT. The price next to it includes French VAT at 20%. Launch, for example, is €39 excl. VAT a month, or €46.80 incl. VAT.
I don’t charge VAT. How are my sales counted?
Then your sales excl. VAT are simply the amounts you collect. The included volume and the rate apply to those amounts.
What counts as a sale?
Sales collected through PayFacile during the billing month. A refund made in the same period is deducted.
Who counts as a user?
Everyone with access to your account, you included. Launch includes 1 user: you. Growth includes 3 and Scale 10, counting the account owner.
What are AI credits for?
PayFacile’s AI features run on credits. Each plan includes a monthly allowance: 100 on Launch, 400 on Growth, 1,200 on Scale, renewed on your plan’s renewal date. If you need more on Launch or Growth, move up to the next plan. On Scale, talk to us: a Custom plan starts from what you need.
How do I move to a higher plan?
The change is immediate and the amount due is added to your next invoice. Sales collected before the change stay billed at your old plan’s rate, and your new plan’s included sales start from zero (Scale takes no commission). Only the account owner can change the plan.
And to a lower plan?
The change applies at once, with a prorated adjustment for the current period: you see the amount before you confirm. It is only possible when your team fits the lower plan’s number of users, so remove the extra members before you ask for the change. Your plan never changes automatically.
Is there a commitment?
Not on monthly plans: you can cancel at any time. Annual billing commits you for one year.
How does annual billing work?
You pay for your plan a year at a time, at the price of 10 months: 2 months free. Launch, for example, is €390 excl. VAT a year. Included sales stay monthly and the rate above them is billed every month (Scale takes no commission). Your AI credits are renewed every month. Annual billing commits you for one year: if you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the current year, with no refund. During the year you can move up to a higher annual plan; a lower or monthly plan is possible at renewal. You choose annual billing on your Billing page, when your 15-day trial starts or later.
I’m already a customer. Is my plan changing?
Your current plan won’t change without advance notice. We’ll write to you before any change.
What about payment gateway fees?
Stripe and GoCardless have their own fees. They take them from the payments you collect, and those fees don’t go through PayFacile. Their pricing is published on their websites.
What about e-invoicing in France?
Since 1 September 2026, every VAT-registered company in France has to be able to receive electronic invoices. Issuing them, and e-reporting, follow on 1 September 2027 for SMEs and micro-businesses. Today, PayFacile generates an invoice for every sale, automatically. On your checkout page and in your account, you can record a business customer’s SIREN and VAT number, and we check that VAT number. We’re preparing for the 2027 obligations.
How can I test PayFacile before I sell?
When you sign up, a test payment gateway is already connected. You make fake payments with the pre-filled test card numbers and see exactly what your customers will see.
