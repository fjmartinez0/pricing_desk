What you get with the Printify Premium plan
The Printify Premium plan costs $39 a month, or $24.99/month on the yearly plan, billed as $299/year. The Premium plan takes up to 20% off most products in the Printify Catalog. On Premium, Printify adds discounts of up to 33% on select newly launched Catalog products and up to 33% on neck labels, one of its custom branding features.
Printify Connect is included in the Printify Premium plan. With it, the Printify Support Team handles eligible order-related issues on your behalf.
The Printify Premium plan raises store connections from five to ten per account, includes 25 AI mockup attempts per day, and gives early access to new features. At the full 20% rate, the Printify Premium plan pays for itself at about $195 of monthly product costs on monthly billing. On yearly billing, your break-even point is about $125.

Get the plan that works for you
Is Printify free? Yes – the Free plan costs $0, with no listing fees or hidden charges.
How much does Printify cost once orders arrive? Printify fees cover the production and shipping costs for each sale. You pay them only after a customer orders.
The Printify pricing plans below compare Free, Premium, and Enterprise. All three include order fulfillment through the same Print Provider network.
Unlike Printify Free, the Premium plan has a monthly fee – and its product discounts offset that subscription cost as your order volume grows. The Premium plan cost starts at $39 a month.

The Printify advantage
Every Printify plan, Free included, runs on the same Print Provider network, Catalog, and quality checks.
No risk, all reward
Printify’s Print Providers make each product only after customers order it. Sellers pay production costs once a sale comes in, which limits financial risk when they launch new custom products.
Maximum profits
You keep full control of retail prices. Compare Print Providers on each product to find the base price and shipping costs that fit your profit margin.
Save time and money
We handle inventory, printing, packaging, and shipping so you can focus on your business.
Fast, secure orders
With Printify Choice, Printify routes each order automatically to a top-scoring Print Provider at a competitive price, and Choice orders work with Express Delivery.
Over 2,000 products
Launch new product lines in a flash from the largest catalog in Print on Demand, and sell to audiences across the globe.
Top quality
Every Print Provider is vetted and ranked on print quality, speed, and shipping. If an order arrives with a manufacturing error, Merchant Protection covers a free reprint or refund within 30 days.
