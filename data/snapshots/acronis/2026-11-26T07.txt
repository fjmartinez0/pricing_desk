Looking for help?
Frequently Asked Questions
What subscription plans are available for Acronis True Image?
Acronis True Image is available in three different subscription plans, Essentials, Advanced, and Premium. Each plan gives users access to backup tools, ransomware protection and system recovery features, with higher tiers offering advanced cybersecurity, such as anti-malware protection, and cloud storage options up to 10TB.
Essentials
Basic local backup, cloning and ransomware protection.
Advanced
Local and cloud backup with up to 500GB of cloud storage and antimalware protection.
Premium
Comprehensive data protection with up to 10TB of secure cloud storage anti-malware protection.
More information can be found here.
Can I use Acronis True Image on multiple computers?
Yes, depending on the license you purchase, you can use Acronis True Image on one or up to five devices. Make sure to review the details on the purchase page to confirm the number of supported machines.
Do I get updates and upgrades with my subscription plan?
Yes, with your subscription plan you get all updates and new version releases during the active subscription period.
What payment methods does Acronis accept?
Acronis supports all major payment methods including credit and debit cards, PayPal and localized payment methods where available. Payments are processed securely to protect your personal information and payment details.
Do you offer refunds if I am not satisfied?
Acronis provides a standard refund policy based on region and product type. If you experience issues or are not satisfied with the product, you may request a refund within the applicable period by contacting Acronis Support or this Knowledge Base article.
How do I download Acronis True Image after purchase?
Once your purchase is complete, you will receive an email with a direct download link and your activation key. You can also sign into your Acronis account at any time to download the installer.
Can I download the software again if I reinstall my system?
Yes, simply log into your Acronis account to access your purchase history, download the software again and reactivate your license.
How do I activate my Acronis True Image license?
During installation, you will be prompted to sign in to your Acronis account or enter your product key. Once verified, your license is automatically activated and synced to your device.
What happens if I upgrade my hardware or switch to a new computer?
You can move your license to a new device by signing out of your Acronis account on the old machine and activating it on the new one. Some plans allow multiple installations, so make sure to check your plan details.
Do I need an internet connection to use Acronis True Image?
An internet connection is required for activation, updates, cloud backup features and cybersecurity protections.
What support options are included with my Acronis True Image subscription plan?
You have access to Knowledge Base articles, the forum and online chat, email and phone support.

For subscription owners, there is free, 24-hour technical support. Refer to the Technical Support Site to find the appropriate support contact.
