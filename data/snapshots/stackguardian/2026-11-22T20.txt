FAQ
Pricing questions
Anything else? Talk to us.
What is included in the 14-day trial?
Every feature, on any paid plan. No credit card needed. At the end of the trial, pick a plan or stay on Free forever.
What happens if I go past my plan’s limits?
Your existing deployments keep running. To add more, an admin adds extra deployments (active workflows) at $1.15 a day or cloud accounts at $1.12 a day, prorated daily. Extra AI credits cost $200 per 1,000. Example: a team on Standard with 25 deployments pays $359 plus 5 × $1.15 × 30 = $531.50 a month.
What is a deployment (active workflow)?
A deployment is one workflow in StackGuardian. For Terraform or OpenTofu, one workflow manages one state file, so 20 state files means 20 deployments. Helm, Ansible, CloudFormation and script workflows count the same way: one workflow, one deployment. It counts as active from its first deploy until it is destroyed, however often you plan, apply or change it, and admins can mark it inactive. Users, runs and managed resources are never counted.
What do AI credits pay for?
AI credits, called SGCredits in the product, pay for all AI work in StackGuardian: generating and codifying infrastructure, remediating findings, troubleshooting failed runs and answering questions about your estate. These AI features are powered by Kyro, our AI agents. Meet Kyro. Each plan includes AI credits every month, and you can add more at $200 per 1,000.
Do private runners cost extra?
No. Private runners run in your own cloud or data center and connect to StackGuardian. Deploy as many as you need without an extra license. They are included from Pro.
Who gets the Standard plan free?
Startups with less than $1M in funding, open-source projects and non-profits. Tell us about your team and we will set you up.
What do migration credits include?
On Pro and Enterprise, a StackGuardian migration expert works with your team to plan the move from Terraform Cloud or another IaC platform, set up your first workflows and onboard your teams.
How do I get early access features?
Join the Founding 50, a small group of design partners who get early access and planned features first and help shape what ships next. Apply to join, and we will set up a 30-minute call.
How can I pay?
By credit card, through AWS Marketplace or Azure Marketplace, or through selected partners. Marketplace purchases can count toward eligible cloud commitment programs.
