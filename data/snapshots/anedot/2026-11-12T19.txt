FAQs: Anedot pricing and fees
What is Anedot’s pricing structure?
Anedot charges no monthly fees, no setup fees, and no hidden fees. You only pay a small percentage of each donation processed.
How much are Anedot transaction fees for nonprofits, ministries, and schools?
For 501(c)(3) organizations:
Credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal: 3.3% + $0.30 per transaction
ACH/bank draft: 0.3% + $0.30 per transaction
Bitcoin: 1.0% per transaction
Donors can optionally cover these fees, so your organization receives maximum impact from each gift
What are Anedot transaction fees for campaigns?
For 501(c)(4) organizations:
Credit/debit cards, ACH, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal: 4.0% + $0.30 per transaction
Bitcoin: 1.0% per transaction
Donors can optionally cover these fees, so your campaign receives maximum impact from each gift
Are there any contracts, setup fees, or limits on users or forms?
No contracts or setup fees. Anedot provides unlimited team members, unlimited donation and lead forms, and unlimited transactions.
Can donors cover the transaction fees on behalf of my organization?
Yes! If you enable Donor Covers Fees, donors can choose to cover the processing fees of the donation.
Can I get a demo or try Anedot with no obligation?
Yes, you can request a demo or sign up and start using Anedot with no contracts or monthly fees.
Is Anedot secure for processing donations?
Anedot is PCI DSS compliant, providing enterprise-grade security for donor data and payments.
Do I pay for integrations with other software or CRMs?
No, all integrations, including Hubspot, Salesforce, Mailchimp, and many more, are included with no additional fees.
Where can I get help with Anedot pricing and account setup?
Anedot offers U.S.-based customer support and an extensive help center for organizations and donors.
