Yes, you can change your plan whenever you like. You can do this by going to
your profile and clicking the subscription icon.

What happens after my free trial ends?
After your trial ends, we will set your subscription to the free plan. You
will be notified in time to be able to upgrade to another plan.
Do you offer lifetime plans?
Yes, we offer lifetime plans. Get in touch
with us to get a quote on the lifetime plans.

How long is the free trial?
You can use ploi.io for free on the pro plan for 5 days, no credit card is
required, and there are no obligations!

Do you offer refunds?
We are not here to take advantage of your money. We want you to be able to
enjoy our product. If ploi is not right for you, that's okay. Just get in
touch with us within 14 days so we can offer you a refund.
Do you offer 24/7 support?
No, we do not offer 24/7 support. However we will always do our best to get
back to you as soon as possible.
Does your pricing include server fees?
No, our pricing does not include server fees. Ploi is separate pricing and a
standalone product.
What happens to my servers and sites if I cancel my subscription?
Ploi will never do anything to your servers
or sites based upon your subscription. No services will be stopped, no servers or sites will be halted.
