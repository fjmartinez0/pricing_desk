How many users do I need?
Unleashed is a collaborative tool that works best when every department in the business is sharing accurate, real-time inventory data.
We recommend the following teams have user licences:
Purchasing / Logistics
Sales
Warehouse / Operations
Finance
Marketing
Customer Service / Admin
It’s even a sensible idea to give your external accountant access to your Unleashed account to make financial reporting and tax time easier for everyone. Your accountant can get a free Unleashed account here.
Are there any other charges?
There are two other optional extras not already covered on this page:
File Storage: Our Core and Pro plans give you 10GB of file storage for things like images, product specs and warranty docs. You can buy 10GB of additional storage for $29/month.
Additional API Calls: You can buy additional API bundles:
Core (250k calls): $159
Pro (500k calls): $239
You can learn more about API calls here.
Sales orders: Each plan has a free allocation of sales orders. If you go over the allocation on any given month an additional allocation of orders will be applied to your account in the months following.
Extra users: You can buy additional user licenses for $69 (Lite & Core) and $89 (Pro).
Integrations: You can buy additional integrations for $39
Can I cancel my Unleashed subscription at any time?
If you’re on a monthly plan you can cancel by giving us 30 days’ notice. If you’re on an annual plan, you can cancel at the end of the 12-month term by giving us the same notice. While we’ll be very sad to see you go, it’s easy. Simply get your Account Owner to put your cancellation in writing.
Can I change plans easily?
Yes, you can change plans or add users or add-ons to your account at any time within Unleashed. Some changes may only take effect after billing or renewal dates.

How does onboarding work?
Every new customer gets a dedicated onboarding specialist to help them get set-up. The cost of onboarding packages varies depending on your inventory management software sophistication, any custom integrations you need and desired go-live date.
You can learn more about Onboarding Support here.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?
We love supporting nonprofits and offer all registered charities and NFPs a discount off their software subscription and add-ons. See more here.
What currencies do you bill in?
We invoice and bill in NZD, AUD, USD or GBP.
