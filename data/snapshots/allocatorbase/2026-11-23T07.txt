Frequently asked
Common Questions About Pricing
How much does AllocatorBase cost?+
AllocatorBase offers four products: Allocator Intelligence Data at $750/month ($9,000/year), a Capital Formation Audit at $2,500 one-time, Infrastructure Implementation starting at $25,000, and an Annual Partnership at $85,000/year that includes everything. All pricing is published. No placement fees, no AUM share, no hidden per-seat charges.
Do I need the audit before subscribing to data?+
No. Each product is independently selectable. Many firms start with the data subscription and add the audit later. Others start with the audit to identify infrastructure gaps before subscribing. There is no required sequence.
Is AllocatorBase a third-party marketer (3PM)?+
No. AllocatorBase charges advisory fees only — no placement fees, no AUM share, no success fees. We are fully aligned with your outcomes, not compensated by allocators or based on capital raised.
What is included in the Capital Formation Audit?+
The audit is a 2–3 week diagnostic that includes a Capital Formation Diagnostic Report, Distribution Infrastructure Scorecard, Capital Probability Map, and Prioritized Implementation Roadmap. Most leadership teams discover 2–4 infrastructure gaps they were not aware of.
Do you replace Dakota, AdvizorPro, or FINTRX?+
AllocatorBase complements and can replace existing data providers. The data subscription covers 120,000+ contacts across 30,000+ firms; 15,155 firms have SEC-corroborated registration records. The infrastructure layer — CRM architecture, scoring models, pipeline automation — is unique to AllocatorBase and not offered by any data provider in this category. See all comparisons.
What CRMs does AllocatorBase integrate with?+
AllocatorBase integrates natively with HubSpot and Salesforce with bidirectional sync, custom objects, pipeline stages, and automation workflows. The data subscription includes basic CRM export. The implementation and partnership tiers include full white-glove CRM architecture.
What is the Annual Partnership?+
The Annual Partnership ($85,000/year) is a full-service engagement that includes everything AllocatorBase offers: Allocator Intelligence Data subscription, Capital Formation Audit, Infrastructure Implementation, ongoing optimization, quarterly strategy reviews, and priority support. One fee, one engagement, everything included for 12 months. It's designed for firms that want the entire capital formation operating system installed and managed.
Can I cancel the data subscription?+
Yes. The Allocator Intelligence Data subscription is ongoing and can be cancelled anytime. There are no long-term contracts required. Annual billing ($9,000/year) is available at the monthly rate.
