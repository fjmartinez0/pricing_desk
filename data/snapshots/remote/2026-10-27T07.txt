Questions we actually get asked.
How much does an EOR cost?
Remote's Employer of Record (EOR) pricing starts at $599 per employee per month.
How much does a PEO cost?
Remote’s Professional Employer Organization (PEO) pricing starts from $99 per employee per month.
Are there setup, onboarding or platform fees?
No. There are no setup or onboarding fees, and the Remote Platform is included at no extra cost on every plan.
Do you require deposits or accruals?
We don’t charge a standard deposit or deposit fee. In certain circumstances, Remote may require a refundable reserve payment to cover potential employment-related obligations. This is not a fee—the funds remain yours and are credited or refunded when they’re no longer needed. If a reserve applies, we’ll confirm the amount during onboarding and disclose it before payment is required. Any unused balance is credited or refunded when it’s no longer required, subject to any outstanding payments..
How are currency conversions and exchange rates handled?
When your billing currency differs from the local currency used for payroll or statutory payments, Remote applies the Remote FX rate. We set one rate for each monthly billing cycle and show it in your invoice breakdown before payment. That rate remains locked for the cycle, so Remote—not you—absorbs any exchange-rate movements during that period, giving you predictable payroll costs without later true-ups or adjustments.
