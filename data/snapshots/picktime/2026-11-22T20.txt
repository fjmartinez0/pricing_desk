FAQs
Frequently Asked Questions
Is Picktime’s Free plan a trial?
No. Picktime has a Free plan that costs $0 and includes unlimited appointments within the plan’s feature limits. You can sign up without a credit card and continue using the included scheduling features. Upgrade to Starter or Pro when you need higher limits or additional features.
What counts as a user in Picktime pricing?
In Picktime, a user is a team member added to your business account, not a customer booking an appointment. Free and Starter each include up to three team members, while Pro starts with 10. Customers booking through your Booking Page don’t count toward your user limit.
Why does Pro cost less per user than Starter?
Pro has a lower per-user rate but starts with a larger team size. With annual billing, Starter costs $3 per user per month for three team members, while Pro starts at $2.25 per user per month for 10 team members. Pro’s lower per-user rate therefore comes with a higher starting subscription cost.
What happens if I cancel my Picktime subscription?
After your paid Picktime subscription ends, your account moves to the Free plan. Your existing appointments, customers, and services remain in your account. You then lose access to paid-only features and higher plan limits.
Can I export my customer contacts from Picktime?
Yes, Picktime lets you export your customer contacts as a CSV file on both Free and paid plans. Open Customer, select Import/Export from the three-dot menu, choose Export Customer, and click the export icon to download your list. You can keep a copy of your contacts for your records and refer to it whenever needed.
Are SMS reminders included in Picktime pricing?
SMS reminders use credits and are available on Starter and Pro. Starter includes 50 SMS credits per month on monthly billing or 600 per year on annual billing. Pro includes 100 per month or 1,200 per year, respectively. Each SMS uses one credit, and additional credits can be purchased when needed.
Does Picktime offer monthly and annual billing?
Yes, Picktime offers monthly and annual billing for Starter and Pro. The per-user rates shown with annual billing are monthly equivalents, with the subscription billed annually. Use the Monthly and Yearly options to compare billing rates before subscribing. For current plan details, see Picktime pricing.
